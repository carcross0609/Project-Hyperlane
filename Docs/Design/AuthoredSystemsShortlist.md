# Authored Systems Shortlist (OQ-2 proposal)

The 15 systems proposed for full authoring in Phase 2 (Framework §1.2's
authored tier; Roadmap Phase 2 deliverable "10–15 authored systems").
Selection rules from Bible 01 §5 and Bible 04 §4: cover the famous
chokepoints, include every allegiance state, and every world must answer
its three questions (war / economy / a nobody's day).

**Status: PROPOSAL** — becomes a decision (and closes OQ-2) when Carson
approves, at latest before Phase 2 content starts.

## Tier 1 — first five (earliest testing: one spine, maximum contrast)

| System | Archetype (Bible 04) | Allegiance | Why first |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coruscant** | Ecumenopolis | Loyalist | The capital; P10 demand pole of every trade loop; Senate/Temple content anchor |
| **Corellia** | Shipyard + Trade Nexus | Loyalist | The double-superhighway chokepoint (Bible 01 §4.3); shipbuilding economy |
| **Malastare** | Trade Nexus (fuel) | Loyalist, leaning | Mid-Hydian chokepoint; fuel producer; Dug/Gran politics texture |
| **Ryloth** | Mining, P5 override | Contested | The poor heavy world (Bible 04 §5); early front; Corellian Run adjacency |
| **Nal Hutta** (+ Nar Shaddaa) | Hutt Fief + Trade Nexus moon | Hutt Sovereignty | The third power's throne; smuggler's moon = underworld career anchor |

Rationale: these five give the first playable galaxy a working trade spine
(Coruscant ↔ Corellia ↔ Malastare), a war zone (Ryloth), and an
outside-the-law pole (Nal Hutta) — every Phase 3 loop and most Phase 4
stories are testable inside them.

## Player-start candidates (Bible 09 §1)

1. **Tatooine** — maximally "nobody," Hutt-fringe contract surface, no war
   overhead at the doorstep; the classic for a reason.
2. **Malastare** — start *on* the trade spine, war-adjacent, busier board.
3. **Nar Shaddaa** — underworld-forward start; strongest smuggler pull.

Recommendation: Tatooine default; the other two become natural starts if
DR-16's fixed start ever loosens to backgrounds.

## Checks

- **Allegiance coverage:** Loyalist ×8, Seceded ×1, Contested ×3, Neutral
  ×1, Hutt ×1, Unaligned ×1 — every state visitable. (Seceded is thin;
  first post-launch authoring candidate: Raxus or Muunilinst.)
- **Chokepoint coverage:** Corellia, Eriadu, Malastare, Nal Hutta/Triellus
  — all Bible 01 §4.3 names present.
- **Archetype coverage:** 9 of 12 archetypes exercised (missing: Agriworld,
  Energy World, Fortress-pure — acceptable; catalog tier supplies them
  statistically, and the miss list seeds the next authoring round).
- **Scalability:** 15 systems ≈ the Roadmap budget; each cites an
  archetype + overrides, so authoring cost is bounded and uniform.
