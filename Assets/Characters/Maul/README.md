# Maul

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maul)

Maul was a Force-sensitive Dathomirian Zabrak male who was apprenticed to the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, and after his defeat by the Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi, he established himself as a crime lord during the Clone Wars and the Imperial Era. The renegade Sith Lord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Jedi and his former Sith Master.
