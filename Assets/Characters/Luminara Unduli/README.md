# Luminara Unduli

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Luminara Unduli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luminara_Unduli)

Luminara Unduli was a Force-sensitive Mirialan female Jedi Master during the final days of the Jedi Order. She commanded the 41st Elite Corps of the Grand Army of the Republic, and was the master of Padawan Barriss Offee. Together, they fought in numerous battlefields of the Clone Wars, before Offee turned against the Jedi Order and bombed the Jedi Temple, much to the horror of Unduli.
