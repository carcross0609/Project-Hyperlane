# Bail Prestor Organa

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bail Prestor Organa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bail_Prestor_Organa)

Bail Prestor Organa of the planet Alderaan was a human male Alderaanian politician, statesman, and revolutionary leader who championed democratic and progressively-minded causes during the decades he represented his homeworld in the Senates of the Galactic Republic and the Galactic Empire. Born Bail Prestor, the once-adventurous young man forsook his familial legacy to marry Breha Organa, Alderaan's queen. Together, the two fought to keep Alderaan strong, with Bail being elected to the Galactic Senate after the Invasion of Naboo in 32 BBY. In the Senate, he built a reputation through his commitment to honesty and justice, forming ties with like-minded senators such as Mon Mothma of Chandrila, Onaconda Farr of Rodia, and Padmé Amidala of Naboo and becoming the Jedi Order's best ally in the Senate. Organa and his faction worked tirelessly to keep the Republic's democratic values alive amid the burgeoning Separatist Crisis.
