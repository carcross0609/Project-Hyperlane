# Galen Marek

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Galen Marek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galen_Marek)

Galen Marek (pronounced /ɡeɪlən ˈmæɹɛk/) was a Force-sensitive human male who became an accomplished warrior during the Imperial Era. Trained in secret by the Sith Lord Darth Vader, and given the codename "Starkiller", Marek was one of Vader's deadliest assassins—but later turned to the light side just shortly before his death. Marek was the son of two Jedi Knights, Kento and Mallie Marek, born on the Wookiee homeworld of Kashyyyk in the aftermath of the Clone Wars. Following his mother's death, Kashyyyk was invaded by the Galactic Empire as a target of the Great Jedi Purge, where Kento was confronted and slain in a lightsaber duel by Vader, who discovered the fugitive Jedi's son in the process. Gauging the orphan's raw strength in the Force, he abducted Marek and raised him to embrace the dark side of the Force.
