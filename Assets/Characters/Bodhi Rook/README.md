# Bodhi Rook

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bodhi Rook](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bodhi_Rook)

Bodhi Rook was a human male from Jedha who served as a cargo pilot for the Galactic Empire prior to the Galactic Civil War. He graduated from the Terrabe Sector Service Academy, serving as a shuttle pilot after. In 1 BBY, Rook defected to the Alliance to Restore the Republic, intending to deliver the Rebellion a message from the Imperial scientist Galen Erso. However, he was captured by the Partisans, a different rebel group, who brought him to Jedha for interrogation. After being rescued by Alliance Intelligence officer Cassian Andor and Jyn Erso, he fought alongside a group of rebels during the Battle of Scarif, where he was killed. His actions paved the way for the Rebellion's success of obtaining the Death Star plans.
