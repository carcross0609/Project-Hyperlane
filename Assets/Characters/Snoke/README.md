# Snoke

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Snoke](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snoke)

Snoke was a Force-sensitive humanoid male who ruled the First Order as Supreme Leader during the New Republic Era and was considered to be a enigmatic seeker of arcane lore who possessed a dark nature from the Unknown Regions. Likely unaware of his true nature, Snoke was an artificial genetic strandcast created on the planet Exegol by the resurgent Dark Lord of the Sith and Galactic Emperor Darth Sidious as a proxy and the product of cloning experiments. Though Snoke possessed an independent mind, he was obliviously influenced by Sidious from afar in an effort to achieve galactic dominance once again.
