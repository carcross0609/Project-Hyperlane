# Ajunta Pall

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ajunta Pall](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ajunta_Pall)

Ajunta Pall was a male Human Jedi Master and the leader of the Dark Jedi who were exiled from the Jedi Order and the Galactic Republic following the Hundred-Year Darkness. Upon his arrival on Korriban, he became the first Dark Lord of the Sith. He was regarded as the manifestation of Typhojem, the Left-Handed God by the Kissai. Long after his death, his spirit was redeemed by the Jedi Knight Revan during the Jedi Civil War.
