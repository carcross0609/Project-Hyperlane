# Nute Gunray

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nute Gunray](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nute_Gunray)

Nute Gunray, a Neimoidian male, was a business executive and politician who served the Trade Federation conglomerate, first as its senator in the Galactic Republic Senate and later as its Viceroy, during the final decades of the Republic Era up until his assassination. Alongside his allies, Gunray rose to prominence after the ill-fated Eriadu Conference left a power vacuum in the Federation's leadership. Secretly backed by the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, Gunray—who had already secured the title of Viceroy of the Trade Federation—took control of the conglomerate while Lott Dod took the seat in the Senate.
