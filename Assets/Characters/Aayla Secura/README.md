# Aayla Secura

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aayla Secura](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aayla_Secura)

Aayla Secura ( pronounced /'eɪlʌ sə'kʊrə/), a Force-sensitive Rutian Twi'lek female, was a Jedi Master during the waning years of the Galactic Republic, serving with distinction as a Jedi General during the Clone Wars. Prior to the start of the war, Secura learned the ways of the Force as the Padawan of Jedi Master Quinlan Vos, being awarded the rank of Jedi Knight sometime later. With the outbreak of civil war between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems in 22 BBY, Secura and her fellow Jedi supported the Republic by becoming officers within the newly-formed Grand Army of the Republic; Secura received the rank of general and took command of the 327th Star Corps, Commander Bly serving as her second-in-command.
