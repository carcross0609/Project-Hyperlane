# Yoda

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yoda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda)

Yoda was a legendary Jedi Grand Master who led the Jedi Order through the time of the High Republic, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Sith, and during the transformation of the Galactic Republic into the Galactic Empire. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Yoda trained generations of Jedi, ultimately serving as the Jedi Order's Grand Master. He played an integral roles in defending the Republic during the Clone Wars, survived Order 66, lived to pass on the Jedi tradition to Luke Skywalker, and unlocked the path to immortality.
