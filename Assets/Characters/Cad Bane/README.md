# Cad Bane

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cad Bane](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cad_Bane)

Cad Bane, formerly known as Colby, was a Duros male bounty hunter and mercenary in the galaxy whose career spanned from the fall of the Galactic Republic, the reign of the Galactic Empire and into the era of the New Republic. Once a street rat, Bane first became involved in criminal activity when he joined the gangster Lazlo's gang. After Lazlo's death, Bane grew in infamy after he killed both a marshal and his former friend Niro as revenge, eventually becoming a bounty hunter.
