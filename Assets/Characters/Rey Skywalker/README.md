# Rey Skywalker

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rey Skywalker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rey_Skywalker)

Rey Skywalker, also known simply as Rey, was a Jedi member of the Resistance during the war against the First Order. The human woman was a scavenger on the planet Jakku, but her life was changed by tumultuous events in the last days of the New Republic Era. Rey's decision to help the droid BB-8 set her on a course to discovering the Force awakening within her, leading her to seek out Jedi Master Luke Skywalker for training. Despite her commitment to the Jedi Order and the light side of the Force, Rey felt the pull to the dark side due to her anger as well as the adversarial relationship she had with Ben Solo, a fallen Jedi who joined the First Order as a warrior named Kylo Ren; they shared a unique bond as a dyad in the Force.
