# Asajj Ventress

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Asajj Ventress](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress)

Asajj Ventress was a Dathomirian Nightsister who lived through the fall of the Galactic Republic, becoming an accomplished warrior in the Clone Wars. She was taken from her family as an infant on the planet Dathomir and enslaved by pirates on the war-torn world of Rattatak, where the stranded Jedi Knight Ky Narec found Ventress and took her under his wing. They defended the local populace against warlords and pirates, but Narec was killed before training was complete, and the Force-sensitive girl hunted Rattatak's warlords in rage. Bereft of guidance, Ventress was discovered and trained as an acolyte by the Sith Lord Darth Tyranus, publicly the Separatist leader Count Dooku. She led Separatist droid forces against the Republic's clone army and the Jedi who defended its democracy during the Clone Wars, clashing blades against Jedi Generals Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker.
