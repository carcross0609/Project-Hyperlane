# Rodian skip-trawler

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rodian skip-trawler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rodian_skip-trawler)

A Rodian skip-trawler was a high-maintenance freighter that was considered the most temperamental of its kind in the Outer Rim Territories during the Cold War between the reconstituted Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic.
