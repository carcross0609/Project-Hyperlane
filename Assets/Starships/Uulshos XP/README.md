# Uulshos XP

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Uulshos XP](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uulshos_XP)

The Uulshos XP, or simply XP, was a model of yacht. The wreckage of one such starship was found by scavengers in the Graveyard of Ships on the desert planet of Jakku.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Uulshos XP
- **Manufacturer:** Uulshos Manufacturing
- **Type:** Yacht
