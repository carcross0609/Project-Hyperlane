# Robida Colossus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Robida Colossus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Robida_Colossus)

The Robida Colossus was a Ganathan starship that intercepted and rescued the Millennium Falcon after it was chased into a dangerous nebula into Ganathan space. Like most of Ganathan technology, the ship was powered mainly through the power of steam.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Robida Colossus
- **Affiliation:** Ganathan
- **Captains:** Captain Ussor
