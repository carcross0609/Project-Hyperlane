# JT-731 Broadhorn transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JT-731 Broadhorn transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JT-731_Broadhorn_transport)

The JT-731 Broadhorn transport, simply known as the Jabiim freight transport, was a model of utilitarian freight transport manufactured by Corellian Engineering Corporation used during the Imperial Era. Two of these transports were used by the Hidden Path, a resistance underground network that smuggled Jedi and other Force-sensitives away from the Galactic Empire, due to the fact that refuse and maintenance craft were often overlooked by Imperial patrols. The ship had data and power conduits located on the outer hull to maximize internal cargo space. In 2 BBY, one of these ships was used to transport Rebel soldiers to Base One on Yavin 4 to be initiated into Massassi Group.
