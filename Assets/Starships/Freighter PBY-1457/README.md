# Freighter PBY-1457

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Freighter PBY-1457](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freighter_PBY-1457)

Freighter PBY-1457 was an undercover freighter used by New Republic Intelligence Service agent Belindi Kalenda during her mission to Corellia. In 18 ABY, it was ambushed upon coming out of hyperspace and eventually crashed in the sea.
