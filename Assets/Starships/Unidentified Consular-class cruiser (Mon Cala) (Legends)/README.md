# Unidentified Consular-class cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Consular-class cruiser (Mon Cala)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Consular-class_cruiser_%28Mon_Cala%29/Legends)

This Consular-class cruiser was used by the Galactic Republic and was in service to Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker and Senator Padmé Amidala to travel to the watery planet known as Dac in a effort to stop a civil war between the Mon Calamari and the Quarren after the assassination of King Yos Kolina
