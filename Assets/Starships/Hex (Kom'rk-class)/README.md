# Hex

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hex (Kom'rk-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hex_%28Kom%27rk-class%29)

The Hex was a modified Kom'rk-class fighter/transport used by the former Sith Lord Maul and his forces during the early Imperial Era. Between 17 BBY and around 13 BBY, the Hex was the main mode of transport used by the Shadow Collective while they were hiding out on the Mid Rim world of Janix during Maul's effort to rebuild his syndicate.
