# CX-03

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CX-03](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CX-03)

CX-03 was a cargolift vessel in the Republic Navy during the Mandalorian Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Mandalorian warrior clans. In 3963 BBY, as the Mandalorians threatened the planet Serroco, CX-03 was attached to Admiral Saul Karath's Battle Group Serroco intended to protect the world. When the Mandalorians arrived, they devastated Serroco with nuclear missiles, and CX-03 was lost. The ship's destruction was later noted in an edition of The Admiral's List, an official publication issued by the Republic Navy, along with two other cargolift vessels: CX-09 and CX-14.
