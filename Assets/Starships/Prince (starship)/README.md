# Prince

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prince (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prince_%28starship%29)

Prince was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport used by Mugaari pirates. It was part of an arms deal between the Rebel Alliance and the pirates in a cargo loading area located in the Tungra sector. The Imperials learned of this deal and attacked. The Transport, along with Destion and Dawn were destroyed in the battle while attempting to attack the frigate Fogger.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Prince
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Width:** 7.8 meters
- **Height:** 5.8 meters
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Mugaari pirates
