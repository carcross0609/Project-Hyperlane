# Nebulon-K frigate

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nebulon-K frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nebulon-K_frigate)

The Nebulon-K frigate, also referred to as the Nebulon-K, was a model of frigate. They were equipped with turbolasers and hyperdrives, but had problems with their combustion-shielding. Nebulon-Ks were used as capital ships by the First Order Navy and could carry a flight of TIE fighters on moorings. A Nebulon-K was among the First Order starships sent to the Uvoss system after receiving a distress signal from the Hevurion Grace, the personal yacht of First Order-aligned Senator Erudo Ro-Kiintor.
