# Com 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Com 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Com_1)

Com 1 was a Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle used by Bothans during the Galactic Civil War. Imperial forces lead by Darth Vader were tasked with capturing 3 Muurian transports Botani. Tan Maarek Stele leading an Assault Gunboat squadron while Com 1 was to be captured under orders from the Secret Order of the Empire. The shuttle attempted to flee, but along with the transports was disabled and captured.
