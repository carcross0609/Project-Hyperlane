# Pride of the Senate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pride of the Senate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pride_of_the_Senate)

The Pride of the Senate was a Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser, commanded by Captain Soontir Fel during his third tour of duty in the Imperial Navy. It was one of three Dreadnaught-class cruisers present at the Battle of Nar Shaddaa. The Pride of the Senate took fairly minimal damage in the battle. When a retreat was ordered, Captain Fel withdrew very slowly so that he could recover all the remaining TIE/LN starfighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pride of the Senate
- **Model:** Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser
