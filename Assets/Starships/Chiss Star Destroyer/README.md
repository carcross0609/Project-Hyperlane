# Chiss Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chiss Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss_Star_Destroyer)

The Chiss Star Destroyer was a kilometer-long capital ship used by the military of the Chiss Ascendancy, first observed during their conflict with the Killik Colony in 35 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Chiss Star Destroyer
- **Manufacturer:** Chiss Ascendancy
- **Line:** Star Destroyers
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,000 meters
- **Countermeasures:** Cloaking device
- **Armament:**
  - Light turbolasers
  - Heavy turbolasers
  - Light megamasers
  - Heavy megamasers
  - Tractor beam projectors
- **Complement:**
  - Nssis-class Clawcraft
  - AirStraekers
  - Landing craft
- **Role:** Destroyer
- **Affiliation:**
  - Chiss Ascendancy
  - Empire of the Hand
