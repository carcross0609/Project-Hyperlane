# Oldskep

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oldskep](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oldskep)

The Oldskep was a bulk freighter that was used by the company Imperial Mining, Ltd. during the Galactic Civil War, which was named after an IML executive. After IML established a base on the planet Alluuvia in the Elrood sector, the Oldskep was laden with supplies and dispatched to the Alluuvian system, accompanied by the starship Berea-3 and eight TIE fighters. However, on arrival in the system, the IML fleet was attacked and defeated by a Rebel Alliance ship and the Shepherd-class Herdship Varnay.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Oldskep
- **Type:** Bulk freighter
- **Complement:** 2 laser cannons
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Mining, Ltd.
