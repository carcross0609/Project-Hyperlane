# Bebel Gas (Xiytiar-class transports)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bebel Gas (Xiytiar-class transports)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bebel_Gas_%28Xiytiar-class_transports%29)

Bebel Gas was a group of three Xiytiar-class heavy transports the presumably belonged to the company. The ships present at Destreg Spaceport where K'Armyn Viraxo's personal yacht, the Highroller, was destroyed by Ace and Emon Azzameen. However K'Armyn had got wind of this and used the yacht as a trap against the Azzameens. Despite this both Ace and Emon escaped the ambush by destroying all of the bounty hunters. Their cargo was tibanna gas.
