# Sivulliq

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sivulliq](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sivulliq)

The Sivulliq was a prototype RK-720 Light Freighter designed and manufactured by the SoroSuub Corporation on Edic Bar, Genarius. The ship was stolen from the manufacturing plant and was eventually recovered two months later by heroes in the Cularin system. However, SoroSuub decided not to put the design into production and so gave the prototype back to the heroes who had rescued it. In their haste, SoroSuub engineers failed to remove a prototype cloaking device from the freighter.
