# WUD-500 star yacht

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [WUD-500 star yacht/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WUD-500_star_yacht/Legends)

The WUD-500 was an outdated CEC star yacht design, with features similar to the freighters of the YT-series. It was also called an N-class freighter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** WUD-500 star yacht
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Star yacht
- **Cost:** 185,000 credits (new), 92,500 credits (used)
- **Length:** 18.5 meters
- **Max Accel:** 1,000 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** x1 (backup x8)
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (2, fire-linked)
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Passengers:** 7
- **Capacity:** 3 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 4 months
- **Role:** Freighter
