# MT dropship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MT dropship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MT_dropship)

The MT dropship was a Corellian Engineering Corporation troop transport used by members of the Galactic Alliance Core Fleet as of 137 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MT dropship
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** MT dropship
- **Type:** Dropship
- **Cost:** 600,000 credits (300,000 used)
- **Max Speed:** 850 km/h
- **Hdsystem:** None
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Hull:** Armored
- **Armament:**
  - Laser cannon battery
  - Concussion missile launchers
  -   Missile payload (24)
- **Complement:**
  - Speeders
  - or
  - Huge walkers (4)
- **Crew:** 15
- **Passengers:**
  - Troops (200)
  - or
  - Supporting troops for heavy vehicles (50)
- **Capacity:** 500 tons
- **Consumables:** 1 week
- **Availability:** Military
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Alliance Remnant
