# Low Altitude Assault Transport/vehicle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Low Altitude Assault Transport/vehicle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Low_Altitude_Assault_Transport/vehicle)

The Low Altitude Assault Transport/vehicle, or LAAT/v, was a Low Altitude Assault Transport variant used by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. Like the Low Altitude Assault Transport carrier, it was designed to transport heavy vehicles, but could house 16 speeders, and had the same loading section as the Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry.
