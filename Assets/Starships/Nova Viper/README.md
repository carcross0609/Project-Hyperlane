# Nova Viper

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nova Viper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nova_Viper)

The Nova Viper was a sleek battle cruiser owned by the bounty hunter Spurch "Warhog" Goa. In 1 BBY he piloted Nova Viper from Level 88 in the Corellian Sector on Nar Shaddaa to Docking Bay 86 in Mos Eisley on Tatooine.
