# Ultimatum

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ultimatum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimatum)

The Ultimatum was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer under the command of Captain Rae Sloane during the reign of the first Galactic Empire. Assigned to Death Squadron, the personal flotilla of Darth Vader, the Ultimatum career was cut brutally short when it suffered a direct collision with an asteroid in the Hoth asteroid belt.
