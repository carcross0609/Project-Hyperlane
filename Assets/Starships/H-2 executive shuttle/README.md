# H-2 executive shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [H-2 executive shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H-2_executive_shuttle)

The H-2 executive shuttle, also known as executive shuttle or executive ship, was a shuttle model that was used by both sides during the Clone Wars. Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine made use of his personal shuttle of the model as a means of escape during the Zillo Beast incident. Separatist Parliament member Mina Bonteri also owned an H-2 executive shuttle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** H-2 executive shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Slayn & Korpil
- **Model:** H-2 executive shuttle
- **Length:** 14.6 meters (47 ft 6 in)
- **Max Speed:** 875 kph (544 mph)
- **Crew:** 1
- **Passengers:** At least 5
- **Role:** Diplomatic transport
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Galactic Republic Navy
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy
  -   Separatist Senate
