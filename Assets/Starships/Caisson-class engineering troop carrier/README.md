# Caisson-class engineering troop carrier

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Caisson-class engineering troop carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caisson-class_engineering_troop_carrier)

The Caisson-class engineering troop carrier was a class of troop transport that was a derivative of the CR-20 troop carrier and was a survivor of the Clone Wars. It was deployed by the Alliance to Restore the Republic in the Galactic Civil War against the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Caisson-class engineering troop carrier
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Troop transport
- **Cost:** 190,000
- **Engine:** Sublight engines (3)
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 3 (Primary)
  - Class 12 (Backup)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Short-range sensor
- **Navigation:** Navigation computer
- **Armament:**
  - Ventral turret-mounted twin medium laser cannons
  -   Port (1)
  -   Starboard (1)
  - Aft-mounted twin auto-blaster (1)
- **Complement:** UEV-M1 Marauder Engineer Squad Carrier (1)
- **Crew:** 3 (1 pilot, 1 co-pilot, 1 comms operator)
- **Passengers:** 40 combat engineers
- **Capacity:** 110
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Alliance Army
