# Orchid

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Orchid (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orchid_%28starship%29)

The Orchid was a starship utilized by the Republic Defense Coalition during the High Republic Era. The vessel was used during a Republic and Jedi Order mission to capture a Nameless creature from a base of the Nihil marauders on the planet Angoth. The Orchid was kept in the hangar of the larger Pacifier-class sector patrol cruiser Invictus while en route to Angoth, separating after arriving above the planet. After the team managed to capture one Nameless, the RDC personnel transported it aboard the Orchid back to Coruscant.
