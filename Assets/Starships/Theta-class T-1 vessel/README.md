# Theta-class T-1 vessel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Theta-class T-1 vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Theta-class_T-1_vessel)

The Theta-class T-1 vessel was a shuttle class manufactured by Cygnus Spaceworks around 980 BBY. Hyperspace-capable and requiring a minimum crew of only one, it was the highest-priced and one of the most luxurious personal shuttles on the market at the time. Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Bane owned and piloted one of these vessels, the Triumph; as did his apprentice, Darth Zannah, who flew in the Victory.
