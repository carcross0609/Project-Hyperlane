# Quinlan Vos's Jedi starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quinlan Vos's Jedi starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos%27s_Jedi_starfighter)

The Jedi Master Quinlan Vos made use of a personal Jedi starfighter during the opening weeks of the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and Confederacy of Independent Systems in 22 BBY. In the days following a bombing on the planet Cato Neimoidia in the Colonies, Vos's starfighter was preparing to launch from a docking bay on the Core Worlds planet Coruscant. When a droid approached to help Vos load spare parts into his starfighter's equipment hatch, he refused the assistance. After winking toward his friend and fellow Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi across the hangar, Vos shut the hatch. By 19 BBY, Vos had started to fly an Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor.
