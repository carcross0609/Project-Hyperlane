# Imperious

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperious (Pellaeon-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperious_%28Pellaeon-class%29)

The Imperious was a Pellaeon-class Star Destroyer in the service of the Fel Empire during the years leading up to the Empire's war with the Galactic Alliance, fought from 127 to 130 ABY. The Imperious served as Grand Admiral Morlish Veed's flagship at the last major engagement of the war, the Battle of Caamas, in 130 ABY, and was later the place of meeting between Veed, Moff Nyna Calixte, and Sith Lord Darth Maladi, as they discussed plans for the future of the Empire.
