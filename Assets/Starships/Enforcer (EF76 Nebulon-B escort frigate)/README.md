# Enforcer (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Enforcer (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enforcer_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Enforcer was an Imperial Nebulon-B frigate during the Galactic Civil War. The ship was sent to protect an Imperial commercial staging area near Bonadan but arrived too late to prevent the destruction of valuable supply containers and freighters by two Rebel Y-wings. Later after the Battle of Yavin, it arrived along with the Star Destroyer Blacken after Keyan Farlander along with an Alliance Stormtrooper Transport Raider stole Imperial food supplies.
