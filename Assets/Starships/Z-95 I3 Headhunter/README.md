# Z-95 I3 Headhunter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-95 I3 Headhunter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-95_I3_Headhunter)

The Z-95 I3 Headhunter was a starfighter produced by the Incom and the Subpro Corporation. The "Improved Model 3" (I3) was one of the most popular models in the Z-95 Headhunter series and saw use on various planets in the galaxy.
