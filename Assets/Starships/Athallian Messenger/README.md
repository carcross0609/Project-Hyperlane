# Athallian Messenger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Athallian Messenger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Athallian_Messenger)

The Athallian Messenger was a courier starship that crash-landed on the planet Tatooine with a cargo of gemstones at some point by 36 BBY. Many treasure hunters subsequently searched for the vessel without success. Sometime by 4 ABY, the Eirrauc traders Daushoroc and Tamoss also attempted to find the Messenger in order to use the profits from the search to liberate other members of their species from enslavement.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Athallian Messenger
- **Role:** Courier
- **Destroyed:** By 36 BBY
