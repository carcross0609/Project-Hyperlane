# Patriot Fist

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Patriot Fist/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Patriot_Fist/Legends)

The Patriot Fist was a Recusant-class light destroyer produced by the Commerce Guild for the Confederate Navy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Patriot Fist
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Hoersch-Kessel Drive, Inc.
  - Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps
- **Model:** Recusant-class light destroyer
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Length:** 1,187 meters
- **Width:** 157 meters
- **Height:** 163 meters
- **Max Accel:** 2,800 G (linear in space)
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2 (backup Class 12)
- **Poweroutput:** 7.7 × 1023 W
- **Power:** Main reactor (8,600 tons of fuel annihilated per second)
- **Sensor:** Electromagnetic sensor antennae
- **Armament:**
  - One prow heavy turbolaser
  - Four heavy turbolaser cannons
  - Six heavy turbolaser turrets
  - Five turbolaser cannons
  - Thirty dual laser cannons
  - Twelve dual light laser cannons
  - Sixty point-defense laser cannons
- **Crew:** 300
- **Passengers:** 40,000 deactivated battle droids
- **Othersystems:**
  - Internal transit shafts
  - Ionization chambers
  - Neutrino radiator
- **Role:** Light destroyer
- **Affiliation:**
  - Commerce Guild
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
