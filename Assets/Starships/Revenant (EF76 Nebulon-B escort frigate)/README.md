# Revenant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Revenant (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revenant_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Revenant was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate that was part of the fleet of Lumiya shortly after the Battle of Saijo. It was capable of carrying 24 TIE/LN starfighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Revenant
- **Model:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Capacity:**
  - 24 TIE/LN starfighters
  - Alpha Squadron
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Lumiya's fleet
- **Owners:** Lumiya
- **Captains:** Captain Valek
