# Black Talon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Black Talon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Talon)

The Black Talon was a Gage-class transport in service with the Sith Empire during the Cold War. It was captained by Revinal Orzik.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Black Talon
- **Model:** Gage-class transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Armament:** Turbolasers (&lt;7)
- **Complement:**
  - Mark VI Supremacy-class starfighter
  - Imperial assault shuttles
- **Crew:** 380
- **Role:**
  - Armed transport
  - Logistical support
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Captain Revinal Orzik
