# Rum Sleg's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rum Sleg's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rum_Sleg%27s_starship)

The bounty hunter Rum Sleg made use of a red-and-gray starship during the Clone Wars. While on a job with fellow hunter Aurra Sing to kill Mira Bridger, a member of the Unseen mining community, Sleg flew the ship over the planet Urubai in pursuit of the Heyblibber Duck, an H-type Nubian yacht on which Bridger was escaping. Sleg fired on the yacht and damaged it, but Bridger's ally Jar Jar Binks was able to jump the ship to lightspeed and escape Sleg.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rum Sleg's starship
- **Armament:** Cannons (4)
- **Role:** Bounty hunting
- **Owners:** Rum Sleg
