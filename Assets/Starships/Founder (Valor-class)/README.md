# Founder

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Founder (Valor-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Founder_%28Valor-class%29)

The Founder was a Valor-class cruiser in the Republic Navy under the service of the Supreme Chancellor. It served as the personal transport of the Supreme Chancellor and their entourage..
