# YT-1210 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YT-1210 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-1210_light_freighter)

The YT-1210 light freighter, also known as the YT-1210 transport, was a class of transport manufactured by Corellian Engineering Corporation and was nearly identical to the YT-1200 transport.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YT-1210 light freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Model:** YT-1210
- **Type:** Transport
- **Cost:** 120,000
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 2 (Primary)
  - Class 16 (Backup)
- **Sensor:** Sensor-laden boom
- **Navigation:** Navicomputer
- **Armament:** Medium laser cannon
- **Crew:**
  - 1 pilot
  - 1 gunner
- **Passengers:** 4
- **Consumables:** 2 months
