# Naigleb

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Naigleb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naigleb)

, Naigleb and Nailati freighters near the Vengeance.]] Naigleb was a group of 6 BFF-1 bulk freighters that, along with sister group Hsiri and Nailati were employed by Airam clan leader Ilay during the Galactic Civil War. The freighters carried warheads and conscripts to the Super Star Destroyer Vengeance. When Ilay was captured by the Rebels she revealed this transaction and B-wings from Rogue and Green Squadron were sent in the Dega system to disable the freighters containing conscripts and destroy the ones carrying warheads. Once the freighters carrying conscripts were disabled Shuttles from Rescue Group were sent to capture them and liberate the conscripts.
