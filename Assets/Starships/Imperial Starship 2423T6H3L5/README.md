# Imperial Starship 2423T6H3L5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial Starship 2423T6H3L5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Starship_2423T6H3L5)

Imperial Starship 2423T6H3L5 was the official designation of a starship used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Galactic War. Piloted by Lieutenant Commander Ina'irolia'kleoni, its transponder codes were stolen by Combo and used by a smuggler captain to board the Imperial treasury ship King's Ransom.
