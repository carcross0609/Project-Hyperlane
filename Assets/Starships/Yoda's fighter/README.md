# Yoda's fighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yoda's fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda%27s_fighter)

Yoda's fighter was a modified Jedi starfighter at one point during the Clone Wars, stationed in the Jedi Temple hangar. Jedi Master Yoda, accompanied by the astromech droid R2-D2, used the starfighter to go on a series of missions of discovery: traveling to Dagobah, to a mysterious planet inhabited by the Force Priestesses, and to Moraband. Similar to the standard Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor used by other Jedi, it had hexagonal panels at the end of its wings, an astromech socket, and a compact, sphere-shaped cockpit.
