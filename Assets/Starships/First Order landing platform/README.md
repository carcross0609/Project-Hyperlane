# First Order landing platform

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [First Order landing platform](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Order_landing_platform)

During the Mission to Ovanis, the First Order used a model of landing platform as a staging area to launch both TIE/fo space superiority fighters and TIE/sf space superiority fighters as well as atmospheric assault landers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** First Order landing platform
- **Armament:** Twin Laser cannons (4)
- **Complement:**
  - At least 2 AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transports
  - At least 2 TIE/fo space superiority fighters
  - At least 6 TIE/sf space superiority fighters
  - At least 1 First Order speeder bike
- **Crew:**
  - At least 14
- **Role:** Platform
- **Affiliation:**
  - First Order
  -   First Order military
