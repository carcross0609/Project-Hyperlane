# Venator-class Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Venator-class Star Destroyer/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Venator-class_Star_Destroyer/Legends)

The Venator-class Star Destroyer, also known as the Venator-class Destroyer, Republic attack cruiser, Jedi cruiser, and later Imperial attack cruiser, was a Kuat Drive Yards capital ship used extensively by the Galactic Republic during the later parts of the Clone Wars, as well as by the Galactic Empire.
