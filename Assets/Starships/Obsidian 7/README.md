# Obsidian 7

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Obsidian 7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obsidian_7)

Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems, Obsidian 7 was the designation of a TIE/ln starfighter serving the Imperial Navy's Obsidian Squadron by 3 ABY. Modified for upper atmosphere pursuits and maneuvers, Obsidian 7 was operated by a TIE pilot nicknamed "Winged Gundark" for an impressive number of career kills in high-altitude battles by 3 ABY.
