# Red Nebula

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red Nebula (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Nebula_%28starship%29)

Red Nebula was a light freighter that served as the personal starship of the Hutt crime lord Sinasu during the time of the Galactic Civil War. Sinasu kept the vessel docked at the little-used freight pad Spaceport 27 on the planet Nal Hutta.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Red Nebula
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Owners:** Sinasu
