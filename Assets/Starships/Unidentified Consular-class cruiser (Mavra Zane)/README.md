# Unidentified Consular-class cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Consular-class cruiser (Mavra Zane)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Consular-class_cruiser_%28Mavra_Zane%29)

A Consular-class cruiser was used by Jedi Master Mavra Zane to transport a group of younglings to the Jedi outpost on Kashyyyk around 22 BBY. She tested them during the trip by using training remotes. The cruiser also carried Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptors aboard.
