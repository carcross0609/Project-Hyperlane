# Goliath

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Goliath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Goliath)

The Goliath was an Imperial escort carrier belonging to the starfleet of the Galactic Empire. Its main function was to carry starfighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Goliath
- **Model:** Imperial escort carrier
- **Type:** Carrier
- **Armament:**
  - 10 Taim & Bak H-eights
  - Krupx missile delivery system
- **Complement:** 1 wing of starfighters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Ros
