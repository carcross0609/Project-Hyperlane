# Scopium

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scopium](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scopium)

Scopium was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle in service of the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era. Around 3 ABY, Major Sharin of the Imperial Security Bureau used the shuttle to travel to the Imperial black facility Second Sun to review the security measures being undertaken there. After she was satisified that the station's commander, Moff Tiaan Jerjerrod, was running a tight ship, she intended to use Scopium to travel to Al'doleem to investigate a recent incident. After she made her way on the shuttle, she was killed by Bevelyn, a worker on the Death Star who intended to leave the station with her partner Jon Melton and inform the Rebel Alliance of the station.
