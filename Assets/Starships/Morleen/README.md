# Morleen

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Morleen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morleen)

The Morleen was a freighter piloted by its owner during the Republic Era. The freighter was captured by the Riftwalkers pirate gang with its owner still aboard and transported to the Riftwalker Compound on the planet Keldooine. The Riftwalkers' leader, Zilastra, boarded the freighter with several of her gang, losing Tal and Krins when the owner tried to fight back. Ultimately, Zilastra killed the owner, claiming the Morleen and its cargo.
