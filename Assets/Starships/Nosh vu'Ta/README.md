# Nosh vu'Ta

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nosh vu'Ta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nosh_vu%27Ta)

The Nosh vu'Ta was a freighter that was used by the Corsignis Property Alliance, and the ship's name translated as "We profit," in Huttese. During the time of the Galactic Civil War, Corsignis and the Jon-Tow Economic Development Group became engaged in a trade dispute and they both blockaded the moon Pinett, in attempt to prevent each other from controlling the trade that passed through Pinett's spaceports. The Nosh vu'Ta participated in the blockade and after a group of agents flew to Pinett, the Nosh vu'Ta and the Jon-Tow ship Herriar both gave conflicting orders to the agents.
