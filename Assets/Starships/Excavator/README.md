# Excavator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Excavator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Excavator)

The Excavator was a Venture-class cruiser used by the former Jedi Master Cohmac Vitus during the High Republic Era. Vitus utilized the cruiser during his search for the Tolemite civilization.
