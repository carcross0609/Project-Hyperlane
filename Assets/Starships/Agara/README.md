# Agara

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Agara](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agara)

Agara was a CR90 corvette. Transporting repair kits along with a group of other civilian vessels, the ship was attacked by the Imperial Star Destroyer Accuser and the Immobilizer 418 cruiser Grappler.
