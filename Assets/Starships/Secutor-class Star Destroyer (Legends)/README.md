# Secutor-class Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Secutor-class Star Destroyer/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Secutor-class_Star_Destroyer/Legends)

The Secutor-class Star Destroyer, also known as the Secutor-class battlecarrier, was a carrier in the Imperial Navy and possibly the Republic Navy.

- **Name:** Secutor-class Star Destroyer

- **Line:** Star Destroyers
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Cost:** 200 million credits
- **Length:** 2,200 meters
- **Hyperdrive:**

  - Class 14 (secondary)
- **Armament:**
  - 15 Heavy turbolaser batteries
  - 15 light turbolasers
  - 15 battleship ion cannon batteries
  - 16 medium ion cannons
  - 12 Tractor beam projector
- **Complement:**
  - 144 starfighters
  - Multiple other crafts
  - Walkers
  - 14,000 troops
- **Bays:** Two ventral hangar bays
- **Crew:** 40,000
- **Consumables:** 2 years
- **Role:**
  - Star Destroyer
  - Carrier
- **Firstuse:** Around 19 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic (possibly)
  - Galactic Empire
  - Dark Empire
