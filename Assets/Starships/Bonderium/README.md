# Bonderium

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bonderium](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bonderium)

Bonderium was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle used by rebel agents to intercept the Imperial transport Elusive on its way from the planetary ruin of Dankayo to the Tolan Navy Yards.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bonderium
- **Model:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Modifier:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1

- **Armament:**

  - 1 tractor beam
- **Crew:** 4-6
- **Passengers:** 10
- **Cargohandling:** 80 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
