# Edgehawk

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Edgehawk (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Edgehawk_%28starship%29)

The Edgehawk was a starship used by the bounty hunter T'onga and her wife, Losha Tarkon, in their search for Cadeliah, the heir to both the Unbroken Clan and the Mourner's Wail Syndicate who had the potential to unite both criminal syndicates. The two were accompanied by a crew they had gathered for their task, including the Gand findsman Zuckuss, the assassin Tasu Leech, and the Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk, the latter of whom the crew rescued on Jekara. In the ensuing battle, the crew attempted to save Beilert Valance from the Galactic Empire, but were thwarted by Darth Vader. The Edgehawk shared its name with an avian creature native to the planet Lothal.
