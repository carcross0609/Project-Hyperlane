# Envoy-class shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Envoy-class shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Envoy-class_shuttle)

The Envoy-class shuttle was a shuttle that was manufactured by Tallaani Shipyards. Envoy-class shuttles were not meant for warfare, since they lacked heavy shielding or significant armament; nor were they very fast or maneuverable. They were used mostly by merchants or travelers for luxury. Envoy-class shuttles were affordable and had an easy-to-use navigation and autopilot system. During the New Sith Wars, an anonymous benefactor donated the Envoy-class shuttle Star-Wake to the Army of Light, to assist in the war effort.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Envoy-class shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Tallaani Shipyards
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 12
- **Role:** Transport, luxury
- **Affiliation:**
