# Lucky Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lucky Star (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucky_Star_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Lucky Star was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate in the Rebel Alliance's fleet. It was frequently used to support Generals Tyr Taskeen and Brenn Tantor's ground operations during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lucky Star
- **Type:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Role:** Ground support, communications
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Alliance of Free Planets
  - New Republic
