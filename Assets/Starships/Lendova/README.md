# Lendova

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lendova](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lendova)

Lendova during the Conflict at Mylok IV.]] Lendova was a Habeen Nebulon-B frigate that was involved in a diplomatic mission to bring the Habeen into the Galactic Empire some time during the Galactic Civil War. At the rendezvous with Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin aboard the Imperial Nebulon-B2 frigate Shamus, the Lendova was attacked by the four Nharwaak CR90 Corellian corvettes of Phazer group.
