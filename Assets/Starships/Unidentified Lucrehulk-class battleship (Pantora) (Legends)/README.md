# Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship (Pantora)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Lucrehulk-class_battleship_%28Pantora%29/Legends)

Around 21 BBY, a Lucrehulk-class Droid Control Ship served in the Trade Federation and was used to blockade the planet of Pantora. Sib Canay, owner of the starship, conspired with the Separatists and hired the Rodian male bounty hunter Greedo and his Gotal accomplice to kidnap Chairman Notluwiski Papanoida's two daughters in order to coerce him into aligning Pantora with the Separatists.
