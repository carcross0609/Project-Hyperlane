# Permondiri Explorer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Permondiri Explorer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Permondiri_Explorer)

The Permondiri Explorer was an old survey craft that, at one point in time, was used by the Jedi Order. The Bureau of Ships and Services Heritage Museum on Coruscant had in its possession an ancient navigational dataplaque from the starship which was eventually stolen and replaced with a forgery by crime lord Dryden Vos of the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Permondiri Explorer
- **Type:** Survey craft
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
