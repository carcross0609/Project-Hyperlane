# Vader's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vader's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vader%27s_starship)

Vader's starship was a starship given to the newly christened Sith Lord Darth Vader in 19 BBY, along with a droid who fit in an astromech socket. Vader destroyed a Rebel base by blowing up a cargo of coaxium with the starship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vader's starship
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Armament:**

  - 2 rear-facing laser turrets
- **Crew:**
  - 1 pilot
  - 1 co-pilot (droid)
- **Lifesupport:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Sith Order
- **Owners:**
  - Darth Sidious
  - Darth Vader
