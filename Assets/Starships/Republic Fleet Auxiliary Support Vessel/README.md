# Republic Fleet Auxiliary Support Vessel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Republic Fleet Auxiliary Support Vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Fleet_Auxiliary_Support_Vessel)

The Republic Fleet Auxiliary Support Vessel was a support ship used by the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. The most notable use of this vessel was when the spacecraft Redeemer picked up Callista Ming, Geith Eris and other members of Djinn Altis's heretical group of Jedi, after they decided to take up commands in the Grand Army of the Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Republic Fleet Auxiliary Support Vessel
- **Role:** Support ship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
