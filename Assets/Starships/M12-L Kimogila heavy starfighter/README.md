# M12-L Kimogila heavy starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M12-L Kimogila heavy starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M12-L_Kimogila_heavy_starfighter)

The M12-L Kimogila heavy starfighter, also referred to as Kimogila Heavy Fighter or Kimogila, was a heavy starfighter manufactured by MandalMotors by commission from the Hutt kajidics. The Kimogila was heavily armed and armored, making it a versatile and expensive long-range fighter. Though designed for the Hutts, the Kimogila became a favored ship for mercenaries, bounty hunters, and pirates.
