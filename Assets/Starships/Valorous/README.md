# Valorous

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Valorous](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valorous)

The Valorous was a Lantillian GX1 short hauler in service to the Galactic Republic that delivered Padmé Amidala, Teckla Minnau, Nix Card, and two Senate Commandos to Scipio during the Clone Wars. The Valorous was then converted into civilian use during the Imperial Era. At some point, the Valorous was pursued and fire upon by two TIE/ln space superiority starfighters over a planet.
