# Z-24 Buzzbug

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Z-24 Buzzbug](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-24_Buzzbug)

The Z-24 Buzzbug, commonly known just as the Buzzbug, was an Incom Corporation starfighter that was used by the Malastare-Sullust Joint Task Force. A single-seat starfighter, the Buzzbug was in use over forty years before 232 BBY, when Hedda Casset joined the task force as a pilot. By 232 BBY, the Republic Defense Coalition used Z-28 Skywings.

## Specifications
