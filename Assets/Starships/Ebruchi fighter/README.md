# Ebruchi fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ebruchi fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ebruchi_fighter)

Ebruchi fighters were starfighters used by the Ebruchi Pirates in 2 ABY. A complement of these fighters were used by Creysis against the Admonitor, during which most of them were destroyed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ebruchi fighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 25 meters
- **Engine:** Gas-drive landing jets
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Sensor pods
- **Armament:**
  - 2 laser cannons
  - 8 projectile weapons
  -   At least 1 grappling line
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Affiliation:** Ebruchi Pirates
