# Moraysian cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moraysian cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moraysian_cruiser)

The Moraysian cruiser was a model of cruiser designed by the Moraysian species. One Moraysian cruiser was utilized by the Pyke Syndicate, and was stolen by the mercenary Ledesmar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Moraysian cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Moraysians
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Pyke Syndicate
