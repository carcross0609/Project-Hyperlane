# Cryptic Warning

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cryptic Warning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cryptic_Warning)

The Cryptic Warning was one of the two EE-104 Fisheyes that were stolen by the Lost Tribe of Sith for use for their armada. It was commanded by Sith Saber Querdan Dei and was used during the Jedi Queen project.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cryptic Warning
- **Model:** EE-104 Fisheye
- **Affiliation:** Lost Tribe of Sith
- **Navy:** Sith armada
- **Captains:** Querdan Dei
