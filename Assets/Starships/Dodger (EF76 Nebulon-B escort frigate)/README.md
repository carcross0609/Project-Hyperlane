# Dodger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dodger (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dodger_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Dodger was a Nebulon-B frigate used by the Rebel Alliance. The ship arrived to attack 3 BFF-1 bulk freighters Rugle with multiple Rebel starfighters. The attack was thwarted by two Assault Gunboats. The attack was recorded and used in the Combat chamber to train future pilots on the Gunboat. One of them was Maarek Stele.
