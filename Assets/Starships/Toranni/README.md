# Toranni

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Toranni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Toranni)

The Toranni was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle that was used by the Galactic Empire. During the Galactic Civil War, the Toranni transported an Imperial exploration team to the newly-discovered planet Alluuvia, in the Elrood sector. On arrival on the world, the shuttle landed on a small atoll and two of the Toranni crew&mdash;Private Kinard and Private Tracher&mdash;disembarked from the vessel. Soon after, the vessel was approached by a group of Anguilla, a sentient species native to Alluuvia, and the Imperials opened fire on the aliens and massacred them.
