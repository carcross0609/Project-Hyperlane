# Cybershop ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cybershop ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cybershop_ship)

Cybershop ships were starships that acted as mobile cybernetics showrooms, installation, and maintenance facilities. Cybershop ships with legal cybernetics masters offered services from basic cybernetic prosthetics to advanced post-injury reconstruction and enhancement and were renowned in those areas. Some unscrupulous crafters offered illegal or illicit modifications, cutting-edge cybernetics with blatantly criminal utility, such as concealed weapons or lock-picking tools, that legal ships avoided installing—or at least advertising.
