# Strat's cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Strat's cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strat%27s_cruiser)

A cruiser was owned by the Hoopaloo Strat during the High Republic Era. In 382 BBY, Jedi Padawan Matthea Cathley and Jedi Knight Oliviah Zeveron traveled aboard the cruiser from the Mid Rim moon Jedha to the Outer Rim planet Dalna to investigate the Path of the Open Hand cult.
