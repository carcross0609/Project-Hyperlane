# Resolute (picket destroyer)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Resolute (picket destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resolute_%28picket_destroyer%29)

The Resolute was a picket destroyer employed by the Galactic Alliance during the Second Galactic Civil War. The Resolute was part of the Blockade of Corellia by the Alliance's Third and Fifth Fleets.
