# Unidentified pirate starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified pirate starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pirate_starship)

A starship was used by the Pirate King of Sector Nine to travel to a town on the Outer Rim planet Ah Re Won in search of their son, Prince Afolonkin. The pirate also brought a tank on the ship, which they used to gain the attention of local authorities. In finding the prince had died while hunting, the infuriated pirate started attacking the townsfolk and their buildings. The noise caused by the ruckus annoyed the Sith apprentice Darth Maul, who was visiting the nearby Sith temple ruins, so he subsequently slaughtered the Pirate King's crew. When the Pirate King attempted to flee in the starship, Maul chucked the tank into the hull of the ship, causing both to explode.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified pirate starship
- **Destroyed:** Between c. 40 BBY and 32 BBY, Ah Re Won
- **Affiliation:** Pirate King of Sector Nine
