# Morgath 5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Morgath 5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morgath_5)

The Morgath 5 was a Container transport used for backup convoy services by Imperial defector Admiral Harkov's Victory-class Star Destroyer, the Protector stationed at XQ1 Platform DS 5. It was intercepted and shot down by Imperial Ace Maarek Stele along with Morgath 1, BFF-1 bulk freighter group Karflo, group SoroSuub, and CR90 corvette group Sundog.
