# Panorama

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Panorama](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panorama)

The Panorama was a luxury liner. While touring through the Tion Hegemony, the future Alliance technician Ral Shawgrim was born onboard while his parents were on vacation.
