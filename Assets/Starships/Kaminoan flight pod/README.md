# Kaminoan flight pod

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaminoan flight pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaminoan_flight_pod)

The Kaminoan flight pod was an escape pod of Kaminoan design. General Grievous commandeered one so he and Asajj Ventress could flee the Battle of Kamino. It was also used by ARC trooper Fives and the medical droid AZI-3 as a diversion to the Kamino garrison as they returned to Tipoca City to investigate the use of inhibitor chips that influenced clone behavior.
