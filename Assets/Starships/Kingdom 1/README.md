# Kingdom 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kingdom 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_1)

Kingdom 1 was a Carrack-class light cruisers used by the Rebel Alliance. They were part of an attempt to wrestle out TIE Defenders from the Grey Wolf but all three were utterly destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele.
