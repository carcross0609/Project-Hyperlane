# GAT-12 Skipray

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GAT-12 Skipray](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GAT-12_Skipray)

The GAT-12 Skipray was a starship that was on the military market during the Clone Wars. It was comparable in role to its contemporaries, the Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft and the SS-54 light assault gunship.
