# Xolochi Dreadnought

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xolochi Dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xolochi_Dreadnought)

The Xolochi Dreadnought was a cruiser-class type of warship used by the empire of the Tionese despot Xim during it's conflict with the Hutt species. One also served as part of a task force to the planet Galuch at that time.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Xolochi Dreadnought
- **Type:** Dreadnought
- **Role:** Dreadnought
- **Affiliation:** Xim's empire
