# Raider I-class corvette

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Raider I-class corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raider_I-class_corvette)

The Raider I-class corvette was a member of the Raider line of corvettes used by the Imperial Navy. The wedge-shaped corvette was often used against starfighters and to patrol isolated star systems. Two Raider I-class corvettes, the Sargantuan and the Warhawk, participated in the Battle of Xorrn.
