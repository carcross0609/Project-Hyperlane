# Bulk transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bulk transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bulk_transport)

Bulk transports were transport starships capable of moving large amounts of cargo. The early Confederacy of Independent Systems navy consisted of bulk transports that had previously been used by the various member corporations to move their wares across the galaxy. However, as the Clone Wars continued, Pammant Docks and other facilities manufactured capital ships to make a credible navy.
