# New Hope

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [New Hope](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Hope)

The New Hope was an antique Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser that was used by the Galactic Empire as a museum. In an act of psychological warfare, it was acquired by the Rebel Alliance and served as their flagship, being the command ship of Mon Mothma.

## Specifications

- **Name:** New Hope
- **Model:** Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser
- **Type:** Heavy cruiser
- **Length:** Over 600 meters
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - New Republic
- **Captains:** Tola
