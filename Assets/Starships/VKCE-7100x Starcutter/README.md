# VKCE-7100x Starcutter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [VKCE-7100x Starcutter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VKCE-7100x_Starcutter)

The VKCE-7100x Starcutter was a model of starship produced by Valkeri-Kuat Consolidated Enterprises and used by the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. The vessel was an experimental design, featuring an integrated hyperdrive. One such ship was the Cantaros, owned by Jedi Master Vernestra Rwoh. The ship was named after Rwoh's former Padawan, Imri Cantaros.

## Specifications

- **Name:** VKCE-7100x Starcutter
- **Manufacturer:** Valkeri-Kuat Consolidated Enterprises
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hyperdrive:** Integrated hyperdrive
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
