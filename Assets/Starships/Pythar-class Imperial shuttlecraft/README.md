# Pythar-class Imperial shuttlecraft

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pythar-class Imperial shuttlecraft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pythar-class_Imperial_shuttlecraft)

The Pythar-class Imperial shuttlecraft was a type of shuttle used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War with the Galactic Republic. Pythar-class shuttles were small vessels with short, curved wings on either side. A Pythar-class shuttlecraft named the Deference was utilized by an Imperial scientist during the war when he performed upgrades on the personal space station of the Sith Emperor. The Deference was shot down in the Battle of Hoth in 3665 BBY, and the shuttle later became the target of a Jedi mission to retrieve the plans for the Emperor's space station stored within the ship's computers.

## Specifications
