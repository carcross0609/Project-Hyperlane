# EX-1-2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EX-1-2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EX-1-2)

EX-1-2 was an Imperial TIE/sa bomber manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems. As a TIE/sa bomber, EX-1-2 boasted incredibly precise computers that allowed its pilot to destroy specific buildings or vehicles. In 4 ABY, EX-1-2 was secured above a hangar bay floor on the Empire's Death Star II superweapon when the Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Vader arrived to discuss the Death Star's lagging construction schedule with Moff Tiaan Jerjerrod, the Imperial officer in charge of the Death Star.
