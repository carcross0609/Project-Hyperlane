# Golden Jewel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Golden Jewel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Jewel)

The Golden Jewel was the starship assigned to Rebel Alliance Lieutenant Talnar during the Galactic Civil War. Talnar was shot down in the Golden Jewel by an Imperial Guardian-class light cruiser and forced to crash-land on the world of Naalol.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Golden Jewel
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Captains:** Lieutenant Talnar
