# Indomitable

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Indomitable (Republic cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Indomitable_%28Republic_cruiser%29)

The Indomitable was a cruiser in the service of the Galactic Republic in the Clone Wars under the command of Admiral Wullf Yularen. The ship participated in the battle of Kothlis, where it was heavily damaged. After hasty repairs were completed, it was sent to Lanteeb, where its task force engaged General Grievous.
