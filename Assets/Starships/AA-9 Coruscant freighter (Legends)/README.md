# AA-9 Coruscant freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AA-9 Coruscant freighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AA-9_Coruscant_freighter/Legends)

The AA-9 Coruscant freighter, also known as the Botajef Freighter-Liner, was a massive freighter model used to transport large groups of people to and from Coruscant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AA-9 Coruscant freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Botajef Shipyards
- **Model:** Botajef AA-9 Freighter-Liner
- **Type:** Bulk freighter
- **Length:** 390 meters
- **Passengers:** 30,000
- **Role:** Bulk freighter
- **Affiliation:** Numerous shipping lines
