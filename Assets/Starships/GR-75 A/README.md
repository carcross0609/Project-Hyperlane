# GR-75 A

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GR-75 A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GR-75_A)

GR-75 A was the designation of a GR-75 medium transport of the New Republic Defense Fleet that was active during the Galactic Civil War. During the war, GR-75 A was part of an automated New Republic convoy crewed by droids that was used in an operation to lure the Imperial ISD Overseer into the Zavian Abyss and destroy it.
