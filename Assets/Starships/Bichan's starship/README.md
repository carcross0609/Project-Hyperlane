# Bichan's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Bichan's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bichan%27s_starship)

The Sith Lord Bichan used a starship to travel to the planet Dolgarak. There, Bichan used the starship's laser cannons to shoot down a vessel used by the mechanic Ara and the Jedi Toul to also arrive at the planet. Consequently, however, Ara and Toul caused Bichan to lose control over and crash his own craft, as well.
