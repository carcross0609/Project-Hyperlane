# Z-300 group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-300 group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-300_group)

Z-300 group was a group of Muurian transports that served the splinter government fleet of rogue Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin during the Zaarin insurrection. It was present at the Battle of To-phalion Base in 4 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z-300 group
- **Type:** Transport squadron
- **Leader:** Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin
- **Affiliation:**
  - Zaarin coup forces
  -   Zaarin's fleet
