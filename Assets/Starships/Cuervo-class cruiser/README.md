# Cuervo-class cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cuervo-class cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cuervo-class_cruiser)

The Cuervo-class cruiser was a model of cruiser in use during the High Republic Era. Baron Boolan, scientist of the Nihil marauders, owned a souped-up Cuervo-class cruiser that radiated a purple glow. Nihil commander Vark Tarpalin spotted the cruiser in the fleet arriving above Lonisa City on the planet Valo for his Dark Fair celebration in 229 BBY. Tarpalin had heard that Baron Boolan may have also brought Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro with him. The Dark Fair was interrupted, however, by Jedi Padawan Ram Jomaram acting as a vigilante known as the "Scarlet Skull." Several of the attending vessels were destroyed, while the rest, including one with a purple glow, departed.
