# Cynda Dreaming

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cynda Dreaming](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cynda_Dreaming)

Cynda Dreaming was a freighter that operated between the planet Gorse and its moon Cynda during the Age of the Empire. It was destroyed by the Imperial Star Destroyer Ultimatum in 11 BBY.
