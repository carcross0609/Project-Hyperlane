# Peacemaker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Peacemaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Peacemaker)

The Peacemaker was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate in service to the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a task force commanded by Admiral Gial Ackbar during the Battle of Ciutric. It sustained heavy damage at the battle, floating dead in space after numerous strikes.
