# Superfreighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Superfreighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Superfreighter)

Superfreighter was a term for a particularly massive freighter. The Sith Empire utilized superfreighters during the Great Galactic War. The superfreighters traveled to an occupied Coruscant in convoys during the last days of the war, during which time they were escorted by Imperial frigates.
