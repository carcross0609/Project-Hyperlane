# MorningStar-C starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MorningStar-C starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MorningStar-C_starfighter)

The MorningStar-C was a starfighter popular with mercenaries around the time of the Battle of Naboo. It was a variant of the MorningStar-B starfighter with 3 concussion missile launchers instead of light laser cannons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MorningStar-C starfighter
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Systems Engineering
- **Model:** MorningStar-C starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Cost:** 43,000 credits (10,000 used)
- **Length:** 16.3 meters
- **Max Speed:** 950 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Armament:**
  - Heavy laser cannons (3)
  - Concussion missile launchers (3)
  -   30 missiles total (10 each)
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Capacity:** 75 kg
- **Consumables:** 3 days
- **Availability:** Restricted
- **Role:** Space superiority fighter
- **Affiliation:** Mercenaries
