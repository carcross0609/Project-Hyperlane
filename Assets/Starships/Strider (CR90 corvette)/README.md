# Strider

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Strider (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strider_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Strider was a CR90 corvette in the service of the Rebel Alliance. During a naval battle, the ship's engines were destroyed, leaving the ship to float dead in space. A group of rebels were sent aboard the ship to evacuate the Rebel personnel still aboard the burning ship.
