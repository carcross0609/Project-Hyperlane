# City-ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [City-ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/City-ship)

A city-ship was a large vessel that served a dual purpose as a habitat. The Mon Calamari used many city-ships when their fleet fled from their planet of Mon Cala.
