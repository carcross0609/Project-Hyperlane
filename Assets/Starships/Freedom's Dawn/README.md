# Freedom's Dawn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Freedom's Dawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedom%27s_Dawn)

The Freedom's Dawn was an MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser manufactured by Mon Calamari Shipyards that served in the New Republic Defense Fleet during the Galactic Civil War. In 5 ABY, the Freedom's Dawn was present on the Inner Rim planet Jakku during the Battle of Jakku, the final battle of the war fought between the New Republic and the Galactic Empire's forces. The Freedom's Dawn was wrecked near Namenthe's Crater and Pilgrim's Road on Jakku's surface. The Star Cruiser had a bulbous, winged, and light brown hull.
