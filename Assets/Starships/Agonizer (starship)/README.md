# Agonizer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Agonizer (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agonizer_%28starship%29)

The Agonizer was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer and the flagship of Admiral Teren Rogriss's Anti-Zsinj Task Force. It housed the 181st Imperial Fighter Group and was sent to Adumar to open negotiations with the Adumari.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Agonizer
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** 2
- **Complement:** 181st Imperial Fighter Group
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Imperial Remnant
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Teren Rogriss
