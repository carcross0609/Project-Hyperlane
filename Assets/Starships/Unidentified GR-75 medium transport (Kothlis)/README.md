# Unidentified GR-75 medium transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified GR-75 medium transport (Kothlis)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_GR-75_medium_transport_%28Kothlis%29)

A GR-75 medium transport was involved in the late stages of the Battle of Kothlis in 3.5 ABY. It contained a commando force led by Rebel Alliance General Crix Madine, at least four BTL Y-wing bombers, and at least three T-47 airspeeders.

- **Name:** Unidentified GR-75 medium transport
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
