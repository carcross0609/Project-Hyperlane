# Aut-O's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aut-O's flagship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aut-O%27s_flagship/Legends)

Aut-O's flagship was a Providence-class carrier/destroyer that was infiltrated by the D-Squad in 20 BBY. This flagship carried an encryption module that was needed by the Galactic Republic to decipher coded transmissions, specifically one sent out by General Grievous, that was believed to hold important attack strategies.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aut-O's flagship
- **Type:** Providence-class carrier/destroyer
- **Armament:** Several cannons
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Navy:** Confederate Navy
- **Captains:** Aut-O
