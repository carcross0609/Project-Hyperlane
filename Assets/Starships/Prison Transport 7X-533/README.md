# Prison Transport 7X-533

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Prison Transport 7X-533](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prison_Transport_7X-533)

Prison Transport 7X-533 was a prison ship that was operated by the Nihil marauder organization. In 228 BBY, the Adrens, Joss and Pikka Adren, along with Republic Defense Coalition personnel Visma Tarko, Elping Tsoo and C-06N, rescued the Mon Calamari Ghal Tarpfen from the prison transport.
