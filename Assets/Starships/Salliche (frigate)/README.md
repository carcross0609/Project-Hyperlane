# Salliche

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Salliche (frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salliche_%28frigate%29)

The Salliche was a frigate in the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. The vessel shared its name with the agriworld Salliche, which was located in the Core Worlds. In 14 BBY, the Salliche was in the flotilla belonging to Sentinel Base, an Imperial facility commanded by Moff Wilhuff Tarkin which oversaw the construction of the Imperial battle station later known as the Death Star.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Salliche
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:** Sentinel Base's flotilla
