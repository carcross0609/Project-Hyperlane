# Raider

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Raider (salvage freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raider_%28salvage_freighter%29)

The Raider was a GS-100 salvage ship captained by the Twi'lek scavenger Ratch. In the aftermath of the Battle of Sullust during the Clone Wars, it recovered Asajj Ventress's Ginivex-class fanblade starfighter and her unconscious inside it. After Ratch refused her request to transport her to Dathomir, she killed him and his crew, and flew the ship herself.
