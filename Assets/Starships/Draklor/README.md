# Draklor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Draklor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Draklor)

Draklor was a Star Galleon-class frigate serving in the Imperial Navy under Grand Admiral Thrawn. The ship was dispatched by the Grand Admiral as part of the Death's Head to ferry Joruus C'baoth and General Freja Covell from his flagship, the Star Destroyer Chimaera to the Mount Tantiss base on Wayland.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Draklor
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Star Galleon-class frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Length:** 300 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
