# Green/Besh

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Green/Besh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Green/Besh)

Green/Besh was an Imperial Navy IPV-1 System Patrol Craft. In the year 0 ABY, the Green/Besh was stationed on the edge of the Mid Rim's Ringali Nebula alongside the IPV-1 Green/Aura and the Lancer-class frigate Rage at the Imperial Bavos-II military space platform Station 1B6/Green. It was armed with four turbolasers, could reach a speed of one thousand kilometers per hour, and acted as an inter-system patrol and customs craft. The Green/Besh was produced by the company Sienar Fleet Systems, and was 150 meters long.
