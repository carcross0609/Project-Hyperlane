# Jendirian Valley

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jendirian Valley/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jendirian_Valley/Legends)

The Jendirian Valley was an AA-9 Coruscant freighter which was famous for helping refugees. Its middle deck had been modified to hold 30,000 beings. Anakin Skywalker and Padmé Amidala journeyed aboard the Valley, disguised as refugees, to Naboo after the attempt on Senator Amidala's life.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jendirian Valley
- **Manufacturer:** Botajef Shipyards
- **Type:** AA-9 Coruscant freighter
- **Length:** 390 meters
- **Passengers:** 30,000
- **Role:** Freighter
