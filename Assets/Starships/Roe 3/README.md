# Roe 3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Roe 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Roe_3)

Roe 3 was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle serving the Alliance Fleet. The ship was part of Roe group which participated in the Skirmish at Outpost D-34. Roe 3, along with Roe 1 and 2 as well as Escro group, attempted to stop Imperial forces from capturing Onece 3 of Onece group which carried their fellow rebels. Both shuttle groups were unsuccessful and were all destroyed in the process.
