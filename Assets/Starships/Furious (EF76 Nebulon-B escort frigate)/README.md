# Furious (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Furious (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Furious_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

Furious was an Imperial EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate that saw service during the Galactic Civil War. The Furious was on patrol in Corporate Sector under orders to search any ships it encountered when it arrived at Stars' End prison and interrupted a Rebel raid to recover two escaped pilots who had stowed away on the BFF-1 bulk freighter Hampton.
