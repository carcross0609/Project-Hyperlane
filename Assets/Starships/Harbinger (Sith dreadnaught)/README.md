# Harbinger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harbinger (Sith dreadnaught)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harbinger_%28Sith_dreadnaught%29)

The Harbinger was a Sith dreadnaught active in 5000 BBY, during the Great Hyperspace War. It was commanded by Saes Rrogon, a Jedi-turned-Sith Lord. The Harbinger and another dreadnaught, the Omen, were transporting Lignan ore to Sith Lord Naga Sadow when the two ships were ambushed by a Jedi starfighter. Harbinger was infiltrated by Relin Druur, Saes' former Jedi Master, who managed to sabotage its hyperdrive while Druur's Padawan, Drev Hassin, held off several of Harbinger own fighters. Eventually, Hassin managed to ram the Infiltrator into the Harbinger's bridge, causing the Harbinger to crash into the Omen just as the vessels escaped into hyperspace. The collision knocked the Omen off-course, causing it to crash into the remote planet of Kesh.
