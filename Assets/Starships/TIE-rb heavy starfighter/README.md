# TIE/rb heavy starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/rb heavy starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/rb_heavy_starfighter)

The TIE/rb heavy starfighter, more formally known as the TIE reinforcement battery heavy starfighter, and simply known as the heavy TIE fighter, TIE Brute, or the Infiltrator was a variant of the TIE fighter series starfighters used by the Galactic Empire, distinguished by its extra pod housing more powerful laser cannons.
