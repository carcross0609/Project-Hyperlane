# Miner group

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Miner group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miner_group)

Miner was a group of three Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports used by the Nami. During a battle of the Eva-T system, the transports were used to deploy space mines around the Strike-class medium cruiser Nuance in an attempt to defend it against an Imperial attack led by Admiral Thrawn. The transports and the mines were all destroyed during the battle, and the cruiser was captured.
