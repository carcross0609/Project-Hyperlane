# Fireater

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fireater](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fireater)

The Fireater was a DP20 gunship in service to the New Republic in 9 ABY and stationed at Durren. It was part of a small fleet of warships hastily assembled by Han Solo and Lando Calrissian to defend the Meridian sector from invasion, along with the Courane. It was destroyed by a swarm of CCIR Needle craft over Nam Chorios.
