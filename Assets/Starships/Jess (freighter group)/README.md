# Jess (freighter group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jess (freighter group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jess_%28freighter_group%29)

Jess was a group of Freighters belonging to the Habassa. Jess 6, 7, and 8 were all operated by the Habassan convoy that was attacked by Assault Gunboats. Keyan Farlander came to their aid in a B-wing. Jess 6 was disabled by the Imperials and was attempted to be looted by Slick, an Imperial freighter, but it was destroyed. Jess 9 hypered in with some Y-wings and saved the crew. Keyan was successful in stopping the attack.
