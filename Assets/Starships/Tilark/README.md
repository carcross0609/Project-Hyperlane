# Tilark

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tilark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tilark)

Tilark was a Xiytiar-class heavy transport belonging to the Imperials. Tilark was part of the Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Corrupter's vital resupply convoy lead by the Nebulon-B Frigate Monitor to the Corrupter in the Mev system. Tilark's cargo was 'Probe Droids'. The ship was then attacked by Rebel raiders and was destroyed.
