# Wasp (CR90 corvette)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wasp (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wasp_%28CR90_corvette%29)

Wasp was the designation given to a pair of CR90 corvettes that served with the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. The ships escorted Cielo as part of a convoy containing supplies for a new base that was attacked by a force of Imperial Navy ships, including Alpha-class Xg-1 Star Wings and the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Red Wind. Despite the Imperial attack, they made it into hyperspace thanks to the efforts of Keyan Farlander, flying in an RZ-1 A-wing interceptor.
