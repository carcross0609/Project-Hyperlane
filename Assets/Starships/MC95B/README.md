# MC95B

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MC95B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC95B)

The MC95B was a class of Mon Calamari Star Cruiser that was active during the later stages of the war between the First Order and the Resistance. Many of these ships were part of the Citizens' Fleet, a gargantuan fleet that consisted of 14,000 vessels that fought against the Sith Eternal forces during the Battle of Exegol.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MC95B
- **Manufacturer:** Mon Calamari Shipyards
- **Type:** MC-series star cruiser
- **Engine:** Sublight engines (at least 8)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Bays:** Hangars (2)
- **Affiliation:** Citizens' Fleet
