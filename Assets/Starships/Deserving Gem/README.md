# Deserving Gem

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Deserving Gem](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deserving_Gem)

The Deserving Gem was a Hapan Battle Dragon and flagship of Ducha Requud during the Battle of Uroro Station. When Hapan Signals Intelligence discovered communications between the Gem and Darth Caedus' flagship, Anakin Solo, they supposed Requund were plotting to overthrow Queen Mother Tenel Ka Djo.
