# Blue Fox

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blue Fox](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Fox)

The Blue Fox was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate under the service to the Galactic Empire. She launched an unsuccessful attempt to destroy the MC80 Star Cruiser Excelsior that was trying to enter hyperspace. Blue Fox was destroyed by T-65 X-wing starfighters and BTL Y-wing starfighters sent by the Excelsior.
