# Vo

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vo (container transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vo_%28container_transport%29)

Vo was a Container transport belonging to the Imperials. Vo was part of the Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Corrupters vital resupply convoy lead by the Nebulon-B Frigate Monitor in the Mev system. Vo was ferrying a fresh supply of Imperial Probe droids. The ship was then attacked by Rebel raiders in the Battle of Bettel and was destroyed.
