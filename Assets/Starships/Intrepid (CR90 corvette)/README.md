# Intrepid

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intrepid (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Intrepid was a CR90 corvette that saw service with the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. It was the consular ship of a Rebel ambassador that had been sent on a mission to win the support of the Airam at an Airam Platform. However, during Operation: Clean Sweep the Galactic Empire sent a strike force with orders to disable and board the Intrepid to capture the ambassador.
