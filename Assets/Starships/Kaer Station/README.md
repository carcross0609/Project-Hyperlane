# Kaer Station

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kaer Station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaer_Station)

Once a Tibanna gas platform on the planet Kaer, Kaer Station was the center of Black Sun's operations in the Siskeen system during the time of the Second Imperial Civil War. It also served as the seat of power for Lun Rask, the Vigo of the Arkanis sector.

- **Name:** Kaer Station
- **Location:** Kaer
