# Bespin Direct StarSpeeder 1000

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bespin Direct StarSpeeder 1000](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bespin_Direct_StarSpeeder_1000)

The Bespin Direct StarSpeeder 1000 was a modified StarSpeeder 1000 spacecraft used by the interstellar travel agency Star Tours, to operate flights bound for the Outer Rim planet of Bespin, prior to 0 BBY. The starship was painted in red and grey along with the Aurebesh letters Wesk, Dorn and Isk. It was named after both the gas giant as well as Bespin Direct, a separate spaceliner company from Star Tours that also was a member of the Galactic Tourist Bureau.
