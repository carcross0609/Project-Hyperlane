# Lucky Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lucky Star (Mos Entha)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucky_Star_%28Mos_Entha%29)

The Lucky Star was a casino located in Mos Entha, in Tatooine. It was owned and managed by Shiri'ani, a protégé of Lady Valarian, the Whiphid crime lord of Mos Eisley.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lucky Star
- **Location:** Tatooine
- **Affiliation:** Valarian Syndicate
