# Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadnaught-class_heavy_cruiser/Legends)

The Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser, or simply the Dreadnaught, was a type of capital ship built for planetary occupation and space combat used by the Galactic Republic, Galactic Empire, New Republic, local governments, and various other organizations. In use for over a century, they were one of the most ubiquitous ship designs in all of the galaxy.
