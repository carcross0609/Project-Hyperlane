# Marauder

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Marauder (frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marauder_%28frigate%29)

The Marauder was a frigate once used by Q'anah's Marauders until Q'anah's demise at the hands of Wilhuff Tarkin. It was later in the possession of the Culoss led by Angel under the name Elegin's Hope during its criminal activities like the Dromedar hijacking where Thrawn outsmarted the pirates.
