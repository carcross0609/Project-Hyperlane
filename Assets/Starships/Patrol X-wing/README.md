# Patrol X-wing

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Patrol X-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Patrol_X-wing)

Patrol X-wings were X-wing starfighters equipped with Fabritech high-gain recon sensor packages. They were used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic as reconnaissance craft. In preparation for the evacuation of Yavin 4, the Patrol X-wing Snoop 2 made a full perimeter circuit to scan for signs of Imperial incursion or passive observation devices.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Patrol X-wing
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Line:** X-wing
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Othersystems:** Fabritech high-gain recon sensor package
- **Role:** Reconnaissance craft
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
