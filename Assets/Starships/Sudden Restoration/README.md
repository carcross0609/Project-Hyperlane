# Sudden Restoration

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sudden Restoration](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sudden_Restoration)

The Sudden Restoration was a Carosite-built and operated hospital ship that saw service throughout the Galactic Civil War, offering free health care to all beings, regardless of political affiliation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sudden Restoration
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Class-C frigate
- **Length:** 330 meters
- **Armament:** 2 laser cannons
- **Complement:** 2 X-wing starfighters
- **Crew:**
  - Officers (20)
  - Crewmen (200)
- **Passengers:**
  - Medics (380)
  - Medical droids (1,000)
  - 2,000 patients
- **Role:** Hospital ship
- **Affiliation:** Carosites
