# Tereant III

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tereant III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tereant_III)

The Tereant III was a civilian GR-75 medium transport in service during the Galactic Civil War. In 1 ABY, the Tereant III was traveling in the Kashyyyk system when it was attacked by the Ghrag Mercenaries in the Tyyyn Nebula. The Civilian Protection Guild therefore dispatched a group of pilots to rescue the damaged transport. The CPG starfighters destroyed several Ghrag pirates and docked with the Tereant III to deliver a new power generator. The Tereant III was then escorted by the CPG to the Kashyyyk Space Station until it finally left the system.
