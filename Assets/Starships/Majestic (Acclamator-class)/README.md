# Majestic

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Majestic (Acclamator-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Majestic_%28Acclamator-class%29)

The Majestic was an Acclamator-class assault ship used by the Republic during the Clone Wars and later by the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. It was refitted with heavy armor plating to enhance its defensive capabilities.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Majestic
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Model:** Acclamator-class assault ship
- **Type:** Assault ship
- **Armament:** Quad turbolaser cannons
- **Role:**
  - Troop transport
  - Carrier
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
