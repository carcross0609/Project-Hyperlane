# Red 9

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red 9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_9)

Red 9 was a T-65 X-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. Flown by Lieutenant Nozzo Naytaan, the starfighter was outfitted with extra cargo room and led the relief fleet to Clak'dor VII.

- **Destroyed:** 0 BBY, Yavin system

- **Captains:** Lieutenant Nozzo Naytaan
