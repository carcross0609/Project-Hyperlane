# Lady of Bestine

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lady of Bestine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_of_Bestine)

The Lady of Bestine was a spacious passenger starship. In 19 BBY, Annileen Calwell left Tatooine on the Lady of Bestine with her two children, Kallie and Jabe, after her college application was accepted by the University of Alderaan.
