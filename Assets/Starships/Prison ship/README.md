# Prison ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Prison ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prison_ship)

A prison ship, also known as a correctional vessel, a dungeon ship, or prison transport, was a type of starship used to contain and transport prisoners. During the Clone Wars, the Galactic Republic's Correctional Authority maintained such craft. Much later in the New Republic Era, the New Republic used a specific model of prison ship crewed mainly by droids. The Knights of Ren flew an Oubliette-class transport, manufactured by Osseriton Assemblages the penal world Osseriton as a prison transport.
