# Ubrikkian star yacht

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ubrikkian star yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ubrikkian_star_yacht)

The Ubrikkian star yacht was a sleek light cruiser produced by Ubrikkian Industries. Built for quick getaways, the yacht came equipped with a hyperdrive, yet carried no weapons, apart from two concealed proton torpedo shafts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ubrikkian star yacht
- **Manufacturer:** Ubrikkian Industries
- **Armament:** Concealed proton torpedo tubes (2)
- **Affiliation:** Slams
