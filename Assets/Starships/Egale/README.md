# Egale

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Egale](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Egale)

Egale was a Nebulon-B frigate used by the Rebel Alliance. The Egale arrived too late to stop an Imperial capture operation on one of two Escort Carriers Prowler. The frigate launched multiple fighters such as B-wings and X-wings but the fighters along with the frigate were destroyed by two TIE Defenders.
