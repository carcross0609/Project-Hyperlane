# Trade Federation Troop Transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trade Federation Troop Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trade_Federation_Troop_Transport)

The Trade Federation Troop Transport was a battle droid transport craft employed by the Trade Federation around the time of the Occupation of Karthakk.
