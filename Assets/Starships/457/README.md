# 457

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [457](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/457)

457 was the designation for a group of three CR90 corvettes that belonged to the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. The warships had been stationed near Vergesso Base during the Second Battle of Bajic; as a result of the action, all three vessels were destroyed.
