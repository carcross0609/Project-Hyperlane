# The Inquisitor's TIE Advanced

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [The Inquisitor's TIE Advanced](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Inquisitor%27s_TIE_Advanced)

The Inquisitor's TIE Advanced was the personal TIE/ad v1 prototype TIE fighter entrusted to the Grand Inquisitor in his mission to hunt down Jedi for the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Inquisitor's TIE Advanced
- **Line:** TIE fighter series
- **Model:** TIE/ad v1 prototype
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 3.57 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,600 kph / 960 miles per hour
- **Engine:** Twin ion engine
- **Armament:**
  - 2 laser cannons
  - Projectile launcher that fired XX-23 S-thread trackers
  -   20 missile shells
- **Crew:** 1 as standard, 2-3 in cramped and unintended quarters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Inquisitorius
  - Spectres
- **Navy:** Imperial Starfighter Corps
- **Owners:** The Grand Inquisitor
