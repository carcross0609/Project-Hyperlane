# Fleet shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fleet shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fleet_shuttle)

The New Republic Fleet Shuttle, known informally amongst crewmen and officers as the gig, was a starship similar in size to an X-wing and a TIE Interceptor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fleet shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** New Republic
- **Length:** ~12 meters
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped, superior to T-65BR X-wing reconnaissance starfighter
- **Armament:** None
- **Crew:**
  - 2 (interstellar role)
  - 1 pilot
  - 1 engineer
- **Skeleton:**
  - 1 (intership role)
  - 1 pilot
- **Passengers:** 5 (typically 1)
- **Role:**
  - Intership personnel transport
  - Long-range interstellar personnel transport
  - Pilot evacuation
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
