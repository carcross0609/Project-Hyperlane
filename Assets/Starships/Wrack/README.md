# Wrack

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wrack](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrack)

Wrack was an interdictor cruiser that served in the Navy of the Imperial Remnant during the Yuuzhan Vong War. Commanded by Captain Mynar Devis, it was equipped with at least three TIE/D Defender starfighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wrack
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Model:** Immobilizer 418 cruiser
- **Length:** 600 meters
- **Width:** 300 meters
- **Height:** 100 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/D Defenders
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Remnant
- **Captains:** Captain Mynar Devis
