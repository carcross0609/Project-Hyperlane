# Loranda (starship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Loranda (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loranda_%28starship%29)

Loranda was a starship owned by Hetton, named after his mother, Loranda. The ship was eighty meters in length and could hold up to twenty passengers. Only one pilot was required to fly the ship. With Hetton's modifications, the ship had enough armor and firepower to tackle a small capital ship. Its interior was described as luxurious.
