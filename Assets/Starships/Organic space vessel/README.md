# Organic space vessel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Organic space vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Organic_space_vessel)

At least one thousand years before the formation of the Galactic Republic, the Morodin utilized organic space vessels to travel the stars. They were used to transport food from the Morodin colony on Varonat to the Morodin homeworld. The ships were grown, and at some point an ecological disaster left the Morodins unable to grow these ships, effectively severing contact between Morodin colonies.
