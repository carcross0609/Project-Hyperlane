# Inquisitor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Inquisitor (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitor_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

Inquisitor was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer used by Grand General Malcor Brashin as his personal starship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Inquisitor
- **Type:** Imperial I-class Star Destroyer
- **Role:** Ground support (personal ship of Grand General Malcor Brashin)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:**
  - Taggert
  - Malcor Brashin
