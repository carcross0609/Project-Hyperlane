# Republic troop carrier

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Republic troop carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_troop_carrier)

The Republic troop carrier was used by the Galactic Republic to deploy Republic rocket-jumpers around the year 4000 BBY. During the Second Battle of Onderon, one such carrier bore the callsign Wing 4.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Republic troop carrier
- **Model:** Republic troop carrier
- **Type:** Transport
- **Passengers:** Republic rocket-jumper squads
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
