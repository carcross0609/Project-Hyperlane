# Unidentified CR90 corvette

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified CR90 corvette (Command Sector North)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_CR90_corvette_%28Command_Sector_North%29)

A CR90 corvette served the Alliance to Restore the Republic in the Rebel Alliance Navy. In 4 ABY, the corvette was shot down during the Battle of Endor, but its crash landing gave its surviving crew an opportunity to raid the DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station.
