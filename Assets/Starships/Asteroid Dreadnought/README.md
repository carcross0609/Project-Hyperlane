# Asteroid Dreadnought

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Asteroid Dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asteroid_Dreadnought)

The Asteroid Dreadnought was a rumored Imperial Navy dreadnought that was camouflaged as a space rock during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 1 BBY, Colonel Haxen Delto of Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service wrote a report to Chief of Rebel Intelligence General Airen Cracken on Imperial superweapon projects, and included a section in the report regarding the rumors of the dreadnought.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Asteroid Dreadnought
- **Type:** Dreadnought
- **Countermeasures:** Camouflage
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire (Rumored)
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy (Rumored)
