# ID-83

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ID-83](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ID-83)

ID-83 was the designation of Sentinel-class landing craft, a transport utilized by the Galactic Empire. In 13 BBY, it was in Coronet Spaceport on the planet Corellia scheduled to depart of the Naval Academy on Carida. Imperial Recruitment Officer Chief Drawd Munbrin informed the recently recruited Han Solo to proceed to the transport.
