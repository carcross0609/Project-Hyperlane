# DFS-2VR

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [DFS-2VR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DFS-2VR)

DFS-2VR was a Vulture-class starfighter that served in the Separatist Alliance during the Clone Wars, and at one point fought alongside R0-GR, a B1-series battle droid. During the Imperial Era, the Freemakers were hired to repair and deliver DFS-2VR to the Imperial Secret Base 79.
