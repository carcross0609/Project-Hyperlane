# Demoneye

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Demoneye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Demoneye)

The Demoneye was a starship used during the reign of the New Republic. In about 19 ABY, the Demoneye traveled from the planet Ventooine to collect a shipment of the spice Tempest from the Bosph system, and some of the spice was eventually received by the Rodian Threnda on the world Budpock.
