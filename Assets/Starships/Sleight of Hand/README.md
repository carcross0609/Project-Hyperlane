# Sleight of Hand

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sleight of Hand](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sleight_of_Hand)

The Sleight of Hand was a modified Telgorn dropship used by Cad Bane during the Clone Wars as a go-to craft for delivering precious cargo, both living and otherwise. The ship has been heavily customized and re-purposed by Bane himself, who installed military-grade armor and weapons and an advanced hyperdrive.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sleight of Hand
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Telgorn dropship
- **Modifier:** Cad Bane
- **Sysmods:** Military-grade armor and weapons
- **Countermeasures:** Sensor jammer
- **Armament:**
  - 1 top mounted laser turret
  - 2 heavy laser cannons
  - 1 ion cannon
- **Role:** Transport
- **Owners:** Cad Bane
