# King's Ransom

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [King's Ransom (treasury ship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/King%27s_Ransom_%28treasury_ship%29)

The King's Ransom was a treasury starship used by the reconstituted Sith Empire around the time of the Cold War. The ship was robbed by two thieves named Vaz Traniff and Combo, who were aided by a smuggler. Among the treasures it held was the entire sum of trade between the Empire and the Hutt Cartel on Nar Shaddaa. The Empire contained the skull of Canderous Ordo within the vessel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** King's Ransom
- **Bays:** At least 1 hangar bay
- **Role:** Treasury starship
