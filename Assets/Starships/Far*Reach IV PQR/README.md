# Far*Reach IV PQR

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Far*Reach IV PQR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Far%2AReach_IV_PQR)

The Far*Reach IV PQR was a type of scout ship used by the Imperial Survey Corps during the Galactic Civil War. The IV PQR was a heavily armed version of Loronar's popular Far Reach scout line.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Far*Reach IV PQR
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Corporation
- **Line:** Far*Reach scout line
- **Length:** 80 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 4
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Four laser cannons
- **Crew:** 6
- **Capacity:** 150 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 6 months
- **Role:** Survey vessel
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
