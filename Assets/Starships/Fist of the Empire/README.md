# Fist of the Empire

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fist of the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fist_of_the_Empire)

The Fist of the Empire was a Pellaeon-class Star Destroyer in the service of the Imperial Navy during the Second Imperial Civil War. In 138 ABY, under the command of Captain Shenes, the Fist of the Empire participated on the allied assault against Coruscant alongside Fel-loyalist vessels such as the War Hammer and the fleet of the Galactic Alliance Remnant. During the battle, however, the Fist of the Empire suffered heavy damage from Sith Annihilator-class starfighters. Though Captain Shenes requested assistance, the starship was destroyed shortly afterwards.
