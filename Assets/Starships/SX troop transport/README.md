# SX troop transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SX troop transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SX_troop_transport)

The SX troop transport, also called a L two hundred, was a transport used by the Republic during the Clone Wars and later by the Empire to transport troops. They had broad wings and pointed bows.
