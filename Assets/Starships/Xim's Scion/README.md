# Xim's Scion

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xim's Scion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xim%27s_Scion)

The Xim's Scion was a pirate cruiser destroyed in a skirmish with the CorSec several centuries before the Battle of Yavin. A group of enterprising Corellians towed the forward 250-meter portion of the cruiser into an elliptical orbit around Gus Treta, and converted into a refueling depot and market over the following years.
