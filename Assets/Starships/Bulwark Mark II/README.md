# Bulwark Mark II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bulwark Mark II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bulwark_Mark_II)

The Bulwark Mark II was a Battlecruiser class and the successor to the Bulwark Mark I battleship manufactured by the Techno Union. The Confederacy of Independent Systems used the Bulwark Mark II as one of its larger warships against the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bulwark Mark II
- **Type:** Battlecruiser
- **Role:** Warship
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
