# Unidentified YT-2400 light freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified YT-2400 light freighter (Clan Markona)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_YT-2400_light_freighter_%28Clan_Markona%29)

A YT-2400 light freighter was held by the Galactic Empire at the Imperial Impound Yard on Novka. It was stolen by Tula Markona during an attack on the Imperial Impound Yard, and used by Clan Markona. The clan briefly rendezvoused with the Rebel Alliance leaders Leia Organa, Luke Skywalker and Han Solo on Brentaal IV, before parting ways to find General Hera Syndulla aboard the Geist, whilst the rebels went in search of the Home One in the Kaliida Nebula.
