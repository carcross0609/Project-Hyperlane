# Keizar-Volvec bulk cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Keizar-Volvec bulk cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keizar-Volvec_bulk_cruiser)

The Keizar-Volvec bulk cruiser was a capital ship used by the Imperial Navy during the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Keizar-Volvec bulk cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Keizar-Volvec
- **Type:** Bulk cruiser
- **Armament:** Missiles
- **Complement:**
  - B28 Extinction-class bombers
  - Mark VI Supremacy-class starfighters
  - VT-22 light troop transports
  - At least one shuttle
- **Bays:** 1 Fore Hangar Bay
- **Othersystems:** Quarantine Bay
- **Role:** Capital ship
- **Firstuse:** 3643 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sith Empire
  - Imperial Navy
