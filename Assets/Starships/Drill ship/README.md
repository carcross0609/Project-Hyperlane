# Drill ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Drill ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drill_ship)

Drill ships were a type of starship with long, sharp noses on the prow of their ship used for ramming into other ships, and were even capable of splitting small asteroids. They were used during the Eiram and E'ronoh War as part of E'ronoh's fleet. in 382 BBY. The Spiral-class Viz-Core Eviscerator was a model of drill ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Drill ship
- **Affiliation:** E'ronoh's military
