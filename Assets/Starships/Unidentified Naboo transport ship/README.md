# Unidentified Naboo transport ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Naboo transport ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Naboo_transport_ship)

A transport was used by the resistance group called the Amidalans to transport their ground troops to Polis Massa, where they engaged the Sith Darth Vader to avenge the Death of their former Queen and Senator Padmé Amidala. The Amidalans fleet was completely crushed by Vader.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Naboo transport ship
- **Type:** Transport
- **Passengers:** At least 11
- **Affiliation:** Amidalans
