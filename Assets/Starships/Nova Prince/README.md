# Nova Prince

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nova Prince](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nova_Prince)

The Nova Prince was a bulk freighter operated by a group of exiles from the Red Nebula during the era of the Galactic Civil War. In 0 ABY, the leader of these exiles, Klysk, kept a number of mercenaries, smugglers and spacers prisoner aboard the Nova Prince&mdash;these beings were kidnapped on fringe worlds like Terminus, in order to serve as thieves for an attempted heist of the Red Nebula's Great Life Jewels.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nova Prince
- **Type:** Bulk freighter
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Crew:** Red Nebula humanoids
- **Passengers:** Many prisoners
- **Role:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** 0 ABY, Sun of the Planet of the Red Nebula
- **Affiliation:** Red Nebula exiles
- **Captains:** Klysk
