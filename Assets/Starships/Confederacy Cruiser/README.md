# Confederacy Cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Confederacy Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Confederacy_Cruiser)

Confederacy Cruisers were air cruisers employed by the Confederacy of Independent Systems from the earliest months of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Confederacy Cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Hull:** Geonosian design
- **Armament:** Cannon (1)
- **Crew:** At least 3
- **Role:** Air cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
