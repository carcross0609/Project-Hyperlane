# Omega's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Omega's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omega%27s_ship)

Omega's ship was a starship used by the clone Omega during the Imperial Era. She kept it docked in a cave next to Pabu cove. Omega eventually left Pabu in the ship to join the Alliance to Restore the Republic, accompanied by the GNK-series power droid Gonky.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Omega's ship
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Armament:**
  - Frontal laser cannons (2)
  - Rear laser cannon (1)
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Owners:** Omega
