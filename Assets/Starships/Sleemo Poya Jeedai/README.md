# Sleemo Poya Jeedai

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sleemo Poya Jeedai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sleemo_Poya_Jeedai)

Sleemo Poya Jeedai was a Hutt-owned yacht. Its name roughly translated as "The Drooling Jedi". It once traveled to Aquella Firth's home system, where the band Distraction were scheduled to perform aboard it. During the concert, Niklas Cories attempted to kill the crowd and damage the ship by throwing a thermal detonator.
