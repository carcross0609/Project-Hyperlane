# Right to Rule

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Right to Rule](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Right_to_Rule)

Right to Rule was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer that served as Grand Admiral Gilad Pellaeon's flagship during the Imperial Remnant's actions in the war with the Yuuzhan Vong from 28 ABY to 29 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Right to Rule
- **Type:** Imperial I-class Star Destroyer
- **Complement:** >200 starfighters
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Remnant
- **Navy:**

  - Fourth Fleet
- **Fleet:** Fleet Group Right to Rule
- **Captains:**

  - Ansween
