# Darth Venamis's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Venamis's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Venamis%27s_starship)

Around 67 BBY, the Bith Sith Lord Darth Venamis possessed a starship. This ship was used to transport him to Sojourn where he dueled the Muun Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Plagueis. In the aftermath of his defeat, the ship was removed and stored.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Darth Venamis's starship
- **Crew:** At least one
- **Affiliation:** Order of the Sith Lords
- **Owners:** Darth Venamis
