# Oggrud

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oggrud](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oggrud)

The Oggrud was a Barloz-class freighter used by a team of New Republic individuals to travel to the Outer Rim's Anoat sector to assess the situation of Imperial Grand Moff Ubrik Adelhard's Iron Blockade there. In the fringes of the Anoat sector, Oggrud was surrounded by ships affiliated with the Spice Runners of Kijimi during the mission. After being threatened by Vigilch aboard the Ragged Claw, pilot Rynn Zenat feigned that they were a merchant vessel. This feign failed, and Marinda Gan asserted that they should destroy the ship, defying Adelhard's wishes. Zeva Bliss struck her for this, meanwhile Zenat charged the Spice Runners' ships with equipped explosives, heavily damaging them. Oggrud crew was captured by Adelhard's forces for interrogation, though were later broken out by Kith Alaytia with help from Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker.
