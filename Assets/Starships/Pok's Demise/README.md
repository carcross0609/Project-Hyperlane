# Pok's Demise

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pok's Demise](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pok%27s_Demise)

Pok's Demise was a freighter owned by the smuggler Platt Okeefe. Her Twi'lek friend, Tru'eb Cholakk, helped secure the finances for the ship, which Okeefe named after her former boss at the Klatooinian Trade Guild, Pok Nar-Ten. He had sold her into slavery for failing to pay her debts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pok's Demise
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Engine:** Ion drives
- **Complement:** Ventral gun mount
- **Role:** Freighter
- **Owners:** Platt Okeefe
