# Colicoid Swarm

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Colicoid Swarm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Colicoid_Swarm)

Colicoid Swarm was a modified Trade Federation Providence-class carrier/destroyer in the Confederate Navy. The vessel was colored a bluish grey, with subtle red bow stripes and dark-toned keel, nose, and structure.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Colicoid Swarm
- **Model:** Modified Providence-class carrier/destroyer
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Armament:** Turbolasers
- **Bays:** 2 main hangars
- **Role:**

  - Destroyer
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Navy:** Confederate Navy
