# Andor family hauler

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Andor family hauler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andor_family_hauler)

The Andor family owned a FR-193D hauler from at least 24 BBY. By 5 BBY, it had become disused and was nonfunctional, but was kept by Cassian Andor as a hideaway.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Andor family hauler
- **Manufacturer:** Ferrix Field Yards
- **Model:** FR-193D cargo transport
- **Type:** Hauler
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Crew:** 2 (1 pilot, 1 co-pilot)
- **Passengers:** At least one
- **Role:** Andor family's transport
- **Destroyed:** Between 24 BBY and 5 BBY (fallen into disrepair)
- **Affiliation:** Andor family
- **Owners:** Maarva Carassi Andor
- **Captains:** Maarva Carassi Andor
