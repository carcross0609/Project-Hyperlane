# Harbinger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harbinger (landing ship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harbinger_%28landing_ship%29)

The Harbinger was a large experimental landing ship designed to conquer and hold planets for the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Custom-built by the Geonosians for Count Dooku, leader of the Confederacy, the ship boasted a compartmentalized efficiency that allowed it to deploy armies within minutes, and its supercharged light drive made it perfect for invading unsuspecting star systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Harbinger
- **Manufacturer:** Geonosians
- **Hdsystem:** Supercharged light drive
- **Power:** 7 power cores
- **Armament:**
  - 12 large turrets
  - Multiple ion cannons
  - 1 prismatic turbolaser
- **Complement:** Several squadrons of tanks and walkers
- **Bays:** 2
- **Role:** Landing craft
- **Destroyed:** 21.9 BBY, Alaris Prime
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Owners:** Dooku
