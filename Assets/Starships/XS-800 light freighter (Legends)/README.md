# XS-800 light freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XS-800 light freighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XS-800_light_freighter/Legends)

The XS-800 light freighter was designed and introduced several years before the Battle of Yavin.

- **Model:** XS-800 light freighter

- **Max Speed:** 950 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:**

- **Armament:** Double laser cannon (1)
- **Escapepods:** 2 escape pods
- **Crew:** 2
- **Passengers:** 8
