# Unidentified smuggling freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified smuggling freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_smuggling_freighter)

A smuggling freighter traveled on the Rimma Trade Route while carrying several tons of explosives before being intercepted by Imperial patrols in 3 ABY. The suspects on board the freighter were arrested but pled not guilty. The incident was then covered on the HoloNet News by reporter Dorna Parros, who claimed that the prosecutors involved in the case expected the accused to receive lengthy sentences after a quick trial.
