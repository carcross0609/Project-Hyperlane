# EE-104 Fisheye

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EE-104 Fisheye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EE-104_Fisheye)

EE-104 Fisheyes were stealth vessels in use throughout the galaxy during the time following the Galactic Civil War. Two were captured by the Lost Tribe of Sith around for use in the Tribe's armada. One of them, the Cryptic Warning, was commanded by Sith Saber Querdan Dei during the Jedi Queen project.
