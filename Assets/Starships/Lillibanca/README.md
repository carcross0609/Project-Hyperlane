# Lillibanca

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lillibanca](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lillibanca)

The Lillibanca was a cruiser that served in the Confederation Fleet in the aftermath of the Battle of Kashyyyk in 40 ABY during the Second Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Alliance and the Confederation. The ship took part in the firefighting efforts against the planetary firestorm raging across the planet Kashyyyk in the aftermath of the battle. During these firefighting efforts, the ship targeted a series of beacons on the planet with turbolaser blasts in order to ignite a controlled burn.
