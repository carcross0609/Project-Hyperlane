# Founder

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Founder (Acclamator-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Founder_%28Acclamator-class%29)

The Founder was one of the three Acclamator-class assault ships dispatched by the Galactic Senate to defend the planet Duro during the Battle of Duro. The ship and its companions were all destroyed, demonstrating General Grievous' prowess as a master tactician.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Founder
- **Model:** Acclamator-class assault ship
- **Destroyed:** 21 - 20 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
