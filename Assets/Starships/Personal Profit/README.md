# Personal Profit

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Personal Profit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Personal_Profit)

The Personal Profit was a modified YT-1300 light freighter that was owned by the Human Chalmer Trillili. The freighter served as the flagship of the The Scourge, a small band of pirates that were led by Trillili, and during the Galactic Civil War, the Personal Profit participated in a Scourge attack on a Rebel Alliance starship in the Alluuvian system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Personal Profit
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Max Speed:** 950 kmph
- **Poweroutput:**

  - 1 proton torpedo launcher
- **Role:** Flagship
