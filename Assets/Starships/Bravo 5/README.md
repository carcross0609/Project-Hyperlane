# Bravo 5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bravo 5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravo_5)

Bravo 5 was the personal N-1 starfighter of Dineé Ellberger during the Invasion of Naboo in 32 BBY. The starfighter was equipped with excellent evasion capabilities. Ellberger used the fighter during the Battle of Naboo.

- **Name:** Bravo 5
