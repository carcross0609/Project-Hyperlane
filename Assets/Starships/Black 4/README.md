# Black 4

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Black 4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_4)

Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems, Black 4 was a TIE/ln starfighter within the Imperial Navy. Armed with a pair of L-s1 laser cannons, The TIE/LN starfighter served in the Galactic Empire's Black Squadron by 0 BBY. By that year, it was operated by the TIE pilot DS-61-4 and stationed on the Imperial DS-1 Orbital Battle Station. Black 4's cockpit featured the standard wheel and yoke control system for TIE pilots.
