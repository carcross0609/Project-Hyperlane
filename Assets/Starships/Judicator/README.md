# Judicator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Judicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Judicator)

The Judicator was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer in service of the Imperial Navy during the reign of the Galactic Empire. Commanded by Admiral Durril, it was part of the One Oh Third Task Force, and it was dispatched by the Imperial High Command to Batonn in order to retake Scrim Island from the Batonn insurgents. However, in part due to Durril's failed tactics, it was crippled by planetary ion cannons and was put out of action for the remainder of the campaign, which would later be suppressed by Admiral Thrawn.
