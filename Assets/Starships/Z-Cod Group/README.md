# Z-Cod Group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Cod Group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Cod_Group)

Cod were a pair of modular conveyors that belonged to Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin. They were sent to loot equipment from ZA-13. However they were disabled by Tan Maarek Stele, then boarded by imperial boarding parties to recover the cargo, before being destroyed.
