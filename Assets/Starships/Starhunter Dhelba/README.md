# Starhunter Dhelba

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starhunter Dhelba](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starhunter_Dhelba)

The Starhunter Dhelba was a Starhunter menagerie ark that operated during the time of the Galactic Republic. In about 800 BBY, it crashed on the forest moon of Endor, in the Outer Rim. It had been carrying various creatures, including teeks, gupins and tree goats. They escaped and established populations on the moon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Starhunter Dhelba
- **Model:** Starhunter menagerie ark
- **Complement:**

  - Teeks
  - Tree goats
- **Destroyed:** c. 800 BBY
