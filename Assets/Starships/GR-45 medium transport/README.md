# GR-45 medium transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GR-45 medium transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GR-45_medium_transport)

The GR-45 medium transport was a civilian-use model of transport ship manufactured by Gallofree Yards. As a member of the GR-series, it had the same hull shape as the GR-75 medium transport, another Gallofree Yards product. Shortly before the Battle of Yavin, one such ship was parked illegally on the Outer Curved Street in Mos Eisley, Tatooine. On their way to Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina, Obi-Wan Kenobi and Luke Skywalker passed by this transport, alongside teams of ASP-7 labor droids loading it.
