# BaKaar group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BaKaar group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BaKaar_group)

BaKaar was a group of three Assassin-class corvettes used by Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's splinter government fleet. Along with Falcon group and Arditi group, it was sent to destroy Lord Vader's flagship, the Garrett. However, Imperial ace Maarek Stele helped the Garrett destroying the corvettes along with the other groups, but the Interdictor Harpax was lost in the meantime.
