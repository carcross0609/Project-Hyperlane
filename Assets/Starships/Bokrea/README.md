# Bokrea

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bokrea](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bokrea)

The Bokrea was a patrol cruiser of the Chiss Ascendancy navy that was present at Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand, having been invited to the battle by Admiral Ar'alani. During the battle, the Bokrea stayed side by side with the Chiss warship Parala, facing a Grysk Prism-class BattleChief.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bokrea
