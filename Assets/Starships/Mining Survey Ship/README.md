# Mining Survey Ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mining Survey Ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mining_Survey_Ship)

The Mining Survey Ship was a model of starship manufactured by Merrick and Kalb Industries. The Bright Seeker, a vessel operated by House Calipsa, was a modified Mining Survey Ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mining Survey Ship
- **Manufacturer:** Merrick and Kalb Industries
- **Type:** Mining ship
- **Role:** Survey vessel
