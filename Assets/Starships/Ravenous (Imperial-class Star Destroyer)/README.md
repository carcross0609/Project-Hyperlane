# Ravenous

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ravenous (Imperial-class Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ravenous_%28Imperial-class_Star_Destroyer%29)

The Ravenous was a Star Destroyer that served in the navy of the Galactic Empire. The Ravenous once stopped Han Solo and Chewbacca's ship, the Millennium Falcon in the Saavin system, though Solo managed to disguise his identity.

- **Name:** Ravenous
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
