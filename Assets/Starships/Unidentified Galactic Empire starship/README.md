# Unidentified Galactic Empire starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Galactic Empire starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Galactic_Empire_starship)

A starship served within the navy of the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, a member of the Empire who served as a mole for the criminal syndicate Crimson Dawn sabotaged the ship's reactor, trapping seventeen engineers aboard. Imperial officer Lieutenant Beilert Valance later rescued the engineers but suffered radiation burns as a result.
