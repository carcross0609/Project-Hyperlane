# Allegiance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Allegiance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Allegiance)

The Allegiance was a Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier which served in the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. In 4 ABY, it began serving the Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing along with two other cruiser-carriers. However, after the New Republic carried out a devastating attack on the 204th at its stronghold in the Pandem Nai system, the Allegiance and fellow Quasar Fire-class cruiser carrier Aerie escaped with much of the fighter wing's personnel.
