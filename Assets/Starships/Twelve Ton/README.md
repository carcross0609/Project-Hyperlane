# Twelve Ton

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Twelve Ton](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twelve_Ton)

Twelve Ton was a transport in service of the Galactic Alliance during the Yuuzhan Vong War. It was produced by the shipyards of Contruum, and named after an animal of the world.
