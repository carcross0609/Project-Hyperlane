# Bentor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bentor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bentor)

Bentor was a Muurian transport. Bentor was just about to enter the hangar of Corvalis when the incident between the Rebel Alliance and the Hurrim took place.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bentor
- **Manufacturer:** Muurian Interstellar
- **Model:** Muurian transport
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Length:** 30.5 meters
- **Width:** 29.4 meters
- **Height:** 8.8 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** 80 SBD
- **Hull:** 30 RU
- **Armament:**
  - 2 double laser cannon turrets
  - 3 laser cannons
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
