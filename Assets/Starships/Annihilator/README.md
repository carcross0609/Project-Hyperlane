# Annihilator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Annihilator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Annihilator)

The Annihilator was a Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. In 4 ABY, it served in the Imperial fleet defending the second Death Star. When the station was attacked by the Rebel Alliance, Imperial Commander Ciena Ree flew past the Annihilator to ensure its bridge was not attacked.
