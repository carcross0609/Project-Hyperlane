# Sicorro

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sicorro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sicorro)

The Sicorro was a Nebulon-B2 frigate under the service of the Galactic Empire. She arrived at Depot XLS for repairs but was also attacked by B-wing starfighters from the Rebel Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sicorro
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Nebulon-B2 frigate
- **Length:** 253.2 meters
- **Width:** 147.7 meters
- **Height:** 94.3 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
