# Y-85 Titan dropship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Y-85 Titan dropship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Y-85_Titan_dropship)

The Y-85 Titan dropship was a large, blocky dropship used by the Galactic Empire to deploy assault walkers onto a planetary surface. Manufactured by Incom Corporation, the Titan was equipped with massive repulsor units, two pairs of forward twin laser cannons, and space to accommodate four All Terrain Armored Transports and four All Terrain Scout Transports simultaneously. Only the largest Imperial warships had hangars capacious enough to hold Y-85 Titan dropships.
