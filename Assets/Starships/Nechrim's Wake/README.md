# Nechrim's Wake

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nechrim's Wake](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nechrim%27s_Wake)

Nechrim's Wake, also known as Necrim's Wake, was a New Republic CR90 corvette led by Captain Gauvis during the New Republic. On the edge of the Fenril sector, it was attacked by Zantarrk Gang starships and forced to crash on Loradil.

- **Name:** Nechrim's Wake
- **Line:** CR-series
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
