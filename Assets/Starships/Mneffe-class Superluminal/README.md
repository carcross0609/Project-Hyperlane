# Mneffe-class Superluminal

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mneffe-class Superluminal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mneffe-class_Superluminal)

The Mneffe-class Superluminal was a rapid passenger shuttle produced by DuroTech during the early years of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mneffe-class Superluminal
- **Manufacturer:** DuroTech
- **Model:** Mneffe-class superluminal
- **Type:** rapid passenger shuttle
- **Cost:** 200,000 credits (new), 100,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 27 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,000 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 1 hyperdrive
  - Class 10 hyperdrive (backup)
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Turret-mounted double laser cannon (1)
- **Crew:** Pilot (1), Copilot (1)
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 20 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Role:** Passenger transport
