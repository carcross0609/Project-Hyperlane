# Sable II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sable II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sable_II)

The Sable II was a Corellian gunship commandeered by the pirate Karn Granzor. It was destroyed in battle with the Anto's Star which was manned by former crewmembers of Sable II.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sable II
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** DP20 gunship
- **Type:** Gunship
- **Hyperdrive:** Yes
- **Role:** Piracy
- **Affiliation:** Karn Granzor's pirates
