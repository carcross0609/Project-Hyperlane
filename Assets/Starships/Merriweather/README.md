# Merriweather

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Merriweather](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merriweather)

The Merriweather was a bulk cruiser and flagship of the Tof invasion fleet. It was a large boat-shaped cruiser, commanded by Tof forces in the Battle of Saijo in 4 ABY. The design was reminiscent of earlier styles, such as the Fairwind.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Merriweather
- **Model:** Tof bulk cruiser
- **Width:** 1,800 meters
- **Complement:** Tof starfighters
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Tof
- **Navy:** Tof invasion fleet
