# Polestar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Polestar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Polestar)

The Polestar was the Royal House of Alderaan's royal yacht. When Princess Leia Organa visited Coruscant for the first time in 5 BBY whilst serving as her father Bail Organa's intern, they flew to Coruscant on the Polestar. She also used the ship to travel with Lieutenant Ress Batten to the planet Crait to investigate the Attack on Calderos.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Polestar
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Affiliation:** House of Organa
- **Owners:** Bail Organa
