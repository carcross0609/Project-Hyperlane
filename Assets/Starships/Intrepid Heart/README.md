# Intrepid Heart

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Intrepid Heart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_Heart)

The Intrepid Heart was a GX1 short hauler of the Galactic Republic that was used as the personal transport of a Jedi Master on a mission to recover Sith artifacts which was eventually lost. During the Imperial Era, sources in the Alliance to Restore the Republic stated that a Rebel Commando team spotted a crashed GX1 short hauler in the mountains of Naator, and records suggested that it might have been the Intrepid Heart. The ship was outfitted with Republic branding.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Intrepid Heart
- **Model:** GX1 short hauler
- **Type:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** Prior to the Imperial Era
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Jedi Order
