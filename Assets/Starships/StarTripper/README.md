# StarTripper

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [StarTripper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/StarTripper)

The StarTripper was a type of yacht manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. It was similar in appearance to SoroSuub's Personal Luxury Yacht 3000. One of these ships, named She's a Chancer, was present at Sinkhole Station in the Maw in 43 ABY. Vestara Khai took command of it following her escape from Jedi Grand Master Luke Skywalker and his son, Ben Skywalker, following a failed ambush of the Jedi. Khai sold it to a mechanic named Monarg on the planet Dathomir. Dyon Stadd later made a legal claim to the ship and renamed it Bright Sun in honor of the newly founded Bright Sun Clan.
