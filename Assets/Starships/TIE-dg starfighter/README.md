# TIE/dg starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/dg starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/dg_starfighter)

The TIE/dg starfighter, also known as the TIE Dagger or the Sith TIE fighter, was a type of Sith Eternal-affiliated TIE fighter series starship that was forged in secret by forces under the command of the reborn Darth Sidious, along with the Xyston-class Star Destroyer, the AAL-2100/9.5 atmospheric assault lander, and other new technology created by the Sith cult.
