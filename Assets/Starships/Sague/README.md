# Sague

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sague](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sague)

Sague was a group of three Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttles employed to defend a Salvage platform called AZ 77. The shuttles attempted to protect the platform and provide time for Corellian transports Lin, Ko and Kaar to escape. The Escort shuttles engaged TIE Avengers and were destroyed in the skirmish.
