# Leilana's Dagger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Leilana's Dagger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leilana%27s_Dagger)

Leilana's Dagger was a modified CloakShape fighter piloted by the male Phindian pilot and mercenary Jayn during the Galactic Civil War. The Dagger was named after Jayn's long-lost love. It was destroyed on Iope, the third moon of Rinn, by the Imperial assassin X-7 shortly after the Battle of Yavin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Leilana's Dagger
- **Model:** CloakShape fighter (modified)
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Owners:** Jayn
