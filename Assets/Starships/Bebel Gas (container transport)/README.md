# Bebel Gas

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bebel Gas (container transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bebel_Gas_%28container_transport%29)

Bebel Gas was a container transport, presumably belonging to the company that was present at Tinoon Station when a pirate raid took place. The raid consisted of Y-wings, Heavy Lifters and Marauder-class corvettes called Wraith. The raid was driven off by the station's security systems, as well as the assistance of Ace Azzameen in the Otana. The ship's cargo was hydrogen.
