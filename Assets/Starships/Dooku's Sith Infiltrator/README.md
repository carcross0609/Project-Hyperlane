# Dooku's Sith Infiltrator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dooku's Sith Infiltrator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku%27s_Sith_Infiltrator)

Count Dooku was presented a Sith Infiltrator after falling to the dark side of the Force and becoming the apprentice of Darth Sidious. It was a modified Star Courier similar in appearance to Darth Maul's Scimitar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dooku's Sith Infiltrator
- **Manufacturer:** Republic Sienar Systems
- **Model:** Modified Star Courier
- **Type:**

  - Experimental starship
- **Length:** 26.5 meters
- **Owners:** Count Dooku
