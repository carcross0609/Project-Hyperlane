# Bogey Three

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bogey Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogey_Three)

Bogey Three was the name given to a light cruiser-sized warship of the Grysk Hegemony by the crew of the Imperial Star Destroyer Chimaera. Along with Bogey Four, Bogey Three joined a battle against Chimaera, during which it was attacked by the Empire's TIE fighters. The ship suffered minor damage but destroyed several of its attackers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bogey Three
- **Affiliation:** Grysk Hegemony
