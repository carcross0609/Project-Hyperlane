# Unidentified CR90 corvette

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified CR90 corvette (Resistance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_CR90_corvette_%28Resistance%29)

A CR90 corvette served in the fleet of the Alliance to Restore the Republic, the Defense Fleet of the New Republic and the naval forces of Resistance during its service. The CR90 corvette saw action during the Galactic Civil War, ranging from the Battle of Scarif to the Battle of Jakku.
