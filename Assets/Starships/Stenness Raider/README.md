# Stenness Raider

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stenness Raider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stenness_Raider)

The Stenness Raider was one of the two ore-raiding ships used by the pirate captain Finhead Stonebone in 3999 BBY to raid Ithullan ore haulers. It was a modified Byblos Drive Yards E-2 asteroid mining vehicle. The Stenness Raider was insectoid in appearance and approximately one hundred meters long.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stenness Raider
- **Manufacturer:** Byblos Drive Yards
- **Model:** Modified E-2 Asteroid Miner
- **Modifier:** Finhead Stonebone's pirate gang
- **Length:** 110 meters
- **Role:** Pirate ship
