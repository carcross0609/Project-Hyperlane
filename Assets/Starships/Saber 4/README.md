# Saber 4

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saber 4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saber_4)

Saber 4 was an Imperial Navy TIE/IN interceptor which was manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems. The interceptor was part of the Imperial 181st Imperial Fighter Wing's Saber Squadron by 4 ABY, and piloted by DS-181-4, who served as the wingman of Major Turr Phennir, also from Saber Squadron. Saber 4 was often assigned to fly in a reserve position in battle, and bore a bloodstripe representing at least 10 kills made by DS-181-4.
