# The Elder's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [The Elder's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Elder%27s_starship)

The Elder's starship was a black and red starship that resembled old Sith ships. It was the Elder's personal starship, which was destroyed after the Elder, Tajin Crosser and Dan G'vash briefly dueled.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Elder's starship
- **Manufacturer:** Sith
- **Armament:** Two laser cannons
- **Crew:** At least 1
- **Passengers:** At least 1
- **Othersystems:** Self-destruct mechanism
- **Destroyed:** Between 832 BBY and 32 BBY
- **Affiliation:** The Elder
- **Owners:** The Elder
