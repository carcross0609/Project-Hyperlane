# Harrow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Harrow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harrow)

The Harrow was a Victory-class Star Destroyer in service to the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At one point, the Star Destroyer flew above a brown-colored celestial body. The ship was equipped with sublight engines. The Harrow was in service to the Galactic Empire in the weeks following its formation. In the aftermath of a skirmish on Etti IV, the Harrow was in orbit over the icy rings of Therezar as construction was occurring underneath it and received the damaged Theta-class T-4a shuttle of Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Vader designated ST-295. Captain Aust Wausau and his subordinate, Lieutenant Marl Semtin, received Lord Vader and the survivors of Shadow Platoon.
