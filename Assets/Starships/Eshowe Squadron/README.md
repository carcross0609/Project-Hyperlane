# Eshowe Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eshowe Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eshowe_Squadron)

Eshowe Squadron was a small Squadron of 2 R-41 Starchasers used by Rneekii pirates. They were deployed to provide cover for transport group Ibutho which were attempting to board and capture Imperial Modified corvette group Hashim. The pirates were unsuccessful and were destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele.
