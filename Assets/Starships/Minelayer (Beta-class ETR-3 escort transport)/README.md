# Minelayer (Beta-class ETR-3 escort transport)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Minelayer (Beta-class ETR-3 escort transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minelayer_%28Beta-class_ETR-3_escort_transport%29)

Minelayer was an Imperial Beta-class ETR-3 escort transport equipped with one or more minelayers. It was tasked with laying a mine field around Comm Center, and was presumably destroyed by either the Rebel Alliance pilots who attacked that station or the Azzameen family, who hacked into the base's systems.
