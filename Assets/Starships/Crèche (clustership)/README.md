# Crèche (clustership)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crèche (clustership)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cr%C3%A8che_%28clustership%29)

The Crèche was a Yuuzhan Vong clustership under the command of Chine-kal. Following the Battle of Gyndine, a number of prisoners of war (including Wurth Skidder, Randa Besadii Diori, and Roa) were taken aboard the Crèche to serve the developing yammosks.
