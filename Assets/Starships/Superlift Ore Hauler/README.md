# Superlift Ore Hauler

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Superlift Ore Hauler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Superlift_Ore_Hauler)

The Superlift Ore Hauler, sometimes referred to as a Superlifter, was a deep space ore hauler. The vessel was manufactured by Corellian Engineering Corporation. The ore hauler was slow, sluggish, unarmed, and had a tiny crew. It could carry 90,000 metric tons, cost 1.5 million credits and was 155 meters long.
