# ST-149

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ST-149](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ST-149)

ST-149, or ST 149 was a Delta-class T-3c shuttle that was used as a transport for Director Orson Krennic and his death trooper unit. Krennic chose the ship due to his appreciation for its unusual geometry, although he held no sentimentality for the craft. Captain Dunstig Pterro held more affection for the ship, nicknaming it Pteradon.
