# Aka'jor-class shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aka'jor-class shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aka%27jor-class_shuttle)

The Aka'jor-class shuttle, or simply the Mandalorian shuttle, was a model of shuttle used by the New Mandalorians of Mandalore. It was produced by MandalMotors and saw use during the Clone Wars on Mandalore.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aka'jor-class shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** MandalMotors
- **Model:** Aka'jor-class shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Length:** 17.97 meters
- **Max Speed:** 900 kph
- **Hdsystem:** None
- **Crew:** At least 2 pilots
- **Passengers:** At least 4
- **Role:**
  - Law enforcement
  - Transport
- **Affiliation:** New Mandalorians
