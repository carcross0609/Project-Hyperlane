# The Stone Heart

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [The Stone Heart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Stone_Heart)

The Stone Heart was a modified YT-1300 light freighter that operated in the Elrood sector during the Galactic Civil War. After the owner of the freighter made weapons upgrades to the vessel without a permit, the freighter was impounded by the Galactic Empire in the docking bay of the Derilyn Space Defense Platform, in orbit of the planet Derilyn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Stone Heart
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
