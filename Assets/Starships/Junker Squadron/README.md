# Junker Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Junker Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Junker_Squadron)

Junker Squadron was a squadron of 4 X-wings used by Bothans during the Galactic Civil War. The fighters, along with Logan Squadron and Yme Squadron attempted to protect Muurian transports Botani, and an escort shuttle Com 1 from being captured by Imperial forces lead by Darth Vader. The fighters were all destroyed by Assault Gunboats, one of which piloted by Maarek Stele and the transports were captured.
