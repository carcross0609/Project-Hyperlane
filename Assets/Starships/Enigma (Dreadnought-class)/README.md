# Enigma

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Enigma (Dreadnought-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enigma_%28Dreadnought-class%29)

The Enigma was a Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser in the Rebel Alliance Navy. Having first served during the Republic Era, the heavy cruiser was eventually appropriated by the Alliance to Restore the Republic. During its service with the Alliance, the starship was allegedly haunted; it suffered power fluctations, new parts on board mysteriously broke or disappeared, and its crew members went missing, leading to speculation of an Imperial saboteur or a curse.
