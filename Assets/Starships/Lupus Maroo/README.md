# Lupus Maroo

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lupus Maroo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lupus_Maroo)

The Lupus Maroo was a medium freighter used by the Black Sun organization. In 1 ABY the ship was used to transport salvage of the destroyed Death Star from the moon Yavin 4. Before it could jump into hyperspace, the freighter was intercepted by a spacer paid by Hutt enforcer Osae Meilea and forced to return to the moon. It landed in a remote spot of the moon, where Meilea and a team were already waiting to capture the ship and retrieve the cargo.
