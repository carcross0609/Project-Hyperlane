# Leonides

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Leonides](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leonides)

The Leonides was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer assigned to the Tapani sector. It was assigned to the planet Achillea. The ships assigned to the planets Achillea and Mrlsst, protected by the Reyna, were strategically located to control the only routes in and out sector.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Leonides
- **Model:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
