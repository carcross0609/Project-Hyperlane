# Tri-Mark VII Interceptor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tri-Mark VII Interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tri-Mark_VII_Interceptor)

The Tri-Mark VII Interceptor was a multi-purpose starship built by Elsinore-Cordova Engineering to serve as a heavy starfighter/bomber/light transport/prison ship. Equipped with three Elsinore-Cordova Turbodyne engines, the Tri-Mark VII was very fast, and its large cargo capacity allowed for the transport of up to twenty troops and four prisoners, in addition to a crew of three. General maintenance was provided by two astromech droids.
