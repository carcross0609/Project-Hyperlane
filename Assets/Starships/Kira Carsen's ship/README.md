# Kira Carsen's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kira Carsen's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kira_Carsen%27s_ship)

Some time before 3626 BBY, Kira Carsen stole a ship to carry Jedi Master Satele Shan and her students when they were infected by Tenebrae's curse. Though Carsen initially wanted to acquire a corvette, Scourge valued the space provided by the carrier's many decks.

- **Name:** Kira Carsen's ship
- **Type:** Carrier ship

- **Owners:** Kira Carsen and Scourge
