# Glazer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Glazer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glazer)

Glazer was a CR90 corvette that the Nharwaak used to ferry weapons technology to the Rebel Alliance Nebulon-B frigate Xerxes. The Glazer was escorted in this mission by two other corvettes, the Coffey and the Janson.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Glazer
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** CR90 corvette
