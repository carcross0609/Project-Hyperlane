# Reliance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reliance (Mandalorian Wars)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reliance_%28Mandalorian_Wars%29)

The Reliance was a Galactic Republic warship that served as the flagship for Admiral Jimas Veltraa during the Mandalorian Wars. During a Mandalorian advance that threatened the planet Taris in 3964 BBY, Veltraa reformed what remained of the shattered Jebble-Vanquo-Tarnith line into a makeshift force to defend Taris from a Mandalorian siege. Although Veltraa and the Reliance held out to the very end, the ship was eventually destroyed with all hands lost.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Reliance
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Destroyed:** 3964 BBY, Taris
- **Affiliation:**

- **Captains:** Admiral Jimas Veltraa
