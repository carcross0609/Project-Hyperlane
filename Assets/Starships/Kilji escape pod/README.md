# Kilji escape pod

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kilji escape pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kilji_escape_pod)

A type of escape pod was found aboard the Kilji Illumine government war cruiser Anvil. Small and made of metal, they were equipped with a viewport, thrusters, and an automatic beacon that broadcast its location after they were jettisoned. A passenger aboard a Kilji escape pod could survive for hours before running out of oxygen. Two pods were located aboard Illumine General Crofyp's Anvil, one to the bridge's portside, covered by an orange-rimmed door, and the other to starboard.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kilji escape pod
- **Type:** Escape pod
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hull:** Metal
- **Lifesupport:** Several hours
- **Communications:** An automatic beacon
- **Role:** Escape craft
