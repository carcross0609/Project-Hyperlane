# Grievous's Providence-class Dreadnought

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grievous's Providence-class Dreadnought (Campaign against the Shadow Collective)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grievous%27s_Providence-class_Dreadnought_%28Campaign_against_the_Shadow_Collective%29)

A Providence-class Dreadnought served as the command ship of General Grievous during the Confederacy of Independent Systems's campaign against the Shadow Collective. It was boarded by the Nightbrother during the Battle of Ord Mantell.
