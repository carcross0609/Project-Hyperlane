# TIE/rc

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/rc](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/rc)

The TIE/rc was a model of reconnaissance TIE series starfighter that was used by the Galactic Empire. It was a variant produced from the base TIE/ln space superiority starfighter by fitting the standard model's fuselage with specialized equipment. For the TIE/rc, this meant replacing the TIE/ln's laser cannons with a suite of advanced sensor equipment to serve specifically for reconnaissance and surveillance missions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TIE/rc
- **Line:** TIE series
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Role:**
  - Reconnaissance
  - Surveillance
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
