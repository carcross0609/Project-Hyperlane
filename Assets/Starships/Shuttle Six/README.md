# Shuttle Six

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shuttle Six](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shuttle_Six)

Shuttle Six was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle used by the Galactic Empire to transport Doctor Aphra and the low-riskers from the wreckage of Hivebase-1 to Accresker Jail.
