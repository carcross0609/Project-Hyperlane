# Quesh Orbital Station

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quesh Orbital Station (Imperial)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quesh_Orbital_Station_%28Imperial%29)

The Quesh Republic Orbital Station was a space station that orbited the planet Quesh in Hutt Space during the Cold War and the subsequent renewed conflict between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire.
