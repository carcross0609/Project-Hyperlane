# Millennium Fist

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Millennium Fist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Millennium_Fist)

The Millennium Fist was a freighter with two giant green gloves on its front. It was allegedly an old ship of Han Solo's that Lando Calrissian kept lying around on Cloud City. However, the Millennium Fist was in fact just a parody of the Millennium Falcon, invented by Han Solo while telling a story to his son Anakin.
