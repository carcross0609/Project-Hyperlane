# XR12

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [XR12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XR12)

The XR12 was a small, capsule-shaped spacecraft that was manufactured by Bendine during the time of the Galactic Republic. One such craft, the Satellite Liberator, had wound up in Watto's junk shop by the time of the Invasion of Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** XR12
- **Manufacturer:** Bendine
