# Unidentified shuttle (Oroko)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified shuttle (Oroko)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_shuttle_%28Oroko%29)

This shuttle was held in the hangar of the transport Oroko in 3964 BBY&mdash;both belonged to the Twi'lek pirate Valius Ying's gang. That year, it brought Ying and his captive, fugitive Zayne Carrick, from the Oroko to Taris.
