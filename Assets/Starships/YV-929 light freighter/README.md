# YV-929 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YV-929 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YV-929_light_freighter)

The YV-929 light freighter was a model of heavily armed and armored transport that was manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. A light freighter in name only, the YV-929 served as a blockade runner and gunboat in the Rebel Alliance, as well as a military transport for private companies and individuals.
