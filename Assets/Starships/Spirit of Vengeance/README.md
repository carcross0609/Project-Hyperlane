# Spirit of Vengeance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spirit of Vengeance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_of_Vengeance)

The Spirit of Vengeance was a large, Mandalorian dreadnaught under the command of Mandalore the Vindicated.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spirit of Vengeance
- **Type:** Ajuur-class heavy cruiser
- **Armament:** Numerous weapon emplacements
- **Consumables:** Ne'tra gal
- **Role:** Command ship
- **Affiliation:** Mandalorians
- **Owners:** Mandalore the Vindicated
