# D-Squad's Maxillipede shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D-Squad's Maxillipede shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-Squad%27s_Maxillipede_shuttle)

A Maxillipede shuttle was used by D-Squad to carry out a mission to steal an encryption module from a Providence-class Dreadnought commanded by the super tactical droid Aut-O. After the module had been stolen and the squad had departed, the Maxillipede shuttle was damaged in a comet storm above Abafar, and crashed on the planet. D-Squad abandoned the destroyed ship, and headed in search of a new starship.
