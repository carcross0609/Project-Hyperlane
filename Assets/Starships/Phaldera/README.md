# Phaldera

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Phaldera](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phaldera)

Phaldera was a group of three modular conveyors that were part of the Imperial Task Force Vengeance during the Galactic Civil War. Two of the freighters were carrying modulators, while the third one was carrying warheads. The one with the warheads was destroyed by Rogue Squadron during their recon mission of the Imperial fleet.

- **Name:** Phaldera
- **Model:** Modular conveyor
