# Panda (transports)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Panda (transports)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panda_%28transports%29)

Panda group was a pair of Lambda-class T-4a shuttles used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. When the Alliance succeeded in stranding the Imperial EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Priam near Kalla VII, General Jan Dodonna gave the order for a "board and capture" operation against the Priam.
