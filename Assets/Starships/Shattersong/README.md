# Shattersong

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shattersong](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shattersong)

The Shattersong was a Longbeam cruiser of the Republic Defense Coalition that served as the flagship of Jedi Master Elzar Mann's attack fleet that was assembled to breach the stormwall.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shattersong
- **Type:** Longbeam cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Navy:** Republic Defense Coalition
- **Fleet:** Occlusion Zone attack fleet
- **Captains:** Elzar Mann
