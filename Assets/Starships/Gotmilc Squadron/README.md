# Gotmilc Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gotmilc Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gotmilc_Squadron)

Gotmilc Squadron was a small Squadron of 3 T-wings employed by Nami Crime Lord Ali Tarrak. They were the first set of fighters trying to stop Modular conveyor Ulzepdi from escaping the destruction of an Imperial Cargo area. They just launched rockets at the conveyor when Tan Maarek Stele intercepted the rockets and destroyed the T-wings.
