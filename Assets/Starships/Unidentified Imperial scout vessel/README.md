# Unidentified Imperial scout vessel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial scout vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial_scout_vessel)

During the Galactic Civil War, an Imperial scout vessel served in the Imperial Navy's Fakir sector fleet. The ship was used to ambush General Lens Reekeene's X-wing while she was on route to a rendezvous with Alliance sector high command. The scout ship's initial attack heavily damaged the X-wing's engines and eliminated Reekeene's R2 unit.
