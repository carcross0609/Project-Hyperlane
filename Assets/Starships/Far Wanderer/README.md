# Far Wanderer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Far Wanderer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Far_Wanderer)

This Z-95 Headhunter, named Far Wanderer, was utilized by the Jedi Knight Jaden Korr throughout his training and involvement in the Disciples of Ragnos campaign. Modified to include a hyperdrive for solo journeys across the galaxy, Korr piloted the starfighter from planet to planet when not traveling with his Master, Kyle Katarn, aboard the Raven's Claw, or when utilizing a shuttle. Korr first used it to travel to Bakura to investigate the mysterious takeover of the Bakura Power Station with Katarn. However, Korr eventually got fed up with waiting next to Far Wanderer and decided to investigate on his own. After saving the power station from being destroyed, Korr returned to his ship to report back to the Jedi Academy.
