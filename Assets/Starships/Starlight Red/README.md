# Starlight Red

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starlight Red](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starlight_Red)

The Starlight Red, formerly known as Faceted, was a YT-1300 light freighter owned by the free-trader and smuggler known as Celia Durasha and piloted by her and U-THR. Before Durasha acquired the ship, it belonged to the bounty hunter Thune.
