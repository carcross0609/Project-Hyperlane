# Patriot Fist

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Patriot Fist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Patriot_Fist)

The Patriot Fist was a Recusant-class light destroyer that served within the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. In 19 BBY, the destroyer participated in an unprecedented assault on the Galactic Republic's capital world of Coruscant, alongside a large Separatist fleet commanded by General Grievous in an attempt to capture Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine.
