# Whirlwind

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Whirlwind (Chiss)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whirlwind_%28Chiss%29)

The Whirlwind was a Chiss starship part of the Chiss Ascendancy's Expansionary Defense Fleet. It was equipped with engines and could be brought to flight status in half an hour. Around 19 BBY, Expansionary Defense Fleet Supreme General Ba'kif traveled aboard the Whirlwind to the Ascendancy's planet Naporar to retrieve Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo, who was undergoing physical therapy in Naporar's Chiss Expansionary Fleet medcenter. After four hours, the starship arrived at its destination, sending a Chiss shuttle to retrieve Thrawn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Whirlwind

- **Complement:** At least one Chiss shuttle
