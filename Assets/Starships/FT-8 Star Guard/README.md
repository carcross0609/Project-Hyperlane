# FT-8 Star Guard

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FT-8 Star Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FT-8_Star_Guard)

The FT-8 Star Guard was a starfighter in the FT-series of Galactic Republic starfighters produced by Rendili StarDrive. Similar to other models of the series, the Star Guard had two wings and two rear engines, and it featured a laser cannon on each wing. The Star Guard was a redesign of the older FT-5A Honor Guard model.
