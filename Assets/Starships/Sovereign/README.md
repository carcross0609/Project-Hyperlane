# Sovereign

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sovereign](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sovereign)

The Sovereign was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer belonging to the starfleet of the Galactic Empire, and served as Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin's flagship for a period of time before the Battle of Yavin.
