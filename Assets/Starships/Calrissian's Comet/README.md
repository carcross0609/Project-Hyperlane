# Calrissian's Comet

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Calrissian's Comet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Calrissian%27s_Comet)

The Calrissian's Comet was a YT-2400 freighter that Faul Odantil sold to Landonis Balthazar Calrissian. After discovering that its systems were not functioning properly during a race with Han Solo, Calrissian returned the ship to the seller.
