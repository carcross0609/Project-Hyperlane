# Rampart (Assassin-class)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rampart (Assassin-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rampart_%28Assassin-class%29)

Rampart was a pair of Assassin-class corvettes that belonged to the Rebel Alliance. They attempted to reinforce the Rebel platfrom RS-32 an XQ2 Platform space installation used by the Rebel Alliance, which housed the electromagnetic pulse warhead technology that was under attack by Imperial forces under the command of Admiral Thrawn. At least one corvette was destroyed in the battle by Tan Maarek Stele.
