# Red 10

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red 10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_10)

Red 10 was a T-65 X-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was assigned to Red Squadron and stationed at the Rebel base on Yavin 4. Red 10 was piloted by Theron Nett with R2-X2 as the craft's astromech droid.

- **Line:** T-65 X-wing starfighter
- **Destroyed:** 0 BBY, Yavin system

- **Captains:** Theron Nett
