# Vector

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vector)

The Vector was an Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser in the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era that notably featured red markings on its hull. At one point, it flew with another red-marked Gozanti cruiser in space, where the Vector launched two of its four TIE/ln space superiority starfighters, which were carried on the ship's underside by docking tubes.

- **Name:** Vector
- **Model:** Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser

- **Complement:** TIE/ln space superiority starfighters (4)
- **Othersystems:** Docking tubes
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
