# NovaFlare

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [NovaFlare](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NovaFlare)

The NovaFlare was a Corellian corvette in service to the Rebel Alliance Fleet during the Galactic Civil War. Tresk Ortola, after spending several years as first officer of a rebel gunship, was promoted to Captain and given command of the NovaFlare prior to the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY.
