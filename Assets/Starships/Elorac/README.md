# Elorac

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Elorac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elorac)

The Elorac was a BFF-1 bulk freighter that was used by a civilian company. The ship was inspected by a TIE Interceptor to find Amber as its manifest had claimed and therefore was free to leave.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Elorac
- **Model:** BFF-1 bulk freighter
- **Role:** Cargo transport
