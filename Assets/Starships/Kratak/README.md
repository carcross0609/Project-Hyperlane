# Kratak

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kratak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kratak)

The Kratak was a cruiser analog used by the Yuuzhan Vong during the Battle of Coruscant. Warmaster Tsavong Lah transferred his flag to the Kratak, after the destruction of the Sunulok.
