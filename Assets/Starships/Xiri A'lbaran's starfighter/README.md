# Xiri A'lbaran's starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Xiri A'lbaran's starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xiri_A%27lbaran%27s_starfighter)

A starfighter was used by Xiri A'lbaran, the princess of the planet E'ronoh and captain of the planet's Thylefire Squadron. In 382 BBY, A'lbaran crashed the ship onto the planet Eiram, which her world was in a war with.

- **Name:** Xiri A'lbaran's starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Thylefire Squadron
- **Navy:** E'ronoh's fleet
- **Owners:** Xiri A'lbaran
