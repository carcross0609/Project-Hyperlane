# Tomra

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tomra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tomra)

The Tomra was a Chiss Defense Force Transport of the Defense Fleet of the Chiss Ascendancy, commanded by Junior Captain Vorlip. Ali'astov served as an ozyly-esehembo aboard the vessel. Mitth'raw'nuru, core name "Thrawn", was aboard this vessel when he traveled to the Taharim academy on the planet Naporar. He encountered Al'iastov here, changing her life for the better with his words of encouragement.
