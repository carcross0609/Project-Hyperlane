# SW-0222

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SW-0222](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SW-0222)

SW-0222 was an Eta-class supply barge of the Galactic Empire. The supply ship was utilized by Administrator Sly Moore's Schism Imperial, a covert faction intending to overthrow the Emperor, as part of its plan to assault Exegol and the Sith Eternal. Captain Corleque took SW-0222 to the edge of the Red Honeycomb Zone, supposedly delivering a shipment of supplies and kyberite to the Sith Eternal. However, some of the crates contained droids as well as the Sith Lord Darth Vader. The Sith Eternal cultists docked their vessels with SW-0222, collecting the cargo unaware of what it truly contained and returning it to Exegol.
