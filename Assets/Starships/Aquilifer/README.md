# Aquilifer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aquilifer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aquilifer)

The Aquilifer was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was built at the Kuat Drive Yards for service in the Imperial Navy and later served as the flagship of Admiral Tyrus Meori. The Star Destroyer was heavily damaged during the Mutiny on Mon Cala.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aquilifer

- **Type:** Star Destroyer

- **Captains:** Tyrus Meori
