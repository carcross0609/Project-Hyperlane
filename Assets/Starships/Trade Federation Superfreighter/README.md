# Trade Federation Superfreighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trade Federation Superfreighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trade_Federation_Superfreighter)

The Trade Federation Superfreighter, or TF Superfreighter, was an armed freighter used by the Trade Federation around the time of the Invasion of Naboo. This model was an advanced version of the Trade Federation Freighter also in use at the time. The class would continue to be used during the lead-in to the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Trade Federation Superfreighter
- **Model:** Super Freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Two double Laser cannons
- **Crew:** Droids
- **Role:** Freighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  -   Trade Federation Droid Army
  - Sabaoth Squadron
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
