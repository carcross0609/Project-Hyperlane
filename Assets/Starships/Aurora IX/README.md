# Aurora IX

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aurora IX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora_IX)

The Aurora IX was a Galactic Republic Longbeam cruiser, a brand-new design from the Republic shipyards on Hosnian Prime during the High Republic Era. During a combined Republic and Jedi Order effort to save the Hetzal system from hyperspace debris, it was commanded by Captain Finial Bright and crewed by Petty Officer Innamin and Ensign Peeples.
