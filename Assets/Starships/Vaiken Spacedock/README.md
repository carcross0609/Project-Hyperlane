# Vaiken Spacedock

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vaiken Spacedock](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vaiken_Spacedock)

Vaiken Spacedock was a massive space station built in orbit of a planet in the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. It was named after Grand Moff Odile Vaiken, the father of the Imperial Navy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vaiken Spacedock
- **Type:** Space station
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories

- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
