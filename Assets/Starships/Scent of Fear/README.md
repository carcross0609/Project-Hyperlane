# Scent of Fear

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scent of Fear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scent_of_Fear)

Scent of Fear was a starship used by Crutag and his crew. During the Galactic Civil War, the Scent of Fear chased a Rebel ship to the swamp planet Trinta, where both ships landed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scent of Fear
- **Model:** Taloron Prey Chaser
- **Length:** 40 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Yes
- **Crew:** 1
- **Passengers:** 25
- **Cargohandling:** 30 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 4 months
- **Owners:** Crutag
