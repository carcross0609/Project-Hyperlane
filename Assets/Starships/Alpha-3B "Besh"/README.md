# Alpha-3B "Besh"

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Alpha-3B "Besh"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alpha-3B_%22Besh%22)

The Alpha-3B "Besh" was a sub-model of the Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter, which served the Republic Starfighter Corps during the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The model added projectile weapons to allow the fighter to serve as a bomber. At least one Alpha-3B "Besh" starfighter saw combat during the Mygeeto Campaign, strafing ground targets with its ordnance.
