# Harbinger courier ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Harbinger courier ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harbinger_courier_ship)

The Harbinger courier ship was a model of courier starship used by the Galactic Empire. The Venator-class Star Destroyer Strikefast carried at least one of such craft when it made contact with Mitth'raw'nuruodo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Harbinger courier ship
- **Role:** Courier
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
