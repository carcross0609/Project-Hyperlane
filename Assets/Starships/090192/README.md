# 090192

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [090192](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/090192)

090192 was a spy ship used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic. During the war, the T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid TX-532 utilized the starship for a secret operation on Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 090192
- **Type:** Spy ship
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Navy:** Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy
