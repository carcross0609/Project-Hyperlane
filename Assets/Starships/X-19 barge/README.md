# X-19 barge

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X-19 barge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-19_barge)

The X-19 barge was produced by Incom Corporation. Two X-19 barges owned by Canistel AgriStar were lost in the Moddell sector, and it was believed that the seeds they carried resulted in the spread of the senoti tress on Endor.
