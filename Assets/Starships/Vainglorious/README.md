# Vainglorious

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vainglorious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vainglorious)

Vainglorious was an Imperial warship that was present at the Battle of ThonBoka. Its captain questioned Rokur Gepta's tactics before the main battle. The Sorcerer of Tund subsequently ordered that both the captain and his executive officer should be thrown out of an airlock for being individualists.
