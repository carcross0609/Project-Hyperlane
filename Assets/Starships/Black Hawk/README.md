# Black Hawk

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Black Hawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Hawk)

Black Hawk was an Imperial Lancer-class frigate that, along with its sister ship Furious escorted a convoy through the Eidoloni system which, belonging to Xizor Transport Systems, transported a wide range of heavy materials used for the construction of the Death Star II.
