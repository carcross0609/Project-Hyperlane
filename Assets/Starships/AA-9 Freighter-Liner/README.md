# AA-9 Freighter-Liner

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AA-9 Freighter-Liner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AA-9_Freighter-Liner)

The AA-9 Freighter-Liner, also known as the Botajef Freighter-Liner or AA-9 Coruscant Starfreighter, was a starship produced by Botajef Shipyards which first brought the small shipbuilding concern to galactic prominence. Initially conceived as a freighter, it went on to be used as a refugee transport and, during the Clone Wars, served as a troopship for the Grand Army of the Republic.
