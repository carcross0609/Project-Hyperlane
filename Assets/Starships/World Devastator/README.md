# World Devastator

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [World Devastator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/World_Devastator)

World Devastators, also known as World Smashers, World Sweepers, Planet Smashers, or City Eaters, were gigantic superweapons with strong tractor beams attached to them that could wreak havoc on planets. They were designed by Umak Leth and employed by the Dark Empire led by the reborn Emperor Palpatine.
