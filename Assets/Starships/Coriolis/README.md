# Coriolis

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coriolis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coriolis)

Coriolis was a Duros-crewed starship of Galactic Alliance during the latter stages of the Yuuzhan Vong War. It was operated by Commander Yurf Col.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Coriolis
- **Destroyed:** 28 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
- **Captains:** Commander Yurf Col
