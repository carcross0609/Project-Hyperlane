# Jedi spacecraft

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jedi spacecraft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_spacecraft)

The Jedi spacecraft was a vessel Aayla Secura piloted on a stealth approach to Kintan, with Quinlan Vos as her gunner. A shielded vessel, its weapons included two wing cannons and a swiveling belly cannon. Despite the stealth attempt, the vessel was detected by the Morgukai and attacked by a CloakShape fighter.
