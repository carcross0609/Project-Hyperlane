# Guardian-class patrol ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Guardian-class patrol ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian-class_patrol_ship)

Guardian-class patrol ships, also known as XL-5 Guardians, were among the few combat airspeeders used by the Galactic Empire. Though they were in high demand throughout the Imperial Military, most of them were used by Imperial Intelligence.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Guardian-class patrol ship
- **Manufacturer:** Tion Mil/Sci Industries
- **Model:** Guardian-class patrol ship XL-5
- **Type:** Orbital and upper atmospheric patrol vessel
- **Cost:** 80,000 credits
- **Length:** 10 meters
- **Speed:** 800 km/h
- **Max Alt:** 100 kilometers
- **Shield Gen:** Yes
- **Armament:**
  - Blaster cannons (2; fire-link optional)
  - Spatial anomaly detectors
  - Lifeform scanners
  - Probescopes
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Co-pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** 12 (prisoners)
- **Capacity:** 150 tons
- **Role:**
  - Counter-insurgent observation craft
  - Coordinating station for Hunter-Killer probots
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Army
  -   Imperial Intelligence
  - Dark Empire
