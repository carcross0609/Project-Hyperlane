# Adarian ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adarian ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adarian_ship)

Adarian ships were freighters manufactured by small companies owned by the Adarian government. The Adarians preferred to use technology of their own design, including their own hyperdrives and Carbon-Ice Drives. Their ships competed with those of Kuat Drive Yards and Corellian Engineering Corporation and were considered by outsiders to be of exceptional quality.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Adarian ship
- **Designer:** Adarians
- **Type:** Freighter
