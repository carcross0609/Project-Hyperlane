# Dhlovo Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dhlovo Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dhlovo_Squadron)

Dhlovo Squadron was a small squadron of 2 R-41 Starchasers used by Rneekii pirates. They were deployed to provide cover for transport group Ibutho so that they could capture Imperial Modified corvette group Hashim. They were unsuccessful and both fighters were destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele.
