# Strident

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Strident](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strident)

The Strident was a Nebulon-B2 frigate under the command of Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin during his attempted coup d'état. It was disabled and destroyed by the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Courageous. Maarek Stele was involved in the operation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Strident
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Nebulon-B2 frigate
- **Length:** 253.2 meters
- **Width:** 147.7 meters
- **Height:** 94.3 meters
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
