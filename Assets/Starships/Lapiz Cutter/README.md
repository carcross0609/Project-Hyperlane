# Lapiz Cutter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lapiz Cutter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lapiz_Cutter)

The Lapiz Cutter was the personal Sheathipede-class transport shuttle of Trade Federation Viceroy Nute Gunray. It was used by Gunray during the Federation's Invasion of Naboo to transport him and Rune Haako to Theed. A decade later, the viceroy escaped from the planet Geonosis aboard the Lapiz Cutter during the First Battle of Geonosis.
