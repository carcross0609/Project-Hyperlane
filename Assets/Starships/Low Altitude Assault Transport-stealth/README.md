# Low Altitude Assault Transport/stealth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Low Altitude Assault Transport/stealth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Low_Altitude_Assault_Transport/stealth)

The Low Altitude Assault Transport/stealth, or LAAT/s, was a Low Altitude Assault Transport variant that was used by the Galactic Republic to deploy inflitrators on Separatist-aligned worlds during the Clone Wars.
