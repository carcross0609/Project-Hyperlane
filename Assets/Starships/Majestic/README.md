# Majestic

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Majestic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Majestic)

The Majestic was a starship that served the Galactic Empire during the government's reign. The vessel was commanded by Captain Canonhaus, who strived to learn about the rest of the crew from their files, as taught by Captain Tabor Seitaron at Carida Academy. The other officers learned his signalling mannerisms, which he called his four watches, and he assured that all operations on the Majestic were up to speed at all times.
