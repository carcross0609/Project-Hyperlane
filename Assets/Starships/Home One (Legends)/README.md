# Home One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Home One/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Home_One/Legends)

Home One, also known as the Headquarters Frigate, was an MC80 Star Cruiser in the Alliance to Restore the Republic's fleet, famous for its role at the Battle of Endor and as one of Admiral Gial Ackbar's flagships. It was the namesake of the Home One type subclass and was noted as being the largest and most advanced of the Rebel Star Cruisers.
