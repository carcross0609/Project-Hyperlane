# Sith Meditation Sphere

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith Meditation Sphere](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Meditation_Sphere)

The Sith Meditation Sphere was an ancient warship used by the Sith Empire for battle meditation. A meditation sphere that survived the Great Hyperspace War was used by both Lumiya's Sith and the Lost Tribe of Sith.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sith Meditation Sphere
- **Manufacturer:** Sith
- **Model:** Sith-Engineered Meditation Sphere and Command Center
- **Length:** 10 meters in diameter
- **Armament:**
  - Force transmitters
  - Laser cannons
  - Magnetic accelerator
- **Crew:** 1
- **Role:** Command ship
- **Firstuse:** 5000 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sith Empire
  - Lumiya's Sith
  - Lost Tribe of Sith
