# Kylo Ren's command shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kylo Ren's command shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kylo_Ren%27s_command_shuttle)

Kylo Ren's command shuttle was an Upsilon-class command shuttle utilized by Kylo Ren, who was a leader of the Knights of Ren and a warlord of the First Order around thirty years after the Battle of Endor. His ship led a group of Atmospheric Assault Landers to the Resurgent-class Star Destroyer Finalizer.
