# Exchequer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Exchequer/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exchequer/Legends)

The Exchequer was a flagship in service to the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. Commanded by Moff Boyse, the Exchequer was part of the blockade of the planet Scipio.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Exchequer
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
