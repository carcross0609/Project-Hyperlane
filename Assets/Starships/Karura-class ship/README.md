# Karura-class ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Karura-class ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karura-class_ship)

Karura-class ships were a model of slim and fast starship used by enforcers of the Osako pirates. Several such ships pursued a cell of the Spice Runners of Kijimi aboard the Vondel, piloted by Poe Dameron, after Dameron's father Kes made a deal with the pirates to find his son.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Karura-class ship
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Osako pirates
