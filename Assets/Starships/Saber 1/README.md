# Saber 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saber 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saber_1)

Saber 1, also designated as Saber Leader, was an Imperial Navy TIE/IN interceptor manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems. Saber 1 had the serial number 000004, and also bore a bloodstripe, representing a minimum of ten kills. By 4 ABY, Saber 1 was assigned to the Imperial 181st Imperial Fighter Wing's Saber Squadron. The interceptor was piloted by Baron Soontir Fel, the leader of the 181st Imperial Fighter Wing.
