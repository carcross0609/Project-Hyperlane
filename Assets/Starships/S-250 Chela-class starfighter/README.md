# S-250 Chela-class starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S-250 Chela-class starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-250_Chela-class_starfighter)

The S-250 Chela-class starfighter was a craft used by the Republic fleet at many battles, including the Battle of Rakata Prime in 3956 BBY. Though a loser in the "Generation Tomorrow Starfighter" competition, it was still purchased in small numbers by the Republic.
