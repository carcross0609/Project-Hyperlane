# Moonseeker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moonseeker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moonseeker)

The Moonseeker was a Republic Defense Coalition patrol vessel active during the High Republic Era. In 231 BBY, the Moonseeker and its pilot were sent in search of a distress beacon picked up by the Galactic Republic space station Starlight Beacon. Tracing the signal to the Darkknell system, the ship found an escape pod from the Republic correctional vessel Restitution. Inside, the pilot found Sestin Blin, a former inmate on the ship.
