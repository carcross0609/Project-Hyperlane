# Red Eclipse

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red Eclipse](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Eclipse)

The Red Eclipse was a starship and enslaver organization that was operated by a crew of Trandoshan and Weequay enslavers affiliated with the Exchange that was based out of Nar Shaddaa. When Meetra Surik traveled to that world, landing within the Refugee Sector, the Ebon Hawk was put down on a pad that was supposedly reserved for the Red Eclipse, despite protests from the pad master, Quello. Upon discovering this the ship's captain, Cahhmakt, and his crew attempted to hijack the Hawk, but were killed by Surik and her companions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Red Eclipse
- **Armament:** Quad laser cannons
- **Crew:**
  - Trandoshans
  - Weequays
- **Role:** Slaving
- **Captains:** Cahhmakt
