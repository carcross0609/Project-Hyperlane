# Heart of Artorias

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heart of Artorias](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heart_of_Artorias)

The Heart of Artorias, originally Tsam P'ah, was a Yuuzhan Vong slaveship used to house captured sentients from Artorias and other planets. Nina and Kaye Galfridian, as well as Jenny and Arbeloa, were held on board.
