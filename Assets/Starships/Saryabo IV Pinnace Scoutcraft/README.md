# Saryabo IV Pinnace Scoutcraft

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Saryabo IV Pinnace Scoutcraft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saryabo_IV_Pinnace_Scoutcraft)

The Saryabo IV Pinnace Scoutcraft was developed near the end of the Clone Wars as a way to transport elite troops to and from key target areas. At the time of the Galactic Civil War only a dozen of these ships were still in existence. To achieve its main objective, the ship was fitted with a top-notch hyperdrive system and nav computer. It was also given minor offensive and defensive components to ensure that the troops reached their destination alive.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Saryabo IV Pinnace Scoutcraft
- **Model:** Saryabo IV Pinnace Scoutcraft
- **Type:** Scout transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1
- **Armament:** 1 laser cannon
- **Crew:** 2 people
- **Passengers:** 10 people
- **Capacity:** 1,000 kilograms
- **Consumables:** 1 month
