# TIE crawler

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE crawler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE_crawler)

The TIE crawler, or century tank, was a model of tank that resembled a TIE starfighter cockpit suspended between two tank treads. It was used to climb a tall hill of debris. The treads could rotate leaving the cockpit on the rear end of the craft. This allowed for crawling over different terrains.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TIE crawler
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Line:** TIE fighter series
- **Model:** TIE crawler
