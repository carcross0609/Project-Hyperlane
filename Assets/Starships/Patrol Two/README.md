# Patrol Two

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Patrol Two](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Patrol_Two)

Patrol Two was a Chiss patrol ship stationed at Taharim Academy on Naporar. Mitth'raw'nuruodo proved that he had not cheated on a flight simulator by flying against Patrol Two and three other ships.

- **Name:** Patrol Two
- **Type:** Patrol ship
