# Space dinghy

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Space dinghy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Space_dinghy)

A space dinghy, commonly called a dinghy, was a stubby, little, plastic-roofed spacecraft used for ferry transit. Its operators, known "ferrymen" or dinghy drivers, piloted the vessel from a side-mounted dome and docked with incoming ships using a dedicated suction tub.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Space dinghy
- **Type:** Starship
- **Hull:** Plastic canopy
- **Crew:** 1
- **Role:** Transport passengers
