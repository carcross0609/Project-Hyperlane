# Pride of Tarlandia

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pride of Tarlandia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pride_of_Tarlandia)

The Pride of Tarlandia was an important battlecruiser in the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. It shared its name with the Core Worlds Tarlandia system. During the Galactic Civil War, the Pride of Tarlandia was part of the Imperial fleet defending the second Death Star, an Imperial battle station located over the Forest Moon of Endor, where the ship was responsible for coordinating Imperial communications.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pride of Tarlandia
- **Type:** Battlecruiser
- **Communications:** Equipped
- **Role:** Communications ship
- **Destroyed:** 4 ABY, over the Forest Moon of Endor
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
