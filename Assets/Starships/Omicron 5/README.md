# Omicron 5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Omicron 5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omicron_5)

Omicron 5 was an Imperial Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle which escorted BFF-1 bulk freighters Degathem and Anterab to deliver research supplies to Grand Admiral Zaarin's XQ6 Platform Research during the Galactic Civil War. Omicron 5 fought with Rebel starfighters attacking the freighters during the Arms Race.

- **Model:** Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle
