# Mindabaal Custom

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mindabaal Custom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mindabaal_Custom)

The Mindabaal Custom was a type of personal yacht. Designed to meet the needs of individual customers, Mindabaal Customs had a reputation for flexibility. In their stock configuration, they were not equipped with weapons; however, some customers would order armament installed, such as James Ortell Donovan did with the Royal Flower.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mindabaal Custom
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Cost:**
  - 400,000 credits (new)
  - 250,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 30.1 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1050 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1.0 (stock)
- **Armament:** None (stock)
- **Crew:** 2
- **Passengers:** 8
- **Capacity:** 50 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 5 weeks
