# Retribution

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Retribution](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Retribution)

The Retribution was a First Order Resurgent-class Star Destroyer that was present during the Cold War and the war between the First Order and the Resistance. In 34 ABY, it was under the ownership of Gideon Hask during the Battle of Kestro City, it used to destroy the Corvus while it was above Vardos. Later on during the conflict, the ship jumped to hyperspace with its destination being crucial to the First Order's plot to claim control over the galaxy. At the same time, it was infiltrated by Iden Versio, Zay Versio, Dio, and Shriv Suurgav in an attempt to find out more about Project Resurrection. During the skirmish, it was damaged and pulled out of hyperspace when Iden and Zay destroyed the hyperdrive core.
