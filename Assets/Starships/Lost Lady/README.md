# Lost Lady

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lost Lady](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Lady)

The Lost Lady was an old dilapidated Dartiss-5 Caravel used to transport illicit cargo and passengers between Nal Hutta and the Smugglers' Moon, Nar Shaddaa. It operated during the early years of the New Order.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lost Lady
- **Manufacturer:** Ubrikkian Industries
- **Model:** Modified Dartiss-5 Caravel
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 50 meters
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 4 turbolaser cannon turrets
  - 4 concussion missile launchers
  -   Standard load: 12 missiles each
- **Crew:** 5
- **Passengers:** 65
- **Capacity:** 150 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 week
- **Role:**
  - In-system ferry
  - Smuggling vessel
- **Affiliation:** Vir Nurb
