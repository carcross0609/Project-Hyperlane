# Corsalys

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corsalys](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corsalys)

The Corsalys was the freighter originally piloted by Jessica Meade circa 15 BBY. When en route to Tammuz-an carrying Mon Julpa and Jann Tosh along with his droids R2-D2 and C-3PO, it came under attack by the pirate Gir Kybo Ren-Cha and was destroyed. The crew was able to escape unharmed as the vessel's cockpit was able to separate from the main part of the craft, doubling as an escape pod.
