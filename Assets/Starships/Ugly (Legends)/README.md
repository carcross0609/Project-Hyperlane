# Ugly

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ugly/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ugly/Legends)

]] An Ugly, also known as a juicecan or buzzzer, was any type of starfighter that had been cobbled together out of parts that had been salvaged from varying origins, including crashed starfighters and ex-military surplus. The term itself seemed to be of Corellian origin.
