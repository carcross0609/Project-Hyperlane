# Cargo Comet

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cargo Comet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cargo_Comet)

The Cargo Comet was a Wayfarer-class medium freighter that was part of Senator Bail Organa's secret resistance movement. During the blockade of Ibaar, Organa ordered the Cargo Comet and the Cargo Sky to be loaded with supplies and equipment and to deliver it past the blockade to the rebel network operating there.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cargo Comet
- **Model:** Wayfarer-class medium freighter
- **Type:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** House of Organa
