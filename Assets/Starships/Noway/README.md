# Noway

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Noway](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noway)

Noway was a group of 3 Lambda-class T-4a shuttles used by the Pakuuni Pirates during the Galactic Civil War. They were used as part of an assassination attempt on then-Vice Admiral Thrawn while he was inspecting NL-1 station, but they arrived too late and instead attempted to fight the TIE bombers, but were all destroyed by Imperial Ace Maarek Stele.
