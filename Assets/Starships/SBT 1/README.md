# SBT 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SBT 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SBT_1)

SBT 1 was a Heavy Lifter used by forces loyal to rogue Grand Admiral Zaarin. The Lifter was present at a Supply Base located in the Gwar system. Imperial forces lead by Captain Kuuztin launched an attack on the base and destroyed the base, but fell into a trap. It is likely that the Lifter was destroyed in the battle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SBT 1
- **Model:** Heavy Lifter
- **Role:** Utility craft
- **Destroyed:** 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
