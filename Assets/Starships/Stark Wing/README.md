# Stark Wing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stark Wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stark_Wing)

The Stark Wing was a Thaereian customs cruiser that was used by the Thaereian military during the time of the Clone Wars. It had a crew of sixty, and was commanded by Captain Jans Tolen. In c. 21 BBY, the ship was sent into the Cularin asteroid belt and encountered a number of shadow lurkers&mdash;the incorporeal spirits of members of the Oblee species&mdash;who attempted to contact the Thaereians by possessing a number of the Stark Wings crew. However, this caused the Thaereians to believe that they were being invaded, so they began attacking each other, resulting in the deaths of everyone aboard the ship. The derelict craft was later discovered by the Heroes of Cularin.
