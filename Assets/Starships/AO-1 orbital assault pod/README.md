# AO-1 orbital assault pod

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AO-1 orbital assault pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AO-1_orbital_assault_pod)

The AO-1 orbital assault pod was an orbital assault pod designed by Cygnus Spaceworks for the use of the Grand Army of the Republic. The assault pod was deemed to be too dangerous by Republic Command, and Cygnus lost its contract. The company later salvaged the programed, resulting with the AO-2 Cometstrike-class orbital assault pod.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AO-1 orbital assault pod
- **Manufacturer:** Cygnus Spaceworks
- **Type:** Orbital assault pod
- **Role:** Troop transport
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Grand Army of the Republic
