# MC80a Star Cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MC80a Star Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC80a_Star_Cruiser)

The MC80a Star Cruiser, also styled as MC80A Star Cruiser, was a combat-focused variant of the MC80 Mon Calamari Star Cruiser fielded by the Alliance to Restore the Republic, and subsequently the New Republic, during the Galactic Civil War and Yuuzhan Vong War.
