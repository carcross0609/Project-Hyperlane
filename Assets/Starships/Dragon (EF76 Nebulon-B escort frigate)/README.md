# Dragon (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dragon (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

Dragon was an Imperial Nebulon-B Frigate that attempted to stop Cathleen on her way to Hoth. Dragon was destroyed on that attempt by combined efforts of Cathleen fleets and Red Squadron's B-wings.
