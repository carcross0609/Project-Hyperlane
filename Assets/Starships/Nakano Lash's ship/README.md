# Nakano Lash's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nakano Lash's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nakano_Lash%27s_ship)

Nakano Lash's ship was a 720 light freighter owned by the Nautolan bounty hunter Nakano Lash during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 3 ABY, Lash used the ship to hide within wreckage of a battle from the Clone Wars in the Ruusan system as she waited for her old protégé, the bounty hunter Beilert Valance, to arrive and take on the task of protecting the young girl Cadeliah until she was old enough to claim her birthright as heir to the warring Unbroken Clan and Mourner's Wail criminal syndicates. The ship, along with Lash, was later destroyed when it was fired on by a ship of the Unbroken Clan.
