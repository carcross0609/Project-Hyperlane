# Dantooine Express StarSpeeder 1000

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dantooine Express StarSpeeder 1000](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dantooine_Express_StarSpeeder_1000)

The Dantooine Express StarSpeeder 1000 was a modified StarSpeeder 1000 spacecraft used by interstellar travel agency Star Tours to operate flights bound for the Outer Rim planet Dantooine prior to 0 BBY. This starship was painted red and white, with a large Aurebesh letter Dorn and smaller aurebesh letters Aurek, Besh, and Grek. It was named after both the planet as well as Dantooine Express, a separate spaceliner company from Star Tours that specialized in more ecological-based tours and was a fellow member of the Galactic Tourist Bureau.
