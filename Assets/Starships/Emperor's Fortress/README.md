# Emperor's Fortress

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Emperor's Fortress](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Fortress)

The Emperor's Fortress, also known as the Emperor's space station, was an ancient space station built for the Emperor of the reconstituted Sith Empire. A fusion of modern and ancient technology, the station served as both temple and fortress for the supreme ruler of the Empire. The station possessed a cloaking device of Gree design, and the station's security grid and power core utilized technology built by the ancient Rakata.
