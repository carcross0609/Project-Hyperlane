# Reclaimer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reclaimer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reclaimer)

The Reclaimer was a customized salvage ship of Belinda Vog. It carried two crew, four passengers, 75 metric tons of cargo, and 1 month's consumables. It had a class 1 hyperdrive and a class 15 backup. It could fly 750 kmh in atmosphere and had a nav computer. It was equipped with shields as well as a heavy laser cannon.
