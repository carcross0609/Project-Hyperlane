# Forger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Forger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forger)

Forger was a Star Destroyer of the Imperial Navy. In the later stages of the Galactic Civil War, she served under the command of Grand Moff Dureya, with Lieutenant Davith Sconn as her first officer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Forger
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Grand Moff Dureya
