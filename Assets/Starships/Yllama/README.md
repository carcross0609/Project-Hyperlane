# Yllama

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yllama](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yllama)

Yllama was a Strike-class medium cruiser used by pirates. The cruiser would arrive at a TIE Defender staging area used by the pirates that was destroyed by two Imperial Missile Boats, after the corvettes Xtram and modified corvette Holdon were destroyed. The cruiser launched Z-95 Headhunters, R-41 Starchasers and TIE Avengers. The cruiser would then be destroyed by the two Missile Boats.
