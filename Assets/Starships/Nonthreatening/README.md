# nonthreatening

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Nonthreatening](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nonthreatening)

A nonthreatening was a type of ship used by the Galactic Empire. Between 4 ABY and 5 ABY, an entry for the Star Destroyer in The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual compared the shape of a nonthreatening to a butter knife, as well as saying the Star Destroyer was shaped like a dagger.

## Specifications

- **Name:** nonthreatening
- **Type:** Starship
- **Role:** Not to be threatening
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
