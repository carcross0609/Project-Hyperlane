# Hatch

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hatch (freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hatch_%28freighter%29)

Hatch was a civilian YT-1300. During the Galactic Civil War it was part of heavy freighter traffic between Navigation buoys B-52 and B-53. The transport was inspected by a single TIE Defender to find equipment inside its cargo. The transport soon jumped to Hyperspace.
