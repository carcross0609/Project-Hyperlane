# UT-60D U-wing; starfighter/support craft

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/UT-60D_U-wing_starfighter/support_craft)

The UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft, also designated as the UT-60D U-wing Troop Transport, was a starship model manufactured by Incom Corporation and used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. U-wings served as transports, gunships, and starfighters that dropped soldiers into battle and provided cover fire. Each vessel had powerful fixed cannons that served well in space and versatile gun ports by the loading doors. U-wings were pivotal in transporting and protecting the Rebel Alliance's ground forces during the Battle of Scarif.
