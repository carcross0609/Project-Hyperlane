# Red Tooth

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red Tooth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Tooth)

Red Tooth was a group of two CR90 corvettes under the service of the Galactic Empire. The corvettes were trying to destroy a B-wing assembly with the help of Assault Gunboats and another corvette group, Purple Claw, which was witnessed by the Habassa. Both of the corvette groups and all of the Assault Gunboats were destroyed.
