# Steadfast

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Steadfast (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Steadfast_%28Sith_Empire%29)

The Steadfast was an Imperial medical transport serving in the Imperial Armada of the Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War. It participated in the Sacking of Coruscant.

- **Name:** Steadfast
- **Model:** Imperial medical transport
- **Type:** Hospital ship
