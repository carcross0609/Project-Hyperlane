# Unidentified Theelin family's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Theelin family's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Theelin_family%27s_ship)

A ship was owned by a Theelin family during the High Republic Era. Between around 397 BBY and 387 BBY, the family was present on the Expansion Region planet Genetia when the Evereni Ro family was attacked by townspeople. During the attack, two members of the Ro family, the mothers of the cousins Marda and Yana, killed the Theelin family and took their ship. After they escaped, the ship's life-support systems began to fail and the Ros experienced days of hunger and cold.
