# Pursuant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pursuant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pursuant)

The Pursuant was a Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier in service to the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At some point, the cruiser-carrier flew over a land-and-water-covered terrestrial body alongside TIE/ln space superiority starfighters. The Pursuant measured 340 meters (1,116 feet) in length and was equipped with sublight engines and four hangar bays, in which it carried TIE series starships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pursuant
- **Line:** Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier
- **Type:** Cruiser-carrier

- **Engine:** Sublight engines
- **Complement:** TIE series starships
- **Bays:** Hangars (4)
