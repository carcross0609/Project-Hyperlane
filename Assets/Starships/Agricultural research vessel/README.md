# Agricultural research vessel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Agricultural research vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agricultural_research_vessel)

The Agricultural research vessel was a starship used for research of agriculture. In 0 BBY one such vessel was hijacked on Alderaan by a Black Sun vigo Slak Sagar. Sagar worked for the Galactic Empire and used the vessel to test a new drug on scientists, until it was destroyed by a Twi'lek mercenary Rianna Saren.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Agricultural research vessel
- **Role:** Agricultural research
- **Affiliation:** Black Sun
