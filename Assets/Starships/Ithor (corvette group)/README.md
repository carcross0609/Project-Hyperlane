# Ithor (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ithor (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ithor_%28corvette_group%29)

Ithor was a group of three CR90 corvettes in the Rebel Alliance. They arrived to pick up a shipment of warheads stolen from Viraxo Industries by Container Transport group Magnum lead by Ace Azzameen in the YT-1300 light freighter Sabra.
