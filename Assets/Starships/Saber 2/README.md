# Saber 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saber 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saber_2)

Saber 2, which was also designated Saber Two, was an Imperial Navy TIE/IN interceptor manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems serving in the 181st Imperial Fighter Wing's Saber Squadron by 4 ABY. Piloted by the 181st's executive officer Major Turr Phennir in that year, Saber 2 participated in the Battle of Endor, which ended with the Alliance to Restore the Republic destroying the Imperial Death Star II battle station, although Phennir himself managed to survive.
