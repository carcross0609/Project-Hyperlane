# Coyote cargo ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Coyote cargo ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coyote_cargo_ship)

The Coyote cargo ship was a model of freighter. It had an appearance similar to that of an animal squatting on its haunches. The pirate Hondo Ohnaka owned one such vessel at his business Ohnaka Transport Solutions on the planet Batuu.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Coyote cargo ship
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Equipped
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** At least 6
