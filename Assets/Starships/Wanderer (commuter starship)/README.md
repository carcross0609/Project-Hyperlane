# Wanderer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wanderer (commuter starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wanderer_%28commuter_starship%29)

A Wanderer was a commuter spacecraft that carried six passengers. Corporate Sector Authority agents Fiolla and Magg used one of these for transport during the former's attempt to break up a slaving ring run by Zlarb and, unknown to Fiolla at the time, Magg himself. During their transit to Bonadan, their Wanderer was rendered inoperable, so it was deposited in a CSA pool hangar while it waited for parts and repairs.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wanderer
- **Length:** 5.5 meters
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Max Accel:** 0.4 lightspeed
- **Engine:** Comet ion engine
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Role:** Commuter craft
