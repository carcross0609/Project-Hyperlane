# Senin Vant's Porax-38 starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Senin Vant's Porax-38 starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senin_Vant%27s_Porax-38_starfighter)

A Porax-38 starfighter was piloted by the Utapau Skyforce Air Commodore Senin Vant. At some point prior to the Battle of Utapau in 19 BBY, the starfighter was taken for an unshielded joyride, during which a Utapaun flying squid collided with the fighter near its cockpit, leaving a dent and a smear. Vant later flew the Porax-38 starfighter during the Battle of Utapau against the forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.
