# Unidentified Squib salvage scow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Squib salvage scow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Squib_salvage_scow)

A Squib salvage scow discovered a copy of the book The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force c. 24 ABY. The book was among artifacts the Squibs recovered from the ruins of the planet Byss. The Squibs handed the volume over to the New Jedi Order for study.
