# Republic police gunship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Republic police gunship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_police_gunship/Legends)

The Republic police gunship was a model of transport gunship utilized by the Coruscant Security Force and the Coruscant Guard on the planet Coruscant during the waning years of the Galactic Republic.

- **Height:** 5.76 meters
- **Armament:**

- **Crew:** 1 pilot and 1 gunner
- **Passengers:** Troop bay
- **Availability:** Law enforcement
- **Role:**

  - Troop carrier
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Coruscant Security Force
