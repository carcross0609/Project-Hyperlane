# Snoke's escape shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Snoke's escape shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snoke%27s_escape_shuttle)

Snoke's escape shuttle, also referred to as Snoke's escape craft, was a shuttle owned by Supreme Leader Snoke. After his death at the hands of Kylo Ren during the Battle of Oetchi, Rey used it to escape from the First Order's flagship the Supremacy, then later abandoned it in space among the shattered ruins of the First Order's fleet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Snoke's escape shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Affiliation:** First Order
- **Owners:** Snoke
