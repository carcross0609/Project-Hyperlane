# Fleen

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fleen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fleen)

The Fleen was a Corellian Engineering Corporation YT-1300 light freighter. Jer Blankuna arranged for the ship to bring a group of adventurers to his mansion orbiting Alderaan in 27 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fleen
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
