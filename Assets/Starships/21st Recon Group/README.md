# 21st Recon Group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [21st Recon Group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/21st_Recon_Group)

The 21st Recon Group was a wing of Recon-X starfighters attached to the New Republic Fifth Fleet. The 21st Recon Group was commanded by Lieutenant Rone Taggar, and formed shortly before the Battle of Doornik-319, although Rone Taggar had served with some of the pilots of the 21st before the conception of the unit.
