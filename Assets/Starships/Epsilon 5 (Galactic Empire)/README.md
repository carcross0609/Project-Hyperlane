# Epsilon 5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Epsilon 5 (Galactic Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epsilon_5_%28Galactic_Empire%29)

The Epsilon 5 was an Imperial Lambda shuttle used in 0 BBY to transport Grand Moff Tarkin and Bevel Lemelisk. This shuttle was part of a group of five Lambda shuttles attacked in the Eriadu system by rebel forces in order to capture Tarkin and Lemelisk. The rebels failed to capture the pair but succeeded in liberating Gial Ackbar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Epsilon 5
- **Model:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
