# Bravo One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bravo One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravo_One)

Bravo One was a UT-60D transport/gunship that served the Extraction Team Bravo of the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. Under the command of General Davits Draven, in 1 BBY, the Bravo Team took a successful mission to break out Jyn Erso&ndash;a human female criminal who would later become a key figure in the theft of the Death Star plans&ndash;from a labor camp that was used by the Galactic Empire on the planet Wobani.
