# Lothalian Corvette

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lothalian Corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lothalian_Corvette)

The Lothalian Corvette was a starship model that saw action at the Battle of Exegol in 35 ABY. Multiple vessels of this corvette class were part of a Citizens' Fleet of volunteer civilians from throughout the galaxy who ensured the Resistance's victory over the Sith forces on Exegol, and at least one Lothalian Corvette joined the victory celebrations hosted by the Resistance at their base on the planet Ajan Kloss shortly after the battle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lothalian Corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Engine:** Equipped (6)
- **Affiliation:** Citizens' Fleet
