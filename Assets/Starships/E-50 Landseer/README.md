# E-50 Landseer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-50 Landseer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-50_Landseer)

The E-50 Landseer was a model of repurposed Corellian shuttle used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic as ambulance ships, conveying medical and repair payloads directly on the battlefield. Its hull bore the universal red sigil, an identifier for medical equipment, vehicles and personnel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-50 Landseer
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Armament:** Twin laser cannon turret (1)
- **Role:** Ambulance ship
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
