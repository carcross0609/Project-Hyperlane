# Spiderspun

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spiderspun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spiderspun)

The Spiderspun was a freighter in the service of the Byne Guild. A new, top-of-the-line starship, the Spiderspun was under the personal command of Scover Byne, the Guild's leader. It was present in the Central Senatorial Hangar on Coruscant when Affie Hollow, Byne's adopted daughter, arrived on the planet in the aftermath of the Great Hyperspace Disaster.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spiderspun
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Affiliation:** Byne Guild
- **Owners:** Scover Byne
- **Registry:** Byne Guild 171
