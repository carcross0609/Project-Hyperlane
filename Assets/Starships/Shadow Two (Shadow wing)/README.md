# Shadow Two

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow Two (Shadow wing)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Two_%28Shadow_wing%29)

Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems, Shadow Two was a TIE/ln starfighter affiliated with the Imperial Navy's Shadow wing during the Galactic Civil War. Shadow Two was used to enforce blockades, and the TIE/LN starfighter had exquisite handling for slashing attacks on unescorted transports and freighters. Shadow Two was operated by the TIE pilot PS-29-2, who was nicknamed "Mynock." As Shadow Two's pilot, PS-29-2 would tweak the starfighter's control systems.
