# Short haul freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Short haul freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Short_haul_freighter)

Short haul freighter were space transports capable of carrying cargo or passengers. They were capable of only a limited number of hyperspace jumps between refuelings. Reymet Autem and Riao Siao fled Coruscant to Felacat.
