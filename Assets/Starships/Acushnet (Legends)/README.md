# Acushnet

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Acushnet/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acushnet/Legends)

The Acushnet was a Corona-class armed frigate, a ship derived from an ancient Surronian cruiser design, used by a Weequay pirate gang on Florrum.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Acushnet
- **Manufacturer:** Haor Chall Engineering
- **Model:** Corona-class armed frigate
- **Type:** Transport
- **Cost:** 3,700,000 credits
- **Length:** At least 320 meters
- **Width:** At least 320 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1.0
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Complement:**
  - 6 Flarestar-class Weequay ships
  - 12 WLO-5 speeder tanks
- **Crew:** 64
- **Passengers:** 200
- **Capacity:** 450 tons
- **Consumables:** 1 year
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Ohnaka Gang
