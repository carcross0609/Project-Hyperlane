# Havoc

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Havoc (Imperial-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Havoc_%28Imperial-class%29)

Havoc was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer under the command of Captain Tulimus, during the Galactic Civil War. A participant in the siege of Edan II, it was destroyed by Rebel Alliance forces led by Colonel Pertarn, when they launched a frontal assault on the ship. With all firepower from the Vindicator aimed at the ship, the Havoc broke apart.
