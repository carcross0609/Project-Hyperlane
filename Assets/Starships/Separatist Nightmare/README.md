# Separatist Nightmare

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Separatist Nightmare](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_Nightmare)

Separatist Nightmare was a LAAT gunship used by the Grand Army of the Republic during the Second Battle of Geonosis, serving in Mundi's squadron.

- **Name:** Separatist Nightmare
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Model:** Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry
- **Length:** 17.69 meters
- **Height:** 6.94 meters
- **Max Speed:** 620 kph
- **Armament:**

  - Pair of laser-beam turrets (optional)

  -   Missile belts (Rear-mounted)
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Co-pilot/Gunner (1)
  - Gunner (2-4)(optional)
- **Passengers:** 30
- **Destroyed:** 21 BBY, Geonosis
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Mundi's squadron
