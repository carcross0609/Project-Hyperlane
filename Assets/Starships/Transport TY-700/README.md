# Transport TY-700

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Transport TY-700](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transport_TY-700)

Transport TY-700 was a Darius G-class freighter used by the First Order to transport fuel. Due to the starship being a model normally used by civilians, its allegiance to the First Order was not immediately apparent. In 34 ABY, it was used to deliver a fuel shipment to the Colossus platform on Castilon. Major Elrik Vonreg travelled aboard the ship for the delivery, as he had an assignment to speak to the platform's owner, Captain Imanuel Doza.

## Specifications
