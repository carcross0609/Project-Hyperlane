# Bravo Flight

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bravo Flight (New Republic)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravo_Flight_%28New_Republic%29)

Bravo Flight, otherwise known as Ferry Flight, was an escort squadron of at least nine T-65BR X-wing reconnaissance starfighters attached to the New Republic Fifth Battle Group.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bravo Flight
- **Type:** Starfighter squadron
- **Leader:**
  - Lieutenant Bos (Bravo Leader)
- **Affiliation:**
  - New Republic
  - New Republic Fifth Battle Group
