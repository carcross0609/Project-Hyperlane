# Razor Crest 2.0

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Razor Crest 2.0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Razor_Crest_2.0)

The Razor Crest 2.0 was the second ST-70 assault ship owned by the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin. It was named after its predecessor, the Razor Crest and served as a new addition to his N-1 starfighter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Razor Crest 2.0
- **Model:** ST-70 assault ship
- **Type:** Gunship
- **Armament:**
  - 2 Laser Cannons
  - 2 Concussion Missile Launchers
- **Crew:** 1 pilot
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - New Republic
  - Clan Mudhorn
- **Owners:**
  - Unidentified Imperial starship collector
  - Din Djarin
