# Mellcrawler II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mellcrawler II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mellcrawler_II)

The Mellcrawler II was an Inyusu-class Star Yacht in the Alliance Fleet during the Galactic Civil War. It was named after the Mellcrawler, the previous starship owned by Rebel pilot Nien Nunb. The ship took part in the Battle of Theed to aid the Alliance's efforts to stop Operation: Cinder, part of the Imperial Contingency plan designed to render Naboo and countless other worlds uninhabitable.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mellcrawler II
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** Inyusu-class Star Yacht
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
