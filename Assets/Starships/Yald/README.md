# Yald

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yald](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yald)

The Yald was a heavy cruiser in the service of the New Republic and the Galactic Alliance. It was the flagship of Commodore Brand during the Battle of Fondor.
