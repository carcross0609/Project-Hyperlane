# Regina Cayli

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Regina Cayli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_Cayli)

Regina Cayli was a M-Class Imperial Attack Transport serving the Galactic Empire. The Regina Cayli was on active duty at New Bakstre and was tasked to secure the smuggler vessel Dorion Discus on the surface after Imperial Customs forced down the freighter.
