# Concord

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Concord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Concord)

The Concord was a Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I that served in the New Republic Defense Fleet during the Galactic Civil War in 5 ABY. It was commanded by Commodore Kyrsta Agate. The ship saw service in a skirmish in Wild Space, the Battle for Kuat Drive Yards, and was incredibly important to New Republic's efforts in the Battle of Jakku.
