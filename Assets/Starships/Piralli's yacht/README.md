# Piralli's yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Piralli's yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Piralli%27s_yacht)

A yacht was owned by the Sullustan Piralli, who had taken ownership of it to settle a debt and worked on rebuilding it using salvaged and purchased parts over the course of eight years. Eventually, the yacht, a Sullustan Mark VIII Legacy-class space yacht, only needed a central processor to be completed. The specific type of processor needed cost around two million Republic credits, and Piralli was unsure if he would be able to complete the yacht due to the cost. The church droid P3-7A obtained a processor for the yacht by stealing one from a yacht belonging to the criminal Ullan Yite.
