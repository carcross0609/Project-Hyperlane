# Vall Kumauri's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vall Kumauri's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vall_Kumauri%27s_flagship)

Vall Kumauri's flagship was a starship used by the warlord Vall Kumauri. When Kumauri's conquests of approximately 10,000 BBY led to a conflict between his empire and the Galactic Republic, the flagship participated in a battle between the two factions. Both the battle and Kumauri's flagship were eventually depicted in a fresco in a Kumauri Empire palace on an Outer Rim Territories moon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vall Kumauri's flagship
- **Commission:** Around 10,000 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Kumauri Empire
- **Owners:** Vall Kumauri
