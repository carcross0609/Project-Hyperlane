# Marnia Un'la'na's racer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Marnia Un'la'na's racer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marnia_Un%27la%27na%27s_racer)

The ace pilot and First Order informant Marnia Un'la'na owned a racer she used to participate in races. In 34 ABY, she brought the vessel to the supertanker fuel depot Colossus for the All Aces Battle Royale competition. During the race, Un'la'na attempted to transmit an encrypted data cache from her racer to the First Order while competing to remain ahead of the others in the event. Although the Twi'lek won the All Aces Battle Royale, another of the event's participants, the Resistance spy Kazuda Xiono, used an encryption recorder to intercept the data she had sent from her racer and sent it to the Resistance.
