# Salon pod

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Salon pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salon_pod)

Salon pods were detachable pods used for diplomatic meetings, and were situated on Consular-class space cruisers and Republic frigates. On standard Consular-class cruisers, the pod was located directly underneath the bridge, and was completely shielded from surveillance and armored against attack. The pods possessed a holoprojector, conference table, and its circumference was lined with couches.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Salon pod
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Role:** Detachable pod for diplomatic meetings or in the event of emergencies
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Judicial Forces
  -   Galactic Republic Navy
