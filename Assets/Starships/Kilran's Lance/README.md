# Kilran's Lance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kilran's Lance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kilran%27s_Lance)

The Kilran's Lance was a space station in the orbit of the planet Aeten Ten in Wild Space that was operated by the Sith Empire during the Cold War with the Galactic Republic. Traitors aboard the station disabled the station's defenses, allowing the Republic to launch a surprise attack that was nonetheless beaten back by the Empire.
