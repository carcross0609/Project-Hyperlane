# Striking Distance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Striking Distance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Striking_Distance)

The Striking Distance was an MC80 Star Cruiser that was owned by General Carlist Rieekan during the Galactic Civil War. It participated in the climactic Battle of Jakku.
