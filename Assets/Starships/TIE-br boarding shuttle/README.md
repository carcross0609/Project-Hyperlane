# TIE/br boarding shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE/br boarding shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/br_boarding_shuttle)

The TIE/br boarding shuttle, also known as the TIE Boarding Craft or Imperial boarding craft, was a variant of the TIE/sa bomber used for boarding and troop transportation and as a boarding craft.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TIE/br boarding shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Line:** TIE Series
- **Model:** Twin Ion Engine/br boarding shuttle
- **Type:** Boarding craft
- **Length:** 7.8 meters
- **Armament:** Rear laser cannon
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** >12
- **Othersystems:**
  - Cutting tool
  - Tractor beam
- **Role:**
  - Shuttle
  - Boarding craft
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
