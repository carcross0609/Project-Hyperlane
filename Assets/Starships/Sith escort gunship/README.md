# Sith escort gunship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith escort gunship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_escort_gunship)

The Sith escort gunship was an attack craft employed by the Sith Empire. It served as the primary warship of the Sith naval forces.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sith escort gunship
- **Manufacturer:** Sith Empire
- **Model:** Escort gunship
- **Length:** 75 meters
- **Armament:** Autoblasters (6)
- **Crew:** 18
- **Passengers:** 30
- **Capacity:** 250 metric tons
- **Role:** Attack craft
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
