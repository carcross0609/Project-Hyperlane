# Missile boat

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Missile boat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Missile_boat)

A missile boat was a type of small warship used by various factions in the Unknown Regions and carried a missile payload. The Chiss Ascendancy used missile boats, while the Nikardun Destiny utilized the Nighthunter gunboats. Several of the Chiss Ascendancy's missile boats participated in a battle over Primea alongside the Vak Combine against the Nikardun Destiny as part of Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet Picket Force Six.
