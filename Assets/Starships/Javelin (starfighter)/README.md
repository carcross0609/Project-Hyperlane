# Javelin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Javelin (starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Javelin_%28starfighter%29)

The Javelin was a model of starfighter in use around the galaxy during the time following the Second Galactic Civil War. When the Lost Tribe of Sith began forming a new Sith armada in 41 ABY, the Sith began ambushing and capturing vessels for use in their fleet. By 44 ABY, there were at least half a dozen Javelins in use in the Sith fleet. Six Javelins traveled to Pydyr as reinforcements for the Sith there, and they engaged in battle with a Jedi wing of StealthXs.
