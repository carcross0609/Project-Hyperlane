# Oriflamme

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oriflamme](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oriflamme)

Oriflamme was the personal Imperial-class Star Destroyer of Grand Admiral Octavian Grant. He presumably handed it over to the New Republic when he defected in 6 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Oriflamme
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/LN starfighters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Pentastar Alignment
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Owners:** Grand Admiral Octavian Grant
