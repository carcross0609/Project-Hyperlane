# Droid drop ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droid drop ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_drop_ship)

Droid drop ships were craft used by the Trade Federation. The dropships were carried on-board the Saak'ak, flagship of the Trade Federation Naboo blockade fleet in 32 BBY. Three of the droid drop ships were landed in the Theed Hangar in the latter stages of the invasion.
