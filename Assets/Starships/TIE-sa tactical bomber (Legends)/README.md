# TIE/sa tactical bomber

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE/sa tactical bomber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/sa_tactical_bomber/Legends)

The TIE/sa tactical bomber, simply known as the TIE/sa bomber, TIE bomber or T/B, was a larger, less maneuverable TIE starfighter used for destroying large starships and pinpoint-precision bombing. The "sa" in the TIE bomber's designation stood for "surface assault."
