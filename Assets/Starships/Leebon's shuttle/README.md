# Leebon's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Leebon's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leebon%27s_shuttle)

Leebon's shuttle was a shuttle owned by Jedi Master Leebon of the Jedi Order when she was stationed on the Mid Rim moon Jedha during the High Republic Era. After Leebon's death during the Battle of Jedha in 382 BBY, the shuttle's ownership transferred to Jedi Master Vildar Mac. Not long after, Mac instructed his Padawan, Matthea Cathley&mdash;who had been Leebon's Padawan before her death&mdash;and Jedi Knight Oliviah Zeveron to travel to the Outer Rim planet Dalna to investigate the Path of the Open Hand cult that had instigated the battle.
