# T-95 Trainer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-95 Trainer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-95_Trainer)

The T-95 Trainer was a type of starship manufactured by Incom Corporation. They were used in the orbital facilities Galactic Republic's training academies, such as the Sullust Sector Spacefarers Academy. When he was a cadet, Wilhuff Tarkin trained using the T-95, practicing maneuvers for hours at a time.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T-95 Trainer
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Model:** T-95
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Role:** Training
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
