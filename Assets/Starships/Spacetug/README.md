# Spacetug

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spacetug](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spacetug)

Spacetugs, also known as tugboats, were utility spacecraft designed to assist in guiding larger vessels and their cargo in maneuvering through orbital transfer yards and transit corridors, by utilizing either tractor beams or magnetic clamps.
