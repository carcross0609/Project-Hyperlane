# Unidentified Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Mandator_IV-class_Siege_Dreadnought)

A Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought was used by the First Order during their assault on the planet Naboo in 34 ABY. General Armitage Hux positioned the Siege Dreadnought above the city Spinnaker, intending to level the city to quell the resistance movement there, which had been engineered by the First Order Supreme Leader Kylo Ren. The dreadnought was attacked by Naboo starfighters, who managed to destroy one of its autocannons. Before Hux could fire on the city, Ren gave the order to withdraw from Naboo.
