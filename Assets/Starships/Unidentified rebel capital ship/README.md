# Unidentified rebel capital ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified rebel capital ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_rebel_capital_ship)

A capital ship of the Alliance to Restore the Republic's fleet was destroyed in the Altyr system of the Outer Rim's Morshdine sector in 3 ABY during a battle against the Galactic Empire's Imperial Navy as part of the Galactic Civil War. The battle in which the capital ship was destroyed was called a brisk reminder of the Empire's superior force by Commander Folimar, who was quoted by reporter Dorna Parros during a HoloNet News broadcast covering the battle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified rebel capital ship
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY, Altyr system
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
