# Unidentified smuggling ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified smuggling ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_smuggling_ship)

A smuggling ship was captured just prior to the Battle of Endor by the Devastator in the Endor system. The crew were taken and then executed by Admiral Jhared Montferrat, despite their protestations that they were not spies. The Admiral then ordered their ship to be cut loose and then used in gunnery practice.
