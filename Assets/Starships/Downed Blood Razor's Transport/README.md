# Downed Blood Razor's Transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Downed Blood Razor's Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Downed_Blood_Razor%27s_Transport)

The Downed Blood Razor's Transport was a camp built around a transport ship of the Blood Razors that had crash-landed on the planet of Lok. Since that time, it had been used as a base of operations for the Blood Razors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Downed Blood Razor's Transport
- **Location:** Mosseru Hills, Lok
- **Affiliation:** Blood Razors
