# Hityamun Kris's personal yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hityamun Kris's personal yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hityamun_Kris%27s_personal_yacht)

The personal yacht of the Black Sun Vigo Hityamun Kris was a large, black yacht, at least three times the size of a YT-1300 light freighter. Kris used the vessel during his pursuit of his former lover, the holostar Javul Charn.
