# Gafra

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gafra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gafra)

Gafra was a BFF-1 bulk freighter used by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. During Operation Strike Fear the Gafra collected a new experimental warhead from Celanon and transported it to a deep space transfer zone.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gafra
- **Model:** BFF-1 bulk freighter
- **Type:** Freighter

- **Height:** 34 meters
