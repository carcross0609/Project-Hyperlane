# Flying Fish

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flying Fish](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Fish)

The Flying Fish was a YT-1300 light freighter owned by the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It was used by a group of Rebels on their mission to infiltrate and destroy the Imperial Data Processing Facility on Tazan. To provide a plausible cover story, the starship was loaded with low-grade medical supplies, while explosives were stored in a number of secret compartments located under the deck plating.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flying Fish
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
