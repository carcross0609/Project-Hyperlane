# Beam Racer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Beam Racer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beam_Racer)

The Beam Racer was Prince Isolder's personal starcutter. Shortly before the Battle of Uroro Station he took it to Nova Station to randezvous with Batag Skiff Blue Slipper carrying Jedi Knight Ben Skywalker. However the ship was captured by suddenly appeared Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Anakin Solo led by Tahiri Veila and later destroyed in Anakin Solo hangar on the order of Darth Caedus.
