# Merr-Sonn (transport group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Merr-Sonn (transport group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merr-Sonn_%28transport_group%29)

Merr-Sonn was a group of three MB-C1 medium transports that were presumably belonging to Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. That was present at Calast, a Golan Space Colony 2 space station during the Rescue at Bundil II. The ship contained Herbs.
