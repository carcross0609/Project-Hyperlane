# Light of Reason

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Light of Reason](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_of_Reason)

The Light of Reason was an old battered Sienar H-10 brig that brought Seti Ashgad and his party onboard the Adamantine to meet Leia Organa Solo. Luke Skywalker believed the ship was so small it only had a capacity to hold six passengers.
