# T-6 shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-6 shuttle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-6_shuttle/Legends)

The T-6 shuttle, also known as the Jedi ambassador shuttle, was a semicircular shuttle manufactured by Slayn & Korpil and in use by the Galactic Republic and the Jedi Order during the Clone Wars. Adorned with a Galactic Roundel and painted in the red-and-white color scheme of the Grand Army of the Republic, T-6 shuttles featured rotating cockpits and engine blocks, and were hyperdrive-equipped. Several craft of this model were utilized by Jedi throughout the Clone Wars on a variety of missions. It was still in use during the Galactic Civil War.
