# 192

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [192](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/192)

192 was a group of three Assassin-class corvettes belonging to the Empire. They were scheduled to be repaired at Depot XLS, but were attacked by Rebel B-wings intending to destroy all the capital ships while they were at their weakest condition.
