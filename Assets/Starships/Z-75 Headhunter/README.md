# Z-75 Headhunter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Z-75 Headhunter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-75_Headhunter)

The Z-75 Headhunter was a model of starship. The mechanic Jarek Yeager used pieces of a Z-75 Headhunter&mdash;rumored to be an engine&mdash;alongside pieces of a T-65B X-wing starfighter, to create the racing starfighter known as the Fireball. According to rumors, Yeager had used a Z-75 inherited from members of his family in the construction of the Fireball.
