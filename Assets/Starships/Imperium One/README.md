# Imperium One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperium One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperium_One)

Imperium One was the designation of an Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser used by the forces of Imperial Governor Tauntaza. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, the governor traveled aboard the cruiser from her flagship to the science colony on the planet Gabredor III where she attempted to extract a piece of technology she had developed.
