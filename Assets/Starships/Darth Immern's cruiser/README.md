# Darth Immern's cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Immern's cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Immern%27s_cruiser)

During the Great Galactic War, Darth Immern commanded his personal cruiser as part of the Imperial Navy of the Sith Empire. Both Immern and his cruiser were destroyed in the First Battle of Bothawui.

- **Name:** Darth Immern's cruiser
- **Skeleton:** 1 astromech droid
- **Role:** Cruiser
- **Destroyed:** 3671 BBY
- **Affiliation:**

- **Owners:** Darth Immern
