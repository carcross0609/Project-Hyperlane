# Z-Omega 4

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Omega 4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Omega_4)

Z-Omega 4 was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport that fought on the side of Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's coup during the Second battle of Imperial Center in around 4 ABY. During the battle, the transport was destroyed after being hit by cannonfire and two pairs of missiles fired from an Imperial TIE Interceptor.

- **Name:** Z-Omega 4
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Type:** Transport

- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
- **Navy:** Zaarin's fleet
