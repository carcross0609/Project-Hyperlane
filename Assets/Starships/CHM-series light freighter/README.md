# CHM-series light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CHM-series light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CHM-series_light_freighter)

The CHM-series was a type of light freighter manufactured by Sublights Products Corporation. It was heavily armored and equipped with two side weapons, a hyperdrive and a long-range comlink. It could be manned by a skeleton crew of a single pilot. A CHM-series freighter named the Outcast was used by bounty hunter Fennec Shand during the early Imperial Era.
