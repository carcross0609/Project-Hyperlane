# Madallo

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Madallo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Madallo)

The Madallo was a luxury starliner operating during the Galactic Civil War. A group of Alliance to Restore the Republic agents booked passage on the liner to reach the planet Spira.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Madallo
- **Type:** Luxury starliner
- **Role:** Luxury passenger transport
