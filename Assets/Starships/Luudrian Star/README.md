# Luudrian Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Luudrian Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luudrian_Star)

Luudrian Star was a modified Ghtroc 720 light freighter owned by Tru'eb Cholakk, Rebel gunrunner. Tru'eb was able to buy the ship with the reward he got from Platt Okeefe after freeing her from the slaver Big Quince.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Luudrian Star
- **Manufacturer:** Ghtroc Industries
- **Model:** 720 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Length:** 35 meters
- **Max Speed:** 750 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 2 (primary)
  - Class 15 (backup)
- **Armament:** 2 Mass Drive Cannons
- **Crew:** 1
- **Passengers:** 10
- **Capacity:** 135 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Owners:** Tru'eb Cholakk
