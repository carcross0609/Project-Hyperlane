# Unidentified Consular-class cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Consular-class cruiser (Quell)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Consular-class_cruiser_%28Quell%29/Legends)

A Consular-class cruiser was part of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars, used by Jedi General Anakin Skywalker to evacuate Jedi Master Aayla Secura and her crew from the Liberty during the Sky Battle of Quell. It was destroyed when it crashed on Maridun.
