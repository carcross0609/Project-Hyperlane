# Nikardun blockade frigate

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nikardun blockade frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikardun_blockade_frigate)

The Nikardun blockade frigate was a class of blockade frigate used by the Nikardun Destiny during their conquests. The Chiss heavy cruiser Springhawk captured one such frigate. Later, the Paccosh subclan leader named Uingali foar Marocsaa captured such a frigate from Nikardun forces fleeing the Rapacc system. He decorated the surface of his captured blockade frigate with a chimaera and named it the Aelos.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nikardun blockade frigate
- **Type:** Blockade frigate
- **Role:** Blockade
- **Affiliation:**
  - Nikardun Destiny
  - Paccian Governance (commandeered)
