# Springhawk

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Springhawk/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Springhawk/Legends)

The Springhawk was a Chiss assault cruiser serving with Picket Force Two of the Chiss Ascendancy's Expansionary Fleet. It served as the personal flagship of Force Commander Mitth'raw'nuruodo, core name "Thrawn".

## Specifications

- **Name:** Springhawk
- **Type:** Assault cruiser
- **Length:** A little bigger than a 115 meter Republic cruiser
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - Connor nets
  - Masers
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Chiss Ascendancy
- **Navy:** Chiss Expansionary Fleet
- **Fleet:** Picket Force Two
- **Captains:** Mitth'raw'nuruodo
