# Luminous Nine's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Luminous Nine's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luminous_Nine%27s_shuttle)

A shuttle was used by the Luminous Nine during their mission to the planet Sophros in 228 BBY to travel to the planet's surface from the Ataraxia. The shuttle landed safely on Sophros, but suffered immense damage to its systems and was unable to fly.
