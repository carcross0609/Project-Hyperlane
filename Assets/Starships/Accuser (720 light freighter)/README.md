# Accuser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Accuser (720 light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Accuser_%28720_light_freighter%29)

Accuser was a modified 720 light freighter owned by the Tiss'shar bounty hunter and assassin Kal-tan-shi. He acquired it from one of his targets in the Lesser Plooriod Cluster. It was modified by his former employers, House Paramexor, but he left their employ to pursue a solo career before the modifications were completed.
