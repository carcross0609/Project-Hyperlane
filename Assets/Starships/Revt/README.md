# Revt

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Revt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revt)

Revt was a group of three Lambda-class T-4a shuttles used by the Rebel Alliance they were present at a Rebel supply outpost consisting of 3 Class-A cargo containers Duce and were unmanned but were going to be docked by Utility Tugs under flight group T-SE to fight back, while the other flight group T-SF was to dock with the Y-wings to ferry pilots to the fighters. Either way, they were destroyed by TIE Interceptors along with the two transports Bultz and the Container transport Moth.
