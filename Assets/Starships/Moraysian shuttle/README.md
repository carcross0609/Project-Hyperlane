# Moraysian shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moraysian shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moraysian_shuttle)

The Moraysian shuttle was a type of shuttle created by the Moraysians. They could be found aboard Moraysian cruisers, such as the cruiser stolen by the mercenary Ledesmar and later used by Kes Dameron and L'ulo L'ampar in their search for Dameron's son Poe, who had joined the Spice Runners of Kijimi. After the Osako pirates chased Dameron and several other Spice Runners aboard the Vondel toward the Moraysian cruiser and forced them to land on the nearby planet Judakann, Dameron and L'ampar followed in a Moraysian shuttle.
