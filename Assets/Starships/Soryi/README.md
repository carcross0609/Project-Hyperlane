# Soryi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Soryi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soryi)

Soryi was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport used by the Galactic Empire during the Battle on the Frontier campaign. During Grand Admiral Thrawn's campaign to expand Imperial presence in the Pakuuni system, Platform NL-1 came under attack by Rebel forces. TIE pilots, Maarek Stele among them, repelled this attack, during which a group of Rebel officers attempted to escape their impending doom aboard Lambda-class T-4a shuttle Shakker. Maarek Stele disabled the Shakker, permitting Transport Soryi to capture the escaping Rebel officers and provide additional intelligence to the Prophets of the Dark Side.
