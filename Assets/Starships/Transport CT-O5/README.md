# Transport CT-O5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Transport CT-O5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transport_CT-O5)

Transport CT-05 was a New Republic transport ship used in the Corellia shipyards to transport heavy parts from a sector to another. The transport was heavily armed with twin fire-linked turrets on its wings as well as on its rear to defend against pursuers, and possessed a hyperdrive unit that allowed it to jump between star systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Transport CT-O5
- **Type:** Transport
- **Hyperdrive:** Two equipped
- **Armament:** 4 twin fire-linked laser cannon turrets
- **Capacity:** At least one Super Star Destroyer hyperdrive core
- **Role:** Heavy parts transport
- **Affiliation:** Morgan Elsbeth's forces
