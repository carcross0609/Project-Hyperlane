# Stardust

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stardust (transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stardust_%28transport%29)

Stardust was a Muurian transport that carried musicians of the Dunari's Rest when the latter was attacked by Imperial forces. The ship and its crew escaped to hyperspace intact thanks to assistance from the Rebel Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stardust
- **Manufacturer:** Muurian Interstellar
- **Model:** Muurian transport
- **Length:** 30.5 meters
- **Width:** 29.4 meters
- **Height:** 8.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 2 double laser cannon turrets
  - 3 laser cannons
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
