# Blazemaker Mark II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Blazemaker Mark II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blazemaker_Mark_II)

The Blazemaker Mark II was a Z-wing that Zander Freemaker assembled at Freemaker Salvage and Repair on the Wheel. He later flew it while escaping Imperial TIE fighters on Coruscant. After joining the Rebel Alliance, Zander and his family refitted the Blazemaker II with laser cannons and a magma cannon. Zander deployed his Blazemaker during a Rebel-Imperial dogfight but the ship blew up when the magma cannon malfunctioned. It had dual chromium plate engines and a durasteel hull.
