# Ursela Kuat's star yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ursela Kuat's star yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ursela_Kuat%27s_star_yacht)

At some point by 311 BBY, a star yacht carrying the principal director of the shipyard company Kuat Drive Yards, Ursela Kuat, was destroyed along the Rothana Route hyperlane by the gravity mines placed there to protect the Outer Rim Territories KDY shipyard planet Rothana. Decades later it was revealed that Kuat's rivals on the KDY directorate had secretly been behind the yacht's destruction and Kuat's death.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ursela Kuat's star yacht
- **Type:** Star yacht
- **Destroyed:** By 311 BBY, Rothana Route
- **Owners:** Ursela Kuat
