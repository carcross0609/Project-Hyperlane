# Renzoh Silliac's garbage scow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Renzoh Silliac's garbage scow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Renzoh_Silliac%27s_garbage_scow)

The Chevin Renzoh Silliac owned an Arkon II level transport he used as a garbage scow. He kept it in his salvage yard on Hallitron-7. He leveraged the ship to help a thief smuggle the Hope of Glee Anselm off of Jaloria, but a comms satellite recorded an image of the ship's escape. The bounty hunter Jango Fett used these images and his contacts to identify the type of ship and deduce its point of origin, leading him to Silliac.
