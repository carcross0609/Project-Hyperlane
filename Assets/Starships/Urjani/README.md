# Urjani

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Urjani](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Urjani)

Urjani was a Rebel Alliance Neutron Star-class Bulk Cruiser that was destroyed by the Death Star II during the Battle of Endor. It was a part of the Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Independence task force at Sullust prior to its destruction.
