# Sanction

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sanction](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sanction)

The Sanction was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that served in the Galactic Empire's navy during the Galactic Civil War. The Star Destroyer took part in the defense of the Trenchenovu shipyards from an anticipated attack by a Rebel Alliance squadron, being loaned a TIE fighter squadron from the Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing. After the 204th's TIEs ambushed the Alliance squadron, the Sanction jumped out of hyperspace to cut off the rebels' escape, the enemy squadron being nearly completely destroyed after.
