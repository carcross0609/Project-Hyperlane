# Republic light assault cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Republic light assault cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_light_assault_cruiser)

The Republic light assault cruiser was the term for armed and modified variants of the Corellian Engineering Corporation Consular-class cruiser and the CSS-1 Corellian star shuttle used by members of the Judicial Forces and the Jedi Order during the time of the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Republic light assault cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:**
  - Modified Consular-class cruiser
  - Modified CSS-1 Corellian Star Shuttle
- **Type:** Light assault cruiser
- **Engine:** One extra engine
- **Sensor:** Additonal communications equipment
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolasers
  - Additional weaponry
- **Escapepods:**
  - Escape pods
  - Evac pods
- **Role:**
  - Gunship
  - Troopship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Judicial Forces
