# Unidentified Acclamator-class assault ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Acclamator-class assault ship (Saleucami)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Acclamator-class_assault_ship_%28Saleucami%29)

This Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship was used by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. During the Battle of Saleucami, it was used to transport Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi, Clone Commander Cody, forces of the 212th Attack Battalion, and forces of the 501st Legion to the surface of the planet Saleucami.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Acclamator-class assault ship
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Line:** Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship
- **Type:** Cruiser-class proto-Star Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Navy:** Galactic Republic Navy
