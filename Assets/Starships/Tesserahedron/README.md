# Tesserahedron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tesserahedron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tesserahedron)

The Tesserahedron was a pirate PB-950 patrol boat owned by a group of Duros, who eventually sold it to Chrag'mak'al.
