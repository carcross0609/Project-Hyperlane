# Lady Luck

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lady Luck/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_Luck/Legends)

The Lady Luck&mdash;a SoroSuub Personal Luxury Yacht 3000&mdash;was the personal starship of gambler and Rebel Alliance/New Republic hero Lando Calrissian. The ship was named after Ymile, the girlfriend of Dominic Raynor, who secretly helped Lando win Cloud City from Raynor, and whom Lando nicknamed his "Lady Luck" for her assistance.
