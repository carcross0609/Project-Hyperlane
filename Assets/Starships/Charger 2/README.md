# Charger 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Charger 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charger_2)

Charger 2 was a CR90 corvette used by the Nharwaak. It fought at the Conflict at Mylok IV and, together with Charger 1, formed the Charger Group.
