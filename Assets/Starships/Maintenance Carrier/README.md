# Maintenance Carrier

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Maintenance Carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maintenance_Carrier)

A Maintenance Carrier was a class of starship used by the Galactic Empire. Three ships of the class, FarRun, Fixer, and Quartermain III, were assigned to Imperial Replenishment Fleet DK-209.
