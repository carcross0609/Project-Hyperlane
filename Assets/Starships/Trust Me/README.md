# Trust Me

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trust Me](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trust_Me)

Trust Me was a starship that formed a part of a pirate gang that operated out of Darknon Station. At one point by the time of the Galactic Civil War, it and two other vessels of the gang laid an ambush on the Helix-class light interceptor Kizbon's Box; however, the latter instead swiftly destroyed all of the pirate ships.
