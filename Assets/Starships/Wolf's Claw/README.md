# Wolf's Claw

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wolf's Claw](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wolf%27s_Claw)

Wolf's Claw was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer. It was one of the three Imperial-class Star Destroyers assigned as support for Admiral Gaen Drommel's flagship, Guardian. The Wolf's Claw participated in the Battle of Tantive V in 4 ABY, where it was captured by New Republic forces and later incorporated into the New Republic Defense Fleet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wolf's Claw
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Affiliation:**

  - Admiral Gaen Drommel
