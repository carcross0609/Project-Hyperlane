# Cruiser 8

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cruiser 8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cruiser_8)

Cruiser 8 was the designation number for an Acclamator-class assault ship during the Battle of Muunilinst. It was used as a blind spot as Anakin Skywalker lured a wing of Nantex-class starfighters into a volley of missiles fired by Red Squadron.
