# Grom 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grom 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grom_1)

The Grom 1 was an Airam Assassin-class corvette which together with its sister ship, the Grom 2, accompanied the Anat on its ill-fated final trip near the Blair Cluster. Upon being pulled out of hyperspace by the Star Destroyer Rage and Interdictor Compellor, the Grom 1 was ordered to engage. It met its end at the hand of Imperial torpedoes along with the Grom 2 and, soon after, the Anat.
