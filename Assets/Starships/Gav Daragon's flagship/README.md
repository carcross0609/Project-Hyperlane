# Gav Daragon's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gav Daragon's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gav_Daragon%27s_flagship)

This battleship was part of the Sith invasion fleet during the Great Hyperspace War and was under the command during the Battle of Koros Major. Gav Daragon fled the battle in this ship, and went to Primus Goluud.
