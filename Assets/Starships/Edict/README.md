# Edict

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Edict](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Edict)

The Edict was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer of the Galactic Empire that operated during the Imperial Era. In the closing stages of the Galactic Civil War, it fell under the control of the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing.
