# Nexus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nexus (Carrack-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nexus_%28Carrack-class%29)

Nexus was a Carrack-class light cruiser belonging to the Empire. Along with its sister ship Xerxes and the Victory-class Star Destroyer Vagrant, it attempted to stop the Defiance Task Force from rendezvousing with the Alliance Fleet at Sullust, but were held back by A-wings, two of which were piloted by Olin Garn and Ace Azzameen.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nexus
- **Manufacturer:** Damorian Manufacturing Corporation
- **Model:** Carrack-class light cruiser
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Length:** 350 meters
- **Width:** 72 meters
- **Height:** 75 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
