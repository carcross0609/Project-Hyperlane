# Implacable

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Implacable (Star Destroyer)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Implacable_%28Star_Destroyer%29/Legends)

The Implacable was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer of unknown specification commanded by Imperial Admiral Apwar Trigit, who served first under Ysanne Isard, and then under Warlord Zsinj, in the years following the Battle of Endor. It was destroyed in 7 ABY by Wraith Squadron, in the Battle of Ession.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Implacable
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/LN starfighters
- **Destroyed:** 7 ABY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Ysanne Isard
  - Warlord Zsinj
- **Captains:** Admiral Apwar Trigit
