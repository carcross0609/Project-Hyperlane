# Crynyd

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crynyd](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crynyd)

The Crynyd was the name given to an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer after its capture by the New Republic at a battle in the Milagro system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Crynyd
- **Line:** Imperial-class Star Destroyers
- **Model:** Imperial II-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Destroyed:** 8.5 ABY
- **Affiliation:**

  - New Republic
- **Navy:** Solo Fleet
- **Captains:** Purdy M. Trico
