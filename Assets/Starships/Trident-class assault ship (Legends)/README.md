# Trident-class assault ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trident-class assault ship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trident-class_assault_ship/Legends)

The Trident-class assault ship, more commonly known as the Trident drill assault craft or simply the Trident drill, was a droid drilling gunship used for stealthy, underwater assaults, as used in the Battle of Kamino and the second battle of Mon Calamari during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Trident-class assault ship
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoid Creation Nest
- **Model:** Trident-class assault ship
- **Type:** Gunship
- **Length:** 88.71 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Laser cannons (8)
  - Mechanical arms
  - Drill-spike
- **Complement:** Battle droids
- **Crew:** 4
- **Passengers:** 12 Aqua droids
- **Role:** Transport
- **Firstuse:** 21 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
