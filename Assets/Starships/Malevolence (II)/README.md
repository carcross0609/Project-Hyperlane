# Malevolence

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malevolence (II)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolence_%28II%29)

A rebuilt version of the Malevolence was constructed around 21 BBY, during the Clone Wars, at Pammant Docks. General Grievous supervised the reconstruction of his flagship, which had been destroyed during the Battle of the Kaliida Nebula earlier in the war. However, this vessel was also destroyed by a Jedi Knight.
