# IR-3F-class light frigate

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IR-3F-class light frigate/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-3F-class_light_frigate/Legends)

The IR-3F-class light frigate, commonly referred to as the IR-3F patrol craft or IR-3F, was a light frigate designed by Sienar Fleet Systems for use as a system patrol and customs ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IR-3F-class light frigate
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Type:**
  - Light frigate
  - Patrol craft
- **Cost:**
  - 2,500,000 credits (new)
  - 1,000,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 110 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,000 kph
- **Engine:** Fast sublight engines
- **Hdsystem:** None
- **Shield Gen:**
  - Equipped
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Turbolaser cannon turrets (4) or heavy laser cannons (4; fire-linked)
- **Crew:** 11, including 8 gunners
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Passengers:** 10 troops
- **Capacity:** 180 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 3 months
- **Role:** Inter-system patrol/customs craft
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Bakuran Defense Fleet
