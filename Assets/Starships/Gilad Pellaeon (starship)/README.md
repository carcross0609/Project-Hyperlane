# Gilad Pellaeon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gilad Pellaeon (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gilad_Pellaeon_%28starship%29)

The Gilad Pellaeon was an Imperial Remnant Star Destroyer named after the deceased Supreme Commander and Head of State, Gilad Pellaeon. It was Head of State Jagged Fel's flagship. It was dark grey and larger than most other classes of Star Destroyers (but much smaller than the Super-class Star Destroyer).

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gilad Pellaeon
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Destroyed:** 44 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Remnant
- **Owners:** Jagged Fel
