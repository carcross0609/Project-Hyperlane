# FT-6 Pike

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FT-6 Pike](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FT-6_Pike)

The FT-6 Pike was a starfighter in the FT-series of Galactic Republic starfighters produced by Rendili StarDrive. Similar to other models of the series, the Pike had two wings and three rear engines. The Pike differed from the PT-7 starfighter in that it featured two cannons on each wing, while the PT-7 only had one.
