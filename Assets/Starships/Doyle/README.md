# Doyle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Doyle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doyle)

Doyle was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle used by Habeen researchers at the Mylok IV Conflict during the Galactic Civil War. Shuttle Doyle docked with Habeen containers at their deep-space research facility, rescuing critical hyperdrive technology from an attack by Nharwaak dissidents.
