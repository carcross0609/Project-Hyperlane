# Spider Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spider Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_Cruiser)

The Spider Cruiser, also known as a Spider Squall cruiser, a Spider Squall, or a Squall Spider, was a class of air and ground transport used by the Nihil marauder group during the High Republic Era. In the aftermath of the Great Hyperspace Disaster, one such vessel, the Squall Spider, traveled to Trymant IV under the command of Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro to evacuate Tromak, a member of the Elders of the Path, during an Emergence that threatened the planet.
