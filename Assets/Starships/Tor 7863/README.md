# Tor 7863

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tor 7863](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tor_7863)

Tor 7863 was a bulk freighter that was used by the company Tor-Ro-Bo Industries. At some point, Tor-Ro-Bo hired a group of spacers to smuggle some semi-processed TOL-type ore off of the planet 244Core and transport it to Tor 7863, in breach of a profit-sharing agreement that Tor-Ro-Bo had signed with the other corporations that operated on 244Core.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tor 7863
- **Type:** Bulk freighter
- **Affiliation:** Tor-Ro-Bo Industries
