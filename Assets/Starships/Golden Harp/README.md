# Golden Harp

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Golden Harp](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Harp)

The Golden Harp was a yacht belonging to the enslaver Arsin Crassus, who was one of the main moneylenders to the Empire and was therefore wealthy. The yacht itself was very opulent and large, made with bronze, carmine and gold among other things. It was by no means a fast or powerful ship, having only shields, but was used to transport Crassus in comfort.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Golden Harp
- **Model:** Ryuni-Tantine Vita-Liner
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Destroyed:** 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Owners:** Arsin Crassus
