# Accordance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Accordance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Accordance)

The Accordance was an MC75 Star Cruiser in service to the Rebel Alliance alongside its sister ship, the Temperance. Manufactured by Mon Calamari Shipyards and active during the Galactic Civil War, it was part of the Alliance Navy and defended the borders of the Hosnian system. At the same time as the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY, it fought a battle alongside the Temperance against an Imperial armada that had launched a surprise attack against the Core Worlds. The Accordance was destroyed by Imperial forces during the battle, although its surviving crew was rescued by the Temperance, which then defeated the Imperials.
