# MC30c frigate

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MC30c frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC30c_frigate)

The MC30c frigate, also referred to simply as the MC30C and MC30c, was a class of Mon Calamari frigate that was manufactured by Mon Calamari Shipyards and saw service during the Imperial Era. The frigates were deployed by the Alliance to Restore the Republic's naval forces during the Galactic Civil War against the Galactic Empire, and they were adopted by the New Republic Defense Fleet, the naval branch of the Alliance's successor state, the New Republic.
