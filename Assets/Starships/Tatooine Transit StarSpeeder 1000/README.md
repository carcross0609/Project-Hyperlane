# Tatooine Transit StarSpeeder 1000

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tatooine Transit StarSpeeder 1000](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tatooine_Transit_StarSpeeder_1000)

The Tatooine Transit StarSpeeder 1000 was a modified StarSpeeder 1000 spacecraft used by interstellar travel agency Star Tours to operate flights bound for the Outer Rim planet Tatooine prior to 0 BBY. This starship was painted blue and light grey with the Aurebesh letters Nern and Dorn. It was named after the planet itself as well as Tatooine Transit, a separate spaceliner company from Star Tours, and a fellow member with Star Tours in the Galactic Tourist Bureau.
