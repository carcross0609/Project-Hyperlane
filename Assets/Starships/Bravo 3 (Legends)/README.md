# Bravo 3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bravo 3/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravo_3/Legends)

Bravo 3 was the personal N-1 starfighter of Arven Wendik during the Invasion of Naboo in 32 BBY. At the time of the invasion, it was the oldest starfighter in the Naboo corps. Wendik used the fighter during the Battle of Naboo.

- **Model:** Starfighter
- **Type:** N-1 starfighter
