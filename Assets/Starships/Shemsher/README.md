# Shemsher

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shemsher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shemsher)

The Shemsher was a Nebulon-B escort frigate used by the Ripoblus. It was present at a meeting between the leaders of both factions that was raided by Imperials who intended to capture the leaders of both factions only to kidnap their children instead.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shemsher
- **Type:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Affiliation:** Ripoblus government
