# Authority

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Authority](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Authority)

The Authority was a Star Destroyer in service to the Galactic Empire. It was commanded by Admiral Bethava Rocwyn. In 1 BBY, the Authority captured a group of Partisans in the Five Points system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Authority
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Bethava Rocwyn
