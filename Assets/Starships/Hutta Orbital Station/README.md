# Hutta Orbital Station

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hutta Orbital Station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutta_Orbital_Station)

The Hutta Orbital Station, or Hutta Space Station, was a space station that orbited the planet Nal Hutta during the Cold War and the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. It was the only official way to reach the planet's surface as an Imperial citizen, as visitors to the world were required to dock at the station and take a shuttle down to the Jiguuna Spaceport.
