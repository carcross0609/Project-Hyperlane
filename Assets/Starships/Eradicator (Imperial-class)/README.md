# Eradicator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eradicator (Imperial-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eradicator_%28Imperial-class%29)

The Eradicator was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that served in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. In 0 ABY, it was in the process of impounding a starship near the world of Mon Torri when the Millennium Falcon came out of hyperspace. After the Battle of Endor, Reagal Eron served as the Star Destroyer's chief gunnery officer.
