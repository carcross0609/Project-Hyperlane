# Merciless

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Merciless (Aggressor-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merciless_%28Aggressor-class%29)

The Merciless was Tyber Zann's personal Aggressor-class destroyer, which served as his and the Zann Consortium's flagship. The basis of the Aggressor-class destroyer, it's design plans were found in the ruins of the destroyed first Death Star by the Zann Consortium. After realizing how effective it could be, it was stolen from the Imperial hands and delivered to Tyber Zann. The Merciless was first used by Zann over Carida to great effect, prompting the creation of more Aggressor-class destroyers. After the Empire's defeat over Endor, Zann, along with an accompanying fleet, moved in on Kuat, finding the Rebel fleet attacking the remaining Imperial warships. The Merciless fought against the crumbling Imperial forces along with the Rebels until the Eclipse was captured.
