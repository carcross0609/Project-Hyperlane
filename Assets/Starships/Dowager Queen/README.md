# Dowager Queen

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dowager Queen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dowager_Queen)

The Dowager Queen was an early colony ship that crashed on Tatooine long before the Galactic Civil War. The upended wreckage later became a landmark in the spaceport town of Mos Eisley, located at the intersection of Dune Street and Inner Curved Street.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dowager Queen
- **Type:** Colony ship
