# Z-Pata group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Pata group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Pata_group)

Z-Pata group was a group of three Carrack-class light cruisers that belonged to the rogue Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin during the Zaarin insurrection. They were part of a second attempt at capturing the Vorknkx at its first location after a previous attempt involving the Nebulon-B2 frigate Z-Borus failed. However, despite support involving TIE/D Defenders and Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transports, the cruisers were destroyed.
