# Rimrunner group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rimrunner group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rimrunner_group)

The Rimrunner group was a group of BFF-1 bulk freighters working for the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It was destroyed by Imperial forces during their attack on Organarms.
