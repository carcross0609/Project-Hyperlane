# Tatoo (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tatoo (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tatoo_%28corvette_group%29)

Tatoo was a pair of Imperial Assassin-class corvettes. They were part of Tan Maarek Stele's allotted reinforcements, should he require them for his defense of Imperial convoy Rope 17.
