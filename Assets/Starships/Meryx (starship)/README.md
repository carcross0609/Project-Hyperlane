# Meryx

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Meryx (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meryx_%28starship%29)

The Meryx was a Gozanti-class cruiser owned by the gemstone thief Pax Maripher and co-piloted by Rahara Wick around 40 BBY. Before their partnership, Maripher went through several pilots, none of whom could stand his quirks. Maripher modified the systems, and also installed a custom scanner-blocking field. The Facet, a small starfighter, was stored in the lower cargo hold.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Meryx
- **Model:** Gozanti-class cruiser
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Modifier:** Pax Maripher
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Antiplasma charges
- **Complement:** Facet
- **Othersystems:** Scanner-blocking field
- **Owners:** Pax Maripher
