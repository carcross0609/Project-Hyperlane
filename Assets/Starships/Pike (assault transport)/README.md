# Pike

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pike (assault transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pike_%28assault_transport%29)

Pike was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport used by the Ripoblus to attack the Dimok Science Station Youst. It was destroyed at the Battle of Science Station Youst.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pike
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Length:** 45 meters
- **Width:** 30.4 meters
- **Height:** 20.5 meters
- **Role:** Boarding craft
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Ripoblus government
