# YV-664 light freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YV-664 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YV-664_light_freighter)

The YV-664 light freighter was a model of light freighter produced by the Corellian Engineering Corporation as part of its YV series. The freighter had deflector shields and a balance of cargo capacity, durability, and speed.
