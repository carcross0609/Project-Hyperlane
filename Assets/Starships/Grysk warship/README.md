# Grysk warship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grysk warship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grysk_warship)

A Grysk warship, also known as a Grysk war vessel, was a type of light cruiser used by the Grysk. Two of these warships engaged the Imperial starship Chimaera when they were deployed from a larger Grysk starship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grysk warship
- **Type:** Light cruiser
- **Armament:** Equipped
- **Complement:** Several Grysk tugs
- **Affiliation:** Grysk Hegemony
