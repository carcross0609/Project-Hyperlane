# Renning-class shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Renning-class shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Renning-class_shuttle)

The Renning-class shuttle was a model of shuttle manufactured by Koensayr Manufacturing sometime prior to 233 BBY. The Star Seeker was a Renning-class shuttle piloted by the droid 0G-LC to explore Tenoo's sector, who eventually discovered the planet Fobaris.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Renning-class shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Koensayr Manufacturing
- **Crew:** 1 pilot
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
