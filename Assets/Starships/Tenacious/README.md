# Tenacious

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tenacious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenacious)

The Tenacious was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer in service to the Galactic Empire that was under the command of Captain Adre L'Mendi. After being notified that there was a Rebel Alliance starship above Wobani, Captain L'Mendi and the Tenacious arrived in the Wobani system and destroyed the rebel CR90 corvette Vigilant and engaged Red Flight.
