# Shadow Keeper

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow Keeper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Keeper)

The Shadow Keeper was a rare model of starship, of which only seven were built. The vessels were deadly space combatants, named for the fierce kkekkrrg rro creature native to Kashyyyk. Black Sun Vigo Sprax owned one of the vessels, and it was the most favorite ship he had in his collections.
