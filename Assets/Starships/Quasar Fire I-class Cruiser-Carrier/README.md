# Quasar Fire I-class Cruiser-Carrier

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quasar Fire I-class Cruiser-Carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quasar_Fire_I-class_Cruiser-Carrier)

The Quasar Fire I-class Cruiser-Carrier was a model of Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier used by the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At one point, one such cruiser-carrier fired its offensive weapons in space, while carrying various TIE series starships, including TIE/ln space superiority starfighters.
