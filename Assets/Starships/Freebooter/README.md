# Freebooter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Freebooter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freebooter)

The Freebooter was the second starship owned by smuggler Devon Fuller during his allegiance to the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Fuller gained possession of the Freebooter after his previous ship, a freighter named the Croc of Gold, was destroyed in a clash with Imperial forces over Tashtor Seneca. Sometime before the destruction of the second Death Star in 4 ABY, the Freebooter suffered a similar fate: it was wrecked by a Star Destroyer when the Imperial ship attacked the Ansarra Rebel base. Fuller did not perish in the destruction of the Freebooter, and the Alliance loaned to him a Ghtroc Industries Class 720 freighter called The Solar Flair, so that he could continue to work on their behalf.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Freebooter
- **Destroyed:** By 4 ABY, Ansarra Rebel base
- **Affiliation:** Rebel Alliance
