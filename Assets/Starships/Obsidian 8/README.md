# Obsidian 8

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Obsidian 8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obsidian_8)

Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems, Obsidian 8 was a modified TIE/ln starfighter serving the Galactic Empire's Obsidian Squadron by the year 3 ABY. Obsidian 8 was operated by OS-72-8, a TIE pilot who was previously stationed aboard the Star Destroyer Devastator. Obsidian 8 was used most often to pursue raiders attempting to take refuge in the upper atmosphere of nearby planets.
