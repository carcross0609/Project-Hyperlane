# Heavy Y-wing

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy Y-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Y-wing)

The Heavy Y-wing was a larger scale BTL Y-wing bomber utilized by the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It possessed four engine pods rather than just two. Four of the Heavy Y-wings were deployed in the Seizure of Coruscant as part of New Republic General Han Solo's strike force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy Y-wing
- **Line:** BTL Y-wing starfighter
- **Model:** Heavy Y-wing
- **Type:** Bomber
- **Engine:** 4
- **Armament:** Proton bombs
- **Role:** Bomber
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
