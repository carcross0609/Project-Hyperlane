# Constant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Constant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Constant)

Constant was a colony ship that, along with Mother Vima, served as a support starship for the colony ship Beneficent Tasia. In approximately 3900 BBY, the three ships departed the planet Grizmallt in an expedition to the dangerous southern quadrant of the galaxy. The ships carried refugees fleeing a brutal civil war who sought fortune and glory. The expedition, led by the scout Kwilaan, was sponsored personally by Grizmallt's Queen Elsinoré den Tasia and was blessed by a famous Jedi Master.
