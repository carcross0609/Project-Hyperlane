# Tector-class

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tector-class](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tector-class)

The Tector-class was a class of Star Destroyer in the service of the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. It notably differed from other Star Destroyer classes, as it lacked a ventral hangar bay. In 4 ABY, one Tector-class vessel, Harbinger, was part of the Galactic Empire's fleet defending the second Death Star over the Forest Moon of Endor.
