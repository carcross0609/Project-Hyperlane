# Starcat

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Starcat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starcat)

The Starcat was a light haulcraft. In 10 BBY, when the criminal Jaylen Vrax and his BX commando droid ND-5 were searching for a pilot for an upcoming heist on the astronomical object Andara, ND-5 contacted the Starcat pilot, Kreden Varec, to see if she would be available and noted her affiliation with the starship to Vrax. However, Varec's schedule had filled up, and ND-5 continued searching for a pilot.
