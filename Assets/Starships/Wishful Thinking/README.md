# Wishful Thinking

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wishful Thinking](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wishful_Thinking)

The Wishful Thinking was a T-1 shuttle utilized by the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. The shuttle was one of the starships in the care of the Jedi quartermaster Nubarron aboard the Starlight Beacon. Nubarron loaned the Wishful Thinking to the Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh and her Padawan, Imri Cantaros; despite Rwoh's history of crashing vessels borrowed from Nubarron, she promised that her Padawan would do a majority of the flying. The two used the shuttle to travel to Kirima so that Rwoh could train Cantaros. While there, they were dispatched to Port Haileap on the planet Haileap to respond to a possible Nihil attack.
