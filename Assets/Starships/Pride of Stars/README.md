# Pride of Stars

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pride of Stars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pride_of_Stars)

The Pride of Stars was a starship in the service of the Galactic Empire during its early years. It was commanded by the decorated Imperial Navy captain Ran Niall, who implicitly trusted his bridge crew.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pride of Stars
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:** Ran Niall
