# YC-123B transport hauler

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YC-123B transport hauler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YC-123B_transport_hauler)

The YC-123B transport hauler was a model of transport manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. A troop transport, the YC-123B transport hauler could deploy up to 120 passengers onto the battlefield via a large carrying compartment under the cockpit. It was armed with two CEC FP-35 forward heavy laser cannons, one on each side of the ship, as well as a CEC AG-3M triple laser cannon turret aft of the cockpit. The Resistance owned one such ship, the Fortitude, which was used during the battle of Exegol and helped in the destruction of the Steadfast.
