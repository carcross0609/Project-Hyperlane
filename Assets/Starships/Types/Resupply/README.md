# Resupply

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Resupply](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resupply)

The Empire employed and retrofitted Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport Resupply to reload starfighters should they run out of warheads in battle. Resupply was present during the Capture and Defense of ZA-13 where the plant was being returned to Imperial control against rogue Grand Admiral Zaarin's insurrection and when the factory was supposed to be receiving upgrades and was attacked by a Rebel strike force during the process. The transport would then show up again at the operation to capture RS-32 to secure Mag Pulse warheads.
