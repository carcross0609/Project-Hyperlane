# ST-70 M-111 Razor Crest series

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ST-70 M-111 Razor Crest series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ST-70_M-111_Razor_Crest_series)

The ST-70 M-111 Razor Crest series, also known as the ST-70 assault ship, ST-70 Razor Crest assault ship or simply as the ST-70 class, was a class of gunship manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. Ships of the class were sometimes informally referred to as a "Razor Crest". Its usage predated the rise of the Galactic Empire, when they were used for local military patrols. One starship of this class was the similarly-named Razor Crest, also designated M-111, the starship used by the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin during the New Republic Era in 9 ABY.
