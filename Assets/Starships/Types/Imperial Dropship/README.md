# Imperial Dropship

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Imperial Dropship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Dropship)

The Imperial Dropship was a vessel of the Galactic Empire. The starship was unarmed, with the rear featuring a detatchable troop transport platform that carried a couple of stormtroopers. The dropship's front had a windowed cockpit that housed a single shadow trooper pilot. Each side of the craft contained two holders for blasters or other equipment. Dark blue, grey, and black in color, the dropship's detatchable stormtrooper platform featured the Imperial crest on each side. The top of the vessel also featured the crest.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial Dropship
- **Type:** Dropship
- **Complement:** Detatchable troop transport platform
- **Crew:** Shadow trooper pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** Stormtroopers (2)
- **Role:** Troop transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
