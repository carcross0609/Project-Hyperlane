# Zeta-class shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zeta-class shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeta-class_shuttle)

The Zeta-class shuttle was a class of shuttle used by the Galactic Empire, including Imperial governors and the Jedi hunting Inquisitorius, during its reign. Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems and Telgorn Corporation, the Zeta-class shuttle was based on the Sentinel-class craft. The Inquisitorius, which was founded in 19 BBY, used the shuttle class during its early missions and for transporting high-ranking Imperial members. Zeta-class shuttles were present in the Battle of Dac City in 19 BBY and the hunt for former Jedi Master Eeth Koth in 14 BBY.
