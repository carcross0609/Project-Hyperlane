# WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WD-2550_Whydah-class_Yield_Conveyer)

The WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer was a model of freighter utilized by the Trade Federation during the High Republic Era. One such freighter, the Fallon, was commanded by Captain Blex.

## Specifications

- **Name:** WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer
- **Manufacturer:** Hoersch-Kessel Drive, Inc.
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Length:** 259.97 meters (850 ft 6 in)
- **Width:** 77.54 meters (254 ft 4 in)
- **Height:** 91.48 meters (300 ft)
- **Mglt:** 85 MGLT
- **Max Speed:** 975 kph (606 mph)
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 2 forward defensive laser cannons
- **Affiliation:** Trade Federation
