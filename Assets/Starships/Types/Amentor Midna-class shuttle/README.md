# Amentor Midna-class shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Amentor Midna-class shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amentor_Midna-class_shuttle)

The Amentor Midna-class shuttle was a class of shuttle. One such shuttle was owned by the criminal syndicate Crimson Dawn during the Imperial Era. In 10 BBY, the shuttle was parked at a spaceport on Sern Prime to pick up Crimson Dawn's accountant Madel Nureth and transport her to Botor. The criminal Jaylen Vrax and his BX commando droid ND-5 were following her through the spaceport and arrived to see the shuttle, but the two were incapacitated by Crimson Dawn and ND-5 was taken on board the shuttle before they took off.
