# TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/ba_Baron_Space_Superiority_Interceptor)

The TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor, also referred to as the TIE/ba interceptor, TIE Baron, or TIE Interceptor and TIE/in was a model of TIE interceptor deployed by the First Order. One ship was used by Major Elrik Vonreg during the Cold War in his encounters with the New Republic and the Resistance.
