# ISB TAC shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ISB TAC shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ISB_TAC_shuttle)

The ISB TAC shuttle, also known as the ISB SWAT Shuttle or ISB SWAT ship, was a type of shuttle used by the Galactic Empire's ISB Tactical task force for their missions. In 1 BBY, a TAC shuttle designated TAC 171 was used by the Imperial Security Bureau during a raid on Tomkip Towers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ISB TAC shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Armament:** 2 forward-mounted cannons
- **Crew:** ISB pilots (2)
- **Passengers:** ISB tactical agents (16)
- **Role:**
  - Tactical troop transport
  - SWAT team transport
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   ISB Tactical
