# Commerce Guild corvette

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Commerce Guild corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commerce_Guild_corvette)

The Commerce Guild corvette was a type of corvette utilized by the Commerce Guild and later the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic. In that time, which saw the Guild side with the Confederacy, the corporation's corvettes were utilized alongside Guild bombers, Diamond-class cruisers and Recusant-class light destroyers to defend strategic worlds located off the Perlemian Trade Route, such as Castell, Escarte, Felucia, Mooga, and Minntooine, which also served as sites for major Commerce Guild interests as well as Separatist strongholds.
