# K-wing

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [K-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-wing)

The K-wing was a model of starfighter in use. The hull of the fighter craft consisted of two large horizontal wings attached to the main hull, with a smaller diagonal wing on the each underside. It was propelled by three sublight engines, one per wing and a third fixed atop the rear of the starfighter's hull. The front of the fighter consisted of two cockpits, and the fighter was armed with a large ventral turret armed with two dual cannons, and a smaller dorsal turret. The fighter could also carry bombs and ordinances under each wing.

## Specifications

- **Name:** K-wing
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Engine:**
  - Sublight engines (3)
  - Advanced SLAM
- **Armament:**
  - 1 ventral dual twin laser cannon turret
  - 1 dorsal dual laser cannon turret
  - Multiple bombs and ordinances
