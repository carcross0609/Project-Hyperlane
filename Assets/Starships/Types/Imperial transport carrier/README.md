# Imperial transport carrier

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial transport carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_transport_carrier)

Imperial transport carriers were used by the Galactic Empire as it built an armory in the center of Palmo, the capital of Ghorman. They were repulsorlift-powered transports used to carry construction materials through Palmo's avenues.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial transport carrier
- **Role:** Transporting cargo
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
