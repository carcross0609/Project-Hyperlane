# Gladiator I

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gladiator I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gladiator_I)

The Gladiator I was a member of the Gladiator-class Star Destroyer line. The ships were originally designed as escorts, but were used by the Galactic Empire primarily as patrol craft. One Gladiator I, the Blood Ambition, fought in the Battle of Xorrn.
