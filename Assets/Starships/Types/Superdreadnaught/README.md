# Superdreadnaught

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Superdreadnaught](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Superdreadnaught)

A superdreadnaught was a class of starship that was used around the time of the Great Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. The Star of Coruscant was considered a superdreadnaught. The term was also utilized around the time of the Galactic Empire as well as the Galactic Civil War &ndash; in particular with the Eye of Palpatine.
