# Heavy Lifter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heavy Lifter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Lifter)

The Heavy Lifter was a tug produced by Patel Ironwerks. It was over 55 meters in length, significantly larger than the Nicholas/Gordon Spacewerks Utility Tug, and was equipped with a hyperdrive.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy Lifter
- **Manufacturer:** Patel Ironwerks
- **Length:** Over 55 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
