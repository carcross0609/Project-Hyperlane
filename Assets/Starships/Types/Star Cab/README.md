# Star Cab

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Star Cab](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Cab)

The Orlean Star Cab was an exploration vessel considered ancient by the time of the early years of the New Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Star Cab
- **Manufacturer:** Orlean
- **Length:** 70 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,050 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 10
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Astromech droid
- **Armament:** 4 blaster cannons
- **Crew:** 4 (1 Human, 3 droids)
- **Passengers:** 11
- **Capacity:**
  - 100 metric tons
  - 40 cubic meters
- **Consumables:** 5 years
- **Role:** Exploration vessel
