# TT4472

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TT4472](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TT4472)

TT4472 was one of three Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports employed by the Rebel Alliance, along with a Corellian corvette Hart, to extract officers from the frigate Anvil which had been severely damaged and needed to be abandoned. 3 X-wings, one of which piloted by Keyan Farlander, provided support and cover for the convoy of ships to escape to Hyperspace. Farlander then scuttled the frigate to prevent its capture.
