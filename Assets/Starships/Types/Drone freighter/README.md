# Drone freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Drone freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drone_freighter)

The drone freighter was a type of unmanned freighter used by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Drone freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Equipped
- **Bays:** At least 1 docking bay
- **Escapepods:** At least 3 escape pods
- **Crew:** None required
- **Skeleton:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Gunner (1)
- **Lifesupport:** Equipped
- **Othersystems:** Command relay
- **Role:** Unmanned freighter
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
