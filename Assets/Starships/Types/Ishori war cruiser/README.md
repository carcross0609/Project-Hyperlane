# Ishori war cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ishori war cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ishori_war_cruiser)

The Ishori war cruiser, also known as the Ishori War Cruiser, was a type of cruiser. One such cruiser, the Predominance, commanded by Captain Av'muru was used by the Ishori Confederene. In 19 ABY, during the Caamas Document Crisis, the Predominance was one of the 200 warships over the Outer Rim Territories planet Bothawui. The cruiser used its engines to approach several Sif'krie skiffs and on its way, brought the YT-1300 light freighter Millennium Falcon aboard with its tractor beam while Av'muru spoke with High Councilor Leia Organa Solo, who was aboard the freighter, through comm.
