# TIE training drone

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE training drone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE_training_drone)

TIE training drones were TIE series drone starfighters which were used for training the Galactic Empire's fighter pilots. The 204th Imperial Fighter Wing deployed several TIE training drones while aboard the Edict.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TIE training drone
- **Line:** TIE series
- **Type:** Drone
- **Role:** Training
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Starfighter Corps
