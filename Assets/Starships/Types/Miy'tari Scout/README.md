# Miy'tari Scout

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Miy'tari Scout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miy%27tari_Scout)

The Miy'tari Scout was a Hapan starfighter model. They had capacity for a pilot and a passenger and also an astromech droid. They were equipped with deflector shields, ion drives and vector plates and were armed with concussion missiles and laser cannons. In 45 ABY, the Jedi Knights Ben Skywalker and Tahiri Veila, accompanied by the R9-series astromech droid Ninette, flew a Miy'tari Scout to the Chiloon Rift nebula while searching for the missing Quest Knight Ohali Soroc.
