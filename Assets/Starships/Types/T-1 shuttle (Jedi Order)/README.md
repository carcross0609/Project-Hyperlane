# T-1 shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-1 shuttle (Jedi Order)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-1_shuttle_%28Jedi_Order%29)

The T-1 shuttle was a model of shuttle utilized by the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. When the Jedi responded to the the Eiram–E'ronoh crisis, the Jedi Masters Laret Soveral and Simmix and their Padawans, Orla Jareni and Cohmac Vitus, took a T-1 shuttle to the Eiram system. Upon arriving at the lost moon, where the rulers of the planets Eiram and E'ronoh had been kidnapped by the Directorate, their vessel crashed, leaving Simmix dead.
