# Fortitude-class assault shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fortitude-class assault shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fortitude-class_assault_shuttle)

The Fortitude-class assault shuttle, also known as the Republic Transport Shuttle, was a shuttle utilized by the Galactic Republic during the Cold War and Galactic War with the Sith Empire. The Fortitude-class was largely identical to the Rendaran-class assault shuttle, its predecessor from the Great Galactic War, though the Fortitude-class was slightly smaller. It carried individuals between Republic orbital stations and planet surfaces.
