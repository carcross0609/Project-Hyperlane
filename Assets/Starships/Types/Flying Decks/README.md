# Flying Decks

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flying Decks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Decks)

Flying Decks was the nickname given to the ancient transport ships used by the Krath to move their shocktroops from planet to planet. The wings and main engines were at the front of the T-shaped ship.
