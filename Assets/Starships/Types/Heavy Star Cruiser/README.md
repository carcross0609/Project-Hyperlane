# Heavy Star Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heavy Star Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Star_Cruiser)

A Heavy Star Cruiser was a type of Star Cruiser classification used by the Mon Calamari species in their warships. The MC80 Star Cruiser line, specifically the MC80 Liberty Type and the MC80A Home One Type were classified as Heavy Star Cruisers by the Alliance to Restore the Republic. The MC85 Star Cruiser, although a heavy cruiser, was not designated as a Heavy Star Cruiser.
