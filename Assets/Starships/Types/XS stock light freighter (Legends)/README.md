# XS stock light freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XS stock light freighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XS_stock_light_freighter/Legends)

The XS stock light freighter was a model of freighter used mostly by smugglers during the Great Galactic War and its resultant Cold War. One is used by Voidhound.

## Specifications

- **Name:** XS stock light freighter
- **Designer:** Bravis Halcyon
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Length:** 101 meters
- **Width:** 88 meters
- **Height:** 27 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 2 Soniver Arms HSG-67 laser cannons
  - Missile batteries
- **Escapepods:** 1
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Availability:** General civilians
- **Role:** Freighter
