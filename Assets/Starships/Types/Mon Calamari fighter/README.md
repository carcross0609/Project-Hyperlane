# Mon Calamari fighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mon Calamari fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Calamari_fighter)

The Mon Calamari fighter was a class of starfighter produced by the Mon Calamari and was active in the New Republic Era during the war between the First Order and the Resistance. During a Resistance mission to Mon Cala, General Nossor Ri used one such starfighter to distract the First Order and allowed the Mon Cala fleet to exit the planet and after a brief encounter, escape the Mon Calamari system. The starfighter was crewed by at least one pilot, was armed with at least two laser cannons, and had at least one engine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mon Calamari fighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Armament:** At least 2 laser cannons
- **Crew:** At least 1 pilot
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Mon Cala fleet
