# Chiss destroyer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chiss destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss_destroyer)

A class of destroyer was utilized by the Unknown Regions Chiss Ascendancy nation's Expansionary Defense Fleet. Chiss destroyers were equipped with engines and a hyperdrive, and possessed electrostatic barriers for shields. Its weaponry included spectrum lasers, plasma spheres and breacher missiles and the hull was constructed of nyix-alloy. In 19 BBY, several of these ships participated in a battle over the Vak Combine nation planet of Primea against the Nikardun Destiny hegemony as part of Admiral Ar'alani's Picket Force Six.
