# DP20 gunship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DP20 gunship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DP20_gunship/Legends)

The DP20 gunship or DP20 frigate, commonly called the Corellian gunship, was one of the few dedicated warship designs produced by Corellian Engineering Corporation, and was the most common member of the Corellian gunship product line. Though small and compact, it was fast and carried heavy firepower for a ship of its size and class.
