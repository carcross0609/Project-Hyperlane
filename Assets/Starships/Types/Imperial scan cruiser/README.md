# Imperial scan cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial scan cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_scan_cruiser)

The Imperial scan cruiser was a model of Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser deployed by the Galactic Empire. In 4 BBY, one scan cruiser visited the agricultural world Mina-Rau to assist in performing the planetary audit.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial scan cruiser
- **Model:** Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
