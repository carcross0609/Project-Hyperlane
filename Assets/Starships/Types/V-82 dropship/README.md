# V-82 dropship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V-82 dropship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-82_dropship)

The V-82 dropship, also known as the Imperial Troop Transport (ITT) was a transport model produced by Ubrikkian Industries and Sienar Fleet Systems. A troop transport like the Ubrikkian-made K79-S80 and K79-H20 Imperial troop transports, also both designated as ITTs, the V-82 was flight-capable. It was used by the Galactic Empire and Moff Gideon's Imperial remnant during the Imperial and New Republic Eras, and could be modified to transport stormtroopers and heavy weaponry to battle zones or imperial prisoners to the prison where they would serve their sentence.
