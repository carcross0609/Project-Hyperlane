# Assault frigate

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Assault frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_frigate)

Assault frigates were a type of starship used by the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. In 5 ABY, an assault frigate, the Redeemer, was in service to the New Republic Defense Fleet and took part in the Battle of Jakku.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Assault frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Affiliation:**
  - New Republic
  -   New Republic Defense Fleet
