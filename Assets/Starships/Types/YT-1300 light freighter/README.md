# YT-1300 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YT-1300 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-1300_light_freighter)

The YT-1300 light freighter, also known as the YT-1300 Corellian freighter, the Corellian YT-1300 transport or simply as the YT-1300 transport was a type of light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation that saw operation in the galaxy during the final days of the Galactic Republic and the reign of the Galactic Empire. By the year 1 BBY, it was considered an outdated model. The Millennium Falcon, a smuggling vessel that became part of the Rebel Alliance fleet, was a heavily modified YT-1300 of the YT-1300f variety.
