# Unidentified Crimson Dawn transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Crimson Dawn transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Crimson_Dawn_transport)

Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, a model of transport was used by the criminal syndicate Crimson Dawn to transport soldiers to the Amaxine Station during their battle with the Galactic Empire at the station. The transport look similar to the V-82 dropship, but deploy soldiers from the side. All of the transports were destroyed by TIE/IN interceptors that escorted the shuttle that brought Darth Sidious and Darth Vader to the station to potentially confront an ancient Sith Lord that had supposedly been sealed away by the Fermata Cage.
