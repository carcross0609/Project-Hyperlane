# Reload

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reload](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reload)

Reload was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport used by the Galactic Empire during the Battle on the Frontier campaign. During the campaign, then, Vice Admiral Thrawn's wanted to inspect Platform NL-1, which had its construction recently completed. He was in another transport when he came under attack by Rebel forces. He was protected by TIE Bombers armed with Advanced concussion missiles, one of which was piloted by Maarek Stele. The transport was present to reload Stele's Bomber with more missiles if he needed them.
