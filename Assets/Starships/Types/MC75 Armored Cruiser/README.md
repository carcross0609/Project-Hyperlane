# MC75 Armored Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MC75 Armored Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC75_Armored_Cruiser)

The MC75 Armored Cruiser, a variant of the MC75 Star Cruiser, was operated by the Rebel Alliance Navy. The Star Cruiser had a bulbous hull, a dorsal mast, a longer ventral mast, and at least four engines at the stern. At one point, an MC75 Armored Cruiser in Alliance service was present above a teal astronomical object.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MC75 Armored Cruiser

- **Type:** Star Cruiser
- **Engine:** At least 4
