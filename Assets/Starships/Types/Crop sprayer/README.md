# Crop sprayer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crop sprayer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crop_sprayer)

Crop sprayers were starships used to dress barq fields after a harvest. The Grand Army of the Republic's Special Operations Brigade used a Nar Shaddaa agri-utility crop sprayer to transport a group of clone commandos from the planet Ord Mantell to the planet Qiilura.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Crop sprayer
- **Type:** Crop sprayer
- **Engine:** Sublight drives
- **Role:** Dress barq fields after a harvest
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Special Operations Brigade
