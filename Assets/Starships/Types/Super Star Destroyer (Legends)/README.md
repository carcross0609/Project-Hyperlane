# Super Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Super Star Destroyer/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Star_Destroyer/Legends)

Super Star Destroyer (SSD) was a term used by Imperial, Rebel, New Republic and Galactic Alliance personnel for many dagger-shaped warship classes larger than Star Destroyers, ranging from Star Cruiser to Star Dreadnought models.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Super Star Destroyer
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Fondor Shipyards
  - Kuat Drive Yards
  - Loronar Corporation
  - Rendili
- **Type:**
  - Battlecruiser (in the Anaxes War College System)
  - Dreadnaught (in the Anaxes War College System)
  - Star Cruiser
  - Star Dreadnought
- **Length:**
  - Known models:
  - 15,000 meters
  - 17,500 meters
  - 19,000 meters
- **Engine:** 13 colossal engine thrusters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped on all known models
- **Armament:**
  - Over 5,000 turbolasers and ion cannons
  - Tractor beam projectors
  - Individual designs also utilized either assault concussion missile launchers, Gravity well projectors or superlaser cannons
- **Role:** Command ship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  - New Republic
  - Warlords
  - Imperial Remnant
  - Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
