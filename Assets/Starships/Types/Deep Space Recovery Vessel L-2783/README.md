# Deep Space Recovery Vessel L-2783

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Deep Space Recovery Vessel L-2783](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deep_Space_Recovery_Vessel_L-2783)

The Deep Space Recovery Vessel L-2783, or simply the L-2783, was a class of bulk cruiser manufactured by Corellian Engineering Corporation that was designed to travel to remote locations to repair or recover damaged starships. At least one L-2783 was owned by the Alliance to Restore the Republic, which served as the flagship of a small flotilla of starfighters and corvettes.
