# DF-11 Scourge

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DF-11 Scourge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DF-11_Scourge)

The DF-11 Scourge was a starfighter used by the Ashiga Clan, Crimson Dawn, the Hutt Clan, the Pyke Syndicate and pirates during the Imperial Era. The DF-11 was armed with two laser cannons and two large sublight engines.
