# D5-Mantis Patrol Craft

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D5-Mantis Patrol Craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D5-Mantis_Patrol_Craft)

The D5-Mantis Patrol Craft was a rare, top-of-the-line starship during the Cold War. It was meant to meet the demands of larger capital ships. One was used by the Hunter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** D5-Mantis Patrol Craft
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Patrol craft
- **Length:** 68 meters
- **Width:** 74 meters
- **Height:** 58 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 3 retractable weapons foils
  - 1 forward cannon
- **Availability:** Rare
- **Role:**
  - Patrol
  - Prisoner transport
- **Affiliation:** Bounty hunters
