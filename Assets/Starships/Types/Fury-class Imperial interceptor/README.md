# Fury-class Imperial interceptor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fury-class Imperial interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fury-class_Imperial_interceptor)

The Fury-class Imperial Interceptor was a starship used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War and its resultant Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fury-class Imperial interceptor
- **Designer:**
  - Darth Mekhis
  - Rycus Kilran
- **Manufacturer:** Sith Empire
- **Type:** Interceptor
- **Length:** 100 meters
- **Width:** 88 meters
- **Height:** 33 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 4 Enmity-class Precision Laser Cannons
  - Heavy Seeker Missiles
- **Passengers:** 15
- **Availability:** Sith
- **Role:** Interceptor and personal transport
- **Firstuse:** Before 3681 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sith Empire
  - Eternal Alliance
