# Maelstrom-class battle cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maelstrom-class battle cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maelstrom-class_battle_cruiser)

The Maelstrom-class battle cruiser was a class of battle cruiser in the proto&ndash;Star Destroyer family of starships manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards. The vessel was armed with heavy cannons, assault concussion missile launchers, and other weaponry. It was capable of serving planetary siege and bombardment duties, as well as fleet command, carrier duties, and anti&ndash;space station operations.
