# Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nantex-class_territorial_defense_starfighter)

The Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter, also known as the Geonosian starfighter or more simply as the Nantex-class starfighter, was a needle-shaped starfighter manufactured by Huppla Pasa Tisc Shipwrights Collective and used by the natives of Geonosis. Many saw service during the Clone Wars to defend the droid factories of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.
