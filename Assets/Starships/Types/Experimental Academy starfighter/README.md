# Experimental Academy starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Experimental Academy starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Experimental_Academy_starfighter)

The experimental Academy starfighter was a prototype starfighter developed at the Corulag Academy in the last decades of the Galactic Republic. It greatly resembled the Z-95 Headhunter, and even more so the later T-65 X-wing starfighter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Experimental Academy starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Affiliation:** Corulag Academy
