# Cronian battlebird

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cronian battlebird](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cronian_battlebird)

The Cronian battlebird was a warship model that was deemed ancient by the time of the First Order-Resistance War. In 35 ABY, a restored battlebird joined the fleet assembled in response to General Lando Calrissian's call to action against the Sith Eternal forces, and then partook in the Battle of Exegol.

- **Name:** Cronian battlebird
