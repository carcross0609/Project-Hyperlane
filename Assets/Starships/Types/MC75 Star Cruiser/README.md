# MC75 Star Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MC75 Star Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC75_Star_Cruiser)

The MC75 Star Cruiser, also referred to as the MC75 modified star cruiser or the MC75 Mon Calamari cruiser, was a model of Star Cruiser in the service of the Alliance Fleet. One such ship, the Profundity, served as Admiral Raddus's flagship during the pivotal Battle of Scarif. Other examples included the Aspiration, the Accordance, the Silver Coronet, and the Temperance.
