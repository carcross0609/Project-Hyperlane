# ZH-40 Tribune-class light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ZH-40 Tribune-class light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZH-40_Tribune-class_light_freighter)

The ZH-40 Tribune-class light freighter was a model of light freighter produced by Starfeld Industries, and a newer model of the ZH-25 Questor-class. During the Imperial Era, these freighters were used by the crime syndicates Crimson Dawn and Zerek Besh. The pirate Hondo Ohnaka owned a modified ZH-40, the Katooni, at Black Spire Outpost on Batuu. At least one ZH-40 was part of the Citizens' Fleet that fought at the Battle of Exegol in 35 ABY.
