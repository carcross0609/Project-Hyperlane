# T-65A2 X-wing starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-65A2 X-wing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-65A2_X-wing_starfighter)

The T-65A2 X-wing was a variant of the T-65 X-wing. The starfighter was flown by pilots of Twin Suns Squadron during the war against the Yuuzhan Vong.
