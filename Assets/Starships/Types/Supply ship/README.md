# Supply ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Supply ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supply_ship)

]] A supply ship was a type of starship that served as a type of support vessel. The Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel was a class of supply ship used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. The Spectres rebel cell conducted a series of raiding missions on Imperial supply ships during the early rebellion against the Galactic Empire that bolstered the young rebel cause and resulted in losses amongst the Empire's supply lines.
