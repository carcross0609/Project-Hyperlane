# Luxury-class shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Luxury-class shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luxury-class_shuttle)

Luxury-class shuttles were civilian passenger shuttles derived from the military Lambda-class T-4a shuttle. Many such shuttles were employed by Tinta Lines. They were equipped with the Astronav P127 Course Plotter and Chepat Supreme Defender deflector shields. Tinta Blue Seven was one such shuttle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Luxury-class shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Shield Gen:** Chempat Supreme Defender deflector shields
- **Navigation:** Astronav P127 Course Plotter
- **Affiliation:** Tinta Lines
