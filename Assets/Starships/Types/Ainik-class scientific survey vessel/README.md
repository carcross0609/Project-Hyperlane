# Ainik-class scientific survey vessel

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ainik-class scientific survey vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ainik-class_scientific_survey_vessel)

The Ainik-class scientific survey vessel was a science research starship. The Alliance to Restore the Republic had these vessels in their naval forces.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ainik-class scientific survey vessel
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Survey vessel
- **Engine:** Engines
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 3 (Primary)
  - Class 12 (Backup)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** Deflector shield generator
- **Hull:**
  - Armored hull
  - Transparisteel
- **Sensor:** Extreme-ranged sensor
- **Navigation:** Navigation computer
- **Armament:**
  - Quad laser cannon turrets (2) (Dorsal, ventral)
  - Forward-mounted light tractor beam projector
- **Complement:** 2 Speeder bikes or 1 landspeeder
- **Crew:** 4 (1 pilot, 1 co-pilot, 2 engineers/scientists)
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 150
- **Consumables:** 6 months
- **Role:** Research
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Rebel Alliance Navy
