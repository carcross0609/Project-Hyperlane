# Chiss shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chiss shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss_shuttle)

The Chiss shuttle, also known as a Chiss courier ship, was a type of shuttle utilized by the Chiss. During a battle against Grysk forces, a Chiss shuttle was present and escaped, although not before Darth Vader and Grand Admiral Thrawn saw it. After the battle, Vader confronted Thrawn about the shuttle and questioned his loyalty to the Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Chiss shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Bays:** None
- **Crew:** At least 1 pilot and 1 navigator
- **Role:**
  - Shuttle
  - Courier ship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Chiss Ascendancy
  -   Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet
