# BTL-A4 LP

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BTL-A4 LP/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BTL-A4_LP/Legends)

The BTL-A4 LP, nicknamed the Longprobe, was a variant of the standard BTL Y-wing starfighter used as a reconnaissance and scouting fighter by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BTL-A4 LP
- **Manufacturer:** Koensayr Manufacturing
- **Line:** BTL Y-wing starfighters
- **Model:** BTL-A4 LP "Longprobe"
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Cost:**
  - 185,000 credits (new)
  - 90,000 credits (used)
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 1.0
  - Backup Class 12
- **Armament:**
  - Laser cannons (2)
  - Ion cannons (2)
  - Proton torpedo launchers (2)
  -   8 torpedoes total
- **Crew:**
  - 1 pilot plus astromech droid
- **Passengers:** None
- **Cargohandling:** 220 kilograms
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Availability:** Military
- **Role:**
  - Long-range reconnaissance
  - Bomber
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
