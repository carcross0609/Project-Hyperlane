# Ajuur-class heavy cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ajuur-class heavy cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ajuur-class_heavy_cruiser)

The Ajuur-class heavy cruiser was a model of cruiser that was used during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Ajuur-class cruisers were a favored starship of the Hutt Cartel, and dozens of the cruisers were used during the Cartel's annexation of neutral star systems. Thirty Ajuur-class cruisers were used to blockade the Makeb system when the Cartel moved to conquer the planet Makeb.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Cruiser
