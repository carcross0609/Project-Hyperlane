# Mediator-class battle cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mediator-class battle cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mediator-class_battle_cruiser)

The Mediator-class battle cruiser was an advanced warship developed by the Mon Calamari for use by the New Republic Defense Fleet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mediator-class battle cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Mon Calamari Shipyards
- **Line:** Mon Calamari Star Cruisers
- **Model:** Mediator-class battle cruiser
- **Type:** Battle cruiser
- **Length:** Over half the size of the prototype Viscount (>1,500 meters)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Hull:** Strong enough to protect reactor and thrusters from internal nuclear detonation
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolasers
  - Ion cannons
  - Tractor beam projectors
- **Complement:** Several starfighter squadrons
- **Role:** Battlecruiser
- **Affiliation:**
  - New Republic
  - Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
