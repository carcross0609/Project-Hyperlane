# Urmenung 300

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Urmenung 300](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Urmenung_300)

The Urmenung 300, also known as a Urmi, was a heavily armed and armored yacht that was popular among people who liked their starships to be a combination of security and luxury. It had a three-pods-on-a-disk configuration. Luewet Wuul owned one and used it to meet Grand Master Luke Skywalker on Crossing Lanes Station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Urmenung 300
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Yacht
