# TIE/sr starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE/sr starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/sr_starfighter)

The TIE/sr starfighter, or TIE scout, was a limited production TIE Series starfighter used by the Galactic Empire as a scout and light reconnaissance vessel. It was one of the many TIE variants in service during the Galactic Civil War.
