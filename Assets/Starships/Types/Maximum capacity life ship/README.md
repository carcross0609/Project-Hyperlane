# Maximum capacity life ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Maximum capacity life ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maximum_capacity_life_ship)

The Maximum capacity life ship was an armed escape pod designed by Faberstien-Lago and installed on the Corellian Engineering Corporation CR90 corvette.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Maximum capacity life ship
- **Manufacturer:** Faberstien-Lago
