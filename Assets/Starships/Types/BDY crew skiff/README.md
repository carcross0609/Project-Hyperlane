# BDY crew skiff

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BDY crew skiff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BDY_crew_skiff)

The BDY crew skiff was a skiff manufactured by Byblos Drive Yards capable of travel in space. The BDY skiff, also classed as a shuttle, was capable of holding at least twenty-four beings. At least three BDY skiffs were pirated by 44 ABY by the Lost Tribe of Sith's armada. The skiffs were carried in an S18 light freighter and used in the ambush of Lando Calrissian's Rockhound.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BDY crew skiff
- **Manufacturer:** Byblos Drive Yards
- **Type:** Skiff
- **Passengers:** At least 24
- **Role:** Shuttle
- **Affiliation:** Sith armada
