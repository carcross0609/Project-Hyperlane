# Sailfish

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sailfish](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sailfish)

A Sailfish was a type of small transport produced by the Mon Calamari Shipyards. During the Swarm War, Squib war profiteers Emala, Grees and Sligh performed their business from a ship of this class, the Real Deal. Han later arranged for the smugglers Jae Juun and Tarfang to have a Sailfish.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sailfish
- **Manufacturer:** Mon Calamari Shipyards
