# Sabaoth starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sabaoth starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sabaoth_starfighter)

The Sabaoth starfighter was a starfighter used by Captain Cavik Toth's Sabaoth Squadron during the years leading up to the Clone Wars. They carried a total of four laser cannons and were piloted by one individual. Sabaoth starfighters saw extensive action during the Occupation of Karthakk.
