# H-22A D-wing heavy attack boat

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [H-22A D-wing heavy attack boat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H-22A_D-wing_heavy_attack_boat)

The H-22A D-wing heavy attack boat was a shuttle manufactured by Incom Corporation. A combination of a starfighter and a landing craft, the H-22A was designed for use in combat environments. It was heavily armored and shielded, and armed with both heavy laser cannons and proton torpedo launchers. The H-22A was often used by Outer Rim pirates and mercenary companies.

## Specifications

- **Name:** H-22A D-wing heavy attack boat
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Cost:** 145,000 credits
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - Dorsal turret-mounted twin heavy laser cannons
  - Ventral turret-mounted auto-blasters
  - Forward-mounted light laser cannons
  - Wing-mounted proton torpedo launchers
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Gunners (2)
- **Passengers:** 12
- **Consumables:** Two weeks
