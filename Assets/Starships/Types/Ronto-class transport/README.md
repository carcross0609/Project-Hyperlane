# Ronto-class transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ronto-class transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ronto-class_transport)

The Ronto-class transport was a blocky cargo vessel built by Damorian Manufacturing Corporation. Ronto-class transports were equipped with at least one dorsal and one ventral laser cannon turret and one escape pod. Their hull could be dented by a Verpine shatter gun, and they had an extremely simple flight deck with a very small viewport. Rontos were considered to be among the slowest, ugliest, and most inefficient ships of the light transport classification in 36 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ronto-class transport
- **Manufacturer:** Damorian Manufacturing Corporation
