# Axet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Axet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axet)

Axet was a group of 4 Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports used by the Rebel Alliance. The transports were present at a Rebel Depot which was attacked by an Imperial corvette Control that launched 3 TIE Bombers. The transports were trying to power up their engines to attack the corvette when the TIE Bombers managed to destroy all 4 transports before they could do any damage to the Control.
