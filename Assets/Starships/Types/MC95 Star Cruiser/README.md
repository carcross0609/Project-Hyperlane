# MC95 Star Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MC95 Star Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC95_Star_Cruiser)

The MC95 Star Cruiser was a class of Mon Calamari Star Cruiser present in the the galaxy by the time following the Battle of Crait during the war between the First Order and the Resistance in 34 ABY. In 35 ABY, several were Star Cruisers were in service to the Mon Calamari monarchy, and several others formed part of the naval forces of the Resistance.
