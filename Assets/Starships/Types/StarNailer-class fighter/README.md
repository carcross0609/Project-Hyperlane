# StarNailer-class fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [StarNailer-class fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/StarNailer-class_fighter)

The StarNailer-class fighter was a starfighter produced by Subpro Corporation in the waning years of the Galactic Republic. It was a rare and expensive ship that contained a class-two hyperdrive and was introduced as a late-model. Zam Wesell used a StarNailer-class fighter during her hunt for Hurlo Holowan.
