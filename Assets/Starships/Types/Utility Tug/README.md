# Utility Tug

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Utility Tug](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Utility_Tug)

The Utility Tug was a spacetug produced by Nicholas/Gordon Spacewerks. At 10 meters, it was twice the size of the Phylon Freight's CT-11, and rather more versatile, being used to tow cargo to and from storage facilities.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Utility Tug
- **Manufacturer:** Nicholas/Gordon Spacewerks
- **Type:** Spacetug
- **Length:** 10 meters
