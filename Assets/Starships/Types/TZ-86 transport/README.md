# TZ-86 transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TZ-86 transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TZ-86_transport)

The TZ-86 transport, otherwise known as the Consortium Vehicle Transport, was a starship used by the Zann Consortium during the Galactic Civil War to land vehicles on planets. It was armed with two laser cannons.
