# YM-2800 limpet ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YM-2800 limpet ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YM-2800_limpet_ship)

YM-2800 limpet ship was a survey and mining vessel used by the Rebel Alliance to deploy troopers into Imperial starships.

- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** YM-2800 limpet ship

- **Max Speed:** 800 km/h
- **Shield Gen:** Heavy particle shielding
- **Armament:**

  - Plasma beam cutter
- **Crew:** 6
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 125 tons
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Availability:** Restricted
- **Role:**
  - Mining vessel
  - Assault/boarding craft
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
