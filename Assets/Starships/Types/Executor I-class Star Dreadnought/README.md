# Executor I-class Star Dreadnought

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Executor I-class Star Dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Executor_I-class_Star_Dreadnought)

The Executor I-class Star Dreadnought was a model Executor-class Star Dreadnought used by the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At one point, one such Star Dreadnought flew over a cloudy, white celestial body, while flanked by multiple Imperial-class Star Destroyers. The model was 19,000 meters long, propelled by red-hued Executor-50.x engines and protected by two Kuat Drive Yard-model deflector shield generator domes, and was succeeded by the Executor II-class Star Dreadnought.
