# Foreman's Labor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Foreman's Labor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Foreman%27s_Labor)

The Foreman's Labor was a Hardcell-class interstellar transport used by the CIS Navy of the Separatist Alliance during the Clone Wars. Foreman's Labor was considered legendary for its ability to hold out under the heaviest of enemy firepower.

- **Name:** Foreman's Labor
- **Model:** Hardcell-class interstellar transport

- **Engine:** Sublight engines (6)
- **Shield Gen:** Deflector shields
