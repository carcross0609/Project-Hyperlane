# CR90 corvette

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CR90 corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR90_corvette)

The CR90 Corvette, also identified as the CR90 class and colloquially known as the Corellian corvette or blockade runner, were consular vessels manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. While the CR90 would see initial use within the late Galactic Republic and Imperial Senate, many vessels would be appropriated by the early rebellion and Rebel Alliance against the First Galactic Empire, despite not being designed as a combat-oriented vessel. They were later used by the navies of both the New Republic and the Resistance.
