# TZ-6 executive shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TZ-6 executive shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TZ-6_executive_shuttle)

TZ-6 executive shuttle was a type of luxury shuttle produced by Corellian Engineering Corporation that was available during 3640 BBY. Jedi Master Gnost-Dural owned one such shuttle called Prosperity as part of his cover as a wealthy businessman during the Operation End Game.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TZ-6 executive shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** TZ-6
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Cost:** At least 50,000 credits
- **Navigation:** Autopilot
- **Role:** Luxury shuttle
