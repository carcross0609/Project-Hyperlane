# Casino cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Casino cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Casino_cruiser)

Casino cruisers were a type of cruiser used on the casino planet of Galator III. The middle decks of the cruisers were used for storage, a fact the smuggler Han Solo used to escape from patrol ships that were chasing his starship, the Millennium Falcon, as he carried out a heist on the planet.
