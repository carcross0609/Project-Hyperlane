# SS-39 Space Skiff

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SS-39 Space Skiff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SS-39_Space_Skiff)

The SS-39 Space Skiff was a small space maintenance vehicle used by the Galactic Empire, manufacturers and asteroid prospectors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SS-39 Space Skiff
- **Manufacturer:** Telex-Delcor
- **Type:** Space skiff
- **Cost:**
  - 12,500 credits (new)
  - 7,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 9.6 meters
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Crew:** 1
- **Passengers:** 8 (in powersuits)
- **Capacity:** 3 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 30 hours
- **Role:**
  - shuttle
  - maintenance vehicle
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
