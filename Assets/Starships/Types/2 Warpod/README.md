# &num;2 Warpod

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [2 Warpod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2_Warpod)

The Ulig Abaha Ltd. #2 Warpod, or warpod pinnace, was a starfighter used by the Corporate Sector Authority. They were also used by the Pirates of Iridium before their defeat in 22 BBY.

- **Name:** &num;2 Warpod

- **Crew:** 1; 4 gunners
- **Passengers:** 6 (troops)

- **Role:** Combat and supply
- **Affiliation:**
