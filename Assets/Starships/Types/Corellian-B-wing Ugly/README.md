# Corellian/B-wing Ugly

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corellian/B-wing Ugly](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellian/B-wing_Ugly)

The Corellian/B-wing Ugly, also known as the C-Wing Ugly, was an Ugly starfighter built by bolting the cockpit of a Corellian YT-series light freighter onto the fuselage of a B-wing starfighter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Corellian/B-wing Ugly
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Corellian Engineering Corporation (cockpit)
  - Slayn & Korpil (fuselage)
  - Pirates (final assembly)
- **Model:** Corellian B-wing "ugly"
- **Type:** Ugly starfighter
- **Cost:** 110,000 credits
- **Length:** 16.9 meters
- **Max Speed:** 650 - 950 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Navigation computer equipped (two-jump limit)
- **Armament:**
  - Anti-infantry turbolaser cannon turret (1)
  - Light ion cannons
  - Proton torpedo launcher
  -   8 torpedoes
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Gunner (1)
- **Passengers:** 2
- **Capacity:** 65 - 450 kg
- **Consumables:** 1 - 2 weeks
- **Availability:** Restricted
- **Role:** Assault fighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Pirates
  - Sacorrian Triad
