# Eternal Empire shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eternal Empire shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Empire_shuttle)

The shuttles of the Eternal Empire military, also known as Zakuul dropships, were shuttles used to transport personnel of the Eternal Empire and later repurposed by the Eternal Alliance.

- **Role:** Transport
