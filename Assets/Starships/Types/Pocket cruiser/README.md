# Pocket cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pocket cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pocket_cruiser)

Pocket cruisers were a type of starship. The Munificent-class star frigate of the Confederacy of Independent Systems was often referred to as a pocket cruiser.
