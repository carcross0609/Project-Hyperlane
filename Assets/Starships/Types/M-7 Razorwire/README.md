# M-7 Razorwire

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M-7 Razorwire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-7_Razorwire)

The M-7 Razorwire was a model of bomber produced by the shipyards at the planet Sernpidal for the reconstituted Sith Empire. Primarily a minelayer, the Razorwire was equipped with laser cannons, seismic mines, and concussion mines as a standard payload.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M-7 Razorwire
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Sernpidal shipyards
- **Type:** Bomber
- **Armament:**
  - Laser cannons
  - Seismic mines
  - Concussion mines
  - Ion mines
  - Interdiction mines
  - Seeker mines
- **Availability:** Military
- **Role:** Drone bomber
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
