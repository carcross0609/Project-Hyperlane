# Kinro 9747

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kinro 9747](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kinro_9747)

The Kinro 9747 was a type of mining ship. Mas Amedda, Grand Vizier and proxy-Emperor of the Galactic Empire, owned one such ship and flew it to meet with New Republic leaders on the ocean planet Velusia in 5 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kinro 9747
- **Type:** Mining ship
