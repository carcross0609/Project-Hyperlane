# Unidentified Zakuul Carrier

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Zakuul Carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Zakuul_Carrier)

A type of transport utilized by the Eternal Empire of Zakuul during the War against Zakuul. It was capable to deploy two World Thrashers on the battlefield. At least three transports of this class were used to deploy World Thrashers during the Assault on Odessen in 3630 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Zakuul Carrier
- **Type:** Transport
- **Complement:** World Thrashers (2)
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Eternal Empire
