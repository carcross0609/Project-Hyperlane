# Infiltrator

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Infiltrator (starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltrator_%28starfighter%29)

An Infiltrator was a type of starfighter used by members of the Jedi Order around 5000 BBY. Jedi Master Relin Druur and his Padawan, Drev Hassin, flew one during an attack on the Sith Empire's mining forces on a moon of Phaegon III.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Infiltrator
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Sensor:** Signature scrambler
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
