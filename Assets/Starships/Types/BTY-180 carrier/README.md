# BTY-180 carrier

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BTY-180 carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BTY-180_carrier)

The BTY-180 carrier, also known as the Pre-Mor Authority Carrier, the Preox-Morlana personnel carrier or simply the Authority Carrier was a class of personnel carrier owned and operated by the Pre-Mor Authority. It was utilized to transport members of the Pre-Mor Enforcement to the different territories and star systems under the jurisdiction of Preox-Morlana. During the Imperial Era, one of these personnel carriers was utilized in 5 BBY to transport three teams of Corpos, including Deputy Inspector Syril Karn, to Ferrix. The teams would collectively stage the operation to apprehend Cassian Jeron Andor.
