# T-1 shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-1 shuttle (Rebel Alliance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-1_shuttle_%28Rebel_Alliance%29)

The T-1 shuttle was a small shuttle used to carry passengers to their desired destinations. A T-1 shuttle was used by Evaan Verlaine, Princess Leia Organa, and R2-D2 after the Battle of Yavin.
