# Grysk starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grysk starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grysk_starfighter)

The Grysk Hegemony of the Grysk species employed a type of starfighter in their military. Equipped with missiles, spectrum laser, and electrostatic barriers, the Grysk starfighters were able to deal significant damage to Chiss heavy cruisers. However, during a skirmish against the Chiss heavy cruiser Steadfast, Chiss Ascendancy Admiral Ar'alani found a weakness in the fighters.
