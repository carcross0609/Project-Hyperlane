# IR-3F-class light frigate

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IR-3F-class light frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-3F-class_light_frigate)

The IR-3F-class light frigate was a class of light frigate deployed by the Galactic Empire, specifically the Imperial Office of Customs. At least one such frigate patrolled the space around Toydaria.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IR-3F-class light frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Role:** Customs
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Office of Customs
