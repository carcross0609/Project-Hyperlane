# Etti light cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Etti light cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Etti_light_cruiser)

The Etti light cruiser was a small capital ship built by the Corporate Sector Authority, and built off of the same space frame as the Etti-class light transport.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Etti light cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Corporate Sector Authority
- **Model:** Modified CSA Etti light cruiser
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Cost:** Not for sale. valued at 1,641,000
- **Length:** 125 meters
- **Max Speed:** 800 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2.0
- **Armament:**
  - Quad laser cannon turrets (10; 4 fore, 2 port, 2 starboard, 2 aft)
  - Tractor beam projector
- **Crew:** 25
- **Skeleton:** 15
- **Passengers:** 15
- **Capacity:** 400 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 3 weeks
- **Role:**
  - Cargo hauler
  - Passenger transport
  - Patrol craft
  - Q-ships (modified cargo vessels with hidden weapons, designed to lure in and destroy pirate forces)
- **Affiliation:** Corporate Sector Authority
