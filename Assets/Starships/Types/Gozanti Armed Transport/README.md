# Gozanti Armed Transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gozanti Armed Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gozanti_Armed_Transport)

The Gozanti Armed Transport was a model of armed transport that came into service by 3 ABY. Gozanti Armed Transports could be spotted in orbit around Akiva during the Imperial Era and a number were used by the Hutt Clan. These starships participated in the Battle of Exegol as part of a Citizens' Fleet of volunteer civilians from across the galaxy gathered by former Rebel Alliance General Lando Calrissian, which took up arms to assist in the defeat of the resurgent Galactic Emperor Darth Sidious and the Sith Eternal's attempt at galactic domination.
