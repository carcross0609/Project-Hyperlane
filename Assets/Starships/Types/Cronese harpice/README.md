# Cronese harpice

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cronese harpice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cronese_harpice)

Cronese harpices were a model of large warship used by the empire of the Tionese despot Xim. Named for the Kingdom of Cron, the starships were believed to be cruiser-class by Ximologist historians thousands of years after the reign of Xim.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cronese harpice
- **Affiliation:** Xim's empire
