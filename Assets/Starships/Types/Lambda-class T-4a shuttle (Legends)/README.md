# Lambda-class T-4a shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lambda-class T-4a shuttle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lambda-class_T-4a_shuttle/Legends)

The Lambda-class T-4a shuttle, also known as the Imperial Shuttle, was a standard light utility craft in common with the Imperial military as a transport for troops and high-ranking individuals.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Sienar Fleet Systems (designer and primary builder)
  - Cygnus Spaceworks (subcontractor)
  - Silviut Corporation (subcontractor)
- **Model:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Type:** Armed government transport shuttle
- **Cost:** Likely valued at 240,000 credits
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Max Accel:** 1,400 G
- **Mglt:** 50 MGLT
- **Max Speed:** 850 km/h
- **Engine:** SFS-204 sublight ion engines (2) or Z-Rk5 ion engines (2)
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 1.0

- **Hdsystem:** SFS S/ig-37 hyperdrive
- **Shield Gen:** Multiple shield generators (rated 100 SBD)
- **Hull:** Reinforced durasteel/carbon fiber hull (rated 66 RU)
- **Navigation:** Navigation computer equipped
- **Armament:** Standard configuration:
2 x Steerable forward twin Taim & Bak KX5 laser cannons 

2 x Taim & Bak GA-60s twin laser cannons

Taim & Bak KX5 laser cannons (8 or 4 x 2 forward, 2 or 1 x 2 aft)
