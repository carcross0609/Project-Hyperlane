# Transpo corsair

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Transpo corsair](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transpo_corsair)

The transpo corsair was a model of starship. At one point, a Galactic Republic transport corsair, with the emblem of the eventual Separatist Alliance, crash-landed on the planet Kenari. When a local tribe of orphans went to investigate with the hope of finding supplies, a surviving member of the crew of the ship killed their leader, causing the tribe to carry the body back to their village. One of their member, Kassa, stayed and explored the wreckage of the transport corsair, and was eventually taken offworld by Clem and Maarva Andor, who were attempting to scavenge the ship but decided to rescue Kassa.
