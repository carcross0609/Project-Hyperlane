# Detainer CC-2200

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Detainer CC-2200](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Detainer_CC-2200)

The Detainer CC-2200 was a model of cruiser and Interdictor used by the Galactic Empire. Moff Wilhuff Tarkin used a CC-2200 to try and capture the stolen corvette Carrion Spike in the Obroa-skai system. The attempt was unsuccessful, and resulted in the Mon Calamari star cruiser Stellar Vista crashing into the CC-2200.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Detainer CC-2200
- **Model:** Detainer CC-2200
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Hull:** Thick armor
- **Armament:** At least 8 gravity well projectors
- **Role:** Interdictor
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Navy
