# TIE Bomber Mark 2

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE Bomber Mark 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE_Bomber_Mark_2)

The TIE Bomber Mark 2 was a proposed upgrade to the TIE/sa bomber suggested by Captain Tomax Bren after analyzing its capabilities during the Hook Nebula campaign. Among his suggestions were greater speed and maneuverability, and a less prominent profile. The design was never realized, but Grand Admiral Thrawn and Sienar Fleet Systems incorporated some of Bren's suggestions into the Scimitar assault bomber.
