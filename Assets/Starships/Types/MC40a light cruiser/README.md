# MC40a light cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MC40a light cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC40a_light_cruiser)

The MC40A Light Cruiser, also known as the MC40a cruiser, or simply as the MC40A and MC40a, was a class of light cruiser that was active during the Imperial Era and served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic.
