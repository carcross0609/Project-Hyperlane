# Unidentified transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified transport (Megalox)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_transport_%28Megalox%29)

A type of transport was used at Megalox, a high-security prison on Megalox Beta. The prison was monitored by a space station in orbit around the planet, and the transport was used to bring guards and visitors to and from the surface.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified transport
- **Passengers:** At least 5
- **Role:** Transporting guards and visitors
- **Affiliation:** Megalox
