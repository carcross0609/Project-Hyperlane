# Recusant-class Support Destroyer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Recusant-class Support Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Recusant-class_Support_Destroyer)

The Recusant-class Support Destroyer was a model of the Recusant-class light destroyer utilized by the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic. A class of destroyer, the starship was equipped with several thrusters, electromagnetic sensor antennas, a neutrino radiator grille, and several heavy turbolaser cannons. The symbol of the Separatist Alliance was also emblazoned on its starboard hull. At one point during the war, a Recusant-class Support Destroyer was located in orbit of a sickly green celestial body alongside two Separatist automated vulture droid deployment stations.
