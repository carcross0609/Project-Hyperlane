# Supply ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Supply ship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supply_ship/Legends)

over Umbara]] A supply ship was a starship carrying a variety of supplies. One was used around 32 BBY as a cargo ship for the asteroid prison of Desolation Alley, and another was the Galactic Alliance ship Verity.
