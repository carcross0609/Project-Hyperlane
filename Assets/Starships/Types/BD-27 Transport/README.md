# BD-27 Transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BD-27 Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD-27_Transport)

The BD-27 Transport was a Corellian Engineering Corporation barge driver. It was the predecessor to the Action series of medium freighters, and defined the basic shape of that transport.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BD-27 Transport
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** BD-series
- **Type:** Barge driver
- **Cost:**
  - 800,000 credits (New)
  - 350,000 credits (Used)
- **Length:** 110 meters
- **Max Speed:** 650 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - x3 (Primary)
  - x15 (Backup)
- **Armament:** None
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Co-pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** 4
- **Capacity:** 75,000 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 3 months
