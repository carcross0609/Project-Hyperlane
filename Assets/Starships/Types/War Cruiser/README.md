# War Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [War Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/War_Cruiser)

The War Cruiser was a type of cruiser. One such cruiser, the Dark Syndicate, was utilized by the Son-tuul Pride criminal syndicate during the Imperial Era. The War Cruiser was present above the planet Jekara for the auction of Han Solo, but was destroyed by the Super Star Destroyer Executor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** War Cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Engine:** At least 1
- **Affiliation:** Son-tuul Pride
