# BattleChief

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BattleChief](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BattleChief)

A BattleChief was a capital ship classification used by the Grysk warlike species. The Grysk Prism-class BattleChiefs were equipped with engines and a hyperdrive and armed with spectrum lasers and missiles, and they were slightly smaller than a Chiss heavy cruiser and larger than a Chiss light cruiser or patrol cruiser.
