# Escape pod

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Escape pod/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Escape_pod/Legends)

Escape pods, also known as life pods, repair pods, or lifeboats, were small escape craft for use in emergencies. They were standard aboard all deep-space starships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Escape pod
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** Escape pod
- **Height:** Usually around 10 meters but varies by design
- **Shield Gen:** Rated 20 SBD
- **Hull:** Rated 9 RU
- **Crew:** 1-6
