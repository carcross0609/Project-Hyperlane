# E-STAP Droid Starbomber

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-STAP Droid Starbomber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-STAP_Droid_Starbomber)

The E-STAP Droid Starbomber was a bomber employed by the Trade Federation, circa 32 BBY during the Invasion of the planet Naboo. Later versions of the craft were equipped with a deflector shield generator.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-STAP Droid Starbomber
- **Type:** Bomber
- **Affiliation:** Trade Federation
