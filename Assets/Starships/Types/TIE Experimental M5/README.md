# TIE Experimental M5

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE Experimental M5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE_Experimental_M5)

The TIE Experimental M5, also known as the TIE Booster, was an experimental member of the TIE Series and a part of the TIE Experimental Project.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TIE Experimental M5
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Line:** TIE Series Experimental
- **Model:** Twin Ion Engine Experimental M5 (T/e5)
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 10 meters
- **Mglt:** 132 MGLT
- **Engine:**
  - Twin ion engines
  - 1 booster engine
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** Optional (20 SBD)
- **Hull:** Titanium alloy hull (11 RU)
- **Navigation:** None (slaved to control ship)
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (2)
- **Crew:** Remote control system
- **Passengers:** None
- **Role:** Space superiority fighter
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
