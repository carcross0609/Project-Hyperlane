# Gatherer-class transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gatherer-class transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gatherer-class_transport)

Gatherer-class transports were personnel carriers of Slayn & Korpil design which could carry about 300 troops each. Their ion drives were considered underpowered for assault craft. The ships' hullform was described as "puffed-wafer silhouettes."

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gatherer-class transport
- **Manufacturer:** Slayn & Korpil
- **Model:** Gatherer-class transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Engine:** Ion drive
- **Passengers:** 300 troops
- **Role:** Troop transport
