# Grysk transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grysk transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grysk_transport)

Shortly after the Battle of Atollon, the Grysks used a type of small transport. Ten of these transports were present above and on Batuu, and were later joined by two long-range heavy freighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grysk transport
- **Model:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Grysk Hegemony
