# Petite Opu-Yacht

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Petite Opu-Yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Petite_Opu-Yacht)

The Petite Opu-Yacht was a model of yacht manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. It had a gold finish and, as such, was very expensive. Before she went on to join the Resistance, Lorica Demaris owned one such yacht, which her wealthy parents bought for her.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Petite Opu-Yacht
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Role:** Transport
