# YT-1010 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YT-1010 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-1010_light_freighter)

The YT-1010 was a model of light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. It consisted of a round main body with a separate, forward-mounted cockpit module. A custom YT-1010 equipped with laser cannon turrets and an escape pod was used by smuggler Sana Starros, who named the ship the Volt Cobra.
