# Warcarrier

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Warcarrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Warcarrier)

The Warcarrier was a bomber model manufactured by Elsinore-Cordova Engineering for the Galactic Republic that saw use during the Galactic War with the reconstituted Sith Empire. Primarily a drone bomber, the Warcarrier carried drones, laser cannons, and various missiles or torpedoes depending on its loadout.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Warcarrier
- **Manufacturer:** Elsinore-Cordova Engineering
- **Type:** Bomber
- **Availability:** Military
- **Role:** Bomber
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
