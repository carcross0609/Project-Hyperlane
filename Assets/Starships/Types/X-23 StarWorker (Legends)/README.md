# X-23 StarWorker

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X-23 StarWorker/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-23_StarWorker/Legends)

The X-23 StarWorker was an intra-system space barge produced by the starship manufacturing corporation Incom Corporation. It was capable of carrying up to 5,000 metric tons of cargo in its 2,000 cubic meter bay.

## Specifications

- **Name:** X-23 StarWorker
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Type:** Intra-system space barge
- **Cost:**
  - 325,000 credits (new)
  - 145,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 38 Imperial standard meters
- **Max Speed:** 650 kilometers per hour
- **Engine:** Incom sublight engines
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Crew:** 2 crew plus one labor droid
- **Capacity:** 2,000 cubic meters with a maximum mass of 5,000 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 week
