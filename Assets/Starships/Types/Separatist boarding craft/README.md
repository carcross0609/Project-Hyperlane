# Separatist boarding craft

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Separatist boarding craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_boarding_craft)

The Separatist boarding craft was a boarding craft used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems. General Grievous used one of these ships to board a Mon Calamari ship. The boarding craft was able to carry B1-series battle droids, B2 super battle droids and destroyer droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Separatist boarding craft
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Passengers:** Unknown amount of battle droids
- **Role:** Boarding craft
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
