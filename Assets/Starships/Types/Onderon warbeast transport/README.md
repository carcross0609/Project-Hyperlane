# Onderon warbeast transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Onderon warbeast transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Onderon_warbeast_transport)

Onderonian warbeast transports were a type of small transport craft that were used during the Great Sith War to shuttle drexl beasts to engage in battle on other worlds, such as Cinnagar.
