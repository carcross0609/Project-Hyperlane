# G-2A gunship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-2A gunship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-2A_gunship)

The G-2A gunship was a model of gunship that was used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War. G-2A gunships razed entire cities on the planet Sullust when the Empire conquered it. The Sullustan company SoroSuub Corporation was later given the "honor" of designing the G-2A's successor, the GSS-5C Dustmaker.
