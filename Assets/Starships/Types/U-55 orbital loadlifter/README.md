# U-55 orbital loadlifter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [U-55 orbital loadlifter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/U-55_orbital_loadlifter)

The U-55 orbital loadlifter, also known as the U-55 loadlifter or Resistance transport and, informally, as a lifeboat, was a type of transport shuttle manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems. It was used by the Resistance during its war with the First Order.
