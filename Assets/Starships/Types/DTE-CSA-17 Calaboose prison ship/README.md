# DTE-CSA-17 Calaboose prison ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DTE-CSA-17 Calaboose prison ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DTE-CSA-17_Calaboose_prison_ship)

The DTE-CSA-17 Calaboose prison ship was a model of prison ship made by Kazellan Corporation and utilized during the High Republic Era. One such starship, the Palwick, ferried a number of prisoners to Coruscant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DTE-CSA-17 Calaboose prison ship
- **Manufacturer:** Kazellan Corporation
- **Type:** Prison ship
- **Length:** 125.9 meters
- **Engine:** K-DT17c sublight engines
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Crew:** At least 2 pilot droids
- **Role:** Prison transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
