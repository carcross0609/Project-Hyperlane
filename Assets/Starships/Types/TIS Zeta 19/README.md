# TIS Zeta 19

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIS Zeta 19](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIS_Zeta_19)

The TIS Zeta 19 was a light aerospace fighter manufactured by the Corporate Sector Authority for use by the Espos. Stolen craft were used by criminals in neighboring space.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TIS Zeta 19
- **Manufacturer:** Corporate Sector Authority
- **Type:** Light aerospace patrol starfighter
- **Cost:**
  - 45,000 credits (new)
  - 4,500 credits (used)
- **Length:** 15 meters
- **Max Speed:** 950 KMH
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 2 laser cannons
- **Crew:** 1; 1 gunner
- **Passengers:** 2
- **Capacity:** 200 kilograms
- **Consumables:** 1 day
- **Role:** Patrol fighter
