# Dark Jedi galleon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dark Jedi galleon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Jedi_galleon)

Following the Battle of Corbos in 6900 BBY which concluded the Hundred-Year Darkness, numerous Dark Jedi Exiles were forced to board unarmed galleons and banished from known space by the Galactic Republic. Deprived of weapons and star-charts, the Exiles wandered through the stars, guided by the instincts of one of their own, Sorzus Syn. Eventually, the Dark Jedi reached a sector unknown to the Republic, which belonged to the fabled Kingdom of the Sith. At least two of the starships in which the Exiles traveled arrived on the planet Korriban, where the Dark Jedi subjugated members of the Sith species.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dark Jedi galleon
- **Type:** Drone
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Equipped (no navigational instruments)
- **Armament:** None
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Exiles
