# B84-Viper

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B84-Viper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B84-Viper)

The B84-Viper was a cruiser used by criminal factions during the Imperial Era including the Ashiga Clan, the Pyke Syndicate, and pirates. One Pyke Syndicate B84-Viper and Pyke JLF-19 Junkros Fighters battled a pair of Mining Guild Freighters from the Hutt Clan and a group of ARC-200 Wing Cutter Fighters in Tatooine's orbit in 3 ABY. The scoundrel Kay Vess and ND-5 stumbled on the battle as they arrived from hyperspace.
