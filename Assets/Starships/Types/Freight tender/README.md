# Freight tender

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Freight tender](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freight_tender)

Freight tenders were a type of starship with ion engines. In 0 ABY, a pair of freight tenders were part of the busy traffic in the space lanes above the planet Giju. When a firefight erupted between a trio of Alliance to Restore the Republic X-wing starfighters and a patrol of Imperial TIE fighters, the two freight tenders tried to fly away from the fighting as quickly as possible.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Freight tender
- **Engine:** Ion engine
