# Goode

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Goode](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Goode)

Goode was a group of four Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports used by the Rebel Alliance. The transports attempted to defend a Rebel foothold consisting of four Class-C cargo containers Berm, a single Class-B cargo container Clip and a Utility Tug T-DP, while two Lambda-class shuttle groups, Trail and Guild, attacked oncoming Imperial starfighters. However, the transports, along with the accompanying Rebel ships, were destroyed by Imperial pilots in a space-battle. The conflict was recorded for the combat chamber to train future TIE pilots, such as Maarek Stele.
