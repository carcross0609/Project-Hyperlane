# Mansk-class escort frigate

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mansk-class escort frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mansk-class_escort_frigate)

The Mansk-class escort frigate was a starship manufactured by the Tallaani Shipyards in the Tapani sector. The Mansk-class vessel, Knife's Edge, was used by House Melantha during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mansk-class escort frigate
- **Manufacturer:** Tallaani Shipyards
- **Cost:** 135,000 credits (used only)
- **Length:** 100 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,000 kph (atmosphere)
- **Engine:** Powerful sublight drives
- **Hdrange:** Class 3 Hyperdrive (Class 12 backup)
- **Shield Gen:** Military-grade shielding
- **Hull:** Military-grade heavy plating
- **Armament:**
  - Dual Laser turrets (6)
  - Ion cannon (1)
- **Crew:** 5 gunners 7
- **Passengers:** 30 troops
- **Capacity:** 80 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Role:** Escort frigate
