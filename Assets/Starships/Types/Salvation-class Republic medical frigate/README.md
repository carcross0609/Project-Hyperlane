# Salvation-class Republic medical frigate

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Salvation-class Republic medical frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salvation-class_Republic_medical_frigate)

The Salvation-class Republic medical frigate was a class of medical frigate utilized by the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era. The Salvation-class vessel Panacea was one of the earliest Great Works of Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Salvation-class Republic medical frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Role:** Medical frigate
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
