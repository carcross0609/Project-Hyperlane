# Del

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Del (Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Del_%28Delta-class_DX-9_stormtrooper_transport%29)

Del was a group of 3 Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports in the service of the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. Transports Del were a part of an assault on XQ1 Platform NL-1 during the Battle on the Frontier. With the combined effort of starfighters from Nebulon-B2 frigate Ludwick and Imperial Aces such as Maarek Stele, the rebel attack failed, and the Del group was destroyed.
