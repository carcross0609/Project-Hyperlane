# Imperial warship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial warship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_warship)

A class of warship was utilized by the Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War with the Galactic Republic. The Ambria's Fury and the Fatality were two starships of this class, though they both crashed during in the middle of the war in battles above Belsavis and Hoth respectively.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial warship
- **Type:** Warship
- **Availability:** Limited
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
