# Procursator-class Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Procursator-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Procursator-class_Star_Destroyer)

The Procursator-class Star Destroyer was a warship in the Star Destroyer design family that was used by the Galactic Empire, the Dark Empire, and the New Republic. The Procursator-class was involved in multiple fleet gatherings throughout the Imperial Era, including gatherings of the Imperial Navy prior to the Battle of Hoth, the Dark Empire's stronghold Byss, and a New Republic fleet gathering. Two vessels of the class participated in the Battle of Calamari, but were destroyed by New Republic.
