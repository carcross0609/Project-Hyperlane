# MTT Droid Fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MTT Droid Fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MTT_Droid_Fighter)

The MTT Droid Fighter was a small fightercraft that could be deployed from within the Trade Federation's MTT to attack Republic ground forces. To accomplish this, the vehicle boasted two rocket launchers that could either be installed on the wingtips for a wide angle of attack or on the mounting point at the back of the main body for a more focused attack. Unlike most of the other starfighters utilized by the Trade Federation and the CIS, the starfighter was itself not a droid, but rather piloted by a battle droid. The droid was seated in a fully enclosed cockpit within the main body.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MTT Droid Fighter
- **Armament:** Reconfigurable missile launcher (2)
