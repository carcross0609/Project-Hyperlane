# Sheathipede-class transport shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sheathipede-class transport shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sheathipede-class_transport_shuttle)

The Sheathipede-class transport shuttle, also known as the Sheathipede-class shuttle and as the Neimoidian escort shuttle, was a short-range transport used by the Trade Federation and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Multiple versions of the shuttle existed including a a larger version with an extended midsection, weaponized and weaponless variants, and a droid-pilot variant. The Sheathipede shuttle also shared many of its characteristics with the much larger Maxillipede shuttle.
