# Lux-400 yacht

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lux-400 yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lux-400_yacht)

The Lux-400 yacht was a class of yacht used in the galaxy in the years leading up to the Galactic Civil War. A modified Lux-400, the Truant, was on the wanted list in multiple sectors due to its crew's habit of smuggling weapons. The Truant was destroyed by the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Executrix in the Obroa-skai system in the year 14 BBY.
