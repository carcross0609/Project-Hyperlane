# HWK-290 light freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HWK-290 light freighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HWK-290_light_freighter/Legends)

The HWK-290 was a light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation in an effort to break into a new market for fast, small cargo ships. The production lasted from the decades leading up to the Battle of Naboo until being discontinued during the Clone Wars.
