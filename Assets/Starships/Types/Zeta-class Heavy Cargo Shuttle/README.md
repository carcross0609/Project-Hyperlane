# Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeta-class_Heavy_Cargo_Shuttle)

The Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle, also known as the Zeta-class cargo shuttle or the Zeta-class Imperial shuttle, or simply the Zeta-class shuttle was a type of cargo shuttle used by the Galactic Empire in its fight against the Alliance to Restore the Republic. They were instrumental in carrying kyber crystals during the construction of the Death Star.
