# YL-2200 light freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YL-2200 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YL-2200_light_freighter)

The YL-2200 light freighter was a light freighter sold around the time of the Battle of Hoth. It is known that the Rebellion made use of this craft.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YL-2200 light freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Cost:**
  - 100,000 credits (new)
  - 25,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 28.2 meters
- **Max Speed:** 800 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 2
  - Class 12 (backup)
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 2 laser cannons
- **Crew:** 1 pilot; 1 gunner
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 100 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Role:** Freighter
