# Rebel smuggling ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rebel smuggling ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rebel_smuggling_ship)

The Rebel smuggling ship was a smuggling vessel used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. These tiny craft were used as smuggling vessels and had two large wing-engines. They were also armed with laser cannons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rebel smuggling ship
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Role:** Smuggling vessel
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
