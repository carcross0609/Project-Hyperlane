# Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acclamator-class_transgalactic_military_assault_ship)

The Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship, also known as Acclamator-class Republic assault ship, Acclamator-class Planetary Assault Ship, Acclamator-class battleship, or Acclamator-class cruiser, was a type of cruiser-class proto-Star Destroyer utilized by the Galactic Republic for ferrying clone troopers across the galaxy. They were first deployed at the First Battle of Geonosis after transporting the clone army from Kamino, and subsequently carried the Grand Army of the Republic from the capital Coruscant to the various arenas of the Clone Wars, a conflict in which they would see extensive use against the Confederacy of Independent Systems.
