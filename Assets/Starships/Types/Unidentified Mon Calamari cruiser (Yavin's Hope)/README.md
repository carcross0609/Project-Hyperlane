# Unidentified Mon Calamari cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Mon Calamari cruiser (Yavin's Hope)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Mon_Calamari_cruiser_%28Yavin%27s_Hope%29)

A cruiser class of Mon Calamari design was part of the Mon Cala Imperial Trade Fleet and was above the planet Mon Cala during a mission undertaken by a Rebel Alliance team led by Leia Organa. The cruiser was later part of the fleet's mutiny to the Alliance to Restore the Republic, with at least two cruisers participating in the Battle of Mako-Ta.
