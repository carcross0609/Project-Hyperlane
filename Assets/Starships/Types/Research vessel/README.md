# Research vessel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Research vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Research_vessel)

Research vessels, or research ships, were a type of starship. The Theoretika and the Jannimak was a research vessel. During the Galactic Civil War, multiple research vessels operated in the Polis Massa system.
