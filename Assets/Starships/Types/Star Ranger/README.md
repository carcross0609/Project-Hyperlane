# Star Ranger

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Star Ranger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Ranger)

A Star Ranger was a type of starship that existed by 44 ABY. That year, Abeloth, in the guise of Lydea Pagorski, piloted a Star Ranger through the Imperial Remnant blockade of Exodo II to reach Admiral Natasi Daala. Jagged Fel thought that the feat impossible to achieve, speculating that a disloyal individual in his fleet had allowed the sensors to ignore the presence of the Star Ranger, but later realized with the input of Tahiri Veila that it must be the doing of Abeloth using Force-flash.
