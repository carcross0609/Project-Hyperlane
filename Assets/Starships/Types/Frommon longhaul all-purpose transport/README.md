# Frommon longhaul all-purpose transport

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Frommon longhaul all-purpose transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frommon_longhaul_all-purpose_transport)

The Frommon longhaul all-purpose transport was one of the first hyperdrive-capable freighters during the early years of the Galactic Republic, as was evidenced by their crude ion coils. It came in at 30 meters and required a crew of two people to operate it. One such ship, the Starkiller, was used by bounty hunter Leshy Drobo.
