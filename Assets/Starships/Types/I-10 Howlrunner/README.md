# I-10 Howlrunner

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [I-10 Howlrunner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I-10_Howlrunner)

The Incom Corporation I-10 Howlrunner was an attack starfighter, and the successor to the Imperial-used I-7 Howlrunner short-range starfighter. During Operation Shadow Hand, Executor Sedriss QL used one of these starfighters when the Dark Empire attacked a New Republic listening outpost on the Fourth Moon of Nuhatu, and later again during the Battle of Entarr.
