# Heavy assault starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heavy assault starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_assault_starfighter)

A heavy assault starfighter was a type of starfighter used in naval warfare. The B-wing heavy assault starfighter was a series of heavy assault starfighters. The A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter could be outfitted with multiple laser cannons, torpedo launchers, or laser bombs and a composite-beam laser. The ARC-170 starfighter was a reconnaissance starfighter that was also considered a heavy assault fighter.
