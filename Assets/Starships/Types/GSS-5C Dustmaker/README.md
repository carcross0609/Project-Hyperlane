# GSS-5C Dustmaker

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GSS-5C Dustmaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GSS-5C_Dustmaker)

The GSS-5C Dustmaker was a starfighter produced by SoroSuub Corporation for the reconstituted Sith Empire. A successor of the G-2A gunship, which was known for razing whole cities on the planet Sullust, the Dustmaker was made to surpass its predecessor's firepower. While its defenses are a bit below the standard base line for gunships and was only able to equip a single rail gun, these weaknesses were curtailed with the ability to carry proton torpedoes and a heavier laser cannon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GSS-5C Dustmaker
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Armament:**

  - 2 laser cannons
  - Additional missiles and torpedoes equippable
- **Role:** Gunship
