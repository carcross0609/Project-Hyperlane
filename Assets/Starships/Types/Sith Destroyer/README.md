# Sith Destroyer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Destroyer)

A Sith Destroyer was a capital ship used by the Sith during the New Sith Wars. The ship type was armed with at least a broadside laser cannon artillery and could overpower several battleships. One such vessel was the Nightfall, Lord Kaan's flagship. It led a fleet of Corsair battleships and Sith Dreadnaughts in the First Battle of Ruusan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sith Destroyer
- **Type:** Destroyer
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Broadside laser cannon artillery
- **Role:** Command ship
