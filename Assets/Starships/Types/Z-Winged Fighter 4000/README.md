# Z-Winged Fighter 4000

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Z-Winged Fighter 4000](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Winged_Fighter_4000)

The Z-Winged Fighter 4000 was a type of starfighter thought of by the Jedi apprentice Christina Starspeeder at one point around 180 BBY. While Starspeeder was investigating a crashed cargo starship on the Outer Rim Territories planet Hoth for any supplies worth scavenging, she found the make of the vessel to be unfamiliar to her. She then wished that Victor, her younger brother who was knowledgeable about starships, were there with her, imagining him nonchalantly telling her that the crashed ship was a third-generation Z-Winged Fighter 4000.
