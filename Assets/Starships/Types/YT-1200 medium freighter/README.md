# YT-1200 medium freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YT-1200 medium freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-1200_medium_freighter)

The YT-1200 medium freighter was a Corellian Engineering Corporation YT-series freighter that served as the basis for Nova-Drive's license-built 3-Z light freighter. They had a tubular, elongated cockpit and rounded hull. Each vessel was 28 meters long.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YT-1200 medium freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Length:** 28 meters
