# Cloud fighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cloud fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cloud_fighter)

A cloud fighter was a type of starship used by the Nihil pirates. Cloud fighters were used by members of the Nihil who were hunting Kitrep Soh, the son of Chancellor Lina Soh, and his boyfriend, Jom Lariin, who were on the Sands of Sarmen, a location on Abtin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cloud fighter
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Nihil
