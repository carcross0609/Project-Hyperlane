# Mk. VII advanced interceptor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mk. VII advanced interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mk._VII_advanced_interceptor)

The Mk. VII advanced interceptor was a model of single-being starfighter that was in use by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War and the Cold War. They were considered new during the Sacking of Coruscant. Eldon Ax flew one during the Independent Operation Sebaddon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mk. VII advanced interceptor
- **Type:** Interceptor
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Role:** Starfighter
- **Firstuse:** 3653 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sith Empire
  -   Imperial Navy
