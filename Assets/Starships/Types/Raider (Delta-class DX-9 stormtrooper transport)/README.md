# Raider

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Raider (Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raider_%28Delta-class_DX-9_stormtrooper_transport%29)

Raider was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport captured by the Rebel Alliance. During the Rebel food shortage following the evacuation of Yavin 4, Raider participated in a raid on an Imperial grain supply depot based in an asteroid field, as a part of Operation Grain Snatch.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Raider
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Width:** 7.8 meters
- **Height:** 5.8 meters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
