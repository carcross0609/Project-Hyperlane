# Unidentified Mon Calamari Star Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Mon Calamari Star Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Mon_Calamari_Star_Cruiser)

A class of Mon Calamari Star Cruiser served in the naval forces of the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War against the Galactic Empire. With the exception of the port and starboard wings, the Star Cruiser physically resembled the MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser. Similar to the Liberty Type, the Star Cruiser had nine engines, the ability to travel in hyperspace, and a bulbous hull like other Mon Calamari vessels.
