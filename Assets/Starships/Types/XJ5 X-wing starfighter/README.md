# XJ5 X-wing starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XJ5 X-wing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XJ5_X-wing_starfighter)

The Incom XJ5 X-wing starfighter, more commonly known as the ChaseX, was a model of X-wing starfighter used during the Chiss-Killik conflict by the New Jedi Order and the Galactic Reconstruction Police. It had numerous improvements to its weapons and shielding over the T-65XJ3 X-wing starfighter. XJ5 X-wings were later used by the Galactic Alliance Guard in 40 ABY and were stationed onboard the Anakin Solo.
