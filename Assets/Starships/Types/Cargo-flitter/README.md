# Cargo-flitter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cargo-flitter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cargo-flitter)

A cargo-flitter was a type of vessel used for moving cargo at Pellezara station in the Duro system. In addition to its typical usage, the children of the station's owners used souped-up cargo-flitters, along with souped-up tugs, to put on flight shows for bored pilots who were waiting for their freighters to be serviced. It was in these shows that future Rebel pilot and Rogue Squadron member Tarrin Datch honed his flying skills.
