# RK-392 mobile tac-pod

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RK-392 mobile tac-pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RK-392_mobile_tac-pod)

The RK-392, simply known as the Mobile Tac-Pod was a model of troop transport and landing craft utilized by the Preox-Morlana's Corporate Tactical Forces, the Pre-Mor Enforcement, for the deployment of corpos to planetary surfaces. In 5 BBY, a Pre-Mor Security Inspection team led by Deputy Inspector Syril Karn and Sergeant Linus Mosk utilized three Tac-Pods to make planetfall on a settlement on Ferrix to arrest Cassian Jeron Andor.
