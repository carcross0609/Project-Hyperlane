# Tempest Zero starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tempest Zero starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tempest_Zero_starfighter)

The Tempest Zero starfighter was a starfighter model utilized by Tofen's Raiders, a group of ace pilots who were believed to have hailed from the planet Valahari, led by Tofen Vane. During the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and Confederacy of Independent Systems, the group continued to use their fighters to fly and operate countless sorties across the northern territories of the Confederacy on their behalf, incorporated into the Separatist Navy. The fighters were considered to be state-of-the-art. In 19 BBY, Tempest Zero starfighters were mentioned in an entry on Tofen's Raiders that was written by ARC trooper Muzzle and published in the survival compendium "Neyo's Almanac of Clankers." The report mentioned how Tofen's Raiders were encountered more often in later stages of the war. Muzzle warned clone troopers about Tofen's Raiders exceptional skills, saying they treated their ships like extensions of their own bodies.
