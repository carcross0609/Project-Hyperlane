# TIE/ph "Phantom" Multi-Role Stealth Starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE/ph "Phantom" Multi-Role Stealth Starfighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/ph_%22Phantom%22_Multi-Role_Stealth_Starfighter/Legends)

The TIE/ph "Phantom" Multi-Role Stealth Starfighter, also referred to as the TIE/ph phantom, TIE Phantom or Phantom V38, was a prototype TIE Series starfighter developed by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. A modified V38 assault fighter, the TIE phantom was the result of a development project led by Grand Admiral Martio Batch and was equipped with both deflector shields and a hyperdrive, along with a technology not seen for decades&mdash;a stygium cloaking device.
