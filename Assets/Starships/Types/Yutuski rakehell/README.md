# Yutuski rakehell

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yutuski rakehell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yutuski_rakehell)

The Yutuski rakehell was a model of large warship used by the empire of the Tionese despot Xim during the later stages of its expansionist period. Named for the planet Yutusk, the starships were believed to be cruiser-class by Ximologist historians thousands of years after the reign of Xim.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Yutuski rakehell
- **Affiliation:** Xim's empire
