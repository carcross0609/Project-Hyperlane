# Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dual_Hemisphere-Omni_Support_Vessel)

The Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel, otherwise identified as the DH-Omni Support Vessel, DH-O Support Vessel, or simply as the DH-O and dubbed as the Separatist supply ship or Separatist support ship, or Separatist transport, was a model of massive transport and supply ship manufactured by Hoersch-Kessel Drive, Inc. and utilized by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars.
