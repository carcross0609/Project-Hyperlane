# Spider battleship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spider battleship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_battleship)

The Spider battleship was a nickname that was given by the Alliance of Free Planets to one of the capital ships that was present among the Nagai fleet at Kinooine during the Nagai–Tof War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spider battleship
- **Designer:**
  - Faruun
  - Nagai
- **Manufacturer:** Faruun
- **Type:** Spider battleship
- **Countermeasures:** Cloaking device
- **Role:** Battleship
- **Firstuse:** Nagai–Tof War
- **Affiliation:** Nagai
