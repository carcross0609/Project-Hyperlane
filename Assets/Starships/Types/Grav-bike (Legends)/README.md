# Grav-bike

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grav-bike/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grav-bike/Legends)

A grav-bike was a space speeder of Verpine design, used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic. A group of Alliance operatives used the grav-bikes during a mission to an Imperial base on an asteroid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grav-bike
- **Designer:** Verpine
- **Role:** Personal transport
