# Jaamn

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jaamn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaamn)

The Jaamn was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport that was apparently captured by the Rebel Alliance at some point. The ship was sent to repair the BFF-1 bulk freighter Op Mus which had been stranded in space after an equipment malfunction. Keyan Farlander, while flying his starfighter, watched over the repairs in case Imperial forces attempted to take either ship.
