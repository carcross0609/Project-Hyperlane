# TIE/hu "Hunter" Multi-Role Starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE/hu "Hunter" Multi-Role Starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/hu_%22Hunter%22_Multi-Role_Starfighter)

The TIE/hu "Hunter" Multi-Role Starfighter, commonly referred to as the TIE Hunter, was a starfighter from the TIE Series used during the Galactic Civil War by the Galactic Empire. Developed by the Empire as a means of countering the Rebel Alliance's T-65 X-wing starfighter, the TIE Hunter was used exclusively by storm commandos.
