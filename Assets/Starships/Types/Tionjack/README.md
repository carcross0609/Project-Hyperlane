# Tionjack

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tionjack](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tionjack)

"Tionjack" was a term used to describe the warships of the Honorable Union of Desevro & Tion during the Tionese War. They were described as copper-colored with red sigils on their conning towers, and were armed with incendiary weapons, dioxis flares, pressure bombs, and plasma cannons that could be used in orbital bombardments.
