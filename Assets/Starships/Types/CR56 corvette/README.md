# CR56 corvette

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CR56 corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR56_corvette)

The CR56 corvette was a class of corvette manufactured by Corellian Engineering Corporation during the Republic Era. One CR56, Assurance, served in the Republic Diplomatic Fleet of the Galactic Republic's Judicial Forces and operated in the Slice against criminal elements such as the Riftwalkers. Assurance was led by Captain Pell Baylo, and served with Jedi Masters Mace Windu and Depa Billaba.
