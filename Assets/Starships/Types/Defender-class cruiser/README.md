# Defender-class cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Defender-class cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defender-class_cruiser)

The Defender-class cruiser was a type of cruiser manufactured by Mon Calamari Shipyards in the years after the Galactic Civil War in the New Republic Era by 9 ABY. At that time, the cruiser was considered new and was deployed in numbers by the New Republic Defense Fleet in several roles, including patrol, prisoner transport, and combat, such as during the Fenril crisis. Multiple ships comprised the New Republic fleet led by General Hera Syndulla, while others were stationed above Corellia.
