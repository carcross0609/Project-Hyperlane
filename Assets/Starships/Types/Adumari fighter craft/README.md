# Adumari fighter craft

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adumari fighter craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adumari_fighter_craft)

An Admurai fighter craft was a ship used in combat by the people of Adumar. The craft was used in the pursuit of Jedi Knight Jacen Solo and his apprentice Ben Skywalker after they spied on and retrieved video footage of an illegal weapons factory on Adumar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Adumari fighter craft
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Affiliation:** Adumar
