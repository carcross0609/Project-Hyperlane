# IPV-1

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IPV-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IPV-1)

The IPV-1 was a type of vessel used by planetary defense forces during the Republic Era. One IPV-1 was captured by the Vile and modified in the Slice, however, it was captured by Hotwire and several enforcers and members of the Riftwalkers gang. The IPV-1 was manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems.

- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
