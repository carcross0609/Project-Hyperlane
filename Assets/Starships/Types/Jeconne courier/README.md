# Jeconne courier

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jeconne courier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jeconne_courier)

A Jeconne courier was a type of transport available for rent on the planet Hosnian Prime. They were considered modern and stylish. Twenty four years after the Battle of Endor, Senator Ransolm Casterfo rented a Jeconne courier for his investigation of the Amaxine warriors on the planet Daxam IV.
