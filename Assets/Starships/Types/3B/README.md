# 3B

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [3B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3B)

The 3B was a model of shuttle used by the Galactic Empire. Between 1 BBY and 4 ABY, Vardos Star Scouts member Deeta Kardon purposefully misidentified an approaching Lambda-class T-4a shuttle as a 3B in order to goad her friend Zan Videx into responding while they and fellow scout Drik Maylon waited for the shuttle to land in Kestro City on the planet Vardos. Vrix nearly started to list the differences between the two models before realising he was being baited.
