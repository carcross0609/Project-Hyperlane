# Fetch

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fetch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fetch)

Fetch was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. Fetch was stationed on the MC80 Star Cruiser Link that was attempting to warn the Rebel Fleet stationed on Sullust that the second Death Star was fully operational and the Rebels were heading into a trap. However, the hyperdrive engines had overloaded and the ship was stranded. Fetch was then dispatched to deliver Fuel Rods from Class-C cargo container Rubol when it was intercepted and destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele.
