# Cronian battlebird

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cronian battlebird/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cronian_battlebird/Legends)

A Cronese battlebird (or Cronian battlebird) was a warship, presumably built by the Kingdom of Cron, during the Pre-Republic era. They originally fought under King Ferece against Xer of Argai, then for him as their king, Xer VIII in the Cronese Sweeps. They would later serve Xer's son Xim the Despot in the Xim Wars.
