# Subjugator-class heavy cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Subjugator-class heavy cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Subjugator-class_heavy_cruiser)

The Subjugator-class heavy cruiser was a type of heavy cruiser, battleship, dreadnought, and Star Dreadnought designed by Ruggle Schmong for use by the Confederacy of Independent Systems' naval forces from a discarded SoroSuub Corporation starship design. Potentially the largest warship that served at the time of the Clone Wars, the Subjugator was heavily armed with hundreds of laser cannons of different types, ion cannons, flak guns, proton torpedoes, heavy tractor beams, and a pair of mega-ion cannons capable of disabling the power of an entire fleet of capital ships in a single blast. The heavy cruiser carried over two-hundred droid starfighters, and a multitude of boarding ships, landing craft, assault vehicles, and shuttles.
