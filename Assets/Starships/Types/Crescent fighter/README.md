# Crescent fighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Crescent fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crescent_fighter)

Crescent fighters were starfighters used by Eiram's military during the Eiram and E'ronoh War. After Plana Van discovered a transport on the moon Eirie, Commander Ailee ordered her to search the transport, and sent four crescent fighters as backup.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Crescent fighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Eiram's military
