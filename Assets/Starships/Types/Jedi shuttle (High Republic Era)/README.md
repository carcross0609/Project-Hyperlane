# Jedi shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jedi shuttle (High Republic Era)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_shuttle_%28High_Republic_Era%29)

A Jedi shuttle was operated during the High Republic Era. Around 233 BBY, Jedi Master Zia Zaldor Zanna piloted one during her trip to the planet Rex Strata with Jedi Initiate Kai Brightstar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jedi shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
