# Trade Federation gunship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trade Federation gunship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trade_Federation_gunship)

A Trade Federation gunship was a class of starship used by the Trade Federation around 32 BBY. One of the vessels appeared at the end of a mission to Obredaan, suggesting to the Jedi Order a link between the Trade Federation and the Jin'ha warriors. The class was also used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Several were present on Vjun in 19 BBY.
