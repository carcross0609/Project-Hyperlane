# Paccian shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Paccian shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paccian_shuttle)

A type of shuttle was used by the Paccian Governance. Around 18 BBY, Paccosh official Uingali foar Marocsaa used several shuttles to avoid a plasma sphere attack on his Nikardun blockade frigate Aelos during an attack at the Paccosh homeworld of Rapacc carried out by Kilji Illumine General Crofyp's task force. Sometime after, the Aelos visited the Chiss world of Sposia, where a Paccian shuttle carried Chiss Senior Captain Thrawn and a Vagaari gravity well projector to the blockade frigate. As a shuttle flew toward the Aelos, Admiral Ar'alani of the Chiss Ascendancy, government of the Chiss species, inquired Senior Commander Biclian if the shuttle was in fact a shuttle, receiving confirmation and a question, then, Ar'alani stated that it was a Paccian shuttle.
