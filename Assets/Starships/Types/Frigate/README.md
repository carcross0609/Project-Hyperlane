# Frigate

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frigate)

Frigates were a military class of starship measuring around 200-400 meters in length, and served various functions, such as combat, medical or supply purposes. The pirate Q'anah's fleet included several frigates. The Chiss Ascendancy utilized Chiss frigates. During the Clone Wars, both the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems used frigates, with the former deploying Republic frigates and Pelta-class frigates. The latter made use of Munificent-class star frigates. The Galactic Empire also used frigates including the Salliche, as did the Alliance to Restore the Republic who fielded the frigate Promise and the Redemption, a Nebulon-B escort frigate which served as a medical ship.
