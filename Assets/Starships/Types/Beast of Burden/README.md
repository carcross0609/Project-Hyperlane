# Beast of Burden

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beast of Burden](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beast_of_Burden)

The Beast of Burden was a Hardcell-class interstellar transport used by the CIS Navy of the Separatist Alliance during the Clone Wars. At one point, the Beast of Burden traveled near a Separatist Munificent-class star frigate. It was equipped with six sublight engines and three forward laser cannons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Beast of Burden
- **Model:** Hardcell-class interstellar transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Engine:** Sublight engines (6)
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (3)
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Navy:** Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy
