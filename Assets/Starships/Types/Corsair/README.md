# Corsair

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Corsair](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corsair)

A corsair was a type of warship and capital ship. The Lioaoin Regime used a variety of starships, including corsairs, which were used as a patrol ship. During the New Republic Era, Pirate King Gorian Shard owned a corsair of the Cumulus-class, which he used to lead his gang of pirates. Shard's corsair was equipped with fourteen ventral ball-shaped turret gunwales. Shard's corsair had an anvil-shaped prow.
