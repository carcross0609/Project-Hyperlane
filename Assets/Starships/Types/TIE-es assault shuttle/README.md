# TIE/es assault shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/es assault shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/es_assault_shuttle)

The TIE/es assault shuttle, or TIE Echelon, was a transport of the TIE fighter series, originally developed by the Galactic Empire, and later used by the First Order. Functioning as a gunship and assault shuttle, it possessed four heavy laser cannons as well as a dual light laser cannon turret, was capable of delivering 12 crew members plus additional cargo, had two clusters composed of three ion engines each (one for each side), as well as advanced sensors capable of monitoring and scanning enemy communications to allow them to avoid hostile encounters.
