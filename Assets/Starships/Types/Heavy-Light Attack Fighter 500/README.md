# Heavy/Light Attack Fighter 500

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy/Light Attack Fighter 500](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy/Light_Attack_Fighter_500)

The Heavy/Light Attack Fighter 500, commonly known as the HLAF-500 starfighter,was a modified version of Corellian Engineering Corporation's earlier LAF-250 patrol fighter, which the Corellian Defense Force demanded to compensate for the combat weakness of the original design.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy/Light Attack Fighter 500
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Cost:**
  - 70,000 credits
  - 40,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 12 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,300 kmh
- **Engine:** 9X2 fusial thrust engines
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2.0
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 2 light laser cannons
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
- **Crew:** 1
- **Capacity:** 65 kilograms
- **Consumables:** 2 days
- **Role:** Multi-purpose starfighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Corellian Defense Force
  - Sacorrian Triad
