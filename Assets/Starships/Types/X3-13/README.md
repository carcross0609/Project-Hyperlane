# X3-13

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [X3-13](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X3-13)

The X3-13, also known as the tactical infiltration pod, was a model of short-distance hyperdrive pod manufactured by Arakyd Industries and used by the Galactic Empire for short-distance transportation and reconnaissance. Due to its role, it sacrificed maneuverability and weaponry for advanced communications equipment, including a long-range transmitter and encryption abilities. The pods were designed to look like normal escape pods in order to aid in infiltration. One such pod was used by the E-XD-series infiltrator droid EXD-9 to travel to Atollon to search for the Phoenix Cell's base.
