# Delemedian starhopper

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Delemedian starhopper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delemedian_starhopper)

A Delemedian starhopper was a type of jump drive&ndash;equipped starship that was in existence by the year 212 BBY. One starhopper belonged to the Human Bosbit Matarcher, who experienced time dilation due to faulty relativistic shielding upon using the starhopper's jump drive. When Matarcher emerged from hyperspace in 22 BBY, the Delemedian starhopper was considered an antique.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Delemedian starhopper
- **Type:** Starhopper
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Crew:** At least one pilot
- **Othersystems:** Relativistic shielding
- **Firstuse:** By 212 BBY
