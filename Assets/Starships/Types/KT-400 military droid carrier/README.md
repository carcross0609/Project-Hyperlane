# KT-400 military droid carrier

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KT-400 military droid carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KT-400_military_droid_carrier)

The KT-400 military droid carrier was a light freighter, and a common sight throughout the galaxy both before and after the Jedi Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KT-400 military droid carrier
- **Manufacturer:** Republic Fleet Systems
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Cost:** 280,000 credits (150,000 used)
- **Length:** 24 meters
- **Max Speed:** 800 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Navicomputer
- **Armament:** Dual laser cannons (2)
- **Crew:** 8
- **Passengers:** 400 (bipedal droids)
- **Capacity:** 40 tons
- **Consumables:** 2 weeks
- **Availability:** Restricted
- **Role:** Military droid carrier
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Sith Empire
