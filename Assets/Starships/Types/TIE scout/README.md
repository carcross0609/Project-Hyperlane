# TIE scout

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE scout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE_scout)

The TIE scout, also known as a scout-class TIE, was a starfighter variant of the TIE family of Imperial warships that was equipped with a hyperdrive and was used for reconnaissance missions. As with all space crafts of the Imperial Era-TIE line, the scout ship was manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TIE scout
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Line:** TIE fighter series
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Navicomputer
- **Role:** Scout ship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
