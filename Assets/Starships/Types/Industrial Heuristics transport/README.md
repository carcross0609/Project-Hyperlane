# Industrial Heuristics transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Industrial Heuristics transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Heuristics_transport)

Four Industrial Heuristics transports were used to transport 1,700 children and adolescents recruited from throughout the Daimanate as student interns for an arxeum&mdash;a mobile war university that the Sith Lord Daiman had purchased from the Bactranate. However, this deal had actually been a bait intended to lure his older brother Sith Lord Odion to the planet Gazzari. During the Battle of Gazzari, the four transports landed on the planet and disembarked the students. However, Odionate forces destroyed the arxeum and almost slaughtered the students. However, the students were rescued by the Jedi Knight Kerra Holt who managed to evacuate them aboard the mercenary Jarrow Rusher's transport Diligence.
