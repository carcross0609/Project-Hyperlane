# Luxe Flightwing

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Luxe Flightwing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luxe_Flightwing)

The Luxe Flightwing was a limited-production, swift, gleaming white starship. The Flightwing consisted of a curved nose and swept-back wings, having such a unique profile that it was nearly unforgettable to those who saw it. Its exterior surface was made of a rare, white ore.
