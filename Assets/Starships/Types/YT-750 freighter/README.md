# YT-750 freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YT-750 freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-750_freighter)

The YT-750 freighter was a model of freighter utilized during the High Republic Era. The former Jedi and monster hunter Ty Yorrick owned a battered YT-750 freighter that she did not care to name, though the starship had various registry numbers in the Galactic Republic records.

- **Name:** YT-750 freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
