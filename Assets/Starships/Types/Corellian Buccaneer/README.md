# Corellian Buccaneer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corellian Buccaneer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellian_Buccaneer)

The Corellian Buccaneer was a corvette manufactured by Corellian Engineering Corporation around the founding of the New Republic. Its design was based on that of the CR90 corvette, and it was armed with ten double turbolasers. Elta Besk owned a Corellian Buccaneer known as the Solar Dynamica.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Corellian Buccaneer
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Armament:** 10 double turbolasers
- **Role:** Blockade runner
