# EE-730

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EE-730](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EE-730)

The EE-730 was a transport manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards that could hold six passengers and crew total. It could be equipped with six beds. The Rebel Alliance had at least one EE-730, which the Alliance used in a mission around the time of the Battle of Yavin. The transport was ambushed on Taanab and Caluan Ematt was captured.

## Specifications

- **Name:** EE-730
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** EE-730
- **Type:** Transport
- **Passengers:** 6 (passengers and crew)
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
