# IF-120 landing craft

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IF-120 landing craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IF-120_landing_craft)

The IF-120 landing craft, also known as Imperial Landing Force ship, was an Imperial starship designed by Sienar Fleet Systems for shuttling troops to a planet and providing support. Much larger than other craft of its type, the IF-120 was designed to serve as a mobile command base after landing. The IF-120 saw use during the Galactic Civil War, including on an expedition to rediscover the lost world of Seoul 5.
