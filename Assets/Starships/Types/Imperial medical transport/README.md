# Imperial medical transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial medical transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_medical_transport)

The Imperial medical transport was a class of transport used by the reconstituted Sith Empire around the time of the sacking of Coruscant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial medical transport
- **Crew:**
  - Members of the Imperial Medical Corps
  - Medical droids
- **Role:** Hospital ship
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
