# Multi-environment Space Boat

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Multi-environment Space Boat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Multi-environment_Space_Boat)

The Multi-environment Space Boat was a space transport also capable of traveling in atmosphere and underwater. The Explorer was a modified Multi-environment Space Boat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Multi-environment Space Boat
- **Type:** Multi-environment vehicle
- **Engine:**
  - Repulsorlift
  - Sublight drive
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 0.5
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Sensor:**
  - Energy-wave detector
  - Radar
  - Sonar
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 2 double laser cannons
  - Tractor beam projector
- **Complement:**
  - 2 speeder-rafts
  - 6 glider-sails
- **Crew:** 6
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 150 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 4 months
