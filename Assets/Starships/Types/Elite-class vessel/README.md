# Elite-class vessel

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Elite-class vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elite-class_vessel)

The Elite-class vessel was a class of science vessel developed by the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era. The Innovator was an Elite-class vessel designed by Vam Targes and constructed at the Cyclor Shipyards by the insectoid Cyclorrians. The vessel had a long and tapered arrowhead shape, with sensors lining a raised area at the rear that housed the operations center. The Innovator was showcased at the Republic Fair on the planet Valo, but was destroyed when the Nihil marauders launched an attack on the celebration.
