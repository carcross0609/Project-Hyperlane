# Colossus I Beta Series asteroid tug

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Colossus I Beta Series asteroid tug](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Colossus_I_Beta_Series_asteroid_tug)

The Colossus I Beta Series asteroid tug was a type of starship manufactured by the BramDorc Corporation. By 44 ABY, the only Colossus I known to exist was Lando Calrissian's Rockhound.
