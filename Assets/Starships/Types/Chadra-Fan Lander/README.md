# Chadra-Fan Lander

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chadra-Fan Lander](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chadra-Fan_Lander)

The Chadra-Fan Lander was a starship that was used by the One Sith to transport their acolytes to Mala during the Battle of the Floating World in 139 ABY. During the fighting, the junk dealer Ania Solo and her Mon Calamari friend Sauk managed to blow up the landing craft, killing several Sith.
