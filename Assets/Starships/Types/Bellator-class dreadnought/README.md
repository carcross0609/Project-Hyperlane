# Bellator-class dreadnought

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bellator-class dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bellator-class_dreadnought)

The Bellator-class dreadnought was a class of Imperial Navy dreadnought that was manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards in limited numbers during the Imperial Era, when the Galactic Empire experimented with creating larger models of battlecruisers between 19 BBY and 3 BBY. Such experimentation also resulted in the creation of the Assertor-class Star Dreadnought, and both dreadnoughts were considered to be in the same family of dreadnoughts as the Mandator-class Dreadnought.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bellator-class dreadnought
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Bellator-class
- **Type:** Dreadnought
- **Firstuse:** Between 19 BBY and 3 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Navy
