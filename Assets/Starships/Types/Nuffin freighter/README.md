# Nuffin freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nuffin freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nuffin_freighter)

The Nuffin freighter was a type of freighter manufactured by the Nuffin species. Princess Nampi owned a Nuffin freighter and, around 5 BBY, used it to trap Jabba Desilijic Tiure aboard it. After her death, the Hutt took the starship to Cabrool Nuum on Smarteel and tried to sell it to him.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nuffin freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Nuffin species
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Armament:** Pulse rockets
- **Affiliation:**
  - Nuffin species
  - Princess Nampi
