# HT-150 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HT-150 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HT-150_light_freighter)

The HT-150 was a model of light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation during the High Republic Era. It included at least one cargo bay and two engines. One such freighter named the Iron Talon was used by prince Cyrus Vuundir of Vuundalla under his pirate alias Taborr Val-Dorn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HT-150 light freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** HT-150
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Bays:** Cargo bay
- **Role:** Transport
