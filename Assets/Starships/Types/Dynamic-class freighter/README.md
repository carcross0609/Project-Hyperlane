# Dynamic-class freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dynamic-class freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dynamic-class_freighter)

The Dynamic-class freighter was a model of starship which was considered ancient by 22 BBY. The family of the Gharal Rokura, who lived on the mining planet Langston, owned one such freighter, the Norriker, which they used to transport select cargoes of the planet's finest ores. After the older generations were killed during an attack by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars, Rokura loaned the Norriker to Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker in exchange for supplies.
