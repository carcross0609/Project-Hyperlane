# Coruscant Freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Coruscant Freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Freighter)

The Coruscant Freighter was a model of cargo freighter named for the planet Coruscant, where it was commonly used during the Clone Wars. One Coruscant Freighter was docked within the Borgo Prime shadowport around 9 ABY. During the First Order-Resistance War, at least one of these freighters served as part of Chewbacca and General Landonis Balthazar Calrissian's Citizens' Fleet at the Battle of Exegol between the Resistance and the Sith Eternal.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Coruscant Freighter
- **Type:** Cargo freighter
- **Engine:** Engines (4)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Role:** Cargo transport
- **Affiliation:** Citizens' Fleet
