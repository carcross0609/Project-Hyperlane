# Porax-28 starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Porax-28 starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Porax-28_starfighter)

The Porax-28 starfighter was a starfighter model in use in the late Republic Era. They were swift and sleek and often painted in vibrant colors. It was armed with laser cannons and its twin-engine scoops allowed the craft to accelerate to high speeds. The starighter shared its name with the Rogue-class Porax-38 starfighter manufactured by Baktoid Armor Workshop and Buuper Torsckil Abbey Devices. Multiple Porax-28 starfighters were in use by the criminal organization of the being known as Chulok on the lawless planet Metagos. The Jedi Master Mace Windu had also flown a Porax-28 starfighter during a reconnaissance mission to Mantis I around 37 BBY.
