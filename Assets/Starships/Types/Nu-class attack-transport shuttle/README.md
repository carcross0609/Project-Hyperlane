# Nu-class attack/transport shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nu-class attack/transport shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nu-class_attack/transport_shuttle)

The Nu-class attack/transport shuttle, also known as the Nu-class attack shuttle or Republic attack shuttle, was a model of shuttle used by the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. In the aftermath of the war, the Nu-class shuttles were absorbed into the fledgling Imperial Navy. It was the predecessor of the Lambda-class T-4a shuttle. One transport fell into the hands of the Free Ryloth Movement, and was used during the Mission to Ryloth.
