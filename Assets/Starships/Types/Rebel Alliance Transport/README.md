# Rebel Alliance Transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rebel Alliance Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rebel_Alliance_Transport)

The Rebel Alliance Transport was a model of transport starship used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic toward the end of the Galactic Civil War. Once such ship was used during an assault on an Imperial outpost on Endor shortly after the destruction of the DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rebel Alliance Transport
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
