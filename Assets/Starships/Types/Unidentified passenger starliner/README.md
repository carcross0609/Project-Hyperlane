# Unidentified passenger starliner

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified passenger starliner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_passenger_starliner)

A type of passenger starliner was active during the New Republic Era. Multiple starliners were present on the city of Mos Espa on Tatooine. During Daimyo Boba Fett's activities on Tatooine, one starliner's passengers included members of the Pyke Syndicate. The Mandalorian bounty hunter, Din Djarin traveled aboard the starline on a non-stop flight from Glavis Ringworld to Mos Eisley.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified passenger starliner
- **Type:** Starliner
- **Engine:** At least 3 engines
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Othersystems:** Landing gear
- **Role:** Transportation
- **Affiliation:** Star Liner Travel
