# Hidden Chain Fleet Ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hidden Chain Fleet Ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Chain_Fleet_Ship)

Hidden Chain Fleet Ships were modified Leviathan-class transports that were used by the Mandalorians of the Hidden Chain as capital ships during the Third Galactic War. At least four of these vessels were stationed on Ruhnuk during the Mandalorian civil war and two of then fought the forces of Mandalore the Avenger's faction during to the Showdown on Ruhnuk. Later, a Hidden Chain Ship was used to transport the Holocron Machine manufactured by the Hidden Chain leader Heta Kol to create an army of Force-sensitive soldiers. The ship was eventually destroyed when the Eternal Alliance boarded the vessel to destroy the machine and recover Darth Nul's holocron.
