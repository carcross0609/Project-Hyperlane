# Trade frigate

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Trade frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trade_frigate)

A trade frigate was a kind of starship affiliated with the Galactic traders. All trade frigates were grounded at Alderaan as the Galactic Empire made its move on the Aquilaean system, and their captains and first officers were escorted to the Ministry of Transport. King Kayos of Aquilae sought to ensure that the guild would support his planet against the Empire through their frigates.
