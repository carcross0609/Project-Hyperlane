# Hospital ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hospital ship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hospital_ship/Legends)

A hospital ship or medical frigate was a medical spacecraft often accompanying large or medium-sized military fleets to provide back-up in battle, though it was also sometimes found without military company. Hospital ships were mobile hospitals equipped with medical gear, such as bacta tanks and medical droids.
