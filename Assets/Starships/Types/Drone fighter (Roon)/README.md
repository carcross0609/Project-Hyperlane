# Drone fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Drone fighter (Roon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drone_fighter_%28Roon%29)

Drone fighters were starfighters used by Governor Koong's Tawntoom province of Roon during the early years of the Galactic Empire. They were piloted by a droid and could be launched from Koong's galley, the Roon Clipper.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Drone fighter
- **Type:** Droid starfighter
- **Armament:** Laser cannon
- **Crew:** Droid pilot (1)
- **Affiliation:** Tawntoom
