# Pantolomin cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pantolomin cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pantolomin_cruiser)

A Pantolomin cruiser was a type of luxury cruiser, containing casinos and cantinas. One Pantolomin cruiser owned by the Black Sun was visited by Han Solo and his fellow cadets at the Carida Academy.

- **Name:** Pantolomin cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Black Sun
