# Penumbra-1 shattersprite

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Penumbra-1 shattersprite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Penumbra-1_shattersprite)

The Penumbra-1 shattersprite was a vessel that, according to legend, was used by the early Jedi. In stories, they were used to attack the Ordu Aspectu citadel before fleeing as the station disappeared. Later, while part of the Accresker Penal Legion in 1 ABY, Chelli found the gundeck of a Penumbra-1 aboard Accresker Jail. She believed the derelict had been aboard the prison complex for centuries and suspected that it housed Force-sensitive relics.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Penumbra-1 shattersprite
- **Engine:** At least four
- **Othersystems:** Gundeck
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
