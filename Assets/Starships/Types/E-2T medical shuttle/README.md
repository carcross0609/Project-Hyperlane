# E-2T medical shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-2T medical shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-2T_medical_shuttle)

The E-2T medical shuttle, also known as the E-2T medshuttle, was a fast ambulance craft used by the Galactic Empire to carry wounded pilots away during battle. Several of these ships were stationed on the first Death Star. The shuttle had limited hyperdrive capability and generally carried a crew of six on board, with facilities to transport and maintain twelve more Human-sized patients. The shuttle was unarmed and marked as noncombat medical transport, meaning that neither the Rebel Alliance or the Empire would fire on it.
