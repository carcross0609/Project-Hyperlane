# AEA-500 mineral extractor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AEA-500 mineral extractor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AEA-500_mineral_extractor)

The House of Tagge Mining Company AEA-500 mineral extractor was produced during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AEA-500 mineral extractor
- **Manufacturer:** House of Tagge Mining Company
- **Type:** Contained mining vehicle
- **Cost:** 25,000 credits
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Max Accel:** 160 kmh
- **Armament:** Tri-beam lasers
- **Crew:** 1 driver, 1 dig operator
- **Capacity:** 10 metric tons
- **Affiliation:** Tagge Mining Company
