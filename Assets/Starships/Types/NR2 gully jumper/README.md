# NR2 gully jumper

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [NR2 gully jumper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NR2_gully_jumper)

The NR2 gully jumper, also known as the NR2 light transport, was a vessel produced by Celestial Industries and used by the Galactic Republic during the Great Galactic War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** NR2 gully jumper
- **Manufacturer:** Celestial Industries
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Role:** Troop transport
