# Tallaan Clipper

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tallaan Clipper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tallaan_Clipper)

Tallaan Clippers were a make of transport vessel with multiple decks used during the early days of the Galactic Empire. One Tallaan Clipper in use in 19 BBY was the Space Slug which the Kedorzhan Senator Dewell Bronk and the Human Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi booked passage on to escape Coruscant after the declaration of the New Order, the former fleeing persecution for his involment in the Delegation of 2000, and the later attempting to transport an infant Luke Skywalker to Tatooine.
