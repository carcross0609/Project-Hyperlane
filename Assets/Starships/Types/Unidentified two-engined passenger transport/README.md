# Unidentified two-engined passenger transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified two-engined passenger transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_two-engined_passenger_transport)

A type of civilian transport was used during the Imperial Era. At least two transports were present on Anchorhead, Tatooine, one of which was boarded by fugitive Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi to travel to Daiyu. The transport had a sloped hull with a flat ventral and dorsal side.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified two-engined passenger transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Engine:** Sublight engines (2)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Role:** Passenger transport
