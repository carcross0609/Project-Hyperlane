# Assault Frigate Mark II

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Assault Frigate Mark II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_Frigate_Mark_II)

The Assault Frigate Mark II was manufactured by Rendili StarDrive and operated by the Rebel Alliance Navy during the Galactic Civil War. The nascent Rebellion retrofitted Republic Era Dreadnought-class cruisers to create the Assault Frigate as a capital ship with a smaller crew requirement and greater maneuverability and speed despite the sacrifice of some durability. The Assault Frigate also retained a similar armament to the weaponry of the Dreadnought-class starship, allowing it to engage much heavier capital ships, including the Star Destroyers of the Imperial Navy.
