# Skipship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skipship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skipship)

A skipship was a type of starship used by the Rakatan Infinite Empire. They were outfitted with Rakatan deflector shields and beam weapons powered by the crystals that focused the Force. Their drives worked by zeroing in on worlds that had high Force concentrations.
