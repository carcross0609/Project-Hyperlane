# Z-95-AF4 Headhunter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-95-AF4 Headhunter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-95-AF4_Headhunter/Legends)

The Z-95-AF4 Headhunter, also known as the AF4 series, was a variant of the Z-95 Headhunter. Manufactured by Incom and Subpro Corporation, it was the last starfighter of the Headhunter series to be produced before the two companies ended their partnership in 22 BBY.
