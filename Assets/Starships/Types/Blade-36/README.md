# Blade-36

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blade-36](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blade-36)

The Blade-36 was a starfighter, presumably of Adumari manufacture, and successor of the Blade-28, Blade-30, Blade-32, and Blade-34. One was used in the mission to prevent the destruction of Kessel in 43.5 ABY.
