# WarMaster

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [WarMaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WarMaster)

A WarMaster was a capital ship classification used by the Grysk warlike species. The Grysks used two classes of WarMasters, the Shatter-class and the StoneCrusher-class. The Shatter-class was three to four times larger than a Kilji war cruiser and around the same size as a Chiss Groundlion man-of-war. The Shatter-class WarMasters were equipped with engines and a hyperdrive. Their armament included spectrum laser, missiles, and dibber missiles. The StoneCrusher-class WarMasters were equipped with engines and a hyperdrive. They were armed with spectrum lasers and missiles.
