# Zebra starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zebra starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zebra_starfighter)

The Zebra starfighter was a basic and inexpensive single-pilot starfighter manufactured by Hyrotii Vehicle Works.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Zebra starfighter
- **Manufacturer:** Hyrotii Corporation
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Cost:** 80,000 credits (30,000 used)
- **Max Speed:** 1,000 km/h
- **Hdsystem:** No
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (2+)
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Capacity:** 65 kg
- **Consumables:** 1 day
- **Role:** Ground-assault fighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Tenloss Syndicate
  - Riders of the Maelstrom
  - Virgillian Free Alignment
  - New Republic
  - Qektoth Confederation
