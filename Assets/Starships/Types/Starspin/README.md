# Starspin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starspin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starspin)

The Starspin was a type of small starship that was manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards. A Starspin was used by the Black Sun operative Mathal during his ill-fated trip to meet with Tarnese Bleyd. The ship was presumably hyperdrive-equipped, however it was relatively small in size.
