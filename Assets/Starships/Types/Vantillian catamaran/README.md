# Vantillian catamaran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vantillian catamaran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vantillian_catamaran)

A Vantillian catamaran was a type of starship that existed in the galaxy. Baron Administrator Lando Calrissian owned one such ship which was docked in the western skipdock of Cloud City on the planet Bespin. After killing the last Imperial sergeant in Cloud City, Calrissian suggested sending the Vantillian catamaran to Han Solo and Leia Organa as a gift for their son, which they could take out on family cruises. Calrissian's friend Lobot deemed the gift as acceptable.
