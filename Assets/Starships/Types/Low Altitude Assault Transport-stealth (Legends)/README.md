# Low Altitude Assault Transport/stealth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Low Altitude Assault Transport/stealth/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Low_Altitude_Assault_Transport/stealth/Legends)

The Low Altitude Assault Transport/stealth, or LAAT/s, was a stealth version of the Low Altitude Assault Transport. They were used by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. Unlike most versions of the LAAT, the LAAT/s had a hyperdrive.
