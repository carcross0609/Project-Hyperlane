# Gunship Bomber

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gunship Bomber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gunship_Bomber)

The Gunship Bomber was a bomber employed by the Clone Army during the Clone Wars similar in appearance to the LAAT/i. During the Battle of Aargonar, a Gunship Bomber Enhanced had crash-landed during the battle, where it was discovered by Jedi Knights Anakin Skywalker and A'Sharad Hett and later fixed up and used to lure a Gouka dragon to a Separatist hideout via its engines, manipulating the dragon's natural attraction to starship engines.
