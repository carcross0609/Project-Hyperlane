# Kruk battle-wagon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kruk battle-wagon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kruk_battle-wagon)

The Kruk battle-wagon was a class of starship from the Clone Wars. Two were used by the Frezhlix in their attempted blockade of Sif'kric in 19 ABY.
