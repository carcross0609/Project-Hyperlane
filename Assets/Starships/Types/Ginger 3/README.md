# Ginger 3

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ginger 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ginger_3)

Ginger 3 was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport that was part of Ginger group that served the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It fought at the Second Battle of Javin where they attacked the Frigate Fogger with torpedoes. They were unsuccessful and were destroyed instead.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ginger 3
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Width:** 7.8 meters
- **Height:** 5.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Krupx Munitions MG7 proton torpedo launcher
  - Proton torpedo
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Fleet:** Ginger group
