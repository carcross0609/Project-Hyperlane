# Modular taskforce cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Modular taskforce cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Modular_taskforce_cruiser)

The modular taskforce cruiser, also known as the Imperial star hauler, was a support vessel class built by Tagge Industries Shipyards Limited for the Dark Empire. These ships functioned as a generic framework on which Mission Function Modules could be mounted and reconfigured as necessary. In 10 ABY, the modular taskforce cruiser Reliance was infiltrated by New Republic commandos to strike at the citadel of Emperor Palpatine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Modular taskforce cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Tagge Industries Shipyards Limited
- **Type:** Multi-task medium transport
- **Cost:**
  - 2,500,000 credits (base cruiser)
  -   +750,000 credits (Hospital module)
  -   +425,000 credits (Inquisition module)
  -   +6,300,000 credits (Observation module)
  -   +500,000 credits (Rescue module)
  -   +1,000,000 credits (Survey module)
- **Length:** 1,150 meters
- **Max Speed:** 800 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 3
  - Class 7
- **Sensor:** Standard sensor suite
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 15 medium turbolasers
  - Inquisition module: Orbital Data Net Eraser unit, Sterilization spore probes, 5 magnepulse bombs
- **Complement:**
  - Hospital module: 5 shuttles
  - Observation module: 12 TIE/LN starfighters, 500 probe droids
  - Survey module: 100 observation airspeeders, 5 Skipray blastboats, 6 TIE/sa bombers
- **Crew:**
  - Varies according to mission profile
  -   Hospital module: 225 doctors, 400 nurses, 1,500 emergency medical technicians, 1,000 medical droids
  -   Inquisition module: 100 Inquisitors, 3,660 CompForce/observation staff, 10,000 interrogation droids
  -   Observation module: 1,550 intelligence officers and communication specialists
  -   Rescue module: 140 doctors, 2,130 technicians
  -   Survey module: 2,000 scouts, 1,500 technicians, 5,000 droids
- **Passengers:**
  - Varies according to mission profile
  -   Hospital module: 2,750 in bacta tanks, 1,000 in quarantine, 19,600 other patients
- **Capacity:**
  - Varies according to mission profile
  -   Hospital module: 5,000 metric tons
  -   Inquisition module: 15,000 prefabricated disintegration chambers, 2 prefabricated garrison bases,
  - 1 complete orbital nightcloak system
  -   Observation module: 5,000 metric tons
  -   Rescue module: 40,000 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 6 months
- **Othersystems:** Mission Function Modules
- **Role:**
  - Multipurpose cruiser
  -   Hospital ship (Hospital module)
  -   Planetary pacification (Inquisition module)
  -   Espionage (Observation module)
  -   Repair and recovery vessel (Rescue module)
  -   Scout vessel (Survey module)
  -   Cargo hauler
- **Affiliation:** Dark Empire
