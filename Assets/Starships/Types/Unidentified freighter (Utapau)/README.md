# Unidentified freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified freighter (Utapau)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_freighter_%28Utapau%29)

A type of freighter was found on the planet Utapau during the Clone Wars. Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi and Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker commandeered such a vessel during their mission to Utapau, in order to pursue a Separatist shuttle ferrying an enormous kyber crystal to a Separatist supply ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Armament:** 1 laser turret
- **Escapepods:** 6
- **Crew:** 2
