# Paataatus heavy frigate

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Paataatus heavy frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paataatus_heavy_frigate)

A class of heavy frigate was used by the Paataatus Hiveborn's armed forces. It was considered a medium-sized starship, and was consistent with the standard Paataatus heavy warship designs, as it was wide and flat, heavily armored with minimal top point defenses, main lasers positioned on the leading edge, and missile tubes added underneath the bow. The heavy frigate's design was considered unusual among the various alien designs met by the Chiss Ascendancy, but was considered to fit well with the Paataatus strategy of approaching an opponent with lasers firing, then pitching up to fire the missiles.
