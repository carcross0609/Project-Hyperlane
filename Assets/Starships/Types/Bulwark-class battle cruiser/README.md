# Bulwark-class battle cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bulwark-class battle cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bulwark-class_battle_cruiser)

The Bulwark-class battle cruiser was utilized by the naval branch of the Separatist Alliance during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic, between 22 BBY and 19 BBY. Sullustan Admiral Dua Ningo, an officer of the Sullustan Home Guard, which had allied with the Confederacy, was charged with overseeing the construction of a flotilla of battlecruisers. Anticipating the fleet's completion two years after the outbreak of the war on the planet Geonosis, Ningo had hoped to deploy the Bulwark-class battle cruisers in an attack against the Republic's capital world of Coruscant and other Core Worlds. However, Ningo overestimated the production and it still was not finished in 19 BBY. Clone Intelligence was aware of these plans and the Bulwark flotilla was mentioned in an entry on the Sullustan Home Guard that was written by ARC trooper Muzzle and published in the survival compendium "Neyo's Almanac of Clankers." It shared its name with the Bulwark system.
