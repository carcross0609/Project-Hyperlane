# SGS-S1 Condor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SGS-S1 Condor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SGS-S1_Condor)

The SGS-S1 Condor was a gunship model produced by Republic Sienar Systems for the Republic Navy, which made use of the Condor during the Galactic War with the reconstituted Sith Empire. The SGS-S1 was developed over the course of a decade by Sienar, who designed it to be able to survive long battles and have a limited close combat capacity. In exchange for a limited sensor range and ammunition capacity, the Condor could carry dogfighting missiles and more powerful short-range laser cannons as its secondary and close-range weaponry, and was faster and more maneuverable than most other gunships.
