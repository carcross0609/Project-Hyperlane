# Stormtrooper transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stormtrooper transport (Skorii-Lei)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stormtrooper_transport_%28Skorii-Lei%29)

This stormtrooper transport was used by the Galactic Empire to transport its stormtroopers into battle. It was notably used to despatch troops at Skorii-Lei, when the Empire was looking for Leia Organa. The ship bore similarities to the AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport used by the First Order roughly thirty years later.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stormtrooper transport
- **Type:** Troop transport
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Stormtrooper Corps
