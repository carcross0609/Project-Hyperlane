# Blade-32 superiority fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blade-32 superiority fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blade-32_superiority_fighter)

The Blade-32 superiority fighter was the craft of choice for many Adumari pilots.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blade-32 superiority fighter
- **Manufacturer:** Tarrvin-on-Kallik
- **Line:** Blade series
- **Model:** Blade-32 superiority fighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Cost:** 60,000 credits
- **Length:** 18 meters
- **Max Speed:** 900 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** None (Blade-32 Beta equipped with Class 3 hyperdrive)
- **Sensor:** Close
- **Navigation:** Yes
- **Armament:**
  - Laser cannons (4; 2 forward-firing, 2 aft-firing)
  - Concussion missile launchers (2)
  -   16 missiles
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Aft Gunner (1)
- **Passengers:** 1 (if no gunner)
- **Capacity:** 150 kg
- **Consumables:** 1 week
- **Affiliation:** Adumar
