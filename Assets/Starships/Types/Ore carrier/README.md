# Ore carrier

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ore carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ore_carrier)

Ore carriers were carriers utilized to carry ore. In 19 BBY, Duke Solha utilized an ore carrier to transport cortosis obtained on his cortosis mine to his droid factory to manufacture cortosis B2 super battle droids for the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ore carrier
- **Type:** Carrier
- **Role:** Carrying ore
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Mokivj droid factory
