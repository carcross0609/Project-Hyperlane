# Star Courier

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Star Courier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Courier)

A Star Courier was a type of starship manufactured by Republic Sienar Systems for wealthy diplomats and business executives who traveled in dangerous areas. The Star Courier had a central spherical cabin for crew and passengers, and an experimental high-temperature ion engine system that required large radiator panels, which folded inward during landings. The courier's defenses included laser cannons and exotic sensor systems.
