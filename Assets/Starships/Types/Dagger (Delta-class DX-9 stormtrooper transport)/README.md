# Dagger

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dagger (Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dagger_%28Delta-class_DX-9_stormtrooper_transport%29)

Dagger was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport in use by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. It was used to transfer an Alliance team to secure the Imperial CR90 corvette Bixby during the ship's defection to the Rebellion.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dagger
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Width:** 7.8 meters
- **Height:** 5.8 meters
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
