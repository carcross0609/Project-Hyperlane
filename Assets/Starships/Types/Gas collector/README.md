# Gas collector

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gas collector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_collector)

A gas collector was a remote controlled vessel larger than most tramp freighters. Controlled from a central station and maintained by droids, gas collectors were used by BlasTech Industries to mine gas giants for prothium.
