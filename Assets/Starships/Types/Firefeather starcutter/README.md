# Firefeather starcutter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Firefeather starcutter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firefeather_starcutter)

Firefeather starcutters were rare and expensive starships. They could be recognized by their distinctive whistling sound. A member of the Rebel Alliance's council flew one such ship to Yavin 4 shortly before the beginning of the Galactic Civil War.
