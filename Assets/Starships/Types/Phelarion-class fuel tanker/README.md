# Phelarion-class fuel tanker

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phelarion-class fuel tanker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phelarion-class_fuel_tanker)

The Phelarion-class fuel tanker was a class of fuel tanker manufactured by Rendili StarDrive. It featured two large external fuel tanks and four rear fusion engines. It was also equipped with a deflector shield and a hangar that could accommodate at least three T-70 X-wing starfighters. In 34 ABY, a Phelarion-class tanker carrying fuel for the Resistance was intercepted by the First Order, who stole the fuel using their own tankers and rigged the ship to bomb the Resistance base.
