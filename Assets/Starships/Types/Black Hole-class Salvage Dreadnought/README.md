# Black Hole-class Salvage Dreadnought

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Black Hole-class Salvage Dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Hole-class_Salvage_Dreadnought)

The Black Hole-class Salvage Dreadnought, also known as an Ugor battle-wagon, was a heavily armed and armored Ugor starship capable of aggressive scavenging operations in space.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Black Hole-class Salvage Dreadnought
- **Manufacturer:** Ugor Salvage Company
- **Type:** Salvage Dreadnought
- **Length:** 150 meters (diameter)
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - Varied; each vessel has sixteen weapon hardpoints
  - Autoblasters
  - Flying fang missiles
  - G-type rotolasers
  - Pseudosonic beamers
- **Crew:** 48
- **Capacity:** 3,000 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 4 months
- **Role:**
  - Refuse collection
  - Warship
- **Affiliation:** Ugors
