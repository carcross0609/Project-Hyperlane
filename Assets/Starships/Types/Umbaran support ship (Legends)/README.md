# Umbaran support ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Umbaran support ship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umbaran_support_ship/Legends)

The Umbaran support ship, also known as the Separatist support ship, was used during the Battle of Umbara to support the Separatist armada clashing with the Republic Navy and to protect a Separatist supply ship sending long range missiles to support the local Umbaran militia.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Umbaran support ship
- **Type:** Support ship
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Crew:** B1-Series battle droids
- **Firstuse:** c. 21 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Umbaran militia
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
