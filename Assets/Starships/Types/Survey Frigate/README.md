# Survey Frigate

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Survey Frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Survey_Frigate)

A Survey Frigate was a type of starship used by Imperial Survey Teams to travel to the various planets and moons the team investigated. It carried crew, vehicles and equipment needed to launch survey missions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Survey Frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
