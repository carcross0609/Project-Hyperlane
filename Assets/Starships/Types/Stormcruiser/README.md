# Stormcruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stormcruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stormcruiser)

The stormcruiser, or storm cruiser, was a type of cruiser utilized by the Nihil marauders during the High Republic Era. Baron Boolan of the Nihil owned a stormcruiser known as the Dalnan Dawn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stormcruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Nihil
