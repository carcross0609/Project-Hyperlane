# T-65C-A2 X-wing starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-65C-A2 X-wing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-65C-A2_X-wing_starfighter)

The T-65C-A2 X-wing starfighter or simply the T-65C-A2 X-wing was a model of space superiority starfighter in use by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War, part of the versatile X-wing starfighter line. Red Squadron used at least one at the Battle of Scarif in 1 BBY. By 5 ABY, the T-65C was considered an older model, but one was used by Temmin Wexley as part of the New Republic's Phantom Squadron in the Battle of Jakku.
