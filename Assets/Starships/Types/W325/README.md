# W325

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [W325](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/W325)

W325 was the designation of a very small, bath-tub shaped object used to inspect and repair the hull of the Wennis, while she was in deep space. It did not quite earn the status of an auxiliary vessel due to its size and power output and it was more of a rigid spacesuit that was employed while the cruiser was stationary.
