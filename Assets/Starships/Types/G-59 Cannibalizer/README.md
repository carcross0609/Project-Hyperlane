# G-59 Cannibalizer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-59 Cannibalizer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-59_Cannibalizer)

The G-59 Cannibalizer was a starfighter that was designed by Syub Kyak and manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation on Edic Bar during the last years of the Old Republic. It was similar to Incom Corporation's Z-95 Headhunter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G-59 Cannibalizer
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** G-59 Cannibalizer
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 11.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Triple fire-linked blasters
  - Concussion missile launcher
- **Crew:** 1 pilot
- **Skeleton:** 1 pilot
- **Capacity:** 85 kg
- **Consumables:** 1 day
- **Role:** Patrol starfighter
- **Affiliation:** SoroSuub Corporation
