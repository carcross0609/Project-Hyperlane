# Gall starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gall starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gall_starfighter)

A model of silver-and-blue colored starfighter was used by the planetary defense force of the moon Gall. They were built with two wings, blaster cannons, and a cockpit for a pilot. Between 22 BBY and 20 BBY, during the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, a battle was fought on Gall in which the defense forces sided with the Republic. The starfighters were used to defend against the Separatist Droid Army attacking the moon, which included Variable Geometry Self-Propelled Battle Droid, Mark Is, a make of droid starfighter used by the Separatists.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gall starfighter
- **Model:** Starfighter
- **Armament:** Blaster cannon
- **Crew:** 1 pilot
- **Role:** Planetary defense
- **Affiliation:** Gall planetary defense force
