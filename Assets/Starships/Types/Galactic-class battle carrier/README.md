# Galactic-class battle carrier

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Galactic-class battle carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic-class_battle_carrier)

The Galactic-class battle carrier was a capital ship in use by the Galactic Alliance Navy by 40 ABY, designed some point after the end of the Yuuzhan Vong War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Galactic-class battle carrier
- **Model:** Galactic-class battle carrier
- **Type:** Battle carrier
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:**
  - One bay:
  - XJ6 X-wing starfighters (1 squadron)
  - Eta-5 interceptors (1 squadron)
  - Shield-equipped TIE/LN starfighters (2 squadrons)
  - B-wing starfighters (1/2 squadron)
- **Role:**
  - Destroyer
  - Carrier
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
