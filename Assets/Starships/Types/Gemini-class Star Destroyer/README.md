# Gemini-class Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Gemini-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gemini-class_Star_Destroyer)

The Gemini-class Star Destroyer, also known as the Twin Star Destroyer, was a model of Star Destroyer used by the dark-side twins Karre and Am in service of the TRIGGER Empire during the time of the Republic. The ship was armed with a hyper cannon capable of destroying entire planets, powered by a kyber crystal.
