# Light corvette

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Light corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_corvette)

Light corvettes were a type of corvette that were used by the Nihil during the High Republic Era. At least one such corvette participated in the Battle of Kur. The Defender-class was one such model of light corvette.
