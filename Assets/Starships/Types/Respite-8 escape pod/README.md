# Respite-8 escape pod

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Respite-8 escape pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Respite-8_escape_pod)

The Respite-8 escape pod was an escape pod designed by TaggeCo. They were usually installed on space stations. They could seat eight passengers and could maintain life support for two standard days.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Respite-8 escape pod
- **Manufacturer:** Tagge Company
- **Type:** Escape pod
- **Passengers:** 8
