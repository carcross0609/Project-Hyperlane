# Snoopship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Snoopship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snoopship)

A snoopship was a secretive vessel employed by Wraith Squadron during the Yuuzhan Vong War. They were known to be faster than any Yuuzhan Vong ship and could carry yammosk jammers. They were also utilized during the Galactic Civil War, with Palpatine having secretly sent one to the Andalia system to recover Grand Admiral Osvald Teshik after having orchestrated a painful defeat in the space battle there as punishment for the latter's failure to rescue an advisor from a mutiny.
