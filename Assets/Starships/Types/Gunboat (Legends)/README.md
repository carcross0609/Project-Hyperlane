# Gunboat

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gunboat/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gunboat/Legends)

A gunboat was a type of starship. An Imperial gunboat was used to support a mercenary attack on a Schenor research station. A gunboat was deployed by the Corporate Sector Authority during their invasion of the Kalinda system.
