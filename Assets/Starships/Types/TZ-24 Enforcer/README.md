# TZ-24 Enforcer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TZ-24 Enforcer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TZ-24_Enforcer)

The TZ-24 Enforcer, also known as the TZ-24 Gladiator by the reconstituted Sith Empire, was a model of starfighter designed before the start of the Great Galactic War. Based on the larger TZ-2 transport, the TZ-24 was designed to serve as a rugged combat fighter for long campaigns in remote regions, and the ship's designers took pride in the simplicity and reliability of the design. It saw significant use by security forces, and thousands of the fighters were captured by the Sith Empire over the decades of war with the Galactic Republic; the Empire redesignated the fighter as the TZ-24 Gladiator. The TZ-24 was very similar to the Plug-6 heavy fighter.
