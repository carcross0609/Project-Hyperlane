# Imperial Prison Transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial Prison Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Prison_Transport)

The Imperial Prison Transport (IPT) was a modified version of the V-82 dropship to serve as a prisoner transport, ferrying prisoners to prisons such as Imperial factory facilities. The repurposed IPT stripped the comforts of the base transport model and added new restraints, with the prisoner passenger cabin located on the aft section. The IPT additionally kept life support systems at the bare minimum, and the cockpit was physically separated from the prisoner hold. The ion sublight drives were supported by struts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial Prison Transport
- **Model:** Modified V-82 dropship
- **Type:** Transport
- **Engine:** Ion sublight drives
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
