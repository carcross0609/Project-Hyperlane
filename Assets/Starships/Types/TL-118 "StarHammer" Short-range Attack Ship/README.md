# TL-118 "StarHammer" Short-range Attack Ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TL-118 "StarHammer" Short-range Attack Ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TL-118_%22StarHammer%22_Short-range_Attack_Ship)

The TL-118 "StarHammer" Short-range Attack Ship was a heavy assault starfighter manufactured by Subpro Corporation. It was 35 meters long, it carried 1 pilot, 1 gunner, and 1 week's worth of consumables.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TL-118 "StarHammer" Short-range Attack Ship
- **Manufacturer:** Subpro Corporation
- **Type:** Heavy assault starfighter
- **Cost:** 72,000 credits
- **Length:** 35 meters
- **Max Speed:** 300 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 3.0
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - Heavy laser cannons (2)
  - Light laser cannons (2)
  - Fire-linked twin laser cannons (2)
  - Concussion missile launcher
  - Air-to-surface bombs
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Gunner (1)
- **Consumables:** 1 week
- **Affiliation:** Various: private and local forces
Intended Buyer: Galactic Republic
