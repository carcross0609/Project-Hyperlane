# Q-wing

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Q-wing/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q-wing/Legends)

The Rebel Alliance deployed the Q-wing alongside other starfighters aganst the Galactic Empire. As Rebel starfighter pilots reported for stand-by as they gathered in space with their X-wings, B-wings, Y-wings, and A-wings, one pilot asked the Q-wing in their contingent to report in. However, the Q-wing pilot was occupied by their craft and so missed their cue; the Q-wing was spinning out of control, causing its pilot to vomit before crashing into an asteroid.
