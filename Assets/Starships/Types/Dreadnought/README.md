# Dreadnought

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadnought)

Dreadnoughts were massive, powerful capital ships. Under the Anaxes War College System, they were classified as ships with a length of 5,000 meters or larger. Dreadnoughts included the Providence-class and the Subjugator-class used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems, as well as the various ships of the Mandator line used by the Galactic Republic, the Galactic Empire, and later the First Order. The Bellator-class was another type of dreadnought used by the Galactic Empire. The Super Star Destroyers of the Galactic Empire, such as the Executor-class, were also referred to as Star Dreadnoughts. The Technocrat was a dreadnought utilized by the Nihil criminal group.
