# Super Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Super Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Star_Destroyer)

Super Star Destroyer, abbreviated SSD, was an umbrella term used by both the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic to refer to several classes of massive Imperial vessels larger than Imperial Star Destroyers, the largest of which was the Executor-class Star Dreadnought.
