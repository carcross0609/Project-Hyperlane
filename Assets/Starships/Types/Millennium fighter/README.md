# Millennium fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Millennium fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Millennium_fighter)

The Millennium fighter was a starfighter designed chronologically after the Millennium Hawk by Millennium Astro-Engineering. It was larger and slower than its predecessor, but boasted a much heavier payload of firepower.
