# Bellator-class dreadnought

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bellator-class dreadnought/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bellator-class_dreadnought/Legends)

The Bellator-class dreadnought, also known as the Bellator-class Star Destroyer, was a divergent design in the Mandator-series of Star Dreadnought that served as a warship in the Imperial Navy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bellator-class dreadnought
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Line:**
  - Super Star Destroyer
  -   Dreadnought
- **Model:** Bellator-class dreadnought
- **Type:** Dreadnought
- **Length:** 7,200 meters
- **Engine:** 14 aft thrusters, producing more speed than Mandator-class forerunner designs.
- **Armament:** Multiple gun batteries
- **Role:** Warship
- **Firstuse:** By 3 ABY
- **Retired:** By 137 ABY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Navy
  - New Republic
  - Dark Empire
