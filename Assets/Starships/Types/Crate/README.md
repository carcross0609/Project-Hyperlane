# Crate

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crate)

A crate was the nickname given to the landing craft of the Yuuzhan Vong by the New Republic after the former used those to send troops down to the surface of Ithor during a battle for that world in 25 ABY.
