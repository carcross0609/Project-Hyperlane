# Thor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thor (Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thor_%28Delta-class_DX-9_stormtrooper_transport%29)

Thor was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport that served the Galactic Empire. It fought at the Battle of the Tungra sector. Its primary role was to resupply TIE bombers with warheads whenever they ran out.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thor
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Width:** 7.8 meters
- **Height:** 5.8 meters
- **Role:** Supply ship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
