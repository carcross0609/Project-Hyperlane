# Robot ramship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Robot ramship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Robot_ramship)

The robot ramship was a form of automated starship used for ramming by a variety of mercenaries and pirates including the Sacorrian Triad and their Corellian allies during the Corellian Crisis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Robot ramship
- **Manufacturer:** Outlaw-techs
- **Model:** Robot ramship

- **Engine:** High-acceleration quad engines
