# Eiram medical cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eiram medical cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eiram_medical_cruiser)

The Eiram medical cruiser was a medical cruiser used by the planet of Eiram during the High Republic Era. Although Eiram possessed no real fleet, it made use of the medical cruiser to assist Starlight Beacon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Eiram medical cruiser
- **Model:** Medical cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Role:** Hospital ship
- **Affiliation:** Eiram government
