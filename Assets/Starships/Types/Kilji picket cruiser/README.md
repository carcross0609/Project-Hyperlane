# Kilji picket cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kilji picket cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kilji_picket_cruiser)

The Kilji picket cruiser was a type of picket cruiser used by the Kilji Illumine, the nation of the Kilji species. The cruisers were shorter than Kilji war cruisers and used in ship-to-ship naval warfare. The Hammer, a Kilji picket cruiser commanded by Illumine Colonel Tildnis, was one of the two cruisers of Illumine General Crofyp's task force, the other being Crofyp's war cruiser Anvil.
