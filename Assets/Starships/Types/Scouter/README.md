# Scouter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scouter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scouter)

Scouter was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. The transport participated in the mission at Argoon, attacking an abandoned cargo area near the planet Argoon. The transport was destroyed in the battle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scouter
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Width:** 7.8 meters
- **Height:** 5.8 meters
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
