# Yoda's Jedi Attack Fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yoda's Jedi Attack Fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda%27s_Jedi_Attack_Fighter)

A green starfighter piloted by the Jedi Master Yoda that strongly resembles a a downsized Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor at some point during the Clone Wars. A key difference between this fighter and its more commonplace look-alike is the presence of onboard hyperdrive, located in nacelles that are folded under the fighter when not in use. These nacelles also provide a location for two missile launchers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Yoda's Jedi Attack Fighter
- **Type:** Attack fighter

- **Armament:**
