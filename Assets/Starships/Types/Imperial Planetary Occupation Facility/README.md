# Imperial Planetary Occupation Facility

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial Planetary Occupation Facility](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Planetary_Occupation_Facility)

The Imperial Planetary Occupation Facility, or simply referred to as the I.P.O.F., was a huge, ready-made headquarters that was delivered to planets recently subjugated and occupied by the Galactic Empire. Once the I.P.O.F. was deposited on a planet's surface, it rarely moved. The Imperial Complex on the planet Lothal was an I.P.O.F. It was taken out when Rebel Alliance forces launched it into atmosphere and triggered a massive explosion to destroy it.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial Planetary Occupation Facility
- **Role:** Headquarters
