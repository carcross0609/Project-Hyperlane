# Light Frigate

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Light Frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_Frigate)

The Sienar Fleet Systems Light Frigate was a smaller starship that was often used in customs enforcement. It was frequently captured by pirates and used in privateering.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Light Frigate
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Cost:** 1,000,000 credits
- **Length:** 40 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,050 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - x2 (Primary)
  - x15 (Backup)
- **Armament:**
  - Heavy laser cannon (4)
  - Ion cannon (1)
  - Tractor beam (1)
- **Crew:** 6
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 100 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Role:** Customs enforcement
- **Affiliation:** Privateers
