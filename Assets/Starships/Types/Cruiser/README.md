# Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cruiser)

was a type of "light cruiser."]] Cruiser was a designation given to starships, usually capital ships, of varying sizes and capabilities. While usually considered to be reserved for ships around 400-600 meters in size, the term sometimes included corvettes and frigates alongside actual cruisers. Examples included the Consular-class light cruiser, the CR90 corvette, the Arquitens-class light cruiser, the MC80 Star Cruiser, and the Venator-class Star Destroyer, often referred to as a "Republic attack cruiser." The Kilji species' government of the Kilji Illumine used Kilji war cruisers and Kilji picket cruisers. One such cruiser was part of the Chiss Ascendancy Pommrio family's fleet. This Chiss Ascendancy made use of diplomatic cruisers.
