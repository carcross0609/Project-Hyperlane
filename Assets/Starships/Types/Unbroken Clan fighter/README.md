# Unbroken Clan fighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unbroken Clan fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unbroken_Clan_fighter)

A type of fighter was used by the Unbroken Clan criminal syndicate in its territory on the planet Corellia. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, a number of the fighters were deployed to chase the crew of the bounty hunter T'onga after they kidnapped Grand Leader Vukorah.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unbroken Clan fighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Affiliation:** Unbroken Clan
