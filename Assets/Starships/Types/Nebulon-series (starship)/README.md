# Nebulon-series

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nebulon-series (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nebulon-series_%28starship%29)

The Nebulon-series, also known as the Nebulon frigate series, was a line of frigates manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards and used by a variety of factions throughout history. The EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate was deployed by the Galactic Empire, planetary defense forces such as Jinata Security, the Alliance to Restore the Republic, and the New Republic. The Nebulon-C escort frigate, the B's successor, was also used by the New Republic as well as the Resistance. The Nebulon-K frigate saw service with the First Order. The Citizens' Fleet was composed of multiple configurations of the Nebulon-C, as well as multiple Nebulon-Bs.
