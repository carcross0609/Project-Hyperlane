# TIE/ca "Punisher" Starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/ca "Punisher" Starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/ca_%22Punisher%22_Starfighter)

The TIE/ca "Punisher" Starfighter, also known as the TIE capital assault or the TIE punisher, was a class of heavy bomber that was used by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. It was armed with multiple payloads that could be used against capital ships and ground targets.
