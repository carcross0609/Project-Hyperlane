# Respite-4 escape pod

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Respite-4 escape pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Respite-4_escape_pod)

The Respite-4 escape pod was an escape pod designed by TaggeCo. They were usually installed on space stations. They could seat 4 passengers and could maintain life support for three standard days.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Respite-4 escape pod
- **Manufacturer:** Tagge Company
- **Type:** Escape pod
- **Passengers:** 4
