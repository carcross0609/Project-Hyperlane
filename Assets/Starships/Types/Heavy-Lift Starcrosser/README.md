# Heavy-Lift Starcrosser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy-Lift Starcrosser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy-Lift_Starcrosser)

The Heavy-Lift Starcrosser was a model of ore transport that was used by the Daimanate during the time of the New Sith Wars. In 1032 BBY, a team of Jedi led by Jedi Master Vannar Treece stole a Starcrosser from the planet Oranessan, as part of Operation Influx.
