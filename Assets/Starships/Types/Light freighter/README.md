# Light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_freighter)

Light freighters were a type of freighter used during the final days of the Galactic Republic and during the rule of the Galactic Empire. The Ghost, a starship owned by Hera Syndulla and used by rebels from Lothal, was a modified VCX-100 light freighter. The Millennium Falcon, a smuggling ship owned by Han Solo that later became part of the Rebel Alliance, was a YT-1300 light freighter.
