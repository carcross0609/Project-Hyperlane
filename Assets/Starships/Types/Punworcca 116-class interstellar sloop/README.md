# Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Punworcca_116-class_interstellar_sloop)

The Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop, also known as the Punworcca 116 sloop, was a yacht developed by Huppla Pasa Tisc Shipwrights Collective. It was also known as a Geonosian solar sailer or simply a solar sailer due to Dooku, a Sith Lord, Count of Serenno, and leader of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, being a notable owner of a Punworcca 116-class sloop, that he had modified to include a solar sail.
