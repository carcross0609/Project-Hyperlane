# GAT-12h

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GAT-12h](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GAT-12h)

The GAT-12h "Skipray" was part of the GAT Blastboat series produced by Sienar Fleet Systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GAT-12h
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Type:** Defense and patrol blastboat
- **Cost:**
  - 285,000 credits (new)
  - 150,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 25 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,200 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Equipped (4 jump limit)
- **Armament:**
  - 3 medium ion cannons
  - 1 proton torpedo launcher
  -   Standard load: 4 torpedoes
  - 2 laser cannons
  - 1 concussion missile launcher
  -   Standard load: 8 missiles
- **Crew:** 2 plus 2 gunners
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Capacity:** 20 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
