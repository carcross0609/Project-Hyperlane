# Coruscant civilian transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coruscant civilian transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_civilian_transport)

Coruscant civilian transports were small freighters used by the citizens of Coruscant to get to other areas of a city, or another part of the planet. They could each hold a fair amount of people, and were usually piloted by only one or two pilots. They were outfitted with light armor plating, to protect from heavy damage in the event of a crash.
