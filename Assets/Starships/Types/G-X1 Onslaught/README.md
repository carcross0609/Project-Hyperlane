# G-X1 Onslaught

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-X1 Onslaught](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-X1_Onslaught)

The G-X1 Onslaught, also known as G-X1 Firehauler, was a bomber model produced by Galactic Solutions Industries during the Galactic War. The design has impressed many pilots within both the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire, the latter of whom made a bulk purchase of G-X1 to add to their starfighter complements.
