# Umbaran starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Umbaran starfighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umbaran_starfighter/Legends)

The Umbaran starfighter, also known as the T-20 fighter, was a starfighter model developed by the Umbaran militia during the Clone Wars. The starfighters saw heavy action in a defense of Umbara against the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Umbaran starfighter
- **Manufacturer:** Umbarans
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 10.38 meters (flight configuration)
- **Height:** 12.47 meters (flight configuration)
- **Armament:**
  - Chin-mounted plasma cannon
  - 2 wing-mounted missile pods
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Othersystems:**
  - Ray-shielded cockpit
  - Flexible airframe
- **Role:** Starfighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Umbaran militia
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Galactic Republic (captured)
  -   501st Legion (captured)
