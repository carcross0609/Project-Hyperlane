# KST-100 Kestrel light executive transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KST-100 Kestrel light executive transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KST-100_Kestrel_light_executive_transport)

The KST-100 Kestrel light executive transport, also known as the KST-100, was a limited run Light freighter produced by Corellian Engineering Corporation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KST-100 Kestrel light executive transport
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Light Freighter
- **Cost:** 120,000 credits
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 3
  - Class 12 (backup)
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Forward mounted twin light laser cannons
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Co-pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** At least 4
- **Capacity:** 70 tons
- **Consumables:** Two months
