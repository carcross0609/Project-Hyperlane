# Battlecruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Battlecruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlecruiser)

, a Resurgent-class Battlecruiser]] The term battlecruiser or battle cruiser designated any warships that were around 2,000-5,000 meters in length in the Anaxes War College System. Known classes of battlecruiser were the Resurgent-class Star Destroyer, the Bulwark-class battle cruiser, the Praetor-class Star Battlecruiser, and the Kilji war cruiser or Kilji battlecruiser.
