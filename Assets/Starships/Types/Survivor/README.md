# Survivor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Survivor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Survivor)

The Survivor was a Corellian Engineering Corporation&ndash;manufactured class of lifeboat. They had enough fuel for six hours of spaceflight, and a week's worth of consumables. Emergency gear included tents, thermosuits, portable hand scanners, substance analyzers, and a hunting rifle for small game.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Survivor
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Lifeboat
- **Cost:**
  - 18,000 credits (new)
  - 5,600 credits (used)
- **Length:** 10 meters
- **Max Speed:** 650 KMH
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Crew:** 1
- **Passengers:** 14
- **Capacity:** Emergency survival gear
- **Consumables:** 1 week
- **Role:** Emergency survival
