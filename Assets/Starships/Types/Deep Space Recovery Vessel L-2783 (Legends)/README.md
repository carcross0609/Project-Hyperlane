# Deep Space Recovery Vessel L-2783

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Deep Space Recovery Vessel L-2783/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deep_Space_Recovery_Vessel_L-2783/Legends)

The Corellian Engineering Corporation Deep Space Recovery Vessel L-2783, more commonly called the L-2783, was a small Bulk cruiser / Bulk transport designed to travel to remote locations to repair or recover damaged starships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Deep Space Recovery Vessel L-2783
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** L-2783
- **Type:** Bulk cruiser
- **Cost:** 6,500,000 credits
- **Length:** Comparable to a CR90 corvette
- **Hyperdrive:** Primary: Class 4, Backup: Class 15
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Long range sensors
- **Armament:**
  - Dorsal turret-mounted turbolaser cannon
  - Port & starboard turret-mounted laser cannons
  - Ventral mounted heavy industrial tractor beam
- **Complement:**
  - 50 maintenance/scavenger pods
  - 4 cargo shuttles
- **Crew:** 100
- **Skeleton:** 25
- **Passengers:** 100
- **Capacity:** Roughly half that of a Action VI transport
- **Consumables:** 1 Year
- **Role:**
  - Repair & salvage craft
  - Carrier
  - Pirate ships
