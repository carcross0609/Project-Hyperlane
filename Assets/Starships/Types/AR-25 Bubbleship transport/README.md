# AR-25 Bubbleship transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AR-25 Bubbleship transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AR-25_Bubbleship_transport)

The AR-25 transport was a ship used by Daedalus Gas Mines Corporation for gas mining on planets like Genarius. Called a "Bubbleship" or a "Bubblecraft," the AR-25 was a mid-sized transport vessel. Gases could be observed through its cargo hold, which was a thick-walled clear bubble. The levels of excitation of the gases could be observed or measured.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AR-25 Bubbleship transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Role:**
  - Mining transport
  - Freighter
- **Affiliation:** Daedalus Gas Mines Corporation
