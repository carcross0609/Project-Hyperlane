# Sith dreadnaught

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith dreadnaught](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_dreadnaught)

The Sith dreadnaught, also known as a Sith Personnel Carrier was a classification of warship used by the Sith Empire during the Great Hyperspace War. These dreadnaughts resembled lanvaroks in appearance. They carried six Sith drop ships that were able to deploy troops to ground invasions, holding sixty troops or two Sith war behemoths each.
