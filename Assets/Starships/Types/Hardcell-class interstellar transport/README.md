# Hardcell-class interstellar transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hardcell-class interstellar transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hardcell-class_interstellar_transport)

The Hardcell-class interstellar transport, also known as the Hardcell-class transport, the Hardcell transport, Techno Union transport, or Techno Union starship, was a model of transport manufactured by the Techno Union and utilized by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the First Battle of Geonosis, the opening battle of the Clone Wars.
