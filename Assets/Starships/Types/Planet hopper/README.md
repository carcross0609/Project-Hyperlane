# Planet hopper

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Planet hopper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planet_hopper)

A planet hopper was a type of starship used to ferry individuals between planets. It was a smaller kind of shuttle often equipped with a simple hyperdrive and a less powerful navicomputer, capable of only small jumps. Other planet hoppers were not equipped with hyperdrives at all. Ponta Two, owned by Akshaya Ponta, was a planet hopper.
