# TIE/ad starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE/ad starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/ad_starfighter)

The TIE/ad starfighter, simply known as the TIE Advanced, TIE/Ad or T/A, was the culmination of a series of starfighter designs first explored with Darth Vader's TIE Advanced x1. To avoid confusion with Lord Vader's personal craft, it was known better by its nickname TIE Avenger.
