# A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A/SF-01_B-wing_starfighter)

The A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter, also designated as the A/SF-01 B-wing assault starfighter, more commonly referred to as the B-wing, was a heavily armed Rebel Alliance single-pilot starfighter and bomber designed by the starship engineer Quarrie and manufactured by Slayn & Korpil. The B-wing saw action during the Galactic Civil War and was subsequently substituted by the B-wing Mark II.
