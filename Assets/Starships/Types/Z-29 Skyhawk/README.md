# Z-29 Skyhawk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Z-29 Skyhawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-29_Skyhawk)

The Z-29 Skyhawk was a model of starfighter and interceptor utilized by the Galactic Republic during the time of the High Republic Era. Produced at the Cyclor Shipyards over the Cyclorrian homeworld of Cyclor, the Skyhawks were to be distributed by the Incom Corporation to Republic worlds throughout the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Type:** Starfighter

- **Role:** Interceptor
