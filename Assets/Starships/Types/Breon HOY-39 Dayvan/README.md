# Breon HOY-39 Dayvan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Breon HOY-39 Dayvan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Breon_HOY-39_Dayvan)

The Breon HOY-39 Dayvan, also called the Breon Dayvan, was a Dayvan starship. One such vessel was in Zorby's Western Shiplot and borrowed by Cassian Jeron Andor to travel to Morlana One in search of his sister, Kerri. He later changed the ship’s ID chip log before his friend Pegla caught him. Following the Ferrix Riot, the ship was piloted by Jezzi to evacuate herself, Bix Caleen, B2EMO, Brasso, and Wilmon Paak to safety away from Ferrix.
