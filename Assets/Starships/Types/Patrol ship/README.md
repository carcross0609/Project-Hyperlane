# Patrol ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Patrol ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Patrol_ship)

A patrol ship, also referred to as a patrol boat or patrol craft, was a type of warship used chiefly to patrol an area. The experimental Galactic Republic IPV-2C Stealth Corvette, the Auzituck anti-slaver gunship, and the Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft were all classified as such. The Chiss Ascendancy had a class of patrol ship. The Zyzek System Defense also employed patrol ships. A patrol craft were used by the Lioaoin Regime. Several patrol ships of their military were stationed above the Lioaoin heartworld along with Lioaoin freighters, Lioaoin corsairs, and space stations. Some patrol boats by the Vak Combine also served as starfighters.
