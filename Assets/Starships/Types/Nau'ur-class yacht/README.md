# Nau'ur-class yacht

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nau'ur-class yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nau%27ur-class_yacht)

The Nau'ur-class yacht, also commonly known as the Kalevalan star yacht or just Kalevalan, was a type of custom-order luxurious yacht manufactured by Kalevala Spaceworks. Notable Nau'ur-class yachts included the First Light, owned by Dryden Vos, a high ranking member of the criminal Crimson Dawn syndicate, and the Coronet, owned by Duchess Satine Kryze of Mandalore. Several Kalevalan yachts were part of the Dawn Fleet.
