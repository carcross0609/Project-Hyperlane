# Storm (Stormtrooper transport)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Storm (Stormtrooper transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_%28Stormtrooper_transport%29)

Storm was a group of 3 Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports that were used by the Rebel Alliance carrying commandos. They were part of a unsuccessful capture operation to disable a freighter called Mharsup that carried TIE Defender prototypes. Despite X-wing escort, they were all destroyed by Maarek Stele.
