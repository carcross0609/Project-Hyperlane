# X-83 TwinTail starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X-83 TwinTail starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-83_TwinTail_starfighter)

The X-83 TwinTail starfighter was a model of X-wing series starfighter used before and during Darth Krayt's rise to power in 130 ABY. Manufactured by Incom Corporation, the X-83 was a cutting-edge vessel and the primary starfighter of the New Jedi Order until its fall and decline following the Sith-Imperial War. Krayt's Empire banned the manufacture of the X-83, claiming it was too powerful for civilian use, but Incom continued producing the X-wings in secret, selling them to opponents of the Empire.
