# Prison ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prison ship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prison_ship/Legends)

Prison ships, also known as dungeon ships, prison barges, or detention barges, were starships used for transport and interrogation of prisoners. They were used by the Galactic Empire and the Mandalorian Protectors.
