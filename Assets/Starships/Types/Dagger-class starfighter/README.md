# Dagger-class starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dagger-class starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dagger-class_starfighter)

The Dagger-class starfighter was a fighter craft designed by Republic Sienar Systems in the last century of the Republic and produced in large numbers around the time of the Battle of Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dagger-class starfighter
- **Manufacturer:** Republic Sienar Systems
- **Model:** Dagger-class starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Cost:** 35,000 credits (8,000 used)
- **Length:** 14.8 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,000 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Shield Gen:** None
- **Navigation:** Navicomputer, stores 3 sets of coordinates
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (3)
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** None
- **Capacity:** 55 kg
- **Consumables:** 1 week
- **Availability:** Restricted
- **Role:** Space superiority fighter
- **Affiliation:**
