# YX-1980 space transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YX-1980 space transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YX-1980_space_transport)

The YX-1980 space transport was a one of the most popular transports in use around 137 ABY. Smugglers Chak and Kee used a YX-1980 named Grinning Liar as their vessel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YX-1980 space transport
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** YX-1980
- **Type:** Transport
- **Cost:** 150,000 credits (80,000 used)
- **Max Speed:** 950 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2 (backup class 10)
- **Navigation:** Navicomputer
- **Armament:** Medium laser cannons
- **Bays:** 6 cargo bays
- **Crew:** 2
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 110 tons
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Availability:** Licensed
- **Role:** Cargo hauler
