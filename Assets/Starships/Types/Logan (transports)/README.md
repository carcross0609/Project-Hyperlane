# Logan (transports)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Logan (transports)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Logan_%28transports%29)

Logan was a pair of stormtrooper transports used by the Rebel Alliance. They were part of a strike force against the Imperial frigate Laantis, with the transports launching warheads at the frigate. The transports along with the other Rebels were defeated and the transports were destroyed.
