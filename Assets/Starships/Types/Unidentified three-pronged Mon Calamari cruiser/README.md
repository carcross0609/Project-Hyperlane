# Unidentified three-pronged Mon Calamari cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified three-pronged Mon Calamari cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_three-pronged_Mon_Calamari_cruiser)

A cruiser was a class of Mon Calamari starship that was active during the Imperial Era. It was part of the Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet and later part of the naval forces of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It had three prongs on its dorsal side, seven engines, and a hyperdrive.

- **Name:** Unidentified three-pronged Mon Calamari cruiser
