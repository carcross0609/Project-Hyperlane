# Scimitar assault bomber

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scimitar assault bomber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scimitar_assault_bomber)

The Scimitar assault bomber, also known as the TIE Scimitar Assault Bomber, was an advanced starfighter/bomber based on TIE Series technology. It was used by the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scimitar assault bomber
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Line:** TIE Series
- **Model:** Scimitar assault bomber
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Cost:** Approximately 120,000 credits
- **Length:** 13.8 meters
- **Max Speed:**
  - 1,150 km/h (cruising)
  - 1,250 km/h (dive-bombing)
- **Engine:**
  - Ion engine (1)
  - Interlocked repulsorlift generators
- **Hdsystem:** None (can be equipped as an upgrade)
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - L-s laser cannons (2)
  - Missile racks (16)
  -   Standard load: 32 concussion missiles
  - Thermal detonators
  - Proton grenades
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Bombardier (1)
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Passengers:** None
- **Capacity:** 200 kilograms
- **Consumables:** 2 days
- **Othersystems:** Detachable command pod
- **Role:**
  - Bomber
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Imperial Remnant
  - Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
