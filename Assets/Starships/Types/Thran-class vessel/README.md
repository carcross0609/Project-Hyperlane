# Thran-class vessel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thran-class vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thran-class_vessel)

Thran-class vessels were a type of starship used by the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. Two newly-built vessels were assigned to the Imperial Fakir sector fleet shortly after the Battle of Yavin. The arrival of the ships was reported to Reekeene's Roughnecks by Green squad who were monitoring communications from the Imperial Communications Center on the planet Iyuta.
