# Auto-fighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Auto-fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Auto-fighter)

Auto-fighters were automated TIE series starfighters that were produced by the forces under Commodore Visler Aqorian Korda III on Rekkana. Eventually, Rekkana was liberated by New Republic General Lando Calrissian, Rekk Chancellor Asera, and the bounty hunter Jaxxon. The resistance was able to take control of the remote fighters and have them fire on the Imperial formation, ruining Korda's presentation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Auto-fighter
- **Line:** TIE series
- **Model:** Auto-fighter
- **Type:** Drone starfighter
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (2)
- **Crew:** None (automated)
- **Firstuse:** After the Battle of Endor
- **Affiliation:** Visler Korda's faction
