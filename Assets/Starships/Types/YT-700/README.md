# YT-700

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YT-700](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-700)

The YT-700 was the second model of light freighter in the YT-series produced by the Corellian Engineering Corporation, following the YT-450. It was an undistinguished vessel, and failed to attain any popularity in the galaxy. Unlike all of its successors in the YT-series, the vessel did not have a saucer-shaped deck plan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YT-700
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Type:** Freighter
