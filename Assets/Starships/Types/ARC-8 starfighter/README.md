# ARC-8 starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ARC-8 starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ARC-8_starfighter)

The ARC-8 starfighter was a model of eight-winged starfighter from the High Republic Era. Centuries later, an ARC-8 was owned by Fraxa, executive of the Kwikhaul Livery Corporation on the planet Kwenn, who attempted to restore the antique. Jedi Master Saesee Tiin flew the starfighter during the Jedi's defense of the planet.
