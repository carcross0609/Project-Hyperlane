# CRSF27 space freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CRSF27 space freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CRSF27_space_freighter)

The CRSF27 space freighter was a starship manufactured on Nox by Craedos Shipyards. The sturdy and reliable ships were used on many settled worlds, in roles varying from freighters to warships. They were capable of interplanetary travel, as well as being a tested atmospheric vessel.

- **Name:** CRSF27 space freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Craedos Shipyards
- **Type:** Freighter
