# MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MPO-1400_Purgill-class_star_cruiser)

The MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser, also known as the MPO-1400 Star Cruiser the Chandrilan Class I starship, or simply an MPO cruiser was a class of Star Cruiser in service from the High Republic Era to the New Republic Era, manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. One Star Cruiser was the Halcyon, which was operated by Chandrila Star Line. It was roughly twice as large as a CR90 corvette, and it had thirteen engines that were arranged in the unique Drabor Configuration.
