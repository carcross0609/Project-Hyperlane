# Lictor-class dungeon ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lictor-class dungeon ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lictor-class_dungeon_ship)

The Lictor-class dungeon ship, also known as an Imperial dungeon ship or Mandalorian Dungeon Ship, was a versatile warship and mobile prison based on a design used by the Mandalorian Neo-Crusaders during the Mandalorian Wars. The design proved resilient enough that it was still in use nearly four thousand years after its conception.
