# B-wing Mark II

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B-wing Mark II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-wing_Mark_II)

The B-wing Mark II was a starfighter model manufactured by Slayn & Korpil and was in action before the Cold War. Some of its parts were used by Resistance technicians to assemble troop transports and transport pods.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B-wing Mark II
- **Manufacturer:** Slayn & Korpil
- **Line:** B-wing starfighter
- **Model:** B-wing Mark II
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Target:** Precision targeting laser
- **Armament:** Gyrhil R-9X heavy laser cannon
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
