# Short Range Transit Shuttle SST-67

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Short Range Transit Shuttle SST-67](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Short_Range_Transit_Shuttle_SST-67)

The Short Range Transit Shuttle SST-67 (also referred to as the "Naboo Shuttle") was one of many SST models of intra-system shuttles from Republic Sienar Systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Short Range Transit Shuttle SST-67
- **Manufacturer:** Republic Sienar Systems
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Length:** 23.2 meters
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** 18
- **Capacity:** 1 metric ton
- **Othersystems:** Two days
- **Role:**
  - Short-range personnel shuttle
  - Courier
- **Affiliation:**
  - Various civilian governments
  -   Naboo
