# VX-5 Ricker

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [VX-5 Ricker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VX-5_Ricker)

The VX-5 Ricker was a starship that saw use throughout the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. The ship had a rectangular body with a a triangular underside and triangular cockpit, and two wings at the rear of the ship that were angled up and away from the body.

## Specifications

- **Name:** VX-5 Ricker
- **Role:** Dropship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Smugglers
  - Mandalorians
  -   Ash'ad
  -   Mandalore the Avenger's faction
