# Eirami starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eirami starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eirami_starfighter)

Eirami starfighters were starfighters used by the military of the Outer Rim planet Eiram. In 382 BBY, the ships were used by Thylefire Squadron of Eiram's twin planet E'ronoh during a battle against the Children of E'ronoh dissident group.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Eirami starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Eiram's military
  - E'ronoh's military
