# Vanguard picket

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vanguard picket](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard_picket)

The Vanguard pickets were needle-nosed ships used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Battle of Belderone in the last year of the Clone Wars. The pickets screened the Separatist fleet from Republic fire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vanguard picket
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
