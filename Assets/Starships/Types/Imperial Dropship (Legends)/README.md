# Imperial Dropship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial Dropship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Dropship/Legends)

The Imperial Dropship was a Galactic Empire dropship type. It had two engines, flanked by maneuvering fins, and a front cockpit. The dropship could be piloted by a shadow stormtrooper or a regular stormtrooper and could transport a squad of up to four stormtroopers on a detachable platform at its back.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial Dropship
- **Type:** Dropship
- **Engine:** 2
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (2) (variants)
- **Crew:** 1 (pilot)
- **Passengers:** 2 or 4
- **Role:** Troop transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
