# Dor Gal-ram's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dor Gal-ram's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dor_Gal-ram%27s_flagship)

Dor Gal-ram's flagship was a Sith battleship commanded by Sith Lord Dor Gal-ram. In 5000 BBY, Gal-ram commanded the ship on the side of Ludo Kressh during the Battle of Khar Delba, although Gal-ram was killed when Naga Sadow ordered the Massassi crew to betray and kill Gal-ram.
