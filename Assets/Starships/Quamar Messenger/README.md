# Quamar Messenger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quamar Messenger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quamar_Messenger)

The Quamar Messenger was a passenger liner possibly operated by Haj Shipping Lines. It was older than the Queen of Empire, and was hijacked by Gallandro on its maiden voyage.
