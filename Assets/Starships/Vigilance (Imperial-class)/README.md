# Vigilance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vigilance (Imperial-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vigilance_%28Imperial-class%29)

The Vigilance was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer active after the Battle of Yavin leading a fleet which included Helix Squadron. Under orders from Darth Vader, the fleet participated in the Battle at Fostar Haven to destroy the Alderaanian refugee convoy. During that battle, the vessel was under the command of Commodore Rae Sloane. According to historian Beaumont Kin, the Vigilance was also present and under her command at the ambush of a rebel convoy at Derra.
