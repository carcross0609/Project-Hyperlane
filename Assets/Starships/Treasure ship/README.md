# Treasure ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Treasure ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treasure_ship)

A treasure ship, also known as a treasury ship, was a starship used to carry valuables. Both the reconstituted Sith Empire and the Galactic Empire used treasury ships. During the Galactic Civil War, a pirate named Fandar teamed up with the Alliance to Restore the Republic to raid one such vessel, but was betrayed by his right hand man. The Rebels and pirates were led into an ambush. At some point, the treasury ship Walker was attacked by six T-65 X-wing starfighters in the Fakir sector. The Walker and its corvette escort suffered heavy damage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Treasure ship
- **Role:** Transporting valuables
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sith Empire
  - Galactic Empire
