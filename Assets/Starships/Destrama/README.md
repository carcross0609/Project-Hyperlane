# Destrama

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Destrama](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Destrama)

The Destrama was a Chiss warship that served as the flagship of Commodore Ar'alani after her promotion to Commodore, and prior to her being given command of the Vigilant. The Destrama was part of Picket Force Six and present for the skirmish above Solitair.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Destrama
- **Type:** Warship
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Chiss Ascendancy
- **Navy:** Expansionary Defense Fleet
- **Fleet:** Picket Force Six
- **Captains:** Ar'alani
