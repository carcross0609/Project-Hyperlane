# Tegret

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tegret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tegret)

The Tegret was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate used by the Galactic Empire. The frigate was to escort a convoy of 3 Modular Conveyor across the Atterol system. However the Rebels sent a strike force to steal at least one of the convoy craft.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tegret
- **Type:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
