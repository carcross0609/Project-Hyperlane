# Consonance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Consonance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Consonance)

The RHA Consonance was a Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette in the Alliance Fleet that participated in the Battle of Scarif. It survived the battle and escaped seconds before the arrival of the Devastator. Its communications officer was Lune Buscon, who monitored Imperial transmissions during the battle and was an Alderaanian fleet trooper.
