# Adamas Space Station

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adamas Space Station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adamas_Space_Station)

Adamas Space Station was an orbital space station operating under the Sith Empire, over the planet Hoth, during the Cold War. Darth Nox was known to pass through this space station during their search for ancient ghosts.
