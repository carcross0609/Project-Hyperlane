# J-type Naboo star skiff

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-type Naboo star skiff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-type_Naboo_star_skiff)

The J-type Naboo star skiff also known as the Naboo star skiff was a starship used by the Naboo senator Padmé Amidala. She flew it to Mustafar to confront Anakin Skywalker after she learned that he had turned to the dark side. Obi-Wan Kenobi snuck aboard to confront Skywalker, and when he emerged from the skiff, Skywalker believed Amidala had betrayed him and Force choked her. After defeating Skywalker in a duel, Kenobi transported her in the skiff to Polis Massa, where she gave birth to Luke and Leia before dying.
