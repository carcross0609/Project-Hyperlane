# Fremond

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fremond](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fremond)

The Fremond was a spacetug used by the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. The starship shared its name with the Fremond system, located in the Inner Rim Territories. In 14 BBY, the Fremond was part of the flotilla belonging to the Imperial facility known as Sentinel Base, which was commanded by Moff Wilhuff Tarkin and oversaw the construction of the Imperial battle station later known as the Death Star.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fremond
- **Type:** Spacetug
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:** Sentinel Base's flotilla
