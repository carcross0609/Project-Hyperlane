# Cavik Toth's starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cavik Toth's starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cavik_Toth%27s_starfighter)

Captain Cavik Toth's personal starfighter was a small, one man attack fighter launched from the hangar bay of his flagship. It was armed with heavy laser cannons and concussion missile launchers. It also possessed phenomenal speed and agility, and a powerful reflective shield to defend it from enemy fire.
