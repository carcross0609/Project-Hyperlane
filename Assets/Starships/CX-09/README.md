# CX-09

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CX-09](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CX-09)

CX-09 was a cargolift vessel in the Republic Navy during the Mandalorian Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Mandalorian warrior clans. When the Mandalorians threatened the planet Serroco in 3963 BBY, CX-09 was part of Battle Group Serroco, a task force put together by Admiral Saul Karath and intended to protect the world. Serroco was devastated with nuclear missiles once the Mandalorians arrived, and CX-09 was lost. The ship's destruction was later noted in an edition of The Admiral's List, an official publication issued by the Republic Navy, along with two other cargolift vessels: CX-03 and CX-14.
