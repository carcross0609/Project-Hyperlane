# Task Force 99's Sentinel-class landing craft

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Task Force 99's Sentinel-class landing craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Task_Force_99%27s_Sentinel-class_landing_craft)

A Sentinel-class landing craft was utilized by Task Force 99, also known as SCAR Squadron, an elite stormtrooper squad of the Galactic Empire. SCAR Squadron, led by Sergeant Kreel, used the Sentinel-class landing craft to travel to the moon Hubin, where prominent Rebel Alliance members Leia Organa, Luke Skywalker, and Han Solo, were in hiding; the squad had learned of their location after capturing and interrogating rebel associate and smuggler Sana Starros aboard the shuttle. During a skirmish on the moon, the shuttle was stolen by the rebels, who used it to escape Hubin along with the resident Clan Markona, led by Thane Markona's daughter, Tula. The rebels traveled in the Sentinel-class landing craft to the Imperial Impound Yard on Novka, where Starros' starship, Volt Cobra, had been taken. After crashing the shuttle through a control tower, the rebels escaped aboard the Volt Cobra while Markona and her clan commandeered another vessel.
