# Impetuous

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Impetuous](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Impetuous)

The Impetuous was a Raider-class corvette that served the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At one point, the corvette, alongside a TIE Advanced x1, passed over a blue celestial body. The Impetuous measured 150 meters long and was equipped with sublight engines and four twin laser cannons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Impetuous
- **Line:** Raider-class corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Length:** 150 meters
- **Engine:** Sublight engines
- **Armament:** Twin laser cannons (4)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
