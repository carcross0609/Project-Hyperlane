# Midnight Blade

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Midnight Blade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Midnight_Blade)

The Midnight Blade was a starship flown by the smuggler Zade Kalliday and his partner Valoss. The two had begun flying it together in 24 ABY. In 34 ABY, when the Resistance spy Vi Moradi was in Oga's Cantina on the planet Batuu, she spoke with Kalliday, who introduced himself and his ship. Moradi inquired as to if Kalliday had a long-range comm that she could use, and Kalliday responded that the Midnight Blade did have such systems aboard. However, he also noted that the Midnight Blade had been impounded by Oga Garra, the owner of the cantina.
