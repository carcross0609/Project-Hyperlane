# Darth Revan's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Revan's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Revan%27s_flagship)

Darth Revan's flagship was the flagship of Darth Revan, who commanded the military forces of the Sith Empire from this vessel during the Jedi Civil War as of 3957 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Darth Revan's flagship
- **Model:** Interdictor-class cruiser
- **Destroyed:** 3957 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
- **Owners:** Darth Revan
