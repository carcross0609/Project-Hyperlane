# Screamer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Screamer (Nagai starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Screamer_%28Nagai_starfighter%29)

Screamer was the personal Nagai starfighter so named after the teezl and used by Dark Lady of the Sith Lumiya against the Alliance of Free Planets' Rogue Squadron and the Mandalorian Resurrector Squadron during the Second Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Screamer
- **Manufacturer:** Faruun
- **Max Speed:** Faster than an T-65 X-wing starfighter
- **Armament:** Forward-mounted twin turbolasers
- **Capacity:** 1 pilot
- **Role:** Starfighter
- **Affiliation:** Nagai
- **Navy:** Nagai expeditionary force
- **Owners:** Lumiya
