# Ultima II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ultima II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ultima_II)

The Ultima II was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer of the Galactic Empire that served under the command of Admiral Kalaxo. The Ultima II was involved in a battle against the Alliance to Restore the Republic during which it was destroyed when Starlight Squadron blast the volcano, creating a massive eruption that bursts through the Star Destroyer, destroying it.
