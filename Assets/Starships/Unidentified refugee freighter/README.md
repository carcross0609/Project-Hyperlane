# Unidentified refugee freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified refugee freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_refugee_freighter)

During the Battle of Coruscant in the Yuuzhan Vong War, a Corellian refugee freighter attempted to escape the besieged planet of Coruscant and was attacked by Yuuzhan Vong coralskippers. When the Kyp's Dozen pilot Veema tried to destroy the freighter's pursuers, she lost control of her X-wing starfighter due to a hit and crashed into the freighter, killing the occupants of both vessels.
