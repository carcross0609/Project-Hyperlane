# Gargon (Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gargon (Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gargon_%28Delta-class_DX-9_stormtrooper_transport%29)

.]] Gargon was group of two Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. Gargon 1 & 2 were apart of two other groups of starships attempting to flee Calamari Cruiser Lulsla before it succumbed to wounds dealt by the Galactic Empire during the Aftermath of Hoth.
