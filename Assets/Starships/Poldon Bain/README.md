# Poldon Bain

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Poldon Bain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poldon_Bain)

The Poldon Bain was a cruiser of the Republic Defense Coalition that was active during the High Republic Era. The ship called for help to the Dreadful Day after the ship's navigation systems were fried. However, the Dreadful Day, piloted by the humans Xylan Graf and Avon Sunvale, jumped to hyperspace after a swarm of scav droids headed towards both ships.
