# Blockade One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blockade One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blockade_One)

Blockade One was one of four Nikardun blockade frigates stationed by the forces of the Nikardun Destiny to blockade the Rapacc system during the Nikardun conquests.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blockade One
- **Type:** Nikardun blockade frigate
- **Shield Gen:** Electrostatic barriers
- **Role:** Blockade vessel
- **Affiliation:** Nikardun Destiny
- **Navy:** Yiv's battle forces
