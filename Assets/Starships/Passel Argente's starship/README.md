# Passel Argente's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Passel Argente's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Passel_Argente%27s_starship)

Passel Argente's starship was the personal starship of the Corporate Alliance Magistrate. During the Clone Wars, Passel Argente used this ship to travel to a rendezvous with the Trandoshan scavenger, Gha Nachkt, aboard his freighter, the Vulture's Claw. Later in the war, he traveled to the city of Lessu on Ryloth where he was storing spoils of war, leaving them in the care of a corpulent Twi'lek named Artruk.
