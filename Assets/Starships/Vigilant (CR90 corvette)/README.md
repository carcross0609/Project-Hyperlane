# Vigilant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vigilant (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vigilant_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Vigilant was a CR90 corvette that was part of the Rebel Alliance's fleet. The Vigilant was sent by Base One to assist Extraction Team Bravo on their extraction of Jyn Erso from the Imperial Detention Center & Labor Camp LEG-817. The Vigilant was then quickly destroyed when an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer arrived in the Wobani system.
