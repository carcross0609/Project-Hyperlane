# Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer (Damaged; Hoth)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial-class_Star_Destroyer_%28Damaged%3B_Hoth%29)

An Imperial-class Star Destroyer was damaged during the Battle of Hoth, having had its left shield generator dome destroyed. The Millennium Falcon, attempting to evacuate Hoth with Leia Organa, put it out of commission by destroying its remaining shield generators and the bridge in order to create enough of an opening to escape.
