# Righteous Fist

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Righteous Fist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Righteous_Fist)

The Righteous Fist was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer used by the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, lieutenants Jyala Haydenn and Beilert Valance were present on the Righteous Fist as they awaited the briefing for their next mission after Valance rescued Imperial engineers from a starship. After they were assigned their next mission, Valance and Haydenn traveled aboard the Righteous Fist to the planet Bestine IV.
