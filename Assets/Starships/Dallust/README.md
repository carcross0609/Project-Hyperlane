# Dallust

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dallust](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dallust)

The Dallust was an Ton-Falk-class escort carrier that was assigned to the fleet that protected the Tallaan and the Tallaani Shipyards. The Imperial-class Star Destroyer Riesa was also assigned to this task.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dallust
- **Model:** Ton-Falk-class escort carrier
- **Length:** 500 meters
- **Height:** 150 meters
- **Role:** Carrier
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
