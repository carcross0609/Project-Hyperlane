# A-36 Pathfinder-class force reconnaissance vessel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A-36 Pathfinder-class force reconnaissance vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-36_Pathfinder-class_force_reconnaissance_vessel)

The A-36 Pathfinder-class force reconnaissance vessel was a scout vessel produced by the Incom Corporation. It was an advanced version of the A-24 Sleuth-class scout ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A-36 Pathfinder-class force reconnaissance vessel
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Type:** Scout ship
- **Cost:** 135,000 credits
- **Hyperdrive:**

  - Class 12 (backup)
- **Armament:**

  - Ion cannon (1)
  - Concussion missile launcher (1)
  -   Concussion missiles (8)
- **Crew:** 4
- **Passengers:** 4
- **Consumables:** 9 standard months
