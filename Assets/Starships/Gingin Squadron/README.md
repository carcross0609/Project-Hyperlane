# Gingin Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gingin Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gingin_Squadron)

Gingin Squadron was a small Squadron of 2 R-41 Starchasers used by Rneekii pirates. They were deployed to provide cover for transport group Ibutho to capture Imperial Modified corvette group Hashim. They were unsuccessful and were destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele.
