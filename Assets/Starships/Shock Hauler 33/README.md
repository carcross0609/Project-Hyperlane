# Shock Hauler 33

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shock Hauler 33](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shock_Hauler_33)

The Shock Hauler 33 was a starfighter produced by Incom Corporation. A modified one, the Goonga Munt Goonga, was the starfighter of the pirate and ace pilot, Scranton Toon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shock Hauler 33
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Model:** Shock Hauler 33 starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 14.5 meters
