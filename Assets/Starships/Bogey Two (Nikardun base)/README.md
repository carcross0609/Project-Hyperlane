# Bogey Two

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bogey Two (Nikardun base)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogey_Two_%28Nikardun_base%29)

A vessel codednamed Bogey Two was used by the Grysk Hegemony at a battle at listening post KR21. The ship, along with Bogey Three and a mobile repair dock escaped into Hyperspace.

- **Name:** Bogey Two
- **Type:** Patrol ship
