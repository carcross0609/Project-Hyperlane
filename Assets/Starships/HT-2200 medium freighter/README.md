# HT-2200 medium freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HT-2200 medium freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HT-2200_medium_freighter)

The HT-2200 was a medium freighter from Corellian Engineering Corporation which was intended to capitalize on the popularity of the YT-1300.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HT-2200 medium freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** HT-2200
- **Type:** Medium freighter
- **Cost:**
  - 240,000 credits (new)
  - 110,000 credits (used)
- **Length:** 54.8 meters
- **Max Speed:** 750 kmh
- **Engine:** Koensayr single-tube ion drive
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 2.0
  - Class 15 (backup)
- **Power:** Lotek 11P power generator
- **Shield Gen:** Novaldex GA21 shield generator
- **Hull:**
  - Reinforced superstructure
  - Trimantium hull plating
- **Armament:** Taim & Bak "Queldor" single-barrel laser cannon turrets (2)
- **Crew:** 2
- **Passengers:** 8
- **Capacity:** 800 metric tons (4 independent bays)
- **Consumables:** 3 months
- **Role:**
  - Freighter
  - Smuggling vessel
  - Troop transport (Rebel modification)
