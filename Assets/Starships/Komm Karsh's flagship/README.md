# Komm Karsh's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Komm Karsh's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Komm_Karsh%27s_flagship)

A ship served as the flagship of the forces of the Yuuzhan Vong empire during the Second Battle of Obroa-skai. Commanded by Supreme Commander Komm Karsh, it was destroyed and Karsh was killed by a New Republic task force.
