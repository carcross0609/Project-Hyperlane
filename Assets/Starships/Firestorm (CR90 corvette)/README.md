# Firestorm

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firestorm (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firestorm_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Firestorm was a CR90 corvette that was part of the fleet of Lumiya shortly after the Battle of Endor. It was modified to carry 4 TIE Fighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Firestorm
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Capacity:** 4 TIE/LN starfighters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Lumiya's fleet
- **Owners:** Lumiya
