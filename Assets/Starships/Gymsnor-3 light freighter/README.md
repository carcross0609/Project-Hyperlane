# Gymsnor-3 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gymsnor-3 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gymsnor-3_light_freighter)

The Gymsnor-3 light freighter was a light freighter used by factions such as the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Spice Runners of Kijimi. One such ship, the Ezra's Gate, was utilized by the human pilot Shara Bey and her husband Kes Dameron.
