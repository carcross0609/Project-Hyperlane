# Imperial Supply Freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial Supply Freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Supply_Freighter)

The Imperial Supply Freighter was a type of freighter in use by the Galactic Empire at the time of the construction of the Death Star II. During the Galactic Civil War, Rebel operatives managed to steal one such starship, along with codes to infiltrate the Death Star.
