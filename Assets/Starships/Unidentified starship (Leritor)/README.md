# Unidentified starship (Leritor)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified starship (Leritor)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_starship_%28Leritor%29)

A starship on a mission for the Sith once carried the legendary dark side artifacts known as the Bracers of Najus. The starship eventually crashed on the planet Leritor, where it remained buried for centuries.
