# Argomon's hauler

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Argomon's hauler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Argomon%27s_hauler)

A black market hauler was operated by the pilot Argomon and the droid B-333 during the High Republic Era. The black market hauler made deliveries for the Nihil marauders within their Occlusion Zone territory behind the Stormwall, including to the prison ship Korvix Vorn. The starship secretly served as a method of gathering information from within Nihil space, which was reported to the Jedi Order by B-333. When the Jedi planned to make use of the Korvix Vorn's imminent emergence from the Occlusion Zone and required a tracker to be placed aboard the vessel, Argomon made a delivery of Mycopram to the scientist Niv Drendow Apruk aboard the prison ship.
