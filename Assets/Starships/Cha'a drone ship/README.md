# Cha'a drone ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cha'a drone ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cha%27a_drone_ship)

Cha'a drone ships were unmanned trade vessels used in 5000 BBY by Cha'a merchants. They were also used by Empress Teta to transport prisoners to Ronika.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cha'a drone ship
- **Type:** Drone ship
- **Role:** Merchant ship
- **Firstuse:** 5000 BBY
