# Panop

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Panop](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panop)

The Panop was a starship owned by Boss Carpo of the Carpo Crime Syndicate. It was located above the planet Lanz Carpo, and used as a fortress for Carpo's guards. When Leia Organa and Han Solo of the Rebel Alliance were sent on a mission to Lanz Carpo to trick the Galactic Empire into thinking Carpo was against them, District Advocate Dar Champion suggested instead tricking Carpo into taking his vessel away from the planet to attack the Empire. However, after Champion and Organa uploaded falsified messages to advance their plan, Solo was captured by Carpo's guards and taken to the Panop.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Panop
