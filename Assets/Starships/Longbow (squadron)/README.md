# Longbow (squadron)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Longbow (squadron)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Longbow_%28squadron%29)

Longbow was a squadron of four Strike-class medium cruisers that were attempting to box in and destroy the MC80 Star Cruiser Chie. They had Avenger Squadron in TIE Avengers and Assault Gunboats as their starfighter superiority against Rogue squadron in X-wings, B-wings and A-wings.
