# Aerial landing platform

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aerial landing platform](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aerial_landing_platform)

The aerial landing platform, also referred to as a sky-base or skybase, was a type of starship used by the Galactic Empire that served as a staging area for invading planetary forces. In 18 BBY, they were deployed to Mon Cala to quell an uprising, following the destruction of all surface cities on the planet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aerial landing platform
- **Model:** Platform
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Dual laser cannons (32)
- **Complement:**
  - At least 5 Lambda-class T-4a shuttles
  - At least 11 TIE/ln space superiority starfighters
- **Crew:** At least 6
- **Role:** Mobile base
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Army
