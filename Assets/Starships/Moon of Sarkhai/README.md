# Moon of Sarkhai

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moon of Sarkhai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_of_Sarkhai)

The Moon of Sarkhai was a boxy cruiser used by the Path of the Open Hand. In 382 BBY, it was part of a mission to retrieve Nameless eggs from Planet X, captained by an individual named Hal and carrying Path members Nanda and Tragor. The ship was vaporized while attempting to cross through the hostile Veil surrounding the planet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Moon of Sarkhai
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Destroyed:** 382 BBY, Veil of Planet X
- **Affiliation:** Path of the Open Hand
- **Captains:** Hal
