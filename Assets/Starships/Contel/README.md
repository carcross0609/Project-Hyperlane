# Contel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Contel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Contel)

Contel was an Imperial Container transport. The transport was part of convoy Rope 17, which was supposed to upgrade the Deep Space Manufacturing Facility ZA-13. Contel carried medical supplies for the personnel of the factory. Despite being attacked by Rebel raiders, Contel managed to deliver its cargo and escape into hyperspace.
