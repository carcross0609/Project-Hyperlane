# Falcon group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Falcon group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Falcon_group)

Falcon was a group of two Assassin-class corvettes used by Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's splinter government fleet. Along with BaKaar group and Arditi group, it was sent to destroy Lord Vader's flagship, the Garrett. However, Imperial ace Maarek Stele helped the Garrett destroy the corvettes along with the other groups.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Falcon group
- **Type:** Corvette squadron
- **Leader:** Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin
- **Affiliation:**
