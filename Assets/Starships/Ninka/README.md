# Ninka

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ninka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ninka)

The Ninka was a Free Virgillia-class Bunkerbuster in service of the Resistance during its conflict with the First Order. Its commanding officer was Vice Admiral Amilyn Holdo.
