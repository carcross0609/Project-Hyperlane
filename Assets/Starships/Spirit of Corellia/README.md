# Spirit of Corellia

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spirit of Corellia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_of_Corellia)

The Spirit of Corellia was a SoroSuub Personal Luxury Yacht 3000 in use by the Jedi from the Talus base during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spirit of Corellia
- **Type:** Personal Luxury Yacht 3000
- **Length:** 50 meters
- **Max Speed:** 850 km/h
- **Engine:** Ion engines (2, housed in exterior nacelles)
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2 (Backup class 14)
- **Shield Gen:** Chempat-6 deflector shield generators (2)
- **Navigation:** Navigation computer equipped
- **Crew:** 1 pilot, 1 co-pilot
- **Skeleton:** 1 pilot
- **Passengers:** 10
- **Capacity:** 100 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Role:** Private Transport
- **Affiliation:** Jedi
