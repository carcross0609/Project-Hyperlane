# Dicer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dicer (starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dicer_%28starfighter%29)

The Dicer was the name given to Eelo Begraas' Z-95 Headhunter. The elite Bothan pilot customized his ship, making it incredibly maneuverable. It was also rumored to contain some prototype technology obtained by the Bothan from a job for the Hutts. Begraas painted the fighter black, with highlights in blood red. Begraas' personal insignia of a clenched fist was also painted on either side of the cockpit.
