# Atrivis Day group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Atrivis Day group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atrivis_Day_group)

The Atrivis Day group was four BFF-1 bulk freighters used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic to ferry supplies. They were part of a convoy traveling to the Organarms Deep Space Manufacturing Facility that encountered an Imperial Missile Boat. Their presence in this engagement was later replayed in an Imperial simulator mission, training pilots for the Missile Boat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Atrivis Day group
- **Model:** BFF-1 bulk freighters
- **Role:** Cargo transports
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
