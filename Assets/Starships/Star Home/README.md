# Star Home

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Star Home](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Home)

The Star Home was the flagship of the Hapan Queen Mothers, one of the most unique and luxurious spaceships in the galaxy&mdash;literally a castle set among the stars, equipped with the military capabilities of a fleet carrier.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Star Home
- **Type:** Space station
- **Cost:** Not for sale
- **Length:** 2,500 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1.0
- **Armament:**

- **Complement:** 60 Miy'til starfighters
- **Crew:**

  - 90 starfighter pilots
  - 50 command staff
  - 100 soldiers
- **Passengers:**
  - 600 (average: nobles and retainers)

- **Capacity:** 7,000 tons
- **Consumables:** 5 years
- **Affiliation:** Hapes Consortium
- **Owners:** The Queen Mother
