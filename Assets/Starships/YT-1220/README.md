# YT-1220

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YT-1220](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-1220)

The YT-1220 was a type of starship that was used during the time of the Clone Wars. The Tourism Council of Oluna Biqua owned one, called the Headliner.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YT-1220
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Length:** 24 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Crew:** 1 or 2
- **Passengers:** 5
- **Cargohandling:** 75 tonnes
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Role:**
  - Transport
  - Shuttle
- **Affiliation:** Tourism Council of Oluna Biqua
