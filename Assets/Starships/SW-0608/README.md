# SW-0608

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SW-0608](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SW-0608)

SW-0608 was a Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle stolen from the Galactic Empire by the Alliance to Restore the Republic and used by the squad Rogue One during the Battle of Scarif.
