# Alif-class Longbeam

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Alif-class Longbeam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alif-class_Longbeam)

The Alif-class Longbeam, also known as the Delta-class starship, was a class of starship used by the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era. In 382 BBY, a party of Longbeams arrived in the Eiram system and was witnessed by E'ronoh's Thylefire Squadron while they were rendezvousing with an ice hauler. The ship was a precursor to the Longbeam-class corvette.
