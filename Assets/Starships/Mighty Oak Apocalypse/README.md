# Mighty Oak Apocalypse

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mighty Oak Apocalypse](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mighty_Oak_Apocalypse)

The Mighty Oak Apocalypse (often referred to as Moa) was an independent cargo YT-2400 light freighter during the Galactic Civil War, captained by the Wookiee Lohgarra. The ship's name was more intimidating in Shyriiwook. It transported only legal cargo, but operated on the fringes, where Imperial oversight was rarely an issue. Besides Lohgarra herself, in the later years of the Galactic Empire the crew usually consisted of the Tarsunt Brill, the Ithorian Methwat Tann and a JJH2 astromech droid.
