# Sword

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sword (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_%28starship%29)

The Sword was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport operated by the Ripoblus in the Sepan Civil War in 3 ABY. It fled from Science Station Youst just as Imperial forces came to defend the Dimoks in the concurrent battle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sword
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Length:** 45 meters
- **Width:** 30.4 meters
- **Height:** 20.5 meters
- **Role:** Boarding craft
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Ripoblus government
