# Spritespray

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spritespray](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spritespray)

The Spritespray was a medium cruiser in service to the Galactic Alliance during the latter half of the Yuuzhan Vong War. It was part of a task force commanded by General Wedge Antilles during the Battle of Bilbringi. It was one of the six warships that survived the engagement.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spritespray
- **Type:** Medium cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
- **Navy:** Alpha Fleet
