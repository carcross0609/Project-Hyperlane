# Unidentified Pellaeon-class Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Pellaeon-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Pellaeon-class_Star_Destroyer)

A Pellaeon-class Star Destroyer was among the fleet of Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire's Imperial Navy in 137 ABY. Commanded by the Sith Lord Darth Stryfe and Captain Meeshal, it was dispatched to Ossus in that year to search for wayward Jedi. The stormtroopers of Joker Squad searched the surface and found nothing, but Stryfe nevertheless ordered a planetary bombardment.
