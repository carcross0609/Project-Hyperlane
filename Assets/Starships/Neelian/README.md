# Neelian

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Neelian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neelian)

Neelian was a Corvette in the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. It fought at the Battle of Praesitlyn where it was the headquarters ship of Commander Anakin Skywalker and was responsible for safeguarding the troopships as they landed. It was commanded by Captain Luhar.
