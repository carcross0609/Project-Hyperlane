# Hyperdrive pod

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hyperdrive pod/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperdrive_pod/Legends)

A hyperdrive pod, also known as a hyperspace pod was a droid-controlled hyperdrive-equipped pod that delivered probe droids to their destinations.
