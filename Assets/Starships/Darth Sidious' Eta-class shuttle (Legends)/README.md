# Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sidious%27_Eta-class_shuttle/Legends)

An Eta-class shuttle was a used by the Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Sidious during the Clone Wars. The shuttle was not painted in the the red and white color scheme commonly seen in use by the Jedi Order, but was instead colored a nondescript gray color.

- **Name:** Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle

- **Armament:** Laser cannons (3)
- **Crew:** Darth Sidious
- **Passengers:** At least 6
- **Role:** Shuttle
