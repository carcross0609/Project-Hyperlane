# Mon Karren

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mon Karren](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Karren)

Mon Karren was an MC80 Mon Calamari Star Cruiser in the Solo Fleet, the less heavily-armored sister ship to the Mon Remonda. During the hunt for Warlord Zsinj she was assigned to Group One of Solo's Fleet. She carried three squadrons of starfighters&mdash;two Y-wing squadrons and the X-wing group Corsair Squadron.
