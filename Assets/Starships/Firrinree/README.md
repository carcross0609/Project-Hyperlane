# Firrinree

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firrinree](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firrinree)

The Firrinree was one of the two Sh'ner-class planetary assault carriers in the Keeramak Entourage during the battle of Bakura in 28 ABY. The ship eventually fell into the hands of the P'w'ecks during the battle when they overthrew their Ssi-ruuk overlords.
