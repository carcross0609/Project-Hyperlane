# Fleeting

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fleeting](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fleeting)

The Fleeting was a starship owned by Ariole Yu, a lieutenant in the Sixth Kin criminal syndicate. Yu and Just Lucky used the vessel to travel to Crimson Dawn's auction of Han Solo in order to track down Gallin Crae, a supposedly traitorous Sixth Kin lieutenant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fleeting
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Sixth Kin
- **Owners:** Ariole Yu
