# Quarren Eye

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quarren Eye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quarren_Eye)

Quarren Eye was a hyperdrive-equipped yacht given to Intelligence officer Garik Loran by the Director of Galactic Alliance Intelligence Service Borath Maddeus as part of his investigation. The ship's communication system was wired to transmit data back to Maddeus, which led to his implication when he tried to kill Loran and a fictitious team of Wraith Squadron members that Loran had been ostensibly transmitting to using the ship's communication system.
