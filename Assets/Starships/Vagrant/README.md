# Vagrant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vagrant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vagrant)

Vagrant was a Victory I-class Star Destroyer of the Imperial Starfleet. Along with two Carrack-class light cruisers, the Nexus and the Xerxes, the Vagrant attempted to stop the Defiance Task Force from rendezvousing with the Rebel fleet at Sullust. They were held back by A-wings, two of which were piloted by Olin Garn and Ace Azzameen.
