# Upsilon 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Upsilon 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Upsilon_1)

Upsilon 1 was a Beta-class ETR-3 escort transport that served with the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. It was present at the shipyard Calenz in the Nocto system not long before the Battle of Nocto.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Upsilon 1
- **Model:** Beta-class ETR-3 escort transport
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Alliance Fleet
