# Lokani Dawn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lokani Dawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lokani_Dawn)

The Lokani Dawn was a sleeper ship that carried its passengers in suspended animation. It was constructed and launched by the people of the planet Coruscant around 25,200 BBY, not long after the fall of the Rakatan Infinite Empire. The progress of the Lokani Dawn was watched by generations of Coruscanti for almost two centuries until an undetected meteor shower disintegrated the starship around 25,000 BBY.

## Specifications
