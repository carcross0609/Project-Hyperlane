# D-Squad's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D-Squad's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-Squad%27s_shuttle)

A Rho-class transport shuttle was used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems to load rhydonium onto a Venator-class Star Destroyer to destroy the Republic strategy conference taking place on the space station Valor above Carida. It was stolen by D-Squad to escape from Abafar. The squad, upon landing on the Star Destroyer, discovered that the capital ship had been turned into a flying bomb. After the Star Destroyer dropped out of hyperspace in the Carida system, all of the members of D-Squad except R2-D2, and the surviving service droids from the capital ship, escaped its destruction in the shuttle.
