# Jordi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jordi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jordi)

Jordi group was a pair of Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. When the Alliance succeeded in stranding the Imperial EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Priam near Kalla VII, General Jan Dodonna gave the order for a "board and capture" operation against the Priam.
