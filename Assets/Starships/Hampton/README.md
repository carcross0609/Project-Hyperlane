# Hampton

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hampton](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hampton)

Hampton was a BFF-1 bulk freighter during the Galactic Civil War. With the assistance of Alliance Intelligence, two captured Rebel pilots were able to escape from Stars' End prison by stowing away on the Hampton. Knowing that the Imperial EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Furious was in the sector and was under orders to search any ships, the Rebels mounted a quick rescue comprising of the Lambda-class shuttle Mercy and two Y-wings.
