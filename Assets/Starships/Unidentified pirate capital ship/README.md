# Unidentified pirate capital ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified pirate capital ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pirate_capital_ship)

A pirate capital ship crewed entirely by decommissioned battle droids that dated back to the Clone Wars wrought havoc in the Tashtor sector during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 3 ABY, the Imperial Navy seized the capital ship and sent the pirate droids for termination. The seizure was then reported on the HoloNet News by reporter Dorna Parros.
