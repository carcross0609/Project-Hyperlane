# Redoubt

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Redoubt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Redoubt)

The Redoubt was a Venator-class Star Destroyer that saw service in the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. It was commanded by Commander Denn Wessex during the Battle of Boz Pity in 20 BBY.
