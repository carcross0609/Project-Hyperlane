# Spinner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spinner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spinner)

The Spinner was a freighter. In 19.9 BBY, after a stop at Orleon, it was infested with stone mites. On approach to Coruscant's Westport, it crashed at Kishi and released its mite cargo.
