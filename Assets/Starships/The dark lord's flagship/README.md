# The dark lord's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [The dark lord's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_dark_lord%27s_flagship)

The dark lord's flagship was a starship that served as the flagship of the Sith known as the dark lord. The ship was destroyed at the end of the Sith rebellion.
