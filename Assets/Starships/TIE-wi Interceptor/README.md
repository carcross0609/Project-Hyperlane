# TIE/wi Interceptor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/wi Interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/wi_Interceptor)

The TIE/wi Interceptor, also known as the TIE whisper Interceptor, was a TIE fighter series starfighter that was used by the First Order, which was similar in design to the TIE/wi interceptor. It was also similar to the TIE/sf space superiority fighter employed by the same government, although the TIE whisper starfighter had upgraded stealth systems. The TIE whisper had a crew of two, a pilot and a gunner.
