# Stalwart

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stalwart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stalwart)

The Stalwart was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer in service to the Imperial Navy during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 2 BBY, the Star Destroyer served as the flagship of Admiral Kinshara's One Twenty-Fifth task force.
