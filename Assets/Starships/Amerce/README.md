# Amerce

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Amerce](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amerce)

The Amerce was a New Republic Star Destroyer under construction at the shipyards of the planet Fondor during the beginning of the Yuuzhan Vong War. In 25 ABY, as the Star Destroyer was a month away from completion, Yuuzhan Vong forces attacked the shipyards. The unfinished Amerce was destroyed when enemy coralskippers committed suicide runs on the Star Destroyer and its construction site.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Amerce
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Destroyed:** 25.7 ABY, Fondor Shipyards
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
