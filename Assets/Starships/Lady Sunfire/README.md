# Lady Sunfire

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lady Sunfire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_Sunfire)

The Lady Sunfire, a modified Corellian Engineering Corporation Barloz-class freighter, was the personal ship of of the smuggler Clyngunn the ZeHethbra. It was armed with three quad turbolaser cannons and four aft-firing concussion missile launchers. However, due to the larger powerplant needed for the Lady Sunfire's weapons, the ship could only carry 70 metric tons of cargo as a result. The Lady Sunfire was configured to operate with a crew consisting only of Clyngunn and his R3-series astromech droid, which was programmed to operate silently.
