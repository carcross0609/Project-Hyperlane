# Valiant Damsel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Valiant Damsel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valiant_Damsel)

The Valiant Damsel was a light freighter operated by the New Republic. It was used by a group of operatives who helped to rescue the Black Curs on the planet Moorja.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Valiant Damsel
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Max Speed:** 800 kmh
- **Armament:** 2 laser cannon turrets
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
