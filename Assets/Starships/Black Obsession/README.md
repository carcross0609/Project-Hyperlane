# Black Obsession

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Black Obsession](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Obsession)

The Black Obsession was a modified Mon Calamari Shipyards A-Z-Z-3 Light Freighter. It smuggled political prisoners from the city of Selkren off the planet Demophon during the Galactic Civil War.
