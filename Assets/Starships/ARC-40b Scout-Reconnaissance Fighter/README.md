# ARC-40b Scout-Reconnaissance Fighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ARC-40b Scout-Reconnaissance Fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ARC-40b_Scout-Reconnaissance_Fighter)

The ARC-40b Scout-Reconnaissance Fighter was a class of medium light-weight starfighter that was used by the Republic Starfighter Corps during the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Separatist Alliance. It carried a crew of three. After the war, many surviving ARC-40bs were scrapped by the Imperial Navy of the Galactic Empire, with some still seeing life in the Outer Rim in mercenary units or with surveyors.
