# Ubote 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ubote 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ubote_2)

Ubote 2 was a CR90 corvette in the service of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It fought at the First Battle of Javin as part of the Ubote group, along with Ubote 1 and Ubote 3.
