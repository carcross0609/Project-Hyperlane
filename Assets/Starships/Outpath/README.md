# Outpath

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Outpath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outpath)

The Outpath was a Longbeam cruiser of the Republic Defense Coalition that served was nominally attached to an attack fleet assembled by Jedi Master Elzar Mann to assault the stormwall of the Occlusion Zone. As part of the breaching plan, the Outpath was piloted by a droid and would be the first to attempt the breach.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Outpath
- **Type:** Longbeam cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Navy:** Republic Defense Coalition
- **Fleet:** Occlusion Zone attack fleet
