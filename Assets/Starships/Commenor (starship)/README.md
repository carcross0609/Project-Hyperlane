# Commenor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Commenor (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commenor_%28starship%29)

The Commenor was a cruiser in the Galactic Republic Navy during the Cold War. The Commenor was was commanded by Anx captain Pipalidi, and saw action during the Independent Operation Sebaddon in 3643 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Commenor
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Complement:**
  - XA-8 starfighters
  - PT-7 Raven-class starfighters
  - NR2 Gully Jumper-class shuttles
  - 1 Personal shuttle.
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Captains:** Captain Pipalidi
