# Broadside

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Broadside (shuttle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Broadside_%28shuttle%29)

The Broadside was a shuttle and troop transport used by the Jedi Coalition during the Battle of Centerpoint Station in 40 ABY. They took the Broadside to connect with Centerpoint Station. The Jedi and Dr. Seyah went in and completed their mission. They then left on the Broadside once again.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Broadside
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Role:** Troop transport
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Coalition
