# Reckoning

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reckoning (YT-1300 light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reckoning_%28YT-1300_light_freighter%29)

The Reckoning was a YT-1300 light freighter owned by the Wookiee bounty hunter Granakk. Its hull was stronger than a stock YT-1300, and it was upgraded with heavy deflector shields and an assault laser cannon, which replaced the laser cannon on the standard freighter model.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Reckoning
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Assault laser cannon
- **Owners:** Granakk
