# Altar Moon group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Altar Moon group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Altar_Moon_group)

The Altar Moon group was a group of Modular conveyors working for the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It was destroyed by Imperial forces during their attack on Organarms.
