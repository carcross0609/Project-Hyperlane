# Paataatus enhanced cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Paataatus enhanced cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paataatus_enhanced_cruiser)

A class of enhanced cruiser was used by the Paataatus Hiveborn's armed forces. At least five were used by the Paataatus, although the Nikardun Destiny gained control of one and used it to subjugate the Prince Militaire of the Hiveborn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Paataatus enhanced cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Paataatus military
