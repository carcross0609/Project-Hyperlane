# Thyfonian

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thyfonian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thyfonian)

The Thyfonian was a modified Lambda-class T-4a shuttle. It had high capacity shield generators and upgraded laser cannons. It was to be Fliry Vorru's private escape shuttle, however Ysanne Isard commandeered it to be her private escape shuttle during the final stages of the Battle of Thyferra. The Thyfonian was destroyed during the battle by Tycho Celchu who was acting on sensor data provided by Corran Horn.
