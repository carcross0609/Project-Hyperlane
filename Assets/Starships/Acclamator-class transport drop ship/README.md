# Acclamator-class transport drop ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Acclamator-class transport drop ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acclamator-class_transport_drop_ship)

The Acclamator-class transport drop ship, also known as the Imperial landing craft or Kuat dropship was a model of large Imperial military transport that was utilized during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Acclamator-class transport drop ship
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Model:** Acclamator-class transport drop ship
- **Type:** Drop ship
- **Engine:** At least two thrusters
- **Armament:** Twin-barreled cannons (at least 12)
- **Capacity:**
  - All Terrain Armored Transports (at least 2)
  - HAVw A6 Juggernaut (at least 2)
  - All Terrain Scout Transports (at least 3)
  - All Terrain Tactical Enforcer (at least 1)
- **Role:** Dropship
- **Firstuse:** At least by 0 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
