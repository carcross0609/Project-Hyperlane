# Varonchagger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Varonchagger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Varonchagger)

The Varonchagger was a starship used by Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh and Padawan Lula Talisola to travel to the planet Valo to respond to a Nihil attack there. Upon arriving at Valo, Rwoh and Talisola participated in a fight against Nihil starfighters in the craft, before Rwoh eventually crash-landed the Varonchagger on a beach near Lonisa City. Rwoh felt that Shipmaster Nubarron would not forgive her for crashing the ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Varonchagger
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 2 laser cannons
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
- **Owners:** Nubarron
- **Captains:** Vernestra Rwoh
