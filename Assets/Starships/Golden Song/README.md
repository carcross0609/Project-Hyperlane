# Golden Song

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Golden Song](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Song)

The Golden Song was a starship owned by the Jedi General Crian Maru. The ship was used by Maru and her Padawan, Dree Vandap, during their quest to hunt down the Sith Lord Kaox Krul.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Golden Song
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
- **Captains:** Crian Maru
