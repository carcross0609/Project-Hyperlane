# Renown

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Renown](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Renown)

The Renown was a Venator-class Star Destroyer utilized by the Galactic Republic and its naval branch, as well as the Open Circle Fleet, during the Clone Wars. The Star Destroyer was eventually stolen from the Republic by the Confederacy of Independent Systems and placed in orbit of Abafar, where it was loaded with huge amounts of rhydonium from the planet.
