# Truant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Truant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Truant)

The Truant was a modified Lux-400 yacht. It and its crew were on the wanted lists in multiple star systems due to their habit of smuggling weapons. In 14 BBY, the starship was pulled from hyperspace by the interdiction field of an Imperial Detainer CC-2200, a CC-7700 frigate, and an Immobilizer 418 cruiser in the Obroa-skai system. After it was identified, it was destroyed by the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Executrix.

## Specifications

- **Model:** Lux-400
