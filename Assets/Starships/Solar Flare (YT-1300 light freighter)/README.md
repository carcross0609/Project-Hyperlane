# Solar Flare

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Solar Flare (YT-1300 light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solar_Flare_%28YT-1300_light_freighter%29)

The Solar Flare was a modified YT-1300 light freighter owned and operated by the Devaronian Trynic. The Solar Flare could often be found prowling the space lanes in the Minos Cluster.
