# Cumulus-class corsair

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cumulus-class corsair](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cumulus-class_corsair)

The Cumulus-class corsair was a type of corsair ship used by the Pirate King Gorian Shard approximately five years after the Battle of Endor.

- **Name:** Cumulus-class corsair

- **Engine:** Multiple engines

- **Armament:**

- **Complement:** At least 10 F3-Vara snubfighters

- **Lifesupport:** Equipped
- **Communications:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Gorian Shard's pirate crew
