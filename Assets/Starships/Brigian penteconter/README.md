# Brigian penteconter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Brigian penteconter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brigian_penteconter)

The Brigian penteconter was a model of large warship used by the empire of the Tionese warlord Xim the Despot. Named for the planet Brigia, the starships were believed to be cruiser-class by Ximologist historians thousands of years after the reign of Xim.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Brigian penteconter
- **Affiliation:** Xim's empire
