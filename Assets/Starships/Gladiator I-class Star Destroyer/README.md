# Gladiator I-class Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gladiator I-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gladiator_I-class_Star_Destroyer)

The Gladiator I-class Star Destroyer was the first sub-class of the Gladiator-class line of Star Destroyers used by the Galactic Empire after the Clone Wars and during the Galactic Civil War. It was succeeded by the Gladiator II-class Star Destroyer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gladiator I-class Star Destroyer
- **Line:** Gladiator-class Star Destroyer
- **Model:** Gladiator I-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Armament:**
  - Dual light turbolaser batteries (6)
  - Point-defense laser cannon batteries (3)
  - Torpedo or medium concussion missile launcher batteries (2)
  -   Medium concussion missiles (300)
  - Tractor beam batteries (3)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
