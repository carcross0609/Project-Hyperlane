# YV-666 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YV-666 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YV-666_light_freighter)

The YV-666 light freighter, also known simply as the YV-series freighter, YV-series light freighter, or YV-666, was a model of light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. A modified YV-series known as the Hound's Tooth was used by the Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk.
