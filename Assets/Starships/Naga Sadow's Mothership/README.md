# Naga Sadow's Mothership

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Naga Sadow's Mothership](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naga_Sadow%27s_Mothership)

Naga Sadow's Mothership was a Sith Meditation Sphere that the Dark Lord of the Sith Naga Sadow used as a flagship during the Great Hyperspace War. In 5000 BBY, the sphere was destroyed when Sadow made Primus Goluud go supernova during the Battle at Primus Goluud.
