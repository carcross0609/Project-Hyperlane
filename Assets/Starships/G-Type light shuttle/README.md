# G-Type light shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-Type light shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-Type_light_shuttle)

G-Type light shuttles, also known as G-Wing shuttles, were small armed transports used during the Jedi Civil War and Sith Civil Wars. They were used to ferry small parties back and forth between planets and capital ships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G-Type light shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Hoersch-Kessel Drive, Inc.
- **Model:** G-Type light shuttle
- **Type:** Transport
- **Cost:** 200,000 credits (95,000 used)
- **Length:** 23 m
- **Max Speed:** 950 km/h
- **Armament:** Double laser cannons
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Passengers:**
  - 3
  - 0 (escape shuttle variant)
- **Capacity:** 10 tons
- **Consumables:**
  - 1 week
  - 4 months (escape shuttle variant)
- **Availability:** Restricted
- **Role:** Transportation
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sith Empire
  - Mandalorian Neo-Crusaders
