# Swoop cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Swoop cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Swoop_cruiser)

The Swoop cruiser was a nickname that was given by the Alliance of Free Planets to one of the capital ships that escorted the Syalann during the Nagai–Tof War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Swoop cruiser
- **Designer:**
  - Faruun
  - Nagai
- **Manufacturer:** Faruun
- **Type:** Swoop cruiser
- **Countermeasures:** Cloaking device
- **Role:** Cruiser
- **Firstuse:** Nagai–Tof War
- **Affiliation:** Nagai
