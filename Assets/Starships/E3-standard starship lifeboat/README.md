# E3-standard starship lifeboat

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E3-standard starship lifeboat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E3-standard_starship_lifeboat)

The E3-standard starship lifeboat, or simply the E3 Escape Pod, was a type of escape pod manufactured by His Grace the Duke Gadal-Herm's Safety Inspectorate. Grand Master Yoda used one in his journey to the planet Dagobah to live in exile after the rise of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E3-standard starship lifeboat
- **Manufacturer:** His Grace the Duke Gadal-Herm's Safety Inspectorate
- **Model:** E3-standard starship lifeboat
- **Type:** Escape pod
- **Length:** 4 meters (with legs retracted)
- **Width:** 3.5 meters (diameter)
- **Max Accel:** 300 G
- **Max Speed:** Over 40,000 kph (during descent)
- **Hdsystem:** None
- **Passengers:** 3
- **Consumables:** 4 weeks' food and air
- **Affiliation:** Yoda
