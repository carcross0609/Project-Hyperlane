# Bi-wing snub fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bi-wing snub fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bi-wing_snub_fighter)

Bi-wing snubfighters were primitive starfighters that were developed during the Pius Dea Crusades. They were used by both the Pius Dea Republic Navy and its opponents over a millennia of internecine warfare and crusades.
