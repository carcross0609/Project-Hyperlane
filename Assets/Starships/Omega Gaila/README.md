# Omega Gaila

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Omega Gaila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omega_Gaila)

The Omega Gaila was a cargo transport ship. The ship was used by the Galactic Empire to transport containers of ammunition from the Regional Naval Supply Area near Gall, to the Death Star I while it was under construction at Despayre.
