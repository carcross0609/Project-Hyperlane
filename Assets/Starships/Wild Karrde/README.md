# Wild Karrde

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wild Karrde](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wild_Karrde)

The Wild Karrde was a heavily modified Corellian Engineering Corporation Action VI bulk transport used by Talon Karrde for smuggling and as a mobile base of operations. The craft's dented and worn hull plates concealed an incredible mobile base of operations for Karrde and his band of smugglers.
