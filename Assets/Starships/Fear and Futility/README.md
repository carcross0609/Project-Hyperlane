# Fear and Futility

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fear and Futility](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fear_and_Futility)

The Fear and Futility was a Warlord Heavy Cruiser used by the Void Wings pirates during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, it operated in the Bright Jewel and Kashyyyk systems where it was spotted together with a group of Void Wings starfighters. The ship was destroyed in 1 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fear and Futility
- **Type:** Warlord Heavy Cruiser
- **Destroyed:** 1 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Void Wings
