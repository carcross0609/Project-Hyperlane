# Triumphant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Triumphant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Triumphant)

The Triumphant was a Venator-class Star Destroyer and the flagship of Jedi Master Plo Koon during the Clone Wars. The starship was destroyed in the Battle of Abregado, during which Koon and his fleet encountered the Subjugator-class heavy cruiser Malevolence.
