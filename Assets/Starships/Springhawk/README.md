# Springhawk

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Springhawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Springhawk)

The Springhawk was a heavy cruiser of the Chiss Ascendancy navy that was commanded by Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo. After the Vagaari pirate operations, command of the ship was temporarily taken away from Thrawn, though he regained control soon after. Command of the Springhawk was relinquished by Thrawn after being exiled by a Chiss military tribunal.
