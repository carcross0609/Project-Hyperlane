# JLF-19 Junkros Fighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JLF-19 Junkros Fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JLF-19_Junkros_Fighter)

The JLF-19 Junkros Fighter was a starfighter used by various criminal factions during the Imperial Era including the Ashiga Clan, Crimson Dawn, the Hutt Clan, the Pyke Syndicate, and an assortment of pirate groups. The JLF-19 was equipped with two KX9 laser cannons, four small sublight engines, and a hyperdrive system.
