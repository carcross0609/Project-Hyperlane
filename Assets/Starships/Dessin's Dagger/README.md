# Dessin Dagger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dessin's Dagger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dessin%27s_Dagger)

Dessin's Dagger was a modified YT-1300 light freighter that was active during the Clone Wars. In about 21 BBY, the freighter traveled to the Druckenwell system and there, the vessel's crew attempted to fit a device to a luxury liner. However, the freight was detected by local Galactic Republic forces and a trio of Republic Z-95 Headhunters were sent to apprehend Dessin Dagger. The freighter sped off in an attempt to flee the system, and the Z-95s followed it in hot pursuit. However, the Heroes of Cularin&mdash;a group of freelance agents from the Cularin system&mdash;exited hyperspace in the Druckenwell system in a freighter, and their ship almost collided with the Dagger. The three fighters approached the YT-1300 and in desperation, Dessin Dagger opened fire on them and destroyed one of the Z-95s.
