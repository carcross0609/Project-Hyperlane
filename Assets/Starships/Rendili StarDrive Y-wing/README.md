# Rendili StarDrive Y-wing

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rendili StarDrive Y-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rendili_StarDrive_Y-wing)

The Rendili StarDrive Y-wing was a variant of Koensayr Manufacturing's BLT Y-wing starfighter designed by Rendili StarDrive. Unlike its predecessor, the Rendili StarDrive Y-wing was constructed with passenger transportation in mind, being much larger than the typical BTL Y-wing model so that it could ferry three passengers alongside a designated pilot and astromech co-pilot. At least one of these vessels was owned by Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker while he was acting as an agent for the New Republic's Senate Planetary Intelligence Network in 5 ABY.
