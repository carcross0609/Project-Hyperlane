# RS Revenge

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RS Revenge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RS_Revenge)

The RS Revenge was a cruiser in the Rebel Alliance's fleet during their civil war against the Galactic Empire. At some point between 0 BBY and 1 ABY, it was captained by a man named Kazan and stationed at an Alliance base that was hidden in an asteroid field. When the Rebel-allied smugglers Han Solo and Chewbacca left the base to retrieve a mystical artifact known as the Talisman, Kazan recorded a starlog journal entry about their mission from the RS Revenge bridge. His entry, which mentioned the cruiser by name, was later used as narration for a cartoon created about the mission.

## Specifications
