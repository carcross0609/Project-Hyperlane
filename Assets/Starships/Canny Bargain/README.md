# Canny Bargain

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Canny Bargain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Canny_Bargain)

The Canny Bargain was a starship equipped with deflector shield generators that served in the New Republic Defense Fleet. In 5 ABY, the vessel participated in the New Republic's assault on the Galactic Empire's loyalist forces above the planet Jakku. During the subsequent battle, the Canny Bargain lost its shields and suffered a hull breach. The ship subsequently withdrew from the fray, and news of its retreat reached the refitted New Republic Star Destroyer Deliverance, where the latter vessel's commanding officer, General Hera Syndulla, was informed of the Canny Bargain withdrawal by one of her bridge officers.
