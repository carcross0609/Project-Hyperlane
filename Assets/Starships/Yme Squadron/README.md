# Yme Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yme Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yme_Squadron)

YME Squadron was a squadron of 3 A-wings used by Bothans during the Galactic Civil War. The fighters, along with X-wings from Logan Squadron and Junker Squadron attempted to protect Muurian transports Botani, and an escort shuttle Com 1 from being captured by Imperial forces lead by Darth Vader. The fighters were all destroyed by Assault Gunboats, one of which piloted by Maarek Stele and the transports were captured.
