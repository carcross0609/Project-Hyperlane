# Prowler 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prowler 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prowler_1)

Prowler 1 was an Imperial CR92a Assassin-class corvette that participated in the Battle of Remitik. It was the first of four Imperial corvettes to arrive at the battle.

- **Name:** Prowler 1
