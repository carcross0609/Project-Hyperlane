# Reckless Abandon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reckless Abandon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reckless_Abandon)

The Reckless Abandon was a Gallofree Yards transport which was escorted to Borleias in 27 ABY during the Yuuzhan Vong War. Delivering vital supplies for the New Republic garrison stationed on the planet, Reckless Abandon was accompanied by the Taanab Yellow Aces. Although attacked by Yuuzhan Vong forces as it journeyed towards Borleias, the transport was protected by several New Republic starships and made its way safely to its destination.
