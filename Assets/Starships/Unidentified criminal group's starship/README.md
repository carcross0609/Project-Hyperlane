# Unidentified criminal group's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified criminal group's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_criminal_group%27s_starship)

A starship was utilized by a criminal group in 3 ABY. The starship was in space above when Luke Skywalker flew his T-65B X-wing starfighter near the ship. The group's boss wanted a T-65 and the captain ordered the starship to use it's magna-harpoons to fire at the ship. As the ship started to bring in Skywalker, Darth Vader used his TIE Advanced to free Skywalker for himself and fired at the ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified criminal group's starship
- **Armament:** Multiple magna-harpoons
- **Crew:** Multiple criminals
- **Affiliation:** A criminal group
- **Captains:** A captain
