# Reliance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reliance (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reliance_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

The Reliance was a Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy during the time of the Galactic Civil War. Under the command of Captain Iolan Gendarr, it guarded the planet Jardeen IV. Following the Battle of Endor, Gendarr evacuated General Arndall Lott from the world and established himself as a warlord.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Reliance
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Complement:**
  - All Terrain Armored Transports
  - All Terrain Scout Transports
  - Scimitar assault bombers
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:** Captain Iolan Gendarr
