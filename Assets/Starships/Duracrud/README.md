# Duracrud

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Duracrud](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duracrud)

The Duracrud was a YV-666 given to Uran Lavint after her capture by Jacen Solo, in recompense for the appropriation of her usual ship, Breathe My Jets. When she insulted him, Jacen ordered that the hyperdrive should be sabotaged to fail upon a second jump, knowing that smugglers usually jumped to a remote location before continuing onto their destination. However, the Dark Jedi Alema Rar had stowed away in the hold, trusting that Lavint would be able to help her track down Han and Leia Solo. Alema helped her repair the hyperdrive in exchange for her help.
