# Xerxes

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xerxes (Carrack-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xerxes_%28Carrack-class%29)

Xerxes was a Carrack-class light cruiser that saw service with the Imperial Navy. Along with its sister ship Nexus and the Victory-class Star Destroyer Vagrant, the Xerxes attempted to stop the Defiance Task Force from rendezvousing with the Alliance Fleet at Sullust. However, the Imperial force was held at bay by a unit of A-wing starfighters. Two of the fighters were piloted by Olin Garn and Ace Azzameen.
