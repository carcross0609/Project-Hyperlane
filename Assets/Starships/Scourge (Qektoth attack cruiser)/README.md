# Scourge

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scourge (Qektoth attack cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge_%28Qektoth_attack_cruiser%29)

The Scourge was a Qektoth attack cruiser that was in use by the Qektoth Confederation as of 8 ABY. It fought against the New Republic corvette FarStar while in the Qu'mock system. The Scourge was defeated as the ship was unable to counter attacks from multiple fire arcs due to the design of the vessel's power generation system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scourge
- **Model:** Qektoth attack cruiser
- **Length:** 103 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 4 Plasma cannons
  - 1 Bio-energy array
- **Affiliation:** Qektoth Confederation
