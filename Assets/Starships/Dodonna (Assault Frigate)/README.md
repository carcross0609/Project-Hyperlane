# Dodonna

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dodonna (Assault Frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dodonna_%28Assault_Frigate%29)

The Dodonna was an Assault Frigate Mark I in service to the Rebel Alliance and later the New Republic. The vessel was commissioned following the supposed death of Rebel general Jan Dodonna. The Dodonna was part of a task force of New Republic warships under the command of General Wedge Antilles during the Darksaber crisis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dodonna
- **Model:** Assault Frigate Mark I
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Length:** 700 meters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - New Republic
- **Captains:** General Wedge Antilles
