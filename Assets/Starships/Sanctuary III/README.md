# Sanctuary III

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sanctuary III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sanctuary_III)

The Sanctuary III was a modified Pelta-class frigate in the service of the Galactic Republic. Its role as an armed and armored hospital ship was instrumental in the effort to curb losses during the Clone Wars. Just before the Battle of the Kaliida Nebula in 21 BBY, the Sanctuary III was one of several in a convoy of Pelta-class frigates sent from the planet Naboo to assist in the transportation of wounded clone troopers from the Republic's Kaliida Shoals Medical Center before the Haven-class medical station could be destroyed by the approaching Separatist warship Malevolence.
