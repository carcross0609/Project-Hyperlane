# Unidentified Mon Calamari starship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Mon Calamari starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Mon_Calamari_starship)

A type of Mon Calamari starship was used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. During the mutiny on Mon Cala, one such vessel was deployed under the command of rebel Admiral Gial Ackbar, as he led an attack on the Galactic Empire's Mon Cala blockade that was preventing ships of the Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet to escape.
