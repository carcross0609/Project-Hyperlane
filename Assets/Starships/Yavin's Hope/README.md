# Yavin's Hope

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yavin's Hope](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yavin%27s_Hope)

Yavin's Hope was a Mon Calamari cruiser that was commanded by General Vanden Willard during the Galactic Civil War. Following the mutiny on Mon Cala, the cruiser was destroyed, along with its crew, when the Galactic Empire's Death Squadron attacked the Rebel Alliance's Mako-Ta Space Docks.
