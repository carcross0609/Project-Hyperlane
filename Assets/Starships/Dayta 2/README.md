# Dayta 2

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dayta 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dayta_2)

Dayta 2 was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport that was carrying workers following the Battle of Hoth. It arrived, along with Onece group at Outpost D-34 in the Javin system, requesting permission to pass through the sector. Maarek Stele inspected Dayta 2 and 1 to find no contraband in the transports, so both transports were allowed to leave. However in Onece 3 there were rebels hidden on the freighter resulting in the Skirmish at Outpost D-34.
