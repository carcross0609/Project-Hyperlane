# Scion shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scion shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scion_shuttle)

The Scion shuttle was the custom starship used by witch Morgan Elsbeth as a transport and private shuttle. In around 9 ABY, Elsbeth used the ship to travel to the planet Seatos, where Baylan Skoll believed he had discovered the reflex point to the Pathway to Peridea to travel to another galaxy and rescue Thrawn from his exile. She later used the same ship to commute to and from the hyperspace transport ring Eye of Sion to prepare it for departure, and later to finally board it for the extragalactic journey. The ship was gold-colored and was equipped with two stabilizers, one on each side.
