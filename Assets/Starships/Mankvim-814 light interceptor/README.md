# Mankvim-814 light interceptor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mankvim-814 light interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mankvim-814_light_interceptor)

The Mankvim-814 light interceptor, also known as the Techno Union Fighter, was an interceptor manufactured by the Techno Union during the era of the Clone Wars. The Grand Hall of the Traders' Guild in Pau City on the planet Utapau was converted by the guild into a factory to construct Mankvim-814s prior to the Battle of Utapau.
