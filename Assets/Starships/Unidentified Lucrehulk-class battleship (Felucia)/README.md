# Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship (Felucia)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Lucrehulk-class_battleship_%28Felucia%29)

A Lucrehulk-class battleship was part of a Confederacy of Independent Systems fleet led by the Nihilist over Felucia. During the rebellion there around 22 BBY, a Jedi-led task force intervened. Master Yoda ordered them to concentrate all fire on the Droid Control ship. A pair of younglings trained by Mavra Zane destroyed the Receiver Stations. The vessel was then destroyed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship
- **Manufacturer:** Hoersch-Kessel
- **Model:** Droid Control Ship
- **Type:** Lucrehulk-class battleship
- **Destroyed:** c. 22 BBU
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
