# Glory of Kamgeddon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Glory of Kamgeddon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glory_of_Kamgeddon)

Glory of Kamgeddon was a cathedral ship that was used by the Galactic Republic during the Pius Dea Era, a time of anti-alien crusades that were instigated by the Pius Dea faith that controlled the Republic. It shared its name with the Kamgeddon system. In modern times, the name Glory of Kamgeddon continued to evoke dread on the worlds it once held captive.
