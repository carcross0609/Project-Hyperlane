# Shadow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow (light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_%28light_freighter%29)

The Shadow was a light freighter owned by the Imperial smuggler Lett Minak. A few years after Emperor Palpatine died in 4 ABY, Minak worked for rogue Imperial Moff Feleea, ferrying "special requests," which involved travel to New Republic space or territories controlled by renegade Moffs. Dissatisfied working for Feleea, the smuggler traveled to the Deep Core and began working for Moff Relans as a spy. Minak continued working for Feleea aboard the Shadow, but lied about his dealings in the Deep Core to avoid arousing suspicion about his regular runs to the region. He ferried Imperial agents and reports between Feleea's territory and the Deep Core. With both Moffs protecting him from inspections in their respective territories, Minak amassed wealth from the contraband he smuggled on the side.
