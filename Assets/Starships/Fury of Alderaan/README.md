# Fury of Alderaan

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fury of Alderaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fury_of_Alderaan)

The Fury of Alderaan was an Alliance to Restore the Republic cruiser that was used to carry members of the Ardan resistance to Jagomir after the Battle of the Gauntlet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fury of Alderaan
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
