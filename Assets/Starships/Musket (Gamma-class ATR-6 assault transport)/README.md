# Musket

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Musket (Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Musket_%28Gamma-class_ATR-6_assault_transport%29)

Musket was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport that was used by the Ripoblus for their attack upon the Dimok-controlled Science Station Youst. It was destroyed during the battle that resulted.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Musket
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Length:** 45 meters
- **Width:** 30.4 meters
- **Height:** 20.5 meters
- **Role:** Boarding craft
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
