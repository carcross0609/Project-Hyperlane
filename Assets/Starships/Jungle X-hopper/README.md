# Jungle X-hopper

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jungle X-hopper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jungle_X-hopper)

The jungle X-hopper, nicknamed the mini-rig, was a custom-designed compact starship created by Rose Tico and the Resistance's Engineering Corps from surplus found in a New Republic depot. Its cockpit was from a T-65 X-wing, fitted with repulsor coils, thrusters, and steering vanes. The X-hopper assisted with traffic control at the Resistance's base on Ajan Kloss, guiding ships in and out of the jungle.
