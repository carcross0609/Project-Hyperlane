# Respite

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Respite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Respite)

The Respite was a hospital ship of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. It was present at the Battle of Praesitlyn. Its complement included bacta tanks and skin-regeneration equipment.
