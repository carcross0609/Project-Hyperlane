# Imperial Cargo Shuttle

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Imperial Cargo Shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Cargo_Shuttle)

The Imperial Cargo Shuttle was a class of shuttle that was used by the Galactic Empire and the Yasaburō clan on the planet Tao. Yasaburō used one such shuttle to travel to an Imperial-class Star Destroyer and confront his daughter Ochō, who had left the clan for the Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial Cargo Shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Engine:** Sublight engines (4)
- **Role:** Transportation
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Yasaburō clan
