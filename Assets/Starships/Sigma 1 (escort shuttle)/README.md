# Sigma 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sigma 1 (escort shuttle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sigma_1_%28escort_shuttle%29)

Sigma 1 was an Imperial Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle. It was one of four such shuttles to participate in an Imperial attack on the factory ZA-13, which at the time was held by traitorous Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sigma 1
- **Type:** Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle
- **Affiliation:** Galctic Empire
