# Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sienar-Chall_Utilipede_Transport)

The Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport was a type of transport vessel. The transport was used by civilian customers as well as criminal organizations such as the Hutt Clan and the Pyke Syndicate. Chef Strono Tuggs owned a modified starship of this class, which he converted into a mobile kitchen and restaurant to take his traveling diner Tuggs' Grub to Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo on Batuu.
