# BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BTA-NR2_Y-wing_starfighter)

The BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter, also known as the Resistance Y-wing, was a variant of the Y-wing starfighter produced by Koensayr Manufacturing that was used by the New Republic and the Resistance.
