# Jannimak

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jannimak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jannimak)

The Jannimak was a technologically advanced research vessel that crashed on the planet Dantooine at some point around 3622 BBY. The research vessel used for a project to study and acclimatize different lifeforms from many worlds including creatures, plants, and microorganisms. Doctor Rotkar, the vessel's creator, was proud of the success of the project.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jannimak
- **Type:** Research vessel
- **Destroyed:** c. 3622 BBY, Dantooine
