# Quicksilver

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quicksilver (Iaco Stark)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quicksilver_%28Iaco_Stark%29)

The Quicksilver was a private starship owned by Iaco Stark at the time of the Stark Hyperspace War. During the war, it was hijacked by Jedi Master Adi Gallia, who used it to transport Minister Nute Gunray and Senator Finis Valorum back to Coruscant from Troiken. There, they informed the Galactic Senate and the Jedi Council of the war, and were able to secure Trade Federation assistance, leading to the Republic victory at the Fifth Battle of Qotile.
