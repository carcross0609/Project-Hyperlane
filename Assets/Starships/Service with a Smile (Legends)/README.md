# Service with a Smile

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Service with a Smile/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Service_with_a_Smile/Legends)

Service with a Smile was a LAAT/i gunship used by the Grand Army of the Republic during the Clone Wars between the Confederacy of Independent Systems and the Galactic Republic. The nose art featured a bomb with Kit Fisto's face on it, and written in Aurebesh underneath was the name of the ship. It was used during a assault on Mon Calamari.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Service with a Smile
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Line:** LAAT series
- **Model:** Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry
- **Type:** Gunship
- **Engine:** Two equipped
- **Armament:**
  - Anti-personnel laser turrets (2 at the front)
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Co-pilot (1)
- **Passengers:**
  - Several clone troopers and a Jedi Master
  -   Master Kit Fisto
- **Role:** Troop Transport
- **Affiliation:** Grand Army of the Republic
- **Captains:** High Jedi General Kit Fisto
