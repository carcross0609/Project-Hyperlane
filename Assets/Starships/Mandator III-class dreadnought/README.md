# Mandator III-class dreadnought

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mandator III-class dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandator_III-class_dreadnought)

The Mandator III-class dreadnought was a dreadnaught-scale warship in the Imperial Navy, as well as the third class of Star Dreadnought developed under the Mandator series, alongside the Bellator-class dreadnought.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mandator III-class dreadnought
- **Line:** Star Dreadnoughts
- **Model:** Mandator III-class dreadnought
- **Type:** Dreadnaught
- **Length:** 12,000 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Equipped with more firepower than the Bellator-class
- **Role:** Dreadnought
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Imperial Navy
