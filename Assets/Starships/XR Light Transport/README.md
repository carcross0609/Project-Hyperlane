# XR Light Transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XR Light Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XR_Light_Transport)

The XR Light Transport was a Corellian Engineering Corporation light transport that was in use during the Third Galactic War. The Mercenary's Honor was a XR Light Transport used by the mercenary Sanlo Raal around 3623 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** XR Light Transport
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
