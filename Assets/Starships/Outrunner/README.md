# Outrunner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Outrunner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outrunner)

The Outrunner was a freighter purchased by Dash Rendar with the profits from his career as a freelance pilot, smuggler, and mercenary. The black ship was heavily armed with both laser turrets and proton torpedo launchers, and could be powered up quickly to enable a rapid escape. Rendar used the Outrunner to disable the luxury liner Star of Empire after the crew abandoned ship when the liner's artificial intelligence, SIM, seized control of the vessel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Outrunner
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Armament:**
  - Laser turrets (at least 1 turbolaser)
  - Proton torpedo launchers
- **Crew:** 1 (Pilot)
- **Owners:** Dash Rendar
