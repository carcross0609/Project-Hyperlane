# S-12 Blackbolt

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S-12 Blackbolt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-12_Blackbolt)

The S-12 Blackbolt was a starfighter used by the Sith Empire which performed in scout roles. Created at Tandankin to fulfill the role of reconnaissance, the Blackbolt was equipped with advanced sensor and communications suites. The scout craft had little-to-no armor and was only lightly shielded relying on its swiftness, the power of two laser cannons and a volley of rockets to harass enemy forces.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S-12 Blackbolt
- **Manufacturer:** Tandankin shipyards
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped

- **Armament:**

  - Equipable rockets or missiles
- **Crew:** 2
- **Role:** Scout
- **Affiliation:** Reconstituted Sith Empire
