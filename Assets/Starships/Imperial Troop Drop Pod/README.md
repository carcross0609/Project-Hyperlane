# Imperial Troop Drop Pod

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial Troop Drop Pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Troop_Drop_Pod)

The Imperial Troop Drop Pod was a troop deployment pod used by the Galactic Empire. They were designed as a stealthy means of deploying two squads of troopers, mainly Imperial special forces such as Storm commandos, while unwary observers would mistake them for meteors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial Troop Drop Pod
- **Type:** Orbit-to-surface deployment pod
- **Length:** 10 meters
- **Passengers:** 20 troops
- **Capacity:** 200 kg
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
