# Pulsar Lines

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pulsar Lines](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pulsar_Lines)

Pulsar Lines was a civilian Ferryboat liner that was present at Calast, a Golan Space Colony 2 space station, during a battle in 3 ABY. There was a dinner party taking place inside the ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pulsar Lines
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** Ferryboat liner
- **Length:** 363 meters
- **Role:** Passenger liner
