# Infiltrator-series long-range fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Infiltrator-series long-range fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltrator-series_long-range_fighter)

The Infiltrator-series long-range fighter, also referred to as simply the Infiltrator series fighter, was a model of long-range starfighter manufactured by Republic Sienar Systems. One such vessel, the Mystic, was in the possession of the Sith Lord Darth Bane. It was produced after the Seventh Battle of Ruusan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Infiltrator-series long-range fighter
- **Manufacturer:** Republic Sienar Systems
- **Type:** Long-range starfighter
- **Crew:** Pilot
