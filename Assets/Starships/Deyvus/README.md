# Deyvus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Deyvus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deyvus)

The Deyvus was a merchant ship that the Separatist General Grievous used as a trap for Jedi General Anakin Skywalker during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Deyvus
- **Armament:** Heavy laser cannons
- **Crew:**
  - B1-series battle droids
  - Droidekas
- **Role:** Merchant ship
- **Destroyed:** 22 BBY, near an unstable nebula
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Owners:** General Grievous
