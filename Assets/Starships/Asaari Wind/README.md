# Asaari Wind

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Asaari Wind](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asaari_Wind)

The Asaari Wind was a luxury yacht stolen from Bimm crimelord Ritinki around 0 ABY by Cecil Noone and his partners-in-crime. Ritinki was killed in the course of the theft. At various times, it operated under the aliases Hieroglyph, Spiraling Shape, Tailchaser, and Voona's Dream II.
