# Ahr's Venator-class Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ahr's Venator-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahr%27s_Venator-class_Star_Destroyer)

This Venator-class Star Destroyer was the flagship of the Imperial Governor Ahr. He oversaw Imperial campaigns throughout his sector onboard this vessel until he was killed by a creature in the Kankalo Belt Containment Zone.
