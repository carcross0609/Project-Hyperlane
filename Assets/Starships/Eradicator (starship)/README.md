# Eradicator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eradicator (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eradicator_%28starship%29)

The Eradicator was a ship that was a part of the fleet of Moff Kentor Sarne during his campaign. Its commanding officer was Captain Thulian Merast and its first officer was Lieutenant Jav Remlyn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Eradicator
- **Affiliation:** Moff Sarne's forces
- **Navy:** Moff Sarne's space forces
- **Captains:** Thulian Merast
