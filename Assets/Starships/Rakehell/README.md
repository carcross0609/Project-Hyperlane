# Rakehell

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rakehell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rakehell)

The Rakehell was an Onager-class Star Destroyer in service to the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At one point, it flew alongside two Victory-class Star Destroyers near a red nebula. The Star Destroyer was equipped with engines, two superweapons on its bow, and two deflector shield generator domes. As an Onager, the Rakehell was 1,288 meters in length.
