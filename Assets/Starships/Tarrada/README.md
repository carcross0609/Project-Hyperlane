# Tarrada

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tarrada](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tarrada)

The tarrada, also known as a tarrada cruiser, was a frigate-equivalent starship used by the Hutts. A number of cruisers were deployed at the First Battle of Vontor. During the Second Galactic Civil War, they were used in the Second Battle of Balmorra and the Battle of Kuat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tarrada
- **Manufacturer:** Hutts
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Affiliation:**
  - Hutts
  - Confederation
