# Irfu Clover

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Irfu Clover](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Irfu_Clover)

The Iru Clover was a heavy cruiser of the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. It was used for stationing for larger missions throughout the galaxy and had a bay with Jedi Vectors.

- **Name:** Irfu Clover
- **Type:** Heavy cruiser
