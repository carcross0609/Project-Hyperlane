# Eriaduan cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eriaduan cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eriaduan_cruiser)

The Eriaduan cruiser was a class of cruiser used by the Eriaduans of the planet Eriadu that was active during the High Republic Era. The cruiser was long, thin, and blade-like, resembling a sword, and had boarding capabilities as well as armament.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Eriaduan cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Eriaduan flotilla
