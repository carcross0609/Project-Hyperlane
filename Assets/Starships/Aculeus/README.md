# Aculeus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aculeus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aculeus)

The Aculeus was a Mandator III-class dreadnought that was part of the navy of the Galactic Empire and its short-lived successor, the Dark Empire. The vessel was one of five Mandator III-class dreadnoughts to be part of the Dark Empire's attack on the New Republic&ndash;controlled Core Worlds in 10 ABY. Although the Imperial assault was initially quite successful, various Imperial factions turned on one another in a mad frenzy known as the Imperial Mutiny. As part of that conflict, which also occurred in 10 ABY, the Aculeus was destroyed in battle with squadrons of TIE/D automated starfighters at the world Drearia.
