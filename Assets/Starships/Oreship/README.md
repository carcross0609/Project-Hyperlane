# Oreship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oreship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oreship)

Oreships were a type of starship used by the Mining Guild in the Celestial Wake at some point before 10 BBY. Tobias Beckett and his crew spent three months attacking Mining Guild oreships on the fringes of the Celestial Wake.
