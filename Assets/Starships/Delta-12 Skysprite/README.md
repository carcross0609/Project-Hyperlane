# Delta-12 Skysprite

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Delta-12 Skysprite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delta-12_Skysprite)

The Delta-12 Skysprite was a type of starfighter utilized by the Jedi Order. The craft was known for its agility and speed. Jedi Master Mace Windu used a Delta-12 Skysprite when embarking on a mission to the planet Metagos.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Delta-12 Skysprite
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hyperdrive:** Hyperdrive ring required
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
