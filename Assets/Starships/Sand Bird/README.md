# Sand Bird

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Sand Bird](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sand_Bird)

The Sand Bird was a gunship used by the bounty hunter Sevn and her droid partner IV-A4. After the Sand Bird was damaged in an attack by Basham agents, Sevn and IV-A4 traveled to the planet Genya to make repairs, where they were hired by the businessman Jin-Sim Canbelon. During the job, Sevn decided to betray Canbelon due to his use of enslaved individuals, and IV-A4 left with the Sand Bird. However, he later returned in it to help Sevn fight against Canbelon.
