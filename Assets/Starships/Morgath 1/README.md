# Morgath 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Morgath 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morgath_1)

Freighter Morgath 1 was a Container transport used for backup convoy services by Imperial defector Admiral Harkov's Victory-class Star Destroyer, the Protector. It was intercepted and shot down by Imperial Ace Maarek Stele along with Morgath 5, BFF-1 bulk freighter group Karflo, group SoroSuub, and CR90 corvette group Sundog.
