# Charger 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Charger 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charger_1)

Charger 1 was a CR90 corvette used by the Nharwaak. It fought at the Conflict at Mylok IV and, together with Charger 2, formed the Charger Group.
