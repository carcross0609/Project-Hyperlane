# Unidentified Mon Calamari cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Mon Calamari cruiser (Sullust system)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Mon_Calamari_cruiser_%28Sullust_system%29)

A model of Mon Calamari cruiser was used by the Alliance Navy during the Galactic Civil War, a pan-galactic conflict between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire. The warship was capable of traveling in space via engines. It had bulbous yet slim features, with a thin protrusion at the rear.
