# Des'maric Two

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Des'maric Two](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Des%27maric_Two)

The Des'maric Two was a GAT-12h Skipray Blastboat operated by the Des'maric Pirates in the Kathol sector around 8 ABY. Along with Des'maric One and Des'maric Three, the vessels were referred to as the Des'maric Blastboats.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Des'maric Two
- **Model:** GAT-12h Skipray Blastboat
- **Length:** 25 meters
- **Affiliation:** Des'maric Pirates
