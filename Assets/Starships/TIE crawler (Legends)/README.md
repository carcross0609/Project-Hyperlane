# TIE crawler

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIE crawler/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE_crawler/Legends)

The TIE crawler, also known as the century tank or TIE tank, was a light armoured vehicle used by the Galactic Empire. It was an unlikely fusion of two vastly different vehicle types.
