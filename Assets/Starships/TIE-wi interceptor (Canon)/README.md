# TIE/wi interceptor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/wi interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/wi_interceptor)

The TIE/wi interceptor, also referred to as the TIE whisper or simply the whisper, was a First Order TIE fighter series interceptor used during the war against the Resistance. During the war, Supreme Leader Kylo Ren utilised his modified TIE whisper in the search for Exegol and to confront Rey on the planet Pasaana.
