# Cularin IV

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cularin IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cularin_IV)

Cularin IV was a capital ship that was used by the Cularin Militia. In c. 19 BBY, the Militia officer Gavin Bresch had the Heroes of Cularin transported to Cularin IV from Hedrett Groundport on the planet of Cularin, so that he could hire them to prevent the Believers from attacking a summit of Cularin system leaders.
