# Exchequer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Exchequer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exchequer)

The Exchequer was a starship of the Galactic Empire under the command of Moff Boyse. It served as the flagship of the Imperial Navy's blockade of the planet Scipio and, along with Imperial advisors, ensured that the Core Five, the ruling council of the InterGalactic Banking Clan, could only loan or allocate funds to those who were purely loyal to the Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Exchequer
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:** Scipio blockade
- **Captains:** Boyse
