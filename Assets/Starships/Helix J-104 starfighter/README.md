# Helix J-104 starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Helix J-104 starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Helix_J-104_starfighter)

The Helix J-104 starfighter, also known as the Bounty Hunter Fighter, was a class of starfighter that was used the reign of the Galactic Empire as well as after its fall. It had a flat appearance with a central cockpit, which could fit one pilot and two wings, where an engine was located between them.
