# Gold One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gold One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_One)

Gold One was a BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber under the command of Gold Leader Captain Jon Vander during the Galactic Civil War. Vander most notably used Gold One in battles such as the Battle of Scarif and the Battle of Yavin, where it was destroyed when Vander was shot down by the Sith Lord Darth Vader.
