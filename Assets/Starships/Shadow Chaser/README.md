# Shadow Chaser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow Chaser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Chaser)

Shadow Chaser was a unique shuttle produced by the Second Imperium, for use by the Shadow Academy. Though she was a unique prototype, the Shadow Chaser was of a recognizably Imperial design, probably standing in the same lineage as the Theta-class and Lambda-class shuttles. Before she was stolen by Luke Skywalker and his students, Brakiss considered the Shadow Chaser to be the Shadow Academy's finest small ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shadow Chaser
- **Type:** Custom shuttle
- **Hull:** Quantum-crystalline armor
- **Armament:** Turreted laser cannons (multiple)
- **Passengers:** 12
- **Role:**
  - Personnel shuttle
  - Cargo transport
  - Escape vessel
- **Affiliation:**
  - Second Imperium
  - Shadow Academy
  - New Jedi Order
