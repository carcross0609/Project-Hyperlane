# BTS-A2 Long-Range Strike Fighter/Bomber

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BTS-A2 Long-Range Strike Fighter/Bomber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BTS-A2_Long-Range_Strike_Fighter/Bomber)

The BTS-A2 Long-Range Strike Fighter/Bomber (Rebel Alliance designation H-Wing) was an addition to Rebel fighter wings manufactured by Koensayr Manufacturing, a company made famous for their rugged Y-Wing starfighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BTS-A2 Long-Range Strike Fighter/Bomber
- **Manufacturer:** Koensayr Manufacturing
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 17.1 meters
- **Engine:** Koensayr R440 Ion Jet Engines
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1.0
- **Sensor:** Fabritech ANx-y sensor package
- **Avionics:** NH-7 flight control avionics package
- **Armament:**
  - 1 KY12 medium laser cannon turret
  - 2 light ion cannons (fire linked)
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
  -   Standard load: 6 proton torpedoes each
- **Crew:**
  - 1 pilot
  - 2 weapons personnel
- **Passengers:** 1
- **Capacity:** 220 kilograms
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Role:**
  - Assault starfighter
  - Bomber
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
