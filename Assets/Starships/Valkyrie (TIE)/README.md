# Valkyrie

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Valkyrie (TIE)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valkyrie_%28TIE%29)

The Valkryie was a TIE Series fighter that was flown by Air Marshal Cargeloch, an Imperial ace pilot who was stationed on Coruscant during the Imperial Period.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Valkyrie
- **Line:** TIE Series
- **Type:** fighter
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:** Cargeloch (pilot)
