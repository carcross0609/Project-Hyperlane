# Pilum

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pilum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pilum)

The Pilum was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport in the service of the Ripoblus government. It served in the Sepan Civil War during the year 3 ABY. It was destroyed by Maarek Stele of the Galactic Empire during the Battle of Science Station Youst.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pilum
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Length:** 45 meters
- **Width:** 30.4 meters
- **Height:** 20.5 meters
- **Role:** Boarding craft
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Ripoblus government
