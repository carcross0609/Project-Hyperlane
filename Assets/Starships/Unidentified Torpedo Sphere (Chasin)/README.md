# Unidentified Torpedo Sphere

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Torpedo Sphere (Chasin)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Torpedo_Sphere_%28Chasin%29)

During the Imperial Civil War, a Torpedo Sphere was present at Chasin. During a battle fought between two Imperial factions, the Torpedo Sphere was destroyed when it was rammed by the Vengeance-class dreadnought Javelin.
