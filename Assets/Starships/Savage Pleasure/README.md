# Savage Pleasure

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Savage Pleasure](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Savage_Pleasure)

The Savage Pleasure was a resort liner. When the Galactic Empire attempted to assassinate Senator Mael Sidras, all aboard were killed. Among the dead were most of the Khzrry family unit, a prominent Xi'Dec family. The only surviving members of the family, K'lial and Diskio Khzrry, vowed to help bring down the Empire in response, becoming agents of Alliance Intelligence.
