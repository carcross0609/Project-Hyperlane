# Phlac-Arphocc Prototype

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phlac-Arphocc Prototype](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phlac-Arphocc_Prototype)

The Phlac-Arphocc Prototype was a sample model of the droid tri-fighter, manufactured by Phlac-Arphocc Automata Industries. From the prototype, the tri-fighter became one of the greatest aerospace superiority starfighters in the galaxy, and the most feared and hated model in the Confederacy of Independent Systems' arsenal. It was equipped with vectoring engine units and one medium laser cannon accompanied by three light laser cannons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Phlac-Arphocc Prototype
- **Manufacturer:** Phlac-Arphocc Automata Industries
- **Model:** Prototype droid tri-fighter
- **Length:** 5.4 meters
- **Width:** 3.45 meters
- **Engine:** Vectoring engines
- **Armament:**
  - 1 medium laser cannon
  - 3 light laser cannon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Phlac-Arphocc Automata Industries
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
