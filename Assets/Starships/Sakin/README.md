# Sakin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sakin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sakin)

Sakin was a group of four cargo ferries used by Rogue Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin during his insurrection against the Empire. The cargo ferries were present at Zaarin's Research platform, where he developed the TIE Defender starfighter, when Loyalist forces sent a strike team to retake the platform. One of the cargo ferries escaped into hyperspace but the rest were destroyed in the battle.
