# Hazmat scow

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hazmat scow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hazmat_scow)

A hazmat scow was a type of low-quality starship used in the galaxy. At the time of the Iron Blockade, numerous hazmat scows were possessed by the Trade Spine League.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hazmat scow
- **Affiliation:** Trade Spine League
