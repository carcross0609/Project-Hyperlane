# Judicator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Judicator/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Judicator/Legends)

The Judicator was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer, commanded by Captain Brandei, that joined Grand Admiral Thrawn in his campaign against the New Republic some five years after the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Judicator
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Line:** Star Destroyers
- **Model:** Imperial I-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Role:** Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:**
  - Death Squadron
  - Thrawn's fleet
- **Captains:**
  - High Inquisitor Adalric Cessius Brandl
  - Captain Brandei
