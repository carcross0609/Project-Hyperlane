# Bogey Three

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bogey Three (Nikardun base)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogey_Three_%28Nikardun_base%29)

A vessel codednamed Bogey Three was used by the Grysk Hegemony at a battle at listening post KR21. The ship, along with Bogey Two and a mobile repair dock escaped into hyperspace.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bogey Three
- **Type:** Patrol ship
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Grysk Hegemony
- **Navy:** Grysk armada
- **Fleet:** Jixtus's fleet
