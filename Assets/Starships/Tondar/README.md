# Tondar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tondar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tondar)

Tondar was an Assassin-class corvette used by the Airam. It was part of the joint-operation that was attacked by rival Airam clan leader Ilay and the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Wasp. Fortunately the operation was a success and Tondar escaped into hyperspace.
