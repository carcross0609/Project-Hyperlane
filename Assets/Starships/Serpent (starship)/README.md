# Serpent (starship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Serpent (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Serpent_%28starship%29)

The Imperial Nebulon-B escort frigate Serpent and the Frigate Ruhger battled in the Greater Plooriod Cluster. She had a squadron of Tie Bombers and was destroyed by the Ruhger, aided by her Y-Wings and transports.
