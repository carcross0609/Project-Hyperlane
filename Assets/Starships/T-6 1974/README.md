# T-6 1974

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-6 1974](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-6_1974)

T-6 1974, also known as T-6 One-Nine-Seven-Four, was a T-6 shuttle used by Ahsoka Tano after the Galactic Civil War when she traveled to the planet Lothal to pick up Sabine Wren and begin their search for Ezra Bridger.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T-6 1974
- **Manufacturer:** Slayn & Korpil
- **Model:** T-6 shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Length:** 22.8 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Double laser cannon (1)
- **Escapepods:** Emergency survival capsules
- **Owners:** Ahsoka Tano
