# Empire

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Empire (Imperial II prototype)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empire_%28Imperial_II_prototype%29)

The Empire, was a prototype Imperial II-class Star Destroyer that served within the Imperial Navy. It was modified with superior shielding, and was commanded by Admiral Kiez.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Empire
- **Model:** Imperial II-class Star Destroyer
- **Sysmods:** Superior shielding to other ships in its class
- **Role:**
  - Destroyer
  - Battleship (downscaled)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:** Admiral Feyet Kiez
