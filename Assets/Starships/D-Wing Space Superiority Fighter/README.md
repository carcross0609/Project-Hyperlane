# D-Wing Space Superiority Fighter

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [D-Wing Space Superiority Fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-Wing_Space_Superiority_Fighter)

The D-Wing Space Superiority Fighter, nicknamed Phoenix, was designed by Elhon Tay of Tay Industries to be a break from the traditional design of most Imperial starfighters due to the fact that many in the Empire had found flaws with the standard TIE/LN starfighters.
