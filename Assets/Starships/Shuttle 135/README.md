# Shuttle 135

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shuttle 135](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shuttle_135)

Shuttle 135 was a Nu-class attack/transport shuttle that operated as part the Galactic Empire's Advanced Science Division. During or after 18 BBY, the shuttle was due to make an arrival at Mount Tantiss on the planet Wayland when a bolt of lightning struck the vessel and knocked out its power, causing the shuttle to fall to the surface. Its crew survived the initial crash, although were later killed by creatures in the Wayland jungles.
