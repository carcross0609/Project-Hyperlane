# Grappa's yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grappa's yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grappa%27s_yacht)

Grappa's yacht was the personal starship of Grappa the Hutt, a crime lord allied with Black Sun. When Grappa killed Macus Kayniph, a high-ranking member of Black Sun, in a fit of rage in 11 ABY, he reluctantly arranged the destruction of his yacht in order to cover up the murder.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grappa's yacht
- **Destroyed:** 11 ABY
- **Owners:** Grappa
