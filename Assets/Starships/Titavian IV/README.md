# Titavian IV

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Titavian IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Titavian_IV)

The Titavian IV was an exploration ship used by the Intergalactic Zoological Society. It was named after the titavian species of Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Titavian IV
- **Role:** Exploration and study
- **Affiliation:** Intergalactic Zoological Society
