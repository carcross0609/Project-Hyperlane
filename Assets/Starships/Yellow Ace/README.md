# Yellow Ace

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yellow Ace](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yellow_Ace)

The Yellow Ace was a ship piloted by Bo Keevil, who performed stunts and tricks while flying it. It was a complex ship to fly, and had wings that could shift into a variety of configurations. Keevil often crashed the ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Yellow Ace
- **Manufacturer:** Ravager Mechanics
- **Model:** Customized "Changeling" Mark 71NB
- **Type:** Racing starfighter
- **Crew:**

  - Astromech droid (1)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Ace Squadron
  - Citizens' Fleet
- **Owners:** Bo Keevil
