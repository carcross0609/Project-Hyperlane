# Prax Inc

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prax Inc](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prax_Inc)

Prax Inc was a Xiytiar-class heavy transport that was present at Calast, a Golan Space Colony 2 space station during the Rescue at Bundil II. The ship contained Holodramas.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Prax Inc
- **Manufacturer:** TransGalMeg Industries, Incorporated
- **Model:** Xiytiar-class heavy transport
- **Length:** 164.8 meters
- **Engine:** 3
