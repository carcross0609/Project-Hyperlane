# Vengeance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vengeance (Vengeance-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vengeance_%28Vengeance-class%29)

The Vengeance was a slender, black Kuat Drive Yards Super Star Destroyer that served as the flagship of the Dark Jedi Jerec in the years before and after the Battle of Endor. It was commanded by Captain Sysco.
