# Scythe 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scythe 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scythe_1)

Scythe 1 was a TIE/LN starfighter of Scythe Squadron during the Battle of Endor. Flown by Major Mianda, Scythe 1 featured the same upgraded Sienar Fleet Systems P-w702 maneuvering jets as the other fighters in its unit, which increased performance in tight quarters. During the battle it pursued Rebel starfighters on Death Star II.
