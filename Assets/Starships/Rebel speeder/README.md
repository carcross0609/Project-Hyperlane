# Rebel speeder

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rebel speeder](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rebel_speeder)

The rebel speeder was a model of starship that was active during the war between the First Order and the Resistance and was used by the Mining Guild. Several of these starships joined the Citizens' Fleet that was assembled by General Lando Calrissian to aid the Resistance against the forces of the Sith Eternal during the Battle of Exegol.

- **Type:** Starship

- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
