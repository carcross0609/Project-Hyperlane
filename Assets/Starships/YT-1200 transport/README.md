# YT-1200 transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YT-1200 transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-1200_transport)

The YT-1200 transport was a transport of the YT-series manufactured by Corellian Engineering Corporation, and was nearly identical to the YT-1210 transport.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YT-1200 transport
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Model:** YT-1200
- **Type:** Transport
- **Engine:** Thrusters (2)
