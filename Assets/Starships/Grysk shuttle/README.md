# Grysk shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grysk shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grysk_shuttle)

A type of shuttle was used by the Grysk operative known as "Jixtus" and his fleet. The shuttles were equipped with engines and could carry several soldiers. Around 18 BBY, Jixtus used one shuttle to travel from his flagship, the Shatter-class WarMaster FateSpinner, to the Kilji war cruiser Whetstone.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grysk shuttle
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Crew:** 1 pilot
- **Role:** Shuttle
- **Affiliation:** Jixtus's fleet
