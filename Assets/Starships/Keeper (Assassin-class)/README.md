# Keeper (Assassin-class)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Keeper (Assassin-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper_%28Assassin-class%29)

The Keeper was a CR92a Assassin-class corvette used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. The corvette attempted to dock and pick up the stolen prototype Missile Boat Sealion from the Rebel XQ4 Platform Enin'sd but was destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele.
