# Verity

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Verity](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verity)

The Verity was a starship of an unidentified class used by the Jedi Order under Jedi Master Luke Skywalker. After returning to find Ben Solo outside the burning ruins of Skywalker's Jedi temple, the three Jedi Hennix, Tai, and Voe used it to pursue him and seek answers regarding the death and destruction.
