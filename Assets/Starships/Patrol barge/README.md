# Patrol barge

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Patrol barge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Patrol_barge)

Patrol barges were starships used by navies to patrol various areas of space. Some were in service of the Imperial Navy around the time of the Battle of Yavin.
