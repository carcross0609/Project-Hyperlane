# Sullustan Cargo Freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sullustan Cargo Freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sullustan_Cargo_Freighter)

The Sullustan Cargo Freighter, also known as the Sullustan Cargo Barge, was a model of freighter with Sullustan influence that was active during the war between the First Order and the Resistance. Many of these starships joined the Citizens' Fleet that was assembled by General Lando Calrissian to aid the Resistance against the forces of the Sith Eternal during the Battle of Exegol. It could hold cargo, had three engines, and was equipped with a hyperdrive.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sullustan Cargo Freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Engine:** Engines (3)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Role:** Cargo transportation
- **Affiliation:** Citizens' Fleet
