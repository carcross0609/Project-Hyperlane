# Stingray

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stingray](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stingray)

The Stingray was a ship manufactured by the Urukabb Slipways that was owned by the family of the Heliko female Haze Delik. Delik used the ship when she was a part of the Nihil pirates.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stingray
- **Manufacturer:** Urukabb Slipways
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:**
  - Delik family
  - Nihil
- **Owners:** Haze Delik
