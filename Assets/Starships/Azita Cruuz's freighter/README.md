# Azita Cruuz's freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Azita Cruuz's freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Azita_Cruuz%27s_freighter)

A freighter was owned by the smuggler Azita Cruuz. When Cruuz obtained the formula for coaxium ultra on the moon Ro Mira, she and the Jedi Mace Windu traveled to her freighter, which was parked at the Radial Spaceport. However, Diya and Muro, Dusk Weavers of the Had'le Path, were waiting at the starship and confronted the two. In the ensuing fight, Windu let the freighter fall on top of the Path's leader known as The Shroud, but the Devaronian managed to lift the ship back up with the Force. Cruuz then shot at her freighter from Windu's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor and it exploded, burying the Shroud underneath.
