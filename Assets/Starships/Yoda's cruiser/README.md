# Yoda's cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yoda's cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda%27s_cruiser)

A Consular-class cruiser belonged to Grand Master Yoda. This transport was used to take him, Jedi Master Yaddle, Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi and his Padawan Anakin Skywalker to the planet of Mawan in 26 BBY.
