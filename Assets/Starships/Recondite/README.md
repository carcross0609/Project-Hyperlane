# Recondite

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Recondite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Recondite)

Recondite was the personal Imperial-class Star Destroyer of Grand Admiral Nial Declann. It was presumably with him at the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Recondite
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/LN starfighters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Owners:** Grand Admiral Nial Declann
