# Whitehawk

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Whitehawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whitehawk)

The Whitehawk was a Nebulon-B Frigate in service with the Rebel Alliance. It arrived to assist Keyan Farlander defending a Leafar Convoy from the Star Destroyer Badi Dea . The operation was a success and the Imperials were thwarted.
