# Realm of Death

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Realm of Death](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Realm_of_Death)

Realm of Death was a Yuuzhan Vong warship that was part of the armada set to be dispatched to the Calamari system in 29 ABY to battle the forces of the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances. Before leaving with the fleet, Realm of Death participated in the Yuuzhan Vong sendoff for the armada in orbit of the planet Yuuzhan'tar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Realm of Death
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Yuuzhan Vong
- **Navy:** Yuuzhan Vong armada
