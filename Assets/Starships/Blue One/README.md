# Blue One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blue One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_One)

Blue One was a T-65 X-wing starfighter that was used by Rebel Alliance pilot General Antoc Merrick. Merrick notably used Blue One during the Battle of Scarif, where he was killed when his fighter was shot down.
