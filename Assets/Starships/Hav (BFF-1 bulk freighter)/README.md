# Hav

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hav (BFF-1 bulk freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hav_%28BFF-1_bulk_freighter%29)

Hav was a group of three BFF-1 bulk freighters that were part of the Imperial Task Force Vengeance during the Galactic Civil War. Two of the freighters were carrying Power units, while the third one was carrying warheads. The one with the warheads was destroyed by Rogue Squadron during their recon mission of the Imperial fleet.
