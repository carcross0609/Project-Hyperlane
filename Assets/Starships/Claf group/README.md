# Claf group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Claf group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Claf_group)

Claf group consisted of three Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttles, in the service of the Mugaari pirates. They were involved in the Second Battle of Javin where Outpost D-34 was recaptured by Imperial forces from the Fogger. They attempted to attack the Fogger but all three escort shuttles were destroyed.
