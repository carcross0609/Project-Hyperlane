# G Cat

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G Cat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G_Cat)

The G Cat was a modified Arakyd Helix-class light interceptor owned by Cryle Cavv. With the help of R2-RC, he claimed it was a simple transport, although it had a military background. He armed the ship with four plasburst laser cannons and two proton torpedo launchers to defend against unwanted company.
