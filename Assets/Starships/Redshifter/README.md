# Redshifter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Redshifter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Redshifter)

The Redshifter was a XS stock light freighter owned by the smuggler Nico Okarr during the years before the Great Galactic War. He claimed it was the "fastest ship in the sector".

## Specifications

- **Name:** Redshifter
- **Type:** XS stock light freighter
- **Owners:** Nico Okarr
