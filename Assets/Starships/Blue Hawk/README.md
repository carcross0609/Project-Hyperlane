# Blue Hawk

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blue Hawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Hawk)

Blue Hawk was an Imperial Lancer-class frigate that, along with its sister ship Cascade, participated in the attack on a Rebel Hospital treating casualties shortly after the Battle of Hoth. They assisted the Imperial Star Destroyer Corrupter in the assault.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blue Hawk
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Lancer-class frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Length:** 250 meters
- **Width:** 57 meters
- **Height:** 60 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
