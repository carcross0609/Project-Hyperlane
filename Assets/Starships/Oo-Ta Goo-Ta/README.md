# Oo-Ta Goo-Ta

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oo-Ta Goo-Ta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oo-Ta_Goo-Ta)

Oo-Ta Goo-Ta was a modified YT-1300 light freighter owned and operated by the Rodian Chordak. Known as one of the fastest ships in the Minos Cluster, Chordak hoped to catch the attention of the Galactic Empire with his ship and receive more valuable Imperial assignments like the regular run to the prison to Gesaril.
