# Unidentified excursion liner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified excursion liner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_excursion_liner)

An excursion liner was owned by the Chiss Ascendancy's Boadil family, one of the Ascendancy's Nine Ruling Families, and captained by a member of the Ruling Ufsa family. The liner had a compact cylindrical shape, with two wide D-shaped wings on either side, housing expensive passenger suites. On one of the liner's voyages prior to 19 BBY, the captain took the vessel too close to a gas giant, and the liner was caught in its gravity well with 8,000 passengers aboard.
