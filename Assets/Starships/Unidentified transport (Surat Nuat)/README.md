# Unidentified transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified transport (Surat Nuat)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_transport_%28Surat_Nuat%29)

A transport owned by the crime lord Surat Nuat and bearing his guild sigil was carrying weapons on the Trabzon Road on the planet Akiva in 4 ABY when it was attacked by members of the Uugteen species, who scorched its outsides but could not get within. Knowing it was Nuat's vessel, his rival junk dealer Temmin Wexley used a cutting torch to gain access to the interior of the transport and scavenge the contents. A trio of Nuat's thugs later confronted Wexley about scavenging the vessel, but he claimed that the damage to the vehicle had meant that he could not tell who it belonged to. As Wexley no longer had the weapons, the thugs demanded his entire junk shop as recompense.
