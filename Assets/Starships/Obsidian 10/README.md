# Obsidian 10

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Obsidian 10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obsidian_10)

Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems, Obsidian 10 was a TIE/ln starfighter serving the Imperial Navy's Obsidian Squadron, which was piloted by OS-72-10. By 4 ABY, Obsidian 10 was stationed aboard the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Thunderflare and was further recalled to defend the Imperial Death Star II battle station during its construction in the Endor system.
