# Formidable

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Formidable/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Formidable/Legends)

The Formidable was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was involved in the Battle of Turkana. It ended up severely damaged alongside the Ajax after Rebel starfighters unloaded their proton torpedoes onto their bridge shields.
