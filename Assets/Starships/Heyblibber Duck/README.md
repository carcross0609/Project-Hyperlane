# Heyblibber Duck

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heyblibber Duck](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heyblibber_Duck)

The Heyblibber Duck was an H-type Nubian yacht flown by Ric Olié on a mission to transport Junior Representative Jar Jar Binks to Urubai to meet with Ambassador Upsina. It was later used by Jedi Master Kelleran Beq during the Great Jedi Purge to transport the youngling Grogu off Coruscant.
