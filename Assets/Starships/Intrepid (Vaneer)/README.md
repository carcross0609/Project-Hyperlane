# Intrepid

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Intrepid (Vaneer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_%28Vaneer%29)

The Intrepid was a starship of the Galactic Empire's Imperial Navy. In 3 ABY, Admiral Vaneer was aboard the Intrepid when it was due to dock with Fuel Station Visavi in orbit of the moon Toshara in the Outer Rim Territories' Toshaal system. Orders regarding the ship were left on one of the Imperial dockyard's terminals and stated that the Intrepid was to be serviced and refueled expeditiously without exposing the station's corrupt secondary operations involving the criminal Pyke Syndicate.
