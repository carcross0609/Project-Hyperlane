# Scarlet Vertha

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scarlet Vertha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scarlet_Vertha)

The Scarlet Vertha was a custom-made Ghtroc Industries 720 light freighter owned by the Morseerian Nabrun Leids. Leids used the starship in his work as a smuggler and a pilot for hire and was active during the reign of the Galactic Empire. The ship was mentioned in an artist's journal, which was restored, expanded, and displayed at the Graf Archive at some point no earlier than 34 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scarlet Vertha
- **Manufacturer:** Ghtroc Industries
- **Model:** 720 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Owners:** Nabrun Leids
