# Amarills-class freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Amarills-class freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amarills-class_freighter)

The Amarills-class freighter was a type of freighter. The ships included multiple rooms, an airlock, and a kitchen. The enslaver known just as Captain owned one such vessel prior to the Battle of Yavin. The freedom fighter-turned escapee Jyn Erso was sent their to alter documents after being captured by Allehander Pso.
