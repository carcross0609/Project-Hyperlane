# Trade Federation Freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trade Federation Freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trade_Federation_Freighter)

The Trade Federation Freighter was a small freighter used by the Trade Federation. It and its successor, the Trade Federation Superfreighter were both common around the time of the Invasion of Naboo.
