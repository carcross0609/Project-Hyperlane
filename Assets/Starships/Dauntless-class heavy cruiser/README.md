# Dauntless-class heavy cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dauntless-class heavy cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dauntless-class_heavy_cruiser)

The Dauntless-class heavy cruiser was the second largest cruiser that saw service with the Rebel Alliance fleet during the course of the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dauntless-class heavy cruiser
- **Model:** Dauntless-class heavy cruiser
- **Type:** Heavy cruiser
- **Length:** >1,200 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Equipped with an excellent sensor-package
- **Countermeasures:** Equipped with an excellent countermeasures system
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolasers
  - Ion cannons
- **Complement:**
  - 4 starfighter squadrons
  - 2 troop regiments
- **Role:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - New Republic
