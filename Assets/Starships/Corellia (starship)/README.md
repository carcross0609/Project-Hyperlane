# Corellia

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corellia (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellia_%28starship%29)

The Corellia was a Valor-class cruiser in the Galactic Republic Navy during the Cold War. Around 3643 BBY, it was occupied by Jedi Grand Master Satele Shan. It was noted as having a golden appearance, rather than the typical red-and-gray or orange-and-gray color scheme of Republic warships. The Corellia was struck by a missile during Independent Operation Sebaddon and boarded by Fastbreeders. The droids overwhelmed and commandeered the ship, destroying it in order to spread themselves throughout the rest of the Republic fleet.
