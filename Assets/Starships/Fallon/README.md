# Fallon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fallon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fallon)

The Fallon was a WD-2550 Whydah-class yield conveyer operated by the Trade Federation during the High Republic Era, commanded by the Neimoidian captain Blex. Verosha Aniseya and Fillik worked as mekneks aboard the freighter for some time. When Aniseya was incriminated with her twin sister Mae-ho Aniseya's murder of Jedi Master Indara, the Jedi Knight Yord Fandar and his Padawan, Tasi Lowa, visited the freighter to apprehend her. Although Osha claimed she was not the murderer, a witness brought in by the Jedi identified her, and the Jedi sent her to Coruscant aboard a prison transport.
