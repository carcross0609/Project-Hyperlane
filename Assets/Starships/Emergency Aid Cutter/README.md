# Emergency Aid Cutter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Emergency Aid Cutter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emergency_Aid_Cutter)

The Emergency Aid Cutter was a class of starcutter employed by the Space Rescue Corps. The red-and-black vessels were considered to be sacrosanct by nearly all spacers, and were respected enough that any order to fire on them would usually cause the crew to mutiny. Emergency Aid Cutters were equipped with universal airlocks, plasma torch hull cutters, emergency medical bays, medical droids, and fire-foam backpacks.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Emergency Aid Cutter
- **Type:** Starcutter
- **Role:** Rescue starship
- **Affiliation:** Space Rescue Corps
