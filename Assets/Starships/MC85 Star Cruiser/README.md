# MC85 Star Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MC85 Star Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC85_Star_Cruiser)

The MC85 Star Cruiser, alternatively spelled MC-85 Star Cruiser, also known as the Resistance Cruiser or the Resistance Carrier, was a heavy Star Cruiser manufactured by the Mon Calamari Shipyards and the Corellian Engineering Corporation after the Galactic Civil War. It was used by the New Republic fleet and the Resistance navy.
