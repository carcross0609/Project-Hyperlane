# Jabba the Hutt's Shad'ruu war barge

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jabba the Hutt's Shad'ruu war barge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jabba_the_Hutt%27s_Shad%27ruu_war_barge)

The crime lord Jabba the Hutt utilized a personal Shad'ruu war barge during the Galactic Civil War. Jabba used the vessel to travel to Jekara to attend an auction that was to be hosted by Crimson Dawn. On his way there, Jabba participated in a meeting with the Grand Hutt Council onboard the war barge.
