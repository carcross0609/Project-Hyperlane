# Post group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Post group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Post_group)

Post group was a group of two Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttles that served the Rebel Alliance. They were in the Second Battle of Javin where they were destroyed by the Frigate Fogger.
