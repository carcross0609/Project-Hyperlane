# Dreadstar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dreadstar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadstar)

The Dreadstar was a battleship that Rae Sloane served aboard. At one point, Sloane refueled the ship at a floating depot on the ninth moon of Tilth's Sea of Carawak. While at the depot, an oncoming storm made the sea look monstrous. In 5 ABY, the anger of Sloane's superior, Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax, reminded her of the stormy Sea of Carawak she witnessed during her time on the Dreadstar.
