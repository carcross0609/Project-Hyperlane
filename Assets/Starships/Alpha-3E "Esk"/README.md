# Alpha-3E "Esk"

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Alpha-3E "Esk"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alpha-3E_%22Esk%22)

The Alpha-3E "Esk" was a sub-model of the Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter, which served the Republic Starfighter Corps during the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. This model was equipped with ion cannons as well as laser cannons. The Alpha-3E "Esk" was meant to operate as an exceptional pursuit craft. At least one Alpha-3E "Esk" starfighter saw combat, firing its ion cannons at a YV-865 Aurore-class freighter near a terrestrial body.
