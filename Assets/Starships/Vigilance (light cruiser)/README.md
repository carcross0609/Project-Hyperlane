# Vigilance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vigilance (light cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vigilance_%28light_cruiser%29)

The Vigilance was a light cruiser that served with the Cularin Militia during the Clone Wars. It was commanded by Captain Suubrin and in circa 19 BBY was sent on a mission to investigate an abandoned Separatist base. It arrived in the system where the base was and attacked some droid starfighters and some Z-95 Headhunters that were there. Troops were landed from it to the base, and Suubrin then flew it to investigate a mystery starfighter that had just arrived. This turned out to be flown by the Cerean Jedi Master Ki-Adi-Mundi, and he landed on the Vigilance.
