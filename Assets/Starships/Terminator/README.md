# Terminator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Terminator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terminator)

Terminator was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. The Terminator was dispatched to attack the Rebel Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Cathleen while it was recovering battle-weary starfighters returning from a successful attack on Imperial shipping in Corporate Sector. The Terminator launched TIE/sa bombers to destroy the vulnerable Rebel ships, but two T-65 X-wing starfighters and two RZ-1 A-wing interceptors from Red Squadron held off the Imperial fighters while the Cathleen recovered the other Rebel ships and escaped.
