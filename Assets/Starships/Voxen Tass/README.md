# Voxen Tass

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Voxen Tass](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voxen_Tass)

The Voxen Tass was a modified Brayl-class bulk freighter that was operational during the Galactic Civil War. Captained by Leona Makk, the Voxen Tass was a ship from Shang Lines. At one point it delivered an Alliance strike team to Argovia, where the team destroyed a sensornet established by the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Voxen Tass
- **Type:** Brayl-class bulk freighter
- **Length:** 100 meters
