# Star Breeze II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Star Breeze II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Breeze_II)

The Star Breeze II was a medium Transport used by the Rebel Alliance to transport a group of refugees from Cloud City to the Alliance Safe world Kaliska some time after the Battle of Hoth.
