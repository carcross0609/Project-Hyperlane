# Furlo group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Furlo group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Furlo_group)

Furlo group consisted of three BFF-1 bulk freighters that were supplying munitions to Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin. They were under escort from T-wings an the Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser Rahmat. The Dreadnaught and the T-wings were all destroyed and the freighters were disabled and captured, despite interference from Zaarin's forces ranging in a Nebulon-B2 frigate Strident, which was destroyed.
