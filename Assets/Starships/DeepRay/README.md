# DeepRay

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DeepRay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DeepRay)

The DeepRay was a Lambda-class shuttle stationed aboard the transport Dust Dancer in 44 ABY. In that year, it was piloted by Jedi Knight Seha Dorvald to return Galactic Alliance Chief of State Natasi Daala's Chief of Staff, Wynn Dorvan, Senator Fost Bramsin, and General Merratt Jaxton to Coruscant after the Tendrando Arms Celebrity Sabacc Charity Challenge. Unknown to the government figures, the DeepRay was also used to sneak Jedi Masters Kyp Durron and Octa Ramis into the Senate Building in order to prepare for Plan Delta, a Jedi plot to overthrow Natasi Daala.
