# Suveron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Suveron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Suveron)

The Suveron was a B-12 Bacta Transport. Sometime before 1 ABY, the ship exploded in the Endor system due to critical reactor failure. The destruction of the Suveron released a half million tons of bacta in space. Although it was not suitable for medicinal use anymore, the bacta survived the vacuum of space and formed a large pocket of space gas later known as the Linear Miasma. This unique phenomenon made the Linear Miasma popular among research communities.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Suveron
- **Model:** B-12 Bacta Transport
- **Destroyed:** Prior to 1 ABY, Endor system
