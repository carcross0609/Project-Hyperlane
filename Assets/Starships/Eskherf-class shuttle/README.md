# Eskherf-class shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eskherf-class shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eskherf-class_shuttle)

The Eskherf-class shuttle was a model of transport manufactured by Sublight Products Corporation. The shuttle could accommodate a small crew, and had a silver-colored hull with four wings pointing downward from the starship's body. A modified Eskherf-class named the Edgehawk was used by bounty hunter T'onga and her crew during their missions until she was forced to sell the ship in order to continue paying her crew.
