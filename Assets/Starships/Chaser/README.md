# Chaser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chaser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chaser)

The Chaser was a modified YT-1300 light freighter that was owned and operated by Baydo Chasdy. He utilized the starship to transport stranded Rebel Alliance agents out of Dac.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Chaser
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Modifier:** Baydo Chasdy
- **Role:** Troop transport
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Owners:** Baydo Chasdy
