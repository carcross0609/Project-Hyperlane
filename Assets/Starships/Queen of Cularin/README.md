# Queen of Cularin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Queen of Cularin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Queen_of_Cularin)

The Queen of Cularin was a Corellian-built luxury liner, that operated in the Cularin system, during the waning years of the Galactic Republic. It often traveled through the Cularin comet cloud, to allow passengers to view the floating space rubble that was located there. It was once attacked by members of the Fireclaw Horde.
