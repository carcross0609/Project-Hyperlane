# Dromii

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dromii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromii)

Dromii was a pair of Imperial Modular conveyors. The Modular Conveyors were part of convoy Rope 17, which was supposed to upgrade the Deep Space Manufacturing Facility ZA-13. Dromii carried equipment for the factory. Despite being attacked by Rebel raiders, the duo managed to deliver their cargo and escape into hyperspace.
