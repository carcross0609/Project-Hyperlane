# Sword of Ieldis

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sword of Ieldis (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Ieldis_%28starship%29)

The Sword of Ieldis was the flagship of the Sith Lord Odion during his campaign against his brother Daiman. The vessel shared its name a Sith artifact of the same name&mdash;the Sword of Ieldis&mdash;the Sith sword of the Sith Lord Ieldis, of the first Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sword of Ieldis
- **Armament:** Laser weaponry
- **Crew:** Givin crew
- **Affiliation:** Odionate
- **Owners:** Odion
- **Captains:** Jelcho
