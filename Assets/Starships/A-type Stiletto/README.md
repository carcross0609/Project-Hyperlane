# A-type Stiletto

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A-type Stiletto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-type_Stiletto)

The A-type Stiletto was designed to serve as a space superiority starfighter by Nubia Star Drives, Incorporated for an anonymous customer. The manufacturer later offered to sell copies of the highly personalized design to customers who could afford them.
