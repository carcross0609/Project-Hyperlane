# ZH-25 Questor-class

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ZH-25 Questor-class](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZH-25_Questor-class)

The ZH-25 Questor-class, also known as the ZH-25 freighter, was a class of freighter manufactured by Starfeld Industries. When Yrica Quell visited the Entropian Hive to recruit Nath Tensent into the New Republic, she noticed a modified ZH-25 freighter fly past. The ZH-40 Tribune-class light freighter, also produced by Starfeld Industries, was a later relative of the ZH-25.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ZH-25 Questor-class
- **Manufacturer:** Starfeld Industries
- **Type:** Freighter
