# Shuttle S257

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shuttle S257](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shuttle_S257)

Shuttle S257 was a Sentinel-class landing craft of the Galactic Empire's naval forces. Sometime in 4 BBY, the shuttle was stolen by the rebels of Phoenix Cell, where it was used by Kanan Jarrus and Rex in their mission to an experimental Interdictor-class heavy cruiser in order to rescue Jarrus' Padawan, Ezra Bridger, and Commander Jun Sato.
