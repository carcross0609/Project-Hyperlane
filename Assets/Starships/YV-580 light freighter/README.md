# YV-580 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YV-580 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YV-580_light_freighter)

The YV-580 was a model of light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. It consisted of a dish-shaped middle section which featured the starship's cockpit, and two side-mounted prongs armed with laser cannons on the inner and outer sides. A YV-580 named the Broken Wing was used by bounty hunter Beilert Valance, who modified the ship to suit his needs.
