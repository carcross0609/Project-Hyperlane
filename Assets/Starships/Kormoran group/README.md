# Kormoran group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kormoran group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kormoran_group)

The Kormoran group was a group of five CR92a Assassin-class corvettes used by Grand Admiral Zaarin. They were sent to destroy a convoy of two container transports, called Neelie. The targets were supposed to be carrying Missile Boats but instead carried munitions. The corvettes were lured into an ambush and destroyed.
