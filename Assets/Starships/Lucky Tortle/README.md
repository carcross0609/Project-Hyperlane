# Lucky Tortle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lucky Tortle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucky_Tortle)

The Lucky Tortle, formerly known as the Sarlacc's Scorn, was a refitted Viperi-class cargo shuttle utilized by Dahara the Hutt. The shuttle was equipped with a Class One hyperdrive, shield technology, and four laser cannons, including one that rotated. It also featured a scouting shuttle. Alongside her brother, Churo, and a number of enforcers from her crew, Dahara used the Sarlacc's Scorn on a mission to meet with the Nihil marauders.
