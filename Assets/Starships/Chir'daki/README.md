# Chir'daki

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chir'daki](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chir%27daki)

The Chir'daki, known in Basic as Deathseed, was a unique type of starfighter developed and used by the Twi'lek species.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Chir'daki
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Incom Corporation (X-wing parts)
  - Sienar Fleet Systems (TIE Fighter parts)
  - Twi'leks
- **Model:** Chir'daki
- **Type:** Ugly starfighter
- **Engine:** Twin ion engines
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Power:** 4L4 fusial thrust engines (powered the lasers, hyperdrive, and shields)
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Taim & Bak KX9 or IX4 laser cannons (4)
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** None
- **Othersystems:** S-foils
- **Affiliation:**
  - Twi'leks
  - Antilles's Rogues
