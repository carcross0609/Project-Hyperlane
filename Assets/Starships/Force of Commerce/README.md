# Force of Commerce

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Force of Commerce](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_of_Commerce)

The Force of Commerce was the Munificent-class star frigate used by General Grievous as his flagship during the Clone Wars at the time of the abduction of Jabba the Hutt's son, Rotta.
