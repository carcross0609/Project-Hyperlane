# Green Squadron 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Green Squadron 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Green_Squadron_1)

Green Squadron 1 was an RZ-1 A-wing interceptor in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The fighter was part of Green Squadron during the Battle of Endor, piloted by Green Leader Arvel Crynyd. The fighter was constructed at the zero-gravity Chardaan Shipyards and was deployed as an escort for B-wing and Y-wing attack missions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Green Squadron 1
- **Line:** RZ-1 A-wing interceptor
- **Destroyed:** 4 ABY, Endor system
- **Affiliation:**

- **Captains:** Arvel Crynyd
