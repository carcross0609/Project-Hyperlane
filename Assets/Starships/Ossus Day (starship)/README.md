# Ossus Day

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ossus Day (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossus_Day_%28starship%29)

Ossus Day was a MC80 Liberty type Mon Calamari Star Cruiser in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. Its name came from the term Ossus Day, a time of remembrance. The vessel was named in honor of those lost in the Rebellion.

## Specifications

- **Model:** MC80 Liberty type Star Cruiser

- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
