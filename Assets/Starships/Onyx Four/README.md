# Onyx Four

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Onyx Four](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Onyx_Four)

Onyx Four was an Imperial Navy TIE/sa bomber manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems that served in the Galactic Empire's Onyx Squadron during the Galactic Civil War. In 1 ABY, Onyx Four and other TIE/sa bombers from Onyx Squadron participated in an attack on the Alliance to Restore the Republic's secret base within the planet Talus, bombing the planet to allow other Imperial forces to carry out a ground assault on the facility.
