# Batil

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Batil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Batil)

The Batil was a gunship equivalent starship used by the Hutts during the Second Galactic Civil War. They were used in the Second Battle of Balmorra and the Battle of Kuat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Batil
- **Manufacturer:** Hutts
- **Type:** Gunship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Hutts
  - Confederation
