# Shekelesh-class freight gunship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shekelesh-class freight gunship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shekelesh-class_freight_gunship)

The Shekelesh-class freight gunship, also known as the Moogan smuggler ship or Moogan cargo ship, was a model of freighter and gunship manufactured by the Techno Union. Techno Union passengers included Skakoan commandos and combat engineers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shekelesh-class freight gunship
- **Manufacturer:** Techno Union
- **Model:** Shekelesh-class freight gunship
- **Type:**
  - Freighter
  - Gunship
- **Height:** 21.1 meters (69 ft 2 in)
- **Max Speed:** 300 kph (186 mph)
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 2 twin laser cannons
- **Role:** Smuggling vessel
- **Affiliation:**
  - Mooga smuggling ring
  - Cad Bane's group (appropriated)
