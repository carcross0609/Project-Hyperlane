# Z-95XT Trainer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-95XT Trainer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-95XT_Trainer)

The Z-95XT Trainer was a training model of the Z-95 Headhunter. It possessed a second seat for a passenger such as a flight trainer, but lacked the standard Z-95's concussion missile system. They were often used by the Rebel Alliance for ferrying personnel in-system or between ships within the same fleet, in addition to their stated use of personnel training.
