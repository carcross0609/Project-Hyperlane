# Mauler

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mauler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mauler)

Mauler was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was part of Kuat's planetary defenses. It was present at the Battle of Kuat. After its TIE squadrons had been overwhelmed to only a few remaining fighters by the Hawk-bats and Vibroaxe Prime, Mauler, to escape being defenseless to the snub fighters, bypassed the engagement zone by a significant distance, only to find itself within the range of Zsinj's Super Star Destroyer, Iron Fist. After a futile attempt of contending with the opposing ship, Mauler found herself spinning inflamed from half a dozen spots along her hull. Presumptuously the captain had believed that he could bring the damage under control.
