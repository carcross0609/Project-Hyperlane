# Umbaran support ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Umbaran support ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umbaran_support_ship)

The Umbaran support ship, also known as the Separatist support ship, was a support starship used by the Umbaran Militia during the Clone Wars. The ship was around half the size of a Munificent-class star frigate and much bigger than a Consular-class cruiser. It featured at least three large, opaque windows.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Umbaran support ship
- **Type:** Support ship
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Affiliation:**
  - Umbaran Militia
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
