# Blue Ruin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blue Ruin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Ruin)

Blue Ruin was a Nebulon-B2 frigate belonging to the Galactic Empire. It responded to a distress call from an Imperial troop convoy that was under attack by Rebel B-wing starfighters, RZ-1 A-wing interceptors, T-65 X-wing starfighters and BTL Y-wing starfighters in the Mylok system. The convoy was under escort from Alpha-class Xg-1 Star Wings belonging to Talon Squadron itself, but was outnumbered, so the frigate called for reinforcements from Avenger Squadron, in the form of TIE Avengers.
