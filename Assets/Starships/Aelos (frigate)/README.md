# Aelos

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aelos (frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aelos_%28frigate%29)

The Aelos was a blockade frigate utilized by the Nikardun Destiny. The frigate had been stationed by the Nikardun at the Paccosh homeworld Rapacc in the Rapacc system, but was captured by the Paccosh after the downfall of General Yiv of the Nikardun Destiny. The vessel was placed under the command of Uingali foar Marocsaa of the Marocsaa subclan, the symbol of which&mdash;the chimaera&mdash;was painted on the underside of the frigate.
