# Unidentified J-type 327 Nubian starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified J-type 327 Nubian starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_J-type_327_Nubian_starship)

A J-type 327 Nubian starship was a vessel whose downed remains were located on the Outer Rim Territories planet Genbara. Twenty years after the Sith rebellion, the J-type 327 Nubian royal starship could be seen embedded in the mountains near a rural settlement. When the former Sith known as "the Ronin" and his astromech droid R5-D56 passed through the region, they observed the derelict yacht from afar and later encountered a former Jedi known as "the Traveler" playing the flute near the ship as they departed the area.
