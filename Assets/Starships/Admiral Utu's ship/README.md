# Admiral Utu's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Admiral Utu's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Admiral_Utu%27s_ship)

Admiral Utu's ship was a Venator-class Star Destroyer under the command of Imperial Navy Admiral Utu during the Imperial Era. The Star Destroyer was part of Utu's battle group at Telos, and was deployed along with Utu to serve under Moff Wilhuff Tarkin and his task force during the Salient Campaign.

- **Name:** Admiral Utu's ship
- **Line:** Venator-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:**
  - Telos battle group
  - Tarkin's task force
