# EVA Pod

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EVA Pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EVA_Pod)

The EVA Pod was a type of vehicle present in the junkyard behind Watto's Shop in Mos Espa on Tatooine in 32 BBY. It had previously been a repair and maintenance vehicle pod of unknown origin.
