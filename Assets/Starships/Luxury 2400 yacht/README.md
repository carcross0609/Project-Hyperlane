# Luxury 2400 yacht

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Luxury 2400 yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luxury_2400_yacht)

The 2400 yacht was a model of luxury yacht that was manufactured and sold by SoroSuub Corporation as a luxury model. Resembling the later Personal Luxury Yacht 3000, it was fifty meters long, with a thin hull and two outrigger propulsion pods, one on each side. Love Commander was one such ship.
