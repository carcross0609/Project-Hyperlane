# CX-14

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CX-14](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CX-14)

CX-14 was a cargolift vessel in the Republic Navy during the Mandalorian Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Mandalorian warrior clans. When the Mandalorians threatened the planet Serroco in 3963 BBY, CX-14 was attached to Admiral Saul Karath's Battle Group Serroco, a task force intended to protect the world from Mandalorian attack. In the ensuing battle, Serroco was devastated with nuclear missiles, and CX-14 was lost. The vessel's destruction was later noted in an edition of The Admiral's List, an official publication issued by the Republic Navy, along with two other cargolift vessels: CX-03 and CX-09.
