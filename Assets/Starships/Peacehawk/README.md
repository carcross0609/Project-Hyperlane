# Peacehawk

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Peacehawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Peacehawk)

The Peacehawk was a starship that served in the Imperial Navy of the Galactic Empire. After saving the life of Captain Strayit of the Marshall Awe, Lieutenant Zertik Strom was promoted to First Officer of the Peacehawk, serving under General Ulric Tagge. Whilst on the Peacehawk, Strom served Tagge brilliantly and he was eventually promoted to commanding his own patrol fleet guarding the Wheel space station.
