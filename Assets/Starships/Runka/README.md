# Runka

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Runka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Runka)

Dimok forces used Assassin-class corvette Runka during the Sepan Civil War. Imperial starfighters encountered Runka near Sepan 8 during the close of the Sepan campaign. Imperial Ace Maarek Stele discovered Imperial arms onboard, passing this information onto the Prophets of the Dark Side.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Runka
- **Type:** CR92a Assassin-class corvette
- **Affiliation:** Dimok government
