# Escape pod

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Escape pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Escape_pod)

Escape pods, also known as life pods, were small escape crafts intended for use in emergencies. They were standard aboard all deep-space starships and detach from their host vessels to flee from danger. Some luxury models were fitted with hyperdrives.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Escape pod
- **Manufacturer:** Various manufacturers
- **Type:**
  - Starship
  - Transport
- **Height:** Typically around 10 meters however it varies by design
- **Mglt:** 30 MGLT
- **Max Speed:** Varies
