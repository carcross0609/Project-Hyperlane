# Olendahu

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Olendahu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Olendahu)

The Olendahu was a YT-1300 light freighter that was used by the Cularin-based company Renna's Transport Service during the waning years of the Galactic Republic.
