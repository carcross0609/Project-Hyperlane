# Retaliator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Retaliator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Retaliator)

The Retaliator was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer which was present at the Battle of Endor. By 4 ABY, the Retaliator was assigned to the planet Venaari. It remained in Imperial service until at least 13 ABY, when it participated in the Battle of Adumar after the planet chose to join the New Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Retaliator
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial II-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/LN starfighters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Imperial Remnant
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
