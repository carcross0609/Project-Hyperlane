# Noa Briqualon's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Noa Briqualon's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noa_Briqualon%27s_starship)

Noa Briqualon's starship was a vessel piloted by Noa Briqualon and Salak Weet on a mission to chart the Modell sector. After crashing on the Forest Moon of Endor, the ship languished for years before being repaired and leaving Endor, with Noa and fellow castaway Cindel Towani aboard.
