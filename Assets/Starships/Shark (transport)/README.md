# Shark

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shark (transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shark_%28transport%29)

Shark was an Imperial Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport stationed aboard Nebulon-B2 frigate Ludwick during the Galactic Civil War. Transport Shark captured Shuttle Electra and its Rebel Advisor occupants during the final engagement of the Battle on the Frontier.
