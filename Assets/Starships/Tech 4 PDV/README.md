# Tech 4 PDV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tech 4 PDV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tech_4_PDV)

The Tech 4 PDV, also known as Plexus Droid Vessels (PDV), were used by Imperial Intelligence courier droids which operated as communications and relay ships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tech 4 PDV
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Intelligence
- **Type:** Unmanned messenger pod
- **Cost:** Not available for sale
- **Length:** 9 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 0.5
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Crew:**
  - 1 I2-CG (storage/receiving)
  - 1 I2AM (captain/astrogator)
  - Analysis/encoding computer (analysis/encoding/translation)
- **Role:** Courier vessel
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Intelligence
