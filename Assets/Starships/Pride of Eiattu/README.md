# Pride of Eiattu

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pride of Eiattu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pride_of_Eiattu)

The Pride of Eiattu was a Nebulon-B escort frigate in service to the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a task force commanded by Admiral Ackbar during the Battle of Ciutric.
