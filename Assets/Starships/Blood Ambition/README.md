# Blood Ambition

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blood Ambition](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Ambition)

The Blood Ambition was a Gladiator I that served the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. It was captained by Ralchio Nervi and was the flagship of a task force that fought in the Battle of Xorrn.
