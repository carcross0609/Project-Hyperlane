# Filcher

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Filcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Filcher)

The Filcher was a pirate Cargo ferry that, along with its sister ship Riirdo, was ferrying stolen Imperial munitions to the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. The Galactic Empire sent Stormtrooper transports and Assault Gunboats to disable the cargo ferry and arrest its crew for aiding and abetting the rebels. The ship was destroyed afterwards. The event was recorded into the Combat chamber to train future pilots such as Maarek Stele.
