# StarViper-class attack platform

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [StarViper-class attack platform](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/StarViper-class_attack_platform)

The StarViper-class attack platform was a model of heavy attack starfighter manufactured by a subdivision of MandalMotors called Mandal Hypernautics. The fighter was lightly armored, but compensated for this with heavy weaponry and fast speed. Its high price meant that it was primarily used by large crime syndicates like Black Sun and the Zann Consortium.
