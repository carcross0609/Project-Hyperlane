# Widowmaker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Widowmaker (frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Widowmaker_%28frigate%29)

Widowmaker was a white wedge shaped frigate of the Imperial Remnant Navy, captained by Arien Yage. Lieutenants Stalgis and Tarl were members of its stormtrooper contingent.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Widowmaker
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Remnant
- **Captains:** Arien Yage
