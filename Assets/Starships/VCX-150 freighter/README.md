# VCX-150 freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [VCX-150 freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VCX-150_freighter)

The VCX-150 freighter was a Corellian Engineering Corporation freighter model that had over half a dozen rooms. One of the models, registered as the Keepsake, was used by the Sullustan resistance rebel cell to smuggle supplies to the planet Sullust's capital city of Pinyumb. It was discovered by the Imperial Security Bureau and subsequently searched by stormtroopers from the Imperial Ninety-Seventh Stormtrooper Legion. The troopers found a Chadra-Fan rebel hiding aboard, who ultimately set off a detonator to avoid being taken into custody.
