# XS-800 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [XS-800 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XS-800_light_freighter)

The XS-800 light freighter was a model of cargo starship manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. It could be entered from the ground either via a ventral air lock situated behind the cockpit and in front of the living quarters, or via two loading elevators to the cargo areas situated on either side of the freighter. The Harvester was one such light freighter.
