# Gray Secant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gray Secant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Secant)

The Gray Secant was an ancient Gree starship that was launched by the Gree thousands of years before the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire with the purpose of studying and exploring the farthest reaches of the galaxy. However, it reappeared over the planet Ilum in 3639 BBY, drawing the attention of both the Gree and the two galactic superpowers as they worked to uncover the vessel's secrets.
