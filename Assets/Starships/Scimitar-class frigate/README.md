# Scimitar-class frigate

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scimitar-class frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scimitar-class_frigate)

The Scimitar-class frigate was a type of frigate, considered as obsolete by the time of the Second Galactic Civil War. Several were utilized by Admiral Daala's Maw Irregular Fleet during the Second Battle of Fondor, and later during the Second Battle of Roche in 41 ABY.
