# Remoh

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Remoh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Remoh)

Around 1 ABY, the Star Destroyer Remoh was sent to assist the CR90 corvette group Geedon with a complement of TIE Avengers at its disposal. However, it arrived too late to save the starship group.
