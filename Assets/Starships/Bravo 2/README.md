# Bravo 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bravo 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravo_2)

Bravo 2 was an N-1 starfighter assigned to Bravo Squadron of Naboo's Royal Security Forces. The fighter was assigned to Officer Porro Dolphe. In 32 BBY, Bravo 2 was damaged by the Trade Federation during their invasion of the planet. Dolphe was able to repair Bravo 2 and participate in the Battle of Naboo.
