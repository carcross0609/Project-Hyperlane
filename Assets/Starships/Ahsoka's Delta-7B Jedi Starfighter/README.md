# Ahsoka's Delta-7B Jedi Starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ahsoka's Delta-7B Jedi Starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahsoka%27s_Delta-7B_Jedi_Starfighter)

Ahsoka's Delta-7B Jedi Starfighter was a Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor piloted by Jedi Padawan Ahsoka Tano during the Clone Wars, periodically flying with R7-A7 as her astromech droid co-pilot. She piloted the ship during the Battle of Ryloth and the Battle of Umbara.
