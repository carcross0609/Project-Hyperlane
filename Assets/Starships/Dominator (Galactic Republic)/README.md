# Dominator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dominator (Galactic Republic)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dominator_%28Galactic_Republic%29)

The Dominator was a warship that was deployed by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. In 21 BBY, the Dominator was assigned to patrol near to the planet Kalarba, in order to dissuade the Confederacy of Independent Systems from attacking the world.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dominator
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Role:** Warship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
