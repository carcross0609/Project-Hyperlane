# Daimanate transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Daimanate transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daimanate_transport)

In 1032 BBY, the Sith Lord Daiman, the ruler of the Sith princedom known as the Daimanate, used a transport to transport the village leaders of the six mining towns of Chelloa following the discovery of the Jedi Knight Kerra Holt. The transport had at least one bright navigation light and was piloted by two Daimanate Sith troopers. The vessel was dark blue with a few red stripes. It also had four rear thruster engines.
