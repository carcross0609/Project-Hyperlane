# E-9 Explorer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-9 Explorer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-9_Explorer)

The E-9 Explorer was a transport used by the Rebel Alliance during its fight against the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. It was deployed by the Tatooine rebel cell during the Battle for Tatooine, operating under Colonel Jennica Pierce, to transport troops and vehicles to battle.
