# Imperial heavy freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial heavy freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_heavy_freighter)

The Imperial heavy freighter was a heavy freighter used by the Galactic Empire. During the Galactic Civil War, Twilight Company carried out a raid on an Imperial heavy freighter in the Redhurne system.

- **Type:** Heavy freighter
