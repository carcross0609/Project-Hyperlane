# Shadow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow (Nebulon-B2 frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_%28Nebulon-B2_frigate%29)

Shadow was a Nebulon-B2 frigate used by Zaarin coup forces and belonging belonging to Rogue Grand Admiral Zaarin. This frigate, along with Serpent, a Strike Cruiser, and Grappler, an Immobilizer 418 cruiser, attempted to trap Admiral Thrawn in his Victory I-class Star Destroyer, Sceltor, in order to steal the Mag Pulse Warhead. The attack was unsuccessful and the Shadow was destroyed.
