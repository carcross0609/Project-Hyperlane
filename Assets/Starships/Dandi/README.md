# Dandi

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dandi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dandi)

Dandi was a Xiytiar-class heavy transport delivering comm arrays to Bretie Facility as part of the TIE Experimental Project during the Galactic Civil War, shortly after the Battle of Hoth. It was accompanied by its sister ship, the Vrexlir.
