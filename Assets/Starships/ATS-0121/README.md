# ATS-0121

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ATS-0121](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ATS-0121)

ATS-0121 was a GR-75 medium transport belonging to the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. ATS-0121 was assigned to the Defiance Task Force and carried a cargo of heavy machinery, presumably brought aboard during the evacuation of Echo Base. ==Appearances== *Star Wars: X-Wing Alliance
