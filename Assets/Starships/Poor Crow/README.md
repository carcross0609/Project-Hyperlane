# Poor Crow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Poor Crow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poor_Crow)

The Poor Crow was a light freighter owned by the pilot Ekiya. It was a long, sharp-nosed ship that had extensible, scarred wings and stenciled writing on its side naming it "the poor crow." On the inside, the Crow was neat and well maintained, though it appeared assembled out of spare parts in certain places.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Poor Crow
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
