# Dark Vendetta

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dark Vendetta (freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Vendetta_%28freighter%29)

The Dark Vendetta was a HT-2200 medium freighter in operation around the time of the Galactic Civil War. Used by the Silent Blades, the ship fought in and survived the Battle of the Silent Blades, along with two Preybird-class starfighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dark Vendetta
- **Model:** HT-2200 medium freighter
- **Affiliation:** Silent Blades
