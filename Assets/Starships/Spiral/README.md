# Spiral

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spiral](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spiral)

The Spiral was a Corellian Engineering Corporation CR90 corvette that operated as a privateer vessel for the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War fought by the Alliance against the Galactic Empire. Operated by a veteran crew with communications expert Del Goren a former member by 0 BBY, the Spiral ambushed multiple Imperial convoys, and the corvette was notorious for arriving at its destination when it was least expected.
