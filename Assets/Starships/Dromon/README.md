# Dromon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dromon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromon)

The Dromon was a Ripoblus frigate that attempted to aid its forces at the Battle of Science Station Youst during the Sepan campaign. It was destroyed while attacking the Victory-class Star Destroyer Protector.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dromon
- **Model:** Nebulon-B2 frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Length:** 253.2 meters
- **Width:** 147.7 meters
- **Height:** 94.3 meters
- **Role:** Attack ship
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Ripoblus government
