# Taloron Prey Chaser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Taloron Prey Chaser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taloron_Prey_Chaser)

The Taloron Prey Chaser was a class of starship used by the Taloron Hunters. One such ship was the Scent of Fear, under the command of Crutag, who chased Rebels to the planet Trinta during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Taloron Prey Chaser
- **Model:** Prey Chaser
- **Length:** 40 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 3 Laser cannons
- **Crew:** 1
- **Passengers:** 25
- **Capacity:** 30 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 4 months
- **Affiliation:** Taloron Hunters
