# Stronghold

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stronghold](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stronghold)

The Stronghold was a Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier in service to the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At some point, the cruiser-carrier sustained damage to its hull and lay in a debris field with two TIE/ln space superiority starfighters. The 340-meter-long (1,116 feet) starship was equipped with sublight engines and four hangar bays.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stronghold
- **Line:** Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier
- **Type:** Cruiser-carrier
- **Length:** 340 meters (1,116 feet)
- **Engine:** Sublight engines
- **Bays:** Hangars (4)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
