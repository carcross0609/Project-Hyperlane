# Tala 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tala 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tala_1)

Manufactured by Alliance to Restore the Republic tactician Adar Tallon, Tala 1 was a Z-95t Headhunter produced during the Galactic Civil War fought by the Alliance against the Galactic Empire. As with all other Z-95t Headhunters, Tala 1 had a class 3 hyperdrive, which was added to allow the starfighter to allow it to operate independently of capital ship support. In addition to the class 3 hyperdrive, the Z-95t Headhunter model also had increased maneuverability compared to the Z-95 Headhunter, and Tala 1 was individually a heavily modified Headhunter.
