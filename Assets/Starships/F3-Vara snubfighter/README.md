# F3-Vara snubfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [F3-Vara snubfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F3-Vara_snubfighter)

The F3-Vara snubfighter, also called Cumulus-class snub fighter, was a type of snub-fighter that was used by pirates during the New Republic Era. A group of pirates, including Vane, attempted to shoot down the Mandalorian Din Djarin using these fighters at the asteroid fields at the edge of Nevarro's system. Djarin destroyed several of their fighters before escaping.
