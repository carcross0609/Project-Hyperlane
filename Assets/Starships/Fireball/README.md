# Fireball

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fireball](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fireball)

The Fireball was an old racer that Team Fireball tried to restore in their repair shop on the Colossus, a refueling station on the planet Castilon. It was given its name because of its tendency to burst into flames and/or explode.
