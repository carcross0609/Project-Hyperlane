# Sate Pestage's yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sate Pestage's yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sate_Pestage%27s_yacht)

This yacht belonged to Grand Vizier of the Galactic Empire Sate Pestage. Following the death of Emperor Palpatine at the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY, Pestage used it to flee Coruscant when Director of Imperial Intelligence Ysanne Isard accused him of treason. After rendezvousing with Moff Leonia Tavira for passage to Ciutric IV, Pestage gave the yacht to its captain.
