# Vulnerator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vulnerator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vulnerator)

The Vulnerator was a Victory-class Star Destroyer assigned to the Galactic Alliance Fifth Fleet. Jedi Knight Jaina Solo commented that it had been in service as long as her parents. The Vulnerator and its two escorting frigates participated in the Battle of Kashyyyk.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vulnerator
- **Type:** Victory-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 900 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Role:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Alliance
- **Navy:** Galactic Alliance Fifth Fleet
