# Inyusu-class Star Yacht

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Inyusu-class Star Yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inyusu-class_Star_Yacht)

The Inyusu-class Star Yacht was a model of yacht manufactured by the SoroSuub Corporation. Designed as a small personal transport, the Inyusu-class had a varied interior layout to accommodate the desires of the customer. The Sullustan smuggler and Rebel Alliance member Nien Nunb owned a heavily modified Inyusu-class vessel, the Mellcrawler, though it was lost aboard the Star Destroyer Shieldmaiden during Operation Yellow Moon. Nunb later acquired and modified another Inyusu-class Star Yacht, the Mellcrawler II.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Inyusu-class Star Yacht
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Yacht
