# Emperor's Wisdom

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Emperor's Wisdom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Wisdom)

Emperor's Wisdom was a Victory-class Star Destroyer of the fleet of the Ciutric Hegemony which participated in the Battle of Ciutric under the command of the self-proclaimed "Prince-Admiral" Delak Krennel. It was destroyed in the Battle of Ciutric by Garm bel Iblis.
