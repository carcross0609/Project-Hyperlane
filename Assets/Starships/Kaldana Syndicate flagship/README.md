# Kaldana Syndicate flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaldana Syndicate flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaldana_Syndicate_flagship)

A starship served as the flagship of the Kaldana Syndicate, a criminal organization that operated in the Corellia system. In 13 BBY, the Kaldana sold the flagship in order to enter an auction for a datacube that contained plans for a shield generator. After the auction was eventually won by the Kaldana, the Droid Gotra paid the auctioneer, an Imperial defector known as the Engineer, to destroy a Kaldana ship that arrived to collect the datacube. Following this, Qi'ra noted to Han that with the loss of their flagship and over a billion credits, only a few of the pirates would remain.
