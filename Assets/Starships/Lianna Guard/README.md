# Lianna Guard

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lianna Guard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lianna_Guard)

The Lianna Guard was a Guardian-class light cruiser commanded by Captain Lodrel. It was part of the Imperial squadron present at the Battle of Nar Shaddaa in 4 BBY. The ship attempted to engage Salla Zend's Rimrunner, but was quickly destroyed by the freighter's rear-firing concussion missile launchers.
