# Daimanate starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Daimanate starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daimanate_starfighter)

Daimanate starfighters were small single-seat starfighters deployed by Sith Lord Daiman during the Republic Dark Age. They had twin ion engines, were fast and maneuverable, and shaped like a tri-pronged dart. In 1032 BBY, these fighters were deployed in an unsuccessful attack on Sith Lord Odion's space station "The Spike." During the Raid on The Spike, the Jedi Knight Kerra Holt hijacked one of these starfighters and infiltrated the station after ejecting in her space suit.
