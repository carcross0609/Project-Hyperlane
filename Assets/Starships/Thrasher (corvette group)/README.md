# Thrasher (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thrasher (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thrasher_%28corvette_group%29)

Thrasher was an Imperial group of two CR90 corvettes that attacked a Habassan freighter convoy above Retep V. They were destroyed by Keyan Farlander.
