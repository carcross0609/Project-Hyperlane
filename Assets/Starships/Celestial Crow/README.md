# Celestial Crow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Celestial Crow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Crow)

The Celestial Crow was a light freighter owned by Feylara Raed during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. One of Skavak's lovers, Raed lured Skavak's rival to her ship by pretending that it was crippled, and attempted to kill the smuggler once they docked.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Celestial Crow
- **Type:** Light Freighter
- **Owners:** Feylara Raed
