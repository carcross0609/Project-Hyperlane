# CL-5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CL-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CL-5)

CL-5 was a combat utility vehicle launched by the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Grey Wolf during the operation to capture Nami Crime Lord Ali Tarrak. Its role was to refill Imperial Starfighters with warheads during battle

## Specifications

- **Name:** CL-5
- **Manufacturer:** BTS Enterprises
- **Model:** Combat utility vehicle
- **Length:** 15 meters
- **Role:** Supply ship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
