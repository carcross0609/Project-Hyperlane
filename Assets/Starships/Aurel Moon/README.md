# Aurel Moon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aurel Moon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurel_Moon)

The Aurel Moon was a Marcadian luxury cruiser used by the Columi brothers, Craitheus and Marvid Qreph to travel through the Chiloon Rift and oversee their financial takeover of the nebula. When the Qreph brothers were negotiating with Lando Calrissian to buy his Sarnus Refinery, the Qrephs took the Aurel Moon to the negotiations on Sarnus. After the capture of Han Solo, the Qreph brothers used Aurel Moon to travel to their secret base.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aurel Moon
