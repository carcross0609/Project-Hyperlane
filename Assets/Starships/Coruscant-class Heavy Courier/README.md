# Coruscant-class Heavy Courier

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coruscant-class Heavy Courier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant-class_Heavy_Courier)

The Coruscant-class Heavy Courier was a model of starship manufactured by Corellia StarDrive. It was used by the Galactic Republic some four thousand years before the Battle of Yavin. The Nebulon Ranger was a Coruscant-class Heavy Courier used by the Jedi Ulic Qel-Droma, Cay Qel-Droma, and Tott Doneeta during their mission to Onderon.
