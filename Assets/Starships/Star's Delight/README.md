# Star's Delight

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Star's Delight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star%27s_Delight)

Star's Delight was a Gymsnor-3 light freighter owned by the Duros weapon trader Lai Nootka. In addition to Nootka, the crew comprised the Devaronian Saricia and a Sullustan pilot. In 6 ABY, the crew were captured by Prefect Mosh Barris for attempting to sell X-wing spare parts and proton torpedoes, keeping the Star's Delight grounded on Garqi. With some help by Corran Horn, Nootka and his crew eventually escaped from Garqi.
