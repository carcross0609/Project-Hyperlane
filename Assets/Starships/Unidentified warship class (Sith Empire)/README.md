# Unidentified warship class

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified warship class (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_warship_class_%28Sith_Empire%29)

A type of starship was used as a transport and warship by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War and subsequent Galactic War with the Galactic Republic. One such ship carried Imperial forces to the surface of Alderaan during the Alderaan Civil War, and a number of them accompanied Darth Marr on his expedition into Wild Space at the close of the war. Several ships of the same class were part of the Alliance's fleet.

## Specifications

- **Complement:** Speeder bikes
- **Passengers:** Several people
- **Role:** Transport
