# Fw'Sen-class picket ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fw'Sen-class picket ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fw%27Sen-class_picket_ship)

The Fw'sen-class picket ships were small gunships used by the Ssi-ruuvi Imperium. Although the vessel's main role was to disable enemy vessels and guard Ssi-ruuvi fleets, their delicate frameworks and inefficient shields resulted in them frequently being used in suicide missions.
