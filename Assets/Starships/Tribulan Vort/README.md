# Tribulan Vort

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tribulan Vort](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tribulan_Vort)

The Tribulan Vort was a Star Cruiser that served as the flagship of Captain Krull in the New Republic Defense Fleet two years after the Battle of Jakku. It deployed squadrons of new RZ-2 A-wing interceptors during the blockade of Substation Grimdock. According to the smuggler Lando Calrissian, it had a half-moon body and its cockpit stretched ahead of the body like a bigger and slicker version of the Volt Cobra piloted by Sana Starros.
