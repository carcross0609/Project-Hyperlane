# Zyle's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zyle's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zyle%27s_shuttle)

Zyle's shuttle was a Nihil escape shuttle utilized by the Nihil marauders that was held in a hangar aboard the vessel of the captain I'nah. In 229 BBY, the shuttle was stolen by the young pirate Zyle Keem, whose mother, Tanana Keem, had stopped aboard I'nah's vessel to barter goods. Keem flew the shuttle to the planet Valo, where they had overheard there was valuable bacta aboard the sunken vessel Innovator. However, as Keem attempted to land on Valo, the shuttle's navigation system deactivated and its wings began to fall apart, forcing them to make an emergency landing. The shuttle was severely damaged in the process and exploded shortly after Keem disembarked. After meeting the Jedi Padawan Ram Jomaram and the younglings Gavi, Kildo, and Tep Tep, who were fighting back against the Nihil occupation of the planet, the group decided to raid the Innovator together. The group retrieved the wreckage of Zyle's shuttle and salvaged components from it to modify Jomaram's ship, an Eviscerator.
