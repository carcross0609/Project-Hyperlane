# Prosperous

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prosperous/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prosperous/Legends)

The Prosperous was a Providence-class carrier/destroyer in the navy of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. In 19 BBY, the Prosperous took part in the Confederacy's surprise invasion of the Republic's capitol planet Coruscant. The Prosperous and the rest of the Separatist fleet arrived out of hyperspace over Coruscant and began to assault the Republic's defense forces. However, during the battle, the Prosperous was boarded by the hundreds of Republic clone marines led by Jedi General Saesee Tiin, who were escaping their own damaged Star Destroyer, the Impavid. General Tiin and his marines fought their way inside the Prosperous, defeated the carrier's battle droid crew, and captured the Separatist vessel.
