# Avenger (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Avenger (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avenger_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

Avenger was an Imperial EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate that served in the Imperial Starfleet during the Galactic Civil War. Because of her attack in the Lesser Plooriod Cluster, a Rebel ship that carried D3RM3 with important food supplies for the Alliance, let it off in order to flee. Avenger sent a squadron of TIEs to finish it and left.
