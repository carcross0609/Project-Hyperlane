# Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sidious%27_Eta-class_shuttle)

The Sith Lord Darth Sidious used a modified Eta-class shuttle as his personal shuttle in the later stages of the Clone Wars. He used the ship to travel to the planet Mandalore to thwart his former apprentice Maul's rise to power.
