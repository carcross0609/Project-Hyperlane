# Tela

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tela (transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tela_%28transport%29)

Imperial Tela was a Rebel Alliance Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport, it served aboard Nebulon-B frigate Xerxes at the Mylok IV Conflict during the Galactic Civil War. Tela fled Frigate Xerxes, carrying Rebel Officers, as Imperial starfighter pilots dealt the final death blows. Tela too met its demize at the hands of Imperial Ace Maarek Stele, just moments after the desctruction of Frigate Xerxes.
