# E3-standard starship lifeboat

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E3-standard starship lifeboat/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E3-standard_starship_lifeboat/Legends)

The E3-standard starship lifeboat was an escape pod designed by His Grace the Duke Gadal-Herm's Safety Inspectorate. Jedi Master Yoda used one such lifeboat to reach the world of Dagobah to begin his self-imposed exile after the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E3-standard starship lifeboat
- **Manufacturer:** His Grace the Duke Gadal-Herm's Safety Inspectorate
- **Model:** E3-standard starship lifeboat
- **Type:** Escape pod
- **Length:** 4 meters (legs retracted)
- **Width:** 3.5 meters (diameter)
- **Max Accel:** 300 G
- **Max Speed:** Over 40,000 km/h (during descent)
- **Hdsystem:** None
- **Passengers:** 3
- **Consumables:** 4 weeks
- **Role:** Escape pod
- **Affiliation:** Yoda
