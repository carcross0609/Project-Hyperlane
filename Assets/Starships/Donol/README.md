# Donol

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Donol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Donol)

Donol was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. Shortly before the destruction of the Scythe convoy, it departed the cruiser Defiance with commandos on board. It and its two X-wing escorts entered hyperspace, bound for parts unknown.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Donol
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Length:** 45 meters
- **Width:** 30.4 meters
- **Height:** 20.5 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
