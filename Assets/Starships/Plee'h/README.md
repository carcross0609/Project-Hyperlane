# Plee'h

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Plee'h](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plee%27h)

Plee'h was a group of BFF-1 bulk freighters. During the supply shortage following the sortie from Yavin 4, they were about to receive grain from seemingly defected Ghorin, near the edge of his territory.
