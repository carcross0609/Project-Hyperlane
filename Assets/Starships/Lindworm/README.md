# Lindworm

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lindworm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lindworm)

The Lindworm was a dreadnaught that was used by the Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War. At some point during the conflict, the Lindworm and two Sith Harrower-class dreadnoughts assisted with a Sith invasion of the planet Ord Radama that was led by the Sith Lord Darth Malgus. However, several weeks into the campaign, the Galactic Republic sent a force of Hammerhead-class cruisers and Thranta-class corvettes to the system, to drive the Sith off of the world. Moments after their arrival in the system, the Republic fleet opened fire on the Lindworm with their turbolasers, before the dreadnaught had a chance to launch any starfighters.
