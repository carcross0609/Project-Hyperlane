# Dagger-D police fighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dagger-D police fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dagger-D_police_fighter)

The Dagger-D police fighter was a short-range starfighter used by the Duro Defense Force to patrol the Duro system. The fighter was sleek and blade-shaped, and possessed a large Novaldex BlasterDrive engine nacelle. Not designed to take heavy damage, the Dagger-D was inexpensive and used primarily for police duty.
