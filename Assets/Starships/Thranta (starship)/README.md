# Thranta

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thranta (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thranta_%28starship%29)

The Thranta was a starship of the Survivor Fleet, a fleet of Alderaanians who survived the destruction of the planet Alderaan by the Death Star and got rescued by rebels Princess Leia Organa and Lieutenant Evaan Verlaine. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, a group of raiders serving Imperial Commander Ellian Zahra, who had a vendetta against Organa for playing a role in the death of her mentor Governor Wilhuff Tarkin during the Battle of Yavin at the hands of Jedi Commander Luke Skywalker attacked the Survivor Fleet, destroying several of its vessels and capturing the Thranta.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thranta
- **Escapepods:** At least 2
- **Passengers:** 100
- **Destroyed:** Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Fleet:** Survivor Fleet
