# Ranger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ranger (IPV-1 System Patrol Craft)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_%28IPV-1_System_Patrol_Craft%29)

Ranger was the group designation of two IPV-1 Patrol Craft sent by the then Vice Admiral Thrawn to guard NL-1, a space station constructed by the Galactic Empire in the Pakuuni system nearby the planet Argoon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ranger
- **Model:** IPV-1 System Patrol Craft
- **Type:** Inter-system patrol/customs craft
- **Role:**
  - Inter-system patrol craft
  - Customs vessel
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
