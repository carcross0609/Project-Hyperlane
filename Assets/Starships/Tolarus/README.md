# Tolarus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tolarus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tolarus)

Tolarus was a Muurian transport owned by Viraxo Industries. It was destroyed near Destreg II in 3 ABY by Ace Azzameen and Emon Azzameen after they tried to assassinate K'Armyn Viraxo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tolarus
- **Manufacturer:** Muurian Interstellar
- **Model:** Muurian transport
- **Length:** 30.5 meters
- **Width:** 29.4 meters
- **Height:** 8.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 2 double laser cannon turrets
  - 3 laser cannons
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Viraxo Industries
