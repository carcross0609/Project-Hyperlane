# Gladiator II-class Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gladiator II-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gladiator_II-class_Star_Destroyer)

The Gladiator II-class Star Destroyer was the second sub-class of the Gladiator-class line of Star Destroyers used by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. It was preceded by the Gladiator I-class Star Destroyer.
