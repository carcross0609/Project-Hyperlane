# Darth Nox's Fury-class Imperial interceptor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Nox's Fury-class Imperial interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nox%27s_Fury-class_Imperial_interceptor)

The Sith Lord later known as Darth Nox was given a Fury-class Imperial interceptor by the Sith's master, Darth Zash, during the search for the artifacts of Tulak Hord. Kallig, as the Sith was first known, continued to use the ship even after leaving Zash's apprenticeship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Darth Nox's Fury-class Imperial interceptor
- **Type:** Fury-class Imperial interceptor
- **Length:** 100 meters
- **Width:** 88 meters
- **Height:** 33 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 4 precision laser cannons
  - Missiles
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
- **Owners:** Darth Nox
