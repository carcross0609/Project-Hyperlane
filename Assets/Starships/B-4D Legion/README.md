# B-4D Legion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B-4D Legion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-4D_Legion)

The B-4D Legion was a model of bomber produced by the shipyards at the planet Sernpidal for the reconstituted Sith Empire. Primarily a drone bomber, the Legion utilized missile sentry drones, laser cannons, and proton torpedoes to attack enemies, and was used by Imperial forces during the Galactic War with the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B-4D Legion
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Sernpidal shipyards
- **Type:** Bomber
- **Armament:**
  - Laser cannons
  - Proton torpedoes, concussion missiles, or seeker mines
  - Missile sentry drone, interdiction sentry drone, or railgun sentry drone
- **Complement:** Repair drone
- **Availability:** Military
- **Role:** Drone bomber
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
