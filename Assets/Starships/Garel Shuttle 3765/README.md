# Garel Shuttle 3765

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Garel Shuttle 3765](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Garel_Shuttle_3765)

Garel Shuttle 3765 was a GX1 short hauler on the planet Garel. In 4 BBY, the shuttle landed in Bay 22 of the Garel City Spaceport. Spectres Sabine Wren, Ezra Bridger and Chopper heard an announcer mention to spaceport passengers that the shuttle had arrived from Coruscant as they entered a docking bay in search of the courier EG-86.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Garel Shuttle 3765
- **Model:** GX1 short hauler
- **Length:** 37.28 meters
- **Armament:** Turret-mounted double laser cannons (diplomatic version only)
- **Escapepods:** 1, Hyperdrive-equipped escape pod
