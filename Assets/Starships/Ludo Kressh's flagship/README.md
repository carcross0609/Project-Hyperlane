# Ludo Kressh's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ludo Kressh's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ludo_Kressh%27s_flagship)

This battleship was under the control of Sith Lord Ludo Kressh in the years leading up to the Great Hyperspace War. It was one of the few ships in Kressh's control which survived the Battle of Khar Delba, but was later destroyed when Kressh decided to fake his death.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ludo Kressh's flagship
- **Manufacturer:** Sith Empire
- **Model:** Derriphan-class battleship
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Destroyed:** 5000 BBY over Khar Delba
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
- **Owners:** Ludo Kressh
- **Captains:** Ludo Kressh
