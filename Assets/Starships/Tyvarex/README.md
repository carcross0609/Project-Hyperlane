# Tyvarex

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tyvarex](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tyvarex)

The Tyvarex was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate that served in the navy of the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. Following the scattering of the fleet after the Battle of Hoth, the Tyvarex was a part of the Fourth Division, which grouped at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three in the Mid Rim. However, the Galactic Empire used cracked rebel codes to track communications between the Fourth Division and another cell, and launched an attack on the Fourth Division. Blockaded against a star by the forces of Commander Ellian Zahra, the Tyvarex was unable to escape, and was destroyed.
