# One Two Many

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [One Two Many](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One_Two_Many)

The One Two Many was a bulk cruiser owned and operated by a crew of Corellians during the Imperial era. Among its crew were Janzel Helot, Shandy Fanaso, and Devoris.
