# Capital ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Capital ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capital_ship)

A capital ship was any large military starship greater than 100 meters in length, and designed for deep-space warfare. Boasting numerous heavy weapons and shields, capital ships had crews that numbered in the hundreds, if not the thousands. In their vast hangar bays, they often carried shuttles, starfighters, and other spacecraft. Types of capital ships included Star Destroyers, Star Cruisers, and battleships. Capital ships were commonly classified by the Anaxes War College System depending on their size, role, and firepower.
