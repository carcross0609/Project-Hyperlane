# Convergence

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Convergence (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Convergence_%28starship%29)

Convergence was a Convergence Tactical Assault Vehicle during the Sith civil war of the Republic Dark Age. It was built by mechanics and engineers of Daiman, whose constant indecision led to countless redesigns. In 1032 BBY, Narsk Ka'hane was sent by Odion to learn the secrets of the ship.
