# T-65C-A2 X-wing starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-65C-A2 X-wing starfighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-65C-A2_X-wing_starfighter/Legends)

The T-65C-A2 X-wing starfighter was a starfighter used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was a variant of the T-65 X-wing starfighter. Both this model and the T-65B were the primary X-wings of the Rebellion. However, the C-A2 model was rare due to the T-65's difficult construction. New X-wings were always a rare sight during the Galactic Civil War. It was seen in use before the Battle of Yavin.
