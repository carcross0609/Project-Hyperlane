# J-type diplomatic barge

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-type diplomatic barge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-type_diplomatic_barge)

The J-type diplomatic barge, also called the Naboo Royal Cruiser or simply the Naboo cruiser, was a diplomatic transport used by representatives of the planet Naboo. Senator Padmé Amidala utilized a custom-built cruiser during the Separatist Crisis, which was damaged on Coruscant in an attempt on her life. She later used the J-type during the Clone Wars, when she was targeted by the bounty hunter Aurra Sing.
