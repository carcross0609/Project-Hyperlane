# Tafanda Bay

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tafanda Bay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tafanda_Bay)

Tafanda Bay was the name of a floating herdship city above the planet of Ithor. Momaw Nadon was high priest of this city during Imperial intervention. Later, during the Yuuzhan Vong War, Relal Tawron served as high priest.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tafanda Bay
- **Builder:** Ithorians
- **Celestialbody:** Ithor
- **Climate:** Warm
- **Interest:**
  - Upper and lower observation decks
  - Market places
  - Great Atrium
  - Botanical Domes
- **Affiliation:**
  - Ithorians
  -   Ithorian ground fleet
