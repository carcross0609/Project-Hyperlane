# Unidentified Trade Federation Superfreighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Trade Federation Superfreighter (Naboo)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Trade_Federation_Superfreighter_%28Naboo%29)

A Trade Federation Superfreighter carrying a cargo of munitions and six Scarab-class droid starfighters was part of a nine-ship Trade Federation convoy after the Invasion of Naboo commenced in 32 BBY. The freighter was at the far end of the convoy and docked with a Trade Federation Freighter when a band of resistance fighters arrived and decided to attack the convoy. Vana Sage disabled the freighter at the behest of Reti, who boarded the freighter to steal munitions. The Toydarian pilot then decided to reprogram the complement of Scarabs to assist the raiders. After finishing loading, Reti departed and the freighter became another casualty to the raiders.
