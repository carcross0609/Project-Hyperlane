# Shuttle 81572

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shuttle 81572](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shuttle_81572)

Shuttle 81572 was a captured Sheathipede-class Type B shuttle that was used by the Galactic Republic in 20 BBY to infiltrate the Separatist-controlled Citadel prison on Lola Sayu. Piloted by a squad of reprogrammed battle droids under the command of the astromech droid R2-D2, the ship, carrying a Republic strike team frozen in carbonite to evade lifeform scanners, gained access onto the planet by claiming that it carried supplies and frozen rations. Landing in a hidden canyon, R2-D2 and his droid crew waited by the ship as the strike team was unfrozen and headed off for the prison to rescue Jedi Master Even Piell and his officers.
