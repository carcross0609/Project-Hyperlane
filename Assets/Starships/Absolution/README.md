# Absolution

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Absolution](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Absolution)

The Absolution was a Resurgent-class Star Destroyer in the Navy of the First Order that Captain Cardinal interrogated a Resistance spy, Vi Moradi, for information about Captain Phasma's past aboard. The Absolution was also where the younger half of the First Order stormtrooper training took place, under the advisory of Cardinal. Brendol Hux had a grand and lavish suite as his living quarters on the officer's deck. A decade after his death it was commandeered by his son, Armitage Hux, when he visited the ship. After capturing Lor San Tekka on Cato Neimoidia, Terex traveled to the Absolution, where he met with Phasma to have the cybernetic implants in his brain removed in exchange for Tekka's location.
