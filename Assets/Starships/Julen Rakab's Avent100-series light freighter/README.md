# Julen Rakab's Avent100-series light freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Julen Rakab's Avent100-series light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Julen_Rakab%27s_Avent100-series_light_freighter)

The farmer Julen Rakab owned a Avent100-series light freighter, which he bought from the spice dealer Trix Sternus while in the Outer Rim planet Batuu's spaceport. It had a hexagonal shape, and was painted with red markings. The smuggler Izal Garsea stole it to rescue Rakab from Ana Tolla's crew during the skirmish at Kat Saka's farm. It was damaged during the skirmish, but after repairs had been made, Garsea taught Rakab how to fly it.
