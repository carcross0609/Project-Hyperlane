# Firestorm (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firestorm (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firestorm_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Imperial Nebulon-B frigate Firestorm failed to destroy the Alliance Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Stimsenj'kat which was having cargo transferred from Transport Group Renhoek, which was made up of at least six Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports. The Firestorm was destroyed by the Stimsenj'kat, along with Keyan Farlander and a few other Rebel pilots.
