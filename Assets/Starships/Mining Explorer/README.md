# Mining Explorer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mining Explorer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mining_Explorer)

A Mining Explorer was a large utility craft used for research and exploration.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mining Explorer
- **Manufacturer:** Tagge Company
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Length:** ~500 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Countermeasures:** aft mine launcher (with heavy duty mines).
- **Armament:** Six double turbolaser turrets: one over bow section, two in middle section , three around aft bridge At least three additional weapons (type unindentified) in the underside.
- **Complement:** Enough room for over a dozen TIE/LN starfighter At least one shuttle
- **Bays:** One primary . At least another secondary for Stock Light Freighters
- **Role:**
  - Research and exploration
  - Flagship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Tagge Mining Company
  - House of Tagge
