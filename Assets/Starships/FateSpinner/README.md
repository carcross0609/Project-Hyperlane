# FateSpinner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FateSpinner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FateSpinner)

The FateSpinner was a massive and pristine Shatter-class WarMaster warship that served as the flagship of the Grysk operative "Jixtus"'s fleet and was commanded by a Grysk captain. Equipped with a nyix hull, missiles shielded by smaller projectiles known as dibbers, and spectrum laser, the starship was capable of destroying the large Illumine Kilhorde battle fleet of the Kilji Illumine in two hours.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FateSpinner

- **Armament:**

- **Destroyed:** 18 BBY, during Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand, Sunrise system
