# Exactor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Exactor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exactor)

The Exactor was a Star Destroyer of the Galactic Empire. Supposedly, the Imperial officer Alger Denholm served aboard the vessel. After Denholm's superior, Lieutenant Marsden, was killed by Darth Vader, Denholm was promoted, but began to go mad, seeing visions of Marsden's death.
