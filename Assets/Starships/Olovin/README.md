# Olovin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Olovin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Olovin)

Olovin was a Galactic Alliance Immobilizer 418 cruiser, produced by Sienar Fleet Systems, that accompanied General Wedge Antilles on a feint in the Duro system during the Yuuzhan Vong War. Olovin helped trap the Yuuzhan Vong forces near the planet Duro with its gravity well projectors, which allowed the Galactic Alliance to take the planet Fondor with little resistance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Olovin
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Type:** Immobilizer 418 cruiser
- **Othersystems:** Gravity well generators
- **Role:** Interdictor
