# Firewolf man-of-war

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Firewolf man-of-war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firewolf_man-of-war)

The Firewolf man-of-war was a type of man-of-war warship used by the Chiss Ascendancy that was named for the firewolf, a creature. Firewolf man-of-wars were equipped with engines and a hyperdrive. Their size was comparable to that of a Shatter-class WarMaster warship of the Grysk warlike species. In 18 BBY, Chiss Defense Force Admiral Ers'ikaro commanded the Bellicose, a Firewolf man-of-war.
