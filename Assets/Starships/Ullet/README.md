# Ullet

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ullet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ullet)

Ullet was a CR90 corvette of the Rebel Alliance. It fought at the Battle of Endor, and shortly afterward at the Invasion of Bakura as part of the Bakura task force.
