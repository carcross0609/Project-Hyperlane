# Charybdis

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Charybdis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charybdis)

The Charybdis was a Marauder-class corvette in service to Viraxo Industries. The ship was escorting a convoy to Industrial complex VXO-33274 when it came under attack by Ace Azzameen, who was raiding supplies for a Rebel outpost in the Vergesso Asteroids. Despite having a fighter escort, the corvette was destroyed by Ace Azzameen.
