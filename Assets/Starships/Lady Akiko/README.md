# Lady Akiko

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lady Akiko](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_Akiko)

The Lady Akiko was the personal yacht of Marshal Levnis, a planetary baron. For a long time, the Lady Akiko lacked a Kalld'n-class stardrive, forcing Levnis to turn to The Most Honourable Guild of Armourers to acquire the exceedingly rare device.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lady Akiko
- **Owners:** Levnis
