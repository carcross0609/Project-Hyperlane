# Unidentified Devaronian exploratory vessel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Devaronian exploratory vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Devaronian_exploratory_vessel)

Early in their history, the Devaronian species dispatched an exploratory vessel to the planet Byblos that used a tumble hyperdrive, though the ship's remains were lost by the time of the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire.
