# Nighthunter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nighthunter (gunboat)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nighthunter_%28gunboat%29)

Nighthunters, also known as Nikardun gunboats or Nikardun missile boats, were a class of gunboat utilized by the Nikardun Destiny. At least two participated in a battle over Primea.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nighthunter
- **Type:**
  - Gunboat
  - Missile boat
- **Affiliation:** Nikardun Destiny
