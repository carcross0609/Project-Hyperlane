# Unidentified core ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified core ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_core_ship)

A converted core ship served as the command center for the Confederacy of Independent Systems's operations on the occupied planet of Utapau. During the Battle of Utapau, Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi confronted Confederate General Grievous in the core ship. Shortly into their lightsaber duel, the 212th Attack Battalion suddenly attacked the battle droids stationed in the ship, convincing Grievous to flee on his TSMEU-6 personal wheel bike.
