# Personal Luxury Yacht 3000

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Personal Luxury Yacht 3000](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Personal_Luxury_Yacht_3000)

The Personal Luxury Yacht 3000, also known as the SoroSuub 3000, the Luxury 3000 space yacht, or the SoroSuub Personal Luxury Yacht 3000, was, as its name implied, a luxurious transport ship manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. Before the Clone Wars, they were typically associated with diplomats for meetings and travel. They quickly fell out of favor as the war began as they were seen as targets for pirates and Separatists. During the Clone Wars, they were concealed with weapons to better protect themselves against Separatist attacks. Pirate captain Hondo Ohnaka piloted a Luxury 3000 space yacht dubbed Fortune and Glory. While undercover as Rako Hardeen, Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi purchased a Personal Luxury Yacht 3000 from a Bith starship dealer on Nal Hutta. The Varluk Organization used a Personal Luxury Yacht 3000 to steal Lando Calrissian's personal puffer pig from his farm on Lothal. Azmorigan escaped the Nixus Hub 218 station on the planet Nixus using a Personal Luxury Yacht 3000 after a skirmish with Ohnaka and Ezra Bridger. Calrissian also owned a Luxury 3000 space yacht called Lady Luck.
