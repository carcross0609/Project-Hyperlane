# F-T2 Quell

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [F-T2 Quell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F-T2_Quell)

The F-T2 Quell was a starfighter in the reconstituted Sith Empire which performed strikefighter roles. Created in the shipyards of Dromund Kalakar, the Quell was designed in accordance to tradition of balance between offense, defense and speed. Carrying two twin laser cannons freed up systems for both a fast engine and the ability to equip proton torpedoes or a range of different missiles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** F-T2 Quell
- **Manufacturer:** Dromund Kalakar shipyards
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 2 twin laser cannons
  - equipable missiles or torpedoes
- **Role:** Strikefighter
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
