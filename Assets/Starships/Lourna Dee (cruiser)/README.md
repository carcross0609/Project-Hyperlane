# Lourna Dee

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lourna Dee (cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lourna_Dee_%28cruiser%29)

The Lourna Dee was a cruiser commanded by Nihil Tempest Runner Lourna Dee, who named the vessel after herself. The cruiser was outfitted with numerous systems, including heat baffling, ablative plating, and double-sealed engines that recycled most of its exhaust signature into the life-support and weapons systems which allowed the cruiser to remain virtually invisible from scanners.
