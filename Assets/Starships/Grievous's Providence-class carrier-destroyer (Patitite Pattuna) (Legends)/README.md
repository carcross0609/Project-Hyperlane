# Grievous's Providence-class carrier/destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grievous's Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Patitite Pattuna)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grievous%27s_Providence-class_carrier/destroyer_%28Patitite_Pattuna%29/Legends)

Around the second year of the Clone Wars, the Separatist General Grievous' flagship, a Providence-class carrier/destroyer, attacked Jedi Master Adi Gallia's flagship in the vicinity of the planet Patitite Pattuna in conjunction with two Munificent-class star frigates. Sometime after the event, the General ordered the crew of the destroyer to fire upon the Weequay pirate starship Marauder as target practice, which caused the pirate vessel to be destroyed. Following the attack on the Marauder, a task force, led by Jedi Master Plo Koon, engaged the Confederate vessel. Koon and a group of clone troopers boarded the craft and destroyed multiple battle droids within it.
