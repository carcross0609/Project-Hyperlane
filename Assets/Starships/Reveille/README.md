# Reveille

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reveille](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reveille)

Reveille was the personal Lambda-class T-4a shuttle of General Tycho Celchu during the Second Galactic Civil War. Selecting Syal Antilles as his personal pilot, he used it to travel to Endor in an attempt to negotiate with the New Jedi Order. While there, he was taken captive and his shuttle, piloted by Han Solo and Luke Skywalker, was used to infiltrate the Anakin Solo.
