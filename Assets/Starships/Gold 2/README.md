# Gold 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gold 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_2)

Gold 2 was the designation of a BTL-A4 Y-wing in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It was flown by Tiree. The fighter was equipped with custom high-power lateral thrusters that gave the craft improved maneuverability which allowed Tiree to successfully draw and evade enemy weapons fire.
