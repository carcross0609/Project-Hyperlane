# G-79

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-79](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-79)

The G-79 was the designation of the drone barge dispatched by Darth Vader to transport Wrenga Jixton to Corulag, on his mission to assassinate Governor Torlock.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G-79
- **Type:** Drone barge
- **Crew:** None
