# Oculus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oculus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oculus)

Oculus was a Carrack-class light cruiser used by the New Republic during the Rebellion on Akiva. Shortly after the Battle of Endor, the Oculus sought refuge in remnants of the comet Kinro.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Oculus
- **Type:** Carrack-class light cruiser
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
- **Navy:** New Republic Defense Fleet
