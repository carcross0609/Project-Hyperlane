# Toone

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Toone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Toone)

Toone was a pair of Utility Tugs used by the Rebel Alliance. They were present trying to deliver pilots to Rebel starfighters as their base fell under attack by an Imperial Nebulon-B frigate Lightning. The tugs were both destroyed, along with the rest of the Rebels.
