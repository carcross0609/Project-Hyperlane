# Nordieus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nordieus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nordieus)

The Nordieus was a civilian transport ship. The ship was used by the Galactic Empire to transport Atour Riten and several other contractors to the DS-1 Orbital Battle Station while it was under construction at Despayre.
