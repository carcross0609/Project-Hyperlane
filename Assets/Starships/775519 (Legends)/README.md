# 775519

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [775519/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/775519/Legends)

775519 was a T-6 shuttle used by Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas prior to the outbreak of the Clone Wars. It was shot down over the desert moon of Oba Diah, killing Sifo-Dyas, and its remains were later discovered by the troopers of Wolfpack c. 20 BBY.

- **Name:** 775519

- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Role:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** 32 BBY, desert moon of Oba Diah
- **Owners:** Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas
