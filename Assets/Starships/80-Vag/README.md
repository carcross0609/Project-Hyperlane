# 80-Vag

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [80-Vag](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/80-Vag)

80-Vag was the starship of Mugaari pirate Icanis Tsur. The ship, manned by a crew of thirty in addition to the captain, was destroyed in the Hoth asteroid field sometime after the Galactic Empire's rise to power. Its wreckage floated among the asteroids for years, often plundered by Squibs and other scavengers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 80-Vag
- **Crew:** 31
- **Destroyed:** Between 19 BBY and 0 BBY (Hoth asteroid field)
- **Affiliation:** Mugaari
- **Navy:** Mugaari pirates
- **Captains:** Icanis Tsur
