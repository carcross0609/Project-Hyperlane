# Space ARC battle cruiser

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Space ARC battle cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Space_ARC_battle_cruiser)

The Space ARC battle cruiser were huge star cruisers which were generally associated with the Galderian space pirates, although a few found their way into the hands of the Rebel Alliance also.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Space ARC battle cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Advanced Race
- **Model:** Space ARC battle cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Length:** 1,000 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1.0
- **Armament:**
  - Dual Turbolaser Batteries (42)
  - Ion cannon batteries (16)
  - Tractor beam projectors (16)
- **Complement:**
  - I-BEAM Star Fighters (at least 24 in 6 fighter A Racks)
  - Other shuttles (at least 3)
- **Crew:**
  - 500 Officers
  - 3,050 Crew
  - 1,000 Slaves
- **Capacity:** 27,000 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 4 years
- **Role:**
  - Carrier
  - Cruiser
  - Pirate vessel
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galderian Pirates
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
