# YZ 3000 tanker

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YZ 3000 tanker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YZ_3000_tanker)

The YZ 3000 tanker was a model of transport vessel. The YZ 3000 was colloquially known as a "Whale" due to its massive size and shape.
