# Selonian Fire

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Selonian Fire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Selonian_Fire)

Selonian Fire was a New Republic Victory I-class Star Destroyer that participated in the Battle of Ciutric, which occurred in 9 ABY, under the command of General Garm Bel Iblis.
