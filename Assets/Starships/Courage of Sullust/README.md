# Courage of Sullust

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Courage of Sullust](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Courage_of_Sullust)

The Courage of Sullust was a modified GR-75 medium transport which was used to carry ten X-wings of the Rogue Squadron during a diplomatic mission to Ryloth. In that occasion, the Courage of Sullust was accompanied by the Pulsar Skate, carrying Wedge Antilles and his X-wing fighter. Later on Coruscant, it was loaded with addictive spice to be used in a top-secret Alliance Intelligence project.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Courage of Sullust
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** Modified GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Complement:** X-wing starfighters (10)
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
