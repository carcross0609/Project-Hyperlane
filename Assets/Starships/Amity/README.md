# Amity

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Amity](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amity)

The Amity was a Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I that served in the New Republic Defense Fleet during the Galactic Civil War in 5 ABY. The Amity took part in the Battle of Jakku where it was subsequently destroyed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Amity
- **Manufacturer:** Nadiri Dockyards
- **Line:** Starhawk-class battleship

- **Type:** Battleship
- **Destroyed:** 5 ABY, Jakku system

- **Navy:** New Republic Defense Fleet
