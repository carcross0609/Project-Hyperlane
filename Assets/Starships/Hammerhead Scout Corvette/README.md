# Hammerhead Scout Corvette

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hammerhead Scout Corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hammerhead_Scout_Corvette)

The Hammerhead Scout Corvette was a model of Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette used by the fleet of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It was equipped with at least two laser cannons and three engines, and served in the capacity of a scout ship. During the Galactic Civil War, one Hammerhead Scout Corvette confronted an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hammerhead Scout Corvette
- **Line:** Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette
- **Model:** Hammerhead Scout Corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Engine:** 3
- **Armament:** At least 2 laser cannons
- **Role:** Scout ship
- **Affiliation:**
