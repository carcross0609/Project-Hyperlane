# Bulton Cruises

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bulton Cruises](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bulton_Cruises)

The Bulton Cruises was a Ferryboat liner that was present at Camen Spaceport when the Hurrim Lambda-class T-4a shuttle Merte, stolen by two members of Dunari's coalition, escaped the Hurrim's asteroid base under heavy pursuit of Preybird-class starfighters. Alliance High Command sent a strike force of two A-wings to cover the retreat until the shuttle reached hyperspace. Her cargo contained tourists.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bulton Cruises
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** Ferryboat liner
- **Length:** 363 meters
- **Role:** Passenger liner
