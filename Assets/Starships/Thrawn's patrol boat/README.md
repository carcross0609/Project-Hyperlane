# Thrawn's patrol boat

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thrawn's patrol boat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thrawn%27s_patrol_boat)

Thrawn's patrol boat was a Vak patrol boat that was commandeered by Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo of the Chiss Ascendancy in order to escape the Vak homeworld of Primea with the caregiver Mitth'ali'astov. Thrawn used modulated laserfire to contact Admiral Ar'alani's Vigilant, which had arrived to collect him. When four Lioaoin cruisers attacked the Vigilant on the orders of the Nikardun Destiny, Thrawn fired upon one of the cruisers in the Vak patrol boat, managing to cause significant damage during the initial confusion. After his starfighter was shot and damaged, Thrawn landed it aboard the Vigilant, which escaped from Primea.
