# Woan Barso's Unar-Con tug

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Woan Barso's Unar-Con tug](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Woan_Barso%27s_Unar-Con_tug)

During the reign of the Galactic Empire, Woan Barso owned a Unar-Con tug which he used to move cargo up to the freighters waiting in orbit above Jedha. By 1 BBY, the Unar-Con container tug was so dilapidated that Barso wore an orange vac-suit at all times because of his distrust of the ship's life support system. Nevertheless, in addition to cargo-hauling, he also smuggled refugees out of Jedha City to the freighters orbiting the moon with his ship.
