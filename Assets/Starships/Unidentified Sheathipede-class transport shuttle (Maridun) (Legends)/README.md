# Unidentified Sheathipede-class transport shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Sheathipede-class transport shuttle (Maridun)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Sheathipede-class_transport_shuttle_%28Maridun%29/Legends)

A Sheathipede-class transport shuttle was brought to Maridun by General Lok Durd of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, who sought to test his new Defoliator cannon on the world. The shuttle was later stolen by a small team led by Jedi Generals Anakin Skywalker and Aayla Secura.
