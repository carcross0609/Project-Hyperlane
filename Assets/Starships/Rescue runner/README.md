# Rescue runner

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rescue runner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rescue_runner)

Margravine Quenelle of Nallastia owned a Nallastian rescue runner. She brought it to a deep-space rendezvous point within the Tapani sector in the year 23 BBY to meet with Senator Rodd of Fondor.
