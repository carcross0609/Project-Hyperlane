# Palpatine family starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Palpatine family starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palpatine_family_starship)

This sleek crimson colored yacht served as the personal starship of House Palpatine. The family's crest was emblazoned upon the side of the vessel. It was later destroyed by the eldest son of the family upon murdering his father, mother, siblings, and their bodyguards, and the destruction was later covered up as an accident.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Palpatine family starship
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Role:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** 65 BBY
