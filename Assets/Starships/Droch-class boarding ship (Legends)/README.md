# Droch-class boarding ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droch-class boarding ship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droch-class_boarding_ship/Legends)

The Droch-class boarding ship, also known as the pod hunter and the Separatist boarding craft and nicknamed "juicers" by the Galactic Republic's clone troopers, was a model of boarding craft used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems and built by the Colicoid Creation Nest.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Droch-class boarding ship
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoid Creation Nest
- **Model:** Droch-class boarding ship
- **Type:** Boarding ship
- **Cost:** 43,000 credits
- **Length:** 18.51 meters
- **Max Speed:** 850 km/h
- **Armament:** Light laser cannons
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Passengers:** 6 (Droid troops)
- **Capacity:** 30 kg
- **Consumables:** 1 day
- **Availability:** Restricted
- **Role:** Boarding craft
- **Firstuse:** 22 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
