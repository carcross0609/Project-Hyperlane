# Empirical

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Empirical](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empirical)

The ISS Empirical was a mobile research laboratory, a modified cruiser that belonged to Darth Vader. Rumoured to be one of many such vessels used by the Dark Lord, the Empirical housed the cyborg's experiments on prisoners and studies conducted on unique alien species. Top-secret, moving only between lifeless star systems in the Outer Rim Territories and escaping even the eyes of Emperor Palpatine's spy network, the vessel was crewed by a platoon of soldiers of the Galactic Empire personally selected by Lord Vader, who monitored the test subjects and eliminated those that escaped confinement before they could get off the ship. One of the many such experiments was Lieutenant Rom Mohc's Dark Trooper Phase Zero project, which operated out of the Empirical until it was shut down for practical reasons.
