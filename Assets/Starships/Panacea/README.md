# Panacea

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Panacea](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panacea)

The Panacea was a Salvation-class Republic medical frigate and one of the earliest Great Works of Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh during the High Republic Era. Serving as a medical aid ship for the Galactic Republic, the Panacea was sent to various conflict zones and disaster sites in the years since its completion. Following the Great Hyperspace Disaster, the Panacea housed and treated survivors of the destruction of the Legacy Run. Several of the Jedi responsible for rescuing the survivors from the wreckage of the Legacy Run greeted them aboard the Panacea.
