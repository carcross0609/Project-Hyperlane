# Vengeance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vengeance (YV-260 light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vengeance_%28YV-260_light_freighter%29)

The Vengeance was a YV-260 light freighter owned by the Rodian Bomu Clan. It was commanded by Heedron Bomu and his brother Ormann who both worked for Zonnos the Hutt. It was armed with twin pairs of ion cannons, a pair of blaster cannons and a turret-mounted tractor beam.
