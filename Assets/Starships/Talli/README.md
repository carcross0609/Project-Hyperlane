# Talli

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Talli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talli)

Talli was a BFF-1 bulk freighter used by the Rebel Alliance. She and Rand were part of Operation Grain Snatch, in which their role was to swap the fresh grain belonging to the convoy with their poisoned grain. The operation was a success.
