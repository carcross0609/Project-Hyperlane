# Backbreaker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Backbreaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Backbreaker)

The Backbreaker was an Imperial Lancer-class frigate in service to Warlord Zsinj and part of his third group. It was present, alongside the rest of the group, at the Battle of Levian Two after being called in to reinforce Zsinj's first group in order to ambush General Solo's fleet.
