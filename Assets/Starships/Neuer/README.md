# Neuer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Neuer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neuer)

The Neuer was a Sentinel-class landing craft that was used by the Fel Empire around 139 ABY. During the Battle of the Floating World, it was used to evacuate wounded and dying Imperial Knights. Later, the shuttle was used to evacuate the wounded Empress Marasiah Fel, who had been attacked by the rogue Sith Darth Wredd.
