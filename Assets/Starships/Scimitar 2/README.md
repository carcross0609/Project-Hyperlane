# Scimitar 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scimitar 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scimitar_2)

Scimitar 2 was a TIE/sa bomber manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems that served in the Imperial Navy's Scimitar Squadron. At one point, the bomber was struck by an asteroid in the Anoat system and underwent repairs. By 4 ABY, Scimitar 2 was stationed on the Galactic Empire's second Death Star battle station.
