# Ullan Yite's yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ullan Yite's yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ullan_Yite%27s_yacht)

A yacht was owned by Ullan Yite, the leader of the Bright Circle criminal organization. Yite attempted to escape to his yacht after an illegal weapons deal he was involved in was infiltrated by the Jedi Rooper Nitani and Silandra Sho, but it was sabotaged by the droid P3-7A, who the yacht's central processor and later gave it to the Sullustan Piralli, allowing him to finish restoring his own yacht.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ullan Yite's yacht
- **Model:** Sullustan Mark VIII Legacy-class space yacht
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Affiliation:** Bright Circle
- **Owners:** Ullan Yite
