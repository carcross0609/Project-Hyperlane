# Badger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Badger (cargo ferry)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Badger_%28cargo_ferry%29)

Badger was the designation given to a pair of cargo ferries used by former Galactic Empire Admiral Harkov's fleet following Harkov's defection to the Rebellion. Both vessels, designated Badger 1 and Badger 2, were present at the Rebel outpost DS 5 in the Parmel system during an Imperial attack on the facility in 3 ABY. During the battle, the ships laid mines around the station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Badger

- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**

- **Affiliation:**
