# Maxillipede shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maxillipede shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maxillipede_shuttle)

The Maxillipede shuttle, also known as the Separatist shuttle or Neimoidian shuttle, was a model of shuttle manufactured by Haor Chall Engineering Corporation and used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Its design was similar to the Sheathipede-class transport shuttle, but featured two large dorsal fins instead of one.
