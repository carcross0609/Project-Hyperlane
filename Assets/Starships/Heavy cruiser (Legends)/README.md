# Heavy cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy cruiser/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_cruiser/Legends)

A heavy cruiser was a capital ship. Following the Anaxes War College System of starship classification, heavy cruisers were medium sized starships at a size of roughly 600 - 1000 meters. They were much larger than cruisers, corvettes or frigates but smaller than ships of the Star Destroyer or dreadnaught classification.
