# Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cad_Bane%27s_Munificent-class_star_frigate)

A Munificent-class star frigate was placed under the command of bounty hunter Cad Bane during the Clone Wars. Bane had been hired by Darth Sidious, the Dark Lord of the Sith, to retrieve a crystal containing the names of every Force-sensitive child known to the Jedi Order from its keeper, Jedi Master Bolla Ropal. In order to facilitate his mission, Sidious, as the secret leader of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, ordered Separatist Council member Nute Gunray to give Bane the ship and an army of battle droids.
