# Tri-Wing S-91x Pegasus Starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tri-Wing S-91x Pegasus Starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tri-Wing_S-91x_Pegasus_Starfighter)

The Tri-Wing S-91x Pegasus Starfighter, or simply the Tri-wing, was a type of starship that was manufactured by Incom Corporation and later classified as a New Republic Light Assault Starship. It was active during the Imperial Era, with one spotted by Princess Leia Organa on Alderaan in 9 BBY. The Tri-wing continued service during the New Republic Era.
