# Mother Vima

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mother Vima](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Vima)

Mother Vima was a colony ship that, along with Constant, served as one of two support starships of the Beneficent Tasia. The three vessels departed the planet Grizmallt on an expedition to the dangerous southern quadrant of the galaxy in approximately 3900 BBY. The starships were filled with Human refugees who hoped to find fortune and glory while escaping a civil war on Grizmallt. The expedition was led by the scout Kwilaan, had the sponsorship of Grizmallt's Queen Elsinoré den Tasia, and was blessed by a famous Jedi Master.
