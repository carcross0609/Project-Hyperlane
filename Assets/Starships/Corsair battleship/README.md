# Corsair battleship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corsair battleship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corsair_battleship)

Corsair battleships, sometimes known simply as corsairs, were ships that served in the navy of the Brotherhood of Darkness during the final stages of the New Sith Wars.
