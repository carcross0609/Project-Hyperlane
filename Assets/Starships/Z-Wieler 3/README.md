# Z-Wieler 3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Wieler 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Wieler_3)

Z-Wieler 3 was a CR90 corvette that fought on the side of Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's coup during the Second battle of Imperial Center in around 4 ABY. During the battle, the corvette targeted Emperor Palpatine's starship Excalibur but was ultimately destroyed by an Imperial rocket.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z-Wieler 3
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Destroyed:** c. 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
- **Navy:** Zaarin's fleet
