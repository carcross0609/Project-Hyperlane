# Omicron 1

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Omicron 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omicron_1)

Omicron 1 was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport that was carrying Imperial officers that were assigned to the Death Star. Alliance Special Forces sabotaged the transport, leaving it stranded in the Corellian system. The Alliance then sent a team consisting of Y-wings from Red Squadron, one of the pilots was Keyan Farlander, and X-wings from Blue Squadron with a Lambda-class shuttle Hunter to board and capture the transport and then bring it to the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Inad.
