# Inexorable

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Inexorable (Imperial I-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inexorable_%28Imperial_I-class%29)

The Inexorable was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer that served in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. This ship was best known for its role during Grand Admiral Thrawn's campaign against the New Republic in 9 ABY. As one of the main ships of Thrawn's fleet, the Inexorable fought at the battles of Bpfassh and Sluis Van.
