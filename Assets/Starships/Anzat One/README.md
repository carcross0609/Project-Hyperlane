# Anzat One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Anzat One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anzat_One)

Anzat One was a transport vessel, employed by the Anzati couple Sint and Nakia Yoru, both assassins working for the Houk Lun Rask, a Vigo of the criminal organization Black Sun. In 137 ABY The Yorus were confronted by Cade Skywalker and his mother, with Sint Yoru being killed by Skywalker. Cade's band took the ship for themselves, painting the Bloody Bones mark on its hull.
