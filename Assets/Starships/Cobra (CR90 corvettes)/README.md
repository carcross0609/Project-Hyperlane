# Cobra (CR90 corvettes)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cobra (CR90 corvettes)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cobra_%28CR90_corvettes%29)

Cobra was the name of a group of four Corellian CR90 corvettes that were used by the Rebel Alliance. They were part of an Attack on ZA-13 but, despite fighter escort and equipped with missile launchers they were all destroyed by TIE Defenders, one of which was piloted by Maarek Stele.
