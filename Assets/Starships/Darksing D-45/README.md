# Darksing D-45

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darksing D-45](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darksing_D-45)

The Darksing D-45 was a heavy freighter owned by the Nallera's mining crew in the Tatoo system during the Galactic Civil War. In 1 ABY, the ship was captured by the Zynt'aia pirates but it was eventually recaptured by the starfighter forces dispatched by Mining Chief Operator A'Lerris Verata.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Darksing D-45
- **Type:** Transport
- **Role:** Heavy freighter
- **Affiliation:** Nallera's mining crew
