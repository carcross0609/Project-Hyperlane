# Unidentified Ithorian herdship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Ithorian herdship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Ithorian_herdship)

Following the Caamas Firestorm, an Ithorian herdship crashed under mysterious circumstances on the planet Caamas in the Core Worlds. The shattered remains of the starship were left on Caamas's smallest landmass in the southern hemisphere of the planet, which allowed the alien jungle contained within to take root there and become the first example of vegetation on the surface since the devastation of Caamas. Only a few Ithorians knew about the existence of the herdship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Ithorian herdship
- **Line:** Ithorian Herdship
- **Destroyed:** After the Caamas Firestorm
- **Affiliation:** Ithorians
