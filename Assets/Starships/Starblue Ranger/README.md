# Starblue Ranger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starblue Ranger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starblue_Ranger)

The Starblue Ranger was a ILH-KK Citadel-class civilian cruiser owned by the Wookiee hunter known as Captain Shorwarr. The Wookiee, who made a living hunting predatory nexu on the planet Cholganna, used the starship during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. The ship, from a line of armored transports manufactured by Koensayr Manufacturing, was customized to included cages and storage compartments in the hull.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Starblue Ranger
- **Manufacturer:** Koensayr Manufacturing
- **Model:** ILH-KK Citadel-class civilian cruiser
- **Type:** Armed transport
- **Othersystems:** Cages, additional storage
- **Role:** Transport
- **Owners:** Shorwarr
- **Captains:** Shorwarr
