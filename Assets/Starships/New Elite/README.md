# New Elite

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [New Elite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Elite)

The New Elite was a modified corvette commanded by Kassav Milliko, a Tempest Runner in the Nihil marauder organization. The vessel served as the flagship of Milliko's Tempest. After Milliko and his Tempest attempted to use one of the Emergences of the Great Hyperspace Disaster to extort Governor Mural Veen of Eriadu, the Galactic Republic became aware of the vessel and the Nihil's involvement in the disaster. At the Battle of Kur, the New Elite was heavily damaged and was subsequently boarded by an Eriaduan force led by Governor Veen, who killed Milliko.
