# Skorr's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skorr's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skorr%27s_starship)

Skorr's starship was a starship used by the bounty hunter Skorr while pursuing the Millennium Falcon from Ord Mantell. The vessel was seized by an Imperial Star Destroyer and Skorr was sent to the Spice Mines of Kessel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Skorr's starship
- **Armament:** Dual laser cannon (1)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire (Captured)
- **Owners:** Skorr
