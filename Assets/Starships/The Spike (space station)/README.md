# The Spike

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [The Spike (space station)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Spike_%28space_station%29)

The Spike was a modular space station that existed during the time of the New Sith Wars. It was used by the Sith Lord Odion as a mobile manufacturing center. In 1032 BBY, it was raided by Daimanate forces and the Jedi Knight Kerra Holt while above Chelloa.
