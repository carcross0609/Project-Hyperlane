# Space Slug

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Space Slug](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Space_Slug)

The Space Slug was a Tallaan Clipper transport in use during the early days of the Galactic Empire. Both the Kedorzhan Senator Dewell Bronk and the Human Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi booked passage on the ship while trying to escape the planet Coruscant in 19 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Space Slug
- **Role:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** 19 BBY
