# MT/191 drop-ship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MT/191 drop-ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MT/191_drop-ship)

The MT/191 drop-ship was a shuttle, troop transport, and dropship manufactured by Meller & Dax.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MT/191 drop-ship
- **Manufacturer:** Meller & Dax
- **Type:** Special-purpose shuttle and troop transport
- **Length:** 52.5 meters
- **Max Speed:**
  - 1,200 kmh (during drop)
  - 650 kmh (during return flight)
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 1 light laser cannon
- **Crew:** 1
- **Passengers:** 160 (troops)
- **Capacity:** 200 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 day
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
