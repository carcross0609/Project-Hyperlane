# Justice Crusader

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Justice Crusader](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Justice_Crusader)

The Justice Crusader was the personal attack cruiser of Raskta Lsu. The ship carried Raskta, Sarro Xaj, Worror Dowmat, Valenthyne Farfalla and Johun Othone to Tython when they pursued the Sith Lords Darth Zannah and Darth Bane. The Jedi engaged in a duel with the Sith, and after their deaths, the ship was left abandoned on Tython.
