# Pride of Selonia

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pride of Selonia (Lancer-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pride_of_Selonia_%28Lancer-class%29)

Pride of Selonia was a modified Lancer-class frigate under the command of Captain Todra Mayn that served in the Galactic Alliance Defense Force during the Yuuzhan Vong War. The warship accompanied the Millennium Falcon on its mission to reestablish contact with several outlying sectors of Galactic Alliance space that had been cut off in the wake of the Yuuzhan Vong attacks on HoloNet relay stations.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pride of Selonia
- **Model:** Modified Lancer-class frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Armament:** AG-2G quad laser cannon (20)
- **Complement:**
  - Clawcrafts
  - X-wing starfighters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
  - Galactic Alliance Defense Force
- **Captains:** Captain Todra Mayn
