# Wayfarer-class medium freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wayfarer-class medium freighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wayfarer-class_medium_freighter/Legends)

The Wayfarer-class medium freighter, also known as the Wayfarer-class medium transport, was a medium transport constructed by Kuat Systems Engineering, a now-folded subsidiary of Kuat Drive Yards.
