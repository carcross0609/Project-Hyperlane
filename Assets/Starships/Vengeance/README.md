# Vengeance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vengeance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vengeance)

The Vengeance was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer used by the navy of the Galactic Empire. After capturing the bounty hunter Beilert Valance, who had attempted to hunt down Darth Vader, he was taken to be imprisoned on the Vengeance, where the Empire tortured him for information.
