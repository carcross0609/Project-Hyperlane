# Silver Arrow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Silver Arrow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silver_Arrow)

The Silver Arrow was, according to General Leia Organa, one of the Rebel Alliance's most reliable supply-run ships during the Imperial Era. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, something went wrong with the ship's droids, causing a member of the ship's crew to contact out for immediate aid. The distress signal was received by Organa a week later, who became concerned about what had happend on the ship and sent Captain Magna Tolvan and her crew to find the ship's crew and return them to the Rebel Base.

- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
