# E-wing starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-wing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-wing_starfighter)

The E-wing starfighter, also known as the E-wing escort fighter or simply the E-wing, was an advanced combat starfighter utilized by the New Republic Defense Fleet during the years of the New Republic Era.

- **Name:** E-wing starfighter
- **Manufacturer:** FreiTek Incorporated
- **Model:** E-wing
- **Type:** Starfighter

- **Mglt:** 120 MGLT
- **Max Speed:** 1,300 km/h
- **Engine:** Ion engines (2) (port and starboard wing-mounted)
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped

- **Crew:**

- **Affiliation:**
