# DGS-286

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DGS-286](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DGS-286)

DGS-286 was an HMP droid gunship that was classified as a heavy repulsorlift airspeeder, reaching 12.3 meters in length, 11 meters in width, and 3.1 meters in height. The droid gunship boasted a single heavy laser cannon and two dual medium laser cannons, and was also equipped with concussion bombs, missiles, and a deflector shield generator. A pair of two binocular rangefinders and rangefinder preprocessors aided DGS-286's long-range spatial and missile guidance systems. But on the forward hull, it received sensor capabilities from four long-range sensor ports and two red-colored photoreceptors. DGS-286 was also equipped with ion-drive engines and repulsorlift generators.
