# Duggenhei

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Duggenhei](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duggenhei)

The Duggenhei was a transport vessel. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, Arihnda Pryce arranged for her parents, Elainye and Talmoor Pryce, passage to Lothal via Paeragosto City on the Duggenhei from Batonn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Duggenhei
- **Type:** Transport
- **Passengers:**
  - Elainye Pryce
  - Talmoor Pryce
