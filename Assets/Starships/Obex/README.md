# Obex

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Obex](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obex)

The Obex was a Nu-class attack/transport shuttle used during the Clone Wars. It was destroyed by BX-series droid commandos during the Defense of Rishi Station, after it had ferried Clone Captain Rex and Clone Commander Cody to the Rishi Station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Obex
- **Model:** Nu-class attack/transport shuttle
- **Engine:** 6
- **Armament:** At least 4 laser cannons
- **Crew:** 2
- **Role:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** 22 BBY, Rishi Moon
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
