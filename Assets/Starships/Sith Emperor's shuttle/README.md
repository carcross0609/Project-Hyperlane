# Sith Emperor's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith Emperor's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Emperor%27s_shuttle)

The Sith Emperor of the resurgent Empire owned a shuttle among the private craft reserved for his use. When the Jedi Knight known as the Hero of Tython confronted the Emperor on Dromund Kaas, the Hero and crew stole the Emperor's shuttle from Kaas City so that they could reach the Emperor at the Dark Temple.
