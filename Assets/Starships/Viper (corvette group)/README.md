# Viper (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Viper (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Viper_%28corvette_group%29)

Viper was a group of three CR90 corvettes that served in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. After the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Intrepid hyperdrive was damaged in an accidental collision with an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate, leaving it stranded in the Circarpous system, Viper group was sent, along with several Assault Gunboats to reinforce it.
