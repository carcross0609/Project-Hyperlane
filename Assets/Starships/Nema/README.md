# Nema

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nema](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nema)

The Nema was a transport used by the Rebel Alliance. During the Galactic Civil War, it transported medical supplies through the Kiax Nebula, and Red Squadron were sent to accompany it. The transport was hijacked by pirates who served the Tech Masters and taken to Trionak. Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa of the Rebel Alliance managed to undertake a mission to Trionak and recover the Nema, bringing it back to the Home One.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nema
- **Type:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
