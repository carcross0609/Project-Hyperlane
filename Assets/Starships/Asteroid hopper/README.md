# Asteroid hopper

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Asteroid hopper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asteroid_hopper)

An asteroid hopper or Verpine asteroid hopper was an unusual space-capable short-range repulsorlift vehicle designed by the Verpine to operate in dense asteroid belts, such as the Roche asteroid field. Rather than relying on ion engines, a Verpine hopper "bounced" from asteroid to asteroid using its repulsor engines, relying on gyroscopic stabilization to minimize discomfort for its pilot and passengers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Asteroid hopper
- **Manufacturer:** Verpine
- **Type:** Repulsorcraft
- **Speed:** 650 kmh
- **Engine:** Repulsorlift
- **Crew:** 1
- **Passengers:** 3
- **Lifesupport:** Yes
- **Role:** Personnel transport
