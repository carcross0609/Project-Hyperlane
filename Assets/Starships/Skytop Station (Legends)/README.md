# Skytop Station

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skytop Station/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skytop_Station/Legends)

Skytop Station was a Confederate listening post during the Clone Wars. A modified core ship, the station was designed to monitor Republic communications, thereby providing the Confederate Droid Army with vital intelligence on the movement of enemy fleets. Skytop Station was built and crewed by the Hyper-Communications Cartel, a business group formed by Confederate leader Po Nudo. It was stationed in the upper atmosphere of one of the moons of Ruusan.
