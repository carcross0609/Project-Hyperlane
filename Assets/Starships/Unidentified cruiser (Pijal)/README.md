# Unidentified cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified cruiser (Pijal)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_cruiser_%28Pijal%29)

A cruiser of the Pijali monarchy's royal fleet was in operation by 40 BBY. When not in use, it was kept in the royal palace's dock. That year, the cruiser was loaned to the Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn and his Padawan, Obi-Wan Kenobi, by Lord Regent Rael Averross for use during their mission to the Inner Rim planet Pijal.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Crew:** 2
- **Affiliation:** Pijali monarchy
- **Fleet:** Royal fleet
