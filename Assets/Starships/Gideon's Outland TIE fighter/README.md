# Gideon's Outland TIE fighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gideon's Outland TIE fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gideon%27s_Outland_TIE_fighter)

Gideon's Outland TIE fighter was a starfighter of the TIE fighter series that was present for the clash on Nevarro and was piloted by Moff Gideon. However, it was destroyed by Din Djarin, who placed a grav charge upon the TIE late in the clash.
