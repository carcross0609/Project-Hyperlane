# Unidentified Star Destroyer (Pash Cracken)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Star Destroyer (Pash Cracken)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Star_Destroyer_%28Pash_Cracken%29)

A Star Destroyer was in the service of the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. After graduating from the Empire's Vensenor Flight Academy, Wing Commander Pash Cracken and his seventy-two-ship-strong TIE fighter wing served aboard the Destroyer. When the entire wing defected to the Rebel Alliance, Lord Darth Vader took a personal interest in the event and placed a new commander in charge of the Destroyer.
