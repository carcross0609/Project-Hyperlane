# Ola Hest's frigate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ola Hest's frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ola_Hest%27s_frigate)

A frigate was owned by the Hutt Clan enforcer Ola Hest during the High Republic Era. It had a hyperdrive and at least two thrusters. By 231 BBY, Hest piloted her frigate to the astronomical object Novor to collect protection money from a children's medcenter run by the Sisters of the Ninth Plane. After the Sisters refused to pay Hest and told her to leave, she aimed her frigate's thrusters at the medcenter, bringing it down and killing all who were inside. Hest later claimed the incident was an accident and was eventually captured by Jedi Master Porter Engle.
