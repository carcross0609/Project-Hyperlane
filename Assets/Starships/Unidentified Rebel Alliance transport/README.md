# Unidentified Rebel Alliance transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Rebel Alliance transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Rebel_Alliance_transport)

The Rebel Alliance used a class of transport during the Galactic Civil War. It was boxy in shape, had a trapezoidal cockpit, and three engines. Nine were deployed along with Rogue Squadron during the Battle of Mako-Ta to disperse the override codes and rescue Alliance dignitaries from the Mako-Ta Space Docks.

- **Type:** Transport
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Crew:** Pilots (2)
