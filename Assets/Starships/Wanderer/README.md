# Wanderer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wanderer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wanderer)

Wanderer was a Far*Reach IV PQR survey vessel in the service of the Imperial Survey Corps during the Galactic Civil War. It was piloted by Brektate at the time of its crash on the crystalline world of Isis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wanderer
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Corporation
- **Model:** Far*Reach IV PQR
- **Length:** 80 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 4
- **Armament:** Four laser cannons
- **Complement:** 1 skiff
- **Crew:** 6 (captain, 1 pilot, 1 engineer, 3 scouts)
- **Cargohandling:** 150 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 6 months
- **Role:** Survey/scouting
- **Destroyed:** 1 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Survey Corps
- **Captains:** Captain Tonth
