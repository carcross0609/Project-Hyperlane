# Nangilima

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nangilima](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nangilima)

The Nangilima was a starship in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic shortly after the Battle of Yavin, and was called to assist in the evacuation of Yavin 4 by Admiral Gial Ackbar. Mon Mothma, who was on board the Esperance, sought to board the Nangilima in order to get to Yavin 4 as soon as possible, as the Nangilima was to jump to Yavin 4 within the hour.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nangilima
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
