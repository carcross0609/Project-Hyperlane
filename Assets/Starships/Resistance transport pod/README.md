# Resistance transport pod

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Resistance transport pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resistance_transport_pod)

The Resistance transport pod was a customized model of two-person light shuttle constructed from the cockpit of a B-wing Mark II by the Resistance. The pods were originally constructed to be grafted to passenger modules and used as troop transports by the Resistance, but with their hyperdrive they could be separated and used on their own. During the Battle of Oetchi by the First Order in 34 ABY, the Resistance agents Rose Tico and Finn used a transport pod to travel to the planet Cantonica, where it was then destroyed by the Canto Bight Police Department.
