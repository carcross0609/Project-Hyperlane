# Beneficent Tasia

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Beneficent Tasia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beneficent_Tasia)

Beneficent Tasia was a colony ship that departed the planet Grizmallt in approximately 3900 BBY in an expedition to seek fortune and glory. Beneficent Tasia was accompanied by its support ships, Constant and Mother Vima. The ships were filled with Human Grizmallti refugees fleeing a civil war on Grizmallt, and they were sponsored by Grizmallt's Queen Elsinoré den Tasia and blessed by a famous Jedi Master.
