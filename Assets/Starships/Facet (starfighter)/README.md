# Facet

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Facet (starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Facet_%28starfighter%29)

The Facet was a Cieran-made Nivex-class starfighter owned by the thieves Pax Maripher and Rahara Wick, who modified it and stored it in the lower cargo hold aboard their freighter Meryx. It had a coppery surface.
