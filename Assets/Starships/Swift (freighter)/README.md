# Swift

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Swift (freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Swift_%28freighter%29)

Swift was a freighter in service to the Galactic Empire. It was this ship that took Ysanne Isard and her team of commandos to the Bilbringi Shipyards in 9 ABY in her attempt to recapture the Executor-class Star Dreadnought Lusankya. The ship was commanded by Captain Wintle.
