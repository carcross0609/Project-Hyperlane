# Etti Lighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Etti Lighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Etti_Lighter)

The Etti Lighter, also known as the Etti Light Transport or Authority lighter, was mid-sized freighter produced and used by the Corporate Sector Authority.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Etti Lighter
- **Manufacturer:** Corporate Sector Authority
- **Type:** Modified medium freighter
- **Cost:**
  - 425,000 credits (new)
  - 650,000 credits (modified)
- **Length:** 125 meters
- **Max Speed:** 800 KMH
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 2
  - Class 10
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Navigation computer limited to four jumps
- **Armament:**
  - 10 quad laser cannons
  - Tractor beam projector
- **Crew:** 25; 13 gunners
- **Skeleton:** 15
- **Passengers:** 15
- **Capacity:** 400 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 3 days
- **Role:**
  - Cargo and passenger freighter (unmodified)
  - Light patrol and interdiction (modified)
