# Seltiss-2 caravel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seltiss-2 caravel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seltiss-2_caravel)

The Seltiss-2 caravel was a pleasure ship manufactured by Ubrikkian Industries and commonly used by the Hutts. Because of this, it was often referred to as the Hutt caravel or Hutt chariot.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Seltiss-2 caravel
- **Manufacturer:** Ubrikkian Industries
- **Type:** Caravel
- **Cost:** 550,000 - 800,000 credits (160,000 used)
- **Length:** 30 meters
- **Max Speed:** 850 km/h
- **Hyperdrive:** None
- **Navigation:** None
- **Armament:** Medium laser cannons (2)
- **Crew:**
  - 1 Pilot
  - 1 Co-pilot
  - 2 Gunners
- **Passengers:** 12
- **Capacity:** 50 tons
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Availability:** Licensed
- **Role:** Yacht
- **Affiliation:** Hutts
