# Starlite Cloud

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starlite Cloud](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starlite_Cloud)

The Starlite Cloud was a Sullustan cruise liner that visited Brentaal in 6 BBY. During its stopover there, the twelve-year-old Platt Okeefe joined the crew as a cabin steward. She worked on the ship for two years before leaving for another job. Also among its crewmembers was the Mon Calamari Mussat Nasrabi who served as chief engineer.
