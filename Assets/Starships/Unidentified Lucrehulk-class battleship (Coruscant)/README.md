# Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship (Coruscant)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Lucrehulk-class_battleship_%28Coruscant%29)

This Lucrehulk-class battleship participated in the Battle of Coruscant in 19 BBY. During the engagement, the Lucrehulk caught a Venator-class Star Destroyer in-between its docking arms. The captive Venator docked with the ship and offloaded teams of clone troopers whom tore through its corridors. During Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi's attempt to rescue the Supreme Chancellor, the clone soldiers had successfully captured the Lucrehulk.
