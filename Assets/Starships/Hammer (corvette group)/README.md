# Hammer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hammer (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hammer_%28corvette_group%29)

Hammer was a group of three CR90 corvettes in the Rebel Alliance Fleet. They were the second group of a three-wave corvette attack on the Imperial EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Red Wind.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hammer
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** CR series
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Length:** 150 meters
- **Width:** 48.6 meters
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
