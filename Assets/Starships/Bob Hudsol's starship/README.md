# Bob Hudsol's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bob Hudsol's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bob_Hudsol%27s_starship)

A starship served as the flagship of Rebel Alliance General Bob Hudsol. It was destroyed during the Battle of Mako-Ta by Darth Vader's Death Squadron.
