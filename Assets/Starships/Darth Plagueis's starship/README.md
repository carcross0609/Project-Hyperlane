# Darth Plagueis's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Plagueis's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Plagueis%27s_starship)

A starship was owned by the Muun Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Plagueis in the years before his death in 32 BBY. He flew it with his personal droid 11-4D and used it for missions to places such as Abraxin and Naboo on missions to find Naat Lare and to kill Ars Veruna respectively.

- **Name:** Darth Plagueis's starship
- **Escapepods:** None

- **Owners:** Darth Plagueis
