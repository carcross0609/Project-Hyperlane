# Mantan Wanderer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mantan Wanderer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mantan_Wanderer)

The Mantan Wanderer was a cruiser of Mon Calamari origin, operated by the Rebel Alliance during the early years of the Galactic Civil War.

- **Name:** Mantan Wanderer
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Complement:** Aegis-class Combat Shuttle (1)
- **Passengers:**

  -   Team 19
- **Role:**
  - Carrier
  - Command ship
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** 14th Roving Line
- **Captains:** Captain Qarl
