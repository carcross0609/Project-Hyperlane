# Eagle (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eagle (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eagle_%28corvette_group%29)

Eagle was a group of two CR90 corvettes in service to the Galactic Empire. They attempted to stop the BFF-1 bulk freighter Ashanti from stealing TIE Avengers from a secret Imperial base. However the corvettes were destroyed by Rebel starfighter ace Keyan Farlander.
