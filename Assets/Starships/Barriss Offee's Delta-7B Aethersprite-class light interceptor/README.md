# Barriss Offee's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Barriss Offee's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barriss_Offee%27s_Delta-7B_Aethersprite-class_light_interceptor)

Jedi Padawan Barriss Offee piloted a blue Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor during the Battle of Umbara. During the battle, she flew alongside fellow Padawan Ahsoka Tano. Her onboard R-series astromech droid was purple with pink plating. The ship itself had blue markings.
