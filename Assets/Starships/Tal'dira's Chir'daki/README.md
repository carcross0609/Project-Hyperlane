# Tal'dira's Chir'daki

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tal'dira's Chir'daki](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tal%27dira%27s_Chir%27daki)

Tal'dira's Chir'daki was an "Ugly"-designed starfighter created by the Twi'lek by attaching the wings and KX9 laser cannons of a T-65 X-wing onto the spherical pod of a TIE Fighter. This particular starfigher was flown by the premier Twi'lek warrior, Tal'dira, where he acted as the flight commander for a squadron of Chir'daki.
