# Galactic Guardian

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Galactic Guardian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Guardian)

The Galactic Guardian was an Acclamator-class assault ship used by the Republic during the Clone Wars, and later by the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Galactic Guardian
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Model:** Acclamator-class assault ship
- **Type:** Assault ship
- **Armament:** Quad turbolaser cannons
- **Role:**
  - Troop transport
  - Carrier
  - Rise of the Empire era
  - Rebellion era
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
- **Navy:**
  - Republic Navy
  - Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:** Open Circle Fleet
