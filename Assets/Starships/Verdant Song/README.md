# Verdant Song

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Verdant Song](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verdant_Song)

The Verdant Song was a starship that was used by the Galactic Republic. During the Clone Wars, the Bith spy Ananah Kragana and a group of Republic operatives traveled aboard the Verdant Song from the planet Clak'dor VII to Mezhra Station, a space station that orbited the world.
