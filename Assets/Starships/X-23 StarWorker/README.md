# X-23 StarWorker

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [X-23 StarWorker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-23_StarWorker)

The X-23 StarWorker was a class of space barge that could transport thousands of tons or thousands of cubic meters of goods. It usually operated in developed star systems, and had limited deflector shields, no weapons, and no hyperdrive.

## Specifications

- **Name:** X-23 StarWorker
- **Type:** Space barge
- **Crew:**
  - 1 pilot
  - 1 co-pilot
  - 1 labor droid
- **Role:** Transportation
