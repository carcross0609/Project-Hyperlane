# Crimson Axe

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crimson Axe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crimson_Axe)

The Crimson Axe was the starship of Rav that later served as his permanent headquarters.

- **Name:** Crimson Axe

- **Complement:**

  - 1 light freighter
  - 12 starfighters
- **Crew:** 550
- **Passengers:** 150 (prisoners)
- **Cargohandling:** 3,000 tons
- **Consumables:** 1 year
- **Role:** Pirate vessel
- **Affiliation:** Rav's pirate gang
- **Owners:** Rav
