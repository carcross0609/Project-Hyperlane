# Lupus-class missile frigate

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lupus-class missile frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lupus-class_missile_frigate)

The Lupus-class missile frigate, also known as the Trade Federation Missile Frigate, was a light frigate used by the Trade Federation prior to and during the Invasion of Naboo and by the CIS Navy early in the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lupus-class missile frigate
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Lupus-class missile frigate
- **Type:** Missile Frigate
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped

  - Point-defense dual laser cannons (6)
- **Role:** Frigate
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
