# Sarpentia

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sarpentia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarpentia)

The Sarpentia was a warship in the navy of the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances, and served as the flagship of Admiral Makin. The Sarpentia participated in the Second Battle of Fondor.
