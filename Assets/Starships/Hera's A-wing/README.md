# Hera's A-wing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hera's A-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hera%27s_A-wing)

Hera's A-wing was an RZ-1 A-wing interceptor used by the rebel pilot Hera Syndulla. Syndulla was the leader of Phoenix Squadron, a squadron of A-wing fighters that was a part of the early rebellion against the Galactic Empire.
