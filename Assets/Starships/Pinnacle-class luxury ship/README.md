# Pinnacle-class luxury ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pinnacle-class luxury ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pinnacle-class_luxury_ship)

The Pinnacle-class luxury ship was a model of sleek luxury yacht manufactured by Vekker Corp. According to the company's advertisement, everything aboard those exclusive vessels was handmade. As a consequence, only the richest could afford them. Pinnacle-class luxury ships were sleek, short amidships but broadening away from the keel, with ostentatious flaring at the wings. Senator Erudo Ro-Kiintor, who represented Hevurion in the Galactic Senate of the New Republic, owned one such yacht, the Hevurion Grace.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pinnacle-class luxury ship
- **Manufacturer:** Vekker Corp
- **Type:** Luxury yacht
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
