# Mine Mine Mine

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mine Mine Mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mine_Mine_Mine)

Mine Mine Mine was the private yacht skiff and star yacht of crimelord Rufus Trammel, who used the vehicle to tour Dorumaa, a moon of the planet Almas. After his bid on a private platform in the Genarian city of Tolea Biqua became public during the last days of the Galactic Republic, Trammel retreated to the ship. The yacht was rarely piloted by Trammel himself, who regularly stood at the vessel's bow with his elbows on its railings.
