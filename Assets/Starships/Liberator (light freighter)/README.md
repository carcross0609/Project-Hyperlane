# Liberator (light freighter)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Liberator (light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liberator_%28light_freighter%29)

The Liberator was a light freighter used by a group of pirates to attack a starship crewed by adventurers who had been contracted to ferry spice from Nigel VI to Lohopa II.
