# Wyvern (smuggler ship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wyvern (smuggler ship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wyvern_%28smuggler_ship%29)

The Wyvern was a ship used by a crew of smugglers during the time of the First Order. During one smuggling job the crew was killed by a Karlox and the sole survivor was executed by the first order.
