# Corrupter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Corrupter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupter)

The Corrupter was a Victory-class Star Destroyer that, during the Imperial Era, belonged to the Imperial Navy. It was equipped with sublight engines and turbolasers. At some point, the Star Destroyer was flanked by at least five TIE/sa bombers in space and fired multiple shots at a target.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Corrupter
- **Line:** Victory-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Engine:** Sublight engines
- **Armament:** Turbolasers
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
