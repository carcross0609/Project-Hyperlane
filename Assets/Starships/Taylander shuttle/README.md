# Taylander shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Taylander shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taylander_shuttle)

The Taylander shuttle was a model of civilian transport shuttle manufactured by Gallofree Yards, Inc. and found throughout the galaxy. It was used as a transport ship for undercover operations by the early rebellion and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. There was also a smaller model, known as the Taylander-XCE shuttle.
