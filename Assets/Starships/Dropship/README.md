# Dropship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dropship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dropship)

Dropships were any craft designed to transport troops to the surface of a planet. Models of dropship included the Imperial Dropship Transport, the Y-45 armored transport hauler, the Theta-class barge, and the Y-85 Titan dropship. The Gozanti-class cruisers could also be used as dropships, as they could carry either four TIE/ln space superiority starfighters, four All Terrain Defense Pods, or even two All Terrain Armored Transports on underside-mounted docking clamps.
