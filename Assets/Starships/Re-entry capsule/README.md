# Re-entry capsule

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Re-entry capsule](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Re-entry_capsule)

A re-entry capsule was a capsule used for protection from atmospheric re-entry. They were used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Re-entry capsule
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoid Creation Nest
- **Owners:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Purpose:** Atmospheric re-entry
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
