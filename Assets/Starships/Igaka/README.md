# Igaka

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Igaka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Igaka)

Igaka was a BFF-1 bulk freighter belonging to the Galactic Empire. The freighter was delivering supplies and repairs to the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Juvaini just as Rebel Alliance RZ-1 A-wing interceptors and B-wing starfighters arrived to destroy the frigate.
