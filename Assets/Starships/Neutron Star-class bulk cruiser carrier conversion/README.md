# Neutron Star-class bulk cruiser carrier conversion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Neutron Star-class bulk cruiser carrier conversion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neutron_Star-class_bulk_cruiser_carrier_conversion)

The Neutron Star-class bulk cruiser was a class of capital ship used by the Galactic Empire and Rebel Alliance, manufactured by Rendili StarDrive. They were designed during the Clone Wars to serve as second-line ships. After the Clone Wars most were sold to local defense forces or private interests, and several were eventually modified to serve as carriers.
