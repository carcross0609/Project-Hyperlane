# DFS-1138

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DFS-1138](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DFS-1138)

DFS-1138 was a Vulture-class starfighter assigned to the Saak'ak. When Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi arrived onboard, DFS-1138 was present in the hangar. Later, when the Jedi's ship was destroyed, DFS-1138 was seen fleeing the hangar with his fellow droid fighters.
