# Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maxima-A-class_heavy_cruiser)

The Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser, alternatively the Maxima-A class heavy cruiser or simply the Maxima-A cruiser, was a wedge-shaped First Order heavy cruiser deployed around thirty years after the Battle of Endor. New Republic Commander Poe Dameron discovered two of the cruisers at a staging point as the Cold War heated up.
