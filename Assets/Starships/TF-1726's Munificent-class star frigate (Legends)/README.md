# TF-1726's Munificent-class star frigate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TF-1726's Munificent-class star frigate/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TF-1726%27s_Munificent-class_star_frigate/Legends)

A Munificent-class star frigate served as the command ship of T-series tactical droid TF-1726 at the Sky Battle of Quell of the Clone Wars. The frigate deployed hundreds of droids against the Venator-class Star Destroyer Liberty to ensure its destruction.
