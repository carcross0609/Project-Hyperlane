# MK-29 shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MK-29 shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MK-29_shuttle)

MK-29 shuttle was a type of passenger shuttle used during the Imperial Era. During 3 ABY, a MK-29 shuttle left from Achra Station in the Akiva system bound for Oba Diah. Prior to its departure an announcement was broadcast of the station's speaker system in Huttese notifying its passengers to go to hanger 2.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MK-29 shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Role:** Transport
