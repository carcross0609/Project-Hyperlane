# Star Traveler

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Star Traveler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Traveler)

The Star Traveler was a light freighter that was owned and operated by the smuggler Renna Strego. It accompanied Roa's Lwyll during the Battle of Nar Shaddaa.
