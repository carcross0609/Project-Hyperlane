# Victory II-class Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Victory II-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Victory_II-class_Star_Destroyer)

The Victory II-class Star Destroyer, or simply Victory II Star Destroyer, was a class of Star Destroyer of the Galactic Empire that served as a successor to the Victory I-class Star Destroyer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Victory II-class Star Destroyer
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Line:** Victory-class Star Destroyer
- **Model:** Victory II-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Cost:** 50,000,000 credits
- **Length:** 900 meters
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 1 (primary)
  - Class 15 (secondary)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Long-range sensor suite
- **Navigation:** Navigation computer
- **Armament:**
  - Configuration 1 (original Victory I-class armament)
  - 128 turbolasers (12 × 4, 40 × 2)
  - Concussion missile launchers
  - 10 tractor beam projectors
  - Configuration 2
  - 40 medium turbolasers (20 × 2)
  - 40 light turbolasers (10 × 4)
  - 20 assault concussion missile launchers, or ion cannons
  - 10 heavy tractor beam projectors
- **Complement:**
  - 24 starfighters
  - Shuttles
  - Landing craft
  - AT-series walkers
- **Bays:** At least 1
- **Escapepods:** Escape pods
- **Crew:** 6,107 officers, pilots, and enlisted crew
- **Passengers:** 1,600 stormtroopers
- **Capacity:** 6,500 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 year
- **Role:**
  - Fleet command ship
  - Patrol ship
  - Planetary defense
  - System defense
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Navy
