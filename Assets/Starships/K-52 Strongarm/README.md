# K-52 Strongarm

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K-52 Strongarm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-52_Strongarm)

The K-52 Strongarm, also known as K-52 Demolisher, was a gunship model produced by Hyrotii Corporation during the Galactic War and sold to customers all over the galaxy without question. One such ship was purchased by Dark Council member Darth Marr, which soon made it popular with other Sith and Imperial military officers. It was also popular with privateers, mercenaries and other non-military sources that loaned their services to the Republic Navy of the Galactic Republic.
