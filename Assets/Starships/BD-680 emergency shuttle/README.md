# BD-680 emergency shuttle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BD-680 emergency shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD-680_emergency_shuttle)

The BD-680 emergency shuttle was a model of emergency shuttle produced by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. The JT-731 Broadhorn transports of the Hidden Path carried BD-680 shuttles, and Obi-Wan Kenobi used one to lure Darth Vader away from the Hidden Path's ships as they fled Jabiim, flying it down to one of Jabiim's moons and causing Vader to follow him.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BD-680 emergency shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Length:** 11.2 meters
