# Garel Honor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Garel's Honor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Garel%27s_Honor)

Garel's Honor was a Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette that served in the naval forces of the Rebel Alliance during the Imperial Era. During that time, it participated in an engagement along with at least two other Sphyrna-class corvettes against a group of Imperial Gozanti-class cruisers while it carried cargo near several celestial bodies. During the engagement, the corvette critically damaged one of the Galactic Empire's Gozanti cruisers by ramming its frontal dorsal bulkhead on the cruiser's midship, causing the front half to buckle and break. It shared its name with the Lothal sector planet Garel, which hosted the Garel City cell and the Phoenix Cell resistance groups during the Imperial Era.
