# Vigilance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vigilance (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vigilance_%28Sith_Empire%29)

The Vigilance was a warship of the reconstituted Sith Empire that participated in the invasion of Taris during the Galactic War. The Vigilance destroyed a Galactic Republic cruiser, though escape pods from the ship managed to land on Taris's surface.|
