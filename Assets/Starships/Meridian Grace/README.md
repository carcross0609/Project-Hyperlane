# Meridian Grace

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Meridian Grace](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meridian_Grace)

The Meridian Grace was a NX-1200 freighter that belonged to the Mirialan smuggler Giana Kayl. Around 3643 BBY it was transporting goods from Tai Cordan for the Balmorran resistance, but was shot down and crashed on Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Meridian Grace
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Affiliation:** Balmorran resistance
- **Owners:** Giana Kayl
