# Daggers Point

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Daggers Point](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daggers_Point)

The Daggers Point was a Warlord Heavy Cruiser used by the Hidden Daggers pirates during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, it operated in the Bright Jewel and Kashyyyk systems where it was spotted together with a group of Hidden Daggers starfighters. The ship was destroyed in 1 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Daggers Point
- **Type:** Warlord Heavy Cruiser
- **Destroyed:** 1 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Hidden Daggers
