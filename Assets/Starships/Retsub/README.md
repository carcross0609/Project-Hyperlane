# Retsub

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Retsub](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Retsub)

Retsub was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. Towards the end of Operation Skyhook the Empire began a new offensive after the completion of the Death Star I. The Retsub was assigned to one of the first attacks, against one of the Alliance to Restore the Republic's main weapons-storage compounds.
