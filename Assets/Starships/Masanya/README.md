# Masanya

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Masanya](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Masanya)

The Masanya was a CR90 corvette manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation that was used by the fleet of the Rebel Alliance as a light escort vessel. It was modified with a hangar for small vessels and equipped with missile-jamming target-disruption scanners. It fought in the Battle of Endor, a decisive engagement between the Rebel Alliance and the Galactic Empire.
