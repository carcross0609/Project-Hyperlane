# Livery Cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Livery Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Livery_Cruiser)

A Livery Cruiser was a class of cruiser that was used during the New Republic Era. It was considered more "pleasant" for interstellar travel than gunships such as the Razor Crest. In 9 ABY, a Mythrol on the ice planet of Pagodon attempted to ingratiate himself to his captor&mdash;the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin&mdash;by suggesting that he could hail a Livery Cruiser for them.
