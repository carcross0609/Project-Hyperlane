# Dominion

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dominion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dominion)

The Dominion was a Star Destroyer at the time of the Liberation of Kashyyyk in 5 ABY. At this time, it was infiltrated by New Republic forces, and used to attack two other Star Destroyers over Kashyyyk, the Neutralizer and the Vitiator, the latter of which it destroyed.
