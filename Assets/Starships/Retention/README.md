# Retention

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Retention](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Retention)

The Retention was an Interdictor cruiser in service to the remnant of the Imperial Navy's Seventh Fleet during the Galactic Civil War. In 3 ABY, at a point when the remnant had been partially exiled from Imperial operations, the Retention took part in an ambush of a Rebel Alliance convoy at the astronomical object Derra IV.
