# StarSpeeder 3000

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [StarSpeeder 3000](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/StarSpeeder_3000)

The StarSpeeder 3000 was a transport spacecraft used by the Star Tours travel agency. It had room to carry up to 40 passengers. The craft were produced sometime around 4 ABY, when Star Tours referred to them as "new."
