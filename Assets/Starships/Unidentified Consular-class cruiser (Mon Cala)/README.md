# Unidentified Consular-class cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Consular-class cruiser (Mon Cala)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Consular-class_cruiser_%28Mon_Cala%29)

This Consular-class cruiser was used by Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker and Senator Padmé Amidala to travel to the watery planet Mon Cala in a effort to stop a civil war between the Mon Calamari and the Quarren after the assassination of King Yos Kolina. The cruiser was later destroyed during the Battle of Mon Cala after being rigged with explosives, when Riff Tamson blew it up in order to prevent the Jedi from escaping.
