# Dar'oosh

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dar'oosh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dar%27oosh)

The Dar'oosh was a transport that was used by a brigade of mercenary shock troopers known as the Specialist Unit Two Hundred Seven during the Republic Dark Age. In 1032 BBY, the mercenaries traveled in the Dar'oosh to the planet Darkknell and their leader, Kr'saang the Togorian, met with the Sith Lord Daiman. However, Kr'saang angered Daiman, so the Sith killed the Togorian, and ordered some of his Correctors to go to the Dar'oosh and push the other mercenaries into service with the Daimanite slave army.
