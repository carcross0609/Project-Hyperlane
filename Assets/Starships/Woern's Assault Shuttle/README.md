# Woern's Assault Shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Woern's Assault Shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Woern%27s_Assault_Shuttle)

Woern's Assault Shuttle was a modified Gamma-class assault shuttle that was used by Laerron Woern as his command ship. He received it after the ship was found abandoned after a battle. He removed all of the launch ports for the Zero-G assault stormtroopers except for two and converted the excess space into storage and benches.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Woern's Assault Shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Gamma-class assault shuttle
- **Length:** 30 meters
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 2 (primary)
  - Class 18 (backup)
- **Navigation:** Equipped (3 jump limit)
- **Armament:**
  - 4 laser cannons
  - Concussion missile launcher
  - Tractor beam
- **Crew:** 5
- **Passengers:** 50
- **Capacity:** 5 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 week
- **Captains:** Laerron Woern
