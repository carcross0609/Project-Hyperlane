# Consular-class light cruiser

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Consular-class light cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Consular-class_light_cruiser)

The Consular-class light cruiser, also known as the Consular-class space cruiser, and commonly referred to as the Republic Cruiser, was a starship used by the Galactic Republic during the events leading up to the Invasion of Naboo and during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Consular-class light cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** Republic Cruiser
- **Model:**
  - Consular-class space cruiser
  - Republic frigate
- **Type:**
  - Light cruiser
  - Corvette
- **Cost:** 3,000,000 credits
- **Length:** 115 meters (377.2 feet)
- **Max Speed:** 900 kph
- **Engine:** Dyne 577 radial atomizer engine (3)
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 2
  - Class 14 (backup)
- **Hdsystem:** Longe Voltrans tri-arc CD-3.2 hyperdrive
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Extreme-range sensors
- **Navigation:** Navigation computer
- **Escapepods:**

  - 2 8-person escape pods
- **Crew:**

  - 4 engineers
- **Passengers:** Up to 24 (depending on configuration)
- **Consumables:** 1 year
- **Role:** Diplomatic cruiser
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Republic Diplomatic Fleet
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Rebel Alliance Navy
