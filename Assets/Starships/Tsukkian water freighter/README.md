# Tsukkian water freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tsukkian water freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tsukkian_water_freighter)

Tsukkian water freighters were large starships produced on Tsukkia, capable of carrying huge cargoes of water to planets with little to no water.
