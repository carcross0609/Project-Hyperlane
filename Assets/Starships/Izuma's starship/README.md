# Izuma's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Izuma's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Izuma%27s_starship)

A starship was used by Lord Izuma's raiders sometime between 19 BBY and 1 BBY. The vessel was an XS stock light freighter that the raiders used to transport and control reprogrammed B1-series battle droids with the intent of capturing and controlling Meevai Village on the planet Keelia. It was later destroyed by Valco during the liberation of Meevai Village.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Izuma's starship
- **Model:** XS stock light freighter
- **Destroyed:** Sometime after Order 66, Keelia
- **Affiliation:** Izuma's raiders
