# Separatist drop ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Separatist drop ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_drop_ship)

Separatist drop ships were a class of dropship that saw service with the navy of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Capable of breaking through enemy picket lines, they carried a complement of multiple squadrons of vulture droids. In 19 BBY, several drop ships were used during the battle on Lokori.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Separatist drop ship
- **Type:** Dropship
- **Complement:** Multiple Vulture-class starfighter droid starfighters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy
