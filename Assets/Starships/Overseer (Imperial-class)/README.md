# Overseer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Overseer (Imperial-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Overseer_%28Imperial-class%29)

The Overseer was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was built at the Kuat Drive Yards for service in the Imperial Navy and later served as the flagship of Titan Squadron. The Star Destroyer managed to win major victories at Jeyell and Pothor. In the former, it managed to destroy several Nebulon-B escort frigates despite being outnumbered. The ship later defended against the New Republic at the Var-Shaa dockyard and was heavily damaged, but still managed to escape its New Republic pursuers and search for survivors of the battle. Due to the heavy damage it had sustained, the Star Destroyer was forced to be retrofitted before returning to the front lines.
