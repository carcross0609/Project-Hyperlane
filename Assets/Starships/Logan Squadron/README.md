# Logan Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Logan Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Logan_Squadron)

Logan Squadron was a squadron of 4 X-wings used by the Bothans during the Galactic Civil War. These fighters, along with Junker Squadron and A-wings from Yme Squadron attempted to protect Muurian transports Botani, and an escort shuttle Com 1 from being captured by Imperial forces lead by Darth Vader. The fighters were all destroyed by Assault Gunboats, one of which piloted by Maarek Stele, and the transports were captured.
