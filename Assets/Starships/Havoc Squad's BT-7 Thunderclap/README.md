# Havoc Squad's BT-7 Thunderclap

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Havoc Squad's BT-7 Thunderclap](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Havoc_Squad%27s_BT-7_Thunderclap)

Havoc Squad's BT-7 Thunderclap was a BT-7 Thunderclap used by the Havoc Squad during the Cold War and Galactic War. It was provided to Havoc Squad Lieutenant and Aric Jorgan by General Elin Garza as the means to track down Harron Tavus and his traitor followers. Following the conslusion of the SpecForce Incident, Havoc continued to use the ship in preparation for the assault on the Gauntlet superweapon, culminating in the Battle of the Gauntlet. The ship was used by Havoc Squad throughout the campaign against General Arkos Rakton.
