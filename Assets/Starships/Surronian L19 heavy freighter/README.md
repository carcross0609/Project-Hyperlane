# Surronian L19 heavy freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Surronian L19 heavy freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Surronian_L19_heavy_freighter)

The Surronian L19 heavy freighter was a class of transport. The smuggler Platt Okeefe suggested that pilots never buy a new L19 freighter unless they had the services of a good mechanic in her addendum added between 19 BBY and 10 BBY to the Smuggler's Guide, a journal written by Maz Kanata, owner of Takodana Castle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Surronian L19 heavy freighter
- **Model:** L19
- **Type:** Heavy freighter
- **Role:** Transport
