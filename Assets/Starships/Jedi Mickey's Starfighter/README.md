# Jedi Mickey's Starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Jedi Mickey's Starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Mickey%27s_Starfighter)

Jedi Mickey's Starfighter was the personal customized Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor of the Jedi Master Mickey Mouse and his astromech droid partner, R2-MK. Mickey and R2-MK used Jedi Mickey's Starfighter to battle droid starfighters and Imperial TIE Fighter squadrons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jedi Mickey's Starfighter
- **Model:** Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor
- **Type:** Interceptor
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Blaster cannons (2)
- **Crew:**
  - 1 pilot
  - 1 astromech droid
- **Role:** Personal starfighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Galactic Republic
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Owners:** Mickey Mouse
