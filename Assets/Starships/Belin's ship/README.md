# Belin's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Belin's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Belin%27s_ship)

Belin's ship was a transport starship piloted by the Ugnaught Belin during the High Republic Era. After Belin was trapped behind the Stormwall in the Occlusion Zone, a region of space captured by the Nihil marauders, he unwillingly used his ship to transport cargo for the Nihil. The Jedi Master Avar Kriss and the astromech droid KC-78, hiding out within the Occlusion Zone, boarded Belin's ship and dispatched the Nihil guards in order to deliver its cargo of grain to the Rodian colony of Prandril. Belin was reluctant to cross the Nihil, but eventually chose to assist Kriss, who later used the ship to visit Hetzal to rescue the reporter Rhil Dairo, also meeting fellow Jedi Master Porter Engle. The Jedi then utilized Belin's ship to board the Nihil vessel Cacophony in order to escape the Occlusion Zone; however, Belin and Dairo chose to remain within Nihil territory to assist the people trapped there.
