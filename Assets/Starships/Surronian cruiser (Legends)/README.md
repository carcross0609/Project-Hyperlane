# Surronian cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Surronian cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Surronian_cruiser)

The Surronian cruiser was an ancient and very rare design of saucer craft. The Corona-class armed frigate was a knockoff of the legendary Surronian cruiser.
