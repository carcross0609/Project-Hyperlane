# NTB-630 naval bomber

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [NTB-630 naval bomber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NTB-630_naval_bomber)

The NTB-630 naval bomber, also known as the NTB-3 bomber, was utilized by the naval forces of the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. A model of bomber produced by the Incom Corporation and Sublights Products Corporation manufacturing companies, it was a cousin in design to the PTB-625 planetary bomber and the lighter Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter, both of which were also constructed by those companies.
