# Shara Bey's A-wing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shara Bey's A-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shara_Bey%27s_A-wing)

Lieutenant Shara Bey piloted at least two RZ-1 A-wing interceptors during the Galactic Civil War in battles such as the battle of Endor and the Battle of Cawa City. In 5 ABY Bey, along with her husband Kes Dameron, retired from the Alliance to Restore the Republic, and Bey was able to keep her A-wing as part of her compensation package. While living on Yavin 4, Bey would fly her A-wing as part of the Civilian Defense Force that was stationed there. When her son Poe was six years old, Bey would teach him how to fly in her A-wing allowing Poe to sit in her lap, learning how to control the ship from her.
