# Unidentitied Nagai transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentitied Nagai transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentitied_Nagai_transport)

Transports were used by the Nagai on the planet Kashyyyk. Commander Ozrei N'takkilomandrife used these craft in an effort to restart the enslavement trade on Kashyyyk, loading captive Wookiees aboard the ships. When Chewbacca, Lando Calrissian, and Han Solo traveled to Kashyyyk to meet with Chewbacca's family, they uncovered the slave trade and prevented the slave ships from leaving the planet.
