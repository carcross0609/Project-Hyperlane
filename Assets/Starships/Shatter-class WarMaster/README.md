# Shatter-class WarMaster

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shatter-class WarMaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shatter-class_WarMaster)

The Shatter-class WarMaster was a class of warship used by the Grysk Hegemony of the Unknown Regions. The FateSpinner was one such vessel, and was commanded by "Jixtus" to project the Grysk's power on Generalirius Nakirre of the Kilji Illumine. Another Shatter-class WarMaster was the HopeBreaker. The Shatter-class was three to four times larger than a Kilji war cruiser, and considered the greatest class of warship within the Grysk armada.
