# Goya

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Goya](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Goya)

The Goya was a Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport used by Imperial forces during the Galactic Civil War. Transport Goya reloaded warheads for essential TIE starfighters attacking Nebulon-B frigate Akaga at the Treachery at Ottega.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Goya
- **Manufacturer:** Telgorn Corporation
- **Model:** Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Width:** 7.8 meters
- **Height:** 5.8 meters
- **Complement:** Warheads
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
