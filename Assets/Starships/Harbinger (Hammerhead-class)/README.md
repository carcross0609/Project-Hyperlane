# Harbinger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harbinger (Hammerhead-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harbinger_%28Hammerhead-class%29)

The Harbinger was a Hammerhead-class cruiser that served the Galactic Republic shortly after the Jedi Civil War. Originally assigned to patrol Republic space and deal with local troubles, the Harbinger was sent in 3951 BBY to the Outer Rim Territories to pick up the Jedi Exile and ferry her to Telos IV.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Harbinger
- **Type:** Hammerhead-class cruiser
- **Engine:** Ion engines
- **Complement:** At least three strike teams
- **Crew:** About 300
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Sith
- **Owners:** Darth Sion
- **Captains:** Unidentified captain
