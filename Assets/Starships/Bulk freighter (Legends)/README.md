# Bulk freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bulk freighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bulk_freighter/Legends)

bulk freighter]] Bulk freighters were huge spaceships used to haul enormous cargo loads. Most of the ship's interior was occupied by cargo bays while the bridge and crew areas were often small and cramped. In spite of this, many spacers signed on to bulk freighters because of the profits involved.
