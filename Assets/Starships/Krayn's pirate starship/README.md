# Krayn's pirate starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krayn's pirate starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krayn%27s_pirate_starship)

Krayn's pirate ship was a massive starship that the T'surr slave-pirate and crime-lord Krayn used to capture other space vessels and to acquire slaves for his illegal spice-processing operations on Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Krayn's pirate starship
- **Type:** Starship
- **Role:** Catpuring ships and slaves
- **Owners:** Krayn
