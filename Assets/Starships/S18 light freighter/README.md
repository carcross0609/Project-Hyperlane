# S18 light freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S18 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S18_light_freighter)

The S18 light freighter, created by the Damorian Manufacturing Corporation, was a vessel in use around the galaxy by 44 ABY. In that year, it was used by the Lost Tribe of Sith's armada to ambush Lando Calrissian and Jaina Solo in an attempt to capture the Rockhound following the conclusion of their mission to the Maw. The S18 light freighter could house up to six BDY crew skiffs.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S18 light freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Damorian Manufacturing Corporation
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Complement:** BDY crew skiffs (6)
