# Nikardun Battle Dreadnought

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nikardun Battle Dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikardun_Battle_Dreadnought)

The Nikardun Battle Dreadnought was a type of Nikardun dreadnought. The Deathless was one such ship, and served as the headquarters of General Yiv as he expanded the Nikardun Destiny with at least three other dreadnoughts. These warships were easily recognizable by their oversized viewports. General Yiv often placed them above worlds he was in the process of conquering as an added "reminder" of the power of the Nikardun Destiny.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nikardun Battle Dreadnought
- **Type:** Battle Dreadnought class
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Spectrum lasers
- **Affiliation:** Nikardun Destiny
