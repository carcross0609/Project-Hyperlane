# Resolution

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Resolution (Venator-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resolution_%28Venator-class%29)

The Resolution was a Venator-class Star Destroyer in service of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. In 20 BBY, the Resolution was dispatched to the planet Drongar to evacuate the Republic troops on the world. The Resolution was successful in its task, evacuating most of the Republic forces and staff, driving back the Separatist army, and securing a considerable amount of the precious bota plant.
