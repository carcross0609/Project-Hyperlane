# Amos Tillian and Kam's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Amos Tillian and Kam's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amos_Tillian_and_Kam%27s_shuttle)

A small shuttle was used by the communications engineers Amos Tillian and Kam, who were part of Republic Communications Team Oh-Arr-Ee-Three-Six-One. In 382 BBY, they found the damaged EX droid EX-8C, and examined it aboard the shuttle.
