# CIS Jammer Prototype

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CIS Jammer Prototype](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CIS_Jammer_Prototype)

The CIS Jammer Prototype was a Confederacy of Independent Systems Lucrehulk-class battleship that served during the Clone Wars. It was outfitted with a modified transmission array that could broadcast jamming signals. In 21.25 BBY, the vessel was part of a Confederate flotilla that arrived over the agriworld Balamak and disrupted the Taldot sector's communications. The Galactic Republic and Jedi subsequently carried out a starfighter attack on the flotilla, and Jedi Anakin Skywalker destroyed the CIS Jammer Prototype with a volley of missiles.
