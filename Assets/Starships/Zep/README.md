# Zep

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zep](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zep)

Zep was a pair of BFF-1 bulk freighters used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. Towards the end of Operation Skyhook Zep group came out of hyperspace at one of the Alliance's main weapons-storage compounds. Discovering that the cache was under attack by the Imperial frigate Retsub, they promptly escaped into hyperspace.
