# Huntsman

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Huntsman (New Republic)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huntsman_%28New_Republic%29)

The Huntsman was a starship that served in the navy of the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The vessel lost its entire crew during a number of attacks carried out by the Galactic Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing some time after the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY. The New Republic Intelligence officer Caern Adan listed the loss of the Huntsman crew among a number of the 204th's actions since the Battle of Endor, several weeks after the battle, when talking with an IT-O Interrogation Unit about the fighter wing.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Huntsman
- **Destroyed:** c. 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
- **Navy:** New Republic Defense Fleet
