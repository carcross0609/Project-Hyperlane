# Harbinger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harbinger (Mon Calamari Star Cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harbinger_%28Mon_Calamari_Star_Cruiser%29)

Harbinger was a heavily armed Mon Calamari Star Cruiser that served as General Garm Bel Iblis' flagship in the later part of the Yuuzhan Vong War. Initially guarding the planet Tallaan from Yuuzhan Vong attack, Harbinger participated in the New Republic rout of Yuuzhan Vong forces in the Treskov system in 28 ABY. The Star Cruiser later aided the New Republic's successor state, the Galactic Alliance, in defending the planet Mon Calamari from a Yuuzhan Vong armada in 29 ABY. Harbinger and the Galactic Alliance forces met the armada at the edge of the Mon Calamari system, where the Star Cruiser made good use of its heavy weaponry. Following the battle, Bel Iblis and Harbinger joined Galactic Alliance fleet elements in liberating the planet Corulag from the Yuuzhan Vong.
