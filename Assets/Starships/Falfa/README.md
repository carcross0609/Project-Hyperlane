# Falfa

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Falfa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Falfa)

The Falfa was the personal Rainhawk-class transport of Baron Notluwiski Papanoida of the Pantoran Assembly. During the Clone Wars Papanoida and his son Ion used the ship to rescue his daughter Che Amanwe Papanoida who was being held hostage by the Trade Federation on Tatooine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Falfa
- **Model:** Rainhawk-class transport
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Armament:** 2 laser cannons
- **Crew:** 2
- **Affiliation:** Pantoran Assembly
- **Owners:** Notluwiski Papanoida
