# White Nova

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [White Nova (Harrower-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/White_Nova_%28Harrower-class%29)

White Nova was a Harrower-class dreadnought in service of the Sith Empire during the Cold War and the Galactic War that followed. It would serve as the flagship of an Imperial fleet directing an attack on the Republic Thranta-class corvette Dorin's Sky and later on the Foundry against the Republic blockade guarding it.

## Specifications

- **Name:** White Nova
- **Type:** Harrower-class dreadnought
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:** Expeditionary Fleet
- **Owners:** Moff Phennir
