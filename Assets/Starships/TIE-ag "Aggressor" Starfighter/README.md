# TIE/ag "Aggressor" Starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TIE/ag "Aggressor" Starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/ag_%22Aggressor%22_Starfighter)

The TIE/ag "Aggressor" Starfighter, also referred to simply as the TIE Aggressor or simply Aggressor, was a model of TIE series starfighter that was used by the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era, including during the Galactic Civil War. As a fast attack and medium strike fighter, the TIE Aggressor was deployed by the Galactic Empire in multiple roles, including against smuggling, shipping, and surveillance.
