# Silver Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Silver Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silver_Star)

The Silver Star was a luxury liner operating during the Galactic Civil War. The vessel was the site of a meeting between an ambassador representing the provision government of the Alliance to Restore the Republic, and the Gaddrian ambassador. The Gaddrian ambassador wanted to meet with an Alliance representative to discuss the possibility of building a fifth column within the ranks of the Imperial Senate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Silver Star
- **Type:** Luxury liner
- **Role:** Luxury transport
