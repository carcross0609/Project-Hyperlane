# Spirit of Jedha

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spirit of Jedha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_of_Jedha)

The Spirit of Jedha was a transport that served the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War during the Galactic Empire. The transport was present at the rebel outpost on the jungle planet Lowik. The rebel forces on Lowik, led by Lieutenant Kondra, were forced to evacuate, and the transport was commanded by Commander Hill Purpura in the escape. The Spirit of Jedha was sabotaged by Purpura, who intended to betray the Rebellion by selling the transponder codes of the rebel armada to the Empire, having lost faith in the cause after its defeat at the Battle of Hoth. As the result of the sabotage of its hyperdrive motivator, the Spirit of Jedha dropped out of hyperspace while in transit.
