# BioCruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BioCruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BioCruiser)

The BioCruiser was a massive, nomadic ship owned by Offworld Mining Corporation (UniFy) and commandeered by the philosophical leader called Uni (the former Kad Chun, brother of corrupted Jedi Bruck Chun). The ship's day-to-day operations were financed by a collective onboard treasury contributed to by its resident passengers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BioCruiser
- **Passengers:**
  - Thousands of disenfranchised from across the galaxy
  - Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi & Anakin Skywalker on their first mission as a Jedi Knight-Padawan team
- **Role:** Passenger liner
- **Affiliation:** Telos IV
- **Owners:** Offworld Mining Corporation (UniFy)
- **Captains:** Uni
