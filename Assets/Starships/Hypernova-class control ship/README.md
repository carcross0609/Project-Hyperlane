# Hypernova-class control ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hypernova-class control ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hypernova-class_control_ship)

The Hypernova-class control ship was a custom starship class utilized by Balada the Hutt. One, known as the Hypernova, floated over the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara. Hypernova-class control ships required energy generated from ground-based power stations to operate at full capacity, with specially designed and monitored power umbilicals linking the two. They had the capacity to display holographic images outside of the hull that encircled the ship, rotating and displaying their information from all sides.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hypernova-class control ship
- **Type:** Control ship
- **Role:**
  - Arena control
  - Advertisement display
- **Affiliation:**
  - Balada the Hutt
  - Hunters of the Outer Rim
