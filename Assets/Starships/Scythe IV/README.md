# Scythe IV

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scythe IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scythe_IV)

The Scythe IV was a G1-M4-C Dunelizard fighter in service during the Galactic Civil War. It was the personal starship of Hortrix Steelarm, a smuggler working for the Hutt crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure. Hortrix Steelarm was killed sometime after the Battle of Yavin when the Scythe IV was destroyed by a pilot working for the rival Valarian Syndicate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scythe IV
- **Model:** G1-M4-C Dunelizard fighter
- **Destroyed:** 1 ABY, Yavin system
- **Affiliation:** Jabba's criminal empire
- **Owners:** Hortrix Steelarm
