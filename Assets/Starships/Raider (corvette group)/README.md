# Raider (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Raider (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raider_%28corvette_group%29)

Raider was a group of Imperial CR90 corvettes sent to assist the Imperial EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Blue Fox in destroying the Rebel Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Excelsior. An RZ-1 A-wing interceptor defending the Excelsior helped destroy the corvettes.
