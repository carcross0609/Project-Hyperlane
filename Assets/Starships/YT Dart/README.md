# YT Dart

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YT Dart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT_Dart)

The YT Dart was a model of small spacetug designed by an authorized affiliate of the Corellian Engineering Corporation to serve as an auxiliary craft for YT-1300 light freighters. A compact ship with a narrow viewport, the YT Dart fit between the front mandibles of the YT-1300, seemingly extending its prow and giving it a sleeker appearance. When Lando Calrissian owned the Millennium Falcon, he had a YT Dart modified into a maneuverable two-passenger life pod by sealing its viewport and installing both a hyperdrive and a more powerful sublight engine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YT Dart
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Spacetug
- **Cost:** 9,000 credits
- **Engine:** Sublight engine (1) (with 4 thrusters)
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 4 (modified)
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped (with modification)
- **Sensor:** Short range sensor
- **Crew:** 1 pilot
- **Passengers:** 3
- **Consumables:** 3 days
- **Role:** Escape pod (with modification)
