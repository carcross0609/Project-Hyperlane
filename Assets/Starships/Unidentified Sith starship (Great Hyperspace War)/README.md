# Unidentified Sith starship (Great Hyperspace War)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Sith starship (Great Hyperspace War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Sith_starship_%28Great_Hyperspace_War%29)

A Sith starship from the Great Hyperspace War was discovered drifting through deep space during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. The derelict starship carried lost artifacts from Korriban that had been evacuated prior to the post–Great Hyperspace War counterinvasion.
