# Widow Maker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Widow Maker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Widow_Maker)

Widow Maker was a modified Incom Corporation "Explorer" Scout Ship used by brother and sister bounty hunters Alic and Vika Surn. In exchange for their transport, the Surns paid a five percent gap to the Neuvalis syndicate, the ship's true owner. It was armed with one dual laser cannon, and included two restraint capsules for holding the bounties the Surns' apprehended.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Widow Maker
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Type:** Modified "Explorer" Scout Ship
- **Length:** 15 meters
- **Armament:** 1 dual laser cannon (turret)
- **Crew:** 1 pilot, 1 gunner
- **Passengers:** 1 (2 restraint capsules)
- **Capacity:** 4 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Owners:** Neuvalis syndicate
