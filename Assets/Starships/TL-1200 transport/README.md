# TL-1200 transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TL-1200 transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TL-1200_transport)

The TL-1200 transport was a class of transport that was used during the Imperial Era, particularly in the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy. The smuggler Platt Okeefe suggested that pilots skip buying the TL-1200, in her addendum to Maz Kanata's Smuggler's Guide.
