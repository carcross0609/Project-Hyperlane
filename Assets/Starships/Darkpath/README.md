# Darkpath

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darkpath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darkpath)

The Darkpath was a frigate utilized by the Black Sun criminal syndicate during the reign of the Galactic Empire. Adorned with a yellow Black Sun logo on the front, the ship was owned by the Black Sun boss Lord Gyuti. The ship had a large hangar that could accommodate many ships. There was a spacious room aboard that included a table and chairs that Lord Gyuti could use to receive guests. Droid attendants would assist with such visits.

## Specifications

- **Owners:** Gyuti
