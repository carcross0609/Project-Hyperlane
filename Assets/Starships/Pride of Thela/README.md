# Pride of Thela

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pride of Thela](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pride_of_Thela)

The Pride of Thela was a Space Rescue Corps ship, flown by Uldir Lochett until his partner, the Peace Brigade agent Dacholder, tried to betray him to the Yuuzhan Vong.

- **Name:** Pride of Thela
