# Blastboat

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blastboat/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blastboat/Legends)

A blastboat was a small combat-capable starship. It filled the gap between the starfighter, and more heavily-armed capital ships. Blastboats were fast enough to intercept intruders, but strong enough to engage and survive well-armed and armored opponents. Blastboats could operate in both realspace and atmosphere.
