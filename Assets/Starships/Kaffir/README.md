# Kaffir

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kaffir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaffir)

Kaffir were a pair of stormtrooper transports that were part of the Rebel Alliance's Operation Grain Snatch. Their part was to replace Overlord Ghorin stormtrooper transports' workers with theirs who would claim that the Rebels were driven off. This was part of the plan to steal Ghorin's supply of grain for the Empire and replace it was poisoned grain.
