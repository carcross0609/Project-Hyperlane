# Unidentified Sheathipede-class transport shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Sheathipede-class transport shuttle (Grievous)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Sheathipede-class_transport_shuttle_%28Grievous%29)

During the Clone Wars, a Sheathipede-class transport shuttle served within the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, where it was utilized as the personal shuttle of the Kaleesh Separatist General Grievous, who was gifted the craft by the confederate government as a result of his high-ranking position. In 19 BBY, Grievous utilized the Sheathipede-class transport shuttle in his transportation to the planet Utapau in the war's final days.
