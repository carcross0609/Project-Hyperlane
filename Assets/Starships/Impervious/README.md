# Impervious

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Impervious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Impervious)

The Impervious was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. Lieutenant Enric Pryde of the ISD Steadfast was temporarily posted to the Star Destroyer between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, during which time he conducted inspection duty in the Hutt Space settlement Zee-Nine City Seven. The city's Imperial garrison was understaffed and underfunded, making it a prime target for the Rebel Alliance. After the Alliance launched an attack on the garrison, Pryde witnessed several explosions on the Impervious hull before watching the Star Destroyer crash into nearby mountains, destroying it.
