# ST-45

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ST-45](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ST-45)

ST-45 was a Star Commuter 2000 transport shuttle that ferried passengers between the neighboring Outer Rim planets of Lothal and Garel. The shuttle was piloted by RX-24, a RX-Series pilot droid. The rebels who operated on Lothal traveled from Lothal to Garel aboard the shuttle during their mission to steal T-7 ion disruptor rifles from the Galactic Empire.
