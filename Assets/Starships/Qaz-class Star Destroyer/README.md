# qaz-class Star Destroyer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Qaz-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qaz-class_Star_Destroyer)

The qaz-class Star Destroyer was a type of Star Destroyer used by the Imperial Navy since the formation of the Galactic Empire. One ship of this class was the Malevolence, which served during the Galactic Civil War. Shortly after the rise of the Empire, the test-firing of an Imperial kyber-crystal&ndash;powered weapon related to the development of the first Death Star had the destructive power of the combined batteries of a qaz-class Star Destroyer.
