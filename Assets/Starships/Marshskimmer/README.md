# Marshskimmer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Marshskimmer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marshskimmer)

Marshskimmer was a freighter used by Morka and Dacna during the High Republic Era. In 228 BBY, the freighter fell under attack by Nihil Cloudships, only to be saved by Jedi Masters Keeve Trennis and Sskeer, who destroyed the raiders' starships in their Jedi Vectors.
