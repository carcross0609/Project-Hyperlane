# Riot Mar's starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Riot Mar's starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Riot_Mar%27s_starfighter)

Riot Mar's starfighter was a customized Helix J-104 starfighter used by the bounty hunter Riot Mar. Riot Mar's starfighter was destroyed by the Mandalorian Din Djarin's gunship the Razor Crest, during a fight between the two in the Tatoo system, in 9 ABY.
