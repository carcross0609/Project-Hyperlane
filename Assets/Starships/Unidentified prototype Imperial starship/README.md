# Unidentified prototype Imperial starship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified prototype Imperial starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_prototype_Imperial_starship)

A prototype Imperial starship was accidentally scrapped on the junk moons of Axxila during the Cold War. Moff Vel of the Sith Empire hired scavengers to recover the starship, and launched an investigation afterwards.
