# Unidentified Gage-class transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Gage-class transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Gage-class_transport)

A Gage-class transport was active during the Great Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Its captain had been disgraced, so as a last act of redemption he rammed his ship into a Republic battle station.
