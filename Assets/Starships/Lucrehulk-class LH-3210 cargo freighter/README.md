# Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucrehulk-class_LH-3210_cargo_freighter)

The Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter, also known as a Profiteer-class freighter, the Lucrehulk-class transport, or the Lucrehulk bulk freighter, was a starship utilized by the Trade Federation to haul freight. By the time of the Clone Wars, the ships had been used for decades. Many of these starships were secretly modified into Lucrehulk-class battleships, which saw extensive use throughout the Clone Wars. A single Lucrehulk could have enough resources to conquer a planet. These ships were used during the blockade and Invasion of Naboo, the First Battle of Geonosis, and the Battle of Coruscant. The Lucrehulk-class vessel Primacy crashed into the planet Koboh.
