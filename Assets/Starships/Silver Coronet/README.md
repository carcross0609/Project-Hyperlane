# Silver Coronet

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Silver Coronet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silver_Coronet)

The Silver Coronet was a New Republic MC75 Star Cruiser that served as the flagship of Anvil Squadron during the Galactic Civil War. It was destroyed during a scouring of Mon Cala when the Silver Coronet arrived as reinforcements against the attacking forces of the Galactic Empire, which included Titan Squadron and the Star Destroyer Overseer.
