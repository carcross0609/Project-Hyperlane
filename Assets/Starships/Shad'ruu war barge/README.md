# Shad'ruu war barge

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shad'ruu war barge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shad%27ruu_war_barge)

The Shad'ruu war barge was a type of warship employed by the Hutt Clan during the Galactic Civil War. Jabba Desilijic Tiure had one such vessel under his command and used it to travel to Jekara to investigate the newly-reformed Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shad'ruu war barge
- **Type:** Warship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Hutt Clan
  -   Jabba Desilijic Tiure's criminal empire
