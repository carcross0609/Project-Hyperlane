# Drill freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Drill freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drill_freighter)

Drill freighters were a type of freighter used by the Galactic Republic in the High Republic Era. In 232 BBY, an Emergence&mdash;a fragment of the freighter Legacy Run&mdash;of the Great Hyperspace Disaster directly struck the Trymant system moon Praknat 3, throwing off the system's gravitational balance and tilting the planet Trymant IV toward the system's second sun. As a result, Trymant IV's aboveground water sources were instantly vaporized.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Drill freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Role:** Underground exploration
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
