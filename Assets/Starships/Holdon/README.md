# Holdon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Holdon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holdon)

Holdon was an Assassin-class corvette. It arrived at a TIE/D Defender staging area shortly after all of the starfighters in the area were destroyed by a pair of Imperial Missile Boats. The operation was later used as a training exercise for Missile Boat pilots.
