# Delphine

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Delphine (cargo ferry)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delphine_%28cargo_ferry%29)

Delphine was a cargo ferry used by Dimok forces during the Sepan Civil War. Delphine aided other Dimok starships in raiding a Ripoblus supply convoy in the beginning of the Imperial Sepan campaign. Intending to drive off the Dimok raiders, Imperial TIE starfighters destroyed the Cargo Ferry Mangonel, among numerous Y-wings, forcing Delphine to retreat to hyperspace.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Delphine
- **Model:** Cargo ferry
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Dimok government
