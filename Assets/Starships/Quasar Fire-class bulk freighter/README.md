# Quasar Fire-class bulk freighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quasar Fire-class bulk freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quasar_Fire-class_bulk_freighter)

The Quasar Fire-class bulk freighter was a freighter manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. The Rebels purchased several freighters at auction and then transported them to hidden Rebel shipyards, where they were dismantled and reconstructed as starfighter carriers over a span of several months and renamed as Quasar Fire-class bulk cruiser.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Quasar Fire-class bulk freighter
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** Quasar Fire-class
- **Type:** Freighter
