# Nashtah Pup

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nashtah Pup](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nashtah_Pup)

Nashtah Pup was a Z-95-AF4 Headhunter owned by the Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk. At some point, the starfighter was used by Bossk to escape an explosion aboard his YV-666 light freighter Hound's Tooth. Bossk usually kept the Nashtah Pup stored on the Hound's Tooth upper deck, the entire upper hold of which had been converted to house the Headhunter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nashtah Pup
- **Model:** Z-95-AF4 Headhunter
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (2)
- **Owners:** Bossk
