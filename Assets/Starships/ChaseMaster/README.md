# ChaseMaster

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ChaseMaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ChaseMaster)

ChaseMasters were frigates produced by Corporate Sector Authority and used as capital ships in the Lost Tribe of Sith's armada around 43 ABY. The Black Wave was one such ChaseMaster; it was commanded, along with ten other ChaseMaster frigates, by High Lord Sarasu Taalon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ChaseMaster
- **Manufacturer:** Corporate Sector Authority
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Role:** Capital ship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Lost Tribe of Sith
  -   Sith armada
