# Sharmok-class troop transport

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sharmok-class troop transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sharmok-class_troop_transport)

The Sharmok-class troop transport, was a vessel designed and used sometime before 44 ABY. It was used during the evacuation of the Jedi from the Jedi Academy on the planet Ossus by the New Jedi Order and Hapes Consortium. Several were stolen by the Lost Tribe of Sith in an attempt to assassinate Queen Mother Tenel Ka Djo and Chume'da Allana Solo with Baradium. However, the explosion went off early, and instead destroyed Volgh Squadron.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sharmok-class troop transport
- **Capacity:** Hundreds
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:**
  - New Jedi Order
  - Hapes Consortium
