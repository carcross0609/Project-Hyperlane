# V-21.1 Chevlex light haulcraft

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V-21.1 Chevlex light haulcraft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-21.1_Chevlex_light_haulcraft)

The V-21.1 Chevlex light haulcraft, more commonly known as the Fondor Haulcraft after its manufacturer, was a class of starship used for transport manufactured by Fondor Yards Commercial Ventures used during the Imperial Era. Luthen Rael used a modified version of the haulcraft that was also equipped with a Fondor Droid Mod.

- **Name:** V-21.1 Chevlex light haulcraft
