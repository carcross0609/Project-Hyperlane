# CR25 troop carrier

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CR25 troop carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR25_troop_carrier)

The CR25 troop carrier was a troop transport utilized by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was able to carry newly constructed vehicles from their factories and ferry them to a nearby landing pad for deployment. The Tatooine rebel cell under the command of Colonel Jennica Pierce, Saponza, and Saponza's partner utilized CR25 troop carriers during the Battle for Tatooine.
