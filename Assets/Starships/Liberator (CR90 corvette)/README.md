# Liberator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Liberator (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liberator_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Liberator, serving temporarily as Phoenix Home, was a CR90 corvette in the service of the Phoenix Cell in the years prior to the Battle of Yavin. She served as the flagship of the Phoenix rebels following the destruction of the original command ship Phoenix Home during an attack by Darth Vader. Unfortunately, she had a tendency to run into trouble due to this status, and twice only escaped capture thanks to the Spectres. She would later be succeeded as Phoenix Home by a stolen Imperial carrier, Phoenix Nest. She became one of the first ships to join the newly-formed Alliance to Restore the Republic and ultimately one of three cruisers to survive the disastrous Battle of Atollon.
