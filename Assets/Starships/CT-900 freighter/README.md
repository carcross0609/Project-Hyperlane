# CT-900 freighter

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [CT-900 freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CT-900_freighter)

A CT-900 freighter was a model of freighter, that has a distinctive hammerhead. One known CT-900 freighter was the StarScavenger, the starship and transport of the Freemakers, a family of scavengers who lived during the Galactic Civil War. The Mon Calamari starship builder Quarrie regarded the CT-900 as an antique ship that had not been seen since the Clone Wars. Zander Freemaker managed to keep the StarScavenger operational by modifying the ship with spare parts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CT-900 freighter
- **Model:** From sometime before the Clone Wars
- **Type:** Freighter
