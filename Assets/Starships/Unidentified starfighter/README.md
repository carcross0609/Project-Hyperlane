# Unidentified starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_starfighter)

During the war between the First Order and the Resistance, Commander Poe Dameron borrowed a starfighter from Grakkus the Hutt to aid the members of Black Squadron during the Battle of Ikkrukk. The starfighter was equipped with four laser cannons, a hyperdrive and six engines.
