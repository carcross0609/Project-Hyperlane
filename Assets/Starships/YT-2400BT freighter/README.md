# YT-2400BT freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YT-2400BT freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YT-2400BT_freighter)

The YT-2400BT freighter was a variant of the YT-2400 light freighter. At least one such freighter flew with the Citizens' Fleet during the Battle of Exegol against the Sith Eternal forces.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YT-2400BT freighter
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Type:** Freighter
