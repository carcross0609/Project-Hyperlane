# Falx

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Falx](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Falx)

The Falx was a Corellian Engineering Corporation Assassin-class corvette that was owned and operated by Dimoks during the Sepan Civil War. Falx aided other Dimok starships in raiding a Ripoblus Modular Conveyors carrying munitions supply convoy in the beginning of the Imperial Sepan campaign.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Falx
- **Type:** CR92a Assassin-class corvette
- **Affiliation:** Dimok government
