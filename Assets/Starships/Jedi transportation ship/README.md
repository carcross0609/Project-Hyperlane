# Jedi transportation ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jedi transportation ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_transportation_ship)

The Jedi transportation ship was a type of shuttle used by the Jedi Order. In the aftermath of the Great Hyperspace Disaster in 232 BBY, Jedi Knight Lily Tora-Asi and Jedi Master Arkoff used a Jedi transportation ship to help transport refugees to the Outer Rim planet Banchii. Later, Tora-Asi and the younglings Nima Allets and Viv'Nia Nia'Viv used the ship to travel to and from the Starlight Beacon space station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jedi transportation ship
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Role:** Transportation
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
