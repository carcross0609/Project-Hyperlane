# Lodestar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lodestar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lodestar)

The Lodestar was an Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship that served as the flagship of New Republic General Hera Syndulla's Barma Battle Group during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lodestar
- **Model:** Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Complement:**
  - X-wing starfighters
  - BTL Y-wing starfighters
  - 1 A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter
  - 1 RZ-1 A-wing interceptor
  - 1 UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Destroyed:** c. 5 ABY, Cerberon system
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - New Republic
- **Navy:**
  - Galactic Republic Navy
  - New Republic Defense Fleet
- **Fleet:** Barma Battle Group
- **Captains:**
  - Hera Syndulla
  - Giginivek
