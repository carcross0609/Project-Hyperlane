# Unidentified refugee transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified refugee transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_refugee_transport)

A transport carrying refugees from the Mandalorian Wars, a conflict between the Galactic Republic and the Mandalorian warrior culture, crash-landed on the planet Makeb. The refugees, among them a number of wealthy businessmen, colonized the planet when they were unable to escape Makeb's unique atmosphere.
