# Bogey One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bogey One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogey_One)

Bogey One was the name given to a warship of the Grysk Hegemony by the crew of the Imperial Star Destroyer Chimaera. It was nearly as big as the Star Destroyer. Along with Bogeys Two, Three and Four Bogey One joined a battle against Chimaera, during which it was attacked by the Star Destroyer itself.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bogey One
- **Armament:** Weapons emplacements
- **Role:** Warship
