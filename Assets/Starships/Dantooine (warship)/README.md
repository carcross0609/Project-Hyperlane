# Dantooine (warship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dantooine (warship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dantooine_%28warship%29)

The Dantooine was a warship in the Republic Navy that was under Fleet Admiral Oteg's command at Taral V in the early days of the Galactic War. It was destroyed at the Battle of Taral V. Like the Serroco and the Telos, it was named after Dantooine, a planet devastated during the Jedi Civil War.
