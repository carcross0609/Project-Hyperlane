# Abrogator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Abrogator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abrogator)

Abrogator was an Imperial Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser that acted as the flagship of the fleet sent to Saloch 2 to quell a group of Lortans during the Reslian Purge.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Abrogator
- **Model:** Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser
- **Complement:** 6/37th
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Captain Lun Tessra
