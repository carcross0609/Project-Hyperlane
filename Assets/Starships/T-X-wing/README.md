# T-X-wing

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [T-X-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-X-wing)

The T-X-wing was a type of X-wing starfighter used by the T-Empire. Like most starfighters used by the Empire, they were salvaged and repaired from ships used during the Galactic Civil War. The former Dark Lord Karre used a T-X-wing to escape the Empire after betraying it to save his twin sister, Am.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T-X-wing
- **Type:** X-wing starfighter
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** T-Empire
