# Revelator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Revelator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revelator)

The Revelator was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. It was destroyed during the Battle of the Revelator in 3 ABY. It served as the flagship of Imperial Security Bureau Zerek Besh division Director Sliro Barsha, who perished during the battle alongside his brother, Jaylen Vrax. The Revelator was destroyed by the combined efforts of the Alliance to Restore the Republic, the Kay Vess' crew and a crime syndicate with whom Vess had a good relationship. The Empire positioned weapons near the wreckage of the Revelator to make it difficult for anyone to approach, possibly to prevent the acquisition of valuable items from the wreckage.
