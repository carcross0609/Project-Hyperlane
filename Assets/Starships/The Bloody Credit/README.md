# The Bloody Credit

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [The Bloody Credit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Bloody_Credit)

The Bloody Credit was a GR-75 medium transport that was used as a slave ship during the early years of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Bloody Credit
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Crew:** 18
