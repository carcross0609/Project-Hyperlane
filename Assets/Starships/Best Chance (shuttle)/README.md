# Best Chance (shuttle)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Best Chance (shuttle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Best_Chance_%28shuttle%29)

Best Chance was a New Republic shuttle in operation during the Yuuzhan Vong War. In 25 ABY, it ferried a commando team of six Noghri and three Jedi: Jacen Solo, Corran Horn, and Ganner Rhysode, hidden within the decoy freighter Lost Hope, from Ralroost to the surface of Garqi.
