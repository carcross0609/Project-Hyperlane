# Red Five

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Five_%28T-65B_X-wing_starfighter%29)

During the Galactic Civil War, several X-wing starfighters used by Rebel pilots bore the name Red Five and the designation AA-589. Cadet Pedrin Gaul operated an X-wing dubbed Red Five in the Battle of Scarif and also used the corresponding callsign, but he was shot down and killed by TIE/ln space superiority starfighters. However, the X-wing's designation became most famous for its use by Luke Skywalker, who first flew under the callsign in the Battle of Yavin. After that victory, the name "Red Five" became synonymous with the X-wing fighter itself that Skywalker flew.
