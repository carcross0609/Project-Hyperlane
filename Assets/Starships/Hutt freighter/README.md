# Hutt freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hutt freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt_freighter)

The Hutt freighter was a type of freighter associated with the Hutt species. Circa 3&ndash;4 ABY, a reprogrammed bounty droid 4-LOM laid a trap for his former partner, Zuckuss, and the latter's companions in a derelict Hutt freighter at the Wreck Belt orbiting Lotho Minor.
