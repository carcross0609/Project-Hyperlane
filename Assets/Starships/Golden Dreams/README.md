# Golden Dreams

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Golden Dreams](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Dreams)

Golden Dreams was a modified SoroSuub medium transport in Drea Renthal's pirate fleet. The ship was fairly large, but lightly shielded and poorly armed. Its role was to carry some of Renthal's Z-95 Headhunters into battle. Golden Dreams would launch the fighters, then fall back to a safe distance while they and the dedicated warships of Renthal's fleet did the fighting.
