# Umgullian transport ship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Umgullian transport ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umgullian_transport_ship)

The Umgullian transport ship was a type of transport used in the galaxy. In 3 ABY, a Umgullian transport ship was illegally docked in Hanger 5 of Hauler's Keep, a space station located in the Khepi system. While it was docked, several gelatinous lifeforms escaped from the the ship. An announcement was made over the station's speaker system informing the owner of the ship that the lifeforms were loose and were interfering with the station's equipment.
