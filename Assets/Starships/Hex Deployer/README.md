# Hex Deployer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hex Deployer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hex_Deployer)

The Hex Deployer, also known as a Hex deployment vessel or a Chem Deployer, was an assault transport used by Sabaoth Squadron, and later placed under the command of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hex Deployer
- **Type:** Assault transport
- **Armament:**
  - Hex missile launchers
  - 2 laser turrets
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sabaoth Squadron
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
