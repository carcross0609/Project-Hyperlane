# Unidentified Taylander shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Taylander shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Taylander_shuttle)

A Taylander shuttle was operated by a human pilot as part of the Rebel Alliance Navy for the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. Around 3 ABY, the shuttle was boarded by the droid C-3PO, who was in the control of Scourge, who killed the human pilot and utilized the Taylander shuttle to traverse to the planet Mustafar.
