# Obedience

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Obedience](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obedience)

The Obedience, formerly known as the Defiance, was a cruiser in the service of the Galactic Empire. The cruiser had served as a training vessel for many years under the command of Commandant Pell Baylo until he attempted to destroy the vessel and assassinate Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine and Darth Vader.
