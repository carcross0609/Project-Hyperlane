# Juliupper

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Juliupper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Juliupper)

The Juliupper was a shuttle equipped with ion drives and a titanium-alloy hull. In 25 ABY, it was one of several starships that attempted to evacuate the residents of the Outer Rim Territories planet Sernpidal, which was located in the Julevian system, while the planet was destroyed by the Yuuzhan Vong species by pulling Sernpidal's moon Dobido into the planet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Juliupper
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Engine:** Ion drives
- **Hull:** Titanium-alloy
- **Destroyed:** 25 ABY, Julevian system
