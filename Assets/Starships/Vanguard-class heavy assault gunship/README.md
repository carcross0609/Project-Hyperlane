# Vanguard-class heavy assault gunship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vanguard-class heavy assault gunship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard-class_heavy_assault_gunship)

The Vanguard-class heavy assault gunship was an assault vessel manufactured by CEC during the tail end of the Republic. Though it was initially met with lukewarm reception due to logistical problems, it found better appreciation during the time of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vanguard-class heavy assault gunship
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** Vanguard-class
- **Type:** Gunboat
- **Length:** 47 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 2 fire-linked turbolasers
  - 2 fire-linked blaster cannons
  - 4 Proton torpedo launchers (8 torpedoes each)
  - 1 Directed Energy Weapon
- **Crew:** 6
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 220 kilograms
- **Consumables:** 2 weeks
- **Affiliation:**
  - planetary defense forces
  - high-profile crime syndicates
