# Unidentified Gozanti-class Assault Carrier

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Gozanti-class Assault Carrier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Gozanti-class_Assault_Carrier)

A Gozanti-class Assault Carrier was used by Gideon's Imperial remnant several years after the Battle of Endor. In 9 ABY, Bo-Katan Kryze, Axe Woves, Koska Reeves, and Din Djarin raided the Gozanti-class Assault Carrier to steal weapons and attempt to learn the location of the Darksaber. Though the cruiser's captain attempted to crash the ship into Trask's ocean, Kryze and her allies were able to save the ship, which they planned to use to liberate their homeworld of Mandalore.
