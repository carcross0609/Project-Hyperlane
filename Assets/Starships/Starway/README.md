# Starway

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starway](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starway)

.]] Starway was group of two Transports used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. Starway 1 & 2 were a part of two other groups of starships attempting to flee Calamari Cruiser Lulsla before it succumbed to wounds dealt by the Galactic Empire during the Aftermath of Hoth.
