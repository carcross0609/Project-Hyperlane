# Kuat Explorer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kuat Explorer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuat_Explorer)

The Kuat Explorer was a Human colony ship. The Explorer was the vessel that colonized the planet of Alsakan in 26,000 BBY. Members of the Hirken family were aboard, making them among the oldest families on Alsakan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kuat Explorer
- **Role:** Scout vessel
