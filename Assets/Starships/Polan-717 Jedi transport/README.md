# Polan-717 Jedi transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Polan-717 Jedi transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Polan-717_Jedi_transport)

The Polan-717 Jedi transport, also simply known as the Polan, was a type of shuttle utilized by the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. The shuttle was manufactured by Lantillian ShipWrights. It was very versatile for both everyday and long-distance missions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Polan-717 Jedi transport
- **Manufacturer:** Lantillian Shipwrights
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Length:** 78.56 meters (257 ft 8 in)
- **Width:** 58.14 meters (190 ft 9 in)
- **Height:** 20.45 meters (67 ft 1 in)
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hyperdrive:** External hyperdrive
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
