# Innovator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Innovator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Innovator)

The Innovator was an Elite-class research vessel designed by the engineer Vam Targes and constructed by the insectoid Cyclorrians at the shipyards over the planet Cyclor during the High Republic Era.
