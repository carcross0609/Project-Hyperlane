# Radiance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Radiance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Radiance)

The Radiance was a passenger liner that ferried beings down to Coruscant's surface after they arrived in-system. When Han Solo and Bria Tharen visited the planet in 10 BBY, they were shuttled planetside aboard this ship.
