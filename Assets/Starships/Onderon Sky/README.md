# Onderon Sky

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Onderon Sky](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Onderon_Sky)

Onderon Sky was a battered and old YT-1300 light freighter. Admiral Gilder Varth offered to give it members of an Alderaanian Resistance team for a mission they were going on to Coruscant in about 17 BBY, if they didn't have their own ship.
