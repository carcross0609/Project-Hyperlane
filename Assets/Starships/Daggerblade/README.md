# Daggerblade

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Daggerblade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daggerblade)

Daggerblade was an Imperial system patrol craft. The Rebel Alliance tentatively identified it as the ship that engaged the CR90 corvette Old Republic shortly after the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Daggerblade
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Model:** IPV-1 System Patrol Craft
- **Length:** 150 meters
- **Shield Gen:** 400 SBD
- **Hull:** 285 RU
- **Armament:**
  - Taim & Bak XV7 turbolasers (4)
  - Krupx VL-4a warhead launcher (1)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
