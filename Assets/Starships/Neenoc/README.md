# Neenoc

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Neenoc](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neenoc)

The Neenoc was a YT-1300 transport that was present at XQ3 Platform AZ 77 when the platform was attacked by Imperial forces lead by the frigate, Implacable.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Neenoc
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Role:**
  - Freighter
  - Smuggling ship
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
