# Scorpion (CR90 corvette)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scorpion (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scorpion_%28CR90_corvette%29)

Scorpion was a CR90 corvette in service with the Imperial Navy. The corvette, along with the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Boltthrower, attempted to prevent the Rebel Alliance from boarding the BFF-1 bulk freighter Libom and stealing new surveillance probes for the Relentless. However, the Scorpion was destroyed by Keyan Farlander while flying a B-wing and the Boltthrower attempts to stop the Rebels failed.
