# Red 6

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red 6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_6)

Red 6 was a T-65 X-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was assigned to Red Squadron and stationed at the Rebel base on Yavin 4. Red 6 was piloted by Jek Porkins with R5-D8 as the craft's astromech droid. Prior to the Battle of Yavin, the craft skipped its most recent inspection of its computer and flight control systems. During the Battle of Yavin, Porkins made several successful strafing attacks on the Death Star with Red 6.
