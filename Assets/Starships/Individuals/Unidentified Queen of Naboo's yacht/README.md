# Unidentified Queen of Naboo's yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Queen of Naboo's yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Queen_of_Naboo%27s_yacht)

By 382 BBY, during the High Republic Era, a yacht was owned by a female individual who served as queen of the Mid Rim planet Naboo. That year, Axel Greylark, the son of Galactic Republic Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark, mentally considered buying the yacht from the queen after his mother told him that, in exchange for reporting back to her about the proceedings of a summit between the warring Outer Rim twin planets Eiram and E'ronoh, she would give him his inheritance. Axel ultimately did not receive his inheritance, as he was arrested and taken into Republic custody for conspiracy, murder, and terrorism.
