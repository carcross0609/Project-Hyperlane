# Mercurial

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mercurial](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mercurial)

The Mercurial was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer in service to the navy of the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. Imperial pilot Gareth Agamar served as the Star Destroyer's aerospace group commander for a long period prior to taking over the 481st Experimental Starfighter Testing Squadron. As an Imperial-class Star Destroyer, Mercurial had a length of 1,600 meters and was manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards.
