# Triumph

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Triumph (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Triumph_%28corvette_group%29)

Triumph was a group of two CR90 corvettes belonging to the Rebel Alliance. They were sent to try and destroy the Immobilizer 418 cruiser Harpax along with three B-wing starfighters and two Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttles. However, all of the Rebel forces were destroyed by Imperial Ace Maarek Stele.
