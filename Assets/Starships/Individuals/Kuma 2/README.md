# Kuma 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kuma 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuma_2)

Kuma 2 was a Nebulon-B2 frigate used by the Zaarin coup forces. It was present at the Battle of Gwar in 4 ABY.
