# Tallie Lintra's RZ-2 A-wing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tallie Lintra's RZ-2 A-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tallie_Lintra%27s_RZ-2_A-wing)

Tallie Lintra's RZ-2 A-wing was a blue RZ-2 A-wing interceptor piloted by Lieutenant Tallissan Lintra of the Resistance during its war with the First Order. It was destroyed when Kylo Ren attacked and destroyed other starfighters in the Raddus during the Battle of Oetchi
