# Ultimo Vista

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ultimo Vista](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimo_Vista)

Ultimo Vista was a Giga-class transport originally established as a leisure cruise ship, but which over the decades became an almost entirely self-sufficient world for its passengers-turned-residents, and circled the skylanes of Coruscant during the last decades of the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ultimo Vista
- **Model:** Giga-class transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Passengers:** Several thousand
- **Role:** Autonomous luxury craft
