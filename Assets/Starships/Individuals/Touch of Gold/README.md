# Touch of Gold

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Touch of Gold](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Touch_of_Gold)

Touch of Gold was a starship that was operated by the Zeltron Black Sun agent Donari Caron during the early years of the Galactic Empire. In about 18 BBY, Caron docked the vessel in Westport on the planet Coruscant and she brokered a deal with the Human black market trader Acer Ash while aboard the ship, to exchange a painting she had recovered for an experimental energy shield that Ash possessed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Touch of Gold
- **Affiliation:** Black Sun
- **Owners:** Captain Donari Caron
