# Regal Zephyr

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Regal Zephyr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Regal_Zephyr)

The Regal Zephyr was a passenger transport operated by Regal Voyager along the Ootmian Pabol hyperspace route during the Republic Era. The transport was the site of an attempted hijacking by a group of criminals&mdash;The Lobber, Ghor, and Wungo&mdash;but the Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn and his Padawan, Obi-Wan Kenobi, who had been traveling aboard the Regal Zephyr to Coruscant, intervened.
