# Cularin 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cularin 1 (Carrack-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cularin_1_%28Carrack-class%29)

Cularin 1 was a Carrack-class light cruiser that was used by the Cularin Militia in the Cularin system during the Clone Wars. The Heroes of Cularin once met Major Xirossk on it and he sent them on a mission to Uffel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cularin 1
- **Manufacturer:** Damorian Manufacturing Corporation
- **Model:** Carrack-class light cruiser
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Length:** 350 meters
- **Width:** 72 meters
- **Height:** 75 meters
- **Affiliation:** Cularin Militia
- **Captains:** Major Xirossk
