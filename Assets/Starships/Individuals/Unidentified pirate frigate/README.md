# Unidentified pirate frigate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified pirate frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pirate_frigate)

A pirate frigate was frigate utilized by a pirate group. During a battle in the Mirrin sector, Rapier Leader Poe Dameron was captured and interrogated on the frigate by the pirates. His droid, BB-8 sent their coordinates to the rest of Rapier Squadron who attacked the frigate and came to their rescue.
