# Blackmist

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blackmist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blackmist)

The Blackmist was a Corellian corvette that was owned by the Hutt criminal Noako during the reign of the Galactic Empire. The Blackmist was Noako's best smuggling ship and it was destroyed after Noako's rival Sinasu had the vessel crash into Noako's own landing pad. In retaliation, agents of Noako torched Sinasu's docking facility Greentop Tower, on the moon Nar Shaddaa.
