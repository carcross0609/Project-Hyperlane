# Truncheon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Truncheon (Lambda-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Truncheon_%28Lambda-class%29)

Truncheon was a Lambda-class shuttle in the complement of the Imperial Star Destroyer Ultimatum, under the temporary command of Imperial captain Rae Sloane in 11 BBY. It was used by Count Denetrius Vidian during his inspection tour of the planet Gorse. Sloane and Vidian took Truncheon on their tour of the Moonglow Polychemical facility. However, the shuttle was blown up by the dissident Skelly, who sought revenge after Vidian inflicted severe injuries on him after he attempted to warn Vidian of the dangers thorilide mining was posing to the structure of Gorse's moon Cynda. Vidian and Sloane were then forced to journey to the Imperial spaceport in Highground to take a new shuttle.
