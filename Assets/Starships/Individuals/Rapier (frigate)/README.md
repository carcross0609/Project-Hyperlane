# Rapier (frigate)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rapier (frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rapier_%28frigate%29)

The Imperial Nebulon-B frigate Rapier failed to destroy the Alliance Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Stimsenj'kat which was having cargo transferred from Transport Group Renhoek (made up of at least six Delta-class DX-9 stormtrooper transports). Keyan Farlander and a few other rebels destroyed this frigate during the operation.
