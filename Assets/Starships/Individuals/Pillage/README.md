# Pillage

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pillage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pillage)

The Pillage was a starship owned by Larken. The vessel was used alongside fellow pirate ships Trust Me and Profit during an attempt to capture the Helix-class light interceptor Kizbon's Box. The operation, which took place at Darknon Station under cover of a magnetic storm, failed when the Kizbon's Box revealed hidden armaments that destroyed Trust Me and Profit.
