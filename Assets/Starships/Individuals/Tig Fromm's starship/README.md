# Tig Fromm's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tig Fromm's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tig_Fromm%27s_starship)

The Annoo-dat gangster Tig Fromm, one of the leaders of the Fromm Gang criminal organization, used a starship as his personal space transport. The triangular-shaped starship was heavily armed and saw use during the early years of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tig Fromm's starship
- **Engine:** One large main engine
- **Armament:**
  - Heavy
  -   Powerful laser
- **Affiliation:** Fromm Gang
- **Owners:** Tig Fromm
