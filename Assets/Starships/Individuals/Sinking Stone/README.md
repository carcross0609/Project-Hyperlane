# Sinking Stone

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sinking Stone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sinking_Stone)

The Sinking Stone was a stolen Imperial vessel. It was used by Soron Hegerty during the Galactic Civil War as a mobile base for her attempts to help the Ssither of Jatee.

- **Name:** Sinking Stone
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Model:** Stellar Mag V Mining Vessel

- **Length:** 542 meters
- **Max Speed:** 850 KMH
- **Hyperdrive:** None

- **Armament:** Turbolaser battery (1)
- **Crew:** 3, 19 droids, 3 gunners
- **Passengers:** 10

- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Role:** Mining vessel
- **Owners:** Soron Hegerty
- **Captains:** Soron Hegerty
