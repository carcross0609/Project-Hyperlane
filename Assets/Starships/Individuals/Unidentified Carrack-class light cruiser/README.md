# Unidentified Carrack-class light cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Carrack-class light cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Carrack-class_light_cruiser)

In 8 ABY, Moff Kentor Sarne deployed a Carrack-class light cruiser to Tanquilla Beach. The captain's mission was to strike at the corvette FarStar and then retreat into hyperspace. Unfortunately, Sarne was unaware of the extent of Tanquilla Beach armaments, and the Carrack was destroyed when faced with the Beach defenses and the FarStar.
