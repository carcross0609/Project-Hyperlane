# Talzin's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Talzin's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talzin%27s_shuttle)

A mysterious shuttle was used by the Nightsister Mother Talzin to travel to the desert moon Zardossa Stix during the Clone Wars. Later during the war, Talzin sent Nightbrothers aboard the transport to the planet Ord Mantell, with instructions to aid her son, Maul, in the coming battle against the Separatists.
