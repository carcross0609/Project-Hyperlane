# Chip (CR90 corvette)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chip (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chip_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Chip was a CR90 corvette under the service of the Rebel Alliance. The Chip along with the Corvette Group Yogi and an RZ-1 A-wing interceptor destroyed the BFF-1 bulk freighter group Sornsen and the Corvette group Shurik.
