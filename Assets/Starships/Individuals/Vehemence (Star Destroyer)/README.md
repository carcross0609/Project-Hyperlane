# Vehemence

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vehemence (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vehemence_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

The Vehemence was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer of the Galactic Empire. During the Galactic Civil War, the Vehemence was attacked by Rebel Alliance forces, sustaining ion cannon fire to its bridge shields.
