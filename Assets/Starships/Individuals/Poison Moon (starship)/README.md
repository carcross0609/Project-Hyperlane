# Poison Moon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Poison Moon (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poison_Moon_%28starship%29)

The Poison Moon was an Interceptor-class frigate and mercenary ship who worked with members of the One Sith. During a mission to capture or kill Alema Rar, the Poison Moon was commanded by Dician, a member of One Sith. The Poison Moon was piloted by an old mercenary and smuggler Wayniss and Ithila was the sensor officer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Poison Moon
- **Type:** Interceptor-class frigate
- **Length:** 150 meters
- **Affiliation:** One Sith
