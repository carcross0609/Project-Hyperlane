# Carida

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Carida (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carida_%28starship%29)

The Carida was a Galactic Republic starship active during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY, it was the target of an attack by Sith forces. Jedi Knight Laranna Fain defended the ship but was wounded in the process. She was healed by the Sith Lord Vivicar posing as a Jedi Master, but was also infected with the Dark Plague. She was rescued by a Jedi Consular.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Carida
- **Model:** Ajuur-class heavy cruiser
- **Role:** Patrol vessel
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Navy:** Republic Navy
