# Extempus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Extempus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Extempus)

The Extempus was a Delta-class carrier that served in the Imperial Navy as the flagship of Moff Nezzor during the Galactic War between the Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic. The Extempus participated in an attack on the agriworld Ruan in 3640 BBY, but was destroyed in a subsequent attack on Duro when Nezzor was caught in an ambush.

- **Name:** Extempus
- **Model:** Delta-class carrier

- **Othersystems:**

- **Affiliation:** Imperial Navy
