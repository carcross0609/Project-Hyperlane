# Unidentified Republic medical frigate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Republic medical frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Republic_medical_frigate)

A medical frigate was part of a Republic rescue fleet sent to the planet Trymant IV during the High Republic Era. The frigate was attacked by the Nihil ship Squall Spider, allowing the Nihil to escape from the Trymant system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Republic medical frigate
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Fleet:** Republic rescue fleet
