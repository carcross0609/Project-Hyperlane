# Sarapin Wanderer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sarapin Wanderer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarapin_Wanderer)

The Sarapin Wanderer was a starship in the service of the Republic Navy during the Great Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The Sarapin Wanderer was traveling with several other Republic vessels when it was attacked by the Imperial Navy, and the vessel's captain was killed. However, the Jedi Master Oteg was aboard the Wanderer, and he took command of the vessel, organizing a counterattack that drove the Imperial attackers into a nearby nebula. The Imperials attempted to return fire, but they were destroyed when their own weapons detonated the gases of the nebula.
