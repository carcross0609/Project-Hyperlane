# Moldy Crow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moldy Crow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moldy_Crow)

Moldy Crow was a HWK-290 light freighter, a model of light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. Valued at 5,000 credits, the freighter was armed with an anti-personnel blaster turret and was connected with the Alliance to Restore the Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Moldy Crow
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** HWK-290 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Cost:** 5,000 credits
- **Armament:** Anti-personnel blaster turret
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
