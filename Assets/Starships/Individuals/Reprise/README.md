# Reprise

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reprise](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reprise)

Reprise was a Guardian-class light cruiser owned by the Tapani Noble House Pelagia. During the Galactic Civil War period it came to the rescue of the Bright Seeker that was being attacked by the Knife's Edge when attempting to recover the hibernating brother and sister of the Pelagian High Lord Theus Paddox.

- **Type:** Guardian-class light cruiser
