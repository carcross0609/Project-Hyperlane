# Resplendent Pearl

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Resplendent Pearl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resplendent_Pearl)

The Resplendent Pearl was a yacht owned by Xylan Graf. He traveled to Everbloom, the family's space station estate, accompanied by his bodyguard Basha, the pilot Sylvestri Yarrow, Jordanna Sparkburn, and the Jedi Cohmac Vitus, Vernestra Rwoh, Imri Cantaros, and Reath Silas. Upon the Pearl arrival, an ambush was set without Xylan's knowledge to kidnap Sylvestri and the two Padawans, as part of arrangements Catriona Graf had made with the Nihil and the scientist Chancey Yarrow.
