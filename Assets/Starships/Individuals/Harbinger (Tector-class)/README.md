# Harbinger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Harbinger (Tector-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harbinger_%28Tector-class%29)

Harbinger was a Star Destroyer of the Tector-class in service to the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. In 4 ABY, it was the sole Tector-class in the Imperial fleet defending the DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station over the Forest Moon of Endor. After Emperor Palpatine lured the Rebel Alliance into assaulting the seemingly incomplete battle station, the Imperial fleet trapped them over the moon.
