# Acceptance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Acceptance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acceptance)

The Acceptance was a Judicial Forces Consular-class cruiser piloted by Lieutenant Antidar Williams. It was one of the transports at the Battle of Galidraan, and Jedi Master Dooku's flagship. It was destroyed at the Battle of Uhanayih, though Williams and Maoi Madakor escaped.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Acceptance
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** Consular-class cruiser
- **Length:** 115 meters
- **Destroyed:** 33 BBY, over Uhanayih
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Captains:** Antidar Williams
