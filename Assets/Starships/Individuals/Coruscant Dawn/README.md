# Coruscant Dawn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Coruscant Dawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant_Dawn)

The Coruscant Dawn was a starship owned by Lina Soh, Chancellor of the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era. Soh used the vessel to travel to the Republic Fair on the planet Valo, accompanied by her subordinates, several Jedi, and the reporter Rhil Dairo. It was boarded by Nihil raiders during the Valo Atrocity, but the crew had managed to fight them off.
