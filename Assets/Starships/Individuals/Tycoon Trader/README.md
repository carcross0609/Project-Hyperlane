# Tycoon Trader

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tycoon Trader](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon_Trader)

The Tycoon Trader was a cargo cruiser piloted by the pilot droid RL-18 during the High Republic Era. RL-18 flew the Trader to transport glitterstim for Bala Waleen and the Hutt Cartel during the High Republic Era. Bala hired his former girlfriend Lourna Dee, without knowing it was her, to protect spice shipments from hijacking by Nihil marauders.
