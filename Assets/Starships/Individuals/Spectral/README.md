# Spectral

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spectral](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spectral)

The Spectral was an Interdictor-class heavy cruiser that was in service to the Galactic Empire during its reign of the galaxy. It was commanded by Admiral Ektol Traz. At some point, it crashed onto the starship graveyard on the planet Jakku after suffering damage from rebel pilot Lieutenant Bak Rychuk.
