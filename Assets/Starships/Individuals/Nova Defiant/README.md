# Nova Defiant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nova Defiant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nova_Defiant)

The Nova Defiant was a Recusant-class light destroyer that served in the naval forces of the Separatist Alliance during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic. The destroyer was equipped with several thrusters, as well as multiple electromagnetic sensor antennas, heavy turbolaser cannons, and a port-mounted neutrino radiator grille. At some point during the war, the Nova Defiant was located in orbit of a green celestial body, while it also remained unpainted of the Separatist Alliance's symbol and blue paint, which was commonly featured on Separatist warships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nova Defiant
