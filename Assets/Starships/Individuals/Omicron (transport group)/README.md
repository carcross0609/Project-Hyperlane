# Omicron (transport group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Omicron (transport group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omicron_%28transport_group%29)

Omicron 1 & 2 were a pair of Rebel Alliance Beta-class ETR-3 escort transports colluding with the Airam pirates at a Goff Platform in the Goff system. Omicron 1 was crewed by regular Alliance personnel, while Omicron 2 carried officers.
