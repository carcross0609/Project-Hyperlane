# Wandering P'orp

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wandering P'orp](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wandering_P%27orp)

The Wandering P'orp was a freighter used by a team of scavengers led by Captain Thea. Douzler was a member of its crew. In 5 ABY, the Wandering P'orp was present on Centares when its crew salvaged durasteel out of Rubyflame Lake.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wandering P'orp
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Affiliation:** Independent scavengers
- **Captains:** Captain Thea
