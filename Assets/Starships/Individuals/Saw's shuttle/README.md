# Saw's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Saw's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saw%27s_shuttle)

Saw's shuttle was owned by the Onderonian freedom fighter Saw Gerrera, the leader of the Partisans, during the the Imperial Era. Gerrera's shuttle saw action during the Campaign at Salient, where Gerrera fought against the Galactic Empire's Naval forces.
