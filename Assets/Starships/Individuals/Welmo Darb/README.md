# Welmo Darb

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Welmo Darb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Welmo_Darb)

The Welmo Darb was a Galactic Alliance Star Destroyer assigned to the First Fleet. It served as the flagship of Admiral Nek Bwua'tu, the joint commander of the First and Sixth fleets, replacing his older flagship the Admiral Ackbar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Welmo Darb
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
- **Navy:** Galactic Alliance First Fleet
- **Captains:** Admiral Nek Bwua'tu
