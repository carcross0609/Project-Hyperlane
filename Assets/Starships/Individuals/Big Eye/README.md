# Big Eye

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Big Eye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Big_Eye)

Big Eye was a GAT-12 Skipray Blastboat converted into a sensor platform that was used by the New Jedi Order. Flown by Tenel Ka, it was used in a mission to capture a voxyn in space around the planet Froz. The mission conincided with Kyp Durron's Dozen-and-Two Avengers, who shared their shadow bomb technique.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Big Eye
- **Model:** GAT-12 Skipray Blastboat
- **Length:** 25 meters
- **Affiliation:** New Jedi Order
- **Captains:** Tenel Ka
