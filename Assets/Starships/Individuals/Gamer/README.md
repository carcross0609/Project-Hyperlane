# Gamer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gamer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamer)

Gamer was a pair of Lambda-class T-4a shuttles used by Pakuuni Pirates during the Galactic Civil War. They were used as part of an assassination attempt on Vice Admiral Thrawn at NL-1 station, but they arrived too late and were ultimately destroyed by Imperial Ace Maarek Stele.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gamer
- **Formation:** Shuttle group
- **Vehicles:** 2 Lambda-class T-4a shuttles
- **Affiliation:** Pakuuni Pirates
