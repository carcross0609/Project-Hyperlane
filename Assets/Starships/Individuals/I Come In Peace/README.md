# I Come In Peace

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [I Come In Peace](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I_Come_In_Peace)

I Come In Peace was a Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry that took part in the Second Battle of Geonosis. The nose of the gunship was customized with a painting of a clone trooper riding a missile, with the name of the vessel written in Aurebesh.
