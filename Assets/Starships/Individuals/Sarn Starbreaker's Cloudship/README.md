# Sarn Starbreaker's Cloudship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sarn Starbreaker's Cloudship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarn_Starbreaker%27s_Cloudship)

A Cloudship was utilized by the Nihil Cloud Sarn Starbreaker during the High Republic Era. During the First Battle of Cyclor, the ship was destroyed by Jedi Padawan Bell Zettifar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sarn Starbreaker's Cloudship
- **Type:** Cloudship
- **Destroyed:** 231 BBY, above Cyclor
- **Affiliation:** Nihil
- **Owners:** Sarn Starbreaker
