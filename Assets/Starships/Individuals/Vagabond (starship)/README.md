# Vagabond

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vagabond (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vagabond_%28starship%29)

Vagabond was a starship in the service of the Galactic Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. In 21 BBY, the ship was destroyed during the Battle of Cavamina Minor by a fleet of the Separatist Navy. The battle of Cavamina Minor was a trap set by Attuma Duum for his personal enjoyment and to increase war profits by prolonging the war. Both fleets eventually withdrew after suffering casualties.
