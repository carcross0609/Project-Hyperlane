# Silooth

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Silooth (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silooth_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

The Silooth was the personal Imperial-class Star Destroyer of Grand Admiral Peccati Syn. He named it after the Silooth, a Sith animal.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Silooth
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Line:** Star Destroyers
- **Model:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/LN starfighters
- **Destroyed:** 4 ABY, over Kashyyyk
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Grand Admiral Peccati Syn
