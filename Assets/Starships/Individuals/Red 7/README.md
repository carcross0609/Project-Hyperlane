# Red 7

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red 7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_7)

Red 7 was a T-65 X-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. Flown by Elyhek Rue, the fighter was assigned to the Griffon Flight Wing where it participated on a strike on the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Devastator during the subjugation of Ralltiir.
