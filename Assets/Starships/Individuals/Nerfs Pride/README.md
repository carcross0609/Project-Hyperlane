# Nerfs Pride

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nerfs Pride](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nerfs_Pride)

The Nerfs Pride was a YT-1300 light freighter owned by the Alderaanian diplomat Darmin Lerspri during the Galactic Civil War. In 1 ABY, Lerspri undertook a mission to the planet Corellia in order to convince the Corellian government to support the Rebel Alliance in the war against the Galactic Empire. However, the Nerfs Pride was attacked in the Corellian system by Imperial starfighters led by Prisk Kith'Vys. The Imperial Navy special unit Black Epsilon destroyed the ship and its escort, thereby killing the diplomat before he could land on Corellia.
