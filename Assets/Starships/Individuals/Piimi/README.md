# Piimi

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Piimi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Piimi)

The Piimi was a Strike-class medium cruiser that was used by the Rebel Alliance to defend the Sealion, a prototype of the Missile Boat that was sent to the XQ4 Platform Enin'sd by the Mon Calamari Cruiser Link. That Strike-cruiser attacked the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Garrett but was destroyed in the battle.
