# Silaban

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Silaban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silaban)

The Silaban was a CEC corvette and part of the Corellian Defense Force. It attempted to keep the Love Commander and the Pulsar Skate, along with their two-X-Wing escort from making orbit above Corellia, but was disabled by the combined flying skills and firepower of Wedge Antilles and Corran Horn in their T-65 fighters.
