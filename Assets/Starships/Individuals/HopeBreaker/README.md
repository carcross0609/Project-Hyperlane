# HopeBreaker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HopeBreaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HopeBreaker)

The HopeBreaker, briefly named by the Chiss as the Unidentified Battle Dreadnought, was a Shatter-class WarMaster of the Grysk that fought against forces of the Chiss Ascendancy above the planet "Sunrise" during the Nikardun campaigns. The HopeBreaker was destroyed during a second skirmish over Sunrise.
