# Violet Hem

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Violet Hem](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Violet_Hem)

The Violet Hem was a space yacht that was owned by an unidentified male Human who started a company making communications equipment. He and his advisers preferred to live on the Violet Hem, which was a reference to Emperor Palpatine's robes. The Violet Hem was destroyed when Dia Passik, the yacht owner's Twi'lek slave, deactivated the yacht's shields before stealing an armed shuttle and destroying the Violet Hem.
