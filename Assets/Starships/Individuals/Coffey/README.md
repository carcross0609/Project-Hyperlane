# Coffey

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coffey](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coffey)

The Coffey was a CR90 corvette that the Nharwaak used to escort the corvette Glazer as it made a technology delivery to the Rebel Alliance Nebulon-B frigate Xerxes. As with the other Nharwaak craft, near Mylok IV the Coffey came under attack by Imperial forces under Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin.
