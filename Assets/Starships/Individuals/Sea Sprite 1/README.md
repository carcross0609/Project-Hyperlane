# Sea Sprite 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sea Sprite 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sea_Sprite_1)

Sea Sprite 1 was a C-3 passenger liner that carried tourists to and from various locations across the galaxy. It was present at the Destreg Spaceport near Destreg II when Emon and Ace Azzameen attempted to assassinate K'Armyn Viraxo by destroying his personal yacht, the Highroller. Sea Sprite 1 was not harmed in the battle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sea Sprite 1
- **Manufacturer:** Leonore Luxury Liners Incorporated
- **Model:** C-3 passenger liner
- **Length:** 409 meters
- **Width:** 132 meters
- **Height:** 94 meters
- **Armament:** None
- **Role:** Passenger liner
