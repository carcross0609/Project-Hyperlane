# Ardency

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ardency](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ardency)

The Ardency was an MC95 Star Cruiser that served in the Resistance navy during the war between the Resistance and the First Order military junta. The Ardency was one of two MC95 Star Cruisers that participated in the Battle of Batuu.
