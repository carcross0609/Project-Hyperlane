# Intombe

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intombe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intombe)

Intombe was a Strike-class medium cruiser used by the Rneekii. The Rneekii attempted to capture the Assassin-class corvette Hashim group, but thanks to Tan Maarek Stele that attempt failed. Although they did capture scientists from the shuttle G'nabgib.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Intombe
- **Model:** Strike-class medium cruiser Rneekii
- **Type:** Medium cruiser
- **Length:** 450 meters
- **Height:** 90 meters
- **Affiliation:** Rneekii
