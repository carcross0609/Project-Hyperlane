# Grimtaash

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grimtaash (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grimtaash_%28starship%29)

The Grimtaash was a starship owned by Ben Solo, a Jedi trainee in Luke Skywalker's rebuilt Jedi Order. The starship shared its name with Grimtaash, a figure from Alderaanian myth. After Solo destroyed Skywalker's Jedi temple, he left in the Grimtaash to escape from fellow students Hennix, Tai, and Voe, who had been off-world during the temple's destruction. Solo took the Grimtaash to Amaxine space station where he met with Snoke.
