# Storm

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Storm (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_%28CR90_corvette%29)

Storm was a CR90 corvette used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. Its mission was to capture Ilay, who was on her Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser Kala'din. Despite attempts to destroy it by Assault Gunboats from Talon Squadron and the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Rage, it managed to capture Ilay and escape into hyperspace.
