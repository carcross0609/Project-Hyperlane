# Moonrise

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moonrise](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moonrise)

The Moonrise was a GR-75 medium transport that was part of an Alderaanian refugee convoy after the destruction of Alderaan. Imperial Commodore Rae Sloane fleet was ordered to destroy the convoy, but were unsuccessful when they intercepted it during the battle at Fostar Haven, as the convoy escaped due to the defection of Helix Squadron Captain Lindon Javes with assistance from the Rebel Alliance's Echo Squadron and a fleet of Alliance warships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Moonrise
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Alderaanian refugee convoy
