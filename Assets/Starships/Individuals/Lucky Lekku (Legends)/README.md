# Lucky Lekku

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lucky Lekku/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucky_Lekku/Legends)

The Lucky Lekku was a LAAT gunship used during the Clone Wars, notably during the Battle of Teth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lucky Lekku
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Line:** LAAT series
- **Model:** Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry repulsorlift gunship
- **Type:** Gunship
- **Length:** 17.4 meters
- **Width:** 17 meters (wingspan)
- **Height:** 6.1 meters
- **Mglt:** 50
- **Max Speed:** 620 km/h
- **Armament:**
  - Anti-personnel laser turrets (1 behind the doors and 2 at the front)
  - Composite-beam laser turrets (2)
  - Mass-driver missile launchers (2)(30 missiles each)
  - Light air-to-air rockets (4 under each wing)
- **Complement:**
  - Speeder bikes (4)
  - IM-6 battlefield medical droid (1)
- **Escapepods:** 1 (cockpit capsule)
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Co-pilot/Gunner (1)
- **Passengers:**
  - 30
  - 12 (in Clone Wars)
- **Capacity:** 17 m³
- **Consumables:** Fuel for 8 hours
- **Role:** Troop Transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
