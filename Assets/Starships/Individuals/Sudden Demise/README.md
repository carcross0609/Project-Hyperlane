# Sudden Demise

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sudden Demise](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sudden_Demise)

The Sudden Demise, also known as the Demise, was a starship owned by the smuggler chief and information broker, Talon Karrde. The vessel was fast, but it was not heavily armed or defended. It flamed out in the first exchange of the Invasion of Yavin 4 during the Yuuzhan Vong War. The crew was presumably lost.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sudden Demise
- **Affiliation:** Car'das smugglers
