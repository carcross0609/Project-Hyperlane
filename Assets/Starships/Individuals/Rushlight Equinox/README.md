# Rushlight Equinox

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rushlight Equinox](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rushlight_Equinox)

The Rushlight Equinox was a starship utilized by the Byne Guild member Vishla's crew during the High Republic Era. In 232 BBY, when hyperspace was being reopened after the Great Hyperspace Disaster, the Rushlight Equinox was present on the Core Worlds planet Coruscant. Leox Gyasi, a friend of Vishla and the captain of the Byne Guild transport Vessel, noted the other ship's presence when he arrived on Coruscant and hoped to swap stories with Vishla about what her crew had done while grounded.
