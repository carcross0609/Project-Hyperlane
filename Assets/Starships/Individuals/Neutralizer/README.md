# Neutralizer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neutralizer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neutralizer)

The Neutralizer was a Star Destroyer at the time of the Liberation of Kashyyyk in 5 ABY. At this time, it surrendered to the New Republic over Kashyyyk, after the Dominion, under the command of New Republic insurgents, destroyed the Vitiator, another Star Destroyer.
