# Rasvin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rasvin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rasvin)

The Rasvin was an Imperial BFF-1 bulk freighter. It was tasked with bringing a new hyperdrive unit to the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate. Although the frigate came under attack by the Rebels, its starfighters were able to drive off the Rebels and the freighter delivered the new hyperdrive unit safely.
