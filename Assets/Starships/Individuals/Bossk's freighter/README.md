# Bossk's freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bossk's freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bossk%27s_freighter)

This unnamed freighter was a starship owned by the Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk prior to owning the Hound's Tooth. Han Solo destroyed the freighter by landing the Millennium Falcon directly on top of it during a botched raid by Bossk on a Wookiee colony on Gandolo IV.

- **Name:** Bossk's freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Owners:** Bossk
