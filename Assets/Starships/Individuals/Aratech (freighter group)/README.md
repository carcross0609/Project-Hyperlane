# Aratech (freighter group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aratech (freighter group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aratech_%28freighter_group%29)

Aratech was a group of six BFF-1 bulk freighters that transported durasteel and were of unknown relation to the Company. Each freighter was designated with a number from 2-7. The ships were present at the Destreg Spaceport when K'Armyn Viraxo's personal yacht, the Highroller, was destroyed by Ace and Emon Azzameen. However, K'Armyn had got wind of this and used the yacht as a trap against the Azzameens. Despite this both Ace and Emon escaped the ambush by destroying all of the bounty hunters.
