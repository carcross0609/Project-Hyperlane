# Alpha Kentrum

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Alpha Kentrum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alpha_Kentrum)

Alpha Kentrum was a starship assigned to the Jedi geologist Michael Tandre. After he crash-landed on Orellon II and passed on the ways of the Jedi to the local Kentra, it became the center of a holy temple built in his honor.
