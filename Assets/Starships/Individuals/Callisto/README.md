# Callisto

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Callisto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Callisto)

Callisto was a Victory-class Star Destroyer in service with the Imperial Navy. When the Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser Dargon came under Rebel attack during the Rebel Alliance's Operation: Quick Strike the Callisto was summoned to its defense.
