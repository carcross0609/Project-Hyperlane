# Spear (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spear (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spear_%28corvette_group%29)

Spear was a pair of Corellian corvettes employed by the Rebel Alliance. The two corvettes were joined by another pair called Sharky and several X-wings to stop one of two Escort carriers Prowler from being captured by an Imperial Interdictor Suelu. The corvettes and fighters were destroyed by two TIE Defenders.
