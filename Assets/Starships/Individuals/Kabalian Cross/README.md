# Kabalian Cross

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kabalian Cross](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kabalian_Cross)

The Kabalian Cross was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was a part of the navy of the Galactic Empire. Manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards and measuring 1,600 meters in length, it shared its name with the Mayagil sector planet Kabal and was in service around 13 BBY.
