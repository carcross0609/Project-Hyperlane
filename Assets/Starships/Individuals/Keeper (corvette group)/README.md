# Keeper (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Keeper (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper_%28corvette_group%29)

Keeper was a pair of CR90 corvettes belonging to the Rebel Alliance. They were fitted with explosives and were attempting to destroy the Dreadnaught Dargon through crashing into the ship. The attempt was foiled by Maarek Stele in a TIE Avenger.
