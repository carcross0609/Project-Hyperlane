# Malka'an Eternal

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malka'an Eternal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malka%27an_Eternal)

The Malka'an Eternal was an enormous pleasure cruiser, which crashed on Edonaaris after its crew was forced to abandon it, leaving behind all its droids. It was later found by a crew of Human scavengers who were killed by the surviving maintenance droid SCr-114, who in turn stole their ship.
