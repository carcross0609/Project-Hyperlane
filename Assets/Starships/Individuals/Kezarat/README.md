# Kezarat

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kezarat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kezarat)

The Kezarat was a tanker that served as the command ship of the Byne Guild's Kezarat Convoy. On the same day as the Great Hyperspace Disaster, the Kezarat and the rest of the convoy disappeared after it entered hyperspace, though it sent out a garbled message. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, members of the Alliance to Restore the Republic led by Amilyn Holdo sought to find the Kezarat Convoy as they were in need of fuel. After they acquired a Nihil Path engine, Holdo inserted the garbled message from the Kezarat into the engine. As the rebels were chased by TIE fighters, the engine activated, and transported them to No-Space.
