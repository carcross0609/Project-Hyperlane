# Hidden Chain flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hidden Chain flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Chain_flagship)

The Hidden Chain flagship was a capital ship used by the Mandalorians of the Hidden Chain during the Third Galactic War. The ship transported the Holocron Machine created by the Hidden Chain leader Heta Kol to create an army of Force-sensitive soldiers. The ship was eventually destroyed when the Eternal Alliance boarded the vessel to destroy the machine and recover Darth Nul's holocron.
