# Silver (shuttles)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Silver (shuttles)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silver_%28shuttles%29)

Silver was a pair of Lambda-class T-4a shuttles used by the Rebel Alliance. They were part of an assault force against the Imperial frigate Laantis while it was vulnerable during a refit. The Rebels underestimated the skills of the pilots defending the frigate and Silver, along with the rest of the Rebels were destroyed. The assault was then recorded and recreated into the Combat chamber to train future pilots, one of them was Maarek Stele.
