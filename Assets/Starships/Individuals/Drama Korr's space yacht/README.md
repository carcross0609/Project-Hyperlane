# Drama Korr's space yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Drama Korr's space yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drama_Korr%27s_space_yacht)

Drama Korr's space yacht was a sizable silver-and-gray luxury vessel owned by Corellian merchant Drama Korr. Korr was killed aboard the yacht in the Karthakk system by Durge, who later engaged Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker in combat aboard the ship.

- **Name:** Drama Korr's space yacht
- **Type:** Yacht
