# Frontier Justice

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Frontier Justice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frontier_Justice)

The Frontier Justice was a starship captained by a Jedi Knight who was personally selected by Supreme Chancellor Leontyne Saresh. The Jedi was given an order to recall Colonel Jace Malcom from the planet Kalandis Seven, where the soldier was fighting on the front lines of the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the resurgent Sith Empire, and the Knight was assigned the Frontier Justice as a transport to bring Malcom back to the Core Worlds. The Jedi flew the Frontier Justice and battled through half a dozen blockades to reach the Kalandis system.
