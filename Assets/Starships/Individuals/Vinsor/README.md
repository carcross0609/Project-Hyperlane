# Vinsor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vinsor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vinsor)

The Vinsor was a Galactic Alliance cruiser that saw use during the early stages of the Second Galactic Civil War. The Vinsor was deployed to support the Anakin Solo during the battle of Centerpoint Station.

- **Name:** Vinsor
- **Type:** Cruiser
