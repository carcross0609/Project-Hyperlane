# The Final

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [The Final](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Final)

The Final, also referred to as Seezelslak's Cantina and Seezelslak's Saloon was a starship and mobile cantina owned by the Azumel bartender Seezelslak. The cantina served drinks, and had a jukebox and a Repulsor Darts board.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Final
- **Owners:** Seezelslak
