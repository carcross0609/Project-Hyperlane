# Grace of Champalan Tides

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grace of Champalan Tides](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grace_of_Champalan_Tides)

The Grace of Chapalan Tides was a Thranta-class warship in service to the Navy of the Galactic Republic during the Great Galactic War. In 3678 BBY, the Grace of Chapalan Tides was under the command of a female commodore and played host to a Miraluka Jedi Master and his Padawan during their search for Exal Kressh.
