# High Hauler

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [High Hauler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Hauler)

High Hauler was an Imperial transport that was captured by the New Republic in 5 ABY and used to sneak a Rebel strike team onto the planet of Ruusan through Jerec's blockade.
