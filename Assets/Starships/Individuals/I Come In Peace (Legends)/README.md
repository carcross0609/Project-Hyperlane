# I Come In Peace

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I Come In Peace/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I_Come_In_Peace/Legends)

I Come In Peace was one of the LAAT/i gunships used to bring wounded clones away from the destroyed Geonosis primary droid foundry Ray Shield Fortress during the Second Battle of Geonosis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** I Come In Peace
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Line:** LAAT series
- **Model:** Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry repulsorlift gunship
- **Type:** Gunship
- **Engine:** Two equipped
- **Armament:**
  - Anti-personnel laser turrets (2 at the front)
  - Composite-beam laser turrets (2 behind the cockpit and 2 others on the wings)
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Co-pilot (1)
  - Gunner (2)
- **Role:** Troop Transport
- **Affiliation:** Grand Army of the Republic
