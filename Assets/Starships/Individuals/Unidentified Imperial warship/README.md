# Unidentified Imperial warship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial warship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial_warship)

A warship of the Sith Empire participated in the Battle of Corellia in 3640 BBY, during the Galactic War with the Galactic Republic, and was heavily damaged during the battle. It clipped a high-rise tower in Corellia's capital of Coronet City, collapsing several floors and nearly toppling the entire tower.
