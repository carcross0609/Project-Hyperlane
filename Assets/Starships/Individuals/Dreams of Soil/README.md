# Dreams of Soil

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dreams of Soil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dreams_of_Soil)

Dreams of Soil was a massive explorer starship that contained an entire artificial ecosystem. Piloted by Humans during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire, Dreams of Soil featured forests, lakes, and a wide variety of plants and animals within its ecosystem.
