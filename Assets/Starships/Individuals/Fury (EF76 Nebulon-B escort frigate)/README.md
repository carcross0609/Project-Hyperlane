# Fury

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fury (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fury_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

Fury was a Nebulon-B frigate of the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. The frigate attempted to stop Yali and Maria on their way to Hoth. She was destroyed in that attempt by combined efforts of fleets and Red Squadron's B-wings.

## Specifications
