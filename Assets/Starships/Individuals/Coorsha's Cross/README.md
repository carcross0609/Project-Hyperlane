# Coorsha's Cross

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coorsha's Cross](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coorsha%27s_Cross)

Coorsha's Cross was an Ton-Falk-class escort carrier that saw service during the Yuuzhan Vong War. The carrier was captured by the Rodian pirate Coorsha, who re-named the starship after himself, and the vessel was subsequently deployed by Coorsha's pirate forces.
