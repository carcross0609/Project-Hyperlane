# Caelus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Caelus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caelus)

The Caelus was a New Republic cruiser stationed at Durren in the Meridian sector in 9 ABY. It was one of two New Republic warships dispatched to deal with pirate attacks on nearby Ampliquen.
