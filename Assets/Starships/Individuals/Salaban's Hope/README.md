# Salaban's Hope

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Salaban's Hope](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salaban%27s_Hope)

The Salaban's Hope was a Kazellis-class light freighter owned by the Storm-hair warlord Nuso Esva. It shared its name with the Arkanian Salaban, and was produced by the Kazellis Corporation years before 0 ABY. That year, Esva and his pilot, "Sorro," used the light freighter to rendezvous with the Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Chimaera. Several starships attacked the Salaban's Hope; however, the Chimaera and the freighter used their combined firepower to destroy all but two of the attackers, which escaped.
