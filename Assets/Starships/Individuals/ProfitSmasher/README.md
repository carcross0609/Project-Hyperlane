# ProfitSmasher

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ProfitSmasher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ProfitSmasher)

The ProfitSmasher was a freighter. When its crew failed to make loan payments to Brebu, his underlings shot down the ship. It crashed on a backwater world. The entire crew perished or were taken as slaves by Brebu. Many criminals tried, without success, to turn the wreck into a base. After the Battle of Yavin, Brebu himself decided to make it his base.
