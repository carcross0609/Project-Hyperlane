# Digit Six

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Digit Six](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Digit_Six)

The Digit Six was an Imperial picket ship stationed at Bakura. It was destroyed at the Battle of Bakura when, after being critically damaged, its pilot crashed into a Ssi-ruuvi ship. All hands died.
