# Wrath

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wrath (Assertor-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrath_%28Assertor-class%29)

The Wrath was an Assertor-class Star Dreadnought within the navy of the Galactic Empire. Both the Wrath and its sister ship, the Assertor, were laid down for construction at Kuat Drive Yards' Kuat shipyards before the Battle of Yavin. At some point after that battle, the Wrath was deployed in a large battle group, which was believed to have included dozens of other ships, to fight in the Galactic Civil War against the Alliance to Restore the Republic along the border between the Mid Rim Territories and Outer Rim Territories.
