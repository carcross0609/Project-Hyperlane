# Mercenary's Honor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mercenary's Honor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mercenary%27s_Honor)

The Mercenary's Honor was a XR Light Transport owned by the mercenary Sanlo Raal. The ship was often referred to by the Raal's Regulators as "the Honor." Most of its cargo space was converted into troop compartments, with extra weapons, communication systems, and deflector shields added to make it a mobile troop carrier and field base.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mercenary's Honor
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** XR Light Transport
- **Affiliation:** Raal's Regulators
- **Owners:** Sanlo Raal
