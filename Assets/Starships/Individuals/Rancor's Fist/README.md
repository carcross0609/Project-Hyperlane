# Rancor's Fist

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Rancor's Fist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rancor%27s_Fist)

.]] The Rancor's Fist was the starship of Jabba Desilijic Tiure's cousin Graballa the Hutt. The ship also served as Graballa's mobile headquarters. It housed his henchmen and several starfighters. Following the Battle of Hoth, the Rancor's Fist was used to hunt down the Freemakers during a race for the Kyber Saber crystals.
