# Equator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Equator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Equator)

Equator was a Nebulon-B2 frigate belonging to the Rebel Alliance. It was present at the XQ4 Platform which was attacked by Imperial forces lead by Darth/Legends Vader on the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Garrett and was destroyed in the battle by Tan Maarek Stele.
