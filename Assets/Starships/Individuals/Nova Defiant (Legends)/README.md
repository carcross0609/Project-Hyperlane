# Nova Defiant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nova Defiant/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nova_Defiant/Legends)

Nova Defiant was a Recusant-class light destroyer in service of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. It was shot down, and crashed on Innton II, a colony world near the Triton system in the Outer Rim Territories. The parts that the Separatists were able to salvage were brought to a nearby cave, and were used to establish a base before the end of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nova Defiant
- **Model:** Recusant-class light destroyer
- **Role:** Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
