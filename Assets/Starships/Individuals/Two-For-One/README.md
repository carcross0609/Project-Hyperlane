# Two-For-One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Two-For-One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Two-For-One)

Two-for-One was a Squib reclamation cruiser, part of the Squib Reclamation Fleet. Macemillian-winduarté was the Assistant-Deputy-Lieutenant-in-Chief-in-charge-of-Bartending on the cruiser, until he got left behind during a stopover on Tatooine.
