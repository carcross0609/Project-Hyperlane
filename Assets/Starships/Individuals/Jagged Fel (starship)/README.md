# Jagged Fel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jagged Fel (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jagged_Fel_%28starship%29)

The Jagged Fel was a Pellaeon-class Star Destroyer in the Empire-in-exile starfleet, named after Jagged Fel, founder of the Fel dynasty and first Galactic Emperor of the Fel Empire. It served as the flagship of the Empire after the Attack on the Hidden Temple and was stationed over Bastion.
