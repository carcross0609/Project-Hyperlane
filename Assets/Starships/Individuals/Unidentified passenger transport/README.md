# Unidentified passenger transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified passenger transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_passenger_transport)

A passenger transport was in operation by the time of the High Republic Era. In 232 BBY, the vessel set out for the Outer Rim location Travnin with several individuals on board. However, the Nihil Tempest Runner Pan Eyta captured seven of the ship's passengers under the orders of his leader, Marchion Ro. The seven individuals were imprisoned on Ro's flagship&mdash;the Gaze Electric&mdash;and Ro reflected on how Eyta had brought them to him off their transport. He mentally considered the unfairness of their captivity and torture aboard the Gaze Electric due to their initial journey being innocuous but then shrugged off the thought.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified passenger transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Passengers:** At least 7
- **Role:** Passenger vessel
