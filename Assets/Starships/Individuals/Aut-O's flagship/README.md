# Aut-O's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aut-O's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aut-O%27s_flagship)

A Providence-class Dreadnought served in the Confederacy of Independent Systems's naval forces and served as the flagship of a Separatist fleet commanded by the ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid Commander Aut-O during the Clone Wars. In 20 BBY, the dreadnought was infiltrated by a squad of Galactic Republic droids, who had been sent to retrieve an encryption module located aboard. The squad later escaped the vessel with the encryption module, but not before Aut-O was destroyed in a brief incursion with the droids.
