# Burning Gear

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Burning Gear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Burning_Gear)

The Burning Gear was a starship that served in the Burning Gear flotilla, a part of the Ebruchi, the fleet of the eponymous species of pirates. The ship was the flagship of the Ebruchi Captain Ness and was equipped with engines and a hyperdrive.
