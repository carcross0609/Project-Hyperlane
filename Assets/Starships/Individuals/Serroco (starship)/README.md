# Serroco (starship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Serroco (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Serroco_%28starship%29)

The Serroco was a warship in the Republic Navy that was under Fleet Admiral Oteg's command at Taral V in the early days of the Galactic War. It was destroyed at the Battle of Taral V. Like the Dantooine and the Telos, it was named after Serroco, a planet devastated during the Mandalorian Wars .
