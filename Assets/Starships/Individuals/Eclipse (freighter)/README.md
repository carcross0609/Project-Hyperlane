# Eclipse

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eclipse (freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eclipse_%28freighter%29)

The Eclipse, also known by the alias Tirion, was a YT-209 freighter used by a small band of smugglers in 13 BBY. The ship was used in a smuggling run to provide medicine for Twi'lek elders on the planet Ryloth. Its captain, Krysiant Rheden, employed a small crew with Hera Syndulla as its main pilot and Eira Tay as the ship's engineer.
