# Drogon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Drogon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drogon)

The Drogon was the Kroford family's personal starship. It was used during the Rebellion era by a group of Rebels to escape a group of cyborgs disguised as Maxeil Kroford's servants.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Drogon
- **Affiliation:** Kroford family
- **Owners:** Maxeil Kroford
