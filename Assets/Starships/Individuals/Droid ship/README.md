# Droid ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Droid ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_ship)

A model of droid ship was utilized by pirate captain Orkaro in an attack on New Republic diplomats on the outskirts of the Hosnian system between 31 and 34 ABY. She utilized four droid ships in the attack, but they were all destroyed by the timely appearance of Resistance pilot Poe Dameron. With the droid ships shot down, and her own vessel disabled by heat seekers, Orkaro ordered her crew to retreat back to their base.
