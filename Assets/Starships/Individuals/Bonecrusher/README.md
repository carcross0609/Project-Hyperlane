# Bonecrusher

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bonecrusher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bonecrusher)

The Bonecrusher was a sleek cruiser owned by the Barabel Galamal. In 382 BBY, it was part of a mission conducted by the Path of the Open Hand cult to retrieve Nameless eggs from Planet X, during which it was captained by Galamal and carried Path members Geth, Shalish, Wole, and Tareen. The Bonecrusher and Scupper made it to the surface of Planet X, surviving the journey through the planet's treacherous Veil, which had vaporized the third ship Moon of Sarkhai. After Galamal's death, the ship was piloted by Tareen on the return journey. To escape the expanding Veil, the Bonecrusher and Scupper attempted to jump to hyperspace from within the Veil. The Scupper was successful, but the Bonecrusher disappeared in an explosion and flash of light. The Scupper sensors were unable to detect whether the Bonecrusher had disintegrated or jumped to hyperspace, but several members of its crew later encountered the Republic vessel Witherbloom.
