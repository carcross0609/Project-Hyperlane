# Ambitious

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ambitious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ambitious)

Ambitious was a supply ship captained by Vin Freen. It was reported by Imperial HoloVision to have crashed into the B'Knos colony due to its navigation systems crashing. The crash was reported to have killed all the residents of the colony. In fact, the colony was attacked by the Ssi-ruu who took all the inhabitants for entechment.
