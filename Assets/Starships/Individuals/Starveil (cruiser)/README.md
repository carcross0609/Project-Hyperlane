# Starveil

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starveil (cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starveil_%28cruiser%29)

Starveil was an Alliance to Restore the Republic cruiser assigned to routine hit-and-fade operations near Wild Space prior to the Battle of Endor. Jak Bremen's first assignment after joining the Rebellion was as a security officer on the Starveil.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Starveil
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
