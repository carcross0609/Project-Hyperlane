# Fatality

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fatality](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fatality)

The Fatality was a destroyer belonging to the reconstituted Sith Empire that was carrying dozens of the highly advanced HK-51 assassin droids. Around 3668 BBY, when the Dread Masters were captured and imprisoned in Section X on Belsavis, one of their apprentices stole the Fatality and tried to rescue them, only to be shot down.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fatality
- **Model:** Imperial warship
- **Type:** Warship
- **Complement:** HK-51 series assassin droids
- **Crew:** At least one pilot
- **Destroyed:** 3668 BBY, Belsavis
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
- **Owners:** Dread Masters' apprentice
