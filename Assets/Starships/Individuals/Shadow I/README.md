# Shadow I

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_I)

The Shadow I was a light transport used by the Jedi hunter Del Korrot. It had room to hold a powerful speeder bike, which Korrot utilized for travel on the surface of a planet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shadow I
- **Type:** Light transport
- **Complement:** Powerful speeder bike
- **Owners:** Del Korrot
