# Ahsoka Tano's corvette

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ahsoka Tano's corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahsoka_Tano%27s_corvette)

In the years before the Battle of Yavin, Ahsoka Tano, also known as "Fulcrum," had a corvette that was used in discreet meetings. This CR90 corvette was primarily white, but had some blue markings on it. On the fifteenth anniversary of the formation of the Galactic Empire, the Ghost docked with the corvette so Hera Syndulla could hand over Tseebo, a Rodian who knew vital Imperial secrets, to Tano to learn more about what he knew.
