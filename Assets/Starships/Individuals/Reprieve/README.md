# Reprieve

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reprieve](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reprieve)

The Reprieve was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate modified as a medical frigate with the New Republic. It was called on to rendezvous with and recover Rogue Squadron after evacuating their base on Talasea, which was attacked by a small Imperial force. Onboard the frigate, Rogue Squadron pilots Corran Horn, Gavin Darklighter and Andoorni Hui were treated for the wounds they received in the fighting on Talasea. The Reprieve was joined by Admiral Ackbar's flagship Home One, and the Admiral flew over to the Reprieve for a memorial service for those who died on Talasea, including Rogue Squadron pilot Lujayne Forge.
