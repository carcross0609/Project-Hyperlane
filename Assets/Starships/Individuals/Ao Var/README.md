# Ao Var

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ao Var](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ao_Var)

The Ao Var was a Ghtroc Industries Class 720 light freighter with a crew capacity of at least one. It was piloted by a smuggler named Pash, but technically was the property of cartel gangster Teemo the Hutt.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ao Var
- **Manufacturer:** Ghtroc Industries
- **Model:** 720 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Crew:** At least 1
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Hutt Cartel
- **Owners:** Teemo the Hutt
- **Captains:** Pash
