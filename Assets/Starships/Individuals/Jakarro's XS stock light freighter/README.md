# Jakarro's XS stock light freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jakarro's XS stock light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jakarro%27s_XS_stock_light_freighter)

Jakarro's XS stock light freighter was an XS stock light freighter owned by the Wookiee smuggler Jakarro during the Galactic War. After Jakarro and his droid companions received death marks from the Order of Revan, they teamed up with Theron Shan and Lana Beniko to uncover their conspiracy. After tracking them to Rakata Prime, Jakarro joined the assault on the Temple of the Ancients, while Lana and Theron took the ship into orbit and monitored the events from there. The team then traveled the Rishi, where after a prolonged period of hiding, Jakarro used the ship to destroy the assets of the Nova Blades pirates. At some point following the battle of Yavin 4, Jakarro's droid, C2-D4, installed a remote piloting system in his ship.
