# Skorp-Ion

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skorp-Ion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skorp-Ion)

The Skorp-Ion was the personal ship of Quinlan Vos during his time as a Separatist double-agent. Tol Skorr, jealous of Vos, always desired it. Following the Siege of Saleucami, Khaleen Hentz flew it to Nar Shaddaa to await the end of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Skorp-Ion
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 2+ lasers
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Galactic Republic
- **Owners:** Quinlan Vos
