# Darth Malgus's personal shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Malgus's personal shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malgus%27s_personal_shuttle)

A shuttle was used by the Sith Lord Darth Malgus during, and soon after, the Great Galactic War. Malgus used the shuttle to leave the planet Alderaan following a battle there. In the year 3653 BBY, shortly after the Sith sacked the Republic capital of Coruscant, he used it again to transport him around the Imperial-occupied planet. The shuttle had a single pilot and an intership comm.
