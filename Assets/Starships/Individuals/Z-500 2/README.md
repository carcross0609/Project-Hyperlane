# Z-500 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-500 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-500_2)

Z-500 2 was a Muurian transport used by the Zaarin coup forces. It was present at the Destruction of the Glory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z-500 2
- **Manufacturer:** Muurian Interstellar
- **Model:** Muurian transport
- **Length:** 30.5 meters
- **Width:** 29.4 meters
- **Height:** 8.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 2 double laser cannon turrets
  - 3 laser cannons
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
