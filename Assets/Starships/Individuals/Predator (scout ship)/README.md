# Predator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Predator (scout ship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Predator_%28scout_ship%29)

Predator was a modified scout ship owned by Khlor, a Human bounty hunter active during the Imperial Era. The bounty hunter's hideout resided on a volcanic island on the planet Velusia. Khlor's prefabricated barracks featured a vehicle bay. Still, when the bounty hunter was hiding on his island, he landed Predator in a gully in the northern portion of his hideout, concealing it with camo netting.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Predator
- **Type:** Scout ship (modified)
- **Owners:** Khlor
