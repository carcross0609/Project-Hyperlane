# Aspirant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aspirant (Acclamator-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aspirant_%28Acclamator-class%29)

The Aspirant was a Republic Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship active in 19 BBY during the Clone Wars. Following a mission to Golatha to destroy a Separatist factory attempting to produce megadroids, in which Jedi Grand Master Yoda sustained minor injuries when he got trapped inside of a megadroid launching into space, the diminutive Jedi received medical treatment from 2-1B surgical droid TB-5 aboard the Aspirant. The megadroid was brought to the ship's cargo bay where engineers examined the specimen, and Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker was debriefed.
