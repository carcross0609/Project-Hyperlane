# King's Ransom

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [King's Ransom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/King%27s_Ransom)

The King's Ransom was a Raider-class corvette owned by Imperial captain Cerasus Ehllo. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, Ehllo traveled aboard the ship to retrieve his wife Aryssha of the Starros Clan from Hosnian Prime as he had installed a birthing suite on the King's Ransom so that she could give birth to their twins. After storming the Starros family ancestral home, he returned with Aryssha to the ship. Sometime later, the ship was present in the Hosnian sector.
