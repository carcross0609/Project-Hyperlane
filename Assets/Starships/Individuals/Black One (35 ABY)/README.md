# Black One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Black One (35 ABY)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_One_%2835_ABY%29)

Black One, also known as Poe Dameron's X-wing fighter, or Poe's X-wing, was a Resistance T-70 X-wing starfighter that was utilized by Commander Poe Dameron during the war between the First Order and the Resistance.
