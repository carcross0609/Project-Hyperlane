# Rampart

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rampart (Immobilizer 418 cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rampart_%28Immobilizer_418_cruiser%29)

The Rampart was an Immobilizer 418 cruiser in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. By the time of the Thrawn campaign, the Rampart was assigned to the Chazwa system to reduce smuggling in the area.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rampart
- **Model:** Immobilizer 418 cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
