# Thalmussen

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thalmussen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thalmussen)

The Thalmussen was a transport vessel used by the Wookiee species from the planet Kashyyyk. It had a grey hull with two orange stripes across its center and was equipped with two engines. Sometime between 39 BBY and 36 BBY, the Thalmussen, alongside three other identical craft, were used to transport some of the first Wookiee settlers to the moon Alaris Prime.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thalmussen
- **Type:** Transport
- **Engine:** 2 engines
- **Affiliation:** Alaris Prime colonists
