# Gamble

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gamble](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamble)

The Gamble was a converted freighter used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. Princess Leia Organa and General Vanden Willard traveled aboard the Gamble on a mission to negotiate the purchase of materials to be used in the construction of Echo Base.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gamble
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Crew:** 20
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Alliance Fleet
