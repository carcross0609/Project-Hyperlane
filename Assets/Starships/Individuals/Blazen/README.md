# Blazen

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blazen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blazen)

Blazen was a group of Imperial CR90 corvettes who arrived too late to save an Imperial outpost. They were unable to stop Keyan Farlander, Lambda-class T-4a shuttles group Leary and Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Cathleen from stealing a group of Alpha-class Xg-1 Star Wings.
