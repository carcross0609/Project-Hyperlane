# Mescue

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mescue](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mescue)

Mescue was an CR92a Assassin-class corvette belonging to the Galactic Empire. The corvette was part of a rescue operation when Grand Admiral Zaarin attempted to launch a coup d'état and captured Emperor Palpatine. When Palpatine's escort shuttle was disabled by Maarek Stele, the Mescue, under protection of the Vanguard, boarded the shuttle and rescued Palpatine.
