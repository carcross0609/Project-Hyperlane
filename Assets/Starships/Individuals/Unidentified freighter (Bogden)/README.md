# Unidentified freighter (Bogden)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified freighter (Bogden)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_freighter_%28Bogden%29)

A freighter marooned on the bog moon of Bogden was used as a makeshift prison by the pirate captain Gir Kybo Ren-Cha at some point in 15 BBY. Ren-Cha and his Pirates of Tarnoonga used it as a temporary base when they kidnapped Princess Gerin, the daughter of Lord Malameu Toda, warrior-chieftain of Tammuz-an's Outer Territories. Toda later raided the base with the help of King Mon Julpa and a squadron of soldiers.
