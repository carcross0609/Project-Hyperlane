# Crusaders' transport craft

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Crusaders' transport craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crusaders%27_transport_craft)

The Crusaders used Low Altitude Assault Transport transport crafts at some point. When the former Sith known as the Ronin was found on the planet Olcatz by the Crusaders, thanks to a report by the Rodian bounty hunter Do N-Yuk, the Crusaders mobilized transport crafts to the planet to support their leader, who was known as the Grand Master. Under the Grand Master's order, after his duel with the Ronin, the LAATs bombarded the AK-BK used by the Anzellan Que-Dama as his mobile gambling hall.
