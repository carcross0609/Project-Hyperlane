# Larty Three-Niner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Larty Three-Niner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Larty_Three-Niner)

Larty Three-Niner was an LAAT/i gunship that participated in the Battle of Teth. During the battle the gunship was called for by Anakin Skywalker to evac the Huttlet Rotta. The gunship was, however, destroyed by a Vulture droid soon after landing. All hands were lost.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Larty Three-Niner
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Line:** LAAT series
