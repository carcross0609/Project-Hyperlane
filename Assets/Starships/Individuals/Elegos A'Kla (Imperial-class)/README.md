# Elegos A'Kla

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Elegos A'Kla (Imperial-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elegos_A%27Kla_%28Imperial-class%29)

The Elegos A'Kla was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer of the New Republic Defense Fleet and later the Galactic Alliance Navy; the vessel was named for Caamasi Senator and martyr Elegos A'Kla. The ship played a major role in a number of conflicts during the later stages of the Yuuzhan Vong War. The ship served as the flagship of General Garm Bel Iblis during most of its service in the war.
