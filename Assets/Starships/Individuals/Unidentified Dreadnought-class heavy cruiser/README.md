# Unidentified Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Dreadnought-class_heavy_cruiser)

A Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser was used by the Galactic Republic and it beared Republic markings. At some point during the Galactic Civil War, the Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser emerged from hyperspace near an abandoned Republic supply depot, damaged and broadcasting a distress call for any Republic forces. The ship had young clone troopers on board who knew nothing of the existence of a Galactic Empire.
