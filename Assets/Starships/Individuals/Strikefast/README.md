# Strikefast

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Strikefast](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strikefast)

The Strikefast was a Venator-class Star Destroyer in service to the Galactic Empire's naval forces. It was the site of a meeting between the Chiss Mitth'raw'nuruodo, Imperial Captain Voss Parck, Colonel Mosh Barris, and Cadet Eli Vanto.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Strikefast
- **Line:** Venator-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Complement:**
  - Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighters
  - Harbinger courier ships
  - Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttles
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Voss Parck
