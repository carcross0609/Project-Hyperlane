# Impounder

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Impounder](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Impounder)

The Impounder was an Imperial Customs Corvette under the command of Captain Mandus Fouc. It was part of the fleet that attempted to take control of the Kalinda system in the years following the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Impounder
- **Type:** Imperial Customs Corvette
- **Role:** Customs ship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:** Mandus Fouc
