# Casino Yego

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Casino Yego](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Casino_Yego)

Casino Yego was a casino ship that was located above Glee Anselm between 12 BBY and 9 BBY. In this time, the Seventh and Ninth Sisters met Eorgus aboard the ship to collect information regarding the whereabouts of Tensu Run. After successfully extracting the information, the Inquisitors executed Eorgus.
