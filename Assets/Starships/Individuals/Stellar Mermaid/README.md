# Stellar Mermaid

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stellar Mermaid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stellar_Mermaid)

The Stellar Mermaid was a ZD-8000 cruise ship, known for its comfort, as well as the matching prices. Sard Nightbringer tried to auction off a prototype set of Imperial body armor on this ship, in an attempt to raise money for her criminal organization. At the time, it was commanded by Captain Glaucus.
