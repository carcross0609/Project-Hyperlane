# Corusca Fire

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corusca Fire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corusca_Fire)

Gauntlet, later renamed Corusca Fire, was a Victory I-class Star Destroyer in the service of the Imperial Navy and the New Republic. It participated in the Battle of Ciutric under the command of General Garm Bel Iblis, and later in the Battle of Dantooine during the Yuuzhan Vong War under the command of Captain Rimsen.
