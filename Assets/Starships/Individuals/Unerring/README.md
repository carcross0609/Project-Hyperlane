# Unerring

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unerring](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unerring)

The Unerring was a starship that served within the New Republic Defense Fleet during the Galactic Civil War against the Galactic Empire. By 5 ABY, the vessel and its escorts sought to destroy a conspiracy by one of the late Emperor Palpatine's viziers. In a meeting with the New Republic High Command that year, Admiral Ho'ror'te spoke about the Unerring and its escorts' sacrifices to the other meeting attendees over hologram.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unerring
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
- **Navy:** New Republic Defense Fleet
