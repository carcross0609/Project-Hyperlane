# Unidentified Imperial starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial starship (Bellassa)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial_starship_%28Bellassa%29)

A prototype starship was used by the Galactic Empire. While over the planet Bellassa, Darth Vader ordered the ship's captain to shoot down two V-wings piloted by members of the Eleven resistance group. The double-agent Ferus Olin and another officer also served aboard the vessel.
