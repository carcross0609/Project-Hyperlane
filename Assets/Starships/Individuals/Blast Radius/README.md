# Blast Radius

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blast Radius](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blast_Radius)

Blast Radius was a CR90 corvette belonging to the Viraxo. Blast Radius, along with the Victory II-class Star Destroyer Furious, arrived at the Twin Sun Station to capture it.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blast Radius
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** CR series
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Length:** 150 meters
- **Width:** 48.6 meters
- **Affiliation:** Viraxo
