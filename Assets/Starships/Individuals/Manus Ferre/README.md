# Manus Ferre

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Manus Ferre](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Manus_Ferre)

Manus Ferre was a Nebulon-B2 frigate used by the Dimoks. She was present at a meeting between the leaders of both factions that was raided by Imperials who intended to capture the leaders of both factions only to kidnap their children instead.

- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
