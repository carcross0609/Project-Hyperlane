# Siroto

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Siroto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Siroto)

The Siroto was a starship utilized by the Orphans, a group led by the bounty hunter Chanath Cha, during the time of the Galactic Empire. The members of the Orphans&mdash;the droid Ladybright, Sear, and Imara Vex&mdash;operated under Cha aboard the vessel. When the Orphans were recruited into the plans of Lady Qi'ra, leader of the resurgent Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate, they utilized the Siroto in a number of anonymous attacks against other syndicates in order to help Qi'ra lead them into war with one another.
