# Twilight's Kiss

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Twilight's Kiss](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight%27s_Kiss)

Twilight's Kiss was a space yacht that was owned by Cularin Star Tours. During the Clone Wars it was hired by Lord Sargo VenHalgon and he along with Lady VenHalgon, his minister Cannison, Mrs. Cannison, Professor Worrita, Mrs. Worrita and the valet droid LF-R3D toured the Cularin system in it. They landed it in the jungle on Cularin and the men left to go hunting. After they didn't return, the women left to find them. When they didn't come back, LF-R3D contacted the crew of the CST safety ship Starchaser IV, which flew over to investigate and landed next to the Kiss.
