# Crucible

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crucible (Imperial-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crucible_%28Imperial-class%29)

The Crucible was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that served as home to Nas Ghent and his elite Black Eight Squadron during the Galactic Civil War. The flight officer was Commander Dorin Millavec.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Crucible
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Complement:** Black Eight Squadron
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
