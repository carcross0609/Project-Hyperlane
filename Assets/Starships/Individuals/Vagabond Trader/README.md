# Vagabond Trader

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vagabond Trader](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vagabond_Trader)

The Vagabond Trader was an SX troop transport stolen from Dellalt by a group of fugitive Jedi and subsequently docked in the hangar bay of the Drunk Dancer. It was later disguised as a civilian ship and christened the Vagabond Trader in an attempt to fool Kashyyyk Commerce Control and ferry Olee Starstone and her fellow Jedi to Kashyyyk. Upon arrival on the Wookiee planet, Cudgel identified the Trader as an L-200.
