# Trail

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trail](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trail)

Trail was a pair of Lambda-class T-4a shuttles used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. The shuttles, along with sister group Guildand the transport group Goode, tried to defend an attack on their foothold consisting of four Class-C cargo containers Berm and Class-B cargo container Clip by Imperial forces lead by the frigate Compton. All Rebel forces were destroyed in the battle. The battle was recreated in the Combat chamber to train future pilots, one of whom was Maarek Stele.
