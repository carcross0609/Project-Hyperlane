# Scimitar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scimitar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scimitar/Legends)

The Scimitar, also known as the Sith Infiltrator, or just The Infiltrator before Maul gave it its name, was a prototype stealth-ship used as the personal starship of Darth Maul, the Sith apprentice of Darth Sidious, prior to the Invasion of Naboo around 32 BBY.
