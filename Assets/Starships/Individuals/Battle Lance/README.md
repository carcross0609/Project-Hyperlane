# Battle Lance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle Lance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Lance)

The Battle Lance was a 1,600 meter&ndash;long Imperial II-class Star Destroyer manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards that served in the Imperial Navy. Sometime before the Battle of Yavin, the warship was selected to test an improved prototype hypermatter reactor. Due to a malfunction, the null-point energy produced by the drive became unstable and vaporized the Battle Lance in a microsecond, killing the ship's entire crew of 37,000. Hora Graneet, the nephew of Imperial gunner Tenn Graneet, served on the ship and died during the accident.
