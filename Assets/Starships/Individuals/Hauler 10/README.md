# Hauler 10

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hauler 10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hauler_10)

Hauler 10 was a cargo hauler owned by the Lant Mining Corporation. Built to transport refined ores, it was assigned to gather its cargo from the Lormar 23 station in the Mangez system. The captain of the Hauler 10 had a tendency for recklessly darting through traffic lanes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hauler 10
- **Type:** Cargo hauler
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Lant Mining Corporation
