# Cobra

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cobra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cobra)

The Cobra was a ship that belonged to Lando Calrissian. Calrissian had many stories of how he acquired the ship and wasn't sure which one he liked the best.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cobra
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Type:** Mu-2 long range shuttle
- **Modifier:** Lando Calrissian
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Alliance of Free Planets
- **Owners:** Lando Calrissian
- **Captains:** Lando Calrissian
