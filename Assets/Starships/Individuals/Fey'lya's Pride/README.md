# Fey'lya's Pride

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fey'lya's Pride](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fey%27lya%27s_Pride)

Fey'lya's Pride was the name of Borsk Fey'lya's personal Lambda-class shuttle, which he used during the Galactic Civil War. It was aboard this shuttle that Fey'lya escorted a single Bothan escaping from the planet Kothlis with a stolen supercomputer that contained the detailed plans and location of the construction of the Death Star II during the Battle of Kothlis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fey'lya's Pride
- **Model:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Owners:** Borsk Fey'lya
