# Wayfarer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wayfarer (freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wayfarer_%28freighter%29)

The Wayfarer was the ship that Roa used during his smuggling days. It was speedy and meticulously maintained. Once, Roa allowed Han Solo to pilot her through the Kessel Run.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wayfarer
- **Role:** Smuggler's ship
- **Owners:** Roa
