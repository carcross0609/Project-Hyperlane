# Glayyd

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Glayyd](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glayyd)

Glayyd was one of seven colony ships launched from the planet Thokos. More than ten thousand years prior to the Battle of Yavin, the Glayyd and the other ships landed on the planet Ammuud. Like the colonists from the other ships, the ones who arrived on the Glayyd lost contact with Thokos, eventually formed a clan and named it after the starship on which they arrived. The House Glayyd would feud with the other Ammuud clans for millennia to come.
