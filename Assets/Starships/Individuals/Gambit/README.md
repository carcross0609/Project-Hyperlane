# Gambit

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gambit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gambit)

The Gambit was a Luxury 2800 yacht manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. Originally belonging to a Ho'Din crime lord, the vessel came into the possession of the Alliance to Restore the Republic after a clash between them and the crime lord's organization. Captain Amarith, commander of Chiron Base, claimed the vessel for herself. She loaned the vessel to a Rebel team investigating an Imperial operation on the planet Ando.
