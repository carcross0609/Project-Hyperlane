# Wild Fire

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wild Fire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wild_Fire)

Wild Fire was a light freighter used by the remnants of the Disac pirates. It was armed with a quad laser cannon and proton torpedo launcher. Along with two MRX-BR Pacifiers, the Wild Fire led an attack on smugglers travelling the Salin Corridor.
