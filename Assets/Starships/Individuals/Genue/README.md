# Genue

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Genue](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Genue)

Genue was a BFF-1 bulk freighter used by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. Genue was due to travel from the prison planet Mytus VII, aka Stars' End, to Kashyyyk when it was boarded by some escaped Rebel pilots, including Sam Raider and Toalagar.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Genue
- **Model:** BFF-1 bulk freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Length:** 120.9 meters
- **Width:** 75.56 meters
- **Height:** 34 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
