# Galaxy Chance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Galaxy Chance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galaxy_Chance)

The Galaxy Chance, a CR90 corvette, was a mobile smuggling base and casino, similar to Booster Terrik's Star Destroyer Errant Venture, but on a much smaller scale. It was owned and operated by a rival of Terrik, who, as a taunt to him, had painted the Galaxy Chance in the same bright red that Terrik desired for the Errant Venture.
