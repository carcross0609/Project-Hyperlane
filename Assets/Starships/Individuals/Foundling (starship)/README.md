# Foundling

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Foundling (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Foundling_%28starship%29)

The Foundling was a Pathfinder ship used by Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two, a Republic Pathfinder team led by Jedi Master Rok Buran. After the team staged an expedition to the ravaged planet Gloam, they were betrayed by the former miner Rillik, who led the team's members into a trap which only Buran survived, before destroying the starship to prevent anyone from escaping.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Foundling
- **Model:** Pathfinder ship
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Destroyed:** 382 BBY, Gloam
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Captains:** Rok Buran
