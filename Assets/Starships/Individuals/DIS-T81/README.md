# DIS-T81

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DIS-T81](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DIS-T81)

DIS-T81 was a droid tri-fighter that served in the Separatist Alliance during the Clone Wars. At one point, DIS-T81 attacked a Galactic Republic Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor in space. In another instance, DIS-T81 flew near wreckage in space and a Republic Consular-class Charger c70 light cruiser alongside other droid tri-fighters.
