# Dalnan Dawn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dalnan Dawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dalnan_Dawn)

The Dalnan Dawn was a Nihil stormcruiser owned by Ithorian scientist Baron Boolan during the High Republic Era. It shared its name with the Outer Rim planet Dalna where Boolan had been a member of the Path of the Open Hand cult as a child. In 229 BBY, Boolan used the Dalnan Dawn to escape from his base of operations, Shingcu Station, after it was attacked by a Jedi team.
