# Khaaza'k

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Khaaza'k](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khaaza%27k)

Khaaza'k was a Rebel Lambda-class T-4a shuttle that ferried the captain of the Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Maximus after it was crippled and abandoned before its destruction.
