# Condemnation

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Condemnation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Condemnation)

.]] Condemnation was a Victory I-class Star Destroyer belonging to the Galactic Empire. It responded to an attack at Viraxo Industries caused by Ace and Emon Azzameen, who were attempting to rescue their uncle Antan. However, the Condemnation arrived too late and the Azzameens escaped into hyperspace.
