# Meridian

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Meridian (light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meridian_%28light_freighter%29)

The Meridian was a light freighter originally owned by the smuggler Ixel Garsea, who bought it from Pall Gopal. When Garsea died, her daughter Izal inherited the starship, becoming a smuggler herself. The freighter featured at least two laser cannons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Meridian
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Role:** Smuggler starship
- **Owners:**
  - Ixel Garsea
  - Izal Garsea
