# Etherway

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Etherway](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Etherway)

The Etherway was one of Talon Karrde's starships. It was a modified Corellian Engineering Corporation Action V transport. It sometimes operated under the alias Garret's Gold. The Etherway was present at the Smugglers' Alliance meeting on Hijarna. After being impounded on Abregado-rae, Aves piloted it during the Battle of Bilbringi. While trying to rescue a group of Jedi students at Yavin 4, its power grid shut down and may have been destroyed by the Yuuzhan Vong.
