# Bright Seeker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bright Seeker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bright_Seeker)

Bright Seeker was a modified Mining Survey Ship which was designed to survey systems for mineral and ore deposits. It had excellent sensors and a powerful laser for boring into asteroids and taking samples. Bright Seeker was commanded by the minor noble, Captain Nils Wender of House Calipsa.
