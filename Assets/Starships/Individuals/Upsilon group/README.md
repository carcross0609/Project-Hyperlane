# Upsilon group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Upsilon group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Upsilon_group)

Upsilon group was a group of five Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transports that served the Zaarin coup forces during the Zaarin insurrection. It was destroyed during the Battle of Ottega.
