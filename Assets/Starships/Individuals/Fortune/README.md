# Fortune

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fortune](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fortune)

The Fortune was a Dynamic-class freighter that served as a mobile headquarters for the Zendiat Collective, a thieving guild led by the human treasure hunter Zool Zendiat. Because one of its passengers, the Patrolian Kanna, belonged to an aquatic species, the Fortune notably housed a water tank.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fortune
- **Model:** Dynamic-class freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Affiliation:** Zendiat Collective
- **Owners:** Zool Zendiat
