# Notow Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Notow Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Notow_Squadron)

Notow Squadron was a small Squadron of 2 T-wings employed by Nami Crime Lord Ali Tarrak. After Gotmilc Squadron and transport group Lucthz were destroyed trying to stop Modular conveyor Ulzepdi from escaping the destruction of an Imperial Cargo area. The Strike Cruiser Bognog launched Notow Squadron and Gotan Squadron to continue pursuing the conveyor but both squadrons were destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele and the Ulzepdi escaped into Hyperspace.
