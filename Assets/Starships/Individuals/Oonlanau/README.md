# Oonlanau

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oonlanau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oonlanau)

The Oonlanau was a Sullustan convoy freighter that operated during the waning years of the Galactic Republic, and it sometimes carried out freelance work for the Rodian Renna. During the Confederation Rock Rush in c. 31 BBY, the ship became caught in the Cularin asteroid belt due to the actions of a shadow creature, which was draining passing starships of power.
