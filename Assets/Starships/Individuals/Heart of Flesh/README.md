# Heart of Flesh

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heart of Flesh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heart_of_Flesh)

The Heart of Flesh was a Qektoth attack cruiser that served the Qektoth Confederation in 8 ABY. The vessel carried five Qektoth Confederation starfighters, and was armed with plasma cannons and a bio-energy array. In 8 ABY, the Heart of Flesh engaged the New Republic CR90 corvette FarStar over the planet Yvara.
