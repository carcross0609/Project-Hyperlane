# H-dash-five-one-eight-zero-two

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [H-dash-five-one-eight-zero-two](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H-dash-five-one-eight-zero-two)

H-dash-five-one-eight-zero-two was the designation of a Coyote cargo ship owned by Hondo Ohnaka of Ohnaka Transport Solutions on the planet Batuu. The Resistance spy Vi Moradi used the freighter when rescuing Siv and Torbi from the planet Parnassos.
