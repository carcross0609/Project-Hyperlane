# Arditi group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arditi group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arditi_group)

Arditi was a group of three Assassin-class corvettes used by Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's splinter government fleet. Along with Falcon group and BaKaar group, it was sent to destroy Lord Vader's flagship, the Garrett. However, Imperial ace Maarek Stele helped the Garrett destroy the corvettes along with the other groups.

## Specifications

- **Leader:** Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin
