# Bloodsniffer (CR90 corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bloodsniffer (CR90 corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodsniffer_%28CR90_corvette_group%29)

Bloodsniffer was a group of CR90 corvettes sent to save the Imperial I-class Star Destroyer Relentless, although they were unable to stop Keyan Farlander from destroying it.
