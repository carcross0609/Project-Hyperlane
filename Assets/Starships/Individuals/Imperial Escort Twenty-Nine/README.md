# Imperial Escort Twenty-Nine

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial Escort Twenty-Nine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Escort_Twenty-Nine)

Imperial Escort Boat Twenty-Nine was an Imperial escort boat that was stolen by members of the Free Ryloth Movement during the Ryloth Insurgency. Members of the movement used the escort boat in an attempt to assassinate Emperor Sheev Palpatine and Darth Vader.
