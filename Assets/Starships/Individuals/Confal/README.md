# Confal

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Confal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Confal)

Confal was a Muurian transport belonging to the Rebel Alliance. The Confal attempted to repair the MC80 Star Cruiser Link, but instead was destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Confal
- **Manufacturer:** Muurian Interstellar
- **Model:** Muurian transport
- **Length:** 30.5 meters
- **Width:** 29.4 meters
- **Height:** 8.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 2 double laser cannon turrets
  - 3 laser cannons
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
