# Intrepid

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intrepid (Munifex-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_%28Munifex-class%29)

Intrepid was a Munifex-class light cruiser of the Imperial Navy. At the time of the Galactic Civil War, it orbited the planet Gesaril under the command of Captain Dulrain serving a dual purpose: to enforce the quarantine on Gesaril, and to guard the prison that was located on a nearby ateroid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Intrepid
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Munifex-class light cruiser
- **Length:** 300 meters
- **Armament:**

  - 12 Ion cannons
- **Crew:**

- **Skeleton:** 200/+10
- **Passengers:** 200 troops

- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:** Dulrain
