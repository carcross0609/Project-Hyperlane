# Stars of Elrood

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stars of Elrood](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_of_Elrood)

The Stars of Elrood was a freighter owned and operated by Shondra Del. Del, an Alliance operative, used the freighter as part of her cover working in the Elrood sector.
