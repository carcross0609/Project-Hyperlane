# Vanguard Flare

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vanguard Flare](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard_Flare)

Vanguard Flare was a Recusant-class light destroyer in service of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Romar Kek served as gunner unboard the ship. The Vanguard Flare was destroyed during the Battle of Coruscant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vanguard Flare
- **Model:** Recusant-class light destroyer
- **Role:** Destroyer
- **Destroyed:** 19 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
