# Pulsar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pulsar (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pulsar_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

Pulsar was a Star Destroyer which was present at the Battle of Endor. Commander Dor Reder served aboard the ship as a junior officer, and Lieutenant Niclara Varnillian was a member of the ships's gunnery crew. Pulsar was forced to surrender after that battle, but members of the crew, including Reder and Varnillian, escaped Alliance to Restore the Republic custody.
