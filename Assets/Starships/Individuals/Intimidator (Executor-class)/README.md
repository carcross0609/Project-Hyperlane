# Intimidator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intimidator (Executor-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intimidator_%28Executor-class%29)

The Intimidator was an Executor-class Star Dreadnought used by the Imperial Navy's Black Sword Command during the Galactic Civil War. Originally undergoing work at a shipyard in the Core Worlds, it was transferred to the Empire's Black-15 shipyard above the planet N'zoth in the Koornacht Cluster to free up space for similarly-sized warships. Eight months after the Rebel Alliance's victory at the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY, the Black Fleet&mdash;comprising the Intimidator and all warships present, along with their crews&mdash;was seized by N'zoth's native Yevetha workers under resistance leader Nil Spaar as part of their mutiny against the Imperials, who had been in the process of withdrawing all military personnel and equipment from the Cluster.
