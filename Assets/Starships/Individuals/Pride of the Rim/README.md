# Pride of the Rim

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pride of the Rim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pride_of_the_Rim)

Pride of the Rim was a ship in the Corellian Resistance and the Alliance to Restore the Republic that got into trouble with an Imperial picket ship and had to be aided by the Retribution, which in turn was damaged.
