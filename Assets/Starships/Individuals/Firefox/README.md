# Firefox

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firefox](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firefox)

The Firefox was a Strike-class medium cruiser that was used by the Rebel Alliance to defend the Sealion, a prototype of the Missile Boat that was sent to the XQ4 Platform Enin'sd by the Mon Calamari Cruiser Link. When the first Strike cruiser Piimi was destroyed by Imperial forces led by Darth Vader in the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Garrett, Firefox arrived to reinforce the station but was destroyed in the battle by Tan Maarek Stele.
