# Trand Cappa

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trand Cappa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trand_Cappa)

The Trand Cappa was a tanker which served in the Galactic Federation Triumvirate's navy during Darth Wredd's insurgency. In 139 ABY, the tanker took part in the Liberation of Mon Calamari Shipyards. Under Admiral Gar Stazi's orders, the tanker was loaded with water and used to flood the Mon Calamari Orbital Shipyards, overwhelming the pirates and allowing the Mon Calamari and Quarren slaves to regain the upper hand.
