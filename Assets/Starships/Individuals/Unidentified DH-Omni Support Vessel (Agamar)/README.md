# Unidentified DH-Omni Support Vessel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified DH-Omni Support Vessel (Agamar)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_DH-Omni_Support_Vessel_%28Agamar%29)

A DH-Omni Support Vessel was in service of the Confederacy of Independent Systems' navy during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic. The supply ship crashed on the planet Agamar in the Outer Rim Territories during a battle between Republic and Separatist forces. Following the war's conclusion and the deactivation of the Separatist Droid Army in 19 BBY, the vessel was left derelict on the planet's surface, and was used to house the super tactical droid General Kalani and his garrison of battle droids, which remained there until 2 BBY during the Galactic Civil War.
