# Korriban's Flame

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Korriban's Flame](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korriban%27s_Flame)

The Korriban's Flame was a space station maintained by the reconstituted Sith Empire in the Unknown Regions during the Cold War and subsequent Galactic War with the Galactic Republic. Located just east of the Outer Rim Territories, near the planet Huk and the Kalee system, the station served a trap for the Jedi Knights Jomar Chul and the Hero of Tython, as part of the fallen Jedi Leeha Narezz's attempt to capture the Hero.
