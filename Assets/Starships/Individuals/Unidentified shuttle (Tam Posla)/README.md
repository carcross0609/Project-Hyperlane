# Unidentified shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified shuttle (Tam Posla)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_shuttle_%28Tam_Posla%29)

An escape shuttle was brought by the vigilante Tam Posla to the wreckage-prison Accresker Jail, in 3 ABY, as part of a deal with the rogue archaeologist Chelli Aphra. Posla was deliverred the escape vessel to the rogue archaeologist in exchange for his arch nemesis Doctor Cornelius Evazan. Aphra and her companion Magna Tolvan attempted to escape Accresker Jail in the shuttle but were quickly shot down by the assassin droids 0-0-0 and BT-1, who were there to capture Aphra.
