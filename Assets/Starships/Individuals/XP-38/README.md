# XP-38

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XP-38](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XP-38)

The XP-38 was a Battle-class ship given to the people of Cularin by the Metatheran Cartel as a gesture of good will after the Cartel was expelled from the planet. The gift caused controversy when a transmitter was discovered mounted on the external gun turrets of the ship, designed to transmit data to an offworld location. Later, a empty container capable of carrying four such transmitters was found; the three other transmitters were never located. While Thurm Loogg tried to quell the rumors of nefarious plans with a speech pointing the blame at other parties, they persisted nonetheless.
