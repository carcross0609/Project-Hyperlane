# Bakuran Blade

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bakuran Blade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bakuran_Blade)

The Bakuran Blade was a YT-2000 light freighter operated by the female tramp freighter captain Dana Galvason, a Rebel Alliance sympathizer. Galvason was one of the few who had clearance to fly through the Deep Core Security Zone and would sometimes use her light freighter to assist the Rebel Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bakuran Blade
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Model:** YT-2000 transport
- **Type:** Light freighter
