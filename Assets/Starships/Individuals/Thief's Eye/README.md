# Thief's Eye

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thief's Eye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thief%27s_Eye)

The Thief's Eye was a yacht owned by King Katuunko of Toydaria. It was commandeered by the Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker during Savage Opress's kidnapping&ndash;and accidental killing&ndash;of the king.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thief's Eye
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Affiliation:**
  - Toydarian Royal Delegation
  - Jedi Order (briefly)
- **Owners:** King Katuunko
