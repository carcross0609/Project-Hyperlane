# Steel Talon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Steel Talon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Steel_Talon)

The Steel Talon was a Star Destroyer of the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. It was commanded by Admiral Conan Antonio Motti, Chief of the Imperial Navy and a member of the Imperial Joint Chiefs, prior to his death during the Battle of Yavin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Steel Talon
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Conan Antonio Motti
