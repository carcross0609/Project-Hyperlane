# Thrifty

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thrifty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thrifty)

The Thrifty was a Squib needle ship and the Momship of the Squib Reclamation Fleet. It served as the personal vessel of the Squib king, Ebareebaveebeedee, at the beginning of the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thrifty
- **Manufacturer:** Squib Merchandising Consortium
- **Model:** Needle ship
- **Length:** 50 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** None
- **Armament:** 10 tractor beams
- **Crew:** 20 Squibs
- **Capacity:** 500 tons
- **Consumables:** 3 months
- **Role:** Salvage vessel
- **Affiliation:** Squib
