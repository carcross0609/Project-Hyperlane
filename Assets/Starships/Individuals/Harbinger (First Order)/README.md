# Harbinger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Harbinger (First Order)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harbinger_%28First_Order%29)

The Harbinger was a Star Destroyer that served in the First Order Navy during the First Order's war with the Resistance. While infiltrating a First Order bacta production plant on Tevel, Finn and Poe Dameron claimed to Major Kander Varak that as they previously examined a field hospital on the Perlemian Reach, a surgeon general questioned the orders of Supreme Leader Kylo Ren, resulting in Ren demoting her on the spot. According to Dameron, the demotion ended up with the surgeon general being sent to mop up bathrooms on the Harbinger.
