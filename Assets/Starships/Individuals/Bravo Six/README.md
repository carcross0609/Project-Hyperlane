# Bravo Six

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bravo Six](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravo_Six)

Bravo 6, or Bravo Six, was an N-1 starfighter manufactured by Theed Palace Space Vessel Engineering Corps that flew as part of the Naboo Royal Space Fighter Corps' Bravo Squadron. The R2-series astromech droid R2-C4 was often assigned to the starfighter's astromech socket. The starfighter was assigned to pilot Gavyn Sykes.
