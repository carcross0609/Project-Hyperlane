# Sharky (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sharky (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sharky_%28corvette_group%29)

Sharky was a pair of Corellian corvettes used by the Rebel Alliance. The two corvettes were joined by another pair called Spear and several X-wings to stop one of two Escort carriers Prowler from being captured by Imperial forces. The whole strike force was destroyed in the battle by two TIE Defenders.
