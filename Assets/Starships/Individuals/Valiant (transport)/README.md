# Valiant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Valiant (transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valiant_%28transport%29)

Valiant was the transport of the Valahari Viscount Harko Vane. In it, he tried to breach the Republic blockade of his planet, but the ship was set to explode by Asajj Ventress, who made it to look destroyed by Republic forces in order to pull the Valahari into war.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Valiant
- **Role:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** c. 21 BBY
- **Owners:** Harko Vane
