# Unidentified Moraysian cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Moraysian cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Moraysian_cruiser)

A Moraysian cruiser was utilized by the Pyke Syndicate. The Moraysian cruiser, along with its complement of droids, was stolen by Ledesmar, a mercenary formerly enslaved by the Pykes. The Spice Runners of Kijimi undertook a mission to locate Ledesmar, which became a skirmish against the New Republic after New Republic Security Bureau officer Sela Trune located Poe Dameron aboard the cruiser. The cruiser was later present in the Torch Nebula, used by Kes Dameron and L'ulo L'ampar, who had allied themselves with the Osako pirates to find Poe following a Spice Runner heist on Guat'a.
