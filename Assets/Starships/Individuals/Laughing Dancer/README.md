# Laughing Dancer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Laughing Dancer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Laughing_Dancer)

Laughing Dancer was a freighter operating during the Galactic Civil War. A ship crewed by friends of the Laughing Dancer was captured by Buula's Raiders and taken to Buula Nen's Rocky Home. The crew of the Laughing Dancer sought to reacquire the freighter and its crew, and were advised by Captain Nomar to try and buy back their goods from Buula rather than risk a frontal assault.
