# Mist Hunter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mist Hunter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mist_Hunter)

The Mist Hunter was a modified Byblos Drive Yards G-1A starfighter owned by the bounty hunter Zuckuss, used by him and his companion 4-LOM. Holding cells aboard Mist Hunter were used to hold Beris Ford and Han Solo after his capture on the planet Valtos. It later crash-landed during a pursuit of Solo and the Wookiee Chewbacca.
