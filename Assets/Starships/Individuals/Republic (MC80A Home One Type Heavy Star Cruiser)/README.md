# Republic

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Republic (MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_%28MC80A_Home_One_Type_Heavy_Star_Cruiser%29)

The Republic was an MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War following the mutiny on Mon Cala by General Jan Dodonna. After the conversion of Mon Calamari Star Cruisers was complete, the Republic served as the flagship of the Alliance Fleet.
