# Red Reaper

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red Reaper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Reaper)

The Red Reaper was an Imperial Dreadnaught that served the reconstituted Sith Empire, under the command of the Sith Lord Darth Ikoral. Commissioned around 3712 BBY by the Dark Council for Ikoral's expedition in search of pureblood Sith, the ship was not seen until almost 70 years later. The ship was destroyed, along with its master, at the Battle of the Red Reaper.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Red Reaper
- **Manufacturer:** Sith Empire
- **Commission:** c. 3712 BBY
- **Destroyed:** c. 3642 BBY
