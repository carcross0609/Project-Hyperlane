# Dukedoom

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dukedoom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dukedoom)

The Dukedoom was a Rebel Alliance ship that was present in the Battle of Endor. One of twenty-two ships provided to the Alliance Fleet by the Virgillian Free Alignment, it appeared to suffer some troubles to its hyperdrive upon arriving at Sullust.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dukedoom
- **Type:** Starship
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
