# Lucky Dragon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lucky Dragon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucky_Dragon)

The Lucky Dragon was a Hapan Battle Dragon used by the Hapes Consortium. After the Second Battle of Fondor during the Second Galactic Civil War, the Hapes Consortium provided the Lucky Dragon to patrol the Transitory Mists. After her encounter with her brother at Nickel One, the Lucky Dragon gave Jaina Solo medical aid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lucky Dragon
- **Manufacturer:** Olanji/Charubah
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Length:** 500 meters
- **Affiliation:** Hapes Consortium
