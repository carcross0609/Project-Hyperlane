# Red Squadron 4

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red Squadron 4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Squadron_4)

Red Squadron 4 was a T-65 X-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The fighter was part of Red Squadron during the Battle of Endor, piloted by Derek "Hobbie" Klivian. At the time of the battle, the fighter had the second highest mission total of any X-wing in active service.
