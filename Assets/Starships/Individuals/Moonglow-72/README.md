# Moonglow-72

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moonglow-72](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moonglow-72)

Moonglow-72, tagged Imperial Provisional 72 and Renegade One by the Galactic Empire, and the Expedient by the human Kanan Jarrus, was a mining transport ship owned by Moonglow Polychemical. It was used by Jarrus to transport baradium bisulfate from Gorse City on Gorse to its moon Cynda where the substance was detonated to free large quantities of thorilide.
