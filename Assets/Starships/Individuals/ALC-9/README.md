# ALC-9

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ALC-9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ALC-9)

ALC-9 was an YV-865 Aurore-class freighter owned by the Snivvian Morlimur Snugg, the Superintendent of the Cybloc Transfer Station, during the Clone Wars. It was stolen by the Nightbrothers Darth Maul and Savage Opress.
