# Ghost Ship of Veragi

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ghost Ship of Veragi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ghost_Ship_of_Veragi)

The Ghost Ship of Veragi was the name eventually given to a starship that shared it with the Veragi system, a part of the Outer Rim Territories' Veragi sector situated on the hyperlane known as the Veragi Trade Route. The ship was equipped with a prototype cloaking device that at one point malfunctioned, causing the vessel to periodically become invisible. That fact led to the rise of a legend about the craft that claimed it was a ghost ship, with reports claiming sightings along Outer Rim hyperspace routes for decades prior to approximately 3643 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ghost Ship of Veragi
- **Countermeasures:** Prototype cloaking device
