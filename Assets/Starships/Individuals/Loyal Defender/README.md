# Loyal Defender

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Loyal Defender](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loyal_Defender)

The Loyal Defender was a Galactic Alliance warship that took part in the Second Battle of Fondor. The vessel was so heavily damaged during the fight that an order to abandon ship was issued. The Compass Star was sent to keep the ship safe from attack as this was going on.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Loyal Defender
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
