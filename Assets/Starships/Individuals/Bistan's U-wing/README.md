# Bistan's U-wing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bistan's U-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bistan%27s_U-wing)

Bistan's U-wing was a UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft piloted by Laren Joma and Taslet Colb during the Battle of Scarif. The U-wing arrived with the rest of Blue Squadron to reinforce Jyn Erso and the rest of Rogue One in their mission to recover the Death Star plans. During the battle, the U-wing was shot down, killing Joma, Colb, and Bistan.
