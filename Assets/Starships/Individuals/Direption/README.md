# Direption

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Direption](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Direption)

Direption was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer that served under the Warlord Admiral Krennel's fleet in 9 ABY. It was later surrendered to the New Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Direption
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial II-class Star Destroyer

  - Ciutric Hegemony under Delak Krennel
  - New Republic
- **Navy:**

- **Captains:**
