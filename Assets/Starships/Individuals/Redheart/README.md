# Redheart

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Redheart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Redheart)

The Redheart was a Duros-crewed warship operated by the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances during the later stages of the Yuuzhan Vong War. Redheart was part of a task force commanded by General Wedge Antilles during an operation over Duro in 29 ABY. It was one of several ships lost when a portion of Antilles' fleet and some local Duros resistance fighters refused an order to fall back.
