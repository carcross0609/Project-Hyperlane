# Ansta

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ansta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ansta)

The Ansta was a warship employed by the Galactic Alliance during the Second Galactic Civil War. The Ansta was part of the blockade of Corellia by the Alliance's Third and Fifth Fleets. It was assigned to the Fifth Fleet, under the command of Admiral Cheb.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ansta
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
- **Fleet:** Galactic Alliance Fifth Fleet
- **Captains:** Admiral Cheb
