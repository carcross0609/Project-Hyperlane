# Firedrake

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Firedrake](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firedrake)

The Firedrake was a Star Destroyer of the Galactic Empire that served as the flagship of Grand Admiral Balanhai Savit and the Imperial Navy's Third Fleet. Its commanding officer was Captain Boulag. The Firedrake and the forces of the Third Fleet were used in a campaign against pirates in the Esaga sector, which was secretly a cover for Savit's theft of resources from Project Stardust.
