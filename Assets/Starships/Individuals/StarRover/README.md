# StarRover

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [StarRover](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/StarRover)

The StarRover, colloquially known as the Rover, was a freighter owned by the pirate queen Maz Kanata. Kanata supplied the StarRover to the Resistance for use in a mission to a bacta production plant on Tevel, undertaken by Finn and Poe Dameron. Before Finn and Dameron could reach Tevel, they were captured by a First Order space station, and were forced to abandon the StarRover to escape. The StarRover was similar in appearance to another starship owned by Kanata, the Epoch Swift.
