# Jedi High Council cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jedi High Council cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council_cruiser)

A cruiser was utilized by the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. An insignia on the front of the vessel indicated that it belonged to the Jedi High Council. In 382 BBY, the cruiser was used to bring two Jedi Council members, the Jedi Masters Ela Sutan and Gluth Andoi, as well as Jedi Master Rinn and Jedi Knight Azlin Rell, to the planet Dalna so they could investigate what had happened to Jedi Master Zallah Macri and her Padawan Kevmo Zink when they had visited Dalna previously.
