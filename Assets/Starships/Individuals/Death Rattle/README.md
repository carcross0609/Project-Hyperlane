# Death Rattle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Death Rattle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Rattle)

The Death Rattle was the personal starship of Tor Vizsla, leader of the Mandalorian Death Watch. It was destroyed in orbit over Corellia when Jango Fett slammed a ship in the engines, therefore decapitating the Death Rattle, where it plunged to its "death" on the planet below.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Death Rattle
- **Escapepods:** Equipped
- **Destroyed:** 42 BBY, Corellia
- **Affiliation:** Death Watch
- **Owners:** Tor Vizsla
