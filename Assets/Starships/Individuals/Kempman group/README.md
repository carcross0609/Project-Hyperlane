# Kempman group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kempman group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kempman_group)

The Kempman group was a group of Container Transports working for the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It was destroyed by Imperial forces during their attack on Organarms.
