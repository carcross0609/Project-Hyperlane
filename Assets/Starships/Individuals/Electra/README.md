# Electra

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Electra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electra)

Electra was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle that saw service with the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. Electra entered the Pakuuni system near XQ1 Platform NL-1 in the final Rebel engagement against the Galactic Empire during the Battle on the Frontier. Heavy fighting had erupted between, then Vice Admiral, Thrawn's task force consisting of Nebulon-B2 frigate Ludwick, Platform NL-1, IPV-1 Patrol Craft Ranger, and various starfighters. Maarek Stele was among Imperial pilots fighting for the Empire during the engagement, breaking off to inspect Electra, revealing the Rebel Advisors onboard. Stele disabled the shuttle for the capture by Imperial Stormtrooper Transport Shark.
