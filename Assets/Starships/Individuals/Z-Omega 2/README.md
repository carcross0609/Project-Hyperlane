# Z-Omega 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Omega 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Omega_2)

Z-Omega 2 was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport that fought on the side of Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's coup during the Second battle of Imperial Center in around 4 ABY. During the battle, the transport was destroyed after being hit by cannonfire and a pair of Imperial missiles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z-Omega 2
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** c. 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
- **Navy:** Zaarin's fleet
