# Reluctant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reluctant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reluctant)

The Reluctant was an Imperial warship that took part in the Battle of ThonBoka. It was one of the Centrality cruisers that was deceived into firing upon one of its sister ships due to the strategy of the Oswaft.
