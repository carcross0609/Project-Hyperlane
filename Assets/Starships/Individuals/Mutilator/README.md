# Mutilator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mutilator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mutilator)

The Mutilator was a YZ-775 medium transport that was owned by Gilflyn, a Dark Jedi who was based on the planet Corellia during the reign of the New Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mutilator
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YZ series
- **Model:** YZ-775 medium transport
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Owners:** Gilflyn
