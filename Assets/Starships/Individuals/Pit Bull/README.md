# Pit Bull

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pit Bull](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pit_Bull)

Pit Bull was a pair of CR90 corvettes belonging to the Galactic Empire. One of them contained armaments the other contained generators. They arrived at the Mayhem location when the Mayhem failed to stop rebels from liberating Habassan slaves.
