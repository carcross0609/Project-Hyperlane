# Hunting Hound

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hunting Hound](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hunting_Hound)

The Hunting Hound was a heavy cruiser that served within the New Republic's fleet. During the New Republic's attack on the Galactic Empire's forces at the planet Jakku in 5 ABY, the cruiser was targeted and destroyed by the Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing. After looking into the loss of contact with the Hunting Hound, the New Republic General Hera Syndulla was able to deduce the 204th's activity above Jakku.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hunting Hound
- **Type:** Heavy cruiser
- **Destroyed:** 5 ABY, Jakku
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
