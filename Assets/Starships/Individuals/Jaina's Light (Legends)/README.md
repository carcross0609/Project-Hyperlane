# Jaina's Light

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jaina's Light/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina%27s_Light/Legends)

Jaina's Light was a CR90 corvette piloted by Han Solo and Chewbacca circa 5 BBY. Solo claimed to Lieutenant Commander Llnewe of the Galactic Empire that it was named after his mother. At the time, the ship was supposedly en route to a small backwater farming world. Solo piloted three separate but identical CR90 corvettes (all bearing the same name) in what turned out to be a ship smuggling operation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jaina's Light
- **Type:** CR90 corvette
- **Captains:** Han Solo
