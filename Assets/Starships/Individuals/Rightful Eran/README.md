# Rightful Eran

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rightful Eran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rightful_Eran)

The Rightful Eran was a starship that was used by the Red Fury Brotherhood, a pirate group that was active in the Cularin system during the waning years of the Galactic Republic. In circa 31 BBY, the pirates flew the Rightful Eran to I-9, an asteroid that was located within the Cularin asteroid belt, and the ship was gutted for spare parts.
