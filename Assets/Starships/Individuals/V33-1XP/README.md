# V33-1XP

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V33-1XP](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V33-1XP)

V33-1XP was the designation of an escape pod jettisoned from the Rebel Alliance CR90 corvette Razor in 3.5 ABY to whisk away the stolen plans to the second Death Star from pursuing Imperial forces safely into the hands of Alliance High Command.

## Specifications

- **Name:** V33-1XP
- **Model:** Escape pod
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
