# Century 3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Century 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Century_3)

Century 3 was a BFF-1 bulk freighter used by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a convoy en route an Imperial base under construction in the Ghorman system carrying power generators. However, the freighter, along with Century 1 and Century 2 was destroyed by a Rebel R-22 Spearhead and a BTL Y-wing starfighter despite the protection of TIE/LN starfighters. This resulted in a delay of the construction of the base for a full year.
