# Breha

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Breha (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Breha_%28starship%29)

The Breha was a diplomatic vessel used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Its design greatly resembled that of a J-type 327 Nubian starship. At some point following the destruction of the Death Star, Leia Organa and Luke Skywalker used the Breha to go on a secret mission to Tibrin, posing as diplomats from Naboo's House Berenko seeking to negotiate a trade deal. The Breha was blown into the water by Imperial forces during the mission.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Breha
- **Destroyed:** Some time after the Battle of Yavin, Tibrin
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - House Berenko (allegedly)
