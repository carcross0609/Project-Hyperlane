# Wat Tambor's Sheathipede-class transport shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wat Tambor's Sheathipede-class transport shuttle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wat_Tambor%27s_Sheathipede-class_transport_shuttle/Legends)

A Sheathipede-class transport shuttle was owned by Techno Union Foreman Wat Tambor, who brought the shuttle along when he invaded Ryloth during the Clone Wars. Tambor's T-series tactical droid, TA-175, escaped from Lessu aboard the shuttle when Jedi General Mace Windu assaulted the capital, leaving Tambor behind on Ryloth to become a Galactic Republic prisoner of war.
