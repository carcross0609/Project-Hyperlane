# Starknife

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starknife](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starknife)

The Starknife was a modified Kuat Drive Yards D9 Runner light freighter owned by the Duros smuggler Kalend Thora. She received the Starknife after the ship's original owner, a fellow member of Black Sun, was ambushed and killed by an unknown party.

- **Role:** Transport
