# Buffalo

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Buffalo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Buffalo)

Buffalo was a pair of BFF-1 bulk freighters traveling through the Petrakis system carrying food. Keyan Farlander used a B-wing starfighter to disable the freighters. However, a group of Y-wing starfighters arrived to destroy him for his actions. He fought them off as transport group Ninja boarded the freighters and escaped to hyperspace.
