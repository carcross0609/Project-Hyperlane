# Aurra Sing's starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aurra Sing's starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurra_Sing%27s_starfighter)

The bounty hunter Aurra Sing had a starfighter. It was carried by the bounty hunter aboard her cruiser. Sing would leave her cruiser drifting and seemingly helpless to distract her pursuers, then surprise-attack them in her fighter. The craft had four powerful laser cannons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aurra Sing's starfighter
- **Model:** Starfighter
- **Armament:** (4) Laser cannons
- **Crew:** (1) Pilot
- **Owners:** Aurra Sing
