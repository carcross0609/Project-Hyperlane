# By'ynium

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [By'ynium](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/By%27ynium)

The By'ynium was a heavy cruiser in the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. It was commanded by Captain Lench and destroyed at the Battle of Praesitlyn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** By'ynium
- **Model:** Heavy cruiser
- **Destroyed:** c. 22 BBY, over Praesitlyn
- **Retired:** Battle of Praesitlyn
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Navy:** Republic Navy
- **Captains:** Captain Lench
