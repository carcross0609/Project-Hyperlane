# AD88

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AD88](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AD88)

AD88 was a CR90 corvette belonging to the Galactic Empire. It was scheduled to be repaired at Depot XLS, but was attacked by Rebel B-wings intending to destroy all the capital ships while they were at their weakest condition.
