# Attacker Two

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Attacker Two](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Attacker_Two)

Attacker Two was a heavy cruiser, designated as such by the crew of the Chiss Ascendancy warship Vigilant, who came under attack from the heavy cruiser and three others above the Vak homeworld of Primea. The Lioaoin Regime sent the cruisers to prevent the rescue of Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo from Primea on behalf of the Nikardun Destiny. In the attack, the cruiser designated Two provided flanking fire from a long range, along with the cruiser One. The Vigilant was able to escape the attack in the shadow of Attacker Four, which had suffered damage from the Vak fighter piloted by Thrawn.
