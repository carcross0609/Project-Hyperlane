# Pincer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pincer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pincer)

The Pincer was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate that was part of Task Force Vengeance during the Galactic Civil War. She was part of the group of reinforcements that were pulled out of hyperspace by the Rebel Modified Strike Cruiser Peregrine and was destroyed along with the rest of the group by the Rebels.
