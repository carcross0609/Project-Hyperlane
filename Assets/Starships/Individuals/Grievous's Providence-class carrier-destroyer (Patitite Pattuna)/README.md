# Grievous's Providence-class carrier/destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grievous's Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Patitite Pattuna)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grievous%27s_Providence-class_carrier/destroyer_%28Patitite_Pattuna%29)

During the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, Separatist General Grievous used a Providence-class Dreadnought. The dreadnought participated in a battle near the planet Patitite Pattuna with the Venator-class Star Destroyer flagship of Jedi General Adi Gallia in the second year of the war, where it was accompanied by two Munificent-class star frigates.
