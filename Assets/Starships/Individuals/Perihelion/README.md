# Perihelion

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Perihelion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Perihelion)

The Perihelion was a space yacht that was owned by Cularin Star Tours. During the Clone Wars, it was hired to the Liana Liberators, who were posing as members of the Alderaanian Biological Research Institute and planned to slice into important computer systems in the Cularin system from it. However, it was attacked by mynocks, although the CST safety ship Starchaser IV was able to rescue it. Afterwards, the Liberators flew it to CMC-3 in the Cularin asteroid belt, where it participated in a battle against the Starchaser and a Z-95 Headhunter flown by Shiana Trist.
