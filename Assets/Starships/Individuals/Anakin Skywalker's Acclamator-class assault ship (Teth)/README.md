# Anakin Skywalker's Acclamator-class assault ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anakin Skywalker's Acclamator-class assault ship (Teth)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker%27s_Acclamator-class_assault_ship_%28Teth%29)

An Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship was used by Torrent Company in order to reach the Wild Space planet Teth. From the assault ship, the company deployed to the surface for the Battle of Teth.
