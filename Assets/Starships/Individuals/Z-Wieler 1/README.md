# Z-Wieler 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Wieler 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Wieler_1)

Z-Wieler 1 was a CR90 corvette that fought on the side of Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's coup during the Second battle of Imperial Center in around 4 ABY. During the battle, the corvette was destroyed after taking two rockets to its engines.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z-Wieler 1
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Destroyed:** c. 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
- **Navy:** Zaarin's fleet
