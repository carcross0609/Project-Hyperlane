# Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer (Star Tours space station)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer (Star Tours space station)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial-class_Star_Destroyer_%28Star_Tours_space_station%29)

During the early stages of the Galactic Civil War (2 BBY-0 BBY), an Imperial-class Star Destroyer was deployed to Spaceport THX1138 after it was discovered that a Rebel agent was attempting to board a StarSpeeder 1000, with several Imperials being deployed to arrest the agent. It ended up severely damaged by smuggler Han Solo while the latter was aiding the flight in escaping during a run-in of his own with the Imperials.
