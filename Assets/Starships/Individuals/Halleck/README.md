# Halleck

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Halleck](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halleck)

The Halleck was an Acclamator-class assault ship in service with the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. It was assigned to Jedi Master Mace Windu during a mission to rescue his Padawan learner Depa Billaba on Haruun Kal. The vessel came under heavy attack when it proceeded to Haruun Kal to extract Windu, and was forced to retreat into hyperspace and return to Galactic Republic territory.
