# Thrawn's freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thrawn's freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thrawn%27s_freighter)

Thrawn's freighter was a customized freighter used by Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo of the Chiss Ascendancy in his campaign against General Yiv of the Nikardun Destiny. It was equipped with a Galactic Republic energy shield that Thrawn had acquired on Mokivj, as well as a customized freighter nose.
