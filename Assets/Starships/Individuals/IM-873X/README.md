# IM-873X

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IM-873X](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IM-873X)

IM-873X was an Imperial Star Galleon used as a freighter. Over a period of six years leading to the Battle of Yavin, the ship made regular trips to the Corva sector. In 0 ABY, a delay of eight days caused Alliance Intelligence to take notice. Extrapolating from its fuel capacity, the Rebels found a limited list of where it could have stopped, and it was estimated that its delay was caused to a trip to the Stylar Nebula, where it had been suspected the Empire was conducting secret military research. The data, coupled with a hacked transmission mentioning Project Shell-cracker, caused the Intelligence to launch a mission to the site.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Star Galleon-class frigate
- **Role:** Transport
