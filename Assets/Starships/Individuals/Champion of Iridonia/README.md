# Champion of Iridonia

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Champion of Iridonia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Champion_of_Iridonia)

Champion of Iridonia was a Hammerhead-class cruiser that was the flagship of a Galactic Republic fleet sent to Iridonia early on in the Jedi Civil War. Acaadi attempted to sabotage it during the Battle of Iridonia.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Champion of Iridonia
- **Manufacturer:** Rendili Hyperworks
- **Model:** Hammerhead-class cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Length:** 315 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Dual heavy Turbolasers (4)
  - Turbolaser cannons (2)
  - Quad laser cannons (2)
  - Point-defense cannon batteries (2)
  - Tractor beam generator (1)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
