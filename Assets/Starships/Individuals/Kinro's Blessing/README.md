# Kinro's Blessing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kinro's Blessing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kinro%27s_Blessing)

The Kinro's Blessing was a cruiser that lost an engine while it was passing through Akiva space while on its way to Oba Diah. The engine crashed down in Lake Amari on the Outer Rim planet Akiva. By 3 ABY, the wreckage formed an island with a small ecosystem and became known as the Cauldron.
