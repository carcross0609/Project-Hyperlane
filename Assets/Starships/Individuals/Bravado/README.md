# Bravado

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bravado](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravado)

The Bravado was a light freighter used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. At some point during that conflict, the freighter delivered a team of Special Forces Infiltrators on a mission to the Wild Space planet NCW-781.
