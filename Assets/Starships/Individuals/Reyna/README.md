# Reyna

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reyna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reyna)

The Reyna was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer assigned to the Tapani sector. It was assigned to the planet Mrlsst. The ships assigned to the planets Mrlsst and Achillea, protected by the Leonides, were strategically located to control the only routes in and out sector.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Reyna
- **Model:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
