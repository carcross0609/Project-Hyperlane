# Enigma

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Enigma (Galactic Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enigma_%28Galactic_Empire%29)

The Enigma was a starship in the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. After receiving prime marks at the Imperial Naval Academy on the planet Carida, the Ilohian Tian Karmiya gave fine service aboard the Enigma. Following this, the Imperial officer manned the starship Victory at Batonn before serving on the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Ultimatum with the rank of commander. In 3 ABY, Captain Canonhaus, Karmiya's commanding officer aboard the Star Destroyer, reflected on her previous service, including her tenure on the Enigma.
