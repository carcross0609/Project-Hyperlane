# Eye of Sion

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eye of Sion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_Sion)

The Eye of Sion was a large hyperspace transport ring built by forces under the supervision of the witch Morgan Elsbeth, with the intent of reaching and rescuing Grand Admiral Thrawn from exile in another galaxy.
