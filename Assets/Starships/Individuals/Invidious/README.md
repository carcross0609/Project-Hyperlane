# Invidious

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Invidious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invidious)

The Invidious was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer. At the time of Emperor Palpatine's death at the Battle of Endor, it was part of a task force commanded by High Admiral Treuten Teradoc, and served as part of his fleet when he became a self-proclaimed warlord.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Invidious
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial II-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/LN starfighters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Greater Maldrood
  - Invids
- **Owners:**
  - High Admiral Treuten Teradoc
  - Leonia Tavira
