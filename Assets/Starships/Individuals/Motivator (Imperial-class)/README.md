# Motivator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Motivator (Imperial-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Motivator_%28Imperial-class%29)

Motivator was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer of the Imperial Navy. The vessel was destroyed by Rogue Squadron while it was trying the capture CR90 corvette Razor to obtain the Death Star II construction data onboard, resulting in it crashing on the surface of planet Kothlis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Motivator
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:**
  - TIE/LN starfighters
  - TIE/IN interceptors
  - AT-AT walkers
  - AT-PT walkers
- **Destroyed:** 3.5 ABY, Kothlis
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Captain Briera
