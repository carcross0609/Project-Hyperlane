# Sabé's H-type Nubian yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sabé's H-type Nubian yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sab%C3%A9%27s_H-type_Nubian_yacht)

An H-type Nubian yacht was utilized by the former handmaiden Sabé during the Imperial Era. Accompanied by a droid, she took the H-type Nubian yacht to Sergia to collect the grifter Warba Calip before traveling to Caranthanax B in order to meet the Jedi Luke Skywalker and test if he had a dark side. Sabé and her droid pretended to kidnap Calip aboard the yacht, but Skywalker leapt atop the starship and deactivated its power, causing it to crash-land. Sabé's droid and Skywalker's astromech droid R2-D2 attempted to repair the vessel, but the members of Administrator Sly Moore's Schism Imperial arrived on the world, intending to eliminate Skywalker. The Schism's battle droids located the yacht, and, after determining there were no life-forms aboard, destroyed the vessel regardless.
