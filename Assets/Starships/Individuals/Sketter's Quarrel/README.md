# Sketter's Quarrel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sketter's Quarrel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sketter%27s_Quarrel)

Sketter's Quarrel was a cargo vessel that was discovered dead in space in 8011 C.R.C. after its crew were stung to death by kheilwar wasps that had escaped from a damaged cargo container the vessel had been carrying. It had been enroute to deliver a range of exotic animals, many of which were illegal to privately own, to Regent Solculvis of the Mol'leaj system.
