# Respectable

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Respectable](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Respectable)

Respectable was an Imperial cruiser that was a part of the blockade of ThonBoka. It functioned as a picket cruiser, challenging Lando Calrissian when he arrived at the blockade. Lando was brought aboard the vessel. He posed as a traveling salesman and gained the trust of a security chief onboard, who gave him contacts on other ships on the blockade he could sell his wares to.
