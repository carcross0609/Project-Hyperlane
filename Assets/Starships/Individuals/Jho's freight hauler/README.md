# Jho's freight hauler

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jho's freight hauler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jho%27s_freight_hauler)

The Ithorian Jho operated an antique freight hauler. A few years before the Battle of Yavin, Jho used it to evacuate the Spanjaf and Leonis families from Lothal ahead of Imperial searchers. Jho described the hauler's hyperdrive motivator as "iffy," but usable for short trips. It also possessed weaponry capable of blasting Imperial speeder bikes into ruins.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jho's freight hauler
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Role:** Transport
- **Owners:** Jho
