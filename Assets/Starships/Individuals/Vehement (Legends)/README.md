# Vehement

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vehement/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vehement/Legends)

Vehement was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer in service of the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. The Vehement was destroyed at the Battle of Endor after participating in the battle between the Alliance forces and the Death Star II.
