# Evanrue

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Evanrue](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Evanrue)

Evanrue was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate in the New Republic Defense Fleet. In 9 ABY, it was part of the fleet assigned to defend Coruscant, and participated in the Siege of Coruscant. Near the end of the battle, Evanrue collided with a cloaked asteroid which had been launched by the Imperial Star Destroyer Chimaera, and suffered heavy damage.
