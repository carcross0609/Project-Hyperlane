# Lapiz Cutter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lapiz Cutter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lapiz_Cutter/Legends)

The Lapiz Cutter was the personal Sheathipede-class shuttle of Trade Federation Viceroy Nute Gunray. He utilized the ship to land on conquered Naboo in 32 BBY, to flee from Republic forces during the First Battle of Geonosis in 22 BBY, to visit potential Rodian ally Senator Onaconda Farr on Rodia during the same year, and to flee Cato Neimoidia during a Republic attack in 19 BBY.
