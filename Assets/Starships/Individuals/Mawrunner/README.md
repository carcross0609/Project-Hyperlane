# Mawrunner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mawrunner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mawrunner)

The Mawrunner was a frigate assigned to the Second Fleet. It was active during the Second Galactic Civil War. The frigate, along with fleet admiral Tarla Limpan and a portion of the Second fleet, were destroyed by a blast from the Centerpoint Station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mawrunner
- **Destroyed:** 40 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Alliance
- **Navy:** Second Fleet
- **Captains:** Tarla Limpan
