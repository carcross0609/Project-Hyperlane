# Grand Imperial Navy

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grand Imperial Navy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Imperial_Navy)

The Grand Imperial Navy, colloquially known simply as the Prakith navy, was the naval branch of the Constitutional Protectorate of Prakith that defended the fortress world of Prakith after Moff Foga Brill established himself as an independent Warlord.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grand Imperial Navy
- **Headquarter:** Prakith
- **Commander:** Moff Foga Brill
- **Campaigns:** Galactic Civil War
- **Affiliation:** Constitutional Protectorate of Prakith
