# Bhishana Bhaga

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bhishana Bhaga](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bhishana_Bhaga)

The Bhishana Bhaga was a guardian corvette used as mobile base of operations by Arakyd Industries for Min Erethen, one of its key researchers. At its center was Sanctum Sanctorum, a modified A4 Juggernaut used by Erethen as a research lab. To keep its precious cargo safe, Arakyd referred to the ship only by a variable codename and kept it mobile across the Mid Rim. While under the codename Bloodhawk, the Bhishana Bhaga hyperdrive failed, dropping the ship into an asteroid field. The battered corvette crashed on nearby Krykas V, spreading its wreckage across 300 meters before resting in a valley.
