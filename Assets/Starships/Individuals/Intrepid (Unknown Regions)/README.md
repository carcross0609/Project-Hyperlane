# Intrepid

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intrepid (Unknown Regions)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_%28Unknown_Regions%29)

Intrepid was an Imperial craft that served the Fel Empire. At some point prior to the Sith–Imperial War, the craft departed on a mission to the Unknown Regions. The Sluissi technician, Chief Bor Alsek and Imperial Knight Treis Sinde served aboard the Intrepid on this mission. They would not meet again until the Second Imperial Civil War.

- **Name:** Intrepid
