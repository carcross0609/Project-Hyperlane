# Risha Synata's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Risha Synata's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Risha_Synata%27s_ship)

Risha Synata's ship was a starship owned by and dedicated to the Frenk actress Risha Synata. It had a gold and dark-red color scheme alongside geometric decorations.

- **Name:** Risha Synata's ship
- **Bays:** At least one
- **Crew:** Over a dozen

- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Owners:** Risha Synata
