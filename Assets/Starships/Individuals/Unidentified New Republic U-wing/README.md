# Unidentified New Republic U-wing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified New Republic U-wing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_New_Republic_U-wing)

A U-wing was in the service of the New Republic. It was piloted by the Lasat Garazeb Orrelios to extract the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin and the foundling Din Grogu after the death of Commander Barro.
