# Mas Ramdar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mas Ramdar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mas_Ramdar)

The Mas Ramdar was a Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser of the Galactic Republic that served in the Coruscant Home Defense Fleet during the Clone Wars. It was attached to Home Fleet Strike Group Five under Lieutenant Commander Lorth Needa for support, and participated in the Battle of Coruscant. The strike group managed to trap the Separatist Alliance flagship Invisible Hand.
