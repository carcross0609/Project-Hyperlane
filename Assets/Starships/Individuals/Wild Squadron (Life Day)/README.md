# Wild Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wild Squadron (Life Day)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wild_Squadron_%28Life_Day%29)

The Wild Squadron was a rumored group of ghosts who scoured the stars each Life Day to collect the souls of the damned to join them in their unending hunt. According to legend, the Wild Squadron used to be Jedi, but had accumulated the souls of hundreds of smugglers, pirates, and even Imperials. The legend also claimed that they could be heard whispering over comms to "join the hunt." The Espirion Saan shared a story of the Wild Squadron to Company 77 member Fry as he attempted to access the ruins of DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station on Kef Bir.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wild Squadron
- **Founder:** Jedi ghosts (Rumored)
- **Locations:** Endor system
