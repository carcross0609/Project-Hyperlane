# Unidentified C-9979 landing craft

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified C-9979 landing craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_C-9979_landing_craft)

A C-9979 landing craft was used by the Trade Federation to invade the planet Naboo in 32 BBY. The Jedi Padawan Obi-Wan Kenobi escaped from the Trade Federation battleship Saak'ak aboard this craft. He was ordered by his Jedi Master, Qui-Gon Jinn, to disable the craft's communications to prevent the craft from warning the rest of the invasion force of the stowaway.
