# Braha'tok

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Braha'tok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Braha%27tok)

The Braha'tok was a Braha'tok-class gunship under the command of Kiles L'toth in the Dornean Navy. In 4 ABY, during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, the planet Dornea loaned the Braha'tok&mdash;along with its sister ship, the Torktarak&mdash;to the Alliance Fleet for its attack on the second Death Star battle station above the Forest Moon of Endor.
