# Gilded Thranta

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gilded Thranta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gilded_Thranta)

The Gilded Thranta was an FX-77 Star Yacht owned by the Rodian Falloon, the former Black Sun account Soolehad, during the Yuuzhan Vong War. It was armed with two turreted, top and bottom mounted dual laser cannons and a concussion missile launcher with twelve missiles. After a group of mercenaries helped him and his droid 9T-LOM evacuate from the planet Sriluur during the Invasion of Sriluur, Falloon brought 9T-LOM to associates on Coruscant and gave the ship to the group of mercenaries and retired.
