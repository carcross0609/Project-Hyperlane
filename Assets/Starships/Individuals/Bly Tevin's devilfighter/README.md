# Bly Tevin's devilfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bly Tevin's devilfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bly_Tevin%27s_devilfighter)

A devilfighter was used by Bly Tevin, a pilot who served as Thylefire Nine in Thylefire Squadron of the planet E'ronoh. The devilfighter had originally belonged to the planet's princes, Xiri A'lbaran, though she instead gave it to Tevin. In 382 BBY, the starfighter was sabotaged, with A'lbaran's its intended victim, though Tevin used it instead and was killed during a skirmish in the Eiram system.
