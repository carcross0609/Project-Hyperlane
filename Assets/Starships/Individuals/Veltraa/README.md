# Veltraa

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Veltraa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veltraa)

The Veltraa was a Republic Hammerhead-class cruiser under the command of Dallan Morvis in service during the Mandalorian Wars. It was named to honor the service of Admiral Jimas Veltraa to the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Veltraa
- **Model:** Hammerhead-class cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Captains:** Dallan Morvis
