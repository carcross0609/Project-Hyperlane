# Unidentified Predak Vane ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Predak Vane ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Predak_Vane_ship)

A ship was owned by the criminal group Predak Vane during the Imperial Era. Around 3 ABY, the ship was blown up by the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra, and the group tracked Aphra to the city Canto Bight on the Outer Rim planet Cantonica where they heard the archaeologist speak about the ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Predak Vane ship
- **Destroyed:** c. 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Predak Vane
