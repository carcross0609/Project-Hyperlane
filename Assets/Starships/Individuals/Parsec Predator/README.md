# Parsec Predator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Parsec Predator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Parsec_Predator)

The Parsec Predator was the personal ship of the Sugi arms dealer Faazah. The vessel was a sleek and rapid black frigate bristling with weapons. Its broad bow was designed to suggest slanting eyes and bloody fangs thrusting from a cruel mouth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Parsec Predator
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Owners:** Faazah
