# Ziost Analysis Services Center

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ziost Analysis Services Center](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ziost_Analysis_Services_Center)

The Ziost Analysis Services Center was a space station in orbit of the planet Ziost. During the Galactic War it was operated by the Galactic Solutions Industries, whose employees performed research on the surface on the planet. During the Invasion of Ziost in 3636 BBY the station also served as a hub for Republic and Imperial forces. When Vitiate wiped out all life on Ziost, those aboard the station survived and watched the destruction unfold.
