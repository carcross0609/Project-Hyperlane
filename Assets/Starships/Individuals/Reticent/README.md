# Reticent

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Reticent](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reticent)

The Reticent was a YT-1000 light freighter. It was owned by a Koorivar captain who had two Sy Myrthian crew. In 14 BBY, the Reticent was caught up in Berch Teller's campaign when the information broker Knotts sent them as decoys into the Obroa-skai system. The ship and her crew were captured by Moff Wilhuff Tarkin and Darth Vader.
