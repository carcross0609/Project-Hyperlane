# Tractate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tractate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tractate)

The Tractate was a Pacifier-class sector patrol cruiser of the Republic Defense Coalition that served as Jedi Master Elzar Mann's attack fleet that was assembled to breach the stormwall. Following the failed assault on the Stormwall, the Tractate was dispatched to the Seswenna sector to attack the Nihil vessel Cacophony along with a sizeable RDC contingent. It later took part in the assault on Naboo.
