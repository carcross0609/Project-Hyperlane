# Z-Tallon 3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Tallon 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Tallon_3)

Z-Tallon 3 was a CR90 corvette that fought on the side of Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's coup during the Second battle of Imperial Center in around 4 ABY. During the battle, it was target by an Imperial TIE fighter.

- **Name:** Z-Tallon 3
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
