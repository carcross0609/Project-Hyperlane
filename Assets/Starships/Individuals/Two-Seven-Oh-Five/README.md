# Two-Seven-Oh-Five

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Two-Seven-Oh-Five](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Two-Seven-Oh-Five)

Two-Seven-Oh-Five was the designation of a First Order medical shuttle. The Resistance members Finn and Poe Dameron stole the shuttle from a space station during their mission to Tevel.
