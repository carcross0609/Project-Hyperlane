# Gloovan Moon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gloovan Moon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gloovan_Moon)

The Gloovan Moon was a starship captained by the Gloovan pirate Muglan Tarantyne during the High Republic Era. It was crewed by members of her gang and a cadre of enforcer droids. After Tarantyne delivered the apprehended Nihil Minister of Advancement Baron Boolan to the Nihil leader, Marchion Ro, the Evereni ordered the Gloovan Moon followed and destroyed. Vulax, a member of Ro's elite She'ar engaged the Gloovan Moon in combat over Lanjer. Tarantyne and her crew were unable to mount an effective defense before the ship was doomed to crash. As it plummeted, however, Jedi Masters Keeve Trennis and Avar Kriss, on Lanjer searching for Tarantyne, observed the freefall and eased the ship's descent slightly with the Force. Despite their efforts, no one aboard the Gloovan Moon ultimately survived the crash.
