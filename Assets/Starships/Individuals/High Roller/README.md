# High Roller

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [High Roller](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Roller)

The High Roller was a ship owned by an unknown Bounty hunter. In 24 ABY, it attacked the Tradewyn in an attempt to capture Raynar and Aryn Dro Thul. The attack was unsuccessful and it was destroyed by a shot from Jaina Solo.
