# Mothma 5-0

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mothma 5-0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mothma_5-0)

Mothma 5-0 was the call sign of a Galactic Alliance starship that was in the company of the Daring and Bounty as the ships patrolled the Bothan sector.
