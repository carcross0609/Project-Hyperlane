# Admiral Ackbar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Admiral Ackbar (Victory-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Admiral_Ackbar_%28Victory-class%29)

Admiral Ackbar was a Victory-class Star Destroyer that served as the flagship of Admiral Nek Bwua'tu of the Galactic Alliance Defense Force following the Yuuzhan Vong War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Admiral Ackbar
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Victory-class
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 900 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolaser batteries (10+)
  - Gravity well projectors
  - Asteroid-tug Tractor beam turret
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
  - The Colony
- **Navy:** Galactic Alliance Fifth Fleet
- **Captains:**
  - Admiral Nek Bwua'tu
  - Prime Unu Unuthul
