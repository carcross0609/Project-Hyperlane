# Carrion Spike

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Carrion Spike](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carrion_Spike)

The Carrion Spike was a corvette and the personal starship of Moff Wilhuff Tarkin while he was stationed at Sentinel Base in the Outer Rim Territories. It was later captured in 14 BBY by the disaffected former Republic Intelligence agent Berch Teller and his rebel cell. Berch used the Carrion Spike in a campaign against Imperial space stations, in which its abilities were unparalleled. Later the ship was abandoned by the rebels at Carida after they discovered their inside source within the Empire, Vice Admiral Dodd Rancit, was planning a betrayal.
