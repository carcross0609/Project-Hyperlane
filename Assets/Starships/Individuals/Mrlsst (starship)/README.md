# Mrlsst (starship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mrlsst (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mrlsst_%28starship%29)

The Mrlsst was a CR90 corvette in service to the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a task force commanded by Admiral Ackbar during the Battle of Ciutric, where it was left dead in space.
