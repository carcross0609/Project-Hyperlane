# Desilijic scout

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Desilijic scout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Desilijic_scout)

A Desilijic scout ship crew discovered the ruined Star Destroyer of Crimson Jack and reported its demise to Jabba Desilijic Tiure, who had invested in the pirate ship. This caused Jabba to renew the bounty on Han Solo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Desilijic scout
- **Role:** Scout vessel
- **Affiliation:** Jabba Desilijic Tiure's criminal empire
