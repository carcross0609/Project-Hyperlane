# Dao's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dao's flagship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dao%27s_flagship/Legends)

Galactic Republic Admiral Dao commanded a Venator-class Star Destroyer during the Battle of Ryloth in 22 BBY, while trying to support Jedi General Ima-Gun Di and contact the Jedi High Council. After the ship's shields were lost and the main reactor was hit, Separatist forces defeated the Destroyer, killing Dao and his men.

- **Name:** Dao's flagship
- **Type:** Venator-class Star Destroyer

- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Communications:** Holographic systems
- **Destroyed:** 22 BBY, Ryloth
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
