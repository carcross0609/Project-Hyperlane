# Dryden Vos's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dryden Vos's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dryden_Vos%27s_shuttle)

Dryden Vos's shuttle was a shuttle used by Dryden Vos during the early Imperial Era to rendezvous with Maul and his surviving allies on Janix after they escaped Darth Vader, the First Brother, and the Eleventh Brother.
