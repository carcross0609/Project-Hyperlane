# Trench's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trench's flagship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trench%27s_flagship/Legends)

Trench's flagship was commanded by Admiral Trench during his blockade of the planet Malastare. It was destroyed in the Battle of Malastare Narrows by a Jedi-led task force.
