# Veil of Skynara

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Veil of Skynara](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veil_of_Skynara)

The Veil of Skynara was a luxury yacht that was constructed in the Vensor system. During the Galactic Civil War it ran cruises along the Five Veils' Tour, between Farstine and Skynara, in the Skine sector. Due to Rebel Alliance activity in the Skine sector, it had an Imperial escorts during the last phase of its journey.
