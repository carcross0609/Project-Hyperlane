# Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tuggs%27_Grub_Sienar-Chall_Utilipede_Transport)

Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport was a modified Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport converted by chef Strono Tuggs into a mobile kitchen for his traveling diner, Tuggs' Grub. Following the destruction of Takodana Castle, Tuggs docked the ship at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo in Black Spire Outpost on Batuu.

- **Role:**
