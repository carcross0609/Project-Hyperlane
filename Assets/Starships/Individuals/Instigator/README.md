# Instigator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Instigator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Instigator)

The Instigator was a Raider-class corvette that served the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. The 150-meter-long corvette featured sublight engines and five twin laser cannons. At one point, the Instigator traveled through asteroids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Instigator
- **Line:** Raider-class corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Length:** 150 meters
- **Engine:** Sublight engines
- **Armament:** Twin laser cannons (5)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
