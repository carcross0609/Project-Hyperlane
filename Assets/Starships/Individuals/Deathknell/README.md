# Deathknell

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Deathknell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deathknell)

The Deathknell was a capital ship that appeared in Xim at Vontor, a holoplay that was written by the holowright Peshosloc. The play portrayed the Deathknell as being the flagship of the warlord Xim the Despot during the Third Battle of Vontor.
