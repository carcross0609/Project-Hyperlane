# Pradeux

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pradeux](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pradeux)

The Pradeux was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was a part of the navy of the Galactic Empire. Manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards and measuring 1,600 meters in length, it had a bridge that was outfitted with a communications tower and at least one ISD-72x deflector shield generator dome.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pradeux
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Line:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Shield Gen:** At least 1 ISD-72x deflector shield generator dome
- **Communications:** Communications tower
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
