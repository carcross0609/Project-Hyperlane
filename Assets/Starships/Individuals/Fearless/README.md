# Fearless

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fearless](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fearless)

The Fearless was an Acclamator-class assault ship commanded by Commander Gett during the Clone Wars. Jedi General Etain Tur-Mukan served aboard the Fearless briefly in 21 BBY. During that time, she helped to evacuate the besieged 41st Elite Corps from Dinlo and rescue Omega Squad.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fearless
- **Model:** Acclamator-class assault ship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Captains:** Commander Gett
