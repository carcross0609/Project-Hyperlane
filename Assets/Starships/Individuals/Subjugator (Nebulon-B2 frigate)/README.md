# Subjugator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Subjugator (Nebulon-B2 frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Subjugator_%28Nebulon-B2_frigate%29)

The Subjugator was a Nebulon-B2 frigate that was part of Task Force Vengeance. She was part of the group of reinforcements that were pulled out of Hyperspace by the Rebel Modified Strike Cruiser Peregrine and was destroyed along with the rest of the group by the Rebels.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Subjugator
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Nebulon-B2 frigate
- **Length:** 253.2 meters
- **Width:** 147.7 meters
- **Height:** 94.3 meters
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Fleet:** Task Force Vengeance
