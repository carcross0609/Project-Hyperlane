# Tellivar Lady

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tellivar Lady](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tellivar_Lady)

The Tellivar Lady was a transport starship commanded by Captain Fane that made regular runs to the Outer Rim planet Tatooine during the reign of the Empire. The Tellivar Lady landed at Docking Bay 9 in the city Mos Eisley on Tatooine in 0 BBY. While looking for a way offworld, the H'nemthe M'iiyoom Onith met Feltipern Trevagg, a Gotal tax collector, in Mos Eisley who told her that he would talk to Captain Fane about having her travel on the Tellivar Lady. However Trevagg&mdash;who was hoping to keep Onith on Tatooine for the night&mdash;took his time getting to Docking Bay 9 and by the time he arrived the ship had departed.
