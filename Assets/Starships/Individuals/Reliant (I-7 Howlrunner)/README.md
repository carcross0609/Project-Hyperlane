# Reliant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reliant (I-7 Howlrunner)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reliant_%28I-7_Howlrunner%29)

The Reliant was Seti Ashgad's heavily modified I-7 Howlrunner. It served as his personal transport and was a major part of the droch Dzym's plans for galactic domination.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Reliant
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Model:** I-7 Howlrunner
- **Crew:** 1 (pilot)

- **Affiliation:**
