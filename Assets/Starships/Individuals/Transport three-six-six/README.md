# Transport three-six-six

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Transport three-six-six](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transport_three-six-six)

Transport three-six-six was a transport belonging to the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era. Shortly before the attack on the Imperial compound, the ship was docked in the ISD Executrix hangar, and a man aboard contacted an Imperial on the Star Destroyer requesting departure. The request was approved and the transport traveled to the Imperial compound on the planet Agaris below.
