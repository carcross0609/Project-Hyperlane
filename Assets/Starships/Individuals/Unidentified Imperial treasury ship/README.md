# Unidentified Imperial treasury ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial treasury ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial_treasury_ship)

The Galactic Empire employed a treasure ship to collect and transport the revenues of a sector to the capital. During the Galactic Civil War, the pirate Fandar enlisted the aid of the Alliance to Restore the Republic to raid the treasure ship, but was betrayed by his own right-hand man. Fandar and the Alliance operatives managed to escape the ambush.
