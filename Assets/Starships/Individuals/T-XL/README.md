# T-XL

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-XL](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-XL)

T-XL was a group of 6 Utility Tugs used by the Rebel Alliance they were located at a Rebel starfighter depot consisting of 2 groups of 3 Class-A cargo containers Vic and Limi. The tugs were scrambling to ferry pilots to the stationary A-wings and X-wings, but were all destroyed by a single TIE Fighter, along with the fighters and finally the containers.
