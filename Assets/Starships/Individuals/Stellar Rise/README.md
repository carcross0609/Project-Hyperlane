# Stellar Rise

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stellar Rise](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stellar_Rise)

The Stellar Rise was an Arquitens-class light cruiser of the Galactic Republic Navy that was active during the Clone Wars. During the war, the Stellar Rise was infiltrated by Separatist Supreme Martial Commander General Grievous and four of his IG-100 MagnaGuards in an attempt to steal lightsabers and relics from the High Republic Era. The Stellar Rise was under the command of Jedi General Anap Ree-Di and Captain Lozz Beraf, and crewed by clone troopers.
