# Aluma

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aluma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aluma)

Aluma was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle operated by the Galactic Empire. Shortly before the Battle of Hoth, it was assigned to rendezvous with container transport Silver Bull to transfer a VIP scientist at the SpecWar Fab Deep Space Manufacturing Facility. Both Rebel Alliance and Demetrius Zaarin's forces attacked the rendezvous and were thwarted by an Imperial Missile Boat.
