# Unidentified Nihil shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Nihil shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Nihil_shuttle)

A shuttle was utilized by the Nihil pirates during the High Republic Era. In 228 BBY, the ship was present on the Gaze Electric, flagship of Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro. At Ro's request, a human Nihil used the shuttle to deliver him a tooka and several other items as he investigated a plague on the fifth moon of the planet Norisyn. After he knocked the human into the plague, Ro commed the shuttle to instruct it to return to his flagship.
