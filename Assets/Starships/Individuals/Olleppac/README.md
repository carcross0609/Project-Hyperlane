# Olleppac

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Olleppac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Olleppac)

The Olleppac was a C-3 passenger liner that was present at Camen Spaceport where the Hurrim Lambda-class T-4a shuttle Merte, stolen by two members of Dunari's coalition escaped the Hurrim's asteroid base under heavy pursuit of Preybird starfighters. Alliance High Command sent a strike force of two A-wings to cover the retreat until the shuttle reached hyperspace. Her cargo contained tourists.
