# Resurgence

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Resurgence (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resurgence_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Resurgence was a Nebulon-B escort frigate used by the Alderaanian Resistance following the end of the Clone Wars. It was in the service of Bail Organa and captained by Adrian Verana. After a mission on Coruscant, the group onboard Resurgence came under attack from a Star Destroyer under the command of Inquisitor Valin Draco and Admiral Gilder Varth. The Resurgence was destroyed in the fighting.
