# Unidentified colony ship; (Holographic symbol of ship depicted)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified colony ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_colony_ship)

A colony ship was part of a group of colony ships containing human Grizmallti refugees that fled a civil war on the planet Grizmallt in approximately 4,000 BBY. The ship crashed on the planet Naboo, and the humans from the ships colonized the world, becoming the Naboo people. The crash of the colony ship was later represented with the symbol of the Royal House of Naboo in a holographic display used in the Naboo's annual Festival of Light in 20 BBY, which commemorated the anniversary of the planet becoming a member of the Galactic Republic.
