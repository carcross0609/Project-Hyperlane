# Verity

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Verity (Galactic Alliance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verity_%28Galactic_Alliance%29)

Verity was a Galactic Alliance supply ship in use in 44 ABY. Following a battle with Abeloth and the Lost Tribe of Sith on the planet Nam Chorios, Jedi Grand Master Luke Skywalker recovered in a bacta tank on Verity.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Verity
- **Complement:** Bacta tank
- **Role:** Supply ship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
