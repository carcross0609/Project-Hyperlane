# Beetle Nebula

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Beetle Nebula (transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beetle_Nebula_%28transport%29)

Beetle Nebula was a Freebooter-class transport that arrived on Coruscant in 40 ABY delivering troops home from the Battle of Hapes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Beetle Nebula
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Model:** Freebooter-class transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 200 meters
- **Role:** Troop transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
