# Winning Hand

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Winning Hand](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Winning_Hand)

The Winning Hand was a pleasure ship the size of a medium transport. In 26 ABY, it made a hyperspace jump from the Corellian sector to the Meridian sector, near Obroa-skai along the Perlemian Trade Route, and vanished after making a garbled transmission. It was located by Uldir Lochett aboard the Pride of Thela.
