# Acidity

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Acidity](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acidity)

The Acidity was a Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. It served as the personal Star Destroyer and flagship of Grand Moff Randd, who commanded the Imperial fleet from the bridge of the Acidity during the Battle of Jakku.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Acidity
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Owners:** Randd
