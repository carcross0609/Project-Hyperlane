# Frander's Bay Shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Frander's Bay Shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frander%27s_Bay_Shuttle)

The Frander's Bay Shuttle was a Model Eleven Corellian transport shuttle used by the smugglers Han Solo and Lando Calrissian to travel from Hanna City to Frander's Bay to search for a pilot.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Frander's Bay Shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** Model Eleven Corellian transport shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
