# Outlaw

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Outlaw](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outlaw)

The Outlaw was a YT-1300 light freighter that may have participated in the effort to rescue Bothan spies shortly before the Battle of Endor. Just after the battle, the freighter was seen jumping out of the area. Scans would reveal that the ship was carrying spice.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Outlaw
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
