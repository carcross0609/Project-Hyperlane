# Nevoota Bee

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nevoota Bee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nevoota_Bee)

The Nevoota Bee was an Acclamator-class assault ship of the Republic Navy that participated in the Battle of Muunilinst in 22 BBY during the Clone Wars, where it operated as a fleet carrier, transporting 156 V-19 Torrent starfighters into battle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nevoota Bee
- **Line:** Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship
- **Type:** Assault ship
- **Length:** 752 meters
- **Width:** 460 meters
- **Height:** 183 meters
- **Engine:** At least 2 sublight engines
- **Complement:** 156 V-19 Torrent starfighters
- **Bays:** Ventral hangar
- **Role:** Fleet carrier
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Navy:** Republic Navy
