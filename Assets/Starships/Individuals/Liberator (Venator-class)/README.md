# Liberator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Liberator (Venator-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liberator_%28Venator-class%29)

The Liberator was a Venator-class Star Destroyer belonging to the starfleet of the Galactic Empire. Five years after the inception of the Empire, it was parked at R/M Facility Four deepdock at Belderone.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Liberator
- **Line:** Venator-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Complement:** At least one Theta-class T-2c shuttle
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Imperial captain
