# Tormentor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tormentor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tormentor)

The Tormentor was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer that served as the flagship of Captain Amos and his fleet following the Battle of Endor. It was destroyed during a battle above Desevro.
