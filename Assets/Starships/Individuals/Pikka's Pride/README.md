# Pikka's Pride

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pikka's Pride](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pikka%27s_Pride)

Pikka's Pride was a Altor-class supply ship used as a transport hauler by a rebel and operated by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. It was capable of carrying a YT-1300 light freighter and three RZ-1 A-wing interceptors. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, Pikka's Pride was used by the rebel to transport fertilizers for algae farms aboard a farming platform in the Coruscant system. Unbeknownst to the Galactic Empire, who stopped the ship to inspect its cargo, Pikka's Pride was also carrying the YT-1300 light freighter Millennium Falcon and 3 RZ-1 A-wing interceptors inside of it so they could carry out an aerial assault on Coruscant. After the hauler passed the Imperial's inspection, Pikka's Pride made its way to the farming platform and also unloaded the rebel ships in order for them to undertake their mission.
