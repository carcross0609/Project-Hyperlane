# Scream of Ragnos

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scream of Ragnos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scream_of_Ragnos)

The Scream of Ragnos was a Harrower-class dreadnought that was present during the Sacking of Coruscant. It was named after the ancient Sith Lord Marka Ragnos.
