# Darth Vengean's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Vengean's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vengean%27s_flagship)

Darth Vengean's flagship was a Harrower-class dreadnought used as a flagship by the Dark Council member Darth Vengean until his death. In 3642 BBY, the ship, commanded by Moff Masken, engaged a Republic cruiser commanded by Admiral Monk over Quesh. Monk was secretly a spy for Vengean's apprentice, Darth Baras, who in turn sent his own apprentice to eliminate him. The apprentice boarded the flagship in search for Monk, where he learned from Masken and Monk and his men jettisoned to the surface of the planet.
