# Blade of Dorin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blade of Dorin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blade_of_Dorin)

The Blade of Dorin was a blue Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor flown by Jedi Master Plo Koon during the Clone Wars in service of the Galactic Republic, alongside his astromech droid R7-D4.
