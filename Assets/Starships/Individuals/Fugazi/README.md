# Fugazi

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fugazi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fugazi)

Fugazi was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. In 1 BBY the frigate Fugazi helped with the evacuation of Brigia during Operation Strike Fear, carrying base supplies and escaping into hyperspace just before the arrival of the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Invincible.
