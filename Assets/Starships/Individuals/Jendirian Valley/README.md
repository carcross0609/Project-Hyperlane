# Jendirian Valley

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jendirian Valley](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jendirian_Valley)

Jendirian Valley was an AA-9 Freighter-Liner in service during the Separatist Crisis. After a failed plot to assassinate Senator Padmé Amidala of Naboo, she and her Jedi escort secretly traveled back to her homeworld aboard the Jendirian Valley.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jendirian Valley
- **Manufacturer:** Botajef Shipyards
- **Model:** AA-9 Freighter-Liner
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Length:** 390 meters (1,276 ft 6 in)
- **Max Speed:** 420 kph (261 mph)
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 4
- **Passengers:** 30,000
- **Consumables:** 3 months of supplies
- **Role:** Refugee transport
