# Coduyo family light cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Coduyo family light cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coduyo_family_light_cruiser)

A light cruiser belonged to the Chiss Ascendancy nation's Coduyo family. In 50 BBY, a breakaway alien sect threatened Coduyo family holdings on the planet Massoss. As the Chiss Defense Force was occupied elsewhere, the family recalled officers and warriors from Defense Force warships to man the light cruiser. They were able to gather three-hundred Coduyo family personnel, and were able to drive off the aliens from Massoss.
