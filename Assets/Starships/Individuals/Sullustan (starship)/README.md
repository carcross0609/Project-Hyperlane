# Sullustan (starship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sullustan (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sullustan_%28starship%29)

Sullustan was a Corellian corvette captained by T'nun Bdu. The ship was intercepted and destroyed by Admiral Daala when she found heavy weapons, communications equipment, and prefabricated building material that could be used to aid the New Republic, as well as maps and information on the New Republic in its databanks.
