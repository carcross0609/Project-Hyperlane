# Good Tidings

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Good Tidings](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Good_Tidings)

The Good Tidings was a light freighter owned by the Dressellian smuggler Has Obitt during the Clone Wars. It was agile, making it good at slipping through blockades. Obitt handled the freighter with skill, which had been learned through years of piloting. Obitt later used the Good Tidings V.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Good Tidings
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Owners:** Has Obitt
