# Z-Rok 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Rok 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Rok_1)

Z-Rok 1 was a corvette that fought on the side of Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's coup during the Second battle of Imperial Center in around 4 ABY. During the battle, the corvette was damaged by an Imperial TIE interceptor and later destroyed by Imperial reinforcements.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z-Rok 1
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Destroyed:** c. 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
- **Navy:** Zaarin's fleet
