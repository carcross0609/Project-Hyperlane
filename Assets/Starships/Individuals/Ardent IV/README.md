# Ardent IV

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ardent IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ardent_IV)

Ardent IV was a cruiser in the service of the Galactic Republic's Judicial Forces, in which participated in the Stark Hyperspace War. Jan Dodonna, still a member of the Navy at the time, served as lieutenant on the craft during the conflict.
