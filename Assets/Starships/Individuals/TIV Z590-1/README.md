# TIV Z590/1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TIV Z590/1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIV_Z590/1)

TIV Z590/1 was the designation of the Traffic Interdiction Vessel used by Omega Squad during a TIOPS mission at the Corellian&ndash;Perlemian hyperspace intersection. Externally, the TIV looked like an anonymous battered utility shuttle. However, it was in fact a compact assault platform armed with at least a dozen weapon systems.
