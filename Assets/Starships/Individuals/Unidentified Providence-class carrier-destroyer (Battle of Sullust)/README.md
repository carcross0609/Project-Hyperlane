# Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Battle of Sullust)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Providence-class_carrier/destroyer_%28Battle_of_Sullust%29)

A Providence-class Dreadnought participated in the Battle of Sullust, in which it served as the command ship of a Separatist fleet commanded by Asajj Ventress, and fought against the Republic forces. The dreadnought was destroyed on the orders of Count Dooku in an attempt to kill Ventress.
