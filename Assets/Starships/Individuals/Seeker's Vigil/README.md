# Seeker's Vigil

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seeker's Vigil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seeker%27s_Vigil)

The Seeker's Vigil was a starship owned by the Mandalorian Ash'ad, serving as a fortified vault and monument to Mandalorian culture. In 3626 BBY, the Seeker's Vigil participated in the assault on the Spirit of Vengeance II.
