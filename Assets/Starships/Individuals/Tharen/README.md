# Tharen

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tharen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tharen)

Tharen was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate in the service of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. One of its gunnery mates was Kesin Ommis.
