# Bogey One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bogey One (Sunrise)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogey_One_%28Sunrise%29)

Bogey One was the codename of a Grysk Warship given to it by the crew of the Springhawk. Bogey One participated in a battle against the Springhawk and the Aelos. Bogey One looked like a smaller version of a Shatter-class WarMaster. Bogey One employed the tactic of dibbers to protects its missiles. It escaped the battle after destroying the Kilji picket cruiser Hammer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bogey One
- **Width:** Larger than a Kilji picket cruiser
- **Armament:**
  - Dibbers
  - At least 10 missiles
  - Lasers
- **Crew:** A Grysk
- **Navy:** Jixtus's fleet
