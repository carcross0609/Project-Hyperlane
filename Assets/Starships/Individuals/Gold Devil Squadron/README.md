# Gold Devil Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gold Devil Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Devil_Squadron)

Gold Devil Squadron was a Squadron of 5 Y-wings employed by Nami Crime Lord Ali Tarrak. After discovering Tarrak's Deep Space Manufacturing Facility where she was building TIE Defenders. Then, Admiral Thrawn launched an assault to put an end to her attempt to proliferate the TIE Defenders. Gold Devil Squadron was dispatched to attack the Star Destroyer Grey Wolf. But were intercepted and destroyed in the battle.
