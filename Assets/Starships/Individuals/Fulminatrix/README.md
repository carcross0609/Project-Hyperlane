# Fulminatrix

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fulminatrix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fulminatrix)

The Fulminatrix was a Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought in the First Order Navy captained by Moden Canady. It participated during the battle of D'Qar, where it fired its orbital autocannons on the Resistance base and eradicated it, though an evacuation of the base had already been completed. Resistance Commander Poe Dameron launched a single-handed attack on the Fulminatrix, destroying its point-defense cannons in order to clear the way for Resistance bombers to make an attack run. The Fulminatrix scrambled its TIE fighters in an almost successful attempt to fend off the attack, leading to the destruction of all but one of the bombers. It was subsequently destroyed by the last bomber when it dropped its bomb payload, going down with all hands.
