# Sabre

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sabre](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sabre)

The Sabre was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport used by the Ripoblus to attack the Dimok Science Station Youst. It was destroyed at the Battle of Science Station Youst by imperial pilot Maarek Stele, who fired two heavy rockets to the transport.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sabre
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Length:** 45 meters
- **Width:** 30.4 meters
- **Height:** 20.5 meters
- **Role:** Boarding craft
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Ripoblus government
