# Preigo's YV-865 Aurore-class freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Preigo's YV-865 Aurore-class freighter/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Preigo%27s_YV-865_Aurore-class_freighter/Legends)

An YV-865 Aurore-class freighter was owned by Preigo and his group of performers around 20 BBY. The group was traveling on Florrum to perform for Hondo Ohnaka and his gang, when they met Jedi Initiates, who pretended to be professional performers. The younglings messed up the show, and the group of performers had to escape.
