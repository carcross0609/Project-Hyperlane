# Tomlas Ore

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tomlas Ore](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tomlas_Ore)

Tomlas Ore was a Virella Systems Cargo freighter that was present at Camen Spaceport where the Hurrim Lambda-class T-4a shuttle Merte, stolen by two members of Dunari's coalition escaped the Hurrim's asteroid base under heavy pursuit of Preybird starfighters. Alliance High Command sent a strike force of two A-wings to cover the retreat. Until the shuttle reached hyperspace. The Cargo Freighter's entire cargo Containers carried Smelted Ore.
