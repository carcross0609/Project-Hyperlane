# Vembri

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vembri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vembri)

Vembri was a Muurian transport owned by bounty hunters working for Viraxo Industries. It was destroyed near Destreg II in 3 ABY by Ace Azzameen and his brother Emon after their failed assassination attempt on K'Armyn Viraxo's life.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vembri
- **Manufacturer:** Muurian Interstellar
- **Model:** Muurian transport
- **Length:** 30.5 meters
- **Width:** 29.4 meters
- **Height:** 8.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 2 double laser cannon turrets
  - 3 laser cannons
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
