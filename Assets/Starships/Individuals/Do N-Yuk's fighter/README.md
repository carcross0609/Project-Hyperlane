# Do N-Yuk&#39;s fighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Do N-Yuk's fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Do_N-Yuk%27s_fighter)

The Rodian bounty hunter Do N-Yuk owned a fighter decades after the Sith rebellion. The starship docked to the AK-BK walker where the gambling hall To-Ba was located, on the planet Olcatz. When the former Sith known as the Ronin dueled the Twi'lek Aneé-san, Do N-Yuk informed the Crusaders of the Ronin's presence, shortly afterwards taking off on his fighter before the destruction of the establishment. Sometime later, after the Crusaders were defeated, the Rodian loaded up the Grand Master's cybernetic implants onto his ship to re-sell them.
