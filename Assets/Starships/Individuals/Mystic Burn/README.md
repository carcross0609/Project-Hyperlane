# Mystic Burn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mystic Burn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mystic_Burn)

The Mystic Burn was a YT-1200 medium freighter owned by Captain Harkan Resch, a small-time merchant bankrupted by the Trade Federation blockade of Naboo. Due to the blockade, he was forced to sell the ship to Deel Surool on Ohma-D'un. 35 meters long, the ship had room for four crew and four passengers, as well as 75 metric tons of cargo. It had an armored hull and modest shields, as well as a laser cannon turret. It was a short-range transport, equipped with two escape pods.
