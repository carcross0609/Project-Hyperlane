# Incom reconnaissance craft X396

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Incom reconnaissance craft X396](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Incom_reconnaissance_craft_X396)

Incom reconnaissance craft X396 was a StealthX starfighter operated by Ohali Soroc, a Jedi Knight of the new Jedi Order. The R9-series astromech droid Nineball functioned as the starship's navigator. In 45 ABY, while searching for for the Mortis monolith, Soroc flew the StealthX to the Chiloon Rift in Wild Space. Soroc was ambushed by Mandalorian Bes'uliik starfighters in the Bubble of the Lost region of the Chiloon Rift near to a monolith of unknown origin. The StealthX was crippled and Soroc was captured by the Mandalorians' employer, Galactic Exploitation Technologies.
