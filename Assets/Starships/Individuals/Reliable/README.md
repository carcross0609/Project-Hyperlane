# Reliable

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reliable](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reliable)

Reliable was an Imperial cruiser that was a part of the blockade of ThonBoka. It was the fourth cruiser Lando Calrissian visited as a traveling salesman and sabacc player. While playing sabacc with a pair of gunners and the cooks Clive and Merle, he was pointed on to the Courteous, which was the blockade ship nearest the mouth of the nebula.
