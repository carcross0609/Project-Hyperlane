# Lark

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lark)

The Lark was a private transport from the planet Eedoq which was destroyed by Imperial forces just prior to the Battle of Yavin. The ship had been on a mission to deliver students to the Clarion Scholars Academy on the planet Daemen when it was intercepted by the Imperial frigate Rand Ecliptic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lark
- **Type:** Transport
- **Role:** Transport
- **Destroyed:** 1 BBY
- **Owners:** Clarion Scholars Academy
