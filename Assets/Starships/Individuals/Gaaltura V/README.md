# Gaaltura V

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gaaltura V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gaaltura_V)

The Gaaltura V was a CR90 corvette that was used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. It was captured in the Kashyyyk system by the Galactic Empire in 1 ABY.

- **Name:** Gaaltura V
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
