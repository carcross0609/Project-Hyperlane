# Red Squadron 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red Squadron 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Squadron_1)

Red 2, later re-designated as Red Squadron 1, was an Incom Corporation T-65 X-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was assigned to Red Squadron and stationed at the Rebel base on Yavin 4. The fighter was piloted by Wedge Antilles during the Battle of Yavin.

- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Line:** T-65 X-wing starfighter

- **Captains:** Wedge Antilles
