# Draosa Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Draosa Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Draosa_Star)

The Draosa Star was a Dynath G1-M4-C Dunelizard fighter piloted by First Officer Atris during the Galactic Civil War. During the Battle of Tansarii Point Station, the Draosa Star was attacked by Black Sun starfighters in the Bright Jewel Ribbon. Atris managed to successfully destroy multiple enemy ships before being overwhelmed. The Draosa Star was eventually rescued by Car'das starfighters dispatched by Aleas Rans'ery.
