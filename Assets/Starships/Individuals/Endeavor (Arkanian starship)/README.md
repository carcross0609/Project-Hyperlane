# Endeavor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Endeavor (Arkanian starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endeavor_%28Arkanian_starship%29)

The Endeavor was a colony vessel operated by the Arkanians. In 1 ABY, it was carrying 100 individuals to a new world. It was waylaid and captured by the Imperial Commander Rezi Soresh, who had fled the Empire and established an outpost in the Outer Rim of the galaxy. Soresh took the hostages to his outpost on a barren moon to use as bait in a trap for the Rebel Alliance pilot Luke Skywalker by threatening the hostages' lives.
