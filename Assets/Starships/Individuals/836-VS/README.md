# 836-VS

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [836-VS](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/836-VS)

836-VS was a Sentinel-class landing craft that was stolen by the Alliance to Restore the Republic and used for a mission to the moon Killun 71. Crewed by members of Phoenix Cell, namely the former Imperial pilot Wedge Antilles, the former Imperial inventory droid AP-5, and the C1-series astromech droid C1-10P "Chopper," 836-VS arrived at Killun Station, where they attempted to steal Imperial clearance codes.
