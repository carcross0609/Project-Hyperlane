# Onyx 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Onyx 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Onyx_1)

By 4 ABY, shortly before the Battle of Endor, Sienar Fleet Systems began manufacturing TIE/D Defenders for the Galactic Empire. Onyx 1 was one such TIE/D Defender produced during that time, with the starfighter's design intended to emulate advantages of starfighters fighting for the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Onyx 1's crew consisted of a TIE pilot, and the Defender was armed with laser cannons, ion cannons, and missile launchers.
