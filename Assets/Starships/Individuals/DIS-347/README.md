# DIS-347

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DIS-347](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DIS-347)

DIS-347 was a droid tri-fighter that served as part of the Separatist Alliance's naval branch during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic. The droid starfighter measured to 5.4 meters in length and 3.45 meters in width, and was equipped with three engine units at its stern and three laser cannons. At some point during the war, DIS-347 engaged a Republic BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber in space combat, which led to the Y-wing being critically damaged.
