# Xtram

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xtram](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xtram)

Xtram was a group of 3 Corellian Corvettes used by pirates. The corvettes arrived at a TIE Defender staging area that was destroyed by two Imperial Missile Boats. They were destroyed by the two starfighters.
