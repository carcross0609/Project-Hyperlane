# Rial Pernon's Z-95 Headhunter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rial Pernon's Z-95 Headhunter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rial_Pernon%27s_Z-95_Headhunter)

A customized blue-and-white Z-95 Headhunter was owned by Count Rial Pernon of Eiattu 6. He customized the engines, as well as painted the starfighter with a blue flame paint scheme. He flew it to assist Rogue Squadron on their mission on the planet after the Battle of Endor.

- **Name:** Rial Pernon's Z-95 Headhunter

- **Model:** Z-95 Headhunter
- **Armament:** Dual Laser cannons (2)
- **Crew:**

  - Astromech droid
- **Passengers:** None
- **Role:** Starfighter
- **Owners:** Rial Pernon
