# Anakin's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anakin's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin%27s_flagship)

Anakin's flagship was a Venator-class Star Destroyer of the Galactic Republic naval forces' Open Circle Fleet. It served as the flagship of Jedi General Anakin Skywalker. Sometime after the destruction of his original flagship, the Star Destroyer Resolute, Skywalker used the Venator as his second command ship for his task force.
