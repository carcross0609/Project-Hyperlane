# Battlecruiser 17

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Battlecruiser 17](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battlecruiser_17)

Battlecruiser 17 was a Munificent-class star frigate that was active in the Confederacy of Independent Systems' naval forces. In 21 BBY, when the Separatists blockaded the planet Ryloth during the Clone Wars, the frigate was assigned to the command of Captain Mar Tuuk. Aboard his Lucrehulk-class Battleship command vessel, Tuuk expected the Galactic Republic to retaliate and positioned Battlecruiser 17 away from the battlezone.
