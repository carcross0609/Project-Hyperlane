# Unidentified Munificent-class star frigate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Munificent-class star frigate (Coruscant)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Munificent-class_star_frigate_%28Coruscant%29)

A Munificent-class star frigate served the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Battle of Coruscant in 19 BBY. It engaged Venator-class Star Destroyer 622 over Coruscant, but was destroyed by flak guns commandeered by the Jedi Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi, who had boarded the CIS flagship Invisible Hand. The two Jedi first destroyed the star frigate's bridge, then its midsection, and the engines last.
