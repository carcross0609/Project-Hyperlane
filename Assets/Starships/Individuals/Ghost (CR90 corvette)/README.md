# Ghost

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ghost (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ghost_%28CR90_corvette%29)

Ghost was a CR90 corvette belonging to the Galactic Empire. The ship responded to a Rebel Alliance attack on the Nebulon-B frigate Juvaini. Despite her assistance, she was destroyed along with Juvaini.
