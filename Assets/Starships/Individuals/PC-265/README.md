# PC-265

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PC-265](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PC-265)

PC-265 was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle used by Governor Wilhuff Tarkin to travel to the Star Destroyer Optima.

## Specifications

- **Name:** PC-265
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Line:** Abecederian line
- **Model:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Type:** Multi-purpose transport
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 2 forward double laser cannons
  - 2 wing-mounted double cannons
  - 1 rear double laser cannon
- **Capacity:** 80 metric tons
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
