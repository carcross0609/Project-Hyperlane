# Shakker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shakker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shakker)

Shakker was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle used by the Rebel Alliance during a raid against Outpost NL-1. The Rebel attack failed due to efforts by Imperial forces defending the station, thus forcing Rebel leaders to flee. Shuttle Shakker was disabled by Maarek Stele in Assault Gunboat Tau 1, and subsequently captured by Transport Soryi.
