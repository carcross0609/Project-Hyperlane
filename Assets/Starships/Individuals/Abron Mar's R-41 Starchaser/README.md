# Abron Mar's R-41 Starchaser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Abron Mar's R-41 Starchaser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abron_Mar%27s_R-41_Starchaser)

The crime lieutenant Abron Mar owned an R-41 Starchaser. He used it to fly in between Takara's stronghold and the planet Katraasii. While being chased by Mara Jade, Mar would have used his R-41 Starchaser to escape the Katraasii Spaceport, but Jade had sealed the hangar that Mar had parked his ship in. As a result, Mar decided to lay a trap for Jade instead of escape.
