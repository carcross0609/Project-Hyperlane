# Gode Takrab's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gode Takrab's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gode_Takrab%27s_shuttle)

A shuttle was used by the Neimoidian trade officer Gode Takrab. It was similar in shape to a big, dusty yellow insect. During the Second Battle of Theed of 32 BBY, Takrab was ordered to fly to the Droid Control Ship in his shuttle, along with his droid TC-11, for an important mission. Two security B1-series battle droids were assigned to protect the shuttle. Nute Gunray then assigned an additional squad, commanded by RK-7, to protect the shuttle, because Takrab's mission was very important. Besides, should Takrab be killed in the battle, Gunray intended to use the shuttle as an escape way.
