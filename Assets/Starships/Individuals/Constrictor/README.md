# Constrictor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Constrictor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Constrictor)

The Constrictor was a CR90 corvette in the service of Warlord Zsinj. Like the Night Caller, its enlarged forward hold was modified to carry four TIE/LN starfighters. Along with the Nebulon-B frigate Provocateur and Night Caller, Constrictor was assigned to support Admiral Apwar Trigit's Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Implacable at the Battle of Talasea. Trigit realized at the last minute that he was heading into a trap and abandoned his support ships, and after taking severe damage from Wraith Squadron's attack, Constrictor surrendered to the New Republic forces.
