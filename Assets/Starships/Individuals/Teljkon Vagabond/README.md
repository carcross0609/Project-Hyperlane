# Teljkon Vagabond

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Teljkon Vagabond](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teljkon_Vagabond)

Teljkon Vagabond was the name given to a 1,500-meter organic starship created by the Qella to house their species' culture and history when their homeworld of Brath Qella plunged into a sudden ice age. The vessel's ultimate goal was to return to Brath Qella, use its energy beams to thaw the planet, and restart its evolutionary process. It acquired its name after a sighting in the Teljkon system.
