# Century 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Century 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Century_1)

Century 1 was a BFF-1 bulk freighter used by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a convoy en route an Imperial base under construction in the Ghorman system carrying construction equipment. However, the freighter, along with Century 2 and Century 3 was destroyed by a Rebel R-22 Spearhead and a Y-wing despite the protection of TIE/LN starfighters. This resulted in a delay of the construction of the base for a full year.
