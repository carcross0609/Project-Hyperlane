# Champion

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Champion (Mon Calamari cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Champion_%28Mon_Calamari_cruiser%29)

Champion was a Mon Calamari&ndash;designed cruiser used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during 3 ABY. Evacuees from the Battle of Hoth boarded Champion to reach the Rebel rendezvous point. The ship came under attack from the Star Destroyer Tyrant on the way there, but fought off the Imperial ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Champion
- **Manufacturer:** Mon Calamari Shipyards
- **Line:** Mon Calamari Star Cruiser
- **Type:** Star Cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
