# Rook

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rook (720 light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rook_%28720_light_freighter%29)

The Rook was a 720 light freighter owned by the Desilijic kajidic. Ghitsa Dogder was taken there to be interrogated to learn of Durga Besadii Tai's dealings with the Orko Consortium. After Ghitsa's rescue by Fenig Nabon and Kyp Durron, who cut their way out of the ship, the Rook took off and was destroyed after hull depressurization.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rook
- **Model:** 720 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter

- **Max Speed:** 750 km/h
