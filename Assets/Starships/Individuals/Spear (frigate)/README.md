# Spear

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spear (frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spear_%28frigate%29)

The Spear was a DP20 gunship that saw service with the Rebel Alliance Fleet during the Galactic Civil War. It was usually seen escorting the Defiance Task Force, and carried a cargo of tauntauns. It managed to escape with the rest of the fleet after Rebel pilots eliminated their pursuers after the disastrous Battle of Hoth.
