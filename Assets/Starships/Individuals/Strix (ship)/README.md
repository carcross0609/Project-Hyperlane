# Strix

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Strix (ship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strix_%28ship%29)

The Strix was a starship owned by the mercenary Saponza. The vessel was used in various battles by Saponza's gang during the Galactic Civil War. It was armed with a laser cannon, but the weapon could only provide one strafe's worth of fire before needing to recharge.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Strix
- **Model:** Modified CR-20 troop carrier
- **Armament:** Laser cannon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Saponza's Gang
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Tatooine rebel cell
- **Owners:** Saponza
