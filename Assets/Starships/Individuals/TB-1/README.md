# TB-1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TB-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TB-1)

TB-1 was one of the TIE bombers Lando Calrissian had stationed on Dubrillion at the start of the Yuuzhan Vong War. During the Battle of Dubrillion, TB-1 was piloted by Gauch. It was destroyed in the battle.
