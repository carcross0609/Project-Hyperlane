# Janson

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Janson (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Janson_%28CR90_corvette%29)

Janson was a CR90 corvette that the Nharwaak used to escort the corvette Glazer as it made a technology delivery to the Rebel Alliance Nebulon-B frigate Xerxes. As with the other Nharwaak craft, near Mylok IV the Janson came under attack by Imperial forces under Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Janson
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** CR series
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Length:** 150 meters
- **Width:** 48.6 meters
