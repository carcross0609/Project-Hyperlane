# Prosperous

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Prosperous](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prosperous)

The Prosperous was a Providence-class Dreadnought manufactured in the factories on the planet Pammant by the Quarren builders of the Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps. The dreadnought served in the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Prosperous
- **Manufacturer:** Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps
- **Model:** Providence-class Dreadnought
- **Type:**

  - Dreadnought
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Galactic Republic (stolen)
- **Navy:** Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy
