# Ravtin 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ravtin 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ravtin_2)

Ravtin 2 was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle serving the Alliance Fleet. The ship was part of Ravtin group which participated in the Skirmish at Outpost D-34. Ravtin 2, along with Ravtin 1 and 3 as well as Tough group, attempted to stop Imperial forces from capturing Onece 3 of Onece group which carried their fellow rebels. Both shuttle groups were unsuccessful and were all destroyed in the process.
