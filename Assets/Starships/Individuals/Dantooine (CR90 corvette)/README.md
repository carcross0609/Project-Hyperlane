# Dantooine

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dantooine (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dantooine_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Dantooine was a CR90 corvette in service to the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a task force commanded by Admiral Ackbar during the Battle of Ciutric.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dantooine
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
