# Vitiator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vitiator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vitiator)

The Vitiator was a Star Destroyer at the time of the Liberation of Kashyyyk in 5 ABY. It was destroyed by the Dominion above Kashyyyk, after the New Republic gained control of the ship, and began firing on the Vitiator.
