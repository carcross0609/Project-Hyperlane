# Aurra Sing's blockade runner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aurra Sing's blockade runner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurra_Sing%27s_blockade_runner)

Aurra Sing's blockade runner was a small CR-series corvette that had once belonged to the Senex pirates who kidnapped Sing as a young girl. It was comparable in size to a Consular-class space cruiser and had a Corellian Engineering Corporation design aesthetic. After finishing her Anzati training, Aurra Sing took revenge on her kidnappers and stole their blockade runner. Sing would leave the ship adrift as a decoy for pursuers while ambushing them from her starfighter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aurra Sing's blockade runner
- **Model:** CR-series corvette
- **Affiliation:** Senex
