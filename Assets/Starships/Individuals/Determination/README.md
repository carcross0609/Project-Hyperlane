# Determination

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Determination](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Determination)

The Determination was a Star Destroyer in service to the Galactic Empire during its reign of the galaxy. Soon after the Battle of Yavin, the Star Destroyer was stationed in the Mid Rim, where it engaged and destroyed a small Rebel force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Determination
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
