# Ceh'let's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ceh'let's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ceh%27let%27s_flagship)

Sub-Predor Ceh'let commanded a flagship from which she oversaw the attack on the planet Shikaakwa in 25,792 BBY. A Rakatan ship shaped like the Star Forge with three prongs, the ship suffered damage during the battle thanks to Deathstalker-class fighters.
