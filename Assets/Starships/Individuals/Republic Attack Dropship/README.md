# Republic Attack Dropship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Republic Attack Dropship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Attack_Dropship)

The Republic attack dropship is a compact fighter utilized by the Republic that shares several characteristics with the Rothana Heavy Engineering's LAAT series. Like its larger brethren, the purpose of this craft is to transport clone troopers into the battlefield and provide the necessary close air support. Due to its small size, it can only ferry two troopers at a time. This is accomplished through a pair of armed backpack units laid back-to-back orientation at the aft section of the vehicle, connected to the trooper only by a harness that engages with their belt.

- **Name:** Republic Attack Dropship
