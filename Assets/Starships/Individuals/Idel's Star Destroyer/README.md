# Idel's Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Idel's Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Idel%27s_Star_Destroyer)

An Imperial-class Star Destroyer was commanded by Commodore Idel during his mission to capture the stolen yacht Imperialis. Following his failure to prevent the yacht escaping the Castell system, Idel committed suicide onboard the vessel's bridge.
