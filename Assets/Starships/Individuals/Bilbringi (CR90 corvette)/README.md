# Bilbringi (CR90 corvette)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bilbringi (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bilbringi_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Bilbringi was a CR90 corvette used by the Habassa. The Imperial frigate Mayhem attempted to attack the Filve and the Bilbringi believing it to be a secret meeting between the Rebels and the Habassa. However, the Rebels leaked the information intending to capture the Mayhem. The operation was a success.
