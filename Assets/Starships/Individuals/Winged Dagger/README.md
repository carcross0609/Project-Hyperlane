# Winged Dagger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Winged Dagger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Winged_Dagger)

The Winged Dagger was a Corporate Sector Authority ChaseMaster frigate serving under Captain Syndor in the Lost Tribe of Sith's armada around 44 ABY. That year, command of the Winged Dagger was given to Leeha Faal, with Syndor as second-in-command. After Faal's death while beyond shadows during the mission to the Maw, however, command of the ship was given back to Syndor.
