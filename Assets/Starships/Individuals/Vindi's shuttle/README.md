# Vindi's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vindi's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vindi%27s_shuttle)

Vindi's shuttle was a Sheathipede-class transport shuttle was used by Nuvo Vindi during the Clone Wars. It was later used by Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker to transport Vindi to Theed for trial.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vindi's shuttle
- **Manufacturer:** Haor Chall Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** Sheathipede-class transport shuttle
- **Type:** Transport shuttle
- **Length:** 14.4 meters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Galactic Republic (Captured)
- **Navy:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy
  - Galactic Republic Navy (captured)
