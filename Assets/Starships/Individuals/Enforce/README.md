# Enforce

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Enforce](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enforce)

Enforce was a Victory I-class Star Destroyer in the Corporate Sector Authority under the command of Baruche Sloane. Commanded by Commander Baruche Sloane, it was assigned to customs duty in the Kalinda system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Enforce
- **Manufacturer:** Rendili StarDrive
- **Type:** Victory I-class Star Destroyer
- **Role:** Customs
- **Affiliation:** Corporate Sector Authority
- **Captains:** Baruche Sloane
