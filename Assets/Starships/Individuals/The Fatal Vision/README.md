# The Fatal Vision

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [The Fatal Vision](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fatal_Vision)

The Fatal Vision was the name that Zhen Mirat gave to the ship Tine's Future after he acquired it from Vazan Felix. At the time that he acquired it, it was a stock KS-500 Spaceways Transport light freighter, but he modified it so that it held two heavy laser cannons, one medium ion cannon, and two concussion missiles. It was used as the base of operations for his pirate group, The Fatal Visionaries.
