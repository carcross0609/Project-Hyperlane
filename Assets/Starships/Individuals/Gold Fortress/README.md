# Gold Fortress

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gold Fortress](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Fortress)

The Gold Fortress was a ship in the Imperial Remnant task force that participated in the Second Battle of Fondor. The crew of this ship warned Darth Caedus to stay away from the Bloodfin as he was trying to rescue his apprentice, Tahiri Veila.
