# Darth Marr's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Marr's flagship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Marr%27s_flagship)

Darth Marr's flagship, also known as Darth Marr's frigate, was a Terminus-class destroyer that was used by the reconstituted Sith Empire's Dark Council member Darth Marr during the Galactic War while campaigning at Ziost and then later within Wild Space before it was destroyed during an engagement with the Eternal Fleet.
