# Gilded Meteor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gilded Meteor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gilded_Meteor)

The Gilded Meteor was a prototype bomber that was developed by the Galactic Republic and was sent on its test flight in the Ferra sector during the Galactic War. The reconstituted Sith Empire attacked the bomber above Hypori, but was repelled.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gilded Meteor
- **Type:** Bomber
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
