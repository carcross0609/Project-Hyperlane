# Quickwit

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quickwit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quickwit)

The Quickwit was a Galactic Republic courier ship. It had a crew of six, including Captain Kendra Tissido. Before the start of the Clone Wars, it was attacked by a frigate and five droid starfighters when its crew dropped out of hyperspace to recalibrate its navcomputer settings in the Ando system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Quickwit
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Crew:** 6
- **Role:** Courier
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Captains:** Kendra Tissido
