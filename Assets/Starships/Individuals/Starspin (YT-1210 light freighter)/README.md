# Starspin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starspin (YT-1210 light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starspin_%28YT-1210_light_freighter%29)

Starspin was a YT-1210 light freighter owned and operated by the smugglers Cev Malanx and Regec Sloom. During the early years of the New Republic, the Starspin smuggled goods throughout the Corva sector.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Starspin
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** YT-1210 light freighter
- **Max Speed:** 800 kmh
- **Armament:**
  - 1 laser cannon
  - 2 Concussion missile launchers
- **Owners:**
  - Cev Malanx
  - Regec Sloom
