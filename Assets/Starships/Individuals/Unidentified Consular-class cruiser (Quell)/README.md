# Unidentified Consular-class cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Consular-class cruiser (Quell)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Consular-class_cruiser_%28Quell%29)

A Republic frigate was part of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. Jedi General Anakin Skywalker used it to evacuate Jedi General Aayla Secura and some of her troops from her flagship Liberty during the Battle of Quell. However, during the frigate's attempt to return to Skywalker's flagship, the Resolute, the ship was damaged by Separatist fire, killing the pilot and causing the hyperdrive to activate, flinging the starship into hyperspace on an unknown trajectory, carrying both Jedi Generals, Skywalker's Padawan Ahsoka Tano, Clone Commander Bly, Clone Captain Rex, and clone troopers Lucky, Flash, and Cameron. Skywalker had also been severely injured holding back an explosion while the others boarded the ship.
