# Commerce

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Commerce (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commerce_%28starship%29)

The Commerce was a scouting vessel that was used by the Corporate Sector Authority. In 403 BBY, the vessel disappeared while attempting to make an orbital scan of the planet Nyriaan, in the Luire system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Commerce
- **Role:** Scout vessel
- **Affiliation:** Corporate Sector Authority
