# Golden Opportunity

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Golden Opportunity](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Opportunity)

Golden Opportunity was a freighter that was operated by the company Skydove Freight during the era of the New Republic, and it was flown by the Quarren pilot Pocka.
