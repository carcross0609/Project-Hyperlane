# Lonely Five

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lonely Five (starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lonely_Five_%28starfighter%29)

Lonely Five was the personal starfighter of the Jedi Knight and archaeologist Rachi Sitra. It was a blue and white Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor named after the Lonely Five Range of mountains on her home planet of Ryloth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lonely Five
- **Model:** Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Armament:**
  - Laser cannons (2)
  - Secondary ion cannons (2)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Owners:** Rachi Sitra
