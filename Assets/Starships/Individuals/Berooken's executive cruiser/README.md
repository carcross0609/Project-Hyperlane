# Berooken's executive cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Berooken's executive cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berooken%27s_executive_cruiser)

During the Great Galactic War, the Supreme Chancellor of the Galactic Republic made use of an executive cruiser to transport him across the galaxy. The cruiser was large, majestic, and heavily armed, but fell in 3653 BBY when the Sith Empire sacked Coruscant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Berooken's executive cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Armament:** Heavy
- **Destroyed:** 3653 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Owners:** Supreme Chancellor Berooken
