# Bounty

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bounty (warship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bounty_%28warship%29)

The Bounty was a warship operated by the Galactic Alliance in the years following the Yuuzhan Vong War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bounty
- **Model:** Viscount-class Star Defender
- **Length:** 17,000 meters
- **Complement:** Mothma Squadron, Qaresi Squadron, Bronzium Squadron
- **Role:** Dreadnaught
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
- **Captains:** Captain Piris
