# Blade of Dorin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blade of Dorin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blade_of_Dorin/Legends)

Blade of Dorin was a Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor used by Jedi General Plo Koon's during the Clone Wars in service to the Galactic Republic. It was named after the planet that the Kel Dor Jedi Master was from, Dorin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blade of Dorin
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Systems Engineering
- **Line:** Delta-series
- **Model:** Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Armament:** Taim & Bak dual Laser cannons (2)
- **Crew:**

  - Astromech droid
- **Passengers:** None
- **Role:** Interceptor
- **Affiliation:**

  - Jedi Order
- **Owners:** Jedi Order
- **Captains:** Plo Koon
