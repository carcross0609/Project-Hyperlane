# K-Com

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K-Com](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-Com)

K-Com was a pair of Xiytiar-class heavy transports that were present at Camen Spaceport where the Hurrim Lambda-class T-4a shuttle Merte, stolen by two members of Dunari's coalition escaped the Hurrim's asteroid base under heavy pursuit of Preybird starfighters. Alliance High Command sent a strike force of two A-wings to cover the retreat. Until the shuttle reached hyperspace. K-Com's cargoes were Communication satellites and Communication computers.
