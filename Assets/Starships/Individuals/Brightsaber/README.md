# Brightsaber

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Brightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brightsaber)

The Brightsaber was a heavy cruiser that served within the fleet of the New Republic. It took part in the New Republic's attack on the Galactic Empire's forces at the planet Jakku in 5 ABY, where it was targeted and destroyed by the Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing. After investigating the loss of contact with the cruiser, the New Republic General Hera Syndulla was able to discover the 204th's actions above Jakku.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Brightsaber
- **Type:** Heavy cruiser
- **Destroyed:** 5 ABY, Jakku
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
