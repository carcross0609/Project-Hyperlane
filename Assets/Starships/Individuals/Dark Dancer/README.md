# Dark Dancer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dark Dancer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Dancer)

The Dark Dancer was a starship that served under the portion of the Lost Tribe of Sith's armada commanded by Sith Saber Gavar Khai. In 44 ABY, when Khai defected from the Tribe to join the dark side entity Abeloth, the Dark Dancer suffered a hit and failed to respond to Khai's attempts to transmit his orders. Even though he knew the vessel might still have been loyal to him, he did not want to risk letting it survive if it was not, and ordered that his flagship, the Black Wave destroy it.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dark Dancer
- **Hyperdrive:** Hyperdrive capable
- **Destroyed:** 44 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Lost Tribe of Sith
- **Navy:** Sith armada
