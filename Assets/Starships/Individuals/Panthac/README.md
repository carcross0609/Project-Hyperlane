# Panthac

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Panthac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panthac)

The Panthac was a Imperial Mandator III-class dreadnought. In 10 ABY, the Panthac and the Executor-class Star Dreadnought Whelm destroyed each other above the Core Worlds planet Alsakan during a battle part of the Imperial Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Panthac
- **Model:** Mandator III-class dreadnought
- **Destroyed:** 10 ABY, Alsakan
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
