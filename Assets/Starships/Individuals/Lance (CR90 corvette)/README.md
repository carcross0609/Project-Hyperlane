# Lance (CR90 corvette)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lance (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lance_%28CR90_corvette%29)

Lance was a CR90 corvette in the service of the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. At the time of the Battle of Hoth, it was part of the Defiance Task Force, and after a brief campaign escaped the Anoat sector along with the rest of the task force. It carried a cargo of thermal units, likely either bound for Echo Base or being evacuated from there.
