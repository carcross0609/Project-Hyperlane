# Double-A

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Double-A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-A)

The Double-A was a modified RZ-1 A-wing interceptor that was created by Novaldex twenty-four years after the Battle of Endor. It had modded fins, and a metal coating that made its hull shiny and reflective like a mirror. Han Solo flew the Double-A for a test run during a Corellian racing event known as The Evil Eye.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Double-A
- **Manufacturer:** Novaldex
- **Model:** RZ-1 A-wing interceptor
- **Type:** Starfighter
