# Harbinger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harbinger (Imperial II-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harbinger_%28Imperial_II-class%29)

The Harbinger was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer that was in service with the Imperial Remnant Navy as of the Second Galactic Civil War in 41 ABY. The warship participated in the Second Battle of Roche and was assaulted during the attack by forces of the Jedi Coalition and Mandalorian Protectors. The attack, which resulted in the warship's destruction, was a cover to allow the Jedi Knight Jaina Solo to penetrate the defenses around Nickel One, in order to kill Darth Caedus.
