# Rubol

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rubol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rubol)

Rubol was a Class-C Cargo Containers near the Mon Calamari Cruiser Link which was attempting to warn the Rebel Fleet stationed on Sullust that the second Death Star was fully operational and the Rebels were heading into a trap. However, the hyperdrive engines had overloaded and the ship was stranded. The Link then sent a transport Fetch to deliver Fuel Rods from Class-C cargo container Rubol when it was intercepted and destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele, who then inspected the container to find fuel rods as well, and destroyed it. Soon the Link was captured and sent to Imperial Intelligence.
