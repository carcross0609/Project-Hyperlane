# Limnate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Limnate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Limnate)

Limnate was a GR-75 medium transport in service with the Alliance Fleet during the Galactic Civil War. The ship was present at the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Limnate
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Alliance Fleet
