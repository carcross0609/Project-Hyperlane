# Silver Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Silver Star (G9 Rigger-class light freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silver_Star_%28G9_Rigger-class_light_freighter%29)

The Silver Star was a G9 Rigger-class light freighter produced by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. During the Galactic Civil War, the Silver Star was captained by Saral Aenet, and was attacked by a Pathfinder-class scout ship carrying bounty hunters hired by Erron Irbian.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Silver Star
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** G9 Rigger-class light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Crew:** 7
- **Captains:** Saral Aenet
