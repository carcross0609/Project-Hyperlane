# Liswarr

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Liswarr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liswarr)

The Liswarr was a Wookiee gunship owned by Doshanalawook, a Trandoshan who earned the nickname "Chainbreaker" for her activities freeing Wookiees from captivity. In 3 ABY, Doshanalawook's clutchmate, Bossk'wassak'Cradossk, boarded the ship in an attempt to capture her, though she escaped and was able to secure a promise from Bossk that he would not hunt Wookiees ever again.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Liswarr
- **Type:** Gunship
- **Captains:** Doshanalawook
