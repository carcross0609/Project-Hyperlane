# Herriar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Herriar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Herriar)

The Herriar was a starship owned by the Jon-Tow Economic Development Group during the Galactic Civil War. The ship participated in the blockade of Pinett.
