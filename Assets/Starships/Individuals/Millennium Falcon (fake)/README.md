# Millennium Falcon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Millennium Falcon (fake)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Millennium_Falcon_%28fake%29)

A fake Millennium Falcon was used by a team of Noghri Death Commandos on Bpfassh in 9 ABY in an attempt to capture Leia Organa Solo and her unborn twins for the Dark Jedi Joruus C'baoth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Millennium Falcon
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Destroyed:** 9 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Noghri Death Commandos
