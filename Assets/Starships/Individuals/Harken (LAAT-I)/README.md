# Harken

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harken (LAAT/I)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harken_%28LAAT/I%29)

The Harken was a Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry (LAAT/i) gunship that saw service with the Grand Army of the Republic during the early stages of the Clone Wars. Harken provided air support for Delta Squad at the First Battle of Geonosis, during which it destroyed an OG-9 homing spider droid that had been threatening squad members Boss and Scorch.
