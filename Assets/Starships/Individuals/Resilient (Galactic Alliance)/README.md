# Resilient (Galactic Alliance)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Resilient (Galactic Alliance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resilient_%28Galactic_Alliance%29)

The Resilient was a Galactic Alliance warship that took part in the Second Battle of Fondor. It was among the vessels that joined with Admiral Cha Niathal and accepted Kral Nevil after he defected to Niathal's Alliance-in-exile.
