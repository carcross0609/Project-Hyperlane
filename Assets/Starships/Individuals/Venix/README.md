# Venix

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Venix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Venix)

The Venix was a starship that made regular deliveries for Viraxo Industries. Sometime after the Battle of Hoth in 3 ABY, Viraxo Industries attempted to sabotage their rivals, the Azzameen-owned shipping company Twin Suns Transport Services. In retribution, Antan Azzameen, co-owner of Twin Suns Transport Services, instructed his niece Aeron Azzameen to install the YT-1300 light freighter Selu with fake transponder codes to pass off that freighter as the Venix.
