# Exquisite Death

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Exquisite Death](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exquisite_Death)

Exquisite Death was a Yuuzhan Vong corvette analog under the command of Commander Duman Yaght during the Yuuzhan Vong War in 27 ABY. It was two decks tall and 122 meters long, with a crew of 98. It had a forward-facing bridge, three aft holds, and four main access corridors.
