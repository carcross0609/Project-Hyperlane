# KJB-87

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KJB-87](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KJB-87)

KJB-87 was a cargo transport used to transfer cargo from orbital warehouses to the first Death Star during its construction over Despayre. It was also used by Celot Ratua Dil during his escape from the planet to transport him from Machine Tool Storage Unit Alpha-Four to the Death Star itself.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KJB-87
- **Type:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
