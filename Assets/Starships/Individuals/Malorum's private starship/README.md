# Malorum's private starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malorum's private starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malorum%27s_private_starship)

The Inquisitor Malorum owned a private starship. He used this ship to get to and investigate Polis Massa after the death of his agent and fellow Inquisitor Sancor, who he had sent to investigate the death of Senator Padmé Amidala so that Malorum may take Darth Vader's position as Emperor Palpatine's right hand. Once Malorum finished his investigations and found only facts that were unrelated and nothing winded up conclusive, he looked back to the senator's funeral and finding it unusual just how quickly it had been organized considering it was the ceremonial people of Naboo who had organized it, alongside the rumors of a Jedi bringing Amidala's body to Naboo for the funeral. Malorum then left Polis Massa in his starship and set course for Naboo.
