# Unidentified Jedi vessel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Jedi vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Jedi_vessel)

Sometime during the Great Galactic War and the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire, the notorious pirate Harridax Kirill captured a vessel belonging to the Jedi Order that was carrying Force-sensitive children. Kirill, better known as the Voidwolf, personally led the boarding party that killed the Jedi defenders aboard the starship, and he took the children to the Sith planet of Korriban as a "gift" to the Empire. The Empire rewarded the Voidwolf by promoting him from privateer to commander in the Imperial Navy.
