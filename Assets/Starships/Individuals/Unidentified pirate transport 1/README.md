# Unidentified pirate transport 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified pirate transport 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pirate_transport_1)

A pirate transport was utilized by Captain Brutus' crew during the New Republic Era. Around 9 ABY, a group of pirates led by First Mate Gunter traveled aboard this vessel to the planet Lanupa, where they captured former Captain Silvo to put him on trial upon returning to their frigate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified pirate transport 1
- **Type:** Transport
- **Armament:** Four cannons
- **Affiliation:** Captain Brutus' crew
