# Marshall Awe

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Marshall Awe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marshall_Awe)

The Marshall Awe was a starship which served in the Imperial Navy of the Galactic Empire. At one point in its use it was controlled by Captain Strayit, and had Zertik Strom serving on board as a Lieutenant. Whilst Strayit and Strom served on the ship they ended a rebellion by the Poporov merchants of Armstiss, during which Strom saved Strayit's life. Due to his heroism, Strom was then promoted off of the Marshall Awe to the position of First officer on board the starship Peacehawk.
