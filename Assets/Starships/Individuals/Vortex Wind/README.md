# Vortex Wind

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vortex Wind](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vortex_Wind)

Vortex Wind was a Mon Calamari Star Cruiser in service to the Galactic Alliance. This powerful warship was part of a task force commanded by Wedge Antilles during the latter part of the Yuuzhan Vong War, in conjunction with Operation Trinity. Along with the Star Destroyer Mon Mothma, Vortex Wind destroyed a Yuuzhan Vong warship. She survived the battle.
