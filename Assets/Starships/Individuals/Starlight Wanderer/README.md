# Starlight Wanderer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Starlight Wanderer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starlight_Wanderer)

The Starlight Wanderer was a CR90 corvette that was in the service of the New Republic and participated in the Battle of Jakku during the Galactic Civil War. During the battle, the ship was carrying secret information that was being coveted by both the Imperials and New Republic. It would become abandoned and then later discovered decades later, and became the focus of a group of scavengers to retrieve this information.
