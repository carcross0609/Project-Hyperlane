# Dark Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dark Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Star)

The Star Destroyer Dark Star arrived as CR90 corvette group Quluiy were making their way to hyperspace following an attack by pirates. The Dark Star and its fighters were unable to stop the corvettes, as were a group of Assault Gunboats that arrived after the Pirates were fended off.
