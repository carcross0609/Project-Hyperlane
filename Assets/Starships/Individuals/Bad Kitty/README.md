# Bad Kitty

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bad Kitty](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bad_Kitty)

Bad Kitty was an LAAT gunship. It had customized nose art painted on either of its sides, with a nexu riding a large bomb and the ship's name written in Aurebesh on the bomb. It was used by the Grand Army of the Republic during the Second Battle of Geonosis in 21 BBY, when the Republic attacked the Geonosis primary droid foundry.
