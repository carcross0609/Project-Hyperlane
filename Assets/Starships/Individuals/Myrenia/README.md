# Myrenia

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Myrenia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Myrenia)

The Myrenia was Coh Veshiv's personal Lambda-class shuttle, stationed aboard the Empire Forever. When Veshiv was captured by Dhas Fenoep Vedij and the crew of the Far Orbit, they also took control of the Myrenia.
