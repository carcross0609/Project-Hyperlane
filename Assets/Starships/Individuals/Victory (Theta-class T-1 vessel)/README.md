# Victory

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Victory (Theta-class T-1 vessel)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Victory_%28Theta-class_T-1_vessel%29)

The Victory was a Theta-class T-1 vessel used by Sith apprentice Darth Zannah. She flew in the shuttle on multiple occasions, including on her first mission to the planet Doan. After finding and recruiting the Dark Jedi Set Harth as her potential apprentice, she traveled in the Victory again to Doan, where she confronted her Master, Darth Bane. The duel ended in a draw, and Zannah was forced to flee the planet in her ship. Shortly after, she received a message in the Victory from Bane to come to Ambria and face him in a final duel for the title of Dark Lord of the Sith, which Zannah won.
