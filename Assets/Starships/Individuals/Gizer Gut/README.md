# Gizer Gut

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gizer Gut](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gizer_Gut)

The Gizer Gut was a starship used by Jedi Knight Leia Organa Solo in 43.5 ABY to smuggle psychotic Jedi Knights Bazel Warv, Seff Hellin, Yaqeel Saav'etu, and Natua Wan to Shedu Maad before they could be captured by Galactic Alliance Security forces and encased in carbonite.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gizer Gut
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Passengers:** At least four
- **Affiliation:** New Jedi Order
