# Freighter G-55-A

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Freighter G-55-A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freighter_G-55-A)

Freighter G-55-A was a freighter that was docked in Mirogana Spaceport in the city Mirogana on the Outer Rim Territories moon Toshara in 3 ABY. A coordinator announced over the speaker system at the spaceport that the freighter would be departing shortly for Mirial, a planet also located in the Outer Rim.
