# Imperator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperator (Immobilizer 418 cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperator_%28Immobilizer_418_cruiser%29)

The Imperator was an Immobilizer 418 cruiser in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. It was assigned to Black Sword Command, located at one of the shipyards in the Koornacht Cluster. It was later overrun by the native Yevetha, who acquired all of the Command's assets, and was renamed Splendor of Yevetha by them. The vessel would later be used against the forces of the New Republic.
