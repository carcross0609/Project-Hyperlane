# Stardust Glider

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stardust Glider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stardust_Glider)

The Stardust Glider was a luxury starship produced around 3626 BBY, hand-crafted with the finest materials, including Corellian gold and the best leather available. The Dar'manda Lieutenant Bask Sunn ordered his minion Jaff to buy personalized Stardust Gliders to add to his collection, which he kept aboard his ship Fortune's Folly. A pair of these personalized Stardust Gliders were a matching set.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stardust Glider
- **Model:** Transport
- **Type:** Starship
- **Capacity:** At least 3 persons
- **Affiliation:** Dar'manda
