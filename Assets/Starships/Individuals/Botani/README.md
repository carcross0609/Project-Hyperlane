# Botani

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Botani](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Botani)

Botani was the name given to three Muurian transports used by the Bothans. These ships were attacked by Imperial forces lead by Darth Vader who were capturing Bothans to find more information regarding an incident with the Suprosa, a freighter which was carrying a supercomputer carrying data regarding the Second Death Star. Despite assistance from X-wings from Junker Squadron and Logan Squadron, and A-wings from Yme Squadron, all three transports were disabled and captured, along with Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle Com 1.
