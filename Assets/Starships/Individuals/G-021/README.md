# G-021

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-021](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-021)

G-021, also referred to as fleet gig 021, was one of many New Republic personnel transports used to ferry pilots and commanders between the ships of the Fifth Battle Group. Plat Mallar was in charge of the craft, which he used to ferry Colonel Bowman Gavin to the Polaron.
