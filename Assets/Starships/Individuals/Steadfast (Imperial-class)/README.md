# Steadfast

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Steadfast (Imperial-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Steadfast_%28Imperial-class%29)

The Steadfast was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. Imperial officer Enric Pryde was assigned to the ship in 6 BBY. At one point, Commodore Freylon who served as commander of the Star Destroyer was killed during the Battle of Ponolapo by 0 ABY. Commodore Scaanos then replaced Freylon and served as Pryde's superior.
