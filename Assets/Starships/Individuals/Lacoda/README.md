# Lacoda

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lacoda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lacoda)

Lacoda was a group of Lambda-class T-4a shuttles operated by the Alliance to Restore the Republic and carried on the Rebel MC40a light cruiser Planka.
