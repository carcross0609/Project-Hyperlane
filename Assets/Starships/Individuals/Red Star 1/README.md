# Red Star 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red Star 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Star_1)

Red Star 1 was a modified TIE/D Defender owned by Countess Iran Ryad. The countess had her fighter painted in a red color scheme. The fighter destroyed in 5 ABY when she was charged with treason and defeated by Baron Soontir Fel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Red Star 1
- **Type:** TIE/D Defender
- **Cost:** 262,500 credits
- **Sysmods:**
  - Improved shield generator
  - Improved ion engine
  - Improved fire control system
- **Destroyed:** c. 5 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Red Star Squadron
- **Owners:** Countess Iran Ryad
