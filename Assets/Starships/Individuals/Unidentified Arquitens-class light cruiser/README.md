# Unidentified Arquitens-class light cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Arquitens-class light cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Arquitens-class_light_cruiser)

An Arquitens-class light cruiser served the navy of the Galactic Empire. After the Empire attacked the Son-tuul Pride crime syndicate and confiscated their fortune, the credits were to be transferred to an Imperial vault aboard the Arquitens-class light cruiser. The rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Aphra, under discreet orders from the Sith Lord and Imperial enforcer Darth Vader, led a crew of bounty hunters in a robbery of the Son-tuul Pride's credits.
