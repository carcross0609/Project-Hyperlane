# Krayt Fang

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krayt Fang](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krayt_Fang)

The Krayt Fang was a YT-1300 light freighter that belonged to Trex, a Trandoshan bounty hunter in the employ of Hutt Cartel crime lord Teemo. The Krayt Fang was docked at Landing Bay Aurek, in Mos Shuuta spaceport on Tatooine, with a broken hypermatter reactor igniter. A group of adventurers stole the ship to escape from Teemo the Hutt's Gamorrean thugs, as well as stormtroopers led by Lieutenant Herkin, an Imperial commander with illegal connections to Teemo.
