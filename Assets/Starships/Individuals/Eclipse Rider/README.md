# Eclipse Rider

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eclipse Rider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eclipse_Rider)

The Eclipse Rider was a modified Corellian Engineering Corporation YT-1300 light freighter that pilot Wedge Antilles rented in 7 ABY during the preparations for the Bacta War, a conflict between Antilles' independent starfighter unit Rogue Squadron and the pro-Imperial government of the Inner Rim planet Thyferra. It was used by Antilles and fellow Rogue Squadron pilot Ooryl Qrygg for a trip from the Core Worlds planet Coruscant to the Outer Rim planet Rishi to purchase a cache of X-wing starfighter parts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Eclipse Rider
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation

- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
