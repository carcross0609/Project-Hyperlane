# Fearless Rain

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fearless Rain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fearless_Rain)

The Fearless Rain was a starship owned by the Nihil during the High Republic Era. After the Brightbird exited out of hyperspace, the Fearless Rain contacted them and told the pilot, Cair San Tekka, to leave immediately. Tekka refused, which resulted in a dogfight between the two and backup Nihil ships. The Brightbird managed to evade the Fearless Rain by descending to the moon of the planet Arli.
