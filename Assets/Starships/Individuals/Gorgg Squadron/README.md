# Gorgg Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gorgg Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gorgg_Squadron)

Gorgg Squadron was a Squadron of 6 T-wings employed by Nami Crime Lord Ali Tarrak. After demonstrating to the Rebel Alliance the power of the TIE Defenders. Tarrak sent a Strike-class medium cruiser Nuance to arrange a trading agreement to a Rebel Carrack cruiser Prism. Then, Admiral Thrawn sent two Missile Boats, one of which piloted by Tan Maarek Stele, in their first mission to capture and reclaim TIE Defenders from the Rebels. Gorgg Squadron were the first T-wings to engage the starfighters and were all destroyed in the battle.
