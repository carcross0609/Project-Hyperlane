# Borogove

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Borogove](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Borogove)

The Borogove was a bulk freighter operated by self-proclaimed master thief Cecil Noone and his gang as of 0 ABY. The vessel was abandoned during a heist on the Ithorian herdship Song of the Clouds in that year, after which the crew transferred to the Bimm crime lord Ritinki's personal star yacht Asaari Wind.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Borogove
- **Type:** Bulk freighter
- **Affiliation:** Cecil Noone's crew
- **Owners:** Cecil Noone
