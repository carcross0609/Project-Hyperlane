# Spectre

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spectre (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spectre_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Spectre was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate that was part of the fleet commanded by Lumiya shortly after the Battle of Saijo. It was capable of carrying twenty-four TIE Fighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spectre
- **Model:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Capacity:** 24 TIE/LN starfighters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Lumiya's fleet
- **Owners:** Lumiya
