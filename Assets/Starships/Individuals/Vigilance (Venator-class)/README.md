# Vigilance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vigilance (Venator-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vigilance_%28Venator-class%29)

The Vigilance was a Venator-class Star Destroyer under the service of Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi and Admiral Block during the final year of the Clone Wars. It was present during the Destruction of the Vanqor listening station, the Second Battle of Christophsis, and the Battle of Utapau.
