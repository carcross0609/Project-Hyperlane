# 10291

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [10291](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/10291)

10291 was a TIE/ln space superiority starfighter that was assigned to Fuel Station Visavi while it was orbiting the Outer Rim Territories moon Toshara. In 3 ABY, after it's Ion engine was replaced it was shipped from the station to another location. A maintenance report left on a datapad at the station detained the transfer however the destination was redacted by the Gallusian Bosnok who was a prisoner accountant aboard the station during this time.
