# Aftab Ackbar's MC95 Star Cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aftab Ackbar's MC95 Star Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aftab_Ackbar%27s_MC95_Star_Cruiser)

The Mon Calamari male Aftab Ackbar utilized an MC95 Star Cruiser during the war between the First Order and the Resistance in 34 ABY. The Star Cruiser was the flagship of the Mon Cala fleet when the First Order arrived to destroy a group of fleeing Resistance operatives and make an example of the planet Mon Cala. Onboard the cruiser, Ackbar ordered the Mon Cala fleet to enter hyperspace immediately in order to avoid losing the battle. Together with the rest of the fleet, the cruiser was instrumental in rebuilding the Resistance navy.
