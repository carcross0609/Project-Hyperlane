# Arbitrage

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arbitrage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arbitrage)

The Arbitrage was a freighter utilized by the company Garello Technologies on the planet Ab Dalis, captained by the Shistavanen Odabba. After Ab Dalis was hit by an Emergence of the Great Hyperspace Disaster, the executive of the company, Larence Garello, ordered his workforce to evacuate on a convoy of six freighters, with the Arbitrage in the front. Before the convoy could escape, it was attacked by the Nihil marauder organization, who destroyed two of the other freighters. The Arbitrage was boarded by the Nihil, who filled its bridge with poison gas.
