# Droid Caller

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droid Caller (transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_Caller_%28transport%29)

Droid Caller was the personal transport of the droid researcher Osaji Uhares. The starship shared its name with droid callers, a device used to control droids. While working for his family's syndicate sometime between 0 BBY and around two months after the Battle of Yavin, Osaji kept Droid Caller with him on the planet Centares.
