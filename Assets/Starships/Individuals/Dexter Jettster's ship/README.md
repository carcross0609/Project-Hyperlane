# Dexter Jettster's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dexter Jettster's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dexter_Jettster%27s_ship)

This small, cubical shuttle served as the personal transport of Besalisk cook Dexter Jettster during the Clone Wars. The vessel was painted white and red and sported a tail fin along with landing gear and a boarding ramp. It was capable of both space and atmospheric flight. Some time during that war, Jettster piloted the vessel to planet Dractu in search of Dractuvian cave slugs. After locating and capturing them, Jettster returned to Coruscant and served them to Obi-Wan Kenobi, who considered them the best dish of the planet's unappetizing cuisine.
