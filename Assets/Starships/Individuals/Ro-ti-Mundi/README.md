# Ro-ti-Mundi

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ro-ti-Mundi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ro-ti-Mundi)

The Ro-ti-Mundi was a Venator-class Star Destroyer of the Open Circle Fleet, a major space fleet of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. The Star Destroyer was notably involved in the defense of Coruscant, capital of the Galactic Republic, against the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. During the battle, the Ro-ti-Mundi served as a carrier for Clone Flight Squad Seven&mdash;a starfighter squadron led by Clone Commander Davijann.
