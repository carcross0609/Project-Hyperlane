# Wanderlust

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wanderlust](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wanderlust)

Wanderlust was a starship that belonged to Daven Tam during the Galactic War. In 3642 BBY Tam and his partner Rostoni used it to evacuate Force-sensitive children from the war-torn Balmorra.
