# Long Shot

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Long Shot](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Long_Shot)

The Long Shot was a modified Lantillian ShipWrights GX1 short hauler captained by the Ithorian Ixsthmus for several missions for the Alliance to Restore the Republic. His crew consisted of his Sullustan first mate Siene Symm and the droids R2-V0 and Four-Onebee. As of 0 ABY and 1 ABY the ship had already served the rebel Irregular unit Reekeene's Roughnecks for several years and some members of Green squad division of the Roughnecks often used the vessel as a mobile headquarter.
