# Fxz'Et

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fxz'Et](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fxz%27Et)

The Fxz'Et was a Ghtroc Industries 720 light freighter that had been abandoned on the planet Kal'Shebbol in the Kathol sector of the Outer Rim Territories prior to 8 ABY. Recovered by the Verpine technician Kyli Ned'Ix, the vessel was heavily modified and enhanced using spare junk components. Ned'Ix used the vessel to tail the New Republic CR90 corvette FarStar during its expedition to find Moff Kentor Sarne, an Imperial warlord who had fled Kal'Shebbol following its liberation by the New Republic.
