# Indefatigable

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Indefatigable (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Indefatigable_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Indefatigable was a CR90 corvette that was used by the Thaereian military during the Clone Wars. It was the flagship of the Thaereian fleet during the Blockade of the Cularin system and Colonel Jir Tramsig, the fleet commander was stationed aboard it. It participated in the Battle of Cularin and was destroyed after a Galactic Republic fleet joined the battle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Indefatigable
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Navy of Thaere
