# Golden City Messenger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Golden City Messenger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_City_Messenger)

The Golden City Messenger was a starliner with gray plating. In 13 BBY, it was parked on a landing pad between the passenger terminal and the operation control center of the spaceport of the planet Corellia's Coronet City, which was also known as the Golden City. The starship was bound for the Outer Rim planet Eriadu, but was still on the landing pad when the scrumrats Han and Qi'ra entered the spaceport.
