# Dark Eclipse

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dark Eclipse](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Eclipse)

The Dark Eclipse was a Warlord Heavy Cruiser used by the Black Sun during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, it operated in the Dathomir system and later in the Bright Jewel system where it was spotted together with a group of Black Sun starfighters. In 1 ABY, the ship attacked a Nova Orion freighter in the Corellian system. The Dark Eclipse was destroyed later the same year.
