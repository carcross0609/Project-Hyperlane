# Scimitar 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scimitar 1/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scimitar_1/Legends)

Scimitar 1 was a TIE/sa bomber manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems. By 4 ABY, the bomber was part of the Imperial Navy's Scimitar Squadron, an elite bomber wing. Scimitar 1 was equipped with an advanced targeting system to increase damage during planetary bombardment.
