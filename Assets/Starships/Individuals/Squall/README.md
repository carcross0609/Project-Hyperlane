# Squall

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Squall](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Squall)

The Squall was a Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier in service to the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At some point, the cruiser-carrier was involved in a battle above a city-covered, terrestrial body, fighting alongside TIE/sa tactical bombers against a group of RZ-1 A-wing interceptors. The vessel was equipped with sublight engines and four hangar bays, and it measured 340 meters (1,116 feet) long.

- **Name:** Squall
