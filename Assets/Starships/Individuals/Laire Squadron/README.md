# Laire Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Laire Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Laire_Squadron)

Laire Squadron was a group of Y-wings belonging to the Mugaari. They participated in the First Battle of Javin against the Imperial platform known as Outpost D-34. They were destroyed in the battle.
