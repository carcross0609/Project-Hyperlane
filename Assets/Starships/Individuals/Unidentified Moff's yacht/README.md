# Unidentified Moff's yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Moff's yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Moff%27s_yacht)

The yacht of a Moff of the Sith Empire was stolen by the Jedi Knight Jaric Kaedan and a team of Special Forces soldiers in order to board the Dread Masters' dreadnaught.
