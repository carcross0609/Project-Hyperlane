# Tide of Progress XII

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tide of Progress XII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tide_of_Progress_XII)

The Tide of Progress XII was a Munificent-class star frigate active in the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. At some point during the conflict, between 22 BBY and 19 BBY, the frigate was situated above an orange-clouded celestial body where it engaged in naval combat with an opponent. The vessel also fought at the Battle of Crombach Nebula in the war, where the Jedi Master Wom-Nii Gnaden died flying into the frigate, leaving an impact mark.
