# Foremost

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Foremost](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Foremost)

The Foremost was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer in service to the Galactic Empire's naval forces. The Foremost joined other members of Admiral Carlou Gendling's task force, including the Thunder Wasp and two Raider-class corvettes during the battle over Umbara. Umbaran insurgents sent vulture droids to attack Gendling's squadron, including the Foremost.
