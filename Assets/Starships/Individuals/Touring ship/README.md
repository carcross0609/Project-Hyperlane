# Touring ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Touring ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Touring_ship)

While searching for Chelli Lona Aphra, the droids 0-0-0 and BT-1 hijacked a touring ship, killing its crew and ejecting several Bith passengers into space. From the ship, they listened in on the vigilante Tam Posla, whose own ship had been acquired from Triple-Zero. After hearing Aphra tell Posla that she was located at the wreckage-prison Accresker Jail, the droids took the ship and jumped into hyperspace, leaving several Bith floating in space.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Touring ship
- **Destroyed:** During the skirmish aboard Accresker Jail
- **Captains:**
  - 0-0-0
  - BT-1
