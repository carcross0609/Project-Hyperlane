# Diamond Tor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Diamond Tor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Diamond_Tor)

The Diamond Tor was a freighter commanded by Captain Oultovar Misk which operated under the protection of the Yomo Council, a group that had broken from the Galactic Empire, in 5 ABY. While the Diamond Tor operated within a convoy in the Midgor star system, it was attacked by the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing, a unit which served the Empire's loyalist forces and was heavily damaged, its escorts dismantled. When a New Republic starfighter wing arrived in the system in search of the 204th, its squadrons assisted what was left of the Diamond Tor and its convoy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Diamond Tor
