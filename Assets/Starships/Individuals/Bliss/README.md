# Bliss

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bliss](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bliss)

Bliss was a pair of Lambda-class T-4a shuttles used by Pakuuni Pirates during the Galactic Civil War. They were used as part of an assassination attempt on, at the time, Vice Admiral Thrawn while he was inspecting NL-1 station, but they arrived too late and were ultimately destroyed by Imperial Ace Maarek Stele.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bliss
- **Vehicles:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttles
