# Unidentified Obbic transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Obbic transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Obbic_transport)

A pirate vessel was disguised as an Obbic transport prior to the breakout of the Vagaari pirate operations. The pirate ship was operated by rogue Chiss criminals belonging to the Chiss Ascendancy's Clarr family, and disguised their starship as a transport owned by the Obbic family. The pirate vessel, referred to by various Chiss as "the Obbic transport," hijacked a Stybla family cargo ship carrying part of the weapon Starflash, leading Stybla Patriarch Stybla'mi'ovodo "Lamiov" to request assistance from the Mitth family for a mission to recover the Vivan cargo ship and its stolen cargo.
