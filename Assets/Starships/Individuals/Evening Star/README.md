# Evening Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Evening Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Evening_Star)

The Evening Star was a Corona-class transport. During the Galactic Civil War a group of rebel agents piloted the Evening Star to the Kantel system during the Mynock virus outbreak.
