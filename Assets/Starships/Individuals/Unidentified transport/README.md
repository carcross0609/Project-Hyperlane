# Unidentified transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_transport)

When Duros newlywed couple Droza and Etro Edthatt were abducted by Human beings while returning from their honeymoon, they were beaten with metal sticks and loaded into a transport that the couple described as "an upside down re-humidifier". The transport carried the couple to a planet reportedly known as "Urthha."

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified transport
- **Skeleton:** Over three
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Human abductors
