# SoroSuub (CNVYR)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SoroSuub (CNVYR)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SoroSuub_%28CNVYR%29)

SoroSuub 1 firing at attacking Alpha-class Xg-1 Star Wings.]] Modular conveyors SoroSuub served in the Rebel Alliance fleet at the Battle for Honor during the Galactic Civil War. Conveyors SoroSuub headed for XQ1 Platform DS 5 midway through the battle, attempting to resupply the stranded Admiral Harkov and his Victory-class Star Destroyer Protector. Imperial forces had positioned themselves near the jump point where Conveyors SoroSuub and additional transports exited hyperspace. Assault Gunboats destroyed SoroSuub with proton rockets, along with the rest of the arriving convoy.
