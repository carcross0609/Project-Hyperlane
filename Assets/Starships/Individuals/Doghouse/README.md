# Doghouse

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Doghouse](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doghouse)

The Doghouse was a freighter in use by the Human male Arigo, a member of the criminal organization the Exchange, by 3653 BBY. To male Human smuggler Zeerid Korr, it looked much like a legless beetle with its landing ramp stuck out of its belly like a tongue.
