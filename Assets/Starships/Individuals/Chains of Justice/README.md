# Chains of Justice

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chains of Justice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chains_of_Justice)

Chains of Justice was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer, part of Warlord Zsinj's fleet. It often paired with the Victory-class Star Destroyer Venom. It fought at the Battle of Selaggis to help protect Zsinj and his damaged flagship Iron Fist but was forced to retreat when forces under the command of General Han Solo destroyed the Venom.
