# Guardstar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Guardstar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardstar)

The Guardstar was an Imperial Patrol Frigate in the Imperial Navy by the time of the Galactic Civil War fought between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. In 3 ABY, the Guardstar was orbiting Tatooine, a planet in the Tatoo system. That year, the bounty hunter Boba Fett assisted the Empire in subjugating Bespin, a planet in the Bespin system.
