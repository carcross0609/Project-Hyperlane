# Bravo 4

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bravo 4 (starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravo_4_%28starfighter%29)

Bravo 4 was the personal N-1 starfighter of Rya Kirsch during the Invasion of Naboo in 32 BBY. Kirsch used the fighter during the space battle and was killed when the ship was destroyed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bravo 4
- **Model:** Starfighter
- **Type:** N-1 starfighter
- **Destroyed:** 32 BBY, above Naboo
