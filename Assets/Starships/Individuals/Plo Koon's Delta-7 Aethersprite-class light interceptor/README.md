# Plo Koon's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Plo Koon's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plo_Koon%27s_Delta-7_Aethersprite-class_light_interceptor)

In 19 BBY during the Battle of Cato Neimoidia, Jedi Master Plo Koon flew a Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor with a paint job similar to that of the Delta-7B starfighter that he flew in the earlier stages of the Clone Wars, Blade of Dorin. Koon's Delta-7 was destroyed when Koon was killed by his clone wingmen during the execution of Order 66, as was its integrated astromech droid, R4-F5.
