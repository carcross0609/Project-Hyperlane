# Green Squadron 3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Green Squadron 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Green_Squadron_3)

Green Squadron 3 was an RZ-1 A-wing interceptor in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The fighter was part of Green Squadron during the Battle of Endor, piloted by Tycho Celchu. The fighter's canopy was modified to provide the pilot great vision when flying through tight confines, and had inlaid fijisi wood panelling. The latter modification led to Celchu suspecting it had been constructed on Cardooine.
