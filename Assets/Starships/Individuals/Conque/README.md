# Conque

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Conque](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Conque)

The Conque was a starship used by the mercenaries of Ronen Tagge. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, the Conque was used by four of Tagge's mercenaries, Kiehart, Venters, Tucker, and Song, to follow the Volt Cobra owned by the smuggler Sana Starros, so they could be led to Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra who was in possession of the Spark Eternal, which Tagge sought.
