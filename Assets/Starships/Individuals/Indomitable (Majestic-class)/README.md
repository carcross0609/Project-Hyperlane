# Indomitable (Majestic-class)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Indomitable (Majestic-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Indomitable_%28Majestic-class%29)

The Indomitable was a Majestic-class heavy cruiser that served as command ship of Task Force Aster in the New Republic's Fifth Battle Group. It participated in the campaign against Zsinj. Years later, it fought at the Battle of Doornik-319, and at the Battle of ILC-905 during the Black Fleet Crisis.
