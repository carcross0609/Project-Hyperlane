# Unidentified Alliance Support Services transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Alliance Support Services transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Alliance_Support_Services_transport)

During the Galactic Civil War, the Galactic Empire captured a transport from Alliance Support Services that was en route to the Rebel Alliance safe world Flitter. The transport's pilot talked under Imperial interrogation, revealing Flitter's location to the Empire.
