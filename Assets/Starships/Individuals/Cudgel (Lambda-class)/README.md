# Cudgel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cudgel (Lambda-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cudgel_%28Lambda-class%29)

The Cudgel was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle in the complement of the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Ultimatum. It was dispatched to the Highground spaceport on the planet Gorse by Imperial Navy Captain Rae Sloane to pick up herself and Count Denetrius Vidian after the dissident miner Skelly blew up their first shuttle, Truncheon. However, the Cudgel was also blown up by Skelly, immediately after it landed at the spaceport. Sloane was then forced to call for a third shuttle, Bastinade.
