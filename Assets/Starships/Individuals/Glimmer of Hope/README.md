# Glimmer of Hope

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Glimmer of Hope](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glimmer_of_Hope)

The Glimmer of Hope was a starship that served in the navy of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Between 2 BBY and 4 ABY, Grand-engineer Lony Coleema was kidnapped by the IG-88 assassin droid IG-88 after providing information about the Galactic Empire to the Rebel Alliance. The droid brought them to the Holowan droid foundry on a planet to access their brain and acquire the information.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Glimmer of Hope
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
