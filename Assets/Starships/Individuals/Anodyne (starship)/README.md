# Anodyne (starship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Anodyne (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anodyne_%28starship%29)

The Anodyne was a medical ship in service during the Cold War, named after the medical substance anodyne. After being severely injured in a fight with a Sith apprentice, Vargai Javis was transferred to the Anodyne for kolto treatment.
