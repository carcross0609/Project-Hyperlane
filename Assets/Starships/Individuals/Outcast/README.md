# Outcast

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Outcast](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outcast)

The Outcast was a starship owned by the bounty hunter Fennec Shand at the beginning of the Imperial Era. As her personal vessel, Shand had the Outcast outfitted with her unique modifications. It was able to store all of her essential gear as well as having a hold for any successful scores.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Outcast
- **Manufacturer:** Sublights Products Corporation
- **Model:** CHM-series light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Length:** 30.8 meters
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Owners:** Fennec Shand
