# Sark-I

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sark-I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sark-I)

The Sark-I was a shuttle. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, an assassin droid deliberately crashed the Sark-I into an Imperial palace on the planet Weerden, which killed the vessel's crew and caused an explosion that claimed the life of the Imperial Grand Inquisitor Laddinare Torbin.
