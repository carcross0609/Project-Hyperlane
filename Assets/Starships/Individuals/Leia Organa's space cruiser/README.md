# Leia Organa's space cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Leia Organa's space cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leia_Organa%27s_space_cruiser)

This small space cruiser was used by the Rebel Alliance for general transportation. Princess Leia Organa used this cruiser to leave Yavin 4 in search of Luke Skywalker, who had gone missing while searching for a new location for the Rebel base and was eventually captured by Crimson Jack before she could find him. The vessel required a single pilot to be operated.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Leia Organa's space cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Captains:** Leia Organa
