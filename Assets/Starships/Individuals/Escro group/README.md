# Escro group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Escro group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Escro_group)

Escro group was a group of three Lambda-class T-4a shuttles of the Rebel Alliance. They attempted to prevent the capture of Onece 3 after Roe group was destroyed but were also destroyed by forces of Outpost D-34. Escro group consisted of Escro 1, Escro 2, and Escro 3.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Escro group
- **Formation:** Shuttle group
- **Vehicles:**
  - 3 Lambda-class T-4a shuttles
  -   Escro 1
  -   Escro 2
  -   Escro 3
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
