# Fang

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fang (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fang_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Fang was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate that was part of Task Force Vengeance during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of the group of reinforcements that were pulled out of hyperspace by the Rebel Modified Strike Cruiser Peregrine and was destroyed along with the rest of the group by the Rebels.
