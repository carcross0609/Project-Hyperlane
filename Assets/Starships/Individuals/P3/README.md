# P3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [P3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P3)

P3 was one of three Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvettes that was sent by Senator Bail Organa to reinforce Commander Jun Sato's unit of the rebellion. P3 could be identified by its distinctive faded yellow and orange markings.
