# Saloon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saloon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saloon)

The Saloon was a Muurian transport used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It was part of the delegation meeting between the Rebels and Dunari who arrived at the meeting in the Personal Luxury Yacht 3000 Raw Deal.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Saloon
- **Manufacturer:** Muurian Interstellar
- **Model:** Muurian transport
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Length:** 30.5 meters
- **Width:** 29.4 meters
- **Height:** 8.8 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Role:** Dipolmatic transport
