# Hydroid Traveler

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hydroid Traveler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hydroid_Traveler)

The Hydroid Traveler was a Quarren freighter which operated around 9 ABY by Quarren Shuggoth. The ship came under attack by Mandalorian Axe Woves' privateer fleet as it was carrying the bounty hunters' current target; a Mon Calamari viceroy's son who had an affair with Shuggoth. It was ultimately forced to dock on Woves' Arquitens-class cruiser in order for several mercenaries, including Koska Reeves, to board it and retrieve the prince, leaving Shuggoth and her crew alone.
