# Iso-One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iso-One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iso-One)

The Iso-One was the wreck of a BFF-1 bulk freighter owned by the Twi'lek Reom, the president of IsoTech, and was his secret headquarters after the loss of his previous base on Raxus Prime.
