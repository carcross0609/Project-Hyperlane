# Staraker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Staraker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Staraker)

The Staraker was an experimental, cutting-edge starship designed by the Galactic Empire and created on the planet Foundry. It was stolen by Luke Skywalker, Leia Organa and Mici Shabandar of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It crashed after being shot down over Alashan by a Death Star-like beam of destructive energy.
