# Sovereign Forge

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sovereign Forge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sovereign_Forge)

The Sovereign Forge was a planned mining station and shipyard designed by the Sith Empire. The plans for the station were stolen by the Mandalorian warlord Varek Tarn, but the Empire recovered them when Tarn attempted to sell them in the Cha Raaba system.
