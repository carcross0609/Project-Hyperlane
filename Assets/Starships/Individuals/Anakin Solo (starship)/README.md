# Anakin Solo

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Anakin Solo (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Solo_%28starship%29)

The Anakin Solo, also known as the Anakin for short or Black Annie to its crew, was a modified Imperial II-class Star Destroyer that served as the command ship of the Galactic Alliance Guard during the Second Galactic Civil War&mdash;a pan-galactic conflict between the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances and the Confederation. Serving as the personal flagship of Colonel Jacen Solo, the Anakin Solo was named after Solo's deceased brother. The commissioning of a GAG Star Destroyer reflected the Guard's expanding influence and responsibilities; by then the Guard had taken a more active role in the war, whereas their original purpose as a covert police force was to enforce domestic security on the capital world of Coruscant.
