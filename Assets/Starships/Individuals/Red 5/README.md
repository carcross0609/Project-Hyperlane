# Red 5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red 5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_5)

Red 5 was a T-65B X-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The fighter was assigned to Luke Skywalker to fly during the Battle of Yavin, with R2-D2 serving as the craft's astromech droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Red 5
- **Manufacturer:** Incom Corporation
- **Line:** T-65B X-wing starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Destroyed:** 0 ABY, Yavin 4
- **Affiliation:**
