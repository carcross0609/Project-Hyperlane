# Valiance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Valiance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valiance)

Valiance was a Nebulon-B escort frigate in the service of the Rebel Alliance during and immediately prior to the Battle of Endor, as a member of the reformed Defiance Task Force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Valiance
- **Model:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Alliance Fleet
- **Fleet:** Defiance Task Force
