# Sa Nalaor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sa Nalaor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sa_Nalaor/Legends)

The Sa Nalaor was a Munificent-class star frigate named for the Separatist corporate executive, Sa Nalaor. The star frigate was commanded by Captain Harsol, who had been a naval officer in the Republic before he defected to the Confederacy of Independent Systems. At the end of the Clone Wars, the Sa Nalaor escaped the Imperial ships pursuing it, but later crashed on the planet Cholganna by dropping too close to its gravity well. As a result, this caused the Sa Nalaor's main sublight engines to fail. Many different factions wanted to track the ship's final whereabouts down, as it was rumored to have a large amount of valuable cargo.
