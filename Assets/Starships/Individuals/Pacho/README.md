# Pacho

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pacho](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pacho)

Pacho was a group of Heavy Lifters that were employed by the Empire. They were inside the Imperial Star Destroyer Adarga to pick up cargo containers carrying TIE Defender prototypes that rogue Grand Admiral Zaarin was trying to steal.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pacho
- **Model:** Heavy Lifter
- **Role:** Utility craft
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
