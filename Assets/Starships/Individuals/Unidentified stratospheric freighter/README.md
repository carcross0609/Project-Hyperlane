# Unidentified stratospheric freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified stratospheric freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_stratospheric_freighter)

An Imperial stratospheric freighter participated in a battle against elements of the Rebel Alliance at the planetoid Ebenmal between 2 BBY and 1 BBY. The freighter delivered to Ebenmal eighty-five Imperial jumptroopers, who overcame a force of more than three thousand Rebel troopers.
