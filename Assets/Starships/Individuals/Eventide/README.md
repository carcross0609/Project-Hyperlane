# Eventide

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eventide](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eventide)

The Eventide was a starship owned by Axel Greylark's father and after his death by Axel Greylark. After Axel was imprisoned, his mother Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark gifted the ship to Jedi Knight Gella Nattai.
