# Squintpipe

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Squintpipe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Squintpipe)

The Squintpipe was a Galactic Republic cannonship in service by 7811 BBY. That year, the starship saw action when the Signatories of Waymancy carried out an orbital bombardment of Immalia as part of the Waymancy Storm. The Squintpipe, equipped with an energy skin and an experimental shield generator, proved instrumental in halting the Signatories' bombardment.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Squintpipe
- **Type:** Cannonship
- **Shield Gen:** Experimental deflector shield generator
- **Countermeasures:** Energy skin
- **Commission:** By 7811 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
