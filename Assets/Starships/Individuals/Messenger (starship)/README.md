# Messenger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Messenger (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Messenger_%28starship%29)

The Messenger was a Ghtroc 720 freighter owned by the Core Courier Service. It routinely delivered mail to Coruscant, Coriallis, and other Core Worlds. In 10 ABY, it was crewed by Taryn Clancy and Del Sato, who helped Colonel Jak Bremen smuggle an important datacard off Coruscant during its bombardment. It was modified with a backup shield generator.
