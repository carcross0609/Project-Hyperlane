# Windswept

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Windswept](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Windswept)

The Windswept was a CR90 corvette that was used as a freighter by the Joruba Consortium. Once, while the corvette was en route to the planet Nyriaan, it was boarded by a group of agents who were working for the Sector Rangers Malbo and Tyri. The agents attempted to steal some code cylinders from a group of Consortium troubleshooters who were aboard the corvette.
