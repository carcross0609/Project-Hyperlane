# Trigon One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trigon One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trigon_One)

Trigon One was a weapons satellite constructed by the Fromm Gang during the early years of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Trigon One
- **Manufacturer:** Fromm Gang
- **Type:** Weapons satellite
- **Armament:** Planet-destroying weapon (claimed, never actually seen destroying a planet)
- **Affiliation:** Fromm Gang
