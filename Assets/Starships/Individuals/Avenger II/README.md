# Avenger II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Avenger II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avenger_II)

The Avenger II was an Imperial warship that was active during the last days of Grand Admiral Thrawn's campaign against the New Republic. After Thrawn's death at the Battle of Bilbringi, Imperial Intelligence sources reported that the Avenger II was on a course that would lead to the Deep Core.
