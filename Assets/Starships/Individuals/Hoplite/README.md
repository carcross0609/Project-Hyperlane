# Hoplite

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hoplite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoplite)

The Hoplite was a Gladiator-class Star Destroyer that served as the flagship of a small Imperial Navy battle group at Ilum. The Hoplite was helmed by Captain Malthegn and was escorted by small frigates and gunboats. As a Gladiator, the Hoplite was 600 meters in length.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hoplite
- **Model:** Gladiator-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 600 meters
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:** Ilum blockade
- **Captains:** Malthegn
