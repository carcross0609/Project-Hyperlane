# Ravager

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ravager (Rakatan)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ravager_%28Rakatan%29)

The Ravager was the flagship of Predor Skal'nas during the invasion of the Tython system in 25,792 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ravager
- **Type:** Rakatan ship
- **Hyperdrive:** Force powered
- **Armament:** At least 3 energy weapons like other Rakatan ships
- **Role:** Warship, Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Infinite Empire
- **Owners:** Predor Skal'nas
- **Captains:** Predor Skal'nas
