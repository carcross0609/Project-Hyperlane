# Unidentified Sheathipede-class transport shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Sheathipede-class transport shuttle (Maridun)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Sheathipede-class_transport_shuttle_%28Maridun%29)

General Lok Durd brought a Sheathipede-class transport shuttle with his forces to Maridun during the Clone Wars. Left at Durd's base while he and his forces left to test his Defoliator weapon on a village of Lurmen colonists, the shuttle was subsequently stolen by a small group of Galactic Republic soldiers stranded on the planet who were aware of Durd's presence and intended to stop him.
