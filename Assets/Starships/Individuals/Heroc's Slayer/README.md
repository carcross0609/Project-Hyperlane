# Heroc's Slayer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heroc's Slayer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heroc%27s_Slayer)

Heroc's Slayer was a modified YT-1300 light freighter used by a group of bounty hunters. They used it in pursuit of a smuggler, who was in over one hundred thousand credits in debt to the loan shark who had hired the hunters, on the agricultural world of Fether. They would later hunt Alliance to Restore the Republic agents to the planet, Issagra.
