# X10-700; "Destroyer"

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X10-700 "Destroyer"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X10-700_%22Destroyer%22)

X10-700 "Destroyer" was the designation of a droid starfighter that used a Z-95 Headhunter chassis. It was a member of the Detritus Satellites, a group of scavenger droids, mostly Vulture-class droid starfighters, operating in the Dantooine system during the time of the Galactic Civil War. By the time of the Battle of Yavin, the Detritus Satellites formed an alliance with the Black Sun criminal syndicate. X10-700 "Destroyer" and Tvinnii Blackrunn were the signers of the alliance agreement.
