# Shadow Three

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow Three (Shadow wing)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Three_%28Shadow_wing%29)

Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems, Shadow Three was a TIE/ln starfighter affiliated with the Imperial Navy's Shadow wing during the Galactic Civil War. Operated by the TIE pilot PS-29-3, the TIE fighter had a command system capable of coordinating the combined power of the entire Shadow wing, while its pilot likewise had designs on being Shadow wing's leading pilot. At some point, Shadow Three flew through a trench.
