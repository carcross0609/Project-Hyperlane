# Crimson Reach

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Crimson Reach](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crimson_Reach)

The Crimson Reach was a hauler used by Qi'ra and her crew to retrieve an item for Dryden Vos. The ship was piloted by Drex and Noyio during the mission. It had a gun turret in the aft section for defense.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Crimson Reach
- **Type:** Hauler
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Armament:** (1) gun turret
