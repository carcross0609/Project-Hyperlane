# Lambda group

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lambda group](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lambda_group)

Lambda group was a group of seven Lambda-class T-4a shuttles owned by members of the Leafar species. While on a business trip, Lambda group was part of a convoy with Yavin Express and Cargo-Go that was ambushed by the Galactic Empire. A number of the shuttles escaped with the aid of forces from the Alliance to Restore the Republic.
