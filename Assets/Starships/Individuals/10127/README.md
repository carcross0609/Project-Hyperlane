# 10127

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [10127](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/10127)

10127 was a TIE/ln space superiority starfighter that was assigned to Fuel Station Visavi while it was orbiting the Outer Rim Territories moon Toshara. In 3 ABY, after it's ion engine was replaced it was shipped from the station to the Fondor Shipyards. A maintenance report left on a datapad at the station detained the maintenance and transfer.
