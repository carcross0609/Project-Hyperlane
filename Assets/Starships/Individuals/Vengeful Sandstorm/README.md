# Vengeful Sandstorm

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vengeful Sandstorm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vengeful_Sandstorm)

Vengeful Sandstorm was a modified SS-19 cargo transport owned by Armanda Durkin of the Notsub Shipping Company sometime during the Galactic Civil War. Durkin used the ship to raid others, which allowed her to finance Notsub Shipping. The first time it was used in this capacity was to steal a load of plastisteel from a CR90 corvette destined for Myomar.
