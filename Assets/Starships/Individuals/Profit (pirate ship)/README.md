# Profit

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Profit (pirate ship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Profit_%28pirate_ship%29)

Profit was a starship which was owned by the male individual named Druug and used as a pirate vessel during an ambush near the Space station, Darknon Station. The ship worked together with two other pirate vessels, one named Trust Me and the other called Pillage, piloted by a male named Larken. The three vessels lay in wait undercover of a magnetic storm for their target, a Helix-class light interceptor named Kizbon's Box, whose location they knew due to a tip from a female named Sahr. When the target arrived the Trust Me sent a binary pulse to signal the others and the ships moved to attack the supposedly unarmed interceptor, only to discover that it was armed with proton torpedoes which it used to destroy the Trust Me immediately. Upon seeing the other ships destruction Druug began cursing over the comm before the interceptor opened fire on Profit and destroyed it as well, leaving behind a burning hull.
