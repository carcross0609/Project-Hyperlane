# Eye of Palpatine

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eye of Palpatine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_Palpatine)

The Eye of Palpatine was a dreadnaught constructed by Galactic Empire at the behest of Emperor Palpatine during the Imperial Era. It was designed by Ohran Keldor as the Empire's first functional superweapon while resembling an asteroid as a means of camouflage.
