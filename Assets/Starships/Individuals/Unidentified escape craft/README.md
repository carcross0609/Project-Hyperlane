# Unidentified escape craft

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified escape craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_escape_craft)

An escape craft was attached to Polan GX-8, a Polan-717 Jedi transport used by the Jedi Order. The escape craft was not equipped with a hyperdrive. Above the planet Brendok, Mae-ho Aniseya stole the escape craft and used it to flee from Jedi Master Sol aboard the Polan. Aniseya flew the vessel into the rings of a moon of Brendok, with Sol pursuing her. Although the escape craft was damaged in the pursuit, Aniseya exited the moon's rings and crash-landed on Brendok.
