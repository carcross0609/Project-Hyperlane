# Daphoros

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Daphoros](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daphoros)

The Daphoros was one of the Hapan Battle Dragons belonging to the Hapan Royal Navy in 44 ABY under command of a female Hapan commander. It assisted in the evacuation of the New Jedi Order from the Jedi Academy on the planet Ossus during the Lost Tribe of Sith emergence, where it tried to lock the Hapan transport Sharmok 718 under a tractor beam.
