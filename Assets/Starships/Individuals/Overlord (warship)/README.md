# Overlord

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Overlord (warship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Overlord_%28warship%29)

The Overlord was a warship in service of the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War with the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Overlord
- **Manufacturer:** Sith Empire
- **Type:** Harrower-class dreadnought
- **Length:** 800 meters
- **Role:** Capital ship
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
- **Captains:** Moff Jocend
