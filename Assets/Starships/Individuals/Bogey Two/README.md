# Bogey Two

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bogey Two](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bogey_Two)

Bogey Two was the name given to a warship of the Grysk Hegemony by the crew of the Imperial Star Destroyer Chimaera. It was nearly as big as the Star Destroyer and could hold two light cruisers behind hatches. Along with Bogeys One, Three and Four Bogey Two joined a battle against Chimaera, during which it was attacked by the Empire's TIE fighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bogey Two
- **Armament:** Laser cannon emplacements
- **Complement:**
  - Bogey Three
  - Bogey Four
- **Role:** Warship
- **Affiliation:** Grysk Hegemony
