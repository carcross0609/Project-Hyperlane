# Argus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Argus (shuttle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Argus_%28shuttle%29)

Argus was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle used by the Rebel Alliance. The shuttle was launched from the MC80 Star Cruiser Ardent, carrying officers. The shuttle was destroyed by TIE Avengers that were attacking the Cruiser.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Argus
- **Model:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Destroyed:** During the Galactic Civil War
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
