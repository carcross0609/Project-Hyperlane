# Digit Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Digit Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Digit_Squadron)

Digit Squadron was an Imperial Navy unit that operated during the time of the Galactic Civil War. The squadron consisted of five IR-3F Patrol Craft and it fought against the Ssi-ruuvi Imperium during the Imperium's attempted invasion of the planet Bakura. Three of the five ships survived the battle, and they eventually entered the service of the Bakuran government.
