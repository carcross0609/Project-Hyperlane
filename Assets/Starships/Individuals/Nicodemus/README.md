# Nicodemus

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nicodemus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nicodemus)

The Nicodemus was a Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser that served with the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. The Nicodemus was part of a group of three Dreadnaught-class cruisers, along with the Lupo and the Topol, that were fitted with explosives in order to ram and destroy the Executor-class Star Dreadnought Vengeance during the Battle of Nocto.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nicodemus
- **Type:** Dreadnaught-class heavy cruiser
- **Role:** Ramship
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Alliance Fleet
