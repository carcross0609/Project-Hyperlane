# Galaxy Wanderer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Galaxy Wanderer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galaxy_Wanderer)

The Galaxy Wanderer was a luxury spaceliner. During its final voyage, it was hijacked by the Malorm family while it was passing through Corporate Sector space. The Malorms raided the vaults of the Galaxy Wanderer and spaced more than 30 of its passengers while holding off the Corporate Sector Authority's Security Police. The Malorms evaded capture and the remaining passengers evacuated the Galaxy Wanderer in escape pods before it was destroyed.
