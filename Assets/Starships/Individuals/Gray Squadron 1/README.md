# Gray Squadron 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gray Squadron 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Squadron_1)

Gray Squadron 1 was a BTL-S3 Y-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The fighter was part of Gray Squadron during the Battle of Endor, piloted by Gray Leader Colonel Horton Salm with Kin Kian serving as his gunner.
