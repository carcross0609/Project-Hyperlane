# Atraken Hope

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Atraken Hope](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atraken_Hope)

The Atraken Hope was one of the light freighters that evacuated refugees from Atraken to the moon Trilos following the Battle of Atraken. By 6 ABY, it was being used as a source of spare parts to keep the Atrakenite's other light freighter, the Atraken Memory, operational.
