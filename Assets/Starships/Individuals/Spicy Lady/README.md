# Spicy Lady

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spicy Lady](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spicy_Lady)

The Spicy Lady was a ship designed after the blue prints of the freighter Millennium Falcon, crossed with an A-wing. The A-wing part of the ship was able to detach and distract a fighter while the Falcon part, which carried the cargo, could escape. They could later be reattached. It was owned by the smuggler Jarril until his death in 17 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spicy Lady
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Owners:** Jarril
