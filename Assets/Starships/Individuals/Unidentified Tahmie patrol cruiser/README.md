# Unidentified Tahmie patrol cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Tahmie patrol cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Tahmie_patrol_cruiser)

A Tahmie patrol cruiser was utilized by the Chiss Ascendancy's Tahmie family. The Tahmie patrol cruiser, among other Tahmie warships, engaged Droc family starships at the Chiss planet Jamiron during a crisis in the Ascendancy.
