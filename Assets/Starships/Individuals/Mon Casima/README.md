# Mon Casima

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mon Casima](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Casima)

Mon Casima was a Mon Calamari Star Cruiser in service to the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a hastily assembled force of New Republic warships dispatched to Adumar in 13 ABY.
