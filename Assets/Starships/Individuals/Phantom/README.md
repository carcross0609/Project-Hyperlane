# Phantom

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phantom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phantom)

The Phantom was a modified VCX-series auxiliary starfighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation that was used by the Spectres during the Imperial Era and the early conflicts of the Galactic Civil War. The Phantom could dock with its mothership, the Ghost. It was widely used by the crew of the Ghost, typically flown by Hera Syndulla or Kanan Jarrus, but it could also be piloted by Chopper or by autopilot. The Phantom was used by the Spectres on numerous missions against the Galactic Empire before it was destroyed at Reklam Station.
