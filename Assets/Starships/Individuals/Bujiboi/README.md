# Bujiboi

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bujiboi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bujiboi)

The Rebel Alliance employed the cargo ferry Bujiboi during the Galactic Civil War. Bujiboi arrived near XQ3 Platform Pondut during the "Arms Race" after Imperial assault forces had successfully disabled all Galactic Electronics craft in the system. Imperial starfighters attacked Ferry Bujiboi while Assault Transports stripped the platform of all technology.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bujiboi
- **Model:** Cargo ferry
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
