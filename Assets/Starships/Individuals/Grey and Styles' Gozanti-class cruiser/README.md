# Grey and Styles' Gozanti-class cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grey and Styles' Gozanti-class cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grey_and_Styles%27_Gozanti-class_cruiser)

A Gozanti-class cruiser was commanded by Commander CC-10/994 "Grey" and Captain "Styles" in the early days of the Galactic Empire. They used the Gozanti-class cruiser in their search for Jedi Padawan Caleb Dume after failing to kill him during the execution of Order 66.
