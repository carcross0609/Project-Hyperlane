# Triumph

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Triumph (Theta-class T-1 vessel)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Triumph_%28Theta-class_T-1_vessel%29)

The Triumph was a Cygnus Spaceworks Theta-class T-1 vessel owned by Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Bane. Bane flew in the shuttle on a mission to the Deep Core planet Prakith in 980 BBY to acquire the holocron of Sith Lord Darth Andeddu. Bane subsequently took by force the knowledge of how to transfer one's essence into another body from the holocron during his journey in the Triumph back to his home on Ciutric IV.
