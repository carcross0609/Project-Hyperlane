# Liberty's Misrule

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Liberty's Misrule](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liberty%27s_Misrule)

Liberty's Misrule, originally known as the Annihilator, was an Executor-class Star Dreadnought that served as the flagship of Grand General Cassio Tagge in the aftermath of the Battle of Yavin. It was later captured by the pirate Eleodie Maracavanya, who expressed distaste at the name Annihilator, and renamed the vessel Liberty's Misrule. The Liberty's Misrule became the center of Maracavanya's nation.
