# Aerie

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aerie (carrier)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aerie_%28carrier%29)

The Aerie was a carrier utilized by the Cloud-Riders to transport them and their swoop bikes during the reign of the Galactic Empire. The ship rarely landed, as the swoops could launch from altitudes of 400 kilometers. It served as the nomadic pirate group's home base.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aerie
- **Model:** Edgehawk-Class Swoop Carrier
- **Type:** Swoop Carrier
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Cloud-Riders
