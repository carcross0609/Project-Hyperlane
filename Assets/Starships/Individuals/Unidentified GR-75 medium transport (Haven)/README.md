# Unidentified GR-75 medium transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified GR-75 medium transport (Haven)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_GR-75_medium_transport_%28Haven%29)

In the aftermath of the Battle of Hoth during the Galactic Civil War, a GR-75 medium transport supported the main Alliance Fleet at the Rebel Alliance's secret rendezvous point beyond the Galactic Rim. The transport was commanded by a Human male and participated in a skirmish after an Imperial Strike-class cruiser happened upon the Alliance Fleet's position.
