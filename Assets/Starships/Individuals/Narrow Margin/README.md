# Narrow Margin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Narrow Margin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Narrow_Margin)

The Narrow Margin was a shuttle used by the Rebel Alliance hero Luke Skywalker to travel to the cities of Coronet, Theed and Anchorhead during the time of the Galactic Civil War. Accompanied by a group of Alliance commandos, he attempted to rally support for the Rebel cause and recruit soldiers for dangerous missions against the Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Narrow Margin
- **Type:** Passenger shuttle
- **Role:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
