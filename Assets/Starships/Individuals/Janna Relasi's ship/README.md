# Janna Relasi's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Janna Relasi's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Janna_Relasi%27s_ship)

This starship was used by the female bounty hunter Janna Relasi during her hunts for House Salaktori. She used the onboard computer to perform research on her prey. After a disaster hunt, a fatally-wounded, barely alive Relasi returned to her ship and, once there, asked her droid GSAD-43 "get them for me." To discover of whom her request pertained, GSAD-43 checked the ship's computer.
