# Theta-2Y

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Theta-2Y](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Theta-2Y)

Theta-2Y was a B-12 Bacta Transport owned by Hamar-Chaktak through his company, Cargo Consolidated, Inc. Hamar-Chaktak allied with the Rebel Alliance to hijack a bacta transport, neglecting to tell them that the transport was one of his and he intended to use its loss as an insurance fraud. Hamar-Chaktak also intended to capture the Rebel operatives and ransom them to the Alliance for a hefty sum.
