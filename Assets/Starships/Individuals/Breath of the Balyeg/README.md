# Breath of the Balyeg

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Breath of the Balyeg](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Breath_of_the_Balyeg)

The Breath of the Balyeg was a pirate ship. It was one of the ships that answered the call to aid the Heart of Artorias when Commander Bylsma sent out a request for support to defend Shramar. It shared its name with the balyeg, one of the most terrifying creatures in the galaxy.
