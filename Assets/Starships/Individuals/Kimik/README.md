# Kimik

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kimik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kimik)

The Kimik was a Nebulon-B2 frigate used by Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's forces to assault Emperor Palpatine's C-3 Passenger Liner Excalibur. With the aid of Maarek Stele and his Missile Boat, the Excalibur escaped into hyperspace, thwarting Zaarin's plans yet again.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kimik

- **Model:** Nebulon-B2 frigate
- **Type:** Frigate

- **Width:** 147.7 meters
- **Height:** 94.3 meters
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
