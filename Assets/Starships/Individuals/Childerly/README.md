# Childerly

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Childerly](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Childerly)

The Childerly was one of two EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigates, along with the Markess Narn, that protected Vergesso Base, one of the largest shadowports in the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Childerly
- **Model:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Type:** Escort frigate
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 12 turbolaser batteries
  - 12 laser cannons
  - 2 tractor beam projectors
- **Affiliation:** Vergesso Base
