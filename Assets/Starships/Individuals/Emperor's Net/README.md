# Emperor's Net

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Emperor's Net](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Net)

The Emperor's Net was an Immobilizer 418 cruiser that joined Warlord Zsinj. It later participated in the Battle of Kuat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Emperor's Net
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Model:** Immobilizer 418 cruiser
- **Length:** 600 meters
- **Width:** 300 meters
- **Height:** 100 meters
- **Affiliation:** Zsinj's Empire
