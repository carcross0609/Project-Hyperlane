# Fuliginous

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fuliginous](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fuliginous)

Fuliginous was a shuttle used by Darth Marr, a member of the Sith Empire's ruling Dark Council, to travel in secret to the Makeb system and meet with his agents there.
