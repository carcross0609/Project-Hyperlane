# Proculus Station

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Proculus Station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proculus_Station)

Proculus Station was a space station of the reconstituted Sith Empire that orbited the planet Belsavis during the Cold War and the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Empire. It was the only official way to reach the planet's surface, as visitors to the prison world were required to dock at the station and take a shuttle down to the surface.
