# Casus Belli

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Casus Belli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Casus_Belli)

The Casus Belli was a battleship in the fleet of the Tagge Corporation. It served as a research facility for the company, and Lady Domina Tagge&mdash;the company's leader&mdash;oversaw experimental weapon development for the company aboard the ship. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, Tagge met with the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra aboard the ship after she undertook a job for Tagge. After the ship's droids were infected by the Scourge, Tagge had the ship crashed onto the moon Asheris, destroying it and all of the droids on board.
