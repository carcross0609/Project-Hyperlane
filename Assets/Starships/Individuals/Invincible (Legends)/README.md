# Invincible

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Invincible/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invincible/Legends)

The Invincible was a capital ship in the Confederate Navy. It served as the flagship of Confederate Admiral Trench.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Invincible
- **Model:** Providence-class carrier/destroyer variant
- **Type:**
  - Carrier/destroyer
  - Dreadnaught
  - Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 2,177.35 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolaser batteries
  - Quad turbolaser batteries
  - Torpedo launcher battery
  -   Tracking torpedoes
- **Complement:** 10 Hyena-class bombers
- **Role:**
  - Carrier
  - Destroyer
  - Command ship (Flagship)
- **Destroyed:** 22 BBY, Christoph system
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Navy:** Confederate Navy
- **Fleet:** Christophsis blockade
- **Owners:** Admiral Trench
