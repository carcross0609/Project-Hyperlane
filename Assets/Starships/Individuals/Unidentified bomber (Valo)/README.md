# Unidentified bomber

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified bomber (Valo)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_bomber_%28Valo%29)

A bomber was used by the Nihil marauders during the High Republic Era. It was part of a class of bomber that ran on the fuel zylium 12. In 231 BBY, the bomber was deployed during the Nihil attack on the Republic Fair on the planet Valo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified bomber
- **Model:** A bomber class
- **Type:** Bomber
- **Affiliation:** Nihil
