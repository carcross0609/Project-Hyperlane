# Obsidian 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Obsidian 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obsidian_1)

Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems, Obsidian 1 was the lead TIE/ln starfighter of the Galactic Empire's Obsidian Squadron. Piloted by Civé Rashon, who operated under the call sign "Howlrunner" and the designation OS-72-1, Obsidian 1 at one point flew over Cloud City on the planet Bespin.
