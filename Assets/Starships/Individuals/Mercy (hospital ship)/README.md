# Mercy

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mercy (hospital ship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mercy_%28hospital_ship%29)

The Mercy was a Imperial frigate, appropriated by the Rebel Alliance while still under construction, and put in service by them as a hospital ship prior to the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mercy
- **Model:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Passengers:** 5000 patients
- **Othersystems:** Bacta tanks (4250)
- **Role:** Hospital ship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - New Republic
- **Navy:**
  - Alliance Fleet
  - New Republic Defense Fleet
