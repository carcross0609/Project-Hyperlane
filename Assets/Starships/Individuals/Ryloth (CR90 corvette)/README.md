# Ryloth

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ryloth (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ryloth_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Ryloth was a Corellian Corvette in service to the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of the New Republic fleet during the Seizure of Coruscant. Along with the Devonian, the ship was dispatched by Ackbar to rescue any survivors on a crippled Golan III Space Defense NovaGun.
