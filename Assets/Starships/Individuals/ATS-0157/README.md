# ATS-0157

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ATS-0157](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ATS-0157)

ATS-0157 was a Gallofree Yards, Inc. GR-75 medium transport that was owned and operated by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the height of the Galactic Civil War. ATS-0157 was assigned to the Defiance Task Force, and carried a cargo of snowspeeders, presumably brought aboard during the evacuation of Echo Base.

- **Name:** ATS-0157

- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Fleet:** Defiance Task Force
