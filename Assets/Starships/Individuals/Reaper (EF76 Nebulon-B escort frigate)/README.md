# Reaper

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reaper (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reaper_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

The Reaper was a Nebulon-B frigate that served in the Imperial Starfleet at the height of the Galactic Civil War. The Reaper arrived after frigate Rapier was destroyed as transport group Renhoek picked up a supply of lonsigars from a group of abandoned containers. The Reaper failed to destroy the Mon Calamari cruiser Stimsenj'kat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Reaper
- **Model:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
