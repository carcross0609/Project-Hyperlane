# Lady Luck

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lady Luck](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_Luck)

The Lady Luck was a Luxury 3000 space yacht belonging to Lando Calrissian, who bought it shortly after the Battle of Endor from an Orthellin royal mistress. Calrissian, Kaasha Bateen, Florx Biggles and Lobot used the ship to travel to Chandrila. Many years later, while living as a "hermit" on the planet Pasaana, he kept the Lady Luck under an unmarked tent owned by an intermediary group. Calrissian later traveled to the moon Ajan Kloss aboard the Lady Luck to help the Resistance.
