# Adon Fox's wife's transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Adon Fox's wife's transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adon_Fox%27s_wife%27s_transport)

During the Galactic Civil War, a transport that was transporting Rebel Alliance pilot Adon Fox's wife was destroyed during the Battle of Hoth by the Imperial I-class Star Destroyer Devastator under the command of Admiral Jhared Montferrat.
