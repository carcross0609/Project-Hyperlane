# Dagger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dagger (Galactic Republic)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dagger_%28Galactic_Republic%29)

Dagger was a starship in the service of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. In 21 BBY, it was sent with a complement of clone troopers under the command of High Jedi General Mace Windu to safeguard Kothlis in the wake of a Separatist invasion there, before being sent to participate in an assault on the planet Lanteeb.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dagger
- **Armament:** Clone troopers
- **Role:**
  - Transport
  - Assault craft
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Navy:** Republic Navy
- **Owners:** High Jedi General Mace Windu
