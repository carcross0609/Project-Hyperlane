# Aspiration

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aspiration (Victory-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aspiration_%28Victory-class%29)

Aspiration was a Victory-class Star Destroyer that came into the Imperial Fleet right after the Battle of Endor and joined Delak Krennel when he installed himself as the Prince-Admiral of the Ciutric Hegemony. It was on station around the fourth planet of the Corvis Minor system. The ship did not bother scanning ships that the commander assumed were part of the fleet and thus allowed the Errant Venture to jump in-system and recover the remains of a fight between Rogue Squadron and Krennel's TIEs.
