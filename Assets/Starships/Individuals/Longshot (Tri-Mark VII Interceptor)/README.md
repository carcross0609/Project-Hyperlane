# Longshot

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Longshot (Tri-Mark VII Interceptor)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Longshot_%28Tri-Mark_VII_Interceptor%29)

The Longshot was a modified Tri-Mark VII Interceptor and Meeko Ghintee's personal starship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Longshot
- **Manufacturer:** Elsinore-Cordova Engineering
- **Model:** Modified Tri-Mark VII Interceptor
- **Type:** Heavy starfighter
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Width:** 30 meters
- **Height:** 15 meters
- **Engine:** 3 Elsinore-Cordova Turbodyne engines
- **Armament:**
  - 6 dual laser cannon turrets (auto-targeting)
  - 1 ventral concussion missile launcher
- **Crew:**
  - 1 pilot
  - 1 copilot/gunner
  - 1 auxiliary turret gunner
  - 2 astromech droids
- **Passengers:**
  - 20 troops
  - 4 prisoners
- **Capacity:** 90 cubic meters (approximate)
- **Role:** Bounty hunter vessel
- **Destroyed:** 32 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Republic Security Force
  - Meeko Ghintee (Stolen)
