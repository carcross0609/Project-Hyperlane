# IG-88's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IG-88's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG-88%27s_starship)

A starship was operated by the droid bounty hunter IG-88. The starship originally was operated by RB-919, a cyborg and droid engineer, and was crewed by a number of droids. When RB-919 was hired by the bounty hunter Deva Lompop to repair IG-88, they traveled in their starship to an astronomical object that hosted the junkyard where the droid's remains were located. RB-919 brought IG-88 aboard their vessel and repaired him; however, the droid bounty hunter, following the first of Lompop's directives to kill those who were no longer needed, shot the engineer and destroyed their droid crew, commandeering their starship.
