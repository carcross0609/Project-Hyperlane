# Relentless

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Relentless](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Relentless)

The Relentless was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer commanded by Admiral Kassius Konstantine. On the day of the Galactic Empire's fifteenth anniversary, it was requisitioned by the Grand Inquisitor, the Emperor's Jedi hunter, to give chase to a group of rebellious individuals who tried to smuggle Tseebo off of the planet Lothal.
