# Ace of Staves

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ace of Staves](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ace_of_Staves)

The Ace of Staves was a cargo ship owned by the pilot Koley Linn during the High Republic Era. Around 230 BBY, Linn and his starship were trapped aboard the space station Starlight Beacon when the Nihil pirates enacted plans to destroy the station. A disreputable individual, Linn was killed during attempts to escape, leading the pirate Nan to steal the abandoned Ace of Staves for herself while fleeing the remains of the bottom half of the station.
