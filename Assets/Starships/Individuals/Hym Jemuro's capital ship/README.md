# Hym Jemuro's capital ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hym Jemuro's capital ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hym_Jemuro%27s_capital_ship)

Captain Hym Jemuro commanded a capital ship in the navy of the Eternal Alliance. Several Skytroopers, battle droids whom had previously been the ground forces of the Eternal Empire that the Alliance opposed, were stationed on the capital ship, but Jemuro imprisoned them in the cargo hold on the grounds that he believed they were plotting a mutiny. The capital ship was later navigating the Kauron asteroid field when it suffered critical damage and was repaired by the escaped skytroopers, saving it and the crew.
