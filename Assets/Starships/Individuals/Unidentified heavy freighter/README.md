# Unidentified heavy freighter; (Wreckage after Holdo maneuver)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified heavy freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_heavy_freighter)

A Resistance heavy freighter was active in the galaxy during the time of the First Order-Resistance War. Concurrently with the Battle of Exegol in 35 ABY, the freighter came into contact with a First Order Resurgent-class Star Destroyer above the Forest Moon of Endor. Utilizing a technique known as the Holdo maneuver, the crew of the freighter rammed into the Star Destroyer at lightspeed, sacrificing themselves to destroy the fearsome vessel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified heavy freighter; (Wreckage after Holdo maneuver)
- **Type:** Heavy freighter
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Destroyed:** 35 ABY, Endor system
- **Affiliation:** Resistance
