# Star Jewel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Star Jewel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Jewel)

The Star Jewel was a starship manufactured by Ubrikkian Industries that was owned by the crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure during the Imperial Era. At some prior to or during 3 ABY, Jabba's majordomo Bib Fortuna was on board the vessel when it made a layover stop on the world of Nar Haaska, where the shipjacker Bandit Billie and her crew of Taurill attempted to steal the ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Star Jewel
- **Manufacturer:** Ubrikkian Industries
- **Model:** Modified Minstrel-class space yacht
- **Affiliation:** Jabba Desilijic Tiure's criminal empire
- **Owners:** Jabba Desilijic Tiure
