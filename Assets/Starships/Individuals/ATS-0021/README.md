# ATS-0021

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ATS-0021](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ATS-0021)

ATS-0021 was a GR-75 medium transport belonging to the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. ATS-0021 was assigned to the Defiance Task Force and carried a cargo of medical supplies.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ATS-0021
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Fleet:** Defiance Task Force
