# Gilded Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gilded Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gilded_Star)

The Gilded Star was a treasury ship used by the Eternal Empire of Zakuul to safekeep the valuables obtained in the years it spent pillaging the galaxy following the Eternal Empire's conquest. The ship was guarded by a full battalion of Knights of Zakuul and Skytroopers and had a vault so strong, it could survive the rest of the ship being reduced to atoms. The ship would spend most of its time hiding deep in the atmosphere of gas giant Vandin, only coming up annually for air. Senya Tirall spent some time overseeing the Gilded Star while serving under the Emperor Valkorion.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gilded Star
- **Cargohandling:** Secure vault for valuables
- **Role:** Treasury ship
