# Banshee

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Banshee (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Banshee_%28starship%29)

The Banshee was a Lancer-class pursuit craft which served as the personal starship of bounty hunter Asajj Ventress during the Clone Wars. The Banshee featured two cabins, a small galley, and a cargo hold, used for holding bounties prisoner.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Banshee
- **Model:** Modified Lancer-class pursuit craft
- **Type:** Pursuit craft
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Countermeasures:** Cloaking device
- **Armament:**
  - Turret-mounted Triple blaster (1)
  - Anti-personnel blaster cannons (2)
  - Heavy laser cannons (2)
- **Destroyed:** 19 BBY, during the second battle of Christophsis
- **Owners:** Asajj Ventress
