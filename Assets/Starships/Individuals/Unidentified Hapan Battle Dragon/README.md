# Unidentified Hapan Battle Dragon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Hapan Battle Dragon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Hapan_Battle_Dragon)

A Hapan Battle Dragon was used as the base of operations for the Jedi Coalition mission that was dispatched to the Roche system in 41 ABY. While the Hapan Battle Dragon was in the Transitory Mists, the operation was launched from its hangars.
