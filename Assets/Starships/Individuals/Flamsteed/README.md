# Flamsteed

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Flamsteed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flamsteed)

The Flamsteed was a large utilitarian freighter in operation around the time of the formation of the Alliance to Restore the Republic in 2 BBY. Manned by a small, peculiar crew, the starship flew the same uneventful route for several dozens of years, and on occasion, its crew accepted high-paying jobs to transport passengers to Yavin 4, an Outer Rim moon that served as an important base for anti-Imperial insurgents of the incipient rebellion. While the crew was comfortable with accepting these risky jobs, they needed to bribe key individuals along the way.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flamsteed
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Role:** Transporting cargo and crew
