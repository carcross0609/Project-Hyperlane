# Lucky Lancer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lucky Lancer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucky_Lancer)

The Lucky Lancer was a Rendaran-class assault shuttle famous for surviving over one hundred combat missions during the Great Galactic War. It was later stored in the Corellian Museum of Starships. While General Elin Garza reccomended destroying the Lancer to clear the landing pad it was parked on, the Corellians instead had Meteor, the commander of Havoc Squad, escort them inside the Museum so they could fly the ship elsewhere.
