# Spider (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spider (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_%28corvette_group%29)

Spider was the designation of a pair of CR90 corvettes belonging to the Galactic Empire. They responded to a Rebel attack at a cargo transfer area where new surveillance probes were to be delivered by the BFF-1 bulk freighter Libom. Both corvettes were disabled and destroyed by Keyan Farlander in a B-wing starfighter.
