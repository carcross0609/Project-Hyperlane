# Enforcer One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Enforcer One (dreadnaught)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enforcer_One_%28dreadnaught%29)

The Enforcer One was a modified Core Galaxy Systems Dreadnaught that served as the flagship of Stenness Node&ndash;based crime lord Bogga the Hutt in the years leading up to the Great Sith War. Captained by an enslaved Near-Human named Norufu, the Enforcer One was nine hundred meters long and held a crew of forty-five, including the Weequay Grimorg, one of Bogga's top enforcers. The Enforcer One was heavily armed, boasting heavy turbolasers, sixteen laser cannons, and four tractor beam projectors.
