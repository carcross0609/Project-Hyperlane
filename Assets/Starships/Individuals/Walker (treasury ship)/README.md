# Walker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Walker (treasury ship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Walker_%28treasury_ship%29)

The Walker was an Imperial treasury ship. During the Galactic Civil War, it was transiting through the Fakir sector with a corvette escort when it was attacked by six Alliance to Restore the Republic T-65 X-wing starfighters. The Walker and its escort were severely damaged in the assault.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Walker
- **Model:** Treasury ship
- **Role:** Treasury transport
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
