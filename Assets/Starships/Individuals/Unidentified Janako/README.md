# Unidentified Janako

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Janako](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Janako)

During the Galactic Civil War, the smuggler Roark Garnet encountered a Janako model MaKing-class transport. A General Spacetronics design which was built under a local license, the Janako featured the rear flanges unique to that model of ship. Modifications had also been made around the gunports, and Garnet felt the ship's firepower had been upgraded from its original specifications.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Janako
- **Manufacturer:** Local license, original design by General Spacetronics
- **Line:** MaKing-class transport
- **Model:** Janako
- **Type:** Transport
- **Armament:** Equipped
