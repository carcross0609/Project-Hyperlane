# Discord

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Discord (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Discord_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

The Discord was a Star Destroyer in service to the Galactic Empire during the reign of the New Republic. It was under the command of Captain Fessix, who maintained a close relationship with SoroSuub Corporation's Varada sector holdings top operating executive Tyanna Tymb and regularly received information from her regarding her New Republic customers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Discord
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:** Fessix
