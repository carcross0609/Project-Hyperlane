# Brentaal Princess

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Brentaal Princess](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brentaal_Princess)

The Brentaal Princess was a Ghtroc 720 freighter that was owned by the Boztrok branch of the Klatooinian Trade Guild. The ship had received numerous modifications from the Guild mechanic, Nazrita Villache. The ship was adorned with extra hull armor, the double laser cannon was given a power boost and the shields overhauled. On top of this, the ship was outfitted with a concealed proton torpedo tube, capable of holding up to five torpedoes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Brentaal Princess
- **Manufacturer:** Ghtroc Industries

- **Modifier:** Nazrita Villache
- **Sysmods:** Proton torpedo tube added
