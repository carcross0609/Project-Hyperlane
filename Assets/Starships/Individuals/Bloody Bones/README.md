# Bloody Bones

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bloody Bones](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bloody_Bones)

The Bloody Bones was the starship of the pirate queen Gunda Mabin, serving as the capital ship for the Blazing Claw during Mabin's leadership.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bloody Bones
- **Affiliation:** Blazing Claw
- **Owners:** Gunda Mabin
