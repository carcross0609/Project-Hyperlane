# Shrike

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shrike (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shrike_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

The Shrike was a Imperial-class Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. Like other ships of its class, the Shrike was 1,600 meters in length and manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shrike
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE fighters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
