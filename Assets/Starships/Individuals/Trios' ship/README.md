# Trios' ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Trios' ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trios%27_ship)

Trios' ship was a starship owned by Queen Trios of Shu-Torun. After contacting the Sith Lord Darth Vader with the location of the newly put together rebel fleet, Trios returned to her ship, during a ceremony held aboard the Mako-To Base celebrating the Alliance's fleet, in an attempt to flee. After the failed launches of rebel groups, Princess Leia Organa ordered General Davits Draven to send security teams to Trios' ship to find an override which could get the cruisers moving.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Trios' ship
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Shu-Torun royalty
- **Owners:** Queen Trios
