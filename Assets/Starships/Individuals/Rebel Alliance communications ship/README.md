# Rebel Alliance communications ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rebel Alliance communications ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rebel_Alliance_communications_ship)

A communications ship attached to the Rebel Alliance Fleet was stationed in orbit of the planet Sullust during the Battle of Endor. It relayed news of the battle's proceedings to other Rebel Alliance forces not present at Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rebel Alliance communications ship
- **Role:** Communications ship
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Alliance Fleet
