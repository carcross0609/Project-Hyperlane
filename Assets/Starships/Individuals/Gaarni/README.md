# Gaarni

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gaarni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gaarni)

Gaarni was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate in service of the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. As the Battle on the Frontier drew to a close, Frigate Gaarni replaced the battle weary Nebulon-B2 frigate Ludwick. The Gaarni arrived as a part of a larger task force responsible for relieving Grand Admiral Thrawn's initial invasion force sent to establish Space Station NL-1 in the Pakuuni system.
