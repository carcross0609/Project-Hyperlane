# Battle of Yavin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle of Yavin (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Yavin_%28starship%29)

The Battle of Yavin was an Imperial Customs Frigate which was captured by the New Republic. It was assigned to the Black Curs, a band of smugglers and outlaws led by Dirk Harkness.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Battle of Yavin
- **Type:** Imperial Customs Frigate
- **Length:** 35 Meters
- **Affiliation:**
  - New Republic
  - Black Curs
