# Jeweled Delight

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jeweled Delight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jeweled_Delight)

The Jeweled Delight was a Hapan Battle Dragon and flagship of Queen Mother Tenel Ka when in 44 ABY she traveled from Hapes to Klatooine to attend the anti-slavery meeting there, and secretly met her daughter Allana.
