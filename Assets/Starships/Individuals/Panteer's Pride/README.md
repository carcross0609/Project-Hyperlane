# Panteer's Pride

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Panteer's Pride](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panteer%27s_Pride)

The Panteer's Pride was a GR-75 medium transport that was part of an Alderaanian refugee convoy after the destruction of Alderaan. The fleet of Imperial Commodore Rae Sloane was ordered to destroy the convoy at Fostar Haven, but failed to prevent the convoy from escaping due to the defection of Helix Squadron Captain Lindon Javes with assistance from the Rebel Alliance's Echo Squadron and a fleet of the Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Panteer's Pride
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** Alderaanian refugee convoy
