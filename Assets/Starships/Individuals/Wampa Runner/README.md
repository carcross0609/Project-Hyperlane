# Wampa Runner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wampa Runner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wampa_Runner)

During the time of the Clone Wars, a Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier with a picture of a snarling wampa on the front of it saw service with the Grand Army of the Republic. A number of clone troopers gave the vehicle the nickname Wampa Runner.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wampa Runner
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Model:** Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
