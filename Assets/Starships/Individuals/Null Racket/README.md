# Null Racket

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Null Racket](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Null_Racket)

The Null Racket was a corvette captained by the smuggler Sava Matar during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, Captain Matar and the Null Racket operated in the Corellian sector. As the ship was able to outrun Imperial vessels, Sava Matar claimed that the Null Racket was the fastest ship in the sector. However, in 1 ABY, Reego Hinterstar's pirate crew intercepted the Null Racket and robbed Captain Matar's cargo. Sava Matar then hired a freelance strike team to track down Hinterstar on Corellia and retrieve the cargo.
