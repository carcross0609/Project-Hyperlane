# Stormwind

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stormwind](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stormwind)

The Stormwind was a Victory-class Star Destroyer that served in the Imperial Navy and was commanded by Captain Jarroth during the reign of the Galactic Empire. It patrolled the Virgillia system for rebellious activity from the Virgillian Free Alignment, which was fighting against the Imperial-aligned ruling government, the Aristocracy, in the Virgillian Civil War. It also searched for potential Alliance to Restore the Republic activity in the system.
