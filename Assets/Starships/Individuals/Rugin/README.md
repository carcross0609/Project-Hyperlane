# Rugin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rugin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rugin)

Rugin was an Imperial Cargo ferry that arrived at the Elliirad Platform to pick up Glavsled while the platform was under attack by a Rebel Alliance strike force consisting of Y-wings that were targeting the freighters, A-wings attempting to provide starfighter support for the Y-wings and a commando unit in a stolen Escort shuttle intending to destroy the station with explosives.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rugin
- **Model:** Cargo ferry
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
