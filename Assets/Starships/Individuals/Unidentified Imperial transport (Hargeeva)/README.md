# Unidentified Imperial transport (Hargeeva)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial transport (Hargeeva)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial_transport_%28Hargeeva%29)

In 3 ABY, an Imperial transport was assigned to transport prisoner Stevan Makintay and metallurgist Ketrian Altronel to Coruscant from Hargeeva. En route, the transport was captured by Uskgarv's pirates.
