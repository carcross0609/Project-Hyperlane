# Coral Fin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coral Fin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coral_Fin)

The Coral Fin was the personal freighter of the Priapulin Charza Kwinn. It was used during the evacuation of Montitia during the Extorin Minor skirmishes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Coral Fin
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Captains:** Charza Kwinn
