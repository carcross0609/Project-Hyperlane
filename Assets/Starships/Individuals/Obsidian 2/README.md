# Obsidian 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Obsidian 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obsidian_2)

Manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems, Obsidian 2 was a TIE/ln starfighter serving in the Imperial Navy's Obsidian Squadron. Obsidian 2 was piloted by Dodson Makraven, who operated under the designation of OS-72-2 and flew as wingman to Obsidian Squadron's lead pilot, Civé Rashon. At one point, Obsidian 2 flew over Cloud City on the planet Bespin.
