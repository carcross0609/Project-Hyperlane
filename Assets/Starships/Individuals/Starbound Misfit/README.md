# Starbound Misfit

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starbound Misfit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starbound_Misfit)

The Starbound Misfit was a heavily modified Corellian YT-1930 light freighter that was owned by the Near-Human tramp freighter captain Lynnori during the Galactic Civil War, after the Battle of Yavin. Lynnori's boyfriend, Talandro Starlyte, purchased the ship as a gift for her and had his Ugnaught mechanic, Scizzic, make some modifications that improved the ship's capabilities. After the modifications, it boasted a much faster hyperdrive and heavier armaments than a stock ship of its class. Lynnori enjoyed having the freedom that came with owning her own ship and she used it as often as she could.
