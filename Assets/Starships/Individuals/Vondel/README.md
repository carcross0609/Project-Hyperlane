# Vondel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vondel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vondel)

The Vondel was a G9 Rigger-class light freighter owned by the smuggler and enslaver Alfris Sotin. During a deal where Sotin betrayed the Spice Runners of Kijimi, Spice Runners Zorii Bliss and Poe Dameron stole the Vondel, and their cell of the gang continued to use the freighter.
