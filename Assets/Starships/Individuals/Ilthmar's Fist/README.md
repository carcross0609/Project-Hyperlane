# Ilthmar's Fist

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ilthmar's Fist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ilthmar%27s_Fist)

The Ilthmar's Fist was a battlecruiser in the navy of the Galactic Empire. A part of Death Squadron, it participated in the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY. Following the battle, a council of war occurred in order to establish the proper course of action for Death Squadron, which was in disarray following its heavy losses at the battle. As the ranking Imperial admiral, Adye Prittick, was indecisive, Admiral Blitzer Harrsk acrimoniously broke up the council of war by announcing he was bringing the Ilthmar's Fist, as well as a substantial portion of Death Squadron, to the Deep Core until an unambiguous line of command could be established. Harrsk's Deep Core forces eventually became a splinter faction known as Zero Command.
