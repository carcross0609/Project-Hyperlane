# Guardian

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Guardian (Venator-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian_%28Venator-class%29)

The Guardian was a Venator-class Star Destroyer that was active during the pan-galactic Clone Wars. The human Jan Dodonna, who went onto serve Rebel Alliance as a general in Sector Command, served as a bridge officer aboard the Guardian during the Clone Wars.

- **Name:** Guardian
