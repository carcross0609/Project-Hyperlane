# Tinmolok

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tinmolok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tinmolok)

The Tinmolok was a Marl-class heavy freighter manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards. Captained by Swori Mdimu and his Etti crew, it was part of the Peace Brigade fleet during the Yuuzhan Vong War. The ship was used to carry supplies and munitions for the Yuuzhan Vong, and carried two Yuuzhan Vong warriors onboard.
