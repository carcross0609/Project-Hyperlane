# Black Ace

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Black Ace](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Ace)

The Black Ace was a modified TIE/ln space superiority starfighter piloted by Griff Halloran. Halloran used it both as a racer and in the defense of the Colossus. The ship was lost for a short time during the Platform Classic, but was later recovered and used later in the attack on Bibo's mother.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Black Ace
- **Type:** Heavily modified TIE/ln space superiority starfighter
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Astromech droid (1)
- **Role:** Racer
- **Affiliation:** Ace Squadron
- **Owners:** Griff Halloran
