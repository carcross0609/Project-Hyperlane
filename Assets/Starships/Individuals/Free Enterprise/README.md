# Free Enterprise

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Free Enterprise](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Free_Enterprise)

Free Enterprise was a colony ship full of Jindas and Tulgahs which left Eriadu for Kuna's Eye in 130 BBY, but crashed on Endor, thus introducing the two species to the Forest Moon, where both thrived.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Free Enterprise
- **Role:** Colony ship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jindas
  - Tulgahs
