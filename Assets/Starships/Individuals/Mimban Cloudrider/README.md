# Mimban Cloudrider

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mimban Cloudrider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mimban_Cloudrider)

Mimban Cloudrider was one of the Bacta Tankers captured by Rogue Squadron during the Bacta War. It was later used to insert covertly Corran Horn, Elscol Loro, Sixtus Quin, Mirax Terrik, and Iella Wessiri in Thyferra. They were disguised as the ships's crew under various pseudonyms and their mission was to make contact with the Ashern faction.
