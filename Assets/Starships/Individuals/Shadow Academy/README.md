# Shadow Academy

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow Academy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Academy)

The Shadow Academy was a battle station that was used as a hidden training center by the Second Imperium. The center was founded by the fallen Jedi Brakiss to train Dark Jedi in 23 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shadow Academy
- **Manufacturer:** Second Imperium
- **Type:** Battlestation
- **Countermeasures:** Cloaking device
- **Armament:** Various gun emplacements
- **Complement:**
  - TIE Fighters
  - TIE/sa bombers
  - GAT-12 Skipray Blastboats
  - Imperial Assault Shuttles
  - Battle Platforms
- **Role:**
  - Mobile training center
  - Battlestation
  - Carrier
- **Affiliation:**
  - Brakiss
  - Second Imperium
