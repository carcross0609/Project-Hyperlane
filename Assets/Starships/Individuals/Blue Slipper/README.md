# Blue Slipper

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blue Slipper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Slipper)

The Blue Slipper was a Batag Skiff, the vessel of Taryn and Trista Zel. The skiff was equipped with anti-theft systems that were not yet released on the open market. Taryn and Trista used the Blue Slipper to ferry Ben Skywalker from Nova Station to the Transitory Mists.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blue Slipper
- **Type:** Skiff
- **Sysmods:** Advanced anti-theft systems
- **Role:** transport
- **Affiliation:** Hapes Consortium
- **Captains:**
  - Taryn Zel
  - Trista Zel
