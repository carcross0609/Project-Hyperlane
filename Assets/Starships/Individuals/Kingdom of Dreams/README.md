# Kingdom of Dreams

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kingdom of Dreams](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_of_Dreams)

The Kingdom of Dreams was a starship capable of interdimensional travel. Cody Sunn-Childe and his followers retreated to an extra-dimensional realm in the Kingdom. Although the starship also existed in realspace, Sunn-Childe claimed that everything in his City of Dreams was composed not of matter, but of "psychic energy."
