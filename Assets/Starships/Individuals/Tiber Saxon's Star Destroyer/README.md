# Tiber Saxon's Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tiber Saxon's Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tiber_Saxon%27s_Star_Destroyer)

Tiber Saxon's Star Destroyer was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer owned by Tiber Saxon, Governor of Mandalore. It was based outside the capital city of Sundari during a mission to Mandalore undertaken by Clan Wren, Clan Kryze, and several members of the Rebel Alliance to destroy the Duchess, an arc generator that was contained within the AT-DP Arc Cannon Prototype. By the end of the mission, the Star Destroyer was destroyed, along with "the Duchess," the AT-DP Arc Cannon Prototype, and Saxon, with the ship crashing near the base of Sundari.
