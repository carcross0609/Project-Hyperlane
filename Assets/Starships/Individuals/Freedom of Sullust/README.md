# Freedom of Sullust

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Freedom of Sullust](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedom_of_Sullust)

Freedom of Sullust was a CR90 corvette of the New Republic Defense Fleet. In 11 ABY, Freedom of Sullust was part of a task force sent to the Kesselian space surrounding the Maw to engage a pirate raid on a spice transport. The Invid forces under the command of Shala the Hutt began taking losses, but were saved by the timely arrival of Leonia Tavira and Invidious. In the subsequent battle, Freedom of Sullust took heavy damage from Invidious guns and was driven from the fight.
