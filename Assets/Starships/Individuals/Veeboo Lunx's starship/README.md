# Veeboo Lunx's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Veeboo Lunx's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veeboo_Lunx%27s_starship)

The bounty hunter Veeboo Lunx owned a starship during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. In 3643 BBY, Veeboo tracked his mark Tyresius Lokai over Tatooine during the Great Hunt and shot down the fugitive over the desert. Eventually, Lokai managed to convince Veeboo to sell him his own ship and let him escape.
