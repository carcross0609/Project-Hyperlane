# Gotan Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gotan Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gotan_Squadron)

Gotan Squadron was a squadron of five T-wings employed by Nami Crime Lord Ali Tarrak. After Gotmilc Squadron and transport group Lucthz were destroyed trying to stop Modular conveyor Ulzepdi from escaping the destruction of an Imperial Cargo area. The Strike Cruiser Bognog launched Gotan Squadron and Notow Squadron to continue pursuing the conveyor but the T-wings were all destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele and the Ulzepdi escaped into Hyperspace.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gotan Squadron
- **Type:** Squadron
- **Strength:** 5 R60 T-wing interceptors
