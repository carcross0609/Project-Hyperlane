# Viscount

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Viscount (Galactic Republic starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Viscount_%28Galactic_Republic_starship%29)

The Viscount was a capital ship that served in the Republic Navy during the Jedi Civil War, a galactic conflict fought between the Galactic Republic and Darth Revan's Sith Empire. In 3957 BBY, the Viscount accompanied a small Republic fleet to the Republic's border with the Sith Empire, to rendezvous with a group of Republic agents. The agents arrived at the meeting point and landed aboard the Viscount, then attended a briefing about an upcoming strike against the Sith.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Viscount
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Complement:** At least 6 Aurek-class tactical strikefighters
- **Passengers:** At least 12 Republic soldiers
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
