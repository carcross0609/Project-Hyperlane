# Torment

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Torment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torment)

The Torment was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer in the naval forces of the Galactic Empire that participated in the Battle of Theed during Operation: Cinder and was helmed by Captain Lerr Duvat.
