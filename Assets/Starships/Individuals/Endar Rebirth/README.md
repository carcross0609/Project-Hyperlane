# Endar Rebirth

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Endar Rebirth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endar_Rebirth)

The Endar Rebirth was a Republic survey vessel in service during the Cold War. It was the first ship to investigate Taris, devastated by orbital bombardment three centuries previous, as a candidate for resettlement by the Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Endar Rebirth
- **Destroyed:** 3648 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
