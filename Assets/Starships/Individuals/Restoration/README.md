# Restoration

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Restoration](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Restoration)

The Restoration was an MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser in the service of the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was tentatively ready for service in the lead up to the Battle of Endor. After the Battle of Endor, it was commanded by General Lando Calrissian. The vessel saw combat in the Battle of Theed, in which it fought against an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer, the Torment, during the Alliance's successful operation to prevent the Galactic Empire from decimating the planet during Operation: Cinder.
