# INS Intimidator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [INS Intimidator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/INS_Intimidator)

The INS Intimidator was an Imperial starship. It was sent to the planet Ananuru when the research details for a secret Imperial RNA brainwashing virus appeared for auction on the Ananuru Express. When Imperial Intelligence agent Secesh Trant attempted to steal the datatape at the auction, she threatened everyone there that the forces of the INS Intimidator were close by if they caused any trouble.
