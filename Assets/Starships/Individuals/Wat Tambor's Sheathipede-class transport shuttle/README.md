# Wat Tambor's Sheathipede-class transport shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wat Tambor's Sheathipede-class transport shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wat_Tambor%27s_Sheathipede-class_transport_shuttle)

This Sheathipede-class transport shuttle was the personal shuttle of Techno Union Foreman Wat Tambor. During the Battle of Ryloth in 21 BBY, Tambor's T-series tactical droid TA-175 used the shuttle to leave the planet, leaving Tambor behind to become a Galactic Republic prisoner.
