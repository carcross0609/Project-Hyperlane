# Zeplin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zeplin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeplin)

The Zeplin was a Victory-class Star Destroyer in the service of the Galactic Empire. It was commanded by Namuura Din, an Imperial captain who followed Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's defection from the Empire shortly before the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Zeplin
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Victory-class Star Destroyer
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire (formerly)
  - Zaarin coup forces (formerly)
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Captains:** Namuura Din
