# Bolide

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bolide](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bolide)

The Bolide was an Acclamator-class assault ship of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. It was destroyed by Separatist forces during the Battle of Duro.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bolide
- **Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering
- **Model:** Acclamator-class assault ship
- **Type:** Assault ship
- **Destroyed:** 21 BBY - 20 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
