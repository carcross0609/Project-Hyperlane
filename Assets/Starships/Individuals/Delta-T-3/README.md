# Delta-T-3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Delta-T-3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delta-T-3)

Delta-T-3 was the designation of a YV-865 Aurore-class freighter that was used by the Zygerrian Slave Empire during the Clone Wars. After a failed mission to Zygerria and the capture of Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi, Governor Gupat Roshti, and Clone Captain CT-7567, Delta-T-3 was used to transport them along with other slaves to the Kadavo system for conditioning at the Kadavo slave processing facility.
