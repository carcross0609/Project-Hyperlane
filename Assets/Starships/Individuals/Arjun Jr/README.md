# Arjun Jr.

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arjun Jr.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arjun_Jr.)

Arjun Jr. was a combat utility vehicle launched by the Strike-class medium cruiser Arjun at the Battle of Organarms to resupply Missile Boat Mu 1.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Arjun Jr.
- **Manufacturer:** BTS Enterprises
- **Model:** Combat utility vehicle
- **Length:** 15 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
