# Resolve

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Resolve (cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resolve_%28cruiser%29)

The Resolve was a cruiser that was used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. The ship served as the flagship of the veteran Alliance Admiral Cov. At some point during the conflict, a number of the Resolve crew were bought out by the Duro-based distribution firm DurAble and they mutineed against Cov.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Resolve
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Captains:** Admiral Cov
