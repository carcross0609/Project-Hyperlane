# Orion Ferret's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orion Ferret's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orion_Ferret%27s_shuttle)

Orion Ferret's shuttle was a small spacecraft owned by disreputable weapons trader Orion Ferret. The shuttle was docked at space station Bazarre, Ferret's base of operations. Luke Skywalker and Lando Calrissian used the hyperdrive-equipped shuttle to travel from Bazarre to Patch-4 to retrieve four TIE/LN starfighters they purchased from Ferret.
