# Gauntlet

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gauntlet (Pre Vizsla)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gauntlet_%28Pre_Vizsla%29)

The Gauntlet was a Kom'rk-class fighter/transport that was used by the leader of the Death Watch, Pre Vizsla, as his personal ship. During the Clone Wars, Vizsla used the starfighter to bring the Dathomirian brothers Maul and Savage Opress to Death Watch's camp on Zanbar after finding them unconscious in an escape pod. After forming an alliance with them called the Shadow Collective, the trio used the ship during their recruitment of the Black Sun and Hutt Clan crime syndicates. They later traveled to Sundari, the capital of Mandalore, prior to its takeover, before Vizsla left the city aboard the ship.
