# Zorba Express

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zorba Express](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zorba_Express)

The Zorba Express, also known as Zorba's Express, was an ancient starship that belonged to Zorba the Hutt, shady businessman and father of the famous crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Zorba Express
- **Model:** Huttian star yacht
- **Type:** Space yacht
- **Role:** Hutt crime lord personal transport
- **Affiliation:** Desilijic
- **Owners:** Zorba Desilijic Tiure
