# Armageddon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Armageddon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armageddon)

The Armageddon was a StoneCrusher-class WarMaster that served in the fleet of the Grysk "Jixtus." The Armageddon was destroyed during a battle over Sunrise.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Armageddon
- **Model:** StoneCrusher-class WarMaster
- **Type:** WarMaster
- **Destroyed:** During the battle over Sunrise
- **Affiliation:** Grysks
- **Fleet:** Jixtus's fleet
