# Tormentor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tormentor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tormentor/Legends)

The Tormentor was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was scheduled to meet a supply convoy (four BFF-1 bulk freighters of group Monru) at Udnil II during Imperial Operation: Clean Sweep. The supply operation came under attack by Rebel forces consisting of X-wings, Y-wings and A-wings as backup. At the time, the Tormentor was not carrying its standard complement of starfighters. Instead, it deployed TIE Avengers to intercept the attack.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tormentor
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE Avengers
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
