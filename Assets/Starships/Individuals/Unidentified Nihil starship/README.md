# Unidentified Nihil starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Nihil starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Nihil_starship)

A starship was utilized by a Cloud of the Nihil marauders on the planet Temerancé. The Nihil used the starship to kidnap a number of Force-sensitive children from the planet, including the Altiri Lene Kostana. The monster hunter Ty Yorrick boarded the vessel to rescue Kostana and the other children, defeating the Nihil crew. Yorrick crash-landed the ship outside the settlement where Kostana lived, returning the children home. However, after her apprentice, Drewen Qweebjillan took Yorrick's own ship on a mission, she returned to the settlement and requested the Nihil vessel. Assisted by her astromech droid R0-VR and Kostana, Yorrick repaired the Nihil ship and intended to leave Temerancé. Yorrick shortly returned to rescue Kostana from the Nihil's Children of the Storm, departing the planet to find Qweebjillan.
