# Sendant Pride

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sendant Pride](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sendant_Pride)

The Sendant Pride was a Galactic Republic military vessel that served in the Republic Navy during the Great Galactic War with the reconstituted Sith Empire. The warship was part of a massive fleet that was preparing for an invasion of the Imperial capital of Dromund Kaas, but the fleet was ambushed by the Imperial Navy near the Hoth system. The ship survived for several days until the battle reached the planet Hoth, when it was among those warships that crashed into the region later known as the Starship Graveyard during the Battle of Hoth.
