# Mace Windu's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mace Windu's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mace_Windu%27s_Delta-7_Aethersprite-class_light_interceptor)

A Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor was flown by Mace Windu during his time as a Jedi Knight and co-piloted by the R-series astromech droid R8-B7. Windu piloted the starfighter during his mission to the moon Ro Mira. After landing, he told R8 to stay with the ship and wait for his signal. After Windu had found his target, Azita Cruuz, and had brought her to Ro Mira's Radial Spaceport, they were attacked by Dusk Weavers of the Had'le Path cult and Windu called upon his astromech to fly his starfighter to his location. After the arrival of the cult's leader, the Shroud, Windu told Cruuz to get in the starfighter. Cruuz then used it's laser cannons to destroy her freighter and bury the Shroud underneath. Afterwards, Windu and Cruuz then cramped themselves in the one-person cockpit of his fighter and flew back to the Jedi Temple on Coruscant.
