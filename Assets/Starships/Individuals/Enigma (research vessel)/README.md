# Enigma

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Enigma (research vessel)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enigma_%28research_vessel%29)

The Enigma was a research vessel once belonged to Darth Nul at some point before 3622 BBY. The Enigma was a self-sustaining vessel designed to be a mobile research facility capable of traveling to the farthest reaches of the galaxy. After Darth Nul's defeat at the hands of the Sith Emperor, her loyal followers scrambled to conceal her holdings from the Emperor's forces. A group tasked with maintaining the Enigma fled to the Wild Space. Over the centuries, the ship became overrun with wildlife and vegetation.
