# Delta-T-3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Delta-T-3/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delta-T-3/Legends)

Delta-T-3 was the designation of one of the YV-865 Aurore-class freighters used by Zygerrian Slavers Guild. After Obi-Wan Kenobi, Clone captain Rex, and Governor Roshti of Kiros were captured by Zygerrian slavers, they were sent to the Kadavo slave processing facility on Kadavo by this ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Delta-T-3
- **Model:** YV-865 Aurore-class freighter
- **Length:** 52.3 meters
- **Width:** 42.33 meters
- **Height:** 16.44 meters
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Role:**
  - Transport
  - Freighter
- **Affiliation:** Zygerrian Slavers Guild
