# Cularin 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cularin 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cularin_1)

Cularin 1 was a capital ship that was used during the Clone Wars. It was originally called the Nimbus and used by the Thaereian military, but it was captured by the Cularin Militia and they renamed it and put it into service with their fleet. Major Xirossk planned the Militia's attack on the Thaereian military's base on Dorumaa while aboard it. The Cularin 1 later served as the Militia's flagship during the Battle of Cularin.
