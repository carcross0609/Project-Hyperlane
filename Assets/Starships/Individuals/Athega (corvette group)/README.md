# Athega (corvette group)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Athega (corvette group)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Athega_%28corvette_group%29)

Athega was the designation assigned to a group of eleven CR90 corvettes which served with the Rebel Alliance Fleet. At some point during the Galactic Civil War, while passing through the Athega system with only light fighter escort, they were attacked by a group of TIE Avengers, Assault Gunboats and the Victory-class Star Destroyer Aggressor.
