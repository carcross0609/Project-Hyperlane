# Ignat

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ignat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ignat)

Ignat was a CR90 corvette used by the Airam. It was part of the joint-operation that was attacked by rival Airam clan leader Ilay and the Imperial-class Star Destroyer, Wasp. Fortunately, the operation was a success and Ignat escaped into hyperspace.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ignat
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Affiliation:** Airam Clans
