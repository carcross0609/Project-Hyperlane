# Banshee of Bestine's starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Banshee of Bestine's starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Banshee_of_Bestine%27s_starfighter)

The First Order starfighter ace known as the Banshee of Bestine flew a black TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor between 31 and 34 ABY. The TIE Baron was destroyed and the Banshee was killed in battle by Resistance pilot Poe Dameron above the planet Bothawui.
