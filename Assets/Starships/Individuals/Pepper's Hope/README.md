# Pepper's Hope

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pepper's Hope](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pepper%27s_Hope)

Pepper's Hope was a stock Ghtroc Industries light freighter that was owned and utilized by the Rebel Alliance pilot and operative Margo Flarestream, while she was stationed at Fabrillan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pepper's Hope
- **Manufacturer:** Ghtroc Industries
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Owners:** Margo Flarestream
