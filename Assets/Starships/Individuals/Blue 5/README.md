# Blue 5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blue 5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_5)

Blue 5, also known as Blue Squadron 5, was an A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The fighter was part of Blue Squadron during the Battle of Endor, and was flown by Sullustan pilot Ten Numb.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blue 5
- **Line:** A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter
- **Affiliation:**

- **Captains:** Ten Numb
