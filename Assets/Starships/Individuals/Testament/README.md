# Testament

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Testament](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Testament)

Testament was the Republic Hammerhead-class cruiser in the Republic Navy around 3963 BBY. Commanded by Captain Telettoh, the ship made a stop on the planet Wor Tandell, where Telettoh and Jedi General Malak were looking for Zayne Carrick and his band of renegades, before departing on the front line of the Mandalorian Wars.
