# Deviant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Deviant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deviant)

The Deviant was a starship in the Alliance Fleet. In 1 BBY, the Deviant fought in the Battle of Scarif, where it and the Heartbound were ordered by Admiral Raddus to box in the Imperial Star Destroyers.

- **Name:** Deviant
