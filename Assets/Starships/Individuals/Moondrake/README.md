# Moondrake

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moondrake](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moondrake)

The Moondrake was a salvaged YT-1300 light freighter owned and operated by the Sareetan smuggler Sassan Sareeta. The Moondrake was a heavily armored craft that Sassan constructed from the hulks of three different YT-1300s that he had acquired. The ship also had a reputation as a blockade runner.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Moondrake
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Length:** 26.7 meters
- **Hyperdrive:**
  - Class 2 (Primary)
  - Class 8 (Backup)
- **Armament:** 1 double laser cannon
- **Crew:** 2
- **Passengers:** 4
- **Capacity:** 100 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Owners:** Sassan Sareeta
- **Captains:** Sassan Sareeta
