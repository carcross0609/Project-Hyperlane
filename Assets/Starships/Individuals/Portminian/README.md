# Portminian

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Portminian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Portminian)

The Portminian was a civilian transport ship. The ship was used by the Galactic Empire to transport Memah Roothes, Rodo, and several other civilian workers to the Death Star I while it was under construction at Despayre.
