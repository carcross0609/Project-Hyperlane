# Uhu

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uhu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uhu)

Uhu was a pair of CR90 corvettes used by the Rebel Alliance. They attempted to lay probes called Keyhole near an Imperial R&D facility that was attacked by Demetrius Zaarin's forces. But they were destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele before they could escape and their probes destroyed.
