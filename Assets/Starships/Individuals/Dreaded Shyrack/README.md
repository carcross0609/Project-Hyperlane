# Dreaded Shyrack

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dreaded Shyrack](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dreaded_Shyrack)

The Dreaded Shyrack was a pirate starship that was a rival of the Scorching Pulsar. The two destroyed each other in a shootout during the Cold War, prompting scavengers to raid the vessels for their treasure.
