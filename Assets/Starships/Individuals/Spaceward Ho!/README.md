# Spaceward Ho!

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spaceward Ho!](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spaceward_Ho%21)

Spaceward Ho! was a Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry utilized by the Galactic Republic's military against the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone War. It was distinguishable by the nose art of a green-skinned female Twi'lek pin-up model, posing in a seductive manner, present on both sides of the gunship's bow&mdash;as well as marked tallies that indicated an amassed total of fourteen vulture droid starfighters, destroyed, accompanied by the total number of, approximately, thirty-three bombs dropped on targets.
