# Libra

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Libra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Libra)

The Libra was a Rebel CR90 corvette active during the Galactic Civil War. It carried the technicians who outfitted the CR90 corvette Ram's Head with the experimental Shield X.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Libra
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** CR series
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
