# Intimidator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Intimidator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intimidator)

The Intimidator was an Imperial I-class Star Destroyer in service of the Galactic Empire. Under the command of Admiral Gorin, it participated in the Battle of Scarif in 1 BBY, where it was rammed by the Star Destroyer Persecutor, which in turn had been pushed by the Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette Lightmaker. The combined wreckage of all three ships then fell into the Scarif Shield Gate, destroying it.
