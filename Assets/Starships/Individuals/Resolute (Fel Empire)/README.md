# Resolute

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Resolute (Fel Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resolute_%28Fel_Empire%29)

Resolute was a starship in the service of the Fel Empire in 130 ABY. Under the command of Admiral Edouard Fenel it participated in the Battle of Caamas, the final battle of the Sith–Imperial War, where it destroyed a number of cruisers too heavily damaged to properly defend themselves. Seven years later, Gar Stazi sarcastically commented Resolute role in battle during a conversation with Fenel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Resolute
- **Affiliation:** Fel Empire
- **Navy:** Bastion Second Fleet
- **Captains:** Edouard Fenel
