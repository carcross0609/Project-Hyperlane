# Harvest Moon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harvest Moon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harvest_Moon)

The Harvest Moon was a 100-ton grain freighter operating during the Galactic Civil War. Working in the Sarrahban system, it was used to smuggle Rebel Alliance refugees and ytterbium out of the system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Harvest Moon
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Capacity:** 100 tons
- **Role:** Grain transport
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
