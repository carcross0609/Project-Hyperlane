# Victory at Endor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Victory at Endor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Victory_at_Endor)

The Victory at Endor was a New Republic assault frigate during the Galactic Civil War. It served in the Bothan Combat Response Element and fought in the Battle of Kathol System, in which it assisted in the defeat of Moff Kentor Sarne.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Victory at Endor
- **Type:** Assault frigate
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 15 laser cannons
  - 20 quad laser cannons
  - 15 turbolaser batteries
- **Role:** Combat vessel
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
- **Navy:** New Republic Defense Fleet
- **Fleet:** Bothan Combat Response Element
