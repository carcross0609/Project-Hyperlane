# The Oteroan Sky

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [The Oteroan Sky](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Oteroan_Sky)

The Oteroan Sky was a massive refinery ship built by OteroaCorp. It was larger than an Imperial-class Star Destroyer, and could hold more than three dozen freighters at one time. In the decades after the ship's construction, OteroaCorp began to neglect the ship, and it was eventually attacked by the Nardithi Nightsisters and forced to crash into the planet Dathomir, where it destroyed the city of Oteroa Zero-Twenty.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Oteroan Sky
- **Type:** Refinery ship
- **Length:** 2 kilometers
- **Width:** 1.75 kilometers
- **Height:** 500 meters
- **Crew:** 80,000
- **Passengers:** 25,000
- **Affiliation:** OteroaCorp
