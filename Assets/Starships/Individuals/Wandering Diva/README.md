# Wandering Diva

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wandering Diva](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wandering_Diva)

The Wandering Diva, or simply the Diva, was a starship piloted by the Jedi explorer known as Ley Neetels at least three thousand years before the reign of the Galactic Empire. Equipped with a hyperdrive, the vessel was used by Neetels to traverse the galaxy's Deep Core. While flying the Wandering Diva, the Jedi relied on the guidance of the Force to safely navigate the region's hyperspace routes, eventually reaching the planet Tython.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wandering Diva
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
- **Owners:** Ley Neetels
