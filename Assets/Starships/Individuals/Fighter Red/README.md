# Fighter Red

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fighter Red](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fighter_Red)

Fighter Red, otherwise known as Red E Squadron or simply Red Squadron, was a squadron of E-wing escort starfighters assigned to the Endurance-class fleet carrier Intrepid of the New Republic Defense Fleet Fifth Battle Group.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fighter Red
- **Leader:** General Etahn A'baht
- **Headquarters:** Intrepid
- **Affiliation:**
  - New Republic
  - New Republic Defense Fleet
  -   New Republic Fifth Battle Group
