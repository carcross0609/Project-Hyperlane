# Dirk Balor's Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dirk Balor's Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dirk_Balor%27s_Star_Destroyer)

An Imperial-class Star Destroyer captained by Dirk Balor was patrolling the Lapez system in 3 ABY. The Human replica droid Altin Wuho and his nightmare demon Reist were stationed aboard the ship.
