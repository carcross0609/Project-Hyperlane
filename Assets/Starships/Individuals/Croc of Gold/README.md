# Croc of Gold

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Croc of Gold](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Croc_of_Gold)

The Croc of Gold was a small freighter purchased by the smuggler Devon Fuller. Despite his shady past, Fuller used the Croc of Gold to deal in legal goods, although he was not very successful until he met Owen Sareth, an operative for the Alliance to Restore the Republic who possessed an acumen for business that Fuller lacked.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Croc of Gold
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Destroyed:** By 4 ABY, Tashtor Seneca
- **Affiliation:** Rebel Alliance
- **Owners:** Devon Fuller
