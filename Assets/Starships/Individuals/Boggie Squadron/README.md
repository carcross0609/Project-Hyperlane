# Boggie Squadron

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Boggie Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boggie_Squadron)

Boggie Squadron was a squadron of TIE Defenders that were built by Nami crime lord Ali Tarrak. After stealing Proto 11, one of the Empire's prototypes. Tarrak arranged a demonstration of the powerful fighters to the Rebel Alliance by destroying a cargo area in the Yrrna system. Tarrack's TIE Defenders destroyed the cargo containers in the area. The surviving crew managed to escape on a Modular conveyor Ulzepdi with cover provided by Tan Maarek Stele in a TIE Avenger.
