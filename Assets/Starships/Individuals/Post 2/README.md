# Post 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Post 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Post_2)

Post 2 was a Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle that served the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It fought at the Second Battle of Javin. Carrying a small amount of equipment that was presumably stolen from Outpost D-34, the shuttle, along with Post 1, was destroyed by the frigate Fogger.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Post 2

- **Model:** Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle

- **Width:** 32.3 meters
- **Height:** 32.4 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Taim & Bak KX5 laser cannons (3; forward)
  - Taim & Bak H9 dual turbolaser (1; aft turret)

- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
