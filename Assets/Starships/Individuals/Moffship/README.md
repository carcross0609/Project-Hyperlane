# Moffship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moffship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moffship)

The Moffship was a Strike-class medium cruiser used by the Central Committee of Grand Moffs. During the Galactic Civil War. the cruiser was used as a mobile headquarters for the committee during their attempt to usurp Ysanne Isard and replace her with Trioculus as Galactic Emperor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Moffship
- **Model:** Strike-class medium cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Owners:** Central Committee of Grand Moffs
- **Captains:** Bertroff Hissa
