# Saesee Tiin's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Saesee Tiin's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saesee_Tiin%27s_ship)

A starship was owned by Jedi Master Saesee Tiin during the final days of the Galactic Republic. At the end of the Clone Wars in 19 BBY, after the siege of the Jedi Temple, Senator Bail Prestor Organa, who had personally witnessed the massacre, found a Jedi beacon inside the ship broadcasting a signal luring any surviving Jedi back to the Temple where they would be murdered by the clone troopers stationed there. Realizing what had happened, Organa managed to warn Master Obi-Wan Kenobi and Grand Master Yoda, who subsequently returned to Coruscant and infiltrated the Jedi Temple and altered the transmission to a holographic recording of Kenobi warning all remaining Jedi to keep away.
