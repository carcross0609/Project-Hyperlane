# Tedevium

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tedevium](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tedevium)

The Tedevium was a EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic that later served as a training ship for starfighter recruits, and was home to the Screaming Wookiee Squadron, a starfighter training squadron that fought as Gold Squadron.
