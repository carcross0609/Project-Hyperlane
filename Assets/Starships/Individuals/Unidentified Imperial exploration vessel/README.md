# Unidentified Imperial exploration vessel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial exploration vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial_exploration_vessel)

An exploration probe vessel dispatched by the Sith Empire to the Unknown Regions returned during the Cold War with the Galactic Republic, and was covered in a purple slime. The substance was identified as the secretions of a mynock-like spaceborne creature.

- **Name:** Unidentified Imperial exploration vessel
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
