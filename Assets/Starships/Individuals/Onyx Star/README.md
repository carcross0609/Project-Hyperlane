# Onyx Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Onyx Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Onyx_Star)

The Onyx Star was the codename for the prototype of the Z-95 Headhunter developed at the Bahalian Shipyards in the Bajic sector. The Onyx Star was stolen by Icaris Tool and Drive but later returned to Sublight Products Corporation for refinement and mass production.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Onyx Star
- **Manufacturer:** Bahalian Shipyards
- **Model:** Prototype Z-95 Headhunter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Icaris Tool and Drive
  - Sublight Products Corporation
