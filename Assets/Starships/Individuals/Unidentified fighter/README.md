# Unidentified fighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified fighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_fighter)

A beat-up fighter was owned by the San Tekka clan member Cair San Tekka during the High Republic Era. The fighter had at least one engine and a hyperdrive. By 228 BBY, the fighter was kept by San Tekka on a dead moon in the Nihil pirates' Occlusion Zone region of space.
