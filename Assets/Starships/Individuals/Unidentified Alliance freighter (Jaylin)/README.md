# Unidentified Alliance freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Alliance freighter (Jaylin)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Alliance_freighter_%28Jaylin%29)

A freighter used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic crashed near Feressee's Point on the desert planet Jakku during the Battle of Jakkuin 5 ABY. The scavenger Jaylin and her friend later lived in the wrecked freighter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Alliance freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Destroyed:** 5 ABY, Jakku
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
