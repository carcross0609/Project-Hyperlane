# Indupar Nova

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Indupar Nova](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Indupar_Nova)

The Indupar Nova was a corvette that saw service during the reign of the Galactic Empire. At some point during the Galactic Civil War, the Indupar Nova was attacked by the Rebel privateer vessel Free Lance in the Ec Pand system while it was carrying the noblewoman Lady Kalena of House Indupar as a passenger. The corvette was boarded and captured, and it's officers were held aboard the Free Lance as prisoners-of-war.
