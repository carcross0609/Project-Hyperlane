# Ark Angel II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ark Angel II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ark_Angel_II)

The Ark Angel II, formerly known as the Ark Angel, was a modified Bellicose-class heavy lifter owned by the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra. It was repaired and enhanced by Aphra with exotic starship components manufactured by the Hapes Consortium, and other components from Kuat Systems Engineering and MandalMotors. During the Battle of Vrogas Vas, the Ark Angel was destroyed. Sometime after the battle, the ship had to be so extensively rebuilt that Aphra decided to rename the ship to the Ark Angel II. It was used by Aphra to transport her crew and Luke Skywalker to the Citadel of Ktath'atn.
