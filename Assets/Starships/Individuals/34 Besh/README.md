# 34 Besh

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [34 Besh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/34_Besh)

34 Besh was an escape pod on board the Munificent-class star frigate Sa Nalaor which served the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars, where they fought the Galactic Republic. When the Clone Wars ended the Sa Nalaor fled Republic space and crashed on the planet Cholganna. During the crash several crew members attempted to abandon ship in escape pod 34 Besh, crashing in a crevasse on Cholganna's surface, where the pod would remain half buried for years.
