# Blue Squadron (Mugaari)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blue Squadron (Mugaari)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Squadron_%28Mugaari%29)

Blue Squadron was a fighter squadron consisting of three Y-wings used by Mugaari pirates. The fighter squadron was present at the Battle of the Tungra sector where they were destroyed attempted to stop Imperial forces from destroying a cargo loading area that was making an arms deal with the Rebel Alliance and the pirates. The arms deal was completely destroyed in the process.
