# Antara Satrap

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Antara Satrap](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Antara_Satrap)

Antara Satrap was a starship that once visited the lost planet Endelaan. As such, navigational information concerning the planet's location was located in its logs. The Antara Satrap was boarded by the Wookiee criminal Renarra, who wanted information on the location of Endelaan. Eventually, the ship was stolen, leading the ship Spiral Dancer on a chase through the Outer Rim.
