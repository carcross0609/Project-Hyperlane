# Aggregator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aggregator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aggregator)

The Aggregator was an Imperial Immobilizer 418 cruiser owned by High Admiral Treuten Teradoc. Prior to finding herself in Teradoc's service, it was used in campaigns against the Rebel Alliance during the days of the Galactic Civil War. Ysanne Isard leased it in her quest to destroy Rogue Squadron.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aggregator
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Type:** Immobilizer 418 cruiser
- **Role:**

  - Heavy cruiser (downscaled)
  - Interdiction
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Greater Maldrood
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Owners:** High Admiral Treuten Teradoc
