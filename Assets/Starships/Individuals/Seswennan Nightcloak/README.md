# Seswennan Nightcloak

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seswennan Nightcloak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seswennan_Nightcloak)

Seswennan Nightcloak was a Carrack-class light cruiser employed by the Galactic Empire during the Battle of Fleyars IV. Following the death of its captain in the battle, the ship was commanded by Akal Zed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Seswennan Nightcloak
- **Manufacturer:** Damorian Manufacturing Corporation
- **Model:** Carrack-class light cruiser
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Role:** Frigate
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Captains:** Akal Zed
