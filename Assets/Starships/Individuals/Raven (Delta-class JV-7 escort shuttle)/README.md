# Raven (Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Raven (Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_%28Delta-class_JV-7_escort_shuttle%29)

Raven was a group of siz escort shuttles used by the Ripoblus. They were used in the final stage of the Empire's campaign to end the Sepan Civil War. Launching from the Nebulon-B2 frigate Shemsher to interfere with the Victory-class Star Destroyer Protector's retrieval of TIE Avengers from Escort Carrier Tropsobor. The Escort shuttles were destroyed in the battle.
