# Sar X

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sar X](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sar_X)

Sar X was a Sullustan Lambda-class shuttle that carried the Sullustan ambassador and docked with the Rebel Alliance ambassador's shuttle Freedom I at the diplomatic meeting between Sullust and the Alliance to Restore the Republic during Operation Strike Fear. The meeting was interrupted by the arrival of the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Invincible. Hamo Blastwell and Keyan Farlander, flying R-22 Spearheads, held off the Imperial fighters until the meeting was concluded and all ships fled into hyperspace.
