# Solii

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Solii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solii)

Solii was a group of three cargo ferries that were part of the Imperial Task Force Vengeance during the Galactic Civil War. Two of the ships were carrying capacitors, while the third one was carrying warheads. The one with the warheads was destroyed by Rogue Squadron during their recon mission of the Imperial fleet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Solii
- **Model:** Cargo ferry
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:** Task Force Vengeance
