# Fortune and Glory

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fortune and Glory/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fortune_and_Glory/Legends)

Fortune and Glory was a Personal Luxury Yacht 3000 owned by the Weequay pirate Hondo Ohnaka, which bore his symbol during the Clone Wars.
