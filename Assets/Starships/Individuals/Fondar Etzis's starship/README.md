# Fondar Etzis's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fondar Etzis's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fondar_Etzis%27s_starship)

Jedi Master Fondar Etzis used a starship when he visited the agricultural planet Mina-Rau to deal with some raids there. Etzis was assassinated while there, and his starship was investigated by the Jedi Qui-Gon Jinn, Mace Windu, and Aayla Secura.
