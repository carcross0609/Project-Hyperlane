# Sullust (CR90 corvette)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sullust (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sullust_%28CR90_corvette%29)

The Sullust was a Corellian Corvette in service to the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a task force commanded by Admiral Ackbar during the Battle of Ciutric, where it was severely damaged.
