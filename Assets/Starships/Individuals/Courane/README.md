# Courane

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Courane](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Courane)

The Courane was a DP20 gunship in service to the New Republic in 9 ABY. It was part of a small fleet of warships hastily assembled by Han Solo and Lando Calrissian to defend the Meridian sector from invasion.
