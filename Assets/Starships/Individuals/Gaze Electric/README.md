# Gaze Electric

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gaze Electric](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gaze_Electric)

The Gaze Electric was a starship used by the Evereni Ro family, whose members were part of the Path of the Open Hand religious cult, and later the Nihil pirate syndicate over the course of many decades of the High Republic Era.
