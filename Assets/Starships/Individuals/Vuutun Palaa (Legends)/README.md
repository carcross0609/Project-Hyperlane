# Vuutun Palaa

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vuutun Palaa/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vuutun_Palaa/Legends)

The Vuutun Palaa was a Lucrehulk-class battleship that powered the Trade Federation Droid Army on Naboo during the Trade Federation's invasion of the planet in 32 BBY. Headed by Captain Daultay Dofine, the ship was responsible for coordinating the invading battle droid armies on the surface of Naboo.
