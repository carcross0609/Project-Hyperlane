# Fogger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fogger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fogger)

The Fogger was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate that served with the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. The vessel was sent to relieve the Imperial I-class Star Destroyer Hammer in the Javin system, where the space station Outpost D-34 was located, after the Battle of Hoth.
