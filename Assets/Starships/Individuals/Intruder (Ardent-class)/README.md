# Intruder

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intruder (Ardent-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intruder_%28Ardent-class%29)

The Intruder was an Ardent-class fast frigate that served in the Imperial Navy during the Second Imperial Civil War. By 138 ABY, the ship was commanded by Captain Vaclen Tor and participated in the attack against the Hidden Temple of the Jedi on Taivas. During the battle, the Intruder was overwhelmed by the allied fleets of the Galactic Alliance and the Empire-in-exile and destroyed.

- **Type:** Frigate
