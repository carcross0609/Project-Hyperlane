# Wen Lu-Dasz's yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wen Lu-Dasz's yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wen_Lu-Dasz%27s_yacht)

A yacht was belonging to Wen Lu-Dasz, a wealthy and eccentric merchant. The replacement parts for the yacht could not be purchased through normal channels and required underworld connections.
