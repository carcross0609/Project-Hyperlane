# Eliminator

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eliminator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eliminator)

The Eliminator was a Star Destroyer in service to the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. In 0 ABY, around one week after the Battle of Yavin, in which the Galactic Empire's Death Star battle station was destroyed, the Eliminator was one of around a dozen Star Destroyers that formed the new core of the Imperial Starfleet.
