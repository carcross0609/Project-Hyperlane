# Togri

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Togri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Togri)

Togri was a MB-C1 medium transport belonging to the Imperials. Togri was part of the Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Corrupters vital resupply convoy lead by the Nebulon-B Frigate Monitor to the Corrupter in the Mev system. Togri's cargo was 'machinery'. The ship was then attacked by Rebel raiders and was destroyed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Togri
- **Manufacturer:** Mobquet Swoops and Speeders
- **Model:** MB-C1 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Length:** 113 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Corrupter repair convoy
