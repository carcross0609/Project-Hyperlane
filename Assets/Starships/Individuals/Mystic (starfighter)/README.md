# Mystic

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mystic (starfighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mystic_%28starfighter%29)

The Mystic was the personal Infiltrator-series long-range fighter of Darth Bane. He acquired it through one of his various contacts. The ship was a medium-sized starfighter. It had light weapons and minimal plating, to focus on speed and maneuverability. The ship had a Class Four hyperdrive, and could hold up to six passengers.

- **Name:** Mystic
- **Manufacturer:** Republic Sienar Systems
