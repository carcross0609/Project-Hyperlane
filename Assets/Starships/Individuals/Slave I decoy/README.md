# Slave I decoy

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Slave I decoy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slave_I_decoy)

This decoy vessel of the Slave I was utilized around 1 ABY by Boba Fett. The decoy was equipped with a beacon transmitter that was encoded with the Slave I identification. An empty shell that was constructed of a flimsy material, it was described by Zuckuss as lacking any mass. He and Bossk boarded this vessel after the latter attempted to destroy it, under the impression that it was the real Slave I.
