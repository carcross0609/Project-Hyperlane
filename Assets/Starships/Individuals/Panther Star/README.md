# Panther Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Panther Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panther_Star)

The Panther Star was a ship in the Galactic Alliance Defense Fleet that fought and was destroyed at the Battle of Centerpoint Station. It was under the command of a Sullustan.
