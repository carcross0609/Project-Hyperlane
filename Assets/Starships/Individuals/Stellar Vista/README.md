# Stellar Vista

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stellar Vista](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stellar_Vista)

The Stellar Vista was a Mon Calamari star cruiser during the Imperial Era. In the standard year 14 BBY, during the star cruiser's service as a luxury liner, it was pulled out of hyperspace by the orders of Imperial Moff Wilhuff Tarkin, who was seeking to recapture the Carrion Spike, his personal starship, which had been stolen by rebels.
