# Fori's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fori's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fori%27s_ship)

Fori's ship was a ship that belonged to the Twi'lek Fori Nagor by around 381 BBY. Around that year, Nagor visited the oldest settlement on the planet Dantooine, where she met Marda Ro&mdash;the Evereni founder of a group of pirates who would eventually become the Nihil. She asked Nagor whether she had a ship and offered Nagor a position in her group once Nagor mentioned the ship. Nagor then worked alongside the human Milan Stor, and they used the ship to search for opportunities for theft and smuggling. At some point no earlier than 379 BBY, the vessel was among the three ships Ro sent to intercept the Evereni siblings Vika and Velya Faer's ship in order to send the two Faers an invitation to meet her.
