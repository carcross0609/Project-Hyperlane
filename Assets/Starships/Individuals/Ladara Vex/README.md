# Ladara Vex

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ladara Vex](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ladara_Vex)

The Ladara Vex was a light cruiser in the navy of the First Order. Commander Branwayne Spiftz acquired the Ladara Vex for a mission to the planet Minfar. The Ladara Vex was destroyed by Poe Dameron, who along with Rose Tico, Rey, and BB-8, was called to assist Minfar by the native Zixon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ladara Vex
- **Type:** Light cruiser
- **Complement:** TIE fighters
- **Crew:**
  - Over 150
  -   50 stormtroopers
  -   TIE Fighter Pilots
  -   100 crew members that are not stormtroopers or TIE Fighter Pilots
- **Destroyed:** 34 ABY, above Minfar
- **Affiliation:** First Order
- **Navy:** First Order Navy
- **Captains:** Branwayne Spiftz
