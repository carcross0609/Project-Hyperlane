# Rampant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rampant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rampant)

Rampant was a Victory-class Star Destroyer in the service of the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. It was damaged during a campaign against the Rebel Alliance in the Boeus sector, around 2 ABY. When it was berthed at the Wroona Stardock for repairs, its port-most ion drive exploded in an attack by Wroonian outlaw Kabalard Vinne, a former representative of the Guild of Armament Distributors. Vinne said the sabotage was a response to Governor Norrin's recent crackdown on some of the less legitimate Wroonian guilds.
