# Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer (asteroid collision)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial-class_Star_Destroyer_%28asteroid_collision%29)

An Imperial-class Star Destroyer served with Sith Lord Darth Vader's Imperial Death Squadron&mdash;a task force assembled specifically for finding the headquarters of the Rebel Alliance&mdash;during the aftermath of the Hoth campaign of 3 ABY. The Star Destroyer was commanded by a captain. During the hunt for the Millennium Falcon in the Hoth asteroid field, the Star Destroyer's bridge tower was struck by a large asteroid, destroying the warship.
