# Auberge

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Auberge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Auberge)

The Auberge, formerly known as the Glitterstim Shine, was a war cruiser owned by a spice dealer from Kijimi until it was confiscated by the Nihil after the formation of the Stormwall. It eventually became the flagship of General Abediah Viess, with the heavy cruiser serving at the Battle of Eriadu.
