# Stormbringer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stormbringer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stormbringer)

The Stormbringer was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer captained by Davin Bryce. A hangar on the starship was hangar 14.

- **Name:** Stormbringer
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Line:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Mglt:** 60 MGLT
- **Max Speed:** 975 kilometers per hour
- **Engine:**
  - KDY Destroyer-I ion engines (3)
  - Cygnus Spaceworks Gemon-4 ion engines (4) (emergency use)
- **Power:** Solar ionization reactor
- **Shield Gen:** Deflector shield generator domes (2)
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolaser batteries (60)
  - Heavy ion cannon emplacements (60)
  - Tractor beam projectors (10)
- **Complement:**
  - Lambda-class T-4a shuttles
  - TIE fighter series starfighters (72)
- **Crew:** Stormtroopers (9,700)
- **Consumables:** 2 years
- **Destroyed:** During the mission to Tibrin
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Davin Bryce
