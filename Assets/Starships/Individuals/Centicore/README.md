# Centicore

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Centicore](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Centicore)

The Centicore was an Arquitens-class command cruiser in service to the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At some point, the light cruiser fired shots at a target from behind an asteroid. It was 230 meters in length and equipped with engines and two twin light turbolaser batteries.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Centicore
- **Model:** Arquitens-class command cruiser
- **Type:** Light cruiser
- **Length:** 230 meters
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Two twin light turbolaser batteries
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
