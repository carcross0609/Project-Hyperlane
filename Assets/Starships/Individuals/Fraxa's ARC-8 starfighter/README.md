# Fraxa's ARC-8 starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fraxa's ARC-8 starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fraxa%27s_ARC-8_starfighter)

An antique ARC-8 starfighter was owned by Fraxa, owner of Kwikhaul Livery Corporation on the planet Kwenn. The ARC-8 starfighter was originally from the High Republic Era, during which it had seen extensive use. Although Fraxa attempted to restore the starfighter, she was mainly limited to cosmetic work. During the Jedi High Council's mission to Kwenn, Jedi Master Saesee Tiin helped to repair the starfighter in exchange for being able to take it for a test flight. Tiin flew the starfighter to defend Kwenn against a pirate attack orchestrated by Zilastra of the Riftwalkers.
