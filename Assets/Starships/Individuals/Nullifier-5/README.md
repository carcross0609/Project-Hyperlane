# Nullifier-5

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nullifier-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nullifier-5)

During the battle, the Nullifier-5 was severely damaged and crashed into Mon Calamari's watery surface near the Algae Sward. The burnt-out hulk sank to the ocean floor, where it became an artificial reef.
