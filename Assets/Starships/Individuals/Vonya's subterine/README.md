# Vonya's subterine

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vonya's subterine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vonya%27s_subterine)

This subterine was operated by the Nightsister Vonya around 31 BBY. Starting as a space freighter, it was captured by the Nightsisters of Dathomir and modified for underground travel. They used it in an attempt to penetrate the Star Chamber beneath one of the Star Temples, which housed the Infinity Gate.
