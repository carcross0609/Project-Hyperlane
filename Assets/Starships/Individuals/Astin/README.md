# Astin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Astin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Astin)

The Astin was an Imperial CR90 corvette that sent a TIE Fighter to destroy a Rebel probe called PR-V74 near Klaymor 4-2 which was 15 Klicks from its location. After the fighter was destroyed the probe the ship returned to pick up the fighter. The fighter returned despite a Rebel Nebulon-B frigate Rogelo arrived to launch Z-95 Headhunters at the craft.
