# Requiem

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Requiem](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Requiem)

The Requiem was an Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser that was part of the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At one point, it flew over a body of water with its complement of four TIE/ln space superiority starfighters, which were carried on the cruiser's underside by docking tubes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Requiem
- **Model:** Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Complement:** TIE/ln space superiority starfighters (4)
- **Othersystems:** Docking tubes
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
