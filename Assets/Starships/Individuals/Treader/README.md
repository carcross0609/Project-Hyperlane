# Treader

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Treader](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treader)

Treader was a BFF-1 bulk freighter in the service of the Galactic Empire. The freighter was scheduled to arrive with the Nebulon-B escort frigate Mayhem with base equipment along with the freighters Strider and Walker. However, the Rebel Alliance attacked and destroyed the freighter along with Strider and, after liberating the Habassa slaves held aboard it, the Walker as well.
