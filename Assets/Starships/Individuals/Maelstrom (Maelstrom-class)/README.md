# Maelstrom

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maelstrom (Maelstrom-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maelstrom_%28Maelstrom-class%29)

The Maelstrom was the lead Maelstrom-class battle cruiser of the Galactic Republic's navy in the time of the Clone Wars, which took place between 22 BBY and 19 BBY. Under the command of Jedi General Darrus Jeht, the battlecruiser served with distinction in the Battle of Kromus and the Battle of Cularin during the war.

- **Model:** Maelstrom-class battle cruiser
