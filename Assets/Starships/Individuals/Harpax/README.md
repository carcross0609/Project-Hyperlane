# Harpax

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harpax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harpax)

The Harpax was an Immobilizer 418 cruiser that served in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of the task force that was dispatched to destroy the forces formerly under the command of the defector Admiral Harkov. It trapped his Victory-class Star Destroyer, Protector while it took on supplies.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Harpax
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Model:** Immobilizer 418 cruiser
- **Length:** 600 meters
- **Width:** 300 meters
- **Height:** 100 meters
- **Armament:** SFS G7-x gravity well projectors (4)
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
