# Tharen Wayfarer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tharen Wayfarer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tharen_Wayfarer)

The Tharen Wayfarer was a starship owned and operated by Kalarba Safari, a Pitareeze family company. The family used the ship to take guests on the "Journey of the Spirit" tour, traveling over the Great Sea and past the Three Peaks of Tharen, Kalarba's symbol of the spirit.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tharen Wayfarer
- **Affiliation:** Kalarba Safari
- **Owners:** Pitareeze family
