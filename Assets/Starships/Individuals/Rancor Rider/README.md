# Rancor Rider

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rancor Rider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rancor_Rider)

The Tholothian mercenary Ty Yorrick flew a starship during the High Republic Era. Accustomed to rejecting attachment to what she perceived as tools, Yorrick gave the YT-750 freighter no name, only using a variety of registration numbers for the vessel when docked in Galactic Republic ports. The Segredo Drewen Qweebjillan nicknamed the ship Rancor Rider, though Yorrick disapprove of the name. Yorrick eventually agreed to the name.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rancor Rider
- **Model:** YT-750 freighter
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Fleet:** Stormwall Defense Fleet
- **Owners:** Ty Yorrick
- **Registry:** Several, depending on job and employer
