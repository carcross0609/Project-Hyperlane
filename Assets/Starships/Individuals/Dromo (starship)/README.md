# Dromo

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dromo (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromo_%28starship%29)

The Dromo was an Imperial supply superfreighter used by the Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War. The ship was part of a convoy that traveled from the Kravos system to the occupied planet of Coruscant. Smuggler Zeerid Korr followed the Dromo to Coruscant space, where he attached his ship, the Fatman, to the superfreighter's hull.

- **Type:** Superfreighter
