# Majestic Gundark

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Majestic Gundark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Majestic_Gundark)

The Majestic Gundark was a YT-1300 light freighter with only slight modifications, including an upper and a lower turret. It was used by the eco-terrorist group Army of Life.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Majestic Gundark
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Transport
- **Length:** 26.7 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Top laser cannon
  - Belly laser cannon
- **Crew:** 1 to 2
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 100 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Affiliation:** Army of Life
- **Captains:** Del Binjitt
