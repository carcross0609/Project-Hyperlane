# Warrior

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Warrior (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Warrior_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

The Warrior was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer set to rendezvous with an Imperial convoy carrying supplies and slaves by Mon Calamari at about 2 BBY. The Warrior arrived early only to discover a Rebel force attempting to liberate the slaves. In responce, TIE/LN starfighters were launched against the Rebels. One Rebel pilot, Halley Kadorto, was able to hold off the TIEs until the Alliance escaped with the slaves.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Warrior
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/LN starfighters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
