# Torton Runner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Torton Runner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torton_Runner)

The Torton Runner was a heavy freighter owned by the Hidden Daggers pirates during the Galactic Civil War. In 1 ABY, the Hidden Daggers obtained sensitive information about Imperial operations on Corellia and planned to deliver the data to the Rebel Alliance. However, the Torton Runner carrying the sensitive information was attacked in the Corellian system by Imperial starfighters led by Prisk Kith'Vys. The Imperial Navy special unit Black Epsilon disabled the Torton Runner engines and captured the ship, thereby securing the data.

## Specifications
