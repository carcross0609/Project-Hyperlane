# Rabid Nek Dog

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rabid Nek Dog](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rabid_Nek_Dog)

The Rabid Nek Dog was a YT-1300 light freighter that was used by a group of smugglers. The Sullustan Syv Bullnumull served aboard the freighter and she led the rest of the crew in a mutiny against the ship's captain and mate, stranding the pair on the planet Hok. Afterward, Bullnumull became the new leader of band of smugglers, and the crew soon adapted to her new regime.
