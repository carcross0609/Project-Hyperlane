# Mule 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mule 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mule_1)

Mule 1 was a Heavy Lifter used by the Galactic Empire. Mule 1, one of five craft from flight group Mule, was tasked to recover usable parts from an enemy supply depot destroyed in the Argoon system during the Battle on the Frontier. Maarek Stele was among pilots responsible for providing starfighter cover while group Mule completed docking operations with various Cargo containers.
