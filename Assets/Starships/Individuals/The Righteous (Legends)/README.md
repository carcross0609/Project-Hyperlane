# The Righteous

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [The Righteous/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Righteous/Legends)

The Righteous was a Venator-class Star Destroyer that served in the Republic Navy. The Righteous fell in battle with the acclaimed Admiral Trench prior to the Battle of Christophsis of 22 BBY. As a member of the Venator-class, it was 1,137 meters in length.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Righteous
- **Type:** Venator-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,137 meters
- **Role:** Destroyer
- **Destroyed:** Prior to the Battle of Christophsis in 22 BBY
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Republic Navy
