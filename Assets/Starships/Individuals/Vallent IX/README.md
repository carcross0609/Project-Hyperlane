# Vallent IX

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vallent IX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vallent_IX)

The Vallent IX was a CR90 corvette that was used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. It was captured in 1 ABY by a traitorous Alliance engineer who planned to deliver it to the Ghrag Mercenaries. However, the ship was retaken by Rebel forces and the Civilian Protection Guild in the Kashyyyk system.
