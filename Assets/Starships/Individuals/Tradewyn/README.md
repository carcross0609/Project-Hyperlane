# Tradewyn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tradewyn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tradewyn)

The Tradewyn was the Mon Calamari Star Cruiser flagship of Bornaryn Trading, and the home of the Thul family after the destruction of Alderaan. Raynar Thul was born on the Tradewyn circa 9 ABY.
