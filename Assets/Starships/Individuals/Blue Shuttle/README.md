# Blue Shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blue Shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Shuttle)

Blue Shuttle was a Beta-class assault shuttle made by Telgorn Corporation and stationed aboard the Eye of Palpatine. In 12 ABY, Triv Pothman used it to evacuate the inhabitants of the Eye.
