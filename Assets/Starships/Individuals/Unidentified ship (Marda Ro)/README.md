# Unidentified ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified ship (Marda Ro)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_ship_%28Marda_Ro%29)

A ship belonged to the Path of the Open Hand cult during the time it was led by a woman called the Mother. After the Path's violent end in 382 BBY, the starship became a possession of former Path member Marda Ro&mdash;the Evereni founder of a group of pirates who would eventually become the Nihil. Ro's pirates then used the ship to carry out their raids, sometimes using it alongside their other ships. At some point no earlier than 379 BBY, the starship was one of those sent by Ro to intercept the ship of the Evereni siblings Vika and Velya Faer while she sent the twins an invitation to meet with her.
