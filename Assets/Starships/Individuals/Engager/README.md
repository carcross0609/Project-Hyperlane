# Engager

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Engager](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Engager)

The Engager was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer under the command of Captain Dalton. Shortly before the Battle of Yavin she was disaptched to Rafft to eliminate Rebel activity on the planet.
