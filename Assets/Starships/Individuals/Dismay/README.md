# Dismay

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dismay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dismay)

The Dismay was an Imperial Star Destroyer during the Galactic Civil War. The Dismay captain intended to marry Praleena Pring, daughter of Imperial Consul-General Dandamont Pring of the planet Bothawui, but Praleena and her lover instead defected to the Rebel Alliance.
