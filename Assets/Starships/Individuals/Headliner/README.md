# Headliner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Headliner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Headliner)

Headliner was a YT-1220, that was owned by the Tourism Council of Oluna Biqua during the Clone Wars. It was used as an in-system shuttle in the Cularin system and was piloted by Dellko, a Rodian. The Council sent it to take the Heroes of Cularin to Oluna Biqua, as they were going there for a luxury holiday. On arrival there, it was told to land at Port Epsilon, which it did.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Headliner
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
