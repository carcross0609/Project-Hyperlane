# Vika and Velya Faer's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vika and Velya Faer's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vika_and_Velya_Faer%27s_ship)

A ship was used by the Evereni siblings Vika and Velya Faer during the High Republic Era. At some point no earlier than 379 BBY, they refueled it in a space dock orbiting the gas giant Siquay. While they were at the dock, Marda Ro&mdash;an Evereni pirate leader hunting others of her species&mdash;caught up to them and invited them onto her ship, an invite that the two Faers accepted. Ro later made a recording for her descendants, in which she mentioned the ship. By 252 BBY, her great-great-grandson Marchion Ro had listened to the message that mentioned the ship.

## Specifications
