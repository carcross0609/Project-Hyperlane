# LAAT Three

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LAAT Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LAAT_Three)

LAAT Three was an LAAT/i Republic gunship deployed during the Battle of Abrion Bridge during the Clone Wars. The gunship was shot down by the Separatist forces before it could land, making it more difficult for General Obi-Wan Kenobi and the 212th Attack Battalion to take the bridge.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LAAT Three
- **Line:** Low Altitude Assault Transport
- **Model:** Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry
- **Type:** Gunship
