# Carnage

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Carnage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carnage)

The Carnage was a Harrower-class dreadnought and flagship of the Sith Lord Darth Achelon. During the Galactic War, the Carnage fought a Republic fleet over Corellia. Moffs Valion Pyron, Dolus, Graham and Lord Kallig were discussing an alliance and decided to use this opportunity to test out the Doombringer Silencer superweapon against the fleet that was engaged against the Carnage.
