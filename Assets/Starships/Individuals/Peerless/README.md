# Peerless

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Peerless](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Peerless)

The Peerless was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. The 1,600-meter-long Star Destroyer featured sublight engines, a hangar, and two deflector shield generator domes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Peerless
- **Line:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Engine:** Sublight engines
- **Shield Gen:** Deflector shield generator domes (2)
- **Bays:** Hangar (1)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
