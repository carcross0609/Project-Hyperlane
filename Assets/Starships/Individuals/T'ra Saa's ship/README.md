# T'ra Saa's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T'ra Saa's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T%27ra_Saa%27s_ship)

T'ra Saa's ship was a ship Jedi Master T'ra Saa used to fly to the Azurbani system on a mission given her by the Jedi High Council to meet with Quinlan Vos and inform him of Volfe Karkko's history. The ship was shot down by the Kiffu Guardians and crashed on Kiffex, although Master Saa survived to complete her mission. It was later repaired by the Devaronian smuggler and friend of Quinlan Vos, Vilmarh Grahrk, who instead of fleeing Kiffex, returned and assisted the Jedi.
