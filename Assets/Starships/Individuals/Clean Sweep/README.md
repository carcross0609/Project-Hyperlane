# Clean Sweep

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Clean Sweep](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clean_Sweep)

Clean Sweep was a modified YT-1300 light freighter that was owned by the Comet Broom Service during the waning years of the Galactic Republic. It was based on Artom Station, in the Cularin system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Clean Sweep
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Type:** Space transport
- **Length:** 26.7 meters
- **Armament:** Laser cannon
- **Crew:** 1 or 2
- **Passengers:** 6
- **Capacity:** 100 tonnes
- **Consumables:** 2 months
- **Affiliation:** Comet Broom Service
