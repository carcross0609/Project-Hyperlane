# Discord

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Discord (light cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Discord_%28light_cruiser%29)

The Discord was a light cruiser that served in the Confederate Navy during the Clone Wars. During the conflict, Captain Gilad Pellaeon of the Republic ship Leveler mistakenly identified the Confederate destroyer Reaper as being the Discord, while in the Fath system, due to flawed data that was supplied to him by Republic Intelligence.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Discord
- **Type:** Light cruiser
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
