# Lina's starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lina's starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lina%27s_starfighter)

A starfighter was used by the E'roni pilot Lina during the war between her homeworld E'ronoh and the planet Eiram. Between 387 BBY and 382 BBY, Lina intended to undertake a mission to Eiram's western isles, though her fighter's thruster malfunctioned moments after she took off and she crashed into Ramshead Gorge.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lina's starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Destroyed:** Between 387 BBY and 382 BBY
- **Affiliation:** E'ronoh's military
- **Navy:** E'ronoh's fleet
- **Owners:** Lina
