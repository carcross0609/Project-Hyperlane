# Rigel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rigel (transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rigel_%28transport%29)

Rigel was a Muurian transport that carried bartenders of the Dunari's Rest when the latter was attacked by Imperial forces. It made it to the hyperspace intact thanks to assistance from the Rebel Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rigel
- **Manufacturer:** Muurian Interstellar
- **Model:** Muurian transport
- **Length:** 30.5 meters
- **Width:** 29.4 meters
- **Height:** 8.8 meters
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 2 double laser cannon turrets
  - 3 laser cannons
  - 2 proton torpedo launchers
