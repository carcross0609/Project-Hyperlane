# Kalif

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kalif](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalif)

Kalif was a Xiytiar-class heavy transport belonging to the Imperials. Kalif was part of the Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Corrupter's vital resupply convoy lead by the Nebulon-B Frigate Monitor to the Corrupter in the Mev system. Kalif cargo was "probe droids". The ship was then attacked by Rebel raiders and was destroyed.
