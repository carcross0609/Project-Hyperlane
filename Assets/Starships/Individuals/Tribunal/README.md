# Tribunal

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tribunal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tribunal)

The Tribunal was a Venator-class Star Destroyer that served in the Galactic Republic naval forces' Open Circle Fleet toward the end of the Clone Wars. In 19 BBY, the Tribunal was used to transport the former Sith apprentice Maul to the Republic's capital planet, Coruscant, following his capture by the 332nd Division and former Jedi Padawan Ahsoka Tano during the siege of the planet Mandalore. However, while traveling to Coruscant, Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Sidious enacted Order 66, which forced the clone troopers of the Grand Army of the Republic to execute their Jedi commanding officers.
