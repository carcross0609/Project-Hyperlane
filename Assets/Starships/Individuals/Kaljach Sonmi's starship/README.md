# Kaljach Sonmi's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaljach Sonmi's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaljach_Sonmi%27s_starship)

The Heptooinian Kaljach Sonmi owned a starship while traveling to Cantonica. The starship was held as ransom by Kaljach's accountant Mosep Binneed until he repaid his debts. However, Orisha Okum stole it while fleeing Cantonica upon losing a round of pazaak.
