# Ascent

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ascent](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ascent)

The Ascent was an Imperial Star Destroyer which, along with the Vigilance and the Vanquish, was present at the planet Akiva for the battle there. It brought one of the Empire's greatest tacticians, General Jylia Shale, to the emergency summit on Akiva. It was destroyed after the summit.
