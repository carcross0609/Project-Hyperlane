# Green/Aura

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Green/Aura](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Green/Aura)

Green/Aura was an Imperial Navy IPV-1 System Patrol Craft, and, alongside the IPV-1 Green/Besh and the Lancer-class frigate Rage, was stationed in the year 0 ABY at the Imperial Bavos-II military space platform Station 1B6/Green on the edge of the Core Worlds's Ringali Nebula. As an IPV-1 measuring 150 meters long and produced by the company Sienar Fleet Systems, the Green/Aura could reach a speed of one thousand kilometers per hour, was armed with four turbolasers, and acted as an inter-system patrol and customs craft.
