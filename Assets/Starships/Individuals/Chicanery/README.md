# Chicanery

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chicanery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chicanery)

The Chicanery was a freighter owned and operated by the smuggler Ramsey Lanclo. While running a cargo of glitterstim, the ship was boarded by Imperial Customs agent Hewat. The quick thinking of Lanclo's cooking droid, XDL-67, distracted Hewat from the contraband by gifting her a marsupial keon to give to her daughter, Kora.
