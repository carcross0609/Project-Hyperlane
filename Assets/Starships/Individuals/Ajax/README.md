# Ajax

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ajax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ajax)

The Ajax was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was involved in the Battle of Turkana. It ended up severely damaged alongside the Formidable after Rebel starfighters unloaded their proton torpedoes onto their bridge shields.
