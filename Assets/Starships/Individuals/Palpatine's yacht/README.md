# Palpatine's yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Palpatine's yacht](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palpatine%27s_yacht)

A yacht was once owned by Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine. It was Palpatine's favorite yacht on Naboo and was utilized on Coruscant at least once for a visit to the Jedi Temple. Sometime after Palpatine's death during the Battle of Endor, the ship came into the hands of General Brendol Hux of the First Order, who crashed it on the planet Parnassos after it was damaged by the planet's defense grid. The armor worn by Captain Phasma was coated in chromium that was salvaged from the yacht after she tore it apart and forged the armor in one of the planet's abandoned mining stations.
