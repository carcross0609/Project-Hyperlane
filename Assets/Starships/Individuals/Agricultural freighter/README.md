# Agricultural freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Agricultural freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agricultural_freighter)

During the last decades of the Galactic Republic, an agricultural freighter in the Baobab Merchant Fleet embarked on an expedition to find Roonstones. After the ship crashed on the planet Roon, Oggem Baobab was stranded there and lived in the freighter for the next sixty years until he was found by his great nephew, Mungo Baobab, in 15 BBY.
