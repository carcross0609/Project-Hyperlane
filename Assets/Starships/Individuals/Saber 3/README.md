# Saber 3

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saber 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saber_3)

Saber 3 was an Imperial Navy TIE/IN interceptor which was manufactured by Sienar Fleet Systems. Part of the 181st Imperial Fighter Wing's Saber Squadron by 4 ABY, Saber 3 was piloted by DS-181-3, who flew as the wingman of Saber Squadron's Baron Soontir Fel, watching Fel's back. In Saber 3's capacity of flying as Fel's wing, the interceptor was often ordered to remain at a distance from the baron in order to give him maneuvering room during starfighter combat.
