# Red Squadron 7

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Red Squadron 7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Squadron_7)

Red Squadron 7 was an Incom Corporation T-65 X-wing starfighter in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The fighter was part of Red Squadron during the Battle of Endor, piloted by Keir Santage. Previously, the fighter had participated in twelve sorties against the Fondor Shipyards.
