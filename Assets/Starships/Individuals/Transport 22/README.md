# Transport 22

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Transport 22](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transport_22)

Transport 22 was the designation for a StarSpeeder 3000 craft owned by the Star Tours travel agency. C-3PO and R2-D2 performed maintenance and repairs on this vessel while working at the travel agency months after the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Transport 22
- **Model:** StarSpeeder 3000
- **Armament:** Laser cannons (2)
- **Crew:** 2 (droids)
- **Passengers:** 40
- **Role:** Passenger transport
- **Affiliation:** Star Tours
