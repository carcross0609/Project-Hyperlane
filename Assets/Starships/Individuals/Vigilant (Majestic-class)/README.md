# Vigilant (Majestic-class)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vigilant (Majestic-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vigilant_%28Majestic-class%29)

The Vigilant was a Majestic-class heavy cruiser that saw service within the Fifth Battle Group of the New Republic Defense Force in 16 ABY. The warship participated in the New Republic's campaign against the Duskhan League during the Black Fleet Crisis.
