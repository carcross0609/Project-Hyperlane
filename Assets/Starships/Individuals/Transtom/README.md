# Transtom

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Transtom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transtom)

The Transtom was an Imperial fuel container active during the Galactic Civil War. It was destroyed while en route to the main fleet. Its loss was reported in the Imperial Commission on the Conduct of the War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Transtom
- **Type:** Fuel container
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
