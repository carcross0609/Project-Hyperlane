# Ace's XS stock light freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ace's XS stock light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ace%27s_XS_stock_light_freighter)

The smuggler more commonly known as Ace owned an XS stock light freighter during the Cold War and the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Having owned it for a while already, the smuggler had the freighter stolen by the criminal Skavak. Ace followed Skavak to the planet Coruscant, where he had taken the vessel, and eventually retrieved it.
