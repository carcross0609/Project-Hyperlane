# Stellar Crawler

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stellar Crawler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stellar_Crawler)

The Stellar Crawler was a modified YT-2400 light freighter commanded by Captain Nel Frel, a member of the Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service, during the Galactic Civil War. The Stellar Crawler regularly stopped over Trivar II's north pole to communicate with the growing rebel network there. It was also involved in Operation Night Fire.
