# Pearl's Girl

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pearl's Girl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pearl%27s_Girl)

The Pearl's Girl was a hoverbarge serving as flagship of the Ravagers pirate gang. It was used as their headquarters together with their cove in Raider's Cove, on Rishi, where the ship was usually docked. In 3637 BBY a team of Imperial and Republic heroes attacked the Ravagers's base in Raider's Cove, so many pirates boarded the Pearl's Girl, trying to leave the planet surface, but the team managed to board the ship before the take off.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pearl's Girl
- **Escapepods:** At least 2
- **Destroyed:** 3637 BBY, Rishi
- **Affiliation:** Ravagers
- **Captains:**
