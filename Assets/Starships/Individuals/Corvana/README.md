# Corvana

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corvana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corvana)

Corvana was a junker ship used by the Balmorran resistance during the Galactic War. It was named by its pilot Ari Skyff in honor of his previous ship, the Corvani.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Corvana
- **Type:** Junker ship
- **Affiliation:** Balmorran resistance
