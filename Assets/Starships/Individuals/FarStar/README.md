# FarStar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FarStar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FarStar)

The FarStar was a CR90 corvette originally known as the Freedom's Messenger that served as a diplomatic ship for the Galactic Republic. After being lost in the Battle of Ord Torrenze, it was recovered by the Imperial Navy and renamed Renegade. After being heavily modified by Moff Kentor Sarne, the ship was captured by the New Republic, renamed FarStar, and used to hunt him down. It was destroyed in the final battle against Sarne at Kathol.
