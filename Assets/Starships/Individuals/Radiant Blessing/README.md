# Radiant Blessing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Radiant Blessing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Radiant_Blessing)

The Radiant Blessing was a starship used by the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. Following the Great Hyperspace Disaster, the Radiant Blessing rescued a stranded party led by Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh from the moon Wevo after Jedi Master Sskeer and his Padawan Keeve Trennis located them while on their way to the Starlight Beacon from Shuraden.
