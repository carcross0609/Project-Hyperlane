# Hunter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hunter (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

Hunter was an Imperial Navy Nebulon-B frigate, it was active in the Galactic Civil War about two years before the Battle of Yavin. Sometime after the Battle of Yavin, along with the Nebulon B Frigate Avenger, it launched an attack on the Habassan freighter group Starlifter. Keyan Farlander and a number of B-Wings were successful in holding off the attack destroying both frigates.
