# Javelin

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Javelin (Harrower-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Javelin_%28Harrower-class%29)

The Javelin was an Harrower-class dreadnought active during the Galactic War. The reconstituted Sith Empire used it during the Battle of Corellia in an attempt to destroy the Rift Alliance Coalition Forces by setting a trap. Acting as if escorting troop carriers to Corellia, the ship was in fact planned to self-destruct after being boarded by the Barsen'thor and the Rift Alliance. However, the Jedi and his allies managed to capture the ship instead.
