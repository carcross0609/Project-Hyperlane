# Victory 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Victory 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Victory_1)

Victory 1 was an Imperial Victory II-class Star Destroyer. It was the vessel responsible for the destruction of the two Rebel Nebulon-B Frigates Cruuz and Fox in the Battle of the Bajic Shipyards.

- **Name:** Victory 1
- **Model:** Victory II-class Star Destroyer
