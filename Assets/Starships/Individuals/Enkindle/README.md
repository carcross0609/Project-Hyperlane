# Enkindle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Enkindle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enkindle)

The Enkindle was a Valor-class cruiser in service to the Republic Navy during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. The Enkindle was damaged during a failed incursion into Imperial space, and despite its attempt to hide in the upper atmosphere of Sarapin Four, Imperial forces destroyed it there before reinforcements arrived.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Enkindle
- **Type:** Valor-class cruiser
- **Destroyed:** c. 3643 BBY, Sarapin system
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
