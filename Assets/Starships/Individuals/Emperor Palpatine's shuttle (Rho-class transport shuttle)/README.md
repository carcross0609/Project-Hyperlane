# Emperor Palpatine's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Emperor Palpatine's shuttle (Rho-class transport shuttle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine%27s_shuttle_%28Rho-class_transport_shuttle%29)

During the early reign of the Galactic Empire Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine used a Rho-class transport shuttle as his personal craft. It was heavily guarded including by Emperor's Royal Guards and, during a visit to Tantiss Base on the planet Wayland, accompanied by four Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighters. Unlike most Rho-class transport shuttles, it lacked the ventral cargo pod and was gray-colored with red highlights on the wings.
