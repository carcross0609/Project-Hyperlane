# Obi-Wan Kenobi's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Obi-Wan Kenobi's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi%27s_Eta-2_Actis-class_interceptor)

During the later period of the Clone Wars, Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi flew an Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor. Kenobi piloted the interceptor during a mission to rescue Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine during the Battle of Coruscant. The starfighter was destroyed aboard the Separatist flagship Invisible Hand.
