# Vigilance

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vigilance (Etti light cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vigilance_%28Etti_light_cruiser%29)

The Vigilance was an Etti light cruiser that was used by the Corporate Sector Authority during the reign of the New Republic. In 19 ABY, the Vigilance and the CSA Invincible-class Dreadnaught Heavy Cruiser Resolute captured the freighter Barabi Run near to the planet Rhilithan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vigilance
- **Manufacturer:** Corporate Sector Authority
- **Model:** Etti light cruiser
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Affiliation:** Corporate Sector Authority
