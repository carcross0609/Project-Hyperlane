# Night Gyre

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Night Gyre](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Gyre)

The Night Gyre was a Z-95 Headhunter that was used by the Jedi Master Darrus Jeht during the waning years of the Galactic Republic. It was purchased by the Jedi Order via the Almas Academy and assigned to Jeht while he was a student, becoming his first starship. The craft suffered from a sensor ghost as a result of an encounter that it had had with an asteroid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Night Gyre
- **Model:** Z-95 Headhunter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 11.8 meters
