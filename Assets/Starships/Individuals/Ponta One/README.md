# Ponta One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ponta One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ponta_One)

The Ponta One was an SC3000 freighter cargo ship owned by Akshaya Ponta. Akshaya's daughter, Tanith Ponta worked aboard the freighter, causing her to become ill with bloodburn. After Tanith's death, Akhaya banned her son Hadder from piloting, fearing he may get the disease.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ponta One
- **Model:** SC3000 freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Destroyed:** Around 4 BBY, above Skuhl
- **Owners:** Akshaya Ponta
