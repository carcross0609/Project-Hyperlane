# Sweet Time

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sweet Time](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sweet_Time)

The Sweet Time was a modified Kuat Drive Yards space yacht used by a Jedi Coalition strike team&mdash;composed of Jedi Ben Skywalker, Leia Organa Solo, Han Solo and Jaina Solo&mdash;as their base of operations during a mission to Coruscant. After Skywalker was captured by Sith apprentice Tahiri Veila and the Galactic Alliance Guard, the rest of the strike team flew the Sweet Time into a Jedi Coalition rendezvous point to report Ben's capture to his father, Luke Skywalker.
