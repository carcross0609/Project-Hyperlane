# Grand Admiral Pitta's torpedo sphere

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grand Admiral Pitta's torpedo sphere](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Admiral_Pitta%27s_torpedo_sphere)

Grand Admiral Pitta's torpedo sphere was a nineteen-hundred-meter vessel that Grand Admiral Danetta Pitta took command of when he became a warlord independent from the Galactic Empire. In 5 ABY's Battle of Tralus, Pitta used the torpedo sphere to decimate the warship armada of rival Grand Admiral Josef Grunger, but the latter then rammed his flagship, the Aggressor, into Pitta's craft, killing both of them.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grand Admiral Pitta's torpedo sphere
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Defense Industries
- **Model:** Torpedo sphere
- **Length:** 1,900 meters (diameter)
- **Captains:** Danetta Pitta
