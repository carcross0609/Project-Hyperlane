# Unidentified YT-1300 light freighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified YT-1300 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_YT-1300_light_freighter)

A YT-1300 light freighter was used by the bounty hunter team known as the Yagaran Four at some point by the time during the Galactic Civil War. It was heavily armed, and its hyperdrive was modified&mdash;albeit dangerously so&mdash;by the mechanic known as "Maniac." When the Yagaran Four activated the freighter's hyperdrive during a pursuit after a target on the celestial body Elin Roe, however, the craft exploded, killing the team and sending a cloud of radioactive dust washing over Elin Roe's fifth moon.
