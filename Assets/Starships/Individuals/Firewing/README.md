# Firewing

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firewing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firewing)

The Firewing was an unmarked starship originally owned by a Wookiee Smuggler and some of his human co-pilots. The bounty hunter Scree killed them aboard a neutral space station above Corellia, taking Firewing to sneak aboard the Founder, the personal flagship of Supreme Chancellor Dorian Janarus.
