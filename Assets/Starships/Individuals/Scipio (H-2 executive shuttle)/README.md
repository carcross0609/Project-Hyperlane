# Scipio

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scipio (H-2 executive shuttle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scipio_%28H-2_executive_shuttle%29)

The Scipio was Rush Clovis's personal H-2 executive shuttle. He used it while on the planet Scipio, which was its namesake. Beneath the foremost viewports flanking the vessel were letterings in aurebesh which spelt its name.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scipio
- **Model:** H-2 executive shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Engine:** 2
- **Crew:** Pilot (1)
- **Role:** Transport
- **Owners:** Rush Clovis
