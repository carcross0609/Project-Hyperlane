# Valiant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Valiant (Imperial cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valiant_%28Imperial_cruiser%29)

The Valiant class was an Imperial cruiser that saw service within the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. In about 0 BBY, the Valiant was dispatched to the Circarpous system accompanied by another cruiser and a Star Destroyer, where the three vessels ambushed the YT-1300 light freighter Millennium Falcon near to the star Circarpous Major. Commander Corsa&mdash;an Imperial officer who served aboard the Valiant&mdash;hailed the freighter and ordered the ship to stand down and prepare to be boarded. However, the Falcon took evasive action and successfully avoided capture by the Imperials.
