# F's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [F's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F%27s_ship)

F's ship was a Clone Z-95 Headhunter flown by the fugitive Jedi F in the time between 19 BBY and 1 BBY. F used the starfighter to travel to the planet Keelia after being summoned there by an old ally, Valco. After F helped a local village fight back against raiders, F departed the planet aboard her starship.
