# Peerless

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Peerless/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Peerless/Legends)

Peerless was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer which served under Grand Admiral Pellaeon during the Battle of Borosk. During the battle, Peerless provided escort to the Defiant, as that ship was equipped with a gravitic amplitude modulator to jam the communications of a Yuuzhan Vong yammosk. Following the Yuuzhan Vong withdrawal from Borosk, Peerless was among the Star Destroyers ordered deployed to assist in the defense of Yaga Minor.
