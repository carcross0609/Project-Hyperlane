# Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zavian Abyss)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Venator-class_Star_Destroyer_%28Zavian_Abyss%29)

The wreckage of a Venator-class Star Destroyer was present in the Zavian Abyss the New Republic's Vanguard Squadron came across the wreckage of a Venator-class, while escorting three GR-75 medium transports, before coming into contact with the Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Overseer. This was part of a New Republic ambush in the Zavian Abyss, where they revealed their prototype Starhawk.
