# Grrtok's H'muck; "Hammer"

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grrtok's H'muck](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grrtok%27s_H%27muck)

Grrtok's H'muck, translated as Hammer, was a modified bulk freighter. It was owned and operated by the Gamorrean Grrtok, and was used to transport his mercenary band around the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grrtok's H'muck; "Hammer"
- **Type:** Modified bulk freighter
- **Armament:** 2 light laser cannons
- **Affiliation:** Grrtok's Band
- **Owners:** Grrtok
