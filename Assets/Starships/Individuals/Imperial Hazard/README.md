# Imperial Hazard

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial Hazard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Hazard)

The Imperial Hazard was a Victory-class Star Destroyer that served in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. Its captain was Sergus Lanox, and the ship participated in attacking the Alliance base on Horob. The ship was damaged due to the bold intervention of Rebel strategist Sayer Mon Neela in the yacht Starcrossed.
