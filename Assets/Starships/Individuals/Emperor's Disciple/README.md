# Emperor's Disciple

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Emperor's Disciple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor%27s_Disciple)

The Emperor's Disciple was a Star Destroyer in the Imperial Navy that served as the personal flagship of the Imperial Grand Admiral Ishin-Il-Raz. Seeking the approval of Emperor Palpatine following his appointment to the rank in 2 BBY, Il-Raz commanded several successful missions from the bridge of the Emperor's Disciple, including the Massacre of Myomar and the bombardment of Shalam.
