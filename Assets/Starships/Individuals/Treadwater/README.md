# Treadwater

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Treadwater](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treadwater)

The Treadwater was a 720 light freighter. At some point during the Galactic Civil War, the Rebel operatives Sabine Wren, Rex, and Chopper went on a mission to retrieve an illegal modification program contained within its drive unit, when they were attacked a dekamite queen who made a home inside the ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Treadwater
- **Manufacturer:** Ghtroc Industries
- **Model:** 720 light freighter
