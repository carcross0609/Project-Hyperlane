# Deathless

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Deathless](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deathless)

The Deathless was a Nikardun Battle Dreadnought that served as the flagship of General Yiv, and the mobile headquarters of the Nikardun Destiny. Yiv oversaw the expansion of the Destiny from the ship's throne room.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Deathless
- **Type:** Nikardun Battle Dreadnought
- **Shield Gen:** Electrostatic barriers
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Nikardun Destiny
- **Navy:** Nikardun military
- **Owners:** Yiv
