# Serendipity

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Serendipity](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Serendipity)

The Serendipity, formerly known as the Miss Fortune, was a 1550-LEX yacht owned by Delia Leighton. The yacht featured a cantina inside its cargo hold and operated on planets such as Cyrkon and Jakku. The Serendipity cantina hosted numerous patrons, so Leighton hired a Shistavanen bouncer named Curtis to verify its guests.
