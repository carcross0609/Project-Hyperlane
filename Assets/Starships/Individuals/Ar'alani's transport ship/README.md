# Ar'alani's transport ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ar'alani's transport ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ar%27alani%27s_transport_ship)

A transport ship was commanded by the Chiss Admiral Ar'alani of the Chiss Defense Fleet. After the Vagaari species were defeated and the Geroon species were freed by the Chiss species and their government, the Chiss Ascendancy in 27 BBY, the transport arrived to take the Geroons to their homeworld of Geroon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ar'alani's transport ship
- **Type:** Transport
- **Crew:** Chiss warriors
- **Cargohandling:** Cargo bay
- **Captains:** Admiral Ar'alani
