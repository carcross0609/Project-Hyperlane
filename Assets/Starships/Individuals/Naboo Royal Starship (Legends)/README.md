# Naboo Royal Starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Naboo Royal Starship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naboo_Royal_Starship/Legends)

The Naboo Royal Starship was a custom-built vessel used by the Royal House of Naboo for official business. Queen Padmé Amidala and her entourage escaped the invasion of Naboo aboard the vessel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Naboo Royal Starship
- **Manufacturer:** Theed Palace Space Vessel Engineering Corps
- **Type:** Transport
- **Cost:** Not available for sale, valued at 1,365,000 credits
- **Length:** 76 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Nubia Star Drives, Incorporated 327 core
- **Crew:** 4
- **Passengers:** 10
- **Capacity:** 4 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 3 months
- **Owners:** Royal House of Naboo
