# Night Havok

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Night Havok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Havok)

Night Havok was a modified YT-1300 light freighter owned and operated by Kara Saffch and her brother Willi Saffch. The ship was often used by them in their scavenging operations and in making cargo hauls in the area of Korbin. The Night Havok was prone to frequent mechanical trouble.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Night Havok
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Type:** YT-1300 light freighter
- **Length:** 30 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Armament:** 1 twin laser cannon, 1 laser cannon
- **Crew:** 2, gunners 2
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Passengers:** 5
- **Capacity:** 200 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 month
- **Owners:** Kara Saffch, Willi Saffch
- **Captains:** Kara Saffch
