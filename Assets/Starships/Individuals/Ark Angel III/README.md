# Ark Angel III

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ark Angel III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ark_Angel_III)

The Ark Angel III was a starship used by the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Aphra in the time following the Battle of Hoth. Aphra utilized the vessel after the destruction of her previous starship, the Ark Angel II. The Ark Angel III was later destroyed by the fleet of the House of Tagge scion Ronen Tagge on the planet Dianth. Aphra and her associates subsequently escaped Dianth on a vessel from the High Republic Era that she later named Ark Angel IV.
