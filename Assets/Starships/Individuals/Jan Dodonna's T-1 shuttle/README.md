# Jan Dodonna's T-1 shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jan Dodonna's T-1 shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jan_Dodonna%27s_T-1_shuttle)

General Jan Dodonna of the Alliance to Restore the Republic maintained a personal T-1 shuttle during the early Galactic Civil War. In 0 ABY, Leia Organa and Evaan Verlaine commandeered the T-1 shuttle without permission and broke the quarantine Dodonna had imposed on Base One to go on an unsanctioned mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan.
