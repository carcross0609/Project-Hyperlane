# Scorpion IV

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scorpion IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scorpion_IV)

The Scorpion IV was a CR90 corvette that was part of the fleet of Lumiya shortly after the Battle of Endor. It carried a missile launcher turret in its tail section.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scorpion IV
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Lumiya's fleet
- **Owners:** Lumiya
