# Thon's Orchard

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thon's Orchard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thon%27s_Orchard)

Thon's Orchard was a GR-75 medium transport that was part of the Alliance to Restore the Republic's fleet. As one of the GR-75 transports stationed at Echo Base, the Alliance headquarters on the gelid planet of Hoth, Thon's Orchard helped evacuate the compromised outpost during the Battle of Hoth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thon's Orchard
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Max Speed:** 650 kph
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 4
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Four twin laser cannons
- **Crew:** 6
- **Capacity:** 19,000 metric tons
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
