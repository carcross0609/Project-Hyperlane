# Luke Skywalker's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Luke Skywalker's starship (Tatooine)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luke_Skywalker%27s_starship_%28Tatooine%29)

This starship was piloted by Luke Skywalker on a mission for the Rebel Alliance to the planet Vorzyd V in 1 ABY. Accompanying Luke were Leia Organa, C-3PO, and R2-D2. Skywalker landed the vessel on pad 583. While Luke was engaged in a secret meeting with a Rebel contact, a gang known as the Freelies tricked C-3PO into allowing them access to the ship, kidnapping Leia and R2-D2. Skywalker and C-3PO were able to rescue them from an Imperial assault ordered by Imperial Intelligence director Blackhole and escape aboard this vessel.
