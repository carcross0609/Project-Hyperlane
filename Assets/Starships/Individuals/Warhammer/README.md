# Warhammer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Warhammer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Warhammer)

The Warhammer was a dreadnaught of the Sith Empire that was active during the Great Galactic War between the Empire and the Galactic Republic. During the war, the Warhammer was captured by Republic forces, and only two individuals aboard the Warhammer escaped capture, including Eckard Lokin, an agent of the Imperial Intelligence. The Warhammer capture was the Republic's first confirmed sighting of Lokin, and analysis of the Warhammer crew roster by Republic forces revealed that Lokin was not listed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Warhammer
- **Type:** Dreadnaught
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
