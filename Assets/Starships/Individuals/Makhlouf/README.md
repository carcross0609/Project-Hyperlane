# Makhlouf

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Makhlouf](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Makhlouf)

Makhlouf was a YT-1300 light freighter belonging to the Rebel Alliance that saw service in the Airam sector. When the decision was made to construct a Rebel shipyard in Airam space. Makhlouf was among the ships that were part of a convoy that was sent there, although the cargo contents were not specified.
