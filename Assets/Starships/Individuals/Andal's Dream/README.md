# Andal's Dream

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Andal's Dream](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andal%27s_Dream)

Andal's Dream was the personal yacht of Moff Villis Andal, governor of the Elrood sector. He used it to travel across the galaxy, engaging in big game hunting with his wealthy friends.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Andal's Dream
