# Moth (Container transport)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moth (Container transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moth_%28Container_transport%29)

Moth was a Container transport belonging to the Rebel Alliance. It was present at a Rebel supply depot that was attacked and destroyed by Imperial forces from the Nebulon-B frigate Lightning. The transport attempted to flee the battle but was destroyed instead. The attack on the Rebel depot was then recorded and recreated to train future pilots in the Combat chamber. One of those pilots was Maarek Stele.
