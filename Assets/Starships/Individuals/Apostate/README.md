# Apostate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Apostate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Apostate)

The Apostate was a starship owned by the bounty hunter Nik Hepho. While Hepho was searching for adherents of the Church of the Force in Jedha City on the moon Jedha in the year 1 BBY, the ship hovered in orbit above, operated by a droid intelligence that waited for the hunter to make contact via his backpack-mounted long-range transmitter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Apostate
- **Othersystems:** Droid intelligence
- **Owners:** Nik Hepho
