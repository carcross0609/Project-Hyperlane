# Vulture

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vulture (Marauder-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vulture_%28Marauder-class%29)

Vulture was a Republic Sienar Systems Marauder-class corvette in use by an Outer Rim pirate group. Accompanied by an escort of Y-wings and Toscan fighters, named Pirate and Fury respectively, Vulture led an attack on noted entrepreneur Dunari aboard his pleasure yacht, the Raw Deal. The attack was defeated, however, due to the fact that Dunari was defended by a covert Rebel Alliance squadron that easily repelled the raiders.

## Specifications
