# Nightbrother

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nightbrother (Gauntlet fighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightbrother_%28Gauntlet_fighter%29)

The Nightbrother was a modified Kom'rk-class fighter/transport, named and used by the former Sith Lord Maul during the Clone Wars and the Imperial Era. Following Maul's death at the hands of Obi-Wan Kenobi in 2 BBY, the Nightbrother was acquired by the Spectres rebel cell, where it was simply called the Gauntlet.
