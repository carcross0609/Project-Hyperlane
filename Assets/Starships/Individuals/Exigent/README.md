# Exigent

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Exigent](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exigent)

The Exigent was a Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier in the service of the Galactic Empire. Following the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY, it was destroyed in the Nuvar system during a routine scouting mission when it was struck by missiles from a nearby defense station that had recently been captured from the New Republic without having its targeting systems cleared. Titan Squadron TIE pilots Shen and Havina Vonreg, who were among the survivors, boarded the station and fixed its targeting, with the Star Destroyer Overseer searching the Nuvar system for survivors.
