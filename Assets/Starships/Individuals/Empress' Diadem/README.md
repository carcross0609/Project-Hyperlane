# Empress' Diadem

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Empress' Diadem](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empress%27_Diadem)

Empress' Diadem was one of the starships in Talon Karrde's smuggling operation. It was a modified YT-1210 light freighter and during the Bacta War it was captained by Melina Carniss. A disk-shaped ship, it was armed with a blaster cannon and concussion missile launch tubes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Empress' Diadem
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YT-series
- **Model:** Modified YT-1210 light freighter
- **Type:** Light freighter
- **Length:** 28 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Blaster cannon
  - Concussion missile launch tubes
- **Affiliation:** Talon Karrde's organization
