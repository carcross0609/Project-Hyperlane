# Runeer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Runeer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Runeer)

Runeer was the designation of a group of two Assassin-class corvettes under the service of the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. They were used by the Alliance as part of their first attack on Space Installation NL-1. But were destroyed by the stations defenses, which included Imperial Ace Maarek Stele.
