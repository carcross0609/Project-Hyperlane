# Unnamed

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unnamed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unnamed)

The Unnamed was a starship used by the Rebel Alliance's intelligence branch during the Galactic Civil War. In 3 ABY, Captain Magna Tolvan commanded the Unnamed, and her unit Strike Team Misericorde served aboard the vessel. After receiving intelligence on the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Aphra, the Unnamed captured an Imperial Lambda-class T-4a shuttle, bringing it into its hangar where Tolvan's strike team stunned all of the shuttle's passengers except for Aphra, who escaped the ion grenade they used.
