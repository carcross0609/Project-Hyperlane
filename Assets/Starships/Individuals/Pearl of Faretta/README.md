# Pearl of Faretta

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pearl of Faretta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pearl_of_Faretta)

The Pearl of Faretta was the personal cruiser of the Nihil Minister of Information Ghirra Starros by 228 BBY. Starros kept moonberry juice aboard the vessel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pearl of Faretta
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Engine:** Equipped
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Ministry of Information
- **Owners:** Ghirra Starros
