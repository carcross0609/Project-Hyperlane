# Vensenor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vensenor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vensenor)

The Vensenor was the decommissioned Venator-class Star Destroyer aboard which the Galactic Empire's Vensenor Flight Academy was based. During the Clone Wars, starfighter ace Bythen Forral, then a captain in the Galactic Republic Navy, was deployed aboard the Vensenor. In orbit of the planet Arkanis, the Star Destroyer later underpinned the Empire's eponymous flight academy, which trained naval officers and pilots and was overseen by Commandant Forral. Vensenor Flight Academy produced graduates who received far more combat decorations than average, as well as more defectors than any other school in the overall Imperial Academy system, facilitated by the undercover resistance operative Gola Chall.
