# Impenetrable

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Impenetrable](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Impenetrable)

The Impenetrable was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that was a part of the navy of the Galactic Empire. Manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards and measuring 1,600 meters in length, it served as the flagship of Grand Admiral Danetta Pitta, who was killed while commanding a torpedo sphere in the Battle of Tralus of 5 ABY.
