# Grim Reaper

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grim Reaper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grim_Reaper)

The Grim Reaper was a Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry that took part in the Second Battle of Geonosis. After the shield generator of the primary droid foundry was destroyed, the gunship was used to take wounded clone troopers away. The nose of the Grim Reaper was customized with a painting of a clone trooper holding a large scythe and the vessel's name written in Aurebesh.
