# Take That!

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Take That!](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Take_That%21)

Take That! was a YT-1210 Corellian light freighter that was owned and captained by Falan Iniro. It was destroyed in the Battle of Nar Shaddaa by the Carrack-class light cruiser Renthal's Vigilance.
