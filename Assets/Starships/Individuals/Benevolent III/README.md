# Benevolent III

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Benevolent III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Benevolent_III)

The Benevolent III was the personal transport of Baroness Omnino as of 37 BBY. It was of the same type as the shuttle used by Senator Palpatine during the years prior to the Invasion of Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Benevolent III
- **Type:** Transport
- **Owners:** Omnino
