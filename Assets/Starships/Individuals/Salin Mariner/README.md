# Salin Mariner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Salin Mariner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salin_Mariner)

The Salin Mariner was a "C-One" luxury liner that was operating by Salin Excursions around the time of the Clone Wars. Around 22 BBY, the Mariner undertook a cruise from Lianna to Botajef. During the voyage, the vessel was sabotaged by the Weequay pirate Hondo Ohnaka, who then kidnapped several of the liner's passengers.
