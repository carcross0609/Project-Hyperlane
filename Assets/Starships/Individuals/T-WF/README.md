# T-WF

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-WF](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-WF)

T-WF was a Utility Tug used by the Rebels the tug was present among 4 Class-A cargo containers Koss that was protected by a small minefield. A single TIE Interceptor was sent to clear the minefield, when the mines were cleared the fighter inspected the containers to find one of them carrying equipment and destroyed the tug before it could enter a space container.
