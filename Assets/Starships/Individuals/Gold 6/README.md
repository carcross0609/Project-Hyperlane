# Gold 6

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gold 6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_6)

Gold 6 was the designation of a BTL-A4 Y-wing in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Piloted by Hol Okand, the fighter led an attack against an Imperial outpost at Kashyyyk. Okand led the attack with Jon Vander as his wingman, disabling the outposts shields with his fighter's ion cannons.
