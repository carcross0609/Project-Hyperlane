# Lourna Dee

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lourna Dee (stormship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lourna_Dee_%28stormship%29)

The Lourna Dee was a Stormship used by Lourna Dee after she joined the Nihil pirate organization. It was previously known as the Toothspike, and belonged to the Colicoid Storm Jinnix until Dee killed him.

- **Name:** Lourna Dee
- **Type:** Stormship
