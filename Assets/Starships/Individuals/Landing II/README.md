# Landing II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Landing II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Landing_II)

The Landing II was a starship utilized by the bounty hunter crew led by the Ubese Boushh. The crew, which consisted of fellow Ubese Brene, Hareck, Ilini, and Tans, who had all been exiled from their homeworld, Uba IV. During the Imperial Era, the crew used the Landing II to travel to the Vermillion, the fortress-flagship of the Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate. After accepting a job from Crimson Dawn to assassinate the Executive Board of the Tagge Corporation, Boushh and his crew returned to the Landing II in order to depart.

## Specifications

- **Affiliation:** Boushh's crew
