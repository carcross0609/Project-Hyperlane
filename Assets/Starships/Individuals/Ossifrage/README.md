# Ossifrage

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ossifrage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossifrage)

The Ossifrage was an RZ-1 A-wing interceptor that was owned by Greer Sonnel. At some point, the ship had been used to fight the Galactic Empire, and had been slightly damaged. Greer purchased the A-wing, modifying it, and used it during a race known as The Gauntlet. Despite minor damages to the Ossifrage, Greer won the race with the help of Han Solo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ossifrage
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Systems Engineering
- **Model:** RZ-1 A-wing interceptor
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 9.6 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,300 kph
- **Engine:** 2
- **Owners:** Greer Sonnel
