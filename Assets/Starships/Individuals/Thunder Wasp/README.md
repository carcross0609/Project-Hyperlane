# Thunder Wasp

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thunder Wasp](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thunder_Wasp)

The Thunder Wasp was an Imperial Arquitens-class light cruiser under the command of Commander Alfren Cheno. Captain Thrawn served as first officer aboard the vessel with his assistant, Ensign Eli Vanto. The vessel was responsible for turning the tide in the battle over Umbara, although it was heavily damaged.
