# Darth Vader's Venator-class Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darth Vader's Venator-class Star Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vader%27s_Venator-class_Star_Destroyer)

Darth Vader's Venator-class Star Destroyer was a Venator-class Star Destroyer owned by Darth Vader during the reign of the Galactic Empire. He murdered several of the ship's crew, including Admiral Mummert, after being interrupted while meditating in his chamber. By 14 BBY, it had been replaced as Vader's flagship by the Perilous.
