# Archai

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Archai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Archai)

Archai was a YT-1300 light freighter manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation and owned by the Human scout Coli Carinth. While Carinth operated the Archai, she became one of a few spacers in the galaxies Outer Rim with the coordinates required to travel to the planet Iego through hyperspace.
