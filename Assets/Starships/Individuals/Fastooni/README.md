# Fastooni

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fastooni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fastooni)

Fastooni was a C-3 passenger liner which contained passengers. The liner, along with a group of other civilian vessels, some of which contained Bothans who made a reconnaissance mission on the Second Death Star, was held up by the Accuser, which also bought the Immobilizer 418 cruiser Grappler to prevent it from leaving. When the Interdictor was driven out by Rebels in X-wings and A-wings all of the ships, as well as the corvette made a run for their hyperspace junctions but were fired upon by the Accuser. Whether or not Fastooni escaped is unknown.
