# Broken 'Spanner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Broken 'Spanner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Broken_%27Spanner)

The Broken 'Spanner was a modified TL-1800 freighter that was owned for over three decades by the smuggler Tiv "Poke" Pocarno and then by Torel Vorne.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Broken 'Spanner
- **Manufacturer:** Suwantek Systems
- **Type:** TL-1800 transport
- **Cost:** 12,600 credits
- **Length:** 30 meters
- **Navigation:** Nav computer
- **Armament:**

  - 1 proton torpedo launcher
- **Crew:** 1&ndash;2
- **Passengers:** 7
- **Capacity:** 55 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 3 months
- **Affiliation:** Smuggler
- **Owners:**
  - Tiv Pocarno
  - Torel Vorne
- **Captains:** Tiv Pocarno
