# Orisson

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Orisson](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orisson)

The Orisson was a light cruiser in service of the Clarr family homestead defense force, the family fleet of the Clarr family of the Chiss Ascendancy. It was stationed at the Clarr fleet depot.

- **Name:** Orisson
- **Type:** Light cruiser
- **Affiliation:**

- **Fleet:**
