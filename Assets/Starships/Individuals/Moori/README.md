# Moori

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moori](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moori)

Moori was the designation of a group of two Assassin-class corvettes under the service of the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. They were part of the Rebels' second attempt to destroy Space installation NL-1 during the Battle on the Frontier, but they were destroyed by Imperial Ace Maarek Stele.

- **Model:** Assassin-class corvette
