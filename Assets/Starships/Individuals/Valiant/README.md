# Valiant

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Valiant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valiant)

The Valiant was an Alif-class Longbeam in service to the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era. In 382 BBY, it was dispatched to the planet Eiram to carry a delivery of requested medical supplies, escorted by Jedi Masters Creighton Sun and Char-Ryl-Roy, Jedi Knight Gella Nattai, and Roy's Padawan Enya Keen.
