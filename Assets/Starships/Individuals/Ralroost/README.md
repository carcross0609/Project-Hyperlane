# Ralroost

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ralroost](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ralroost)

The Ralroost was a Bothan Assault Cruiser commissioned at the end of the Galactic Civil War for the defense of Bothawui.

- **Model:** Bothan Assault Cruiser
- **Type:** Capital

- **Hyperdrive:**

- **Crew:** 1,240
- **Passengers:** 250 troops and pilots
- **Capacity:** 5,000 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 2 years
- **Affiliation:**
  - New Republic
  - Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
