# Unidentified shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified shuttle (Cymoon 1)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_shuttle_%28Cymoon_1%29)

A shuttle was owned by the Hutt Clan during the Galactic Civil War. The Alliance to Restore the Republic later used the shuttle to infiltrate the Imperial weapons factory on Cymoon 1.
