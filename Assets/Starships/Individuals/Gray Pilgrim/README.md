# Gray Pilgrim

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gray Pilgrim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gray_Pilgrim)

The Gray Pilgrim was the mobile Jedi academy upon which the Padawan Maris Brood was taught the ways of the Jedi by her master. Brood was aboard this ship with her master in the Outer Rim during the Clone Wars at the time of the issuance of Order 66.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gray Pilgrim
- **Complement:** At least one Jedi starfighter
- **Bays:** At least one
- **Communications:** Communications transceiver
- **Role:** Jedi starship
