# Halberd

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Halberd (Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halberd_%28Gamma-class_ATR-6_assault_transport%29)

The Halberd was a Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport used by the Ripoblus to attack the Dimok Science Station Youst. It was destroyed at the Battle of Science Station Youst.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Halberd
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Length:** 45 meters
- **Width:** 30.4 meters
- **Height:** 20.5 meters
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
