# Best Cargo

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Best Cargo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Best_Cargo)

The Best Cargo was a modified light freighter assigned to the some Rebels for a mission to the Graveyard. It was 35 meters long. It could be operated by a single person in a pinch but typically had five crew including one gunner. It carried six passengers, 150 metric tons of cargo, and two months' consumables. It had a class 1 hyperdrive and a class 15 backup. It could fly 750 kmh in atmosphere and had a nav computer. It was equipped with shields and two laser cannon turrets.
