# Switch (Modular conveyors)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Switch (Modular conveyors)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Switch_%28Modular_conveyors%29)

Switch was a pair of civilian Modular conveyors. The transports were carrying supplies to an unknown location and were passing through Navigation buoys B-52 and B-53. The ships were inspected by a TIE Defender and were allowed to pass on without incident, while Rebel starfighters attempted to interfere with the TIE Defender's task of inspecting and disabling Cargo ferry Juble 2.
