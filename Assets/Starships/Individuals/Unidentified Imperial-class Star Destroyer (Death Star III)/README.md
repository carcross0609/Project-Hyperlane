# Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer (Death Star III)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial-class_Star_Destroyer_%28Death_Star_III%29)

This Imperial-class Star Destroyer was one of three that participated in the Battle of Death Star III. The ship caught a wayward Star Tours StarSpeeder 3000 in its tractor beam during the battle, but Alliance of Free Planets starfighters helped the spaceliner escape.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Model:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Othersystems:** Tractor beam projector
- **Affiliation:** Kaarenth Dissension
