# Icy Moon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Icy Moon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Icy_Moon)

Icy Moon was a Sienar Fleet Systems Light Patrol Ship owned and operated by the bounty hunter Rafe Finna. During the Galactic Civil War, it was present at Gadde on Lish V to transport Rebel prisoners.
