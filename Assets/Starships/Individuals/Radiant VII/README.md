# Radiant VII

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Radiant VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Radiant_VII)

The Radiant VII was a Consular-class space cruiser of the Galactic Republic. It transported Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn and Padawan Obi-Wan Kenobi to the planet Naboo, where they were to negotiate with Viceroy Nute Gunray of the Trade Federation. The cruiser delivered the Jedi ambassadors on time, but was destroyed along with its crew by order of Darth Sidious, the Federation's secret master.
