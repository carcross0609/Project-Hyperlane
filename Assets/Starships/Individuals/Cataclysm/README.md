# Cataclysm

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cataclysm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cataclysm)

The Cataclysm was an Onager-class Star Destroyer in service to the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. At one point, the Star Destroyer was stationed near two gravity wells, and was approached by a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle flanked by a pair of TIE fighters. The Cataclysm was equipped with engines, a pair of superweapons at its bow, and two deflector shield generator domes. As an Onager, the Cataclysm was 1,288 meters in length.
