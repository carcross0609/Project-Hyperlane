# Lore Seeker

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lore Seeker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lore_Seeker)

The Lore Seeker was a reddish-orange Jemlaat-class yacht owned by the Jedi historian Tionne Solusar. She acquired the ship sometime before 11 ABY from a Randoni trader on one of her frequent research trips to Borgo Prime, literally for the price of a song. Tionne often took young Jedi with her on these trips, using it as an opportunity for practical experience.
