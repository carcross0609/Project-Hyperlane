# Heavenly Sphere

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heavenly Sphere](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavenly_Sphere)

The Heavenly Sphere, briefly known as the Righteous during Queen Fanry's ownership, was the flagship of the royal fleet of Pijal. After Fanry's revolution during her coronation, she commanded the starship in an attempted assault on the Czerka Corporation vessel Leverage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavenly Sphere
- **Role:** Royal flagship
- **Affiliation:** Pijali monarchy
- **Fleet:** Royal fleet
- **Captains:** Queen Fanry
