# Tuima

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tuima](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tuima)

The Tuima was a GR-75 medium transport that saw service with the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. During and immediately prior to the Battle of Endor, it was a part of the reformed Defiance Task Force, centered around the Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Defiance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tuima
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Medium transport
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Alliance Fleet
- **Fleet:** Defiance Task Force
