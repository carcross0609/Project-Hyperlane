# Blunt Force

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blunt Force](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blunt_Force)

The Blunt Force was a YV-929 armed freighter that was used by the Peace Brigade during the Yuuzhan Vong War. At some point during the conflict, the Blunt Force and the Peace Brigade Corellian gunship Vigor attacked the refugee ship Wanderhome.
