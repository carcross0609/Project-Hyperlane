# Tranquility

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tranquility](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tranquility)

The Tranquility was a Venator-class Star Destroyer of the Galactic Republic naval forces' Open Circle Fleet, where it served as the flagship of Jedi General Luminara Unduli and her fleet group during the Clone Wars. In 21 BBY, following the capture of Separatist leader and Trade Federation Viceroy Nute Gunray, the Tranquility and its commander were due to transport the Viceroy to the planet Coruscant, where he was to stand trial for war crimes. However, while en route to Coruscant, the Star Destroyer was infiltrated by Sith assassin Asajj Ventress, who managed to retrieve Gunray and escape from the vessel, but not before causing extensive damage to the Tranquility.
