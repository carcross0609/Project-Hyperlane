# Rudi's ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rudi's ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rudi%27s_ship)

Rudi's ship was a shuttle piloted by Wookiee Rudi who deliver food for the village's Life Day feast on Kashyyyk before being shot down by the Ganguls in their cruiser Rathtar's Revenge.
