# Kreytaka

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kreytaka (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kreytaka_%28starship%29)

The Kreytaka was an enormous starship created by the Faruun species. It was meant for use by the Nagai during their invasion of the galaxy. The vessel was part of an expeditionary force that arrived at Kinooine. It was named Kreytaka for a city on Nagi, although members of the Alliance of Free Planets referred to the vessel a "longneck transport."
