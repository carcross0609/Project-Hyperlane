# Padmé Amidala's H-type Nubian yacht

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Padmé Amidala's H-type Nubian yacht (Malevolence)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padm%C3%A9_Amidala%27s_H-type_Nubian_yacht_%28Malevolence%29)

A H-type Nubian yacht was piloted by Senator Padmé Amidala in 22 BBY. She was given the vessel after a failed assassination attempt destroyed her previous ship. Amidala used it to travel to Tatooine and Geonosis with Anakin Skywalker. During the Clone Wars, Amidala used the ship to travel to alleged negotiations with the InterGalactic Banking Clan. It was however intercepted by General Grievous's flagship Malevolence and pulled aboard via a tractor beam, in the hopes of capturing Amidala and using her as a hostage to force the Republic fleet attacking the Malevolence to stand down. However, Amidala set the yacht's power system to overload to act as a distraction and take out any droids that came to capture her; the yacht exploded shortly after Grievous himself boarded it, destroying the two battle droids with him, but the cyborg survived.
