# Blood Freeze

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blood Freeze](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Freeze)

Blood Freeze was a Ghtroc Industries freighter owned and operated by the smuggler Bom Vimdin. At some point during the Galactic Civil War, Vimdin docked the Blood Freeze in Docking Bay 65 in Mos Eisley on Tatooine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blood Freeze
- **Manufacturer:** Ghtroc Industries
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Max Speed:** 750 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Armament:** Double laser cannon
- **Role:** Smuggling vessel
- **Owners:** Bom Vimdin
