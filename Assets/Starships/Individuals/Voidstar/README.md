# Voidstar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Voidstar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voidstar)

The Voidstar was a prototype battleship belonging to the Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War. It went missing during the conflict and was rediscovered during the following Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Voidstar
- **Model:** Harrower-class dreadnought
- **Type:** Dreadnaught
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolasers
  - Tractor beams
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
