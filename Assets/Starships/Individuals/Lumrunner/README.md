# Lumrunner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lumrunner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lumrunner)

The Lumrunner was the personal PB-950 patrol boat of the space pirate Shamus Falconi. He inherited the ship from his grandmother. The Lumrunner was over 300 years old when his grandmother captured it at the beginning of her pirate career.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lumrunner
- **Owners:**
  - Shamus Falconi's grandmother
  - Shamus Falconi
