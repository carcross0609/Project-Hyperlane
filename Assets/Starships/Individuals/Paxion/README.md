# Paxion

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Paxion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paxion)

The Paxion was an Alif-class Longbeam starship that served the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era. It was under the direct supervision of Supreme Chancellor Orlen Mollo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Paxion
- **Model:** Alif-class Longbeam
- **Type:** Longbeam cruiser
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Destroyed:** 382 BBY, Eiram system
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Owners:** Orlen Mollo
