# Random Mallet

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Random Mallet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Random_Mallet)

The Random Mallet was a hammer-shaped frigate owned by the Corellian pirate captain Hethra Mcgrrrr and his men, the Black Hole Gang. During the Clone Wars, the Random Mallet was trapped within the Unknown Regions by the gravitational pull of a black hole.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Random Mallet
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Length:** 200 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolasers
  - Quad Laser Cannons
- **Affiliation:** Black Hole Gang
- **Captains:** Hethra Mcgrrrr
