# Castellan

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Castellan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Castellan)

The Castellan was a cruiser operational during the High Republic Era. After falling under attack by a fleet of Nihil starships, the Castellan made an emergency jump to hyperspace, crashing on the moon Toshara. In the many years following the crash, the wreckage was scavenged for parts by scavengers. During the Imperial Era, the scoundrel Kay Vess entered the wreckage of the cruiser in search of a nav computer for the EML-850 light freighter Trailblazer.
