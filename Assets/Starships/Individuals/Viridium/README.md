# Viridium

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Viridium](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Viridium)

Viridium was an Imperial Lambda-class T-4a shuttle of the same group as Tydirium. It was stationed at Outpost 327, when the latter was captured by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during their preparations for the upcoming attack on the Death Star II at Endor.
