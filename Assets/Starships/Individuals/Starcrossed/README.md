# Starcrossed

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starcrossed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starcrossed)

The Starcrossed was a SoroSuub Luxury Cruiser 200 operated by Captain Heedon during the Galactic Civil War. It was refurbished by Heedon to resemble a bird, but much of the interior was left undone due to the fact that, due to Imperial oppression, not many could afford passage onboard the ship.
