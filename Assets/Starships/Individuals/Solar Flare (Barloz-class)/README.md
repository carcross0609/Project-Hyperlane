# Solar Flare

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Solar Flare (Barloz-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solar_Flare_%28Barloz-class%29)

The Solar Flare was a modified Barloz-class medium freighter used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. It saw heavy usage throughout the war, and was at one stage used by a group of Rebel spacers who attempted to set up a Rebel cell in the Tapani sector. Its modifications included changes to the weaponry, as well as reduced cargo capacity but increased passenger space.
