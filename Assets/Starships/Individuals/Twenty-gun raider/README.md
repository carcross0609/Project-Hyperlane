# Twenty-gun raider

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Twenty-gun raider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twenty-gun_raider)

During the Battle of Jakku, the Galactic Empire deployed several twenty-gun raiders. During the fighting, Captain Ciena Ree toyed with the idea of convincing Grand Moff Randd to send one of the twenty-gun raiders to support the TIE fighters engaging New Republic starfighters near the planet Jakku's surface. Her idea was rendered moot when her Imperial Star Destroyer Inflictor was boarded by a New Republic boarding force that included her lover Thane Kyrell.
