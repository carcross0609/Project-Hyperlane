# Breathe My Jets

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Breathe My Jets](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Breathe_My_Jets)

Breathe My Jets was the vessel of Uran Lavint. It had enough cargo space to fit several YT-1300 light freighters in its cargo hold. The ship was captured by the Anakin Solo after Uran sold out her fellow smugglers to the Galactic Alliance. She intended to leave with her ship, but Jacen Solo stuck her with another.
