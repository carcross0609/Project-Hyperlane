# Misanthrope

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Misanthrope](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Misanthrope)

The Misanthrope was a cargo ship that crash-landed on D'vouran, a planet that devoured anything it came in contact with. The Misanthrope was captained by Bebo, who was, with Lonni and eighteen other people, a survivor of the encounter.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Misanthrope
- **Type:** Cargo barge
- **Destroyed:** 0 ABY, D'vouran
- **Captains:** Kevreb Bebo
