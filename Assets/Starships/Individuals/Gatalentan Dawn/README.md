# Gatalentan Dawn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gatalentan Dawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gatalentan_Dawn)

The Gatalentan Dawn was a BT-7 Thunderclap used by General Ardinondu Dipolus Daeruun and Jedi Knight Arn Peralun during the Battle of Khar Shian around 3622 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gatalentan Dawn
- **Model:** BT-7 Thunderclap
- **Length:** 96 meters
- **Width:** 61 meters
- **Height:** 80 meters
- **Shield Gen:** Deflector shield
- **Armament:** Missiles
