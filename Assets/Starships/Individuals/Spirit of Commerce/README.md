# Spirit of Commerce

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spirit of Commerce](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_of_Commerce)

The Spirit of Commerce was a freighter Ben Skywalker acquired passage on as a transport from Coruscant to Endor in 40 ABY, where he would have a conversation with the ship's engineer who believed the Ewoks to be savages and that he would shoot them all.
