# Xesre

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xesre](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xesre)

Xesre was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle serving the Mugaari pirates. In 3 ABY, it was present at a cargo loading area in the Tungra sector during an arms deal between the Muggaari and the Rebel Alliance. It was destroyed in the ensuing Battle of the Tungra sector against Imperial forces along with all the other Mugaari ships.

## Specifications
