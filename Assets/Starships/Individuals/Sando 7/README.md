# Sando 7

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sando 7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sando_7)

Sando 7 was an YZ 3000 tanker used to haul water from Da Soocha to its moon of Napdu. In 137 ABY it was used by Galactic Alliance Remnant in an operation to smuggle Mon Calamari refugees off the planet.
