# Ventress' ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ventress' ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ventress%27_ship)

Ventress' ship was the personal starfighter of bounty hunter Asajj Ventress. The former Sith apprentice flew the ship during the Imperial Era, using it to travel to the planet Pabu during or after 18 BBY to investigate the former Republic special forces squad Clone Force 99.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ventress' ship
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Length:** 9.1 meters
- **Width:** 20.3 meters
- **Engine:** Equipped (6)
- **Armament:** Proton torpedo launchers
- **Owners:** Asajj Ventress
