# Pran (starship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pran (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pran_%28starship%29)

The Pran was one of the starships used by the New Republic to search for the Teljkon Vagabond in 16 ABY. After the search for the Vagabond was called off, the Pran was sent elsewhere.
