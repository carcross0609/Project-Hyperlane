# Quandary

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quandary](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quandary)

The Quandary was a tramp freighter which operated in the Outer Rim Territories during the Galactic Civil War. The run-down ship was piloted by an old crew who weren't in good condition also. Tinian I'att Azur-Jamin traveled aboard this ship when she was a fugitive from Ksiczzic III to Silver Station.
