# Raptor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Raptor (Preybird)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raptor_%28Preybird%29)

The Raptor was one of the two SoroSuub Preybird-class starfighters owned by the smuggler Mazzic. The Raptor was painted with distinctive red bird of prey designs, while its companion Skyclaw had a similar blue scheme. Mazzic often used the unusual but large and intimidating starfighters to frighten his enemies, and to provide protection for his smuggling vessels in dangerous operations.
