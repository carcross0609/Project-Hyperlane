# Eyeshine

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eyeshine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eyeshine)

Eyeshine was a modified Corellian Engineering Corporation Barloz-class freighter that was owned by the Shistavanen smuggler Mal Biron. The vessel had been upgraded with the addition of an X10-D droid brain, which helped Biron to operate and maintain the ship effectively. The ship was named for a female Defel that Biron had once romanced in his younger years.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Eyeshine
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** Barloz-class
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Capacity:** 80 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 6 months
- **Role:** Smuggling
- **Owners:** Mal Biron
