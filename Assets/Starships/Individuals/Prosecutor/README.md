# Prosecutor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prosecutor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prosecutor)

The Prosecutor was a slightly modified Acclamator-class assault ship in the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. The ship became infamous in 21 BBY when it was boarded by a hostile crew of Trandoshan slavers and mercenaries. After wiping out the majority of the crew and taking control of the ship, the Trandoshans planned to hand the damaged but functional Prosecutor over to the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The Clone Commandos of Delta Squad were later sent to investigate what had happened and ultimately kept the Prosecutor from being handed over to the enemy.
