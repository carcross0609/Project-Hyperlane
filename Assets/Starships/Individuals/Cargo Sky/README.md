# Cargo Sky

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cargo Sky](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cargo_Sky)

The Cargo Sky was a Wayfarer-class medium transport that was part of Senator Bail Organa's resistance movement During the blockade of the planet Ibaar, Organa ordered the Cargo Sky and the Cargo Comet to be loaded with supplies and military equipment and to deliver it to the rebel network operating there.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cargo Sky
- **Model:** Wayfarer-class medium freighter
- **Type:** Transport
- **Affiliation:** House of Organa
