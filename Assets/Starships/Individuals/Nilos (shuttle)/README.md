# Nilos

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nilos (shuttle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nilos_%28shuttle%29)

The Nilos was a Sienar Fleet Systems Imperial Lambda-class shuttle, shot down near Iziz on the planet Onderon during the Galactic Civil War. Moff Lasre Dardano took the shuttle for its personal use at Whisper Base, before the attack by Alliance Special Operations members.
