# Unidentified Alliance corvette

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Alliance corvette](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Alliance_corvette)

A corvette served the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. While being chased by a Star Destroyer, both ships were captured by an extra-galactic species. The crews of both vessels were pitted against each other in a series of contests that the Alliance won.

- **Model:** Corvette
