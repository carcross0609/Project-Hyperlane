# Invincible

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Invincible (Stark Hyperspace War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invincible_%28Stark_Hyperspace_War%29)

The Invincible was a Republic light assault cruiser, the command ship of Ranulph Tarkin and the flagship of his fleet. Besides Tarkin, its crew included Antidar Williams and Jace Dallin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Invincible
- **Model:** Republic light assault cruiser

- **Destroyed:** 44 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Ranulph Tarkin's paramilitary
- **Captains:** Ranulph Tarkin
