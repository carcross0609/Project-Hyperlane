# Towani family star cruiser

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Towani family star cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Towani_family_star_cruiser)

The Towani family star cruiser was a starship used by the Towani family. It crash landed on the forest moon of Endor and was later repaired by Jeremitt Towani in 3 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Towani family star cruiser
- **Type:** Star cruiser
- **Power:** Crystal oscillator power unit
- **Crew:** 4
- **Affiliation:** Towani family
