# Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Rebel Alliance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Providence-class_carrier/destroyer_%28Rebel_Alliance%29)

A heavily modified Providence-class Dreadnought saw service in the Alliance to Restore the Republic, where it participated in a battle against Star Destroyers of the Imperial Navy alongside newer Mon Cala warships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer
- **Manufacturer:** Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps
- **Model:** Modified Providence-class Dreadnought
- **Type:** Dreadnought
- **Sysmods:**
  - Shell-like hull with additional engines
  - Fin-like protrusion with auxiliary engines added to stern
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
