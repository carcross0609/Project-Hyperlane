# Ambria's Fury

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ambria's Fury](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ambria%27s_Fury)

The Ambria's Fury was an Imperial warship belonging to the Sith Empire that participated in the Battle of Hoth in 3665 BBY. The ship's cargo was unknown to most of the Imperial Military, but high-ranking Moffs were distressed when the Fury was shot down over the ice planet of Hoth during the last days of the massive battle. It crashed in the northern end of the Glacial Fissure region, breaking in half but remaining largely intact, and it was later discovered that among the Fury cargo were several power cores that could be used in HK-51 assassin droids.
