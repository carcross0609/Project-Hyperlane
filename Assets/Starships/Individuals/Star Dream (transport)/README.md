# Star Dream

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Star Dream (transport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Dream_%28transport%29)

The Star Dream was a transport ship that was owned and operated by Hoban, around the time of the Galactic Civil War. The Jizz band, Evar Orbus and His Galactic Jizz-Wailers used this vessel to travel to Tatooine.

- **Type:** Transport
