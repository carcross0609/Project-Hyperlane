# Worldhopper

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Worldhopper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Worldhopper)

The Worldhopper was the freighter owned by Rennik and crewed by his twin sons. In 9 ABY, it brought the Rogue Squadron astromech droids Whistler and Gate to Oradin, a city on the planet Brentaal IV.
