# Valcyn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Valcyn/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valcyn/Legends)

The Valcyn was a T-class long-range personal cruiser gifted to Qordis by Lord Kaan for his work with the apprentices at the Sith Academy on Korriban. The Valcyn was one of the finest vessels in the Sith fleet. It was equipped with the latest and advanced technology at the time.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Valcyn
- **Model:** T-class long-range personal cruiser
- **Engine:** Ion engine
