# Tributum

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tributum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tributum)

The Tributum was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer that was under the command of Imperial officer Kanchar. During the Galactic Civil War it fought against Rebel Alliance forces during their mission to Shu-Torun.
