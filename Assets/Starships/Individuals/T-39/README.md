# T-39

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-39](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-39)

T-39 was a GR-75 medium transport used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. In 3 ABY, T-39, along with T-37 and T-38, carried wounded survivors of the Imperial attack on the Hormuuz facility near Gelgelar to the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Redemption.
