# Lucid Voice

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lucid Voice/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucid_Voice/Legends)

The Lucid Voice was a modified Trade Federation Providence-class carrier/destroyer that saw service with the Confederate Navy during the Clone Wars. The vessel was colored a medium blue, with subtle red bow stripes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lucid Voice
- **Model:** Modified Providence-class carrier/destroyer
- **Armament:** Turbolasers
- **Bays:** 2 main hangars
- **Role:**
  - Carrier
  - Destroyer
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Navy:** Confederate Navy
