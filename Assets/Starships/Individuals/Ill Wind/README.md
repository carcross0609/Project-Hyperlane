# Ill Wind

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ill Wind](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ill_Wind)

Ill Wind was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer assigned at the time of the Battle of Endor under the command of Warlord Zsinj. It later participated in the Battle of Kuat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ill Wind
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Imperial II-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Complement:** TIE/LN starfighters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Warlord Zsinj
