# Archer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Archer (Assassin-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Archer_%28Assassin-class%29)

The Archer was a CR92a Assassin-class corvette that served in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. A few months after the Battle of Yavin, the ship was piloted by Ensign Nanda during Darth Vader's five-day mission to punish every person responsible for the infiltration of Rebel agent Kell Bircher into the Galactic Empire.

- **Name:** Archer

- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
