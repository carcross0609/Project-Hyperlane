# Gorian Shard's pirate Corsair

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gorian Shard's pirate Corsair](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gorian_Shard%27s_pirate_Corsair)

A Cumulus-class Corsair was owned by Pirate King Gorian Shard and his pirate crew around 9 ABY. It was involved in a dogfight in the Nevarro system, and later launched the Pirate siege on Nevarro itself, during which it was destroyed by the Mandalorians.
