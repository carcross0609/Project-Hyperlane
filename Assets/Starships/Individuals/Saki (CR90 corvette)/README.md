# Saki

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saki (CR90 corvette)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saki_%28CR90_corvette%29)

Saki was a CR90 corvette in the Alliance Fleet. Saki was part of the the Independence task force at Sullust, it was also present during the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Saki
- **Model:** CR90 corvette
- **Destroyed:** 4 ABY, above Endor
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Alliance Fleet
- **Fleet:** Independence task force
