# Rahm Kota's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rahm Kota's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rahm_Kota%27s_starship)

A starship was used by Jedi Master Rahm Kota during his time with the Rebel Alliance. He used the ship to travel to the planet Dantooine to recruit his former Padawan, Falon Grey, to the Rebel cause. However, he found that Grey was dead, and a Force-sensitive clone created from Grey's genetic template was tending a farm on the surface. X2, as the clone was known, joined the Rebel Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rahm Kota's starship
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Owners:** Rahm Kota
