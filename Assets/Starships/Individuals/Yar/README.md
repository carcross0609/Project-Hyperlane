# Yar

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yar)

Yar was a Star Galleon-class frigate present at Destreg Spaceport that was present when K'Armyn Viraxo's personal yacht, the Highroller, was destroyed by Ace and Emon Azzameen. However, K'Armyn had got wind of this and used the yacht as a trap against the Azzameens. Despite this, both Ace and Emon escaped the ambush by destroying all of the Bounty Hunters. Her cargo was landspeeders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Yar
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Star Galleon-class frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Length:** 300 meters
