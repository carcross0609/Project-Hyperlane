# Hilo

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hilo (BFF-1 bulk freighter)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hilo_%28BFF-1_bulk_freighter%29)

Hilo was a BFF-1 bulk freighter belonging to the Galactic Empire. Hilo was part of the Imperial II-class Star Destroyer Corrupter's vital resupply convoy lead by the Nebulon-B Frigate Monitor. The ship's cargo was durasteel. Hilo was attacked by Rebel raiders and was destroyed.

- **Name:** Hilo

- **Height:** 34 meters
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
