# G30 racing starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [G30 racing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G30_racing_starfighter)

The G30 racing starfighter was a type of racing ship manufactured by Incom-Freitek. The starfighter's S-foils could be adjusted for better aim of the wingtip-mounted Taim & Bak KX10B laser cannons or to accommodate the need for speed or maneuverability. The Green Ace flown by racer Hype Fazon was a modified G30.
