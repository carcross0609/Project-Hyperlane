# Z-Holi 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-Holi 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-Holi_2)

Z-Holi 2 was a corvette that fought on the side of Grand Admiral Demetrius Zaarin's coup during the Second battle of Imperial Center in around 4 ABY. During the battle, the corvette was destroyed by Imperial loyalists shortly before the destruction of Z-Holi 1.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z-Holi 2
- **Type:** Corvette
- **Destroyed:** c. 4 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Zaarin coup forces
- **Navy:** Zaarin's fleet
- **Fleet:** Z-Holi group
