# Seakyte

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seakyte](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seakyte)

Seakyte was a group of 6 escort shuttles used by the Ripoblus, during the final stage of the Empire's campaign to end the Sepan Civil War. They were launched by the Nebulon-B2 frigate Shemsher to attack the Victory-class Star Destroyer Protector. They were unsuccessful and were likely all destroyed in the battle.
