# Trax Express

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trax Express](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trax_Express)

Trax Express was a small starship, used for transportation, that was known for its low fares and minimal comfort. It ferried a group of Alliance to Restore the Republic agents, and the merchant Hen Jamos, from Draenell's Point to Entrus in the Trax sector during the Galactic Civil War.
