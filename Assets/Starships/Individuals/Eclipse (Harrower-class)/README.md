# Eclipse

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eclipse (Harrower-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eclipse_%28Harrower-class%29)

The Eclipse was a Harrower-class dreadnought belonging to the reconstituted Sith Empire that was present during the Battle of Corellia in 3640 BBY. The corrupted Jedi Master Tol Braga attempted to detonate the cruiser's engines above Coronet City's Government District, but the Jedi Knight known as the Hero of Tython stopped Braga and deactivated the engines.
