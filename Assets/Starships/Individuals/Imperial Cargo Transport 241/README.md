# Imperial Cargo Transport 241

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial Cargo Transport 241](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Cargo_Transport_241)

Imperial Cargo Transport 241 was a cargo ship of the Galactic Empire helmed by a mustachioed captain. At some point during 3 BBY, the transport docked at the Horizon Base. Upon leaving the dock, however, an astromech droid known as Chopper, affiliated with anti-Empire rebels, had embarked as a stowaway on the transport. After incapacitating the captain and jettisoning the Imperial stormtroopers along with the freight assembly, Chopper took control of the transport, with the help of the craft's own inventory droid, AP-5.
