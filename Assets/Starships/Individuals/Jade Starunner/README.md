# Jade Starunner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jade Starunner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jade_Starunner)

The Jade Starunner was a starship in operation during the Galactic Civil War. The vessel was owned by Vendi Kjleno, a war hero of the Rebel Alliance who fought during the Battle of Yavin. By 1 ABY, Kjleno was attacked by Imperial forces in the Endor system. The Jade Starunner was shot down above the Forest Moon of Endor and Vendi Kjleno was killed. A short time later, an explorer by the name Tinrilo x'Rilsam found the crash site of the Jade Starunner on Endor. In the wreckage, the explorer discovered a damaged datadisk containing information about the last flight of the Jade Starunner and the fate of the war hero.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jade Starunner
- **Destroyed:** c. 1 ABY, Endor
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
