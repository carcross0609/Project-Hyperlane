# Megador

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Megador](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Megador)

The Megador was a Star Dreadnought in the service of the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances during the Swarm War, when it served as the flagship of Supreme Commander Gilad Pellaeon and then later at the Battle of Uroro Station during the Second Galactic Civil War.
