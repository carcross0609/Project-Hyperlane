# Invincible

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Invincible (Strike Fear)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invincible_%28Strike_Fear%29)

The Invincible was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer that served in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. It was the leader of the task force that conducted Operation Strike Fear before the Battle of Yavin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Invincible
- **Line:** Star Destroyer
- **Model:** Imperial I-class Star Destroyer
- **Destroyed:** 1 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
