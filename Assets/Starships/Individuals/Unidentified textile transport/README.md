# Unidentified textile transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified textile transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_textile_transport)

During the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire, a transport carrying Ayelixe Fabrico textiles once exploded in the atmosphere over the planet Rhinnal, scattering its cargo for kilometers.
