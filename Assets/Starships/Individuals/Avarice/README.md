# Avarice

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Avarice](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avarice)

The Avarice was an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer. During the Battle of Hoth in 3 ABY, former clone trooper X2 breached the ship, found his "brother" X1, and dueled him. The Avarice was ultimately destroyed during the battle via explosives planted by Grey Squadron.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Avarice
- **Type:** Imperial II-class Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Fleet:** Death Squadron
- **Captains:** X1
