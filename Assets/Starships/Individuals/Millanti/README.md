# Millanti

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Millanti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Millanti)

Millanti was the YV-666 light freighter owned by husband and wife Duros Ohwun and Chachi De Maal. The ship had over a dozen secret cargo holds, and was known to have been used by the couple to smuggle bacta to the Rebel Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Millanti
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Line:** YV-series
- **Model:** YV-666 light freighter
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Length:** 100 meters
