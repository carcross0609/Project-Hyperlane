# Victory at Batonn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Victory at Batonn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Victory_at_Batonn)

During the Imperial Era, the Victory at Batonn was a starship that served the Imperial Navy. After she received prime marks at the planet Carida's Imperial Naval Academy, the Ilohian Tian Karmiya finely served on the Enigma&mdash;another starship&mdash;and the Victory at Batonn. Following this, Karmiya held the rank of commander and was stationed as executive officer of the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Ultimatum. In 3 ABY, Karmiya's commanding officer aboard the Star Destroyer, Captain Canonhaus, reflected on her previous service, including her time on Victory at Batonn.
