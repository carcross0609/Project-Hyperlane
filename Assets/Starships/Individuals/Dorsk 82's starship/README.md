# Dorsk 82's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dorsk 82's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorsk_82%27s_starship)

In 26 ABY, the Khommite Jedi Knight Dorsk 82 utilized a starship in an effort to rescue a number of droids from the planet Ando during the Yuuzhan Vong War. Along with his pilot, Hhen, Dorsk 82 tried to get thirty-eight droids off-planet ahead of a mob of Aqualish intent on destroying them to appease the Yuuzhan Vong. The ship was destroyed on the order of a security officer, killing Hhen and destroying the droids on board.
