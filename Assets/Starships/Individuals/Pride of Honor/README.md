# Pride of Honor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pride of Honor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pride_of_Honor)

The Pride of Honor was a capital ship that served with the New Republic Defense Fleet during the Yuuzhan Vong War. The vessel participated in the Battle of Coruscant during the height of the invasion, where it was supported by the starfighters of Green Squadron. When the squadron was withdrawn to protect a shuttle, which was carrying a senator, the Pride of Honor suffered extensive damage.
