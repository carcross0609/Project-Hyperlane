# Ahsoka Tano's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ahsoka Tano's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahsoka_Tano%27s_Eta-2_Actis-class_interceptor)

Ahsoka's Jedi Interceptor was an Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor flown by Jedi Padawan Ahsoka Tano. The ship color was red similar to her previous Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor. Her droid R7-A7 continued to serve as her copilot.
