# V-484

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V-484](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-484)

V-484 was a cargo ship of the Chiss Ascendancy's Stybla family. While traveling from Csilla to Sposia containing cargo that included a section of a tachyon flow synchrotron accelerator from the second Starflash weapon, the starship was hijacked by members of the Clarr family disguised as an Obbic family transport and forcefully taken into hyperspace. After the hijackers had stolen its cargo, they left it in an elliptical orbit around the blackdock Glastis 3, intending for it to be noticed by the blackdock crew by the time that they had already sold its stolen goods at the planet Pleknok. With the help of Mid Commander Mitth'raw'nuru and Syndic Mitth'ras'safis, Senior Aide Stybla'ppin'cykok aboard the patrol ship Jandalin located V-484, subsequently tracking down the stolen cargo.
