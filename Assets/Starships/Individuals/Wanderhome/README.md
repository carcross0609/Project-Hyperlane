# Wanderhome

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wanderhome](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wanderhome)

The Wanderhome was a Gallofree medium transport that evacuated Ranat and Ryn refugees from Gyndine after the planet was attacked by the Yuuzhan Vong.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wanderhome
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Type:** Space transport
- **Cost:**
  - New: 425,000 credits
  - Used: 150,000 credits
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 3
- **Armament:** Twin laser cannons (4)
- **Crew:** 6
- **Passengers:** 60
- **Capacity:** 18,000 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 6 months
