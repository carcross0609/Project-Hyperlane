# Solicitude

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Solicitude](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solicitude)

The Solicitude was a 1,600-meter-long Imperial I-class Star Destroyer in service to the Imperial Navy during the Imperial Era. Moden Canady began his career in the Imperial Navy as the commander of the Star Destroyer, at which point he held the military rank of major.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Solicitude
- **Line:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer
- **Model:** Imperial I-class Star Destroyer
- **Type:** Star Destroyer
- **Length:** 1,600 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Captains:** Moden Canady
