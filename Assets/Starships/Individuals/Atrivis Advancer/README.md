# Atrivis Advancer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Atrivis Advancer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atrivis_Advancer)

The Atrivis Advancer was a starship owned by Alliance to Restore the Republic scout Flinor Tekkirl. A Pathfinder model manufactured by Vangaard Industries, the starship remained unmodified by the scout. At thirty-six meters in length, the scout ship was equipped with a single turret-mounted laser cannon and could attain speeds of 950 kilometers per hour in atmosphere. It was hyperdrive-equipped, with a Class 3 as primary, and a Class 15 as backup, enabling Tekkirl to use it in his various scouting missions to unexplored and dangerous regions of the galaxy.
