# Voyager Dawn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Voyager Dawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voyager_Dawn)

The Voyager Dawn was a medium-sized cruiser used by the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era, where it carried a meditation retreat for elder Jedi. After the Great Hyperspace Disaster, the Voyager Dawn took significant damage and required immediate assistance for the rescue of its hundreds of passengers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Voyager Dawn
- **Type:** Cruiser
- **Complement:**
  - 1 Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor
  - 3 Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptors
  - 1 Phi-class shuttle
- **Passengers:** Hundreds
- **Destroyed:** Between 12 BBY and 9 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
