# Ranger-class gunship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ranger-class gunship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger-class_gunship)

The Ranger-class gunship, also referred to as a Ranger gunship, was a type of starship designed by the Corellian Engineering Corporation and used by the New Republic's Defense Fleet in the Yuuzhan Vong War against the invading Yuuzhan Vong. Capable of destroying many starfighters with its dozens of laser batteries, the Ranger-class gunship saw action at Helska IV and Ord Mantell. One Ranger gunship, Justice, participated in an attack by the Galactic Alliance, the New Republic's successor state, on the Yuuzhan Vong&ndash;held Bilbringi system in 29 ABY.
