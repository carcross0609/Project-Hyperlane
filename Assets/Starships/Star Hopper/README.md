# Star Hopper

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Star Hopper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Hopper)

The Star Hopper was a cruiser used by the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era, where it served as a Padawan Academic Cruiser led by Grand Master Yoda and Jedi Master Torban Buck. After the Nihil attacked Trymant IV, the Star Hopper, being the closest Jedi or Galactic Republic starship, was dispatched to assist.
