# Rayc Ryjerd's starship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rayc Ryjerd's starship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rayc_Ryjerd%27s_starship)

The smuggler Rayc Ryjerd purchased a starship sometime before or during the year 4 ABY, putting himself into debt in the process. He worked for the Hutt crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure in an effort to pay these debts.
