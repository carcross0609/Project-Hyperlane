# Defiance's Banner

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Defiance's Banner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defiance%27s_Banner)

Defiance's Banner was a starship that served as the flagship of the Sy Myrthian ex-senator Toonbuck Toora's Confederacy of Independent Systems holdout forces in the aftermath of the Clone Wars. During the Sy Myrthian Insurrection in 19 BBY, the Galactic Empire sent a task force led by the Praetor-class Star Battlecruiser Battalion to hunt down Defiance Banner.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Defiance's Banner
- **Role:** Flagship
- **Affiliation:** Separatist holdouts
