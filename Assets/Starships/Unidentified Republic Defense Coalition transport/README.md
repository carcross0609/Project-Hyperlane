# Unidentified Republic Defense Coalition transport

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Republic Defense Coalition transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Republic_Defense_Coalition_transport)

A transport was utilized by the Republic Defense Coalition during the High Republic Era. It was crewed by Corporal Elping Tsoo, Lieutenant Visma Tarko, and the protocol droid C-06N. It was attacked by the Nihil pirates and three escaped in an escape pod.
