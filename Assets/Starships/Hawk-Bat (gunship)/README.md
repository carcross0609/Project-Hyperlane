# Hawk-Bat

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hawk-Bat (gunship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hawk-Bat_%28gunship%29)

The Hawk-Bat was a jet-black Tempest-class gunship famous for being the personal ship of Arwen Cohl, and recognized as such on sight by many lowlifes throughout the Outer Rim Territories. It led the attack on the Revenue over Dorvalla in 33 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hawk-Bat
- **Model:** Tempest-class gunship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Nebula Front
  - Jedi Order
- **Owners:** Arwen Cohl
