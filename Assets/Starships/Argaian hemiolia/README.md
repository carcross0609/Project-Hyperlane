# Argaian hemiolia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Argaian hemiolia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Argaian_hemiolia)

Argaian hemiolias were a model of warship named for the planet Argai dating to before the rise of the Galactic Republic. Hemiolias were one of many models of warship used by the empire of the Tionese despot Xim, mixed into squadrons also including Livien cutters, Thanium star-glaives and Cronian battlebirds.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Argaian hemiolia
- **Affiliation:** Xim's empire
