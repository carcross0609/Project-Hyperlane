# Unidentified Yuuzhan Vong capital ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Yuuzhan Vong capital ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Yuuzhan_Vong_capital_ship)

A capital ship, slightly smaller than a Star Destroyer, was used by the Yuuzhan Vong at the Battle of Kalarba. It contributed to the destruction of the Bothan Assault Cruiser Champion.
