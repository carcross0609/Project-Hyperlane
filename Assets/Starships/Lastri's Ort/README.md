# Lastri's Ort

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lastri's Ort](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lastri%27s_Ort)

The Lastri's Ort was one of the starships in Talon Karrde's smuggling operation. It was a 300 meter long Kuat Drive Yards Star Galleon-class frigate, and as such was the largest and best-armed ship in Karrde's fleet.
