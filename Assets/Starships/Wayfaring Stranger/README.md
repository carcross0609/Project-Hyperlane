# Wayfaring Stranger

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wayfaring Stranger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wayfaring_Stranger)

The Wayfaring Stranger was a poorly armed transport. Following the Battle of Bakura, the Wayfaring Stranger transported passengers, including twenty merchants, away from the planet. Former Imperial officers Denno Yanglan, Rafe Shahverdian, and Mayr Stillman sought passage on the vessel. Yanglan and Shahverdian intended to defect to the Alliance, while Stillman, who was still loyal to the Empire, hoped to infiltrate them.
