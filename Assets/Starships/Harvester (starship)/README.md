# Harvester

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harvester (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harvester_%28starship%29)

The Harvester was an Acclamator-class assault ship active under the Galactic Empire, commanded by Captain Ransley. In 19 BBY, the Harvester intercepted the starship of Dezono Qua by the desert world of Prine. However, Qua's ship was being flown by wanted Jedi fugitive Dass Jennir, after having killed the vessel's former owner. When Jennir refused to stand by and be boarded and instead attempted to make for the planet, Ransley gave the order for two Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighters to destroy Jennir's ship.
