# Justifier-class assault transport

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Justifier-class assault transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Justifier-class_assault_transport)

The Justifier-class was a class of assault transport manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards. The starship was heavily armed with four laser cannons mounted on the edge of its two wings, a powerful hyperdrive and two engines with a reorienting central thruster that folded flat against the sides of the ship to decrease its size during landings. The cockpit featured several large viewports that provided a wide field of view for pursuing targets. The bounty hunter Cad Bane used a Justifier-class transport named the Justifier during the early Imperial Era.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Justifier-class assault transport
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Justifier-class
- **Type:** Assault transport
- **Engine:** 2 engines
