# Unidentified Paccian gunboat

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Paccian gunboat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Paccian_gunboat)

A Paccian gunboat was piloted by the Chiss Mid Captain Csap'ro'strob "Apros" of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet at the battle in the Sunrise system known as Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand. During the battle, Apros piloted the Paccian gunboat, which initially spun in circles spewing metallic black smoke to appear derelict. The black smoke, an invention of the Paccosh species who had also provided the gunboat, also served the protective function of diffusing and dissipating laser fire in combat.
