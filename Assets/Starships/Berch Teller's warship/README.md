# Berch Teller's warship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Berch Teller's warship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berch_Teller%27s_warship)

Berch Teller's warship was a modified Providence-class Dreadnought created and used by a rebel cell five years after the inception of the Galactic Empire. As a result of not being the product of a major shipbuilding conglomerate, the starship lacked both a name and a registered signature.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Berch Teller's warship
- **Model:** Modified Providence-class Dreadnought
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Length:** 920 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 12 turbolasers
  - 10 point-defense ion cannons
  - 6 proton torpedo launchers
- **Complement:** Droid starfighters
- **Crew:** 30 sentients
- **Destroyed:** 14 BBY, near the Gulf of Tatooine
- **Affiliation:** Berch Teller's rebel cell
- **Owners:** Berch Teller
- **Captains:** Berch Teller
