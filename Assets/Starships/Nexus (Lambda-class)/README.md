# Nexus (Lambda-class)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nexus (Lambda-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nexus_%28Lambda-class%29)

Nexus en route to dock with Shuttle Fairfax.]] The Nexus was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle used by Imperial forces, led by Grand Admiral Zaarin, at the Mylok IV Conflict during the Galactic Civil War. The Nexus docked with Habeen Shuttle Fairfax to finalize arrangements for completed hyperdrives and citizenship of the Habeen.
