# Vo Granee Droi

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vo Granee Droi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vo_Granee_Droi)

The Vo Granee Droi was a YT-2000 Light Freighter operated by Boonda, a Hutt who held Rebel Alliance sympathies. After failing to graduate from the Corulag Military Academy, Boonda would use the Vo Granee Droi to operate a shipping business, a business that would ferry supplies to the Rebel Alliance throughout the Galactic Civil War. The ship was known as a tramp freighter
