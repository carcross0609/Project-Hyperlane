# Shell-cracker prototype

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shell-cracker prototype](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shell-cracker_prototype)

The Shell-cracker prototype was an Imperial R&D starship. It was developed for Operation: Shell-cracker after the Battle of Yavin at The Hammer. The prototype's purpose was to eliminate enemy starships. It had a complex shield structure containing lattice-like ionic energies designed to disrupt particle and ray shields, and deliver a series of bombs against the target.
