# Bellicose

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bellicose (Firewolf man-of-war)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bellicose_%28Firewolf_man-of-war%29)

The Bellicose was a Firewolf man-of-war commanded by Chiss Admiral Ers'ikaro of the Chiss Ascendancy nation's Defense Force. In 18 BBY, the Bellicose stopped the battle at Csaus between the Chiss Tahmie and Droc families before it escalated. The Clarr family's Captain Clarr'os'culry and Patriarch Clarr'ivl'exow mentioned the warship's intervention while discussing information retrieved by the Grysk operative "Jixtus" about a crisis in the Ascendancy.
