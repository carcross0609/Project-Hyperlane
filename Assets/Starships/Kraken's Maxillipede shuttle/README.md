# Kraken's Maxillipede shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kraken's Maxillipede shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kraken%27s_Maxillipede_shuttle)

A Maxillipede shuttle, commanded by Super tactical droid Commander Kraken, was unsuccessfully used to transport clone trooper Tup from Ringo Vinda to Count Dooku during the Clone Wars. However, after ARC trooper Fives fired a grappling hook he attached to one of his DC-17 repeater hand blasters, the ARC trooper, along with Jedi General Anakin Skywalker and Clone Captain Rex boarded the shuttle. They destroyed Kraken and the droid crew before they could escape the system, successfully rescuing Tup so he could be taken to Kamino for examination.
