# Verdant Dawn

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Verdant Dawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verdant_Dawn)

The Verdant Dawn was a freighter captained by the Dug Suhlarla Tug during the Imperial Era. An independent vessel, Tug and her crew were a tight-knit bunch who worked together for decades, with some of them even being second- or third-generation. The Verdant Dawn frequently made transport runs between the Core Worlds and the Outer Rim Territories.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Verdant Dawn
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Captains:** Suhlarla Tug
