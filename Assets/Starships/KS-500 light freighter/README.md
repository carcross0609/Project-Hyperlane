# KS-500 light freighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KS-500 light freighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KS-500_light_freighter)

The KS-500 light freighter was a model of light freighter produced by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, the criminals Jaylen Vrax and ND-5 used a KS-500 light freighter called the Successor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KS-500 light freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
