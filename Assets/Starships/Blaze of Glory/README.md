# Blaze of Glory

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blaze of Glory](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blaze_of_Glory)

The Blaze of Glory was a modified YT-1930 light freighter owned and operated by the Bimm smuggler Rycar Ryjerd. The Blaze of Glory was at one time docked in Rycar's hidden asteroid base in the Obana Belt.
