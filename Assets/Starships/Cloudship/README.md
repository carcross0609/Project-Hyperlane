# Cloudship

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cloudship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cloudship)

Cloudships were a class of starship utilized by the Nihil marauder organization. The Cloud Ultident Margrona utilized two Cloudships in a raid on Elphrona, one of which was destroyed by Jedi Master Loden Greatstorm. The other Cloudship was damaged, but escaped, carrying the prisoners Ottoh and Ronn Blythe aboard. Jedi Indeera Stokes rescued Ronn, and Greatstorm boarded the damaged Cloudship to rescue Ottoh. The Nihil Tempest Runner Lourna Dee entered the Cloudship, kidnapping both Greatstorm and Blythe. A number of Cloudships were present at the Battle of Kur against the Galactic Republic, but were easily destroyed when hit.
