# Dutyfree

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dutyfree](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dutyfree)

The Dutyfree was a GR-75 medium transport that was part of the Alliance to Restore the Republic's fleet, most notably taking part in the Battle of Hoth, where it was escorted off-planet by T-65B X-wing starfighters flown by Luke Skywalker and Wedge Antilles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dutyfree
- **Manufacturer:** Gallofree Yards, Inc.
- **Model:** GR-75 medium transport
- **Length:** 90 meters
- **Max Speed:** 650 kph
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 4
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Four twin laser cannons
- **Crew:** 6
- **Capacity:** 19,000 metric tons
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Navy:** Rebel Alliance Navy
