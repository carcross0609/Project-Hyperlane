# XTS-673

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XTS-673](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XTS-673)

XTS-673 was a Bulk cruiser operated by Xizor Transport Systems. It interrupted an attempt by the Azzameen family to assault the Viraxo munitions cache near 1 by launching its squadrons of Supa Fighters. Ace Azzameen was successful in destroying all of the Supa Fighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** XTS-673
