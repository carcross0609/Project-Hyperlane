# Sprint-class rescue craft

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sprint-class rescue craft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sprint-class_rescue_craft)

Sprint-class rescue crafts were a class of nimble starship that saw use by the Galactic Republic's Space Rescue Corps around the time of the Clone Wars against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Among the rescue corps' most commonly deployed starship models, the Sprint-class was utilized to locate missing starships and space battle survivors, and respond to distress calls, while larger assignments were handled by the disc-hulled Corellian rescue frigate.
