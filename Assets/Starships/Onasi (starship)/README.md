# Onasi

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Onasi (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Onasi_%28starship%29)

The Onasi was a warship in the Republic Navy that was under Jedi Master Oteg's command at the Battle of the Maelstrom in the early days of the Galactic War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Onasi
- **Destroyed:** c. 3641 BBY, Maelstrom Nebula
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Navy:** Republic Navy
- **Fleet:** First Expeditionary Fleet
