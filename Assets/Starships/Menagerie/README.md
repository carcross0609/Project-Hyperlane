# Menagerie

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Menagerie](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Menagerie)

The Menagerie was once a smuggler ship which was acquired by Kelly Vermillion in the spice mines of Kessel. Originally named Long Shot, Vermillion and his gang renamed it in honor of their group of misfit aliens.
