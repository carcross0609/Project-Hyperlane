# Jek-14's stealth starfighter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Jek-14's stealth starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jek-14%27s_stealth_starfighter)

Jek-14's stealth starfighter was a customized starship resembling the much later E-7 E-wing multi-role starfighter piloted by Jek-14 himself during the Clone Wars. The starfighter had earlier belonged to Count Dooku and General Grievous. Jek-14 then used it to escape from Count Dooku from Geonosis and also evade the forces of the Galactic Republic who also hunted him. The starfighter was first maintained by a yellow plated R-series astromech droid and then by R4-G0.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jek-14's stealth starfighter
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Armament:** 2 missile launchers
- **Crew:**

  - 1 astromech droid
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
