# PTB-625 planetary bomber

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PTB-625 planetary bomber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PTB-625_planetary_bomber)

The PTB-625 planetary bomber was a model of bomber used by the Galactic Republic's naval forces during the Clone Wars against the Confederacy of Independent Systems, fought from 22 BBY to 19 BBY. Constructed by Incom Corporation and Sublights Products Corporation for planetary bombing, the ship shared design similarities with the companies' NTB-630 naval bomber and lighter Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter. Like its cousins, the PTB-625 bomber saw deployment by the Republic in the Outer Rim Territories during the war.
