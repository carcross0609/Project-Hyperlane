# StarTour 7

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [StarTour 7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/StarTour_7)

StarTour 7 was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle that was used by a tourist company. The shuttle was inspected by a TIE Interceptor pilot at a customs checkpoint who identified the cargo as "Tourists," and the craft was permitted to leave the station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** StarTour 7
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Type:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Role:** Transport
