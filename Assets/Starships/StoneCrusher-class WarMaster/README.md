# StoneCrusher-class WarMaster

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [StoneCrusher-class WarMaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/StoneCrusher-class_WarMaster)

The StoneCrusher-class WarMaster was a class of warship used by the Grysk Hegemony of the Unknown Regions. The Armageddon, the Emery, and the SkySweep were all examples of the class. A StoneCrusher ship was smaller than a Nightdragon man-of-war but larger than a heavy cruiser, both from the Chiss Ascendancy. They were also smaller than the Grysk's Shatter-class WarMaster, but larger than the BattleChiefs, also part of the Grysk armada. They were usually commanded and crewed by Grysks, but their navigator, according to Qilori of Uandualon, was an Attendant of an unknown species.
