# Compass Star

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Compass Star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Compass_Star)

The Compass Star was a Galactic Alliance warship that took part in the Second Battle of Fondor. During the course of the battle it was sent to the aid of the Loyal Defender, which had been so heavily damaged that an evacuation order had been issued.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Compass Star
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
