# Interloper

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Interloper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Interloper)

Interlopers were medium-range troop transports used by the Sith forces fighting for the Brotherhood of Darkness during the final years of the New Sith Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Interloper
- **Type:** Dropship
- **Hyperdrive:** None
- **Shield Gen:** None
- **Crew:**
  - 1 Pilot
  - 1 Co-Pilot
- **Passengers:** 10
- **Role:** Troop Transport
- **Retired:** 1000 BBY
- **Affiliation:** Brotherhood of Darkness
