# Valor-class cruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Valor-class cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valor-class_cruiser)

The Valor-class cruiser served in the Republic Navy during the Great Galactic War and the subsequent Cold War. At least some of the cruisers are produced by Kuat Drive Yards.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Valor-class cruiser
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Armament:**
  - Turbolaser cannons
  - Quad-laser cannon turrets
  - Ion cannons
  - Proton torpedoes
  - Concussion missiles
  - Ion torpedoes
- **Complement:**
  - 110 Starfighters
  -   Liberator-class starfighters
  - 40 bombers
  - 25 shuttles
- **Crew:** 1,900
- **Passengers:** 5,000 (troops)
- **Role:** Warship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Republic Navy
