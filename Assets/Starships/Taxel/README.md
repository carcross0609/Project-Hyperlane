# Taxel

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Taxel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taxel)

Taxel was the name of a group of two Rebel Alliance Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transports that were used as part of a last ditch attack on Station NL-1 during the Battle on the Frontier. Vice Admiral Thrawn's Task force consisting of Nebulon-B2 frigate Ludwick, Platform NL-1, 2 System Patrol Craft, and various starfighters, destroyed both Transports along with their Rebel comrades.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Taxel
- **Model:** Gamma-class ATR-6 assault transport
- **Role:**
  - Transport
  - Boarding craft
  - Convoy escort
- **Affiliation:** Rebel Alliance
