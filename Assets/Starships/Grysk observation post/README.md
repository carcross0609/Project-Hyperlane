# Grysk observation post

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grysk observation post](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grysk_observation_post)

The Grysk observation post was a mobile space station used by the Grysk Hegemony. Sampling nozzles, sensor clusters, drive flares and the telltale vents and power cables of a hyperdrive could be seen along its hull.
