# Star Roamer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Star Roamer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Roamer)

The Star Roamer was a medium freighter manufactured by Damorian Manufacturing Corporation. It was owned by CorDuro Shipping and in 26 ABY was used to deliver a shipment of bacta to the Yuuzhan Vong. They were intrigued by its healing properties, and would later try and sabotage its production using a virus carried by fallen Jedi Bey Gandan.
