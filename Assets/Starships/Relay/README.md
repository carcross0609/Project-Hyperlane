# Relay

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Relay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Relay)

Relays were small Incom Corporation ships slightly larger than CloakShape Fighters. They were used c. 19 BBY. The Drunk Dancer carried a Relay in its docking bay.
