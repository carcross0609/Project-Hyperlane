# B-wing/E starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B-wing/E starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-wing/E_starfighter)

The B-wing/E starfighter, or B-wing Expanded, was an upgraded B-wing starfighter with an extended cabin to accommodate a gunner, in addition to a pilot.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B-wing/E starfighter
- **Manufacturer:** Slayn & Korpil
- **Model:** B-wing/E assault fighter
- **Type:** Heavy assault starfighter
- **Cost:** 250,000 credits (new)
- **Modifier:** Gial Ackbar
- **Length:** 16.9 meters
- **Max Accel:** 2,390 G
- **Mglt:** 70 MGLT
- **Max Speed:** 1,000 km/h (higher speeds possible with deflector shields on)
- **Engine:**
  - Quadex Kryomaster engine (1)
  - or
  - Slayn & Korpil JZ-5 Fusial Thrust Engine (Rated 290 KTU) (4)
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2.0
- **Hdsystem:** Slayn & Korpil HYd-997 hyperdrive motivator
- **Power:** Vinop O2 K cryogenic power cells and ionization reactor
- **Shield Gen:** Sirplex Zr-41 Deflector shield generators (Rated 125 SBD)
- **Hull:** Reinforced durasteel (Rated 60 RU)
- **Sensor:** Fabritech ANv-9q Sensor Array with PH-5s Phased Tachyon Detection Array and PK-8f short-range primary threat analysis grid
- **Target:** Fabritech ANq 3.6 tracking computer with IN-344-B "Sightline" imaging system
- **Navigation:** Microaxial LpM-549 navigation computer
- **Avionics:** Narmox HG.6w flight control avionics
- **Armament:**
  - Standard configuration:
  - ArMek SW-7a light ion cannons (3)
  - Fire-linked laser cannons (2)
  - Krupx Munitions MG9 proton torpedo launchers (B-Wing/E: 1; B-Wing/E2: 2)
  -   8 proton torpedoes total
  - Removable ammo magazine (B-Wing/E2)
  -   Additional 12 proton torpedoes
- **Crew:**
  - Pilot (1)
  - Gunner (1)
- **Capacity:** 50 kilograms
- **Consumables:** 1 week
- **Role:** Heavy assault starfighter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Alliance of Free Planets
  - New Republic
