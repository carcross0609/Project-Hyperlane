# Fraunesaa

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fraunesaa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fraunesaa)

The Fraunesaa was a hauler starship belonging to the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. The ship had been seized from a Hutt after he had been forced to stop at the Starlight Beacon space station, and added to the fleet of ships overseen by Jedi Master Nubarron. An old, cube-shaped vessel, Nubarron issued the ship to Master Cohmac Vitus when he and his Padawan were summoned to Coruscant to make a report to the Jedi High Council and Galactic Senate. They were accompanied by Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh and her apprentice Imri Cantaros, who had also been called back to Coruscant, as Rwoh's history of crashing ships had led Nubarron to bar her from being issued any more at Starlight. The Fraunesaa was involved when the group responded to a Nihil attack on the planet Tiikae while en route to Coruscant.
