# Shuttle 2743

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shuttle 2743](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shuttle_2743)

Shuttle 2743 was a Lambda-class T-4a shuttle that was tasked with transporting the Imperial clone engineer Penn Pershing in 9 ABY. After being disabled by Slave I ion cannons, the shuttle was taken over by Din Djarin and Marshal Cara Dune. The shuttle was subsequently used as part of the plan to rescue Grogu from Moff Gideon's forces. After pretending to be under attack by Slave I, the shuttle crash-landed into the launch tube of Gideon's light cruiser. Afterwards, Cara Dune, Bo-Katan Kryze, Koska Reeves, and Fennec Shand assaulted the hangar, with Din Djarin not far behind.
