# Corporate Sector gunship

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corporate Sector gunship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corporate_Sector_gunship)

Corporate Sector gunships were used in the Lost Tribe of Sith's armada in 44 ABY. The Corporate Sector Authority-made gunships were deployed during Operation Shieldfall on the planet of Nam Chorios. However, the Sith fleet was defeated by a flight of Jedi StealthX starfighters, and forced to retreat.
