# T-5 Deliverance

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-5 Deliverance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-5_Deliverance)

The T-5 Deliverance was a repurposed Lambda-class T-4a shuttle that was used by the Galactic Empire to dispatch medical and repair payloads directly into the battle. Its hull bore the red sigil, a symbol used to identify military medical services.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T-5 Deliverance
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Line:** Abecederian line
- **Model:** Lambda-class T-4a shuttle
- **Type:** Personnel shuttle
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 2 forward double laser cannons
  - 2 wing-mounted double cannons
  - 1 rear double laser cannon
- **Role:** Medical ship
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Navy
