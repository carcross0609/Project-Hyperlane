# Unidentified Rebel Alliance vessel

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Rebel Alliance vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Rebel_Alliance_vessel)

A type of starship was used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War against the Galactic Empire. At least one such starship was present in the Inner Rim as part of the Alliance Fleet's Fourth Division following the Battle of Hoth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Rebel Alliance vessel
- **Engine:** At least 1 sublight engine
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Rebel Alliance Navy
