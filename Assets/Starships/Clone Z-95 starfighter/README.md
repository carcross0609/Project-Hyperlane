# Clone Z-95 starfighter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clone Z-95 starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Z-95_starfighter)

The clone Z-95 starfighter, also known as the clone Z-95 Headhunter and more commonly known as the Headhunter starfighter, was a model of starfighter and variant of the standard Z-95 Headhunter used by the Galactic Republic's clone trooper pilots during the Clone Wars. Although not introduced until some time into the war, the clone Z-95 became one of the most popular forms of Republic air support. The versatile fighter saw action from the Battle of Umbara until the end of the war.
