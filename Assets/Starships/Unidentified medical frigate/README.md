# Unidentified medical frigate

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified medical frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_medical_frigate)

A medical frigate was attacked by the nascent Alliance to Restore the Republic from the Galactic Empire in 3 BBY. Though the frigate fell into Rebel possession after the attack, the Empire falsely reported that the ship had remained in their hands.
