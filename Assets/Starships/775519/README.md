# 775519

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [775519](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/775519)

775519 was a T-6 shuttle used by Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas around the time of the Invasion of Naboo. It was shot down over the moon of Oba Diah, killing Sifo-Dyas whereas Silman survived to be imprisoned by the Pyke Syndicate, and its remains were later discovered by the clone troopers of the 104th Battalion under the leadership of General Plo Koon during the last months of the Clone Wars.
