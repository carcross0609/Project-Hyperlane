# Nightdragon man-of-war

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nightdragon man-of-war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightdragon_man-of-war)

The Nightdragon man-of-war was a type of Man-of-war warship utilized by the Chiss Ascendancy's Expansionary Defense Fleet. It was equipped with a hyperdrive and engines, as well as electrostatic barriers for countermeasures. Its armaments included spectrum lasers, plasma spheres, and breachers. The ships' hull was constructed of nyix-alloy, and it was equipped with escape pods.
