# Warder

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Warder](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Warder)

The Warder was a Nebulon-B frigate converted to medical duty by the Rebel Alliance. The frigate continued in operation after the transition to the New Republic, and made up part of the fleet assigned to General Han Solo during the hunt for Warlord Zsinj as part of Group Two.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Warder
- **Model:** EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Type:** Frigate
- **Role:** Medical ship
