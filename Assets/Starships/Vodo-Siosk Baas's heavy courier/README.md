# Vodo-Siosk Baas's heavy courier

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vodo-Siosk Baas's heavy courier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vodo-Siosk_Baas%27s_heavy_courier)

Vodo-Siosk Baas's heavy courier was used by Jedi Master Vodo-Siosk Baas to travel to Coruscant along with Tott Doneeta, Nomi Sunrider, Cay Qel-Droma, Dace Diath, Shoaneb Culu, Qrrrl Toq, and Sylvar, shortly before the Krath assault on the planet.
