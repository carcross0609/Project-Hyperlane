# Bright Nebula

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bright Nebula](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bright_Nebula)

The Bright Nebula was a GR-75 medium transport in service to the New Republic Defense Fleet. It was one of three GR-75 medium transports stationed at Folor Base in 7.5 ABY, and was the second transport to evacuate the moon base during the Battle of Folor.
