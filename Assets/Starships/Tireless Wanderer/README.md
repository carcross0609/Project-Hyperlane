# Tireless Wanderer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tireless Wanderer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tireless_Wanderer)

The Tireless Wanderer was a SoroSuub Luxury 3000 space yacht. It was bought used by Yeel Dresk from a scam artist, and crash-landed on Toydaria soon after his purchase. The experience made Dresk distrustful of the planet's residents, so he lived in the ship for weeks while waiting for offworld mechanics to arrive and repair the ship.
