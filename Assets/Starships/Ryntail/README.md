# Ryntail

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ryntail](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ryntail)

Ryntail was the personal yacht of Tan Dicium, a princeling from the celestial body Sifuchi. It partially shared its name with the Ryn, a sentient species with tails. Sometime between 0 BBY and around two months after the Battle of Yavin, Dicium visited the Inner Rim trade world Tirahnn along with his four Yuzzem bodyguards.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ryntail
- **Type:** Yacht
- **Owners:** Tan Dicium
