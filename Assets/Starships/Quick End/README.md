# Quick End

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quick End](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quick_End)

The Quick End was a Nargi-class pursuit frigate used by the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances in 43 ABY. Alongside the Fast Death and Bolt Squadron, Quick End chased Han Solo in the Millennium Falcon in 43.5 ABY, who was actually providing a distraction for his wife, Leia Organa Solo, to escape offworld with four psychotic Jedi Knights.
