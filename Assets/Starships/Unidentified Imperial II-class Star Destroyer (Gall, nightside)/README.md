# Unidentified Imperial II-class Star Destroyer

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Imperial II-class Star Destroyer (Gall, nightside)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Imperial_II-class_Star_Destroyer_%28Gall%2C_nightside%29)

An Imperial II-class Star Destroyer of the Galactic Empire protected the nightside orbit of the Imperial-held moon of Gall during the Galactic Civil War. Along with a secondary Star Destroyer patrolling Gall's dayside orbit, this Star Destroyer defended the moon against a Rebel Alliance attack in 3.5 ABY.
