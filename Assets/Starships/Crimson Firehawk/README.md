# Crimson Firehawk

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Crimson Firehawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crimson_Firehawk)

The Crimson Firehawk was a shuttle piloted by Nash Durango during the High Republic Era. The ship was named after the avian firehawk. Kai, Lys and Nubs often used this shuttle to their missions.
