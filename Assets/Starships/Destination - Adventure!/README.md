# Destination: Adventure!

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Destination: Adventure!](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Destination%3A_Adventure%21)

The Destination: Adventure! was a luxury liner in use during the Galactic Civil War. In 3 ABY, a group of smugglers docked at the Adventure! after taking on the Sisar Run at the behest of the Shistavanen Mal Biron. At that time, the liner was the site of a space battle between pirates and the Galactic Empire.
