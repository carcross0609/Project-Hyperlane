# Imperial assault shuttle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial assault shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_assault_shuttle)

The Sith Empire utilized a class of assault shuttle during the Great Galactic War, Cold War, and Galactic War against the Galactic Republic. There existed a larger variation of these shuttles, but the Imperial assault shuttles were used far more frequently than their larger counterparts in the Sith Empire's many wars with the republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial assault shuttle
- **Type:** Assault shuttle
- **Crew:** 3
- **Skeleton:** 2
- **Passengers:** 30
- **Role:** Military use
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
