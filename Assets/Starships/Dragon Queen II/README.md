# Dragon Queen II

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dragon Queen II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Queen_II)

The Dragon Queen II was the flagship of the Hapan Royal Navy in 44 ABY under command of Hapan Queen Mother Tenel Ka Djo. It assisted in the evacuation of the New Jedi Order from the Jedi Academy on the planet Ossus during the Lost Tribe of Sith emergence. Later, it served as the location for the wedding of Jaina Solo and Jagged Fel.
