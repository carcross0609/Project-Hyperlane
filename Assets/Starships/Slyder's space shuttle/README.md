# Slyder's space shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Slyder's space shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slyder%27s_space_shuttle)

Slyder's space shuttle was a small and heavily armed space vessel, owned by the bounty hunter Slyder, who kept it aboard the Star of Empire. Slyder however operated mainly onboard the Star, tracking and hunting down those who did illegal activities on the ship, such as Rebel agents.
