# Unidentified pirate transport 2

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified pirate transport 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pirate_transport_2)

A pirate transport was utilized by Captain Brutus' crew during the New Republic Era. Around 9 ABY, a group of pirates led by First Mate Gunter used the vessel on their journey to the planet Lanupa, where they captured former Captain Silvo at a local hotel. However, the transport was destroyed by a New Republic squadron of X-wing starfighters, which arrived to rescue the invaded hotel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified pirate transport 2
- **Type:** Transport
- **Armament:** At least one laser cannon
- **Affiliation:** Captain Brutus' crew
