# Hardy Harrow

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hardy Harrow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hardy_Harrow)

The Hardy Harrow was a carvanium mining ship, owned by Denon-Ardru Mutual, that disappeared on Ord Sigatt. The ship was captured and hidden by Dexter Jettster and his friends to protest the tyrannical rule of Denon-Ardru Mutual on the planet.
