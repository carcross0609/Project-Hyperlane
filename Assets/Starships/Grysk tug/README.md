# Grysk tug

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grysk tug](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grysk_tug)

During a battle around Mokivj, the Grysk, a warlike species from the Unknown Regions, utilized a type of starship that was believed to be a tug. Commodore Karyn Faro initially believed them to be starfighters, but Grand Admiral Thrawn thought that they were more likely spacetugs. They could be carried and launched from a Grysk warship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grysk tug
- **Type:** Spacetug
- **Role:**
  - Tug
  - Kamikaze
- **Affiliation:** Grysk Hegemony
