# Smelting vessel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Smelting vessel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Smelting_vessel)

Smelting vessels were industrial starships. They were commonly sighted at the Auction, a gathering of the underworld to buy starship stolen by shipjackers.
