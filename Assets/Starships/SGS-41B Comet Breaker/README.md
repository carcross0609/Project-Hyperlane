# SGS-41B Comet Breaker

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SGS-41B Comet Breaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SGS-41B_Comet_Breaker)

The SGS-41B Comet Breaker was a starfighter produced by Republic Sienar Systems for the Galactic Republic. Named after accidentally firing its weapon and destroying a comet during a test run, the gunship was known as a flying armory. At the cost of its moddable rail gun and a bit of its defensive capabilities, the gunship was able to equip proton torpedoes and heavy laser cannons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SGS-41B Comet Breaker
