# Cavalcade

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cavalcade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cavalcade)

Cavalcade was an HT-2200 medium freighter used by the BloodScar pirates in 0.5 ABY. Shakko was its captain and Tannis was its pilot. Vickers and Bisc were other crew members. Mara Jade infiltrated the ship and was forced to kill Shakko. She commandeered the ship in order to continue the mission.

- **Name:** Cavalcade
- **Model:** HT-2200 medium freighter
- **Type:** Medium freighter
