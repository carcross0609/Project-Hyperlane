# Limit of Vision

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Limit of Vision](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Limit_of_Vision)

Limit of Vision was a Hoersch-Kessel Seltaya-class fast courier flown by Jedi Master Jai Maruk while performing recon missions along the Hydian Way in the late stages of the Clone Wars.

- **Name:** Limit of Vision

- **Model:** Seltaya-class fast courier
- **Type:** Starfighter
