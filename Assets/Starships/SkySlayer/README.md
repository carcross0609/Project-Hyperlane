# SkySlayer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SkySlayer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SkySlayer)

The SkySlayer was a model of light assault starship manufactured by Aratech Repulsor Company. The Grasp, owned by the bounty hunter Teehl, was an Aratech SkySlayer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SkySlayer
- **Manufacturer:** Aratech Repulsor Company
- **Model:** SkySlayer
- **Type:** Light assault ship
- **Length:** 20 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 1
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Equipped
- **Armament:**
  - 2 quad laser cannons
  - 4 blaster cannon
- **Crew:** 7
- **Capacity:** 50 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 4 months
