# Republic battlecruiser

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Republic battlecruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_battlecruiser)

Ships referred to as Republic battlecruisers by Aleema Keto participated in the Republic fleet blockading Onderon during the Naddist Revolt in 3998 BBY. Another, more general term for them was Republic military ships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Republic battlecruiser
- **Type:** Battlecruiser
- **Hdsystem:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Armament:** Gun batteries (at least 7)
- **Crew:** Commander (1)
- **Role:** Battlecruiser
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
