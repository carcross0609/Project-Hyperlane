# Slasher

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Slasher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slasher)

The Slasher was a Sienar Fleet Systems GAT-12j Skipray Blastboat. It was one of two Blastboats used by Bazak's Gang.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Slasher
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Fleet Systems
- **Type:** GAT-12j Skipray Blastboat
- **Length:** 25 meters
- **Max Speed:** 1,200 kmh
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Shield Gen:** Equipped
- **Sensor:** Equipped
- **Navigation:** Equipped (limited to 4 jumps)
- **Armament:**
  - 1 Concussion missile launcher
  - 2 laser cannons
  - 3 medium ion cannons
  - 1 proton torpedo launcher
- **Crew:** 2; 2 gunners
- **Skeleton:** 1
- **Passengers:** 10
- **Capacity:** 20 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 1 months
- **Affiliation:** Bazak's Gang
- **Owners:** Lekk Bazak
