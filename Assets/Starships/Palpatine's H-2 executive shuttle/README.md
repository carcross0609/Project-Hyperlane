# Palpatine's H-2 executive shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Palpatine's H-2 executive shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palpatine%27s_H-2_executive_shuttle)

Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine owned an H-2 executive shuttle during the Clone Wars. He used the shuttle to escape when the Zilllo Beast attacked him. However, the beast grabbed the H-2 executive shuttle before it could fly to safety and forced Anakin Skywalker, one of the passengers, to cut it in half with his lightsaber in order to affect a rather risky escape plan. The remnants of the shuttle were then crushed by the beast in pursuit of Palpatine.
