# Gorgon

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gorgon (EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gorgon_%28EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate%29)

Gorgon was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate which served in the Imperial Navy during the Galactic Civil War. Gorgon was dispatched to prevent the defection of the CR90 corvette Bixby during a Rebel attack on an Imperial convoy near Dellalt system. However, Gorgon arrived too late to prevent the Rebels escaping with their new ship.
