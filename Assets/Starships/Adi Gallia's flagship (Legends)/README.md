# Adi Gallia's flagship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adi Gallia's flagship/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adi_Gallia%27s_flagship/Legends)

Adi Gallia's flagship was a Venator-class Star Destroyer commanded by Jedi Master Adi Gallia during the Clone Wars. It was destroyed in a battle near the planet Patitite Pattuna by General Grievous's forces, and Gallia was subsequently taken prisoner by the Confederate General.
