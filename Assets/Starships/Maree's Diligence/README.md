# Maree's Diligence

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maree's Diligence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maree%27s_Diligence)

The Maree's Diligence was a freighter owned by Garello Technologies during the High Republic Era, named after company head Larence Garello's mother. It was destroyed during a Nihil attack shortly after the Great Hyperspace Disaster, torn apart by several pirate harpoons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Maree's Diligence
- **Type:** Freighter
- **Passengers:** Thousands
- **Destroyed:** 232 BBY, Ab Dalis system
- **Affiliation:** Garello Technologies
