# Hevurion Grace

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hevurion Grace](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hevurion_Grace)

The Hevurion Grace was a Pinnacle-class luxury ship that belonged to New Republic senior Senator Erudo Ro-Kiintor, a Centrist delegate for Hevurion. During the Cold War, the Resistance leader General Leia Organa authorized a mission by Poe Dameron and his colleagues from Rapier Squadron to steal the Hevurion Grace in order to uncover Ro-Kiintor's collusion with the First Order. Dameron succeeded in boarding the luxury ship and forcing the Senator, his servant, and the pilot to evacuate in escape pods.
