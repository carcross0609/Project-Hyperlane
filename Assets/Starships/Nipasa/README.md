# Nipasa

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nipasa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nipasa)

The Nipasa was an Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle that was used during the Sepan Civil War by the Dimoks.

- **Name:** Nipasa
- **Manufacturer:** Cygnus Spaceworks
- **Model:** Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Length:** 30 meters
- **Width:** 32.3 meters
- **Height:** 32.4 meters
- **Armament:**

  - Taim & Bak H9 dual turbolaser (1; aft turret)
  - Projectile launchers (2)
- **Destroyed:** 3 ABY
- **Affiliation:** Dimok government
