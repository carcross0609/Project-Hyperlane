# A-vek Iiluunu

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A-vek Iiluunu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-vek_Iiluunu)

The A-vek Iiluunu was a type of Yuuzhan Vong capital ship designed to deploy a large force of the alien fightercraft known as coralskippers, and various referred to as a carrier analog, fighter-carrier, or skip carrier.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A-vek Iiluunu
- **Manufacturer:** Yuuzhan Vong
- **Model:** A-vek Iiluunu fighter carrier
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Length:** 800 meters
- **Engine:** Dovin basals
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 3
- **Shield Gen:** Dovin basals
- **Armament:**
  - Yaret-Kors (20)
  - Dovin basals
- **Complement:** 200 Coralskippers
- **Crew:** 320
- **Passengers:** 480
- **Capacity:** 280 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 6 months
- **Role:** Carrier
- **Affiliation:** Yuuzhan Vong
