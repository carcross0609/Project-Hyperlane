# Sicarus's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sicarus's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sicarus%27s_shuttle)

A shuttle was utilized by the Nihil Child of the Storm Sicarus during the High Republic Era. The hulking gray shuttle was small and squat, composed of a variety of dilapidated components and equipped with clusters of weapon turrets, including laser cannons. Its weapons were operated by a droid whose processor had been repurposed by Baron Boolan. Sicurus used the starship to travel with several Nameless creatures to the planet Oisin, where he kidnapped Jedi Master Eve Byre and held her aboard the shuttle. He later pursued former Master Cohmac Vitus and Jedi Knight Reath Silas to the planet Tolis, where the automated defenses shot down the transport Vessel when it approached the surface to rescue the pair. When the Republic Defense Coalition vessel Invictus, piloted by the mercenary Ashton Vol, arrived, the shuttle also fired upon it. Vol's droid, L-77, launched missiles at the shuttle, but the Nihil ship's shields resisted much of the damage. The explosion from the missiles' detonation struck the Invictus, causing it to crash. The Vessel captain, Affie Hollow, boarded Sicarus's shuttle and rescued Byre, and the Child of the Storm was killed shortly after by one of his own Nameless.
