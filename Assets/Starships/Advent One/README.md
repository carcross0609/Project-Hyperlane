# Advent One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Advent One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advent_One)

Advent One was the designation of a two-seat BTL Y-wing starfighter used by Alliance to Restore the Republic forces on Edan II. It was salvaged by Scruts' outlaws and turned over to an Alliance team who used it to attack the Imperial Collection Facility processing civilian prisoners.
