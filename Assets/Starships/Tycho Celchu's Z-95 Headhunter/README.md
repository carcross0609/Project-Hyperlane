# Tycho Celchu's Z-95 Headhunter

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tycho Celchu's Z-95 Headhunter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tycho_Celchu%27s_Z-95_Headhunter)

A sleek black-and-gold Z-95 Headhunter was one of six purchased by Flight Captain Tycho Celchu for the pilots of Rogue Squadron in the weeks proceeding the Liberation of Coruscant. Of the six, this particular Z-95 Headhunter was flown by Tycho as he considered it to be the best one he could buy and used it to cover members of Rogue Squadron during their initial guerrilla operations on Coruscant. The New Republic Captain later transferred ownership of the starfighter to his squadmate, Corran Horn, during a stratospheric skirmish above Coruscant’s cityscape; during which Corran used the callsign ‘Hunter Lead’ as he piloted the Z-95 Headhunter.
