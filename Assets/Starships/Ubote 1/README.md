# Ubote 1

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ubote 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ubote_1)

Ubote 1 was a CR90 corvette in the service of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It fought at the First Battle of Javin as part of the Ubote group, along with Ubote 2 and Ubote 3.
