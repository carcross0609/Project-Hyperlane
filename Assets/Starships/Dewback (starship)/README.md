# Dewback

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dewback (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dewback_%28starship%29)

The Dewback was a warship in the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances that participated in the Second Battle of Fondor. Kral Nevil, captain of the Anakin Solo wanted to bring the Dewback in to aid the Anakin Solo attack on Fondor.

- **Name:** Dewback
- **Type:** Warship
