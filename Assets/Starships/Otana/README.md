# Otana

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Otana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Otana)

The Otana was a YT-2000 Light Freighter owned by the Azzameen family. The Otana was originally owned and operated by Tomaas Azzameen until he and his son, Galin were killed by the Galactic Empire following the Battle of Hoth. Azzameen's other son, Ace took possession of the Otana and traveled to the Anoat Sector with his other siblings to seek refuge among the Alliance to Restore the Republic.
