# Jericho's Pride

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jericho's Pride](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jericho%27s_Pride)

Formerly known as Royal Flower, and sometimes referred to as Josephine's Pride, Jericho's Pride was a personalized Mindabaal Custom pleasure yacht owned by the smugglers Jericho Donovan and Josephine Donovan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jericho's Pride
- **Model:** Personalized Mindabaal custom Pleasure Yacht
- **Type:** Personal yacht
- **Length:** 30.1 meters
- **Armament:** 2 heavy turbolaser cannons
- **Crew:** Pilot, navigator
- **Passengers:** 4
- **Capacity:** 35 metric tons
- **Consumables:** 5 weeks
- **Role:**
  - Transport
  - Smuggling
- **Affiliation:**
  - James Ortell Donovan
  - Jericho Donovan
  - Josephine Donovan
