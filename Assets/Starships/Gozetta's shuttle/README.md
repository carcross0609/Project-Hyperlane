# Gozetta's shuttle

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gozetta's shuttle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gozetta%27s_shuttle)

The big game hunter Gozetta owned a sleek blunt-nosed shuttle. It was black and gold, with platinum bearings, had eight ion engines, and a rear hatch. Its design greatly resembled the Botajef SS-54 assault ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gozetta's shuttle
- **Type:** Shuttle
- **Cost:** Expansive
- **Engine:** Ion engines (8)
- **Hull:** Black and gold with platinum bearings
- **Role:** Personal transport
- **Destroyed:** C. 18 BBY
- **Owners:** Gozetta
