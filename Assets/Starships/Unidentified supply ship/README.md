# Unidentified supply ship

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified supply ship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_supply_ship)

A supply ship served within the fleet of the Tagge Corporation during the Imperial Era. In 3 ABY, the supply ship was attacked by the D'Armin Syndicate, which led to a drop for the company in the Hadi system. The following day, the attack on the ship was noted by Lapin Tagge&mdash;the secretary of the Tagge Corporation leader Lady Domina Tagge&mdash;when Domina asked what had accounted for the company's drop in the system. In response to the attack, Domina ordered the syndicate be cut off at the celestial body Feriae Junction and immobilized before they were pushed into the sun the next day.
