# HHG-42 Bulkstar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HHG-42 Bulkstar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HHG-42_Bulkstar)

The HHG-42 Bulkstar was a Chandrilan cargo ship used to travel to Chandrila's offworld colonies. Following the attack on Chandrila, Grand Admiral Rae Sloane used a Bulkstar to escape the planet. She was joined by the stowaway Brentin Lore Wexley, a former Rebel Alliance operative who had been installed with an inhibitor chip that made him an unwilling assassin. After convincing Wexley that their true enemy was Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax, the two used the Bulkstar to travel to Jakku.
