# Goshyn Queen

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Goshyn Queen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Goshyn_Queen)

The Goshyn Queen, a modified Kuat Drive Yards Space Master medium transport, was the flagship of the smuggler Billey. It was named for the Goshyn Detention Center, where Billey had served time prior to buying the ship. The ship was often in poor condition, as Billey was only willing to pay for the most essential repairs.
