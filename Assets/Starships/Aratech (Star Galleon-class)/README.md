# Aratech

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aratech (Star Galleon-class)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aratech_%28Star_Galleon-class%29)

Aratech was Star Galleon-class frigate of uncertain relation to the company. The ship was present at Calast, a Golan Space Colony 2 space station during the Rescue at Bundil II, where it bore a shipment of firearms.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aratech
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Model:** Star Galleon-class frigate
- **Length:** 300 meters
- **Role:** Cargo/Escort Frigate
