# Strato Driver

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Strato Driver](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strato_Driver)

The Strato Driver was a starship model in use during the early years of the Galactic Empire. One such vessel was stolen from the Fromm Gang by Jord Dusat, Thall Joben, Kea Moll and the droids C-3PO and R2-D2 as they escaped from Sise Fromm's stronghold on the planet Annoo in 15 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Affiliation:** Fromm Gang
