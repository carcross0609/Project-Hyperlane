# Transport One

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Transport One (Longbeam cruiser)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transport_One_%28Longbeam_cruiser%29)

Transport One was the designation of a Longbeam cruiser of the Galactic Republic's Republic Defense Coalition that assisted in the Republic and Jedi Order's raid on the Korvix Vorn, a prison ship of the Nihil marauders, intended to serve as a transport to rescue the prisoners. However, the Longbeam took significant damage from the Korvix Vorn topside blister bomb cannons, and Admiral Pevel Kronara ordered it to retreat. As a result, the smaller Republic and Jedi starfighters had to retrieve the prisoners individually.
