# Liberator-class starfighter

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Liberator-class starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liberator-class_starfighter)

The Liberator-class starfighter, known by the reconstituted Sith Empire as the Talon fighter, was a model of fighter used by the Galactic Republic prior to and during the Great Galactic War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Liberator-class starfighter
- **Type:** Starfighter
- **Armament:**
  - 2 Laser cannons
  - Concussion missile launcher (6 missiles)
  - 4 Concussion missiles (Striker variant)
- **Crew:**
  - 1 pilot
  - 1 Astromech droid (Striker variant)
- **Consumables:**
  - Field survival kit
  - Medpac
  - Blaster pistol
- **Lifesupport:** 150 hours
- **Role:**
  - Multi-use starfighter
  -   Escort
  -   Interceptor
  -   Air-to-ground attack craft
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Republic Navy
  - Eternal Alliance
  -   Eternal Alliance fleet
