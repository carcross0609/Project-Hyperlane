# Hunu

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hunu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hunu)

Hunu was an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate belonging to the Rebel Alliance. Hunu attempted to capture TIE Defenders during a transfer operation. The attempt was thwarted and the frigate was destroyed by Tan Maarek Stele.
