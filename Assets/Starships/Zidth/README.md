# Zidth

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zidth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zidth)

The Zidth was a starship operated by an intergalactic slaving ring. Posing as a Mrissi science ship, the Zidth landed in the Adsila Rifts to conduct geological studies. However, their true purpose was to enslave members of the Asilyr tribe as their hypersensitivity to ground tremors made them ideal slaves to work in mining operations.
