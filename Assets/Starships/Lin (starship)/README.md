# Lin (starship)

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lin (starship)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lin_%28starship%29)

Lin was a modified Corellian YT-1300 transport that was used by criminals that were hiding from the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. The transport was the first of three transports to try to leave a XQ3 platform that served as a salvage yard but was also responsible for being a hiding point for fugitives, including a Rebel spy.
