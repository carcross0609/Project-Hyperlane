# T-SE

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-SE](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-SE)

T-SE was a group of three Utility Tugs used by the Rebels. The tugs were to deliver pilots to shuttles Revt to defend a supply depot conisting of Y-wings, shuttles, two stormtrooper transports Bultz and three containers Duce from Imperial TIE Interceptors and Bombers. The tugs were all destroyed in the battle. The battle was then recreated and adapted in the Combat chamber to train future pilots such as Maarek Stele.
