# Perilous

**Kind:** Individual ship  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Perilous](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Perilous)

The Perilous was an Imperial-class Star Destroyer serving as the flagship of Darth Vader, up until the skirmish with the Free Ryloth Movement in which it was destroyed.

- **Name:** Perilous
- **Line:** Imperial-class Star Destroyer

- **Type:** Star Destroyer

- **Engine:** Equipped

- **Complement:**

  - At least one shuttle
- **Destroyed:** 14 BBY, above Ryloth
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
- **Navy:** Imperial Navy
- **Owners:** Darth Vader
- **Captains:** Luitt
