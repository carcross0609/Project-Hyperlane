# Dirconite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dirconite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dirconite)

Dirconites were a sentient species of rotund humanoids with light skin and small, sharpened teeth that had the ability to produce hypersonic shrieks capable of stunning other humanoids. During the early days of the Galactic Empire, a male Dirconite crime lord known as Kleb Zellock controlled Tyne's Horky's criminal underworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dirconite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Capable of producing hypersonic shrieks, rotund, sharpened teeth
