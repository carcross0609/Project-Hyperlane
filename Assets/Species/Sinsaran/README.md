# Sinsaran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sinsaran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sinsaran)

The Sinsarans were a sentient species that was found on the planet Sinsara. Corlis Rath and Gara were members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sinsaran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Purple
  - Blue
  - Light
- **Eyecolor:** Black (Yellow or white pupils)
- **Origin:** Sinsara
