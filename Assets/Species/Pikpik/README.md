# Pikpik

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pikpik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pikpik)

Pikpik were a diminutive, sentient rodent species. Some Pikpiks worked as scrappers on the planet Bracca during the High Republic Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pikpik
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Fair
- **Haircolor:** Shades of gray and brown
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
