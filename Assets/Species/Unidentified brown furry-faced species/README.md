# Unidentified brown furry-faced species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified brown furry-faced species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_brown_furry-faced_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their long brown fur that covered almost all of their faces aside from their eyes, as well as sometimes a white horn extending from the top of their heads. In 4 ABY, at least one member of this species could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren, and another worked for the crime lord Allic at a casino on the planet Qiaxx.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified brown furry-faced species
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**

  - Horns
