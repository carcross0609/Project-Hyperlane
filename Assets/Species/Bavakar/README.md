# Bavakar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bavakar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bavakar)

The Bavakar were a sentient species, members of which specialized in biological enhancements and related technologies. The species shared its name with the Bavakar Medical Research Labs company that was in business by 3956 BBY.
