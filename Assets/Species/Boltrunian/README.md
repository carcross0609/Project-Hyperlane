# Boltrunian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Boltrunian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boltrunian)

Boltrunians were a sentient species which existed in the galaxy. Some Boltrunians were present in the medcenter of the Starlight Beacon space station when Administrator Velko Jahen arrived and was escorted through the facility by Okana. They originated from the Boltrunia system in the Outer Rim Territories.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Boltrunian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Boltrunia system
