# Wrean

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wrean](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrean)

Wreans were a sentient species native to the planet of Wrea. They were aquatic lifeforms who dwelled deep in the oceans of their homeworld.
