# Myykian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Myykian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Myykian)

Myykians were a species of elephantine humanoids that lived in the galaxy. The species possessed blue skin and was elephantine in appearance, including a long trunk-like nose and two eyes. They could also possess a head of hair. The youngling Neel and his family were members of the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Myykian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Elephantine
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Haircolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Black
