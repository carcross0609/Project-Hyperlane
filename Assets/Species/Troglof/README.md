# Troglof

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Troglof](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Troglof)

The Troglof were a rare sentient species with tentacle-like arms native to Troglofa. A diminutive species, the Troglof had a pleasant natural odor. Troglofs could also move their eyestalks independently. Thamm was a Troglof who worked as a croupier in the Canto Casino in the city of Canto Bight. Another Troglof worked as a spa attendant and waiter at the Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa on Lanupa.
