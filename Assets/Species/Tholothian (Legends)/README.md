# Tholothian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tholothian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tholothian/Legends)

Tholothians were the native species of Tholoth. Adi Gallia and Stass Allie, both members of the Jedi Order, were Tholothians, although Gallia's parents, both diplomats, were born on Corellia. Katooni, a Jedi youngling was also a Tholothian.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tholothian
- **Skincolor:** Dark
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Blue
  - Indigo
- **Origin:** Tholoth
