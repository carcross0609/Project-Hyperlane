# Prae'tus

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Prae'tus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prae%27tus)

Prae'tus was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. Volo Bolus was a member of this species. They were commonly mistaken for the similar-looking Nikto Ijosap'sai species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Prae'tus
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue and tan
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four arms
  - Scales
