# Celegian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Celegian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celegian/Legends)

Celegians were a nonhumanoid species from Celegia, a planet with raging seas located in the Expansion Region. Celegians were relatively isolationist as a species, but had good relations with the Galactic Republic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Celegian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Invertebrates
- **Height:** 1.7 - 2.2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Reds and pinks
- **Lifespan:**
  - Child: 1-10 years
  - Young adult: 11-20 years
  - Adult: 21-142 years
  - Middle age: 143-210 years
  - Old: 211-250 years
  - Venerable: 251+ years
- **Origin:** Celegia
- **Language:** Telepathy
