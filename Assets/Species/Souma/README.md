# Souma

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Souma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Souma)

The Souma were a species of sentient felines hailing from the planet Soum. Their whole bodies were covered with a thick coat of fur, and they had gentle, frank-looking eyes. The Souma species had a 1-to-5 male-to-female ratio. Compared to females, the male Souma were considered intellectually challenged. As such, they were usually relegated to menial tasks.

## Biology & Culture
