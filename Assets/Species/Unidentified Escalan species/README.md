# Unidentified Escalan species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Escalan species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Escalan_species)

A sentient species lived on the primitive Outer Rim Territories planet Escalan. The species did not speak basic. Between around 8 BBY and 2 BBY, the smuggler Han Solo was dumped onto the world after he had been shot by the bounty hunter Greedo. Two younger members of the species then took him to Doctor Aber, who did her best to save his life despite not having the full knowledge she needed of human anatomy. A week later, Solo awoke and Aber called for Elder Sy'Son to visit her. Sy'Son arrived with another individual, and the doctor led the two to Solo's body, and informed them he had spoken what she considered strange words, his name Han Solo.
