# Kassk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kassk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kassk)

The Kassk were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy around 34 ABY. Two Kassks were the members of the Warbird gang led by Kragan Gorr.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kassk
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Olive
- **Eyecolor:** Black
