# Karf

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Karf](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karf)

Karfs were delicate, arboreal creatures that lived in the tisvollt forests of Barlok. They were a minority of the planet's Brolf inhabitants, and were also smaller.
