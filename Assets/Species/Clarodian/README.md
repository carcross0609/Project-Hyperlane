# Clarodian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clarodian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clarodian)

The Clarodians were a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. Brothers Tianris and Guanlos Mez were members of this species.

- **Name:** Clarodian
- **Designation:** Sentient

- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
