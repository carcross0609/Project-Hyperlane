# Xendekian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Xendekian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xendekian)

The Xendekians were a sentient species native to the planet Xendek. The Xendekians believed the argora, a bird species, brought rain and crops and was responsible for all good fortune on Xendek.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Xendekian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Blue
  - Brown
  - Green
  - Reddish-brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Origin:** Xendek
