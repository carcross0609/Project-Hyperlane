# Rekk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rekk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rekk)

The Rekk were a sentient species and the native inhabitants of the planet Rekkana. They were known for their expertise in creating automatic computer-controlled systems. When encountering Imperial TIE fighters above Rekkana shortly after the Battle of Endor, Lando Calrissian guessed that the fighters had been modified by the Rekk.
