# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Genya)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Genya%29)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were diminutive humanoids with long arms that nearly reached their feet, as well as wide mouths, upturned noses, and long, curved, fleshy points extending from the sides of their heads. At least two members of this species were present on the planet Genya, the older member having joined an insurgent group in an attempt to rescue a younger member of their species from the Zonus Corporation, which they eventually succeeded at with help from the bounty hunters Sevn and IV-A4.
