# Agee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Agee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agee)

The Agee were a tiny, winged, sentient species that came in two varieties: those capable of flight and those not. In 17 ABY, several Agee resided on Coruscant, the galactic capital. One such Agee was almost trapped in the Crystal Jewel casino when it started to collapse following the Bombing of the Senate Hall. Another Agee went to visit Mon Mothma following her ascension to Chief of State of the New Republic after incumbent Leia Organa Solo resigned to rescue her brother, Luke Skywalker; however, the Agee left before she could meet with Mothma.
