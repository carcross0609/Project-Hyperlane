# Gozzo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gozzo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gozzo)

The Gozzo were a sentient species that were native to the planet Drahgor III which was located in the Outer Rim of the galaxy. Han Solo negotiated with a group of Gozzo living on Troiken during the Galactic Civil War. Flix, Flanx, and Floog were members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gozzo
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Feathers:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Origin:** Drahgor III
- **Habitat:** Forest
- **Language:**

  - Galactic Basic Standard
