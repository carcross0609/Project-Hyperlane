# Cliffborer worm

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cliffborer worm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cliffborer_worm)

Cliffborer worms were parasitic, almost mindless, burrowing arthropods common throughout the galaxy. They flourished specially on the many planets that featured mainly arid and rocky climates, such as Tatooine.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cliffborer worm
- **Designation:**
  - Non-sentient
  - Sentient (intelligent descendants)
- **Class:** Arthropod
- **Length:** 3 to 4 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown (intelligent descendants)
- **Eyecolor:** Brown (intelligent descendants)
- **Distinctions:** Large flaps of skin on either side of head (intelligent descendants)
- **Origin:** Tatooine
- **Diet:** Razor moss
