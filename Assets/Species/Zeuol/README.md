# Zeuol

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zeuol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeuol)

The Zeuols were a species of sentient, pale-faced beings recognizable for their large, solid-black eyes and coarse, off-white hair. At least one member of the species ventured into the galaxy at large, and at the end of the Clone Wars, the Mid Rim world of Ord Zeuol was homeworld to a female Zeuol named Eeusu Estornii who worked as a senator for the Galactic Republic. Estornii attended the funeral of Padmé Amidala, senator and former queen of the planet Naboo, in 19 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Zeuol
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale white with pink accents
- **Haircolor:** Off-white
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Large eyes
