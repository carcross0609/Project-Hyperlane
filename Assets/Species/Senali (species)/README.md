# Senali

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Senali (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senali_%28species%29)

Senali were the native species of the Rutan moon of Senali. They were an offshoot of Rutanians from Rutan, and the rivalry between the two species was provoked by the Yuuzhan Vong into war, leaving both Rutan and Senali weak and vulnerable to invasion.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Senali
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Near-Rutanian
- **Origin:** Senali
