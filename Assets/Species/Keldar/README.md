# Keldar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Keldar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keldar)

The Keldar were a species of the galaxy. They settled on the Outer Rim Territories planet of Nag Ubdur after being transplanted along with Artiodac refugees and the Ubdurian natives.
