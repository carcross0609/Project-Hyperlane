# Andraven

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Andraven (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andraven_%28species%29)

Andravens were a sentient elephantine species who lived on the frigid planet Andraven. They had gray or light blue colored skin. Gumar, Jam, Varna, and Onderrs were members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Andraven
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Elephantine
- **Skincolor:**
  - Gray
  - Light blue
- **Haircolor:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Short trunk
- **Origin:** Andraven
