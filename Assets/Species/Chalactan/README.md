# Chalactan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chalactan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chalactan)

Chalactans were humans native to the planet Chalacta. Two prominent Chalactans were Jedi Master Depa Billaba and her sister Sar Labooda.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Chalactan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Human
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Haircolor:**

  - Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Origin:** Chalacta
