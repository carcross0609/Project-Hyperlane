# Shistavanen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shistavanen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shistavanen/Legends)

The Shistavanen, colloquially nicknamed wolfmen for their lupine and wolf-like appearance, were a sentient species from the planet Uvena Prime.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Shistavanen
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Lupine mammalian
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Mass:** 60 kilograms
- **Skincolor:** Gray to brown
- **Haircolor:** Shades of brown
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow, amber, green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Lupine appearance
  - Enhanced senses
  - Increased speed
  - Increased strength
- **Lifespan:** 96 years
- **Origin:** Uvena Prime
- **Language:**
  - Shistavanen (native)
  - Basic
