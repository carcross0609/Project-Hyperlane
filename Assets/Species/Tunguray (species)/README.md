# Tunguray

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tunguray (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tunguray_%28species%29)

Tunguray were a sentient species with vulpine features, purple fur, yellow eyes, and two leaflike antennae. The entire species was evacuated from the volcanic planet Tunguray, near Sullust, to Coruscant by a Galactic Republic Pathfinder team. Cherro was a member of this species, and she was imprisoned with Axel Greylark.
