# Rok's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rok's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rok%27s_species)

A green-skinned species lived in The galaxy. A group of the species, who served as woodsmen, landed on Endor to harvest the moon's trees. They also enslaved a number of Ewoks and foonars in order to further their efforts, including Dooni's mother.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
