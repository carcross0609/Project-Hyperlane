# Unidentified elephantine species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified elephantine species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_elephantine_species)

A species of sentient elephantine existed during the High Republic Era. The species was seen on Batuu, Tenoo, Starlight Beacon, and Yarrum.
