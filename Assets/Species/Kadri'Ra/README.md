# Kadri'Ra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kadri'Ra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kadri%27Ra)

The Kadri'Ra were a large sentient species believed to be related to the Duinuogwuin. The planet of Arapia was their adopted homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kadri'Ra
- **Length:** 5–200 meters
- **Distinctions:** Dragon-like
- **Lifespan:** 1000–1500 years
- **Origin:** Arapia (adopted) ; (original homeworld unknown)
- **Language:** Kadri'Ra
