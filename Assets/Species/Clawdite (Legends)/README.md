# Clawdite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Clawdite/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clawdite/Legends)

Clawdites were a humanoid sentient species native to the planet of Zolan. They were one of the few shape-shifting species in the galaxy, and were sometimes referred to as Changelings.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Clawdite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Changeling
- **Height:** 1.6 - 1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Green to yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Lifespan:** Up to 70 standard years
- **Origin:** Zolan
- **Language:** Clawdite
