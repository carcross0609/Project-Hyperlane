# Vernol

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vernol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vernol)

The Vernols were a race of sentient bipeds. They had a large colony on Garnib.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vernol
- **Height:** 1.5 meters
- **Skincolor:** Blue and orange
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Squat, frog-like bodies
- **Origin:** Garnib (adopted)
