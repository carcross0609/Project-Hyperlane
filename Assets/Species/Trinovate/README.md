# Trinovate

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trinovate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trinovate)

The Trinovates were a sentient species with three eyes. They were native to Trinovat, a planet in the Outer Rim Territories' Gordian Reach sector, located on the Junction-Tierell Loop. Their homeworld was verdant, with numerous farms and mines. In 2 ABY, the Trinovate Pilliask was present on the planet Toprawa, playing the card game Seven card comet against the Human Havet Storm. The Trinovate was almost out of credits and was preparing to call for the next round to be a "Tail game"—wherein credits from the "Comet tail" or pot were added to the credits available to win that round—when another player beat him to the call.
