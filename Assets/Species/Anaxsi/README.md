# Anaxsi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Anaxsi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anaxsi)

The Anaxsi were the Human residents of Anaxes. They were fervently loyal to the Galactic Republic and later the Galactic Empire, and the planet was one large Imperial Navy training and staging ground.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Anaxsi
- **Origin:** Anaxes
