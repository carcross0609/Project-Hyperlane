# Unidentified curved-beak species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified curved-beak species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_curved-beak_species)

This species was a sentient species distinguished by their pink skin and long, downward-curved beaks. At least one member of this species was present on the planet Orvax IV in 19 BBY.
