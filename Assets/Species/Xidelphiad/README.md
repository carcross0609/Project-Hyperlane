# Xidelphiad

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Xidelphiad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xidelphiad)

The Xidelphiads were a sentient species in the galaxy. Individuals within the species would change sex at several points in their life, and would do so by encasing themselves in a chrysalis. One famous Xidelphiad was the fine artist Sannab Ro, who notably created a propaganda poster for the Commission for a Safe and Secure Republic.
