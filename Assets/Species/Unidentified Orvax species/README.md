# Unidentified Orvax species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Orvax species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Orvax_species)

A sentient species resided on their homeworld, the Outer Rim planet Orvax, by 382 BBY. Around that year, a Republic Pathfinder team led by the Jedi Knight Gella Nattai undertook an expedition to make contact with the species. However, following three days of failed attempts to establish communication, the equipment of Nattai's team began to disappear. Ultimately, the team left Orvax unsuccessful and reported back to the Jedi Council.
