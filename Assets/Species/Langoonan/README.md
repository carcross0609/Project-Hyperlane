# Langoonan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Langoonan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Langoonan)

The Langoonans were a sentient species native to the planet Langoona, located within Hutt Space. The species was enslaved by the Hutts, who favored them until the Langoonans unsuccessfully rebelled. This resulted in the Langoonans being stripped of all technology and almost being driven to extinction, with only small numbers kept alive as prey for Hutt safaris.

- **Designation:** Sentient
