# Krikthasi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krikthasi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krikthasi)

The Krikthasi were large sentient marine mollusks indigenous to Baralou.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Krikthasi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mollusk
- **Length:** 2.5 meters
- **Skincolor:** Black to brown
- **Distinctions:** Color changing chromanin, eight tentacles, water propulsion
- **Origin:** Baralou
- **Diet:** Fish
