# Froffli

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Froffli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Froffli)

The Froffli species were a New Republic member race easily recognized by their prominent hair-spurs. While many Humans found their appearance ridiculous, others (such as Talon Karrde's associate Chin) arranged their own hair in a similar fashion.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Froffli
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Prominent hair-spurs
- **Origin:** Froffl system
