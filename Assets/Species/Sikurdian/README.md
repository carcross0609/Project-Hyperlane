# Sikurdian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sikurdian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sikurdian)

Sikurdians were a sentient species from the Sikurd system. During the Clone Wars, Sikurdian privateers supported the Corporate Alliance, which had pledged its resources to the Confederacy of Independent Systems, in defending its headquarters on the planet Murkhana. During the Imperial Era, Sikurdian pirates were active in the Lower Flora Cloud, and Luke Skywalker claimed to have been attacked by them while bluffing his way through a stormtrooper patrol at a refueling station over Devaron.
