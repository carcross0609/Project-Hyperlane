# Keshian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Keshian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keshian)

Keshians were a sentient species nearly identical to humans from the planet Keshi. The only visible distinction between the two were the Keshians' eyes, which were large and oddly colored, allowing them to detect a broad spectrum ranging from ultraviolet to infrared. Keshian Iolo Arana, a New Republic pilot, used this to his advantage in dogfights.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Keshian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Fair
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:** Large eyes capable of detecting a broad spectrum of radiation
- **Origin:** Keshian
