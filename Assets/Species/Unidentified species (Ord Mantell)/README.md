# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Ord Mantell)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Ord_Mantell%29)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The humanoid species was distinguished by their green skin. By 3 ABY, several members of this species worked as armed enforcers within the Scraplands of the planet Ord Mantell, where they eventually encountered the Human smuggler Dash Rendar during his attempt to locate the droid IG-88.
