# Trome

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trome](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trome)

Tromes were small humanoid beings found on the Forest Moon of Endor. They were characterized by their small size, high voices, and large beards. Despite their small size, their favorite sport was wrestling; they had wrestling tournaments annually.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Trome
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Haircolor:** Grey
- **Distinctions:** Small size, long beards
- **Origin:** Endor
