# Euceron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Euceron (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Euceron_%28species%29)

The Eucerons were a species native to the planet Euceron. They were tall humanoids with large, domed heads and delicate limbs.
