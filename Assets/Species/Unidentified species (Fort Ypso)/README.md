# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Fort Ypso)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Fort_Ypso%29)

This species was a sentient species. The humanoid species was distinguished by their large, hairless craniums, with solid dark eyes, pronounced brows, overlapping, folded cheeks, ribbed chins, small mouths, and a lack of visible ears or nostrils. In 10 BBY, a member of this species gambled at Fort Ypso on the planet Vandor.
