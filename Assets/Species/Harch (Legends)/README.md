# Harch

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harch/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harch/Legends)

The Harch were a sentient arachnid species native to the Mid Rim planet of Secundus Ando.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Harch
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Spider-like with six eyes, eight limbs and chelicerae
- **Origin:** Secundus Ando
