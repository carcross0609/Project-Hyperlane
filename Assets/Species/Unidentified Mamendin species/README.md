# Unidentified Mamendin species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Mamendin species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Mamendin_species)

The planet Mamendin was the homeworld of a sentient species of humanoids. In 25 BBY, Obi-Wan Kenobi, Siri Tachi, Anakin Skywalker and Ferus Olin impersonated the Slams, a criminal gang composed of members of this species.
