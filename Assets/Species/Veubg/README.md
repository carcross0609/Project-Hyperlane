# Veubg

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Veubg](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veubg)

Veubgri (Veubg for singular form) were an insectoid species native to the high-gravity world of Gbu in the Outer Rim. They were tall six-legged beings, with tendrils for arms.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Veubg
- **Distinctions:** Six-legged insectoids
- **Origin:** Gbu
