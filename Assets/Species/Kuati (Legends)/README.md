# Kuati

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kuati/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuati/Legends)

A Kuati was a Human from the planet of Kuat, in the Core Worlds. Their society was in some aspects matriarchal and dominated by powerful ruling families that constantly fought among themselves and employed telbuns to prevent inbreeding. Kuati nobles referred to themselves in the royal we.
