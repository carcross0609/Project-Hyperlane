# Barundi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Barundi (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barundi_%28species%29)

The Barundi were a humanoid species native to the planet Barundi and encountered on an unnamed planet in Wild Space. A primitive society, the Barundi fashioned their lives around the promotion of perpetual happiness. The Barundi found in Wild Space worshiped the Yavin Vassilika as a god.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Barundi
- **Races:** Thelvin Order
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Skincolor:** Blue-gray
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Sloped foreheads, broad mouths
- **Origin:** Barundi
- **Language:** Dialect of The Thelvin Order of Barundi
