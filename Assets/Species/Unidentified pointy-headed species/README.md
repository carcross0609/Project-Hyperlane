# Unidentified pointy-headed species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified pointy-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pointy-headed_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their red skin with large, pointed heads. In 5 BBY, one member of this species was present at the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport on the planet Coruscant.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified pointy-headed species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Pointed head
