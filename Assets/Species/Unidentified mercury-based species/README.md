# Unidentified mercury-based species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified mercury-based species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_mercury-based_species)

A sentient species of beings mostly made of mercury and capable of shapeshifting was native to the Outer Rim planet Sevetta, which was mercury-rich and had a toxic atmosphere. The rogue clone trooper Hunter was aware of the species, and during or after 18 BBY, while on the mercury-rich planet Tryth, he told fellow clone Omega about them, which amazed her.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified mercury-based species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Mercury-based
  - Capable of shapeshifting
- **Origin:** Sevetta
