# Lorrdian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lorrdian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lorrdian/Legends)

The Lorrdians, a Human culture from the planet Lorrd in the Kanz sector, were genetically identical to baseline Humans. However, because their unique culture and history set them apart from other Humans, they were sometimes classified as Near-Humans.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lorrdian
- **Height:** 1.4–2.0 meters
- **Skincolor:** Medium to light brown
- **Distinctions:** Skilled mimics
- **Origin:** Lorrd
- **Language:**
  - Galactic Basic Standard
  - Kinetic communication
