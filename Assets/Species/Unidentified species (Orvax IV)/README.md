# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Orvax IV)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Orvax_IV%29)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their tan skin, round eyes, and elongated limbs. One member of this species was present on the planet Orvax IV in 19 BBY.
