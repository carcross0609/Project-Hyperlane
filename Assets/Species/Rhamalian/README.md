# Rhamalian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rhamalian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rhamalian)

The Rhamalians were the native Human race who inhabited the planet Rhamalai. The original colonists who settled Rhamalai were Cherishites, who abstained from using modern technology to live a more simpler and rural existence. Over four hundred years, the planet's population grew from five hundred to eleven million, and the populace held true to their beliefs, mandating that any outsiders who wished to settle on the planet had to destroy their technology and starships.
