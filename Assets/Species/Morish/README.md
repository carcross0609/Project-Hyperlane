# Morish

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Morish](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morish)

The Morish were a short, furry species from the planet Morishim in the Outer Rim. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, a secret communications center was established in a cave on Morishim; after the Empire pulled out, a Morish known as the Old Recluse found the abandoned communications link, and used its newsfeeds to fool others into believing he had clairvoyant abilities. Other Morish, meanwhile, plied their wares at a local marketplace.
