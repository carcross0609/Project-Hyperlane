# Ahak Maharr

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ahak Maharr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahak_Maharr)

The Ahak Maharr were a tusked sentient species. They spoke a guttural, deep-pitched language. They had a traditional toast that, once translated into Galactic Basic Standard, meant "May we rend the flesh of our enemies, and drink their blood as we do this liquid before us."
