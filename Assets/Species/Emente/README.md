# Emente

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Emente](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emente)

Ementes were a sentient species with six eyes. In 10 BBY, an Emente managed speeder rentals at a busy spaceport on the outskirts of Witzer, the second largest urban area on the planet Sern Prime. When the criminals Jaylen Vrax and ND-5 arrived at the spaceport, she approved their rental, although four of her eyes lingered on ND-5 longer then Vrax. In response, ND-5 informed her that some visible damage to his chestplate was not a risk to anyone, and she offered a weak smile in return. After she explained the terms of the rental contract to Vrax, she carried out a retinal scan on him with a datapad and nodded when it was complete.
