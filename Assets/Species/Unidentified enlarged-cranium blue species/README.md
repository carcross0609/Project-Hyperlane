# Unidentified enlarged-cranium blue species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified enlarged-cranium blue species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_enlarged-cranium_blue_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The bipedal humanoid sentient species was distinguished by their enlarged craniums, blue skin, and white markings on their faces, as well as a lack of visible ears. In 9 ABY, a member of this species was present at Gor Koresh's Honky Tonk, fleeing when Koresh attempted to take Din Djarin's Mandalorian armor.
