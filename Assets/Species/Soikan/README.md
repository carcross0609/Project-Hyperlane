# Soikan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Soikan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soikan)

Soikans were a sentient species from the planet Soika. Tall in build and having pointed ears, they were divided into at least two groups, Gagics and Livtaks, both of which had distinctive colorations. White hair, silver skin and lilac eyes were typical for the Livtak. Velko Jahen, a member of the species, served aboard the Galactic Republic space station Starlight Beacon during the High Republic Era.
