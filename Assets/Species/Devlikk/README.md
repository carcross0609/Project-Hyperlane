# Devlikk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Devlikk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devlikk)

Devlikk was a sentient species. Wan Sandage, a podracer from Ord Radama, who participated in the Boonta Eve Classic race held on the planet Tatooine during 32 BBY was a Devlikk.
