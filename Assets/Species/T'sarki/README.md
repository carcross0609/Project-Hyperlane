# T'sarki

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T'sarki](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T%27sarki)

The T'sarki were a seal-like species native to Kalishik in the Gordian Reach sector. They were generally regarded as a friendly species, but were quick to take offense at what they perceived to be routine dishonesty and swindling at the hands of other species. They played a part in the industry of the Back Reach; they regularly sought work permits for the factories of Feldwes, and the mining venture on the planet Usta was a joint venture between the T'sarki and the inhabitants of the Arda system. The gambler Groomert, who played in a game of seven card comet with the Trinovate Pilliask and the Humans Havet Storm and Vain, was a T'sarki.
