# Unidentified fin-headed beaked species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified fin-headed beaked species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_fin-headed_beaked_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished their wide, beak-like mouths and fin-like crest that fanned from the sides of their chins to the top of their head where it grew thickest. Some time prior to 22 BBY, at least one member of this species was a member of the Jedi Order, their busts displayed within the Temple Archives.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified fin-headed beaked species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Head crest and fins
  - Beak-like mouths
