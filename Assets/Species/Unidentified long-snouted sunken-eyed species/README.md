# Unidentified long-snouted sunken-eyed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified long-snouted sunken-eyed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_long-snouted_sunken-eyed_species)

This species was a sentient species distinguished by their gray skin, with elongated probiscis-like snouts, as well as sunken eyes and pronounced brows. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species were present in a casino on the planet Qiaxx.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified long-snouted sunken-eyed species
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Probiscis-like snout
