# Plattahr's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Plattahr's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plattahr%27s_species)

An alien race with huge, muscular bodies and heads that resembled the tips of stalks on their necks. No eyes or noses were visible on their heads, and a small opening revealed their mouths. Plattahr was a member of the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Plattahr's species
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Distinctions:**
  - Large bodies
  - Stalk-like heads
  - Eyeless
- **Language:** Plattahr's species language
