# Nixor (species)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nixor (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nixor_%28species%29)

The Nixors were a race from the planet Nixor in the Nixor system. They were in a longstanding feud with the rest of the system during the early years of the Galactic Empire. The Nixors operated the decrepit Nixor Spaceport.
