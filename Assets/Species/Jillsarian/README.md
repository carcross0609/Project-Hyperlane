# Jillsarian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jillsarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jillsarian)

Jillsarians were a sentient species with four arms that hailed from the Jillsaria system in the Outer Rim Territories. Doctor Quadpaw, who otherwise kept his name a secret, was a male Jillsarian who lived during the Imperial Era and found work as a for-hire physician. Beyond his four dexterous arms, Quadpaw made use of cybernetic-enhancements, which enhanced his eyes and ears; together, his enhancements and Jillsarian biology allowed him to carry out precise, if expensive, work as a surgeon on the Outer Rim moon Frezno in an unassuming tent.
