# Hysalrian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hysalrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hysalrian)

Hysalrians were a sentient species from the Hysalria system. N'Kata Del Gormo, rumored to have been an early instructor of future Grand Master Yoda, was said to have been a Hysalrian. In 32 BBY, when Jedi Master Mace Windu recognized a sand sculpture in Yoda's quarters as depicting Hysalria, he thought of the rumor of the Hysalrian teaching Yoda, but the Grand Master dismissed the sculpture and used the Force to form it into an Alderaanian rose.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hysalrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Hysalria system
