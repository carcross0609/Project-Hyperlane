# Hasikian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hasikian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hasikian)

The Hasikian were a humanoid species from the Hasiki system. They were known to have prominent scales over their heads. A notable Hasikian was Jedi Padawan T'Seely, who escorted Jedi Master Yoda to the Jedi Temple on Coruscant.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hasikian
- **Distinctions:** Prominent scales over their heads
- **Origin:** Hasiki system
