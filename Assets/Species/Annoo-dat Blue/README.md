# Annoo-dat Blue

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Annoo-dat Blue](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Annoo-dat_Blue)

The Ret were sapient reptilians, indigenous to Gelefil. They had heavy-lidded eyes and scales which changed colors with their emotions. They were also long-lived with the average lifespan of hundreds of years.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Annoo-dat Blue
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** Scales change color with emotions
- **Lifespan:** Venerable at 900 Years
- **Origin:** Gelefil
- **Language:** Dat-an; previously: Tra'ang'a
