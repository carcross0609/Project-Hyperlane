# Crulkon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Crulkon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crulkon)

The Crulkon were a sentient species with a presence on the Outer Rim planet Turrak. During the High Republic Era, there were tensions between the Crulkon and the native Scalvi, the latter of whom contacted the Jedi Grand Master Yoda for assistance.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
