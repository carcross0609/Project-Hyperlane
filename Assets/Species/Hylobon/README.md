# Hylobon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hylobon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hylobon)

Hylobons were a sentient species native to the Hylobi system. Aemon Gremm and his men, including Toht Ra, were all members of this species. They were part of the Hylobon Enforcers, who served Dryden Vos as bodyguards in the criminal organization Crimson Dawn.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hylobon
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Grey
- **Haircolor:** Orange
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Brow ridges
  - Flat faces
- **Origin:** Hylobi system
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
