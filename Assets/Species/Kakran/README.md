# Kakran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kakran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kakran)

Kakrans were a species of rock people who lived on the planet Kakra, a sentient world that was a giant Kakran itself.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kakran
- **Distinctions:** Body made of rock
- **Origin:** Kakra
