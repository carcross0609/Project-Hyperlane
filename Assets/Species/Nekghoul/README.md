# Nekghoul

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nekghoul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nekghoul)

Nekghouls were a more evolved and intelligent strain of rakghouls that had evolved in the ruins of Taris after driving the Outcasts to extinction by the time of the Cold War, three centuries following the planet's destruction in 3956 BBY, where for a while they were the planet's only inhabitants. They were larger and much smarter than rakghouls, which suggested that they may have qualified as their own separate species, and they also stood upright, compared to the standard hunched stance of rakghouls. Nekghouls were also unable to infect or transform their victims.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nekghoul
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Rakghoul
- **Skincolor:** Grey

- **Habitat:** City ruins
