# Crast

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crast](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crast)

Crast, also called food-kin, were a race of crustaceans from Pria. They had a symbiotic relationship with the Priapulins. Crast eggs began to be incubated after being digested by a Priapulin. The Crast were willing to act as Priapulin food, and in return Priapulins cared for their children.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Crast
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Pria
