# Jostran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jostran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jostran)

Jostrans were a sentient species native to the planet Munlali Mafir. They were a type of arthropod that could grow up to as large as a Human arm, and resembled slow-moving centipedes. Their civilization was wiped out when the living planet Zonama Sekot passed through their star system and caused prolonged periods of seismic and volcanic activity that was almost responsible for their extinction.
