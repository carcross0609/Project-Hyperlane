# Lasat

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lasat/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lasat/Legends)

The Lasat were a sentient species indigenous to the arid Wild Space planet Lasan.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lasat
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammals
- **Height:** 1.2–1.9 meters
- **Haircolor:** Light brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Heat-dissipating ears
  - Large eyes for twilight vision
- **Origin:** Lasan
- **Diet:** Carnivorous
