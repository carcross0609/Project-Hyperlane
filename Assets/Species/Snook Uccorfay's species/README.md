# Snook Uccorfay's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Snook Uccorfay's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snook_Uccorfay%27s_species)

A sentient species of mole-like humanoids could be found on the planets Cantonica and Daiyu. Snook Uccorfay was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Snook Uccorfay's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Mole-like heads, radial flanges around snout
