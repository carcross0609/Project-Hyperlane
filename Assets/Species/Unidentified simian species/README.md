# Unidentified simian species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified simian species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_simian_species)

Three members of an unidentified simian species were among the prisoners of Stars' End, freed during the Liberation of Stars' End in 2 BBY. This species had long arms and great strength, and were able to swing and climb with skill. One of the simians was killed by Security Police during their escape. The other two swarmed the policeman, and their strength was great enough to hurl him against a high ceiling with ease.
