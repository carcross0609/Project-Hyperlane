# Silinese

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Silinese](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silinese)

The Silinese were a sentient species hailing from the planet Siline. They had crystalline bones, facial horns and teeth and diverse vibrant skin colors with matching hair and eyes. Because of their physiology, they were stronger and tougher than many species, including humans. The Silinese were originally a warrior people, but in the High Republic Era they became more peaceful and civilized and unified under one government. Apart from male and female, the Silinese also had other genders in their society.
