# Tinian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tinian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tinian)

The Tinians were a sentient species that existed on a planet in the galaxy. They had very sensitive skin and considered blindness to be a gift and sight to be a disability. Around 2000 BBY, the Tinians forged the Tinian Codex, a stone carving with metal inlay which could be read by touch. In 3 BBY, while at a Senate Investiture party, Kleya Marki briefly described the Tinians while removing a listening device from the Tinian Codex.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tinian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Sensitive skin
- **Origin:** Unidentified Tinian planet
