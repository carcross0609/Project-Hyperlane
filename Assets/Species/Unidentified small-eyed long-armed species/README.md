# Unidentified small-eyed long-armed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified small-eyed long-armed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_small-eyed_long-armed_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their long arms and small black eyes. In 4 ABY, at least two member of this species could be found on the planet Tatooine.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified small-eyed long-armed species
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
