# Lizling

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lizling](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lizling)

The Lizling were a species of sentient reptiles that were known for their diminutive size. One individual was named Boz.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lizling
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptile
