# Frecker

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Frecker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frecker)

Freckers were a species of small sentient rodents. Though wild and brutal, they were capable of intelligent speech. Freckers also wore clothes, denoting some manner of culture.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Frecker
- **Skincolor:** Brown
