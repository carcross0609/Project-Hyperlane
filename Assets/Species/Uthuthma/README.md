# Uthuthma

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Uthuthma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uthuthma)

The Uthuthma were an alien species native to the planet Jakku. They were described as having a "socket-eyed skull face" with a "toothy maw." They wore chains around their necks as scarves. Gazwin was a member of this species that was present on Jakku during the New Republic Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Jakku
