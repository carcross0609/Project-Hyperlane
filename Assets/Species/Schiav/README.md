# Schiav

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Schiav](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Schiav)

The Schiav were a sentient species. Standing at over two meters tall, they were distinguished by their tan-colored skin, long brown hair and two fangs projecting upward from their bottom jaws.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Schiav
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:** Two fangs projecting upward from their bottom jaws
