# Noorian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Noorian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noorian)

Noorians were a sentient humanoid species from the Expansion Region planet Noori. They had dark skin and striped eyes. A known member was Jedi Master Tahl, who was a friend of Qui-Gon Jinn.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Noorian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Dark honey
- **Haircolor:** Golden brown
- **Eyecolor:** Gold and green (striped)
- **Origin:** Noori
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
