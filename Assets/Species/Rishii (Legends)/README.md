# Rishii

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rishii/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rishii/Legends)

The Rishii were a sentient, avian species native to the planet Rishi, possessing a generally humanoid body structure, albeit with arms that functioned more like wings, a full-body covering of feathers, and eagle-like talons for feet. Rishii wings were uniquely "finger"-tipped, as well, an adaptation which, unlike most avian species, allowed them to both fly and manipulate objects. However, the species remained largely primitive in terms of engineering or technological advancement. A carnivorous species, the Rishii were well-regarded as friendly and accepting of others. They lived in small communes or groups known as nests, which existed harmoniously as well as with their off-worlder and non-Rishii neighbors, including followers of the H'kig religion who had settled on their homeworld.
