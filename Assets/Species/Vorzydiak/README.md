# Vorzydiak

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vorzydiak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vorzydiak)

The Vorzydiaks were a Near-Human species indigenous to Vorzyd IV and Vorzyd V. They were tall and gaunt with yellowish-green skin, larger than usual eyes, and a pair of antennae.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vorzydiak
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Greenish-yellow
- **Haircolor:** Black or brown
- **Distinctions:** Antennae, wide eyes
- **Origin:** Vorzyd IV, Vorzyd V
- **Language:** Vorzydiak, Galactic Basic Standard
