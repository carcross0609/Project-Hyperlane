# Duu'ranh

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Duu'ranh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duu%27ranh)

The Duu'ranh were a race of humanoids. This species' members included a young woman who was among the Fallanassi at the time of the Black Fleet Crisis.
