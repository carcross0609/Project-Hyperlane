# Kriek

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kriek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kriek)

The Krieks were a species of sentients that were indigenous to the world of Kriekaal in Wild Space.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kriek
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Height:** 1.2–1.5 meters
- **Length:** 3 meters
- **Distinctions:** Able to see into the infrared spectrum
- **Origin:** Kriekaal
- **Diet:** Lichens
