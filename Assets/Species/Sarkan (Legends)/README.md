# Sarkan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sarkan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarkan/Legends)

The Sarkans were a reptilian species of sentient bipeds from Sarka, notable for their wealth and their complicated protocol rituals.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sarkan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.9 to 2.2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Green to blue
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Cold-blooded reptiles, complex protocol rituals
- **Lifespan:** 96 years
- **Origin:** Sarka
- **Language:** Sarkese, four other primary languages
