# Ubdurian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ubdurian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ubdurian)

Ubdurians were a species of sentient humanoids native to Nag Ubdur and Ubduria. Their bald heads featured dark eyes seated deep in their sockets, but no visible nose.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ubdurian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Bald head, no visible nose
- **Origin:**
  - Nag Ubdur
  - Ubduria
- **Language:** Ubdurian
