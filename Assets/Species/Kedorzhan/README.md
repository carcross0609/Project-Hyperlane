# Kedorzhan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kedorzhan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kedorzhan)

The Kedorzhans were a rotund, rodent-like species—native to Kedorzha in Senex-Juvex—who operated mines in SoroSuub Landing on the planet Taris in the years prior to its devastation by Darth Malak. Kedorzhans had excellent senses of smell, but were virtually blind after hours in the darkness of the mines.
