# "Bedlam Spirits"

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bedlam Spirits](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bedlam_Spirits)

The Bedlam Spirits were an omnipotent group of entities that inhabited the planet Bedlam near the Bedlam Pulsar. They consisted of Tilotny, Horliss-Horliss, Cold Danda Sine and Splendid Ap. The spirits were very powerful beings, capable of manipulating time and mass. However, they were prone to childish behavior.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** "Bedlam Spirits"
- **Distinctions:**
  - Ability to change physical form
  - Ability to manipulate time and matter
- **Origin:** Bedlam
