# Nikto

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nikto/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto/Legends)

The Nikto were a reptilian humanoid species from the planet Kintan in the Si'Klaata Cluster in Hutt Space with many subspecies.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nikto
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Subspecies:**
  - Kajain'sa'Nikto
  - Kadas'sa'Nikto
  - Esral'sa'Nikto
  - Gluss'sa'Nikto
  - M'shento'su'Nikto
  - Numol'elrul
- **Height:** 1.6 - 1.9 meters
- **Mass:** 60 kilograms
- **Skincolor:** Varied with subspecies
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Varied with subspecies
- **Origin:** Kintan
- **Language:** Nikto
