# Jablogian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jablogian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jablogian)

Jablogians were a sentient species native to the planet of Nar Kanji. They were noted for their overweight torsos, blemished red skin, pointed ears and beady eyes. Azmorigan, Drahric and Nazgorigan were members of that species. The species shared its name with the language of the same name, which was spoken by the human Tasu Leech.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jablogian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Eyecolor:** Green and Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Overweight torsos
  - Four-fingered hands
  - Pointed ears
- **Origin:** Nar Kanji
