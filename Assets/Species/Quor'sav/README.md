# Quor'sav; (Image depicts legs)

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quor'sav](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quor%27sav)

The Quor'sav were a sentient species that moved about on at least two long, stilt-like legs. One such being was present in Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine in 1 BBY on the same day Luke Skywalker sold his landspeeder to a Vuvrian named Wioslea. At some point prior to or during 3 ABY, the Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk'wassak'Cradossk assisted a Quor'sav narcotics agent on the astronomical object Uaua. When Bossk later confronted his sister Doshanalawook on the gunship Liswarr, he noted that her projector console showed a detailed personal history of him, including a mention of the Quor'sav agent.
