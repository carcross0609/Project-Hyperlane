# Jungle Felucian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jungle Felucian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jungle_Felucian)

Jungle Felucians were a primitive sentient species of amphibians native to the Outer Rim Territories planet Felucia. Members of the species were tuned to the ever-changing presence of the Force on their homeworld. Jungle Felucians largely kept to themselves, but when members of the Gossam species arrived on the world they drove the Jungle Felucians into hiding deep within the planet's wild forests. The Jungle Felucians were one of two species native to Felucia with the other being the more peaceful Felucians.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jungle Felucian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Origin:** Felucia
- **Habitat:** Wild forest
