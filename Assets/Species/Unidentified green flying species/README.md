# Unidentified green flying species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified green flying species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green_flying_species)

This species was a sentient species distinguished by their green skin and ray-like frames, lacking arms or legs, instead possessing a pair of wing-like limbs. Capable of flight, they seemingly had few facial features, save for a mouth on the underside of their bodies and potentially squinted eyes. They donned only strapped satchels for clothing. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species were present in a casino on the planet Qiaxx.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified green flying species
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Wing-like limbs
