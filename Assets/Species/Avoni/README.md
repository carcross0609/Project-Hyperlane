# Avoni

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Avoni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avoni)

The Avoni were the indigenous species of Avon. In 27 BBY, the Avoni attempted to conquer Radnor after a bioplague struck the planet, which resulted in death followed by evacuation. They sent Dol Heep, the ambassador of Avon, to conquer the planet when everyone was gone from Radnor.
