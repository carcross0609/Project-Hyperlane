# Xan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Xan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xan)

The Xan were a small-bodied sentient species. Some of them had established a colony on the planet Gan Moradir, in the galaxy's Mid Rim. Geeska Dotalo was a male member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Xan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Small-bodied
- **Origin:** Gan Moradir (colony)
