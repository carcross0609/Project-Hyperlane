# Immalian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Immalian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Immalian)

The Immalians were natives of the Expansion Region world Immalia. They maintained a mining colony on Mayvitch 7, which was wiped out by the Yinchorri in 33 BBY.

- **Type:** Planetary cultural group
