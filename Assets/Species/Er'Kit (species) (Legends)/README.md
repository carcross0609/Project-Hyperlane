# Er'Kit

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Er'Kit (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Er%27Kit_%28species%29/Legends)

Er'Kits were a sentient species of tall and thin reptilians native to the Outer Rim planet of Er'Kit. Er'Kits were slightly humanoid in appearance and were characterized by their long limbs, tough hides, and elongated, conical craniums. Members of the species identified with at least the male gender and their skin was light blue with pink and yellow stripes in color.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Er'Kit
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Skincolor:** Light blue, pink, and yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Elongated craniums
  - Tough hides
  - Long limbs
- **Origin:** Er'Kit
