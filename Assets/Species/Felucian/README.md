# Felucian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Felucian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Felucian)

Felucians were a sentient species native to the plains and grasslands of the planet Felucia. At the time of the Clone Wars, many Felucians, including Casiss Midagatis, were peaceful farmers, growing nysillin and other crops.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Felucian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green, orange, pink, yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Red with black pupils
- **Origin:** Felucia
- **Habitat:** Plains and grassland
