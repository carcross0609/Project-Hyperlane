# Langhesi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Langhesi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Langhesi)

The Langhesi were a race of red-skinned humanoids indigenous to the planet Langhesa, presumably in the Langhesa system. The Langhesi could mold and shape life into new forms. They spoke Langhesan.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Langhesi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Origin:** Langhesa
- **Language:** Langhesan
