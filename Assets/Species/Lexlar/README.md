# Lexlar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lexlar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lexlar)

The Lexlars were a species of sentient, hair-covered hominid. Odeon Farnish was a three-feet-tall, Basic-speaking Lexlar who worked as a customs inspector during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance. At one point, Farnish referred to another being as "hairless one."

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lexlar
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Hominid
- **Height:** 3 feet
- **Distinctions:** Hair-covered body
