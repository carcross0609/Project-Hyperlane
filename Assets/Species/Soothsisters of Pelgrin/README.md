# Soothsisters of Pelgrin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Soothsisters of Pelgrin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soothsisters_of_Pelgrin)

The Soothsisters of Pelgrin were a sentient species. According to Evocar by Direus'pei, they were made extinct by Xim because they prophesied his empire would fall.
