# Mriss

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mriss](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mriss)

The Mriss were a sentient avian species. While incapable of flying, the diminutive Mriss still used their wings for balance. In 22 BBY, a Mriss named Idda served as an engineer aboard the starship Namrelllew.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mriss
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian
- **Distinctions:** Vestigial wings
