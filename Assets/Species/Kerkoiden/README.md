# Kerkoiden

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kerkoiden](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kerkoiden)

The Kerkoiden, known also by the slang term Kerk, were a sentient species from the planet Kerkoidia. The Confederacy of Independent Systems General Whorm Loathsom was a Kerkoiden, as was one of the many guards employed by the crime lord Talvee Chawin on the planet Coruscant some time after the Battle of Endor. The Retail Caucus operated a security force of Kerkoiden warriors.
