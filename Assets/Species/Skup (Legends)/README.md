# Skup

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skup/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skup/Legends)

The Skups, or Sionian Skups, were a near-Human species who differed from standard Humans due to their small, closely spaced eyes, delicate hair, and skin the color of dianoga cheese.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Skup
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Skincolor:** Color of dianoga cheese
- **Distinctions:**
  - Small, closely spaced eyes
  - Brittle hair
- **Origin:** Sionia
