# Ruusanian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ruusanian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruusanian/Legends)

Ruusanians were approximately 20,000 preindustrial Near-Humans from Ruusan. They developed due to inbreeding amongst the small Human population of Ruusan; they were hardy and well-adapted to a desert environment.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ruusanian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Origin:** Ruusan
