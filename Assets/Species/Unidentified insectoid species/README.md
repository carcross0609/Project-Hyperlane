# Unidentified insectoid species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified insectoid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_insectoid_species)

A sentient species of ancient insectoids were active in the galaxy following the Battle of Jakku. Navigators known as the Attendants were members of the insectoid species. At least two of the species were present on the Supremacy, the flagship of First Order Supreme Leader Snoke in 34 ABY.
