# Jaing's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jaing's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaing%27s_species)

The humanoid species training master Jaing belonged to possessed skulls that bore a striking resemblance to that of a male mythosaur.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jaing's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Elongated head, downward curving horns, quadactyl feet
- **Lifespan:** At least several millennia
