# Hoover

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hoover](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoover)

Hoovers were a species of small, quadrupedal sentients that had long, blood-sucking trunks. One such hematophagous alien, a male named Attark, was owned as a pet by Jabba the Hutt. Members of the species were often mistaken for pests due to their appearance.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hoover
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Elephantine
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Long trunk
- **Diet:** Hematophagous
