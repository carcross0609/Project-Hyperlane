# Blood Carver

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blood Carver](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Carver)

Blood Carvers were a sentient insect-like race native to the planet Batorine.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Blood Carver
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Gold
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Three joints per limb
  - Orange blood
- **Lifespan:**
  - Child: 1-8 years
  - Young adult: 9-11 years
  - Adult: 12-38 years
  - Middle age: 39-65 years
  - Old: 66-82 years
  - Venerable: 83+ years
- **Origin:** Batorine
- **Language:** Batorese
