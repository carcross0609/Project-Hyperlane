# Sharalian

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Sharalian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sharalian)

Sharalians were a sentient species of owl. They had light skin with tan feathers, blue eyes, and a beak. They had two digits and thumbs on their hands, and five digits on their feet. Sharalians had pointed ears that were emotive.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sharalian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Owl
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Feathers:** Tan
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:**
  - A beak
  - Pointed, emotive ears
  - Two digits on hands
