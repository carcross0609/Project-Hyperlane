# Torphlusian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Torphlusian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torphlusian)

The Torphlusian were a tentacled sentient species that lived in the galaxy in 5 ABY. One such Torphlusian represented their homeworld Torphlus in the Galactic Senate of the New Republic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Torphlusian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Tentacles
- **Origin:** Torphlus
