# Nuknog

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nuknog/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nuknog/Legends)

The Nuknog were a bipedal reptilian species with small brains and short tempers. They originated from the planet Sump. They spoke Nuknog.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nuknog
- **Height:** 1.2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Beaks
- **Origin:** Sump
- **Language:** Nuknog
