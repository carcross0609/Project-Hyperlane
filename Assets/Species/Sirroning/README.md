# Sirroning

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sirroning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sirroning)

The Sirroning were a six-limbed, sentient, tan-skinned species. One known as the Eye of Webbish Bog was locked in symbiosis with a blind Areobernos on the planet Mustafar.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sirroning
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Distinctions:** Six limbs
