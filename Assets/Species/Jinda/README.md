# Jinda

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jinda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jinda)

Jindas were a sentient species from the Jinda system that were introduced to the moon of Endor at some point. During the High Republic Era, a cruiser with a troop of Jinda dancers attended the Republic Fair on the planet Valo. During the Fair, Skuun reported the details of the accidental explosion of a the cruiser.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jinda
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Jinda system
