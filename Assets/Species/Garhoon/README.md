# Garhoon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Garhoon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Garhoon)

The Garhoon were a species of vampiric sentients. Bail Organa presided over a court case between the Garhoons and their prey. At the end of the case, the prey returned to the Garhoons, saddening Leia Organa.
