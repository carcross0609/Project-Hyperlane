# Nord

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nord)

The Nord were a dwarf-like, three-foot-tall humanoid species native to Nordra. They were a hardy race that had learned to live under constant threat of danger from the harsh environment of their homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nord
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Nordra
