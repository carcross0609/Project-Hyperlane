# Tchuukthai

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tchuukthai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tchuukthai)

Tchuukthai, also called Wharls, were sapient ceratopsian quadrupeds from the Unknown Regions.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tchuukthai
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian quadruped
- **Height:**

  - 3 meters (standing on hind legs)
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Haircolor:** Gray-white
- **Eyecolor:** White
- **Distinctions:** Armored quadrupedal body
- **Lifespan:**
  - Child: 1–12
  - Young adult: 12–20
  - Adult: 21–189
  - Middle age: 190–249
  - Old: 250–299
  - Venerable: 300+
- **Origin:** Unidentified Unknown Regions planet
- **Language:** Tchuukthese
