# Clantanni

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clantanni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clantanni)

The Clantanni were a sentient species in the galaxy. In 382 BBY, a member of this species was a follower of the Black Eye on Jedha.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Clantanni
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Light blue
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:** Long ears
- **Origin:** Clantaano system
