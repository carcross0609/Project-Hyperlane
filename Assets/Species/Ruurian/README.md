# Ruurian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ruurian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruurian)

Ruurians were a species of multi-limbed sentients with feathered antennae from the planet Ruuria. Slyyth, a Ruurian criminal, lived on Lothal around 3272 LY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ruurian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Eight pairs of limbs
  - Feathery antennae
  - Four-fingered hands
  - Large multifaceted eyes
  - Stunted wings
- **Origin:** Ruuria
