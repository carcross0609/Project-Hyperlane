# Kel Dor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kel Dor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kel_Dor)

Kel Dors were a sentient species native to the planet Dorin. In order to survive oxygen-rich environments, they wore specialized breath masks that covered their eyes and nostrils. Jedi Master Plo Koon was a Kel Dor who served on the Jedi Council in the last years of the Jedi Order and the Galactic Republic. Bo Keevil was also a member of the species, who served as a pilot in Ace Squadron.

## Biology & Culture
