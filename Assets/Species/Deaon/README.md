# Deaon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Deaon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deaon)

Deaons were a sentient species native to the astronomical object of Ord Gimmel. Members of the species possessed long fingers and manufactured a variety of makes and models of the short, squat variable holdout blasters, which were shaped to fit their hands.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Deaon
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Long fingers
- **Origin:** Ord Gimmel
