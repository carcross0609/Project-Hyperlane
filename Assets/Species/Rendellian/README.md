# Rendellian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rendellian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rendellian)

The Rendellians were a species native to the planet Rendel. Rendellians possessed fur that ranged in color from purple, green and orange, as well as wings that matched the color of their hair. Zalla and Bellan were members of this species. One such Rendellian told the story of the Wild Squadron to Rode, a Galactic Empire pilot, while on the planet Bray.
