# J'feh

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J'feh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J%27feh)

The J'feh were a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. Lady Nadrian DuQuosenne, a J'feh female, served as the senator for her homeworld Yabrenito during the twilight years of the Galactic Republic and into the early reign of the Galactic Empire.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** J'feh
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Yabrenito
- **Language:** J'feh language
