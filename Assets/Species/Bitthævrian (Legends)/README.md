# Bitthævrian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bitthævrian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bitth%C3%A6vrian/Legends)

The Bitthævrians were warlike, bipedal sapients native to Guiteica, in the Kadok Regions.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bitthævrian
- **Height:** 1.7 to 2.2 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Black
  - Dark brown
  - Dark Yellow
  - Reddish-brown
  - Tan
- **Distinctions:**
  - Infrared vision
  - Regenerative quills on elbows and knees
  - Sharp fangs
  - Six sharp teeth
  - Thick skin
- **Origin:** Guiteica
