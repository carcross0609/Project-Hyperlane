# Vaathkree

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vaathkree (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vaathkree_%28species%29)

The Vaathkree people evolved on the planet of Vaathkree. They were covered by armored plates, making them appear to be made from stone and metal. Vaathkree were well-known for their devotion to trade and bartering. In fact, the name of their religion (roughly translated to Basic) was "The Deal."

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vaathkree
- **Height:** 1.5–1.9 meters
- **Skincolor:** Dark bluish-gray
- **Distinctions:**
  - Armored skin
  - High resistance to physical attacks
  - Long life spans
- **Lifespan:** 300–350 years
- **Origin:**
  - Vaathkree
  - Garnib (adopted)
- **Language:** Vaathkreea, Galactic Basic Standard
