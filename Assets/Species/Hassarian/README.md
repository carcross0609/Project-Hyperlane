# Hassarian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hassarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hassarian)

The Hassarians were a species of the galaxy with willowy physiques, lilac hair, and long snouts. As of the High Republic Era, the species had a centuries-long rivalry with the Skembo. During the later period of the Republic Era, the Hassarians joined the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hassarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Lilac
- **Distinctions:** Willowy physiques, long snouts
- **Origin:** Hassaria system
