# Acherin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Acherin (race)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acherin_%28race%29)

The Acherin were a sentient humanoid species native to the planet Acherin. During the Galactic Empire's subjugation of the planet, the term was also used to describe anyone who supported the freedom of the planet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Acherin
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Humanoid
- **Origin:** Acherin
