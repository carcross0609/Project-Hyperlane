# Unidentified barbed yellow species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified barbed yellow species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_barbed_yellow_species)

A sentient species with yellow skin existed in the galaxy. These aliens were bipedal humanoids that possessed four pointed barbs protruding from their faces, two below their mouths and two above, with a pair of fin-like growths also extending from their cheekbones around the sides of their heads and ending in points above and behind their craniums.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified barbed yellow species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Head fins
  - Facial barbs
