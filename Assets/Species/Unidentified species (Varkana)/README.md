# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Varkana)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Varkana%29)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The humanoid species was distinguished by their tan, almost orangish skin, with two large pointed protrusions extending from the sides of their heads, resembling either conical ears or fleshy horns. One member of this species was present on the planet Varkana in 34 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Distinctions:** Cranial protrusions
