# Tritonite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tritonite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tritonite)

The Tritonites were an insectoid species from the moons of Triton. They were followers of Gactimus, and as acolytes they were a common sight in spaceports around the galaxy, offering tracts to individuals for their personal enlightenment. They operated the Triton Moons, famous as checkpoints.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tritonite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Origin:** Triton
- **Language:** Tritonite
