# Chagrian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chagrian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chagrian/Legends)

Chagrians were a sentient species of amphibious humanoids native to the water planet of Champala.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Chagrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Height:** 1.9 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Light blue to deep indigo
- **Distinctions:**
  - Horns
  - Lethorns
  - Lack of taste-buds
  - Black forked tongue
- **Lifespan:** Up to 76 standard years
- **Origin:** Champala
- **Language:** Chagri
