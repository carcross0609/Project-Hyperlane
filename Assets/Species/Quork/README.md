# Quork

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quork](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quork)

Quorks were a species of sentient furred bipeds found on the moon Endor. After word spread that the Ewoks of the Bright Tree tribe and a group of Yuzzums had seen off a group of Imperials in 4 ABY, a Quork was among a number of neighbors of the Ewoks who gathered at Bright Tree Village to share stories and work in unity with others.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Quork
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Haircolor:** Brown and gray
- **Distinctions:** Pink horns
