# Dania Francis's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dania Francis's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dania_Francis%27s_species)

Dania Francis belonged to a race of seahorse mermaids that had white hair and eyes and purple scales. The species was one of the eleven minor species in the School.

- **Name:** Dania Francis's species
- **Skincolor:** Purple scales
- **Haircolor:** White
