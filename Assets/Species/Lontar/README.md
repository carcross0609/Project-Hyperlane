# Lontar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lontar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lontar)

The Lontars were a sentient species native to the Lontar system of the north-western portion of the Colonies Region. Several hundred years before the end of the Galactic Republic, the Lontars attacked Batorine, a neighboring planet in the Colonies and the homeworld of the Blood Carvers, a feudal, reclusive species that was extremely adept at assassination.

- **Designation:** Sentient
