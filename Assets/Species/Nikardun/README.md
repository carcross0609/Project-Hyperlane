# Nikardun

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nikardun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikardun)

Nikarduns were a sentient species found in the Unknown Regions. General Yiv was a Nikardun. The Nikardun government was the Nikardun Destiny. At the time of the Clone Wars, it began to invade a number of nearby societies. General Yiv was thwarted by Thrawn.
