# Glymphid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Glymphid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glymphid)

The Glymphid were a sentient species with orange or red colored eyes and beige, blue, gray, green, and yellow colored skin. The podracer Aldar Beedo from Ploo II was a Glymphid.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Glymphid
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Beige
  - Blue
  - Gray
  - Green
  - Yellow
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Orange
  - Red
- **Origin:** Ploo II
