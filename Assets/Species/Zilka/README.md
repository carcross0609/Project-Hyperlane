# Zilka

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zilka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zilka)

The Zilka were a sentient buglike species. They possessed four legs and were armored. Two Zilka enforcers encountered the undercover Jedi Master Mace Windu on the planet Metagos.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Zilka
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Distinctions:** Four legs
- **Origin:** A planet
