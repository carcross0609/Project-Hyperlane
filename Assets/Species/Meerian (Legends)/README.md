# Meerian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Meerian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meerian/Legends)

The Meerians were a race of short, silver-haired Near-Humans indigenous to the planet of Bandomeer.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Meerian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.1 to 1.7 meters
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Haircolor:** Metallic; usually gold or silver
- **Origin:** Bandomeer
- **Language:** Meerian
