# Triffian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Triffian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Triffian)

Triffians were a sentient species with yellow eyes and black, blue, yellow, and cream skin native to Triffis. The podracer Ebe E. Endocott was a Triffian.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Triffian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Black, blue, yellow, cream
- **Origin:** Triffis
