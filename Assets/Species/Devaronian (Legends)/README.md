# Devaronian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Devaronian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devaronian/Legends)

Devaronians were a horned humanoid species from the world of Devaron in the Colonies, a planet of low mountains and deep valleys linked by thousands of rivers. It was believed that they were descended from a race of primates that lived in the mountains of Devaron, and that their horns were a genetic mutation that proved useful in fending off predatory birds.
