# Squidge

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Squidge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Squidge)

Squidge was the insulting name given by the Vindar to the race of beings they oppressed. They were diminutive blue creatures with elongated skulls and wavy black patterns on their skin. They spoke a primitive form of Basic and were organized under "den mothers".

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Squidge
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Wavy black patterns on skin, short height
- **Language:** Primitive dialect of Galactic Basic Standard
