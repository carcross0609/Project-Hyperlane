# Kulless

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kulless](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kulless)

Kulless were a sentient species from the planet Gerres Gule. Members of the species were bipedal, with a round torso and a thick tail. In 24 BBY, the Kulless Shrivel Braittrand competed on the professional Podracing circuit, piloting a Podracer of his own design. Braittrand was particularly favored on the SoroSuub Facility track.
