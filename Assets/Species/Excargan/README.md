# Excargan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Excargan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Excargan)

Excargans were a Near-Human sentient species native to Excarga. During childhood, they were genderless; at around thirteen years old, their bodies would change and their gender would be determined. Many inhabitants of their planet worked for ore-processing corporations. They were attacked by the Separatists during the Clone Wars, which led to Republic intervention, and they sold materials to the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War.
