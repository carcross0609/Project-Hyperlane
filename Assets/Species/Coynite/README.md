# Coynite

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Coynite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coynite)

Coynites were a sentient species from the Coyn system. One such being was involved in a gladitorial fight in the city of Sclavos on Zygerria, which ended with the Coynite's death.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Coynite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Reddish
- **Origin:** Coyn system
