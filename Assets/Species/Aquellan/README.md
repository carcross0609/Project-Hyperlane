# Aquellan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aquellan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aquellan)

The Aquellans were a cultural group of Human originating on the Mid Rim world of Aquella. They were known to speak a guttural-sounding language.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Aquellan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Human race
- **Distinctions:** Light skin with purple-green tattoos
- **Origin:** Aquella
- **Language:** Aquellan language
