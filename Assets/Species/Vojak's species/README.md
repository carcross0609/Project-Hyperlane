# Vojak's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vojak's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vojak%27s_species)

This species was a species of tall, muscular, purple-skinned humanoid sentients. Vojak and his brother were both members of this species.
