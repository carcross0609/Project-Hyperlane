# Unidentified mouthless species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified mouthless species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_mouthless_species)

A mouthless green-skinned alien species lived in the galaxy. The species was humanoid in form. Two black eyes were set in its face, along with a pronounced nose. The species could be fed nutrients through a special suit.
