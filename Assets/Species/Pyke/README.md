# Pyke

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pyke](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pyke)

The Pykes were a species of sentient humanoids native to the planet Oba Diah. The species had two distinct physiological groups. While both shared elongated heads, one group was characterized by their oversized craniums with undersized faces, while the other bore more humanoid proportions with piscine features. The Pyke Syndicate was the main focus of the Pyke economy and the galaxy's preeminent supplier of spice, a term for various types of narcotic substances mined by slaves on the planet Kessel and a number of other planets.
