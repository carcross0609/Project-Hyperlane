# Unidentified long-necked furry species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified long-necked furry species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_long-necked_furry_species)

An unidentified long-necked furry species, with slender bodies, long necks, and elongated faces, possessing tufts of hair around their mouths, as well as four-fingered hands and medium-length tails. While still early into their development, this species was discovered by the Karflo Corporation, who converted their homeworld into a mining colony and illegally exploited and abused the native population, forcing them to mine valuable raw material for minimal wages.
