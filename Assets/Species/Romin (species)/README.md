# Romin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Romin (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Romin_%28species%29)

Romin were a humanoid species with a somewhat smaller stature than that of Humans. Romin had golden skin and eyes. Their noses were flat, barely raised on their faces, and their mouths were wide and expressive.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Romin
- **Skincolor:** Golden
- **Eyecolor:** Golden
- **Distinctions:** Flat noses, wide mouths
- **Origin:** Romin
