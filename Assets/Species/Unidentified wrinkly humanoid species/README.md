# Unidentified wrinkly humanoid species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified wrinkly humanoid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_wrinkly_humanoid_species)

This species was a bipedal humanoid sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their wrinkled skin and sunken eyes. In 9 ABY, a member of this species was present at Gor Koresh's Honky Tonk when Koresh attempted to take Din Djarin's Mandalorian armor.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified wrinkly humanoid species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Dark
- **Haircolor:** Black
