# Mugaari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mugaari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mugaari)

The Mugaari were a long lived species of humanoids native to Mugaar in the Bruanii sector. They were heavy-browed and lantern-jawed, with slate-colored skin.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mugaari
- **Skincolor:** Slate
- **Origin:** Mugaar
- **Language:** Mugaar
