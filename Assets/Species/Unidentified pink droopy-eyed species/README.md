# Unidentified pink droopy-eyed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified pink droopy-eyed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pink_droopy-eyed_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their pink skin, with rounded heads featuring black eyes with drooping eyelids, pointed ears, and an open tube extending up from the tops of their heads. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified pink droopy-eyed species
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Cranial tube
  - Pointed ears
