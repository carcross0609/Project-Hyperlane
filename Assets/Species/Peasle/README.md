# Peasle

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Peasle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Peasle)

The Peasle were an insectoid sentient species. They possessed orange shells and a natural urge to roll into a ball at the first sign of danger. During the High Republic Era, the Peasle Skuun served as an assistant to space traffic controller Milon Thakkery on a space traffic control satellite above the planet Valo. Skritt was a Peasle who was a member of the Rebel Alliance during the time of the Galactic Empire.
