# Moraysian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moraysian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moraysian)

Moraysians were a species of tan, stout humanoids from the Moraysi system. They were skilled inventors and builders of starships, and had an ability to actualize their schematics. They were overrun by criminal elements such as the Pyke Syndicate, who forced the Moraysians to outfit them with technology.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Moraysian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Origin:** Moraysi system
