# Thradian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thradian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thradian)

The Thradians were a species of humanoid sentients from Thrad. They had yellow skin, full black eyes, and annulated appendages that grew from the top of their skulls. Senator Nadea Tural was a female Thradian.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Thradian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Annulated head growths
- **Origin:** Thrad
