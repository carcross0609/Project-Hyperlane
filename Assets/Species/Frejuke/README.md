# Frejuke

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Frejuke](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frejuke)

The Frejukes were a sentient species that possessed heat-retaining blubber. Wolentic Dudge, a resident of Kijimi City on the planet Kijimi, was a Frejuke whose blubber made him a good fit for the world's snowy climate.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Frejuke
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:** Heat-retaining blubber
