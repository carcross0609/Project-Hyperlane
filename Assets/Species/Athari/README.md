# Athari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Athari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Athari)

Athari was a term used for the Fathalian inhabitants of the city Athar, located on the planet JanFathal. They had dark skin, and most of them wore their hair in neatly-coiled plaits. They spoke Basic with an accent.
