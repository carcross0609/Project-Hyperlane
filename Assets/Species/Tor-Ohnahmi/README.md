# Tor-Ohnahmi

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Tor-Ohnahmi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tor-Ohnahmi)

The Tor-Ohnahmi were a sentient Repto-mammalian species known as Torons to those more familiar with the race, originating from the planet Ohnahmi. This species was naturally strong in the force. The Torons were all but wiped out by the Galactic Empire when they refused to be won or by them.
