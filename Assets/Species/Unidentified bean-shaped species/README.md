# Unidentified bean-shaped species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified bean-shaped species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_bean-shaped_species)

The species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their rounded, bean-shaped bodies, with thin limbs, pink or brown skin, and striations running across their body, extending out from their face. The species possessed large eyes with yellow sclera and beady black pupils, as well as dark red or grayish-brown lips, and pointed ears.
