# Kairos's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kairos's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kairos%27s_species)

A humanoid species with chitinous features was native to a jungle planet. The species perceived there to be two worlds, their own and everything beyond it, and did not wish to come into contact with anything from outside of their planet. Its members wore wrapped garments and masks in order to conceal themselves and remain pure, additionally believing that doing so could heal their spirit if they did become unpure.
