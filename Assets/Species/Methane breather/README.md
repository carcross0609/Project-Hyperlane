# Methane breather

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Methane breather](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Methane_breather)

Methane breathers were species that needed to take in methane from the atmosphere to maintain life. Such species included the Morseerians and the Skakoans.
