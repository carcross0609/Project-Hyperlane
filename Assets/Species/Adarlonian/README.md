# Adarlonian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adarlonian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adarlonian)

The Adarlonians were the Human natives of the planet Adarlon. Drun Cairnwick the Jedi initiate who served the Jedi Order and escaped the fall of the Order during the Great Jedi Purge and the rise of the Galactic Empire was from Adarlon.
