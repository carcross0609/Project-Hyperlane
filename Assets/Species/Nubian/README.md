# Nubian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nubian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nubian)

Nubians were Humans native to the planet Nubia. T'chooka D'oon was an Nubian Jedi Master who lived during the waning years of the Galactic Republic.
