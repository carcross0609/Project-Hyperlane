# Sulituan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sulituan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sulituan)

Sulituans were a species with nautilus-like heads and shells with long tentacles that served as arms and legs. They lived on Archae Teuthis. The Podracer Tzidik Wrantojo was a Sulituan.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sulituan
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Origin:** Archae Teuthis
- **Habitat:** Water
