# Unidentified saurian species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified saurian species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_saurian_species)

A member of an unidentified saurian species was among the prisoners of Stars' End, killed by Security Police during the Liberation of Stars' End in 2 BBY. This species was tall and thin.
