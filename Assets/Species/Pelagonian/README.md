# Pelagonian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pelagonian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pelagonian)

The Pelagonians were the Human race native to the planet Pelagon in the Tapani sector. Due to reflection of their sun's rays off the surface of the world's ocean, Pelagonians had tough, leathery skin. A patient and determined people, the generation that survived the Imperial assault on Pelagon were grim and pessimistic. They held a deep respect for a person's honor, and cared for quality over quantity.
