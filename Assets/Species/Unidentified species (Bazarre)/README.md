# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Bazarre)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Bazarre%29)

A humanoid sentient species lived in the galaxy. Several members of this species served as security guards on the space station Bazarre. A representative of this species was also present at the First Conference of Free Peoples in 4 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species

- **Eyecolor:**

- **Distinctions:**

  - Short fur
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
