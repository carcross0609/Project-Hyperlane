# Nelwyn

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Nelwyn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nelwyn)

The Nelwyn were a species of Near-Humans from Andowyne. They were shorter than the Daikini and had different proportions. Given their similarities to the Human and Brownie races of Andowyne, it is likely that the three groups shared a common ancestry. One notable Nelwyn was Willow Ufgood, who was instrumental in the overthrow of Queen Bavmorda.
