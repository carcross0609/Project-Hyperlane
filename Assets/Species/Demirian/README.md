# Demirian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Demirian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Demirian)

The Demirians were a species that inhabited the iron planet Demir in the Unknown Regions. When Captain Phasma of the First Order led an attack on Demir, the Demirians defended their world.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Demirian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Demir
