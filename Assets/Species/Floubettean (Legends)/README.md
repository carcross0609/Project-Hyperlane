# Floubettean

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Floubettean/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Floubettean/Legends)

The Floubetteans were an avian race from the Floubette system. They were known for their complex mating dance. It was considered an art form by other avians but revolted most Humans.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Floubettean
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian
- **Distinctions:** Complex mating ritual
- **Origin:** Floubette system
