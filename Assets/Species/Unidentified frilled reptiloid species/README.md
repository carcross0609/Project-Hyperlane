# Unidentified frilled reptiloid species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified frilled reptiloid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_frilled_reptiloid_species)

This reptiloid race had large frills on their necks and broad tails. A member of this species was a New Republic diplomat.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified frilled reptiloid species
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:**
  - Large frills on their necks
  - Broad tails
