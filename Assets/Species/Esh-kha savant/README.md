# Esh-kha savant

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Esh-kha savant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Esh-kha_savant)

The Esh-kha savants was a caste of the Esh-kha species that consisted of the species' Force-sensitives. Savants had more prominent nostrils on the front of their faces, and lacked the ear-like protrusions that the mainline species possessed; instead, their heads were more triangular.
