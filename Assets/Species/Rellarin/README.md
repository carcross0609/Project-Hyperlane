# Rellarin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rellarin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rellarin)

The Rellarins were sentient bipeds indigenous to Rellnas Minor, a moon orbiting the gas giant Rellnas in the Rell system.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Rellarin
- **Races:** At least 57 cultural groups
- **Height:** 1.7 - 2.3 meters
- **Skincolor:** Dark brown
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Thick plated skin, feeding spikes, reputation for wisdom
- **Origin:** Rellnas Minor
- **Language:** Nanilas, many others
