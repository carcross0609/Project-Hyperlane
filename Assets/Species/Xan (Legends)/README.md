# Xan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xan/Legends)

The Xan were a sentient species indigenous to Algara II.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Xan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.5–1.75 meters
- **Skincolor:** Green, yellow, pink
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:** Large, bulbous head with a slender build
- **Lifespan:** 80 standard years
- **Origin:** Algara II
