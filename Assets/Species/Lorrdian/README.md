# Lorrdian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lorrdian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lorrdian)

Lorrdians were a sentient bipedal species that gave off a distinctive natural scent and had a very expressive body language. Lorrdian members of the Brotherhood of the Beatific Countenance wore concealing robes that acted as a physical vow of silence. Nee Alavar was a Lorrdian senator of the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lorrdian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Strong scent
  - Very expressive body language
- **Origin:** Lorrd system
