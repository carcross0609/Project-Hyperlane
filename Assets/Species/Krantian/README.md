# Krantian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Krantian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krantian)

Krantians were a sentient species that hailed from the Krant system in the Mid Rim. A family of Krantians, including mother Lerahel and child Sarry, attended the Republic Fair on the planet Valo. When the Nihil marauders attacked the fair, Lerahel and Sarry were the only ones of the family who survived.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Krantian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Origin:** Krant system
