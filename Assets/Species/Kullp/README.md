# Kullp

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kullp](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kullp)

The Kullp were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. When Han Solo and Lando Calrissian were searching for a pilot on Frander's Bay, Solo dodged a lumbering Kullp after he pushed his way through a group of Teek.
