# Melodie

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Melodie](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Melodie)

Melodies were a humanoid water-based species native to Yavin 8. The species lived deep within the purple mountains of their homeworld, most notably Sistra Mountain.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Melodie
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1-1.6 meters
- **Haircolor:** Red, Brown, White, Black.
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Grow mermaid tails when 20 years old
- **Origin:** Yavin 8
- **Language:** Melodese
