# Unidentified long-necked species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified long-necked species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_long-necked_species)

A bipedal species lived in the galaxy during the Imperial Era. A species of tall, thin-necked beings, at least one member resided in a mining village on Mapuzo in 9 BBY, while another was present on the Spur Orbiting Market in 3 ABY. That individual was on the space station during an altercation between an Arcona bounty hunter and the cyborg Beilert Valance.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified long-necked species
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Four eyes
