# Caamasi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Caamasi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caamasi/Legends)

The Caamasi were a sentient mammalian species of furred humanoids known for their compassionate and peace-loving nature; indeed, the name Caamasi came to mean "friend from afar" and "stranger to be trusted" in some languages across the galaxy.
