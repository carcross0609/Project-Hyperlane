# Unidentified one-eye-stalk species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified one-eye-stalk species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_one-eye-stalk_species/Legends)

A sentient species was distinguished by their tall eyestalk and solid eye perched atop their head, with a large, pointed ear also extending outward from each side, as well as a wide mouth with pronounced lips, what seemed like bristly hair growing from chin, and bony necks that folded inward. By 22 BBY, a member of this species had been a Jedi of the Jedi Order, well known enough to have multiple busts of them displayed around the Jedi Archives.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified one-eye-stalk species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Eyestalk
  - Pointed ears
