# Iotran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Iotran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iotran)

The Iotrans were a humanoid species from the Iotra system. When Merei Spanjaf and Jix Hekyl tried to smuggle a pulse-mag into a data center on Lothal, they encountered a hulking, bald example of this species with a blaster who warned them that they'd be "two smoking spots" if they took another step. Iotrans were a burly militaristic species, and considered short and stocky. The Separatist Iotran Guard was made up of Iotrans.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Iotran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Distinctions:** Short and stocky, burly
- **Origin:** Iotra system
