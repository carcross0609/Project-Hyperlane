# Force wielder

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Force wielder](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_wielder)

Force wielders, or Force-wielders, were an enigmatic sentient species of highly Force-sensitive beings with the power to alter their forms. The Force wielders were known for their affinity to the Force and it was believed they were god-like beings.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Force wielder
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 2.13 to 2.48 meters (humanoid form)
- **Length:** At least 4.79 meters (as gargoyle)
- **Wingspan:** At least 11 meters (as gargoyle)
- **Skincolor:** Gray, pale, white
- **Haircolor:** Green, white
- **Feathers:** Green and white (as griffin)
- **Eyecolor:** Blue, green, red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Powerful sensitivity to the Force
  - Shapeshifting
- **Lifespan:** Immortal (vulnerable to the Dagger of Mortis)
- **Origin:** Mortis (adopted realm)
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
