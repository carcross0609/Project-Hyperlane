# Mluki

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mluki](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mluki)

The Mlukis were a sentient primate species indigenous to Belsavis. They grew to adolescence at the age of seven and were considered old at the age of thirty. Jevax was a Mluki.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mluki
- **Eyecolor:** Deep-set green
- **Distinctions:** Primates
- **Lifespan:** 30+ years
- **Origin:** Belsavis
