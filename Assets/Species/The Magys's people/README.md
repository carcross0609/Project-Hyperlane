# The Magys's people

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [The Magys's people](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Magys%27s_people)

The Magys's people were a sentient species that resided within the Unknown Regions and hailed from the planet Sunrise. The Magys was a Force-sensitive member of the species.
