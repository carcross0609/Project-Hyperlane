# Ootoo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ootoo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ootoo)

Ootoos were a sentient species from Ootoo Prime. Ayva Ackler was an Ootoo miner who lived during the High Republic Era.
