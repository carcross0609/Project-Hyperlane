# Teek

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Teek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teek)

Teeks were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. At some point, a crashed ark brought Teeks to the moon of Endor. When Han Solo and Lando Calrissian were searching for a pilot on Frander's Bay, Solo pushed his way through a group of Teek before dodging a lumbering Kullp. A Teek worked at Port Borgo around 9 ABY.
