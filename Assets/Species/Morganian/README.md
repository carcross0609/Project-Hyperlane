# Morganian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Morganian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morganian)

The Morganians were a species from the Morgania system in the Outer Rim Territories. At least one Morganian attended a gala in the Palace of Jadalla on Waskiro in 228 BBY, and encountered the Sephi Tey Sirrek, who considered the individual to be "pretty" and "little". However, the Morganian informed the Harch Vulax of Sirrek's presence, complicating his mission.
