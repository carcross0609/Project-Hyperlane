# Aurra Sing's father's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aurra Sing's father's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurra_Sing%27s_father%27s_species)

This species had chalk white skin, sunken black eye sockets, and fingers with one more joint than a Human finger. Their long fingers were used for drawing blood. The lifespan of this species was much longer than that of a human. Aurra Sing's father was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Aurra Sing's father's species
- **Skincolor:** Alabaster
- **Distinctions:**
  - Dark, sunken eye sockets
  - Long fingers
