# Oblee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oblee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oblee)

Oblee were three-armed sapients indigenous to Oblis, although originally from the Unknown Regions.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Oblee
- **Height:** 1.6 meters
- **Skincolor:** Bright pink to chalky white
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four eyes
  - Three arms
- **Origin:** Oblis
- **Language:** Oblee
