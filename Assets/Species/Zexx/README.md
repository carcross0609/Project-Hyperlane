# Zexx

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zexx](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zexx)

Zexx were a gray-skinned species, featuring cone-like heads and long tusks. They were aggressive and unintelligent. It is possible they were related to the Esoomians.
