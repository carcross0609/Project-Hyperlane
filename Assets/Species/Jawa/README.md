# Jawa

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jawa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jawa)

Jawas were a nomadic sentient species of meter-tall furry humanoids native to the Outer Rim desert world of Tatooine. Jawas who migrated from Tatooine were referred to as Offworld Jawas.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jawa
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Height:** 1 meter (3 ft)
- **Mass:** 30 kilograms
- **Skincolor:** Black
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Yellow
  - Orange
  - Red
- **Lifespan:** 80 standard years
- **Origin:** Tatooine
- **Habitat:** Desert
- **Diet:** Omnivorous
- **Language:**
  - Jawaese
  - Jawa Trade Talk
