# Qhuloskian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Qhuloskian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qhuloskian)

Qhuloskians were a sentient species from Qhulosk. During the Imperial Era, they resisted the Galactic Empire, leading to a battle in the city of Howlan.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Qhuloskian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Four arms
- **Origin:** Qhulosk
