# Null

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Null (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Null_%28species%29)

The Nulls were a species of tall Near-Humans who inhabited the eponymous planet of Null in the Mid Rim. A fierce and individualistic people, they had a culture of hunters and stonemasons, and they based their planetary economy on hunting tourism. In 22 BBY, when the Clone Wars broke out between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, their planet seceded to the Confederacy, although much of the populace had a great respect for the Jedi, the devoted protectors of the Republic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Null
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Height:** Up to 2.88 meters
- **Distinctions:** Tall stature, heavy beards (in males)
- **Origin:** Null
