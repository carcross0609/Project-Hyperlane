# Gargantelle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gargantelle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gargantelle)

The Gargantelles were a species of six-armed giants that inhabited Boz Pity until their extermination at the beginning of the Ductavis Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gargantelle
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Height:** 20 meters
- **Distinctions:** Six arms, one or two eyes
- **Origin:** Boz Pity
