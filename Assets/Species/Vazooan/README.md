# Vazooan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vazooan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vazooan)

The Vazooans were a furry humanoid species that existed in the galaxy. Roona from the planet At Attin was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vazooan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:**
  - Green
  - Blue
  - Pink
  - Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Antennae (small ossicones)
  - Four arms
  - Large, pointed ears
  - Large, round, pupil-less eyes
