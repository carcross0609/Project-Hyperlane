# Reshtini

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Reshtini](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reshtini)

Reshtini was a sentient alien species. Members of the species could have orange eyes on the sides of their head and could have tan and blue skin. The species had a long neck, a proboscis, two small bumps on top of their heads, and two arms with multiple fingers. Kesmer Faloodi was a member of the species who ate noodles at the Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop on the planet Ueda in 132 BBY.

## Biology & Culture
