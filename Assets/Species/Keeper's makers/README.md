# Keeper's makers

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Keeper's makers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keeper%27s_makers)

The Keeper's makers were humanoids hailing from Keeper's World. They were a strictly non-violent people, and feared war. When a galactic war entered their system they chose to flee their planet. To restore their homeworld after the war's devastation, they created The Keeper, in the hopes that they might one day return. The Keeper created the androids Earth, Fire, Rain, and Wind in the image of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
