# Ociock

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ociock](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ociock)

The Ociocks were a sentient avian species that lived in the galaxy. A family of Ociocks including a female and her mother were imprisoned at Imperial Detention Center & Labor Camp LEG-817 on the planet Wobani during the time Jyn Erso was there.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
