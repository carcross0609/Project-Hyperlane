# Sephi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sephi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sephi)

Sephi were a humanoid sentient species, found on both Isobe and Thustra in the Thustra system, who boasted long life and an affinity with droids and machinery. They had long tapered ears that could twitch, swivel and flatten in accordance with the emotional state of the subject. Their skin tones included lavender and peach, and their eyes could be green or blue.
