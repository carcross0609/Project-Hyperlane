# Patitite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Patitite/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Patitite/Legends)

The Patitites, also known as the Pattuna, were a species of diminutive sentients native to the planet Patitite Pattuna.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Patitite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 0.4 meters
- **Skincolor:** Light blue, green, purple
- **Eyecolor:** Pink, yellow
- **Distinctions:** Bioluminescent stripes
- **Origin:** Patitite Pattuna
