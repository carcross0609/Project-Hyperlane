# Gran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gran/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gran/Legends)

The Gran were sentient mammalian humanoids native to the planet Kinyen, though they had colonies across the galaxy, including settlements on the planets Hok and Malastare.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Height:** 1.5 - 1.8 meters
- **Mass:** 80 kilograms
- **Skincolor:**
  - Blue
  - Red
  - Tan
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Black
  - Purple
- **Distinctions:** Three eyes and goat-like snout
- **Lifespan:** Up to 79 standard years
- **Origin:** Kinyen
- **Language:** Gran
