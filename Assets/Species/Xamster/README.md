# Xamster

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Xamster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xamster)

The Xamsters were a sentient species. The species had exceptional brains capable of thinking quickly and processing sensory data at high speed. The podracer Neva Kee was a Xamster.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Xamster
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue and pale
- **Distinctions:** Bat-like ears, bi-pointed beak,
