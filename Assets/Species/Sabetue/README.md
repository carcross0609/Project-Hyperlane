# Sabetue

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sabetue](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sabetue)

The Sabetue were a species, genderless members of which were distinguished by a white skin. One Sabetue was a member of a gang of criminals in Coronet City of the planet Corellia in 13 BBY. That year, the Sabetue and their fellow gang members unsuccessfully attempted to purchase several vials of the coaxium hyperfuel from the human thief Han before attacking and then pursuing him through the city.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sabetue
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White (genderless individuals)
- **Distinctions:** Claws on hands and feet
