# Nosaurian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nosaurian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nosaurian)

Nosaurians were a horned sentient species that was native to New Plympto and had red eyes and orange, white, and yellow skin. They were mainly found in forested environments. They were considered ill-tempered, and often refused to communicate in Galactic Basic Standard. They would instead converse in their native language, which consisted of loud barking and hissing.

- **Name:** Nosaurian
