# Talid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Talid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talid/Legends)

The Talid were a sentient species that were indigenous to the planet of Ando Prime. Ten-Abu Donba was a Talid.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Talid
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Haircolor:**
  - White
  - Blond
  - Brown (fur)
- **Distinctions:**
  - Six limbs
  - Four nostrils
- **Origin:** Ando Prime
