# Unidentified Angcord species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Angcord species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Angcord_species)

A sentient species was found to the planet Angcord, commonly described as the first Pilgrims of Angcord among the Angcord kingdom, and comprising the majority of the Pilgrim Warriors by 482 BBY. They had elongated heads, with blue skin, black or brown hair, and gray eyes.
