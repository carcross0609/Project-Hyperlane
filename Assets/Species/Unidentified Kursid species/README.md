# Unidentified Kursid species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Kursid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Kursid_species)

A sentient humanoid species resided on Kursid. In 104 BBY, Darth Plagueis and Darth Tenebrous fought members of the species—warriors specifically—and Plagueis later took his own Sith apprentice, Darth Sidious to murder members of this species with him.
