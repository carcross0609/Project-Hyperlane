# Fantanine

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fantanine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fantanine)

The Fantanines were a sentient species with insectoid features. They had long lifespans and a small population, which led them to strongly avoid conflict within their species. The Ben Diffle Fantanines were a group of Fantanines who operated a mining colony.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fantanine
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Skincolor:**
  - Brown
  - Green
- **Eyecolor:** Orange
- **Lifespan:** Twenty times longer than humans
