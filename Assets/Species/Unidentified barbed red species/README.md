# Unidentified barbed red species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified barbed red species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_barbed_red_species)

A sentient species with red skin existed in the galaxy. These aliens were humanoids that possessed two pointed barbs protruding from their faces, extending from the sides of their upper lips, as well as pointed ears black hair. One member of this species was present at The Pits on the moon of Shakari during or after 11 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified barbed red species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Facial barbs
  - Pointed ears
