# Pa'lowick

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pa'lowick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pa%27lowick)

Pa'lowicks were a sentient species of long-limbed reptilian humanoids that had spotted skin, eyes that protruded from the tops of their heads, and trunk-like mouths. Sy Snootles, the lead singer of the Max Rebo Band, was a Pa'lowick.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pa'lowick
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Skincolor:** Yellow, with blue or purple spots
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** Long limbs, protruding eyes, spotted skin, trunk-like mouths
- **Origin:** Lowick
- **Habitat:** Swamp
