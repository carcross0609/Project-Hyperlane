# Emmerian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Emmerian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emmerian/Legends)

The Emmerians were a stout race from Emmer who evolved from Humans. Due the their planet's surface being covered by large, fast-growing grasses, road-building was impractical, leading the Emmerians to rely on walkers for transportation, with many of their innovations with respect to this technology being incorporated into the Imperial walkers.
