# Weequay

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Weequay/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay/Legends)

Weequays were a race of humanoids who came from the Outer Rim planet of Sriluur. Their home was a harsh desert planet, leading to the species' tanned skin tone and rough, sandy, wrinkled skin. Their eyes were dark and slightly recessed into their skull.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Weequay
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.6 - 1.9 meters
- **Skincolor:** Tan to dark brown
- **Distinctions:** Pheromone communication
- **Lifespan:** Up to 90 standard years
- **Origin:** Sriluur
- **Language:** Sriluurian
