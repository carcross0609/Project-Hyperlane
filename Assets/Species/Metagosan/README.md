# Metagosan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Metagosan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Metagosan)

The Metagosan were a reptilian sentient species. Possessing dense musculature, they were similar to the Trandoshans. The species had a typical sibilant hiss when speaking. One Metagosan was Vin-Vin Sunfall, the bartender of Vin-Vin's Wheelhouse in the city New Xaxxis on the Outer Rim planet Metagos.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Metagosan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
