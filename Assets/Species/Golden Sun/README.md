# Golden Sun

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Golden Sun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Sun)

Golden Sun was a Force-sensitive communal intelligence made up of thousands of polyps that lived on a coral reef in the oceans of the planet Sedri. The native Sedrians worshipped Golden Sun, and built a dome to house its cave in the Sedrian capital city of Fitsay. The worship of Golden Sun was fundamental to Sedrian society, with the High Priest of the Golden Sun being the leader of all Sedrians.
