# Unidentified googly-eyed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified googly-eyed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_googly-eyed_species)

An extra-galactic sentient species with googly-eyes visited the galaxy during the Galactic Civil War. They pitted the crews of an Alliance to Restore the Republic corvette and an Imperial Star Destroyer against one another in a form of sporting contest. They declined to become involved in the war, however, as they were heading back to their home galaxy.

- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Googly-eyes
