# Ortolan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ortolan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ortolan)

Ortolans were a sentient elephantine species of squat, blue-skinned bipeds with large, floppy ears, beady black eyes, and a trunk-like nose that were native to the cold planet Orto. They consumed large amounts of food in order to develop a warm layer of blubber due to the frigid temperatures of their homeworld. Maximilian Rebo, founder of the Max Rebo Band, was a male Ortolan.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ortolan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Elephantine
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Sensitive ears, articulated nutrient-absorbing digits
- **Lifespan:** 71+ years
- **Origin:** Orto
- **Habitat:** Ice and snow
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
