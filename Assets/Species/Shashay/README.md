# Shashay

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shashay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shashay)

The Shashay were sentient avians indigenous to Crytal Nest planet in Wild Space. They were renowned for their natural skills as singers and astrogators, giving them the common nickname of Space Singers. They had a reputation for fiery tempers and a strong loyalty to their species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Shashay
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian
- **Height:** 1.3–1.6 meters
- **Feathers:** White and red
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:**
  - Sharp beak and long tail
  - Thick plumage and wings for gliding
  - Two pairs of taloned limbs
  - Affinity for singing and astrogation
- **Origin:** Crytal Nest
- **Language:** Shashay language
