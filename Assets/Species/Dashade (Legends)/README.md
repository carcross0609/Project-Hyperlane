# Dashade

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dashade/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dashade/Legends)

The Dashades were a race of large reptilian humanoids notable for their resistance to the powers of the Force.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dashade
- **Height:** 1.7 - 2 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Black
  - Dark gray
  - Olive green
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Orange
  - Red
  - Black
- **Distinctions:** Heat dissipation, high Force-resistance, radiation resistance
- **Lifespan:**
  - 85 standard years
  - Several known to have lived for many centuries
- **Origin:** Urkupp
- **Language:** Dashadi
