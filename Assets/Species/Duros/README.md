# Duros

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Duros](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duros)

The Duros were a humanoid species common in the galaxy native to the planet Duro. Notable Duros included bounty hunter Cad Bane who operated during the Clone Wars and Shriv Suurgav, a Duros who was a member of the New Republic Inferno Squad, and later the Resistance Inferno Squad.
