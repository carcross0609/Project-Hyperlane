# Sebiri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sebiri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sebiri)

The Sebiri were a reptilian sentient species native to the planet of Sebiris in the Kathol sector.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sebiri
- **Skincolor:** Greenish-brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Thick scaly hide
  - Nightvision
  - Enhanced olfactory senses
- **Origin:** Sebiris
