# Skakoan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skakoan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skakoan/Legends)

The Skakoans were a sentient species of humanoid mammals distinguished by their dependence on a bulky pressurized suit to survive away from their homeworld, Skako. A highly xenophobic people, the Skakoans rarely departed their homeworld unless need drove them.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Skakoan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Skincolor:**
  - Green
  - Greyish-white
- **Distinctions:** Atmospheric pressure sensitive
- **Origin:** Skako
- **Language:**
  - Galactic Basic Standard
  - Skakoform
  - Skakoverbal
