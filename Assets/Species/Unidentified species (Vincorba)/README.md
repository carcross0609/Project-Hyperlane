# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Vincorba)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Vincorba%29)

A sentient species resided on the Expansion Region planet Vincorba. The species was bipedal, possessing protrusions on either side of their face and tendrils atop their head. On Vincorba, a member of the species held the leading title of pontiff during the Imperial Era. The pontiff allied with the Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate, but was assassinated by members of the Assassins Guild.
