# Christophsian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Christophsian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Christophsian)

Christophsians were a sentient race native to the planet Christophsis. They looked similar to humans in appearance. During the Battle of Christophsis, Christophsian rebels fought against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. During the Clone Wars, they were represented by a senator in the Senate of the Galactic Republic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Christophsian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Christophsis
