# Courataine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Courataine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Courataine)

Courataines were a sentient species of humanoids from a homeworld with lower than standard gravity. Their bodies were wobbly-fleshed, resulting in them wearing exoskeletal travel suits when they traveled. On their homeworld, they breathed a very thin atmosphere; to simulate this offworld, they used respirators built into their suits for comfort. At some point between 2 BBY and 1 BBY, a number of Courataines were present on the planet Bonadan at the same time that the smuggler Han Solo was there investigating an enslavement ring.

## Biology & Culture
