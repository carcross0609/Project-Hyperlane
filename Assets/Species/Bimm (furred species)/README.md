# Bimm

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bimm (furred species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bimm_%28furred_species%29)

The Bimms were a sentient species characterized by their fur, feather-like whiskers and long, floppy ears. They spoke a language called Bimmini. A Bimm named Uumay Hawlatha served as a Jedi Master in the late Republic Era, while Voh was an elderly Bimm male who served as the seneschal of the Jedi outpost Sanctuary Mount on the planet Kwenn. A different species, similar to diminutive humans, was also called Bimms.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bimm
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammalian
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Feather-like whiskers
  - Long, floppy ears
- **Lifespan:** More than a century
- **Language:** Bimmini
