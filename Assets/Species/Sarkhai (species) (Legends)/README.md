# Sarkhai

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sarkhai (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarkhai_%28species%29/Legends)

The Sarkhai were a people native to the Mid Rim planet Sarkhai. Force-sensitives were uncommon among the Sarkhai, so much so that Nadia Grell was the only one known to have the abilities on her entire planet, though word of a child with similar traits reached her ears after she became a Padawan to the Barsen'thor of the Jedi Order.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sarkhai
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** White painted face with blue painted patterns
- **Origin:** Sarkhai
