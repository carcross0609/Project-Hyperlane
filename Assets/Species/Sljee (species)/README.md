# Sljee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sljee (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sljee_%28species%29)

The Sljees were nonhumanoid sentients indigenous to the planet Sljee.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sljee
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Length:** 1.4–2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Multiple limbs and tentacles, blindness, acute sense of smell
- **Origin:** Sljee
- **Language:** Sljee
