# Esh-kha

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Esh-kha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Esh-kha)

The Esh-kha were an ancient alien species that inhabited the galaxy before being imprisoned by the Rakata on the planet Belsavis thousands of years before the Cold War.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Esh-kha
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Haircolor:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Horizontal head protrusions
  - Tridactyl limbs
  - Digitigrade
- **Language:**
  - Esh-kha language
  - Rakata
