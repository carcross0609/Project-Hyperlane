# Cosian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cosian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cosian)

Cosians were a species of long-lived sentient reptilian humanoids from Cosia. Jedi Master Tera Sinube was a Cosian who served the Jedi Order from the High Republic Era to the Clone Wars.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cosian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Subspecies:** Nu-Cosian
- **Height:** 1.8 meters at full height
- **Skincolor:** Mottled green
- **Haircolor:** White to blue
- **Eyecolor:** Orange or yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Beaked mouths
  - Four-fingered hands
  - Long tails ended with tuft of hair
- **Lifespan:** Up to two hundred years
- **Origin:** Cosia
- **Language:** Cosian
