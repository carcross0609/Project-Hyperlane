# Monnok

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Monnok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Monnok)

Monnoks were a sentient biped species that were feared by many, including the desert nomads of Socorro. Although they looked primitive, they used spears, and were excellent hunters on their homeworld Socorro. Their appearance was used for one of the holographic pieces in the game dejarik, which had red and yellow skin, black eyes, and carried a staff. By at least 11 ABY, a Monnok gladiator was one of several creatures and gladiators belonging to species featured in dejarik that was unleashed upon the Mandalorian Din Djarin and Rotta the Hutt in the Pits on the moon Shakari before being slain by Djarin.
