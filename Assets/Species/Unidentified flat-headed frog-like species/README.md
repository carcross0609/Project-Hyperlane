# Unidentified flat-headed frog-like species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified flat-headed frog-like species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_flat-headed_frog-like_species)

A frog-like sentient species with green skin and a flat dome-shaped head existed in the galaxy. The species had two bulging yellow eyes with large black pupils, and scattered bumps on their head. They also had a thin long pink mouth that stretched to both sides of their face. The aliens had two thick legs and two thin arms that ended in three fingers and a thumb, with the tips of each colored orange.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified flat-headed frog-like species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Bulging eyes
  - Large black pupils
  - Thin wide lips
