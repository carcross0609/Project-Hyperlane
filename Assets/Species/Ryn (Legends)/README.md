# Ryn

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ryn/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ryn/Legends)

The Ryn were an uncommon humanoid species, with prehensile tails, beak-like noses, long fingers, and white hair. They were known for their musicality and diligent cleanliness.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ryn
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammalian
- **Height:** 1.4 - 1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Light purple to dark blue black
- **Haircolor:** Light brown to snow white
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Amber
  - Brown
  - Blue
  - Green
  - Hazel
- **Distinctions:** Huge tails, beak-like noses
- **Lifespan:** Up to 81 standard years
- **Origin:** Ryn homeworld
- **Language:**
  - Ryn
  - Galactic Basic Standard
