# Yresilini

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yresilini](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yresilini)

The Yresilini were a species found in the Unknown Regions of the galaxy. The species had at least one gender, which was masculine, had the capacity to laugh, and were regarded as untrainable creatures by the Chiss species.
