# Unidentified furry species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified furry species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_furry_species)

A furry, humanoid species lived in the galaxy. One eyepatch wearing member of this species was present at a cantina on Keyorin when Han Solo, Lando Calrissian, and Chewbacca visited. A representative of this species was also present at the First Conference of Free Peoples in 4 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified furry species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:**
  - Orange (possibly due to lighting)
  - Purple
- **Eyecolor:**
  - White
- **Distinctions:**
  - Claws
  - Long fur
