# Nebalite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nebalite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nebalite)

The Nebalites were a species of starfighter-sized sentient cephalopod. Nebalites had starships constructed that enveloped their bodies like suits of clothing. Grazdoc was a Nebalite trader who operated in a sector of the Unknown Regions and employed X-17-D, a small protocol droid who could not speak but could communicate through gestures, as an intermediary for face-to-face interactions.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nebalite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Cephalopod
- **Length:** Starfighter-scale
