# Anlari

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anlari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anlari)

The Anlari were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. They had fleshy horns on their heads that could curl and twist, and those that belonged to younger individuals were only as long as a human finger. G'kolu was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Anlari
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Fleshy horns
