# Orphne's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orphne's species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orphne%27s_species/Legends)

This reptilian, humanoid, alien species had green skin, purple eyes, brown, branchlike hair and resided in the Underworld of Aleen. A notable member of this species was Orphne.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Orphne's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.75 meters
- **Skincolor:** Light green, white patterning
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Purple
- **Distinctions:** Bulbous fingers, mottled skin, eyes lacking pupils, extensible tongue
- **Origin:** Aleen
