# Kitonak

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kitonak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kitonak)

The Kitonak were a sentient species with pink skin and small eyes that was native to the planet Kirdo III. Their bodies released a vanilla-like scent. They boasted thick, leathery skin, which allowed them to adapt to harsh climates as their tough skin protected them from the sun.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kitonak
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Origin:** Kirdo III
- **Habitat:** Deserts and wastelands
- **Language:** Kitonese
