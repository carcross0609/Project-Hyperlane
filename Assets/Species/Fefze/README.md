# Fefze

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fefze](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fefze)

The Fefze were an insectoid species who came from the planet Fef. While a single Fefze was non-sentient, swarms of Fefze collectively formed a sentient intelligence.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fefze
- **Designation:** Collective sentience
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Subspecies:** Fefze beetle
- **Height:** 1 meter tall
- **Length:** 1.5–3.0 meters
- **Skincolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Swarm intelligence, advanced digestion
- **Lifespan:** 10 years (individuals), centuries (swarms)
- **Origin:** Fef
