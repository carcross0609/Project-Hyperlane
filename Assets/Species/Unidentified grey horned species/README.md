# Unidentified grey horned species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified grey horned species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_grey_horned_species)

This species were grey-skinned humanoids with muzzles and cranial horns which sprouted from the back of their heads. A member of this species was a scavenger in Mos Espa who sold parts scavenged from destroyed podracers. A number of other members of this species served as guards under the authority of the Hutts in Mos Espa around 32 BBY. The species was forceful, but slow, and individuals were relatively obedient if paid to guard something or the like.
