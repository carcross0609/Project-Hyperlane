# Unidentified brutish blue species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified brutish blue species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_brutish_blue_species)

This humanoid species tended to be light blue in appearance. They had hairless heads except for sideburns across their cheeks. Their eyes tended to be mostly white, and they had relatively flat noses. They had a large stature, approximately twice that of the average Human. The Jedi Jastus Farr was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
