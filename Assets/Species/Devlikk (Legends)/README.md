# Devlikk

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Devlikk/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devlikk/Legends)

Devlikks were an avian species native to the planet Ord Radama. They had long necks, shoulders covered in green feathers, a small green tail, and oddly-shaped heads. The average lifespan for a Devlikk was about nine to 10 years; most Devlikk succumbed to senility at age 9. The Podracer Wan Sandage was of this species, as was his son Wan Sandage Jr., along with Raido, Sherloo, and Narralla.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Devlikk
- **Height:** ~1.4 meters
- **Distinctions:**
  - Green feathers
  - Short lifespan
  - Long claws
  - Short stature
- **Lifespan:** 10 years
- **Origin:** Ord Radama
