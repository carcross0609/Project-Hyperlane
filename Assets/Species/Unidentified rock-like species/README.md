# Unidentified rock-like species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified rock-like species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_rock-like_species)

A rock-like species that was humanoid in shape, with four limbs, was found on a planet with a rocky terrain. The species' bodies blended into the surface, appearing to be tall rock formations until they moved. They spoke and were intelligent enough to wield blasters.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified rock-like species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
