# Unidentified orange scaly species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified orange scaly species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_orange_scaly_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their orange skin and scales, with pointed ears, solid black eyes, large lips, and sharp teeth. In 4 ABY, two members of this species came into conflict with some Ubuugans in Gorb Drig's cantina on the planet Phorliss.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified orange scaly species
- **Skincolor:** Orange
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Point ears
  - Scales
  - Sharp teeth
