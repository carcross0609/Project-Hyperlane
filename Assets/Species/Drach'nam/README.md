# Drach'nam

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Drach'nam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drach%27nam)

The Drach'nam were a sentient reptilian alien race from La'drach that was known more for size and strength than intelligence. They had three fingers on each hand and three toes on each foot. Drach'nam had pale, pinkish blood.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Drach'nam
- **Designation:** Reptilian
- **Distinctions:** Tire-like mark running down center of face
- **Origin:** La'drach
