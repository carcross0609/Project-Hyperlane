# Dantari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dantari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dantari)

The Dantari were a primitive species of Near-Humans that hailed from the planet of Dantooine, in the Raioballo sector of the Outer Rim. They lived a simple nomadic life, wandering about the plains of Dantooine in tribes.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dantari
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Heavy muscle structure, large faces
- **Lifespan:** 70 years
- **Origin:** Dantooine
- **Language:** Dantarian
