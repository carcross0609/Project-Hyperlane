# Mandallian Giant

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mandallian Giant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandallian_Giant)

The Mandallian Giants were a race of large, heavily muscled humanoids who were bigger and bulkier than Wookiees.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mandallian Giant
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Greenish-turquoise
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Fin-like ears
- **Origin:** Mandallia
