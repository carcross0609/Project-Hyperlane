# Colicoid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Colicoid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Colicoid)

The Colicoids were a species of sentient, insectoid native to the planet Colla IV. Known for their completely emotionless and murderous ways, they created the design of the three-legged droidekas in their own image. They also led the Colicoid Creation Nest, a company that notably manufactured the Pistoeka sabotage droid and the Trident-class assault ship. The Trade Federation bought shipments of droidekas from the Colicoids in exchange for exotic meats.
