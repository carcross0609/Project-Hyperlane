# Molemen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Molemen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Molemen/Legends)

Molemen were sentient white-skinned blind humanoids that wore clothing. During a night in 22 BBY, three members of the species visited the Outlander Club, nightclub and gambling hall in the Uscru Entertainment District on the planet Coruscant. While the Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker interrogated the bounty hunter Zam Wesell outside the club, a tour guide led the molemen to the Outlander's Digisee Gaming Floor.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Molemen
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White
