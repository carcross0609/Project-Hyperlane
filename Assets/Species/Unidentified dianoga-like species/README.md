# Unidentified dianoga-like species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified dianoga-like species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_dianoga-like_species)

A sentient cephalopod species existed that had one eye mounted on a single eyestalk and a mouth with appendages on both sides at the base of their eyestalk. The species had anterior flipper-like appendages and a long, flexible tail. At least one member had reddish-brown skin with black stripes and a hazel eye. They were similar to the dianoga, a species native to the planet Vodran. The species' culture controlled their environment through terraforming abilities.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified dianoga-like species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Cephalopod
- **Skincolor:** Reddish-brown with black stripes
- **Eyecolor:** Hazel
- **Distinctions:**
  - One eye on a single eyestalk
  - Mouth at base of eyestalk with small appendages on sides
  - Anterior flipper-like appendages
  - Long, flexible tail
