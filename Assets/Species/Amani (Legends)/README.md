# Amani

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Amani/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amani/Legends)

The Amanin (singular, Amani), sometimes called Amanaman, were a primitive race from the planet Maridun. Amanin stood two to three meters tall, and had bright yellow and green skin, arms long enough to reach the ground, and very short legs. They were found in the galaxy at large as mercenaries, heavy laborers and wilderness scouts.
