# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Glavis Ringworld)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Glavis_Ringworld%29)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The humanoid species was distinguished by their reddish skin their elongated faces and noses, as well as large head of black hair. In 9 ABY, a member of this species was present on the Glavis Ringworld space station when the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin arrived with the severed head of the Klatooinian Kaba Baiz.

- **Skincolor:** Reddish
