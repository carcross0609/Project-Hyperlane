# Lo's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lo's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lo%27s_species)

A species of green-skinned aliens were native to the planet Botor. The ranks of the Flesh Mongers were made up of this species, as was the youngling Lo.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lo's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Bald head, slit nostrils
- **Origin:** Botor
