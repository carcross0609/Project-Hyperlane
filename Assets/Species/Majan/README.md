# Majan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Majan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Majan)

The Majan were a humanoid species from the planet of Baroonda. The majority of Majans lived in Baroo, a large city that encircled the Nazwa ruins. The Majans looked very much like baseline Humans, only with a few, subtle differences such as having three lobes per ear. All Majans had tan skin, with dark hair and eye color.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Majan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Haircolor:** Dark
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:** Three lobes per ear
- **Origin:** Baroonda
