# Wroonian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wroonian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wroonian)

Wroonians were near-human species that originated from the Wroona system. Around six millennia before the Battle of Yavin, Wroonians colonized Saleucami. Wroonians were similar in appearance to Pantorans, both having blue skin. This made the two species easy to confuse with one another.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Wroonian
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:** Light Blue
- **Origin:** Wroona system
