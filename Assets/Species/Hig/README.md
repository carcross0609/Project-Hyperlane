# Hig

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hig](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hig)

The Hig were a slender, blue-colored sentient species. The Hig Ginbotham served aboard the Yavin under Wedge Antilles' command, and saw action against the forces of the terrorist Kueller at Almania. Another Hig was a supporter of the Diversity Alliance, a pro-alien, anti-Human organization, donating her ore hauler to the cause.
