# Sy Myrthian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sy Myrthian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sy_Myrthian)

Sy Myrthians were a sentient species from Sy Myrth who moved on one large "foot" (similar to the Hutts). They were covered in short fur and had elongated heads. Later some Sy Myrthians joined the Confederacy of Independent Systems, although not all Sy Myrthians joined Count Dooku's movement. One part of the galaxy was known as Sy Myrthian space.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sy Myrthian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White and grey
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Origin:** Sy Myrth
