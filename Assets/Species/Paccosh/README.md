# Paccosh

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Paccosh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paccosh)

The Paccosh, also known as the Pacc, were a sentient species from the planet Rapacc. The Paccosh held females of their own and other species in high, almost venerated, regard. After a group of refugees led by the Magys, escaping a civil war on their homeworld, arrived at Rapacc, they were given shelter by the Paccosh. The Nikardun Destiny, led by General Yiv, blockaded Rapacc, having been manipulated by Haplif of the Agbui.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Paccosh
- **Designation:** Sentient
