# Locan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Locan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Locan)

The Locans were a sentient species of near-Humans that shared its name with the Wild Space celestial body Loce. They had pointed ears, prominent incisor teeth, and distinctive light purplish-blue skin. One Locan, Talas Piran, had a thick body frame and a heavy build with large arms the skin on which was black and mottled. Piran had short black hair. Locans identified as belonging to at least one—male—gender.
