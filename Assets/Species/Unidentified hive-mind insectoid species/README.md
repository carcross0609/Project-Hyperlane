# Unidentified hive-mind insectoid species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified hive-mind insectoid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_hive-mind_insectoid_species)

A species of sentient grasshopper-like beings produced sound and speech by rubbing together their legs. Members of the species shared several bodies via single hive mind. During the Galactic Civil War, one such alien worked as a bartender and at one point sold information to the smuggler Roark Garnet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified hive-mind insectoid species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Length:** Grasshopper-sized
- **Distinctions:**
  - Hive mind
  - Produces speech by rubbing legs together
