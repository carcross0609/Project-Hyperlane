# Vahla

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vahla](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vahla)

The Vahla were a nomadic, near-Human species who roamed the galaxy in search of their homeworld. They were tall and slender, and possessed cartilaginous skeletons, which gave them exceptional flexibility. All were Force-sensitive, and they were regarded as having a natural shrewdness. Many Vahla belonged to a dark-side cult called the Ember of Vahl, and all Vahla were expected to heed the dictates of the cult's central coven.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vahla
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Dusky brown to dark gray
- **Haircolor:** Combinations of black, gray, and white; rarely, red
- **Eyecolor:** Pale shades of blue, gray, or lavender
- **Distinctions:** Force-sensitivity, cartilaginous skeletons
- **Origin:** Lost Vahla homeworld
- **Language:** Vahla
