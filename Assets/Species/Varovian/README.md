# Varovian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Varovian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Varovian)

Varovians were a sentient species from the planet Varovia. Naarok of the Varovian Order of Seers was a Varovian.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Varovian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Distinctions:**
  - Three eyes
  - One tusk
- **Origin:** Varovia
