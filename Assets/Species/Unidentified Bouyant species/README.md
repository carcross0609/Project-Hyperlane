# Unidentified Bouyant species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Bouyant species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Bouyant_species)

A sentient species was at one point present on the planet Buoyant. The species terraformed the planet, causing it to rotate faster. The species also used a number of machines on Buoyant and imported various animal species to the world.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Buoyant
