# Fluggrian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fluggrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fluggrian)

The Fluggrians were a sentient species with yellow eyes and green, purple, white and pink skin that was native to Ploo IV. Elan Mak was a Fluggrian podracer who was active on Tatooine.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fluggrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Green
  - Purple
  - White
  - Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Origin:** Ploo IV
