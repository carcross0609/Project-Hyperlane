# H'nemthe

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [H'nemthe (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H%27nemthe_%28species%29)

H'nemthe were a reptilian sentient species with sharp tongues. M'iiyoom Onith was a member of this species, and she was courted by the Gotal tax collector Feltipern Trevagg, who did not know the dangers of romancing a H'nemthe.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** H'nemthe
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Sharp tongues
- **Origin:** H'nemthe system
