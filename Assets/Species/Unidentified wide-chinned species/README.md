# Unidentified wide-chinned species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified wide-chinned species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_wide-chinned_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. They were distinguished by their elongated faces and wide, ribbed chins. One member of this species was present at Grappa the Hutt's palace in 11 ABY.
