# Quermian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quermian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quermian/Legends)

Quermians were a mammalian species of sentients hailing from the planet Quermia after being genetically designed from Xexto DNA by the Arkanian race.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Quermian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammalian
- **Height:** 2.4 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Brown
  - Pasty white
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Blue
  - Red
  - Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Extended necks
  - Two brains
- **Lifespan:**
  - Child: 1–11 years
  - Young adult: 12–16 years
  - Adult: 17–42 years
  - Middle age: 43–65 years
  - Old: 66–85 years
  - Venerable: 86+ years
- **Origin:** Quermia
- **Language:** Quermian
