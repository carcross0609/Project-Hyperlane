# Muttani

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Muttani](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Muttani)

Muttani were a species from the planet Kalidorn in Wild Space. The Muttani had a sickly appearance and peculiar body odor. Their bodies were generally hairless, and their mouths were filled with sharp teeth.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Muttani
- **Skincolor:** Greenish-gray
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Large sharp teeth, pointed ears, sickly appearance and peculiar body odor
- **Origin:** Kalidorn
