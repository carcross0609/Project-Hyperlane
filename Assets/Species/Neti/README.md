# Neti

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neti)

The Neti were a mythical sentient species that appeared similar to trees. A number of Neti supposedly resided on the forest moon of the planet Ossus. According to the Qel-Droma Epics, T'alla was a Neti Jedi Master who constructed the Gardens of T'alla within the Great Jedi Library on Ossus, subsequently planting herself in the center and transforming into a tree.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Neti
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Botanical
- **Distinctions:** Similarity to trees
