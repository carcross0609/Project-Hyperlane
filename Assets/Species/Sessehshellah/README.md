# Sessehshellah

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sessehshellah](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sessehshellah)

The Sessehshellah, or Sesseh, were a fierce aboriginal race of mustalids native to the planet Shaymore. Though skilled at swimming, combat, and stealth, the Sessehshellah had little in the way of advanced technology; resultantly, they were easy prey for the Galactic Empire, which exploited Shaymore's hydrocarbons. The Sessehshellah were subsequently aided by the Rebel Alliance, who supplied them with modern weapons; both parties worked together in a successful bid to derail the hydrocarbon extraction effort and force the Imperials off Shaymore.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sessehshellah
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mustalid
- **Height:** Three feet
- **Distinctions:** Resemblance to the river otter
- **Origin:** Shaymore
- **Language:** Galactic Basic
