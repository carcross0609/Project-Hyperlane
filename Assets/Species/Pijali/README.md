# Pijali

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pijali](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pijali)

The Pijali were a cultural group of humans from the Inner Rim planet Pijal and its moon. By 40 BBY, a group of Pijali known as the Opposition began to protest the signing of the Governance Treaty, which would form a democratic Assembly that would lack proper representation for the Pijali citizens of the planet's moon.
