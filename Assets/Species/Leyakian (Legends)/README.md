# Leyakian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Leyakian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leyakian/Legends)

Leyakians were a humanoid race. One notable member of this species was By Bluss, a senator of the Separatist Parliament during the Clone Wars.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Leyakian
- **Skincolor:** Yellow-green
