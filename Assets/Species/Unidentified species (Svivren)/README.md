# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Svivren)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Svivren%29)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their red skin and thin, elongated heads with large bluish-gray eyes situated on the sides. In 4 ABY, at least four members of this species could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren, while at least three others disembarked from a transport known as the Tippler's Groan onto the planet Phorliss shortly thereafter.
