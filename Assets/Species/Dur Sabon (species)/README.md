# Dur Sabon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dur Sabon (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dur_Sabon_%28species%29)

The Dur Sabon were a species of tall, eel-like sentients native to the planet of the same name.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dur Sabon
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** Twice the height of an average human
- **Skincolor:** Bluish-green, gray, purple
- **Eyecolor:** Orange
- **Distinctions:**
  - Eel-like body
  - A pair of muscular, five-fingered hands with webbing
  - Several fins near the head
- **Origin:** Dur Sabon
