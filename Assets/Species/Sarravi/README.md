# Sarravi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sarravi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarravi)

The Sarravi were a sentient species in the galaxy. In 382 BBY, the Sarravi V'Kara worked in the Shrine of Sarrav on Jedha, before she was killed.
