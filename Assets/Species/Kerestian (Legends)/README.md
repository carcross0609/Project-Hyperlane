# Kerestian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kerestian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kerestian/Legends)

The Kerestians were a species of humanoids, best known for their mastery of a unique weapon called the darkstick. They were descended from mammalian carnivores on the planet Kerest.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kerestian
- **Races:** Civilized and barbaric groups
- **Height:** 2.15 meters
- **Skincolor:** Yellow to tan
- **Haircolor:**
  - Black
  - Dark brown
- **Eyecolor:** Emerald green
- **Distinctions:** Breather tubes on face, darkstick use
- **Origin:** Kerest
- **Language:** Kerestese
