# Araquia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Araquia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Araquia)

The Araquia were arachnid sapients indigenous to Kirtania.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Araquia
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Arachnid
- **Length:** 2 meters with a leg span of up to three meters
- **Skincolor:** Greenish-brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Origin:** Kirtania
- **Habitat:** Rain forests
- **Diet:** Rodents, insects and birds
