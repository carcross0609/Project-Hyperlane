# Kete

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kete](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kete)

The Ketes were a large insectoid species that resided on the forest moon of Endor. A group of Ketes lived inside a hive on the Endor moon, although they usually lived in spiral mounds made out of a sticky substance.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kete
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Origin:** Endor
- **Habitat:** Kete hive
