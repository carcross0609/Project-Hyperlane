# Nuxan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nuxan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nuxan)

The Nuxan were a sentient humanoid species from the Nux system in the Mid Rim Territories. They were distinguished by their unpleasant, rotten odor. The species possessed green skin riddled with brown spots, a pair of green eyes which had brown markings directly above and below, a pair of five-fingered hands, and fin on the back of their heads. Bodo Linx was a Nuxan informant during the Imperial Era who could speak Galactic Basic Standard.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nuxan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Rotten odor
  - Head fins
  - Brown spots around their body
  - Face markings above and below their eyes
- **Origin:** Nux system
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
