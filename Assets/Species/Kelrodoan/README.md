# Kelrodoan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kelrodoan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kelrodoan)

The Kelrodoans were a sentient species native to the planet Kelrodo-Ai. During the Western Reaches Operation in 17 BBY, the forces of the Galactic Empire attacked Kelrodo-Ai and several Kelrodoans, including the warrior chieftain Fara, were assigned to act as grooms for the pilots of the Empire's Sword Squadron.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kelrodoan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Origin:** Kelrodo-Ai
