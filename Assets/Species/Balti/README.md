# Balti

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Balti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balti)

The Balti were a species of furry bipeds with prominent tusks. The smuggler Bingo Mehndra was a Balti.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Balti
- **Haircolor:** Light blue
- **Distinctions:** Prominent tusks
