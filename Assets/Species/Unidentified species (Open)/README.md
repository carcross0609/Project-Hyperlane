# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Open)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Open%29)

This species was a humanoid sentient species that could be found on the icy planet of Carnelion IV. This bipedal near-human species was distinguished by their pale skin and blue or silver hair. These aliens were one of two species descended from an advanced civilization that had called Carnelion IV home. At some point prior to the Invasion of Naboo, these ancestors had engaged in a civil war resulting in their seeming extinction, though unbeknownst to the outside galaxy due to the harsh debris field surrounding the planet, the surviving factions would evolve into the two near-human species that made up the Open and Closed groups, this one making up the ranks of the former, while other nearby human settlers would instead mutate into the Celadon Sea's Fisher species.
