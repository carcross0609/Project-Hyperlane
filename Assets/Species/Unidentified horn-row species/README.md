# Unidentified horn-row species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified horn-row species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_horn-row_species)

A species that existed in the galaxy. This bipedal humanoid sentient species was distinguished by the row of horns that extended from their forehead up and over the top of their craniums. By 138 ABY, a member of this species served as a Sith Lord among the One Sith and Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire, defeated and left to die that year on the planet Daluuj after a confrontation with Cade Skywalker and his allies.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified horn-row species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Cranial horns
