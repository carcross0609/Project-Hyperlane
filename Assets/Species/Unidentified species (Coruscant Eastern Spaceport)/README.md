# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Coruscant Eastern Spaceport)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Coruscant_Eastern_Spaceport%29)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their yellow, blue, and pink skin, as well as their long necks and elongated faces. In 5 BBY, one member of this species was present at the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport on the planet Coruscant.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow, blue, and pink
