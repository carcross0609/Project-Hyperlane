# Unidentified four-eyed tendrilled species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified four-eyed tendrilled species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_four-eyed_tendrilled_species)

The species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their elongated heads, with four dark or yellow eyes, as well as a cluster of tendrils dangling from the bottoms of their faces. They also possessed serrated, claw-like hands, and green pigmentation. At least one member of this species was present on the planet Coruscant in 22 BBY, while another visited Bezz Drexx's tower around the same time during the Clone Wars.
