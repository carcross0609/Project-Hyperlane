# Felin

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Felin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Felin)

The Felin were a sentient species originating from the planet Heleth 4. Greel Ptavvishh was known as the only great Felin philosopher and was credited with saving the Felins from extinction by reorganizing the government to prevent brutal wars between clans.
