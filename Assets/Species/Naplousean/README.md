# Naplousean

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Naplousean](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naplousean)

Described as a tangle of flesh, the Naplousean walked on three legs and possessed many glittering eyes. It is theorized that when they also relied on pheromones to communicate, evidenced as a dull smell would accompany their anger.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Naplousean
- **Haircolor:** Orange
- **Distinctions:**
  - Multiple eyes
  - Three legs
- **Origin:** Naplousean system
- **Language:** Accented Galactic Basic Standard
