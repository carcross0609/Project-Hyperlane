# Tefaun

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tefaun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tefaun)

Tefauns were a blue-skinned, sentient humanoid species with ridged heads and bulging eyes. The Tefaun banker Kelek purchased the Wheel, a space station gambling resort, launching a brutal gladiatorial competition called the Big Game. When the Wheel lost clientele and experienced reduced revenues, Kelek borrowed money from the InterGalactic Banking Clan to win back customers but was unable to meet her payment schedule, forcing her to sell her shares to businessman Dominic Raynor. During the Galactic Civil War, Soth Petikkin from Tefau served as a persuasive recruiter for the Rebel Alliance, using his contacts in crime lord Jabba the Hutt's Palace on the planet Tatooine to gain support for the Alliance.
