# Arkanian Offshoot

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arkanian Offshoot](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arkanian_Offshoot)

The Arkanian Offshoots were a sub-species of the Arkanian race, genetically engineered by the famed Arkanian scientists. The Jedi Knight Dagan Gera was a member of the sub-species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Arkanian Offshoot
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Arkanian
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Haircolor:** Silver-gray
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Origin:** Arkania
