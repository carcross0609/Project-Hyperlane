# Alazmec

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Alazmec](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alazmec)

The Alazmec were a sentient species on the planet Mustafar. The Alazmec of Winsit were Sith cultists who honored Darth Vader by protecting the ruins of his castle and a nearby bog, Corvax Fen. Loriach was a Alazmec sniper who was a member in the Assassin's Guild.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
