# Hysalrian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hysalrian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hysalrian/Legends)

Hysalrians were a species of four-armed, four-eyed, semi-serpentine individuals who hailed from a swamp world and the Hysalria system. One of their members, N'Kata Del Gormo, trained eventual Jedi Grand Master Yoda in the ways of the Force.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hysalrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptile
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Serpentine lower halves
  - Quadruple eyes and arms
- **Origin:** A swamp planet
