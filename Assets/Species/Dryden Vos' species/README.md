# Dryden Vos' species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dryden Vos' species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dryden_Vos%27_species)

Dryden Vos was a member of a near-human species which were nearly identical to baseline humans with the exception of their facial markings, which would change color when they were angry, and their clawed thumbs.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dryden Vos' species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Haircolor:** Blond
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:**
  - Color-changing facial markings
  - Clawed thumbs.
