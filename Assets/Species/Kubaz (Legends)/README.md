# Kubaz

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kubaz/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kubaz/Legends)

The Kubaz or Kubazian were an insectivorous species native to the planet Kubindi in the Outer Rim Territories. Although relatively new to the galactic community at the time of the institution of the Galactic Empire, the species was already ubiquitous throughout the Republic during the Great Galactic War and the Clone Wars.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kubaz
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammalian
- **Height:** 1.7-2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Greenish-black
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Able to see into the infrared spectrum
  - Prehensile snouts
- **Lifespan:** 81 years
- **Origin:** Kubindi
- **Diet:** Insects
- **Language:** Kubazian
