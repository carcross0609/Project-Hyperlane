# Phydolon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phydolon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phydolon)

The Phydolon were a paired symbiotic sentient species native to the planet Mydasos that existed in the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Phydolon
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Eyecolor:** White iris with dark sclera and white fungal threads
- **Distinctions:** Symbiotic fungus/humanoid, gangly limbs, three fingers
- **Origin:** Mydasos
