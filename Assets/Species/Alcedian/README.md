# Alcedian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Alcedian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alcedian)

Alcedians were an avian sentient species of humanoids with light gray skin that originated from the Outer Rim planet Valo. At one point the species had wings though by the time of the High Republic Era, the wings had been lost but some kept some of their feathers on their shoulders and arms. During this time, two Jedi Initiates—Kildo and Tep Tep—were members of the species. During the Imperial Era, the archaeologist Kho Phon Farrus was a member of the species.
