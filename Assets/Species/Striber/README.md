# Striber

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Striber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Striber)

Stribers were an aquatic species originally native to Julsujod III. The Stribers relocated to the water-covered planet Iskalon to escape a cataclysm that struck their homeworld around five millennia before the Galactic Civil War, and became a part of the Iskalonian School, a cultural group of several aquatic species that had traveled to Iskalon with the aid of spacefaring benefactors. Blue-skinned and telepathic, Stribers were known as very slow to react when addressed, as they preferred to carefully examine all aspects of a situation before acting. By the Galactic Civil War, the Stribers were the rarest species among the School, and biologists estimated that they would die out completely within the next few generations.
