# Kessurian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kessurian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kessurian)

Kessurians were a sentient species that originated in the Kessur system. They had red skin and elongated ears and montrals, which increased their senses.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kessurian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Haircolor:**
  - Brown
  - Grey
  - Red-brown
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Black
  - Grey
  - Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Elongated ears with keen hearing
  - Montrals to aid with space-perception
- **Origin:** Kessur system
