# Sea-dragon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sea-dragon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sea-dragon)

The sea-dragons or waterworms of Drexel were gigantic sentient reptiles who dwelt in the never-ending ocean of their world. They communicated with each other using ultrasonic signals.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sea-dragon
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red, green yellow or purple
- **Distinctions:** Reptilian, large-sized adults
- **Origin:** Drexel
- **Language:** Ultra-sonic communication
