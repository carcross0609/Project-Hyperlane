# Ubuugan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ubuugan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ubuugan)

Ubuugans were a sentient species from the planet Ubuuga. They had grayish skin, three eyes, and a long snout. The Ubuugans developed a toxic secretion that protected them from the deadly Ubuugan fleshborers. Some of them even considered the fleshborers as a delicacy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ubuugan
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Three eyes
- **Origin:** Ubuuga
