# Rhen Varite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rhen Varite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rhen_Varite)

The Rhen Varites were a species indigenous to Rhen Var, where they built beautiful cathedral-like buildings. They went extinct when their homeworld entered an ice age.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Rhen Varite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Rhen Var
