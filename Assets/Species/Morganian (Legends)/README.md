# Morganian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Morganian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morganian/Legends)

The Morganians were a species of slender, white-skinned Humanoids. They had pointed ears and were renowned for their beauty.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Morganian
- **Skincolor:** White
