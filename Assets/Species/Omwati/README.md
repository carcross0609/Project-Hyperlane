# Omwati

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Omwati](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omwati)

Omwati were a sentient species whose members had a crown of feathers atop their heads, spindly fingers, and beaks which clacked during speech. Shi Shu, the second of the pirate captain Eleodie Maracavanya, was an Omwati.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Omwati
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Crown of feathers, spindly fingers, beaks
- **Origin:** Omwat system
