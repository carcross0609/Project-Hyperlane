# Karvathian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Karvathian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karvathian)

Karvathians were a sentient species from the Karvathia system that could hear high-pitched sounds. They created the music style trusk, which, during the time of the Old Republic, was originally performed in dingy establishments at pitches that only members of the species could hear.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Karvathian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Ability to hear higher pitches
- **Origin:** Karvathia system
