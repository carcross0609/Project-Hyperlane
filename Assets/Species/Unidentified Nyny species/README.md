# Unidentified Nyny species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Nyny species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Nyny_species)

The Outer Rim planet Nyny was the homeworld of a sentient species with three heads. When wounded, the species leaked out black ichors. In 17 ABY, a member of the species represented Nyny in the New Republic Senate. In the bombing of the Senate Hall on the planet Coruscant that year,—orchestrated by the darksider Kueller in an attempt to incapacitate the New Republic—the senator was killed. Han Solo, the husband of the Chief of State Leia Organa Solo, saw the senator lying on the lawn outside the Senate Hall, with all three of his heads bent backward at an awkward angle.
