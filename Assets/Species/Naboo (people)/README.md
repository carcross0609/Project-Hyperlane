# Naboo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Naboo (people)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naboo_%28people%29)

The Naboo (singular "Naboo"), also known as Naboolians or Nabooians, were a cultural group of humans from the planet Naboo. Contrary to the native Gungans, with whom they shared their world, the Naboo originated offworld as human colonists from Grizmallt. The Naboo aristocrats were known for their sartorial extravagance. Unlike the Gungans, who were a proud warrior species, the human colonists of Naboo were peaceful. This contrast of ideology caused tension between the two civilizations for centuries.
