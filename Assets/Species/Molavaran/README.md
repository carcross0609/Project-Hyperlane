# Molavaran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Molavaran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Molavaran)

Molavarans were a sentient species indigenous to Molavar. They had caramel colored skin and a long face. Their scalps had multiple tendrils on the sides and back of their head. They bore pointed teeth and large cheek bones.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Molavaran
- **Skincolor:** Caramel
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long faces
  - Tendrils
- **Origin:** Molavar
