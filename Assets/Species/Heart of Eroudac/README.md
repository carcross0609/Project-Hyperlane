# Heart of Eroudac

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heart of Eroudac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heart_of_Eroudac)

The Heart of Eroudac were a sentient species native to the planet Eroudac. They were white, pink, and green, with hair that imitated thorny plants. They buried themselves underground within a location known as the Oasis, waiting travelers who came searching for mythical flowers.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Heart of Eroudac
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Haircolor:**
  - Pink
  - Green
- **Distinctions:** Plant-like hair
- **Origin:** Eroudac
- **Habitat:** The Oasis
