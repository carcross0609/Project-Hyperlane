# Tursha

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tursha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tursha)

The Tursha were a humanoid species distinguished by their head-tails and eleven-fingered hands. One of them was a bounty hunter who tried to capture Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker on their training exercise on Ragoon-6 using a stokhli stick to paralyze them. He was killed in 27 BBY with a fast poison injected in the neck.
