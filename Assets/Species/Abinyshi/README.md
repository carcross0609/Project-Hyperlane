# Abinyshi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Abinyshi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abinyshi)

Abinyshi were a sentient species native to the planet of Inysh.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Abinyshi
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Height:** 1.2–1.6 meters
- **Skincolor:** Yellow-green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Pupil-less eyes
  - Prehensile two-forked tail
- **Origin:** Inysh
