# Giants of living stone

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Giants of living stone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Giants_of_living_stone)

Giants of living stone were a species of giants made of a type of blue stone imbued with the Living Force that were native to a planet in the Vagadarr system.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Giants of living stone
- **Origin:** Vagadarr Prime
