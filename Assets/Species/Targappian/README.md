# Targappian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Targappian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Targappian)

The Targappians were a species of sentient humanoids characterized by their webbed fingers and iridescent hair. Four years after the Invasion of Naboo, their homeworld of Targappia was represented in the Senate of the Galactic Republic by a female senator.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Targappian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Haircolor:** Iridescent
- **Distinctions:** Tall with webbed fingers
- **Origin:** Targappia
