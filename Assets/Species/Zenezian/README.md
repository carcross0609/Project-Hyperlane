# Zenezian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zenezian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zenezian)

Zenezians were a sentient species. The musician Infrablue Zedbeddy Coggins was a Zenezian, as was Sudswater Dillifay Glon. It was considered a major insult to refuse a musical instrument from a Zenezian.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Zenezian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Origin:** Zenezia system
