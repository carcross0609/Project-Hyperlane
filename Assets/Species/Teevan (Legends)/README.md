# Teevan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Teevan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teevan/Legends)

Teevans were a species from Teevan. Their bodies were highly flexible and able to bend in ways most humanoids found difficult to impossible. They also had silver-hued skin. Jedi apprentice Tru Veld was a Teevan.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Teevan
- **Skincolor:** Silver-hued
- **Distinctions:** Increased flexibility
- **Origin:** Teevan
- **Language:** Teevo
