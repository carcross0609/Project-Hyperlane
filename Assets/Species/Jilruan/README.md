# Jilruan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jilruan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jilruan)

The Jilruans were a sentient species native to a planet located in the Jilrua system in Hutt Space that was conquered by the Hutt Cartel, which was controlled by the Hutts from the neighboring world of Nal Hutta, who turned them into slaves or vassals.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jilruan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** A planet in the Jilrua system
