# Gazian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gazian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gazian)

Gazian was a living planet in the Mid Rim's Gazian system. It was covered by the Living Sea, a large, deep growth of mushrooms with hallucinatory spores. The area was strong in the Force.
