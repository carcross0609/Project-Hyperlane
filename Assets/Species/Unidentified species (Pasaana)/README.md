# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Pasaana)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Pasaana%29)

A sentient species had three eyes and an upright body build with two arms. The aliens had white skin, pink lips, no visible nose, and prominent eyelids surrounding their black eyes that had no visible sclerae.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Three eyes
