# Kadrillian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kadrillian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kadrillian/Legends)

Kadrillians were sentient terrapins who colonized Kadril in 6 BBY. They were the only inhabitants of Kadril to survive the Stenax Massacres.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kadrillian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptile
- **Subspecies:** Nociv
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Black
