# Faksir's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Faksir's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Faksir%27s_species)

The Nihil Faksir was a member of a sentient species with bright orange blood. The species was slightly more resilient to the poison that Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro used to kill Faksir, as he did not dissolve from the inside-out as Ro had expected. Their blood was also slightly acidic, as it had burned Ro when Faksir had spat some of his blood into the Eye's face.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Faksir's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Bright orange blood
  - Acidic blood
  - Slight resilience to some poisons
