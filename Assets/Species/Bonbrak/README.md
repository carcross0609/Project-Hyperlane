# Bonbrak

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bonbrak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bonbrak)

Bonbraks, also known as the barat-karabak, were a diminutive hairy sentient species native to the planet Bartokan V. They were classified as a protected species by the Galactic Republic. Several Bonbraks worked in the garage of the Jedi Padawan Ram Jomaram during the High Republic Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bonbrak
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Origin:** Bartokan V
- **Language:** Bonbreez
