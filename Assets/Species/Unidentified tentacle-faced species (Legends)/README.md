# Unidentified tentacle-faced species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified tentacle-faced species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_tentacle-faced_species/Legends)

A sentient species characterized by tentacle-like growths on its members' faces, was found on several planets in the galaxy around the times of the Clone Wars.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified tentacle-faced species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Tan
  - Blue
  - Green
  - Purple
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Tentacle-like facial growths
  - Stout hands and feet with no nails
