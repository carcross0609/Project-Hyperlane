# Nothoiin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nothoiin (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nothoiin_%28species%29)

The Nothoiins were a species of gold-skinned humanoids that hailed from the planet of the same name. Long before the Galactic Civil War, the Nothoiin aristocrats founded the Noble Court that ruled the entire Anoat sector. By the time of the Iron Blockade, the Court-in-exile was more diverse in species, but its leader, only known as Her Majesty, was still a Nothoiin.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nothoiin
- **Designation:** Sentient
