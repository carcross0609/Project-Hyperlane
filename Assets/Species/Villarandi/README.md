# Villarandi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Villarandi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Villarandi)

Villarandi were a sentient insectoid species that had segmented bodies and multiple limbs. The species had excellent hearing. Kradon Minst was a male Villarandi who owned the Enlightenment tapbar in the Holy City of the Moon Jedha.
