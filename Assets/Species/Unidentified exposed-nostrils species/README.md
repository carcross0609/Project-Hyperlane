# Unidentified exposed-nostrils species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified exposed-nostrils species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_exposed-nostrils_species)

This species was a sentient species that was found within the galaxy. These bipedal humanoid sentients were distinguished by their dark green skin covered in bumps, as well as stalky muscular builds, with heads featuring wideset solid black eyes, large white teeth, exposed nostrils, and a fleshy facial flap connecting their jowls to their nose ridge, in addition to three distinct cranial ridges. By around 3 ABY, a group of members of this species belonging to the Predak Vane criminal group confronted Chelli Lona Aphra and company at Club Lazuli in Canto Bight on the planet Cantonica, claiming she destroyed their ship before engaging their targets in a losing brawl.
