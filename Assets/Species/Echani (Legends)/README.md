# Echani

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Echani/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani/Legends)

The Echani were a Near-Human species from the Inner Rim planet Eshan. The Sun Guard arose from the Thyrsian subspecies of the Echani.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Echani
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Subspecies:** Thyrsians
- **Skincolor:** Chalk-pale, dark
- **Haircolor:** White, dark
- **Eyecolor:** Silver
- **Distinctions:** Light skin, white hair and eyes, remarkable familial similarity.
- **Origin:** Eshan
