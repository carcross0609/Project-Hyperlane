# Galacian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Galacian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galacian)

The Galacians were humanoids from the planet Gala. Their skin was a distinctive pale bluish tone, which they called "moonlight." They were therefore frequently called "moon people."

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Galacian
- **Races:** Hill people
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Origin:** Gala
