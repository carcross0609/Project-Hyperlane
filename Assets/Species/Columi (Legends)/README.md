# Columi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Columi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Columi/Legends)

The Columi were one of the oldest sentient species in the galaxy. They were easily recognized by their large heads (which made up over one-third of their approximately 1.75 meter height) and their vestigial arms and legs.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Columi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.0 – 1.75 meters
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Very large cranium, atrophied limbs
- **Origin:** Columus
