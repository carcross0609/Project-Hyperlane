# Imroosian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imroosian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imroosian)

Imroosians were a sentient species that existed within the galaxy from the volcanic planet Imroosia. Imroosians developed flinty, chalk-like skin, cracked like dried out desert ground, to adapt to their heated climate and were hairless.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Imroosian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Flinty, chalk-like skin
  - Adapting to their heated climates
- **Origin:** Imroosia
- **Language:** Imroosian
