# Elfish humanoid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Elfish humanoid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elfish_humanoid)

These elvish humanoids were a sentient species that lived on a small planetoid. They were ruled by a king until he was murdered during the invasion by the Yahk-Tosh warlord Lord Gar-Oth. The king's daughter, Princess Lourdes, managed to kill the invader with the help of Jedi Knight Yoshi Raph-Elan.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Elfish humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Fair
- **Haircolor:** Blonde
- **Eyecolor:** Amber
- **Distinctions:**
  - Humanoid appearance
  - Pointed ears
- **Origin:** Lourdes' homeworld
