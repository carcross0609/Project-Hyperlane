# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (seven-ringed homeworld)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28seven-ringed_homeworld%29)

A sentient humanoid species indigenous to a seven-ringed world that had green skin, large ears, and a horn on their faces. One member of this race once swore by their planet's rings.
