# Gensang

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gensang](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gensang)

The Gensang were a sentient race. A spice thief of this species acquired a Sekotan Personal Starship at some point before 29 BBY from an unfortunate young Rodian who had been murdered by the guardsmen of the emperor-governor of Serpine.
