# Vindalian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vindalian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vindalian)

Vindalians were a humanoid species. They appeared Human, but their facial features bore a vulpine resemblance. The females of the species were larger than the males, and size was considered a desirable quality in females.
