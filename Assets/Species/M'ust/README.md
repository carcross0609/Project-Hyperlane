# M'ust

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M'ust](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M%27ust)

M'usts were a species of subterrestrial-dwelling humanoids from a homeworld that was conquered by the Galactic Empire in 3 BBY. The M'usts had a religious culture that worshiped an eternally burning flame beneath their planet's surface, and generally followed a simple, peaceful way of life. After the Imperial conquest of their world, the M'usts came across Rebel warrior Cody Sunn-Childe, who shocked the natives by becoming the first to survive immersion in their holy flame. The M'usts thus took Sunn-Childe to be their god, and worshiped and supported the Jashwik as he learned their peaceful philosophy and how to command his newly awakened psychic abilities.
