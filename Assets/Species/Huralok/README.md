# Huralok

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Huralok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huralok)

Huraloks were sapient reptilians indigenous to Djurmo. They were known to be a vicious species that held long grudges with other beings and even their own. Huraloks were extremely long-lived, living to as old as the mid 300s.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Huralok
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.5 meters
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Triangular heads, thick claws, black-striped scales
- **Origin:** Djurmo
- **Language:** Huralok language, Galactic Basic Standard
