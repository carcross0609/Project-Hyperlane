# Gordelian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gordelian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gordelian)

Gordelians were a large, bloated, and slow-moving sentient species hailing from the Nishmar system, on the fringe of the Unknown Regions. They were often considered lazy and rarely seen away from their homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gordelian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Origin:** Nishmar system
