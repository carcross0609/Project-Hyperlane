# Unidentified fin-headed beaked species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified fin-headed beaked species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_fin-headed_beaked_species/Legends)

A sentient species was distinguished by their beak-like mouths, with sunken eyes and a three-pronged head crest, the largest portion extending from the top, while two others jutted from the sides of their heads. By 22 BBY, a member of this species had been a Jedi of the Jedi Order, well known enough to have multiple busts of them displayed around the Jedi Archives.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified fin-headed beaked species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Beak-like mouth
  - Head crest
