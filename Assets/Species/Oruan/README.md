# Oruan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oruan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oruan)

The Oruans, also known as the Oru, were a group of humans native to the planet Oru. A warrior people, the Oruans fought back when their planet was taken over by the First Order. A resistance group of Oruans led by Mezlo almost succeeded, but were forced to flee to Horizon Base, where the Resistance undertook a mission in order to recruit them.
