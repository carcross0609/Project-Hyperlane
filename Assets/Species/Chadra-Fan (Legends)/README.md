# Chadra-Fan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chadra-Fan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chadra-Fan/Legends)

Chadra-Fan were short, rodent-like humanoids, usually no more than one meter tall, with bat-like faces. They hailed from the watery planet Chad.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Chadra-Fan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Mass:** 20 kilograms
- **Haircolor:**
  - Black
  - Brown
  - Dark umber
  - Light tan
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Able to see into the infrared spectrum
  - Acute senses
  - Bat-like faces
  - Clear blood
  - Four toes
  - Rodent-like appearance
  - Two hearts
  - Upturned circular nose with four nostrils
- **Lifespan:** 40 standard years
- **Origin:** Chad/Chadra
- **Language:** Chadra-Fan
