# Unidentified large-headed tendriled species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified large-headed tendriled species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_large-headed_tendriled_species)

A sentient humanoid species existed in the galaxy. Members of the species had large heads and green or pink skin. They had two small tendrils that danged from their upper lips, large black eyes, and lacked any visible nostrils. At one point no earlier than 1 BBY, at least two adult members of the species were present at Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina in the city of Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine. Both individuals wore similar outfits featuring gloves and a full body suit with straight grooves on the shoulders and torso.
