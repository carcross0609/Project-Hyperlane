# Del Andue

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Del Andue](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Del_Andue)

The Del Andue were a strange-looking sentient species. In 3 ABY, the Jedi Luke Skywalker spent some time living in a hovel on the planet Tatooine with two Del Andues while he made preparations to rescue his friend Han Solo from the clutches of the crime lord Jabba the Hutt.
