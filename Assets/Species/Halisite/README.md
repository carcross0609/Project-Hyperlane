# Halisite

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Halisite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halisite)

The Halisite were a sentient species that originated on a planet on the frontier and were considered rare by the time of the High Republic Era. They possessed a bone fin that crested their head and pulsed red and blue as it flushed with blood in time with their heartbeat. One Halisite, Quith Meglar, was a member of the Nihil marauders who wished to defect.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Halisite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale-gray
- **Distinctions:** Bone head crest
