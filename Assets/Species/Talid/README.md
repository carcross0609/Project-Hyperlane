# Talid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Talid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talid)

The Talid were a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. A male podracer Gen-Goma was a member of the species. By sometime during or after 5 ABY, a large statue of a Talid could be found on the planet Ando Prime.
