# Atriik

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Atriik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atriik)

The Atriik were a sentient species known to exist during the time of the Clone Wars. Members of the species possessed horns on the sides of their heads and mottled skin.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Atriik
- **Distinctions:**
  - Mottled face
  - Horns protruding from the sides of the face
