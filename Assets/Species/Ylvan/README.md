# Ylvan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ylvan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ylvan)

The Ylvan were a sentient species of humanoids. Their skin was reddish-brown and they featured large ears. Tallin was a member of the species. Around 0 BBY, Tallin visited Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina located in the city of Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine and witnessed a shootout between the bounty hunter Purong and the Ariwook Rff Huff.
