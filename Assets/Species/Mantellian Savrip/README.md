# Mantellian Savrip

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mantellian Savrip](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mantellian_Savrip)

Mantellian Savrips were a sentient, but technologically primitive, reptilian species from the planet Ord Mantell whose appearance was used for one of the holographic pieces in the game dejarik. Large in size, they had gray skin and horned heads with red eyes, attached to their upper torsos by long necks partially covered in strands of orange hair. Mantellian Savrips had a pair of long, powerful arms that terminated in claw-tipped, four-fingered hands and two short legs with three-toed feet. Earning fame, Kalhar was a Mantellian Savrip who found work as a gladiator and brawler. By at least 11 ABY, a Mantellian Savrip gladiator was one of several creatures and gladiators belonging to species featured in dejarik that was unleashed upon the Mandalorian Din Djarin and Rotta the Hutt in the Pits on the moon Shakari.
