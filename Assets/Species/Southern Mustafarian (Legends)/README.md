# Southern Mustafarian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Southern Mustafarian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Mustafarian/Legends)

Southern Mustafarians were a subspecies of Mustafarians, being one of two. Southern Mustafarians were shorter and burlier than their northern counterparts, and they could resist greater temperatures. Mustafarians evolved from extremophile arthropods living in caves of dormant volcanoes.

- **Origin:** Mustafar
