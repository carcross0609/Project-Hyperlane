# Karnak Alphan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Karnak Alphan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karnak_Alphan)

The Karnak Alphans, alternately Karnak, were the indigenous sapient species of the planet Karnak Alpha. Other than their hair, none of their bodies could be seen. The hair of young females was knotted into ribbons while young males had bells tied to strands of their hair.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Karnak Alphan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Hair covering entire body
- **Origin:** Karnak Alpha
