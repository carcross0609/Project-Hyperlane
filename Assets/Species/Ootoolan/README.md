# Ootoolan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ootoolan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ootoolan)

The Ootoolans were a species of amphibious sentients indigenous to the beautiful aquatic world of Ootoola. They generally possessed humanoid features, but their skin was a mottled bright green, and their heads were adorned with a fin-like ridge and several finned jowls. Due to their fish-like appearance, members of this species were derogatorily nicknamed «fishfaces» outside Ootoola.
