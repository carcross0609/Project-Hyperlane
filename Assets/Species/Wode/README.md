# Wode

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wode](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wode)

The Wodes were an octopedal sentient species native to a planet with a gravitational pull twice that of the galactic standard. When traveling to worlds with lesser gravities, the Wodes took very heavy steps, unaccustomed to their lighter weight.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Wode
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Octupedal
- **Origin:** Wode homeworld
