# Skrilling

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Skrilling](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skrilling)

The Skrilling were a three-fingered, bipedal sentient species with green skin and brown eyes. The arms dealer Pote Snitkin was a Skrilling, as was the Jedi Knight Ord Enisence.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Skrilling
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
