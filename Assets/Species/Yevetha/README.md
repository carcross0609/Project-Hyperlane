# Yevetha

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yevetha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yevetha)

The Yevetha were a lithe, skeletal species indigenous to N'zoth in the Koornacht Cluster in the Core Worlds, known for their xenophobia and death-centered culture.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yevetha
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.9 meters
- **Skincolor:** Green and yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Retractable dew claws, six fingers (one thumb), six toes
- **Lifespan:** 81 years
- **Origin:** N'zoth
- **Language:** Yevethan
