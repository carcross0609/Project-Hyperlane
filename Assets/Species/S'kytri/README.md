# S'kytri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S'kytri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S%27kytri)

The S'kytri (meaning the Windborn in their language) were a sentient species of winged humanoids indigenous to the planet of Skye, also known to the Galactic Empire as Marat V. They were normally tall, muscular and thin, which is why they were regarded as one of the most beautiful species in the galaxy. Throughout history, the S'kytri had built up a reputation of noble warrior bards.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** S'kytri
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Height:** 2.0–2.2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Blue (males) or soft green and cyan (females)
- **Haircolor:**
  - Violet
  - Yellow
  - White
  - Maroon
- **Feathers:** Blue (males) or soft green (females)
- **Distinctions:** Flight-capable wings
- **Lifespan:** 75 standard years
- **Origin:** Skye
- **Language:** S'kytric
