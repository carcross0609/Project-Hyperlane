# Brixian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Brixian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brixian)

Brixians were a sentient species that originated from the Brix system. The Butcher of Brix was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Brixian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**

  - Red
  - White
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - 4 Eyes
  - Mandibles
- **Origin:** Brix system
