# Gab's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gab's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gab%27s_species)

A sentient species of humanoid amphibians lived in the galaxy. One member of the species was Gab, who was a farmer on the planet Tenoo during the High Republic Era. Another member of this species lived at a nerf ranch on an unidentified desert world.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gab's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Pink
