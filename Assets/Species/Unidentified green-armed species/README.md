# Unidentified green-armed species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified green-armed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green-armed_species)

This species was a bipedal humanoid sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their white faces, green arms and necks, and tan hands. In 9 ABY, a member of this species was present at Gor Koresh's Honky Tonk when Koresh attempted to take Din Djarin's Mandalorian armor.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified green-armed species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green, white, and tan
- **Haircolor:** Black
