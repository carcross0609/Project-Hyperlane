# Halaisi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Halaisi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halaisi)

The Halaisi were a sentient species in the galaxy. Humanoid in shape with a build similar to that of humans, they had golden skin and eyes, and four limbs with five-fingered hands. Their eyelids and lips could be blue, and they could have black eyebrows. It was possible for Halaisi to be Force-sensitive.
