# Kiffar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kiffar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kiffar/Legends)

The Kiffar were a species of Near-Humans from the planet Kiffu. As such, they were sometimes mistakenly called "Kiffu."

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kiffar
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.7 meters (average)
- **Distinctions:**
  - Qukuuf facial tattoos
  - Psychometric abilities
- **Origin:**
  - Kiffu
  - Kiffex
- **Language:** Kiffar
