# Thisspiasian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thisspiasian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thisspiasian/Legends)

Thisspiasians were an omnivorous, sentient species of reptiles from the planet Thisspias.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Thisspiasian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.5 meters (while coiled)
- **Skincolor:** Pale to green
- **Haircolor:** Black, brown, blonde (usually turning to grey or white with age)
- **Distinctions:** Four arms, serpentine
- **Lifespan:** 90+ years
- **Origin:** Thisspias
- **Language:** Thisspiasian
