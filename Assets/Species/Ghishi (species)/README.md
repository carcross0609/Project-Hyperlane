# Ghishi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ghishi (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ghishi_%28species%29)

The Ghishi were a race of warrior-mantids from the planet of the same name. Ghishi were thin and lanky, with long insectile limbs and large orange eyes. Kraid Nemmeso was a Ghishi who became well-known on the podracing circuit during the final years of the Galactic Republic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ghishi
- **Designation:** Insectoid
- **Skincolor:** Bluish gray
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow with orange irises
- **Origin:** Ghishi
- **Language:** Huttese
