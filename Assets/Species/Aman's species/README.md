# Aman's species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Aman's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aman%27s_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were humanoid, with white skin, and a singular horn growing out of the left side of their forehead. They also possessed serrated teeth, pointed ears, dark, circular eyebrows, and irises in their eyes that almost resembled suns. One member of this species, Aman, fought the Jedi Nakime and her master above the sacred tree forest. Like many dark side users, Aman possessed red and yellow eyes, and covered her body in what appeared to be superficial red, black, and blue markings.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Aman's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow and red (Dark side of the Force)
- **Distinctions:** Singular horn
