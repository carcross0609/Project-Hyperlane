# Mere

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mere](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mere)

The Mere were sentient humanoids that hailed from the aquatic planet of Maramere. They had orange fur covering their bodies, and could breathe underwater. They had both noses and gills.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mere
- **Haircolor:** Orange
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Aquatic
- **Origin:** Maramere
