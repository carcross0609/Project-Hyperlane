# Saurton

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saurton](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saurton)

The Saurtons were a sentient species of reptiles indigenous to the planet Essowyn.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Saurton
- **Class:** Reptile
- **Height:** 1.75–1.9 meters
- **Skincolor:** Golden
- **Distinctions:**
  - Three clawed digits
  - Large snouts
  - Sharp teeth
- **Origin:** Essowyn
