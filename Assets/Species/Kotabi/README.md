# Kotabi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kotabi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kotabi)

The Kotabi were a humanoid sentient species in the galaxy. Jedi bond-twins Terec and Ceret were members of the species and originated from the planet Sagamore The planet Kotab was also a homeworld of the twins.
