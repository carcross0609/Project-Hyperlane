# Eklaad

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eklaad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eklaad)

The Eklaad were quadrupedal vegetarian sapients indigenous to Sirpar. They were peaceful and primitive, not having advanced technology.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Eklaad
- **Height:** 1–1.5 meters
- **Length:** 1.5–2 meters
- **Origin:** Sirpar
- **Language:**
  - System of hooting sounds
  - Galactic Basic Standard
