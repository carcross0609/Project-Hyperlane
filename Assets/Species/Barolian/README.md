# Barolian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Barolian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barolian)

Barolians were a sentient species. During the High Republic Era, Barolian pilots Jano Brayson and Teff, who both hailed from Baroli, served in the Republic Defense Coalition's Firebird Squadron.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
