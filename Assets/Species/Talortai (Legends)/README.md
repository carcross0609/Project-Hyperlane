# Talortai

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Talortai/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talortai/Legends)

The Talortai were a mysterious species with both avian and reptilian qualities that had rapid regeneration and lacked aging, with the ability to distinguish between the light and dark sides of the Force, and was a large part of this species' life; however, for unknown reasons, many did not become a Jedi or Sith.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Talortai
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Haircolor:** Gray
- **Feathers:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Avian features
  - Rapid regeneration
  - Lack of aging
  - Force sensitive
