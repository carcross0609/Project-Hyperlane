# Fryiaan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fryiaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fryiaan)

The Fryiaans were a sentient four-armed species from the Fryiaa system. In the Imperial Era, a female Fryiaan was a dancer and singer of a band.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fryiaan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Four arms
- **Origin:** Fryiaa system
