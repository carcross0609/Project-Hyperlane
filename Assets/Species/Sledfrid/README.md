# Sledfrid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sledfrid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sledfrid)

Sledfrids were a sentient species that resembled jellyfish. Members of the species were notable for their lighter-than-air, gelatinous physiology, which allowed them to float, as well as their electric fronds that could be targeted at threats and were quite sticky. Sledfrids were incapable of speaking Basic without a vocabulator.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sledfrid
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Electric, sticky fronds
  - Lighter-than-air physiology
