# Draflago

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Draflago](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Draflago)

The Draflago were a sentient species from the Draflago system that was active in the galaxy in the early years of the Galactic Empire. The Tion Hegemony's sole university, the University of Rudrig, had a number of luxury mud tanks available for visiting Draflago.
