# Duinuogwuin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Duinuogwuin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duinuogwuin/Legends)

The Duinuogwuin, colloquially called Star Dragons, were among the galaxy's more secretive and strange species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Duinuogwuin
- **Races:** Highly variable
- **Length:** 10 to 110 meters
- **Skincolor:** Variable shades of gray as well as purple
- **Distinctions:** Large size, long life spans, high regenerative capabilities, hardened skin, natural repulsorlifts, survival in vacuum, ability to travel through hyperspace, fire-breathing
- **Origin:** "Xuaquarres"
- **Language:** Duinuogwuin
