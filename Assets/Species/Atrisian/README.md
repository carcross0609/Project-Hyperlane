# Atrisian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Atrisian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atrisian)

The Atrisians were Humans native to the planet Atrisia. One Atrisian was Rom Mohc, who served with the Republic Military during the Clone Wars and eventually became a general in the Galactic Empire's military forces, where he oversaw the Imperial Dark Trooper Project.
