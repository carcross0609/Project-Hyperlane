# Sikurdian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sikurdian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sikurdian/Legends)

Sikurdians were short, round bipeds native to Sikurd who invented the Sikurdian battle-axe.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sikurdian
- **Skincolor:** Purple and orange
- **Distinctions:** Tentacle limbs
- **Origin:** Sikurd
