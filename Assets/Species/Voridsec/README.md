# Voridsec

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Voridsec](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voridsec)

Voridsec were a sentient species with insectoid features. They possessed four arms and had yellow spines on their back. Chilla Zin was a Voridsec male member of the Assassin's Guild during the Imperial Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Voridsec
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Dark gray
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four arms
  - Yellow spine
