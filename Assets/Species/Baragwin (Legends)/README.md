# Baragwin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Baragwin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baragwin/Legends)

Baragwin were a species of sentient reptilians native to the lost world Old Barag. One of the earliest spacefaring races, the Baragwin could be found across the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Baragwin
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptile
- **Height:** 1.5 – 2.2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Drab green to dark olive
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Hunchback
  - Large head
- **Lifespan:**
  - Child:1–10
  - Young adult: 11–17
  - Adult: 18–44
  - Middle Age: 45–71
  - Old: 72–89
  - Venerable: 90+
- **Origin:** Old Barag
- **Language:**
  - Galactic Basic Standard
  - Baragwinian
