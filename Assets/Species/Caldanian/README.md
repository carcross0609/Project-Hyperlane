# Caldanian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Caldanian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caldanian)

The Caldanians were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. They had dark brown slimy skin, tendrils, and a flexible cartilage instead of bones. A criminal was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Caldanian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Dark brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Boneless
  - Slimy skin
  - Tendrils
  - Flexible cartilage
