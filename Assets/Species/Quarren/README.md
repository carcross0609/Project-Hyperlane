# Quarren

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quarren](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quarren)

The Quarren, also known by the derogatory nickname "Squid Heads," were a humanoid, squid-like sentient species native to the planet Mon Cala, where they coexisted with the Mon Calamari in a peaceful, albeit tense, relationship.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Quarren
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Orange
  - Pale
  - Purple
  - Pink
  - Salmon
  - Tan
  - Green
- **Eyecolor:** Turquoise
- **Distinctions:** Four tentacles protruding from jaw; finned or suction-cup tipped fingers; ability to spit ink in defense
- **Origin:** Mon Cala
- **Habitat:** Aquatic
