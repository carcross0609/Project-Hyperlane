# Teevan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Teevan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teevan)

The Teevan were a sentient species that originated from the Teevan system. In 382 BBY, a Force-sensitive Teevan man was a member of the Path of the Open Hand cult.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Teevan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Silver-tinted
- **Origin:** Teevan system
