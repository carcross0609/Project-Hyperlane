# Fairfolk

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fairfolk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fairfolk)

The Fairfolk were a very small race that resembled ethereal, floating Jawas. They were strong in the Force and a number of them working for Darth Vader on Dantooine attempted to sway an oblivious Luke Skywalker to the dark side by setting up a psychic link between Skywalker and Vader, who was stationed on board the Star Destroyer Conqueror. The arrival of Leia Organa freed Luke from the Fairfolk's trance.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fairfolk
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** around 0.5 meters
- **Distinctions:** Force-sensitive
- **Origin:** Dantooine
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
