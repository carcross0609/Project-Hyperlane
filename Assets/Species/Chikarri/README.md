# Chikarri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chikarri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chikarri)

The Chikarri were sapient rodents from Plagen. They were a sentient species with mammalian features. They had two arms and two legs with a long tail which made them very agile on their feet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Chikarri
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Rodent
- **Height:** 1.3–1.5 meters
- **Haircolor:** Gray, reddish-brown, tan, brown
- **Origin:** Plagen
- **Diet:** Herbivore, mostly nuts, berries and grains
- **Language:** Chikarri
