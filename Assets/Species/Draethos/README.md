# Draethos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Draethos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Draethos)

The Draethos were a sentient humanoid species. A Nihil Storm was a member of the species. They possessed massive teeth and had blue-colored skin, black eyes, and four-fingered hands. By the Imperial Era, a Draethos skeleton still remained sprawled at the feet of his great chair in his office in the Chamber of Antiquities in the Great Jedi Library on Ossus, while two stone guardians stood as silent sentinels to protect the chamber from intruders.
