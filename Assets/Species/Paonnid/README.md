# Paonnid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Paonnid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paonnid)

The Paonnid were a sentient species from the Paonid system. In 9 ABY, while wandering on a marketplace on the planet of Bimmisaari, the New Republic hero Han Solo spotted a being who looked like a Paonnid among the crowd.
