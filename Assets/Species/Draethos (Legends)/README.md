# Draethos

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Draethos/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Draethos/Legends)

Draethos were a predatory humanoid species from the planet Draethos, notable for their long natural lifespans.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Draethos
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Gray
  - Mauve
  - Pink
  - Violet
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Four fingers, large teeth, telepathy, long life spans
- **Lifespan:** Up to 800 standard years
- **Origin:** Draethos
- **Language:** Draethos
