# Unidentified yellow large-headed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified yellow large-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_yellow_large-headed_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their yellow skin with small facial features and enlarged cranium with a crease running down the middle, as well as tentacle-like arms with underarms featuring multiple folds. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren.
