# W'iiri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [W'iiri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/W%27iiri)

The W'iiri were a six-legged sentient species with horn plating and pincers. A number of W'iiri worked for J'uoch, the leader of a criminal mining operation, on the planet Dellalt. They were present when J'uoch's mining camp came under attack from reactivated war-robots that had been part of Xim's Guardian Corps; three W'iiri died while defending the camp against the attack.

## Biology & Culture
