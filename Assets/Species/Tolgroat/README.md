# Tolgroat

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Tolgroat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tolgroat)

Togroats were a sentient species that could be found in the galaxy. They had blue skin with darker blue spots and multiple appendages stemming from their head that faded into white on the ends. Togroat's eyes were yellow-orange and they had dark blue eyebrows. They also had three fingers on each hand and had no toes.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tolgroat
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue and white
- **Haircolor:** Dark blue (eyebrows)
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow-orange
- **Distinctions:** Multiple head appendages
