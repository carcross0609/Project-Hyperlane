# Zylurian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zylurian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zylurian)

Zylurians were a sentient humanoid species with blueish skin, long hanging nostrils and three fingers. The crew of the ZZ-class freighter Mingula consisted of four Zylurians who were all killed by a swarm of Ubuugan fleshborers. The bounty hunter Boba Fett discovered the corpses when he searched the freighter for valuables.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Zylurian
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:**
  - Three fingers
  - Long hanging nostrils
- **Origin:** Zyluria
