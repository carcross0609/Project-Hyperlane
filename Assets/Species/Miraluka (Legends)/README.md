# Miraluka

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Miraluka/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miraluka/Legends)

The Miraluka, or Miralukans, were a Near-Human, Force-sensitive species. Their language was Miralukese, although some, like Visas Marr and the Dark Jedi Jerec, preferred Basic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Miraluka
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Pale to Brown
- **Eyecolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Lack of physical eyes, inherent Force sight
- **Lifespan:** Up to 84 standard years
- **Origin:**
  - Alpheridies (adopted)
  - Miraluka homeworld (uninhabitable)
- **Language:** Miralukese
