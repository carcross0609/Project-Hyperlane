# Nessie

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nessie](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nessie)

, a Nessie]] The term Nessies was used to refer to the twenty-five native humanoid populations of the system Stenness Node, a group of mining worlds in the Inner Rim The Nessies operated the mines in the region and traded with outworlders. They also used Ithullian ore haulers, which were made from the carapaces of the colossus wasps of Ithull.
