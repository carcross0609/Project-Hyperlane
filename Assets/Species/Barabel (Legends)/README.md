# Barabel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Barabel/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barabel/Legends)

Barabels were a species of sentient cold-blooded reptilians native to the dark, murky world Barab I. A highly aggressive and territorial species, Barabel who traveled off their homeworld often found work as fringers or soldiers.
