# Unidentified Neijaia species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Neijaia species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Neijaia_species)

A humanoid species native to a Neijaia that spoke the language Neijaian. Members of the species chased Poe Dameron and BB-8, and later caught them, along with Kori Ong, in a trap. With Ong's help, Dameron and BB-8 escaped the natives, and were able to leave the planet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified Neijaia species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Purple

- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Origin:** Neijaia
- **Language:** Neijaian
