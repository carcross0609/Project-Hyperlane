# Voegliss's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Voegliss's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voegliss%27s_species)

A sentient species were predatory beings with red-colored carapace-like skin and a tooth-filled maw. Two members of the species at one point attacked the team of the Galactic Empire scientist Devlor Samt on a world that the former were not native to, being eventually killed themselves. Samt subsequently raised the alien's infant son, Voegliss, as his own child, with the young being ultimately being pressed into service as an Imperial assassin-in-training.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Voegliss's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Carapace-like skin
  - Hooded eyes
  - Tooth-filled maw
- **Lifespan:** At least 15 years
- **Diet:** Carnivorous
