# Sylar Saris' species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sylar Saris' species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sylar_Saris%27_species)

Sylar Saris was a member of an insectoid sentient species closely resembling the Yam'rii species. Saris' species was potentially a subspecies of Yam'rii. The species was capable of spitting green acid out of their mouths.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sylar Saris' species
- **Class:** Yam'rii subspecies (unconfirmed)
- **Skincolor:** Green
