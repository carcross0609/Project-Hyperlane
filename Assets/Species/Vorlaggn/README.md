# Vorlaggn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vorlaggn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vorlaggn)

Vorlaggn were a sentient species native to Vorlag, a cavernous planet. They had skin like the cracked char on a piece of fire-cooked meat whose fissures suppurated clear fluid, three hollow-set eyes, and spoke in the third person, which could confuse individuals of other species. Slussen Urla-fir Kal Kethin-wa Canker, a male Vorlaggn crime lord, owned a palace on Vorlag.
