# Da-Vlunnian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Da-Vlunnian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Da-Vlunnian)

Da-Vlunnian were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. During the High Republic Era, one Da-Vlunnian, Masakene, served as the chief steward for Skarabda the Hutt on the planet Ballum.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Da-Vlunnian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Light brown
- **Eyecolor:** White
- **Origin:** Da-Vlunn system
