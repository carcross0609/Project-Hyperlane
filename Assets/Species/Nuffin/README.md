# Nuffin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nuffin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nuffin)

Nuffins were sapient humanoids, noted for their cleanliness and gentle nature. Princess Nampi's ship was a converted Nuffin freighter, and she kept a Nuffin slave on board in order to fool would-be pirates into boarding, whereupon they would be ambushed by the princess's soldiers.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nuffin
- **Skincolor:** Pale pink
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Beady eyes, noseless
- **Language:** Nuffese
