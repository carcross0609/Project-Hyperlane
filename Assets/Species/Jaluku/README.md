# Jaluku

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jaluku](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaluku)

The Jaluku were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy that had white skin and an elongated chin. Jaluku lived on worlds such as Vandor and Daiyu. Seleno Chandro was a Jaluku male bounty hunter who formerly worked in the Bounty Hunters' Guild and would later join Citizens' Fleet during the Battle of Exegol. Jaluku could be commonly seen wearing goggles to cover their eyes, Chandro wore data goggles in particular.
