# Ogemite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ogemite/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ogemite/Legends)

Ogemites were a near-Human species indigenous to the planet Ogem. They had pale skin and large frills of feathery hair on their heads. They were known throughout the galaxy as traders who generally remained politically neutral. The Rebel operative Anduvil was an Ogemite.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ogemite
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Haircolor:** Blonde
- **Distinctions:** Near-Humans with feather-like hair
- **Origin:** Ogem
