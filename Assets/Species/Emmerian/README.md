# Emmerian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Emmerian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emmerian)

Emmerians were a sentient species from the Emmer system. During the High Republic Era, one member of the Nihil was an Emmerian Cloud who was part of Pan Eyta's Tempest.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Emmerian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Emmer system
