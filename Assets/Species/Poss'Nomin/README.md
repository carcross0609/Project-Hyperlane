# Poss'Nomin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Poss'Nomin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poss%27Nomin)

The Poss'Nomin were a sentient species indigenous to the planet Illarreen. Thickly built humanoids slightly larger than the average Human, they were easily recognized by their large, shovel-shaped jaws and their three eyes.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Poss'Nomin
- **Height:** 1.7–2.1 meters
- **Skincolor:** Red, with black- or brown-spotted forearms in some races
- **Distinctions:** Three eyes, shovel-like jaws
- **Origin:** Illarreen
