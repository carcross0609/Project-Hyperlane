# Unidentified floating species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified floating species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_floating_species)

A floating sentient species lived in the galaxy. They had an elongated, ovular body shape with several tentacles at the rear, a fanged mouth at the front, parapodia along their sides, and striped skin. They could move by floating through the air. Two members of this species attended a party on Alderaan where Princess Leia Organa was present.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified floating species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Tusks and sharp teeth
  - Parapodia
  - Stripes
  - Tentacles
