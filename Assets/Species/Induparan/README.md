# Induparan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Induparan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Induparan)

The Induparans were the Human race that inhabited the Induparan Crown Worlds. Ruled by King Dahon Indupar during the Galactic Civil War, the Induparans were also controlled by several noble political houses.
