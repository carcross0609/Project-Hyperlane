# Orgon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orgon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orgon)

The Orgons were intelligent plants, and the native sapient species of Gorsh.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Orgon
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Botanical
- **Height:** Up to 1.5 meters
- **Length:** 0.5 meter diameter, limbs up to 4 meters
- **Skincolor:** Deep-green or yellow
- **Distinctions:** Plant based species, tentacles
- **Origin:** Gorsh
