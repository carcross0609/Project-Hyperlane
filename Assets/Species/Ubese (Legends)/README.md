# Ubese

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ubese/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ubese/Legends)

The Ubese, or Ubesian were a sentient species. To most inhabitants of the galaxy members of the enigmatic species were generally known as mysterious wanderers and nomadic savages. Their xenophobic nature made them ideally suited for professions such as mercenaries or bounty hunters. Normally, they were a very aggressive people, though loyal to one another. The Ubese homeworld was little known to the rest of the galaxy, as they tended to be very secretive, associating only within their own circles.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ubese
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Races:** "True" Ubese (Uba IV), "yrak pootzck" Ubese (Ubertica)

- **Haircolor:** Dark
- **Eyecolor:** Variable, generally bright green or blue
- **Distinctions:** Raspy, whispering voices
- **Lifespan:** Up to 71 standard years
- **Origin:** Uba IV, Ubertica
- **Language:**
