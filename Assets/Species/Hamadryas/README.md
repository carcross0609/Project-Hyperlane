# Hamadryas

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hamadryas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hamadryas)

The Hamadryas were a simian humanoid species with green skin and ape-like snouts. They seemed to not possess the need to blink, and the species lacked a true carotid artery.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hamadryas
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Lack of a carotid artery
- **Language:** Hamadryas
