# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Republic Senate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Republic_Senate%29)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The humanoid species was distinguished by their dark, almost greenish skin, as well as light tips at the ends of their fingers. In 22 BBY, at least two members of this species were associated with the Galactic Senate, present at the Senate Building.

- **Designation:** Sentient
