# Inchichtok

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Inchichtok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inchichtok)

The Inchichtok were a xenophobic sentient species. Around 200 BBY, the Inchichtok had their first contact with the Galactic Republic, meeting the scout expedition commander Arnoth Draenell and his crew in an unscouted star system that later became known as the Bissillirus system in the Mid Rim. Though Draenell's crew attempted to continue their expedition, the Inchichtok prevented them from doing so by ending their lives.
