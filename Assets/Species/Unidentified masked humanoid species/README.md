# Unidentified masked humanoid species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified masked humanoid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_masked_humanoid_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The humanoid species was distinguished by their distinctive masks which possessed an almost beak-like shape, as well as a curved protrusion extending upwards from their cranial area. In 9 BBY, a member of species attempted to apprehend a young Leia Organa on the planet Daiyu before being shot in the back by Jedi-in-hiding Obi-Wan Kenobi. In 35 ABY, another member of this species was present on the planet Pasaana during the Festival of the Ancestors.

## Biology & Culture
