# Tostonian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tostonian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tostonian)

The Tostonians were a sentient bipedal species. Members of the species had large, round, black eyes and mottled pink skin. They possessed two arms ending in three-fingered hands with sharp claws. In 9 BBY, a male Tostonian who spoke an alien language worked on the planet Daiyu, at the spice laboratory known as the Den. The human Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi followed the Tostonian and acquired his clothing, which the Jedi then used to infiltrate the Den.
