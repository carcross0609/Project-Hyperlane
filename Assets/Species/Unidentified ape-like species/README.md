# Unidentified ape-like species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified ape-like species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_ape-like_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The humanoid species was distinguished by their ape-like faces. By 32 BBY until at least 22 BBY, four members of this species were associated with the Galactic Senate.
