# Tof

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tof/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tof/Legends)

The Tof were green-skinned near-humans from the planet Tof, located in Firefist, a dwarf satellite galaxy orbiting the main galactic disc, within the wider Unknown Regions. They were ruled by patriarchs who usually had military experience and were in control of their own interstellar empire known as the Tof Kingdom.
