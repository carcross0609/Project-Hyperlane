# Trianii

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Trianii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trianii)

The Trianii were a species of bipedal felinoid sentients from the Trian system. In an attempt to impress a Trianii debutante, Lando Calrissian once took a swing at the Esoomian brawler Bumblethunk, who beat him down and stole the Smuggler's Guide from him.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Trianii
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Feline humanoid
- **Origin:** Trian system
