# Rughja

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rughja](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rughja)

Rugjha were non-humanoid sentients with fifteen flexible limbs, glossy mauve skin, and multiple eyes. To Humans, they appeared to be a tangle of limbs with the occasional eye peeping out.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Rughja
- **Skincolor:** Glossy mauve
- **Distinctions:** Fifteen limbs, multiple eyes
