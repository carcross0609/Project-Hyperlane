# Unidentified Banas species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Banas species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Banas_species)

A sentient species was native to the planet Banas. They had blue, gray, or white colored skin. Halorum and Moular were members of this species.
