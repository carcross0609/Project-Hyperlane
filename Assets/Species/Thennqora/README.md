# Thennqora

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thennqora](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thennqora)

The Thennqora were a sentient species from the Thennqor system that influenced Saffa paintings after their initial contact, several millennia before the Galactic Civil War. Tamish was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Thennqora
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Thennqor system
