# Snogar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Snogar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snogar)

Snogars were a tall, furry sentient species native to the ice-covered planet Ota in the Mid Rim. The Snogars were descended from a technologically advanced race referred to as the "Old Ones," who built cities within Ota's ice canyons. Over time the Snogars forgot how to use their ancestors' technology, and reverted to primitive lifestyles inside their ancient cities. By 1 ABY, that had resulted in a major crisis, as the Snogars were unable to power the heating machines that kept their homes habitable, threatening their way of life on their frigid homeworld.
