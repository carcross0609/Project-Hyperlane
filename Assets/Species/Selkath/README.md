# Selkath

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Selkath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Selkath)

The Selkath were a species of aquatic beings native to the planet Manaan. The species was most comfortable underwater and preferred to be immersed in liquid. Because of the Selkath's water-dwelling nature, their chest armors were fitted with misting vents in order to keep their skin moist.
