# Unidentified long-headed black-eyed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified long-headed black-eyed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_long-headed_black-eyed_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their wrinkled brown skin, with elongated snouts and craniums, as well as solid black eyes. In 4 ABY, at least three members of this species, a mother and two children, could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified long-headed black-eyed species
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Long snouts
