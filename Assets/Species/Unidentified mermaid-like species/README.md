# Unidentified mermaid-like species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified mermaid-like species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_mermaid-like_species)

An aquatic mermaid-like species lived in the galaxy. Several members of this species once performed as dancers at Chalmun's Cantina in Mos Eisley.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified mermaid-like species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Haircolor:** Light blue
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long fish-like tail
  - Webbed underarm
