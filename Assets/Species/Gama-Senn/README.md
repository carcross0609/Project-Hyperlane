# Gama-Senn

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gama-Senn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gama-Senn)

The Gama-Senn were a sentient, humanoid species from the Gama system. Gama-Senn had two thumbs per hand and flat facial features. In 11 ABY, the Gama-Senn ruler was convinced to swear the Gama system's loyalties to the reborn Galactic Emperor Palpatine after a demonstration of the Dark Empire's superweapon, the Galaxy Gun. Palpatine scoffed at the Gama-Senn representative for withholding allegiance until swayed by fear.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gama-Senn
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellowish-brown
- **Distinctions:** Double-thumbed hands
- **Origin:** Planet in the Gama system
