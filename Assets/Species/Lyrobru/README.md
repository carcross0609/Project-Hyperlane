# Lyrobru

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lyrobru](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lyrobru)

Lyrobru was a humanoid sentient species that was distinguished by their blue and tan or brown skin and dog-like faces. In the year 132 BBY, during the High Republic Era, the Lyrobru Orlut had been detained by the Galactic Republic, breaking free alongside Verosha Aniseya, but being re-apprehended by the Jedi Order shortly thereafter. During the reign of the Galactic Empire in 5 BBY, a member of the species was on the planet Niamos, lounging on the beaches around Pier Nine. In 3 BBY, a Lyrobru was present on the planet Ghorman.
