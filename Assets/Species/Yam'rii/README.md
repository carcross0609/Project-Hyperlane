# Yam'rii

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yam'rii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yam%27rii)

The Yam'rii, also known as the Huk, were an insectoid sentient species from the Wild Space planet Huk. Members of the species were characterized by a triangular-shaped head poised on a long neck, large forward-facing eyes, and prominent upper limbs that were bent and held together at an angle. They were greedy and destructive insects that resembled praying mantises with superior technology.
