# Virgillian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Virgillian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Virgillian)

Virgillians were the residents of the Virgillia system in the Koradin sector. Virgillian society had a nobility, which included the family of Rannd Delckis. The Duros navigator Monnda Tebbo, a Rebel Alliance member who was sent to Virgillia for several weeks, was one of the few non-Humans to fight in the Virgillian Civil War and was considered to be an honorary Virgillian citizen.
