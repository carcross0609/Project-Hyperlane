# Rayeth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rayeth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rayeth)

The Rayeth were an amphibious sentient species that lived on Inusagi. They had long and slender bodies as well as membranes. Bardbee was a member of the Rayeth species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Rayeth
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Eyecolor:** White
- **Origin:** Inusagi
