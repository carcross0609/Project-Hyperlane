# Unidentified Jomark species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Jomark species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Jomark_species)

This sentient species was native to Jomark. They were an ancient people that were the builders of the High Castle, who had long-since vanished by the time of the Galactic Civil War.
