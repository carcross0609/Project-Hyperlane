# Enami

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Enami](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enami)

The Enami were a species of furry humanoids native to the planet Ena. Their fur could range in shades of brown with speckles of color which could lose its hue or turn white with old age. After the destruction of Starlight Beacon, the Enami were subjugated by the Hutt Cartel led by Tregarra the Hutt. Sometime around 229 BBY, Jedi Master Keeve Trennis arrived on the planet with Republic forces to aid in the liberation of the Enami.
