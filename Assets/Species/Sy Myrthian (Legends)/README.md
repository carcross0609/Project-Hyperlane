# Sy Myrthian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sy Myrthian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sy_Myrthian/Legends)

Sy Myrthians were the sentient natives of Sy Myrth.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sy Myrthian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Haircolor:** White and grey
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Fur-covered Hutt-like lower halves
  - Elongated heads
  - Numerous goatees
- **Origin:** Sy Myrth
