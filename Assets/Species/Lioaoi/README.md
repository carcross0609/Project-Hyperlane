# Lioaoi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lioaoi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lioaoi)

The Lioaoi, also known as the Lioaoin, were a sentient species who hailed from the Lioaoin heartworld in the Unknown Regions. They were allies of General Yiv's Nikardun Destiny.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lioaoi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Lioaoin heartworld
