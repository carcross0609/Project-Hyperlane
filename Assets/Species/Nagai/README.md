# Nagai

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nagai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nagai)

Nagai were a sentient humanoid species hailing from the astronomical object Nagi. They had black hair, two red eyes, and pale white skin, along with pointy ears and unique face markings. The species was shown to be strong-willed and spoke in Galactic Basic Standard.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nagai
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Pale white
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Pointy ears
- **Origin:** Nagi
