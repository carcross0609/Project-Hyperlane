# Kath

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kath/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kath/Legends)

The Kath were a reptilian sentient species that shared its name with the ocean world Kath II, also known simply as Kath, of the Mid Rim's Dustig sector. During the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, the Kath Thlan-sa served as an Alliance operative. At some point by 2 ABY, the Shatras bounty hunter Ssach'thirix, who only pursued bounties posted on individuals of reptilian species, captured Thlan-sa.
