# Cosian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cosian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cosian/Legends)

Cosians were a species of sentient bipeds who had beaked mouths, four-fingered hands, hair, and a long, muscular tail ending with a tuft of hair. They were capable of speaking Basic. Jedi Master Tera Sinube was a Cosian.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cosian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Mottled green
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Yellowish-brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Beaked mouths
  - Four-fingered hands
  - Long muscular tails ended with bony clubs
- **Origin:** Cosia
