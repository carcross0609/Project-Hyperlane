# Rodent

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rodent](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rodent)

Rodents were a group of animals which included both sentient species like the Ranat, and non-sentient species like rats. Wilhuff Tarkin killed a rodent on the planet Halcyon while on a mission there, and cooked it for he and his squadmates to eat. Rodents could also be found on planets like Tatooine, Gorse and Lothal.
