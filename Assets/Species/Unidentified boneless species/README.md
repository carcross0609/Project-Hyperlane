# Unidentified boneless species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified boneless species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_boneless_species)

A species of boneless sentients with crystalline eyes existed during the time after the Imperial Period. They spoke through their prehensile trunks. These trunks had fine tendrils at the ends, and members of the species had at least five trunks. They formed a colony on Chalcedon, and were its bureaucrats. One member of the species, who worked in the center of a glass building for a strange water pool, was known as the Indexer.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified boneless species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Boneless bodies
  - Prehensile trunks
