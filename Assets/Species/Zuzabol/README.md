# Zuzabol

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zuzabol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zuzabol)

The Zuzabol were a sentient bipedal species shorter in height than humans. The Zuzabol Avga Rosene and another member of the species worked as a fueling crew for the Resistance in the year 34 ABY. Both were present in the Resistance base on the planet D'Qar shortly before the Battle of Starkiller Base.
