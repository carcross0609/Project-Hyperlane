# Chevin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chevin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chevin)

The Chevin were a large sentient elephantine species with massive heads that stretched the length of their entire bodies. They could have gray skin, black eyes, and blonde hair. The Hutt crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure's head of security Ephant Mon was a Chevin. A colonial species, they had migrated and adapted to dominate many worlds.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
