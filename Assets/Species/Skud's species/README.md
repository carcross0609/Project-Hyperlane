# Skud's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skud's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skud%27s_species)

Skud's species were a race of red-skinned humanoids. They had a frill of spines atop their heads, and what appeared to be naturally armored bodies. Their armor was not strong enough to stop a blaster bolt, however, as Skud learned at his peril when he was killed in a fight with Han Solo and Chewbacca on Orleon.
