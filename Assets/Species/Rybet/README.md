# Rybet; (Querm Rybet subspecies pictured)

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rybet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rybet)

The Rybet were an amphibious, frog-like, sentient species that lived in the galaxy. One Rybet was killed in Oga's Cantina on Batuu during the Clone Wars. One was also killed on the Elegencia as he got shot for doubting Pan Eyta's decisions. The Rybet were considered an ancient species and had long since scattered across the galaxy, resulting in numerous subspecies such as the Querm Rybet from Quermia.
