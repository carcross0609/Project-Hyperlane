# Dobatan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dobatan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dobatan)

The Dobatan were a sentient species found within the galaxy. This humanoid species was distinguished by their short stature, with large heads, ribbed chins, downturned mouths, wideset eyes, and large, pointed ears. By around 10 BBY, one Dobatan from Ylesia, Weebahn, had been a podracer working for Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi, though had since gone missing.
