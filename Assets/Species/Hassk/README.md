# Hassk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hassk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hassk)

The Hassks were a sentient species native to Hasskyn. They were almost completely covered in fur, except for their gray-skinned faces. They had sensitive hearing and keen night vision, all the better for hunting. Hassk were near-feral, and were thought of by most as wild animals. They lived in a primitive culture with limited technology. When they were first confronted with space travel, many left their homes and sought out worlds to wreak havoc, particularly in places of ill repute. A number of Hassks came to join Kragan Gorr's pirate gang. The Hassk triplets were thugs who spent time at Takodana Castle stirring up trouble.
