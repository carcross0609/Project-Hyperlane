# Togorian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Togorian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Togorian)

Togorians were a sentient species of large, feline beings with digitigrade feet. Their bodies were covered in short, thick fur that could be brown, white, orange or red with stripes, and their eyes could be yellow or green with a yellow hue. Togorians had retractable claws, and were a proud race. They moved with grace and spoke with a sibilant voice. H'sishi, owner of the Amon Yinchom Dojo, was a female Togorian. The male Jedi Knight Jak'zin, who encountered Count Dooku on Sullust, was a Togorian.
