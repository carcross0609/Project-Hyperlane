# Unidentified brown-skinned species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified brown-skinned species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_brown-skinned_species)

A species with brown skin, a tail ending in a heart-shaped tip, and four limbs ending in three digits each could be found on the forest moon Endor. The species also had a long snout with red markings around their mouth at the tip and a large dark-brown protrusion on each side of their head.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
