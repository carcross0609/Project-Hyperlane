# Waroot

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Waroot](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Waroot)

The Waroot were a sentient species native to the planet Ab'Bshingh. They warred with another indigenous species, the Farang, for centuries, developing unique tactics and customs. Those tactics were studied by Imperial strategist Crat Dakerno, who used them as the basis for the holo boardgame B'shingh.
