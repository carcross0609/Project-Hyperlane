# Palliduvan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Palliduvan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palliduvan)

Palliduvans were a near-human species From the Wild Space planet Palliduva, characterized by their lanky frames, chalk-white skin, and long, bony fingers used to draw blood. Palliduvans included the bounty hunter Aurra Sing and the pirate Valik.
