# Gormak

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gormak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gormak)

The Gormak were a reptilian sentient species in the galaxy. The Nihil pirate Trilok was a Gormak who served in the Tempest of Tempest Runner Pan Eyta during the High Republic Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gormak
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Eyecolor:** Orange
- **Distinctions:** Frills and mandibles
