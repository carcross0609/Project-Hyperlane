# Unidentified four-breasted species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified four-breasted species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_four-breasted_species)

A four-breasted species was a sentient humanoid species that existed in the galaxy. Around 9 ABY, a light-skinned, dark-haired female member of the species was present at Starport Borgo, performing solo dances as a door girl to attract customers to a local dance club.
