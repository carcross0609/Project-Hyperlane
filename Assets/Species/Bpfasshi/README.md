# Bpfasshi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bpfasshi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bpfasshi)

The Bpfasshi were a humanoid species associated with the religion the Truthsayers of Bpfassh. In 382 BBY, one member of the species who was a Truthsayer confronted Ran Yaltan for pretending to be a Truthsayer in the Holy City on the moon Jedha. They originated from the Bpfassh system.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bpfasshi
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Dark facial stripes
- **Origin:** Bpfassh system
