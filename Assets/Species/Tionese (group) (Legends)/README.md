# Tionese

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tionese (group)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tionese_%28group%29/Legends)

The Tionese were a cultural group of humans native to the Tion Cluster. The Tionese established a proud ancient civilization within the Tion Cluster in opposition to the Galactic Republic established in the Core Worlds.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tionese
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Tion Cluster
- **Language:** Tionese
