# Elnacon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Elnacon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elnacon)

Elnacons were a large peaceful sentient species that hailed from Nacon, a world that had an ammonia-rich atmosphere. When on other worlds, they had to wear special breathing apparatus. They had noseless faces and four-fingered hands. They were often mistaken as thugs because of their alarming height and strength, but were actually a very peaceful, family-oriented species. Dava Cassamam, a deep cloud-miner on gas giant planets, was a keen sabacc player.
