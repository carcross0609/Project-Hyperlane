# Praelion

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Praelion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Praelion)

The Praelions were a sentient species that existed in the galaxy, specifically from the Japrael sector. Axut Klem was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Praelion
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Dark
- **Haircolor:** Dark and light
- **Distinctions:**
  - Bumpy skin
  - Elongated cranium
- **Origin:** Japrael sector
