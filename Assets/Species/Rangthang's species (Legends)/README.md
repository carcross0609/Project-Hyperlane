# Rangthang's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rangthang's species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rangthang%27s_species/Legends)

The DJ Rangthang was a member of a species that was humanoid race with 4 arms and 4 legs. The species also had tentacles on their faces.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Rangthang's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - 4 arms
  - 4 legs
  - Tentacles on their faces
