# Mongo Beefhead

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mongo Beefhead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mongo_Beefhead)

Mongo Beefhead were a species of individuals on the edge of extinction. The group was native to the Outer Rim planet of Tatooine and were members of the Rebel Alliance. The Arkanian mutant Zeta Magnus consumed Mongo Beefhead meat, though it was nutritionally poor.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mongo Beefhead
- **Skincolor:** Orange-brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Face protrusions
  - Head tendrils
- **Origin:** Tatooine
