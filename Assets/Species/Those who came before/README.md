# Those who came before

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Those who came before](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Those_who_came_before)

"Those who came before" was the term used by the Sebiri to describe an advanced species who they believed had created the jungle on their homeworld of Sebiris, and performed miracles with the aid of glowing charms. They also built a stone temple in the planet's equatorial region without the use of tools.
