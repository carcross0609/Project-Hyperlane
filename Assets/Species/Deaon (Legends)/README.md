# Deaon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Deaon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deaon/Legends)

The Deaons, also known by the derogatory name "Gimmers," were a sentient species native to the planet Ord Gimmel. They made up 42% of the planet's population, and were the primary labor force in its factories.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Deaon
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Origin:** Ord Gimmel
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
