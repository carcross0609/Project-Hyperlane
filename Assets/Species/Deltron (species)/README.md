# Deltron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Deltron (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deltron_%28species%29)

The Deltrons were a sentient species from the planet of the same name. They had a torso, lungs, a heart, a lipped mouth, and iron-based blood. At least one individual, Brisha Shard, had golden skin and eyes the color of bronzium.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Deltron
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Golden
- **Eyecolor:** Bronzium-colored
- **Distinctions:** Iron-based blood
- **Origin:** Deltron
