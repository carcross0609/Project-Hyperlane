# Jaltiri tribals

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jaltiri tribals](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaltiri_tribals)

The Jaltiri tribes were a cultural or/and planetary community that inhabited the world of Toskhowwl VI in the Mid Rim. Around 5 ABY, the Jaltiri tribals were known beyond their homeworld for their custom of constructing stacks made from severed heads.
