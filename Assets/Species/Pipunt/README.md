# Pipunt

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pipunt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pipunt)

Pipunt were a sentient species native to the planet Rena. Their occupations included hunting, winegrowing and winemaking. Pipunt revered the stars and a local predator, known as Renan bloodwolf, whom they called the "Great Beast Prince".
