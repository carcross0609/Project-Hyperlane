# Fluggrian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fluggrian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fluggrian/Legends)

Fluggrians were a sentient amphibious species with high foreheads that lived on the planet Ploo IV.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fluggrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:**
  - High forehead
  - Neural bundles
- **Origin:** Ploo IV
