# Ilohian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ilohian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ilohian)

Ilohians were a cultural group of humans from the planet Iloh. The most distinguishing phenotypic trait Ilohians were known for was having naturally green hair, a highly-irregular color for humans outside of the use of hair dyes. Ilohian fashion was heavily influenced by Iloh's tropical biome, and Ilohians commonly wore “iridescent combs made of (local) shells” to hold and style their hair, and simple pearl fixtures were worn as jewelry.
