# Lazerian (people)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lazerian (people)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lazerian_%28people%29)

The Lazerians were the native Human population of the planet Lazerian IV. Lazerian society was divided into a series of social classes; Royalty, Nobility, Administrators, Military, Merchants, Landowners, Craftsmen, Tradesmen, Laborers, and Rabble. The classes were not rigid, and Lazerians believed that anyone willing to work hard could get what they deserved and rise through the social ranks
