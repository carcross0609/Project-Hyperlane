# Sepan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sepan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sepan)

The Sepan were a sentient species of humanoids native to the eponymous Sepan system, a part of the Sepan sector in the Slice portion of the Expansion Region.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sepan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Races:**

  - Ripoblus
- **Origin:** Sepan system
