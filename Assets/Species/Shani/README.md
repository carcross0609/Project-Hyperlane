# Shani

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shani](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shani)

The Shani were a sentient species of tall, multicolor-feathered reptilian humanoids from a homeworld within Wild Space. They possessed extremely long lifespans, and could unhinge their jaws to devour creatures and beings whole. Shani were considered by the Hutts to be the galaxy's original bounty hunters.
