# Donadi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Donadi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Donadi)

The species was best known for its meditation techniques, in which Donadi lowered themselves into a deep trance where they could see deeper meanings in images. This method was integral to their famed Donadi stain-paintings.
