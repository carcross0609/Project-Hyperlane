# Charon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Charon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charon/Legends)

Charon were a sentient species with both humanoid and arachnid features. They had four arms, two of which were extremely powerful, and four legs, which made them very stable on their feet and were known for their violent, emotionless nature. Originally from realspace, the Charon were essentially a subspecies of the Charr Ontee of Kathol, who came from ships which were "flipped" into otherspace to a cold, bleak planet in a pocket dimension made up of black holes beyond the known confines of the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Charon
- **Races:** Warrior and bioscientist castes
- **Height:** 2.0–2.5 meters
- **Skincolor:** Gray to dark brown
- **Origin:** Unidentified planet in otherspace
