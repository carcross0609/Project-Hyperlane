# Corasgh

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corasgh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corasgh)

The Corasgh were a sentient species who, under the New Order, established a nest colony located in the Koornacht Cluster of the Core Worlds. This colony was wiped out when its planet was conquered in the Yevethan Purge.
