# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Keyorin)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Keyorin%29)

A tusked humanoid species existed in the galaxy. One individual of this species was drinking at a cantina on Keyorin when Han Solo, Lando Calrissian, and Chewbacca visited the location. A representative of this species was also present at the First Conference of Free Peoples in 4 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:**

- **Eyecolor:**

- **Distinctions:**
  - Tusks
  - Ridges on head
