# Boltrunian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Boltrunian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boltrunian/Legends)

Boltrunians were a large and muscular Near-Human species with hairless heads, craggy faces, wide noses, and heavy brow ridges. Boltrunians could live to be over 70 years old. Well-known Boltrunians included Warto and the Dark Jedi Maw.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Boltrunian
- **Skincolor:** Light brown to green
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:**
  - Warty skin
  - Heavy brow ridges
  - Reptilian appearance
- **Lifespan:** 70+ years
- **Origin:** Boltrunia system
