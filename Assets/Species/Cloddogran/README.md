# Cloddogran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cloddogran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cloddogran)

Cloddograns were a sentient species of which Bargwill Tomder was a member. At some point, two Cloddograns menaced Mahjo Reeloo in Galagolos spaceport on the planet Galagolos V until Hondo Ohnaka saved her.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cloddogran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four arms
  - Nose tendrils
- **Language:** Cloddogran
