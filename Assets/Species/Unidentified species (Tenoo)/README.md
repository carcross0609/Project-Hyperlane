# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Tenoo)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Tenoo%29)

A sentient species was seen on Tenoo during the High Republic Era. The species was also seen on Rex Strata, Batuu, Starlight Beacon, Hazun, Yarrum, and an unidentified desert world.
