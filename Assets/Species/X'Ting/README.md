# X'Ting

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X'Ting](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X%27Ting)

The X'Ting were an insectoid species, native to the planet Ord Cestus. The X'Ting were also called Cestians, although the term also came to embody anyone of any species native to the world. They were governed by the Hive Council. The X'Ting were all part of a single colony, but had many hives around the world.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** X'Ting
- **Skincolor:**
  - Gold
  - Blue
  - Red
- **Eyecolor:** Rust-red or emerald
- **Distinctions:**
  - Gender-shifting
  - Three stomachs
- **Origin:** Ord Cestus
- **Language:** X'Tingian
