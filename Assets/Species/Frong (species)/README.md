# Frong

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Frong (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frong_%28species%29)

The Frong were a species hailing from a planet of the same name. Tightly-knit and clannish, the Frong had little to offer the galaxy, although looked to the New Republic and criminal organizations such as Black Sun and the Red Key Raiders for protection during the events leading to the Battle of Jakku.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Frong
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Face-tubules, forearm suckers
- **Origin:** Frong
