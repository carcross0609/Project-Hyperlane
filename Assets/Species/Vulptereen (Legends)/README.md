# Vulptereen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vulptereen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vulptereen/Legends)

Vulptereens were a barrel-shaped sentient species with shovel-like mouths from the planet of Vulpter.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vulptereen
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:** Yellowish-green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Snouts
  - Tusks
- **Origin:** Vulpter
- **Diet:** Carnivorous
- **Language:** Vulpterish
