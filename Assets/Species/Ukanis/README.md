# Ukanis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ukanis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ukanis)

Ukanis were a species of sentients with a round body. By 16 ABY, at least one female Ukanis could speak Galactic Basic Standard and had joined the Fallanassi.
