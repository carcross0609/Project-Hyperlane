# Equani

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Equani](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Equani)

Equani were a sentient equinoid species native to Equanus in the Nharl system. They were tall bipeds who were covered in fur. All Equani possessed empathic abilities.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Equani
- **Haircolor:** Slate gray
- **Eyecolor:** Slate gray
- **Distinctions:**
  - Empathic
  - Fur-covered
- **Origin:** Equanus
