# Unidentified head-tendril species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified head-tendril species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_head-tendril_species)

The Jedi twins Tiplee and Tiplar were members of a humanoid sentient species. Their skin color was red and green, respectively, and both had blue eyes.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified head-tendril species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:**
  - Yellow-green
  - Red
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** Several tendrils in the head
