# Medoslean

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Medoslean](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Medoslean)

Medosleans were a sentient species from the Medoslea system which resembled large jellyfish. Shortly after arriving on the Starlight Beacon, Administrator Velko Jahen saw one Medoslean, spread over two cots, being administered drugs by Doctor Gino'le, the Chief of Medical Operations. The patient had an adverse reaction to the treatment, choking the nurse Okana with her fronds, but Jahen was able to stabilize her by injecting a booster.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Medoslean
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Jellyfish-like
- **Origin:** Medoslea system
