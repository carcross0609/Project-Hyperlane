# Banannaman

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Banannaman](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Banannaman)

A highly evolved type of droid that adapted organic material, such as their yellow-orange "skin," the "Banannaman" had a humanoid head, with gray photoreceptor eyes. At least one wore the clothing of an Imperial officer.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Banannaman
- **Sensor:** Gray
- **Plating:** Yellow-orange "skin"
