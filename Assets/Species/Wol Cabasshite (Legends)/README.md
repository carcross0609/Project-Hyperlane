# Wol Cabasshite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wol Cabasshite/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wol_Cabasshite/Legends)

Wol Cabasshites were sentients originating on Wol Cabassh. Evolving from a non-sentient parasitic species, the Wol Cabasshites could survive in vacuum due to their genetic makeup, making them widespread across the galaxy by the Imperial Era. Their culture was built entirely on philosophy, rather than technology, and for communication they were able to exude a magnetic field wherein they "sang" to one another. This led to confusion amongst the other sentients of the galaxy, and the Wol Cabasshites soon came to be regarded as "bizarre".

- **Name:** Wol Cabasshite

- **Origin:** Wol Cabassh
- **Diet:** High-plasma diet
- **Language:** Wol Cabasshite language
