# B'ankora

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B'ankora](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B%27ankora)

The B'ankora were a sentient species of ruminants in the galaxy. Their homeworld suffered a cataclysmic collision with a meteor at some point before around 1300 BBY, which led the Galactic Republic to give them a small sanctuary, that eventually became the B'ankor Refuge, on their capital planet Coruscant. Chasen Piian, a Supreme Chancellor at the time of the Republic granted the land in perpetuity to the surviving members of the species so they could resume their sedentary lifestyle with humble homes, fields, and trails.
