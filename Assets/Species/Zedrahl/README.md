# Zedrahl

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Zedrahl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zedrahl)

The Zedrahls were a species of sentient, reptilian Humanoids. Their skin was covered in scales. The species was in a major nuclear conflict amongst themselves caused by Arglos, a fanatical, religious leader who sought to eliminate all life on their planet.
