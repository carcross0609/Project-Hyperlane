# Samhari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Samhari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Samhari)

The Samhari were a sentient species of Near-Humans who inhabited Samhar, a planet in the Grumani sector. Their homeworld, covered in vast, green forests, was near the hyperlane known as the Duros Space Run; however, the Samhari held themselves aloof from the surrounding territories. Their insularity saw them focus more on their own internal intrigues than those of the wider galaxy.
