# Siniteen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Siniteen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Siniteen/Legends)

Siniteen were hyperintelligent humanoids, capable of calculating hyperspace jumps without the assistance of a navicomputer.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Siniteen
- **Skincolor:** Olive to gray
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Pupilless
  - Extremely powerful brains
