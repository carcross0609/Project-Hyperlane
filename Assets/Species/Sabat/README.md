# Sabat

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sabat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sabat)

The Sabat were a sentient species in the galaxy. The Sabat had blue, pink, or tan colored skin, large foreheads and flattened heads. Leevan Tenza, a member of Saw Gerrera's Partisans, was a Sabat. Gelan Yees was a Sabat member of the Cloud-Riders. At least one Sabat was a Jedi Initiate during the Republic Era.
