# Farn Klin's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Farn Klin's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Farn_Klin%27s_species)

This species had light pink skin. At least two juvenile members of this species, including youngling Farn Klin, were present in the Jedi Temple on the galactic capital Coruscant around 21 BBY for a hologram transmission from General Grievous of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Farn Klin's species
- **Skincolor:** Light pink
- **Eyecolor:** Light brown
