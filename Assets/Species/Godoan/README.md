# Godoan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Godoan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Godoan)

The Godoans were a sentient species of deeply religious people who had a great reverence for the Dancing Goddess idol. They originated from the Godo system.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Godoan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Godo system
