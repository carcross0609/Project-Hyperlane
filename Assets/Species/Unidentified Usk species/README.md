# Unidentified Usk species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Usk species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Usk_species)

The planet Usk, in the Usk system, was home to a native sentient species. At some point, the planet's indigenous inhabitants were enslaved by the Hutt species, and their homeworld was considered part of the region of the galaxy known as Hutt Space, controlled by their masters, as early as 25,127 BBY. Some time after they were enslaved, the species was driven to extinction, and, eventually, the name of the species was forgotten by the rest of the galaxy.
