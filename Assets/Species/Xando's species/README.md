# Xando's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xando's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xando%27s_species)

A sentient species had horns, toothed mandibles, and glossy dark eyes. In 0 BBY, six members of the species who were part of the Norclu Order were seeking to undergo a pilgrimage from the planet Tatooine to the world of Norclune. They attempted to arrange transportation with the smuggler Han Solo; however, he refused the aliens' request.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Xando's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Reddish brown
- **Eyecolor:** Dark red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Horns
  - Mandibles with teeth
  - Three-digit hands
