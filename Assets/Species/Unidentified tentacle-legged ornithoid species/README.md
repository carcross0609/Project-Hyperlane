# Unidentified tentacle-legged ornithoid species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified tentacle-legged ornithoid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_tentacle-legged_ornithoid_species)

This sentient species were distinguished by their yellow skin, including the cluster of tentacles they used for locomotion, as well as a lumpy patch on the front of their face. Their heads resembled those of many avian species, covered in blue feathers, and featuring an orange beak, as well as squinted eyes. Two members of this species visited the Bovris Tapcafe on planet Ifron during 4 ABY..
