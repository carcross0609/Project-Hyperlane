# Shungbeek

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shungbeek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shungbeek)

Shungbeek were a sentient species that pupated at some point in their lifetime. They breathed an argon-rich atmosphere, and had to wear an enclosed perspex dome to survive in other conditions. Kid Malmash was a young Shungbeek who worked as a pilot, transporting refugees to safe destinations.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Shungbeek
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Enclosed perspex dome
