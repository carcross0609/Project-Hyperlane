# Unidentified six-eyed tall-headed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified six-eyed tall-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_six-eyed_tall-headed_species)

The species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their elongated heads, with six eyes featuring red pupils situated on horizontal eye stalks, as well as ridges on the center of their face and downturned mouths with large lips right above their necks, though lacked of any other visible facial features. They also sometimes possessed reddish-brown skin.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified six-eyed tall-headed species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Reddish-brown
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Six-eyed
