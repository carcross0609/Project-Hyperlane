# Veknoid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Veknoid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veknoid/Legends)

Veknoids, also known as Velkoids, were a species native to the planet Moonus Mandel.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Veknoid
- **Height:** 1.2–1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Dark brown to pale orange
- **Distinctions:**
  - Fanged teeth
  - Long tails
- **Origin:** Moonus Mandel
