# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Qiaxx)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Qiaxx%29)

This species was a sentient species of long-necked humanoids with tan skin, solid black eyes, and beak-like mouths. Several members of this species could be found on the planet Qiaxx by 4 ABY, all employed at Dequc's casino.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Beak-like mouths
  - Long necks
