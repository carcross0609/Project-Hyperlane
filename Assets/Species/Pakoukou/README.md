# Pakoukou

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pakoukou](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pakoukou)

Pakoukou was a species of large intelligent mollusk that existed in the galaxy. At the time of the Invasion of Naboo, one such sentient mollusk kept records for Senator Tikkes, who represented his homeworld of Mon Cala in the Galactic Republic's Senate. It rested in a pool in Tikkes's office at the Senate Building.

- **Designation:** Sentient
