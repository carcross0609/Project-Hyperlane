# Kesselian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kesselian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kesselian)

The Kesselians were a cultural group of humans who called the planets of Kessel and Little Kessel home, located in the Kessel sector of the Outer Rim Territories.

- **Name:** Kesselian
- **Class:** Human
- **Origin:**
