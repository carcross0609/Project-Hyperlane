# Vonomi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vonomi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vonomi)

The Vonomi had four arms and four legs, each having a tentacle-like appearance. DJ Rangthang was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vonomi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - 4 arms
  - 4 legs
  - Tentacles on their faces
