# Corellian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corellian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellian/Legends)

Corellian was a term that referred to inhabitants of the Corellian system, such as the Human natives of the planet Corellia. The Drall Ebrihim, who was raised on the Corellian system planet Drall, was a Corellian who looked after the children of the Human Corellian Han Solo, a smuggler–turned–New Republic agent. The Human Kaj Nedmak, who hailed from Drall, was also a Corellian smuggler.
