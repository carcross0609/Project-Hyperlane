# Balosar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Balosar (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balosar_%28species%29/Legends)

Balosars were sentient near-human species native to the planet Balosar in the Core Worlds of the galaxy. Easily recognized by a row of retractable antennapalps which sprouted from their skulls and allowed them to listen into the subsonic range.
