# Eddel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eddel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eddel)

The Eddel, or Eddelians, were a sentient species known for their quick wit. They were native to Fehern, a hospitable Mid Rim planet. The Eddelians traveled throughout the Trax sector, and their distinctively styled Eddelian starships, carrying Eddelian traders, were a common sight in neighboring star systems.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Eddel
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Quick-witted
