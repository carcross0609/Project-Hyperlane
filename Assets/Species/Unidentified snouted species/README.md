# Unidentified snouted species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified snouted species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_snouted_species)

A bipedal sentient species had large, angular skulls; wide-set, reptilian eyes; and a long, low-slung, flat-nosed snout. They could have gray or brown skin, black hair, and black eyes. The mercenaries Dered Karno and Ommo Twilto belonged to the species, as did the identical twins Bosvarga and Cerensco, who worked for Ochi of Bestoon.
