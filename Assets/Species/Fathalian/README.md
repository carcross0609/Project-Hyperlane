# Fathalian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fathalian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fathalian)

Fathalians were human immigrants who inhabited the Outer Rim world of JanFathal. Many of them resided in JanFathal's capital, Athar, and were known as Athari. They had dark skin, and wore their hair in neatly-coiled plaits.
