# Unidentified bubble-eyed species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified bubble-eyed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_bubble-eyed_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their blue and red skin with large heads, bubble eyes, and downturned mouths. In 5 BBY, one member of this species was present at the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport on the planet Coruscant.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified bubble-eyed species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue and red
- **Distinctions:** Bubble eyes
