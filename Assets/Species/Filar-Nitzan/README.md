# Filar-Nitzan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Filar-Nitzan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Filar-Nitzan)

The Filar-Nitzan were a sentient species in the galaxy. The High Republic Era Jedi Master Nubarron, who looked like a pink-and-purple cloud shot through with lightning, refused to identify their species, leading to speculation they were a member of a previously unknown race of Filar-Nitzan.
