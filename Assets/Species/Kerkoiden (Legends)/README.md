# Kerkoiden

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kerkoiden/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kerkoiden/Legends)

Kerkoidens were a rough-skinned alien species with clawed hands and elongated snouts, from the planet Kerkoidia in the Expansion Region.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kerkoiden
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.4 meters
- **Mass:** 55 kilograms
- **Skincolor:** Green to dark blue
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Conical head
  - Snout
  - Long claws
  - Jutting teeth
- **Lifespan:** Up to 90 standard years
- **Origin:** Kerkoidia
- **Language:** Kerkoidese
