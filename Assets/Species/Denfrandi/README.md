# Denfrandi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Denfrandi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Denfrandi)

, a Denfrandi.]] The Denfrandi were a tribe of Humans native to Taloraan, who were generally friendly towards strangers. They lived aboard airships called sleft-chuffni, which were in reality domesticated creatures inhabiting the higher atmosphere of Taloraan with gondolas attached to them.
