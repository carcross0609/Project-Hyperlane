# Zisian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zisian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zisian)

Zisians were Near-Human species native to Zisia in the Hutt Space. ==Anatomy== Zisians had scaly green skin and orange eyes. In their old age, they often suffered from rheumy eyes and wrinkled skin hanging in loose flaps. One individual of the race was Ganar Tos. ==History== Zisians evolved on Zisia, a rich planet which belonged inside the region of space dominated and controlled by the Hutts, known as the Hutt Space. Zisians were docile and compliant and were enslaved, millennia before the Imperial Era. ==Behind the scenes== While the physical description of Ganar Tos seems somewhat reptilian, his voice is described as "human," the term humanoid is used in the text rather than Near-Human, and he apparently expected to be able to have children with a Human woman, Bria Tharen. For this to be possible, the Zisian species would have to be closely related to baseline Humans.
