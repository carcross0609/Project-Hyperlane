# Yaga

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yaga (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yaga_%28species%29)

The Yagai (singular, Yaga) were sentient insectoids indigenous to Yaga Minor. They were one of the many species enslaved and exploited by the Galactic Empire.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yaga
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Subspecies:** Yaga drone
- **Height:** 1.5–1.8 meters
- **Distinctions:** Three legs, nine fingers
- **Origin:** Yaga Minor
