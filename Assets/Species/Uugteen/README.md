# Uugteen

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Uugteen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uugteen)

The Uugteen were pale near-humans who resided on the planet Akiva. They were usually kept to jungles, canyons, and caves, although some lived in an abandoned droid factory in the catacombs beneath the city of Myrra. They lived in groups. The group of Uugteen that lived in the factory under Myrra wore battle droid parts as armor.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Uugteen
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Distinctions:** Noseless
- **Origin:** Akiva
- **Habitat:** Caves, canyons, and forests
