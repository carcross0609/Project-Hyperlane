# Keshiri

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Keshiri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keshiri)

The Keshiri were a sentient species from the Wild Space planet Kesh. They could have skin colors of either, different shades of lavender and violet or blue, and could have red eyes and purple hair. Sohi and Chellwinark Frethylrin were members of the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Keshiri
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Different shades of lavender and violet
  - Blue
- **Haircolor:** Purple
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Origin:** Kesh
