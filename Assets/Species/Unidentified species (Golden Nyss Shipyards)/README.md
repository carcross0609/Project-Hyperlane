# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Golden Nyss Shipyards)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Golden_Nyss_Shipyards%29)

A diminutive species of pale humanoids. A distinctive feature of the species was the presence of three fingers on each of their four hands and round white eyes without pupils.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** Around 1 meter
- **Skincolor:** Light blue
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** White
- **Distinctions:**
  - Two pairs of three-fingered hands
  - Pointed ears
  - Hairless body
  - Lack of nose
