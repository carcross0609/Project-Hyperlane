# Zoa'mon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zoa'mon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zoa%27mon)

The Zoa'mon were a sentient species. According to the play Evocar, which was written by the Hutt playwright Direus'pei between 920 BBY and 671 BBY, the Zoa'mon were driven to extinction by the Tionese warlord Xim during his conquests of the Tion Cluster that occurred between 25,130 and 25,100 BBY. In the play, Xim is captured and placed on trial by the Hutt Empire. During the trial, Xim justified the extinction of the Zoa'mon and other species by arguing that they were all slavers. However according to The Despotica, a collection of literary works about Xim and his conquests, Direus'pei bypassed accepted history and chronology while writing Evocar in order to use Xim and other famous figures as a political screed to excoriated the Hutts' continued subjugation of the Evocii, a species native to the planet Nal Hutta.
