# Yuvernian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yuvernian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuvernian)

Yuvernians were a two-headed sentient species from the Yuvern system, they each had two spot-covered heads sitting atop a long neck. Cane Adiss, a member of Jabba the Hutt's criminal empire, was a male Yuvernian.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yuvernian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Two heads
- **Origin:** Yuvern system
