# Regineer Teed's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Regineer Teed's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Regineer_Teed%27s_species)

A species of sentient humanoid with yellow-and-blue skin and a pair of protruding tusks existed in the galaxy by the time of the reign of the Galactic Empire. Regineer Teed was a member of the species who, in 10 BBY, was part of the Cloud-Riders pirate gang, with the membership of the group being drawn from worlds victimized by the Five Crime Syndicates. Nineteen years later, several members of the same species were also present in various Outer Rim Territories locations.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Regineer Teed's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow with blue mottling
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Tusks
