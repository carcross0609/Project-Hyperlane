# Unidentified long-necked dark-eyed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified long-necked dark-eyed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_long-necked_dark-eyed_species)

This species was a sentient species distinguished by their long necks and large dark eyes. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified long-necked dark-eyed species
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:** Long necks
