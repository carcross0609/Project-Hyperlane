# Syren plant

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Syren plant/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syren_plant/Legends)

The carnivorous Syren plants were native to the planet Kashyyyk. They could be found in the dangerous jungles of the planet's surface, far below the canopy level where the Wookiees dwelled.
