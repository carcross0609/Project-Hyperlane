# Evocii

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Evocii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Evocii)

The Evocii were a primitive humanoid race from Evocar, which later became the capital Hutt world Nal Hutta.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Evocii
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Distinctions:**
  - Frail, gangly stature
  - Wide, distinctive noses
  - Preferred diet of dried fruits and boiled vegetables
- **Origin:** Evocar
- **Language:** Evocii
