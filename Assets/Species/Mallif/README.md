# Mallif

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mallif](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mallif)

Mallif were the native inhabitants of the planet Recopia. Descendants of the Seyugi Dervishes, they were mysterious religious monks, who broadcasted their strange philosophies through the HoloNet. A few of them continued to maintain the fortress on the Mallif Cove, in which a thousand of Seyugi Dervishes were kept frozen in carbonite, awaiting release.
