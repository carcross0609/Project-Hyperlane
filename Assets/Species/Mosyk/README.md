# Mosyk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mosyk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mosyk)

The Mosyk were a sentient reptilian species. They had green skin, a bone-like structure on the back of their heads—similar to Vurk—as well as as three fingers on each hand. The Jedi Master Eeko-Dio Daki, who survived Order 66 and went into hiding with his Padawan Devon Izara on the planet Janix, was a member of the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mosyk
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Skincolor:** Green and grey
- **Distinctions:**
  - Bone-like structure on the back of their heads
  - Three fingers
