# Unidentified thin-chinned species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified thin-chinned species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_thin-chinned_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. They were distinguished by their elongated faces and thin chins. One member of this species was present at Grappa the Hutt's palace in 11 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified thin-chinned species
- **Skincolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:** Elongated face
