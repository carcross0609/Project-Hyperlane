# Ilosian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ilosian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ilosian)

A sentient species, the Ilosians hailed from the agricultural world Ilos. Millennia before the reign of the Galactic Empire, the Ilosians settled many nearby worlds before becoming engulfed in Hutt Space and enslaved by the Hutts. The Ilosians, however, preserved relatively more freedom than other species in the Hutts' servitude.
