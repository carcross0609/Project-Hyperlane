# Kadrillian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kadrillian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kadrillian)

Kadrillians were a sentient species from the Kadril system in the Outer Rim Territories. They possessed terrapin half-shells, and had scaled skin which could be orange in hue. During the High Republic Era, Detective Tals Trilby of the Temple District Police on the planet Coruscant was a Kadrillian.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kadrillian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Orange
- **Distinctions:** Terrapin half shell
- **Origin:** Kadril system
