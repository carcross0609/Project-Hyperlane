# Storm-hair

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Storm-hair](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Storm-hair)

The Unknown Regions warlike species known to the insectoid Quesoth as the Storm-hairs emerged from an Unknown Regions planet, led by the brutal Storm-hair Warlord Nuso Esva, and ravaged Unknown Regions and Wild Space. By 0 ABY, Nuso Esva's realm had expanded its reign—considered a reign of terror—to the Galactic Empire's fringes.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Storm-hair
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Multicolored
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Skin shimmering like a rainbow
  - Multifaceted eyes
- **Origin:** Nuso Esva's point of origin
- **Language:** Nuso Esva's language
