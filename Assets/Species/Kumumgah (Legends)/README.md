# Kumumgah

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kumumgah/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kumumgah/Legends)

The Kumumgah were an ancient species from Outer Rim planet of Tatooine. They developed spaceflight, drawing the attention of the Rakata, who conquered, enslaved, and annexed them to their Infinite Empire in 25,793 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kumumgah
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Origin:** Tatooine
