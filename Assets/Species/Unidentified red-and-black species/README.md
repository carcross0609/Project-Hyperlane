# Unidentified red-and-black species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified red-and-black species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_red-and-black_species)

A sentient species existed in the galaxy. These aliens were humanoids that were distinguished by their red skin, as well as black pointed stripes on their face and black hair. One member of this species was present at a bar on the moon of Shakari during or after 11 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified red-and-black species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Haircolor:** Black
