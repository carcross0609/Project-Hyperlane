# Ginmid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ginmid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ginmid)

The Ginmids were a sentient species. They were bipedal with two five-fingered hands, light green skin, and a flattened nose with prominent nostrils. At some point prior to the outbreak of the First Order-Resistance War in 34 ABY, the Ginmid Tommet Sozach was the commander of a mercenary army composed exclusively of Ginmids. After the galaxy entered a prolonged period of peace, the army was disbanded and Sozach struck out on his own, making a living as a crowd-pleasing trick shooter.
