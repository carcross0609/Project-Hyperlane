# Themian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Themian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Themian)

The Themians were a sentient species of Near-Humans from the Inner Rim planet Themis. During the Clone Wars, they were ruled by Prince Lumon.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Themian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Skincolor:** Brown and black
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Origin:** Themis
