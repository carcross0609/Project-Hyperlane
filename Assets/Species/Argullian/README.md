# Argullian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Argullian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Argullian)

Argullians were a sentient, near-Human species from the Mid Rim planet Argul, located in the D'Aelgoth Sector.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Argullian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Broad, flat noses
- **Origin:** Argul
