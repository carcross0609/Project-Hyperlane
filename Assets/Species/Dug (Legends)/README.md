# Dug

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dug/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dug/Legends)

Dugs were an aggressive mammalian species native to the planet Malastare that used their upper limbs for locomotion and their lower limbs for grappling.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dug
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Mass:**
  - 30 kilograms (female)
  - 35 kilograms (male)
- **Skincolor:**
  - Brown
  - Purple
  - Gray
  - Red
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Yellow
  - Blue
- **Distinctions:** Arms used as legs and legs used as arms
- **Lifespan:** Up to 75 standard years
- **Origin:** Malastare
- **Language:** Dug
