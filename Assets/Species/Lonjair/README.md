# Lonjair

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lonjair](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lonjair)

The Lonjair were a shy, self-centered species that primarily occupied a cluster of three systems in the South Arm. Lonjair each had four turquoise eyes, indigo skin, and a tuft of blue hair on top of one's head.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lonjair
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** ½ meter
- **Skincolor:** Indigo
- **Haircolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Turquoise
- **Distinctions:** Four eyes
- **Origin:** Lonjair Prime system
