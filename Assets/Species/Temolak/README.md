# Temolak

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Temolak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temolak)

Temolak were a sentient species indigenous to Zirulast. They were renowned as scavengers throughout the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Temolak
- **Height:** 1.9 – 2.3 meters
- **Skincolor:** Reddish
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Heavily muscled, fin-like structure on underside of forearms
- **Lifespan:** 100 years
- **Origin:** Zirulast
- **Language:** Temolish
