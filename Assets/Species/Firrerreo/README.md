# Firrerreo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Firrerreo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firrerreo)

The Firrerreo were a sentient species from the Firrerre system in the Outer Rim Territories. The Jedi Initiate Connis Dav, who was active shortly after the beginning of the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems in 22 BBY, was a Firrerreo.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Firrerreo
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Firrerre system
