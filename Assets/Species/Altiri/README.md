# Altiri

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Altiri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Altiri)

The Altiri were a sentient species. They were known to have purple skin and white hair. Some individuals had a gem in the middle of their forehead and above their eyes. Jedi Master Lene Kostana and her family belonged to the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Altiri
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Purple
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Purple (with brown sclera)
- **Origin:** Altiria/Anarris system
