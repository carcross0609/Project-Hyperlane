# Falleen

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Falleen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Falleen)

Falleen were a repto-mammalian sentient species native to the planet of the same name. They were capable of projecting powerful pheromones. During the Clone Wars, the Falleen Ziton Moj replaced a cabal of Falleen nobles in leading the Black Sun criminal syndicate from their fortress on Mustafar.
