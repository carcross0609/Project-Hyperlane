# Saheelindeeli

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saheelindeeli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saheelindeeli)

The Saheelindeeli were sapient primates indigenous to Saheelindeel. The ape-like aliens were green-furred humanoids. They had limited technology, and were just starting to move ahead toward automation by 0 ABY. They had a matriarchal society.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Saheelindeeli
- **Haircolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Densely green-furred primates
- **Origin:** Saheelindeel
- **Language:** Saheelindeeli language
