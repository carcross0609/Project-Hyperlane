# K'aarg

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [K'aarg](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K%27aarg)

K'aargs were large, bipedal reptilian humanoids from the planet Shira. They were described as being not very accommodating, and were feared for their mouth full of sharp teeth.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** K'aarg
- **Class:** Reptile
- **Height:** 1.8–2.2 meters
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Immunity to poison
- **Origin:** Shira
