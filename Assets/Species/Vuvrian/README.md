# Vuvrian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vuvrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vuvrian)

Vuvrians were a sentient species of humanoids whose oblong, insectile head featured twelve eyes and two long antenna-like appendages. Hailing from Vurdon Ka, they never established any colony world. Thanks to their heightened senses, Vuvrians were exceptionally perceptive. They were renowned across the galaxy for their skills in diplomacy, sales, and negotiation, and many of them excelled as Jedi. In 1 BBY, a Vuvrian named Wioslea bought Luke Skywalker's x-34 landspeeder in Mos Eisley, Tatooine.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vuvrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid with insectile traits
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Oblong head with twelve eyes
- **Origin:** Vurdon Ka
