# Unidentified orange species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified orange species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_orange_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their orange skin, large noses, and low-set mouths. In 5 BBY, a member of this species was enjoying the beaches of the planet Niamos with another alien individual when Cassian Jeron Andor was wrongly arrested by the Galactic Empire.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified orange species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Orange
