# Khramboan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Khramboan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khramboan)

The Khramboans were a diminutive species of blue-skinned, black-eyed sentients. Khramboan eyes were on the sides of horizontal eyestalks at the center of their egg-shaped heads, and their mouth was covered with tentacle-like appendages. Four holes were present at the center of their "forehead." Khramboans had also three-fingered stubby hands. Droogan, a vendor who operated on Coruscant, was a Khramboan, as was his grandfather.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Khramboan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Two eyes on horizontal eyestalks
  - Mouth hidden behind two thick face-tentacles
  - Three-fingered stubby hands
- **Origin:** Khramboa system
