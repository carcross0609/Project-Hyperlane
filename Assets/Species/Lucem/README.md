# Lucem

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lucem](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucem)

Lucem were a species of humanoids found in the galaxy. Lucem were distinguishable by their nearly-translucent pale skin, white hair, and unique eyes. A Lucem's eyes were fully black with many small white pupils. The Lucem had control over their many pupils, which they could move around their eyes as a defense mechanism to cause vertigo in individuals perceived as a threat.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lucem
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black iris and sclera, with many white pupils
