# Unidentified large-upper-teeth species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified large-upper-teeth species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_large-upper-teeth_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their long snouts and buck teeth. In 4 ABY, at least one member of this species could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren, and another in a casino on the planet Qiaxx.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified large-upper-teeth species
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long snouts
  - Buck teeth
