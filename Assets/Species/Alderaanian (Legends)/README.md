# Alderaanian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Alderaanian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alderaanian/Legends)

Alderaanians were natives, mostly Human, of the planet Alderaan. Notable members included Bail Organa, Leia Organa Solo, Tycho Celchu, Winter Celchu, Ulic Qel-Droma, Cay Qel-Droma, Wade Vox, and Raymus Antilles. They were celebrated as a peaceful and cultured race, with a long-standing involvement in galactic politics.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Alderaanian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Alderaan
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
