# Riorian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Riorian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Riorian/Legends)

Riorians were an insectoid sentient species native to the planet Rior. They were characterized by their large, red eyes and sensitive hearing. While most were peaceful, a maniacal male Riorian known as Stalnus was wanted for murder by the New Republic by the year 7 ABY. In addition, at least one Riorian was a member of the Karazak Slavers Cooperative.
