# Atterran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Atterran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atterran)

The Atterran were a race of pale-skinned humans that inhabit the planet Atterra Bravo. Casca Panzoro was an Atterran.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Atterran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Human
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Origin:** Atterra Bravo
