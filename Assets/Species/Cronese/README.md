# Cronese

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cronese](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cronese)

The Cronese were culture of the Human Tionese people that inhabited the region of the historical Kingdom of Cron in the Tion Cluster, a region later known as the Cronese Mandate. The Cronese people were mindful of their history, considering themselves superior to the rest of the Tionese owing to the fact that they had been a kingdom under King Ferece long before the rest of the Cluster had been united by Xer VIII.
