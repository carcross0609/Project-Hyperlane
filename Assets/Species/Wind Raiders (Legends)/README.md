# Wind Raiders

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wind Raiders/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Raiders/Legends)

, a Wind Raider.]] The Wind Raiders were a tribe of Humans native to Taloraan. While normally peaceful, they became more warlike when Genogri assumed the title of chieftain some time during the Galactic Civil War. Greedy and power-hungry, he transformed the otherwise peaceful people into a fierce warrior people.
