# Qiraash

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Qiraash](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qiraash)

The Qiraash were a near-human sentient species identifiable by their pink skin and large craniums. Leesub Sirln was a Qiraash who was present at Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina shortly before the Battle of Yavin.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Qiraash
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale pink
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Large craniums
