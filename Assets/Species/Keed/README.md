# Keed

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Keed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keed)

The Keed were a near-Human species from the Outer Rim Territories planet Keedad. They shared their homeworld with Human colonists since the early history of the Galactic Republic. Around 26 BBY, the Keed/Human hybrid Ytavarg Aleema was born on Keedad. Ger Plortor, a scout for the Shad Furies shockball team, recruited Aleema after witnessing him participate in a lettranin game on Keedad. Afterward, the Keed athlete eventually became the most renowned shockball player in the galaxy. At one point during the Galactic Civil War, after killing a group of Human Galactic Empire agents in retaliation for their murder of a fellow alien shockball player, Aleema also became a secret Rebel Alliance patron and agent.
