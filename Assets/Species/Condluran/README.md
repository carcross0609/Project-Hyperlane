# Condluran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Condluran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Condluran)

Condlurans were a sentient species that existed within the galaxy. The Condluran Briff worked as a smuggler on Jedha during the High Republic Era. During the Imperial Era, Freck was a Condluran male who worked as a transport driver for the Galactic Empire on the mining planet Mapuzo.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Condluran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Snout with small tendrils protruding around it
