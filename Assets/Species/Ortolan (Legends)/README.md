# Ortolan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ortolan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ortolan/Legends)

Ortolans were a race of stocky, bipeds with long snouts, and large floppy ears. Ortolans came from the frigid, mineral-rich planet Orto. Two of the most famous Ortolans were the Jedi Knight Nem Bees and the musician Max Rebo.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ortolan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Elephantine
- **Races:** Ortolan tribe
- **Height:** 1.1–1.7 meters; 1.4 meters average
- **Skincolor:** Covered in short fur in various colors
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Stocky builds, trunklike noses, floppy ears, small mouths
- **Lifespan:** 71+ standard years
- **Origin:** Orto
- **Language:** Ortolan
