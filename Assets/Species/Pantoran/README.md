# Pantoran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pantoran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pantoran)

The Pantorans were blue-skinned near-humans native to the moon Pantora. They were part of the Galactic Republic, during the waning years of that government.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pantoran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Haircolor:** Pastel colors
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Gold
  - Black
  - Blue
- **Origin:** Pantora
- **Habitat:** Marshy tundra
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
