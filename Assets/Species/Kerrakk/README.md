# Kerrakk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kerrakk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kerrakk)

The Kerrakk were an insectoid sentient species whose members were grouped in hives. A hive of Kerrakk enforcers operated during the Imperial Era, but were killed by the bounty hunter crew led by T'onga after they attempted to rob the Carbon Score Cantina on Burnin Konn.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kerrakk
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
