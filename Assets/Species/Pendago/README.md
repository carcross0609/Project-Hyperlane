# Pendago

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pendago](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pendago)

Pendago were a sentient species with multiple tentacled limbs. One Pendago was a member of the Razorclaw Gang on Serenno, and had several of its limbs cut off by Asajj Ventress, who was attempting to learn the location of the Crolute criminal Glute.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pendago
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** At least 4 tentacled limbs
