# Unidentified small-mouthed species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified small-mouthed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_small-mouthed_species)

A bipedal sentient species with distinguishably small mouths and tan skin existed in the galaxy. In 5 BBY, one member of the species walked around on the planet Ferrix. Decades later, in 35 ABY, another member of the species was in a bar at Kijimi City of the planet Kijimi.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified small-mouthed species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Tan
