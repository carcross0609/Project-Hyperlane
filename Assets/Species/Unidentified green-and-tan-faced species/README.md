# Unidentified green-and-tan-faced species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified green-and-tan-faced species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green-and-tan-faced_species)

A sentient species with green-and-tan skin existed in the galaxy. The aliens were short and had two legs and two arms that ended in three fingers each. They had pointed ears, red eyes, upturned noses, and ridges on their forehead. Members of the species were capable of consuming a type of beverage.

- **Designation:** Sentient

  - Upturned nose
