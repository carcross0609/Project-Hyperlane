# Unidentified one-eyed tentacled species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified one-eyed tentacled species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_one-eyed_tentacled_species)

This species was a sentient species distinguished by their green and white skin, with starfish-shaped bodies, including tentacles, as well as a single large eye in the center of their body sporting a bright red iris. In 4 ABY, at least three members of this species were present in a casino on the planet Qiaxx.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified one-eyed tentacled species
- **Skincolor:** Green and white
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Tentacles
