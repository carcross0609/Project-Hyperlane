# Keteerian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Keteerian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keteerian)

Keteerians were a sentient species that inhabited the galaxy. Teroj Kee, an ally of the First Order, was a Keteerian, as was Terrib Igmusk, a hotel employee at Canto Bight.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Keteerian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Brown
  - Orange
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
- **Origin:** Keteeria system
