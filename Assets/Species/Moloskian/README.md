# Moloskian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moloskian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moloskian)

Moloskians were a species originating within the Moloskia system. They had eyes in the backs of their heads.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Moloskian
- **Distinctions:** Eyes in the back of the head
- **Origin:** Within the Moloskia system
