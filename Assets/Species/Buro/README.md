# Buro

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Buro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Buro)

The Buro were a species of sentient insects that were considered ethereally beautiful by some other species. They were native to Kaisa, a planet located in the Corva sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The Buro had no presence offworld, but their homeworld did receive some visitors, among them a Human-crewed, geological survey starship. The survey crew negligently destroyed a forest on Kaisa, and the destruction of this habitat led to the extinction of the Buro species.
