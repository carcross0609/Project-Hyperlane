# Noti

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Noti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noti)

The Noti were an extragalactic, sentient, crustacean-like species native to the planet Peridea, a ringed planet located in the far galaxy, far away from the main galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Noti
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 90 cm (2 ft 11 in)
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:**
  - Eyestalks
  - Rock-like shells
- **Origin:** Peridea
- **Habitat:** Grasslands
- **Language:** Noti language
