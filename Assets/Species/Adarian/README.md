# Adarian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Adarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adarian)

Adarians were a sentient species from the Adari system in the Inner Rim that possessed a cranial aperture that enhanced their focus and amplified sound waves. One Adarian called Twirm served as submister in Latts Razzi's criminal syndicate and recorded events in the Smuggler's Guide.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Adarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Cranial aperture
- **Origin:** Adari system
