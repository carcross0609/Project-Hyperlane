# Lurker (Anoat)

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lurker (Anoat)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lurker_%28Anoat%29)

The Lurkers were the mutated, degenerated survivors of the gassing of Anoat by the Galactic Empire. By the time of the Iron Blockade, they roamed in the abandoned cities of Anoat, accompanied by packs of Akk dogs.
