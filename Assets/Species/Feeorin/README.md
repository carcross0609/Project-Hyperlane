# Feeorin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Feeorin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Feeorin)

Feeorins were a sentient species with thick tendrils hanging from the back of their heads. During the early years of the Galactic Empire's reign, a Feeorin named Warjak performed as a gladiator, holding the title of champion of the Outer Rim Carve-up. Zira was a female pirate who was also a member of this species.
