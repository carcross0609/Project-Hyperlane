# Pakiphantos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pakiphantos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pakiphantos)

Pakiphantos were a sentient elephantine species that existed in the galaxy. They had large ears, pillar-like feet, and trunks which they could use to make a trumpeting noise.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pakiphantos
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Elephantine
- **Distinctions:**
  - Large ears
  - Trunk
