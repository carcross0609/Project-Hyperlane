# Unidentified Davnar II species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Davnar II species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Davnar_II_species)

A reptilian species was from the planet Davnar II. They were bipedal and covered in scales, with long snouts. The species as a whole was highly regarded for their flair for execution. A Davnarian was employed by the loan shark Ploovo Two-For-One as a bodyguard during his encounter with Han Solo on Etti IV in 2 BBY.
