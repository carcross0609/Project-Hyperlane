# Ocsin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ocsin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ocsin)

The Ocsin, also known as the Ocsinin, were a near-Human species with white skin and black, pupilless eyes that hailed from the Wild Space celestial body Farana. Believed to be descended from the Human Tionese culture of the Tion Cluster, the Ocsin established first contact with the Galactic Republic at some point before 4500 BBY. The species' members included merchants and explorers, and it established at least several dozen colonies in Farana's galactic neighborhood, including the worlds Ocsin and Saclas. The female Ocsin Cene Gilvent grew up on Saclas, eventually traveling offworld where she became one of the founding members of the Zulirian Swordmasters group of melee weapon afficionados.
