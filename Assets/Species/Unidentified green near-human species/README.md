# Unidentified green near-human species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified green near-human species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green_near-human_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their green skin and colorful hair. In 4 ABY, numerous members of this species could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren, serving who was believed to be Jeodu criminal Dequc, though it turned out to only be a decoy.
