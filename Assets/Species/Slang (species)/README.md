# Slang

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Slang (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slang_%28species%29)

The Slangs were small, nomadic beings that traveled the galaxy as true space scrappers. They had four arms with long, thin fingers, a stocky thorax, and an elongated face.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Slang
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Four arms
