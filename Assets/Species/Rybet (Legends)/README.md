# Rybet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rybet/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rybet/Legends)

The Rybet were a species of sentient amphibians. Some aspects of their life cycle resembled those of insects.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Rybet
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian with insect-like life cycle , Batrachian (frog-like)
- **Height:** 1.2 to 1.9 meters
- **Skincolor:** Bright green, Deep olive
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Webbed hands, multi-use tongue
- **Origin:** Varl (possibly)
- **Diet:** Insectivore
- **Language:** Rybese
