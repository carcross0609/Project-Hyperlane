# Unidentified pink pachydermoid species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified pink pachydermoid species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pink_pachydermoid_species/Legends)

A sentient pink-skinned pachydermoid species existed in the galaxy that was short of stature with large three-fingered hands and two-toed feet. The species had a prominent trunk and tusks, but lacked visible ears.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified pink pachydermoid species
- **Class:** Pachydermoid
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Trunk
  - Tusks
