# Tof

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tof](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tof)

The Tof were a sentient humanoid species in the galaxy. They had green skin and were much taller than the average human. The species had potential to be quite strong as warriors, and could speak in Galactic Basic Standard. During the Fenril crisis, Anzati sorceress Reyna Oskure had several Tof soldiers in her employ on Nagi.
