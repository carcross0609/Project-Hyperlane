# Ame Llom's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ame Llom's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ame_Llom%27s_species)

Ame Llom's species was a sentient Near-Human species that possessed blue-green skin and black hair. In 22 BBY, Ame Llom, a female member of the species, visited the Outlander Club on her homeworld, the planet Coruscant, when the bounty hunter Zam Wesell hid there.
