# Leviathan

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Leviathan (Arrakan)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leviathan_%28Arrakan%29)

The Leviathans were a tentacled sentient species residing on the waterworld Arrakan. The thousand-year-old Force-sensitive Leviathan Kithnarrock was worshipped as a god by the planet's Battrach species, and during the Galactic Civil War, he helped a group of Jedi and their companions reach a secret Rebel Alliance sanctuary offworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Leviathan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Tentacles
- **Lifespan:** Up to one thousand years
- **Origin:** Arrakan
- **Habitat:** Ocean
