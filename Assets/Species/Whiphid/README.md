# Whiphid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Whiphid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whiphid)

Whiphids, or simply a Whiph, were a sentient species of large, furry bipeds that had two large ivory tusks protruding from their lower jaws native to the frozen planet Toola. J'Quille and Valarian were both Whiphids.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Whiphid
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Pink
  - Gray
  - Green
- **Haircolor:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Black
  - Blue
- **Distinctions:** Two large ivory tusks
- **Origin:** Toola
- **Habitat:** Ice and snow
