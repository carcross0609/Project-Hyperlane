# Hargeevan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hargeevan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hargeevan)

The Hargeevans were the native Human population of the planet Hargeeva. The Hargeevans settled Hargeeva 2,000 years before the Galactic Civil War but when support was cut off they reverted to a feudal state. The planet was incorporated into the Empire around 13 BBY and the local ruler, Makintay, was raised to the post of Governor. Makintay's son, Stevan Makintay, eventually joined the Alliance to Restore the Republic, followed several years later by the metallurgist Ketrian Altronel.
