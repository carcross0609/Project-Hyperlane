# Zhell

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zhell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zhell)

The Zhell were a historical sentient species of the Core Worlds planet Coruscant. The species was believed to be the origin for the name of Zhellday, a day in the standard week. The species shared its name with the Taung & Zhell Society, a student group at the University of Coruscant, and the Zhell Reversal card game.

- **Designation:** Sentient
