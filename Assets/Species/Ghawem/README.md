# Ghawem

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ghawem](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ghawem)

The Ghawems were a sentient species of large, scaly beings who breathed methane. When in a methane-poor atmosphere, members of the species employed methane-spouting backpacks to make the air breathable for themselves. The Ghawems had enslaved another species, known as the Myills who they used for labor.
