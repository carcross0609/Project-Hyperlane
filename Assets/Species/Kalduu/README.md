# Kalduu

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kalduu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalduu)

The Kalduu were sapients indigenous to Ropagi II.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kalduu
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Length:** .5 meters
- **Distinctions:**
  - Transparent hemispherical bodies
  - Four tentacles
- **Lifespan:** 500 years
- **Origin:** Ropagi II
