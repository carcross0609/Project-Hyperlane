# Skembo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Skembo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skembo)

The Skembo were a sentient species in the galaxy. They had short legs and long, prehensile tongues with which they could seize food. The Skembo had a rivalry with the Hassarians. Ceeril, one member of the species, served as the Ambassador of Rion during the High Republic Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Skembo
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Short legs
  - Long, prehensile tongue
