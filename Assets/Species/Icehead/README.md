# Icehead

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Icehead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Icehead)

Iceheads were a species of sentient humanoids with lean, black bodies that held spiked, rectangular heads that resembled blocks of ice. Their large mouths brimmed with jagged teeth, and their three-fingered hands sported sharp claws. While they could walk like other humanoid species, members of the species could travel faster by flipping onto their heads and sliding on icy surfaces.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Icehead
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** White, black
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** Can move on snow at full speed, can skate about on their heads
