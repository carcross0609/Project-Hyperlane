# Holessian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Holessian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holessian)

The Holessians were portly humanoids native to Holess, believed to be related to the Lannik. They were obsessed with law and regarded legal cases like a sport, although betting on outcomes was considered sacrilege. They revered judges like gods and regarded lawyers as celebrities.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Holessian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Holess
