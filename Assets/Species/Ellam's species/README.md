# Ellam's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ellam's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ellam%27s_species)

This four-armed alien race were blue-skinned humanoids with large heads and their V-shaped mouths gave them the appearance of a perpetual smile. Their homeworld's gravity was much less than standard, so they were confined to anti-grav chairs. Ellam was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ellam's species
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four arms
  - Large heads
  - V-shaped mouths
- **Origin:** Ellam's species homeworld
