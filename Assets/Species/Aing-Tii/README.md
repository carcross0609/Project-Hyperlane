# Aing-Tii

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aing-Tii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aing-Tii)

The Aing-Tii were a sentient species. Their neural activity resembled the motion and reactions of the plasma within wayfinders, navigational devices used by both ancient Jedi and Sith.
