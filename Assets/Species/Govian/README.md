# Govian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Govian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Govian)

Govians were a sentient species from the Govia system. A female member of this species was a receptionist at one of Dr. Cornelius Evazan's "clinics" who was stunned unconscious by Jodo Kast as he pursued the bounty on Evazan's head.
