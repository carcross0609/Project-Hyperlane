# Nu-Cosian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nu-Cosian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nu-Cosian)

Nu-Cosian were a genetically-engineered offshoot of the Cosian species. They were created to colonize the planet Cos Secundu. They were long-necked bipeds with muscular tails they used for balance and self-defense. Bobbajo belonged to the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nu-Cosian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow and purple
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Distinctions:** Long necks, muscular tails
- **Origin:** Cos Secundu
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
