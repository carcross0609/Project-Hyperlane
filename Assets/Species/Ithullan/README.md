# Ithullan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ithullan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ithullan)

The Ithullans were a humanoid species that hailed from the planet Ithull. As natives of the Stenness Node, they were one of the 25 species collectively called "Nessies".

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ithullan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Origin:** Ithull
