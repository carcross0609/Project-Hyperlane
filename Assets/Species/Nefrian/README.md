# Nefrian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nefrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nefrian)

Nefrians were a sentient humanoid species that existed in the galaxy. Most Nefrians lived on the planet Refnu. They were gigantic in stature, with big bones to hold their weight, and an extra skin layer to keep them warm in cold climates. Dario and Cat were members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nefrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Nefri system
