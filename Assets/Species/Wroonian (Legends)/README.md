# Wroonian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wroonian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wroonian/Legends)

The Wroonians were a blue-skinned species of Near-Humans from the planet Wroona in the Inner Rim, a world in the same sector as the Bestine system.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Wroonian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.7–2.2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Haircolor:** Dark blue
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Origin:** Wroona
- **Language:** Wroonian
