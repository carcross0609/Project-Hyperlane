# Unidentified purple-faced species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified purple-faced species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_purple-faced_species)

This species of bipedal sentients lived on a planet in the Outer Rim, near the border of Galactic Republic space. Their face was split down the middle, flaring out in two flaps over a mouth full of sharp white teeth. They wore goggles over their two eyes.
