# Caspus Pillar's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Caspus Pillar's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caspus_Pillar%27s_species)

A yellow haired species with blue eyes existed in the galaxy. One member was Caspus Pillar, who lived during the war between the First Order and the Resistance. Other individuals could be seen on Ferrix in the years of the Galactic Empire.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Caspus Pillar's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
