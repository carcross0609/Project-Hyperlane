# Unidentified yellow snouted species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified yellow snouted species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_yellow_snouted_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. They were distinguished by their yellow skin and slightly elongated snouts. One member of this species was present at Grappa the Hutt's palace in 11 ABY.
