# Aruzan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aruzan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aruzan)

The Aruzans were a blue-skinned near-human sentient species. During the Imperial Era, the Aruzan Manaroo, who hailed from Aruza, dated the human bounty hunter Dengar.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Aruzan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Origin:** Aruza
