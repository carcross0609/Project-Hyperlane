# Symeong

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Symeong](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Symeong)

The Symeong were a sentient ape-like species who had large, sensitive ears. The Symeong metalsmith Albrekh, who worked aboard the Knights of Ren's starship Night Buzzard, used his ears to determine the heat of metal from the tone it produced when struck. Another Symeong, Dathan, was a slave of the Sith Eternal who helped a young Strand-Cast of Darth Sidious escape the planet Exegol.
