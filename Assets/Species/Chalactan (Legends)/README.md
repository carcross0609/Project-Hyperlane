# Chalactan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chalactan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chalactan/Legends)

The Chalactans were a very peaceful and spiritual Near-Human species native to the planet Chalacta. They were universally respected, and famous for their spiritual order, the Chalactan Adepts. On Chalacta, Chalactans lived in small farms and cities.

## Biology & Culture

- **Origin:** Chalacta
