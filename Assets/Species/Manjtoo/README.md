# Manjtoo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Manjtoo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Manjtoo)

Manjtoo were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. Members included Scrapjaw Motito, a scavenger who worked on the planet Jakku, and Gapgrin Dozito, a prisoner on Selnesh.
