# Dizonite

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dizonite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dizonite)

The Dizonites were a sentient species native to the Outer Rim Territories moon Dizon Fray. At some point during the Imperial Era prior to 5 BBY, all of the Dizonites present on their homeworld were massacred by Imperial forces, and the genocide was broadcast and recorded as proof of mission.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dizonite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Dizon Fray
