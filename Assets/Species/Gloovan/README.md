# Gloovan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gloovan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gloovan)

Gloovans were a sentient species that possessed a bulky figure and lumpy purple skin. Scram was a Gloovan gangster who led the Scram Gang during the time of the Galactic Empire. The Gloovan sniper Glunge worked alongside the swoop racer Kuru in an attempt to capture the bounty on the smuggler Han Solo during the Rion Swoop, but was disarmed by the Jedi Luke Skywalker.
