# Ashaftan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ashaftan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ashaftan)

Ashaftans were a diminutive sentient species that had wooly fur. In 28 ABY, the Ashaftans were represented in the New Republic Galactic Senate.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ashaftan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** Small
- **Distinctions:** Wooly fur
