# Iphigini

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iphigini](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iphigini)

The Iphigini species came from the planet Iphigin, one of the main cargo transfer points for three sectors in the Core Worlds. In 19 ABY, they offered to help mediate a dispute between their neighbors, the Ishori and the Diamala. They were unable to do so, even with the help of Han Solo, Chewbacca, and Luke Skywalker, and the Ishori/Diamala dispute soon became part of the greater Caamas Document crisis.
