# Kerch Kushi's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kerch Kushi's species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kerch_Kushi%27s_species/Legends)

Senator Kerch Kushi was a member of a Near-Human species with blue skin, red eyes, and teal hair. They inhabited Rattatak, Raxus Secundus, and Zardossa Stix, and many, including Kushi, were members of the Separatist Parliament.
