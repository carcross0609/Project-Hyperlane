# Frid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Frid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frid)

The Frid were an aquatic sentient species native to the planet Mackar. The Frid relocated to the water-covered planet Iskalon to escape a cataclysm that struck Mackar around five millennia before the Galactic Civil War, and became a part of the Iskalonian School, a cultural group of several aquatic species that had traveled to Iskalon with the aid of spacefaring benefactors. A fishlike species, Frid were characterized by their bulbous violet eyes, long mouth-flaps and green, scaly skin. Frid were sexually dimorphic, as males had fins on their heads which females lacked; they were also incapable of physical speech, instead communicating solely through telepathy. The Frid were noted for their extreme pacifism, refusing to fight back even when attacked.
