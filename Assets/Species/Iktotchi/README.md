# Iktotchi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Iktotchi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iktotchi)

Iktotchi were a horned sentient species from the moon Iktotch. Jedi Master Saesee Tiin, a member of the Jedi High Council prior to and during the Clone Wars, was an Iktotchi.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Iktotchi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Distinctions:** Large cranial horns
- **Origin:** Iktotch
- **Habitat:** Rocky
- **Language:** Iktotchese
