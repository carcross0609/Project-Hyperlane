# Czerialan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Czerialan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Czerialan)

Czerialans were a sentient species native to the planet Czerialus. They resembled humans with high cranial domes with tufts of white hair. Jannitha Xoczuko was a member of this species. During the Imperial Era, their homeworld was under Imperial control and the Xoczuko family sought to return after being disheartened on Corellia, unaware of the Empire's control on the planet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Czerialan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Distinctions:** High cranial domes
- **Origin:** Czerialus
