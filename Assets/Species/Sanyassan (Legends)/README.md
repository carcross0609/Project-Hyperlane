# Sanyassan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sanyassan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sanyassan/Legends)

The Sanyassans were a reptilian, humanoid species from the stormy world of Sanyassa IV, located in the Inner Zuma Region. They had ape-like faces, scaly skin, and a tall stature that averaged two meters in height. They engaged in piracy from ancient times, becoming the scourge of the greater Moddell sector. In the wider galaxy, these sentients had the reputation of being fierce fighters, armed with short tempers and even shorter levels of intelligence. They were sometimes employed as bodyguards or mercenaries, and at least one of them, Tun-Badon, pledged allegiance to a Sith Lord.
