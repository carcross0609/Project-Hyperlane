# Syblin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Syblin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syblin)

The Syblins was the name given to individuals of the species of Queen Sybil, an insectoid being who resided on the planet Metagos and whose species' name was unknown. The hatchlings of Sybil's thousands of eggs would be left to survive on their own and exterminate each other until they were ten years old, after which they would be given numbers.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Syblin
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Distinctions:** Six legs
- **Origin:** Metagos
