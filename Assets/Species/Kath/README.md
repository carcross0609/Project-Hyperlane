# Kath

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kath)

The Kaths were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. A shopkeeper was a member of this species and had scales and at least one scaly hand. To refer to someone as a member of this species could be an insult to some, as a man she spurned referred to Jyn Erso as a "coldhearted Kath."

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kath
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Kath system
