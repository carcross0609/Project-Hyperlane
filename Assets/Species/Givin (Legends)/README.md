# Givin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Givin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Givin/Legends)

The Givin were the indigenous sentient species of the planet Yag'Dhul. They were best known for their natural mathematical ability which made them expert starship builders and astrogators. Other notable features of the Givin were their skeletal appearance, and their ability to survive in a vacuum.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Givin
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Height:** 1.7 - 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Pale white
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Skeletal appearance, able to survive in a vacuum for short periods
- **Lifespan:** 80 years
- **Origin:** Yag'Dhul
- **Language:** Givin
