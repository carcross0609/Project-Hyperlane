# Unidentified species (Nentan)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Nentan)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Nentan%29)

An alien species constructed a city on the planet Nentan. By the time of the Galactic Civil War, the city was a ruin in the Nentan wastes and no one knew anything about the original builders.
