# Wirutid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wirutid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wirutid/Legends)

The Wirutid were a sentient, ambulatory species of fungus. They were hyper-intelligent and evolved into a form that, outwardly, was virtually indistinguishable from near-Human species. Through impersonating Humans, they could secure shelter and trap their prey. Wirutids had no known homeworld and were encountered in disreputable districts of the planet Coruscant.
