# Genian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Genian (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Genian_%28species%29)

Genians were a species native to the planet also known as Genian. They had violet or lavender skin and were known as skilled diplomats.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Genian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Violet
  - Lavender
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Origin:** Genian
