# Mawan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mawan (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mawan_%28species%29)

The Mawan were a species of humanoids from the planet Mawan. They had pale skin, two hearts, and blue blood vessels close to the surface of the skin. It was considered a mark of refinement for a Mawan's veins to show through the skin.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mawan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Origin:** Mawan
