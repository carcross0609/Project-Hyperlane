# Blubreen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blubreen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blubreen)

Blubreen were amphibious near-Humans from a planet in the Ploo sector.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Blubreen
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibious humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Ranges from purple to red, multicolored mixed
- **Distinctions:** Amphibious, aquiline features, webbed fingers and toes, gills, smooth-skinned skin
