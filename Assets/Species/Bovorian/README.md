# Bovorian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bovorian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bovorian)

Bovorians were a species of sapient mammals indigenous to Bovo Yagen.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bovorian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammals
- **Height:** 1.8–2.3 meters
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Distinctions:**
  - Able to see into the infrared spectrum
  - Increased sensitivity to respiratory ailments
  - Vestigial wings
- **Origin:** Bovo Yagen
- **Language:** Bovorian language
