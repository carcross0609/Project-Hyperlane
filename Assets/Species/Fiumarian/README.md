# Fiumarian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fiumarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fiumarian)

Fiumarians were a sentient species from the Fiumar system in the Core Worlds with pale pink skin, pupilless eyes, a sloping forehead, and yellow blood. They were deceptively fast and could remain neutral in heated negotiations, making them adept diplomats or smugglers.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fiumarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale pink
- **Origin:** Fiumar system
