# Gueal

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Gueal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gueal)

The Gueal was a sentient species with red eyes, slit pupils, long snouts, and two pointy ears. Members of the species possessed four legs and beige skin. At some point, a Gueal wrote a lament about their journey with love.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gueal
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Beige
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long snout
  - Pointy ears
  - Slit pupils
