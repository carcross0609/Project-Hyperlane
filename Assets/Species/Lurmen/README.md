# Lurmen

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lurmen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lurmen)

Lurmen were a diminutive, pacifistic species from the Outer Rim planet Mygeeto. At least one settlement of Lurmen was present on the planet Maridun at the time of the Clone Wars.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lurmen
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Height:** About 1 meter
- **Haircolor:**
  - Brown
  - Gray
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Origin:** Mygeeto
- **Habitat:** Plains and grasslands
