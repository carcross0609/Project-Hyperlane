# T'zaki

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T'zaki](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T%27zaki)

The T'zaki were a species of sentient insectoids that shared a unified consciousness in the form of a hive mind. By the time of the Republic Era, T'zaki culture more readily accepted slavery compared to many human cultures. In 40 BBY, the Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn theorized that the species' acceptance of the practice could be because T'zaki language roughly translated the term for freedom to mean having no purpose.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** T'zaki
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Distinctions:** Hive mind
