# Jillsarian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jillsarian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jillsarian/Legends)

Jillsarians were a species of muscular humanoid sentients with four arms from the Jillsaria system. Their integument varied, with some individuals possessing orange skin while others were covered in brown fur.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jillsarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Height:** Over 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Orange
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:** Four arms
- **Origin:** Jillsaria system
