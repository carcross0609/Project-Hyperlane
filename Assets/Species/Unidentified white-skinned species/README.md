# Unidentified white-skinned species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified white-skinned species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_white-skinned_species)

This white-skinned species was a sentient species of bipedals that possessed three-fingered hands, webbed toes and glowing blue eyes.
