# Quaggian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quaggian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quaggian)

The Quaggians were a sentient species from the Quaggia system. They were known for having a hand gesture called the Quaggian hand turtle, which meant "It's great" or "Something's great."

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Quaggian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Quaggia system
