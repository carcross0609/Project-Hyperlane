# H'nemthe

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [H'nemthe (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H%27nemthe_%28species%29/Legends)

H'nemthe were bipedal reptilian humanoids with four horns and ridges of bone and skin on their faces. They had long, gently curving noses, three-fingered hands, and pink to blue-gray skin. Their homeworld and native language were also named H'nemthe. The H'nemthe loved the arts, particularly music. They were rarely found off H'nemthe.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** H'nemthe
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Skincolor:** Bluish-gray to pink
- **Eyecolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Long curved noses, four horns, deadly mating rituals
- **Origin:** H'nemthe
- **Language:** H'nemthe
