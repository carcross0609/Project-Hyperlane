# Unidentified tall-headed green-eyed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified tall-headed green-eyed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_tall-headed_green-eyed_species)

The species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their elongated heads covered in yellow hair, as well as solid green eyes, though they lacked any other visible facial features. At least one member of this species was present on the planet Coruscant in 22 BBY, while another visited Bezz Drexx's tower around the same time during the Clone Wars.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Green
