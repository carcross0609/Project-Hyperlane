# Irrukiine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Irrukiine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Irrukiine)

The Irrukiine were four-armed sentients indigenous to Malrev IV. They were sometimes referred to as Irks.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Irrukiine
- **Skincolor:** Light brown
- **Distinctions:** Four arms
- **Origin:** Malrev IV
