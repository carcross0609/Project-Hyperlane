# Brogune

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Brogune](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brogune)

The Brogune were a sentient species with four eyes. In 22 BBY, a Brogune served as a concubine for the Hutt Soergg Vosadii Bezhin, who was a Bossban—a criminal leader—based on the Mid Rim planet Ansion. The Brogune was one of his many concubines, which included Humans and members of other species. Brogune were among the species whose females the Hutt found attractive.
