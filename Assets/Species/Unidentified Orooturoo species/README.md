# Unidentified Orooturoo species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Orooturoo species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Orooturoo_species)

A species was indigenous to Orooturoo. With small tentacles, four of which ended in a two-prong feature, extended from a large, purple-colored slug-like body, the species had a mating ritual which involved the female devouring the male, similar to some insects. Jabba the Hutt learned of the ritual when Princess Nampi captured him and ate his traitorous aide Scuppa.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified Orooturoo species
- **Skincolor:** Purple
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Slug-like, with four arms
- **Origin:** Orooturoo
