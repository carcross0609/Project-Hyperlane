# Cacop

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cacop](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cacop)

The Cacops were a sentient species native to the celestial body Veccacopia in the Outer Rim Territories' Tunka sector. They had eyes with retinae, and the visual appearance of individual Cacops tended to be difficult to distinguish for members of other species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cacop
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Veccacopia
