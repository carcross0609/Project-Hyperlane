# Yokaa

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yokaa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yokaa)

The Yokaa were a sentient serpentlike species. They possessed tri-lensed eyes with multi-spectral vision. Kalo'ne was a Yokaa who drove a DN-25 treadable on the planet Pasaana, her species' vision allowing her skills to remain sharp.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yokaa
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Haircolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** Tri-lensed multi-spectral vision
