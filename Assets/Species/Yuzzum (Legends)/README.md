# Yuzzum

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yuzzum/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuzzum/Legends)

Yuzzums, or Yuzzumi, were sentient, neckless, furred and round-bodied humanoids from the forest moon of Endor.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yuzzum
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Height:** 2.0 - 2.5 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown to red
- **Haircolor:**
  - Brown
  - Blue
  - Black
- **Distinctions:** Long skinny limbs, neckless
- **Origin:** Endor
- **Language:** Yuzzum
