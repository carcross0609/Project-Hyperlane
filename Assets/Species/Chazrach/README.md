# Chazrach

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chazrach](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chazrach)

The Chazrach (also known as "reptoids") were a short, stocky, reptilian extra-galactic sentient species from a distant galaxy with snubbed snouts who served the Yuuzhan Vong as slaves and soldiers.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Chazrach
- **Distinctions:** A presence in the Force
- **Language:** Yuuzhan Vong
