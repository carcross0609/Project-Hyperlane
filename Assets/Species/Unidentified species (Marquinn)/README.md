# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Marquinn)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Marquinn%29)

A near-human species lived in the galaxy. The species greatly resembled humans, but they had blue skin and dark blue hair. One member of this species was Zefin Tuzo, the owner of a resort casino on Marquinn. Her security forces were of her own species and were all female.

## Biology & Culture
