# Karduran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Karduran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karduran)

The Kardurans were a sentient species native to Kardura system. They were slim humanoids known for their dancing troupes. They were a client species of House Kassido.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Karduran
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Origin:** Kardura system
