# Truishii

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Truishii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Truishii)

The Truishii were a species of space-traveling people that were known for their large caravans. A group of Truishii traders whose caravan was shipwrecked following a Hyperspace accident became the original colonists of Trevi IV.
