# Voe Atell's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Voe Atell's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voe_Atell%27s_species)

Voe Atell was a member of a near-human species distinguished by the double rows of four horns that ran along the sides of their heads, as well as their blue or green skin and purple or yellow eyes. Members of this species inhabited the planets Rattatak and Raxus Secundus and the moon Zardossa Stix.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Voe Atell's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:** Blue, green
- **Eyecolor:** Purple, yellow
- **Distinctions:** Double rows of horns
