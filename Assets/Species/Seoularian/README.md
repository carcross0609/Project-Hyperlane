# Seoularian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seoularian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seoularian)

The Seoularians were a race of Humans that were resistant to Force powers. They used an eponymous language, and once ruled an area of space from their homeworld Seoul 5. They were extinct by the time of the Galactic Civil War.
