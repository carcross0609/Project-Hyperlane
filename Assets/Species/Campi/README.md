# Campi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Campi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Campi)

The Campi were a sentient three-armed humanoid species that existed in the galaxy. Two members of the species were the emissaries Xiering and Stingxi.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Campi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Purple
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Three arms
