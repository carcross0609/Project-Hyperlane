# Unidentified Eyo-Dajuritz species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Eyo-Dajuritz species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Eyo-Dajuritz_species)

The planet Eyo-Dajuritz was once inhabited by a sentient species. By 19 BBY, Eyo-Dajuritz had become a fossilized ecumenopolis, and the species had vanished.
