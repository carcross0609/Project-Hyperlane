# Assimilator

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Assimilator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assimilator)

The Assimilators were powerful cyborg warriors created by the Arkanians.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Assimilator
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Ability to assimilate the body parts of organics and droids, self-repair, and upgrade themselves.
- **Origin:** Arkania
