# Sipsk'ud

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sipsk'ud](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sipsk%27ud)

The Sipsk'ud were a sentient species native to the Sipsk system in the Kanz sector. They were broadly avian in appearance, with a yellow duck-like beak and white feathers. They had a large cluster of seven eyes on their heads, and at least one Sipsk'ud wore a sealed spacesuit while in an atmosphere suitable for species such as Humans.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sipsk'ud
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian
- **Feathers:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** 7-eye cluster
- **Origin:** Sipsk system
