# Anora's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anora's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anora%27s_species)

This species was a sentient species that was found within the galaxy. These bipedal humanoid sentients were distinguished by their cream-colored skin, quadruple arms, yellow hair, and large, pointed ears with pink interiors. By sometime between 232 BBY and 230 BBY, a member of this species named Anora was a drummer who played for the band the Star Strummers, performing alongside fellow band the Ku-Bops on the planet Andraven.
