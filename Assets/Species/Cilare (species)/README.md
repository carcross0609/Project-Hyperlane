# Cilare

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cilare (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cilare_%28species%29)

The Cilare were a species of humanoids that subscribed to martial values. Around 25 ABY during the Yuuzhan Vong War, their homeworld was invaded by the Yuuzhan Vong who exterminated the entire species except for Arbeloa during a one-sided engagement.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cilare
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Origin:** Vonak
