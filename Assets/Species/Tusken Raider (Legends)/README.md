# Tusken Raider

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tusken Raider/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tusken_Raider/Legends)

Tusken Raiders, less formally referred to as Sand People or simply as Tuskens, and by the Jawas as Giants or People of the Sand, were a culture of nomadic, primitive sentients indigenous to Tatooine, where they were often hostile to local settlers. The term Sand People was given to them due to their existence in the desert, and was in use from at least around 4000 BBY; but the more formal name of Tusken Raiders was acquired much later, due to a period of concerted attacks on the settlement at Fort Tusken in 98-95 BBY. Although this name was often used as a common term for the race, it actually properly referred only to the participants in the attack on the settlement.
