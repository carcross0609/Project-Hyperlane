# Polydroxol

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Polydroxol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Polydroxol)

The Polydroxol were a species of sentient, denantium-based shapeshifters that were native to the world of Sevetta. They resembled two hundred kilogram masses of mercury while in their liquid state. Their native environment was the toxic volcanic pools of their homeworld.
