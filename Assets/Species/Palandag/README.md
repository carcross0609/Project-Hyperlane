# Palandag

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Palandag](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palandag)

Palandags were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. They were notable for having an exolung. Dhuz, Hhex, and Jhat were Palandags who worked as musicians. Palandags' language was comprised of sounds that some considered to be naturally musical.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Palandag
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Exolung
  - Tentacles
