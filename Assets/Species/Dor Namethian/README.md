# Dor Namethian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dor Namethian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dor_Namethian)

Dor Namethians were a sentient species from the Dor Nameth system. Lexo Sooger, who worked as a masseur was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dor Namethian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Blue
  - Gray
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Small heads
  - Long firm necks
  - High shoulders with long arms
- **Origin:** Dor Nameth system
