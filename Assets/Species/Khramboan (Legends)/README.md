# Khramboan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Khramboan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khramboan/Legends)

The Khramboans were a sentient species from the Khramboa system. Droogan and his grandfather were both members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Khramboan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Two eyes on horizontal eyestalks
  - Mouth hidden behind two thick face-tentacles
- **Origin:** Khramboa system
