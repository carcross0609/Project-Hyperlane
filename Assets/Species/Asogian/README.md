# Asogian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Asogian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asogian)

Asogians were a sentient species that partly shared their name with both the Asog system and the Brodo Asogi system. At least three Asogians were affiliated with the Galactic Senate of the Republic at the time of Naboo's invasion.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Asogian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:** Large eyes, extending necks
