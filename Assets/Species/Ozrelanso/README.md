# Ozrelanso

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ozrelanso](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ozrelanso)

The Ozrelanso were a sentient species found in the galaxy. Male members of the species had lekku that grew on the sides of their skulls.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ozrelanso
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown-gray
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Lekku
  - Aural cavity
