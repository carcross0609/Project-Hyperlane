# Vrakolian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vrakolian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vrakolian)

The Vrakolian were a species with thick skin from the planet Vrakolia. They used the spinblade for harmless games of skill. Other species adopted them as weapons.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vrakolian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Thick skin
- **Origin:** Vrakolia
