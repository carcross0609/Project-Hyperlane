# Arhan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arhan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arhan)

The Arhan were a sentient near-Human species most of which had thin, frail bodies with long features. One Arhan had light-colored skin, hair, and eyes. Arhans identified as belonging to at least one—female—gender.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Arhan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Haircolor:** Light
- **Eyecolor:** Light
- **Distinctions:** Thin, frail
