# Adnerem precursor species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adnerem precursor species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adnerem_precursor_species)

A species of primitive mammalian scavenger/hunters evolved on the Expansion Region planet of Adner. Members of the species were semi-social and lived in tribes ranging in size from five to twenty individuals. The species eventually evolved into the sentient Adnerem species, who maintained their precursors species' social units, which became known as steri.

- **Designation:** Sentient
