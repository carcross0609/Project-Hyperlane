# Tsyklen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tsyklen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tsyklen)

Tsyklen were a sentient species native to the planet Tsyk in Hutt Space. The species had exceptional vision and nimble hands of nine digits each. The Tsyklen were considered wily, and the Hutt species saw them as a prized client species. During the Yuuzhan Vong War, the Yuuzhan Vong invaders attacked the Tsyklen homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tsyklen
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Taut skin, round shape, large eyes, nimble hands
- **Origin:** Tsyk
