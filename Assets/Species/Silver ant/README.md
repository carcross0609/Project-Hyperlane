# Silver ant

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Silver ant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silver_ant)

The silver antz (sing. silver ant) were a semi-sentient variety of the antz native to Kubindi. They were bipedal, warlike insectoids who traveled through forests to gather food in armies.
