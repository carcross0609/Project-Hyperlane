# Unidentified four-eyed Jawa-like species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified four-eyed Jawa-like species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_four-eyed_Jawa-like_species)

The species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their short stature and four glowing eyes, commonly wrapping their bodies in cloth and robes. At least four members of this species were present on the planet Coruscant in 22 BBY.
