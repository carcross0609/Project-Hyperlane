# Conjeni

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Conjeni/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Conjeni/Legends)

Conjeni were sapients who resembled red-furred starfish from the Conjenus system, located in the Mid Rim's Vish sector.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Conjeni
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Red
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Star-shaped
- **Origin:** Conjenus system
