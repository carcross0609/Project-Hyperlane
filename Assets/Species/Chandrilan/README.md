# Chandrilan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chandrilan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chandrilan)

The Chandrilans were a cultural group of humans who called the planet of Chandrila home. They were regarded as peaceful but slightly haughty, pedantic, and interested in politics.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Chandrilan
- **Class:** Human
- **Origin:** Chandrila
- **Language:** Chandrilan
