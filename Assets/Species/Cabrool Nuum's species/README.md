# Cabrool Nuum's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cabrool Nuum's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cabrool_Nuum%27s_species)

This species were a species of near-Human humanoid sentients possessing gray skin and ridged craniums. Male members of this species were hairless, with wrinkled skin, while females possessed light blue hair and much smoother features. Cabrool, Norba, and Rusk Nuum were all members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cabrool Nuum's species
- **Designation:** Near-Human
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Haircolor:** Light blue
- **Eyecolor:** Pink
- **Distinctions:** Ridged craniums
