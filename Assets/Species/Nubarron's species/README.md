# Nubarron's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nubarron's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nubarron%27s_species)

The non-binary Jedi Master Nubarron was a member of a sentient species that presented as a purple gaseous cloud. It was speculated that the species was a race of Filar-Nitzan, though Nubarron kept it a secret and Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh recalled the speculation that the species also came from a planet in Wild Space.
