# Skilla

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Skilla](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skilla)

The Skilla were a sentient species. Their vital organs, including photosensitive orbs for sight, vibrating cilia for hearing, and smell receptors, lined an uvula-like bulb suspended from the roof of their mouth, behind rows of sharp teeth. The bounty hunter Carib Diss was a Skilla who had an unsettling appearance as a result of his species' biology and wore a sensor dome cap to gather data about his surroundings without needing to open his mouth.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Skilla
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Vital organs located within mouth
