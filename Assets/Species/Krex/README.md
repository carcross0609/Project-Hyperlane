# Krex

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krex](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krex)

The Krex were a sentient species native to the planet Rokirex. Humanoid in shape, the species possessed a large build, five-fingered hands, big jaws and was taller than many other Humanoid species due to the large horn on top of their heads. A Krex named Grenkk worked at Club Vertica Casino as a bouncer during a Soovada event. In addition, General Ardinondu Dipolus Daeruun was a Krex.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Krex
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Height:** c. 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Orange
- **Distinctions:** Large horn on top of the head
- **Origin:** Rokirex
