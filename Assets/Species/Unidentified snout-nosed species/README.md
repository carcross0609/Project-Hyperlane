# Unidentified snout-nosed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified snout-nosed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_snout-nosed_species)

This sentient species had long snouts and tusks, similar to a Pacithhip, although the tusks were much shorter and thicker. Behind the trunk and between the tusks was a heavily lipped mouth containing small square, white teeth. The back of their heads were crowned with a row of four spikes with a single row of spikes going down the back of their necks.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified snout-nosed species
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Spikes on their necks
  - Snouts
  - Tusks
