# Ryn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ryn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ryn)

The Ryn were a humanoid sentient species with white hair and prehensile tails. They were known to be itinerant and were rarely seen in the Core Worlds. Shortly before the end of the Clone Wars, two Ryn followed Lyra Erso only to offer a scintillating pendant.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ryn
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Distinctions:** Prehensile tails
