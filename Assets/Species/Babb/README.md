# Babb

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Babb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Babb)

The Babbs were a sentient species that were small in stature. Consequently, they were occasionally thought of in contrast to Wookiees. In 27 BBY, a number of Babbs were present on the planet Euceron, which was hosting the Galactic Games, and they were seen by the Jedi Padawan Anakin Skywalker on a landing pad.

- **Designation:** Sentient
