# Kthoo-Ra's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kthoo-Ra's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kthoo-Ra%27s_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. These bipedal humanoid sentients were distinguished by their hulking frames, towering over most the average human, with green skin and solid white eyes with red patches underneath them. By around 0 ABY, one member of this species, Kthoo-Ra, was trained as a gladiator by the Xonti Brothers, though he was eventually killed by the Wookiee bounty hunter Krrsantan. Later, in 34 ABY, two members of this species served as body guards for the owner of the Coruscant Hotel and Casino, Jorka Edy, equipped with full suits of armor and potentially cybernetics.
