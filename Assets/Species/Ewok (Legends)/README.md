# Ewok

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ewok/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ewok/Legends)

Ewoks were sentient, diminutive, furry bipeds native to the forest moon of Endor. They were most notable for helping the Rebel Alliance defeat the forces of the Galactic Empire at the Battle of Endor, allowing the shield generator there to be destroyed, and in turn, the second Death Star.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ewok
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Mass:** 50 kilograms
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Haircolor:** Shades of white, brown, and black
- **Distinctions:** Furry, short stature, acute sense of smell
- **Origin:** Endor
- **Language:** Ewokese, Ewok-Basic pidgin
