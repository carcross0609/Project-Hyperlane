# Tallimarian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tallimarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tallimarian)

The Tallimarians were a sentient species. The aristocrat Centada Ressad from the Ressad Dynasty from Tallimar Prime and the scavenger Velorie Sarcon on the planet Jakku were members of the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tallimarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Distinctions:** Elongated cranium
