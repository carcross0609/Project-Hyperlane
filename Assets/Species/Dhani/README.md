# Dhani

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dhani](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dhani)

The Dhanis, also known as Aldhanis, were a cultural group of humans who called the planet of Aldhani home. Centuries prior to Imperial takeover, the hills of the planet were covered by hundreds of settlements that housed at least 40,000 Dhanis across the highlands. However, after about a decade of the Empire's presence, the Dhanis were cleared out and relocated to an Enterprise Zone in the Lowlands in the southern region of the planet. The Enterprise Zone was comprised of factories and new Imperial housing. By then, the only people who remained living in the hills were a few shepherds, nature lovers, mystics, and dead-enders. Once every three years, the Dhanis gathered for a celestial event called the Eye of Aldhani.
