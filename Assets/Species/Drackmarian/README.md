# Drackmarian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Drackmarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drackmarian)

Drackmarians were a methane-breathing reptilian species that hailed from Drackmar. They were recognized as ferocious warriors.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Drackmarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Distinctions:** Methane-breathing
- **Origin:** Drackmar
