# Quarf

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quarf](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quarf)

Quarfs were shapeshifting creatures that had the ability to fly and send signals through a horn on the middle of their foreheads. The only known quarf was seen on the forest moon of Endor, nicknamed "Silky" by young Ewoks from Bright Tree Village. In reality, "Silky" was the servant of the Dark Enchantress Jadru, sent to kidnap the Ewoks' Princess.

## Biology & Culture
