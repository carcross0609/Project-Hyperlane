# Khepi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Khepi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khepi)

The Khepi were a theocratic species on the planet of Khepi, a group which worshipped the Stars. The Khepi existed in the Khepi system, moving to the Sayma Nebula after their world was predicted to be destroyed. Within a tomb constructed to preserve the species, they remained concealed from the galaxy until their tomb was reached by the Rokana Raiders.
