# Frizznoth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Frizznoth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frizznoth)

The Frizznoth were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. They were short in stature, and at least some members utilized giant mechanized battle armor to compensate for their small size. Mozeen Parapa, head of the Parapa Cartel, was a male Frizznoth.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Frizznoth
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray-green
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Wide head
  - Four arms
- **Language:** Frizznothese
