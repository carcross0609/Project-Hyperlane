# Haserian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Haserian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Haserian)

The Haserians were a sentient species, native to the Haseria system in the Mid Rim. Around 18 BBY, a group of young Coruscanti Haserians played a game of shockball against a group of similar aged Rodians. The game attracted a large crowd, which included the spectators Jax Pavan, Dejah Duare and I-5YQ.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
