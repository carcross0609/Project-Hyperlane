# Kerch Kushi's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kerch Kushi's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kerch_Kushi%27s_species)

Senator Kerch Kushi was a member of a near-human species with blue skin, red eyes, and teal hair. Members of this species inhabited the planets Rattatak and Raxus Secundus and the moon Zardossa Stix. Many, including Kushi, were members of the Separatist Parliament.
