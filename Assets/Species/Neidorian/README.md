# Neidorian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neidorian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neidorian)

The Neidorians were a sentient species hailing from the world Neidoria. This humanoid species was distinguished by their short stature, with round, fleshy heads and tan with with mottled brown spots, as well as specialized masks covering their faces. By around 10 BBY, one Neidorian, the cyborg Paghee'Dhe Dahglyn, performed as a podracer.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Neidorian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Tan and brown
- **Origin:** Neidoria
