# Unidentified crested reptiloid species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified crested reptiloid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_crested_reptiloid_species)

A primitive unidentified crested reptiloid species, with crested craniums, three-fingered hands, and boney backs and shoulder, was encountered by the New Republic Bureau of Scouting and Exploration Services who attempted to initiate communication despite noting potential risks.
