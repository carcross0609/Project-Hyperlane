# Andulian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Andulian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andulian)

The Andulians were a sentient species native to the Andulia system. They had white hair and furry brows, gray skin, and atrophied gills in their cheeks. Sarit was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Andulian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Distinctions:**
  - Atrophied gills in cheeks
  - Furry brows
- **Origin:** Andulia system
