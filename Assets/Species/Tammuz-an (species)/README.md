# Tammuz-an

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tammuz-an (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tammuz-an_%28species%29)

The Tammuz-an were a species of humanoids from the planet of the same name that had taller frames and longer legs than average. They were ruled by a monarch. When the fledgling Galactic Empire refused to mass-produce Kuat Systems Engineering's R-22 A-wing, the Tammuz-an bought the existing prototypes to complement their planetary defense fleet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tammuz-an
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Distinctions:** Tall frame, long legs
- **Origin:** Tammuz-an
