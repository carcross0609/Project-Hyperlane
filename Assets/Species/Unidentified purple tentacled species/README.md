# Unidentified purple tentacled species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified purple tentacled species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_purple_tentacled_species)

An aquatic sentient species existed in the galaxy. They had purple or pink skin. Three notable members of this species were Moggee, Sylvestarr, and Ziggee.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified purple tentacled species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**

  - Purple
  - Yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Head tentacles
