# Trogodile

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trogodile/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trogodile/Legends)

The Trogodiles were sentient reptilian species. During the final years of the Old Republic, at least one member of the trogodile species competed in the Rattataki Arena, where it was killed by Asajj Ventress.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Trogodile
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Skincolor:** Green, yellowish brown, greige
- **Eyecolor:** Red,, yellow, black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Protruding, crocodilian jaw
  - Clawed fingers and toes
  - Ridge running down their back
- **Diet:** Carnivorous
