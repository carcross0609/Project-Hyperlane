# Martigrade

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Martigrade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Martigrade)

Martigrades were a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were very large beings with heads roughly the size of the average adult human's torso. According to Casca Panzoro, they were persecuted by the Galactic Empire.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Martigrade
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Silver
- **Haircolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Three-fingered hands
