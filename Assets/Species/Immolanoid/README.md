# Immolanoid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Immolanoid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Immolanoid)

The Immolanoids were a sentient species that were known to combust. By the year 18 BBY, some Immolanoids had lived and commuted on Coruscant, the capital planet of the Galactic Republic. The Besalisk chef Dexter Jettster mentioned the species in the ice cream section of the menu for his CoCo Town Coruscant restaurant, Dex's Diner, prior to its destruction at some point during the first two years of the Galactic Empire.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Immolanoid
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Known for combusting
