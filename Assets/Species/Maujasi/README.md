# Maujasi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Maujasi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maujasi)

Maujasi was a name given to the Human members of the Mauja clans of the planet of Haurgab. During the Clone Wars Maujasi allied themselves with the Confederacy of Independent Systems and fought against the Galactic Republic in the Battle of Haurgab.
