# Duros

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Duros/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duros/Legends)

The Duros (occasionally Durosians) were a humanoid reptilian species native to the planet Duro who were among the galaxy's first space faring civilizations.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Duros
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptile
- **Races:**
  - Neimoidian
  - Near-Duros
- **Height:** 1.7 – 2.2 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Blue
  - Green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Noseless
  - Large red eyes
  - Affinity for space travel
  - Green blood
- **Lifespan:**
  - Child: 1-9 years
  - Young adult: 10-14 years
  - Adult: 15-35 years
  - Middle age: 36-49 years
  - Old: 50-69 years
  - Venerable: 70+ years
- **Origin:** Duro
- **Language:** Durese
