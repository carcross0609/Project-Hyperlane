# Dornean

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dornean](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dornean)

The Dorneans were a species native to the planet Dornea. The Dorneans designed the Braha'tok-class gunship, a gunship class manufactured by the Dornean Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate to protect their homeworld from the Galactic Empire. The starship became the representative ship of the Dornean race. A number of these gunships came to aid the Rebel Alliance, notably after Dornea joined the faction following the Battle of Yavin.
