# Bah'r Kilido

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bah'r Kilido](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bah%27r_Kilido)

The Bah'r Kilido were a species pink-skinned humanoids with long faces and thin limbs. Their noses and chins were studded with a pair of short tusks. Suppoon was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bah'r Kilido
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Large horns on their chins and where their nostrils should be
