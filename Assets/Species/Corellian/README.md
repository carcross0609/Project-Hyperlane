# Corellian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Corellian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellian)

Corellians were a cultural group of humans native to the planet Corellia. Han Solo, captain of the Millennium Falcon, was a Corellian who fought for the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Corellian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Human
- **Origin:** Corellia
- **Language:**
  - Galactic Basic Standard
  - Old Corellian
