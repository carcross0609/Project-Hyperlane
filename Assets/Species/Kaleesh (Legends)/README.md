# Kaleesh

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kaleesh/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh/Legends)

Kaleesh were reptilian humanoids from the planet Kalee. They had scaly skin, tusks, and long nostrils, though few outsiders ever saw a Kaleesh face that was not obscured behind a traditional mask. Kaleesh culture was deeply spiritual and warlike. War was seen as an essential part of life, and wars between tribes were common.
