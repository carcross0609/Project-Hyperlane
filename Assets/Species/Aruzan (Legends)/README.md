# Aruzan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aruzan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aruzan/Legends)

Aruzans were the native people of Aruza. They were small humanoid or Near-Human beings with faintly blue skin and dark, almost black, hair.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Aruzan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Bluish
- **Haircolor:** Dark blue, near black
- **Distinctions:** Attanni cybernetic implants
- **Origin:** Aruza
