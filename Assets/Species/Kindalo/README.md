# Kindalo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kindalo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kindalo)

Kindalo were a species of tall, bioluminescent, tree-like sentients who could be found in the subterranean caverns of the planet Aleen. The air on the surface of Aleen was poisonous to those botanical beings, who guarded the passages between the two biotopes and were capable of provoking groundquakes to seal any breaches.
