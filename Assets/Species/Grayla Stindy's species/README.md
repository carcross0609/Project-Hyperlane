# Grayla Stindy's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grayla Stindy's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grayla_Stindy%27s_species)

A species of white-skinned beings counted among its members Grayla Stindy, an individual who once attended the Canto Casino and Racetrack on the planet Cantonica. Members of the species sported large skin flaps that arced down from their bulbous heads.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Grayla Stindy's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
