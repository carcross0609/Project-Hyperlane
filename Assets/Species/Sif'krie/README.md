# Sif'krie

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sif'krie](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sif%27krie)

The Sif'krie, from the planet Sif'kric in the Sif'kric system, had an economy based on the export of the highly perishable and valuable pommwomm plants. They shared their system with the Frezhlix, who began feuding with them as soon as the Galactic Empire pulled out. They had a government.
