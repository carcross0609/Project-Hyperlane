# Glarsaur

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Glarsaur](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glarsaur)

The Glarsaurs were reptilian sapients indigenous to Gelgelar. Brutes by nature, they had curled tails and sharp claws.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Glarsaur
- **Skincolor:** Green (scales)
- **Origin:** Gelgelar
