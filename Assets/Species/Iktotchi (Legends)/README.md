# Iktotchi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iktotchi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iktotchi/Legends)

Iktotchi were a species that originated on the harsh, windy moon of Iktotch, which orbited the planet Iktotchon in the Expansion Region.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Iktotchi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Mass:** 80 kilograms
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Distinctions:**
  - Horns
  - Precognition
  - Telepathy
  - Piloting
- **Lifespan:** 90 years
- **Origin:** Iktotch
- **Language:** Iktotchese
