# Soona Taj's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Soona Taj's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soona_Taj%27s_species)

A near-human species existed in the galaxy. Members of the species were almost identical to humans, but had two hearts and had pink to fair-colored skin, white hair, black eyes and narrow, almost non-existent, black eyebrows. Soona Taj and their father and other parent were members of the species and lived on Kiranno. Another member of this species was present on the planet Janix sometime during or after 19 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Soona Taj's species

- **Haircolor:** White

- **Distinctions:** Two hearts
