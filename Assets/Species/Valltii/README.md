# Valltii

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Valltii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valltii)

The Valltii were a sentient species native to the planet of Vallt. They were stout, with large round faces and mouths designed to chew meat, ice-blue skin, and lustrous growth of facial hair. According to Galen Erso, they were skilled stoneworkers. Some Valltii rode taqwa as Valltii riders in the planet's military.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Valltii
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** Large round faces and mouths
- **Origin:** Vallt
- **Language:** Valltii language
