# Tharnakan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tharnakan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tharnakan)

Tharnakans were sentient species from the planet Tharnaka. When Tharnaka was flooded due to storms, the Tharnakans left the planet, leaving behind the Tharnakan Story Stone. Once the floods receded, Ishbul Ekwesh was a Tharnakan who was sent to search for the Tharnakan Story Stone during the High Republic Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
