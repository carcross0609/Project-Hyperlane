# Zeltron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zeltron/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeltron/Legends)

Zeltrons were a near-Human species, made physically distinctive by their skin which came in different shades of red ranging from light pink to deep red, and hair which could be red or blue. Most of them were considered highly attractive by Human, near-Human, and even some non-humanoid standards.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Zeltron
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Light pink to deep crimson
- **Haircolor:**
  - Red
  - Blue
  - Brown
  - Black
  - White
  - Silver
  - Pink
- **Distinctions:**
  - Capable of producing powerful pheromones
  - Two Livers
- **Lifespan:** Up to 80 standard years
- **Origin:** Zeltros
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
