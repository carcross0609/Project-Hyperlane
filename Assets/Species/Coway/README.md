# Coway

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coway](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coway)

The Coway were a primitive race of humanoids that were native to the planet Mimban. Because of their intense dislike of outsiders, they spent most of their lives in the caves beneath the planet's fog-shrouded swamps.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Coway
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Height:** 1.6 meters
- **Origin:** Mimban
- **Language:** Coway
