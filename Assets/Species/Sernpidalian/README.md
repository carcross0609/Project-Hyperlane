# Sernpidalian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sernpidalian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sernpidalian)

Sernpidalians were a near-Human species native to the planet Hettitite. They had white hair, pale skin and red eyes, resembling Human albinos. The traditional Sernpidalian dress consisted of red-striped white robes with huge hoods. They were a deeply religious people, with one of their most revered deities being the goddess Tosi-karu. Most Sernpidalians were killed when the Praetorite Vong used the Yo'gand's Core tactic on Sernpidal, employing a large dovin basal to pull one of the planet's moons, Dobido, against the globe.
