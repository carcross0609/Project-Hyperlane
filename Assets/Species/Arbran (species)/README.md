# Arbran (species)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arbran (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arbran_%28species%29)

, a being created by the negative emotions of the Arbran people]] The Arbrans were a civilization which lived on Arbra circa 25,100 BBY, during the days of Xim the Despot. They may have been related to the Sith, as they had a similar language.
