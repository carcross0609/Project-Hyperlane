# Malagarrian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malagarrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malagarrian)

Malagarrians were sentients native to Malagarr. Their society was highly advanced, causing them to plumb the depths of their planet's natural resources. Eventually, they turned the entire planet into a giant reactor, which backfired and quickly eradicated their race.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Malagarrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Malagarr
