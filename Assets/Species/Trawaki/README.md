# Trawaki

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Trawaki](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trawaki)

The Trawaki were a small cave-dwelling sentient species native to the world Rushan IX that spoke the Trawak language. The species evolved on its world independently of all other life. The Trawaki eventually went extinct over a thousand years before the Imperial Era, and their language subsequently died.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Trawaki
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Rushan IX
- **Habitat:** Caves
- **Language:** Trawak
