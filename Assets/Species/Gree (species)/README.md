# Gree

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gree (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gree_%28species%29)

The Gree were a little-known alien species with tentacles who were native to the Gree system of the Gree Enclave in the Outer Rim Territories. They also shared their name with the Outer Rim astronomical object Gree. Like the Aing-Tii, the Gree exhibited neural activity that greatly resembled the motion and reactions of the plasma within ancient wayfinders.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gree
- **Distinctions:** Tentacled
- **Origin:** Gree system
