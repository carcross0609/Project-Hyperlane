# Forshul

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Forshul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forshul)

The Forshul were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy that possessed long gray hair. During the High Republic Era, at least one Forshul was present on Valo during the Nihil attack on Valo.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Forshul
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long gray hair
