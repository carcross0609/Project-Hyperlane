# Lunnix

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lunnix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lunnix)

The Lunnix were a sentient species with white fur and yellow eyes that lived in the galaxy. Zorahda was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lunnix
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Black teeth
