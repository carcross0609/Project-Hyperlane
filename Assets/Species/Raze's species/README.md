# Raze's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Raze's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raze%27s_species)

This alien race were immense and had pale skin. They were distinguished by their long limbs and tall, wattled necks. Their heads were dominated by a pair of heavy tusks that protected their mouths and necks, and their long arms gave them an incredible reach. Raze was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Raze's species
- **Height:** Immense
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:** Heavy tusks, long arms, tall wattled necks
