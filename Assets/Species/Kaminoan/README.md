# Kaminoan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaminoan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaminoan)

The Kaminoans were a tall, thin amphibian sentient species that hailed from the aquatic planet of Kamino in Wild Space. Living in large stilted domed cities that rose high above the ocean surface, Kaminoans were known to specialize in pioneering cloning and genetic engineering technology, as well as developing more conventional, non-biomedical, technologies for various clients, including the Galactic Republic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kaminoan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black sclerae, white pupils
- **Distinctions:** Ultraviolet vision
- **Origin:** Kamino
- **Habitat:** On water
- **Language:** Kaminoan
