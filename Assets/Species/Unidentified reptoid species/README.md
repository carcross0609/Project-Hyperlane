# Unidentified reptoid species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified reptoid species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_reptoid_species)

The species of sentient reptoids from the depths of the Unknown Regions had long-vanished before the formation of the Galactic Empire. Known members of the extinct reptilian race included the Kanzer Exiles, a fanatical flock led by the Lord Ravager Korman Lao. Lao’s group worshipped demons and claimed that they had been able to mentally enslave many minds at once.
