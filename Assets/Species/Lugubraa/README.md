# Lugubraa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lugubraa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lugubraa)

The Lugubraa were a sentient species native to the Stratos Distribution in the Unknown Regions.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lugubraa
- **Height:** 1.5 meters
- **Skincolor:** Pale white to dark gray
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Eyeless, gaping maw
- **Lifespan:** Over 150 years
- **Origin:** Stratos Distribution
- **Language:** Lugubraal
