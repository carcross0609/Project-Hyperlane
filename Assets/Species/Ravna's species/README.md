# Ravna's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ravna's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ravna%27s_species)

The species of Ravna was a sentient species of vampiric creatures that could infect others and transform them into similar creatures.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ravna's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Infected scratches to transform other into similar creatures
  - Energy absorption from blaster bolts and lightsabers
