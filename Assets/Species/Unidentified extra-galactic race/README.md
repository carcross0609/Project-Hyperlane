# Unidentified extra-galactic race

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified extra-galactic race](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_extra-galactic_race)

A sentient, ancient race existed in an unknown galaxy. Millions of years ago, the race designed a gigantic, planet-sized artificial probe with the mission to examine all evolved life forms in the universe and to suppress those which endangered the balance established by the Force. An old man named God was a member of this race.
