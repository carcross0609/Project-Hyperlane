# Bomodon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bomodon (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bomodon_%28species%29)

The Bomodons were a sentient species native to the planet Bomodon in the Churba sector of the galaxy's Mid Rim. In 0 BBY, a number of Bomodon bounty hunters were present on the Outer Rim planet Tatooine and were witnessed by the Rodian bounty hunter Greedo entering Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina in the spaceport city Mos Eisley.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Bomodon
