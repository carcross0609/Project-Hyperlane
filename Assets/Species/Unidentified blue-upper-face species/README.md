# Unidentified blue-upper-face species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified blue-upper-face species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_blue-upper-face_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The humanoid species was distinguished by their blue upper faces. One member of this species was a member of the Jedi Order, though they were killed by members of the 501st Legion during the attack on the Jedi Temple.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified blue-upper-face species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue and tan
