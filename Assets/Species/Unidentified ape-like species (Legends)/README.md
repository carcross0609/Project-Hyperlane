# Unidentified ape-like species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified ape-like species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_ape-like_species/Legends)

A humanoid sentient species was distinguished by their ape-like features, including sunken eyes and prognathous jaw. By 32 BBY, at least four members of this species were members of the Galactic Senate.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified ape-like species
- **Designation:** Sentient
