# Teltior

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Teltior](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teltior)

The Teltiors were a tall humanoid race. They were closely related to the Meris, who also hailed from the planet Merisee in the Meris system.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Teltior
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.5–2.2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Varies from blue to black
- **Origin:** Merisee
