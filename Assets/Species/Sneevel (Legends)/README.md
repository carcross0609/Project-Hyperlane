# Sneevel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sneevel/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sneevel/Legends)

Sneevels were a furry, humanoid species from the Mid Rim planet Sneeve. They were characterized by their oversized, portly heads, monochromatic eyes, and distinctive smell, which they used for communication. Most Sneevels were extraordinarily nimble.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sneevel
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.6–2 meters
- **Haircolor:** Blond, black, gray
- **Eyecolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Fur; snout; musk
- **Lifespan:** 76 standard years
- **Origin:** Sneeve
- **Diet:** Omnivorous
- **Language:** Snee
