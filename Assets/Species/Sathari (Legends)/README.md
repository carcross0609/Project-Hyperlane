# Sathari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sathari/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sathari/Legends)

The Sathari were a reclusive sentient avian species native to the planet Arbooine. The species possessed hand-tipped wings capable of manipulating tools. The avians lived mostly in small settlements in the forest canopy of their homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sathari
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian
- **Height:** 1.7 to 2 meters
- **Feathers:**
  - Blue
  - Purple
  - White
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Hand tipped wings
  - Four eyes
- **Origin:** Arbooine
- **Habitat:** Tree-cities
- **Language:** Arbori
