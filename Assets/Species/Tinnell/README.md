# Tinnell

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tinnell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tinnell)

The Tinnell were a felinoid species of sentients from the Mid Rim. While considered naïve, they were master mechanics with the skill to improvise repairs that made things work better than designed.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tinnell
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.2–1.8 meters
- **Distinctions:** Feline
- **Origin:** Tinnell homeworld
