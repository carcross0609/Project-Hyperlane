# Theelin-human hybrid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Theelin-human hybrid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Theelin-human_hybrid)

A Theelin-human hybrid was a sentient hybrid species that consisted of an individual that was both Theelin and human. The Max Rebo Band dancer Rystáll Sant was one such individual who was a Theelin-human hybrid, as was Jedi Padawan Jecki Lon during the High Republic Era.
