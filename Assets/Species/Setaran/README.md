# Setaran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Setaran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Setaran)

Setarans were a sentient species that had domed, ridged heads, pale skin, and gills running from the bottom of the neck to jutting cheekbones, framing two deep-set eyes. Setarans did not possess a mouth, and instead communicated with other sentients via telepathy. The species was known for their Force-related tracking abilities. Calar was a Setaran associated with the Path of the Open Hand.
