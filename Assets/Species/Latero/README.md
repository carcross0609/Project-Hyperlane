# Latero

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Latero](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Latero)

Latero, also known as Lateron, were a sentient species from the planet Lateron that possessed four arms. The species had a keen sense of smell. Marino Pomana was a member of the species who worked as a chef on the planet Begamor. Greez Dritus, the captain of the S-161 "Stinger" XL Stinger Mantis, was a Latero. The company Latero Spaceworks, creator of the S-161 "Stinger" XL, shared its name with the species. The Latero Goonral Monshi was a Jedi during the High Republic Era. The Latero Hap was a chef and owner of Hap's Sap Tap on the planet Tenoo during the High Republic Era.
