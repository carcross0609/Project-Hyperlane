# Esoomian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Esoomian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Esoomian/Legends)

Esoomians were hulking humanoids indigenous to Esooma who bore a strong resemblance to the Zexx. They reached 3 meters in height and were immensely strong, but tended to be simpleminded and to speak in a garbled manner. They were regarded as among the best hired muscle in the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Esoomian
- **Height:** 3 meters
- **Skincolor:** Blue-gray
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Origin:** Esooma
