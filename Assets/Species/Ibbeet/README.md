# Ibbeet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ibbeet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ibbeet)

Ibbeets, also known as Ibbe, were a serene sentient species from the Wild Space planet with the same name. They tried to live in harmony with the rest of their planet. Between 230 BBY and 229 BBY, the princess-pirate Tanabi Yar recommended the Jedi knight Vernestra Rwoh to visit the Ibbe's planet to heal and move on.
