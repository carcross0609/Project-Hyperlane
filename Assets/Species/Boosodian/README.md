# Boosodian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Boosodian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boosodian)

The Boosodians were a sentient species with gray skin and twenty eyes that lived in the galaxy. Amuncie Tidian was a Boosodian who worked as a forger. In 9 BBY, a Boosodian vendor on Daiyu sold clothes to Obi-Wan Kenobi and Leia Organa whilst they were escaping the planet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Boosodian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:** White
- **Distinctions:** Twenty eyes
- **Origin:** Boosodia
