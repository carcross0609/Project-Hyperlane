# Grizmallti

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grizmallti/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grizmallti/Legends)

The Grizmallti were the Human inhabitants of the planet Grizmallt. They worshipped a deity named Nabu and were engaged in a civil war around 4000 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Grizmallti
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Human
- **Origin:**
  - Grizmallt
  - Naboo
