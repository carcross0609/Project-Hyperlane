# Unidentified Uro arachnid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Uro arachnid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Uro_arachnid)

A species of sentient arachnids were native to the planet Uro. The species' arachnid instincts led them to eat their weakest children. In 40 BBY, the Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn questioned his colleague, the Grand Master Yoda, on why the Galactic Republic allowed corporations such as the Czerka Corporation to enslave others, a practice he felt was barbaric. In response, Yoda related that Uro's arachnid species consumed their weak offspring, offering it as an example of unethical actions the Republic did not prevent.
