# Darshi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darshi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darshi)

The Darshi, known as Thinfaces to residents of Batuu, were a wealthy sentient species that were employed by the warlike Grysk from the Unknown Regions. The Darshi themselves were believed by Thrawn to be a border society that traveled both Wild Space and the Unknown Regions. The culture of the Darshi were not deeply studied by the Chiss Ascendancy, and stories did not portray them as violent. As such, according to the stories told, the Darshi were not generally believed to show violent tendencies to outsiders. However, their bodies were evolutionarily built in such a way that suggested they were a violent people: Darshi had long claws that would do well in combat, while their thin bodies and heads would keep them well-defended.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Darshi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Narrow head and body
  - Long clawed limbs
