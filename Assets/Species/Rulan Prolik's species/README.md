# Rulan Prolik's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rulan Prolik's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rulan_Prolik%27s_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. They were distinguished by their polymorphic capabilitue. Upon death, members of this species will erupt into a bright ball of light before reverting to what may be their original form. One member of this species, Rulan Prolik, served as one of the four overseers of GenoHaradan by 3956 BBY, though they were eventually killed by Jedi Knights in the Shadowlands of the planet Kashyyyk after impersonating members of the native Wookiee population such as Grarwwaar.
