# Aggoron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aggoron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aggoron)

The Aggoron were a sentient species. Many Aggorons were traders, and—along with Devaronians, Humans, Nalroni, and Squibs—they formed part of the thousands of non-Gree who lived in Satikan, the largest city on Asation, which was the main trading post and access system to the Gree Enclave.
