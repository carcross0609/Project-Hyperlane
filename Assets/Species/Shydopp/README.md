# Shydopp

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shydopp](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shydopp)

Shydopps were a sentient species from the planet Morak. After the fall of the Empire, Shydopp pirates attacked shipments of rhydonium being delivered to an Imperial remnant, as well as Din Djarin and Migs Mayfeld, who were disguised as members of the remnant. The Shydopp Vorg Alsum was a prisoner of the New Republic. Another Shydopp served as a fighter pilot in Gorian Shard's crew.
