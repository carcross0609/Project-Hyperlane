# Mizi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mizi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mizi)

The Mizi were a sentient species with long limbs, a long nose, and a lower susceptibility to radiation than most other species. As a result, Mizi often transported cargo that other species were unable to. The Mizi also preferred to remain nameless to strangers and additionally had a rivalry with the Orincans, another species from the same area of the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mizi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long limbs
  - Long nose
  - Radiation immunity
