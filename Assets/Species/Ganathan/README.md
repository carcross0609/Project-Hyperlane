# Ganathan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ganathan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ganathan)

The Ganathans, or Ganathians, were a sentient Near-Human species indigenous to the planet Ganath, located within a largely uncharted cloud of gases within Hutt Space known as the Oktos Nebula. Cut off from the outside by the uncharted and unexplored expanses of highly irradiated interstellar gases that surrounded their pocket of space, Ganathan technology remained at an archaic level in comparison to much of the rest of the galaxy. They had limited space-travel capabilities, making use of steam-powered spacecraft capable of slow, inter-system journeys.
