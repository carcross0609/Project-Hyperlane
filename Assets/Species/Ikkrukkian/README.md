# Ikkrukkian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ikkrukkian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ikkrukkian)

Ikkrukkians, also known as Ikkrukki, were a sentient species native to the Mid Rim planet Ikkrukk. One member of the species, Porter Engle, served the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. Their physiology enabled them to be able to live for centuries.
