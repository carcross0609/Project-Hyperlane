# Ereesi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ereesi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ereesi)

The Ereesi were a species from the Ereesus system in the Outer Rim Territories. The bulky Ereesi Argaz, a member of Marchion Ro's Sh'ear, attended a gala in the Palace of Jadalla on Waskiro in 228 BBY.
