# Cemas

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cemas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cemas)

The Cemas were a species of sentient beings that had feet. At least one member of the species had entered the galactic community by 17 ABY. That year, the smuggler Han Solo helped a Cemas to its feet in the Crystal Jewel casino, on the planet Coruscant, after a bomb went off in the New Republic Senate Hall nearby.
