# Oni

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oni)

The Oni were a sentient species native to the planet Uru. They were a militant species who were often known to work off-planet as mercenaries. Female Oni were electrophoretic and could store static electricity in a unique capacitor gland within their bodies. This species' members included a female mercenary who was a patient of Lieutenant Kornell Divini's on Drongar.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Oni
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Capacitor gland (females)
  - Electrophoretic (females)
- **Origin:** Uru
