# Pinurquian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pinurquian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pinurquian)

Pinurquians were a species of horned humanoids active in the galaxy. One well known member was Tinketh Fo, the leader of the planet Keyorin.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pinurquian
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Horns, Fur on face
- **Origin:** Pinurquia system
