# Jiruusi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jiruusi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jiruusi)

Jiruusi were the sentient inhabitants of the planet Jiruus. Most of them barely spoke Galactic Basic Standard, and they strongly opposed the Galactic Empire.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jiruusi
- **Designation:** Sentient
