# Unidentified multi-chinned species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified multi-chinned species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_multi-chinned_species/Legends)

A sentient species was distinguished by their tall, fleshy necks, with multiple overlapping chins, downturned mouths, exposed nostrils, enormous ears, spines lining their brow, and long hair extending from the sides and back of their head. By 22 BBY, a member of this species had been a Jedi of the Jedi Order, well known enough to have multiple busts of them displayed around the Jedi Archives.
