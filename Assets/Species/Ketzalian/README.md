# Ketzalian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ketzalian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ketzalian)

Ketzalians were a small reptilian sentient species from the Ketzali system that possessed wings that allowed them to fly and hover. One Ketzalian, Lita, worked in the smuggler crew led by Ana Tolla.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ketzalian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Skincolor:** Purple wings
- **Distinctions:**
  - Wings
  - Humanoid head
  - Feathers in the head
  - Short arms
  - Tail
- **Origin:** Ketzali system
