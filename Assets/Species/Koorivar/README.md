# Koorivar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Koorivar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Koorivar)

The Koorivar were a sentient species of near-humans distinguished by their reptilian skin and large cranial horn that spiraled upwards from the center of their skull.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Koorivar
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:**
  - Green
  - Magenta
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Green
  - Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Cranial horns
  - Scaly, reptilian skin
- **Origin:**
  - Kooriva (Adopted)
  - Koorivar homeworld (Abandoned)
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
