# Unidentified Jandoon species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Jandoon species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Jandoon_species)

A species once inhabited the planet Jandoon. Several centuries before the Galactic Civil War, the species went extinct but none ever learned what exactly happened to them. Rumors sprang up, with tales about angry spirits that stalked the planet keeping most travelers away from Jandoon.
