# Terrelian Jango Jumper

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Terrelian Jango Jumper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terrelian_Jango_Jumper)

Terrelian Jango Jumpers were tall, slender humanoids who were known for their acrobatic abilities. The criminal Cassilyda "Cassie" Cryar was a Terrelian Jango Jumper, as were the Eighth Brother, a member of the Galactic Empire's Inquisitorius, and Lika, who was held captive by Trandoshan big game hunters.
