# Sorrusian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sorrusian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorrusian)

Sorrusians were a Near-Human species from the planet Sorrus. They differed from baseline Humans in that their skeletal system could compress, allowing them to squeeze through tight places.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sorrusian
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Skincolor:** Pale brown
- **Distinctions:** Elastic skeleton
- **Origin:** Sorrus
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
