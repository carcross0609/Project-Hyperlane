# Urodel

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Urodel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Urodel)

The Urodel were sentient amphibians. Bollie Prindel and Vetch were members of this species, as was an individual present in CoCo Town on the planet Coruscant. Nooa was an Urodel who trained as a Jedi Padawan during the High Republic Era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Urodel
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Skincolor:**

  - Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
