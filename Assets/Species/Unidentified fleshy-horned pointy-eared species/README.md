# Unidentified fleshy-horned pointy-eared species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified fleshy-horned pointy-eared species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_fleshy-horned_pointy-eared_species)

A humanoid sentient species existed in the galaxy. Members of the species had tan skin with a pair of short, flesh-covered horns protruding from the upper fronts of their heads. They also had two pointed ears and could grow gray mustaches and eyebrows. At one point no earlier than 1 BBY, at least two adult members of the species were present at Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina in the city Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine. One of the individuals had no facial hair and wore goggles while the other had facial hair and a scar over their right eye. Both individuals drank from cups at the bar.
