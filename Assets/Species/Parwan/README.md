# Parwan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Parwan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Parwan)

Parwans were a sentient species native to Parwa. During the High Republic Era, Parwan Jedi Master Obratuk Glii was a respected diplomat within the Jedi Order. During the Clone Wars, the Parwan Derrown was considered one of the best bounty hunters in the galaxy by Count Dooku.
