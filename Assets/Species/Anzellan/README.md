# Anzellan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anzellan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anzellan)

Anzellans were a diminutive sentient species. Their size was comparable to infant members of other species. The eyes of Anzellans had floating corneal micro-lenses that could see microscopic details and they also had purple blood.
