# Reigat

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reigat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reigat)

The Reigats were a species of large humanoids with grayish, mottled skin and dark blue hair, eyelids, and lips. They were generally a species of loners whose yearly "breeding drive" was probably all that kept them from becoming extinct.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Reigat
- **Height:** 1.9 – 2.3 meter
- **Skincolor:** Grayish
- **Haircolor:** Dark Blue
- **Origin:** Reigalius
