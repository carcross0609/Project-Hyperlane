# Mustafarian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mustafarian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mustafarian/Legends)

Mustafarians were an insectoid sentient species native to the volcanic planet Mustafar. They were divided into two subspecies: the taller, thinner northern Mustafarians, and a smaller, stockier, and hardier southern Mustafarians.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mustafarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Subspecies:**
  - Northern Mustafarian
  - Southern Mustafarian
- **Height:**
  - 2.29 meters (northern)
  - 1.5 meters (southern)
- **Mass:** 65 kilograms (northern)
- **Distinctions:** Extremophiles
- **Origin:** Mustafar
- **Language:** Mustafarian
