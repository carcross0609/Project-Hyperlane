# Hemes Arbora

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hemes Arbora](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hemes_Arbora)

The Hemes Arbora were a sentient species from the Unknown Regions. They hailed from the planet Carrivar, but their migrations brought them to the planets Umaren'k and, eventually, Osseriton. During their stay on Umaren'k, the sentient planet Zonama Sekot moved into the system, compelling the native Umaren'k'sa to adopt much of Hemes Arbora culture.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hemes Arbora
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Migratory
- **Origin:** Carrivar
