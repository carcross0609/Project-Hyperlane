# Rodisar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rodisar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rodisar)

Rodisar was a sapient reptilian species from the planet Rodis in the Outer Rim Territories. Rodisars were a primitive inhabitants and waged seasonal wars with one another. General Nilo was a member of this species that was kidnapped from his homeplanet by a Zygerrian slavers. He eventually ended up in the gladiatorial arena of Rattatak around 22 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Rodisar
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Eyecolor:** Black and blue
- **Distinctions:** Long snout, broad paws and dragging tail
- **Origin:** Rodis
