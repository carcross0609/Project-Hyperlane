# Quaraxian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quaraxian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quaraxian)

Quaraxian was a sentient species that aged very slowly and possessed significant intellectual prowess. By the time of the Republic Era, the species were virtually extinct, with the exception of individuals such as the Quaraxian female criminal Phaedra.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Quaraxian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Light blue
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow (with black sclera)
- **Distinctions:**
  - Slow aging
  - Enhanced intelligence
