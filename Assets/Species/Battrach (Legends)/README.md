# Battrach

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battrach/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battrach/Legends)

Battrachs were sentient, amphibious chelonoids indigenous to the planet of Arrakan. They were a bio-engineered race created by the Duors.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Battrach
- **Distinctions:** Bio-engineered race created by the Duors
- **Origin:** Arrakan
