# Ailon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ailon (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ailon_%28species%29/Legends)

The Ailon people were sapients indigenous to Ailon. They were famous for their military tradition, which dated back to at least 13,000 BBY. To them, military training was a form of religious devotion.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ailon
- **Distinctions:** Militaristic traditions
- **Origin:** Ailon
