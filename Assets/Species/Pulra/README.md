# Pulra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pulra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pulra)

The Pulras were sentients indigenous to Kuras III, which they called "Host". They shared their planet with two other species, the Aganof and the Hairy Savages.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pulra
- **Skincolor:** Brown, green, or black (changeable)
- **Distinctions:** Amorphous, able to combine to form larger creatures
- **Origin:** Kuras III
