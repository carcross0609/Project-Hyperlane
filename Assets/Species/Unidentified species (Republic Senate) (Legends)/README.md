# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Republic Senate)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Republic_Senate%29/Legends)

An humanoid sentient species was distinguished by their dark skin and light fingers. By 22 BBY, more than one member of this species was a member of the Galactic Senate.
