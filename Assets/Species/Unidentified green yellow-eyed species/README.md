# Unidentified green yellow-eyed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified green yellow-eyed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green_yellow-eyed_species)

The species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their green skin and solid yellow eyes, with flat lips, naturally downturned mouths, hairless heads, and a lack of visible nostrils. At least two members of this species, clad in identical yellow and purple robes and headgear, were present in Bezz Drexx's tower some time during the Clone Wars.
