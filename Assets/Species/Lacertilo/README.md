# Lacertilo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lacertilo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lacertilo)

The Lacertilo were an amphibious species with compound lungs. Resistance member Bo Rogiave was a Lacertilo, using his biology to his advantage when on missions where oxygen levels were thin. Two Lacertilo bailiffs served at an Imperial courthouse on Niamos and attended the sentencing of Cassian Andor in 5 BBY for a crime he did not commit. A handful were also present at Samaria's Imperial Court of Criminal Justice.

## Biology & Culture
