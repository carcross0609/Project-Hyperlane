# Woostoid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Woostoid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Woostoid)

The Woostoids were sapient humanoids indigenous to Woostri.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Woostoid
- **Height:** 1.6–1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Reddish-Orange
- **Haircolor:** Red
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Tall, wiry frames, long life spans, large eyes, intelligence
- **Origin:** Woostri
- **Language:** Woostese
