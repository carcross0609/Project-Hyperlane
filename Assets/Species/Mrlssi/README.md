# Mrlssi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mrlssi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mrlssi)

The Mrlssi or Mrlssti of Mrlsst in the Tapani sector were a species renowned throughout the sector as scholars and scientists.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mrlssi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian
- **Height:** 0.5–1.3 meters
- **Skincolor:** Light blue
- **Feathers:** Gray on body, brown on head (bright colors developing with age)
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Flightless avians
  - Blunt noses
- **Origin:** Mrlsst
- **Language:**
  - Mrlsstese
  - Galactic Basic Standard
