# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Phorliss)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Phorliss%29)

This species was a sentient species distinguished by their green skin, with enormous heads, with tall, smooth, bulbous craniums, large squinted eyes, and elongated, drooping probiscis-like snouts. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species were present on the planet Phorliss.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Probiscis-like snout
