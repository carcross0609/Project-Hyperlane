# Orincan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Orincan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orincan)

The Orincans were a porcine sentient species from the Orinca system in the Outer Rim Territories. They appeared similar to Gamorreans, though had paler skin. The Orincans had a dislike for the Mizi, another sentient species from the same region. An Orincan starship was one of several vessels that became stranded on the Amaxine space station when hyperspace travel was limited after the Great Hyperspace Disaster. The Orincans came into conflict with a group of Mizi who were among the others stranded on the space station, though the two groups later celebrated together when travel to Coruscant was permitted.
