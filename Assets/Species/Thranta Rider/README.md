# Thranta Rider

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thranta Rider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thranta_Rider)

Thranta Riders, also known as wing riders, were humanoid sapients who tamed flying thrantas and rode them, performing aerial circus-like shows around the world of Bespin. They liked to paint or tattoo themselves with complex patterns.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Thranta Rider
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Ochre yellow
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow, black
- **Distinctions:** Elongated bodies, two-toed feet, four-fingered hands, colorful tattoos
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
