# Agorffi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Agorffi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agorffi)

The Agorffi were a sentient species. They were known for their strength and brawn, and were often bracketed with other strong species such as Gamorreans and Wookiees; their resilience, however, made them a prime target for the Slaver Syndicate. The Slaver Syndicate was a large bounty hunter syndicate operating during the Galactic Civil War. They captured bounties of low value, then bought the ownership rights to their captives and sold them as enslaved individuals.
