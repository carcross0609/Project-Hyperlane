# Vollick

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vollick/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vollick/Legends)

The Vollick were a species that Osika Kirske belonged to. This species was one of many that inhabited the planet of Rattatak, and they found themselves constantly battling their Rattataki neighbors.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vollick
- **Skincolor:** Chalk-White
- **Haircolor:** Black, however commonly bald
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Race of Near-Rattataki sentients
- **Origin:** Rattatak
