# Stauz Czycz's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stauz Czycz's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stauz_Czycz%27s_species)

This alien race were tall, muscular beings with orange skin, three-fingered hands, five green eyes, and a large, triangular head with snakelike tendrils in place of hair. Their homeworld was razed by the Empire. Stauz Czycz was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Stauz Czycz's species
- **Skincolor:** Orange
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Large triangular heads
  - Snake-like tendrils in place of hair
  - Five eyes
  - Three fingers
- **Origin:** Stauz Czycz's species homeworld
