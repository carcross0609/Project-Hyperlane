# Ssori

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ssori](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ssori)

The Ssori were a sentient species of diminutive size, who were typically seen in mechanical suits to compensate for their stature.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ssori
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** Diminutive
- **Distinctions:** Diminutive size, bug-eyes, sharp teeth
- **Origin:** Ssori Fragments
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
