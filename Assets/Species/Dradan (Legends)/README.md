# Dradan; Illusions of Dradan, as produced by the Dark Jedi Halagad Ventor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dradan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dradan/Legends)

The Dradan were a species of peaceful sentients native to a planet in the Drada system of the Outer Rim Territories. After the Galactic Empire was formed in 19 BBY and began to hunt and kill the Jedi Knights, the Dradan offered their homeworld as a refuge to Jedi fugitives. The Empire discovered the Dradan's treachery by 1 BBY and retaliated with genocide of the species and despoliation of their homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dradan; Illusions of Dradan, as produced by the Dark Jedi Halagad Ventor
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Eyecolor:** Red (Halagad Ventor's illusion)
- **Origin:** A world in the Drada system
- **Diet:** Meat, fruit, vegetables (Halagad Ventor's illusion)
- **Language:** Simple language (Halagad Ventor's illusion)
