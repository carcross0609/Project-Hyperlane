# Vashkan apidactyl

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vashkan apidactyl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vashkan_apidactyl)

Vashkan apidactyls, also called dacs for short, were a species of giant, winged insects that hailed from the warm planet of Vashka. They were the size of a small humanoid, had an armor-like chitinous exoskeleton, two sets of wings, and venomous stingers. They were eusocial insects who lived in towering communal hives, and they were aggressive toward intruders. Apidactyls secreted a heavy gold wax with which they built the hexagonal, thin-walled cells of their hives.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vashkan apidactyl
- **Designation:** Sentient
