# Pappfak

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pappfak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pappfak)

A Pappfak was the name of an alien species known for possessing long, blue-colored tentacles. Pappfaks could regularly be found inhabiting the marketplaces at Mos Eisley on the planet, Tatooine.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pappfak
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Turquoise tentacles
