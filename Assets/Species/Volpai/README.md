# Volpai

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Volpai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Volpai)

Volpai were a four-armed sentient species found in the galaxy. Notable Volpai included Robbs Ely, Tiggs Leo, and Moregi.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Volpai
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Blue
  - Orange with violet stripes
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** Four arms and eyes
