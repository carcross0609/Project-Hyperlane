# Anzati

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anzati](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anzati)

Anzati were a long-lived humanoid species that hailed from the Mid Rim Territories planet of Anzat. They were feared and hated as they fed on the life force of other sentient species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Anzati
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Haircolor:**
  - Black
  - White
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Origin:** Anzat
