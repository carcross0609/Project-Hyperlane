# Selonian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Selonian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Selonian/Legends)

Selonians were a sentient humanoid species from the planet of Selonia in the Core Worlds and were large musteline carnivorous mammals with long, flexible spines.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Selonian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Haircolor:**
  - Brown
  - Black
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Needle-sharp fangs and claws
  - Powerful tails
  - Long pointed faces with bristly whiskers
- **Lifespan:** 75 years
- **Origin:** Selonia
- **Language:** Mandaba
