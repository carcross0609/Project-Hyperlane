# Pyke

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pyke/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pyke/Legends)

The Pykes were a sentient species of humanoids from the planet Oba Diah.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pyke
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:** Purple
- **Distinctions:**
  - Large skulls and small faces
  - Long arms and feet
- **Origin:** Oba Diah
