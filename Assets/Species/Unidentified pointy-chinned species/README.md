# Unidentified pointy-chinned species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified pointy-chinned species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pointy-chinned_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. They were distinguished by their dark eyes and pointed chins. One member of this species was present at Grappa the Hutt's palace in 11 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified pointy-chinned species
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:** Pointed chin
