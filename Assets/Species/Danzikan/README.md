# Danzikan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Danzikan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Danzikan)

Danzikans were a swamp-dwelling, two-headed sentient species native to the swamp world of Danzik. Similar to the Troigs, both heads were independent and they used this to their advantage in card games and business deals. They were also often thought of as untrustworthy because of their tendency to cheat. Lark and Jonk were members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Danzikan
