# Fian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fian)

The Fian were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy that possessed a higher resistance to radiation than that of humans. During the High Republic Era, Ar Prace, a female Fian, served as a maintenance supervisor aboard the Galactic Republic space station Starlight Beacon until its destruction around 230 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Higher resistance to radiation
