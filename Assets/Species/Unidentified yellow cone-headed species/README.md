# Unidentified yellow cone-headed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified yellow cone-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_yellow_cone-headed_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their yellow skin and cone-shaped cranium. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species were present in a casino on the planet Qiaxx.
