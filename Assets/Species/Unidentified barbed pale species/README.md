# Unidentified barbed pale species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified barbed pale species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_barbed_pale_species)

A sentient species with pale skin existed in the galaxy. These aliens were bipedal humanoids that possessed four pointed barbs protruding from their faces, two below their mouths and two above, with a pair of jagged fin-like growths also extending from their cheekbones around the sides of their heads and ending in points above and behind their craniums.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified barbed pale species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Distinctions:**
  - Head fins
  - Facial barbs
