# Dressellian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dressellian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dressellian)

Dressellians were a species of sentient humanoids that were native to Dressel. They lived in peaceful isolation from any conflict until the Galactic Civil War. As a species, they were loyal to the Galactic Republic and after its demise many Dressellians joined the Rebel Alliance. They had hairless heads and wrinkled visages and cranium. Orrimaarko, a male Dressellian, was a soldier for the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dressellian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Bald head, wrinkled visage
- **Origin:** Dressel
