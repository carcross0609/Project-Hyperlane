# Ganimed

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ganimed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ganimed)

Ganimed were a humanoid sentient species distinguished by the single black horn on top of their hairless heads. The species had pointy ears and a ruddy brown and muddy yellow skin tone. The bounty hunter Purong was a member of the species. By 0 BBY, Purong visited Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina in Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine and engaged in a lightfight against the Ariwook Rff Huff.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ganimed
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Muddy yellow, ruddy brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Single black horn
  - Pointed ears
