# k'Jtari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K'Jtari/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K%27Jtari/Legends)

The k'Jtari were a sentient species. They created a martial art that influenced its practitioners both mentally and physically. By the time of the Galactic Civil War, their martial art was considered ancient. The Human Dunan Par'Ell, who worked as head of security at the Ace of Sabres resort on the planet Kluistar, was proficient in it.
