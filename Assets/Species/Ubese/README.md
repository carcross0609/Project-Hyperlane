# Ubese

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ubese](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ubese)

The Ubese were a sentient species that spoke Ubese. Originating from the planet Uba IV, the Ubese were notoriously isolationist, rarely conducting trade with off-worlders. When an Ubese was exiled from Uba IV, their identities were removed from Ubese public record, and they were unable to return and reintegrate into their society.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ubese
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Uba IV
- **Language:** Ubese
