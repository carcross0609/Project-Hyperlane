# Suiraon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Suiraon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Suiraon)

Suiraons were a lizard-like humanoid species. They were a cold blooded species requiring the heat of native area to survive. Their eyes were snake like and their tongues were forked.
