# Unidentified feathered species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified feathered species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_feathered_species)

A member of an unidentified feathered species was among the prisoners of Stars' End, killed by Security Police during the Liberation of Stars' End in 2 BBY. This species had feathers, a beak, and translucent blood.
