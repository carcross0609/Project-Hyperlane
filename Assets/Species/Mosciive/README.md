# Mosciive

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mosciive](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mosciive)

The Mosciives were a near-Human species that inhabited the Inner Rim planet of Simocadia. They were distinguished from the Humans only by their distinctive red eyes.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mosciive
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Snake-like red eyes
- **Origin:** Simocadia
