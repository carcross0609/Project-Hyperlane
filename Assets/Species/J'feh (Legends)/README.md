# J'feh

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [J'feh/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J%27feh/Legends)

The J'feh were a cephalopod species characterized by their red skin and numerous tentacles. Members of the species usually lived within large, hovering globes. Skijid Vrescot was a J'feh crime lord and the Count of Cheelit who operated on The Wheel during the last decades of the Galactic Republic. The rubbery-skinned J'feh crossed the Glottalphib piratess Kliskud, who ate Vrescot, finding the alien tasty if tough to chew. The J'feh Lady Dhol later occupied the Hive Palace on Cheelit, and there attempted to entrap the Sith Lord Darth Vader in a game of firepath during the Galactic Civil War, but was incinerated in the game.
