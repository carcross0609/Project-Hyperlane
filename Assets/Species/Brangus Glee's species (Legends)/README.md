# Brangus Glee's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Brangus Glee's species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brangus_Glee%27s_species/Legends)

A sentient species existed with a dexterous anatomy that included a head and a humanoid torso with two thin arms and long, wiry fingers. Their head featured two eyes, two nostrils, and a mouth, with a long neck connecting their head to their chest.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Brangus Glee's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long necks
  - Thin arms with long, wiry fingers
