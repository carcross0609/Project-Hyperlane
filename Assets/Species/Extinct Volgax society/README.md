# Extinct Volgax society

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Extinct Volgax society](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Extinct_Volgax_society)

Long prior to the Mandalorian Wars, a civilization existed on the Core World of Volgax. This society constructed large buildings on the planet but eventually died out, leaving their ruins behind. These facilities were later adopted by the slavers of The Crucible during the wars.
