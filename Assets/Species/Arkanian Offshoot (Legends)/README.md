# Arkanian Offshoot

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arkanian Offshoot/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arkanian_Offshoot/Legends)

The Arkanian Offshoots were a sub-species of the Arkanian race, genetically engineered by the famed Arkanian scientists and genetic researchers of the Old Republic era.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Arkanian Offshoot
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Distinctions:**
  - Human hybrid:
  - Five-fingered hands
  - Human-like eyes
  - Pure white skin
  - Sephi hybrid:
  - Pointed ears
- **Lifespan:** Up to 55 standard years
- **Origin:** Arkania
- **Language:**
  - Arkanian
  - Basic
