# Ongidae

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ongidae](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ongidae)

The Ongidae were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. Pemmin Brunce was an Ongidae who worked as the head of security in the Canto Bight casino on Cantonica.

- **Designation:** Sentient
