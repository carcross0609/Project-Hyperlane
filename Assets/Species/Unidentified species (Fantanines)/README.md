# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Fantanines)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Fantanines%29)

A humanoid, saurian species lived on a Fantanine strip-mining planet. They had red skin and white eyes. While adults were humanoid, younglings were small quadrupeds. The Fantanines tried to domesticate them, but they could not take to the work, and started shipping them off-world. Lando Calrissian, when he arrived to re-take possession of the admin facility, noticed such a shipment, and intervened. He freed the natives from the shipment, but it led to him losing his control of Cloud City.
