# Spalexian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spalexian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spalexian)

The Spalexians were a sentient species were native to the planet Spalex. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, the Empire attempted to mine Spalex for its thorilide but were met with strong resistance from the native species. The species were eventually able to force the Empire offworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Spalexian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Hazel
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Elongated skulls
  - Antennae
- **Origin:** Spalex
