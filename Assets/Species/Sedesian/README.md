# Sedesian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sedesian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sedesian)

The Sedesians were the Human inhabitants of the planet Sedesia. Because of Sedesia's high gravity, Sedesians were short, wide, and stockily built. It also gave them greater than average strength. Independently minded, Sedesians were stubborn, tough, and determined. Sedesians spoke Basic with an accent.
