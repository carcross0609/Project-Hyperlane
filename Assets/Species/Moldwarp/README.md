# Moldwarp

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moldwarp](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moldwarp)

Moldwarp were a scaled sentient species. One Moldwarp, Edmo Ectacle, hailed from Lespectus, and was present in the casino in Canto Bight on the planet Cantonica in around 34 ABY. Another, Draiven Bosh was a con-man during the High Republic Era.
