# Unidentified pink hairy species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified pink hairy species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_pink_hairy_species)

This species was a sentient species distinguished by their pink skin and hair. At least one member of this species was present on the planet Orvax IV in 19 BBY.
