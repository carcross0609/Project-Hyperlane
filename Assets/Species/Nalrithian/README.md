# Nalrithian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nalrithian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nalrithian)

Nalrithians were a very muscular species of sentient insectoids native to the Outer Rim Territories. Nalrithians born from the same clutch of eggs were known as eggmates and were known to share a hive mind, which meant that their memories would be shared among them.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nalrithian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 2.10 meters
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Insectoid
  - Very muscular body
  - Four eyes.
- **Origin:** Nalrith
