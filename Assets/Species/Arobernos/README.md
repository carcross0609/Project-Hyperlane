# Arobernos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arobernos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arobernos)

The Arobernos were a large, sentient, red-skinned species with pointed ears. They could survive being engulfed in lava. One member of the species was blind and locked in symbiosis with the Eye of Webbish Bog on the planet Mustafar.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Arobernos
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Capable of surviving being engulfed in lava
  - Pointed ears
