# Tarong

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tarong](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tarong)

The Tarongs were large sentient avians from the twin worlds of Iri and Disim in the Epidimi system. Notably skilled mimics, Tarongs were typically talkative and friendly people. They were fond of space travel, gems, and protocol droids.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tarong
- **Races:** Hybridized from species from Iri and Disim
- **Wingspan:** 10 - 20 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown, tan, or olive green
- **Feathers:** Similar to skin color
- **Distinctions:** Quadrupedal avians, keen vision, vocal mimicry
- **Origin:** Iri, Disim
