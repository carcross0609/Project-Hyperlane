# Kloodavian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kloodavian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kloodavian)

The Kloodavians were small, yellow-skinned reptilians. They had the ability to regenerate lost limbs and were immune to mind tricks.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kloodavian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptile
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Origin:** Kloodavia system
