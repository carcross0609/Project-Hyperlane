# Nazzar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nazzar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nazzar)

Nazzar were a sentient species that lived in relative harmony with other species on the planet Coruscant. Krrl and Akray were members of the species.
