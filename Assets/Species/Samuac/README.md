# Samuac

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Samuac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Samuac)

The Samuac were a near-Human species from the Outer Rim Territories moon New Kisge. Around 12 BBY, the male Samuac Sisquoc graduated from the Galactic Outdoor Survival School, where he was a record-setting student. During the Galactic Civil War, the Samuac allied themselves with the Rebel Alliance, with the Alliance establishing its Delta Base on New Kisge.
