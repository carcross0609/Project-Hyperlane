# Centran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Centran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Centran)

The Centrans were a sentient humanoid Near-Human species that hailed from Erilnar, capital of their home territory, the Centrality. Their civilization were descended from human setters, and partitioned into a separate race due to Erilnar unusual geography and harsh environment.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Centran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Lifespan:** Up to 100 standard years
- **Origin:** Erilnar
