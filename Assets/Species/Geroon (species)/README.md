# Geroon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Geroon (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geroon_%28species%29)

The Geroon were a sentient species hailed from the epynomous Unknown Regions planet. They were captured and enslaved by the Vagaari species in 27 BBY. Around nine Geroons were killed by B1-Series and droideka battle droids under the directive of the Vagaari leader, the Miskara, and many of them imprisoned within zero-g plastic bubbles on the Vagaari fleet's hulls. They were freed by the Chiss species and their government, the Chiss Ascendancy, after the Chiss defeated the Vagaari. Ascendancy Admiral Ar'alani provided foodstuff to the Geroons aboard a transport.
