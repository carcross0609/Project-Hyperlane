# Bloxian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bloxian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bloxian)

Bloxians were a sentient species native to the Bloxia system in Wild Space. The species was characterized by their large size and red-hued skin. Members of this species included Bakk and Chokk, enforcers for the Pliith loan shark Linkaas.
