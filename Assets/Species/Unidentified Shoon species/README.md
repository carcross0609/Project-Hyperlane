# Unidentified Shoon species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Shoon species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Shoon_species)

At some point, a species of gigantic humanoids lived on Shoon, a world located in the Senex sector within the Mid Rim's Senex-Juvex region. The species built structures across their homeworld's surface. By 43 ABY, the humanoids had gone extinct, with the ruins of their structures left as the only testament to their former existence. Like the other worlds of the Senex-Juvex, Shoon eventually came under the control of the region's aristocratic Humanocentric Ancient Houses, which had settled the Senex-Juvex between 1000 BBY and 700 BBY and had enslaved the native alien species.
