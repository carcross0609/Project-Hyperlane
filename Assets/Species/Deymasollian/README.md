# Deymasollian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Deymasollian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deymasollian)

The Deymasollians were a sentient species native to the Colonies' Deymasoll system. They had two arms with delicate fingers and two legs—both limb pairs being relatively short—and a large mouth and prominent lower jaw. The Deymasollians had tan skin, dark eyes, and a coat of white fur. All Deymasollians had keen memory, and members of the species' society identified as belonging to at least one—female—gender.
