# Ru

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ru](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ru)

The Ru were a small, furry, and primitive mammalian sentient species native to the planet Phorose. Despite the fact that the locals considered them rodents, scientists believed that the Ru were, in fact, sentient beings, due to the fact that they worshiped in temples that they built.
