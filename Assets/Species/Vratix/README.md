# Vratix

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vratix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vratix)

The Vratix were a species of sentient insectoids from the planet Thyferra. They were the inventors of bacta, a gelatinous substance that encouraged the rapid healing of serious injuries. The production of bacta gave Vratix an almost mystical pleasure. It had such an intoxicating effect on them that they felt the need to produce the substance.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vratix
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Origin:** Thyferra
