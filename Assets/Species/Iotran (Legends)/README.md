# Iotran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iotran/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iotran/Legends)

The Iotrans were a militaristic species hailing from the planet of Iotra in the Outer Rim Territories. Their military history was long and storied, and they originated several famous military orders, including the Kilandran Royal Forces, the Lurathi Guard, and the Jengardin Millennial Warriors.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Iotran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Height:** 1.75 meters
- **Skincolor:** Dark brown to black
- **Distinctions:** Very strong; spikes protruding from elbows, shoulders, and hands; militaristic
- **Origin:** Iotra
- **Language:** Tranese
