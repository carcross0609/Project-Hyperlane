# Wermal

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wermal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wermal)

Wermals were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. They had one large eye, nose-slits, pink skin, and three hearts. They could live for well over a century. Kedpin Shoklop was a Wermal from Werma Lesser.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Wermal
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Distinctions:** One eye, three hearts
- **Lifespan:** Well over a century
- **Origin:** Werma Lesser
