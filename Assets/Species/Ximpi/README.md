# Ximpi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ximpi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ximpi)

The Ximpi were a sentient species of small, winged humanoids with gray skin, broad heads and pointed ears from the planet Shuraden on the Galactic Frontier. A number of Ximpi were in the employ of the crime lord Papa Toren. Those individuals would translate Toren's otherwise unintelligible mumbles to his discussion partners and, if necessary, protect him from harm.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ximpi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 28 centimeters (11 in)
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Two pairs of wings
- **Origin:** Shuraden
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
