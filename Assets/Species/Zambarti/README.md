# Zambarti

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zambarti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zambarti)

The Zambarti were lavender-hued humanoids with wide bodies and huge heads. Their eyes were shaped like saucers, and their hair resembled a slate-colored bird's nest. Their large ears were pink in coloration, and looked like wings. Members of this species had more than one heart. Big Bunji was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Zambarti
- **Class:** Humanoids
