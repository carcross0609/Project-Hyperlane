# Unidentified multi-chinned species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified multi-chinned species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_multi-chinned_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished their long, blubbery necks with multiple chin folds, as well as stringy hair. Some time prior to 22 BBY, at least one member of this species was a member of the Jedi Order, their busts displayed within the Temple Archives.
