# Arkanian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arkanian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arkanian/Legends)

The Arkanians were a near-Human species from the frozen planet of Arkania in the Perave system of the Colonies region of the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Arkanian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Subspecies:** Arkanian Offshoot
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** From tan to pale
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** White
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four clawed digits
  - Extreme intelligence
  - Able to see into the infrared spectrum
- **Lifespan:**
  - 100 standard years
  - Child: 1-11 years
  - Young adult: 12-14 years
  - Adult: 15-52 years
  - Middle age: 53-84 years
  - Old: 85-102 years
  - Venerable: 103+ years
- **Origin:** Arkania
- **Language:** Arkanian
