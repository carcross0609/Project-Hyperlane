# Imyni

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imyni/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imyni/Legends)

In the opera The Kallea Cycle, the Imyni were a sentient species from the Outer Rim Territories world Imynusoph. The Human scout Freia Kallea treated them presumptuously while enlisting their aid in exacting revenge on her rivals, the Neimoidians Thoax and Farge Osaax. As a result, when Kallea ordered the Imyni to bind the Osaaxes while she sprung her trap in a temple on Imynusoph, the natives instead turned on all the offworlders, leading to a climactic battle.
