# Unidentified Neona species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Neona species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Neona_species)

A sentient species lived on the planet Neona, which was located in the Freeworlds Territory of the Colonies' Tapani sector. The species developed an advanced culture and constructed an extensive network of structures on Neona's surface. Around 19,997 BBY, Neona underwent a sustained warming period: polar ice caps melted, raising the level of Neona's oceans until all the planet's landmasses were covered except for a frozen continent at each pole. The flooding of their cities led to the extinction of the species.
