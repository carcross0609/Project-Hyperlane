# Schizode

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Schizode](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Schizode)

Schizodes were an insectoid sentient species. Though they were considered to be good combatants and crafters, they were not very individualized.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Schizode
- **Height:** 1.2-1.7 meters
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four arms with pincers
  - Little individuality
  - High mimicry capacity.
