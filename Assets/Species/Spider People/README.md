# Spider People

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spider People](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_People)

The Spider People were a sentient species native to the planet of Ord Cestus. They were most likely related to the Cave spiders that were still extant in more modern times. This species was quick to jump to conclusions. After losing to the X'Ting in a war, the Spider People were driven underground from the planet's surface into the underground caves of Ord Cestus.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Spider People
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Ord Cestus
