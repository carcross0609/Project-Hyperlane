# Draedan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Draedan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Draedan/Legends)

Draedans were a species of sentient amphibians with a reputation for constantly fighting one another. Their homeworld, Sesid, was divided into many different nations, ranging from democratic republics to military dictatorships.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Draedan
- **Height:** 1.3—1.7 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Silver
  - Green
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Origin:** Sesid
