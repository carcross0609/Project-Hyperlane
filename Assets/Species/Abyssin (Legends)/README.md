# Abyssin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Abyssin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abyssin/Legends)

Abyssins were a species of mammalian, cycloptic humanoids native to the Outer Rim world Byss in the Abyss system. As a people the Abyssin were often described as "primitive and violent", and were often hired as petty thugs.

- **Name:** Abyssin
- **Designation:** Sentient

- **Origin:** Byss
- **Language:** Abyssin
