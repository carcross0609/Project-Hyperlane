# Unidentified dead red moon species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified dead red moon species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_dead_red_moon_species)

This sentient species was native to a dead red moon orbiting the third planet in the Sixela system. They had been extinct for millennia before their homeworld was visited again by Rezi Soresh in 1 ABY. They built underground tunnels, lasting long after their extinction, which Soresh would use during his time on the moon.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified dead red moon species
- **Origin:** A dead moon
