# Askajian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Askajian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Askajian)

Askajians were a sentient humanoid species that were native to the planet Askaji. On their homeworld, they lived in tribes led by a chief. Jedi Master Klefan Opus and Yarna d'al' Gargan, who was forced to dance for Jabba the Hutt as one of his slaves, were both Askajians.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Askajian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Fair
- **Haircolor:** Dark brown
- **Distinctions:** Epidermal sacs
- **Origin:** Askaji
