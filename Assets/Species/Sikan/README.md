# Sikan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sikan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sikan)

The Sikan (singular: Sika) were a race of sentient beings that were native to the planet of Sika. Their planet was invaded by opportunistic Chekut forces during the Second Galactic Civil War.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
