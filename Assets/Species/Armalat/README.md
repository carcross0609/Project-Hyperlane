# Armalat

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Armalat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armalat)

The Armalat species was a giant, green-skinned non-humanoid race that weighed several hundred kilos. Each one of their hands only had three fingers. Some Armalat settled on Ansion, and were represented by Tolut on the Unity of Community's Unity Council.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Armalat
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Three fingers
  - Chisel-like teeth
  - Enormous girth
