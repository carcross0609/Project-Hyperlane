# Chev

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chev](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chev)

The Chevs were one of the two native sentient species of the planet Vinsoth. These humanoids were slaves of the Chevin, Vinsoth's other indigenous people, for most of their recorded history, a fact which shaped their culture and their interactions with the rest of the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Chev
- **Height:** 1.2 – 1.6 meters or 1.4 – 1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Pale white to slate gray
- **Haircolor:**
  - Pale white
  - Slate gray
  - Wheat-colored
- **Eyecolor:** Black to violet
- **Distinctions:**
  - Lidless eyes, two hearts
  - High technical aptitude
- **Lifespan:** 75 to 300 years (sources differ)
- **Origin:** Vinsoth
- **Language:** Chev
