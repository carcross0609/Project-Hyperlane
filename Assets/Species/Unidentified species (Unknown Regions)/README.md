# Unidentified species (Unknown Regions)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Unknown Regions)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Unknown_Regions%29)

This sentient species originated in the Unknown Regions and had developed a primitive form of space travel. They were roughly humanoid in shape. At some point before 0 ABY, a military leader of this species was in conflict with Imperial Chiss officer known as Thrawn.
