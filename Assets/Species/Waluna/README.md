# Waluna

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Waluna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Waluna)

The Waluna were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. One Waluna, Doma Dendra, was a female Waluna who lived on the planet Koboh in 9 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Waluna
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Beige and blue
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four legs
  - Lekku
