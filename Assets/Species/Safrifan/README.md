# Safrifan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Safrifan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Safrifan)

Safrifans were a sentient species native to the swamp planet Safrifa. They had skin the color of stagnant water, large oval eyes, and hair like pondweed. Despite their perceived scrawny nature, the Safrifans were industrious, able to raise crops in Safrifa's marsh terrain.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Safrifan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Safrifa
- **Habitat:** Swamps
- **Language:** Safrifan language
