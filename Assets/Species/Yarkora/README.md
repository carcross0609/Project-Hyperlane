# Yarkora

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yarkora](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yarkora)

Yarkora, also known as Yarkorans and Yak Faces, were a sentient species found in the galaxy. Saelt-Marae and Jedi Master Adampo were Yarkorans. Due to most Yarkora eating constantly throughout the day, they had two stomachs, four kidneys, and three livers.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yarkora
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four kidneys
  - Three livers
  - Two stomachs
