# Ran Deezy's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ran Deezy's species/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ran_Deezy%27s_species/Legends)

This alien race had green skin. Ran Deezy, a Jedi Initiate during the Clone Wars, was a member of the species. Deezy was present in the Jedi Temple on the galactic capital Coruscant around 22 BBY for a hologram transmission from General Grievous of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ran Deezy's species
- **Skincolor:** Light Green or blue
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
