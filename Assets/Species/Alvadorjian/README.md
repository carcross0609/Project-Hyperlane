# Alvadorjian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Alvadorjian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alvadorjian)

Alvadorjians were a sentient species native to the Alvadorjia system. They were known to the Galactic Republic as "Xivalians," but Xivalia was not their home.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Alvadorjian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Scalp plating
  - Eyespots on the neck and throat
- **Origin:** Alvadorjia system
