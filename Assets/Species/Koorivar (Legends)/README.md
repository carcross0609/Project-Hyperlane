# Koorivar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Koorivar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Koorivar/Legends)

The Koorivar were a sentient species of humanoids distinguished by their banded noses and a slender cranial horn that spiraled upwards from the center of their skull. A shrewd people, the Koorivar strove to make their society one where corporate-mindedness was essential to each citizen. Through the combined efforts of the Koorivar people, these sentients were able to purchase a new homeworld called Kooriva from the Galactic Republic, following the loss of their native planet.
