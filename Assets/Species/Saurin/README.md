# Saurin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Saurin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saurin)

The Saurin were a species of sentient reptiles from the planet of Durkteel, in the Mid Rim of the galaxy. They had imposing, jagged teeth, knobby skin, and ridges along the length of their heads.
