# Godoan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Godoan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Godoan/Legends)

The Godoans were sentient humanoids indigenous to Godo. They were a simple, deeply religious people, who appeared to have very limited contact with the galaxy at large.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Godoan
- **Distinctions:** Child-like faces, antennae
- **Origin:** Godo
