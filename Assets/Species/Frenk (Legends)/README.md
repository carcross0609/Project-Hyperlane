# Frenk

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Frenk/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frenk/Legends)

Frenk were a sentient humanoid species from the planet Gorobei. The species had an armed force called the Frenk military, of which Rumi Paramita may have been a former member.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Frenk
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Red
  - Green
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:**
  - 4 nostrils
  - Thin bodies
- **Origin:** Gorobei
