# Unidentified long-headed alien species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified long-headed alien species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_long-headed_alien_species)

A sentient alien species lived during the Imperial Era. They had dark gray or dark blue skin and possessed an elongated head. One such being was once at the Mrlsst Trade and Science Academy.
