# Kexing

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kexing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kexing)

Kexing were a sentient species that had gray skin. The species also could grow hair and had vestigal teeth along their cheekbones. By 228 BBY, the Kexing Jayd served as one of the Nihil Minister of Information Ghirra Starros's most trusted aides.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kexing
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Cheekbone vestigal teeth
