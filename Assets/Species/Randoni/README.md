# Randoni

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Randoni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Randoni)

The Randoni were Humans native to the planet Randon, known as merchants and traders . One could distinguish Randoni merchants from others by their rough-woven garb and loose hairstyle. Female ward-cousins were greatly highly honored due to their potential inheritance. It is also likely that the Randoni were the origin of Randoni Yellow Plague, a drink.
