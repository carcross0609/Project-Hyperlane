# Zilkin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zilkin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zilkin)

Zilkins were a diminutive humanoid sentient species whose members had spotted green skin and stood at an average of roughly 0.33 meters in height. Colonel Meebur Gascon, a native of Great Zilk, who served on Republic Strategic Command during the Clone Wars was a member of this species, as was Fasca Tiradé, a member of the Resistance during the First Order-Resistance War. On the planet Kamil, a member of the species specialized in selling robas.
