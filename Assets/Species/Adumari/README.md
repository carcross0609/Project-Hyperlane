# Adumari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adumari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adumari)

The Adumari were the Human natives of the planet Adumar, which they colonized approximately ten thousand years ago after staging a rebellion against the Old Republic. They were defeated and spared so long as they went far away and ceased making trouble.
