# Ho'Din

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ho'Din](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ho%27Din)

The Ho'Din were a species of yellow-green humanoids in the galaxy. Ho'Dins possessed two large dark eyes, long suction cup-tipped fingers and, on their heads, red-violet fleshy tendrils.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ho'Din
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow-green
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Suction cup-tipped fingers and head tendrils.
