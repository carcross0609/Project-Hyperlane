# Unidentified fleshy hook-headed species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified fleshy hook-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_fleshy_hook-headed_species)

A sentient species that possessed a fleshy appearance, with various bulges and folds, including multiple chins and layered jowls, with large ears, and a curved, an almost snake-like head-stalk that ended in a pair of eyes with exposed, vertical nostrils situated between them. They also had various wart-like growths spread across their skin, as well as a small patch of hair under their bottom lip.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified fleshy hook-headed species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Head-stalk
