# Unidentified green thin-headed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified green thin-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green_thin-headed_species)

This species was a sentient species distinguished by their green skin and long, almost noodle-like necks and head. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species were present in a casino on the planet Qiaxx.
