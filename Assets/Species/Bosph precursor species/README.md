# Bosph precursor species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bosph precursor species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bosph_precursor_species)

A species of primitive mammalian omnivores evolved on the Outer Rim planet of Bosph. Members of the species had six limbs and were hunted by predators called Luprisi which they had to out smart to avoid. The species eventually evolved into the sentient Bosph species, who maintained their precursors species six limbs.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bosph precursor species
- **Designation:** Non-sentient
- **Class:** Mammal
- **Distinctions:** Six limbs
- **Origin:** Bosph
- **Diet:** Omnivorous
