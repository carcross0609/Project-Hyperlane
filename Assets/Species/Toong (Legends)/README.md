# Toong

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Toong/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Toong/Legends)

The Toong were a sentient species from the planet Toong'l. In comparison with many other humanoids, their heads were disproportionately large for their bodies.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Toong
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.6 - 2.4 meters
- **Origin:** Toong'l
- **Language:** Toongese
