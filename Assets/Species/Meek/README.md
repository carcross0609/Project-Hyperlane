# Meek

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Meek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meek)

Meeks were a primitive sentient species, with three eyes. A village of Meeks was afraid that a rancor would eat them.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Meek
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Orange
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Antennae,Three eyes
- **Origin:** Meek homeworld
