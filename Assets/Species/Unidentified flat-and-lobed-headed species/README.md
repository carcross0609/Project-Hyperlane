# Unidentified flat-and-lobed-headed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified flat-and-lobed-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_flat-and-lobed-headed_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They possessed reddish-brown skin with large flat heads lined with drooping lobes, lacking any other discernable facial features. Sometime between 0.5 ABY and 1 ABY, at least three members of this specie could be found on the streets of Panna City on the moon Panna Prime when Boba Fett and Chewbacca arrived in search of a serum for the sleeping virus which was affecting Han Solo and Luke Skywalker.
