# Talortai

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Talortai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talortai)

The Talortai were an avian sentient species that possessed feathers and clawed feet. Talortai hailed from Talor, an astronomical object that featured tropics. The species was strong in the Force, and Talortai could use their affinity to navigate the galaxy with precision; however, in the case of the Talortai navigator Udi Dis, their connection to the Force did not extend to the premonition that the Jedi were known for.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Talortai
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian
- **Distinctions:**
  - Clawed feet and feathers
  - Strength in the Force
- **Origin:** Talor
