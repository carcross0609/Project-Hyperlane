# Grysk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grysk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grysk)

The Grysks were a warlike sentient species that hailed from a planet in the Unknown Regions. Known to be half of myth, the Grysks were a nomadic warrior society that ruled an empire known as the Grysk Hegemony. They used a common script and a rare language.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Grysk
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** Much smaller than a Kilji
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:**
  - Wide shoulders
  - Angled brow ridges
  - Tapered skull
  - Deep-set eyes
  - Two arms
- **Origin:** Grysk homeworld
- **Language:** Grysk language
