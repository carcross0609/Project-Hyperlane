# Taurill

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Taurill](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taurill)

Taurill were a multi-limbed, furry species that shared a hive mind. At one point, Bandit Billie and Ratty-Ratty hired a group of Taurill to help them steal a starship, thinking their hive mind would work to their advantage.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Taurill
- **Designation:** Sentient (hive mind)
- **Distinctions:** Furry coat, multiple limbs
