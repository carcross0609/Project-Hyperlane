# Tuulabi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tuulabi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tuulabi)

The Tuulabi were the Human inhabitants of the planet Tuulab. Originally a colony of 721 Humans led to the planet by the Corellian Ingas Lek, the population rose to around 6,000 during the early years of the New Republic. The Tuulabi preferred to live a rural existence without interference from galactic politics or the Galactic Civil War. The Tuulabi did not form a government, or ally themselves with either the Galactic Empire or New Republic.
