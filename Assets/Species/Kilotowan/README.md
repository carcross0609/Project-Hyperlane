# Kilotowan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kilotowan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kilotowan)

Kilotowans were a sentient species in the galaxy with pink skin and hair, as well as gentle-peaked ears. One member of the species was Leyli Romero.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kilotowan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Haircolor:** Pink
- **Distinctions:** Gentle-peaked ears
- **Origin:** Kilotowa
