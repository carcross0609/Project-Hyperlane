# Unidentified Yorgraxx species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Yorgraxx species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Yorgraxx_species)

The natives of Yorgraxx were a sentient species on the Outer Rim Territories planet Yorgraxx. They had developed a level on civilization on their planet prior to 1000 BBY, before the Sith Quorlac Fornayh arrived on the planet. He used them as slave labor to build his fortress, eventually causing the extinction of the species.
