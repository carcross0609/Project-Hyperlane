# Unidentified barbed orange species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified barbed orange species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_barbed_orange_species)

A sentient species with orange skin existed in the galaxy. These aliens were bipedal humanoids that possessed four pointed barbs protruding from their faces, two below their mouths and two above. They also possessed two tube-like protuberances wrapping around each side of their faces, flanking their ears, as well as a pair of jagged fin-like growths also extending from the top sides of their heads and ending in points above and behind their craniums.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified barbed orange species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Orange
- **Distinctions:**
  - Head fins
  - Facial barbs
