# Nobillian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nobillian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nobillian)

Nobillians were a species of insectoid beings found in the galaxy. Neeva and Neevaan were two Nobillians that lived on Nar Shaddaa and were enemies of the criminal Boggs Triff. Multiple members of the species attended the Canto Casino and Racetrack on Cantonica in 34 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nobillian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
