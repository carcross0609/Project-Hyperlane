# Unidentified cylindrical-headed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified cylindrical-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_cylindrical-headed_species)

The species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their tall, cylindrical heads that ended in points, with small, solid eyes set low on their faces. At least three members of this species were present on the planet Coruscant in 22 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified cylindrical-headed species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Tall heads
