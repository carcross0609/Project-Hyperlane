# Sho'ban Do's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sho'ban Do's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sho%27ban_Do%27s_species)

This near-human race was native to Pitann. They were short and barrel-chested, with large, muscular arms and legs, and dark, leathery skin. Since their homeworld had a higher gravity than Dayark, they seemed to bounce when they walked. Sho'ban Do was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sho'ban Do's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Skincolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:** Barrel-chested
- **Origin:** Pitann
