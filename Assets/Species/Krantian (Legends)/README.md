# Krantian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krantian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krantian/Legends)

Krantians were a bipedal sentient humanoid species from the planet Krant. They were pale-skinned near-Humans known for their ultrasound speaking capabilities. During the Clone Wars, a group of expatriated Krantians protested on Coruscant after their neutral homeworld was the site of a battle. They helped the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War.
