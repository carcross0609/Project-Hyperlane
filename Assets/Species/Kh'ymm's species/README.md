# Kh'ymm's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kh'ymm's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kh%27ymm%27s_species)

A sentient winged species lived within the galaxy. The species possessed gray and brown feathers and had the ability to fly. The astronomer Kh'ymm was a member of the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kh'ymm's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Feathers:** Gray and brown
- **Distinctions:** Feathered wings
