# Yunu

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yunu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yunu)

The Yunu were a large, furred, sentient species native to Arkinnea. They lived in the planet's forests, and allied with the Jedi refugees led by Jedi Master K'Kruhk, helping them establish a secret temple there.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yunu
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Black
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Origin:** Arkinnea
