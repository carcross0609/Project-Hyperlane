# Teedo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Teedo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teedo)

Teedos were a sentient reptilian species native to the planet Jakku, in the Western Reaches. They were diminutive in height and had gray-green skin, most of which was covered in wrappings to protect them from the intense heat of their homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Teedo
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Height:** 1.24 meters
- **Mass:** 28 kilograms
- **Skincolor:** Gray-green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Short stature
  - Wrappings on their skin
  - Three digit hands and feet
- **Origin:** Jakku
- **Habitat:** Desert or wastelands
- **Language:** Teedospeak
