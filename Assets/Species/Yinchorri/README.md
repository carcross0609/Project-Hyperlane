# Yinchorri

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yinchorri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yinchorri)

Yinchorri were a reptilian sentient species. They possessed broad, rounded snouts and unlike other reptilian species such as the Trandoshan and Saurin, had smooth scaleless skin. Jodak, a pilot for the Riftwalkers, was a Yinchorri.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yinchorri
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:** Smooth scaleless skin
- **Origin:** Yinchorr system
