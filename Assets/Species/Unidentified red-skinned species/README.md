# Unidentified red-skinned species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified red-skinned species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_red-skinned_species)

Somewhere in the galaxy lived a species, known for their red skin. A number of them were painted on patina-darkened vases owned by Temmin Wexley in his junk shop on the planet Akiva. This race of people were often seen to have been dressed in dark robes.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified red-skinned species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Red
