# Kushiban

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kushiban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kushiban)

Kushibans were sentient lagomorphs, with two big ears and mood fur covering their bodies. This mood fur was white when the Kushiban were at peace, and changed colors according to their mood.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kushiban
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1 meters
- **Length:** 0.5 meters
- **Haircolor:** White at rest, changes color with mood.
- **Eyecolor:** Change color, have been seen blue, brown, green.
- **Distinctions:**
  - Lagomorph
- **Lifespan:** 100 standard years
- **Origin:** Kushibah
- **Language:** Kushiban
