# Trodatome

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Trodatome](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trodatome)

The Trodatome were a sentient species. While they had no arms, they possessed prehensile sensory antennae and flippers. The Trodatome Klaud worked as a mechanic in the Resistance. At least one Trodatome, Gido, was present on the planet Koboh in 9 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Trodatome
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Blue
  - Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - No arms
  - Eight sensory antennae
