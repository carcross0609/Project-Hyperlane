# Ballian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ballian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ballian)

The Ballian were a small furry species that originated from the Balli system in the Outer Rim Territories. The brother of Maisie was a Ballian on the planet Metagos.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ballian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Balli system
