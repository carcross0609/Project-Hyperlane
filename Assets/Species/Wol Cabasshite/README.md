# Wol Cabasshite

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wol Cabasshite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wol_Cabasshite)

Wol Cabasshites were a sentient species of sedentary, limbless beings that sported a long tongue from Wol Cabassh. Because of their appearance, they were frequently mistaken for pests. At the time of the Galactic Civil War, a Wol Cabasshite named Ghoel resided in the palace of Jabba Desilijic Tiure, glued to the walls and arches. The mollusk-like being possessed a prehensile tongue as long as his body, which he used to lick courtiers. He was present when C-3PO and R2-D2 arrived to deliver a message from Luke Skywalker.
