# Abron Mar's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Abron Mar's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abron_Mar%27s_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. These humanoids were distinguished by their dark green skin and tiered noses. One member of this species, Abron Mar, served as a lieutenant in Takara's criminal organization by 10 ABY.
