# Zinchorian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zinchorian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zinchorian)

The Zinchorians were a sentient species that originated from a world with a methane-rich atmosphere in the Zinchori system. Sage Kinzem was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Zinchorian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Light and dark blue (When not in native methane atmosphere)
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Cranial horns
  - Color-changing skin
- **Origin:** Zinchori system
