# Anx

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Anx/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anx/Legends)

The Anx were a large, reptilian species of bipeds native to the planet Gravlex Med, a high gravity world located in Anx Space on the Outer Rim Territories.
