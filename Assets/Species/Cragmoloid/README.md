# Cragmoloid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cragmoloid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cragmoloid)

The Cragmoloid were a sentient elephantine species that lived in the galaxy. During the High Republic Era, a young Cragmoloid named Flim was begging in the Holy City of Jedha.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cragmoloid
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Elephantine
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
