# Vendaran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vendaran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vendaran)

The Vendarans were a species of near-Humans that resulted from an ancient colonization attempt. The Vendarans developed legends wherein giant forms of a native predator, the skeeg, were hunted for meat.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vendaran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Origin:** Vendara
