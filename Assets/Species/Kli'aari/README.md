# Kli'aari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kli'aari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kli%27aari)

The Kli'aari were a four-armed species native to Kli'aar. They were known for their gentleness and their mechanical skills. Enclaves of Kli'aari could be found on most inhabited worlds located in the Gordian Reach.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kli'aari
- **Distinctions:** Four arms
- **Origin:** Kli'aar
