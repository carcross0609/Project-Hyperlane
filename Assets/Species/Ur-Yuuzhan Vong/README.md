# Ur-Yuuzhan Vong

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ur-Yuuzhan Vong](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ur-Yuuzhan_Vong)

The Ur-Yuuzhan Vong were a extra-galactic sentient, species from Yuuzhan'tar in a distant galaxy. Ur-Yuuzhan Yong lived as symbiotes with their homeworld and the species as a whole. Ur-Yuuzhan Yong were the ancestral species of the Yuuzhan Vong, which they devolved into after they were cut off from the Force.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ur-Yuuzhan Vong
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Yuuzhan'tar
