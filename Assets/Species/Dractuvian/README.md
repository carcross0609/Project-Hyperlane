# Dractuvian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dractuvian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dractuvian)

Dractuvians were a species of sentient, red-skinned humanoids from the planet Dractu. They built paved streets and resided in homes made of stone.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dractuvian
- **Skincolor:** Red
- **Haircolor:** Red
- **Origin:** Dractu
