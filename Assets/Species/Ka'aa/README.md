# Ka'aa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ka'aa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ka%27aa)

The Ka'aa were a sapient species, known for wandering the stars. And were thought old enough to have seen the youngest stars of the Koornacht Cluster come into being. The called the Koornacht cluster, No'aat padu'll, which translated to the Little Nursery.
