# Fromprath

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fromprath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fromprath)

The Fromprath were a sentient, snakelike, six-armed species that lived in the galaxy. Hundreds of years before 3 BBY, the Fromprath lived on an Outer Rim planet but were forced to live in exile away from the planet after being forced out by the Nightsisters, who renamed the world Dathomir.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fromprath
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Height:** 3 meters
- **Distinctions:**
  - Multiple eyes
  - Six arms
  - Six legs
- **Origin:** Dathomir (later exiled)
