# Falleen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Falleen/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Falleen/Legends)

The Falleen were the indigenous reptilian sentient species of the planet Falleen.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Falleen
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Height:** 1.4 - 1.6 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Gray-green
  - Green
  - Orange
  - Red
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:** Small scales, naturally exude pheromones at will
- **Lifespan:** Up to 250 standard years; up to 400 years in rare cases
- **Origin:** Falleen
- **Language:** Falleen
