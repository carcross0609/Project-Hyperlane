# Yarkora

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yarkora/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yarkora/Legends)

The Yarkora were bipedal, anthropomorphic beings resembling cameloids. They hailed from an unknown world located in the galaxy's Outer Rim, and were rarely seen amongst the galactic populace.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Yarkora
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown

  - Green
  - Gray
- **Distinctions:**

  - Four kidneys

  - Powerful senses
- **Lifespan:** Over 200 standard years
- **Origin:** Yarkora homeworld
- **Language:** Yarkora
