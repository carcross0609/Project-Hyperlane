# Sakiyan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sakiyan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sakiyan)

The Sakiyans were a humanoid sentient species with bulbous, hairless heads and sharply tapered ears. Sakiyans hailed from the Hutt Space planet Saki, and as such were often enslaved by the Hutts. Thanks to their keen senses and quick reflexes, Sakiyans were skilled hunters. Sakiyan skin tones ranged from gleaming black to jade and plum.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sakiyan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Black, jade, plum
- **Distinctions:** Enlarged hairless craniums, keen senses and quick reflexes, tapered ears
- **Origin:** Saki
