# Ertraxi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ertraxi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ertraxi)

The Ertraxi were a peaceful humanoid species with multiple eyes from the Wild Space planet Ertrax. At some point, a Galactic Republic starship crash-landed on the planet, and the Ertraxi eventually repaired the vessel and flew it offworld in order to contact other species. They subsequently made first contact with a group of explorers, who informed the wider galactic community of the discovery of the species and their homeworld, prompting traders, slavers, and would-be conquerors to attempt to exploit Ertrax.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ertraxi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Distinctions:** Multiple eyes
- **Origin:** Ertrax
