# Farnym

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Farnym](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Farnym)

Farnym were sapient creatures noted for their roundness. They had close-cut orange fur and small noses. They had a peculiar odor that smelt of ginger mixed with sandalwood. Leia Organa Solo's copilot aboard the Alderaan, Lieutenant Tchiery, was a Farnym.
