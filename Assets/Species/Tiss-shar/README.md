# Tiss-shar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tiss-shar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tiss-shar)

The Tiss-shar were a sentient reptilian species in the galaxy. Oos-Rin-Arrek was a Tiss-shar contortionist who made a name for himself in the Gullet Gang. They were native to the Tiss'Sharl system in the Outer Rim Territories.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tiss-shar
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Tiss'Sharl system
