# Toma

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Toma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Toma)

The Toma were the sentient natives of the planet Oznek. They were distinguished by their green fur, yellow eyes, and brown skin.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Toma
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Haircolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Fangs
  - Green fur
- **Origin:** Oznek
