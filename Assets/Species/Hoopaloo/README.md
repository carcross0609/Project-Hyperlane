# Hoopaloo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hoopaloo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoopaloo)

Hoopaloo were a sentient species covered head to toe in ruffled feathers, which could be brown with blue highlights. They had a sharply hooked beak, and their feet had three toes forward and one back. One known Hoopaloo was the information broker Graysom, while another, Skreech, worked for the criminal Rekias Nodo.
