# Nyriaanan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nyriaanan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyriaanan)

The Nyriaanans, also known as Nyriaanan natives, were the near-Human inhabitants of the planet of Nyriaan.

- **Name:** Nyriaanan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-Human
- **Skincolor:**

- **Distinctions:**

- **Origin:** Nyriaan
- **Language:** Nyriaanan
