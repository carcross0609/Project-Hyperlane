# Kari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kari)

The Kari were small sentient insectoids covered with a black exoskeleton. They were the only sentient race indigenous to Karideph. Their language was similar to singing, appearing closer to the whistling and beeping of droids rather than a sentient species' speech.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kari
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Distinctions:** Insectoid
- **Origin:** Karideph
