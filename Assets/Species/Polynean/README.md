# Polynean

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Polynean](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Polynean)

Polynean were the human inhabitants of the planet Polyneus. When speaking with Caern Adan about Wyl Lark, New Republic General Hera Syndulla noted that she had seen two Polynean tributes in her career and had been impressed with their knack for piloting.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Polynean
- **Class:** Human
- **Origin:** Polyneus
