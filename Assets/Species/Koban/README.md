# Koban

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Koban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Koban)

Kobans were the Human inhabitants of the planet Koba. They were enslaved by the Advozsec warlord Tulak. The planet and its people were freed when Yaddle defeated Kalut.
