# Kilmo's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kilmo's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kilmo%27s_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. These bipedal humanoid sentients were distinguished by their red skin, with heads featuring eyes with green irises, a wide nasal ridge but lack of visible nostrils, distinctly segmented cheeks that cut off around the upper lip, two rows of blond facial hair, one on the aforementioned ridge and the other along their jaw, and massive pointed ears. In 13 BBY, a member of this species, Kilmo, worked as a criminal in Coronet City on the planet Corellia, coming into conflict with fellow criminal Han, though the latter would escape.
