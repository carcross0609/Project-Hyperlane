# Belugan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Belugan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Belugan/Legends)

The Belugans were a crystalline-aquatic species native to the planet Quarzite.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Belugan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Cetacean
- **Height:** Around 1.98 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown, Gray
- **Haircolor:** Red/Blue
- **Distinctions:** Clawed fingers, tentacled mouths, sideburns
- **Origin:** Quarzite
