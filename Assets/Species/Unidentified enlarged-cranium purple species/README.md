# Unidentified enlarged-cranium purple species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified enlarged-cranium purple species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_enlarged-cranium_purple_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The bipedal humanoid sentient species was distinguished by their enlarged craniums, purple skin, and white markings on their faces. In 9 ABY, at least two other purple members of the species frequented Garsa Fwip's Sanctuary in Mos Espa on the planet Tatooine, with one of them being present when Sanctuary was blown up by the Pyke Syndicate.
