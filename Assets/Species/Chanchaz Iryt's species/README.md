# Chanchaz Iryt's species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Chanchaz Iryt's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chanchaz_Iryt%27s_species)

A sentient species hailed from the high-gravity Mid Rim planet Kaump III. Its members had a short stature and limbs as well as a dense body structure and a flat face. The mercenary and pirate Chanchaz Iryt was a member of the species who grew up on Kaump III. Initially studying repulsorlift mechanics, he eventually turned to a life of crime and committed a series of high-profile robberies before leaving his homeworld aboard a luxury liner. Iryt subsequently went on to become a member of the Fatal Visionaries pirate gang.
