# Peeples's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Peeples's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Peeples%27s_species)

Peeples's species was a species of sentient beings with nineteen eyes and a snout-like proboscis. The species displayed a unique understanding of what was an appropriate moment to tell a joke. In 232 BBY, Galactic Republic Ensign Peeples was a member of the species who served aboard the Longbeam cruiser Aurora IX of the Republic Defense Coalition. He helped rescue the personnel of a damaged solar collection station during a disaster in the Hetzal system, for which he was promoted to lieutenant.
