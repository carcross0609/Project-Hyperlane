# Rur's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rur's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rur%27s_species)

This alien race was a race of humanoids similar in appearance to Siniteen, Sunesi or Vultan with thick cranial nodules on their bald plates. Rur was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Rur's species
- **Distinctions:** Thick cranial nodules on head plates
