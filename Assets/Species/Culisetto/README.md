# Culisetto

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Culisetto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Culisetto)

Culisetto were a sentient species of small, tube-mouthed insectoids that fed off of blood and also wore traditional brown traveling smocks. The Dengue sisters were members of this species as well as the bounty hunter Vorhdeilo.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Culisetto
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Skincolor:**
  - Pink
  - Yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Thoracic carapace
- **Diet:** Blood
- **Language:** Culisetto
