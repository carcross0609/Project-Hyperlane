# Finhead Stonebone's species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Finhead Stonebone's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Finhead_Stonebone%27s_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. Finhead Stonebone was a member of this species. These humanoids were distinguished by their wrinkled skin, as well large trunks, sometimes flanked by a pair of smaller openings.
