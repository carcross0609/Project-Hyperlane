# Fere

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fere (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fere_%28species%29)

The Fere were a sentient species of humanoids native to the world of Feree. They were considered to be master shipbuilders of their time.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Fere
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Height:** Tall
- **Skincolor:** Ivory
- **Distinctions:** Twelve fingers
- **Origin:** Feree
