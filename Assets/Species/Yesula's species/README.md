# Yesula's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yesula's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yesula%27s_species)

The child Yesula and her father were members of a sentient species. At some point no earlier than 227 BBY, multiple members of the species were present on the astronomical object Veek when the home of Yesula and her father caught fire. They were rescued by Jedi Master Kelnacca and his Padawan, Yarzion Vell. Once they escaped, Vell returned to the building to save their droid, Zeekay, whose databank had holos of Yesula's deceased mother.
