# Tzorbi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tzorbi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tzorbi)

The Tzorbi were a sentient species. At some point, they established a medical laboratory at their homeworld. The Tzorbi eventually became one of the first species to join the Galactic Republic, which was founded in 25,053 BBY. Millennia before 3643 BBY, however, they became extinct, with the remains of some of the members of the species eventually becoming fossilized at their homeworld.
