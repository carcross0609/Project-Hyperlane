# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Fox-Ear's underlings)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Fox-Ear%27s_underlings%29)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The red-skinned species was diminutive and appeared humanoid, seeming to posses two arms and two legs, with rounded heads featuring only one mouth with elongated, circular lips, as well as two large eyes with small dark pupils. Numerous members of this species worked as underlings for the crime boss Fox-Ear by sometime during or after 1 BBY.
