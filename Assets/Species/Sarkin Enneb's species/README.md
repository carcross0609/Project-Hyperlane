# Sarkin Enneb's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sarkin Enneb's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarkin_Enneb%27s_species)

This species was a sentient species found within the galaxy. These bipedal humanoid sentients were distinguished by their pointed, high-set ears, wrinkled faces, split upper lips, and green eyes with vertical black pupils. They also possessed brown hair and sharp brown fingernails.
