# Moggonite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moggonite/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moggonite/Legends)

The Moggonites of Arorua were better known as one of the most obnoxious and treacherous species in the galaxy. They were short mammalian creatures, standing approximately 1/2 meters tall. They were able to interbreed with at least one other species: Big Gizz, the leader of Jabba Desilijic Tiure's swoop gang, was a Moggonite hybrid.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Moggonite
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Origin:** Arorua
