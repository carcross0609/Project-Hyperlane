# Besalisk

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Besalisk/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Besalisk/Legends)

Besalisks were a race of multi-armed sentient humanoids from the cold ocean world of Ojom.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Besalisk
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.78–1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Feathers:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four arms (males)
  - Up to eight arms (females)
- **Lifespan:**
  - Child: 1-8
  - Young adult: 9-12
  - Adult: 13-48
  - Middle Age: 49-60
  - Old: 61-74
  - Venerable: 75+
- **Origin:** Ojom
- **Language:**
  - Besalisk
  - Galactic Basic Standard
