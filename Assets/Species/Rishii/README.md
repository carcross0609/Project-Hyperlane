# Rishii

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rishii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rishii)

The Rishii were the inhabitants of the planet Rishi. They were an intelligent avian species that lived in high mountains. The Rishii were cordial and uninterested in advanced technology, choosing to remain relatively isolated. During the Clone Wars, they were led by Chief Gwark, and they cooperated with the Galactic Republic to repel Geonosian Separatists.
