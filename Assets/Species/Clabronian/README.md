# Clabronian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clabronian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clabronian)

The Clabronians were a sentient species from the world of Clabron. During the Clone Wars, they were led by Grand Minister Stin, who considered siding with the Galactic Republic, despite the fact that many Clabronians wished to stay out of the conflict. When Stin organized a meeting with Republic senator Padmé Amidala, the Separatists assassinated Stin. He was replaced by Second Minister Tarmin, who, while not wanting to fight in the war, promised to provide aid to the Republic in other ways.
