# Croke

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Croke](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Croke)

The Croke were a species of sentient, multi-legged beings from the planet Crakull in the Unknown Regions who were extremely long lived; one individual enjoyed a lifespan of more than 20,000 years. In their natural form, the species resembled bristly-legged snails that were diminutive enough to be held in a Human hand. However, Croke were naturally skilled in illusion casting, a Force ability that allowed them to appear in larger guises.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Croke
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Either arthropods or mollusks
- **Length:** Small enough to fit in a Human hand; no bigger than a Wookiee's fist
- **Skincolor:** Black, gray
- **Distinctions:** Extended lifespan; natural illusion ability; oily skin; many legs; vacuum resistance
- **Lifespan:** Over 20,000 years
- **Origin:** Crakull
- **Language:** Croke
