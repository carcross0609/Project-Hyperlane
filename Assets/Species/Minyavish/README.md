# Minyavish

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Minyavish](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minyavish)

The Minyavish were a sentient species native to the planet Qaras. For seven thousand years they saw themselves as managers of the planet with native Jessar species as their servants. In 44 ABY, the Minyavish were revolted against by the enslaved Jessar. The Jessar then forced the Minyavish to leave the planet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Minyavish
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** pale blue
- **Feathers:** purple, green, gold
- **Eyecolor:** gold with dark gold pupils
- **Distinctions:** Large head and eyes
- **Origin:** Qaras
- **Language:** Basic
