# Verpine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Verpine/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verpine/Legends)

The Verpine were a sentient insectoid species from the Roche asteroid field. Best known for their technological aptitude, the Verpine were considered to be among the finest starship engineers in the galaxy.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Verpine
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Races:** Hermaphroditic dominant form and sterile worker drones
- **Height:** 1.9 meters
- **Mass:** 60 kilograms
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Black
  - Red
- **Distinctions:** Radio wave senses, technological aptitude, hardened carapace, short snouts, and small, toothless mouths
- **Origin:** Roche asteroid field(Nickel One)
- **Language:** Verpine
