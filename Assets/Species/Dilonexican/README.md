# Dilonexican

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dilonexican](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dilonexican)

The Dilonexicans were the inhabitants of Dilonexa XXIII, an agriworld, and the only habitable planet in the Dilonexa system.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dilonexican
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Dilonexa XXIII
- **Diet:** Allergic to foods with significant amounts of metal
