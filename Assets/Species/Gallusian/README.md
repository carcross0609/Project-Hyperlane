# Gallusian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gallusian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gallusian)

Gallusians were a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. Luleo Primoc, who was a chart-topping singer who also performed on the First Light, was a member of this species. Bosnok was a Gallusian with a knack for numbers; the Imperial Governor Thorden offered him a job managing records of the Empire's shady dealings on the Outer Rim moon Toshara.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gallusian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Origin:** Gallus system
