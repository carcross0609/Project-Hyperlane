# Wayuning

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wayuning](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wayuning)

Wayunings were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. Topi Topi was a diminutive member of the species from Ardennia who by 34 ABY had a bounty placed on their head for stealing a Kowakian monkey-lizard from the Creature Stall in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu. Topi Topi later attended the Festival of the Ancestors on the planet Pasaana in 35 ABY.
