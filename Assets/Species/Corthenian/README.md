# Corthenian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corthenian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corthenian)

Corthenians were a sentient alien species from the Corthenia system in the Mid Rim. In 21 BBY, a Corthenian attended a hive ball with a Human and a Wookiee that was being held in honor of a pair of Galactic Republic diplomats, Barrister Doolb Snoil and Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Corthenian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Corthenia system
