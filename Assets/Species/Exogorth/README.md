# Exogorth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Exogorth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exogorth)

Exogorths, also known as space slugs, were members of a silicon-based sentient species that resided in the hollows of asteroids, who prided themselves on maintaining a pleasant ecosystem within their bodies. At least one such being, named Sy-O, lived in the Hoth asteroid belt.
