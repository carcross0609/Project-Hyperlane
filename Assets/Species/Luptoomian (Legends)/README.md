# Luptoomian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Luptoomian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luptoomian/Legends)

Luptoomians were Humans that lived on the planet Luptoom. The Luptoomians were known for wearing clothing that was fashioned to resemble plants.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Luptoomian
- **Class:** Human
- **Origin:** Luptoom
