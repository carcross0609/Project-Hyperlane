# Yahk-Tosh

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yahk-Tosh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yahk-Tosh)

The Yahk-Tosh were a slug-like sentient species from the planet Xolu in Hutt Space and were believed to be distantly related to the Hutts. Their bodies were slug like in appearance, with four thick arms, which they used for moving around, and their scaled skin had a dirty green color. Four thin antennae sprouted from the tops of their heads and their pointed snouts were filled with rows of blocky teeth. Like the Hutts, the Yahk-Tosh were also immune to Force mind tricks.
