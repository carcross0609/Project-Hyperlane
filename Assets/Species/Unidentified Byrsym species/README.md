# Unidentified Byrsym species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Byrsym species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Byrsym_species)

This sentient species were native to Byrsym. Their homeworld was close to its system's sun, making it very hot during the day. During the night, when it was cooler, they became more active.
