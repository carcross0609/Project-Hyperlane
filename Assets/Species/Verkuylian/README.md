# Verkuylian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Verkuylian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verkuylian)

Verkuylians were the native Human population who lived on the planet Verkuyl. Originally indentured workers to Verkuylian BactaCo, the Verkuylians were freed from their servitude when the Galactic Empire nationalized the company.
