# Mouse

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Mouse (sentient species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mouse_%28sentient_species%29)

The mice (singular form, mouse) were a species of diminutive, rodent-like sentient beings with large ears who hailed from the world of Hyperion. The renowned Jedi Master named Mickey Mouse, who fought during the Clone Wars and survived the Great Jedi Purge, was one of them.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mouse
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** About 0.7 meters
- **Skincolor:** Fair
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Four-fingered hands, large round ears
- **Origin:** Hyperion
