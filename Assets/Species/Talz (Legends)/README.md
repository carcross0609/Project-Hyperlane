# Talz

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Talz/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talz/Legends)

The Talz were a large furry species with two distinct sets of eyes: one for day vision, and one for night vision. Their homeworld was Alzoc III, a frozen world with much mineral wealth. They also established a colony on the world of Orto Plutonia. Talz were rarely seen in the galaxy away from their homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Talz
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 2 - 2.5 meters
- **Mass:** 90 kilograms
- **Haircolor:** White to gray, pink
- **Eyecolor:** Black, dark red, dark blue
- **Distinctions:** Two pairs of eyes, thick light-colored hair
- **Lifespan:** Up to 52 standard years
- **Origin:** Alzoc III
- **Language:** Talz
