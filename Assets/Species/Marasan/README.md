# Marasan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Marasan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marasan)

The Marasans were sentients indigenous to Marasai, a planet deep within the Marasa Nebula. They were notable for their extensive use of cyborg implants.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Marasan
- **Height:** 1.4 - 2.3 meters
- **Distinctions:** Cybernetic implants
- **Origin:** Marasai
