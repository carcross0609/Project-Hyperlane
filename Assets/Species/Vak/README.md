# Vak

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vak)

The Vaks were a sentient species that hailed from the planet Primea in the Unknown Regions. Their government was known as the Vak Combine. They had pale-amber skin, stubby black hair, and dull brown eyes. Neighboring powers often claimed the Combine was unable to make decisions. The majority of the population wore hooded cloaks to protect themselves from both sunlight and rain. One Vak citizen claimed that their species never made friendships with others, though General Yiv the Benevolent sought to bring them into the Nikardun Destiny.
