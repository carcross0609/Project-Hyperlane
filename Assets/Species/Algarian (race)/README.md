# Algarian (race)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Algarian (race)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Algarian_%28race%29)

The Algarians were the native Humans that lived on the planet Algara II. Their society was divided into strict social classes, with upper classes receiving better treatment and services. Marriage between classes was forbidden, and all Algarians and the native Xan were required to wear badges identifying their rank. The Xan were not allowed to ascend past the level of Talent.
