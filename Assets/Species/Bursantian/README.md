# Bursantian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bursantian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bursantian)

Bursantians were a bipedal species native to the planet Bursant. Bursantians were covered in short black and purple fur, had two large red eyes, two arms that ended in three clawed fingers, and two legs with four clawed toes.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bursantian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Black and purple
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Origin:** Bursant
