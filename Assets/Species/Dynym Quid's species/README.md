# Dynym Quid's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dynym Quid's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dynym_Quid%27s_species)

A sentient species of cephalopod-like humanoids lived in the galaxy. In 132 BBY, a member of this species was present at the Jedi Temple during a conversation between Jedi Masters Sol and Vernestra Rwoh. During the Great Jedi Purge, a member of this species was among the refugees on Jabiim who were given passage to Tessen aboard Kawlan Roken's Jabiim freight transport. Another member was Dynym Quid, who spent time at Canto Casino in 34 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dynym Quid's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray and Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
