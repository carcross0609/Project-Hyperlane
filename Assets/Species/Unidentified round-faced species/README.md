# Unidentified round-faced species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified round-faced species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_round-faced_species)

A humanoid sentient species had yellow skin and black eyes. The species had round faces, large chins, and a lack of visible nostrils. One female member of the species was active by 34 ABY, often frequenting the establishment Vranki's Hotel and Casino as a patron. That year, the elderly woman played a game at one of the casino's brightly lit machines, laughing in delight when she won.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified round-faced species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**

  - Large chin
  - Lack of visible nostrils
