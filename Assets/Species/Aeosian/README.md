# Aeosian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aeosian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aeosian)

Aeosians were a sentient species native to the Outer Rim planet Aeos Prime. One tribe of Aeosians were ruled by a queen. During the First Order-Resistance War, representatives of the Colossus including Kazuda Xiono and Griff Halloran met with their ruler.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Aeosian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Blue or Green
- **Eyecolor:** Purple
- **Origin:** Aeos Prime
