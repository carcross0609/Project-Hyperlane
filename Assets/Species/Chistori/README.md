# Chistori

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chistori](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chistori)

The Chistori were a saurian sentient species. They were known to be fierce warriors whose independence had kept them secret from the archives of the Jedi.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Chistori
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Height:** 1.9 meters
- **Skincolor:** Blue and purple on the back, brown and tan on the abdomen
