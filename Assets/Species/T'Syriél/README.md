# T'Syriél

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T'Syriél](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T%27Syri%C3%A9l)

The T'Syriél were tall, muscular humanoids native to the planet Shiva IV. United into the Twelve Tribes of T'Syriél, the T'Syriél were ancient rivals of the Human Calian Confederacy, with which they warred for generations en route to building a strong and respected warrior tradition. In 2 ABY, those battles were brought to an end with the signing of the Concordat of Peace, although many disaffected T'Syriél were used as troops by Imperial General Bentilais san Sk'ar's attempt to conquer the world.
