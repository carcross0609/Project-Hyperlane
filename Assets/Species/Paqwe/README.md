# Paqwe

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Paqwe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paqwe)

The Paqwe were aliens native to the planet Paqwepor Major. They loved good bargains, tall tales, and had big ambitions. Most hated politics and any law that could limit their expressive freedom, basically any law.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Paqwe
- **Skincolor:** Yellow-green
- **Origin:** Paqwepor Major
