# Shozer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shozer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shozer)

Shozers were a sentient species with pale-colored skin and black eyes. The Resistance technician Goss Toowers was a member of the species. Krel Noviip battled against the crumbling Galactic Empire during the Uprising in the Anoat sector, shortly after the second Death Star was destroyed.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Shozer
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Skincolor:** Pale orange
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Round faces
  - Three-fingered hands
