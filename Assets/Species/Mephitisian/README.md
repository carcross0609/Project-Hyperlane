# Mephitisian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mephitisian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mephitisian)

The Mephitisians were a four-armed sentient species from the planet Mephitis. Their homeworld had a noxious atmosphere, and as a consequence, the species had evolved adaptations to limit exposure to these gases, such as lids over their eyes and the lack of a nose.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mephitisian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Purple-tinged
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four arms
  - Lack of a nose
- **Origin:** Mephitis
