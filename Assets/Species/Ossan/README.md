# Ossan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ossan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossan)

The Ossan were a mammalian species used—and sold—as slaves by the Ancient Houses of the Senex sector. Their homeworld was Ossel II, and they had sizable populations on Asmeru and Karfeddion, the latter being used by House Vandron as an Ossan breeding planet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ossan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.4–1.6 meters
- **Distinctions:** Strong, long toothy snouts, single nostril, floppy ears
- **Origin:** Ossel II
