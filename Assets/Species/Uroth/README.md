# Uroth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uroth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uroth)

The Uroths were a species of large, sentient bipedal cephalopods. They inhabited the planet of Wizar II, where they followed a primitive way of life in isolation from much of the galaxy at large. Their native religious beliefs incorporated several deities and a desire to enter the afterlife without fear of death.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Uroth
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Cephalopod
- **Height:** Large
- **Distinctions:** Bipedal
- **Origin:** Wizar II
