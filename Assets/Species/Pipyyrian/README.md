# Pipyyrian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pipyyrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pipyyrian)

Pipyyrians were a sentient species native to the planet Pipyyr.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Pipyyrian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** Shorter than a Wookiee
- **Haircolor:** Shades of black, white and grey
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long limbs
  - Fur
  - Fangs
- **Origin:** Pipyyr
