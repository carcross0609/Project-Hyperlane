# Korun

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Korun/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korun/Legends)

The Korunnai (singular, Korun) were a race of humans who called the planet of Haruun Kal home. They were a nomadic "tribe" of uplanders (which was the rough translation of "Korunnai" into Basic). The Korunnai were born into clans or extended families called Ghôsh. For example, Mace Windu and Kar Vastor were both part of the same group, and thus Dôshalo, clan-brothers, to each other. They fought against the Balawai (downfolk) in the Summertime War and were insultingly called Kornos by these enemies.
