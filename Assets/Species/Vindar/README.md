# Vindar

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Vindar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vindar)

The Vindar were a species of white insectoids with four arms and a tough [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carapace carapace]. They possessed large black beady eyes, and they were able to fire powerful purple energy blasts from their hand. Long after the death of Luke Skywalker, they oppressed a species they referred to derogatorily as the Squidge. The Vindar considered all things not of themselves to be inconsequential, and worked to destroy the culture and heritage of those they oppressed.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vindar
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insect
- **Skincolor:** White
- **Distinctions:** Insectoid carapace, four arms
