# Ottethan (species)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ottethan (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ottethan_%28species%29)

The Ottethans were a sentient species from the Outer Rim. Their empire contained twelve systems, of which the Ottethan system was the capital, ruled by a warlord around 31 BBY.
