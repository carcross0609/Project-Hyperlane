# Khormai

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Khormai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khormai)

Khormai were a sentient species from the Khorm system. One Khormai bothered Asajj Ventress in the Belsallian Cantina on Serenno, and the Sith apprentice knocked him unconscious with a tray from a serving droid.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
