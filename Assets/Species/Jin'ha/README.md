# Jin'ha

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jin'ha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jin%27ha)

The Jin'ha were a enigmatical species famous for their work with the lightsaber-resistant cortosis ore. The Jin'ha also sold some of their cortosis ore to the Trade Federation, which utilized it for their experimental assassin droids. The common weapons the Jin'ha used were pikes, maces and blasters.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jin'ha
- **Origin:** Obredaan
