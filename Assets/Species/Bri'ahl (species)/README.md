# Bri'ahl

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bri'ahl (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bri%27ahl_%28species%29)

The Bri'ahls were humanoids indigenous to the forested planet of Bri'ahl. They had pink skin, large eyes that lacked colored irises, squarish noses, large sharp teeth, funnel-shaped ears, and three fingers and a thumb on each hand.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Bri'ahl
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Distinctions:** Large eyes, squarish noses, sharp teeth
- **Origin:** Bri'ahl
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
