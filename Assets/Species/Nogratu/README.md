# Nogratu

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nogratu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nogratu)

The Nogratu was a sentient species with a humanoid shape and head tentacles. The Fourth Sister, who was a Force-sensitive female member of the species and was an Inquisitor during the Imperial Era, was from the Ehosiq sector.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nogratu
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Yellow
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Head tentacles
