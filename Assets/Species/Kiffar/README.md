# Kiffar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kiffar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kiffar)

The Kiffar were a near-human species from the planets Kiffu and Kiffex. Although they seemed human in appearance, Kiffar were distinguished by their scarlet blood and facial tattoos, which indicated their clan affiliation on their species' homeworlds. Jedi Master Quinlan Vos, who took part in the Clone Wars, was a male Jedi Master Kiffar. Vildar Mac was also a Jedi Knight during The High Republic. A small number of the species, including Vos, possessed the gift of psychometry, the ability to read the memories of inanimate objects through touch.
