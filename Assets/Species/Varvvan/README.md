# Varvvan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Varvvan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Varvvan)

Varvvans were large sentient creatures from the Varvva system. The species had a reputation for bravery and fearlessness. Their eyeballs could be extended out of their sockets, and in states of extreme agitation or anger, the eyeballs oozed.
