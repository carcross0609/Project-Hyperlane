# Gharal

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gharal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gharal)

Gharal were a sentient species of long-lived, scaled beings. Their life spans meant that Gharal were considered adolescents even into their thirties. The species sported thick neck ridges and light gray skin. During the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and Confederacy of Independent Systems, a family of Gharal lived on the planet Langston, where they fell victim to an attack from the Separatists in 22 BBY. A Republic relief mission not long after saw Jedi Initiate Mill Alibeth deliver aid to the family, including its eldest surviving member, a thirty-year old girl named Rokura. These Gharal spoke in a broken form of Galactic Basic Standard.
