# Baragwin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Baragwin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baragwin)

The Baragwin were a large sentient species with pink and gray skin and yellow eyes. The thug Hermi Odle was a Baragwin. As a colonial species, they had migrated and adapted to dominate many worlds.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Baragwin
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Brown
  - Bluish-gray and pink
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Orange
  - Yellow
- **Origin:** Old Barag system
