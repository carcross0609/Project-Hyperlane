# Qubey

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Qubey](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qubey)

The Qubey were a sentient species of alien native to the moon of Caliban. They were short creatures, and in terms of technology, were not very advanced. Their homeworld was disturbed during the Clone Wars, due to an invasion by Separatist forces led by General Grievous.
