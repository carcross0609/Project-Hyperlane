# Epicanthix

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Epicanthix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epicanthix)

The Epicanthix were a warrior species of Near-Humans native to the Outer Rim world of Panatha, within the Pacanth Reach.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Epicanthix
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.8-2.5 meters
- **Skincolor:** Pale
- **Haircolor:** Brown-Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Narrow eyes
  - Near ubiquitous black hair
  - Slightly longer faces
  - Powerful mental shielding against The Force
- **Lifespan:** Up to 130 standard years
- **Origin:** Panatha
- **Language:** Epicant
