# Unidentified gray-skinned pointy-eared species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified gray-skinned pointy-eared species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_gray-skinned_pointy-eared_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. The bulky humanoid species was distinguished by their gray and pointed ears. One member of this species was present on the planet Varkana in 34 ABY, commonly seen alongside another human, the two fleeing together when the First Order arrived.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified gray-skinned pointy-eared species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Pointed ears
