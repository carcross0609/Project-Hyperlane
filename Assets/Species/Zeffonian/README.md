# Zeffonian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zeffonian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeffonian)

The Zeffonians, or Zeffo for short, were an ancient sentient species native to the planet Zeffo. Many Zeffonians could wield the Force, which they referred to as the "Life Wind," and those who did became known as sages.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Zeffonian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Eyecolor:** Green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Specific shape of the frontal lobe of the skull and absence of a nasal septum and nostrils
  - High Force-sensitivity
- **Origin:** Zeffo
