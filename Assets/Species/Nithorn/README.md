# Nithorn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nithorn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nithorn)

Nithorns were an avian sentient species that existed in the galaxy with leathery orange-and-green skin, black eyes and beaked faces. Members of the species were both present at the Lodge on Vandor and the First Light. One was also seen shopping on Ghorman in 3 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nithorn
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Green
  - Orange
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Beaked faces
  - Three-fingered hands
  - Feathered heads and hands
