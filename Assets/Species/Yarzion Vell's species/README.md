# Yarzion Vell's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yarzion Vell's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yarzion_Vell%27s_species)

The Jedi Yarzion Vell was a member of a sentient species which could have red or green skin, red eyes, black hair, and pointed ears. When Vell returned to his home planet, he had a skin-scribe of the same species tattoo him with runes from his ancestor's language that spelled the name of his greatest teacher—his Jedi Master, Lulad—in accordance with the planet's tradition.
