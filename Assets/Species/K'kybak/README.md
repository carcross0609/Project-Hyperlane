# K'kybak

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K'kybak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K%27kybak)

The K'kybak were a sentient species who lived millions of years before the Battle of Yavin on the planet Seymarti V, and were responsible for creating the hyperspace nullifier superweapon. Eventually the species became extinct.
