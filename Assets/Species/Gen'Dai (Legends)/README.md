# Gen'Dai

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gen'Dai/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gen%27Dai/Legends)

The Gen'Dai were a seemingly immortal, formless species which originated on an unidentified planet in the galaxy. Devoid of any bones, the Gen'Dai typically wore heavy armor to give themselves a humanoid structure.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gen'Dai
- **Height:** 2.5 meters
- **Mass:** 145 kilograms
- **Skincolor:**
  - Purple
  - Red
- **Eyecolor:** Bright red to dull yellow-orange
- **Distinctions:** Regenerative properties
- **Lifespan:** 4,000-7,000 standard years
- **Origin:** Gen'Dai homeworld
- **Language:** Gen'Dai
