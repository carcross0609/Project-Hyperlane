# Stokhli

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stokhli/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stokhli/Legends)

The Stokhli were a species of nomads from the world of Manress. They were the inventors of the Stokhli spray stick, a nonlethal capture device often employed by Noghri, bounty hunters, and Mara Jade.
