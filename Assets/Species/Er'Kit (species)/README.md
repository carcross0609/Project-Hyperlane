# Er'Kit

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Er'Kit (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Er%27Kit_%28species%29)

The Er'Kit were a species of humanoids with large, elongated craniums and long, skinny legs. One Er'Kit was Ody Mandrell, a famous podracer who took part in the Boonta Eve Classic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Er'Kit
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.63 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Shades of blue
  - Red
  - Purple
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Elongated skulls, long skinny legs
- **Origin:** Er'Kit
