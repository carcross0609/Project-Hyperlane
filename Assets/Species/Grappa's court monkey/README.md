# Grappa's court monkey

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grappa's court monkey](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grappa%27s_court_monkey)

Grappa the Hutt had a court monkey that sat with him at his palace on Genon. It often got pushed around a lot by Grappa, for he had a wild temper.
