# Unidentified gray wide-set-eyes species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified gray wide-set-eyes species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_gray_wide-set-eyes_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their gray skin and tall heads with wide-set eyes, some members of the species also sporting a series of small lobes extending from the back of their head, possibly consisting of hair or feathers. In 4 ABY, at least three members of this species, two adults and one child, boarded a transport headed for the planet Phorliss alongside the Human Mara Jade.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified gray wide-set-eyes species
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Haircolor:** Light blue
- **Eyecolor:** Black
