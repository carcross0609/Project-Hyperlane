# Salenga

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Salenga](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salenga)

The Salenga were a sentient species which existed in the galaxy. They had pink skin, yellow eyes with vertical eyelids and triangular pupils, and hands with one thumb, one finger and a single wide appendage where humans and species with similar hand shapes had more fingers. Salenga also had a flap of skin hanging below the back of their head which resembled long hair. Ione Marcy, a female Salenga, was a thief on Coruscant during the Clone Wars.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Salenga
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**

  - Three fingers with a wide hand appendage
  - Head flaps
