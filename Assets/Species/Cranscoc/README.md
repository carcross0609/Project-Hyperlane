# Cranscoc

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cranscoc](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cranscoc)

The Cranscoc were an insectoid species from the planet Cartao who controlled Spaarti Creations. They reproduced slowly, and communicated by changing colors.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cranscoc
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Races:** Twiller
- **Distinctions:**
  - Slow reproduction
  - Communication by changing colors
- **Origin:** Cartao
