# Kwa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kwa/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kwa/Legends)

The Kwa were an ancient species native to the Outer Rim world of Dathomir that built the Infinity Gates and Star Temples during the Pre-Republic era. The Infinity Gates were permanent structures capable of enabling instantaneous travel between interstellar locations and were housed within the Star Temples.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kwa
- **Subspecies:** Kwi
- **Height:** 3 meters
- **Skincolor:** Cobalt-blue
- **Distinctions:** Telepathic abilities
- **Origin:** Dathomir
