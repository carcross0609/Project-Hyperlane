# Brayan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Brayan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brayan)

Brayans were a sentient species from the planet Bray. During the Clone Wars, a village on Bray was terrorized by Lord Ravna, leading to the local Brayan population to ask the Jedi Council for help. The Jedi Masters Obi-Wan Kenobi and Adi Gallia were then sent to the planet, and the Jedi successfully defeated both Ravna and Count Dooku, freeing the Brayans.

## Biology & Culture
