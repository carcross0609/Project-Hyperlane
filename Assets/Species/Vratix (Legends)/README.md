# Vratix

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vratix/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vratix/Legends)

The Vratix, also known as Thyferrans, were native to the planet of Thyferra. They were the species that invented bacta. They were led by a Canir, their elected ruler, and utilized hermaphroditic reproduction.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vratix
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.8–2.6 meters
- **Skincolor:** Greenish-gray
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Insectoid
  - Hermaphroditic reproduction
  - Two pairs of arms
- **Origin:** Thyferra
- **Language:** Vratix
