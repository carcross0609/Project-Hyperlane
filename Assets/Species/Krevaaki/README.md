# Krevaaki

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krevaaki](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krevaaki)

The Krevaaki were a race of sentients native to Krevas. The Krevaaki people possessed an exoskeleton. The face of a Krevaaki was composed of a series of shifting, chitinous plates with a limited range of expression. During face-to-face communication, it was difficult for anyone who was not a Krevaaki to read their facial expressions.
