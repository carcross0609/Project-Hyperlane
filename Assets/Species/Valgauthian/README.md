# Valgauthian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Valgauthian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valgauthian)

Valgauthians were the human inhabitants of the planet Valgauth. They had a reputation as berserkers when boarding starships. As such, Valgauthians were regarded as valuable additions to any pirate crew.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Valgauthian
- **Class:** Human
- **Origin:** Valgauth
