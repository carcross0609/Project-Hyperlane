# Ka'hren

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ka'hren](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ka%27hren)

The Ka'hren were sentient humanoids indigenous to V'shar.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ka'hren
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 2.0 - 2.3 meters
- **Skincolor:** Dark red to black
- **Distinctions:** Crested foreheads; long arms; huge hands; fur in forearms and temples; thick flesh
- **Lifespan:** 140 years
- **Origin:** V'shar
