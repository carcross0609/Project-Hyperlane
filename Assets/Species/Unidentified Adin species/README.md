# Unidentified Adin species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Adin species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Adin_species)

This species were a race of thin humanoid aliens with red skin and white facial stripes indigenous to Adin. Other distinctive features were their two-fingered hands and pale pink teeth. Senator Meido was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified Adin species
- **Skincolor:** Red with white facial stripes
- **Distinctions:** Thin build, two-fingered hands, pale pink teeth
- **Origin:** Adin
