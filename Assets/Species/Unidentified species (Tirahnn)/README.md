# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (Tirahnn)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28Tirahnn%29)

A species of blue-gray-skinned humanoids with pointed ears. One such being on Tirahnn, recognized Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa and reported them to Kharys.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Skincolor:** Blue-gray
- **Distinctions:** Pointed ears
