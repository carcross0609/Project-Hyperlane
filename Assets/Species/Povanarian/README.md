# Povanarian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Povanarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Povanarian)

Povanarians were a frog-like sentient species native to the planet Povanaria. They were one of the member species of the New Republic in 19 ABY, serving alongside Bagmims and Humans in the New Republic Defense Fleet.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Povanarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Distinctions:** Frog-like characterisitics
- **Origin:** Povanaria
