# Kaminoan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kaminoan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaminoan/Legends)

Kaminoans were a tall, thin, amphibious species with pale skin and long necks from the isolated, extra-galactic oceanic planet of Kamino, located in Wild Space. They lived in large, stilted cities, supported by massive pillars that were drilled deep into the seabed below, with most of the inhabited structures rising above the ocean’s surface—such as Tipoca City. Many important or high- ranking Kaminoans wore a large, pale collar around their necks. The Kaminoans are best known for their genetic and bioengineering abilities, primarily as cloners, as they were the specialists behind the production of the Grand Army of the Republic, which fought against the Separatist Droid Army, during the Clone Wars (a galaxy-wide conflict named after the clone troopers—also known simply as clones—the Kaminoans designed specifically for the Galactic Republic).
