# Noghri

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Noghri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noghri)

The Noghri were a sentient species native to Honoghr. They had gray skin, a row of horns on their head and ran on all fours. Noghri were known for their combat skills and keen sense of smell.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Noghri
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Green
  - Silver
- **Distinctions:** Keen sense of smell
- **Origin:** Honoghr
- **Language:** Noghri language
