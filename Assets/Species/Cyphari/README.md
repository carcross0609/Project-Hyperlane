# Cyphari

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cyphari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyphari)

The Cyphari were a sentient species native to the planet Cyphar. Cyphari were described as resembling large stick insects with snouts similar to that of a Rodian. They were covered in neat rows of short red fur. One group of Cyphari was known as the Afe clan. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, the Afe Clan was led by a Cyphari named Chief Joko.
