# Kalzerian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kalzerian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalzerian)

The Kalzerians were near-Humans from the planet Kalzeron with gray, leather-like skin. They also had black teeth, and yellow eyes. Developing from a feudal, tribal people with a defining sense of battlefield honor, the Kalzerians had not yet reached the atomic era by the time of the Galactic Civil War.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kalzerian
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Black teeth, yellow eyes.
- **Origin:** Kalzeron
