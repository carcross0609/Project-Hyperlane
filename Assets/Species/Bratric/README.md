# Bratric

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bratric](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bratric)

The Bratric were a species mentioned by Jod Na Nawood when introducing the younglings Wim, Neel, KB, and Fern while they were disguised as the "Wise Elders" in order to gain entry to the Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa. According to Na Nawood the Bratric physiology did not allow them to speak or understand Galactic Basic. In order to reinforce this, the group spoke gibberish to each other while around the Lanupa hotelier.
