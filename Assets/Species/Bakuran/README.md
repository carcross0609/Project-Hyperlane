# Bakuran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bakuran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bakuran)

A Bakuran was a Human from the planet Bakura. Due to their relative isolation, and decades of independence from the Galactic Republic, they developed a culture distinct from the rest of the galaxy before their conquest by the Galactic Empire.
