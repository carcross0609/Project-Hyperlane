# Jumerian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jumerian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jumerian)

The Jumerians were a sentient species native to the Jumeria system of the Core Worlds. Some Jumerians later became members of the New Republic. In 8 ABY, a number of Jumerians presented Princess Leia Organa Solo with a singing lamp as a gift for her wedding. Although she disliked the item, she kept it in the Coruscanti apartment she shared with her husband, Han Solo.
