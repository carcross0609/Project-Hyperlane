# Eellayin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eellayin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eellayin)

The Eellayin were a civilization who resided on Polis Massa before their extinction. By the end of the Clone Wars, some Kallidahin archaeologists and exobiologists believed the Eellayin were their species' ancestor, so they began excavating Polis Massa in an attempt to recover Eellayin tissues and use Kaminoan cloning technology to recreate life.
