# Unidentified Cianap species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Cianap species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Cianap_species)

A sentient species lived on Cianap. They had blue skin and hair of various colors. Mira was a member of this species. They were a much more advanced society, but the Ender, destroyed their society, forcing them to live underground.
