# Altiri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Altiri/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Altiri/Legends)

The Altiri were a Near-Human species that evolved on the planet Altiria/Anarris. Their skin tones ranged from light blue to deep indigo, and their hair color ranged from pure white to purple to black.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Altiri
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Skincolor:** Light blue to deep indigo
- **Haircolor:** Pure white, purple or black
- **Origin:** Altiria/Anarris
- **Language:** Altirish
