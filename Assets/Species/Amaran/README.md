# Amaran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Amaran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amaran)

Amarans were a fox-like species that existed in the galaxy. During the Imperial Era, the Amaran trader Tykyl was present on the planet Coruscant where she helped the protocol droid TC-99. Another Amaran was also present in Fortress Vader on the planet Mustafar, with a drone having been implanted into her body.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Amaran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Fox-like
- **Height:** Small
- **Haircolor:** Red-orange
