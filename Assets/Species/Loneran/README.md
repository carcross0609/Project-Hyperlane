# Loneran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Loneran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loneran)

Lonerans were a sentient species from the Expansion Region planet Lonera that had long, silky fur the color of gold and four thin, extended limbs that allowed them to walk on two feet or all fours with equal ease. Varish Vicly, a representative in the Galactic Senate of the New Republic, was a Loneran female.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Loneran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Golden
- **Distinctions:** Four limbs, two forms of locomotion
- **Origin:** Lonera
