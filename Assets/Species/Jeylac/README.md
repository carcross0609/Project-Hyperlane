# Jeylac

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jeylac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jeylac)

The Jeylac were a sentient species. During the Clone Wars, the Separatist sympathizer Aviso carried a Jeylac slave aboard his starship, and he attempted to use XNA-580—an experimental device that could alter the brainwaves of some species—to influence the slave's mind.
