# Melida

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Melida/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Melida/Legends)

The Melida were a faction of people on the planet Melida/Daan. Governed by the Melida Council, they were at war with the Daan until the Young ended the Melida/Daan Civil War.
