# Delrakkin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Delrakkin (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delrakkin_%28species%29)

The Delrakkin were a species of sentient predatory reptiles from the Delrakkin system. They were covered in scales which could be dun in color, and had claws. Senator Ta'am Khlaides, a Delrakkin woman, served in the the Imperial Senate in the early days of the Galactic Empire. They originated from the Delrakkin system.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Delrakkin
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Distinctions:** Scales, claws
- **Origin:** Delrakkin system
