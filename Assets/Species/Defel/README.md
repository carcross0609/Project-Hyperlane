# Defel

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Defel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defel)

Defels were a sentient species whose members had the ability to become almost invisible by bending light around themselves. Shortly before the Battle of Yavin, a Defel named Arleil Schous was a patron of Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina when Luke Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi arrived in search of transport to Alderaan. Glahst Ombra was a Defel mercenary who worked with Chelli Aphra until she was killed and skinned by Magna Tolvan.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Defel
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Black
  - Red
- **Distinctions:** Ability to absorb visible light
