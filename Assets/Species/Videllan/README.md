# Videllan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Videllan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Videllan)

The Videllans were a gold-skinned sentient species native to the Videll system. Kearn-sa'Davit was a Videllan who was a member of the Rebel Alliance.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Videllan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gold
- **Origin:** Videll system
