# Unidentified one-eye-stalk species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified one-eye-stalk species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_one-eye-stalk_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by a singular eyestalk perched atop their head, with two elongated, pointed ears, and a large mouth. Some time prior to 22 BBY, at least one member of this species was a member of the Jedi Order, their busts displayed within the Temple Archives.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified one-eye-stalk species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Singular eyestalk
  - Pointed ears
