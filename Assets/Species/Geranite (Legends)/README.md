# Geranite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Geranite/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geranite/Legends)

Geranites, also known simply as the Geran, were a species of Near-Humans that inhabited the planet of Geran in the Mneon system. Their features usually consisted of pale shades of purple, blue, green, and pink skin tones, white eyes, and three-digit hands. To survive offworld, they needed to carry special containers filled with hydron-three, and often wore specialized suits to sustain their needs. Their religion was based on a belief in the Sky Seraphs. Doallyn was one of the best known members of the species.
