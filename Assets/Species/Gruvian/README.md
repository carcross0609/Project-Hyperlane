# Gruvian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gruvian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gruvian)

The Gruvians were the native sentients of the planet Gruvia in the Expansion Region.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gruvian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:**
  - Eight eyestalks
  - Four arms with three-fingered hands
  - Two mouths
  - No visible legs
- **Origin:** Gruvia
