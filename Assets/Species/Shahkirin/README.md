# Shahkirin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shahkirin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shahkirin)

The Shahkirin were a sentient species from the Inner Rim Territories' Shahkir system. One Shahkirin was a member of the Rebel Alliance in 2 BBY, being present at the Alliance base on the moon Yavin 4 that year. Another member of the species, Yipsit, grew up on the planet Bestine IV and served as a Resistance mechanic at its base on the moon Ajan Kloss by 35 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Shahkirin
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Haircolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:** Compressible cartilaginous skeleton
- **Origin:** Shahkir system
