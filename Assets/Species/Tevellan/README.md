# Tevellan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tevellan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tevellan)

The Tevellans were a sentient species from the planet Tevel. The Tevellans were covered in orange fur with white or dark brown stripes, long tails, and membranes between their arms and torsos which allowed them to glide. After the First Order seized Tevel to take control of a bacta production plant, several Tevellans contacted the Resistance, requesting help. Finn and Poe Dameron of the Resistance managed to liberate the production plant, saving the Tevellans.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tevellan
- **Designation:** Sentient
