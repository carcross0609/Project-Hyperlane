# Caskadag

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Caskadag](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caskadag)

Caskadags were a sentient species with two stomachs that hailed from a desert world. Anglang Lehet, who worked as an assassin, was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Caskadag
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Gray
  - Tan
- **Distinctions:** Two stomachs
- **Origin:** Caskadag homeworld
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
