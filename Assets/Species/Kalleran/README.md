# Kalleran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kalleran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalleran)

Kallerans were a tall sentient species native to the planet of Kaller.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kalleran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Height:** +2 meter
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Haircolor:** Hairless
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Green
  -   Distinct black markings
- **Distinctions:**
  - Three fingers
  - Three toes
  - Noseless
  - Olfactory antennae
- **Origin:** Kaller
- **Language:**
  - Kall
  - Galactic Basic Standard
