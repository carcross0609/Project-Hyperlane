# Shikitari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shikitari (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shikitari_%28species%29)

The Shikitari were a rare, dim-witted insectoid species native to the planet Shikitari in the Unknown Regions. They possessed a multi-stage life cycle, and during the final years of the Old Republic, very few individuals were aware of the Shikitari, with fewer still considered experts on their unique physiology.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Shikitari
- **Designation:** Subsentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Skincolor:** Blue-green
- **Distinctions:** Multiple life cycles
- **Lifespan:** Multi-stage; the pupae form lasting only two standard days
- **Origin:** Shikitari
