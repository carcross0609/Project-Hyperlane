# Kintan strider

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kintan strider/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kintan_strider/Legends)

The Kintan strider was a ferocious creature with incredible self-healing abilities. Extinct on their homeworld of Kintan, they were used as guard beasts by many Hutt gangsters.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kintan strider
- **Designation:** Semi-sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Bizarre features
  - Self-healing abilities
  - Long arms
- **Origin:** Kintan
