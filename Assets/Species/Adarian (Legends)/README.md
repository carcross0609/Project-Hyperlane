# Adarian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adarian/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adarian/Legends)

Adarians were a species of bipedal humanoids from the planet Adari in the Inner Rim of the galaxy; although Adari was not believed to be their original homeworld.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Adarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Mammalian
- **Height:** 1.5—2 meters (1.7 meters on average)
- **Skincolor:**
  - Blue-grey
  - Brown-yellow
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Solid black
  - Metallic-gold
- **Distinctions:** Unusual cranial structures
- **Lifespan:**
  - 65 standard years
  - Child: 1-13 years
  - Young adult: 14-18 years
  - Adult: 19-44 years
  - Middle age: 45-55 years
  - Old: 56-64 years
  - Venerable: 65+ years
- **Origin:** Adari (adopted)
- **Language:** Adarese
