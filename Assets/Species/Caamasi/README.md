# Caamasi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Caamasi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caamasi)

The Caamasi were a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were covered in grey or brown fur, and had two pointed ears and a snout. Like the Alderaanians, they were noted for their pacifism. The species shared its name with the Caamasi cheese pies and the Caamasi light sculptures. The Caamasi Jedi Master Ela Sutan was a member of the Jedi High Council during the High Republic Era.
