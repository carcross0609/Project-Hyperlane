# Unidentified barbed light blue species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified barbed light blue species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_barbed_light_blue_species)

This species was a bipedal humanoid sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their light blue skin, as well as a fin-like growth on top of their head and four pointed barbs extending from their face. In 9 ABY, a member of this species was present at Gor Koresh's Honky Tonk when Koresh attempted to take Din Djarin's Mandalorian armor.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified barbed light blue species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Light blue
- **Distinctions:**
  - Facial barbs
  - Cranial fin
