# Unidentified jawline-horns species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified jawline-horns species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_jawline-horns_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were a bipedal humanoid species distinguished by a series of pointed boney horns protruding from their jawline. In 9 ABY, a member of this species was present at Gor Koresh's Honky Tonk when Koresh attempted to take Din Djarin's Mandalorian armor, attacking the bounty hunter before being taken down.
