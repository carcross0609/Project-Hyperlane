# Nephran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nephran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nephran)

The Nephrans were a sentient crustacean species that were native to the water-rich planet Nepotis. Therm Scissorpunch was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nephran
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Crustacean
- **Skincolor:**
  - Olive green
  - Purple
  - Red
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Long lifespan
- **Origin:** Nepotis
- **Habitat:** Water
- **Language:** Nepotisian
