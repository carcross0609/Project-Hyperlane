# Nazren

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nazren](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nazren)

The Nazren were an alien race native to the planet Nizon in Maldrood sector. They were occupied by the Galactic Empire and enslaved by Trandoshan slavers after the end of the Clone Wars, but an anti-Imperial group helped the local resistance free their world.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nazren
- **Height:** 2.1 meters
- **Skincolor:** Brown to light yellow
- **Distinctions:** Thick and hide-like skin, no nose, prominent brow ridge, visible spine
- **Origin:** Nizon
