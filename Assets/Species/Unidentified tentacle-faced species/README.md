# Unidentified tentacle-faced species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified tentacle-faced species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_tentacle-faced_species)

A sentient species was characterized by tentacle-like growths on its members' faces. A group of these species lived on the planet Balnab after being shipwrecked there. Oked and Loubo were members of this tentacle-faced species. A member of this species lived in Pons Ora on Abafar.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified tentacle-faced species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:**
  - Tentacle-like facial growths
