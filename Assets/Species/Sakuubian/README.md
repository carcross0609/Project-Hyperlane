# Sakuubian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sakuubian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sakuubian)

Sakuubians were the native species of Sakuub. They were humanoids with blue skin, yellow cat-like eyes, distinctive facial ridges, three-fingered hands, and four horns on their forehead. Dular and Pharika were Sakuubians. Nevertheless, it was unsure whether Pharika was a true Sakuubian or was simply wearing a disguise.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sakuubian
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - Facial ridges
  - Three-fingered hands
  - Four horns
- **Origin:** Sakuub
