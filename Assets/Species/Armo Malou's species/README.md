# Armo Malou's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Armo Malou's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armo_Malou%27s_species)

A species of beings was found throughout the galaxy that included among its members Armo Malou, a bartender who worked at the Canto Casino and Racetrack on Cantonica. Another member worked as a supply store clerk on Tatooine during the Imperial Era. Both individuals sported metal bolts protruding from their snouts.
