# Troukree

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Troukree](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Troukree)

The Troukree were a reptilian sentient species, native to their homeworld in the Unknown Regions. Some of them served Thrawn in his fight against Nuso Esva on Poln Major, using their prowess with blades in close-quarters combat. They had a strong belief in an honor system. Several Troukree fought alongside renegade stormtrooper Daric LaRone and his Hand of Judgment as well.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
