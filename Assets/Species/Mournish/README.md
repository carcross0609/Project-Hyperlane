# Mournish

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mournish](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mournish)

Mournish were a species of humanoids found in the galaxy. They had pink skin, pointed ears, and large black eyes. Mournish could be further distinguished by the long tendrils that fell back from the crest of their heads. These tendrils could be tied to cascade along the back of a Mournish.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mournish
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Long head tendrils, pointed ears, and large pupil-less eyes
