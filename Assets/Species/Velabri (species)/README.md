# Velabri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Velabri (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Velabri_%28species%29)

The Velabri were a humanoid race from the planet also known as Velabri. They were generally known as a warlike race with a strict code of honor, despising crime. The Velabri Bloodvow was practiced among the Velabri, similar to the Life debt used by Wookiees and Gungans.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Velabri
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Silver
- **Eyecolor:** White
- **Origin:** Velabri
