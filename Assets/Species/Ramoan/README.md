# Ramoan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ramoan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ramoan)

The Ramoans were a sentient species whose homeworld was ruined by the Yuuzhan Vong. Six years after the Yuuzhan Vong War, they were still being relocated to new worlds. The last stages of their relocation were supervised by Lowbacca, who abandoned the project to join the side of the Killiks.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ramoan
- **Skincolor:** Jade green
- **Distinctions:**
  - Pig-like snouts
  - Bulky
  - Tusks
- **Origin:** Ramoa
- **Language:** Ramoan
