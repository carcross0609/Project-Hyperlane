# Droch

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droch/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droch/Legends)

Drochs were parasitic insects that burrowed under the skin of the host and drank their life energies. Their feeding was the source of Death Seed plague.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Droch
- **Length:** 5 millimeters
- **Distinctions:** Life-energy draining parasites, non-sentient early in lifespan
- **Origin:** Nam Chorios
