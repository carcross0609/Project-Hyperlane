# Mannovan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mannovan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mannovan)

The Mannovans were a race of sardonic gray-furred beings native to the planet Mannova. They were seemingly unruffled by the ecological disaster their homeworld became.

- **Haircolor:** Gray
