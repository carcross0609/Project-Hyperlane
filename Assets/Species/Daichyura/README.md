# Daichyura

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Daichyura](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daichyura)

The Daichyura were a sentient species found in the galaxy during the Galactic War. Humanoid in shape, the species possessed five-fingered hands, small mouths, and had a crest on top of their heads similar to those of Vurk. A Daichyura named Sellexith worked at Star Cluster Casino as a bouncer during a Soovada event.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Daichyura
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Yellow-blue fin on the head
