# Urchiv-ki

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Urchiv-ki](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Urchiv-ki)

The Urchiv-ki were a sentient species from the Unknown Regions planet Urch. The Urchiv-ki's nation was the Tower Dimension, one of several governments under the control of General Yiv and the Nikardun Destiny, the Hegemony of the Nikardun species, in 19 BBY. The Urchiv-ki's government was later released from Nikardun control after Chiss Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo, whose core name was "Thrawn," defeated Yiv with help from the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet. The Urchiv-ki were very proud of their art galleries, which Thrawn wished to visit as he enjoyed studying art. The species also had a treaty with the Chiss, which allowed diplomatic meetings and relations.
