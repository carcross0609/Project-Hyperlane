# Ariwook

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ariwook](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ariwook)

The Ariwook were a sentient mammalian species of brown, hairy humanoids with black eyes. Around 0 BBY, a member of the species, Rff Huff, visited Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina, located on the planet Tatooine, and engaged in a shootout with the Ganimed bounty hunter Purong.
