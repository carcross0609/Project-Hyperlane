# Platypus

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Platypus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Platypus)

A platypus was a sentient species with teal fur, a duck-like bill, a flat, orange tail, and webbed feet. It communicated by making a repetitive clicking noise with its mouth. Their eyes could be brown. The Alliance to Restore the Republic agent Perry was a member of the species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Platypus
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Teal
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:**
  - A duck-like bill
  - A flat, orange tail
  - Webbed feet
