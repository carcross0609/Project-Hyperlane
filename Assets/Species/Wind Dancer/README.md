# Wind Dancer

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wind Dancer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Dancer)

The Wind Dancers were a sentient species native to the planet Galloa II, a planet in the D'Astan sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Members of the species were noted for their innate grace; accordingly, in 9 ABY, the Human historian Voren Na'al compared the Human smuggler Mara Jade to a Wind Dancer in his personal journal. Their homeworld was integrated into the greater galaxy at some point between 3956 and 1000 BBY. During the Clone Wars of 22–19 BBY, it fell within territory controlled by the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Between 4 and 6 ABY, after the fall of the Galactic Empire, the planet was within the empire of the Imperial warlord Zsinj.
