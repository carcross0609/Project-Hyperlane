# Unidentified lupine species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified lupine species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_lupine_species)

A lupine species of bipedal sentients lived on a planet in the Outer Rim Territories, near the border of Galactic Republic space. They had lupine lower legs, with high ankle joints and three-clawed feet. The species had pointed ears and gray skin, with red eyes. They had sharp teeth and typically wore hoods.
