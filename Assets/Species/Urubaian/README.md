# Urubaian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Urubaian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Urubaian)

The Urubaians were a sentient species that could be found on Urubai. They were a humanoid species distinguished by their teal and pink skin, as well as black hair and pointed ears.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Urubaian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Teal and pink
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Origin:** Urubai
