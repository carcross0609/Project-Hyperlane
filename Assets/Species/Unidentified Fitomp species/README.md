# Unidentified Fitomp species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Fitomp species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Fitomp_species)

A sentient species was native to the Unknown Regions planet Fitomp. When a tribe of the Rakata species settled the world after the collapse of the Rakatan Infinite Empire around 25,200 BBY, the newcomers engaged in warfare against Fitomp's native sentients.
