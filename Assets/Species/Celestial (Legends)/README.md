# Celestial

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Celestial/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial/Legends)

The Celestials were ancient entities in the Force, the energy field that existed in all things, whose true nature could not be understood by the mortals of the universe. The Force was influenced under the Celestials' dominion, and three of them were known to have manifested into physical beings known as the Ones, Force wielders who were made up of the Father, the Daughter, and the Son.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Celestial
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Immortal entities in the Force impossible to be understood by mortals
  - Can take any physical form
- **Origin:** Abeloth's planet (the Ones)
