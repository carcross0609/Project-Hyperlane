# Hisseenian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hisseenian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hisseenian)

The Hisseenians were a group of humans who inhabited the planet Hisseen. In 22 BBY, when the planet was attacked by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars, the Galactic Republic intervened to protect the Hisseenians.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hisseenian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Human
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Origin:** Hisseen
