# Muur

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Muur](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Muur)

The Muurs were a sentient species hailing from the Inner Rim's Muuria system. Their body featured numerous tentacles, which could be strong enough to allow to fling a Human across a room. Muurs could speak in a hissing and spitting manner and could also laugh.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Muur
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Numerous tentacles
- **Origin:** Muuria system
