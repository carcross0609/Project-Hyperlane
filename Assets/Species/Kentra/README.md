# Kentra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kentra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kentra)

The Kentra were a winged feline sentient species indigenous to Orellon II. They were simple and friendly with a sense of humor and honesty. Even though they were supple in terms of truth, they placed much emphasis and value on honor, which was more than just a simple social grace. Honor was the primary social measure among Kentra. A dishonorable Kentra would be banished from society forever.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Kentra

- **Height:** 1.8–2.4 meters
