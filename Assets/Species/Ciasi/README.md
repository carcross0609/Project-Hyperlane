# Ciasi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ciasi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ciasi)

The Ciasi were a rodent-like sentient species with tall, pointed ears and thin, trunk-like noses. Ciasi could be found with gray-colored skin and could grow prominent tusks. One famous Ciasi was the thin Podracer Slide Paramita, who performed in Podraces across the galaxy around the year 32 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ciasi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Grayish-brown
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Tusks
  - Long snouts
  - Pointed ears
  - Double-jointed legs
  - Tail
