# Crintlian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crintlian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crintlian)

Crintlians were an alien species native to the Crintlia system. They were known to aggressively defend their territory, even against foes with a significant numerical or technological advantage.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Crintlian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Crintlia system
