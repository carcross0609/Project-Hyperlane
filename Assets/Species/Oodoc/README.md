# Oodoc

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oodoc](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oodoc)

Oodocs were a sentient species known for their great size and strength but not their intelligence. They had spiked arms and backs and massive torsos. In 17 ABY, a number of Oodocs on Coruscant and the asteroid Skip 1 were affected by the Almanian Uprising, during which the Almanian dictator Kueller exploded droids across the galaxy to destabilize the New Republic.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Spiked arms and backs
