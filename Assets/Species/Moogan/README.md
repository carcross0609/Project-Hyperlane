# Moogan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moogan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moogan)

Moogans were a sentient humanoid species native to the planet Mooga. They had slender builds with long limbs and a long neck as well as bright green blood.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Moogan
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green
- **Eyecolor:** Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Long, slender body, green blood
- **Origin:** Mooga
