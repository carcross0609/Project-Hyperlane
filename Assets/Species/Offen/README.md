# Offen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Offen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Offen)

The Offen was a species that was relatively new to the concept of space travel by the time of the New Republic. They where led by a Offen queen who was 6,000 years old in 17 ABY.
