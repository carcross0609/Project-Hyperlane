# Vulptereen

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vulptereen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vulptereen)

Vulptereens were a sentient species that had a shovel-like mouth with six tusks, a feeler on their snout, and two pointed ears. A Vulptereen podracer named Dud Bolt, who was native to Vulpter, participated in the Boonta Eve Classic on Tatooine in 32 BBY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Vulptereen
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Mottled gray
- **Origin:** Vulpter system
