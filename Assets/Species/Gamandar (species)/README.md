# Gamandar (species)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gamandar (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamandar_%28species%29)

The Gamandars were a species of near-Humans from Gamandar. Many of the species were enslaved on their homeworld by the Empire.
