# Dhanduese

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dhanduese](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dhanduese)

The Dhanduese were a flightless avian sentient species that inhabited Dhandu 3, a moon of the planet Dhandu. The low gravity of the moon did not affect them, as their clawed feet allowed them to grip onto the ground.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dhanduese
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian
- **Distinctions:**
  - Clawed feet
  - Feathers
- **Origin:** Dhandu 3
