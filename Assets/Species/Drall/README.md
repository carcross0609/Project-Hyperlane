# Drall

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Drall](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drall)

The Drall were a species with eyes that were especially vulnerable to light. They inhabited the Corellia system alongside humans and Selonians. The non-lethal weapon known as the flashstick was particularly effective against Drall and was used almost exclusively by the Drall constabulary, with most users wearing polarized goggles to prevent the weapon blinding them as well as the target.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Drall
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Light-sensitive eyes
