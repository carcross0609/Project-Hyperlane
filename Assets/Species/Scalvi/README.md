# Scalvi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scalvi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scalvi)

The Scalvi were a sentient species native to the Outer Rim planet Turrak. During the High Republic Era, the Scalvi of their homeworld were assisted by the Jedi Grand Master Yoda for a crisis with the Crulkons they were faring.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Scalvi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Purple
- **Haircolor:** Black
- **Eyecolor:** Black
