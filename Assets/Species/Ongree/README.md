# Ongree

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ongree](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ongree)

The Ongree were an amphibious sentient species native to the planet Skustell. The Jedi Pablo-Jill was a member of this species, as were Jedi Master Coleman Kcaj and the Anklebiter Brigade member Urk G'lar.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ongree
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Skincolor:**
  - Orange
  - Yellow
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Orange
  - Yellow
- **Distinctions:**
  - 4 nostrils
  - Lipless mouth
- **Origin:** Skustell
