# Taung

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Taung](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taung)

The Taung were an ancient sentient species of the Core Worlds planet Coruscant. The day of Taungsday was named for the species. The beskad, a traditional Mandalorian sword, was based on an ancient Taung design. The Taung & Zhell Society, a student group at the University of Coruscant, shared its name with the species.
