# Genetian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Genetian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Genetian)

Genetians were a race of humans hailing from the astronomical object Genetia. Force-sensitive Genetians, such as Imri Cantaros, possessed powerful empathic abilities which could be difficult to control without the proper training. The Jedi Samara the Blue wrote about the empathic powers of the Genetians.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Genetian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Human
- **Distinctions:** Force-sensitives with strong Force empathic abilities
- **Origin:** Genetia
