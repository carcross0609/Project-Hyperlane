# Kuranu

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kuranu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuranu)

Kuranu were a sentient species that possessed smooth purple skin and pupil-less eyes. The species also had an almost pathological fear of germs. The inventor Mantessa Chekkat and her daughter, Klerin, who lived during the High Republic Era, were Kuranu.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Purple
