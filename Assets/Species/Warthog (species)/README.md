# Warthog

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Warthog (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Warthog_%28species%29)

The Warthogs were a sentient species. They were notable for their long snouts and tusks. One Warthog served as a pirate for Captain Gorian Shard onboard his corsair while another Warthog pirate was a member of Silvo's pirates.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Warthog
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Bluish-gray
  - Red
- **Haircolor:** Gray
- **Distinctions:**
  - Long snout
  - Multiple tusks
