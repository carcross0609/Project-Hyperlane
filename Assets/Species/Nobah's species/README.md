# Nobah's species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nobah's species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nobah%27s_species)

A sentient species existed in the galaxy. These aliens were near-human humanoids distinguished by their flat, wide noses with a scale-like texture, as well as a pointed nasion. One member of this species, Nobah, served as prefect of a community on an ice planet who paid tribute to the Neo-Imperial warlord Barro for protection during or after 11 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Nobah's species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Near-human
- **Skincolor:** Light
- **Distinctions:** Flat, wide noses with a scale-like texture
