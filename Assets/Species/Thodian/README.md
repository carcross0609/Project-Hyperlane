# Thodian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thodian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thodian)

Thodians were a blunt-faced sentient species that originated from the Thodia system. During the High Republic Era, at least one Thodian was present on Valo during the Nihil attack on Valo.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Thodian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Blunt face
  - Huge eyes
- **Origin:** Thodia system
