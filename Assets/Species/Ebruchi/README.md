# Ebruchi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ebruchi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ebruchi)

The Ebruchi were a sentient race native to the Unknown Regions. They were humanoids with creamy white skin and mouths that were surrounded by worm-shaped tentacles. The Ebruchi were a clan—based civilization that dwelled in the unknown space where their held territory over a handful of star systems, and prowled the edges of the Outer Rim Territories and Wild Space, looking for prey that wandered. The Ebruchi were mainly composed of Ebruchi, but were known to accept others into their ranks, usually outcasts from the various alien species found in the Unknown Regions.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ebruchi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Creamy white
- **Haircolor:** None
- **Distinctions:** Mouths surrounded by worm-shaped tentacles
- **Diet:** Omnivores
- **Language:** Ebruchese
