# Unidentified knobbly-headed species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified knobbly-headed species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_knobbly-headed_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their large, three-toed feet, as well as massive, three-fingered hands. They also possessed long faces with knobbly craniums. In 4 ABY, two members of this species traveled aboard a transport known as the Tippler's Groan headed from Darsie to the planet Phorliss alongside the Human Mara Jade.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified knobbly-headed species
- **Skincolor:** Brown
- **Distinctions:** Large limbs
