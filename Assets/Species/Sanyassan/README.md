# Sanyassan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sanyassan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sanyassan)

Sanyassans were a humanoid species in the the galaxy; among its members was a pirate named Gruk who led other individuals of the species in criminal escapades during the New Republic Era. These pirates came into conflict with the crew of Sidon Ithano in a section of the galaxy known as the Lost Clusters, where Ithano's group managed to subdue Gruk's forces and commandeer his Munificent-class star frigate.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Sanyassan
