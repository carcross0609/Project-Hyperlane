# Tognath

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tognath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tognath)

Tognath were a sentient species that were native to the planet Yar Togna. The Tognath had a striking look thanks to their insectoid and mammalian characteristics; another factor was their having both an endo- and exoskeletons. They also possessed a very primitive nervous system, which provided them with immunity to most pain (a helpful trait in combat).

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tognath
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insecto-mammalian
- **Height:** 1.8 to 2 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Pink
  - Tan
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Primitive nervous system
- **Origin:** Yar Togna
- **Language:** Tognath
