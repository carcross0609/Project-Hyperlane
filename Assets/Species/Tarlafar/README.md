# Tarlafar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tarlafar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tarlafar)

Tarlafars were a short, white-furred sentient species of which Wamoth, Ailish, and Bayrn were members.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tarlafar
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Pink
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:**
  - Four ears
  - Three fingers
