# Harf

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harf](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harf)

The Harf were a sentient species with an elongated snout and massive arms. They had goggle-like eyes that shined red. In 4 ABY, a drunken Harf was present in the Mos Eisley cantina when the Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk and the Mandalorian Boba Fett had a face-off. Bossk won, with Fett paying him a large number of credits.

## Biology & Culture

- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:** Long snouts
