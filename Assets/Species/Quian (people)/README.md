# Quian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quian (people)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quian_%28people%29)

The Quians were the Human natives of the planet of the same name. They had extremely dark skin, light brown hair, and aqua-green eyes. Benald, his father, and his mother Karin Orlan were Quians.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Quian
- **Skincolor:** Dark
- **Haircolor:** Light brown
- **Eyecolor:** Aqua-green
- **Origin:** Quian
