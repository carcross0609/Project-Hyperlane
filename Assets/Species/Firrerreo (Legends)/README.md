# Firrerreo

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firrerreo/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firrerreo/Legends)

The Firrerreo, also referred to as Firrerreons, were Near-Human natives of the planet Firrerre.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Firrerreo
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Haircolor:** Multicolored
- **Distinctions:** Near-Humans, two-tone hair, nictitating eyelids, gold-colored skin, rapid healing factor
- **Origin:** Firrerre
- **Language:** Firrerreo
