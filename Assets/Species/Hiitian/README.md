# Hiitian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hiitian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hiitian)

Hiitians were a species of human-sized sentient avians. They had feathers growing from their backs.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hiitian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Avian humanoid
- **Distinctions:** Feathered backs
- **Origin:** Hiit system
