# Gengh-Noghri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gengh-Noghri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gengh-Noghri)

The Gengh-Noghri were a subspecies of the Noghri native to the planet Tulpaa in the Unknown Regions, distinguishable by their crocodilian heads filled with flesh-tearing teeth and were over two meters in height, and were the descendents of Noghri slaves taken by ancient Rakata from the Outer Rim Territories to the Tulpaa years before the fall of the Infinite Empire.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gengh-Noghri
- **Class:** Noghri
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Distinctions:** Crocodilian heads filled with flesh-tearing teeth
- **Origin:** Tulpaa
