# Elomin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Elomin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elomin)

Elomin were humanoids from the planet Elom with tapered ears, a crown of horns around their head, and tusks on their cheekbones.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Elomin
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:**

  - Pale
  - Red
- **Eyecolor:**
  - Blue
  - White
  - Yellow
- **Distinctions:** Crown of horns, tusks on cheekbones
- **Origin:** Elom
- **Language:** Elomin
