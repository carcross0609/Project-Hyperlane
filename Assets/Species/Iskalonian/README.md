# Iskalonian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iskalonian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iskalonian)

Iskalonian was the general term for seventeen different aquatic sentient species who lived in the oceans of the planet Iskalon. At least six of these species were relocated from their own homeworlds to Iskalon circa 5000 BBY.
