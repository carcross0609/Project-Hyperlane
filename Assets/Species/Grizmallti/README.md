# Grizmallti

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grizmallti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grizmallti)

The Grizmallti were the human inhabitants of the planet Grizmallt in the Core Worlds. Their religion involved the worship of a deity named Nabu. Around 100 years before 4000 BBY, the Grizmallti discovered a planet in the Mid Rim Territories that they named Nabu in honor of their god. Grizmallti aristocrats used the planet as a vacation spot. Around 4000 BBY, Grizmallt was engaged in a brutal civil war. A group of refugee colony ships fled Grizmallt and arrived at Nabu, where one crashed, while other ships landed and the Grizmallti began colonizing the planet.
