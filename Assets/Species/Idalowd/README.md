# Idalowd

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Idalowd](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Idalowd)

Idalowd was a species of sentient beings that were identified by their purple, puncture-proof skin. Countess Alissyndrex delga Cantonica Provincion was a member of the species. Another member attended a dinner party hosted by the Imperial Senator Mon Mothma and her husband Perrin Fertha on Coruscant in 5 BBY.
