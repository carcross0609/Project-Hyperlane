# Krawg

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Krawg](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krawg)

The Krawg were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. At some point during the reign of the Galactic Empire, a group of Krawg pirates raided credits from the planet Mykapo and later used them to pay the bounty hunter Sana Starros for a crate of Imperial blasters with the promise she would deliver them twelve more. However, she turned around and turned them in as the thieves to the Empire. In 3 ABY, the Rokana Raiders pirate group lost multiple starships full of stolen parts to the Krawg. One member of the raiders complained about this to a comrade and claimed their group was spread too thin while guarding the Khepi Tomb in the Khepi system.
