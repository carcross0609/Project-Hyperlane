# Huloon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Huloon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huloon)

The Huloons were a sentient species whose anatomy included hands. During the reign of the Galactic Empire a Huloon youngling lived as a beggar on the Five Points space station. While crouched in supplication in a park on board the station, the Huloon was given a handful of credits by the criminal Jyn Erso after she stole them.

## Biology & Culture

- **Designation:** Sentient
