# Hrasskis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hrasskis (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hrasskis_%28species%29)

Hrasskis were a sentient race from the planet of Hrasskis. They were distinguished by large, veined air sacs on their backs; they were represented in the New Republic Senate by the belligerent Senator Cian Marook.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hrasskis
- **Distinctions:** Air sacs on their back
- **Origin:** Hrasskis
