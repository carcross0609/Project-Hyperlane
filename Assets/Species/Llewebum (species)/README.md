# Llewebum (species)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Llewebum (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Llewebum_%28species%29)

The Llewebum were a sentient species with large bumps across their bodies, including a secondary series of bumps below their arms. The senator from Llewebum was severely injured in the explosion caused by Kueller in the Senate Hall on Coruscant.
