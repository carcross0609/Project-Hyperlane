# Gank

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gank](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gank)

Ganks were a sentient species native to a planet located in Hutt Space that was conquered by the Hutt Cartel. The cartel was controlled by the Hutts from the neighboring world of Nal Hutta, who turned the Gank into slaves or vassals.

- **Designation:** Sentient
