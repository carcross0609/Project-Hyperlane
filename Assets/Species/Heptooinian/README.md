# Heptooinian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heptooinian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heptooinian)

Heptooinians were a sentient species that originated from the Heptooine system. Kaljach Sonmi and Haki Zeophrine were members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Heptooinian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Light-gray
  - Ashen
- **Haircolor:**
  - Black
  - Silver
  - Grey
  - White (with age)
- **Eyecolor:** Blue
- **Distinctions:**
  - Flat noseless faces
  - Long ears
- **Origin:** Heptooine system
