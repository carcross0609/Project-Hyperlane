# Banasthaur

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Banasthaur](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Banasthaur)

The Banasthaurs were a species of winged sentients native to the Banasthau system, located in the Inner Rim. They were present in the galaxy by the time of the Galactic Civil War.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Banasthaur
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Winged
- **Origin:** Banasthau system
