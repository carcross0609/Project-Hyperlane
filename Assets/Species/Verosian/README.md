# Verosian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Verosian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verosian)

Verosians were a sentient species native to the planet Veros. Their faces looked like masses of fleshy wrinkles. When they were emotional, their skin flushed green.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Verosian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:**
  - Changing their skin green
  - Wrinkle face
- **Origin:** Veros
