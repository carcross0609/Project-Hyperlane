# Aaroun

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aaroun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aaroun)

The Aaroun were a sentient species of carnivores. The powerful beings were covered in golden fur. The species were bred as slaves and gladiators by the Viis Empire, a significant polity in the Unknown Regions until it was decimated by the shape shifting, malevolent entity known as the Mnggal-Mnggal.
