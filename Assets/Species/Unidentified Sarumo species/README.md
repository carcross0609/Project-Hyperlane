# Unidentified Sarumo species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Sarumo species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Sarumo_species)

A humanoid sentient species that could be found on the planet Sarumo during the High Republic Era. This species could be tan or pink in coloration, and though they were naturally bald, they could still develop facial hair. They had solid black eyes featuring small pink pupils, with four thin, white points extending from the pupil in all directions. Sarumo's Minister of Defense Beeth and mayor Seurath were members of this species.
