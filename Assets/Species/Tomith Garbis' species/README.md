# Tomith Garbis' species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tomith Garbis' species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tomith_Garbis%27_species)

Tomith Garbis and his family belonged to a species of tall humanoids with a bald cranium, whitish skin with black patterns, five-fingered hands and slit nostrils. While males of the species were broad-shouldered, the females were more spindly.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tomith Garbis' species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Humanoid
- **Skincolor:** White, with black patterns
- **Eyecolor:** Blue, amber
