# Cyrillian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cyrillian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyrillian)

Cyrillians were a species of tall, sentient reptiles native to the industrialized planet Cyrillia in the Expansion Region. The species invented the DUM-series pit droid to aid them in vehicle maintenance and made use of it during the reign of the Galactic Republic, when their homeworld became part of the galactic circuit of podracing.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Cyrillian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptile
- **Height:** 2.1 meters
- **Origin:** Cyrillia
