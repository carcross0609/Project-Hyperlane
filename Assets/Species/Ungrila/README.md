# Ungrila

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ungrila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ungrila)

The Ungrila were a sentient species in the galaxy. They had several pairs of horns above their mouths. Yasto Attsmun, Brushaun, and a crime boss were members of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Ungrila
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:**
  - Brown
  - White
- **Haircolor:** White
- **Eyecolor:** Black
- **Distinctions:** Several pairs of horns
