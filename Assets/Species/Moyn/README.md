# Moyn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Moyn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moyn)

The Moyn were a sentient species of brown furred humanoids that inhabited the galaxy during the Clone Wars and the reign of the Galactic Empire. They had two protruding eyes with horizontally closing eyelids, and several small tentacle-like appendages that surrounded their mouth. The species was capable of speaking Galactic Basic Standard and could breathe unfiltered Clouzon-36 gas. Members of the species included Level 1313 moneylender Pintu Son-El and the bad-tempered Mining Guild boss Yushyn, who operated an Asteroid Belt Gas Refinery in 3 BBY.
