# Mon Calamari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mon Calamari/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Calamari/Legends)

The Mon Calamari (also known as Calamari, Calamarians, Mon Cal, Mon Calamarians, or rarely Mon Cala) were one of the sentient species indigenous to the world of Dac.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mon Calamari
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Amphibian
- **Height:** 1.3-1.8 meters
- **Skincolor:**
  - Pale Green Streaks
  - Reddish-brown
  - Salmon
- **Eyecolor:** Orange
- **Distinctions:**
  - Bulbous heads
  - Unique vision spectrum
- **Origin:** Dac
- **Language:**
  - Mon Calamarian
  - Galactic Basic Standard
  - Quarrenese
