# Hyallp

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hyallp](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyallp)

The Hyallp were a sentient race of insectoid beings. They were multi-legged arthropods. Little was known of their planet of origin or culture. Noy Waglla was a Hyallp.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Hyallp
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Insectoid
- **Distinctions:** Multi-legs
