# Jumurra

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jumurra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jumurra)

The Jumurra were a sentient species with tan skin, pointed ears, and brown eyes. They also possessed jowls. During the High Republic Era, the elder Naasa, a member of the species, was in the Brendok coven.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Jumurra
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Tan
- **Eyecolor:** Brown
