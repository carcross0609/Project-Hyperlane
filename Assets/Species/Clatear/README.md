# Clatear

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Clatear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clatear)

The Clatear were a species of sentient humanoids that were recognizable by their horned heads. They had been feuding with their neighbors, the Nhoras, since approximately 580 BBY. Both species lived in a sector that was possibly near Cilpar.
