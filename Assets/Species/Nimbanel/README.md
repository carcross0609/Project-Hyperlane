# Nimbanel

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nimbanel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nimbanel)

The Nimbanels were a sentient reptilian species native to Nimban. They could be found throughout the galaxy demonstrating their talents, particularly in the areas of business, politics, and general problem-solving. Jay Igno was a Nimbanel air taxi driver on Coruscant. Five years before the Battle of Yavin, a Nimbanel bounty hunter and his Ranat colleague attempted to apprehend the Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk, but were knocked unconscious.
