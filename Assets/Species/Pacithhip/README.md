# Pacithhip

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pacithhip](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pacithhip)

The Pacithhip were a sentient species with wrinkled gray or green skin, long trunks, and twin elaborate tusks. They had two eyes situated on the sides of their head, and the back of their skull was protected by a thick ridge. A Pacithhip named Bok Askol was an acquaintance of the Toydarian junk dealer Watto and attended the Boonta Eve Classic from his private box. Another Pacithhip, named Ketwol, was in Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina, Mos Eisley, on the fateful day that Luke Skywalker first met Han Solo.
