# Gree

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gree (species)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gree_%28species%29/Legends)

The Gree were a species of tentacled gray cephalopods from the planet Gree in the Outer Rim Territories. Each Gree had six tentacles and an oversized head, in comparison to baseline and near-Human body proportions, and stood between 0.8 and 1.2 meters tall. Gree evolved in a Type II atmosphere, and generally covered their lower faces with odd spongy devices (presumably breath masks) in Type I atmospheres.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Gree
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Height:** 0.8–1.2 meters
- **Skincolor:** Gray

- **Origin:** Gree
- **Language:** Gree
