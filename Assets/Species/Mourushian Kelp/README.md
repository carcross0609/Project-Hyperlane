# Mourushian Kelp

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mourushian Kelp](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mourushian_Kelp)

Mourushian Kelp was a type of sapient kelp that originated from the Mid Rim Mourushia system. During the reign of the Galactic Republic, the Republic political figure Lady Hasquil acknowledged the sapient rights of the kelp, which resulted in Hasquil being cocooned for fifty years.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Mourushian Kelp
- **Designation:** Sapient
- **Class:** Kelp
- **Origin:** Mourushia system
