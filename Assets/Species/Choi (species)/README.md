# Choi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Choi (species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Choi_%28species%29)

The Choi were aliens from the planet Choi. A Choi named Susejo was eaten by the Sarlacc on Tatooine and talked with Boba Fett when he was trapped there. This Choi unintentionally helped Fett escape.

- **Designation:** Sentient
