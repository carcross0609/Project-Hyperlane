# Unidentified fan-crested species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified fan-crested species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_fan-crested_species)

This species was a sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished the fan-like crest that ran from the bridge of their nose and over their head. Some time prior to 22 BBY, at least one member of this species was a member of the Jedi Order, their busts displayed within the Temple Archives.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified fan-crested species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Distinctions:** Head crest
