# Unidentified species

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified species (RTK111)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_species_%28RTK111%29)

This species was a bipedal humanoid sentient species that existed in the galaxy. They were distinguished by their green skin, with mottled dark spots over their faces, and green and blonde hair. In 9 ABY, a member of this species was present at Gor Koresh's Honky Tonk when Koresh attempted to take Din Djarin's Mandalorian armor.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified species
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Green, white, and tan
- **Haircolor:** Green and blonde
- **Distinctions:** Mottled face spots
