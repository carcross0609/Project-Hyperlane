# Candovantan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Candovantan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Candovantan)

Candovantans, or Candovants, were a sentient species from the planet Candovant recognized by their bulbous, hairless heads and brown, tan, and red skin. The New Republic senator Zygli Bruss was a member of the species, as was the Resistance pilot Zizi Tlo. They were considered by some to be a powerful force within the political landscape of the galaxy at one point in time. In Candovantan culture it was considered a grave offense to refuse a drink when offered.
