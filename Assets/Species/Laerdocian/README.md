# Laerdocian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Laerdocian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Laerdocian)

The Laerdocians were a species who spoke Basic with an unusual accent (pronouncing "nothing" as "nothjing", for example.) A Laerdocian named Bodwae served under the smuggler Booster Terrik as a technician on the Errant Venture circa 19 ABY.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Laerdocian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Origin:** Laerdocia system
- **Language:** Galactic Basic Standard
