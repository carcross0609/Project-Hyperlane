# D'farian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D'farian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D%27farian)

The D'farians were a member species of the New Republic who allied themselves with the Bothans and the Diamala during the Caamas Document crisis. D'farian vessels fought alongside the Diamala during a battle at Bothawui in 19 ABY.
