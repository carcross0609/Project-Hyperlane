# Unidentified purple mouthless species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified purple mouthless species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_purple_mouthless_species)

This species was a sentient humanoid species distinguished by their purple fur that extended up in points on the upper sides of their heads, as well as dark eyes. In 4 ABY, at least two members of this species could be found in the city Wrils on the planet Svivren.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Unidentified purple mouthless species
- **Skincolor:** Purple
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
