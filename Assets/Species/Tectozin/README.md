# Tectozin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tectozin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tectozin)

The Tectozin were a sentient species native to the planet The Deep. They had stalked eyes and retractable talon fingers. U'rum was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Tectozin
- **Designation:** Sentient
