# Kenari

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kenari (people)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kenari_%28people%29)

The Kenari were a group of humans native to the planet Kenari. They spoke the language Kenari. They were described as having dark features and large, dark eyes. After large scale mining devastated Kenari, tribes of orphaned children were the only Kenari to survive on world. Cassian Jeron Andor was a Kenari who was adopted by Maarva Andor and Clem Andor.
