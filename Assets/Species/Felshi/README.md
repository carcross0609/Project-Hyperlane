# Felshi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Felshi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Felshi)

The Felshi were a sentient species found within the galaxy. They were defined by their bulky builds and large limbs, with blue skin and small eyes. The one-armed boxer Kabb Uppercut was a member of this species.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Felshi
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Skincolor:** Blue
- **Haircolor:** Blue
- **Eyecolor:** Dark
- **Distinctions:** Large limbs
