# Druulgothan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Druulgothan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Druulgothan)

The Druulgothans were a species of lizard-like sentients native to the Druulgotha system, located in the Outer Rim's Thesme sector. They were present in the galaxy during the Galactic Civil War. In the year 0 BBY, several Druulgothans visited a cantina on the planet Toprawa. The cantina, Al The Alchemist's, was patronized by gun-runners, mercenaries, and bounty hunters from across the galaxy; the infamous Boba Fett among them.
