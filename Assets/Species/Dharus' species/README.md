# Dharus' species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dharus' species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dharus%27_species)

A humanoid species was little-known throughout the galaxy. Members of the species lacked visible ears and had a flat nose and a ridge on the brow. At least some of them had red eyes and orange-brown skin. They also had hair that could be brown. Dharus, a member of the species, was tall for a humanoid and used technology designed for Humans.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Dharus' species
- **Skincolor:** Orange-brown
- **Haircolor:** Brown
- **Eyecolor:** Red
- **Distinctions:**
  - Lacking visible ears
  - Flat noses
  - Ridges on their brows
