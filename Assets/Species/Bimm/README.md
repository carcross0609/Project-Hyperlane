# Bimm

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bimm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bimm)

The Bimms were a species of diminutive sentients who resembled short humans. Rycar Ryjerd, a patron of Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina in Mos Eisley, Tatooine, was a Bimm. A different species of furred individuals with floppy ears was also called Bimm.
