# Stennes Shifter

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stennes Shifter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stennes_Shifter)

The Stennes Shifters were a sentient species that had an innate ability to use the Force to pass invisibly through crowds and were typically hyper-wary of strangers. They could have tan skin and brown hair. Known members of the species included Trinto Duaba and Braconnor Bakiska.
