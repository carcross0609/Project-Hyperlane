# Rasta Alien

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rasta Alien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rasta_Alien)

Rasta Aliens were a humanoid species that had spines on their calves, a long rippling strain near their neck, and short stubs on their heads. Known as dangerous predators, they were capable of shooting deadly spikes from their abdomen in three-pronged bursts. They could be found in the deserts of the planet Tatooine, where they primarily kept to caves, including the Tusken Cave where many Tusken Raiders could be found as well.
