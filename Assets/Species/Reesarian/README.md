# Reesarian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Reesarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reesarian)

Reesarians were a sentient species from the Reesaria system. Possessing almost simian features, they had acute senses and sharp eyesight that allowed them to see in infrared and ultraviolet. When a pair of Reesarians bonded, they became highly attuned to each other's whereabouts. Ort was a crimson-skinned Reesarian with long, plaited hair who served the Path of the Open Hand in the High Republic Era. Phenlass and Odla Sur were a pair of bonded Reesarians who joined the Resistance as soldiers and scouts.
