# Columi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Columi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Columi)

The Columi were a sentient species that lived in the galaxy. Imperial Flag Lieutenant Edwig warned fleet and garrison commanders to be wary of members of the Columi species in a transmission sent on behalf of Admiral Firmus Piett.
