# Bardottan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bardottan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardottan/Legends)

The Bardottan, also known as Phuii, were a species of sentient, saurian bipeds with sagging, wrinkled skin. Their anatomy featured a long, swooping neck, lanky arms, and stubby legs. Some Bardottans had long tails, while others did not. Members of the species had a fleshy crest on top of the head and a pronounced growth under each eye. Their needle-pointed snouts were full of blunt teeth. The species hailed from Bardotta, a planet located in the Colonies region of space that was renowned for its spirituality.
