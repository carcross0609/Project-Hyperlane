# Corragut

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corragut](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corragut)

The Corragut were a sentient species hailing from Altawar, a planet in the Core Worlds' Corellian sector. After the species was discovered by Corellians, the Corragut were offered opportunities upon introduction to the wider galaxy. However, they subsequently became a "client planet" for the planet Corellia—which was governed by a Human-centric culture—and had little say in governing themselves.

- **Designation:** Sentient
