# Savarian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Savarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Savarian)

The Savarians were a cultural group of humans who called the the Outer Rim Territories planet Savareen home. They were a deeply spiritual people and lived in scattered villages, including the Pnakotic Coast village with it's Bis Refinery.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Savarian
- **Origin:** Savareen
- **Species:** Human
- **Language:** Sign language
