# Farghul

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Farghul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Farghul)

The Farghul were felinoid sapients indigenous to Farrfin. They had claws, incredible strength and speed, and were covered with tawny fur and possessed prehensile tails.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Farghul
- **Height:** 1.7 - 2.0 meters
- **Skincolor:** Tawny
- **Distinctions:** Prehensile tails, natural con artists
- **Lifespan:** 75 standard years
- **Origin:** Farrfin
- **Language:** Farghul
