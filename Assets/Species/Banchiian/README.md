# Banchiian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Banchiian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Banchiian)

Banchiians were a diminutive avian sentient species native to the Outer Rim Territories planet Banchii. A primitive species, the Banchiians did not have a concept of personal property, believing that everything on Banchii was for everyone to share, and did not speak Galactic Basic Standard. When Banchii was settled by offworlders during the High Republic Era, the Banchiians took utensils and dishes from the settlement, leading to their discovery by Jedi Knight Lily Tora-Asi, her Padawan Keerin Fionn, and the younglings Nima and Viv'nia.
