# Lothalite

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lothalite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lothalite)

Lothalites, also called Lothalians, were a cultural group of humans native to the Outer Rim planet Lothal. Most had a copper sheen to their skin, as exemplified by Ezra Bridger, Ryder Azadi, and Morad Sumar.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Lothalite
- **Designation:** Human
- **Origin:** Lothal
