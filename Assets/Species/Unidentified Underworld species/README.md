# Unidentified Underworld species

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Underworld species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Underworld_species)

An unidentified Underworld species dwelled in the sewers of the Coruscant underworld around 0 ABY. They were a small, winged species with green fangs, which lived in the ruins of ancient buildings. They revered Wookiees for unknown reasons.
