# Alzarian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Alzarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alzarian)

Alzarians were a reptilian sentient species from the Alzar system. During the High Republic Era, one Alzarian was a member of the Nihil and would later participate during the Nihil attack on Valo.

## Biology & Culture

- **Name:** Alzarian
- **Designation:** Sentient
- **Class:** Reptilian
- **Skincolor:** Pus-colored
- **Distinctions:**
  - Horns
- **Origin:** Alzar system
