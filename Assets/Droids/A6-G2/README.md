# A6-G2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [A6-G2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A6-G2)

A6-G2, also known as "Aces," was a cheerful astromech droid assigned to Obi-Wan Kenobi for his mission to Lenahra in 41 BBY. She was adept at flying and eager to follow directions.
