# PR-85T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PR-85T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PR-85T)

PR-85T, known fully as PR-85T the Other Half, was a bounty droid who worked for the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. PR-85T was heavily modified by Corde the Half, who cannibalized parts from other droids, leaving the droid full of perilous surprises at the cost of empathic programming. The droid was destroyed by Cal Kestis on the moon Jedha alongside Corde the Half.
