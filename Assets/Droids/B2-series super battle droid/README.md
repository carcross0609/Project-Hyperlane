# B2-series super battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B2-series super battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2-series_super_battle_droid)

B2-series super battle droids, also known as B2 super battle droids, were an advanced battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Although one model of the B2-series was only equipped with wrist blasters on their right forearm, another was outfitted with wrist blasters on both forearms. Super battle droids were also equipped with wrist rockets and a rapid fire function for their wrist blaster.
