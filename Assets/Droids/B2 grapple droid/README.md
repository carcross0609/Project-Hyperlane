# B2 grapple droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B2 grapple droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2_grapple_droid)

The B2 grapple droid was a variant of the B2-series super battle droid model which was designed for close-range combat. They served as a successor to the B1 grapple droid. The B2 grapple droid was one of the droids that participated in the Battle of Ord Mantell. One unit discovered Gar Saxon, a member of Black Sun, and a Pyke hiding behind cover during the battle and punched through to their position, grabbing the Pyke and pulling him through the wall.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B2 grapple droid
- **Line:** B2-series super battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Electrified pincers
