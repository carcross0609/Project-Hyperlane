# M-6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M-6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-6)

M-6 was an astromech droid that assisted SIS Agent Theron Shan during the Cold War. The droid had a domed bronzium head and communicated in the beeps and whistles of droidspeak.
