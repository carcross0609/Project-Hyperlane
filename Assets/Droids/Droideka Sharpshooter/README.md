# Droideka Sharpshooter

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Droideka Sharpshooter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droideka_Sharpshooter)

The Droideka Sharpshooter, also known as the sharpshooter droideka, the sniper droideka, the sniper droid, or the sharpshooter droid, was a variation of droideka that specialized in sniping. Unlike the original droideka, it was equipped with a single blaster rifle in the middle, and used its armored shell to cover its front while in combat, instead of deflector shields. Sharpshooter droidekas were part of the Separatist forces commanded by the Zygerrian Darts D'Nar on Kiros.
