# TB-2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TB-2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TB-2)

TB-2 was a 2-1B surgical droid in service of the Galactic Republic, stationed as a medic aboard the freighter Twilight during the Clone Wars. Originally a hologram, they were uploaded into a body and continued to serve aboard Jedi General Anakin Skywalker's freighter, as well as on his Republic Star Destroyer Resolute.
