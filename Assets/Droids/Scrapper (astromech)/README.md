# Scrapper (astromech)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scrapper (astromech)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scrapper_%28astromech%29)

Scrapper was a R5 series astromech droid that worked for the Rokana Raiders while it was based at the Khepi Tomb in the Sayma Nebula during 3 ABY. During that year, Scrapper stole a keycard to an area of the base's forge where a number of items had been stashed. One of the raiders tried to get it back from it but couldn't find a tool small enough to force open its panel. The raider considered blasting Scrapper but decided not to because they knew it would upset a fellow raider named Adden. Later when the lights were turned off in the forge area the raider lost track of Scrapper and eventually wrote a data message to Adden; in which they told Adden to forget about the stash.
