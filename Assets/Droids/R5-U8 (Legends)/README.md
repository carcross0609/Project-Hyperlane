# R5-U8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R5-U8/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-U8/Legends)

R5-U8 was an R5-series astromech droid that served in Shadow Squadron as the astromech droid for Flyby. Their BTL-B Y-wing starfighter was disabled during the Battle of the Kaliida Nebula.
