# Unidentified EV-series droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified EV-series droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_EV-series_droid)

In 34 ABY, an EV-series droid called in to the radio show Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7 on station BSO 401.72, somehow bypassing the show's filter. The droid berated the show's host, B6-24, claiming that she was an abomination for believing she could speak her mind, as droids were only made to follow orders. B6-24 cut off the call before the EV-series droid could finish and apologized to her listeners for the call. The droid spoke in an evil, monotonous, grating, and mechanical voice. The droid was manufactured by MerenData, like all EV-series supervisor droids.
