# KR-82 Expulser

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KR-82 Expulser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KR-82_Expulser)

KR-82 Expulser was a battle droid that served as the center of the Kaon's automated security system artificial intelligence. During the Rakghoul outbreak on Kaon the droid motherboard got corrupted by the virus and was destroyed to restore the system to order.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KR-82 Expulser
- **Line:** Battle droid
