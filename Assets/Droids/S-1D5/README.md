# S-1D5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S-1D5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-1D5)

S-1D5 was a shopkeeper droid in the service of the Sith Empire. During the Battle of Corellia in the Galactic War, it was stationed in Coronet Spaceport in the Incorporation Islands region of the planet Corellia, distributing starship upgrades.
