# 4E

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [4E](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/4E)

4E, nicknamed Foree, was a conical astromech droid with the emblem of Alphabet Squadron on its chassis that served the New Republic toward the end of the Galactic Civil War. The astromech droid was assigned to the T-70 X-wing starfighter flown by Yrica Quell, former New Republic pilot of Alphabet Squadron, when she returned to the New Republic from within the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing.
