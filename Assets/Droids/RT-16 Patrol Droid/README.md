# RT-16 Patrol Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RT-16 Patrol Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RT-16_Patrol_Droid)

The RT-16 Patrol Droid was a model of cold-resistant probe patrol droid designed by the Ugnaught Kerkarr for the Galactic Republic to use on the planet Hoth. However, Kerkarr's ship crashed, and his droids went rogue.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RT-16 Patrol Droid
- **Creator:** Kerkarr
- **Class:** Probe patrol droid
