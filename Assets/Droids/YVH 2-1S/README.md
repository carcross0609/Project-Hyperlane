# YVH 2-1S

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YVH 2-1S](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YVH_2-1S)

YVH 2-1S was a YVH S-series war droid developed by Tendrando Arms. 2-1S was part of the Myrkr strike team in the Mission to Myrkr during the Yuuzhan Vong War in 27 ABY. 2-1S was destroyed in action, sacrificing himself to optimize enemy losses and maintain the strike team's cover.
