# Unidentified R2 unit (Wes Janson)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified R2 unit (Wes Janson)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_R2_unit_%28Wes_Janson%29)

An R2 unit was used by Wes Janson during a battle over Gall. It was programmed by the traitorous Viera Cheran, Chief Technician for Rogue Squadron, to take over control of Janson's X-wing fighter and shoot down Luke Skywalker. It failed, as Skywalker was able to destroy the droid and disable Janson's fighter.
