# Number Five

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Number Five](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Number_Five)

Number Five was one of the medical droids who served Fyzen Gor's Brotherhood of Wire and Bone, and one of the Original Dozen. The droid was modified and repaired, partially using body parts taken from Wookiees. Number Five, along with Number Seven, accompanied Gor to the chamber in the Mesulan Remnants Belt, confronting Lando Calrissian. Gor ordered the two droids to take Calrissian's weapons, while he activated the Phylanx. Number Five was destroyed, along with the Phylanx's chamber, by Calrissian, who managed to escape the resulting explosion.
