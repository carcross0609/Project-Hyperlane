# Mark V Enforcer Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark V Enforcer Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_V_Enforcer_Droid)

The Mark V Enforcer Droid was a model of three-legged battle droid that was used by the forces of the Specialists, a mercenary unit. Mark V Enforcer Droids were deployed under the control of QO-99 to the planet Makeb during the Conquest of Makeb.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark V Enforcer Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Affiliation:** Specialists
