# EGL power droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EGL power droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EGL_power_droid)

The EGL power droid was a model of power droid with a long cylindrical body atop two short legs. The manumitted EGL power droid EGL-21 dwelt in Niima Outpost on the planet Jakku in the year 34 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** EGL power droid
- **Manufacturer:** Veril Line Systems
- **Class:** Power droid
- **Degree:** Class 5
- **Plating:** Gray
