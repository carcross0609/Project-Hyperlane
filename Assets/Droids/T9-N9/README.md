# T9-N9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T9-N9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T9-N9)

T9-N9 was a astromech droid during the Cold War. It was owned by the Revanite Torrun, who resided in the Revanite compound in the planet of Dromund Kaas.
