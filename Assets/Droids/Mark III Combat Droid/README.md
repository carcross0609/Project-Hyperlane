# Mark III Combat Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark III Combat Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_III_Combat_Droid)

The Mark III Combat Droid was a model of humanoid battle droid that was used by the forces of the Specialists, a mercenary unit. Mark III Combat Droids were deployed under the control of QO-99 to the planet Makeb during the Conquest of Makeb.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark III Combat Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Affiliation:** Specialists
