# BAF-999 Prototype Siege Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-999 Prototype Siege Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-999_Prototype_Siege_Droid)

The BAF-999 Prototype Siege Droid was a prototype battle droid produced by Balmorran Arms on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. BAF-999 Siege Droids were among the forces used by the Galactic Republic during their retreat from the Balmorran Arms Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-999 Prototype Siege Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
