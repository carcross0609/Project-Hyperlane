# ET-4B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ET-4B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ET-4B)

ET-4B was a protocol droid fluent in over five million forms of communication. It assisted a group of Rebels on their mission to Orellon II to find a safeworld for the remains of the recently disbanded Pegasus Strike Force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ET-4B
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
