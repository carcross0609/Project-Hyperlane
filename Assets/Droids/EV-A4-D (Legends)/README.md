# EV-A4-D

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EV-A4-D/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EV-A4-D/Legends)

EV-A4-D, also known as A4-D or Ay-Four-Dee, was an A4 laboratory assistant droid who served the cyborg General Grievous of the Confederacy of Independent Systems as a medical/repair droid during the Clone Wars. As with many EV-model droids, a programming defect caused EV-A4-D to have a sadistic personality. Prior to his work with Grievous, EV-A4-D served as a supervisor in a hospital on the world of Tauber, but he was relieved from duty after ordering the medical droids under his command to operate on patients without administering painkillers. He was later purchased by the InterGalactic Banking Clan and absorbed into the Confederacy.
