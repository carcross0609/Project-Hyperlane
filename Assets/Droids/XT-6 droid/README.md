# XT-6 droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XT-6 droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XT-6_droid)

XT-6 droids were a series of service droids used around 4000 BBY. Their limbs could be adapted to serve as prosthetic replacements for injured Humans. During the attack on Iziz, Cay Qel-Droma lost his left arm and replaced it with that of one such droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** XT-6 droid
- **Class:** Service droid
