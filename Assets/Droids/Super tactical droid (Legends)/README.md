# Super tactical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Super tactical droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Super_tactical_droid/Legends)

The super tactical droid was a model of tactical droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems as a battle droid commander during the Clone Wars. Super tactical droids often served as generals in the Separatist Droid Army.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Super tactical droid
- **Class:** Tactical droid
- **Height:** 1.94 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programing
- **Armament:** E-5 blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
