# R5-X2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R5-X2/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-X2/Legends)

R5-X2 was an R5-series astromech droid owned by Marlo the Hutt before ownership of it was transferred to crime lord Jabba the Hutt. The droid frequently traveled with it's new owner and was used to calculate odds of gambling events. During Podraces, R2-X2 would recalculate Jabba's potential winnings and later report them.
