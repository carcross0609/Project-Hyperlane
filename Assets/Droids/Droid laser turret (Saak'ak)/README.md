# Droid laser turret (Saak'ak)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Droid laser turret (Saak'ak)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_laser_turret_%28Saak%27ak%29)

Droid laser turrets were a type of turret-droid armed with turbolasers. They consisted of a gray oblate spheroid body with two barrels protruding from it and a trunk ascending from the top of the spheroid to the surface that the turret was attached to. A droid laser turret was concealed within the ceiling of a hangar within the Trade Federation's modified Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter Saak'ak.

- **Name:** Droid laser turret
- **Class:** Turret-droid
