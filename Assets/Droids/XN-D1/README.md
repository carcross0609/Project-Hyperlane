# XN-D1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XN-D1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XN-D1)

XN-D1 was a droid active during the Cold War who was obsessed with dissecting biological life. He maintained a number of laboratories where he conducted experiments, and one of those laboratories was discovered and raided for research data.
