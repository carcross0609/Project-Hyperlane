# V1C-0RY

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V1C-0RY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V1C-0RY)

V1C-0RY was an shopkeeper droid stationed aboard Carrick Station, a Galactic Republic space station, during the Cold War between the Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The droid functioned as a business vendor on the station and claimed to have an extensive inventory.
