# Serenno flying droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Serenno flying droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Serenno_flying_droid)

The Serenno flying droid was a droid present at Count Dooku's castle on Serenno during the bounty hunter tournament there. It was used by Moralo Eval to attack Rako Hardeen, who was really the persona Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi was disguised in.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Serenno flying droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Various hues of blue
- **Armament:** Two pairs of blasters
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
