# Annihilator X-12

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Annihilator X-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Annihilator_X-12)

The Annihilator X-12 was a powerful tripedal battle droid that was stationed in the assembly line of the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. During the Cold War, it was destroyed by Imperial forces that invaded the factory.

- **Name:** Annihilator X-12
- **Death:** 3643 BBY, Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
