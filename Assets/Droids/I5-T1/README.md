# I5-T1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I5-T1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I5-T1)

I5-T1 was a battle droid that was part of the reconstituted Sith Empire's forces during the Korriban Incursion, when the Galactic Republic invaded the planet Korriban in 3637 BBY. during the Galactic War. I5-T1 was tasked with guarding the force field erected in the Valley of the Dark Lords next to the Tomb of Ajunta Pall, but was destroyed by Republic forces in their push to the Sith Academy.
