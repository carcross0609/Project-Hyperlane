# Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Naboo)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Naboo)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_T-series_tactical_droid_%28Naboo%29)

A T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid with feminine programming served Count Dooku on Naboo. She escorted in a disguised Jar Jar Binks to see General Grievous. Later, she is going for the prisoner exchange that the B1-series battle droids carry the Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker to free General Grievous. As Grievous is freed, she threw his lightsaber to Anakin and return to the shuttle.
