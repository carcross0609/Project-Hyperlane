# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Citadel)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Citadel)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Citadel%29)

This B1 battle droid was stationed on the planet Lola Sayu, where he guarded the CIS prison known as the Citadel in around 20 BBY. When R2-D2's droid squadron, arrived on the Citadel's landing bay with a group of prisoners, this droid asked OOM-10, the of the squa,d where they were taking the prisoners. OOM-10 replied that the prisoners were being taken to Point Tarron. This made the droid suspicious as there were no outposts on Point Tarron, but OOM-10 insisted that they had orders, so this droid ultimately let them go. After a few seconds however, this droid was informed by K2-B4 that the prisoners were actually lead by reprogrammed battle droids and that they were escaping. The droid, before he could react, was destroyed by Obi-Wan Kenobi, one of the escapees.
