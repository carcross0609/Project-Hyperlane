# YVH 1-25A

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YVH 1-25A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YVH_1-25A)

YVH 1-25A was a YVH 1 combat droid developed by Tendrando Arms. It accompanied YVH 1-1A onto the vessel Byrt during the evacuation of Coruscant to rescue C-3PO and Ben Skywalker and was destroyed in the attempt by a singularity mine.
