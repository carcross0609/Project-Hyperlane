# Ivee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ivee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ivee)

Ivee was an clear domed R3-series astromech droid who served as the astromech of Resistance pilot Jessika Pava during the Resistance's mission to Cato Neimoidia, where she sacrificed herself to save the pilot. Prior to the mission, she fixed Poe Dameron's X-wing starfighter Black One for fellow astromech BB-8.
