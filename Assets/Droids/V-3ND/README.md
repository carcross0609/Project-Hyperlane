# V-3ND

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V-3ND](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-3ND)

V-3ND was a protocol droid that was programmed by the Twi'lek mechanic Pyya Bocc to collect and sell almakian apples and other goods from a stall beside the garage of Pyya's mother, Sornah Bocc, on the planet Malastare. Around 10 BBY, the podracer Volo Bolus, who worked at the garage, believed that the droid's mediocre apple sales might be the garage's best income stream.

## Specifications

- **Name:** V-3ND
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Sensor:** Yellow

- **Affiliation:** Sornah's Garage
