# 6R-0WL

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [6R-0WL](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/6R-0WL)

6R-0WL was an R2-series astromech droid that was active during the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Like all R-series astromech droids, 6R-0WL built by Industrial Automaton and stood 0.96 meters tall. 6R-0WL also had a particularly vicious attitude. On Life Day during the war, the droid was offered for sale by the vendor droid 80-T5, who operated a stall in the Jedi Temple on the planet Coruscant. 6R-0WL's plating was gray and included decals to make it look like a wampa at the time he was available for sale.
