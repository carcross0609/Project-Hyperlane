# Unidentified Nahasta clan archivist droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Nahasta clan archivist droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Nahasta_clan_archivist_droid)

An archivist droid was kept by the Alvadorjian Nahasta clan to record memories of the group and its members. The droid was cylindrical and able to hover three meters above the ground. After the Galactic Empire invaded Ylagia and destroyed encampments outside of Ologage, the Nahasta clan was exterminated, the droid being the sole survivor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Nahasta clan archivist droid
- **Armament:** Targeting laser
- **Equipment:** Cycler
- **Affiliation:** Nahasta clan
