# XA-LX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [XA-LX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XA-LX)

XA-LX, nicknamed "Exalex" by Ello Asty, was an astromech droid in service to the Resistance during its war with the First Order. During the Resistance's attack on Starkiller Base, she flew with Asty and was killed.
