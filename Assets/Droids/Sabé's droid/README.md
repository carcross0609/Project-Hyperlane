# Sabé's droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sabé's droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sab%C3%A9%27s_droid)

A droid assisted the former handmaiden Sabé during the Imperial Era. The droid accompanied Sabé to collect the thief Warba Calip from Sergia and then to Caranthanax B, where the former handmaiden intended to test whether the Jedi Luke Skywalker had a dark side. The droid assisted Sabé in pretending to kidnap Calip in an attempt to provoke Skywalker.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sabé's droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Gray
