# Xet's droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Xet's droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xet%27s_droid)

A droid was owned by Xet, a Falleen member of the Black Sun criminal syndicate who was secretly a spy for the Crimson Dawn syndicate. The droid communicated in whirring sounds and hovered in the air, possessing gray plating and a single blue sensor. The droid was also equipped with a arm that featured a scomp link. Xet and the droid attended Crimson Dawn's auction of the carbonite-frozen smuggler Han Solo aboard the starship Vermillion, where Xet's necklace that contained data crystals, was sliced by the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra.
