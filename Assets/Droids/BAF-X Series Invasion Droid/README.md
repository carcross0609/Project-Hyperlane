# BAF-X Series Invasion Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-X Series Invasion Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-X_Series_Invasion_Droid)

The BAF-X Series Invasion Droid was a bipedal battle droid produced by Balmorran Arms on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. BAF-X droids were armed with vibroswords, and were among the forces used by the Galactic Republic to defend the Balmorran Arms Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-X Series Invasion Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Vibrosword
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
