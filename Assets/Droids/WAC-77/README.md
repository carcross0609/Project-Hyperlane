# WAC-77

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [WAC-77](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WAC-77)

WAC-77, or "Wack," was a pit droid created on the planet Ojom. The repair droid had a black photoreceptor and brown and tan plating. It served with the Besalisk pilot Javlin on her freighter, along with the Twi'lek mechanic Chalan Talosi. When the Black Sun criminal syndicate caused a fire to break out on the freighter's engines, WAC-77 worked with Talosi to extinguish the flames but negotiated with Talosi to receive desired upgrades.
