# Unidentified protocol droid (Dennet Clyde)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified protocol droid (Dennet Clyde)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_protocol_droid_%28Dennet_Clyde%29)

A droid worked for Dennet Clyde. It was tasked to deliver refreshments to Qi'ra who was posing as a buyer. It recognized her from her previous dealings with the InterGalactic Banking Clan droids and Qi'ra realized she could use this to her advantage. She learned that the Hammers were receiving trade route information about Crimson Dawn operations in the Karthakk sector from a member of the criminal organization. It requested that the information be wiped from its memory so it wouldn't compromise anything before it could be freed. She wiped all knowledge of her conversation and interactions with the droid from its memory and left it shutdown.
