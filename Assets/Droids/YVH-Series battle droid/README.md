# YVH-Series battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YVH-Series battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YVH-Series_battle_droid)

The YVH-Series battle droid, also known as the YVH series Yuuzhan Vong Hunter, was a series of battle droids built by Tendrando Arms to specifically combat the Yuuzhan Vong. They saw some use after the Yuuzhan Vong War, as well.

## Specifications

- **Name:** YVH-Series battle droid
- **Manufacturer:** Tendrando Arms
- **Class:** Class four droid
- **Cost:** 28,830 credits
- **Plating:** Laminanium
- **Equipment:**
  - Vocabulator
  - Low power Repulsorlifts
  - Self-destruct mechanisms
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
