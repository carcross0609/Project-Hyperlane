# R5-M2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-M2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-M2)

R5-M2 was an astromech droid who served the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War against the Galactic Empire. The droid was programmed to calculate optimal evacuation trajectories. When the Alliance lost a battle to the Empire on the planet Hoth, R5-M2 helped evacuate Rebel personnel from Echo Base. The droid had black, blue and white plating.
