# IP-CPT

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IP-CPT](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IP-CPT)

IP-CPT was a battle droid created by the Sith cult known as the Unmasked to guard their research station in Sector R-4. It was created by Lady Dominique with the assistance of ARIA, an AI. It was destroyed by the Alliance Commander during the mission to the R-4 anomaly and it's remains were likely destroyed when the station exploded.
