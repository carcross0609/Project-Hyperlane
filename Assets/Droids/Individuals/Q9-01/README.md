# Q9-01

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Q9-01](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q9-01)

Q9-01, also known as Zero-One, was a Q9-series astromech droid equipped with a vocoder. Zero-One was Kyp Durron's personal astromech during the Yuuzhan Vong War that he received from a Mon Calamari philosopher who was fascinated with ancient cultures and technologies. He was so fond of a computer system based on binary that he named his droid after the numbers "0" and "1".
