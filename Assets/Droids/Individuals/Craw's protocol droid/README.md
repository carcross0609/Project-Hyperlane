# Craw's protocol droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Craw's protocol droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Craw%27s_protocol_droid)

For some time, a protocol droid served Craw, the self-titled Dictator-Forever of the planet Targonn. At some point, Craw told a visiting Hardexian ambassador a joke about three Hardex and a bantha. When the ambassador was not amused by the protocol droid's translation of the joke, Craw had the droid deactivated and turned into a fountain. In his new function, the droid's frame stood posed on one leg with both arms outstretched while water flowed from his mouth and birds used the structure as a perch.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Craw's protocol droid
- **Death:** By 5.5 BBY
- **Class:** Protocol droid
