# T0-B1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [T0-B1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T0-B1)

T0-B1 was a Force-sensitive droid that dreamed of being a Jedi. He was originally Tobi, the Padawan of Professor Mitaka. Although Tobi died during the Great Jedi Purge following Order 66, Mitaka placed his soul inside a droid body to resurrect him into T0-B1. T0-B1 was Force-sensitive, powered by a kyber crystal, and aspired to become a Jedi. Mitaka focused on researching a way to bring life to desert planets, starting with an uninhabited planet where he conducted research in a compound full of droids, including T0-B1 and C03.
