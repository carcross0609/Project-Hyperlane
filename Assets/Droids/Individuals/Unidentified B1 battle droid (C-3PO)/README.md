# Unidentified B1 battle droid (C-3PO)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid (C-3PO)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_%28C-3PO%29)

A B1 battle droid in the Geonosian droid army served in the Petranaki Arena during the First Battle of Geonosis. The battle droid had its head accidentally grafted onto the chassis of C-3PO when the protocol droid had gotten trapped in the droid factory's conveyor belt system. Meanwhile, C-3PO's own head was grafted onto a B1 battle droid chassis.
