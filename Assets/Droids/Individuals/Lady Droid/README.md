# Lady Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lady Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_Droid)

This droid was an aspiring actress who went to talent school to achieve this. She was a girlfriend of F-29-PSKx, who called her multiple times from a Star*Link booth from the Star Tours spaceport while traveling.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lady Droid
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
