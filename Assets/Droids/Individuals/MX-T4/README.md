# MX-T4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MX-T4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MX-T4)

MX-T4 was a protocol droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War with the reconstituted Sith Empire. During the Battle of Corellia they directed Republic troops to the Legislature Building.

- **Name:** MX-T4
- **Line:** Hollis-series steward droid
