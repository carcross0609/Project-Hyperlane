# R0-4L0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R0-4L0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R0-4L0)

R0-4L0, referred to simply as ROLO, was an R0 astromech droid that served in the Resistance thirty years after the Battle of Endor. It served as Jessika Pava's astromech aboard her T-70 X-wing starfighter during the assault on Starkiller Base.
