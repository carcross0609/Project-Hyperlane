# DN-20

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DN-20](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DN-20)

DN-20 was a hubdroid who served within the Vardos Star Scouts on the planet Vardos during the Imperial Era. Between 1 BBY and 4 ABY, the droid was reprogrammed by spy Deeta Kardon and served within the Alliance to Restore the Republic until his destruction.
