# G2-5T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G2-5T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G2-5T)

G2-5T was a masculine G2 repair droid that screened luggage in Spaceport THX1138's Star Tours terminal. Shortly before the Galactic Empire's raid on the space station around the year 1 BBY, G2-9T wondered what had happened to G2-5T, who he expected to relieve him. As he worried that G2-5T had been sacked, a disassembled G2 droid could be seen inside a piece of luggage passing through the scanner behind him.
