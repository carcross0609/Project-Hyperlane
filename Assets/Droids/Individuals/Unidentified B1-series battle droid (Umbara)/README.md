# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Umbara)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Umbara)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Umbara%29)

During the Clone Wars, a B1-series battle droid served within the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, under the command of T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid TM-171. In 20 BBY, the battle droid participated in a multi-day campaign that took place on the planet Umbara, after it had seceded from the Galactic Republic to the Confederacy during the war, which led to the planet's invasion by the Republic. Aboard the Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel of TM-171, the B1 battle droid assisted the tactical droid in strategic resupplying operations throughout the campaign, but was later destroyed following the destruction of the supply ship in orbit.
