# Dee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dee)

Dee was a droid who assisted Jedi Master Zia Zaldor Zanna during the High Republic Era. They were often tasked with taking care of the Tenoo Jedi Temple while Master Zanna was away. Dee was also in charge of escorting the younglings on field trips, such as to the lava fields of Tiss'ell.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Jedi
- **Name:** Dee
- **Model:** Temple caretaker droid
- **Class:** Caretaker droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Bronze and gold
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
