# C1-RN8W

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C1-RN8W](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C1-RN8W)

C1-RN8W was an astromech droid of the C1 series with a black chassis and rainbow-colored detailing. This droid lived in the domed city located on the planet Plazir-15. In 9 ABY, C1 travelled to the Peacekeeping zone on the same day Din Djarin and Bo-Katan Kryze visited the metropolis.
