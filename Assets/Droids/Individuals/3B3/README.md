# 3B3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [3B3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3B3)

3B3 was a B1 battle droid that served in the Trade Federation Droid Army. During the invasion of the planet Naboo in 32 BBY, 3B3 discovered the Jedi Knights Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi and the Gungan Jar Jar Binks sneaking into the capital city Theed. 3B3 ordered them to drop their weapons but was swiftly destroyed by Jinn.
