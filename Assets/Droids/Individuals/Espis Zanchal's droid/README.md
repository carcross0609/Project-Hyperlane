# Espis Zanchal's droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Espis Zanchal's droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Espis_Zanchal%27s_droid)

The baron and lord Espis Zanchal of the Galactic Empire owned a droid that served him at the Zanchal Estate on the planet Coruscant. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, the baron was mentioned in a list of members of the Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate, prompting the Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Vader to send his crew of assassins to eliminate him.
