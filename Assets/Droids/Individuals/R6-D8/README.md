# R6-D8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R6-D8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R6-D8)

R6-D8 was an R6 astromech droid that served in the Resistance as the astromech of Temmin Wexley. He perished alongside his master when their T-70 X-wing starfighter was downed into a Star Destroyer by the Sith Eternal forces during the Battle of Exegol, moments before the arrival of the Citizens' Fleet.
