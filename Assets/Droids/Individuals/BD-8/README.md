# BD-8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BD-8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD-8)

BD-8, nicknamed Beady, was a Yuuzhan Vong Hunter-series battle droid that was owned by the Solo family until it was destroyed by Killiks under Raynar Thul.
