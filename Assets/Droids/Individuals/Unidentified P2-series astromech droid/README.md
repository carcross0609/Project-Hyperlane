# Unidentified P2-series astromech droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified P2-series astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_P2-series_astromech_droid)

A P2-series astromech droid was owned by Devon Fuller around 3 BBY. The droid was later acquired by Roark Garnet, who took the unit when Fuller offered it to him in place of a sum of credits he owed him. During a battle between Garnet's ship and Imperial forces, the droid misunderstood a simple command, and dropped Garnet's deflector shields. Garnet later spaced the droid and vowed to have words with Fuller.
