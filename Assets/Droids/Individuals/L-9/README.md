# L-9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [L-9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L-9)

L-9, or Ell-Nine, was a serving droid that worked at the Soft Heart Cantina. It was a "trash-can-sized" droid that was propelled on wheels and equipped with extendable arms and a magnetic plate atop of its head with which to anchor drinks.
