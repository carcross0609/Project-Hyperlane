# Medical Unit AZ-01

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Medical Unit AZ-01](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_Unit_AZ-01)

Medical Unit AZ-01 was a medical droid used by Arlaia Zayzen, a gladiator in the Arena Grand of Zakuul. Along with other droids such as Combat Unit AZ-02, Advanced Combat Unit AZ-03, Assault Unit AZ-A and Assault Unit AZ-B, this droid was designated after Arlaia's own initials and assisted her during the fights. In 3630 BBY Arlaia was put up against a newcomer known as "the Mysterious Stranger," who defeated her along with Medical Unit AZ-01 in the first round of the Arena fights.
