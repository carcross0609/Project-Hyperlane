# AV-9R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AV-9R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AV-9R)

AV-9R was a protocol droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War. In 3639 BBY AV-9R was on the Carrick Station, where he suffered a mobility malfunction. While being repaired by technician Mua Niab, AV-9R relayed to an important figure a coded priority message from Supreme Chancellor Leontyne Saresh regarding the Battle of Denova.
