# Treadwell

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Treadwell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treadwell)

Treadwell was a WED 15 "Septoid 2" Treadwell droid who served the Millennium Falcon. Treadwell was acquired by Han Solo three years before the Battle of Hoth. He had previously been with some Jawas, and before that he had worked repairs on a Republic cruiser during the Clone Wars.
