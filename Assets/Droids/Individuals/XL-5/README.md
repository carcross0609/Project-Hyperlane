# XL-5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XL-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XL-5)

XL-5 was a war droid that was used by a scavenger crew to protect their operations during the early years of the New Republic. XL-5 was armed with a blaster rifle and a repeating blaster.
