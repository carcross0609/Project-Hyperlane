# 121-247

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [121-247](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/121-247)

121-247 was a security droid who resided on Anoat at the time of the Iron Blockade. During this time, Tam Bastion of the Alliance to Restore the Republic sent a young smuggler to disable the droid, and the smuggler succeeded in doing so.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 121-247
- **Death:** During the Iron Blockade
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Silver, blue faceplate
- **Armament:** Quarterstaff
