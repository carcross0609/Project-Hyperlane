# R4-Salvatron

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R4-Salvatron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-Salvatron)

The R4-Salvatron was a large salvage droid that worked in the Barasin Refuse Yards on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. Similar in design to the X2H-8 Overseer Droid, the R4-Salvatron was destroyed by Imperial forces.

- **Death:** 3643 BBY, Balmorra
- **Class:** Salvage droid
