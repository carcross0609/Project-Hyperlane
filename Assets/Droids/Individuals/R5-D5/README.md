# R5-D5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-D5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-D5)

R5-D5 was an R5 series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. In 9 BBY, the R-series astromech droid was operating on the Imperial-controlled planet Mapuzo, where it worked alongside the loader droid NED-B on a landing pad atop a flat mesa.
