# Beedee-Beedee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beedee-Beedee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beedee-Beedee)

Beedee-Beedee was an R-series astromech droid in service to the Resistance during its war with the First Order. Beedee-Beedee flew in the T-70 X-wing starfighter of Karé Kun during Black Squadron's mission to gather allies and bring new systems into the Resistance.
