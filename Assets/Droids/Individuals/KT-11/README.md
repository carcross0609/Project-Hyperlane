# KT-11

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [KT-11](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KT-11)

KT-11, sometimes referred to as KayTee, was a TK-11 Mining Droid manufactured by the SoroSuub Corporation. At one point, she and another identical mining droid, were present on a rocky world. KT-11 was equipped with an underbelly drill, as well as three grabber arms.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KT-11
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** TK-11 Mining Droid
- **Class:** Mining droid
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** Dark blue
- **Plating:** Brown, tan, and gold
- **Equipment:**
  - Mining drill
  - Grabbing arms
