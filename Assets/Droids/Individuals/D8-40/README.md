# D8-40

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D8-40](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D8-40)

D8-40 was a droid with a male programming who resided in the Anoat sector at the time of the Iron Blockade. A lover of romantic literature and tragic holo stories, he was also one of the best (and most unusual) safecrackers in the galaxy. Although he claimed to be many centuries old, his love of storytelling put everything he said in doubt.
