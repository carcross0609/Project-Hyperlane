# TA1313

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TA1313](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TA1313)

TA1313 was a T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid who was part of the Confederacy of Independent Systems' navy during the Clone Wars. In 22 BBY, the tactical droid served in the personal flotilla of Separatist leader Count Dooku. In that year, when the Separatist Droid Army launched an invasion of the planet Ryloth, TA1313 intercepted a coded transmission between the Jedi High Council and Senator Bail Organa of the Galactic Republic.
