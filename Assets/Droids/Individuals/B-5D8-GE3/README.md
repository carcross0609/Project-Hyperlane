# B-5D8-GE3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B-5D8-GE3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-5D8-GE3)

B-5D8-GE3 was a GE3-series protocol droid who worked for Vogga the Hutt as a clerk for his droid-run warehouse on Nar Shaddaa. He unwittingly allowed the warehouse to be infiltrated by T3-M4, who was looking for a way to save Meetra Surik.
