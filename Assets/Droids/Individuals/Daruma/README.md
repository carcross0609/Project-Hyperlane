# Daruma

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Daruma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daruma)

Daruma was a droid of the Jedi Order and served as a companion to the Padawan Nakime and her Jedi Master. At some point, the group traveled to an astronomical object where they encountered the Sith apprentice Aman in a lightsaber duel atop a mountain. During the battle, Nakime performed a handspring off of Daruma as he used his thrusters to boost her jump. The attempted attack allowed for Aman to slash the Padawan's eyes, incapacitating and blinding her. Later, separated from her group, Nakime experienced visions featuring her master and Daruma.
