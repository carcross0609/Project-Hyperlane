# Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Onderon)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Onderon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_%28Onderon%29)

An OOM command battle droid was the leader of Patrol 118 in the Separatist Droid Army. During the Onderonian Civil War, the droid led a patrol in the city of Iziz on Onderon when they were ambushed by the Onderon rebels. The droid called for reinforcements before it was destroyed by Steela Gerrera.
