# Station Guardian One

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Station Guardian One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Station_Guardian_One)

Station Guardian One was a battle droid developed by Kuat Drive Yards during the Galactic War. During the Battle of Kuat in 3637 BBY it was destroyed aboard the Kuat Defensive Station.
