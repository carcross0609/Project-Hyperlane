# MD-1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-1)

MD-1 was an MD-1 medical droid assigned to the Imperial Navy EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate. Its service carried over once the ship's crew mutined and took over control of the vessel. MD-1 was charged with overseeing the medical lab to handle pathological investigations, occasionally assisted by MD-2 and MD-3.
