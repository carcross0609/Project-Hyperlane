# 4-4TM

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [4-4TM](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/4-4TM)

4-4TM was a droid musician that served as a member of the Leisurenauts, an all-droid musical band that performed in Kaas City's Nexus Room cantina on Dromund Kaas during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire.

- **Type:** Musician
