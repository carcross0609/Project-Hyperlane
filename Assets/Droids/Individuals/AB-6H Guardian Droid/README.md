# AB-6H Guardian Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AB-6H Guardian Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AB-6H_Guardian_Droid)

AB-6H Guardian Droid was a battle droid used by the Shroud to guard the asteroid level of The Coil, his base on Obus VI. In 3630 BBY, the droid detected and engaged intruders, though it was destroyed by HK-55 and Z0-0M.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AB-6H Guardian Droid
- **Death:** 3630 BBY, the Coil above Obus VI
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
