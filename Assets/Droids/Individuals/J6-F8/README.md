# J6-F8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J6-F8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J6-F8)

J6-F8 was an RX-series pilot droid that piloted a transport shuttle operating out of Batuu's Black Spire Outpost. She ferried passengers from the MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser Halcyon down to the outpost and back up to the cruiser, but worked for an entity local to the outpost.
