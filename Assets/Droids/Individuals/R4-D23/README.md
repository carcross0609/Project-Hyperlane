# R4-D23

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-D23](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-D23)

R4-D23 was an R4 astromech droid operating on Coruscant. He was a typical R4, but also wore a blue cone-shaped hat with star and moon shapes in its design and included black "ears." He was known to frequent the local droid spa for oil baths and recharging.

- **Model:** R4 astromech droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid

- **Plating:** Silver, red, blue
