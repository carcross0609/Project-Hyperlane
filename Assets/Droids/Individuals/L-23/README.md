# L-23

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [L-23](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L-23)

L-23 was a protocol droid who belonged to Byrom and Ensa, two farmers living on the planet of Molavar. During one of the farmers' routine repair runs on their antiquated moisture vaporators, L-23 was sliced in half by Dark Jedi Lycan's lightsaber, as he approached them.
