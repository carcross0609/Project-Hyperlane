# R4-M1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-M1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-M1)

R4-M1 was an R4 astromech droid who served the Galactic Republic and Jedi Order during the Clone Wars. When Anakin Skywalker and Mace Windu were trapped in the wreckage of the Endurance at Vanqor, R2-D2, arriving at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant to play their distress message, jostled the R4 unit as he sped past.
