# G-3PO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [G-3PO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-3PO)

G-3PO was a 3PO protocol droid aboard a starship that was attacked by pirates. Along with several other droids, including Z-3PO and B-3PO, G-3PO discovered Pax Maripher, the only survivor of the attack. The protocol droids raised Maripher until he was rescued fifteen years later, whereupon the droids returned to their regular duties.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G-3PO

- **Model:** 3PO-series protocol droid
