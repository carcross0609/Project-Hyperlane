# Unidentified OOM pilot battle droid (Bothawui)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM pilot battle droid (Bothawui)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_pilot_battle_droid_%28Bothawui%29)

During the Clone Wars in 22 BBY, an OOM pilot battle droid served within the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The OOM pilot battle droid served aboard the bridge of the frigate of Kaleesh Separatist General Grievous during the time of the Battle of Bothawui, but was later destroyed during the battle at the hands of the Galactic Republic fleet of Jedi General Anakin Skywalker.
