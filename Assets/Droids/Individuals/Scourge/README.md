# Scourge

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scourge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scourge)

The Scourge, whose main body was also known as Scourge Prime, was a malevolent droid created from the union of the Spark Eternal and a droid intelligence once locked away by the Sith. In an effort to quell the hunger it felt, the Scourge spread itself into numerous other droids to learn what was in their memory banks, taking over their bodies and adding their minds to its collection in the process. While the Scourge was indeed a new being created by the union of the Spark and ancient droid, it once referred to itself as the same individual as the droid found by the Spark Eternal. However, on another occasion, the Scourge affirmed that the droid was simply a part of itself, much like the Spark and every mind it consumed.
