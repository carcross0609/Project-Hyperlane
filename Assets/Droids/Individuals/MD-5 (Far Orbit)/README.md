# MD-5 (Far Orbit)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-5 (Far Orbit)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-5_%28Far_Orbit%29)

MD-5 was an MD-5 medical droid assigned to the Imperial Navy EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate. Its service carried over once the ship's crew mutined and took over control of the vessel. MD-5 was the ship's general practitioner droid, performing regular check ups on the crew and minor surgical procedures. The droid was assisted by MD-0, who saw the patients and diagnosed them prior to MD-5 seeing them.
