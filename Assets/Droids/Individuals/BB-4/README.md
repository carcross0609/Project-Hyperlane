# BB-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BB-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BB-4)

BB-4 was a khaki-plated BB-series astromech droid in the service of the Resistance. Similarly to 2BB-2, another BB unit in the service of the Resistance, the droid's sensor was not fully encased in his head. BB-4 also had a Resistance crest on his body.
