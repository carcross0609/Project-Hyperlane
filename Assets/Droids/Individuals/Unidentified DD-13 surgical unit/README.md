# Unidentified DD-13 surgical unit

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified DD-13 surgical unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_DD-13_surgical_unit)

The droid was one of the supposedly rogue droids that attacked Constable Zuvio during his investigation of a banking ship robbery in Niima Outpost. As a part of Rikard Lovas's plot to steal the electronic credits of the citizens of Niima Outpost on Jakku, several droids were sent to an abandoned farmhouse to wait for Constable Zuvio. During the skirmish at the abandoned Jakku moisture farm was able to slash a clean cut across the Zuvio forearm. However during the fight it was pulled out of the second story window when the astromech B33's tow clamp became attached to it and Zuvio kicked the astromech out the window pulling the surgical droid with it.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified DD-13 surgical unit
- **Death:** Sometime before the Escape from Jakku in 34 ABY
- **Model:** DD-13 medical assistant droid
- **Armament:** Scalpel
