# NX-88

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [NX-88](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NX-88)

NX-88 was a droid member of the Ganguls pirate gang during the High Republic Era. NX acted as the pilot for the Ganguls' flagship, the Rathtar's Revenge, during the gang's raid on Deppani for starships following a storm. NX-88 was later present with the Ganguls during their takeover of Yarrum Tower.
