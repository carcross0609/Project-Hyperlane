# LXC-3TF

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LXC-3TF](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LXC-3TF)

LXC-3TF was as assassin droid that served alongside Jasta Finn during the early years of the New Republic. It was heavily armed with a proton torpedo launcher and a blaster cannon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LXC-3TF
- **Model:** Assassin droid
- **Height:** 2.3 meters
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Plating:** Matte black
- **Armament:**
  - Blaster cannon
  - Portable proton torpedo launcher
- **Affiliation:** Jasta Finn
