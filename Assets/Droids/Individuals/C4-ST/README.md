# C4-ST

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C4-ST](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C4-ST)

C4-ST was a protocol droid who operated as a merchant in the Outer Rim's Anoat sector during its blockade by the Galactic Empire in the aftermath of the Battle of Endor in 4 ABY. The droid joined the crew of the Smuggler, a young thief active in the sector during the Iron Blockade, who dispatched C4-ST on a variety of missions throughout the Anoat sector.
