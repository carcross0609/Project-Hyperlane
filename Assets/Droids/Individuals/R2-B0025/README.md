# R2-B0025

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-B0025](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-B0025)

R2-B0025 was an R2-series astromech droid that was present on the planet Corvus. The droid was painted in orange and black, with green and gold accents. They were equipped with a photoreceptor, radar eye, a cargo compartment, quick-release fuel system latch, and hydraulic arm shaft.
