# Cleaver

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cleaver](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cleaver)

"Cleaver" was a rebuilt 2JTJ personal navigation droid serving the Galactic Republic unit "Breakout Squad." Originally known as "Teejay," the droid was destroyed during the Mission to Kynachi and was rebuilt with parts from BX-series droid commandos by Breaker (CT-8863), a clone that got his name by recognizing a KynachTech circuit board, prompting him to be renamed Cleaver due to being adept with a shock stick.
