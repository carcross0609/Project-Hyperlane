# R3-S6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R3-S6/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-S6/Legends)

R3-S6 was a masculine programmed R3-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton on the planet Nubia around the time of the Clone Wars. On his homeworld, R3-S6 was stolen by a group of agents hired by the Confederate General Grievous. They took R3-S6 to the planet Milagro where the astromech droid underwent reprogramming to become a spy for Grievous.
