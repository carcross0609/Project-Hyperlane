# D-3P8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D-3P8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-3P8)

D-3P8 was an independent droid who ran a store on Ord Mynock. He was very persistent and would follow customers around his store until they purchased something. He was hard to bargain with and would stand firm on his prices, especially if he knew that he was the only place to get an item.

## Specifications

- **Name:** D-3P8
- **Homeworld:** Ord Mynock
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Affiliation:** Pandor's Hydrospanners
