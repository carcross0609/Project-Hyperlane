# Ceeril's bodyguard droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ceeril's bodyguard droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ceeril%27s_bodyguard_droid)

A bodyguard droid with masculine programming was owned by Ambassador Ceeril of Rion during the High Republic Era. While being treated in the medcenter of the Starlight Beacon space station, Ceeril used his bodyguard droid to stage an assassination attempt against himself, hoping to frame two Hassarians also being treated onboard as their species were long-time rivals of his own, the Skembo. The droid was destroyed in the process, but Administrator Velko Jahen and the astromech droid KC-78 discovered evidence in its remains which exonerated the Hassarians and proved Ceeril's guilt.
