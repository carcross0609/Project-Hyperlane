# DG-93

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DG-93](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DG-93)

DG-93 was a protocol droid who served the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War against the Sith Empire. In 3640 BBY, the droid greeted a group of Republic aligned individuals on the planet Ilum, who then traveled to take part in the Battle of Ilum.

- **Class:** Protocol droid
