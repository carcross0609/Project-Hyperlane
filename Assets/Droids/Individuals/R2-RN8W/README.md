# R2-RN8W

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-RN8W](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-RN8W)

R2-RN8W was a rainbow-colored astromech droid that enjoyed celebrating with others on the Core Worlds planet Coruscant. Its dome had primary photo receptors, radar eyes, a data card reader, and a Herner optical holographic projector. The front of its body had a Burtt acoustic signaler, system ventilation, hi-powered recharge coupling, and heat exhaust.
