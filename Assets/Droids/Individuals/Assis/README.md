# Assis

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Assis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assis)

Assis was a TDK-160 research-assistant droid. Sometime before the rescue of Galen Erso, the droid worked with a group of scientists notably including Galen and Lyra Erso on the planet Vallt.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Assis
- **Birth:** Prior to 21 BBY
- **Model:** TDK-160 research-assistant
