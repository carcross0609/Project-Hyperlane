# Lump

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lump](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lump)

Lump was an IG-series droid owned by the Ortolan shopkeeper Gamodar and used to protect his artifacts. Sometime between 19 BBY and 14 BBY, an acquaintance of Gamodar's, the former Jedi Iskat Akaris, accompanied by the Grand Inquisitor, came to remove Gamodar's collection. Lump was destroyed by Akaris while attempting to defend its master and his property.
