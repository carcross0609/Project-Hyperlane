# RC-3T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RC-3T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RC-3T)

RC-3T was a protocol droid who belonged to the Ithorian swooper Draknus during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, RC-1Z, along with RC-1Z and RC-2X, helped Draknus to manage the Mos Entha race track on the planet Tatooine.

## Specifications

- **Model:** LOM-series protocol droid
- **Class:** Protocol droid

- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:** Draknus
