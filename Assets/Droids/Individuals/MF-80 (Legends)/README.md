# MF-80

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MF-80/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MF-80/Legends)

MF-80 was Gardulla Besadii the Elder's protocol droid during the Clone Wars. When Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi and Quinlan Vos went to Nal Hutta in order to talk to the Hutt Grand Council, MF-80 was present and translated the conversation between her master and the two Jedi. Her metal plating had a two-color scheme.
