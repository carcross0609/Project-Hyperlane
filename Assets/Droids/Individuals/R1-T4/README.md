# R1-T4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R1-T4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R1-T4)

R1-T4 was an R1-series astromech droid produced by Industrial Automaton. R1-T4 was programmed with coded information pertaining to the location of Emperor Palpatine's hidden weapon stores. The droid was part of a cargo shipped to Janelle Serap on Seikosha from Simms Jonndril sometime after the Battle of Endor.
