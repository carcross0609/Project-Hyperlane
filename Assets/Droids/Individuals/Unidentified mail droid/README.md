# Unidentified mail droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified mail droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_mail_droid)

Around 9 ABY, an R-series astromech droid served as a mail droid at a New Republic skyscraper on the planet Coruscant. At one point that year, the droid bumped into a New Republic archival office employee while transporting a caddy of datacards, which the archival worker Penn Pershing then took. Later, the droid delivered another set of datacards to the office of Requisitions Colonel Tuttle, where it deposited it on his desk despite the colonel's protests.
