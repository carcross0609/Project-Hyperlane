# H2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [H2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H2)

H2 was a pilot droid for Dezono Qua, an Esseles native and sadistic cannibal. After Jedi Master Dass Jennir killed Qua, he took the droid for himself. Although willing to do Jennir's every bidding in their adventures in the coming months, H2 was outwardly dissatisfied with his new master's behavior, often spouting sarcastic remarks on Jennir's altruistic decisions and referring to him as "Man-who-killed-my-master." During this time, H2 covertly assisted bounty hunter Falco Sang — hired by Qua's family — with tracking Jennir down, using his security channel to communicate with him. This secret exchange came to an end at Telerath when H2 was critically damaged while delivering news from the mob controlled town to Fish's hut. After Jennir's victory over the Slavers, Jennir sought to repair the droid. Possibly due to the usage of number five memory cable splicer instead of the required number six affecting his personality modulators, the droid's acidic sarcasm was replaced by a cheerful personality. Furthermore, it fully accepted Jennir as his Master, something possibly aided by the amnesia it also suffered in its new state.
