# R3-Y2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R3-Y2/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-Y2/Legends)

R3-Y2 was a yellow-orange trimmed R3-series astromech droid who served the Rebel Alliance in its command center on the planet Hoth. R3-Y2 is part of the R3 series of astromechs and sports a clear dome. It has the same markings as R3-A2, who also worked at the Echo Base.
