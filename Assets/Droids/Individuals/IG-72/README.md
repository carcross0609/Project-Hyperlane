# IG-72

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IG-72](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG-72)

IG-72 was an IG-series assassin droid manufactured by Holowan Laboratories who worked as an assassin and a bounty hunter. By 9 ABY, the droid had self-destructed while taking part in the hunt for the Republic Era fugitive Adar Tallon on the planet Tatooine.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Bounty hunter
- **Name:** IG-72
- **Death:** By 9 ABY, Tatooine
- **Manufacturer:** Holowan Laboratories
- **Line:** IG-series
- **Class:** Assassin droid
