# GT-3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GT-3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GT-3)

GT-3 was a scout droid and a member of Gella Nattai's Pathfinder team which took rest of the expedition of Orvax by 382 BBY. After three days into the expedition, the equipment of the team disappeared. Gella Nattai sent GT-3 to survey the nearly forests but the droid disappeared too.
