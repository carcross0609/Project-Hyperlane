# A1-A1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [A1-A1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A1-A1)

A1-A1, also known as Ay-One-Ay-One, was a protocol droid who oversaw daily functions at the Barsha family's compound on the moon Gus Treta. The droid provided Jaylen Barsha with news archives so he could search for his missing brother Sliro Barsha.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A1-A1
- **Death:** 19 BBY, Gus Treta
- **Model:** Protocol droid
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
