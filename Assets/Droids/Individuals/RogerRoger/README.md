# RogerRoger

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [RogerRoger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RogerRoger)

"RogerRoger" was the online alias of a B1-series battle droid who served in the Clone Wars. At one point, he left a one star review of the All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport, citing the fact that it stomped on big metal legs as a reason for his disdain. The review was eventually published in a book written by Poe Dameron
