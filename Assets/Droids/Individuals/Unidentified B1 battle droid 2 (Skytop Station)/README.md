# Unidentified B1 battle droid 2 (Skytop Station)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 2 (Skytop Station)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_2_%28Skytop_Station%29)

This B1 battle droid lived on Skytop Station in 22 BBY. Another battle droid complained to him that his servo-motors were being corroded by the moisture of Ruusan 2, and in response this droid suggested that he to level eight to get his head adjusted. Just as they finished talking, the droids were both destroyed by clone troopers who were part of a Jedi-led clone strike team sent to destroy Skytop Station.
