# Unidentified B1 battle droid captain (Christophsis)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid captain (Christophsis)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_captain_%28Christophsis%29)

A B1-series battle droid served as a captain in the CIS Separatist Droid Army during the Clone Wars. The battle droid served within the army of General Whorm Loathsom and participated in the Battle of Christophsis. When a number of the army's Armored Assault Tanks were destroyed and prevented the others from moving forward, General Loathsom asked the captain why the tanks stopped. The captain then informed General Loathsom that it was a result of the Galactic Republic's AV-7 Anti-vehicle Artillery Cannons.
