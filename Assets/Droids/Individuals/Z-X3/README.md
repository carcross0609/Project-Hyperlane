# Z-X3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-X3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-X3)

Z-X3, also known as Zee-Exthree, was a Z-X3 experimental droid trooper created by Tagge Industries during the era of the Galactic Civil War. An experimental model rejected for service in the Imperial military, Z-X3 instead found a home on Kligson's Moon, an all-droid society led by a cyborg named Kligson. However, Z-X3 secretly harbored Imperial loyalty, and plotted to overthrow Kligson and seize "Droid World" in the Empire's name. Z-X3 finally made his move in 3 ABY, only to be outsmarted and defeated in the ensuing civil war.
