# Unidentified OOM pilot battle droid (Confederate space)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM pilot battle droid (Confederate space)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_pilot_battle_droid_%28Confederate_space%29)

An OOM pilot battle droid was on the bridge of General Grievous's Munificent-class star frigate during the Galactic Republic's mission to sweep outer corridor of space. During the battle Grievous ordered to launch all starfighters to combat Anakin Skywalker. The droid questioned this order, and stated that it did not believe every fighter was necessary to take out Skywalker, but ultimately followed this order.
