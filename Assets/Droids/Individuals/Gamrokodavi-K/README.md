# Gamrokodavi-K

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gamrokodavi-K](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamrokodavi-K)

Gamrokodavi-K was a Gree research droid dispatched by the starship Gray Secant to study the planet Hoth. It was accompanied by a squad of more heavily-armed Gree droids, and during the Cold War it stopped transmitting because of frost buildup on its transmission module until its repair systems repaired it, a process that took several dozen years.
