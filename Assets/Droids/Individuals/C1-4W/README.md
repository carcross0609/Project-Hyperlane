# C1-4W

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C1-4W](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C1-4W)

The C1-4W, also known as the C1-4W claw machine gonk droid, was a model of bipedal power droid available during the Third Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. A functional miniature version of this droid known as a "Replica C1-4W" also existed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C1-4W
- **Plating:**
  - Silver
  - Dark gray
- **Equipment:** Claw grabber
