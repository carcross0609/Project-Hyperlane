# SD

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SD](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD)

SD was a droid at Ania Solo's junk yard that served as Ania's security and enforcer, throwing out any unruly customers and protecting the property. It was destroyed by Darth Wredd, who was impersonating Yalta Val, when the imposter attempted to kill Ania and Sauk. Gesaw rats promptly began gnawing on its severed circuits, distracting the fake Val's thugs and allowing the pair to escape.
