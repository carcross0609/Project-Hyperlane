# Torch

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Torch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torch)

Torch was the astromech droid companion of rebel pilot and later Resistance pilot Venisa Doza. He accompanied her on many missions, including to the worlds Bakura and Lehon. During one of the initial training sessions that Venisa gave her daughter, Torra, as a starfighter pilot, Torra ejected Torch from his astromech socket. Torch was good friends with Jarek Yeager's astromech, Bucket.
