# JJ-EP9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JJ-EP9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JJ-EP9)

JJ-EP9 was a GNK-series power droid that fought in a droid battle in Galma on the Outer Rim planet Batuu sometime during the First Order-Resistance War. The battle was broadcast over the local radio station BSO 401.72 and resulted in a controversial victory for the droid R-3PO.
