# X-10

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [X-10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-10)

X-10 or Ex-Ten, was a droid member of the Nihil marauder organization during the time of the High Republic Era. When a number of Nihil, including Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, were stationed on Quantxi, the Junk Moon of Ord Mantell, X-10 remained aboard their vessel. After the Nihil on the moon came under attack from the resident Junk Mavens, Ro ordered the marauder Zagyar to have X-10 ready the starship.
