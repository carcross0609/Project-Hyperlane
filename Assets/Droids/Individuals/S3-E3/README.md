# S3-E3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S3-E3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S3-E3)

S3-E3 was a droid with masculine programming belonging to Celestra, an engineer who served the Galactic Republic as part of the Republic Corps of Engineers during the Cold War. He was stationed on the planet Ord Mantell during part of the war, where he aided Celestra and the Republic Army against the Mantellian Separatist Movement in the Separatist War.
