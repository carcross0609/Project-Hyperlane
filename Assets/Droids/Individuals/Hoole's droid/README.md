# Hoole's droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hoole's droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoole%27s_droid)

A droid served the researcher Hoole and his wards, Zak and Tash Arranda. At one point, when the two Arrandas became distracted after being tasked by Hoole with researching energy spiders, the droid went to remind them of their job. The two returned to Hoole's starship, but later slipped away during the night to investigate a nearby cave. While there, the Arrandas had an encounter with a group of cultists, but were able to escape. The droid subsequently found the two, questioning what they were doing.
