# Unidentified design droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified design droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_design_droid)

A droid assisted High Magistrate Greef Karga during a city planning. Around 9 ABY, The droid helped design and plan for Nevarro City but was cut short after an attack was made by Pirate King Gorian Shard.
