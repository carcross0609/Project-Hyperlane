# Fillik's PIP droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fillik's PIP droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fillik%27s_PIP_droid)

The meknek Fillik owned a PIP droid during the High Republic Era. Manufactured by Pui-ui Implement Products, it was eighteen centimeters (seven inches) tall, had black and orange plating, and was equipped with an internal carousel that included custom tools. Fillik did not coddle his PIP droid, unlike his colleague Verosha Aniseya, who was unusually tender toward her PIP droid, Pip.
