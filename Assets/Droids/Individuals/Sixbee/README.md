# Sixbee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sixbee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sixbee)

Sixbee was a droid who served the Colman family on Estarvera during the High Republic Era. In 228 BBY, the droid was destroyed by an autocart while attempting to return to Ryden and Calina Colman.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sixbee
- **Death:** 228 BBY, Estarvera
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Affiliation:** Colman family
