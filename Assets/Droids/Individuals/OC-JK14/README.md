# OC-JK14

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [OC-JK14](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OC-JK14)

OC-JK14, also known as Seejay, was an astromech droid who served the Resistance during its war against the First Order. It served at the Resistance base on the moon Ajan Kloss, where it could often be found underfoot while roaming the undergrowth looking to be productive. The droid quietly tended to low-priority maintenance jobs and crossed them off to-do lists that few ever looked at. In 35 ABY, OC-JK14 was among those in the base who gathered around the body of General Leia Organa to mourn her death.
