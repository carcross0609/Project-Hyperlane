# Unidentified blue Cyban Front droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified blue Cyban Front droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_blue_Cyban_Front_droid)

A droid represented the Cyban Front at the Auction of Rur with another Cyban Front droid. The two questioned the crystal's technopathy abilities and the host, Chelli Aphra, let the crystal possess it until her companion, Krrsantan, destroyed it. Another of Aphra's companion's, 0-0-0, then put the crystal inside it while it was being repaired and the droid was used to massacre other members of the auction. That was until Darth Vader intervened and destroyed it.
