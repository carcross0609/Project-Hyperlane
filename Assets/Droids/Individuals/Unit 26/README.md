# Unit 26

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unit 26](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unit_26)

Unit 26, also known as Unit 2-6, was a BX-series droid commando who participated in the takeover of Rishi Station during the Clone Wars along with other commando droids, such as Unit 07.
