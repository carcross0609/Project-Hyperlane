# Gonky (Takodana Castle)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gonky (Takodana Castle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gonky_%28Takodana_Castle%29)

Gonky was a droid owned by a human as of 5 ABY. According to her owner, she loved roaming around, giving advice and having oil baths. Around 5 ABY, Gonky went missing. Her owner was distraught about her disappearance, and vented to another patron at Takodana Castle about his sadness.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gonky
- **Pronouns:** She/her
