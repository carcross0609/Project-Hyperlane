# GG-36

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GG-36](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GG-36)

GG-36 or Gigi was a GG-series hospitality droid on Telerath around 3963 BBY. The droid greeted the disguised Jarael and Camper upon their arrival and explained the banking planet and its resemblance to a vacation resort. Gigi later got a job working for Gryph's restaurant on Coruscant.
