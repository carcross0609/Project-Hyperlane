# B6-31

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B6-31](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B6-31)

B6-31 was an astromech droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War with the reconstituted Sith Empire. In 3639 BBY it was stationed in the Section X area of Belsavis, where he asked a Republic operative to check the wreckage of the Fatality cruiser in the Frozen Lake.
