# Unidentified BX-series droid commando 2 (The Citadel)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando 2 (The Citadel)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_2_%28The_Citadel%29)

This droid commando was stationed inside the Citadel. During a skirmish at the landing platform following the successful rescue of Even Piell and his men who were being held prisoner at the Citadel by a republic rescue team led by Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, the commando droid along with several others rushed outside to confront the intruders. However, all of this droid's comrades were destroyed. After surviving, the droid threw aside his shield and mounted a nearby turret in a bid to destroy the rescue team's only mode of transportation, their shuttle. He was met under fire by Skywalker and Piell on a hijacked STAP, being forced to fire back. The droid successfully destroyed the STAP but not Skywwalker and Piell who both survived. Afterwards, the droid continued his original intent to destroy the shuttle, which was protected by ARC Trooper Echo, who was using one of the commando droids' personal energy shields. Ultimately, the commando droid succeeded at destroying the shuttle with the turret and apparently killing Echo, only to ironically kill himself as well when a piece of the shuttle's debris crushed the turret he was in, causing a huge explosion.
