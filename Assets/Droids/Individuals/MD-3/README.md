# MD-3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-3)

MD-3 was an MD-3 medical droid assigned to the Imperial Navy EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate. Its service carried over once the ship's crew mutined and took over control of the vessel. MD-3 was responsible for operating the medical bay's bacta tanks, and operated the pharmacy. The droid also assisted MD-1 on occasion with duties in the medical lab.
