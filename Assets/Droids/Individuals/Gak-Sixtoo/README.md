# Gak-Sixtoo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gak-Sixtoo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gak-Sixtoo)

Gak-Sixtoo was a nav droid who served the Hutt Bokku, operating aboard Bokku's barge. When Bokku guided the Sith Lord Darth Vader and the Sith assassin Ochi to Zee-Nine City Seven in order to locate the carbonite-frozen smuggler Han Solo, Gak-Sixtoo piloted the vessel, communicating with Ochi as he and Vader disembarked.
