# M0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M0)

M0 managed the droid cantina The Resistor on the planet Plazir-15 in 9 ABY. When Mandalorians Din Djarin and Bo-Katan Kryze arrived at the cantina to investigate a suspicious spark pad carried by one of Plazir's malfunctioning droids, the bartender droid attempted to dissuade them from investigating its patrons. With Djarin turning violent, the bartender ultimately pleaded with the Mandalorians to allow them and their patrons to assist them with their work since they had nothing but the well-being of their organic creators in mind. Djarin accepted the offer and the bartender successfully identified the common factor between all malfunctioning droids; a spiked batch of Nepenthé which had been laced with nano-droids to reprogram the droids.
