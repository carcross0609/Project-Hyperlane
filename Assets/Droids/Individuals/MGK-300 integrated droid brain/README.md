# MGK-300 integrated droid brain

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MGK-300 integrated droid brain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MGK-300_integrated_droid_brain)

The MGK-300 integrated droid brain was a droid brain installed aboard Imperial TIE/rb starfighters for pilot support. One was on Han Solo's TIE/rb heavy starfighter while serving the Empire in Onyx Squadron under the callsign Onyx Nine. The droid was upset when Solo disobeyed command and flew alone to save his friend from pirates and almost destroyed their fighters too.
