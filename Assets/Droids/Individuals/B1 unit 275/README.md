# B1 unit 275

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B1 unit 275](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1_unit_275)

B1 Unit 275 was a B1-series battle droid that served the Confederacy of Independent Systems on the planet Akiva, where a droid factory produced units for the Separatist cause until an attack by the Galactic Republic. 275 was destroyed, and their remains were left in the factory. In 3 ABY, ND-5 was shot by an Imperial, causing his memory to malfunction and to call B1 unit 275 to respond upon entering the facility.
