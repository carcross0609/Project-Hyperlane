# FR3-D0M

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FR3-D0M](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FR3-D0M)

FR3-D0M was a battle droid that was part of Mantellian Separatist Movement stationed on Kessan's Landing, a small and forgotten island on Ord Mantell during the third Galactic War. FR3-D0M was reprogrammed by the Mantellian Separatists to spread their propaganda and eliminate anyone who opposed them.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FR3-D0M
- **Death:** c. 3624 BBY, Ord Mantell
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Gray and yellow
- **Equipment:**
  - Arm-mounted blaster cannon
  - Small blaster turret
  - 2 high-powered blaster cannon
- **Affiliation:** Mantellian Separatist Movement
