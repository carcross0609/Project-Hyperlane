# Beepers

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beepers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beepers)

Beepers was an astromech droid from the Old Republic-era. During the High Republic Era, he served the droid mechanic Rek Minuu before learning of Minuu's thievery of droid parts. Beepers left Minuu and joined the Jedi Initiates Kai Brightstar, Nubs, and Lys Solay at Dotti's Droid Workshop—alongside fellow astromechs Dozer and Gigi—sometime between 232 BBY and 230 BBY. At the workshop, Dotti replaced Beepers' old vocabulator with a new one.
