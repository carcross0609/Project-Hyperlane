# Ee-Seventeen

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ee-Seventeen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ee-Seventeen)

Ee-Seventeen was an astromech droid working at the Yarvellian Institute medcenter on Protobranch during the High Republic Era. After the Nihil Minister of Advancement Baron Boolan arrived at the medcenter delivering a supposed cure for the Blight, a computer virus began to corrupt internal systems throughout the facility. Ee-Seventeen was connected to the internal systems via scomp link, and was encouraged to disconnect by a reception droid to keep from being infected. He, however, became increasingly concerned as a speeder bike operated by Twi'lek bounty hunter Lourna Dee rapidly sped towards the institute's entrance. Although the droid attempted to lock the doors, he was too slow, and Dee crashed her bike through the glass.
