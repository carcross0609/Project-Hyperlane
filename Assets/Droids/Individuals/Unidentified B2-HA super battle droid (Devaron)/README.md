# Unidentified B2-HA super battle droid (Devaron)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified B2-HA super battle droid (Devaron)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B2-HA_super_battle_droid_%28Devaron%29)

This B2-HA super battle droid was part of Nashtah Team under the command of the bounty hunter Aurra Sing during Cad Bane's attack on the Temple of Eedit on Devaron. It was unable to differentiate between Sing and Asajj Ventress, which led to one of its B1 colleagues calling him a "glitch-head."
