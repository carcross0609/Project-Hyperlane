# AK-1A "Hammer"

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AK-1A "Hammer"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AK-1A_%22Hammer%22)

AK-1A "Hammer" was a battle droid used by HK-47 in his attempts to take over the planet of Mustafar. It was armed with two laser cannons and an arsenal of missiles. It was destroyed by a group of spacers who went on to defeat HK-47.
