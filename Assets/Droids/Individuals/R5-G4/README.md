# R5-G4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-G4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-G4)

R5-G4 was an astromech droid belonging to Norra Wexley, a pilot of the Rebel Alliance and later the New Republic. R5-G4 was with Norra at the Battle of Endor in her Y-wing during the Alliance's attack against the second Death Star, but was destroyed by enemy fire.
