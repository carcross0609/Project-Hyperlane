# Unidentified assassin probe 2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified assassin probe 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_assassin_probe_2)

An assassin probe was one of three smuggled onto the Coronet during its voyage to Coruscant, to assassinate Duchess Satine Kryze. It reached the dining level of the Coronet, where it was destroyed by Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi.
