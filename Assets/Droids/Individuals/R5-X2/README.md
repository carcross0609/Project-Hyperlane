# R5-X2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-X2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-X2)

R5-X2 was an R5 series astromech droid who served in the criminal empire of Jabba Desilijic Tiure. The Hutt crime lord, who also acted as the grandmaster host of the Boonta Eve Classic, used the astromech droid as a high-speed calculator to determine betting odds for podraces.
