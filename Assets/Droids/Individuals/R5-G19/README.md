# R5-G19

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-G19](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-G19)

R5-G19 was an R5 series astromech droid that served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War against the Galactic Empire. In 4 ABY, the astromech droid was stood beside another astromech in the briefing room of the MC80 Star Cruiser Home One when members of the Alliance leadership, including Admiral Gial Ackbar, Mon Mothma, and General Crix Madine, gave a briefing to the assembled rebels on their plans to attack the Empire's Death Star II superweapon in the Endor system. The Home One later took part in the successful attack.
