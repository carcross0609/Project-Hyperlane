# 8R-UH

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [8R-UH](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/8R-UH)

8R-UH was an astromech droid who served the Resistance on the planet Ajan Kloss during the First Order-Resistance War. The droid was one of five astromechs who worked under R5-2JE and who were all absent from their jobs at the landing pad one day. 8R-UH was sunbathing atop a nearby cliff, seeking to soak up every last ray of sunshine before returning to work. After being interrupted from sunbathing, the 8R-UH remarked that this had been his "worst tropical vacation ever."
