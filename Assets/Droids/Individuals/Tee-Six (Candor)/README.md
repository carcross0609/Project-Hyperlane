# Tee-Six (Candor)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tee-Six (Candor)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tee-Six_%28Candor%29)

Tee-Six was a droid that accompanied Amilyn Holdo, a former senator apprentice, aboard the Rebel Alliance arms–smuggling ship Candor sometime between around 2 BBY and 4 ABY. Tee-Six helped Holdo with tasks such as watering the ship's plants and checking its systems, even though its work did not always satisfy Holdo. When the ship was attacked by an Imperial Star Destroyer, Holdo asked the droid about what had happened to the lights, but Tee-Six gave no answer. After the attack, the Candor was able to escape with few casualties thanks to Holdo's command skills.
