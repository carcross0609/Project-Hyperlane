# R5-B0019

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-B0019](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-B0019)

R5-B0019 was an R5 series astromech droid that once spent time among the droids carried inside a Jawa sandcrawler on Tatooine. The droid was colored in black and white, and included a Burtt acoustic signaller.
