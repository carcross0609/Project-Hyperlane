# SR-78 Bulwark Defense Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SR-78 Bulwark Defense Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SR-78_Bulwark_Defense_Droid)

The SR-78 Bulwark Defense Droid was a three-legged battle droid used by the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. The deactivated SR-78 was stationed at the Shroud's factory on the moon Nar Shaddaa.
