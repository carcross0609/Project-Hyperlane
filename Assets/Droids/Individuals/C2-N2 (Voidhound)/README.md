# C2-N2 (Voidhound)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C2-N2 (Voidhound)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C2-N2_%28Voidhound%29)

C2-N2 was a Hollis-series steward droid owned by the famous smuggler known as Voidhound. C2-N2 was assigned to the smuggler's XS stock light freighter as its steward in 3643 BBY, and was responsible for maintaining the starship and ensuring that the crew remained comfortable.
