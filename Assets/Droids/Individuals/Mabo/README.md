# Mabo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mabo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mabo)

Mabo was an MA-B0 cargo lifter droid who joined the Church of the Force. He was a believer and a pilgrim who believed in the Force. Mabo traveled to the planet Christophsis with four other pilgrims to follow the orders of Brin Izisca and return a crate of kyber crystals back to where they belonged. On the twelfth day Mabo was destroyed when he fell down in a big hole of the cave.

## Specifications

- **Death:** 5 ABY, Christophsis
- **Model:** MA-B0 cargo lifter droid
