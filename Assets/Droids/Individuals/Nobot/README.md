# Nobot

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nobot](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nobot)

Nobot was a silver-plated protocol droid with white sensors. In 32 BBY, the droid was walking down a street on the edge of the Mos Espa spaceport on the planet Tatooine when Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn arrived in the city with Queen Padmé Amidala and the Gungan Jar Jar Binks. The trio walked past Nobot, who had burns down the right side of its body that scarred its plating and right photoreceptor black.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nobot
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Silver (charred black)
