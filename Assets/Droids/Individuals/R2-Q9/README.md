# R2-Q9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-Q9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-Q9)

R2-Q9 was an astromech droid who served onboard the Death Star II battle station in 4 ABY. The droid regularly made trouble for the stormtrooper TK-423, locking them out of their quarters, running over their feet, and even threatening them with an arc welder.
