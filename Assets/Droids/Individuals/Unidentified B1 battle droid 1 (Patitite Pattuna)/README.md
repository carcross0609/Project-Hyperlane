# Unidentified B1 battle droid 1 (Patitite Pattuna)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 1 (Patitite Pattuna)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_1_%28Patitite_Pattuna%29)

This battle droid was stationed onboard General Grievous's Providence-class carrier/destroyer at the time of the Battle of Patitite Pattuna. Some time after the battle, while the ship was traveling through space, he notified Grievous that an unknown pirate ship had been detected. General Grievous then ordered that the ship will be used for target practice and opened fire.
