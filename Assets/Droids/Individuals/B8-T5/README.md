# B8-T5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B8-T5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B8-T5)

B8-T5, nicknamed "Bait," was a safari droid that belonged to creature cartographer, Ardis San Tekka. B8-T5 would often reluctantly lure creatures out of hiding for San Tekka, as she wrote her book of various creatures in the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B8-T5"Bait"
- **Class:** Safari droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts
