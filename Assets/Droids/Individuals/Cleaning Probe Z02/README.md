# Cleaning Probe Z02

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cleaning Probe Z02](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cleaning_Probe_Z02)

Cleaning Probe Z02 was a cleaning droid used in Fa'athra's Palace on Nal Hutta during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY Cy Krolo and his partner who worked for Suudaa Nem'ro got the droid out of the palace and then planted transmitters on the remaining droid to gather information on Voontara Fa'athra.

- **Name:** Cleaning Probe Z02
- **Line:** Cleaning droid
- **Pronouns:** new

- **Affiliation:** Voontara Fa'athra
