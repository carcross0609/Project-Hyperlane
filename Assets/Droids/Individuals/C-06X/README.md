# C-06X

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C-06X](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-06X)

C-06X was a battle droid unit created by the Colicoid Creation Nest during the Cold War. It was acquired by the Sith Empire during the Cold War to serve as a courier for Darth Malgus and inform Imperial heroes of the upcoming Colicoid war games.
