# R2-F2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-F2/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-F2/Legends)

R2-F2 was an R2-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton that served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic, though it was suspected of being a double agent. The astromech droid was assigned to Biggs Darklighter, who flew as Red Three during the Battle of Yavin. Both Darklighter and R2-F2 were killed when their T-65B X-wing starfighter was shot down by Darth Vader.
