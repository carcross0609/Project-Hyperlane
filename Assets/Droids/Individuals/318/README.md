# 318

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [318](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/318)

318 was a medical droid who worked at a medical tent in Tyne's Horky, a mining town located on the planet of the same name. In the year 15 BBY, it was requested over the tent's public address system that it report to the emergency sick bay.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 318
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Affiliation:** Tyne's Horky medical tent
