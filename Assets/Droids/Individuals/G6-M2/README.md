# G6-M2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G6-M2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G6-M2)

G6-M2 was a shopkeeper droid served the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War with the Galactic Republic. It was stationed aboard the Black Talon, a transport in service to the Imperial Navy, when it attacked the Republic warship Brentaal Star. During the attack, the droid sold stimulants and medpacs to Imperial personnel.
