# QC-ME

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [QC-ME](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QC-ME)

QC-ME, also known as Q-See, was a droid that worked in the morgue of a Coruscant Security Force facility during the time of the Galactic Republic. When the body of a Trandoshan, who worked for the crime lord Cenevax in a plot to gain profit from the sabotage of the racer Ramil during the forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally, was brought to the morgue, the corrupt Inspector Sartori visited QC-ME to cover up the crime. The Jedi Dooku and Qui-Gon Jinn entered the morgue to investigate for themselves, and Dooku destroyed QC-ME with his lightsaber, as part of an act to intimidate Sartori into fleeing to Cenevax.
