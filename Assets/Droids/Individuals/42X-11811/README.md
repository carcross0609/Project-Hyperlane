# 42X-11811

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [42X-11811](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/42X-11811)

42X-11811 was a Separatist B2-HA super battle droid created by Baktoid Combat Automata that was reprogrammed by the Gen'Dai Rayvis to serve the Bedlam Raiders in 9 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Criminal
- **Name:** 42X-11811
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Combat Automata
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.93 meters (6 ft, 4 in)
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Armament:**
  - Wrist blaster (right arm)
  - Integrated rocket launcher (left arm)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Bedlam Raiders
