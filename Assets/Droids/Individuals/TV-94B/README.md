# TV-94B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TV-94B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TV-94B)

TV-94B was a T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid that was used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. He was assigned to General Grievous as a replacement for TV-94. He oversaw the space portion of the Battle of Kamino.
