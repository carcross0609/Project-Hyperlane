# Unidentified enforcer droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified enforcer droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_enforcer_droid)

An enforcer droid served the Chance Encounters casino on the space station known as Station Zeta. After the Jedi Arath Tarrex, who was sent to Station Zeta as a diplomat, gained debts to the casino by cheating, the droid was sent to collect the money from him. When Tarrex failed to pay, the droid threatened and attacked him, and Tarrex fought back, damaging the droid. The Jedi Padawan Qui-Gon Jinn spotted Tarrex attacking the droid, and Tarrex claimed that the droid had tried to rob him. The droid attempted to protest, but his vocabulator failed, and Tarrex sent the damaged droid away.
