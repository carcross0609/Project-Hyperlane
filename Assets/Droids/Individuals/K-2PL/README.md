# K-2PL

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [K-2PL](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-2PL)

K-2PL, abbreviated K-P or K-2 was a droid who served the Moldwarp thief and collector Draiven Bosh alongside the astromech droid R4-13 during the High Republic Era. The droid had yellow sensors and silver-bronze plating.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Criminal
- **Name:** K-2PL
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Silver and bronze
