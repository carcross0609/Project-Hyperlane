# S-0D3-GE3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S-0D3-GE3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-0D3-GE3)

S-0D3-GE3 was the designation of a GE3-series protocol droid that was active during the time known as the Dark Wars. The droid was owned by a Bith male named Bahima—the bartender of the cantina in the city of Iziz on the planet Onderon—and was used as a waiter for the cantina. S-0D3-GE3 discovered the corpse of the murdered Captain Sullio shortly after the incident and was destroyed by Sullio's murderer moments after his discovery. Meetra Surik, an exile of the Jedi Order, discovered S-0D3-GE3's head while investigating the murder of Sullio in hopes of clearing Doctor Dhagon Ghent of the crime. With the help of a Twi'lek patron of the cantina named Kiph, Surik was able to extract a recording of the droid's last moments from its memory banks, which she used to prove Ghent's innocence.
