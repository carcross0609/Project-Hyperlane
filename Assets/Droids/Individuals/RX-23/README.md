# RX-23

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RX-23](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RX-23)

RX-23 was an RX-series pilot droid who operated during the Galactic Civil War. At one point, the droid flew a GX1 short hauler from Iego, but the ship was hijacked by R2-D2 to fly to Gallios to find and recruit 0-0-0 for D-Squad.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RX-23
- **Model:** RX-series pilot droid
- **Class:** Pilot droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Red and white
