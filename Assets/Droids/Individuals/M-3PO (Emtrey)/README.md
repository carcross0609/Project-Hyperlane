# M-3PO (Emtrey)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M-3PO (Emtrey)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-3PO_%28Emtrey%29)

M-3PO, also known as Emtreypio or Emtrey, was a masculine M-3PO military protocol droid that served as the quartermaster of Rogue Squadron. Cobbled together from parts of other military protocol droids, he served the Rogues from their reformation in 6 ABY through to the Yuuzhan Vong War. Because of his improvised nature, he had a protocol droid's body and the head of a spaceport control droid.
