# C-RG9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C-RG9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-RG9)

C-RG9 was a battle droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War. During the Battle of Ilum in 3640 BBY it was used to guard the war room of Supreme Commander Rans, who planned a retaliation strike against Darth Malgus' New Empire.
