# MT-7T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MT-7T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MT-7T)

MT-7T was a public relations droid that worked for the corporation Renew on the planet Korad, during the time of the Old Republic. When Renew went bankrupt, the corporation's staff abandoned the world, and as there was only one vessel available for evacuation purposes, the droid was left behind on Korad.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MT-7T
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** Renew
