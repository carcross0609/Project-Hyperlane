# M-6NK

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M-6NK](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-6NK)

M-6NK, nicknamed Monk, was a bartender droid who worked at Pyloon's Saloon on the planet Koboh as the business partner of the Latero Greez Dritus. Monk was known for keeping a close eye on the patrons of the saloon and for his extensive knowledge of beverages.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M-6NK"Monk"
- **Model:** Bartender droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** White and yellow
- **Equipment:**
  - A small tank filled with alcoholic beverage
  - 2 arms as dispensers
- **Affiliation:** Pyloon's Saloon
