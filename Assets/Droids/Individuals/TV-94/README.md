# TV-94

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TV-94](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TV-94)

TV-94 was a T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid who served within the Confederacy of Independent Systems' naval branch, under the command of Kaleesh General Grievous, during the Clone Wars. Stationed aboard Grievous's Recusant-class light destroyer flagship, in 21 BBY, the tactical droid assisted his commander in an attack on the Steadfast, a Galactic Republic Venator-class Star Destroyer utilized as the flagship of Jedi General Eeth Koth, near the Saleucami system. Resulting in his capture, the Jedi Master was brought to the planet Saleucami by the Kaleesh, with TV-94 accompanying the general aboard his destroyer, where they set a trap to lure the Republic to the Saleucami.
