# GR-18

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GR-18](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GR-18)

GR-18 was a droid that operated during the time of the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the resurgent Sith Empire. The droid was present on Gravity Hook Seven during the Conquest of Makeb.
