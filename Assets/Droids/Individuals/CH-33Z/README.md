# CH-33Z

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CH-33Z](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CH-33Z)

CH-33Z was a MSE-6 series repair droid. At one point, the Chadra-Fan Vosa stood atop the MSE series droid at the Day Star Hotel while she spoke to her spy, Faddey. In the era of the New Republic, CH-33Z was scurrying around the halls of the New Republic Correctional Transport Bothan-5 when he was shot and destroyed by Burg, a member of Ranzar Malk's crew of mercenaries who were there to rescue the prisoner Qin. CH-33Z's destruction alerted N5 sentry droids to the crew's presence.
