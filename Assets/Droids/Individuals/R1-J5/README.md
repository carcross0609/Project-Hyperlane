# R1-J5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R1-J5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R1-J5)

R1-J5, commonly known as "Bucket," was an R-series astromech droid owned by Jarek Yeager, the former Rebellion pilot. R1-J5 raced with Yeager, serving as his co-pilot before the pair retired to the Colossus refueling platform and began Jarek Yeager's Repair Station and formed Team Fireball. R1-J5 worked alongside mechanics Tamara Ryvora and Neeku Vozo and Resistance spy Kazuda Xiono, who was masquerading as a mechanic. R1-J5 helped repair starships in the garage, the Fireball in particular.
