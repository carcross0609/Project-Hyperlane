# KLE-0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KLE-0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KLE-0)

KLE-0, nicknamed Cleo, was a bounty droid who worked for the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. They were the companion droid of the Finau brothers, Masi and Fenn, assisting them in every facet of their bounty hunting profession. The droid was destroyed by Cal Kestis.
