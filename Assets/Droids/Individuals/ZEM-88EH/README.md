# ZEM-88EH

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ZEM-88EH](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZEM-88EH)

ZEM-88EH was an elite Skytrooper, a type of battle droid used by the Eternal Empire of the planet Zakuul, that was among the Skytrooper forces deployed to board Darth Marr's flagship during a battle with the Eternal Fleet in 3636 BBY. The droid was destroyed in battle with an enemy combatant.
