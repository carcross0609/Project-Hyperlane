# Unidentified spaceport police droid (Cybloc Transfer Station)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified spaceport police droid (Cybloc Transfer Station)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_spaceport_police_droid_%28Cybloc_Transfer_Station%29)

A spaceport police droid was stationed aboard the Cybloc Transfer Station during the Clone Wars. In 19 BBY, the station was raided by the Sith Lord Darth Maul and his brother, Savage Opress. While the droid attempted to halt the intruders, he was instead destroyed by Maul.
