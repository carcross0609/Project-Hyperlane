# Iron Assembler

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iron Assembler/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Assembler/Legends)

The Iron Assembler was a rogue Vulture-class droid starfighter active in the Naboo system during the Galactic Civil War. This droid was a member of the Scintilla Scavengers, a group of rogue droid starfighters that mined iron asteroids in the Naboo system.

- **Name:** Iron Assembler
- **Model:** Vulture-class starfighter
- **Class:** Droid starfighter

- **Plating:** Silver
