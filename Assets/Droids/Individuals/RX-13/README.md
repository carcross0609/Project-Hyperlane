# RX-13

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RX-13](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RX-13)

RX-13, also known as Crash, was an RX-series pilot droid owned by Sirona Okeefe. Crash worked as Okeefe's co-pilot aboard the Banshee and had a tendency to complain just about everything rather than doing his chores. He participated in an early Alderaanian Resistance mission to Felucia to rescue Admiral Gilder Varth.
