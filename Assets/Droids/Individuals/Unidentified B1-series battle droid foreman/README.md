# Unidentified B1-series battle droid foreman

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid foreman](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_foreman)

A reprogrammed B1-series battle droid worked as a foreman on Plazir-15 during the New Republic Era. The droid oversaw a number of B2-series super battle droids that had been reprogrammed for load lifting. Upon seeing reports that droids had been malfunctioning on Plazir-15, the foreman had the B2 loaders undergo maintenance, which was certified. After he did so, he was questioned by Lady Bo-Katan Kryze as Din Djarin interfered with some of the B2 battle droids. After answering some of Kryze's questions, the droid warned Djarin against interfering with the droids as, although they had been re-certified, they still had been originally designed for warfare and could be dangerous. Djarin then pushed a B2 droid down, who then fled and was attacked by Kryze and Djarin, the latter of whom immediately chased after the B2. Kryze mocked the foreman's claims that the loaders had been re-certified, and the droid again repeated that Djarin should not have interfered with the loader as Kryze joined Djarin's pursuit.
