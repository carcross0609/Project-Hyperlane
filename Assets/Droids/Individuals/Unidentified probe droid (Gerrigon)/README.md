# Unidentified probe droid (Gerrigon)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified probe droid (Gerrigon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_probe_droid_%28Gerrigon%29)

A probe droid of the Viper probe droid model was a member of the Galactic Empire. Between 14 BBY and 9 BBY, the probe droid undertook a mission to the astronomical object Gerrigon with the Fifth Brother inquisitor. The droid explained the history of Gerrigon before they were attacked by the Jedi Knight, Tensu Run.
