# T-9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-9)

T-9, or Tee-Nine, or Teenine, was a cam droid owned by Rhil Dairo, reporter for GoNet during the High Republic Era. The droid assisted Dairo in reporting on the events of the Republic Fair on the planet Valo, and was present for the subsequent attack by Nihil marauders. During the attack, T-9 was shot by the Nihil Quin Amarant, but was repaired by the Jedi archivist OrbaLin.
