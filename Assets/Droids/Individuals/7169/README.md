# 7169

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [7169](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/7169)

7169 was a B1-series battle droid who served in the Trade Federation's Droid Army. In 32 BBY, they were part of the forces deployed to the city of Theed on the planet Naboo by the Federation during its invasion of the world. 7169 was part of a group of battle droids led by the OOM command battle droid 2163 that were tasked with guarding the Naboo Royal Starship in the Theed Hangar.
