# SD-357

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SD-357](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-357)

SD-357 was a police droid that was active during the Clone Wars, serving at the Cybloc Transfer Station belonging to the InterGalactic Banking Clan. It was destroyed by the Sith Lord Darth Maul when he and his brother Savage Opress raided it and eliminated all the droids that faced them.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Corporate
- **Name:** SD-357
- **Death:** 19 BBY, Cybloc Transfer Station
- **Model:** Spaceport police droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Height:** 1.78 meters
- **Affiliation:** InterGalactic Banking Clan
