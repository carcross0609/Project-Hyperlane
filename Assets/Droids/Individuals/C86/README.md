# C86

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C86](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C86)

C86 was a battle droid in service of the Sith Empire during the Cold War. On Alderaan, C86 was part of a detachment sent to disable the defenses of a House Ulgo fortress, but the unit met heavy resistance and all except C86 were destroyed. Badly damaged, C86 asked an Imperial hero to avenge his fallen comrades and finish the mission before deactivating.
