# Unidentified TC-series protocol droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified TC-series protocol droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_TC-series_protocol_droid)

A TC-series protocol droid was part of the droid pool aboard the Super Star Destroyer Executor by 3 ABY. This TC-series droid was requisitioned to welcome the bounty hunters who arrived aboard the Executor during the Hoth campaign to participate in Darth Vader's hunt for Han Solo and the Millennium Falcon.
