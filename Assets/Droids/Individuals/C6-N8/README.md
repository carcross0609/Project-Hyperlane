# C6-N8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C6-N8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C6-N8)

C6-N8 was a protocol droid active during the Cold War. It could be found on Alderaan. In 3643 BBY, the droid tasked a traveling Republic affiliated individual with helping its master, Selia Antares, defuse a House Ulgo bomb on a transport ship. The hero succeeded, and was able to return the defusal codes to Antares.
