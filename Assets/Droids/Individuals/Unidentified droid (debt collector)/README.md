# Unidentified droid (debt collector)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid (debt collector)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_%28debt_collector%29)

A droid owned and operated a moisture farm on desert planet Tatooine. By 3 ABY, they were unable to pay the water tax to the Hutt Cartel crime syndicate after a group of Jawas had stole parts of his equipment.
