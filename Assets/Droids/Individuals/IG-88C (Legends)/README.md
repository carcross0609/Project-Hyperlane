# IG-88C

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IG-88C/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG-88C/Legends)

IG-88C was an IG-88 series assassin droid. He was third to be activated by IG-88A and thus was given the designation "C". He and IG-88D acted as sentries for Mechis III.
