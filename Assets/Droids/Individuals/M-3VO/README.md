# M-3VO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M-3VO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-3VO)

M-3VO, nicknamed Meevo, was a droid who was a member of Zero Company during the Clone Wars who piloted their starship the Caisson and served as the crew's medic at their base, the Den.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M-3VO
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Green, red and black
- **Affiliation:** Zero Company
