# Battle droid 513

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle droid 513/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_droid_513/Legends)

Battle droid 513 was kept by the Mandalorian Death Watch for target practice. This particular droid was created from the cobbled remains of a Retail Caucus LR-57 combat droid, a standard battle droid, and a LM-432 crab droid, with a deep Vocabulator.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Battle droid 513
- **Death:** c. 21 BBY, Carlac
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Retail Caucus
  - Techno Union
  - Baktoid Combat Automata
- **Class:** Junk droid
- **Height:** 3.39 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** Twin blaster cannons
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Death Watch (prisoner)
  - R2-D2
