# J-DH3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-DH3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-DH3)

J-DH3 was a Haxion Brood Bounty Droid who served the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. Once a loadlifter droid, the Haxion Brood reprogrammed the droid and enhanced J-DH3 with salvaged parts. They had yellow-and-blue plating, white sensors, and a built-in wrist blaster.
