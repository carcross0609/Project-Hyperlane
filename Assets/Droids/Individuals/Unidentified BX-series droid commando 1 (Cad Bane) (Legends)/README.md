# Unidentified BX-series droid commando 1 (Cad Bane)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando 1 (Cad Bane)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_1_%28Cad_Bane%29/Legends)

This BX-series droid commando was a member of Cad Bane's posse. He impersonated the voice of Captain Jayfon to cover their infiltration of the Republic Executive Building and later wore the Senate Commando's armor during the remainder of the Senate hostage crisis. He later successfully left the crime scene with the other members of the team and Ziro Desilijic Tiure.
