# 1138 (Geonosis)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [1138 (Geonosis)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1138_%28Geonosis%29)

1138 was a B1-series battle droid that served the Confederacy of Independent Systems as part of a droid army on the planet Geonosis. The B1's designation was written in Trade Federation Script on the back of their comlink booster pack. In 22 BBY, the B-series battle droid was among those deployed to the Petranaki Arena to combat a Jedi assault team that had infiltrated the arena to rescue three prisoners due for execution: Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, and Padmé Amidala.
