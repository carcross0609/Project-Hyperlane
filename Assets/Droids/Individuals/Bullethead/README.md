# Bullethead

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bullethead](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bullethead)

Bullethead was a member of the Droid Gotra who provided security for the Twins, a pair of Hutt crime lords, alongside Arges in their Hutt Palace on the planet Nal Hutta. During or after 11 ABY, the Mandalorian mercenary Din Djarin and his son, Din Grogu, fought Bullethead and Arges in order to reach the Hutts after they had unsuccessfully tried to execute Djarin.
