# TF-1726

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TF-1726](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TF-1726)

TF-1726 was a masculine-programmed T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid who served in the Confederacy navy during the Clone Wars. He commanded his own Munificent-class star frigate at the Battle of Quell, where he defeated Jedi General Aayla Secura and Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker.
