# EG-57

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EG-57](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EG-57)

EG-57 was a green-plated GNK-series power droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton that in 10 BBY witnessed a slave and droid uprising in one of the Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel. During the event it stored an internal data record consisting of an image of the mine's control room, showing the freed droids P6B-LT2, P1-SZE, C00-L05C, P4T-GM, S1D6-SA-5, and DV-4D4.
