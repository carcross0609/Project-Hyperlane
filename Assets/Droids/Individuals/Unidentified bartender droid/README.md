# Unidentified bartender droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified bartender droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_bartender_droid)

An unidentified bartender droid operated at the Cluster cantina on Nar Shaddaa circa 30 BBY. It appeared to be a modified protocol droid with a tankard of ale attached to the back of its gray, weathered chassis. It dispensed ale from its fingers, connected by a series of tubes to the tankard.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified bartender droid
- **Class:** Bartender droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Gray
- **Equipment:** Ale-dispensing system
