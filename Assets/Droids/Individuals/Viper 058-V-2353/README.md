# Viper 058-V-2353

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Viper 058-V-2353](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Viper_058-V-2353)

Viper 058-V-2353 was a viper probe droid that served the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era. By 3 ABY, it had been sent to the planet Vassek where it undertook a six week surveillance mission and recorded conversations between farmers, including one called Roni who planned to steal Imperial generators. An agent of the Imperial Security Bureau was subsequently sent to capture Roni and two members of Roni's family.
