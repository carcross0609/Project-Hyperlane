# Unidentified droid (Boolan)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid (Boolan)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_%28Boolan%29)

An old droid was repurposed by Baron Boolan of the Nihil marauders during the High Republic Era. The droid's processor was enslaved and imprisoned to the weapons targeting controls of a shuttle used by the Child of the Storm Sicarus, who could command the droid to fire upon nearby starships. Sicarus wondered whether the droid still had the ability to think; he assumed it would likely be insane, a thought that appealed to him.
