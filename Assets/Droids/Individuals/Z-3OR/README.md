# Z-3OR

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Z-3OR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-3OR)

Z-3OR was a droid with masculine programming who was a member of a group of thieves on the planet Tenoo during the High Republic Era alongside Emmett and Chipp. Z-3OR was part of the group's effort to steal credits from a hovertrain after stealing a group of Dunnels. Later, they were a part of an attempt to take the droid MB-4 for the locations of mines in the droid's memory bank.
