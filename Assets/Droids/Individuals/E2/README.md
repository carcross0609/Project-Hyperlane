# E2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [E2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E2)

E2 was a black spherical looking droid belonging to the former Sith Lola. After Lola left her old Sith master she became a reclusive artist. Some time later Lola's old master tracked her down. In the ensuing chase E2 used a laser cannon mounted inside his head to shoot the pursuers down but the Sith master used the Force to rip apart E2's three legs. After Lola defeated her old master she repaired E2 and the two departed on her triple-bladed starship to find a more welcoming world.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E2
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Gray-silver
