# S0-M1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S0-M1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S0-M1)

S0-M1 was an astromech droid in service to the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. Stationed at the Vaiken Spacedock, S0-M1 relayed to Imperial forces an urgent transmission from Darth Malgus regarding the situation on Cademimu V.
