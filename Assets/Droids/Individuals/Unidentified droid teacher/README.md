# Unidentified droid teacher

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid teacher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_teacher)

A droid teacher of a service droid model taught at At Attin Middle School Gamma on the planet At Attin. Around 9 ABY, The droid's class was visited by Undersecretary Fara the day before the students were due to take their Career Assessment Test.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified droid teacher
- **Homeworld:** At Attin
- **Model:** Service droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Silver, Yellow and White
