# 2767

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2767](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2767)

2767 was a B1-series battle droid in the Trade Federation Droid Army. In 32 BBY, he was part of the forces deployed to the city of Theed on the planet Naboo by the Federation during its invasion of the world. 2767 was part of a group of battle droids led by the OOM command battle droid 2163 that were tasked with guarding the Naboo Royal Starship in the Theed Hangar.
