# R-DUO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [R-DUO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-DUO)

R-DUO was an R-series astromech droid who served Am and Karre, Dark Lords of the T-Empire. At some point after the Battle of Exegol of 35 ABY, while aboard a Gemini-class Star Destroyer, R-DUO accompanied Karre as he entered the Star Destroyer's reactor core chamber, where he stole the reactor core and kyber crystal within it. As they walked toward a hangar to escape, several T-Stormtroopers attempted to stop them, but Karre defeated them using the Force. After arriving at the hangar, R-DUO slipped away to steal a T-X-wing starfighter while Am and her forces confronted Karre. R-DUO fired on them, allowing Karre to reach the X-wing, then piloted it out of the hangar.
