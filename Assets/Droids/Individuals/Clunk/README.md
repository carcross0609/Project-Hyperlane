# Clunk

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clunk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clunk)

Clunk was a Haxion Brood Bounty Droid that served the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. Once a loadlifter droid, the Haxion Brood reprogrammed the droid, who was enhanced with salvaged parts. They had yellow, silver, and red plating, red sensors, and a built-in wrist blaster.
