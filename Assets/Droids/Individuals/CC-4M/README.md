# CC-4M

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CC-4M](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CC-4M)

CC-4M was a white loadlifter droid with fabric dust shroud on its left shoulder that was forced to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. Before coming to Kessel, he was the leader of a team of construction droids on an ice moon. He missed his friends from his old team while he was forced to work in the mines on Kessel. In 10 BBY, CC-4M was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.
