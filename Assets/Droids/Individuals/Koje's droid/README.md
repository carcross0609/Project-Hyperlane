# Koje's droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Koje's droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Koje%27s_droid)

A droid working for an individual named Koje who resided on the desert planet Tatooine. Around 2 ABY, their employer owed money to the Hutt Clan and, when he wasn't able to pay, he hid out in Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina. While there, Koje ran up a fifty credits tab before being found and killed by the Hutts in the back of the cantina. At the time, droids weren't allowed in the cantina, so the droid waited outside, ignorant of their employer's fate.
