# Unidentified WED Treadwell (Zuvio)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified WED Treadwell (Zuvio)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_WED_Treadwell_%28Zuvio%29)

The droid was one of the supposedly rogue droids that attacked Constable Zuvio during his investigation of a banking ship robbery in Niima Outpost. As a part of Rikard Lovas's plot to steal the electronic credits of the citizens of Niima Outpost on Jakku, several droids were sent to an abandoned farmhouse to wait for Constable Zuvio. During the skirmish at the abandoned Jakku moisture farm the droid ended up striking an MSE droid which resulted in an ionic discharge. The droid was later destroyed when an EG-6 power droid caused the farmhouse to explode.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified WED Treadwell
- **Death:** Sometime before the Escape from Jakku in 34 ABY
- **Model:** WED Treadwell repair droid
