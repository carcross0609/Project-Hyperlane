# EX-426

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EX-426](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EX-426)

EX-426 was a battle droid used by Alirra Semhess in her laboratory on the hidden X7-Cratus space station. It was destroyed during the attack on the X7-Cratus by the Eternal Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** EX-426
- **Death:** 3629 BBY, X7-Cratus
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Grey
- **Affiliation:** Alirra Semhess
