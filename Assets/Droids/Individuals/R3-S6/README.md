# R3-S6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R3-S6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-S6)

R3-S6, also known as "Goldie" or "Stubby," and as "Agent R3" to the Separatists, was an R3-series astromech droid assigned to Anakin Skywalker, during the Clone Wars to replace his lost astromech droid R2-D2. Serving as a double agent for the Confederacy of Independent Systems, R3-S6 often sabotaged Republic missions.
