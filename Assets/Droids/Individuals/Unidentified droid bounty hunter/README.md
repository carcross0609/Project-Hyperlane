# Unidentified droid bounty hunter

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid bounty hunter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_bounty_hunter)

A droid bounty hunter operated during the time of the Galactic Empire. The bounty hunter was a monster droid, made from the body of a B2-series super battle droid and the head of a BX-series droid commando. The droid was one of several bounty hunters hired by the Empire to track down the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Aphra on the planet Arbiflux, but was destroyed by fellow hunter Krrsantan, who wanted Aphra alive in order to collect a debt from her.
