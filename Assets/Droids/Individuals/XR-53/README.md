# XR-53

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XR-53](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XR-53)

XR-53 was a corrupted battle droid created by the Rakata with the ability to repair itself by harvesting resources. Around 3623 BBY, XR-53 was present on the ruins of the Emperor's Fortress on Ilum after its destruction during the Battle of Ilum.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Infinite Empire
- **Name:** XR-53
- **Death:** c. 3623 BBY, Ilum
- **Manufacturer:** Rakata
- **Class:** Battle droid
