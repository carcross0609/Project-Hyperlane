# Unidentified droid (Elphrona Outpost)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid (Elphrona Outpost)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_%28Elphrona_Outpost%29)

A droid was stationed at the Jedi Order's Elphrona Outpost during the High Republic Era. It alerted the Jedi that inhabited the outpost of any pressing matters, including those that involved the wellbeing of Elphrona's citizens. Around 232 BBY, the droid informed these Jedi—Loden Greatstorm, Indeera Stokes, Porter Engle, and Bell Zettifar—that an Er'Kit mine had collapsed in Grid 24B-30, with at least one individual trapped inside. Greatstorm and Zettifar leapt into action, traveling aboard a Vanguard to rescue the miner.
