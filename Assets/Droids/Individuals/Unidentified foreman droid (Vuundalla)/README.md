# Unidentified foreman droid (Vuundalla)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified foreman droid (Vuundalla)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_foreman_droid_%28Vuundalla%29)

A foreman droid was worked for the King and Queen of the planet Vuundalla. At the stable construction site, Cyrus Vuundir commanded the foreman droid supervising the loadlifter droids to hand over the supplies. Kai Brightstar backed him up. The foreman droid refused, saying that the supplies are needed to build the royal stables. When Cyrus said he is changing the orders, the foreman droid countered that he does not have the authority to change orders issued by the king or queen. Cyrus was defiant and confronted the droids, who surrounded him. Later Cyrus deactivated the loadlifter Droids and the foreman droid escaped.
