# DA1-4XB

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DA1-4XB](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DA1-4XB)

DA1-4XB was a 4XB programming droid that was forced to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, DA1-4XB was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.

## Specifications

- **Model:** 4XB programming droid
- **Class:** Programmer droid
