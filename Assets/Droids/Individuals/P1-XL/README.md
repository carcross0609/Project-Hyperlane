# P1-XL

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [P1-XL](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P1-XL)

P1-XL was a floating cube-shaped droid who was present on the Core Worlds planet Alderaan around 3625 BBY. P1-XL's plating was a highly visible orange color with silver plating around most of the droid's edges, ensuring that his owners would not lose sight of him. The droid had a photoreceptor on the center of one of his side faces and two holoprojectors, one below his photoreceptor, one on his right side, and one on his top face. P1-XL had two vents on his left face and a periscope on his top face.

## Specifications

- **Name:** P1-XL
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Orange
- **Equipment:** Three holoprojectors
