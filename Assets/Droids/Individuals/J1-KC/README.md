# J1-KC

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J1-KC](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J1-KC)

J1-KC was a red and white R5 series astromech droid that belonged to the the Lurmen Jat Kaa, owner of Jat Kaa's Coolers in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu. The droid was reprogramed to drive a small cart around the outpost from which various beverages were delivered and sold to visitors.
