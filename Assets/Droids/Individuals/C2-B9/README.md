# C2-B9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C2-B9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C2-B9)

C2-B9 was an R5 series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. It at some point served in the palace of Jabba Desilijic Tiure on the planet Tatooine. While there, it recorded an internal data record of reprimanding someone for their attitude and asking about repairing a barge. The record included an image of several denizens of the palace, including two Jawas, the Frog-dog Buboicullaar, and the Snivvian Geezum.
