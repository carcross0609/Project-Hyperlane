# K7-22

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K7-22](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K7-22)

K7-22 was a battle droid of the Sith Empire stationed on the Harrower-class dreadnought Ziost Shadow during the Galactic War. During the outbreak of the rakghoul plague in the Tion Hegemony, they transported an Imperial strike team to Doctor Sannus Lorrick's Lost Island.
