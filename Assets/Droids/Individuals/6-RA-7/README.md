# 6-RA-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [6-RA-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/6-RA-7)

6-RA-7, also spelled "Six-Aray-Seven," was an RA-7 protocol droid in service of the Inquisitorius. On Iskat Akaris' trip to Pkori, she brought 6-RA-7 under the assumption that the native people did not speak Basic.
