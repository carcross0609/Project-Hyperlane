# Head Chef Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Head Chef Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Head_Chef_Droid)

The patented Head Chef Droid was a J4-SN Chef Droid owned by Hutt Cartel members Gaboorga the Abundant and Duuba the Magnanimous, serving as Head Chef of The Sarlacc Pit cantina and Bog's Bounty Banquet Hall during the Feast of Prosperity on Nar Shaddaa in 3626 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Head Chef Droid
- **Class:** Cooking droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Gold
- **Equipment:**
  - Repulsorlift
  - Four multipurpose appendages
  - Custom chef hat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Hutt Cartel
  -   Duuba the Magnanimous
  -   Gaboorga the Abundant
