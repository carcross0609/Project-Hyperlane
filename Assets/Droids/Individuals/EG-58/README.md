# EG-58

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EG-58](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EG-58)

EG-58 was a white-plated GNK-series power droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. In 34 ABY, during the First Order's occupation of the planet Batuu, EG-58 was part of the Resistance forces stationed in Black Spire Outpost on the world. The power droid worked at Resistance Supply, where it stood with fellow droid EG-57 storing power for the Resistance next to a short-range antenna near the stall.
