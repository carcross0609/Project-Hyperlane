# Unidentified droid Nihil

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid Nihil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_Nihil)

A droid served as a Nihil pirate during the High Republic Era. After tracking down the human Kitrep Soh to the planet Abtin, the droid attempted to kill the Segredo Drewen Qweebjillan after he tried to prevent the droid from reaching the boy. However, the droid was destroyed after their attempted victim's master, the former Tholothian Jedi Ty Yorrick, made her presence known and sliced their head in half with her lightsaber.
