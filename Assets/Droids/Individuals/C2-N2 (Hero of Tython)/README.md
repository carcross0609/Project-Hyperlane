# C2-N2 (Hero of Tython)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C2-N2 (Hero of Tython)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C2-N2_%28Hero_of_Tython%29)

C2-N2, also known as Seetoo Enntoo, was a Hollis-series steward droid produced by Automata Galactica and owned by the Jedi Knight known as the Hero of Tython. Assigned to the Hero's Defender-class light corvette as its steward in 3643 BBY, C2-N2 was responsible for maintaining the starship and ensuring that the Jedi's crew remained comfortable. It served aboard the Defender-class corvette for the next several years, caring for the Jedi and the ship's crew as they participated in the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire.
