# R3-T7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R3-T7/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-T7/Legends)

R3-T7 was a R3-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton on the planet Nubia years before the start of the Clone Wars in 22 BBY. The droid served the Galactic Republic on a patrol ship before moving into the service of an up-and-coming senator on the planet Coruscant.
