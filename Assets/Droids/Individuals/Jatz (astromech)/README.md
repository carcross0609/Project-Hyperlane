# Jatz (astromech)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jatz (astromech)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jatz_%28astromech%29)

Jatz was the name of an R3 astromech droid and was a member of Tualon Yaluna's company. He was given the name "Jatz" because he enjoyed music far more than other droids. He would often play music that fit the mood, and would provide background music during times of relaxation.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Galactic Republic
- **Name:** Jatz
- **Line:** R-series
- **Model:** R3-series astromech droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Plating:** Green and indigo
- **Affiliation:**
