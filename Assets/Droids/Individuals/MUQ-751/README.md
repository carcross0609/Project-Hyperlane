# MUQ-751

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MUQ-751](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MUQ-751)

MUQ-751 was a B1-series battle droid who served as a commander in the Droid Army of the galactic company known as the Trade Federation. As with all B1 battle droids, MUQ-751 was 1.91 meters tall, had masculine programming and wielded an E-5 blaster rifle. The droid's head and some other parts of its body were colored white, while the rest was brown.
