# Unidentified IG-86 sentinel droid 2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified IG-86 sentinel droid 2/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_IG-86_sentinel_droid_2/Legends)

During the Clone Wars, a IG-86 sentinel droid was owned by Trandoshan scavenger Gha Nachkt along with two others. When Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano where searching the Vulture's Claw, R3-S6 activated the droid and the other one. After a small battle, both droids were destroyed.
