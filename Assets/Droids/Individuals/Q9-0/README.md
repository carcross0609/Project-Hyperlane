# Q9-0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Q9-0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q9-0)

Q9-0, also known as Zero or Z, was a modified RA-9O protocol droid who worked as a mercenary during the time of the New Republic. Zero was part of a crew of mercenaries led by Ranzar Malk, consisting of Migs Mayfeld, Burg, Xi'an, and the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin, who were sent by Malk to rescue Qin, a prisoner aboard the New Republic prison ship Bothan-5.
