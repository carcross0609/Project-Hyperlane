# TV-94B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TV-94B/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TV-94B/Legends)

TV-94B was a T-series tactical droid that was used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. He was assigned to General Grievous as a replacement for TV-94. He oversaw the space battle of the Battle of Kamino, led personally by Grievous and Count Dooku's Dark Acolyte, Asajj Ventress. The goal of the invasion was to destroy the cloning facilities of Tipoca City, halting the Galactic Republic's production of new clone troopers, and retrieve the DNA of clone template Jango Fett to unlock new tactical possibilities for the Confederacy. Commanding droid forces from the General's command ship while Grievous and Ventress went to complete their mission with the ground forces, TV-94B later retreated with the rest of the fleet when the invasion was a failure due to the efforts of Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi, Shaak Ti and Anakin Skywalker.
