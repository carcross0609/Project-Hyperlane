# QT-KT

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [QT-KT](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QT-KT)

QT-KT, also known as Qutee-Kaytee and often referred to as Qutee, was an astromech droid and a member of D-Squad, under the command of Colonel Meebur Gascon, during the Clone Wars. She belonged to Jedi Knight Aayla Secura. She was upgraded by the Parwan Doctor Gubacher to have a controlled, hovering super magnet to use. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, during the Imperial Era, QT-KT rejoined the squad to assist her old companion, R2-D2, in his fight against the Scourge.
