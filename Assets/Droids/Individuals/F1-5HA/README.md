# F1-5HA

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [F1-5HA](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F1-5HA)

F1-5HA was an astromech droid who served the Resistance on the planet Ajan Kloss during the First Order-Resistance War. The droid was one of five astromechs who worked under R5-2JE and who were all absent from their jobs at the landing pad one day. F1-5HA was busy trying to fish a king-sized catch down by the river and refused to leave until the droid caught a large catch.
