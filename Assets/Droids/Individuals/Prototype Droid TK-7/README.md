# Prototype Droid TK-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prototype Droid TK-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prototype_Droid_TK-7)

Prototype Droid TK-7 was a battle droid used by Galactic Republic during the Conquest of Makeb. The droid, along with Prototype Droid HL-1 was guarding the schematics of Isotope-5 droids employed by the Hutt Cartel and was destroyed by an individual seeking to acquire those schematics for the Galactic Solutions Industries
