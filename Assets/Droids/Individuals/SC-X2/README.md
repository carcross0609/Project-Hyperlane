# SC-X2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SC-X2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SC-X2)

SC-X2 was an astromech droid belonging to Bo Keevil of Ace Squadron. When Keevil flew his ship, Yellow Ace, SC-X2 made calculations to assist whenever the ship reconfigured its wings. SC-X2 was highly reinforced to withstand most crashes and ship malfunctions. It rolled along a track for better traction and direction control. Due to Bo Keevil's Kel Dor anatomy, SC-X2 carried emergency antiox breath mask parts inside its body, along with other gadgets.
