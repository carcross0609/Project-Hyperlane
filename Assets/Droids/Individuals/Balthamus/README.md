# Balthamus

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Balthamus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balthamus)

Balthamus, also known as Number Ten, was a medical droid in service of Fyzen Gor's Brotherhood of Wire and Bone, specifically of the Original Dozen created by Gor himself. He wore a hood and his droid hands had been replaced with two grayish, bloated human hands. In 7 ABY, he was in service on the moon Grava when Han Solo and Lando Calrissian arrived there. He brought them to Seven-Seven Dirgeos as they requested but was later destroyed when Lando severed his head from his body with his blaster and took his head with him. Lando later used a map from Balthamus' head to find the location of the Phylanx Redux Transmitter.
