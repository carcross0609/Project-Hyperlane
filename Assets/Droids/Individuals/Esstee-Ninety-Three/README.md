# Esstee-Ninety-Three

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Esstee-Ninety-Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Esstee-Ninety-Three)

Esstee-Ninety-Three was a PJ unit droid who worked at the shipyard of the criminal mechanic Rekias Nodo during the time of the Galactic Empire. Esstee-Ninety-Three was a hulking bipedal droid with a pair of powerful piston arms that featured pincers. The droid had a singular red sensor and gray plating with yellow stripes. Esstee-Ninety-Three was a close friend to Gorko, another of Nodo's employees. The employees of the shipyard, located on a jungle planet, dismantled stolen starships for scrap.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Esstee-Ninety-Three
- **Death:** After 10 BBY, a jungle planet
- **Class:** PJ unit
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Affiliation:** Nodo's shipyard
