# Unidentified baker droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified baker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_baker_droid)

This baker droid was a DG-1B catering droid who was employed by Senator Padmé Amidala to cater a function in honor of Senator Aang, whom she was trying to sway as the deciding vote on a bill she hoped to pass in the Senate. The droid helped in the preparation of a jogan fruitcake for the gathering she would be holding in honor of Aang. As the cake still lacked its jogan fruit garnish, Amidala dispatched her droids to purchase some. As the gathering began before their arrival with the jogans, the baker droid questioned as to what they were going to do.
