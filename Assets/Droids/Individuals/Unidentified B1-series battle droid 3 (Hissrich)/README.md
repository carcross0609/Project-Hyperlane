# Unidentified B1-series battle droid 3 (Hissrich)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid 3 (Hissrich)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_3_%28Hissrich%29)

A B1-series battle droid was stationed on the planet Hissrich in 22 BBY. They carried an E-5 blaster rifle and a comlink booster pack. As a member of a patrol squadron traveling through sector two zero nine, they engaged in combat with the Jedi Master Mace Windu and Jedi Knight Rissa Mano. Mano deflected a blaster bolt into the battle droid's head, destroying them.
