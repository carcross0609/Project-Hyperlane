# Kompaktor

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kompaktor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kompaktor)

Kompaktor was a Haxion Brood Bounty Droid who served the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. Once a loadlifter droid, the Haxion Brood reprogrammed the droid, who was enhanced with salvaged parts. They had green plating, yellow sensors, and a built-in wrist blaster.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Bounty hunter
- **Name:** Kompaktor
- **Death:** 14 BBY
- **Line:** Haxion Brood Bounty Droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Green
- **Armament:** Wrist blaster
- **Affiliation:** Haxion Brood
