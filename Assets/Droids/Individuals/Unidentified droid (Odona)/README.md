# Unidentified droid (Odona)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid (Odona)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_%28Odona%29)

A droid was a mercenary hired by Frax to kill Han Solo during the mission to Odona. The droid was about to flee with Selt, but Frax was against their leaving and gunned the both of them down.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified droid
- **Death:** Prior to 3 ABY, Odona
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Dark blue
