# Kaythree

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaythree](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaythree)

Kaythree was a R4 astromech droid that served as Jessika Pava's astromech in the Resistance, after her previous droid, O-R10N, was destroyed during the mission to Megalox Beta. During Black Squadron's attempt to recover stolen fuel from the First Order, Kaythree piloted the bulk freighter Romary and was subsequently destroyed along with the freighter.
