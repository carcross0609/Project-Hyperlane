# T-3PO (S-R6)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-3PO (S-R6)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-3PO_%28S-R6%29)

T-3PO was a blue-colored protocol droid who served on Trevura, during the Galactic Civil War. T-3PO served under S-R6, an assassin droid who moved about Voma, a city on Trevura, in order to buy illegal and experimental versions of Imperial blasters that were imported by Merno Blask, the governor of Voma.
