# R9-D9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R9-D9/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R9-D9/Legends)

R9-D9 was the personal astromech droid of King Sanjay Rash on Onderon during the Clone Wars. R9-D9 showed holographic images of Steela Gerrera and Lux Bonteri, members of the Onderon rebels.
