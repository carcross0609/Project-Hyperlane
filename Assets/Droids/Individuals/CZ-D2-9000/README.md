# CZ-D2-9000

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CZ-D2-9000](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-D2-9000)

CZ-D2-9000 was part of the designation of a CZ-series secretary/business communications droid manufactured by Serv-O-Droid, Inc. During the era of the New Republic around 9 ABY, the droid was left deactivated on a table among an inventory of broken down old droid models in a droid repair shop on the pirate-controlled asteroid Port Borgo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CZ-D2-9000
- **Manufacturer:** Serv-O-Droid, Inc.
- **Model:** CZ-series secretary/business communications droid
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** White
