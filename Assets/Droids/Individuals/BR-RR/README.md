# BR-RR

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BR-RR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BR-RR)

BR-RR was an R5-series astromech droid that was around during the Clone Wars. This unit was decorated and used during Life Day celebrations, in particular resembling a snowman, with Life Day also being considered its favorite time of year because of this.
