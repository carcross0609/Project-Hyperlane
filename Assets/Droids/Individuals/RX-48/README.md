# RX-48

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RX-48](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RX-48)

RX-48 was an RX-series pilot droid with a masculine programming. He once piloted pleasure liners for the Galactic Tourist Bureau, until he accidentally crashed a tour bus into a space station. At other points in time, he also worked for Jabba Desilijic Tiure, a Hutt crime lord based on Tatooine.
