# Mantoid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mantoid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mantoid)

Mantoid was a combat droid gladiator in the Cauldron on Rattatak. During the final years of the Old Republic, Mantoid was destroyed by gladiator Grunda Dolma in close combat.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Athlete
- **Name:** Mantoid
- **Death:** 22 BBY, Rattatak
- **Class:** Combat droid
- **Sensor:** Red and purple
- **Armament:** Variety of bladed weapons
