# Arch-E

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arch-E](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arch-E)

Arch-E was a navigator droid who worked with the smuggler Jayko, and was also able to play the drums. Arch-E and Jayko were present in a cantina when the smuggler's former crewmate Tan entered, thanking Jayko for writing about him in his book, A Smuggler's Life.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Arch-E
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Gray
