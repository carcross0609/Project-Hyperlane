# Battle droid 513

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Battle droid 513](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_droid_513)

Battle droid 513 was one of many monster battle droids assembled by the Mandalorians of Death Watch for target practice during their stay on the planet Carlac in the midst of the Clone Wars. Death Watch cobbled their training droids together from droid parts of various models, including many originating from the Confederacy of Independent Systems' Droid Army. Battle droid 513 specifically was composed of the body of an LR-57 combat/retail droid, the right arm of a B1-series battle droid and a leg from a LM-432 crab droid.
