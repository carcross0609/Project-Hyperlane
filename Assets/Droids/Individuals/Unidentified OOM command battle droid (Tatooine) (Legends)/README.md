# Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Tatooine)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Tatooine)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_%28Tatooine%29/Legends)

Following the Battle of Teth, this OOM command battle droid was stationed on Tatooine with Count Dooku, leader of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. He reported on the approach of Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker and his Padawan Ahsoka Tano, who were returning the kidnapped Rotta the Huttlet to his father, Jabba.
