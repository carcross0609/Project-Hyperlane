# Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Anaxes)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Anaxes)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_T-series_tactical_droid_%28Anaxes%29)

A T-series tactical droid was active during the Clone Wars, it was present on the surface of the planet Anaxes during the Battle of Anaxes. It arrived at the aftermath of a short firefight between Clone Force 99 and a group of B1-series battle droids, holding a hologram of Admiral Trench. A nearby BX-series droid commando addressed the hologram, reporting that the clones had used unorthodox methods to defeat the droids. After the report, the tactical droid returned to the speeder in which it arrived and left the scene. The droid continued to report the clone troopers' actions during the battle to the Harch.
