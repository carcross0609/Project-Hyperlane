# Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Devaron)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Devaron)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_%28Devaron%29)

This OOM command battle droid was present on Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate during the Battle of Devaron, linking all ships functions to Bane's wrist gauntlet. When the clone troopers and Jedi who boarded the ship reached the bridge, the droid panicked and told them that the battle droid standing next to him was the commander, not him. Nevertheless, the clones gunned down both the OOM commander and his fellow droid.
