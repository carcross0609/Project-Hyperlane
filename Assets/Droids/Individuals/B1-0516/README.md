# B1-0516

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B1-0516](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1-0516)

B1-0516, nicknamed Bats, was a B1-series battle droid in the droid army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. During the Clone Wars, B1-0516 fought in a battle against the Galactic Republic on Horain, where his processor was short-circuited by a stray blaster bolt. The damage caused B1-0516 to fight with the Republic army, and assisted General Obi-Wan Kenobi and Captain Rex, who gave the battle droid the nickname "Bats" for easier pronunciation.
