# T1M8-LT2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T1M8-LT2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T1M8-LT2)

T1M8-LT2 was an orange- and green-plated A-LT Utility Droid that was forced to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, T1M8-LT2 was freed from slavery after its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T1M8-LT2
- **Model:** A-LT Utility Droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Black and white
- **Plating:** Green and orange
