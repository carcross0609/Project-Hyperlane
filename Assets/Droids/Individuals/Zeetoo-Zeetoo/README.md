# Zeetoo-Zeetoo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zeetoo-Zeetoo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeetoo-Zeetoo)

Zeetoo-Zeetoo, nicknamed "Zeet," was an astromech droid belonging to Shepherd Squadron pilot Bak Rychuk. The droid was destroyed after a brief encounter with a scavenger named Rey on the Imperial Interdictor Spectral.

## Specifications

- **Type:** New Republic
- **Name:** Zeetoo-Zeetoo
- **Death:** Sometime before the attack on Tuanul, Jakku
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Gold, with purple accent panels
- **Affiliation:**
  - New Republic
  -   Shepherd Squadron
