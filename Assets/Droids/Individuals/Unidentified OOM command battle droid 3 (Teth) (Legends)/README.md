# Unidentified OOM command battle droid 3 (Teth)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid 3 (Teth)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_3_%28Teth%29/Legends)

During the Battle of Teth, this OOM command battle droid commanded Separatist forces during the Galactic Republic's renewed assault on the B'omarr Monastery. They had been ordered by Asajj Ventress, upon the Jedi's Republic warships arriving at the planet, to hold off the invading force until her mission had been completed, to which the droid complied with the order, and proceeded to scramble the Separatist forces' starfighter squadrons.
