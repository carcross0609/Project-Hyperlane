# Ironholgs

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ironholgs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ironholgs)

Ironholgs was nickname given to an upgraded version of a Holgorian IM-220 maintenance droid associated with Je'daii Ranger Lanoree Brock. Ironholgs was responsible for basic onboard maintenance and security of Brock's ship; though it did not have arms. In addition to its basic protocols, Ironholgs was capable of remotely piloting the Peacemaker-class cruiser during missions. Brock also used it to record her journal. After their adventures on Sunspot, a damaged Ironholgs was in need of repair and Brock left him at Anil Kesh on Tython by a Je'daii Journeyer who specialized in mechanics.
