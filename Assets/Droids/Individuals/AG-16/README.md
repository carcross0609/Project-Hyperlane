# AG-16

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AG-16](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AG-16)

AG-16 was a droid used by Doctor Larek on Belsavis during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. AG-15, AG-16, and a number of additional droids were sent out to locate ancient Rakata security droids and extract their memory cores, but the droids were destroyed before they could complete their mission.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AG-16
- **Death:** c. 3641 BBY, Belsavis
- **Pronouns:** new
