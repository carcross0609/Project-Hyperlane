# Unit 437

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unit 437](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unit_437)

Unit 437 was a B1-series battle droid member of MT-7's unit. After a storm separated the crew during its mission on Akiva, 437 made a difficult journey to the Blood Tree. There, 437 surveilled the activity of enemy insurgents. Though aware that the positions of his unit would inevitably be compromised, 437 remained determined that the group succeeded.
