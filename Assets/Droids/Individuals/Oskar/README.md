# Oskar

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oskar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oskar)

Oskar was a BDX droid who was active at Savi & Son Salvage in Black Spire Outpost on the Outer Rim planet Batuu by 34 ABY. The biped was highly energetic and enjoyed pranks. Oskar also was trained by Resistance operative Vi Moradi to assist her in covertly gathering First Order intelligence.
