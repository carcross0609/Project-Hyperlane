# Elkew-Nine

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Elkew-Nine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elkew-Nine)

Elkew-Nine was an IG-86 sentinel droid who worked alongside the bounty hunter Aurra Sing. Together with other IG-86 droids, Elkew-Nine was tasked with ensuring her security while she copied a list of Republic agents from Asajj Ventress at the Doric Prime power plant. The droid encountered Clone Force 99, who were attempting to recover the list, and attacked them before being destroyed by Wrecker.
