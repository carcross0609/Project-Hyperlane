# Droideka Delta

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droideka Delta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droideka_Delta)

Droideka Delta was a member of IG-88's Droid Army during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a radical group of droids that would have started a Droid Revolution had they not been destroyed in 4 ABY.
