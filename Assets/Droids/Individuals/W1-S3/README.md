# W1-S3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [W1-S3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/W1-S3)

W1-S3 was an astromech droid who served the Resistance on the planet Ajan Kloss during the First Order-Resistance War. The droid was one of five astromechs who worked under R5-2JE and who were all absent from their jobs at the landing pad one day. W1-S3 meditated in a cave instead of doing R5-2JE's work.
