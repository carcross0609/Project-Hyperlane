# DD-1A

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DD-1A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DD-1A)

DD-1A, also known as Deedee-Wunay, or simply as Deedee, was a droid that served the Jedi Order on the planet Banchii during the High Republic Era. Deedee assisted the Jedi stationed on Banchii, Jedi Master Arkoff, Archivist Ru-Ru, Padawan Keerin Fionn, and the younglings Nima and Viv'nia. The droid helped in monitoring the forest around the Banchii Jedi temple, maintaining a map of the area.
