# Cleaning Probe X101

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cleaning Probe X101](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cleaning_Probe_X101)

Cleaning Probe X101 was a cleaning droid used in Fa'athra's Palace on Nal Hutta during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY Cy Krolo's partner who worked for Suudaa Nem'ro planted a transmitter on the droid to gather information on Voontara Fa'athra, but was killed before he could activate it. Cy Krolo then asked a passerby to infiltrate the palace and activate the droid's transmitter.
