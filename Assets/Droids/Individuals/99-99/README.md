# 99-99

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [99-99](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/99-99)

99-99, nicknamed Four-Nines, was a droid companion to Lah Kara on the Outer Rim planet Hy Izlan. The droid was capable of self-propelled flight and of firing blaster bolts that Kara could deflect with a lightsaber.
