# Donar family protocol droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Donar family protocol droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Donar_family_protocol_droid)

A butler droid was owned by the family of Governor Dol Donar II. The protocol droid was tasked with allowing guests to meet with he governor. The droid was required to wear a formal vest over his plating and sometimes emulated a coughing sound when he thought that he was interrupting.
