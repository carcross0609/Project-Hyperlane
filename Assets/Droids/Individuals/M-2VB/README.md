# M-2VB

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M-2VB](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-2VB)

M-2VB was a medical droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War. In 3639 BBY he was stationed on Carrick Station, where he examined Republic General Conlath, who suffered a mental trauma during an encounter with Dread Masters forces on Belsavis.
