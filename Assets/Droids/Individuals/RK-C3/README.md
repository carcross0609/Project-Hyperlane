# RK-C3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RK-C3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RK-C3)

RK-C3 was a Czerka Corporation protocol droid stationed at the remote moon CZ-198. During the Seizure of CZ-198 in 3638 BBY, the droid was expropriated and reprogrammed by the Galactic Republic. Now a Republic patriot, the droid eagerly invited Republic personnel to report to the Department of Adjudication members aboard the Gav Daragon.
