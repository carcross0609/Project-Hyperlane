# Durge's pilot droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Durge's pilot droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Durge%27s_pilot_droid)

Durge's pilot droid flew Durge's ship during the Gen'Dai's final year of life. The droid somewhat resembled Durge in appearance and temperament. The droid was decapitated by Obi-Wan Kenobi, although its head remained functional. Obi-Wan captured the head and used it to track down Dooku and Asajj Ventress to Boz Pity.
