# 1138 (Trade Federation)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [1138 (Trade Federation)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1138_%28Trade_Federation%29)

1138 was a B1-series battle droid serving under the Trade Federation Droid Army during the Naboo Crisis. 1138 took part on the Battle of Naboo under the command of OOM-9. The droids were able to create a victory for the Federation, but this was short-lived, as every Federation battle droid was shut down when Anakin Skywalker destroyed the Droid Control Ship above Naboo.
