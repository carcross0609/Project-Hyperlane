# R4-B0018

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-B0018](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-B0018)

R4-B0018 was an R4 astromech droid that was employed as a serving droid on Cloud City. The droid was colored in white, yellow, and orange, and included a Burtt acoustic signaller. It was also equipped with a head mounted serving pincer that could serve refreshments to patrons.
