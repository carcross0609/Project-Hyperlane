# HXI-54 Juggernaut

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HXI-54 Juggernaut](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HXI-54_Juggernaut)

The HXI-54 Juggernaut was a battle droid stationed aboard the Republic Navy's Thranta-class corvette Dorin's Sky. During the Galactic War, the Dorin's Sky was boarded by the reconstituted Sith Empire, and the HXI-54 Juggernaut was destroyed in the battle.

## Specifications

- **Death:** 3642 BBY, the Dorin's Sky
- **Class:** Battle droid
