# Unidentified super tactical droid (Anaxes)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified super tactical droid (Anaxes)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_super_tactical_droid_%28Anaxes%29)

An ST-series super tactical droid was in active service to the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. In the year 19 BBY, he served under Admiral Trench during the Battle of Anaxes as they attacked the Republic's vital shipyards on Anaxes.
