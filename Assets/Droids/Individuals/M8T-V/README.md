# M8T-V

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M8T-V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M8T-V)

M8T-V was an astromech droid of the Galactic Republic posted on the Carrick Station during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY it delivered to Republic personnel a hologram message from Satele Shan who asked the Republic heroes to participate in a dangerous Colicoid wargame in order to acquire new weapons for the Republic.
