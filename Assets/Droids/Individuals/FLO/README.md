# FLO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FLO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FLO)

FLO, also known as WA-7, or Wanda, was the nickname of a WA-7 waitress droid working at Dex's Diner on Coruscant. She once served Obi-Wan Kenobi when he came to the diner asking for help from Dexter Jettster.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FLO
- **Death:** 16 BBY
- **Model:** WA-7 waitress droid
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Affiliation:** Dex's Diner
