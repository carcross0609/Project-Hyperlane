# Unidentified KX-series security droid (Star Destroyer)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified KX-series security droid (Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_KX-series_security_droid_%28Star_Destroyer%29)

A KX-series security droid served the Galactic Empire aboard an Imperial II-class Star Destroyer. After the Rebel Alliance protocol droid C-3PO was captured by the Empire and imprisoned aboard the Star Destroyer, the astromech droid R2-D2 boarded the vessel in an X-wing starfighter to rescue him, causing chaos aboard the Star Destroyer. During the chaos, Imperial stormtroopers, only aware that the damage was being caused by a droid, confronted the KX-series security droid. The security droid insisted that he was innocent, but a stormtrooper ordered his fellow troopers to shoot the droid regardless.
