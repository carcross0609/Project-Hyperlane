# E-3PO (Clone Wars)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-3PO (Clone Wars)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-3PO_%28Clone_Wars%29)

E-3PO was a protocol droid that was owned by the Human Korlana Toryn. During the Clone Wars, Toryn was sent into hiding on the moon Nar Shaddaa by her father, Senator Jasso Toryn, and E-3PO accompanied her to the world.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-3PO
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** Korlana Toryn
