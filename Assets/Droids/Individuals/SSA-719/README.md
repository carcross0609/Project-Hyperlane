# SSA-719

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SSA-719](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SSA-719)

SSA-719 was a Baktoid Combat Automata OOM security battle droid serving the Trade Federation during its Invasion of Naboo in 32 BBY. During the Naboo's bid to take back the planetary capital of Theed, SSA-719 was assigned to guard the entrance to the Theed Royal Palace throne room, where the battle droid was destroyed by a blaster bolt to the head.
