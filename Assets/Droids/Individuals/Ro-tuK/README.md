# Ro-tuK

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ro-tuK](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ro-tuK)

Ro-tuK was a droid of the Gree who lived during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Upon the reappearance of the ancient Gree starship Gray Secant, Ro-tuK invited members from both sides of the war to come to Ilum to assist the Gree in their experiments.

- **Class:** Protocol droid
