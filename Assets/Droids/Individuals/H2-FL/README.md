# H2-FL

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [H2-FL](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H2-FL)

H2-FL was a labor droid that was employed by Genetics Laboratory G-1, a genetic research facility on the planet Manaan during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. H2-FL worked on the surface section of the facility, receiving shipments of medical supplies and the like.

## Specifications

- **Name:** H2-FL
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Blue and white
