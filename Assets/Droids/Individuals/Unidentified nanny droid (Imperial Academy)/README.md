# Unidentified nanny droid (Imperial Academy)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified nanny droid (Imperial Academy)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_nanny_droid_%28Imperial_Academy%29)

This nanny droid, with feminine programming, was employed by the Galactic Empire in service of the Education and Economics Ministries. After twenty years of caring for children, the nanny droid had several parts in need of replacement and was retired for use in a training exercise held on the grounds of the planet Lothal's Academy for Young Imperials. Though old, she was of a newer model than that of Auntie Nags, a similar nanny droid that had been in the Leonis family for many generations. She was captured as a "high-value target" as part of the training exercise and then obliterated by Nazhros Oleg via blaster, even though nobody had given an order to fire.
