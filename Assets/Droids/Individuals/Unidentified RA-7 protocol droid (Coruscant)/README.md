# Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Coruscant)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Coruscant)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RA-7_protocol_droid_%28Coruscant%29)

A RA-7 protocol droid was on Coruscant with an astromech droid in 21 BBY during the Clone Wars. The RA-7 protocol droid and astromech droid were both in a dark alley in the lower market districts of Coruscant. When assassin droid HELIOS-3E and techno-service droid Todo 360 were pursuing R2 series astromech droid R2-D2, HELIOS-3E, thinking he had discovered R2, stunned the RA-7's astromech droid companion. The RA-7 was startled and ran away, believing that the astromech had been killed
