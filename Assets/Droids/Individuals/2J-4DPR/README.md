# 2J-4DPR

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2J-4DPR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2J-4DPR)

2J-4DPR, also known by landing area staffers as Silvertreads, was an astromech droid manufactured by Lerrimore Automata assigned to repair and assess starships visiting the Jedi Temple on Coruscant.
