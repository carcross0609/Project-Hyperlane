# R5-C4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [R5-C4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-C4)

R5-C4 was an R5 astromech droid and a member of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. During the Galactic Civil War he was held aboard a Star Destroyer and later rescued by C-3PO and R2-D2. He claimed to be R5-D4's cousin.
