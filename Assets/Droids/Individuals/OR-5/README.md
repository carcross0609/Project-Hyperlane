# OR-5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OR-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OR-5)

OR-5 was a B1-series battle droid Colonel in the droid factory on the planet Mustafar. OR-5 was destroyed when HK-47 tricked a group of spacers into reactivating the factory.
