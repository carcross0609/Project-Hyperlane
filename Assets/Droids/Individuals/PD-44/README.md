# PD-44

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PD-44](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PD-44)

PD-44 was the designation of a battle droid of the reconstituted Sith Empire stationed on Taral V during the Cold War. It was destroyed around 3642 BBY.
