# TM-171

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TM-171](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TM-171)

TM-171 was a T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid who served in the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. In 20 BBY, the droid officer was dispatched to the planet Umbara during a campaign against the Galactic Republic, where he commanded strategic resupply operations for the Umbaran Militia on the surface from a Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel in orbit.
