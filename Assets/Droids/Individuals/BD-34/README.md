# BD-34

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BD-34](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD-34)

BD-34 was a B1-series battle droid that operated during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, BD-34 served Jabba the Hutt's criminal empire on Tatooine under Jabba's sadistic EV-series supervisor droid EV-9D9. In 1 ABY, BD-34 and three other droids, R3-B57, RA-316 and LIN-34, escaped Jabba's Palace and took refuge within the Darklighter cache. EV-9D9 thus hired a spacer to track down the droids to the cave. According to EV-9D9's punitive instructions, the spacer planted pain bolts to the droids before destroying them.
