# Unidentified protocol droid (Seven Flames)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified protocol droid (Seven Flames)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_protocol_droid_%28Seven_Flames%29)

An unidentified protocol droid was in the service of the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War, stationed on the planet Seven Flames. The droid was responsible for debriefing a Rebel diplomatic team after their mission.
