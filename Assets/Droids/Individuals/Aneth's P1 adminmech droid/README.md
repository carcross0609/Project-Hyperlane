# Aneth's P1 adminmech droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aneth's P1 adminmech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aneth%27s_P1_adminmech_droid)

The human Aneth, co-owner of the Mina-Rau General Store on the planet Mina-Rau, owned a shabby P1 adminmech droid with red and white plating. In 4 BBY, the droid rolled round the store babbling while Aneth, her spouse Kellen, and their daughter Beela unloaded a delivery of supplies.
