# SD-89

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SD-89](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-89)

SD-89 was a protocol droid who served as a translator, teacher, and associate of C-3PO. SD-89 was capable of speaking many languages, including Galactic Basic, Droidspeak, Shyriiwook, Ewokese, and Huttese, and enjoyed teaching sentient organics how to speak each of them as well.
