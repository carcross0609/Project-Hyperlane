# MSE-Y

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [MSE-Y](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MSE-Y)

MSE-Y was an MSE-series mouse droid who served the Resistance. He was part of C-3PO's party whilst stranded on Taul before being chased by away from the group by a Spice spider, presumably being destroyed in the process.
