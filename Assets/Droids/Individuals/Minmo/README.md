# Minmo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Minmo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Minmo)

Minmo was a droid created by the droidsmith Rek Minuu sometime before or during 230 BBY. They were a smaller version of a spider droid model also made by Minuu.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Minmo
- **Birth:** Before or during 230 BBY
- **Creator:** Rek Minuu
- **Model:** Spider droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:**
  - Black
  - Blue
- **Armament:** Two small electrobatons
