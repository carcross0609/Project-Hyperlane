# FX-6 (Begamor)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FX-6 (Begamor)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-6_%28Begamor%29)

FX-6 was a waiter droid at the presidential manor on Begamor during the High Republic Era. During Sean's mission to Begamor, FX-6 detected allergens in the kaadu ribs the chef Bellon had prepared. Out of anger, Bellon attacked the droid, but FX-6 was able to stun him.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FX-6
- **Model:** waiter droid
- **Gender:** Feminine
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Equipment:** Poison- and allergen-detecting sensors
- **Affiliation:** Presidential manor
