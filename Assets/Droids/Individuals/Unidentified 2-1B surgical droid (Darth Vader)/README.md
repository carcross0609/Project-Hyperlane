# Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Darth Vader)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Darth Vader)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_2-1B_surgical_droid_%28Darth_Vader%29)

A 2-1B surgical droid was present at the Grand Republic Medical Facility with a DD-13 medical assistant droid, FX-6, and another FX-9 surgical assistant. The droids helped rebuild the body of Darth Vader following his defeat at the hands of Obi-Wan Kenobi on Mustafar in 19 BBY. The three droids were later moved to Fortress Vader on Mustafar.
