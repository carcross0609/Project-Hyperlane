# HHG-RX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HHG-RX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HHG-RX)

HHG-RX was an RX-series pilot droid that existed during the early stages of the Galactic Civil War. It could frequently be found hitch-hiking at Spaceport THX1138. It was identifiable with it giving the thumb gesture for hitchhiking and holding a pendant resembling the Marksman-H combat remote.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HHG-RX
- **Manufacturer:** Reubens Robotic Systems
- **Model:** RX-series pilot droid
- **Class:** Pilot droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Blue, white when illuminated
- **Plating:** Brushed metal and gold with blue trim
- **Affiliation:** Star Tours
