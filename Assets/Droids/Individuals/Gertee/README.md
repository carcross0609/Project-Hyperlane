# Gertee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gertee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gertee)

Gertee was a droid who was part of the Second Revelation, a group of self-aware droids. Around 3 ABY, Gertee was present in the Colony of the Second Revelation, and after the colony received a message from one of their Crusaders, a droid known as Petyr, about a new threat known as the Scourge, Gertee informed the Revelation's leader, Ajax Sigma, of the development. After the two spoke and Sigma decided the group needed to destroy the Scourge, Gertee told Sigma she would inform the other members of the Second Revelation and then begin their preparations. After attending an assembly informing the Second Revelation of the Scourge, she allowed Sigma to remove her neural core to store it inside a giant warship in order to transport the other droids to the Scourge's Star Destroyer.
