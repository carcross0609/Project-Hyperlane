# L4-R6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L4-R6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L4-R6)

L4-R6 was an R4 astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton who functioned as a repair droid. In 1 BBY, L4-R6 was in the possession of a tribe of Jawas on Tatooine. The droid recorded a memory transcript of the Jawas' sandcrawler visiting the Lars homestead, which included two Jawas discussing how they planned to sell the astromech droid R5-D4 despite the fact that its motivator had not been repaired. The droid also recorded an image of R5-D4 and the droid R2-D2 standing outside the sandcrawler.
