# T6-D4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T6-D4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T6-D4)

T6-D4 was an astromech droid at the time of the Galactic War. After the Conquest of Makeb, it was sent to Keylander Station as a courier by Supreme Commander Jace Malcom to help him enlist the services of the Republic strike force regarding the mysteries surrounding the survival of the planet, Makeb.
