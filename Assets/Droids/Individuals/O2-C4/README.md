# O2-C4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [O2-C4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/O2-C4)

O2-C4 was a droid chef that had worked at the Almas Academy. A group of Jedi students accidentally destroyed him, after which Mother Missira took over his duties.
