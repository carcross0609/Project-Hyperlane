# S1D6-SA-5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [S1D6-SA-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S1D6-SA-5)

S1D6-SA-5 was a red-colored SA-5 protocol droid that saw use in a control hub of the Spice mines of Kessel. S1D6-SA-5 and the other droids used by the Pykes were freed in 10 BBY during an slave uprising started by the droid L3-37.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S1D6-SA-5
- **Model:** SA-5 protocol droid
- **Class:** Protocol droid
