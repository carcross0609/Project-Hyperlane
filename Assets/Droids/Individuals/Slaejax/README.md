# Slaejax

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Slaejax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slaejax)

Slaejax was a bounty droid who served the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. Slaejax was killed when hunting the Jedi survivor Cal Kestis.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Bounty hunter
- **Name:** Slaejax
- **Death:** 14 BBY
- **Line:** Haxion Brood Bounty Droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Red and silver
- **Affiliation:** Haxion Brood
