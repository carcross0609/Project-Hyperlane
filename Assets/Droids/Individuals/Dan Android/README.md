# Dan Android

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dan Android](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dan_Android)

Dan Android was a masculine PR droid with blue photoreceptors. By the year 4.3 ABY, he was in charge of public relations for the Star Tours travel agency, introducing other tours on Tourscan.
