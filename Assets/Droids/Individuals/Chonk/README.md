# Chonk

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chonk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chonk)

Chonk was a bounty droid who worked as an enforcer for the Haxion Brood criminal syndicate during the Imperial Era. Chonk was destroyed in 14 BBY, after engaging Cal Kestis in battle.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Bounty hunter
- **Name:** Chonk
- **Death:** 14 BBY
- **Line:** Haxion Brood Bounty Droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** White and Yellow
- **Affiliation:** Haxion Brood
