# T5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T5)

T5, or Tee-five, was a C1-series astromech droid owned by Nath Tensent. T5 had served Tensent in his Rebel Alliance squadron, and later in the New Republic's Alphabet Squadron. Four months after the Battle of Jakku, Nath Tensent let T5 fall under the ownership of Wyl Lark after the latter had visited Tensent on Freerock. Six years after the end of the Galactic Civil War, T5 was present when Wyl Lark asked fellow Alphabet Squadron member Yrica Quell if she wanted to meet a survivor of the Destruction of Nacronis after he had departed from a visit with Quell and Chass na Chadic at their home on Spirana.
