# SE-4Z

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SE-4Z](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SE-4Z)

SE-4Z, nicknamed Forzee was a Butler droid who served aboard Resistance Starship Ninka under Admiral Amilyn Holdo during the war against the First Order. Holdo programmed SE-4Z to watch over Mersh Pellus, a brilliant but absent-minded scientist. SE-4Z was responsible for reminding Mersh his magni-lens goggles were on the top of his head, and stopping him from entering the bridge during battles.
