# F-24-AMTx

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [F-24-AMTx](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F-24-AMTx)

F-24-AMTx, also known simply as F-24, was a masculine F series repair droid with green photoreceptors and silver plating, with a red trim. By the year 4.3 ABY, he was employed by the Star Tours travel agency at their Tomorrowland Starport. Along with F-23-DKMx, F-24 performed the final assembly of StarSpeeder 3000 and serviced their ion engines, and was stationed in front of a vertical moving lift.
