# R5-P8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-P8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-P8)

R5-P8 was an astromech droid owned by the Ohnaka Gang during the Clone Wars. In addition to a unique paint job, the droid was also heavily modified with a Weequay blaster pistol mounted atop his head.
