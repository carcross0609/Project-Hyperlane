# G2-9T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [G2-9T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G2-9T)

G2-9T was one of the supposedly rogue droids that attacked Constable Zuvio during his investigation of a banking ship robbery in Niima Outpost.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G2-9T
- **Homeworld:** Jakku
- **Death:** Sometime before the Escape from Jakku in 34 ABY
