# Unidentified medical droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_medical_droid)

A medical droid served the Jedi Order in the Jedi Temple on Coruscant during the High Republic Era. The medical droid treated Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh after she sustained injuries during a confrontation with Nihil leader Marchion Ro during a mission on the planet Vixoseph I.
