# PT04

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PT04](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PT04)

PT04 was the designation of a DUM-series pit droid. PT04 was one of several pit droids owned by the Toydarian junk dealer Watto, who offered the droid for sale along with its counterparts, PT02 and PT03, around 32 BBY. Most of the potential buyers were Podracer pilots. Watto priced each of his pit droids at 1,000 truguts, selling them from his shop on the planet of Tatooine. PT04 was tan in color, and had a black colored shape on his torso.
