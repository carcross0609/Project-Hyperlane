# R4-G7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R4-G7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-G7)

R4-G7 was an astromech droid assigned to Derek Klivian's X-wing in 5.5 ABY, around the time of the Battle of Mindor. Klivian called him "Arfour."
