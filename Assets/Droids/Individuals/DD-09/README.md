# DD-09

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DD-09](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DD-09)

DD-09, also known as Dee Dee, was an R2-series astromech droid. The battered droid was the property of the thief Esroc and assisted its master with petty crime.
