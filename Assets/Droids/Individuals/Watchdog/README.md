# Watchdog

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Watchdog](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Watchdog)

Watchdog was a remote combat platform created by the Sith cult known as the Unmasked to guard their research station in Sector R-4. It was created, in part, using the alchemical product known as nihrot and with the assistance of AIRA, an AI. It was destroyed by the Alliance Commander during the mission to the R-4 anomaly and it's remains were likely destroyed when the station exploded.
