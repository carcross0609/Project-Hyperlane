# GX-8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GX-8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GX-8)

GX-8 was a sewing assistant droid that was employed by Arta Kleidun, owner of Black Spire Outfitters, to help keep up with demand at her store. It helped with mending clothing and other minor sewing tasks using its many arms, each one of which was specially designed for mending and sewing garments.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GX-8
- **Model:** Sewing assistant droid
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Orange, black, and white
