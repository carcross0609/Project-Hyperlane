# Lone battle droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lone battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lone_battle_droid)

During the Clone Wars, a lone battle droid deserted the Separatist Droid Army after being disconnected from the droid collective. A B1-series battle droid forged by the Trade Federation, the droid had no independent thought, as its thinking was done by a Central Control Computer. It fought in numerous battles during the Clone Wars, but was continually damaged or destroyed, only to be rebuilt with new parts.
