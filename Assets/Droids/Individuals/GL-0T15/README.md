# GL-0T15

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GL-0T15](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GL-0T15)

GL-0T15 was a black-plated GL vendor droid owned by the criminal syndicate Crimson Dawn during the reign of the Galactic Empire. It served as the operator for the principal turbolift onboard the First Light, the personal yacht of Crimson Dawn's leader Dryden Vos.
