# R2-B3 (Jaina Solo)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-B3 (Jaina Solo)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-B3_%28Jaina_Solo%29)

During the Yuuzhan Vong War, R2-B3 was an astromech droid in Jaina Solo's X-wing when she was in command of the Twin Suns Squadron on Borleias, sometime after the Battle of Coruscant. The droid was nicknamed Cappie to honor deceased wingmate Anni Capstan.
