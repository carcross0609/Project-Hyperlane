# RZ-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RZ-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RZ-7)

RZ-7, or Arzee-Seven, was a masculine droid built by Karr Nuq Sin from spare parts he found, including the circuitry of a protocol droid, the hard drive of an astromech, and the casing of a medical droid. He later accompanied the Force-sensitive Nuq Sin on his travels throughout the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RZ-7
- **Death:** 32 ABY, Kijimi (later rebuilt)
- **Creator:** Karr Nuq Sin
- **Class:** Junk droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Plating:** Metallic blue
