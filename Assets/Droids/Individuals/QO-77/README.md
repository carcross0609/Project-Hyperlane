# QO-77

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [QO-77](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QO-77)

QO-77 was a battle droid of unknown origins available for purchase during the war against Zakuul. The unit was similar in appearance to QO-99, a member of Specialists.

- **Class:** Battle droid
