# Unidentified power droid (Jabba's Palace)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified power droid (Jabba's Palace)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_power_droid_%28Jabba%27s_Palace%29/Legends)

This EG-6 power droid was present in Jabba's Palace around 4 ABY. It was in the palace of Jabba the Hutt when C-3PO and R2-D2 came to the palace as part of the rescue of Han Solo. It squealed in pain as 8D8, under the command of its supervisor droid EV-9D9, lowered a hot glowing iron on the soles of its feet.
