# BB-19H

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BB-19H](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BB-19H)

BB-19H was a BB-series astromech droid that had a gold, red, and translucent red body, with a black sensor. The droid spent time at Maz Kanata's castle on Takodana where he quietly observed the smugglers, brigands, scouts, and spies there.
