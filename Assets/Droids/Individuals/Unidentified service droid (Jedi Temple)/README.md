# Unidentified service droid (Jedi Temple)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified service droid (Jedi Temple)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_service_droid_%28Jedi_Temple%29)

A service droid served the Jedi Order while stationed at the Jedi Temple on the planet Coruscant. During the High Republic Era, the service droid attempted to assist the human Avon Sunvale after the terminal she was at was malfunctioning due to her own actions. The droid managed to successfully help Sunvale before walking away.
