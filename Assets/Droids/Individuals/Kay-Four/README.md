# Kay-Four

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kay-Four](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kay-Four)

Kay-Four, or simply Four, was one of the K-series droids built by J-6 on the planet Aricho. Four and the other K droids were constructed as an army to aid the small resistance in fighting back against the Nihil marauders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kay-Four
- **Homeworld:** Aricho
- **Creator:** J-6
- **Model:** K-series droid
- **Affiliation:** Aricho's resistance force
