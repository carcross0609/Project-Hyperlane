# N-3RO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [N-3RO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N-3RO)

N-3RO was a droid who lived on the junk planet of Zoh. On Zoh he and other droids was led by Jek-14. Shorty after the Freemakers visited Zoh seeking Jek-14's help. N-3RO led a droid uprising against Jek-14 and the Freemakers. N-3RO succeeded to convince his fellow droids, including BL-OX and Fixer that humans can not be trusted. They failed and N-3RO was still on Zoh and had been fitted for a restraining bolt.
