# Thirteen

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thirteen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thirteen)

Thirteen was one of the medical droids who served in Fyzen Gor's Original Dozen. Two years after being repaired by Gor, the droid required replacement limbs. Before Gor could repair the droid, Cli Pastayra and the Wandering Star attacked the canyon he was hiding in, capturing both Gor and the surviving droids.
