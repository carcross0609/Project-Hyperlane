# VX-19

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [VX-19](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VX-19)

VX-19 was a modified LOM-series protocol droid with what appeared to be a standard 3PO-series head. VX-19 was left abandoned after a group of smugglers were slain and was taken in by the Ardan resistance. When a traitor was revealed in the ranks of Rebel Alliance, VX-19 was a possible suspect of treachery.
