# C2-N Dominator Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C2-N Dominator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C2-N_Dominator_Droid)

The C2-N Dominator Droid was a battle droid used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Galactic War. In 3640 BBY the droid, along with TZ-3 Dominator Droid and DX-2 Dominator Droid was used to defend the approach to the Bastion in the Coronet City on Corellia, but was destroyed during the assault by the Galactic Republic forces.
