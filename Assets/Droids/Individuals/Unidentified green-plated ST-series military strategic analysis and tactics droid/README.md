# Unidentified green-plated ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified green-plated ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green-plated_ST-series_military_strategic_analysis_and_tactics_droid)

An ST-series super tactical droid was a member of a Separatist crew that operated on the planet Loradil during the New Republic Era after their Providence-class carrier was shot down. Around 6 ABY, the super tactical droid encountered the humans Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, Beilert Valance and Rynn Zenat on the planet and fought them, but was eventually destroyed by Valance.
