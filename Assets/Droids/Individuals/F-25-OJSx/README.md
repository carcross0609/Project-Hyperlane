# F-25-OJSx

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [F-25-OJSx](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F-25-OJSx)

F-25-OJSx, also known simply as F-25, was a masculine F series repair droid with red photoreceptors and silver plating, with a red trim. By the year 4.3 ABY, he was employed by the Star Tours travel agency as a "Spacecap Droid" at their Tomorrowland Starport, where he worked in the hanger as a coat check. He was easily spotted by his red cap and bowtie.
