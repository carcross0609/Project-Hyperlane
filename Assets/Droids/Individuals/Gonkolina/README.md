# Gonkolina

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Gonkolina](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gonkolina)

Gonkolina was a GNK-series power droid who resided on Kashyyyk at an unknown time. They were protected on their journey through one of Kashyyyk's enormous wroshyr trees to fulfill their "electric destiny."
