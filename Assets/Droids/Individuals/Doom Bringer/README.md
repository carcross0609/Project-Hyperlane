# Doom Bringer

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Doom Bringer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doom_Bringer)

Doom Bringer was a unique battle droid found in the Neimoidian droid factory on Mustafar during the Galactic Civil War. It was destroyed along with its hands of doom by a group of spacers who went on to shut down the factory that provided battle droids for HK-47.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Doom Bringer
- **Death:** 1 ABY, Mustafar
- **Line:** Union Sentry Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** HK-47's Droid Army
