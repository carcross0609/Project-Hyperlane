# 8K6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [8K6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/8K6)

8K6 was a science droid in the service of the Sith Empire. He was aboard the Harbinger when Phaegon III's moon was stripped, and perished when the bridge on the Harbinger was destroyed.
