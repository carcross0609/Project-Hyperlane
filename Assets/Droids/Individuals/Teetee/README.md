# Teetee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Teetee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teetee)

Teetee was a junk droid created by Akar Duel on Lotho Minor. Despite having a restraining bolt, Teetee was unaffected by Duel's programming, and attempted to convince the other droids on Lotho Minor to rise up against their ruler. The arrival of the Millennium Falcon, along with C-3PO, a droid who could act for himself, led the droids to turn on their master and free themselves.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Teetee
- **Homeworld:** Lotho Minor
- **Creator:** Akar Duel
- **Class:** Monster droid
