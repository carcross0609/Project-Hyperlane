# Vee-Six

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vee-Six](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vee-Six)

Vee-Six was Doune's personal droid. He was always at his master's side, and always attempted to curb his master's obsessive gambling. He was abused verbally by his boss when the Herglic got angry.
