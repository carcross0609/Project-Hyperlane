# N4-01

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [N4-01](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N4-01)

N4-01 was the designation of a astromech droid in the service of the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. It relayed to Imperial forces an urgent transmission from Darth Malgus, asking them to come to Vaiken Spacedock for a mission regarding the Advozse Hegemony.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Sith Empire

- **Line:** X2-C3 Imperial astromech
- **Class:** Astromech droid

- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Black and red
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
