# G-1FY

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [G-1FY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-1FY)

G-1FY or Iffy was an old protocol droid who worked on the Wookiee starship Namrelllew in 22 BBY. Shortly after the beginning of the Clone Wars, he encountered Senator Padmé Amidala when she went undercover on the ship on a mission for the Galactic Senate. Iffy was secretly reprogrammed by the Neimoidian Oje N'deeb.
