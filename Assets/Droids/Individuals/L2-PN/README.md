# L2-PN

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [L2-PN](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L2-PN)

L2-PN was an astromech droid in the service of the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War. It was stationed aboard the Telos where it contacted a Republic strike team prior to the beginning of their mission to Taral V.
