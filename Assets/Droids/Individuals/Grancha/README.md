# Grancha

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grancha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grancha)

Grancha was an ancient Hutt Guard Droid that roamed an abandoned Hutt settlement on the planet of Mataou. Several months into the blockade of the Anoat sector by the Galactic Empire, Grancha was destroyed in a fight against the Smuggler, who was trying to reactivate a scanner in the droid's settlement.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grancha
- **Death:** During the Iron Blockade
- **Class:** Hutt Guard Droid
