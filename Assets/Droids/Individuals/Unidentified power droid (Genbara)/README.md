# Unidentified power droid (Genbara)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified power droid (Genbara)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_power_droid_%28Genbara%29)

A power droid belonging to a Sullustan male teahouse shopkeep on Genbara led to a meeting with the Ronin when the temperamental GNK-series power droid had sprung a leak. The Ronin repaired the droid and then drank tea that was heated on the power droid. After Kouru's Bandit Troopers attacked the village below the teahouse, the power droid wore sandals as it ran with the shopkeep to thank the Ronin.
