# Unidentified R5 unit (Jawas)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified R5 unit (Jawas)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_R5_unit_%28Jawas%29)

This R5 unit was owned by a Jawa clan who had presumably salvaged it. The droid was defective, and despite hours of long work clan's mechanic failed to completely repair it. The frustrated Jawa angrily kicked the droid causing it to topple over. As the Jawa mechanic angrily chased away fellow clan members who had been mocking the mechanic, the droid suddenly reactivated, apparently functioning perfectly.
