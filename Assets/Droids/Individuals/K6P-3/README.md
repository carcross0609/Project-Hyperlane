# K6P-3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K6P-3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K6P-3)

K6P-3, or Kaysixpee, was an astromech droid that Grand Master Satele Shan used as a messenger to recruit allies to combat the dark side threat that arose from the planet Athiss.

## Specifications

- **Name:** K6P-3
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Gold and grey
- **Affiliation:**
