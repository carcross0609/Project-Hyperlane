# Unidentified B1 battle droid 4 (Malevolence)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 4 (Malevolence)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_4_%28Malevolence%29)

A B1 battle droid was stationed on the Separatist Subjugator-class heavy cruiser Malevolence in 22 BBY. During the Battle of Abregado, the droid reported on the arrival of three Galactic Republic Venator-class Star Destroyers to Count Dooku, who ordered the droid to jam their transmissions.
