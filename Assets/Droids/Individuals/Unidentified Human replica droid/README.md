# Unidentified Human replica droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Human replica droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Human_replica_droid)

A male-patterned Human replica droid infiltrated the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. During its mission, the replica droid had its face damaged and killed a member of the Alliance. The droid's Human guise possessed light skin and brown hair.
