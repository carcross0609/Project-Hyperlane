# G0-T0-Telerath

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G0-T0-Telerath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G0-T0-Telerath)

G0-T0-Telerath was a G0-T0 Infrastructure-Planning droid developed by Aratech Repulsor Company assigned to serve as a central organizational hub for the planet Telerath. It was one of many G0-T0 droids whose memories were corrupted by the massive amounts of data flowing through them, inadvertently creating personalities and independent motivations. G0-T0-Telerath temporarily took control of one of the galaxy's largest financial institutions and caused an economic emergency when it demanded control of the Coruscant Financial Exchange.
