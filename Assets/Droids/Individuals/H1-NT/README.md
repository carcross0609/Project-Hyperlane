# H1-NT

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [H1-NT](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H1-NT)

H1-NT was a protocol droid designed to give hints and advice to travelers across the galaxy. During various points between 32 BBY and 35 ABY, he was stationed on multiple planets and moons including: Ahch-To, Ajan Kloss, Bespin, Cantonica, Coruscant, Crait, Dagobah, D'Qar, Endor, Exegol, Geonosis, Hoth, Jakku, Kamino, Kashyyyk, Kef Bir, Mustafar, Naboo, Pasaana, Takodana, Tatooine, Utapau, and Yavin 4. Over the years, he became bored with his constant feeding of information and his personality became more jaded. The droid had yellow sensors, and blue and orange plating. He had a blue paw print logo on the right side of his chest plating.
