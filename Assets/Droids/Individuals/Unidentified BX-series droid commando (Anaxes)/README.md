# Unidentified BX-series droid commando (Anaxes)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando (Anaxes)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_%28Anaxes%29)

This BX-series droid commando served on Anaxes during the battle between the Grand Army of the Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. It reported to Admiral Trench on the unusual tactics employed by Clone Force 99 during their infiltration of the Cyber Center. Trench ordered the droid and his forces to sweep the area to search for the clone troopers.
