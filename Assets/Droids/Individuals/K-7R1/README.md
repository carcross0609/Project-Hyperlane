# K-7R1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [K-7R1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-7R1)

K-7R1 was a KX-series security droid manufactured by Arakyd Industries. Standing 2.16 meters tall, it had white sensors and black plating marked with white patterning. The security droid hoped to be reactivated, but was dismantled by Mubo, owner of the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu, who displayed the parts of K-7R1 in the store.
