# Spikewheel

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spikewheel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spikewheel)

Spikewheel was a droid scavenger who captained the starship Salvage-1. A group of scavengers, including the Nikto Gritz, hoped to join her crew, though Spikewheel was only interested in recruiting Gritz as her first mate aboard the Salvage-1.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spikewheel
- **Death:** Between 32 BBY and 20 BBY, Salvage-1, above Lotho Minor
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Golden
