# S4-C8-GE3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S4-C8-GE3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S4-C8-GE3)

S4-C8-GE3 was a GE3-series protocol droid with feminine programming in 3951 BBY. She had what appeared to be a personality quirk: an obsession for pazaak. She was a regular at the Pazaak Den next to the Nar Shaddaa Refugee Sector cantina. One reason she was so popular was because, despite having a passion for the game, she was very bad at it, and would often lose large sums of credits on wagers.
