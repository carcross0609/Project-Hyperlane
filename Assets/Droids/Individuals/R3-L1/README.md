# R3-L1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R3-L1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-L1)

R3-L1 was an R3-series astromech droid that had blue and green plating and a transparent dome. It served the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War against the Alliance to Restore the Republic. In a story told by Bobbajo, R3 was on duty on the control room of the battle station's tractor beam when a Snee called Qyp tried to disabled the tractor beam. R3 tried to stop him but Qyp faced down the droid.

- **Death:** During the Battle of Yavin, Death Star I, Yavin system
