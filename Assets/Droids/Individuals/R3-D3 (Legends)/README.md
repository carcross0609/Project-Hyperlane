# R3-D3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R3-D3/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-D3/Legends)

R3-D3 was an R3-series astromech droid working for Star Tours. R3-D3 was in the Droidnostics Center of the Tomorrowland Starport with other damaged droids waiting to be repaired around the time of the Battle of Death Star III. It had a bad motivator.
