# TC-11

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TC-11](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TC-11)

TC-11 was a TC-series protocol droid owned by the Trade Federation. TC-11 accompanied Gode Takrab to the surface of Naboo during the Trade Federation's invasion of the planet.
