# DLC-13

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DLC-13](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DLC-13)

DLC-13 was a DLC-13 mining droid with masculine programming who once helped the B1-series battle droid R0-GR save his friend Rowan Freemaker from certain doom. After the incident, DLC-13 stayed in contact with Roger, and even sent him cards on Life Day.
