# Control (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Control (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Control_%28droid%29)

Control was a Terreene Brainiac model of control droid tasked with running the Imperial mobile mining operation on the planet Alabash. It was well-programmed to carry out its task, finding and extracting ores from the surface of a planet. It was also capable of defending itself, but lacked the equipment to do so and instead deployed the various droids under its control to deal with threats.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Control
- **Model:** Terreene Brainiac
- **Class:** Control droid
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
