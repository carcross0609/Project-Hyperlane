# R1-J1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R1-J1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R1-J1)

R1-J1 was an R1-series astromech droid with green plating and a yellow trim. It stood 1.94 meters tall and, like all R-series astromech droids, was manufactured by Industrial Automaton. During the occupation of the planet Batuu by the First Order in 34 ABY, R1-J1 belonged to the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on Batuu. The droid was stood behind railings on a plinth outside the front of the depot alongside fellow droids B-R72, EG-01, L4-R6, and C2-B9.
