# TR0-F33

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TR0-F33](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TR0-F33)

TR0-F33 was a trophy droid created by the Mon Calamari Sprocket. He was programed to be the main objective in the game of Trophy Chase, and possessed a mischievous and childlike personality, loving to dance and be part of the action whenever possible. While most found TR0-F33 charming, the droid J-3DI viewed him as an annoying little brother who did not take his job in the game seriously enough.
