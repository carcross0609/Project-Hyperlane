# B9-D7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B9-D7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B9-D7)

B9-D7 was an astromech droid who served the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. The droid was stationed on the planet Nar Shaddaa where it acted as a messenger, projecting holograms of Colonel Siturn so he could communicate with Imperials.
