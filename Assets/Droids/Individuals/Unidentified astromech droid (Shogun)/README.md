# Unidentified astromech droid (Shogun)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified astromech droid (Shogun)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_astromech_droid_%28Shogun%29)

An astromech droid accompanied the Jedi known as Shogun while they traveled on the snowy mountain Mount Hakamori on an astronomical object. When they saw a group of bandits attempting to steal the astromech droid R5-D56 from the former Sith known as the Ronin, the two left to avoid being hit by a stray blaster bolt. At night, Shogun and the astromech droid met the Ronin and R5-D56 at a hot spring and joined them in it. As they departed the next day, the astromech and R5-D56 waved goodbye to each other.
