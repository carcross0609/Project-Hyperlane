# Spybot

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spybot](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spybot)

Spybot was a spy droid who served the former Sith Lord Maul and the Shadow Collective on the planet Janix during the Imperial Era. He was destroyed by the First Brother when the latter threw his lightsaber through the droid's body and caused it to fall into acid.
