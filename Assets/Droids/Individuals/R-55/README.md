# R-55

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-55](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-55)

R-55 was a police droid on Vorzyd V. In 1 ABY, R-55 responded when C-3PO called out for help after Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa had been captured by Blackhole troopers. R2-D2, however, tricked R-55 into believing that C-3PO had a malfunction so as not to reveal Skywalker and Organa's secret mission to Vorzyd V. Suggesting that C-3PO report to the local repair center, R-55 left again.

## Specifications

- **Homeworld:** Vorzyd V
- **Class:** Police-droid
