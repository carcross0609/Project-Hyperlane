# WED-15-ST2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [WED-15-ST2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WED-15-ST2)

WED-15-ST2 was a WED-15 Septoid Treadwell droid on Tatooine. It belonged to Fixer, working at Tosche Station in Anchorhead. Luke Skywalker was envious of the droid due to the limited capabilities of his own treadwell, WED-15-77.
