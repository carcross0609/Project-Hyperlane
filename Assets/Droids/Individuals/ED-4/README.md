# ED-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ED-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ED-4)

ED-4 was a slicer droid who was one of the three droid brains that made up the computer of the starship Millennium Falcon. The other two were known as V5-T and L3-37. Long before the Millennium Falcon was known to Lando Calrissian or L3-37, the droid's consciousness was uploaded onto the ship to join V5-T, who had been the first to join the Falcon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ED-4
- **Class:** Slicer droid
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Affiliation:** Millennium Collective
