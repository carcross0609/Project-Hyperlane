# S4-E Support Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S4-E Support Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S4-E_Support_Droid)

S4-E Support Droid was a probe droid used by the Order of Revan during the Galactic War. During the Battle of Rishi in 3637 BBY it assisted the Revanites Rarrook and Marko Ka who tried to stop a strike team from reaching the signal jammer on the Razorback Island, but failed and were killed along with the droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S4-E Support Droid
- **Death:** 3637 BBY, Rishi
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Order of Revan
