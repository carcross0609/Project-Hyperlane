# OOM-87

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [OOM-87](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OOM-87)

OOM-87, also known as OOM87, was a B1-series battle droid who served in the Separatist Droid Army during the Clone Wars. During a battle on the planet Florrum, the droid was destroyed by Jedi younglings Zatt and Petro while making a report to General Grievous.
