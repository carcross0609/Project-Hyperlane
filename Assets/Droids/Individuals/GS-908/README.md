# GS-908

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GS-908](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GS-908)

GS-908 was a battle droid manufactured by Galactic Solutions Industries. In 3637 BBY it was escorting the GSI survey team working on mapping the Emperor's Eye on Ziost. When the Invasion of Ziost happened and Vitiate wiped all life on the planet, the droid malfunctioned. It later attacked those who returned the surface of the dead world, only to be destroyed.
