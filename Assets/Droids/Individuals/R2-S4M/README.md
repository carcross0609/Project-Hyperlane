# R2-S4M

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-S4M](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-S4M)

R2-S4M was an R2-series astromech droid that had learned enough respect to serve as a bartender for the Hutt crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure. In 4 ABY, he served drinks with a drinks dispenser and tray aboard the Khetanna, Jabba's sail barge, while it was traveling to the Great Pit of Carkoon on the planet Tatooine.
