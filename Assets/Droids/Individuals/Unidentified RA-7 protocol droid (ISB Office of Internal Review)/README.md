# Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (ISB Office of Internal Review)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (ISB Office of Internal Review)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RA-7_protocol_droid_%28ISB_Office_of_Internal_Review%29)

An RA-7 protocol droid worked for the Galactic Empire's Imperial Security Bureau at the ISB Office of Internal Review on Coruscant. The RA-7 protocol droid greeted Admiral Corleque, Governor Tauntaza, Agent Fabarian, and Council Member Xanto when the four Imperials were sent to the office on charges against the Empire. Confirming their identities, the protocol droid sent them in to speak with Sub-Administrator Sly Moore.
