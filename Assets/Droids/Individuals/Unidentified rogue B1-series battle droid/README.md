# Unidentified rogue B1-series battle droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified rogue B1-series battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_rogue_B1-series_battle_droid)

A B1-series battle droid served with the Separatist Droid Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. After that army's deactivation in 19 BBY, the droid remained in operation and at some point went rogue, eventually becoming a bounty hunter. At one point, the droid was present at the scene of a battle in a desert area.
