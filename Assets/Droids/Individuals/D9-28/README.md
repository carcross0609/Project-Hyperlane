# D9-28

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D9-28](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D9-28)

D9-28 was an astromech droid sent to the planet Ilum as part of a survey team to investigate the wreckage of Darth Malgus's space station during the Third Galactic War around 3623 BBY. D9-28 was destroyed during the mission, but its log records remained intact. Later, the Alliance Commander and Lana Beniko reviewed the log records and played a recording from just before the droid's destruction.

## Specifications

- **Name:** D9-28
- **Death:** c. 3623 BBY, Ilum
- **Line:** X2-C3 Imperial astromech
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Gray (with red stripes)
- **Affiliation:** Eternal Alliance
