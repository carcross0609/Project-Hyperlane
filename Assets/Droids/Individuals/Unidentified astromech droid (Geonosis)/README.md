# Unidentified astromech droid (Geonosis)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified astromech droid (Geonosis)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_astromech_droid_%28Geonosis%29)

An astromech droid served the Galactic Empire onboard an Imperial construction module over the planet Geonosis. When the Spectres arrived on the module, the astromech closed several blast doors to trap the rebels and allow ISB agent Alexsandr Kallus and a group of stormtroopers to attempt to apprehend them. The rebels' C1-series astromech droid, Chopper, was alerted to the situation and engaged in battle with the astromech before shutting him down.
