# Unidentified safety droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified safety droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_safety_droid)

A safety droid was in the service of At Attin's government in 9 ABY. When Wendle tried to find the barrier access code to try and sneak a message out to find his son Wim, the safety droid noticed a surge coming from the terminal.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified safety droid
- **Homeworld:** At Attin
- **Line:** Safety droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** White
- **Affiliation:** At Attin's government
