# Bee-Zerobee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bee-Zerobee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bee-Zerobee)

Bee-Zerobee was a WED Treadwell repair droid. He was owned by the Klatooinian Trade Guild on Boztrok, and was given to Platt Okeefe along with her first ship the Brentaal Princess.
