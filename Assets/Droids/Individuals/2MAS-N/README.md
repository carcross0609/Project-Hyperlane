# 2MAS-N

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2MAS-N](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2MAS-N)

2MAS-N was second foot droid to High Magistrate Greef Karga of the planet Nevarro, serving alongside his first foot droid, 1BAR-0. In 9 ABY, the droids held up Karga's Mantle of Office cape while he greeted his allies Din Djarin and Grogu in Nevarro City and later when the trio confronted a group of pirates in the city. They also accompanied the High Magistrate and held his cape when he took Djarin and Grogu to the workshop of the Anzellan Droidsmiths.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 2MAS-N
- **Class:** Foot droid
- **Plating:** Red, gray, and white
