# SK-Z38

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SK-Z38](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SK-Z38)

SK-Z38 was a Bird Droid found at the Star Tours travel agency. Two of them were befriended by Star Tours droid G2-4T a few months after the Battle of Endor. They usually were located on the droid lifts and other high places, so they could make their nests. They were also capable of laying eggs, something 4T was unaware of, although their chances of the eggs hatching were smaller than RX-24 making his first flight without problems.
