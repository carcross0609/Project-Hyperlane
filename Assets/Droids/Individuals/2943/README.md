# 2943

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2943](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2943)

2943 was a B1-series battle droid that served in the Trade Federation Droid Army. During the Naboo Crisis, 2943 was escorting Queen Padmé Amidala and other captives throughout Theed. 2943 was destroyed by Qui-Gon Jinn, who jumped from a ledge and bisected the droid in half.
