# Thax's sabotage droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thax's sabotage droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thax%27s_sabotage_droid)

During the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, a droid was planted on the starfighter of Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi by the agent of Separatist Duchess Thax to sabotage the starfighter in an attempt to discredit the Republic in the negotiations on the planet Themis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thax's sabotage droid
- **Death:** 21 BBY, Themis moon
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Plating:** Grey
- **Equipment:** Laser cutter
