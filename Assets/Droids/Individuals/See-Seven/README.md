# See-Seven

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [See-Seven](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/See-Seven)

See-Seven was an RA-7 protocol droid in the service of the Galactic Empire. According to a story told to the Mustafarian youths Giggek and Tuttel by the elder Lellis, See-Seven assisted Imperial Inspector Vaneé when he was sent to investigate a number of insane stormtroopers stationed at Fortress Vader, of the Sith Lord Darth Vader on the planet Mustafar. See-Seven stunned one of the victims after the trooper threatened and attacked Vaneé, who requested that the protocol droid have the trooper restrained. Vaneé entered deeper within the fortress to investigate the circumstances, subsequently being driven mad due to lava fumes leaking into the structure.
