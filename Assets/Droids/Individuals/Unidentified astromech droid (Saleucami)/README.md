# Unidentified astromech droid (Saleucami)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified astromech droid (Saleucami)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_astromech_droid_%28Saleucami%29)

An Imperial astromech droid of R-series was on the planet Saleucami in 19 BBY. When Omega tried to get clearance codes, she accidentally bumped into the droid, who tried to sound the alarm before getting destroyed by Wrecker of Clone Force 99.
