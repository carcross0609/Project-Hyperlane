# Number Seven

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Number Seven](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Number_Seven)

Number Seven was one of the medical droids Fyzen Gor's Original Dozen, who had been repaired using limbs taken from Wookiees. Number Seven was one of the droids who assisted Gor in his activation of the Phylanx Redux Transmitter in the Mesulan Remnants Belt. Upon confronting Lando Calrissian, Gor ordered Number Seven and Number Five to take his weapons, but failed to notice the thermal detonators Calrissian had. Number Seven was destroyed when Calrissian used the detonators to destroy the Phylanx Redux Transmitter's chamber.
