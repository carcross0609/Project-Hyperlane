# J1-B2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J1-B2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J1-B2)

J1-B2 was an R4 astromech droid, manufactured by Industrial Automaton. In 1 BBY, the droid was present in the Great Temple, headquarters of the Alliance to Restore the Republic on the moon Yavin 4, when the Alliance forces within were scrambled for an attack on the planet Scarif. The droid recorded the scramble orders and an image of the temple in a memory transcript.
