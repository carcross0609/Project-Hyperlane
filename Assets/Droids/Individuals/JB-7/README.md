# JB-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JB-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JB-7)

JB-7 was a BB-series astromech droid who had all of its vital parts upgraded by its kind master in 34 ABY. Following the upgrade, JB-7 called in to the radio show Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7 on station BSO 401.72 in order to offer to donate its old parts to a droid in need.
