# MGAD-47

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MGAD-47](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MGAD-47)

MGAD-47 was an AD-series weapons maintenance droid who served the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. MGAD-37 was assigned to the Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Mantan Wanderer, overseeing the armory in Section Desh-32, which stored weaponry for Alliance Special Forces taskforce Team 19.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MGAD-47
- **Model:** AD-series weapons maintenance droid
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
