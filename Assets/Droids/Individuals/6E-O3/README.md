# 6E-O3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [6E-O3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/6E-O3)

6E-O3 was a droid that sold gear to the forces of the reconstituted Sith Empire forces while stationed on CZ-198, during the time of the Galactic War between the Sith Empire and Galactic Republic. Its droid model was similar in function and appearance to Imperial DroidWorks' Seneschal-series factotum droid.
