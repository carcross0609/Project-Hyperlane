# Remedy

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Remedy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Remedy)

Remedy was an MD-5 medical droid who served as the resident medical droid at The Broken Tusk on Reuss VIII. Fighters who needed medical attention after losing fights in the Dool Arena received free treatment from Remedy.
