# Unidentified EX droid (Emerick Caphtor)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified EX droid (Emerick Caphtor)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_EX_droid_%28Emerick_Caphtor%29)

An EX droid was sent into the Occlusion Zone, a region of space controlled by the Nihil marauder organization, during the High Republic Era, where it was found and utilized by Jedi Master Emerick Caphtor to send messages to the Galactic Republic in Republic-controlled space.

- **Class:** Communications droid
