# Unidentified super tactical droid (Carida)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified super tactical droid (Carida)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_super_tactical_droid_%28Carida%29)

A ST-series super tactical droid without a name served as the overseer of a mission in order to destroy the Republic strategy conference. To do so, he hijacked the Venator-class Star Destroyer Renown and loaded it with explosive rhydonium. However, D-Squad boarded the ship after escaping Abafar, and with hopes of completing their mission, went to the bridge to hand in an encryption module. They were confronted by the droid, who ordered them to surrender or be destroyed.
