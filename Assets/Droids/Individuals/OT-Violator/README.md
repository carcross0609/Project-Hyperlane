# OT-Violator

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-Violator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-Violator)

OT-Violator was a mysterious droid active in the Naboo system during the Galactic Civil War. Around the time of the Battle of Yavin, a group of starships led by OT-Violator ambushed an unit of the Imperial Navy commanded by Captain Silverline in the Area D-512 of the Naboo system. The Imperial forces suffered heavy casualties and were not able to identify this hostile force that was code-named Penumbra Omen. Captain Silverline, Imperial Authoritarian, was only able to report that the leader of the assailants identified itself as the OT-Violator. The Empire later prohibited all travel in the Area D-512, renamed Penumbra Omen Quadrant.
