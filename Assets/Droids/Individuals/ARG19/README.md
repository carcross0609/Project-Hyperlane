# ARG19

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ARG19](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ARG19)

ARG19, also known as "Aggie," was a mechanically simple, yellow-plated droid who resided on the planet Mina-Rau around 1 BBY. She became friends with the droid B2EMO. In 1 BBY, ARG19 and B2EMO raced alongside a table.

- **Name:** ARG19"Aggie"
