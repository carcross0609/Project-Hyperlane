# Iggy

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iggy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iggy)

Iggy was a IG-97 assassin droid who rose to prominence on Tatooine during the Galactic Civil War. Working with Big Gizz, Spiker, Onoh, and Klepti B'ay, the group was able to destroy a rogue dark trooper.
