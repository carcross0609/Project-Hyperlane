# Arfive (Jhensara)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arfive (Jhensara)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arfive_%28Jhensara%29)

Arfive was an R5-series astromech droid who belonged to the starship captain Jhensara. Arfive was one of three droids on the ship alongside Artoo and Arthree.
