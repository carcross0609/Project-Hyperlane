# Unidentified WED-15 Treadwell (Lars homestead)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified WED-15 Treadwell (Lars homestead)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_WED-15_Treadwell_%28Lars_homestead%29)

A WED-15 Treadwell repair droid, produced by the Cybot Galactica company, worked for moisture farmer Owen Lars on the planet Tatooine during the Galactic Civil War. A fifth-degree droid, the Treadwell sometimes misidentified the noise of sand flies for malfunctioning equipment and followed them.
