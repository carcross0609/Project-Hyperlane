# TM-09

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TM-09](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TM-09)

TM-09 was the designation of a security droid tasked as a warden team leader on the Belsavis by the Rakata. Stationed in the Tomb section of Belsavis prison around 3641 BBY, it requested assistance from a Republic citizen after his subordinate droids were reprogrammed by the Esh-kha and asked to reprogram them back.
