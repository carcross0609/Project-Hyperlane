# RM-T2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RM-T2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RM-T2)

RM-T2 was an astromech droid that worked on Tatooine during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. RM had a shop in the Anchorhead market, where it sold equipment commendations.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RM-T2
- **Homeworld:** Tatooine
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Silver and yellow
