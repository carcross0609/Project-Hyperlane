# Unidentified bank supervisor droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified bank supervisor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_bank_supervisor_droid)

A supervisor droid was in charge of a bank tower owned by the Gran crime lord Nico Deemis. During a raid on a bank tower, the bank droid was kidnapped by the Nightbrother Scorn, one of the Shadow Collective personnel, and brought back to a safehouse. Cutting the droid's head with his lightsaber, Maul used this to frame Deemis's rival Looti Vario and cause a fight on neutral territory near a port.
