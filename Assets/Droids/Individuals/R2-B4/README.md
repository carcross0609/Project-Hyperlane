# R2-B4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-B4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-B4)

R2-B4 was an astromech droid who was dispatched by Amber Comark to assist an Alliance Special Operations team during their mission to expose her father's Death Hunter project on Zeta Zero Nine. A battered droid, R2-B4 was nonetheless feisty and loyal. It carried Comark's messages and orders to the Alliance team and was instrumental in the outcome of the mission.
