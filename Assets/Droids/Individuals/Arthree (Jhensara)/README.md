# Arthree (Jhensara)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arthree (Jhensara)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arthree_%28Jhensara%29)

Arthree was an R3-series astromech droid who belonged to the starship captain Jhensara. Arthree was one of three droids on the ship alongside Artoo and Arfive.
