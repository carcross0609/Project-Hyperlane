# Sarnath

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sarnath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarnath)

Sarnath was an ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid under the direct command of Confederacy of Independent Systems Head of State Count Dooku. Unlike other ST-series tactical droids, Sarnath was constructed with prototype processors, giving him greater problem-solving abilities than other tactical droids, as well as a calm and methodical demeanor. Sarnath accompanied Count Dooku, secretly the Sith Lord known as Darth Tyranus, to a mission to Diso, piloting an experimental Techno Union transporter.
