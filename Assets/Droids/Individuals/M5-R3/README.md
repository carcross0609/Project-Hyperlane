# M5-R3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M5-R3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M5-R3)

M5-R3 was an R5 series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. It had red, black and white paneling and an R5 dome with a black sensor. In 34 ABY during the occupation of the planet Batuu by the First Order, M5-R3 was present in Black Spire Outpost. The droid spent time waiting for an oil bath in the Service Yard droid repair station of the outpost's Droid Depot while the astromech droid S2-M8 used the bath.
