# TT-32-GE3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TT-32-GE3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TT-32-GE3)

TT-32-GE3 was the thirty-second GE3-series protocol droid to find itself in the employ of Tien Tubb, its predecessors all having been destroyed in accidents (including explosions). TT-32 asked Meetra Surik to retrieve IT-31 from Kodin and later rewarded her for completing the task.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TT-32-GE3
- **Homeworld:** Nar Shaddaa
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Corporation
- **Model:** GE3-series protocol droid
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Tien Tubb
