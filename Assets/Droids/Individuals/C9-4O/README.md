# C9-4O

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C9-4O](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C9-4O)

C9-4O was a shopkeeper droid who was active during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. During the war the droid worked on Carrick Station, where it sold targeting devices and blue sparkle powder. C9-4O had golden plating, yellow sensors, and was capable of projecting a holographic interface from its left arm.
