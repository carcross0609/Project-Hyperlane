# Unidentified red Cyban Front droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified red Cyban Front droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_red_Cyban_Front_droid)

A red droid operated on behalf of the Cyban Front criminal organization during the time of the Galactic Empire. Around 1 ABY, along with a blue droid, the red droid represented the Cyban Front at the auction of the Rur Crystal on the Sorca Retreat space station, hosted by the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Aphra. The droids aimed to destroy the crystal due to the technopathy abilities of Eternal Rur, the consciousness that inhabited it, asserting that the power to control technology was detrimental to droid rights.
