# 4/NG

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [4/NG](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/4/NG)

4/NG or Four-Slashing was a service droid owned by Grappa the Hutt as part of his operations on Genon. A valet and majordomo, 4/NG was repeatedly the target of droid-hating Gamorrean Tront.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 4/NG
- **Homeworld:** Genon
- **Line:** Service droid
