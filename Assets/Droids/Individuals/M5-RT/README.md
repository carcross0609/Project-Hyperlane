# M5-RT

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M5-RT](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M5-RT)

M5-RT was an astromech droid at the time of the Galactic War. After the Conquest of Makeb, it was sent to the Imperial Orbital Station as a courier by Under-Moff Bensen to help him enlist the services of the Imperial strike team regarding the containment and destruction of evidence of Imperial presence on the planet, Makeb.
