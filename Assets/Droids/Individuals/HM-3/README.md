# HM-3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HM-3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HM-3)

HM-3 was a Legal analyst droid active in the halls of the Galactic Alliance Senate during the Second Galactic Civil War. HM-3 helped Jacen Solo change a law about changing laws in order to get more supplies to the GAG, but the law was later used to remove Cal Omas from office in 40 ABY. HM-3 reminded Jacen of C-3PO; humanoid in shape with a personality, but was strongly professional.

- **Class:** Analysis droid
