# QT-3PO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [QT-3PO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QT-3PO)

QT-3PO was a protocol droid. Shortly before the Battle of Yavin, in 0 BBY, QT-3PO and its master set down in Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine in their freighter. While the freighter was being repaired, QT-3PO wandered off to explore, but was forcibly abducted by the Jawas of Nebit's clan at blaster-point. There, QT-3PO observed Jawa society and the interior of their sandcrawler.

## Specifications

- **Name:** QT-3PO
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** QT-3PO's master
