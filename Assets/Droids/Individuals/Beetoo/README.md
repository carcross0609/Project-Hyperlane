# Beetoo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beetoo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beetoo)

Beetoo was an R-series astromech droid who accompanied Jedi Master Jocasta Nu in the days following the proclamation of the New Order. The duo flew a gold Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor from Jocasta Nu's school to the Coruscant Underworld for Nu to infiltrate the former Jedi Temple to retrieve a list of Force-sensitive children across the galaxy.
