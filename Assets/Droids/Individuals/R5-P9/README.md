# R5-P9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R5-P9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-P9)

R5-P9 was a black and gray astromech droid that served onboard Ekelarc Yong's Y-wing starfighter during the Battle of Endor. The droid was destroyed, along with Yong and his fighter during the battle.
