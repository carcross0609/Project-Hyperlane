# Unidentified droideka (Zonus Corporation)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified droideka (Zonus Corporation)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droideka_%28Zonus_Corporation%29)

A golden droideka owned by Zonus Corporation was at one point employed by businessman Jin-Sim Canbelon. The large droideka variant could be directly controlled by a human pilot. Canbelon used the droideka in his duel against the bounty hunter Sevn.
