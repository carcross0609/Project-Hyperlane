# NP-87

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [NP-87](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NP-87)

NP-87 was a droid who was a companion of the Pooba spacer Zeepohz. During the High Republic Era, NP-87 and Zeepohz were transporting cargo on a starship to the planet Tenoo. When the starship crashed in a forest, they were helped by three Jedi younglings Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay and the Pooba Nubs. After encountering several obstacles, they managed to reach Kublop Springs spaceport and deliver their cargo to another starship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** NP-87
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Silver and gray
