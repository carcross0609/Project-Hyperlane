# Unidentified BX-series droid commando (The Citadel)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando (The Citadel)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_%28The_Citadel%29/Legends)

This unidentified droid commando was a BX-series droid commando that was assigned to a Separatist prison named the Citadel during a Republic attack to free Jedi Master Even Piell. When the Jedi managed to escape with the help of a squad led by Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, this droid and a number of other commando droids accompanied the Phindian Osi Sobeck to the dungeon where Piell's naval fleet officers and their captain, Wilhuff Tarkin had been held. The commando reported to Sobeck that Piell and Piell's officers had escaped, and the angered Phindian responded by ordering another BX-series commando to execute the droid.
