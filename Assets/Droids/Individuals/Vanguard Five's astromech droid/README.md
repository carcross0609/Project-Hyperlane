# Vanguard Five's astromech droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vanguard Five's astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard_Five%27s_astromech_droid)

An astromech droid was assigned to Vanguard Five some time after the Battle of Endor and flew with them whenever they were in an X-wing starfighter or a BTL Y-wing starfighter. This droid was present at the Incursion in the Bormea sector, the Battle of Yavin Prime, and the Strike on Esseles.

- **Type:** New Republic
- **Name:** Vanguard Five's astromech droid

- **Affiliation:**
