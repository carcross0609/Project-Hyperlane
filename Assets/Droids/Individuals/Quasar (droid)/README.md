# Quasar (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quasar (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quasar_%28droid%29)

"Quick-draw" Quasar was a monster droid racer who participated in Riot Racing on the planet Serolonis. The droid combined the body of a 3PO-series protocol droid with the head of a B1-series battle droid.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Athlete
- **Name:** Quasar
- **Model:** Monster droid (B1/3PO hybrid)
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Purple, yellow, and gray
