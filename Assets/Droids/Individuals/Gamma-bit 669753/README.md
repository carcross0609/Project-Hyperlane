# Gamma-bit 669753

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gamma-bit 669753](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamma-bit_669753)

Gamma-bit 669753 was a member of Droid Gotra. Sometime after Droid Gotra attacked and eliminated an armed Ubrikkian freighter commanded by Red Key Raiders at the Perlemian Spur Waypoint 643Besh, Gamma-bit sweeped the wreckage and retrieved the Smuggler's Guide previously owned by Magnus the Bloodsoaked. Droid Gotra lost the logbook in the battle with Guavian Death Gang, and it was recovered by Bala-Tik.
