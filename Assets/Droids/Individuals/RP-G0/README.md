# RP-G0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RP-G0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RP-G0)

RP-G0 was an astromech droid in Industrial Automaton's R5 line that served in the Resistance during their conflict with the First Order. The droid was assigned to the Sullustan pilot Nien Nunb. Around thirty years after the Battle of Endor, it was stationed in the Resistance's base of operations on the planet D'Qar and took part in the preparation for their attack on the First Order's Starkiller Base superweapon. RP-G0 had red and white plating and stood 0.97 meters tall. It was equipped with a Burtt acoustic signaler.
