# Ronto Roasters R5 unit

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ronto Roasters R5 unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ronto_Roasters_R5_unit)

This R5 unit was employed by Bakkar's Ronto Roasters restaurant at Black Spire Outpost on Batuu. The blue colored droid could be seen carrying Ronto Wraps and beverages to sell to potential customers who passed by the restaurant alongside 8D-J8. The R5 unit was seen doing that when Remex Io once passed by the establishment.
