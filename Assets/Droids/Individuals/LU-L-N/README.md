# LU-L-N

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [LU-L-N](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LU-L-N)

LU-L-N was an 8D smelter droid who served as a reporter for The Emperor's Court. After a trial for the murder of Greedo, LU-L-N interviewed the defendant, Han Solo, who lost the case and was sentenced to life frozen in carbonite. After the short discussion, LU-L-N directed him to the chamber and Boba Fett. The plaintiff, Neela, the mother of Greedo, was interviewed second. Afterwards, LU-L-N concluded the segment with a tease for the next episode.
