# V-18

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V-18](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-18)

V-18 was a droid owned by the Jedi Padawan Ram Jomaram on the planet Valo during the High Republic Era. Possessing a boxy appearance, V-18 had been painted purple by Jomaram to avoid him having the appearance of a crate. When Jomaram investigated a security breach at Crashpoint Tower, a communications tower outside Lonisa City, he provided V-18 with a mobility upgrade so that the droid could act as transportation. Over the course of the ensuing Nihil attack, the Bonbraks Tip and Breebak gave V-18 several more upgrades, including weapons systems and more advanced propulsion.
