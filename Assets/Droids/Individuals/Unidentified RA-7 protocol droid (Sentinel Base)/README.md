# Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Sentinel Base)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Sentinel Base)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RA-7_protocol_droid_%28Sentinel_Base%29)

An RA-7 protocol droid served the Galactic Empire during the early years of the organization's reign. The droid was stationed on the facility known as Sentinel Base, where on one occasion it acted as the tailor of Governor Wilhuff Tarkin. The RA-7 unit made suggestions based on the fashion of the planet Coruscant which Tarkin was initially uninterested in. The droid managed to change the Governor's mind and presented a projection of the proposed uniform, which Tarkin began modifying. Before the fitting could be complete, Tarkin was drawn away to deal with reports of an attack on the nearby Rampart Station.
