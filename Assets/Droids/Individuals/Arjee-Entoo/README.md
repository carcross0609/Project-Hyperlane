# Arjee-Entoo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arjee-Entoo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arjee-Entoo)

Arjee-Entoo was a gold plated droid that worked for Phaedra between the years 40 BBY and 34 BBY. The droid was destroyed after being shot down in Phaedra's shuttle by the bounty hunter Zam Wesell in the Y'Toub system.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Criminal
- **Name:** Arjee-Entoo
- **Death:** Between 40 BBY and 34 BBY, Y'Toub system
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Plating:** Gold
