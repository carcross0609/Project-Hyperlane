# Droideka Gamma

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droideka Gamma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droideka_Gamma)

Droideka Gamma was a reprogrammed droideka and part of IG-88's Droid Army during the Galactic Civil War. It was part of a radical group of droids that planned to start a Droid Revolution before they were destroyed in 4 ABY.
