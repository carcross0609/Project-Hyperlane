# Jaum's wheeled battle droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jaum's wheeled battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaum%27s_wheeled_battle_droid)

The Andelm IV gangster Jaum owned a wheeled battle droid that was used to quell trouble among his slave miners in the Andelm beetle caverns. It was a wheeled red battle droid with two large wheels around a central chassis. The chassis had two sets of laser cannons on each arm. During a mission to help the local girl Zarro, her father Arrax, and the other miners, the Wookiee Chewbacca fought this droid and disabled it with an improvised rod.
