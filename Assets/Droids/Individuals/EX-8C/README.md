# EX-8C

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EX-8C](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EX-8C)

EX-8C, or Atesee, was an EX droid assigned to Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two, a team of Republic Pathfinders active in 382 BBY. The team was betrayed during an expedition to the planet Gloam, with its leader, Jedi Master Rok Buran, the sole survivor. Buran was able to retrieve EX-8C from the ruins of the Foundling, the expedition's starship, and sent the damaged droid away with a message asking for help. On its journey, EX-8C was further damaged by an asteroid storm before being found by Amos Tillian and Kam, Galactic Republic communications engineers who worked with Master Silandra Sho's Pathfinder team.
