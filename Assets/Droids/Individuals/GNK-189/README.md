# GNK-189

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [GNK-189](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GNK-189)

GNK-189 was a GNK-series power droid who served the Resistance. The Droid was part of C-3PO's party whilst stranded on Taul, before being attacked by a spice spider and separated from the group.
