# Unidentified waitress droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified waitress droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_waitress_droid)

A waitress droid served at the Belsallian Cantina on the planet Serenno. When the Sith apprentice Asajj Ventress entered the cantina to search for the Crolute criminal Glute, she used the waitress droid's tray to knock a Khormai patron that had been harassing her unconscious. The waitress droid offered to deal with the unconscious Khormai, but Ventress insisted that he be left where he was. The droid eventually returned to Ventress, informing her that several members of the Razorclaw Gang, who Glute was formerly affiliated with, were present in the cantina.

## Specifications

- **Class:** Service droid

- **Affiliation:** Belsallian Cantina
