# Unidentified B1 battle droid (Abafar)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid (Abafar)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_%28Abafar%29)

This B1-series battle droid was stationed at the Rhydonium mining installation on the planet Abafar. Between 20 BBY and 19 BBY, the droid was part of a task force at the mining installation; the force's mission was to acquire rhydonium and load it onto the hijacked Venator-class Star Destroyer Renown in Abafar's orbit so they could destroy the Republic strategy conference in the Carida system. When D-Squad tried getting to the Renown by using the shuttle that was being used to load the rhydonium onto the Star Destroyer, this B1-series battle droid stopped them and demanded that they show their authorization when they got to the mining installation. Immediately afterwards, this droid and his fellow droid were both shot and destroyed by the clone commando Gregor, who was helping D-Squad with their escape.
