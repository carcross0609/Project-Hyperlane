# Unidentified B1 battle droid 2 (Rugosa)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 2 (Rugosa)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_2_%28Rugosa%29/Legends)

A B1 battle droid was part of Sith apprentice Asajj Ventress' droid forces stationed on the planet of Rugosa around 22 BBY and participated in the Battle of Rugosa under command of battle droid commander 224 during a deal between Count Dooku of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and King Katuunko of the Toydarians. When Jedi Master Yoda intervened and began destroying Ventress' droids, battle droid commander 224 and several of their droids were sent out to defeat them, with the winner of the ensuing battle gaining Katuunko's trust. During the battle, the battle droid was destroyed by Yoda, along with its companions.
