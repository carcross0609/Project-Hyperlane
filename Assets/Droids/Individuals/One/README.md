# One

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/One)

The droid designated one was a K droid constructed by the droid J-6 on Aricho. Along with the other K droids, one was built by J-6 to aid the resistance force of Aricho against the Nihil marauders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** One
- **Homeworld:** Aricho
- **Creator:** J-6
- **Model:** K-series droid
- **Affiliation:** Aricho's resistance force
