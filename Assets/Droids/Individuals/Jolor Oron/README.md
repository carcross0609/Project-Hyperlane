# Jolor Oron

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jolor Oron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jolor_Oron)

Jolor Oron was a replica droid that had the appearance of a tall, slender long-lived near-Human species. Oron was modified by a technician whose family had been killed by Separatist droids during the Clone Wars, turning Oron into a weapon, to destroy all of the droids in the galaxy. Oron eventually became the leader of the Droid Equality Foundation, a large and powerful droid rights group, and he planned to use the organization to turn the opinion of the galactic population against droids.
