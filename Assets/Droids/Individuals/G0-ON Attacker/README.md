# G0-ON Attacker

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G0-ON Attacker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G0-ON_Attacker)

G0-ON Attacker was a battle droid model used by Nocturno and Drake Raven, a pair of gladiators in the Arena Grand of Zakuul. Along with G0-ON Abductors, these droids assisted them during the fights. In 3630 BBY Nocturno and Drake were put up against a newcomer known as "the Mysterious Stranger," who defeated them along with their G0-ON Attackers in the seventh round of the Arena fights.
