# EV-2-E

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EV-2-E](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EV-2-E)

EV-2-E was an EV-series supervisor droid manufactured by MerenData. Sometime between the years 2 BBY and 4 ABY, it served in the Alliance Fleet's 14th Roving Line as a droid overseer onboard the line's flagship, the Mantan Wanderer, during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Model:** EV-series supervisor droid
- **Class:** Supervisor droid
