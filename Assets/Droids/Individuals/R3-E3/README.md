# R3-E3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R3-E3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-E3)

R3-E3 was an astromech droid who served with Captain Porter in an E-wing starfighter. At some point, they engaged in battle with Shin Hati and her starfighter.

- **Type:** New Republic

- **Class:** Astromech droid
