# G9-RX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G9-RX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G9-RX)

G9-RX was a Battle droid produced at some point before the war against Zakuul. By 3630 BBY it was used by Dust Vipers gang based on Tatooine. When the Eternal Alliance forces attacked the Dust Viper Den to reclaim the stolen plans for the Alliance base, Dust Viper Bel Nerodia confronted them alongside G9-RX, but after intense fight Nerodia was killed and G9-RX destroyed.
