# MK221

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MK221](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MK221)

MK221, also known as Emmy-Kaye, was an attendant and nanny droid who served five generations of the N'Vaari family. MK221 was owned by Brinaloy N'Vaari during the New Order.
