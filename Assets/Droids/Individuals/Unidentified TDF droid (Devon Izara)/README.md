# Unidentified TDF droid (Devon Izara)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified TDF droid (Devon Izara)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_TDF_droid_%28Devon_Izara%29)

A police droid served in the Tactical Defense Force within Janix Civil Defense on the planet Janix. Between 17 BBY and around 13 BBY, while patrolling Janix city, the droid physically bumped into the Twi'lek Devon Izara causing her to drop several melons she had just stolen from a Unidentified fruit vendor. Noticing the dropped melons, the vendor alerted the police droid to the situation who placed the Twi'lek under arrest and transported her to Janix Civil Defense HQ. Once their the droid handed Izara over to another police droid.
