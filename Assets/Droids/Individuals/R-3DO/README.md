# R-3DO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R-3DO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-3DO)

R-3DO was a protocol droid working at the Resistance base on D'Qar. The red-colored droid monitored the Resistance's network of spy droids. R-3DO was present at an assembly of Resistance members watching the Millennium Falcon take off with Rey at the controls, leaving to find Luke Skywalker.
