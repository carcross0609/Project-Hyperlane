# Sludgegulper

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sludgegulper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sludgegulper)

Sludgegulpers were flying droids that came in a variety of designs. The droids were popular with pirates due to their sinister-looking appearance and ability to commandeer starships by attaching themselves to the outside and eating through the hull. The Great Heep used a a number of sludgegulper designs during the capture of the Baobab Merchant Fleet ship Argo Moon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sludgegulper
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Armament:** Knockout gas
- **Affiliation:** The Great Heep
