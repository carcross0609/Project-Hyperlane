# 3V-2L

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [3V-2L](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3V-2L)

3V-2L was a dark side-empowered battle droid created by Lord Umbral from the chassis of an astromech droid as part of his Master's Top Secret Army during the third Galactic War. The droid was defeated by the Alliance Commander at some point on Tatooine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 3V-2L
- **Death:** c. 3625 BBY, Tatooine
- **Creator:** Umbral
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Yellow
- **Affiliation:** Master's Top Secret Army
