# Captivator (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Captivator (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Captivator_%28droid%29)

Captivator was a bounty hunter assassin droid tasked with tracking down Han Solo and Chewbacca. Captivator was hired by Jabba the Hutt to hunt down Solo. He worked with a human, a Gran, and a Kubaz bounty hunter. Later he joined with Alecia Beck and the Imperials to find Solo.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Bounty hunter
- **Name:** Captivator
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Height:** 2 meters
