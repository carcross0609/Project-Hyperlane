# MD-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-4)

MD-4 was an MD-4 medical droid assigned to the Imperial Navy EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate. Its service carried over once the ship's crew mutined and took over control of the vessel. MD-4 oversaw the ship's surgery unit with assistance from MD-2.
