# T-5RZ Onslaught Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-5RZ Onslaught Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-5RZ_Onslaught_Droid)

The T-5RZ Onslaught Droid was a battle droid that was utilized by the Coronas, a criminal gang that worked for the corrupt Corellian politician Harmon Torvix. During the Galactic War, the T-5RZ Onslaught Droid was destroyed in the Black Hole region of Corellia by Imperial forces that salvaged the droid's memory core.
