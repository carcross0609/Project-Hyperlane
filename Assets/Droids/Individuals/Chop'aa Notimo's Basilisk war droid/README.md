# Chop'aa Notimo's Basilisk war droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chop'aa Notimo's Basilisk war droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chop%27aa_Notimo%27s_Basilisk_war_droid)

This Basilisk war droid was owned by the Mandalorian crime lord Chop'aa Notimo during the time of the Great Jedi Purge. During the rescue of Garm Bel Iblis, it was destroyed by Starkiller.
