# Gen Tiraan's R2-series astromech droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gen Tiraan's R2-series astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gen_Tiraan%27s_R2-series_astromech_droid)

An R2-series astromech droid was owned by Gen Tiraan, an engineer who helped design the Incom Corporation's X-wing starfighter. Tiraan had the droid with him in the Corellian system around 7 ABY, where he was of interest to both the New Republic and the Galactic Empire for the development of a newer and more efficient sublight engine.
