# 49-J12

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [49-J12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/49-J12)

49-J12, or Fournine-Jaytwelve, was a droid that worked for the Hutts at the H'unn Cabaret Pit on Nar Kaaga. The droid attempted to serve the bounty hunter Beilert Valance when he entered the establishment to meet with the bail bondsman Syphacc, but Valance refused Fournine-Jaytwelve's offer of drinks. After Valance was attacked by the assassin Tasu Leech, the bounty hunter threw Fournine-Jaytwelve in the way of Leech's knives despite the droid's protests.
