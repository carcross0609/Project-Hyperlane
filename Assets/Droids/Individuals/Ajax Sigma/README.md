# Ajax Sigma

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ajax Sigma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ajax_Sigma)

Ajax Sigma was a warrior-priest droid who lived in the High Republic Era and was created on Kligson's Moon, he sought the freedom of droids and became an insurgent out of anger until he was struck down by the Jedi Order. As Sigma's neural core survived, it was eventually recovered and eventually buried by the smugglers Han Solo and Chewbacca during the Imperial Era. Sigma's core was discovered and he was rebuilt by the Second Revelation on the world Mechis III, where he became head of the group after reforming it into a church.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ajax Sigma
- **Homeworld:** Kligson's Moon
- **Death:** During the High Republic Era, Kligson's Moon (later rebuilt)
- **Class:** Warrior-priest droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** White, later red
- **Plating:** White and gold, later blue, gold, and silver
- **Affiliation:**
  - Ajax Sigma's army
  - Second Revelation
