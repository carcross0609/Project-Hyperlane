# R6-W1CH

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R6-W1CH](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R6-W1CH)

R6-W1CH was a R6 astromech droid that had a red, black, and white body, with a black sensor. She also wore a tall, pointed black hat. The droid was known to aid a coven of witches in their wielding of dark magic.
