# R23-X9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R23-X9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R23-X9)

R23-X9 was an astromech droid owned by Torra Doza of Ace Squadron. Though it resembled an R-series astromech, it was a more specialized version custom-built for Doza, and its color scheme was made to match Doza's state-of-the-art racer, the Blue Ace.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R23-X9
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton (Modified)
- **Model:** Custom-built
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Blue
- **Affiliation:**
  - Ace Squadron
  - Team Colossus
