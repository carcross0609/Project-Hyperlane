# Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Kiros)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Kiros)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_T-series_tactical_droid_%28Kiros%29)

This T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid was stationed on Kiros under Darts D'Nar's command. He was destroyed after D'Nar activated a bomb on his back in an attempt to kill Obi-Wan Kenobi, who used the Force to push the droid out of a window, where he exploded in midair. D'Nar used the diversion to escape from the tower this occurred in via the turbolift.
