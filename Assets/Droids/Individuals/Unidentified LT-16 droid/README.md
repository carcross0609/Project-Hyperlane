# Unidentified LT-16 droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified LT-16 droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_LT-16_droid)

An LT-16 droid served on the medical tower aboard Starlight Beacon during the High Republic Era. During the destruction of Starlight Beacon, the droid was the first to make contact with the outside world and asked for help with a medical ship from the planet Eiram.
