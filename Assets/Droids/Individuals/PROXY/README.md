# PROXY

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PROXY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PROXY)

PROXY was a prototype holodroid who served as a companion and long time friend of Galen Marek and traveled with him on the Rogue Shadow. Despite their friendship, PROXY would routinely try to kill Marek during training, as this was his primary directive provided by Darth Vader.
