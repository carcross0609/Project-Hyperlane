# 6L-CH

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [6L-CH](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/6L-CH)

6L-CH was an overstressed green-plated BB-series astromech droid in the service of the Resistance. It was stationed on the Raddus above the planet D'Qar shortly after the Battle of Starkiller Base, where it was repaired alongside other BB units.
