# L0-LA59

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L0-LA59](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L0-LA59)

L0-LA59, nicknamed "Lola," was a feminine L0 toy droid owned by the House of Organa and was in the personal possession of the young Princess Leia of Alderaan during the Imperial Era. She was built with white and red plates on the top half of her, and silver plates on the bottom. She also had small pairs of wings, legs, photoreceptors, and an antenna near her back. Finally on her inside was a compartment to store miniature tools.
