# Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Jedidiah)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Jedidiah)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_2-1B_surgical_droid_%28Jedidiah%29)

When the Velmoc named Jedidiah was discovered by members of the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War, a 2-1B medical droid examined the Velmoc to assess his incoherent mental state. The medical droid determined that Jedidiah's brain was too severely damaged to be replaced or repaired.
