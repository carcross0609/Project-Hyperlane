# Mick

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mick/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mick/Legends)

"Mick" was one of the thousands of small, flying INS-444 window installation droids on Coruscant. It worked together with CLE-004, taking instructions from a central computer. Together, the two droids replaced the bedroom window of Senator Amidala's Coruscant apartment after the original window was destroyed through the actions of ASN-121 and Obi-Wan Kenobi.
