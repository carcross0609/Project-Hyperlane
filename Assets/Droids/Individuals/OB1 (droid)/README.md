# OB1 (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [OB1 (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OB1_%28droid%29)

OB1, or "Obiwan" was a protocol droid owned by the old Sullustan, Vendor. He was present when his master was killed by the mercenary Hydra under the guise of a consul, having recorded the ordeal despite being decapitated during the process.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OB1
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
