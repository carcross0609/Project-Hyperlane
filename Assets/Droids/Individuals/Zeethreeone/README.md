# Zeethreeone

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zeethreeone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeethreeone)

Zeethreeone was an Imperial probe droid that, under the control of Major Stipling, was assigned the task of finding a source of the metal alloy doonium on Yen-2. The droid was destroyed by Limna Yith, during Black Sun's attempt to overrun the Yen-2 mining operation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Zeethreeone
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Equipment:** Long range sensor
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
