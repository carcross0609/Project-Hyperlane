# DNM-908

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DNM-908](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DNM-908)

DNM-908 was a B1-series battle droid in the service of the Trade Federation. The battle droid served as a commander in the Trade Federation Droid Army. Like other B1-Series battle droids, DNM-908 had masculine programming, was 1.91 meters tall, and wielded an E-5 blaster rifle. The droid had light tan plating with brown markings and was equipped with a backpack that had an antenna attached.
