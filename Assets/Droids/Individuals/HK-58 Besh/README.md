# HK-58 Besh

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HK-58 Besh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HK-58_Besh)

HK-58 Besh was a HK-series assassin droid used by HK-47 in his attempts to take over the planet Mustafar. It was destroyed by a group of spacers who went on to defeat HK-47.
