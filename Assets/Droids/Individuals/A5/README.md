# A5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A5)

A5 was an A5-series Assassin Droid operational during the Galactic Civil War. After being sufficiently reprogrammed, A5 served as the bouncer at the Farrimmer Cafe.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A5
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Height:** 1.6 meters
- **Armament:** Internal blaster rifle
- **Equipment:**
  - Humanoid torso
  - Repulsorlift propulsion unit
  - Two audio sensors
  - Two photoreceptors
  - Broadband antenna
  - Vocabulator
- **Affiliation:** Farrimmer Cafe
