# F-29-PSKx

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [F-29-PSKx](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F-29-PSKx)

F-29-PSKx, also known simply as F-29, was a masculine F series repair droid with blue photoreceptors and silver plating, with a blue trim. By the year 4.3 ABY, he worked for the Star Tours travel agency. While visiting its Tomorrowland Starport, F-29 felt homesick and often called his girlfriend using a Star*Link booth.
