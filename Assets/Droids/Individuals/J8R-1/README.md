# J8R-1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [J8R-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J8R-1)

J8R-1, nicknamed Jayterr, was an astromech droid active during the Cold War. J8R-1 served the Republic as a courier on, Coruscant, Taris and later Carrick Station, delivering messages and mission briefings from the Emergency Response Corps to Republic personnel aboard. During the Cold War, he transmitted a message from Grand Master Satele Shan to a group of heroes, asking them to come to Carrick Station for a mission. He was excited about them working for Master Satele and hoped to hear their stories when they would come back.
