# HK-57

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HK-57](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HK-57)

HK-57 was a HK-series assassin droid produced in an old Separatist droid factory on Mustafar under the control of rogue assassin droid HK-47. After flaws in the HK-57 and subsequent HK-67 models were discovered, HK-47 initiated the creation of a new model, the HK-77 droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HK-57
- **Homeworld:** Mustafar
- **Line:** HK-series assassin droids
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** HK-47
