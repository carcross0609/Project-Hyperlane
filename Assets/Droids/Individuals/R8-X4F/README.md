# R8-X4F

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R8-X4F](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R8-X4F)

R8-X4F was a battle droid in service of the Galactic Republic on Ossus at the start of the new conflict with the Sith Empire. During the Invasion of Ossus, it went rogue and was brought down by Imperial or Republic forces.
