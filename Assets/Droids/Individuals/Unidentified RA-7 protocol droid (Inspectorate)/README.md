# Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Inspectorate)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Inspectorate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RA-7_protocol_droid_%28Inspectorate%29)

An RA-7 protocol droid served the Galactic Empire's Inspectorate at the Imperial Inspectorate HQ on Coruscant, controlling access to the organization's databanks. After being promoted to Lieutenant-Inspector in the Inspectorate, Magna Tolvan visited the Inspectorate headquarters, where she requested that the RA-7 protocol droid display files on the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Aphra. The protocol droid informed her that the file's contents had been deleted by authorization of Directive 081-Omega.
