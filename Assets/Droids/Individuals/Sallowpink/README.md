# Sallowpink

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sallowpink](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sallowpink)

Sallowpink was a B2-series super battle droid manufactured by Baktoid Combat Automata that was enhanced with a variety of additional gray armor plating across its body and spikes along its arms. Between 10 BBY and 3 ABY, the droid was planned to participate as a gladiator droid in a Pit Fight Night at Grakkus Arena on the moon Nar Shaddaa.
