# MSE-6-G735Y

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MSE-6-G735Y](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MSE-6-G735Y)

MSE-6-G735Y, or G7 for short, was a MSE-6 series repair droid stationed on the Death Star in 1 BBY, assigned to the Maintenance Unit in Sector AA-345. It was maintained by the stormtrooper designated TK-421, who considered it the fastest mouse droid in the Imperial fleet. TK-421 wanted to be transferred to Coruscant and promised to take G7 with him to enter it into the racing circuit.
