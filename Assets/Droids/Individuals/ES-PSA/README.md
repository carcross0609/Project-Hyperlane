# ES-PSA

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ES-PSA](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ES-PSA)

ES-PSA, also known as Espasa, was a RIC-920 rickshaw droid manufactured by Serv-O-Droid, Inc.. He operated as a rickshaw driver in the spaceport of Mos Espa on Tatooine, during the time of the Separatist Crisis and the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ES-PSA
- **Manufacturer:** Serv-O-Droid, Inc.
- **Line:** RIC-series general labor droid
- **Model:** RIC-920 rickshaw droid
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Height:** 1.1 meters
