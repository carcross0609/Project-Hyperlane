# J2-KC

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J2-KC](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J2-KC)

J2-KC was a red and white R5 series astromech droid that belonged to the Lurmen Jat Kaa, owner of Jat Kaa's Coolers in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu. The droid was reprogrammed to drive a small cart around the outpost from which various beverages were delivered and sold to visitors.
