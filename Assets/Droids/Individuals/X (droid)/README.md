# X (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [X (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X_%28droid%29)

X was a battle droid active during the Galactic Civil War. He was found in his manufacturer Kyrotech Corporate Combine's warehouse by a spaceport worker. Via the black market, the latter sold the droid to the pirate leader Zhen Mirat. During a confrontation Mirat's gang had with a group of Rebel Alliance agents, the latter discovered that X had a programmed-in weakness.
