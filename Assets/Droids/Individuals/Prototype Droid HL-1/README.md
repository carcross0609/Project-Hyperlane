# Prototype Droid HL-1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prototype Droid HL-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prototype_Droid_HL-1)

Prototype Droid HL-1 was a battle droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Conquest of Makeb. The droid, along with Prototype Droid TK-7 was guarding the schematics of Isotope-5 droids employed by the Hutt Cartel and was destroyed by an individual seeking to acquire those schematics for the Galactic Solutions Industries
