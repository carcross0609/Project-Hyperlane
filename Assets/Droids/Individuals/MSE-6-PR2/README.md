# MSE-6-PR2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MSE-6-PR2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MSE-6-PR2)

MSE-6-PR2 was an MSE-6 series repair droid that had somehow wound up on Hoth with a crate of similar droids during the Galactic War. It requested help from an individual in recovering data from the wreck of the Sendant Pride.
