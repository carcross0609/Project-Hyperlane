# 3B3-21

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [3B3-21](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3B3-21)

3B3-21 was a B1-series battle droid that served in the Droid Army of the Trade Federation. In 32 BBY, they were part of the force that invaded the planet Naboo. 3B3-21 was deactivated along with their fellow droids when the Droid Control Ship orbiting the planet was destroyed during the Battle of Naboo.
