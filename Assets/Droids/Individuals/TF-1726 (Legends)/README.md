# TF-1726

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TF-1726/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TF-1726/Legends)

TF-1726 was a masculine-programmed T-series tactical droid who served in the Confederate Navy during the Clone Wars. In 22 BBY, the droid commanded a fleet of Munificent-class star frigates during the Sky Battle of Quell, in which he ambushed a flotilla of Venator-class Star Destroyers led by Jedi General Aayla Secura. During the course of the battle over Quell, TF-1726 lost one of his own frigates, but his remaining forces were able to destroy two of Secura's Star Destroyers, leaving only Secura's own command ship, the Liberty, maintaining altitude. The tactical droid then sent hundreds of B2-RP battle droids to invade the damaged destroyer.
