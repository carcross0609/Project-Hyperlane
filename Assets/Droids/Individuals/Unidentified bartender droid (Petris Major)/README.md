# Unidentified bartender droid (Petris Major)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified bartender droid (Petris Major)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_bartender_droid_%28Petris_Major%29)

A droid worked as a bartender in Rebunda City on the planet Petris Major. By around 482 BBY, they heard tales about the Jedi Wayfinder Tar'ak Borran being seen with the local Oolorian community in the nearby Creekback Hills. When Jedi Master Barnabas Vim came to Petris Major to search for Borran, the bartender informed him of Borran's rumored whereabouts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified bartender droid
- **Class:** Bartender droid
- **Plating:** Gray and gold
