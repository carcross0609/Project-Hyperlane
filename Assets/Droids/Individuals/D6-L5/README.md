# D6-L5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D6-L5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D6-L5)

D6-L5, sometimes known as Deesix, was a protocol droid which served Mon Mothma during the Galactic Civil War. It traveled with her to Agamar on the Mon Calamari Star Cruiser Independence when she went to address the Agamar Resistance, hoping to convince them to join the Alliance to Restore the Republic.
