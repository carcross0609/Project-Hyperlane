# Unidentified LEP-series service droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified LEP-series service droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_LEP-series_service_droid)

An LEP-series service droid was employed as a ratcatcher in the palace of crime lord Boba Fett. Originally serving under Bib Fortuna, the LEP droid encountered Fett when the latter infiltrated the castle around 9 ABY. The droid deactivated itself in fear of the former bounty hunter after a chase in the palace kitchen. Later, when Fett returned and killed Fortuna, the LEP droid continued its service under him.
