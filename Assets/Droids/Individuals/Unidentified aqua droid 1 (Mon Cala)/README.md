# Unidentified aqua droid 1 (Mon Cala)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified aqua droid 1 (Mon Cala)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_aqua_droid_1_%28Mon_Cala%29)

This Aqua droid participated in the Battle of Mon Cala around 21 BBY under the command of Separatist commander Riff Tamson. After Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker destroyed the building that housed the radar and sonar in the Mon Calamari capital city, this droid informed Tamson that the Separatists had lost all radar and sonar scanning and they could not reestablish connection since the building was destroyed.
