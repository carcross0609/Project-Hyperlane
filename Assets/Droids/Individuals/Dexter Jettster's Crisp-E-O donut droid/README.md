# Dexter Jettster's Crisp-E-O donut droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dexter Jettster's Crisp-E-O donut droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dexter_Jettster%27s_Crisp-E-O_donut_droid)

The chef Dexter Jettster owned a Crisp-E-O donut droid on the planet Coruscant. It worked for him first at Dex's Donuts and later at Dex's Diner. Its internal database included over 2,400 donut recipes.
