# R2-C4 (Bail Organa)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-C4 (Bail Organa)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-C4_%28Bail_Organa%29)

R2-C4 was an astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. During the Imperial Era it served Alderaan Senator Bail Organa, providing cover for him while in a secret meeting with fellow Senator Riyo Chuchi of Pantora. R2-C4 later notified Chuchi that Organa wished to see her, and it took her to meet him and their associates, Clone Force 99, who had arrived on Coruscant to assist him in obtaining proof of Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart's wrongdoings.
