# Unidentified taxi droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified taxi droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_taxi_droid)

Around 9 ABY, a taxi droid piloted Doctor Penn Pershing in an air taxi from the Galaxies Opera House to Amnesty Housing on the planet Coruscant. During the journey, the taxi droid congratulated Pershing on making it into the New Republic Amnesty Program and, upon learning that Pershing had only recently arrived on Coruscant, recommended a series of Coruscant locations that the doctor should visit.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified taxi droid
- **Class:** Taxi droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Gray and bronze
