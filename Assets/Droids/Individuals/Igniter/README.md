# Igniter

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Igniter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Igniter)

Igniter was an ancient, masculine-programmed mining droid who served the Hutts on Mataou. He was modified with an experimental Imperial combat design, causing him to be an effective fighter with slight insanity. During the Iron Blockade of the Anoat sector, Igniter joined the crew of a young smuggler.

- **Class:** Mining droid
