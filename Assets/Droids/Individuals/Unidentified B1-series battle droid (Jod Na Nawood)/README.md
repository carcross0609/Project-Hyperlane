# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Jod Na Nawood)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Jod Na Nawood)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Jod_Na_Nawood%29)

An B1-series battle droid of the Separatist Droid Army was left deactivated since the Clone Wars in a droid repair shop on the asteroid Port Borgo around 9 ABY. While looking for the droid SM-33, the pirate Jod Na Nawood activated the B1-series battle droid to check if it was SM-33. Upon being activated, the droid asked if they had won before their head fell off and they deactivated.
