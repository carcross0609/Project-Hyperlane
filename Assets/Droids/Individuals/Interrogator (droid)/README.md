# Interrogator (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Interrogator (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Interrogator_%28droid%29)

Interrogator was an interrogation droid that joined the Directive 7 movement headed by Mentor during the era of the Galactic War. Adopting a new name of its own choosing, Interrogator was among the droids that gathered on the moon Zadd under Mentor's leadership, and was one of the few droids who were not absorbed into Mentor's consciousness. Interrogator was involved in Mentor's experiments with converting organics into cyborgs, and Interrogator was destroyed while trying to protect Mentor's core from invaders.
