# Gadget

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gadget](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gadget)

Gadget was the astromech droid assigned to Ton Phanan during his service with Wraith Squadron. Gadget remained with Phanan until his X-Wing was destroyed, leaving both pilot and droid without a vehicle. The droid was later transferred to Myn Donos when his own astromech, Shiner, was destroyed. The R2 unit's biggest mission was when he had to help Piggy infiltrate the modified CR90 corvette Night Caller onboard the Lunatic; the plan worked and the vessel was seized.
