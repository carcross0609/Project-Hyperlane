# I7-S7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I7-S7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I7-S7)

I7-S7 was an Astromech droid of the Sith Empire active in the aftermath of the war against Zakuul. In 3630 BBY it was brought to Iokath to provide assistance during the the War on Iokath.

- **Class:** Astromech droid
