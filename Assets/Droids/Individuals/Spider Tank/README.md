# Spider Tank

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spider Tank](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_Tank)

The Spider Tank was a large, manually-controlled model of droid utilized by a cyborg that resided on the planet Mandalore. In 9 ABY, the cyborg utilized its Spider Tank to capture and sedate the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin. It was later destroyed by Bo-Katan Kryze when she arrived on Mandalore to save Djarin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spider Tank
- **Death:** 9 ABY, Mandalore
- **Equipment:**
  - An underside cage
  - Sedation equipment
