# TD-54

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [TD-54](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TD-54)

TD-54, often referred to as Tee-Dee, was a 3PO-series protocol droid active during the Clone Wars. Once owned by crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure, the Hutt sold the droid and the Geonosian droid factory command codes he contained to the Shadow Collective. Seeking control of his own battle droid army, Darth Maul dispatched the 3PO unit to the world of Geonosis. There, the operation was discovered by the Confederacy of Independent Systems, and General Grievous attempted to force him to explain who activated the foundry.
