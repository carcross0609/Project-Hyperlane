# Unidentified B1 battle droid 5 (Rugosa)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 5 (Rugosa)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_5_%28Rugosa%29)

A B1 battle droid served in the Separatist Droid Army during the Clone Wars. In 22 BBY, it was among the forces deployed to the moon Rugosa to try and prevent Jedi Grand Master Yoda from negotiating with Toydarian King Katuunko for the planet Toydaria to join the Galactic Republic. After the OOM-series battle droid 224 was destroyed by Yoda, the battle droid reported 224's demise to Asajj Ventress, commander of the Separatist forces on the moon, via hologram. The droid was then also destroyed by the Jedi.
