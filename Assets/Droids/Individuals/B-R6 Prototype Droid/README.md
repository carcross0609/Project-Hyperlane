# B-R6 Prototype Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B-R6 Prototype Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-R6_Prototype_Droid)

The B-R6 Prototype Droid was a tripedal prototype battle droid designed in the Research and Development sector of the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. It was armed with two powerful blaster cannons on its arms. During the Cold War, it was destroyed by Imperial forces that invaded the factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B-R6 Prototype Droid
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Death:** 3643 BBY, Balmorra
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Class:** Battle droid

- **Plating:** Gray
- **Armament:** 2 blaster cannons
