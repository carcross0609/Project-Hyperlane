# Fiver

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fiver](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fiver)

Fiver was Anakin Solo's modified R7 astromech droid a year into the war with the Yuuzhan Vong. When the Peace Brigade attacked the Jedi praxeum at Yavin 4 a little over a year into the invasion, Fiver flew Anakin's X-Wing to distract the Brigade.
