# 327-T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [327-T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/327-T)

327-T, also known by the nickname 327, was a spunky, simpleminded WED-15 Treadwell repair droid who served aboard the Republic Venator-class Star Destroyer Tranquility during the Clone Wars, maintaining many of the ship's systems on board. He survived a series of explosions detonated by Asajj Ventress in the ship's reactor room, but was trapped beneath rubble until recovered by the Jedi aboard, Ahsoka Tano and Luminara Unduli.
