# JBLX-24

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JBLX-24](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JBLX-24)

JBLX-24 was a blue- and white-plated humanoid protocol droid that was repurposed as a labor droid to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, JBLX-24 was carrying a case of kesselstone on its back through the mines when it was freed from slavery after its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.
