# C03

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [C03](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C03)

C03 was a small droid living in the residence of Professor Mitaka, one of several droids in his possession. C03 often tagged along with T0-B1, such as in his quest for kyber crystals. Along with T0-B1, C03 was hidden and thus spared from the destruction that ensued when the Professor's location was leaked to the Galactic Empire. Both surviving droids repaired the others and carried on Mitaka's dream of generating life on the planet they lived in.
