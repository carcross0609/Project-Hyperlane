# Shaker

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shaker)

Shaker was an R2-series Drewwa spaceport maintenance astromech droid that accompanied Ben Skywalker on his mission to find the Amulet of Kalara on Ziost. During the search, Shaker became a kind of conscience for Ben, wondering why he would leave a little girl stranded on a dangerous planet.
