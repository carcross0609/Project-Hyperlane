# R2-A6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-A6/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-A6/Legends)

R2-A6 was an astromech droid under the service of the Royal Naboo Security Forces in 32 BBY. It was on board Bravo 1 during the Battle of Naboo. Ric Olié continued to use it because of his superstition that R2-A6 was a good-luck charm.
