# Unidentified B1 battle droid 3 (Skytop Station)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 3 (Skytop Station)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_3_%28Skytop_Station%29)

This B1 battle droid was stationed aboard Skytop Station during the Clone Wars in 22 BBY. He complained to a fellow droid that the moisture of Ruusan 2, which Skytop was in orbit of, was corroding his servo-moters. The other droid suggested to this one that he go to level eight to get his head adjusted. As their conversation ended, they were attacked by clone troopers who were part of a Jedi-led strike team sent to destroy Skytop Station. Ironically, this droid got a head adjustment of his own when his head was ripped out by one of the clones. The other droid was destroyed as well.
