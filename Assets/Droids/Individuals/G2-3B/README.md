# G2-3B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G2-3B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G2-3B)

G2-3B was a spindly G2 repair droid who supervised the work being done by G2-9T and G2-4T for Star Tours in the aftermath of the Galactic Civil War. He did not have a conventional head for his droid model.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G2-3B
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** G2 repair droid
- **Class:** Maintenance droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** Star Tours
