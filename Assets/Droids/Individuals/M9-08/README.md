# M9-08

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M9-08](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M9-08)

M9-08 was an astromech droid used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. Posted at the Vaiken Spacedock, M9-08 relayed to Imperial forces an urgent transmission from Darth Malgus regarding the reemergence of the Red Reaper, the flagship of Darth Ikoral.
