# SE-2-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SE-2-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SE-2-4)

SE-2-4, also known as Twofour, was an SE-2-4 servant droid in the service of Lord Vaskel Savill. Savill treated the droid poorly, and continuously insulted the droid. SE-2-4 was ashamed of being treated that way.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SE-2-4
- **Model:** SE-2-4 servant droid
- **Class:** Service droid
- **Affiliation:** Lord Vaskel Savill
