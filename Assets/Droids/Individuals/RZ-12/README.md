# RZ-12

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RZ-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RZ-12)

RZ-12, nicknamed Raz, was a bounty droid who worked for the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. Raz began their career as a bunker buster for a small insurgent cell on Ruusan, but eventually betrayed and collected bounties on several of their companions before being pulled into Sorc Tormo's employ. The droid was destroyed by Cal Kestis on the planet Koboh.
