# Z2-1B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z2-1B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z2-1B)

Z2-1B was a 2-1B surgical droid that went rogue and adopted an independent agenda to create the ultimate droid. Also known as The Doctor by its creations, the droid was once a regular medical droid. This was until a lethal plague wiped out the entire population of the planet on which it was working on.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z2-1B
- **Model:** 2-1B surgical droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Equipment:** Heuristic processor
