# R2-F1P

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-F1P](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-F1P)

R2-F1P was an R2-series astromech droid with white, silver, yellow, and orange plating and a black sensor. Like all R2 astromech droids, it stood 0.96 meters tall and was manufactured by Industrial Automaton. Its status indicator could be red or blue in color when on.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R2-F1P
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** R-series
- **Model:** R2-series astromech droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Height:** 0.96 meters (3ft 2in)
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** White, orange, yellow and silver
