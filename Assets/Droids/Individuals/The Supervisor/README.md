# The Supervisor

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [The Supervisor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Supervisor)

"The Supervisor" was a massive droid in charge of the government of the planet of At Attin. Located in the Supervisor's Tower, he controlled both the entire planet’s power supply and security systems along with its droids. He had an office where Fara served as an Undersecretary.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Bureaucrat
- **Name:** "The Supervisor"
- **Death:** c. 9 ABY, At Attin (deactivated)
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Red and blue
- **Plating:** Dark gray
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   At Attin's government
