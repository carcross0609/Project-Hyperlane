# CB-99

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CB-99](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CB-99)

CB-99 was a droid Jabba Desilijic Tiure used to keep his will. After his death shortly before the Battle of Endor, Zorba Desilijic Tiure, Jabba's father, reclaimed Jabba's will and everything he owned through the droid.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Hutt Cartel
- **Name:** CB-99
- **Pronouns:** He/him
