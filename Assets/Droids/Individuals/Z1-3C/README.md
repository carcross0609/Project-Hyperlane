# Z1-3C

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z1-3C](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z1-3C)

Z1-3C was an astromech droid serving the reconstituted Sith Empire at the time of the Galactic War. During the Conquest of Makeb, it was sent to the Drill Site Control Center as a courier by Darth Acina to help her enlist the services of the Imperial strike team regarding the Seeds of Rage.
