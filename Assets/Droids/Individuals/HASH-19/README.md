# HASH-19

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HASH-19](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HASH-19)

HASH-19 was an assassin droid who was operating in the years of the Galactic Civil War. Appearing similar to a surveillance spy-eye, HASH-19 was a gold-chrome metallic ball. However, when provoked, six extensible arms were revealed, each armed with vibroblades. Employed by Moff Bandor, he was sent to patrol the Game Chambers of Questal.

## Specifications

- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Armament:** Vibroblades (6)
- **Equipment:** 6 arms
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
