# 1138 (Trade Federation)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [1138 (Trade Federation)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1138_%28Trade_Federation%29/Legends)

1138 was a B1-series battle droid serving under OOM-9 in the Trade Federation Droid Army. The droid was a part of Viceroy Nute Gunray's occupation force, installed following the Federation's successful invasion of Naboo. When the planet's native Gungans attempted to strike against the Federation's forces, 1138 was a part of OOM-9's task force dispatched to meet them at the Great Grass Plains. The Gungans were routed, but before 1138 could prove instrumental in the capture of one of their leaders, Jar Jar Binks, the droid was deactivated, along with all other B1s on the field when the Federation's Droid Control Ship was destroyed.
