# NR-02

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [NR-02](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NR-02)

NR-02 was an advanced protocol droid owned by Grand Moff Rycus Kilran. In addition to his standard translation and protocol capabilities, he claimed "manslaughter" and "calumniation" among his functions. The droid acted as Kilran's representative aboard the Black Talon, an Imperial transport ship. Following Kilran's demise at the battle at the Maelstrom Prison, ownership of NR-02 was transferred to Darth Xarion, a Dark Council member within the reconstituted Sith Empire.
