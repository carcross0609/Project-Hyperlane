# 3C-FD

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [3C-FD](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3C-FD)

3C-FD was a 3C-series utility droid that operated during the Dark Wars. The droid was on the light freighter Ebon Hawk when it was coming back from the Unknown Regions carrying Meetra Surik—an ex–Jedi Knight also known as the Jedi Exile—returning to the Galactic Republic after her exile.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 3C-FD
- **Death:** 3951 BBY, Peragus Mining Facility
- **Manufacturer:** Duwani Mechanical Products
- **Model:** 3C-series utility droid
- **Class:** Utility droid
- **Height:** 0.96 meters
- **Sensor:** Blue, yellow, and red
- **Equipment:**
  - Auditory and sonar scanner
  - Radar eye
  - Multi-function arm
