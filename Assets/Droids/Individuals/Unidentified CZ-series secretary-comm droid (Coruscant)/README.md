# Unidentified CZ-series secretary/comm droid (Coruscant)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified CZ-series secretary/comm droid (Coruscant)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_CZ-series_secretary/comm_droid_%28Coruscant%29)

When Jaina Solo was a child, a malfunctioning CZ-series secretary/comm droid attempted to corrupt her to the dark side. Jaina had been playing with her father and siblings in a garden in the Imperial Palace. She found the droid's hand in a bush, and followed it after it scurried away. The hand led her to a trash chute that opened up to a room full of trashed droids. The CZ droid was happy to have finally lured a Force-user, and expressed his plans to train Jaina to be the new Emperor and help reinstate him. Chewbacca followed Jaina down the chute and exploded out, punching the head off the droid. The droid warned Chewbacca that his body was a switch to a self-destruct sequence. As the timer counted down, the other scrapped droids awoke and attempted to capture Chewbacca. The Wookiee barely managed to escape up the trash chute with Jaina in his arms before the chamber exploded.
