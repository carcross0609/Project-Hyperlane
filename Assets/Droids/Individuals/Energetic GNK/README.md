# Energetic GNK

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Energetic GNK](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Energetic_GNK)

"Energetic GNK" was a EG-6 power droid who participated in a gonk droid beauty pageant run by a Pageant Host on the planet Pasaana in 35 ABY during the Festival of the Ancestors. They were excitable, spirited and constantly jumped up and down. The GNK droid was also sometimes anxious: after the pageant ended, they confessed to an individual that the pressure of the pageant "almost fried [their] circuits".
