# D-7RZ

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D-7RZ](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-7RZ)

D-7RZ was a protocol droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War. In 3638 BBY D-7RZ was on the Carrick Station, where he relayed to an important figure a priority request from Supreme Chancellor Leontyne Saresh to report to Major Vom Dresid on the Gav Daragon.
