# BB-H20

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BB-H20](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BB-H20)

BB-H20 was a BB-series astromech droid that had a red and white body, with a black sensor. The droid aided the Resistance along with R2-H25 during their time on Crait.
