# Guardian of Dark History

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Guardian of Dark History](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian_of_Dark_History)

The Guardian of Dark History was a battle droid guardian of the Annals of Dark History located underneath the Old Paths on Voss. It was destroyed by an Imperial who had breached the archives.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Guardian of Dark History
- **Death:** 3641 BBY, Voss
- **Line:** Battle droid

- **Sensor:** Red
