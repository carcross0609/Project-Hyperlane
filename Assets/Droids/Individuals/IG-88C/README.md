# IG-88C

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IG-88C](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG-88C)

IG-88C was an IG-88 assassin droid who worked as a bounty hunter. IG-88C was one of five identical IG-series assassin droids manufactured by Holowan Laboratories. Upon their activation, the droids slaughtered their constructors and escaped their laboratories into the galaxy. IG-88C flew an Aggressor-class Assault Fighter and wielded a blaster.
