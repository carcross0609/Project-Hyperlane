# L-77

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L-77](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L-77)

L-77 (El-Seventy-Seven), nicknamed Sevens, was a combat droid who worked alongside the mercenary Ashton Vol during the High Republic Era. The pair were recruited by the Jedi Order and Republic Defense Coalition to assist in a mission to the planet Angoth to capture a Nameless creature from the Nihil marauders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** L-77
- **Class:** Combat droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Armament:**

  - Back-mounted blaster
- **Equipment:** Holoprojector
