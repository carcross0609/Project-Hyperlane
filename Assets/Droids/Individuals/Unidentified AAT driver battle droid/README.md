# Unidentified AAT driver battle droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified AAT driver battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_AAT_driver_battle_droid)

An AAT Drivrer Battle Droid served the Sith apprentice Asajj Ventress during the Clone Wars. The battle droid was under the command of 224 in the battle on Rugosa. They were destroyed alongside the rest of the droid forces deployed to the area by Jedi Grand Master Yoda.
