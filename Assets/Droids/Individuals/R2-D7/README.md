# R2-D7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-D7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-D7)

R2-D7 was a conical-headed R4 astromech droid who worked at the Star Tours travel agency's Tomorrowland Starport, tending to StarSpeeder 3000s and operating the Inventory Droid Assistant.
