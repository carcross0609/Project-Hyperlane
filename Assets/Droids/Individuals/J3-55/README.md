# J3-55

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J3-55](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J3-55)

J3-55 was a gray loadlifter droid that was forced to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, J3-55 was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.
