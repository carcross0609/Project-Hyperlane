# Seth Cambriel's WBY-102 FirstMate droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seth Cambriel's WBY-102 FirstMate droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seth_Cambriel%27s_WBY-102_FirstMate_droid)

During the Galactic Civil War, Rebel spy Seth Cambriel owned a WBY-102 FirstMate droid that he used to help crew his yacht, the Cambus Gale. He had disabled the ability to transmit a distress signal in the standard comlink built into the droid, and gave it regularly memory wipes so that it did not retain a personality. When a group of Rebel agents were sent to Cambriel's aid on Spira to smuggle vital supplies off-planet, Cambriel taught the agents to sail his vessel through the use of his droid.
