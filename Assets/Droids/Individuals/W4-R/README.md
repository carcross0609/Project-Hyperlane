# W4-R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [W4-R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/W4-R)

W4-R was a designation of a heavy Skytrooper Battle droid. It was used by followers of the disgraced Battalion Commander Tassar, who were constructing a new Star Fortress on Rakata Prime's surface. Following the death of Empress Vaylin in 3630 BBY they were discovered by the Eternal Alliance forces and a strike team was dispatched to stop Tassar and her efforts, destroying W4-R.

- **Model:** Skytrooper
