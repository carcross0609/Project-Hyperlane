# R6-D0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R6-D0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R6-D0)

R6-D0 was the designation of a astromech droid residing on the Vaiken Spacedock during the Cold War. It relayed to Imperial forces a transmission from Darth Malgus regarding the situation on Athiss.
