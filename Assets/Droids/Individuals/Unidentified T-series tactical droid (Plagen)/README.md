# Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Plagen)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Plagen)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_T-series_tactical_droid_%28Plagen%29)

A T-series tactical droid served the Confederacy of Independent Systems at the time of the Clone Wars, a galaxy-wide conflict between the Confederates and the Galactic Republic. Manufactured by Baktoid Combat Automata and standing 1.93 meters tall, the droid was under the command of Kerkoiden General Piar Nagelsa prior to the First Battle of Plagen. Shortly before the battle, the droid assassinated Nagelsa due to issues that most tactical droids had with serving under unfit organic leaders. The droid then assumed command over the Confederate forces as it threw away Nagelsa's battle plan and led the Confederate forces to win the battle.
