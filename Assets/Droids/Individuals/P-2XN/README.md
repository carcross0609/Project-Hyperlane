# P-2XN

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [P-2XN](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-2XN)

P-2XN was a droid that served the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War with the reconstituted Sith Empire. P-2XN was stationed as a courier aboard the Republic's Carrick Station, delivering messages and mission briefings to the Republic personnel aboard. In 3640 BBY, he was dispatched to Corellia during the Battle of Corellia in order to recruit Republic operatives to deal with the threat of the Directive 7.
