# IT-3PO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IT-3PO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IT-3PO)

IT-3PO, or Eyetee–Three, was a protocol droid who operated during the Clone Wars and later the Galactic Civil War. He belonged to Rel Harsol, captain of the Sa Nalaor, then to Ropok, Reom's father and owner of IsoTech. Arrogant and aloof, the droid had operated for nearly forty years without a memory wipe by the time of the Galactic Civil War.
