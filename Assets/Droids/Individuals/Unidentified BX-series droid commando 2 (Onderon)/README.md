# Unidentified BX-series droid commando 2 (Onderon)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando 2 (Onderon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_2_%28Onderon%29)

This BX-series droid commando was part of the reinforcements sent to the planet Onderon during the Onderonian Civil War to deal with the Steela Gerrera's rebels after the deposed King of Onderon, Ramsis Dendup was rescued from the capital city of Iziz and held at the rebel's hidden base. After assaulting the base with another BX-series droid commando from atop an HMP droid gunship, the droid questioned a surviving rebel soldier for Dendup's location. When she refused to reveal anything the droid commando executed her.
