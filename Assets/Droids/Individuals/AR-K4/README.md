# AR-K4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AR-K4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AR-K4)

AR-K4, also known as Arkfour, was a top-of-the-line Galactic Republic protocol droid. In 382 BBY, she was assigned to the Paxion, an Alif-class Longbeam that belonged to Republic Supreme Chancellor Orlen Mollo.
