# Fourdee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fourdee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fourdee)

Fourdee was a receptionist droid with a tinny, feminine voice who served as Alliance to Restore the Republic General Airen Cracken's secretary during the Alliance's war against the Galactic Empire. After the Rebel victory at the Battle of Yavin, Fourdee admitted the Rebel soldier recruits Carns Capra, Denis Rygelli, Ben Spax, and Trinna Orani to be briefed by Cracken. The general found the task taxing and grew angry with Fourdee when the droid reminded him of an upcoming meeting with another batch of recruits.
