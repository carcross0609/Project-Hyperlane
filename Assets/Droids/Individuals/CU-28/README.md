# CU-28

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CU-28](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CU-28)

CU-28, nicknamed Copper, was a was a protocol droid that served as the assistant to High Magistrate Greef Karga on the planet Nevarro during the early New Republic Era, helping Karga with daily tasks and administrative work and informing him of events in the city. CU-28 was considered fastidious. He had green photoreceptors and his plating had a brilliant copper finish, leading Karga and his staff to usually only call him Copper.
