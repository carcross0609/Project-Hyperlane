# 6-5C

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [6-5C](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/6-5C)

6-5C was a medical droid that served aboard the Imperial Star Destroyer Ravagor in 0.5 ABY. It treated Ranulf Trommer after he was injured in an attack over Aguarl 3.
