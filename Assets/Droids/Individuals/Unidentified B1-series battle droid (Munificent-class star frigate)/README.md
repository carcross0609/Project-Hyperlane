# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Munificent-class star frigate)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Munificent-class star frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Munificent-class_star_frigate%29)

A B1-series battle droid was stationed aboard Count Dooku's Munificent-class star frigate around the time of the Malevolence Campaign. When General Grievous ended a transmission with Dooku regarding the fate of the Malevolence, this battle droid surmised that Grievous had ended the transmission himself.
