# 7824

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [7824](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/7824)

7824 was a B1-series battle droid that served in the Trade Federation Droid Army. During the Naboo Crisis, 7824 fought against the Gungan Grand Army at the Great Grass Plains. The B1's designation was written on the back of it's comlink booster pack.
