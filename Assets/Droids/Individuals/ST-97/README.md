# ST-97

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ST-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ST-97)

ST-97 "Steel" was a battle droid model used by the Twin Suns of Tatooine during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY the droid was guarding a mine in the Jundland, which was taken over by the pirates, where it was destroyed by a Galactic Republic individual sent by Jundo Martik to reclaim the mine.
