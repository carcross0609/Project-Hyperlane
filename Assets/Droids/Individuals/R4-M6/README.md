# R4-M6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R4-M6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-M6)

R4-M6 was an R4-P astromech droid socketed into Mace Windu's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor. The droid was destroyed during the Battle of Coruscant, after Mace Windu collided with a fleet of vulture droids and droid tri-fighters.
