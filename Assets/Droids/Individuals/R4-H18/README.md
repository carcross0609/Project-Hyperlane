# R4-H18

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-H18](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-H18)

R4-H18 was an R4 astromech droid that had a translucent white body with silver highlights. The droid worked serving drinks to guests at the Canto Casino on Canto Bight. He was equipped with a drink tray as well as a liquid dispenser arm that could fill glasses it carried.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R4-H18
- **Model:** R4 astromech droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Silver
- **Plating:** White, silver, translucent
- **Equipment:**

  - Liquid dispenser
