# Linwodo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Linwodo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Linwodo)

Linwodo was an ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid who served as a general in the droid army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. During the Outer Rim Sieges in the final year of the war, in 19 BBY, Linwodo was involved in a defense of the planets Metalorn and Boz Pity and served as the commanding officer of the Confederacy's battle droid forces during their assault on the world of Kashyyyk. While confident in his ability to outmaneuver Jedi Generals Luminara Unduli and Quinlan Vos, who were leading the Galactic Republic's clone troopers, Linwodo's forces were defeated, and the droid general was later tracked down and destroyed in the battle's aftermath by the Republic's Ranger Corps.
