# 1BAR-0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [1BAR-0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1BAR-0)

1BAR-0 was first foot droid to High Magistrate Greef Karga of the planet Nevarro, serving alongside his second foot droid, 2MAS-N. In 9 ABY, the droids held up Karga's Mantle of Office cape while he greeted his allies Din Djarin and Grogu in Nevarro City and later when the trio confronted a group of pirates in the city. They also accompanied the High Magistrate and held his cape when he took Djarin and Grogu to the workshop of the Anzellan Droidsmiths.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 1BAR-0
- **Class:** Foot droid
- **Plating:** Red, gray, and white
