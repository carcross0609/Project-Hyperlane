# 8t88

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [8t88](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/8t88)

8t88 was a silver-plated, humanoid-shaped mercenary droid with red, blue, and white sensors. Residing on the moon Nar Shaddaa, the droid was willing to negotiate changing loyalties—at some point, 8t88 double-crossed a human and threatened them with a DL-44 heavy blaster pistol.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Criminal
- **Name:** 8t88
- **Sensor:** Red, blue, and white
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Armament:** DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
