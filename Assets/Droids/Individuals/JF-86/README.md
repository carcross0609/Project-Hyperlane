# JF-86

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JF-86](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JF-86)

JF-86 was a tactical droid that served in Admiral Trench's army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars at the Battle of Anaxes. Following the cataclysmic destruction of Anaxes near the end of the war in 19 BBY, JF-86 was deactivated alongside the rest of the Separatist Droid Army in a Separatist stronghold on the planetoid PM-6986 in the Anaxes asteroid belt.
