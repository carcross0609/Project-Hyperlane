# LE-12

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LE-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LE-12)

LE-12 was an older-model LE-series droid who resided on the Colossus. It moved on two springy legs, and regularly performed tasks for mechanics and salvagers who resided on the platform. It was aided in these tasks by its two clamps and a head capable of rotating 360 degrees.
