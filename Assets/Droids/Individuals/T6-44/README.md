# T6-44

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T6-44](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T6-44)

T6-44 was an astromech droid that served the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. It was tasked with delivering supplies to the Thul research camp on the planet Alderaan, but Killiks attacked and stole the supplies. T6-44 then recruited Imperial forces to recover the supplies.
