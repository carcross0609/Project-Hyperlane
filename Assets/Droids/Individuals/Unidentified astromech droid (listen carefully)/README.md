# Unidentified astromech droid (listen carefully)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified astromech droid (listen carefully)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_astromech_droid_%28listen_carefully%29)

A R5 series astromech droid worked with a Weequay technician on a moisture farm in the Great Chott salt flat region of the Outer Rim planet Tatooine during the Imperial Era. It had a white chassis with accents of red and blue. While working on the moisture farm, the astromech droid was responsible for communicating with the farm's moisture vaporators. However in 3 ABY, a group of Jawas stripped a number of components from the droid leaving it unable to communicate with the vaborators. Due to the Weequay's failing eye sight, they were unable to fix the droid themselves.
