# K-3PO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [K-3PO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-3PO)

K-3PO was a 3PO protocol droid who served as a captain in the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. He was stationed at Echo Base on the planet Hoth, where he was charged with organizing the base's droidpool. He was destroyed due to falling debris when the Rebel command center sustained enemy fire during the Battle of Hoth.
