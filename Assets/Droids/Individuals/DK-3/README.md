# DK-3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DK-3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DK-3)

DK-3 was an R-series astromech droid in the service of a Gran trader on Pantora. The protocol droid CG-67 supervised DK-3 and the trader's other droids, fellow astromech 225 and the LE-series Clink. DK-3 and the other droids assisted in repairing the Marauder, the shuttle of the former clone trooper unit known as the Bad Batch.
