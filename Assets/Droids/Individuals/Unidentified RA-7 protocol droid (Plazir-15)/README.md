# Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Plazir-15)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Plazir-15)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RA-7_protocol_droid_%28Plazir-15%29)

An obsidian-black-plated RA-7 protocol droid once worked for the Galactic Empire. After fall of the Galactic Empire, the droid was reprogrammed to serve the Royal family of the independent planet Plazir-15. In 9 ABY, the RA-7 waited together with a similarly black-plated R-series astromech droid on a docking platform outside the domed city where the Royal family resided as a Gauntlet starfighter descended. The RA-7 then greeted the exiting Mandalorians Din Djarin, Bo-Katan Kryze, and Grogu and told them to proceed to their assigned hyperloop pod waiting for them. After the two droids were out of listening reach, Djarin whispered to Kryze and asked her why there were Imperial droids on an independent world and Kryze replied that she didn't knew either.
