# Lomasigaro-K

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lomasigaro-K](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lomasigaro-K)

Lomasigaro-K was a Gree research droid dispatched by the starship Gray Secant to study the planet Voss. Accompanied by a squad of Gree battle droids, it refrained from transmitting in order to not interfere with the native Voss and Gormak's technological advancement.
