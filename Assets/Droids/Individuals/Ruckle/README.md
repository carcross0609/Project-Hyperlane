# Ruckle

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Ruckle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruckle)

Ruckle was a droid that operated during the Clone Wars, a galactic conflict fought between the Confederacy of Independent Systems and Galactic Republic. It had four spider-like legs, but was capable of standing on just two of them and using its other legs as arms. Atop its head was a single antenna.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ruckle
- **Height:**

  - Over 1.08 meters (Two legs, antenna included)
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Teal, gray, yellow, and white.
