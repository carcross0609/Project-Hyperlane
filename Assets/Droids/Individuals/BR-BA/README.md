# BR-BA

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BR-BA](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BR-BA)

BR-BA was an astromech droid in service to the Resistance during its war with the First Order. During the Resistance's attack on Starkiller Base, BR-BA flew with Pallaris Ven and was killed.
