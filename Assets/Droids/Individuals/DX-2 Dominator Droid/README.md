# DX-2 Dominator Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DX-2 Dominator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DX-2_Dominator_Droid)

The DX-2 Dominator Droid was a battle droid used by the Sith Empire during the Galactic War. In 3640 BBY the droid, along with TZ-3 Dominator Droid and C2-N Dominator Droid was used to defend the approach to the Bastion in the Coronet City on Corellia, but was destroyed during the assault by the Galactic Republic forces.
