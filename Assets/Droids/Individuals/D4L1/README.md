# D4L1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D4L1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D4L1)

D4L1 was a data processing droid owned by Xira, a member of the Free Ryloth Movement. During the movement's attempt to assassinate Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine and the Sith Lord Darth Vader, he aided Xira in coordinating an attack on the Imperial Star Destroyer Perilous. He was topped with a silver dome.
