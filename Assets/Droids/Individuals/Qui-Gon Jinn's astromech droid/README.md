# Qui-Gon Jinn's astromech droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Qui-Gon Jinn's astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qui-Gon_Jinn%27s_astromech_droid)

An R-series astromech droid of the Galactic Republic was used by the Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn during the Republic Era. In 37 BBY, Jinn, unsure of his role as a peacekeeper within a conflict on the planet Bri'n, took his astromech droid and traveled to a remote world, seeking guidance from the Force. He and the droid observed that the world was discovered long ago but never given a designation. As Jinn explored its surface on foot, the droid remained in the astromech socket of the Jedi's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor. The astromech had a black sensor and green and white plating.
