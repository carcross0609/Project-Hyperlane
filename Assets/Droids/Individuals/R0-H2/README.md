# R0-H2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R0-H2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R0-H2)

R0-H2 was an R0 astromech droid that served in the Resistance during its conflict with the First Order. During the Battle of Takodana, R0-H2 served as Commander Poe Dameron's astromech.
