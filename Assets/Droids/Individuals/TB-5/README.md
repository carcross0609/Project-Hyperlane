# TB-5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TB-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TB-5)

TB-5 was a 2-1B surgical droid that served the Galactic Republic on the Acclamator-class assault ship Aspirant. In 19 BBY, during the Clone Wars, the droid tended to injuries sustained from Yoda after a mission to Golatha.
