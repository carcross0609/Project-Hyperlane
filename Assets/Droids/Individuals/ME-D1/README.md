# ME-D1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ME-D1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ME-D1)

ME-D1, pronounced Meedee-one, was a ME-D probe droid built by the Jedi Knight Leeha Narezz during the Cold War. It, alongside the other Meedees, was taught Jedi history and philosophy by Leeha in hopes of proving that even droids could use the Force.
