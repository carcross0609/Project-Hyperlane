# R4-00

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R4-00](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-00)

R4-00 was an astromech droid who belonged to Seren Song. He accompanied her on a mission to Oaka Prime. He was destroyed by IG-88B in the year 0 ABY.
