# T4-M7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T4-M7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T4-M7)

T4-M7 was an astromech droid serving the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Galactic War. During the Conquest of Makeb, it was sent to the Drill Site Control Center as a courier by Darth Mortis to help him enlist the services of the Imperial strike team regarding the Shroud.

- **Line:** X2-C3 Imperial astromech
- **Class:** Astromech droid

- **Plating:** Red and black
