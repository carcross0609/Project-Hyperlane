# BX-2R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BX-2R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BX-2R)

BX-2R, or Toor, was a renovated BX-series droid commando assigned to janitorial work at Moff Lasre Dardano's Whisper Base. Toor was fitted with a restraining bolt and was responsible for cleaning the garage and filing reports. In 0 ABY, the droid encountered a team of Rebel Alliance operatives trying to capture the base.
