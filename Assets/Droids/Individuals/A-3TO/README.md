# A-3TO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A-3TO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-3TO)

A-3TO was a green-plated protocol droid who welcomed any mercenary or smuggler visiting The Yard on the world of Valgauth. He helped them get in touch with Syndicate One.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A-3TO
- **Homeworld:** Valgauth
- **Class:** Protocol droid
