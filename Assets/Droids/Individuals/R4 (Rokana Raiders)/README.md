# R4 (Rokana Raiders)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4 (Rokana Raiders)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4_%28Rokana_Raiders%29)

R4 was an R4 series astromech droid who worked with the Rokana Raiders pirate group at their base in the Sayma Nebula in 3 ABY. Later that year, R4 tried to leave the base on her own by taking an a empty starship. A member of the later talked to a comrade about R4's behavior who insisted that the ionization of the nebula must be effecting the raider's droids.
