# Kreven

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kreven](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kreven)

Kreven was a six-armed droid that worked with the pilot Reen. The two were hired by the Guild of Technicians to transport cargo aboard the starship Trustworthy, but Kreven betrayed Reen, intending to sabotage the vessel so that pirates could take the cargo. Kreven disabled two of the Trustworthy thrusters, and the starship crashed. An Ewok stowed away on the ship as Reen attempted to make repairs, uncovering a comlink that Kreven had used to contact the pirates. With Kreven's treachery revealed, Reen placed the droid in binding cuffs.
