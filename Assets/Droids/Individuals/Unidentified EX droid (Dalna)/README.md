# Unidentified EX droid (Dalna)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified EX droid (Dalna)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_EX_droid_%28Dalna%29)

In 228 BBY, an EX droid was sent into the Nihil Occlusion Zone by the Galactic Republic's Stormwall Defense Fleet. After discovering that the two main flashpoints in the Occlusion Zone were the planets Eriadu and Naboo, the EX communication droid collided with a Nihil Stormcruiser and crashed onto the planet Dalna. There, the droid was recovered by monster hunter Ty Yorrick and returned to Jedi Master Elzar Mann and the fleet.
