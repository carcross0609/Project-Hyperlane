# KL-03

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KL-03](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KL-03)

KL-03, or K-L, was an admin unit and protocol droid owned by the former Jedi Ty Yorrick, who worked as a saber-for-hire during the High Republic Era. KL-03 preferred to be called "Klo." Like her other possessions, such as her astromech droid R0-VR and starship, Yorrick did not see a point in naming the admin unit, seeing the droid as a tool that she did not need to become attached to. Klo was sarcastic and frequently chided Yorrick for being more machine than she was.
