# Z23-Y75

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z23-Y75](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z23-Y75)

Z23-Y75 was a B1-series battle droid who served in the Droid Army of the Trade Federation as a commander. The droids had light tan plating with brown markings. Like other B1-Series battle droids, Z23-Y75 had masculine programming, stood at 1.91 meters tall, and wielded an E-5 blaster rifle. The droid also had a backpack equipped with an antenna.
