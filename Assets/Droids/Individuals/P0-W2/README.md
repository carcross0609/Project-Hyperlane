# P0-W2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [P0-W2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P0-W2)

P0-W2 was a Class Five cleaning droid that served the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War, though they were at times unwillingly forced to serve under the Galactic Empire. Wielding a water blaster, P0-W2 took up the role of cleaning off equipment and starships owned by their superiors.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Rebel
- **Name:** P0-W2
- **Model:** Cleaning droid
- **Class:** Class five droid
- **Equipment:** A water blaster
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Galactic Empire (unwilling)
