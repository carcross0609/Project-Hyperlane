# QO-99

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [QO-99](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QO-99)

QO-99 was a droid of unknown origins that served in the mercenary unit known as the Specialists during the Galactic War. The unit was led by the Devaronian bounty hunter known as the Ratcatcher, and QO-99 operated the Ratcatcher's probe droids and assassin droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** QO-99
- **Death:** 3638 BBY, Makeb
- **Pronouns:** new
