# KRONOS-327

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KRONOS-327](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KRONOS-327)

KRONOS-327 was an IG-86 sentinel droid who worked in Ziro the Hutt's criminal organization during the Clone Wars. He was Ziro's favored assassin until he failed an assignment and was ordered for destruction.
