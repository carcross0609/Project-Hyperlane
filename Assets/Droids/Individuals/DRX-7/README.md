# DRX-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DRX-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DRX-7)

DRX-7 or DRX-752B was a protocol droid who served Lando Calrissian on Cloud City, Bespin. He was reprogrammed by a mysterious man to assassinate Lando. He was defeated by Kaasha Bateen and Lando.
