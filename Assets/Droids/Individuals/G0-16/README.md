# G0-16

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G0-16](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G0-16)

G0-16 was a fan-tan dealer droid that worked at the Fifteen Moons Casino on the planet Ord Mantell. The droid lacked sympathy for the players, and would routinely skip over players who didn't meet its eye during gameplay.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G0-16
- **Model:** Dealer droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Plating:** Silver and black
- **Affiliation:** Fifteen Moons Casino
