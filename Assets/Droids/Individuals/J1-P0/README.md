# J1-P0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [J1-P0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J1-P0)

J1-P0 was a protocol droid who was owned by Kulton Woodle, a retired Mon Calamari freighter captain, sometime after the Battle of Yavin. During that period, the droid spent time standing with Woodle outside of the Theed Spaceport on the planet Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** J1-P0
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Silver
