# TC-332

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TC-332](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TC-332)

TC-332 was a turquoise-plated protocol droid with yellow sensors was present on the independent planet Plazir-15 during the early New Republic Era. TC-332 was a frequent patron at the droid-exclusive cantina, The Resistor, and on one occasion in 9 ABY, stood at a table together with a yellow R-series astromech droid, two B1-series battle droids, and another droid near the entrance door. When the Mandalorians Din Djarin and Bo-Katan Kryze entered the cantina during their investigation of malfunctioning droids on Plazir-15, the protocol droid stared with all other droids at the two human interlopers and followed them with its gaze as they walked to the counter, before it turned back around. When Djarin threatened the bartender droid M0, TC-332 tried to walk to the door, but stopped when Djarin saw it and said that nobody would leave. It thus walked back to its table and continued to stare at the two humans.
