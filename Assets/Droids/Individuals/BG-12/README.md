# BG-12

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BG-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BG-12)

BG-12 was a BG-series assassin droid. He was in the employ of Themog, a criminal operating in the town of Corestrike on the planet Chrona.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BG-12
- **Model:** BG-series droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Armament:** Two blaster pistols in arm recesses
- **Equipment:**
  - Armored chassis
  - Broadband antenna
  - Enhanced visual and audio sensors
  - Gerdoor GG-22 tactical decision-making node
  - Holographic recorder/projector
  - Verbo 212-38 vocabulator and language interpreter
- **Affiliation:** Themog
