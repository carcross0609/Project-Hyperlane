# N0-AH

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [N0-AH](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N0-AH)

N0-AH was an SK series astromech droid that had white body with orange, teal and turquoise plating. After the Battle of Endor, a group of Jawas on Tatooine offered N0-AH in exchange for a camtono of silicax oxalate crystals in the possession of Cobb Vanth, however Vanth instead settled for a set of Mandalorian armor, which originally belonged to bounty hunter Boba Fett. The droid was equipped with a restraining bolt, and was stained and worn by Tatooine's environment.

## Specifications

- **Name:** N0-AH
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** SK series
- **Class:** Astromech droid

- **Plating:** Orange, teal, turquoise and white
