# OM-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OM-7/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OM-7/Legends)

OM-7 was a B1-series battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. During the Battle of Ryloth, OM-7 along with OM-5 were sent by their Commander to check out a Republic Gunship that was blocking their path. OM-5 warned OM-7 of clone troopers, but instead found the gunship full of explosives. A clone trooper found the pair in close range of the gunship and activated the charges. The explosion destroyed OM-7 and OM-5, and also made the hills collapse, making a huge barrier for the droid army's path.
