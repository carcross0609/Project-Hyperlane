# Unidentified MSE-series droid (Zuvio)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified MSE-series droid (Zuvio)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_MSE-series_droid_%28Zuvio%29)

The MSE droid was one of the supposedly rogue droids that attacked Constable Zuvio during his investigation of a banking ship robbery in Niima Outpost. As a part of Rikard Lovas's plot to steal the electronic credits of the citizens of Niima Outpost on Jakku, several droids were sent to an abandoned farmhouse to wait for Constable Zuvio. During the skirmish at the abandoned Jakku moisture farm the mouse droid was dispatched after colliding with a Treadwell droid causing an ionic charge. The mouse droid was thoroughly destroyed when an EG-6 gonk droid caused the farmhouse to explode.

## Specifications
