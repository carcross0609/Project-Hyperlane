# CAM-E

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CAM-E](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CAM-E)

CAM-E was a cam droid affiliated with the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts who was stationed on the Youngling Care Space Station alongside Safari droid M1-RE. CAM-E cataloged footage of M1-RE taking care of younglings, and the recordings were eventually broadcasted to the HoloNet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CAM-E
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Corporation
- **Class:** Holocam droid
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts
