# R5-T9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-T9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-T9)

R5-T9, also known as simply T9, was a top-of-the-line R5 series astromech droid. In 3 ABY, he was associated with the criminal Pyke Syndicate on the Outer Rim moon Toshara. That year, he was trapped at a bandit camp and was unable to return to his Pyke handler.
