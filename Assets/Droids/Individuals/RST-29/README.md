# RST-29

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [RST-29](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RST-29)

RST-29, or "Rusty," was a DUM-series pit droid who lived on the Island. He was built by Laurasta Vertoa to help Darnyn Mott. At some point, Darnyn Mott asked RST-29 to build him a BTL Y-wing starfighter. He was also asked to build a Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor, which he modeled after the one piloted by Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker. RST-29 also built a replica of a Sith Infiltrator based on Republic Sienar Systems' Star Courier. With the help of fellow droid TU-T0R, RST-29 was able to find original blueprints of the ship.
