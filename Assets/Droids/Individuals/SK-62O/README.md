# SK-62O

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SK-62O](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SK-62O)

SK-62O was an astromech droid present on Chandrila Star Line's MPO-1400 star cruiser Halcyon during the war between the First Order and the Resistance. The droid had special modifications that included an antenna.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SK-62O
- **Line:** SK series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Orange, gray and white
