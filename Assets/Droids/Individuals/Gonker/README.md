# Gonker

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Gonker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gonker)

Gonker was the only child of Mama Gonk's who attended gonk school and spoke more eloquently than other EG-6 power droids they knew. A resident of Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine, Gonker once sought assistance in being escorted through groups of armed hostiles to Gonk-Stores Paradise, where they were employed to maintain the door.
