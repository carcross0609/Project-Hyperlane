# LEP-171B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LEP-171B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LEP-171B)

LEP-171B was a dedicated LEP-series service droid that served in the LEP droid pool of the Coruscant Security Force during the Clone Wars. It was assigned to Lieutenant Tanivos Divo, who made extensive use of it to manage his collection of datapads during investigations, as the eccentric officer liked to keep a separate datapad for each part of a case. In 21 BBY, LEP-171B accompanied Divo to the office of Onaconda Farr, a senator who had been murdered, in order to investigate the crime scene.
