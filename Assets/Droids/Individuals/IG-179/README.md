# IG-179

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IG-179](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG-179)

IG-179 was an IG-100 MagnaGuard in the service of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. After the fall of the Galactic Republic, IG-179 would later reappear as chief lieutenant to Iron Knight Luxum.
