# Mort Coblin's astromech

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mort Coblin's astromech](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mort_Coblin%27s_astromech)

An astromech companion to Mort Coblin was on Hauler's Keep in 3 ABY. During the astromech droid's time on the space station they were involved in an altercation with members of the station's staff. As a result, an announcement was made over the station's speaker system requesting Coblin's presence at Hanger 4.
