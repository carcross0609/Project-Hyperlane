# 9B2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [9B2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/9B2)

9B2 was a B1-series battle droid stationed on the planet Gavos and was part of the New Droids' Rights Movement, a group of droids that was focused on spreading violent rebellion against organic life forms throughout the galaxy.
