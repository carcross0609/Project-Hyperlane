# P-T48

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [P-T48](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-T48)

P-T48 was a protocol droid who worked as a merchant during the Galactic War between the Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic. During the war it ran a stall within the Republic controlled Shipwright Auxiliary Spaceport on the planet Corellia. P-T48 had yellow sensors and gold plating.

## Specifications

- **Name:** P-T48
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Gold
