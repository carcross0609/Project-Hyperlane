# Seven

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Seven](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seven)

Seven was the designation of a K droid built by the droid J-6 on Aricho. The K droids were made to aid the planet's resistance force in liberating its people from the control of the Nihil marauders, although seven was incomplete. When the Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh encountered J-6 on Aricho, the latter requested that she help remove the energy core from a speeder bike for seven.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Seven
- **Homeworld:** Aricho
- **Birth:** 228 BBY, Aricho
- **Creator:** J-6
- **Model:** K-series droid
- **Affiliation:** Aricho's resistance force
