# Unidentified droid (Branth)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid (Branth)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_%28Branth%29)

This droid, on a quick inspection, looked very similar to an R2-series astromech droid but was in fact of an earlier model which was slightly larger, being 1.1 meters, rather than 1.0. It also did not seem to have the retractable legs of the R2's. The paint scheme on the droid was gray, much like Branth, the planet it inhabited. The wheel housings of this droid were missing from the bottom of the droid's "legs", and small metal plates had been fixed there instead so that the droid was still mobile, much like the runners on a sled. The droid moved along slowly, with a hobble which often caused people to feel sympathy for it.
