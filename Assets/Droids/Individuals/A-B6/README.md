# A-B6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A-B6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-B6)

A-B6, or Ay-BeeSix, was the fourth member of the Farns swoop gang in Mos Eisley, along with Geordi Hans, Jeff Pill, and Franklin Scott. She often asked questions and got in the way, but the gang put up with her. Once Franklin asked her to go into Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina and get a hydrospanner, which led to an altercation with a Gamorrean which damaged her. Since being repaired the gang has treated her with more care.
