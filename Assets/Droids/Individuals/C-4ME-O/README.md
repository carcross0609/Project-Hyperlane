# C-4ME-O

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C-4ME-O](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-4ME-O)

C-4ME-O, or Fourmio, was a secretary droid stationed on MedStar Four during its deployment to Despayre in 0 BBY. It stood on a single gyroscopically balanced wheel. It was later assigned to aid Dr. Kornell Divini during his stint aboard Death Star I.
