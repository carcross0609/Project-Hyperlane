# ZZ-4Z

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ZZ-4Z](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZZ-4Z)

ZZ-4Z, also known as "ZeeZee," was a JV-Z1/D butler droid produced by Serv-O-Droid, Inc. in the centuries prior to the formation of the Galactic Empire.
