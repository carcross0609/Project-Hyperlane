# WED 15 Septoid 2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [WED 15 Septoid 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WED_15_Septoid_2)

WED-15 "Septoid 2" was a treadwell droid on Tatooine. He belonged to Fixer, who used him at his job at Tosche Station. Luke Skywalker was envious of the droid, due to his own treadwell, WED-15-77's limited capabilities.
