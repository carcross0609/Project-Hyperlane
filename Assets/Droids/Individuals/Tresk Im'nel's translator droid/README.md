# Tresk Im'nel's translator droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tresk Im'nel's translator droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tresk_Im%27nel%27s_translator_droid)

A translator droid was owned by the Bothan politician and Jedi named Tresk Im'nel. Before Im'nel owned the droid, it had another owner. Im'nel used the translator droid during his time as a New Republic diplomat, and later during his time as a Jedi Knight in the New Jedi Order. The droid was owned by Im'nel from the time of the New Republic's formation to the years of the Yuuzhan Vong's invasion of the galaxy.
