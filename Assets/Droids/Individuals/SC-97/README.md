# SC-97

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SC-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SC-97)

SC-97 was a BB-series astromech droid with a black sensor and dark red and white paneling. As a member of the BB-series, it was manufactured by Industrial Automaton and stood 0.67 meters tall. In 34 ABY, during the First Order's occupation of the planet Batuu, SC-97 was displayed among droid parts on one of the shelves inside Droid Depot, a store in Batuu's Black Spire Outpost.
