# K-344

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [K-344](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-344)

K-344, nicknamed Kurti, was a droid who was the guitarist of the band Star Waver. Formerly abandoned, K-344 found her purpose in music. She had a calm demeanor and an authoritative tone, but let her playing do most of the talking.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Musician
- **Name:** K-344
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Gold and gray
- **Affiliation:** Star Waver
