# Unidentified astromech droid (Gold Two)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified astromech droid (Gold Two)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_astromech_droid_%28Gold_Two%29)

An astromech droid was assigned to Gold Two's BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber during Operation Handoff in 2 BBY. When Gold Two became injured and was unable to pilot their Y-wing, the droid watched Commander Ezra Bridger of Phoenix Squadron take over for her.
