# R2-C4 (Naboo)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-C4 (Naboo)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-C4_%28Naboo%29)

R2-C4 was an R2-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton that belonged to the Space Fighter Corps of the Royal House of the planet Naboo, often serving in the astromech socket of the N-1 starfighter Bravo 6. This droid had been assigned to pilot Gavyn Sykes. During the occupation of Naboo by the Trade Federation in 32 BBY, R2-C4 was one of several Fighter Corps droids that was held in the hangar of the Theed Royal Palace in the capital city of Theed.
