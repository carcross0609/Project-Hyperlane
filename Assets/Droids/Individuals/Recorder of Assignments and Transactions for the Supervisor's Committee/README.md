# Recorder of Assignments and Transactions for the Supervisor's Committee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Recorder of Assignments and Transactions for the Supervisor's Committee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Recorder_of_Assignments_and_Transactions_for_the_Supervisor%27s_Committee)

The Recorder of Assignments and Transactions for the Supervisor's Committee, nicknamed Chuckles by Jedi Master Luke Skywalker, was a TT-40 librarian droid serving as a records keeper in the city of Griann on the planet Teyr. Skywalker and his companion, Akanah Norand Goss Pell, consulted the droid for census information in their search for the Fallanassi sect.
