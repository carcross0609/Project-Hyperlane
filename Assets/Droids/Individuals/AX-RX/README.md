# AX/RX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AX/RX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AX/RX)

AX/RX was a modified MD-series medical droid owned by Kh'aris Fenn and was active in 30 BBY. Kh'aris used it as a torture droid, utilizing its knowledge of the anatomy to inflict maximum pain.
