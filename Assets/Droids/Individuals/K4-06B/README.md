# K4-06B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K4-06B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K4-06B)

K4-06B, also known as "Kay," was a masculine secretary droid who was partnered with Uurk, a Gamorrean lawkeeper of the Emmer police. He was specialized in translating Uurk's Gamorrese, defusing bombs, dealing with kidnappers and terrorists, and doing paperwork.
