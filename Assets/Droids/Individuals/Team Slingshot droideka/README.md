# Team Slingshot droideka

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Team Slingshot droideka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Slingshot_droideka)

The team Slingshot droideka was a modified droideka that was active following the fall of the Galactic Empire. Discovered by the Ugnaught Dizzy, she would be claimed by the mechanic and heavily modified to carry him at great speeds. The two went by the united persona of Slingshot in the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara.
