# J4-SN Chef Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [J4-SN Chef Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J4-SN_Chef_Droid)

The J4-SN Chef Droid was a model of floating droid used as a cooking droid by Hutt Cartel members Gaboorga the Abundant and Duuba the Magnanimous during the Feast of Prosperity on Nar Shaddaa in 3626 BBY. J4-SN Chef Droids were commonly plated in a gold coloration, with a blue photoreceptor and four multipurpose appendages. It was also possible to attach a large custom chef hat on top of them. The Sarlacc Pit cantina and Bog's Bounty Banquet Hall Head Chef Droid was a J4-SN Chef Droid.
