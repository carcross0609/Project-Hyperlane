# DX-TX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DX-TX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DX-TX)

DX-TX was a droid leader of the DX annihilator network located on the moon of Nar Shaddaa. This group allied with Godoba the Hutt and the Nar Shaddaa Labor Alliance to form the Shadow Syndicate.
