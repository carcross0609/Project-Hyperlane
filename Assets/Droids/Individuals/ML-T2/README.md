# ML-T2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ML-T2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ML-T2)

ML-T2 was a droid that worked as a vendor on Tatooine during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. It maintained a shop in the Anchorhead market, where it constructed and sold armor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ML-T2
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Silver and yellow
