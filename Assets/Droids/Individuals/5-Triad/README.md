# 5-Triad

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [5-Triad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/5-Triad)

5-Triad was a security protocol droid with masculine programming who worked at a detention center in Lonisa City on Valo during the High Republic Era. When Ty Yorrick, Mantessa Chekkat and Jedi Padawan Ram Jomaram were all locked up in the facility at the beginning of the Republic Fair, he served as their jailer, disbelieving Jomaram's protests regarding his identity.
