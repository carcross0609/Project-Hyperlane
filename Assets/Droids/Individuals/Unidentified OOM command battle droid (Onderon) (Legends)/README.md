# Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Onderon)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Onderon)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_%28Onderon%29/Legends)

This OOM command battle droid was part of the droid occupation present on Onderon in the city of Iziz and was in command of Patrol 118. The group was ambushed by Onderon rebels lead by Saw Gerrera in hope for the droids to contact reinforcements that included a tank to proceed further within their plan. This droid was destroyed by Steela Gerrera after it had called for backup.
