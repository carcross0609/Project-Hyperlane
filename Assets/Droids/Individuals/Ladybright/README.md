# Ladybright

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ladybright](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ladybright)

Ladybright, also known as Bright, was a droid member of the Orphans, a group led by the bounty hunter Chanath Cha. Similar to the other members of the Orphans—who, as the group's name suggested, had lost their parents—Ladybright was the last of their factory line. She wielded two DC-15A blaster carbines and wore a sniper rifle on her back. During the time of the Galactic Empire, the Orphans were recruited into the plans of Lady Qi'ra, leader of the Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate. As part of Qi'ra's goal to bring an end to the Sith Darth Sidious and Darth Vader, Ladybright and the Orphans undertook attacks against other criminal syndicates in order to turn the syndicates against each other.
