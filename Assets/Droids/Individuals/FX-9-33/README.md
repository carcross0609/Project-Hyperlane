# FX-9-33

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [FX-9-33](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-9-33)

FX-9-33 was a surgeon droid and a colleague of medical droid 2-1B33. FX-9-33 worked aboard the Rebel medical frigate, the Asclepios, and specialized in cardiovascular operations.
