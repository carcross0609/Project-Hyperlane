# RA-7-D4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RA-7-D4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RA-7-D4)

RA-7-D4 was an RA-7 protocol droid who served aboard the First Order Resurgent-class Star Destroyer Finalizer as a spy for the Resistance. In the year 34 ABY, it translated and logged IT-000-XZ 1594's report on the interrogation of Poe Dameron, which the droid sent to C-3PO, who operated the Resistance's droid spy network.
