# MA-55H

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MA-55H](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MA-55H)

MA-55H, nicknamed Mash, was a bounty droid who worked for the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. In 9 BBY, the droid traveled to a lunar satellite facility above the Shattered Moon of the planet Koboh in search for the Jedi Knight Cal Kestis, who had a bounty on his head.
