# UTT-90

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [UTT-90](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/UTT-90)

UTT-90 was a B1-series battle droid in the service of the Trade Federation and its Droid Army as a commander. Like all B1 battle droids, UTT-90 had masculine programming, stood 1.91 meters tall, and was equipped with an E-5 blaster rifle. The droid had light tan plating with brown markings, and had a backpack with an antenna.
