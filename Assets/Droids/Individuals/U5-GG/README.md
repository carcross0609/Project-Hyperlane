# U5-GG

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [U5-GG](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/U5-GG)

U5-GG, also known as Gigi, was a U5 astromech droid who served the Resistance. Gigi was assigned to the spy Vi Moradi. The two were captured in a starhopper by the First Order, and Moradi wiped U5-GG's memory before stormtroopers could access any data. The stormtroopers were subsequently ordered to haul off the droid for parts. Though the droid originally had its factory-standard paint job of white and blue, Moradi repainted it to brandish yellow and copper colors to mimic her own bleached hair and skin tone, respectively.
