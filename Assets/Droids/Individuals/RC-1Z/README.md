# RC-1Z

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RC-1Z](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RC-1Z)

RC-1Z was a protocol droid who belonged to the Ithorian swooper Draknus during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, RC-1Z, along with RC-2X and RC-3T, helped Draknus to manage the Mos Entha race track on the planet Tatooine.
