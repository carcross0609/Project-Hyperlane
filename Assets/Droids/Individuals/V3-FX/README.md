# V3-FX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V3-FX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V3-FX)

V3-FX was a protocol droid who operated during the time of the Galactic Civil War. Sometime following the Battle of Yavin in 0 BBY, he operated out of the city of Moenia on the planet Naboo.
