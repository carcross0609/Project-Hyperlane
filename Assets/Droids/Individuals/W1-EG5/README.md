# W1-EG5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [W1-EG5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/W1-EG5)

W1-EG5 was a white repair droid that was forced to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, W1-EG5 was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37. Shortly after his freedom, the spunky droid used its moving flap as a fist against the Pyke Syndicate's control room.

## Specifications

- **Name:** W1-EG5
- **Model:** Repair droid
- **Sensor:** White, Black
- **Affiliation:** Pyke Syndicate (as slave)
