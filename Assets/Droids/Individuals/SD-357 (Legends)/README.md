# SD-357

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SD-357/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-357/Legends)

SD-357 was a police droid that was active during the Clone Wars and served at a large space station. It was destroyed by the Sith Lord Darth Maul when he and his brother Savage Opress raided the station and eliminated all the droids that faced them.

- **Model:** Spaceport police droid
