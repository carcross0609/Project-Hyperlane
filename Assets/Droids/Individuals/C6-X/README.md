# C6-X

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C6-X](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C6-X)

C6-X was a Probe droid used by the Eternal Alliance in the aftermath of the war against Zakuul. In 3629 BBY, Lana Beniko took the droid with her on a scouting mission to Iokath. There, the droid was deployed to take readings of an Iokath superweapon, but was shot down by the Galactic Republic soldiers, becoming the first casualty of the War on Iokath.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C6-X
- **Death:** 3629 BBY, Iokath
- **Class:** Probe droid
