# Unidentified Sentinel (Imperalis)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Sentinel (Imperalis)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Sentinel_%28Imperalis%29)

A Sentinel droid served as pilot to a replica of the Imperialis during the yacht's voyage from the planet Jakku to the Unknown Regions to recreate the Galactic Empire in accordance with the late Emperor Palpatine's Contingency Plan, carrying aboard it Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, the Hux family and a group of child soldiers previously trained by Gallius Rax.
