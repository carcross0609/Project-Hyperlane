# FEG-13

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FEG-13](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FEG-13)

FeeJee-13 was a FEG-series pilot droid owned by the pirate Rav that operated the helm of his starship. He acted as backup whenever they landed on a planet. FeeJee was present at the Duel on Roon.
