# N-7F5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [N-7F5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N-7F5)

N-7F5 was a shopkeeper droid who served the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War against the Sith Empire. In 3640 BBY, the droid was stationed on the planet Ilum during the Battle of Ilum. On Ilum the droid manned a small cantina within the Republic base camp. N-7F5 had gold plating and yellow sensors.
