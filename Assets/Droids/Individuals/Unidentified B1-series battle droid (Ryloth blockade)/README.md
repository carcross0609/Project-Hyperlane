# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Ryloth blockade)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Ryloth blockade)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Ryloth_blockade%29)

During the Clone Wars, a B1-series battle droid served within the naval forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. In 21 BBY, the B1-series battle droid was part of a Separatist blockade in orbit of the planet Ryloth under the command of Neimoidian Captain Mar Tuuk. However, the droid was later destroyed following the destruction of Tuuk's Lucrehulk-class Battleship during engagements with a Galactic Republic fleet led by Jedi General Anakin Skywalker.
