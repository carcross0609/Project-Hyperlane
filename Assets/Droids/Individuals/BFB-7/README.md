# BFB-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BFB-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BFB-7)

BFB-7 was a droid nicknamed "Boost-Bot," who was active during the war between the First Order and the Resistance. The droid had a servomotor-equipped riser at its top, and a simplified logic center on its front.

- **Name:** BFB-7
- **Plating:** Dark gray
