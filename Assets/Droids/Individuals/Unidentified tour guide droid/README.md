# Unidentified tour guide droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified tour guide droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_tour_guide_droid)

A tour guide droid with masculine programming was welcoming a group of Shadow Collective soldiers to Peace Park, located in the New Mandalorians' capital city of Sundari, during their takeover of the planet Mandalore when one of them blasted him in the face, destroying the hapless droid.
