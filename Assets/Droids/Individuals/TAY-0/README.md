# TAY-0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TAY-0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TAY-0)

TAY-0 was a conceited, refurbished Czerka CM3-series protocol droid and racer droid who piloted a modified 12 series speeder repulsorcraft and participated in the celerity sport of Riot Racing on the desert planet Serolonis between 19 BBY and 18 BBY. He was bet to win a race for the information broker Ciddarin Scaleback, against her old associate Grini Millegi, with Jet Venim, the reigning champion of the Safa Toma Classic, as his racer.
