# Unidentified droid (Morenia)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid (Morenia)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_%28Morenia%29)

A droid flew a ZH-40 Tribune-class light freighter for the casino cruiser Morenia during 3 ABY. He took part in a supply mission to Renpalli Station for the Morenia in 3 ABY where he bought twenty tanks of veron gas and fifteen units of hyperfuel. On his way back to the Morenia he was followed by the scoundrel Kay Vess who was trying to find a way aboard the casino. When he was part way through the Toshaal system's debris field he noticed a EML-850 light freighter was following him and he tried to lose it in the field. He was able to make it to a relay beacon and get the coordinates to rendezvous with the Morenia. However Vess was able to follow him to the beacon and decrypt the coordinates.
