# JJH2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JJH2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JJH2)

JJH2 was an astromech droid model of the same name who was active during the time of the Galactic Civil War. Colored in purple and black, JJH2 was a crew member of the Mighty Oak Apocalypse, a freighter owned by the Wookiee captain Lohgarra. After the MOA crew joined the Rebel Alliance, the droid was assigned to Corona Squadron, serving as a co-pilot of Corona Two during the Battle of Endor.
