# R-9XR

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-9XR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-9XR)

R-9XR was a battle droid that was part of the Galactic Republic's forces during the Korriban Incursion, when the Republic invaded the planet Korriban in 3637 BBY. during the Galactic War. R-9XR was tasked with guarding the force field erected in the Valley of the Dark Lords next to the Tomb of Ajunta Pall, but was destroyed by Imperial forces in their push to retake the Sith Academy.
