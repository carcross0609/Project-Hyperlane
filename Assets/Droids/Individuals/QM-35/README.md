# QM-35

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [QM-35](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QM-35)

QM-35 was a droid on the planet of Taris. He served as the guardian of the Tarisian Archive after the ruling members of the Taris governement froze themselves in carbonite during the orbital bombardment of the planet by Darth Malak. He would remain there for over 300 years, guarding the Archive from rakghouls and scavengers in the depths of Transport Station Five, until the Archive was rediscovered by a Republic-allied individual.
