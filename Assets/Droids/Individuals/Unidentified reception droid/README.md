# Unidentified reception droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified reception droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_reception_droid)

A reception droid worked in the lobby of the Yarvellian Institute medcenter on Protobranch during the High Republic Era. After the Nihil Minister of Advancement Baron Boolan arrived at the medcenter delivering a supposed cure for the Blight, a computer virus began to corrupt internal systems throughout the facility. The droid encouraged the astromech Ee-Seventeen to disconnect his scomp link from the internal systems to prevent being infected. Ee-Seventeen and the reception droid, however, became increasingly concerned as a speeder bike operated by Twi'lek bounty hunter Lourna Dee rapidly sped towards the institute's entrance. The pair were unable to lock the doors before Dee flew her speeder bike through the glass.
