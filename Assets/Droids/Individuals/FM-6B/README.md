# FM-6B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FM-6B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FM-6B)

FM-6B was a medical droid that served the Empire at the Arginall City garrison during the New Order. This droid injected Ketrian Altronel with a slow-release poison to prevent her from defecting to the Alliance.
