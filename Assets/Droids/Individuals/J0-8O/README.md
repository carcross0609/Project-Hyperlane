# J0-8O

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J0-8O](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J0-8O)

J0-8O was an archival droid who worked at the Galactic Museum and Archives on the planet Chandrila. The droid's role related to storing some of the rarest testimonies and records relating to atrocities and genocides committed by the Galactic Empire and making them available. In 35 ABY, the historian Beaumont Kin claimed everyone should be eternally grateful for J0-8O's dedicated work at the museum in his book The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** J0-8O
- **Model:** Archival droid
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Museum and Archives
