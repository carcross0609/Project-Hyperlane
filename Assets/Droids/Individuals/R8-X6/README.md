# R8-X6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R8-X6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R8-X6)

R8-X6 was a battle droid that was part of the security forces of the Jedi Colony established on Ossus. During the Invasion of Ossus, it was destroyed by the Sith Empire forces who raided a Republic communications control bunker.

- **Death:** 3628 BBY, Ossus
