# Unidentified B1 battle droid 1 (Kiros)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 1 (Kiros)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_1_%28Kiros%29)

This B1 battle droid was stationed on the planet of Kiros and participated in the Battle of Kiros in 20 BBY. Under the command of Darts D'Nar, he guarded a thermal annihilator bomb on Kiros along with two other B1-series battle droids and a group of B2-series super battle droids. However, this droid was destroyed along with his fellow droids by Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano on AT-RTs.
