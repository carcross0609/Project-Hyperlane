# CZ-94

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CZ-94](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-94)

CZ-94 was a droid that served in the portmaster's office at the Serenno Spaceport. It was destroyed by Asajj Ventress, Sith apprentice to Dooku, who visited the spaceport in search of her master's sister Jenza.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CZ-94
- **Death:** Before 22 BBY, Serenno
