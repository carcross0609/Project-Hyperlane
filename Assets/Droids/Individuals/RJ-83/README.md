# RJ-83

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RJ-83](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RJ-83)

RJ-83, or simply referred as RJ, was the personal droid of Nash Durango and also a friend to the Jedi Initiates Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay, and Nubs during the High Republic Era.
