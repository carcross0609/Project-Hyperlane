# 1234

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [1234](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1234)

1234 was a B1-series battle droid that served in the Trade Federation Droid Army. During the Naboo Crisis, 1234 fought against the Gungan Grand Army at the Great Grass Plains, but the battle droid was destroyed by Bombad General Jar Jar Binks.
