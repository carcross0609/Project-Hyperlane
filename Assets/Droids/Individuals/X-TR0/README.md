# X-TR0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X-TR0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-TR0)

X-TR0 was a battle droid with a feminine programming used by the Shroud to guard the museum on the volcano level of The Coil. In 3630 BBY X-TR0 detected intruders in the museum and attacked them, while politely asking them to leave. However, HK-55 and Z0-0M managed to defeat and destroy her.

## Specifications

- **Name:** X-TR0
- **Death:** 3630 BBY, Obus VI
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Grey
- **Armament:** Missile launchers
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
