# Unidentified B1-series battle droid commander

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid commander](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_commander)

A B1-series battle droid commander served the ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid Sarnath during the Imperial Era. In 19 BBY, the commander was destroyed by several 501st Legion clone troopers after they retook the bridge of a Techno Union transporter.
