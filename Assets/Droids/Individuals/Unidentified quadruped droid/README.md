# Unidentified quadruped droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified quadruped droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_quadruped_droid)

A quadruped droid was utilized by the bounty hunter Boba Fett while he worked on the Outer Rim Territories planet Carajam between around 2 BBY and 4 ABY. Fett rode the droid while transporting the corpse of a rebel pilot he had captured across a desert. On his journey, he encountered a camp containing several criminals: Zingo Gabnit, Turfitch, and another human.
