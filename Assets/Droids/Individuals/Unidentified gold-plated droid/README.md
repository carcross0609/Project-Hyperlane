# Unidentified gold-plated droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified gold-plated droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_gold-plated_droid)

A gold-plated droid was present on the Outer Rim Territories planet Batuu between 3 ABY and 4 ABY. There, the droid was destroyed during a battle with the Sith Lord Darth Vader.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified gold-plated droid
- **Death:** Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, Batuu
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Gold
