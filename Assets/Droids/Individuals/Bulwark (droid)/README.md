# Bulwark (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bulwark (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bulwark_%28droid%29)

Bulwark was a battle droid that joined the Directive 7 movement headed by Mentor during the era of the Galactic War. Adopting a new name of its own choosing, Bulwark was among the droids that gathered on the moon Zadd under Mentor's leadership, and was one of the few droids who were not absorbed into Mentor's consciousness. However, Bulwark was destroyed while trying to protect Mentor's core from invaders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bulwark
- **Death:** 3640 BBY, Zadd
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Red and white
- **Armament:** Mounted blaster cannons
- **Affiliation:** Directive 7
