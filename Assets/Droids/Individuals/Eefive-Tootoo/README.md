# Eefive-Tootoo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eefive-Tootoo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eefive-Tootoo)

Eefive-Tootoo was an E522 assassin droid. Originally in the service of Jabba the Hutt, Eefive was fed to the rancor. Two Jawas salvaged the remains, stripped the droid of its assassin droid programming and sold the unit to Lady Valarian.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Eefive-Tootoo
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Intelligence Systems
- **Model:** E522 assassin droid
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jabba's criminal empire
  - Valarian Syndicate
