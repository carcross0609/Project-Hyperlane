# CZ-7OB

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon

CZ-7OB, or SeeZee-Seven, was a CZ-series tutor droid in the employ of the Royal House of Alderaan. He served as a teacher to Crown Princess Leia Organa.
