# DD-11A

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DD-11A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DD-11A)

DD-11A, or DeDe, was a Tendrando Arms Defender Droid owned by Queen Mother Tenel Ka to protect her daughter, Allana. A modified YVH 1 combat droid, DeDe was made to be a nanny and bodyguard droid for Allana. She had weapon arms, a synthskin torso, and a "cherubic" face.
