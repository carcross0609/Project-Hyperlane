# ODX-9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ODX-9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ODX-9)

ODX-9 was a protocol droid used by the Sith Empire during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY, ODX-9 was stationed on Balmorra, where they were assigned to the office of Lieutenant Major Pirrell.
