# IG-88D

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IG-88D](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG-88D)

IG-88D was an IG-88 assassin droid who worked as a bounty hunter. IG-88D was one of five identical IG-series assassin droids manufactured by Holowan Laboratories. Upon their activation, the droids slaughtered their creators and escaped their laboratories into the galaxy. At some point, the droid was present at a starship wreckage with several rebel fleet troopers. The assassin droid wielded two blasters.
