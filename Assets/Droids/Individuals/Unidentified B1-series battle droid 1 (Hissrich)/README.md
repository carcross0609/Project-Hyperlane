# Unidentified B1-series battle droid 1 (Hissrich)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid 1 (Hissrich)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_1_%28Hissrich%29)

This B1-series battle droid was stationed on the planet Hissrich early in the Clone Wars. The droid served as a lookout, and showed an appreciation for the beauty of the planet. It later witnessed Mace Windu's squad take out a droid patrol squad, and reported this to AD-W4.
