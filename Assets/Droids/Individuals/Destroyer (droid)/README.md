# Destroyer (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Destroyer (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Destroyer_%28droid%29)

The Destroyer was the feared killer droid of Governor Kugg on the planet Dodz. Kugg used the droid as an enforcer to his heavy tax increases on the merchants of the planet Dodz.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Destroyer
- **Homeworld:** Dodz
- **Class:** Killer droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Armament:** Grappling arms
- **Affiliation:** Governor Kugg
