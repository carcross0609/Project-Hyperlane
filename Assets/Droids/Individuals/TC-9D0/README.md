# TC-9D0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TC-9D0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TC-9D0)

TC-9D0 was a TC-series protocol droid from the planet Coruscant with gray plating and yellow photoreceptors. He was manufactured by Cybot Galactica and was affiliated with linguists and other protocol droids. At some point by 32 BBY, sensitive information regarding the personal affairs of a politician had become stored within TC-9D0's memory circuits. A special branch of the senatorial guard placed a bounty on the droid, offering 2,500 credits for his retrieval, or 1,500 for his destruction.
