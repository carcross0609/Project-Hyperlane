# XT-9T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XT-9T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XT-9T)

XT-9T was a battle droid that served the Sith Empire at the Imperial base camp on the planet Ilum during the Battle of Ilum in the Galactic War against the Galactic Republic. It recruited Imperial operatives for Grand Moff Ilyan Regus after Darth Malgus betrayed the Empire and established his New Empire.
