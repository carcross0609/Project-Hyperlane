# Tee-Four

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tee-Four](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tee-Four)

Tee-Four was an astromech droid in the service of the Republic Defense Coalition during the High Republic Era. In 231 BBY, the droid was stationed onboard the patrol starship Moonseeker as it located a distress beacon in the Darkknell system coming from an escape pod belonging to the Galactic Republic correctional vessel Restitution. The pilot of the Moonseeker instructed Tee-Four as the droid brought the pod onboard, advising the astromech to be gentle. Once the pod was onboard, the pilot reported to the Starlight Beacon space station, before the Moonseeker droids opened up the pod, inside of which the pilot found former Restitution inmate Sestin Blin.
