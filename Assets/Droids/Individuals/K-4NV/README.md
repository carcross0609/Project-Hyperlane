# K-4NV

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K-4NV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-4NV)

K-4NV was a battle droid of the reconstituted Sith Empire. Stationed by Labor Valley Tram Station on Corellia during the Battle of Corellia of the Galactic War, K-4NV relayed to Imperial forces a transmission from Moff Jiak regarding the renegade droids of the movement Directive 7.
