# AR-72

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AR-72](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AR-72)

AR-72 was a droid in the employ of the Sith Empire during the Galactic War. AR-72 was designed to interface with the "brain" of the the Tomb, a Rakata prison complex on the planet Belsavis, so that he could bring the prison's communication systems online for the Empire. After forty-seven straight hours of being connected to the brain, AR-72 was being overwhelmed by the Rakata technology, and was "dreaming" at times. AR-72 recruited the help of Imperial forces to retrieve the key for the command console, as the Esh-kha were guarding the key, and later remotely recruited more Imperials to destroy the radiation-generating machines in the overrun machine vault.
