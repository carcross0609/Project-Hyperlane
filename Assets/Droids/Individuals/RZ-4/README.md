# RZ-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RZ-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RZ-4)

RZ-4 was a snake-like Hutt Guard Droid with a male programming. Built by Shizu the Hutt long before the Galactic Civil War, RZ-4 eventually outlived his master. When the Galactic Empire locked down the Anoat sector, the guard droid was still functional and loyal to the Hutt ideals. RZ-4 ended up joining the crew of the Smuggler, the informal leader of the sector's anti-Empire Uprising.
