# RX-7246

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RX-7246](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RX-7246)

RX-7246 was a custom-made battle droid that was acquired by the Rodian gladiator Zayda for use as his personal combat assistant. In about 17 BBY, RX-7246 and Zayda participated in a gladiatorial fight in the palace of the Hutt crime lord Darga Jiramma Mionne on the planet Cato Neimoidia, fighting against the Gamorrean Karg brothers and a group of undercover Alderaanian Resistance agents.
