# Unidentified B1 battle droid (Rishi Station)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid (Rishi Station)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_%28Rishi_Station%29)

A B1-series battle droid took part in the Defense of Rishi Station, where he was part of a reinforcement squadron sent to Rishi Station after the team of BX-series droid commandos sent there was destroyed. During the skirmish, the droids managed to drive of the remaining squadron of clone troopers stationed there, with the exception of Hevy. After a brief firefight, the clone was overpowered by the droids. However, Hevy managed to set off a tibanna bomb, killing himself and destroying the listening post. All the battle droids, including this one, were destroyed in the ensuing explosion.
