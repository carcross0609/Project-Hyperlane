# Unidentified assassin droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified assassin droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_assassin_droid)

This assassin droid was created prior to or during 29 BBY, and its model was prone to killing their makers. This droid killed one individual who was involved in the models design due to flaws in the model. This droid would later have its verbobrain destroyed by security droids, and would be placed in the personal museum of Raith Sienar, an engineer in the final years of the Galactic Republic who had attended the Rigovian Technical University alongside the student involved in the models design, as a reminder to not overstep one's competence.
