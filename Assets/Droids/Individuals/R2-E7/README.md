# R2-E7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-E7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-E7)

R2-E7 was a R2-series astromech droid. A droid merchant in Mos Espa once tried to sell this droid to Anakin Skywalker when the young Jedi visited the planet Tatooine in 21 BBY to investigate rumors of a new kind of battle droid.
