# FX-9 surgical assistant

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FX-9 surgical assistant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-9_surgical_assistant)

An FX-9 surgical assistant, also known as the FX-9 , was a FX-series medical droid manufactured by Medtech Industries. During Darth Vader's reconstruction after his duel with Obi-Wan Kenobi, a FX-9 supplied the wounded Sith Lord with a blood transfusion.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FX-9 surgical assistant
- **Manufacturer:** Medtech Industries
- **Line:** FX-series
- **Model:** FX-9
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Equipment:** Multiple arms and medical tools
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
