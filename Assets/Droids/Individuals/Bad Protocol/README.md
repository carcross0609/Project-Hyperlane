# Bad Protocol

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bad Protocol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bad_Protocol)

Bad Protocol was a Haxion Brood Bounty Droid that served the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. Once a loadlifter droid, the Haxion Brood reprogrammed the droid, who was enhanced with salvaged parts. They had yellow and silver plating, white sensors, and a built-in wrist blaster.
