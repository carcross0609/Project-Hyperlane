# Q-2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Q-2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q-2)

Q-2, also spelled as Cuetoo, was a droid that assisted the Jedi Master and investigator Emerick Caphtor during the High Republic Era. The droid accompanied him on missions and had the ability to perch on his shoulder. When Jedi Master Stellan Gios assigned Caphtor to look into the cause of the death of Master Loden Greatstorm during the Battle of Grizal, Cuetoo assisted him.
