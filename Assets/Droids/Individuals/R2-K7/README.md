# R2-K7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-K7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-K7)

R2-K7 was an R2-series astromech droid. During the Rebellion era, R2-K7 was seen as a member of the Mytaranor Slaving Council, sheepherding prisoners from their cells to auction blocks, and sharing maintenance tasks with Verpine technicians. During this time, R2-K7 showed an amount of cruelty that could have been due to a design failure or a provoked alteration, but this wickedness made R2-K7 a valued member of the Council.
