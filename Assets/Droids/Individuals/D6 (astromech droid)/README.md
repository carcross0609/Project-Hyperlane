# D6 (astromech droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D6 (astromech droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D6_%28astromech_droid%29)

D6 was an astromech droid who served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the lead-up to the Galactic Civil War. In 1 BBY, D6 was assigned to Jory of Red Flight who was deployed to assist Extraction Team Bravo in the Wobani system. During the extraction, D6 was tasked with repairing Jory's top port-side engine so that it would no longer expel smoke after Jory collided with a small asteroid while talking to Red Four.
