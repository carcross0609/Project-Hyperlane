# TX-21

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TX-21](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TX-21)

TX-21 was a T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid that served Poggle the Lesser during the Clone Wars. He was destroyed by Ahsoka Tano during the Second Battle of Geonosis.
