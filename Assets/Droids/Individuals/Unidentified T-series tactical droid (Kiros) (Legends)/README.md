# Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Kiros)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Kiros)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_T-series_tactical_droid_%28Kiros%29/Legends)

A T-series tactical droid was stationed on Kiros under the command of Zygerrian slaver Darts D'Nar. He was destroyed after a bomb on his back was activated, and subsequently Force pushed out of a window by Obi-Wan Kenobi.
