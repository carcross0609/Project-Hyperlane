# P2 (prototype astromech droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [P2 (prototype astromech droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P2_%28prototype_astromech_droid%29)

In 1 BBY, the prototype astromech droid P2 stood within the equipment-filled garage of Tosche Station on the planet Tatooine, dwarfing a nearby R5 droid who was repairing a generator. Soon after the Tantive IV, a ship of the Rebellion, battled the Galactic Empire in the planet's orbit, the droids bore witness to an attempted theft of parts from Fixer's five-engined landspeeder by a couple of Jawas, a diminutive scavenger species native to Tatooine.
