# L-X1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L-X1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L-X1)

L-X1, also referred to as Lexi, was an announcer droid at the Grand Arena on Vespaara together with Boz Vega. L-X1 was an apparent fan of the Hunter J-3DI, keeping a figurine of him on her desk, and commenting on his performance during exhibitions. L-X1 has also stated that "Trophy Chase" is one of her favorite arena games.

- **Name:** L-X1
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
