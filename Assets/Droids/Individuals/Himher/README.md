# Himher

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Himher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Himher)

Himher was a dealer droid that worked in the Black Hole cantina in Farpoint on the planet Fhost in the year 41.5 ABY. It was the dealer in a sabacc game in which Khedryn Faal, Earsh, Flaygin, and Reegas Vance were all players. Himher was named for its voice which, by some manufacturing defect, changed from that of a male to that of a female mid sentence.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Himher
- **Class:** Dealer droid
- **Gender:** Alternating male to female (manufacturing defect)
- **Pronouns:** It
- **Affiliation:** Milsin
