# R6-H5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R6-H5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R6-H5)

R6-H5, nicknamed Ar-Six, was the astromech droid of Jedi Master Kit Fisto. R6 accompanied his master on many missions during the Clone Wars aboard his Jedi starfighter.
