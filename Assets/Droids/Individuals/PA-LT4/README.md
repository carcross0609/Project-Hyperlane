# PA-LT4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PA-LT4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PA-LT4)

PA-LT4 was a white and gold plated A-LT Utility Droid that was owned by the criminal Crimson Dawn syndicate during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, it was one of a team of droids serving food in the lounge of the First Light, a yacht owned by Crimson Dawn's leader Dryden Vos. As the vessel landed on the planet Vandor, PA-LT4 had a fresh fruit assortment in the temperature-controlled serving tray atop its body and did its best to remain inconspicuous and not get underfoot. The droid was equipped with two precision engineered traction treads.
