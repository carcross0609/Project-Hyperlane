# EV-8D3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EV-8D3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EV-8D3)

EV-8D3 was an EV-series supervisor droid who was figurehead of the New Droids' Rights Movement, a group of droids that was focused on spreading violent rebellion against organic life forms throughout the galaxy.
