# Unidentified translation droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified translation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_translation_droid)

During the Clone Wars, the Hutt crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure employed a feminine vendor droid as a translator in his palace on the planet Tatooine. When Maul and the Shadow Collective attacked Jabba's Palace to force an allegiance between the Hutt Clan and the Shadow Collective, the translator droid informed Maul that Jabba had agreed to work with their criminal alliance.
