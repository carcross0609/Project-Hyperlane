# SK-89

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SK-89](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SK-89)

SK-89 (Esskay-Aytnine), known as Esskay, was a droid that operated for Kwikhaul Livery Corporation on the planet Kwenn. Formerly a tour guide, SK-89 was assigned to operate one of the taxi service's landspeeders when its owner, Fraxa, replaced all the organic drivers with droids due to lack of funds, although the droid was a general-purpose model not specialized for driving. The Jedi Masters Plo Koon and Saesee Tiin caught SK-89's speeder from the Capital Key Spaceport after arriving on Kwenn, being her first ever passengers. Rioting drivers wishing to destroy SK-89 chased down her landspeeder to the Kwikhaul complex, where they had previously destroyed many of the company's other droids. However, the two Jedi Masters managed to resolve the conflict between Kwikhaul and its workers.
