# Maya-8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maya-8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maya-8)

Maya-8 was a Maya-series holodroid who was an acrobatic dancer in the Xaxxis Axis Quartette. They were a sister of Maya-14, a fellow dancer, and Maya-12, and worked at the bar Vin-Vin's Wheelhouse on the planet Metagos.
