# JG-1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JG-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JG-1)

JG-1, also known as the Junk Giant, was a monster droid created by Taborr Val Dorn and his gang. She was later adopted by the Chadra-Fan junk keeper Marlaa Jinara to work in her junkyard. JG-1 had a special system of rechargeable power cells to energize her massive frame. One of these cells was stolen by rogue droidsmith Rek Minuu to power his custom droid Apexx.
