# R3-RN8W

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R3-RN8W](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-RN8W)

R3-RN8W was a black and rainbow-colored astromech droid of the R3-series with a transparent dome. This astromech called the Coruscant Underground its home and roamed the lower levels of Coruscant.

- **Name:** R3-RN8W
- **Homeworld:** Coruscant
