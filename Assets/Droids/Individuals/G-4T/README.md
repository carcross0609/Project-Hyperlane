# G-4T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-4T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-4T)

G-4T was a probe droid of the Sith Empire who lived during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Deployed at Imperial base camp during the Battle of Ilum G-4T recognized that a Republic camp on Ilum was a potential threat to the Empire's crystal mines and recruited an Imperial ally to poison their climate control with his coolant and destroy their forces.
