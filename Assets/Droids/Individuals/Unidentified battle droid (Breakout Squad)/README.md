# Unidentified battle droid (Breakout Squad)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified battle droid (Breakout Squad)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_battle_droid_%28Breakout_Squad%29)

A Separatist battle droid that served aboard General Grievous's battleship, the Malevolence, survived the warship's crash on the Dead Moon of Antar in 22 BBY. The droid was destroyed by the Galactic Republic's Breakout Squad.
