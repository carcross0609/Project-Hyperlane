# O.M.5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [O.M.5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/O.M.5)

O.M.5, also known as OM 5, was a B1 battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars, serving under Poggle the Lesser. They and another B1 followed Poggle in a sandstorm but was swept away by it and left for dead.
