# X10-D

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X10-D](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X10-D)

X10-D, also known as Exten-dee and X-10-D, was a red and bronze colored X10-D draft droid. The Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk used X10-D to control a variety of systems on his starship, Hound's Tooth.

- **Name:** X10-D
- **Homeworld:** Trandosha
