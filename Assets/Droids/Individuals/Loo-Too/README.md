# Loo-Too

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Loo-Too](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loo-Too)

Loo-Too was an astromech droid who served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the lead-up to the Galactic Civil War. In 1 BBY, Loo-Too was assigned to Red Four of Red Flight who was deployed to assist Extraction Team Bravo in the Wobani system.
