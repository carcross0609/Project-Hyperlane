# M-TD

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M-TD](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-TD)

M-TD, short for "miniaturized translator droid" and nicknamed Em Teedee, was a specialized translator droid used by Lowbacca while attending Luke Skywalker's Jedi Praxeum. Like almost all Wookiees, "Lowie" was physically unable to speak Galactic Basic, and so M-TD was programmed to translate his Shyriiwook to a form his friends would understand.
