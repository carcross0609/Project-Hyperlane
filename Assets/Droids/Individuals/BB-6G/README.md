# BB-6G

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BB-6G](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BB-6G)

BB-6G was an astromech droid in service of the Galactic Republic at the time of the Galactic War with the reconstituted Sith Empire. During the Conquest of Makeb, it was sent as a courier by Jedi Master Cedral Gend to help him enlist the services of the Republic strike force regarding the Seeds of Rage. The droid could be found at the Coruscant Spaceport, Carrick Station and Giant's Spear mesa on Makeb.
