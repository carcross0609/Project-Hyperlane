# R2-M3 (astromech droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-M3 (astromech droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-M3_%28astromech_droid%29)

R2-M3 was an R2-series astromech droid used to compensate for the One Liner lack of a navigation computer. He was programmed with the location of Doctor Soron Hegerty on Jatee, and led Alliance to Restore the Republic agents to the doctor to assist with the evacuation of the Ssither during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R2-M3
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Model:** R2-series astromech droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Equipment:**
  - Three wheeled legs (one retractable)
  - Retractable heavy grasper arm
  - Retractable fine grasper arm
  - Extendable .3m video sensor (360 degree rotation)
  - Video display screens
  - Holoprojector/recorder
