# L3-V5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [L3-V5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L3-V5)

L3-V5 was an astromech droid in the service of the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War. It was stationed aboard the Gav Daragon where it contacted a Republic spacer regarding the threat posed by Directive 7.
