# C1-4B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C1-4B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C1-4B)

C1-4B was a C1-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton and owned by the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu. During the First Order occupation of the planet Batuu in 34 ABY, C1-4B was displayed on a shelf of droid parts within the depot. The C-series unit had red, gray and blue paneling and a black sensor. On the left side of its dome the droid bore the logo of the Imperial Press Corps. That year, the author Eloc Throno included an image of C1-4B in the Droid Buyer's Guide section of his book, Traveler's Guide to Batuu.
