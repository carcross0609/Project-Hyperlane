# Ba-Gonk

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ba-Gonk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ba-Gonk)

Ba-Gonk was a droid that served the Haxion Brood crime syndicate as a bounty hunter during the reign of the Galactic Empire. It had white and dark blue plating and orange and cyan sensors. In combat, Ba-Gonk used a wrist blaster mounted on its right arm.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Bounty hunter
- **Name:** Ba-Gonk
- **Death:** 14 BBY
- **Line:** Haxion Brood Bounty Droid
- **Sensor:** Orange and cyan
- **Plating:** White and dark blue
- **Armament:** Wrist blaster
- **Affiliation:** Haxion Brood
