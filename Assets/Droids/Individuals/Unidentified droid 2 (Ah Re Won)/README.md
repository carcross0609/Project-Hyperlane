# Unidentified droid 2 (Ah Re Won)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid 2 (Ah Re Won)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_2_%28Ah_Re_Won%29)

A droid worked at a hotel on the Outer Rim planet Ah Re Won alongside another droid between around 40 BBY and 32 BBY. After Prince Afolonkin and hunters of his hunting party were killed by what the droids presumed to be karafankata around the planet's Sith temple, the droid helped informed the prince's father, the "Pirate King of Sector Nine," about his son's demise. Angered, the man shot the two droids in frustration, before shortly being killed himself by the Sith apprentice Darth Maul, the one who truly eliminated Afolonkin.

- **Type:** Corporate

- **Death:** Between c. 40 BBY and 32 BBY, Ah Re Won
