# Maya-14

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maya-14](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maya-14)

Maya-14 was a Maya-series holodroid who was an acrobatic dancer in the Xaxxis Axis Quartette on the planet Metagos. They were a sister of Maya-8, a fellow dancer, and Maya-12, operating in the bar Vin-Vin's Wheelhouse in the city of New Xaxxis.
