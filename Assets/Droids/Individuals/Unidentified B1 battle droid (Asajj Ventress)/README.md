# Unidentified B1 battle droid (Asajj Ventress)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid (Asajj Ventress)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_%28Asajj_Ventress%29)

A B1-Series battle droid was used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems as Asajj Ventress' bodyguard in her journey to Coruscant. At some point during 22 BBY, Asajj Ventress was sent to Coruscant while the political group Bureaucratosis was giving a speech. The droid was provided by the Separatists to accompany her.
