# BG01-1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [BG01-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BG01-1)

BG01-1 was a BG01-1 farming droid programmed for farming and security in Habo village on the planet Habo. It was capable of moving back and forth on a repulsorlift, automatically collecting weeds and monitoring the fields. It was conical in shape and had a black photoreceptor on its grey, white, and green outer shell. The top of the droid had an antenna and was able to rise from the base, revealing a second black sensor and a red sensor. It also had three retractable arms with two prongs each for plucking weeds.
