# 2684

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2684](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2684)

2684 was a B1-series battle droid that served the Confederacy of Independent Systems as part of a droid army on the planet Geonosis. In 22 BBY, the B-series battle droid was among those deployed to the Petranaki Arena to combat a Jedi assault team that had infiltrated the arena to rescue three prisoners due for execution: Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, and Padmé Amidala.
