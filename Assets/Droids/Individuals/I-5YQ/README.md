# I-5YQ

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I-5YQ](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I-5YQ)

I-5YQ (pronounced Eye-Fivewhycue), or I-Five, was a modified 5YQ-series protocol droid that had his creative dampeners disabled by information broker Lorn Pavan. Finding a kindred spirit in I-Five, Lorn made him his business partner and treated the droid as an equal.
