# R3-S1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R3-S1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-S1)

R3-S1, nicknamed "Threece," was an R3-series astromech droid with feminine programming that served as part of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. By 1 BBY, she was recognized as chief of the astromech droid pool in the rebel headquarters on the moon Yavin 4, where she was responsible for the upkeep of the base's equipment rather than being assigned to a starfighter. R3-S1 was vain and argumentative, as well as inordinately proud of her overclocked Intellex V processor.
