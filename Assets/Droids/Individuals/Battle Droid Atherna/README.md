# Battle Droid Atherna

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle Droid Atherna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Droid_Atherna)

Battle Droid Atherna was an experimental assault battle droid constructed by the Eternal Empire of Zakuul. In 3630 BBY, it was deployed to protect an outpost of the Knights of Zakuul under attack by Havoc Squad, but was destroyed by Aric Jorgan and The Commander
