# Unidentified hubdroid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified hubdroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_hubdroid)

A hubdroid led a squad aboard the Galactic Empire prison Accresker Jail. The hubdroid was sabotaged by Chelli Lona Aphra during an attack on an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate, causing it to explode, killing all the members of its squad aside from Aphra and Lopset Yas.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified hubdroid
- **Death:** During the attack on an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate
- **Model:** Hubdroid
- **Sensor:** Green
- **Plating:** Gray and red
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Chelli Lona Aphra's squad
