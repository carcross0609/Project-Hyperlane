# Unidentified RX-series pilot droid (school tram)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RX-series pilot droid (school tram)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RX-series_pilot_droid_%28school_tram%29)

During the New Republic Era, a droid was an RX-series pilot droid with red sensors and gray and red plating served as a tram-operator on the isolated planet At Attin, where he piloted a school tram bound for At Attin Middle School Gamma. Receiving little to no respect from his minor passengers, the droid was vulnerable to bullying, such as having gum stuck to the back of his head by unruly younglings.
