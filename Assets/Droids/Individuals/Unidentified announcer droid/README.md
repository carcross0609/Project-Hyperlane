# Unidentified announcer droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified announcer droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_announcer_droid)

A hovering announcer droid announced the arrival of the Abominor Great Heep to several droids captured by his forces on Biitu, an agriworld in the Cassander sector, in 15 BBY. After one of the captured droids, the protocol droid C-3PO, inquired the name of his new master, the announcer droid used its loudspeakers to loudly repeat "Heep." The droid had black photoreceptors and two loudspeakers attached to its black-and-grey spherical body by orange connectors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified announcer droid
- **Plating:** Black, grey, and orange
