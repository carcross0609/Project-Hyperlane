# Dee-Twelve

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dee-Twelve](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dee-Twelve)

Dee-Twelve was a droid owned by the family of Jerlyn, a resident of Peka village on the world Batuu. Around 379 BBY, while working on the family's farm at night, Dee-Twelve was taken by a salmaca that was raiding Peka and brought to its nest. The next day, Jedi Padawan Rooper Nitani arrived in Peka, and after speaking with Jerlyn, agreed to recover Dee-Twelve. After traveling to the salmaca's cave, she successfully rescued Dee-Twelve and returned him to Jerlyn.
