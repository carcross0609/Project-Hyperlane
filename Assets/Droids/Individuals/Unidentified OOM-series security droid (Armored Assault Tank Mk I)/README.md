# Unidentified OOM-series security droid (Armored Assault Tank Mk I)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM-series security droid (Armored Assault Tank Mk I)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM-series_security_droid_%28Armored_Assault_Tank_Mk_I%29)

A OOM-series security droid was a member of a Separatist crew that operated on the planet Loradil during the New Republic Era after their Providence-class carrier was shot down. Around 6 ABY, the OOM-series security droid ordered for another security droid to locate the human Jedi Luke Skywalker under their Armored Assault Tank, only to be destroyed when Skywalker used the Force to send the droid into them moments later.
