# HM-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HM-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HM-7)

HM-7 was pack droid owned by the human farmer Roog on the planet Omaka. HM-7 was quadrupedal with an octagonal bin on his back. He spoke binary and had a yellow sensor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HM-7
- **Homeworld:** Omaka
- **Class:** Pack droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Gray and green
