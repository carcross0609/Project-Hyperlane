# Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Devaron)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Devaron)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_%28Devaron%29/Legends)

This OOM command battle droid was present on Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate during the Battle of Devaron. He linked all ships functions to Bane's wrist gauntlet. When the clones and Jedi arrived on the bridge, the droid panicked and claimed that the battle droid next to him was the commander. After that droid was shot down, the clones shot the commander.
