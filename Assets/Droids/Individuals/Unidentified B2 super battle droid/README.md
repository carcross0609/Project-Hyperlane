# Unidentified B2 super battle droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B2 super battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B2_super_battle_droid)

A B2 super battle droid served in the droid army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. The B2-series super battle droid, among many others, were dispatched in an army commanded by the Sith apprentice Asajj Ventress. Ventress was accompanied by the army on the moon Rugosa, where she hoped to convince King Katuunko of Toydaria to side with the Confederacy rather than the Galactic Republic, who themselves had sent forces led by Jedi Master Yoda.
