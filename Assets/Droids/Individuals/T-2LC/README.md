# T-2LC

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-2LC](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-2LC)

T-2LC also known as "Elsie" was a protocol droid that served as a nanny for Han Solo and Leia Organa around the time of the Battle of Jakku. Senator Tolwar Wartol, a rival of Chancellor Mon Mothma, planted a listening device on T-2LC in order to gain an advantage over his political rival.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T-2LC
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
