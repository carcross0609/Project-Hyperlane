# Unidentified protocol droid (Accresker Jail)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified protocol droid (Accresker Jail)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_protocol_droid_%28Accresker_Jail%29)

A protocol droid served the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War against the Galactic Empire. The protocol droid was aboard a Rebel Alliance frigate when it came under attack by the Imperial wreckage-prison Accresker Jail and Accresker Penal Legion, its attack force composed of prisoners. The protocol droid, alongside an astromech droid, attempted to hide in an escape pod, which landed back in the wreckage. Chelli Aphra, a prisoner aboard Accresker, came across the escape pod and destroyed the droids.
