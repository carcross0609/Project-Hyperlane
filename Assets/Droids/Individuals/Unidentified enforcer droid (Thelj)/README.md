# Unidentified enforcer droid (Thelj)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified enforcer droid (Thelj)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_enforcer_droid_%28Thelj%29)

An enforcer droid monitored the Graf family compound's sentry point. It was very shiny and had plenty of panels. In 382 BBY, the droid granted Path of the Open Hand members Yana Ro, Kor Plouth and Treze access to the residence after the Rite of Hospitality was invoked.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified enforcer droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Affiliation:** Graf family
