# 397-10B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [397-10B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/397-10B)

397-10B was a cleaning droid inside Besadii casino's underground facility on Katalla. During Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano's mission to destroy the casino vault, 397-10B was alerted to their presence when he found an unexplained piece of debris in one of the tunnels of the facility.
