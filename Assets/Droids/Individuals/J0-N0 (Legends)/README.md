# J0-N0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [J0-N0/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J0-N0/Legends)

J0-N0 was an 8D smelter droid who worked for Cad Bane during the Clone Wars. J0-N0 hacked into C-3PO and R2-D2's memories so he could obtain the blue prints for the Senate Building for Bane's plan to invade the senate. After they successfully obtained the blueprints, Bane wiped the two droids' memories and dumped them back on the streets of the lower market districts.
