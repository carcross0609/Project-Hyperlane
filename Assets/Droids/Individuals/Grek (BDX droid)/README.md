# Grek (BDX droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grek (BDX droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grek_%28BDX_droid%29)

Grek was a BDX droid who was active at Savi & Son Salvage in Black Spire Outpost on the Outer Rim planet Batuu by 34 ABY. The diminutive biped was shy, but with a keen attention to detail, which made her effective at cataloguing. She joined her team of droids later than her counterparts. Grek was also trained by Resistance operative Vi Moradi to assist her in covertly gathering First Order intelligence.
