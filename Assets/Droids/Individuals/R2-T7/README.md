# R2-T7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-T7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-T7)

R2-T7 was the R2-series astromech droid that served onboard Red Five Grizz Frix's X-wing starfighter during the Battle of Endor, in which the droid was destroyed along with Frix and his fighter.
