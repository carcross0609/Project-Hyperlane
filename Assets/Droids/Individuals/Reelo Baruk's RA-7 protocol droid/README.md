# Reelo Baruk's RA-7 protocol droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reelo Baruk's RA-7 protocol droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reelo_Baruk%27s_RA-7_protocol_droid)

Reelo Baruk, a Rodian gangster who operated on Nar Shaddaa, owned a masculine RA-7 protocol droid. While searching for Baruk, Kyle Katarn stumbled upon the droid, who assured him that he was not evil, just his master. The droid also told Katarn to watch out for Baruk and asked not to be deactivated.
