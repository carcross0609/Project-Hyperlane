# Flirt

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flirt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flirt)

Flirt was Chenlambec's personal handheld translator. Externally, she looked like a small decorative silver cube, but she was actually a positronic processor, too small to actually be considered a droid. She was a one-of-a-kind illegal creation, designed and built by a friend of Chenlambec, who had been slain. Flirt was programmed to infiltrate and seduce computer systems into cooperating with her and was able to open data streams, override commands with those from her master, and completely shut down security systems if she was able to seduce the computer she was accessing. More importantly, she was able to do this without having to jack into an information socket.

## Specifications
