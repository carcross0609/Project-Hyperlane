# K-4D8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [K-4D8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-4D8)

K-4D8 was an enhanced, masculine KX-series security droid who served Director Orson Callan Krennic of the Galactic Empire. Around 18 BBY, K-4D8 was dispatched to the planet Agaris to serve Imperial Governor Wilhuff Tarkin and oversee a transportation of quadanium steel to Sentinel Base.
