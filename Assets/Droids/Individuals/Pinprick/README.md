# Pinprick

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pinprick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pinprick)

"Pinprick" was the nickname the Rebels of the Anoat sector gave to an IT-O interrogator droid they salvaged from an inexplicable wrecked starship. It could not speak, its memories were encoded, and strange things would happen wherever it went.

## Specifications

- **Name:** "Pinprick"
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Department of Military Research
- **Model:** IT-O Interrogation Unit
- **Class:** Interrogator droid
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire (Formerly)
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
