# Arges (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arges (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arges_%28droid%29)

Arges was an enforcer of the Droid Gotra who served as a guard for The Twins alongside Bullethead during or after 11 ABY. He was gravely damaged by Grogu and The Mandalorian. Arges was destroyed for good by Mando after the Twins' deaths.
