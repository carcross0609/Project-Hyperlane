# Unidentified air taxi driver droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified air taxi driver droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_air_taxi_driver_droid)

A droid worked as an air taxi driver on the planet Alderaan during the time of peace negotiations between the Galactic Republic and reconstituted Sith Empire on the planet in 3653 BBY. It wore a cloth hat, aiming to look more Human by donning it, and also had an integrated card scanner in its hand. The droid drove rogue Jedi Knight Aryn Leneer to the Eeseen spaceport while she was on her way to avenge the death of her Jedi Master, Ven Zallow. Though it tried to initiate its social programming and make small talk, Leneer told the droid that she wanted to ride in silence. The droid sped up the air taxi as they moved into a higher altitude used for commercial travel. Once they arrived at the spaceport, the droid held out its integrated card scanner so she could pay. The Jedi quickly used her credcard before departing.
