# PK-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PK-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PK-7)

PK7 was a PK-series worker droid stationed on the planet Gavos and was part of the New Droids' Rights Movement, a group of droids that was focused on spreading violent rebellion against organic life forms throughout the galaxy.
