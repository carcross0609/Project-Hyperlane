# IT-O (Death Star)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IT-O (Death Star)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IT-O_%28Death_Star%29)

IT-O, also known as Eyetee-Oh, was an IT-O interrogation droid in service to the Galactic Empire. The droid was employed as an interrogator for prisoners held aboard the first Death Star. IT-O was equipped with probes and needles, including a hypo that administered the truth serum Bavo Six. The droid would perform "surgery" on prisoners, and its sensors were capable of analyzing and determining the pain threshold of its subject and the truthfulness of their answers. IT-O was responsible for the interrogation of Princess Leia Organa after her capture from the Tantive IV.
