# AC-38

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AC-38/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AC-38/Legends)

AC-38, nicknamed Ace or Ace-38, was an AC series pilot droid that worked for Star Tours as a pilot sometime between 1 BBY and 0 BBY. During one flight, Ace was the intended pilot and was supposed to fly passengers in a StarSpeeder 1000.
