# R2-A5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-A5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-A5)

R2-A5, also known as Ayfive, was a R2-series astromech droid with green and white plating and masculine programming. In 1 BBY, it spent time causing trouble in the spaceport of Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine.
