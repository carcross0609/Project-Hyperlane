# R4-H5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-H5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-H5)

R4-H5 was an astromech droid utilized by the Nautolan Jedi Master Kit Fisto at the First Battle of Geonosis in 22 BBY. Since that battle marked the outbreak of the Clone Wars, Fisto was assigned a new droid, R6-H5, by the Jedi Order.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R4-H5
- **Class:** Astromech droid
