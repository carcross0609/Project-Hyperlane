# R2-W3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-W3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-W3)

R2-W3 an R2-series astromech droid owned by the company Renna's Transport Service during the waning years of the Galactic Republic. Circa 21 BBY, R2-W3 was assigned to pilot the YT-1300 light freighter Second Breath on an assignment to ferry a cargo of explosives from the planet Cularin to a buyer on the world of Druckenwell in the Doldur sector.
