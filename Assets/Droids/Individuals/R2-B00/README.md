# R2-B00

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-B00](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-B00)

R2-B00 was an R2-series astromech droid that was a space pirate, and could be found on the planet Takodana. The droid was painted with a phosphorescent colorant, and included a Burtt acoustic signaller.
