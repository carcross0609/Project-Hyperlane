# D-2B8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D-2B8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-2B8)

D-2B8 was an astromech droid in service of the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War. A counterpart of AG-11 who was fond of him, D-2B8 was stationed aboard the frigate Toron Voq when the ship was attacked by the Sith warship Red Reaper in the Rhinnal system and was destroyed along with the droid.

- **Death:** c. 3641—3640 BBY, Toron Voq
- **Class:** Astromech droid
