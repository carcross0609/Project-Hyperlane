# RD-RR

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RD-RR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RD-RR)

RD-RR was an astromech droid that resembled an R-5 series unit and was red in color. The droid's batteries ran down before it was able to complete a mission, while its builder put the droid in a training exercise. The astromech remained stuck in the lower levels in a cave of the Tosche Station. RD-RR also told jokes.
