# RA-7-81

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RA-7-81](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RA-7-81)

RA-7-81 was an RA-7 protocol droid who was stationed on board one of the Galactic Empire's science vessels. After the vessel journeyed from Imperial Station 003 above the planet Coruscant to Tantiss Base on the planet Wayland during or after 18 BBY, RA-7-81 discovered the stowaway clone Echo in the vessel's cargo hold disguised as a TK stormtrooper. When the protocol droid was not convinced by Echo's claim that he had been checking the manifest, the clone shot and destroyed the droid, taking its hand to hide his cybernetic scomp link.
