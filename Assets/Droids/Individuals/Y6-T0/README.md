# Y6-T0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Y6-T0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Y6-T0)

Y6-T0 was an astromech droid who served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The droid was stationed aboard a rebel flight school and during General Hera Syndulla's training of a pilot, Syndulla took remote control of the X-wing starfighter X-332 via Y6-T0.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Y6-T0
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Rebel Alliance Navy
