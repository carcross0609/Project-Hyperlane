# Unidentified WA-2G droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified WA-2G droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_WA-2G_droid)

During the Republic Era, a WA-2G droid worked for the Pijali monarchy on the Inner Rim planet Pijal in the world's royal palace. In 40 BBY, the attendant droid helped Pijal's Jedi lord regent, Rael Averross, prepare for the upcoming coronation of the Pijali Princess Fanry. The WA-2G droid cut Averross's hair, shaved his face, and helped the man dress in a set of fancy Pijali court tunics. When the regent inquired whether he looked crazy or great, the droid implied he embodied both, which made Averross laugh.
