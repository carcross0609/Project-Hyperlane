# Advanced Jedi Training Droid 6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Advanced Jedi Training Droid 6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Jedi_Training_Droid_6)

Advanced Jedi Training Droid 6 was a training droid used to referee sparring matches between Jedi younglings. It was in use around 45 BBY. The Advanced Jedi Training Droid model had a number of sensors that allowed it to record not only the actions of the two combatants, but also their physiological state, such as body temperature and heart rate.
