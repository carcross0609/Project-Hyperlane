# KT-310

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KT-310](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KT-310)

KT-310 was a red-plated, cylindrical KT power droid that lived on the desert planet Jakku. Around thirty years after the Battle of Endor, it was present in the junk dealer Unkar Plutt's shop in Niima Outpost on Jakku shortly on the First Order's attack on the nearby Tuanul village. While the droid stood behind the booth Plutt worked in the scavenger Rey brought him several pieces of scrap worth one quarter portion of foodstuff. The droid moved away before the next in line, the pet trader Bobbajo, moved up to the booth. The next day, KT-310 was still in Niima and witnessed Rey knock down the First Order deserter Finn with her quarterstaff inside one of the outpost's structures. The First Order then attacked Niima shortly after.
