# Unidentified IG-86 sentinel droid (Cad Bane)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified IG-86 sentinel droid (Cad Bane)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_IG-86_sentinel_droid_%28Cad_Bane%29)

An IG-86 sentinel droid participated in the Senate hostage crisis. It was part of Cad Bane's crew when they went to break Ziro Desilijic Tiure out of prison. The droid was destroyed during the Senate hostage crisis that followed, as an unarmed Anakin Skywalker destroyed the droid, whose body was found by fellow bounty hunter Shahan Alama.
