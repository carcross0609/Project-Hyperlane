# JN-47

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JN-47](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JN-47)

JN-47 was a bipedal droid who, between 232 and 230 BBY, damaged his vocabulator beyond repair. JN-47 then went to Dotti's Droid Workshop in Kublop Springs on the planet Tenoo for maintenance. Although JN-47's model was considered rare, making it challenging to find replacement parts, Dotti managed to find him a new vocabulator. As Dotti walked three Jedi Initiates through the repair, a pair of spider droids owned by the droidsmith Rek Minuu stole the part. The Jedi younglings pursued the spider droids back to Minuu's starship, where they recovered the stolen vocabulator.
