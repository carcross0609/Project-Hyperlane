# TC-70

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TC-70/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TC-70/Legends)

TC-70 was Jabba Desilijic Tiure's protocol droid during the time of the Clone Wars. When Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi went to negotiate with the Hutt upon Rotta's kidnapping, TC-70 helped to translate his master and the Jedi's conversation. The droid stated that Jabba wanted his son back and the kidnappers found either dead or alive. He also received Anakin Skywalker as he entered Jabba's palace in search of his Padawan, he asked for the Jedi's lightsaber, which he then recovered.
