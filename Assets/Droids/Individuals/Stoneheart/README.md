# Stoneheart

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stoneheart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stoneheart)

Stoneheart was an artificial intelligence computer that was used by the Galactic Republic. It was installed on an asteroid and designed to analyze enemy tactics and counter them. Eventually, Stoneheart outlived its usefulness and was shut down.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stoneheart
- **Class:** AI computer
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
