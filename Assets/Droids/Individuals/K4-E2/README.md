# K4-E2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K4-E2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K4-E2)

K4-E2 was a model of protocol droid during the Galactic War. It was sent to the orbital station on Makeb as a courier by Moff Varnus Olec to help him enlist the services of the Imperial strike team regarding the build up of mercenary forces on the planet of Darvannis.
