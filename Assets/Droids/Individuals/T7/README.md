# T7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T7)

T7 was a copper-plated protocol droid who worked for the Weequay Jarl by 22 BBY. When the Mirialan Servit revealed to Jarl that he had a valuable EX droid that year, T7 helped Jarl hunt him down to try and acquire it, ending up at Dex's Diner. During the ensuing fight there, the owner, Dexter Jettster, threw brakkenback stew at T7 and got it in his inner workings, knocking the droid out of commission for the rest of the skirmish.
