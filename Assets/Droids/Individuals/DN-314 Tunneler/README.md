# DN-314 Tunneler

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DN-314 Tunneler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DN-314_Tunneler)

DN-314 Tunneler was a mining droid repurposed for defense by the Advozse Hegemony on Hammer Station during the Cold War. The droid used a targeted laser during combat and could also summon demolition drones to aid it. In 3643 BBY, it was destroyed by the strike team sent to secure the station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DN-314 Tunneler
- **Death:** 3643 BBY, Hammer Station
- **Class:** Mining droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** Advozse Hegemony
