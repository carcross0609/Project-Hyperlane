# S-5V3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [S-5V3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-5V3)

S-5V3 was a protocol droid that served the Alliance to Restore the Republic. The droid had blue plating with a gold Alliance starbird adorned on its torso. After the Rebel Alliance fleet split following the Battle of Hoth, S-5V3 was aboard the flagship Home One. The droid summoned pilot Wedge Antilles for a meeting with the Contessa about rebuilding his squadron.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S-5V3
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Plating:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
