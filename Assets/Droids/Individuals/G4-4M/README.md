# G4-4M

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [G4-4M](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G4-4M)

G4-4M was an astromech droid who served the Resistance on the planet Ajan Kloss during the First Order-Resistance War. The droid was one of five astromechs who worked under R5-2JE and who were all absent from their jobs at the landing pad one day. G4-4M was busy making a fanfic instead of doing R5-2JE's work.
