# MD-2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-2)

MD-2 was an MD-2 medical droid assigned to the Imperial Navy EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate. Its service carried over once the ship's crew mutined and took over control of the vessel. MD-2 served as the medical bay's anesthesia/life monitor droid and occasionally assisted MD-1 in the medical lab and MD-4 during surgical procedures.
