# Heep Splitling

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heep Splitling](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heep_Splitling)

The Heep Splitling was a droid. Between 10 BBY and 3 ABY, the droid was one of the participants in matches being held at Grakkus Arena, a gladitorial arena owned by the Hutt Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi on the moon Nar Shaddaa. Grakkus was presented with a draft advertisement to approve for the event that listed a fight between Heep Splitling and B1 Beatdown as the cycle's undercard match.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Hutt Cartel
- **Name:** Heep Splitling
- **Affiliation:** Grakkus Arena
