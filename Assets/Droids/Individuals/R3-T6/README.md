# R3-T6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R3-T6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-T6)

R3-T6 was an R3-series astromech droid that served the Galactic Empire and had astronavigational duties on board the first Death Star. During the Battle of Yavin, the astromech droid was on board the Death Star when the battle station was attacked by the Alliance to Restore the Republic.
