# TQ3-R1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TQ3-R1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TQ3-R1)

TQ3-R1 was a droid with silver and yellow plating and a gray sensor. It had two legs, a cylindrical body, and a dome-shaped head. In 35 ABY, the droid was on the planet Kijimi in the Thieves' Quarter workshop of the droidsmith Babu Frik, a member of the Spice Runners of Kijimi.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TQ3-R1
- **Sensor:** Gray
- **Plating:** Yellow and silver
