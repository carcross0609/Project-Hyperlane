# D4-N7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D4-N7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D4-N7)

D4-N7 was a droid who lived on the planet Tatooine during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. On Tatooine he worked in the Stillsand Waves area of the Dune Sea.
