# R8-H23

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R8-H23](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R8-H23)

R8-H23 was an R8 series droid astromech droid whose main task was to spread holiday cheer and treats to all. He was colored in red, white, and green, and wore a festive wreath with a gold bow.

- **Line:** R8 series droid
