# Unidentified XLT-014 labor droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified XLT-014 labor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_XLT-014_labor_droid)

A XLT-014 labor droid was working on the cruise ship Galactic Breeze during the last years of the Galactic Republic. The droid was reprogrammed to enter information into the ship's computer in order for the ship to go to the wrong location. When the Galactic Breeze reached the location, which was near the Inner Rim planet Bestine IV, a group of pirates hijacked the ship.
