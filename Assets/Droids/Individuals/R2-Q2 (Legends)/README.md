# R2-Q2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-Q2/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-Q2/Legends)

R2-Q2 (also spelled Artoo Kyootoo) was an astromech droid of the R2-series, known for expertise in computer uplinking, serving in the Galactic Empire. R2-Q2 spent several decades serving with an Imperial reconnaissance fleet in the Expansion Region aboard the Star Destroyer Devastator. He had one of the most extensive maps and list of systems inside of his databanks.
