# Fixer (mouse droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fixer (mouse droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fixer_%28mouse_droid%29)

Fixer was an MSE-6 series repair droid who lived on the junk planet of Zoh. On Zoh, Fixer and other droids were led by Jek-14. Shorty after the Freemakers visited Zoh seeking Jek-14's help, a ungrateful droid named N-3RO led a droid uprising against Jek-14 and the Freemakers. N-3RO succeeded to convince his fellow droids, including Fixer that humans can not be trusted. They failed and N-3RO was still on Zoh and had been fitted for a restraining bolt.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fixer
- **Homeworld:** Zoh
- **Model:** MSE-6 series repair droid
- **Affiliation:** Jek-14
