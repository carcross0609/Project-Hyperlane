# OOM-10

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [OOM-10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OOM-10)

OOM-10 was a B1-series battle droid who was reprogrammed and used by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. During the mission to the planet Lola Sayu, OOM-10 was second-in-command of R2-D2's battle droid squadron.
