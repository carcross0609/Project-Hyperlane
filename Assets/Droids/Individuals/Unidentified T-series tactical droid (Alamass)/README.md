# Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Alamass)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Alamass)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_T-series_tactical_droid_%28Alamass%29)

According to legend, a T-series tactical droid served the Confederacy of Independent Systems aboard General Grievous' flagship in 22 BBY. Growing weary of the general's disregard for droids, the tactical droid deliberately altered the orders before the Battle of Alamass. The new strategy ultimately resulted in a loss for the army, but it significantly cut down on casualties. Grievous stormed the bridge in response and destroyed the tactical droid with his lightsabers. Word spread of the droid's actions, eventually honored by the Galactic Republic.
