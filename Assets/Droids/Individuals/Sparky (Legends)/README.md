# Sparky

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sparky/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sparky/Legends)

"Sparky" was the nickname given to a BX-series droid commando captain by clone trooper Hevy who led a team of other BX-series droid commandos in the battle of the Rishi moon. Unlike his other commandos, Sparky carried a vibrosword and bore a white symbol on his forehead.
