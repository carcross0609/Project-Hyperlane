# O-R10N

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [O-R10N](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/O-R10N)

O-R10N was a blue astromech droid who served as Jessika Pava's astromech in the Resistance. During Black Squadron's mission to Megalox, O-R10N assisted in Operation Upside alongside BB-8, R2-HA, and R4 in an attempt to free Grakkus the Hutt until it was destroyed by an unidentified droid.
