# 0G-LC

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [0G-LC](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/0G-LC)

0G-LC, often simply referred to as 0G, was a droid who piloted the Star Seeker during the High Republic Era. He explored the Outer Rim Territories for the Jedi Order, but after his ship was damaged in an asteroid field, he crash-landed on a planet while on his way to the planet Tenoo. He eventually fixed the ship but was too frightened to pilot it again, opting to remain stranded.
