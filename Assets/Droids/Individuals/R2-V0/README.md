# R2-V0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-V0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-V0)

R2-V0 was a R2-series astromech droid with purple adornments. R2-V0 was owned by the Ithorian Ixsthmus, a captain of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. The droid served the Green squad of the Reekeene's Roughnecks, a team of the Alliance, and was stationed aboard Ixsthmus's ship, the Long Shot.
