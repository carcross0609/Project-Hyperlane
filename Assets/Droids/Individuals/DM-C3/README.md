# DM/C3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DM/C3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DM/C3)

DM/C3, operating under the codename "Dagger," was a protocol droid who served the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. He was programmed by the Alliance to go undercover with the Galactic Empire, operating as the personal decoding droid of Moff Vanko.
