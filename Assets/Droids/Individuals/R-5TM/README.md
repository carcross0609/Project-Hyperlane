# R-5TM

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-5TM](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-5TM)

R-5TM was a battle droid who served the reconstituted Sith Empire on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. He and B-6NX were sent on a joint mission, but R-5TM got damaged and B-6NX, who possessed experimental emotional programming, tried to help him instead of focusing on the mission. B-6NX asked a passing Imperial hero to retrieve necessary parts to fix R-5TM, while the droid itself refused help and insisted that B-6NX's compassion was a threat to the mission and had to be removed.
