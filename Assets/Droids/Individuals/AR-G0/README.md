# AR-G0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AR-G0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AR-G0)

AR-G0 was a class three droid owned by the smuggler Beryl Thorne operating out of Taris during the Cold War. Thorne named AR-G0 the second in command of her operation on Taris after suffering the losses of many contacts to rakghouls.

- **Class:** Class three droid
