# Vonogarusa-K

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vonogarusa-K](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vonogarusa-K)

Vonogarusa-K was a Gree research droid dispatched by the starship Gray Secant to study the planet Coruscant. It was accompanied by a squad of more heavily-armed Gree droids, and during the Cold War it stopped transmitting to avoid detection by new Gree droids nearby.
