# MD-CH1 Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-CH1 Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-CH1_Droid)

The MD-CH1 Droid was a model of small bipedal droid available during the Third Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. The MD-CH1 Droid was associated with the Sith Empire, and more specifically Darth Malgus.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Sith Empire
- **Name:** MD-CH1 Droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:**
  - Silver
  - Black
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
