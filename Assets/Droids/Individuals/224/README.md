# 224

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [224](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/224)

224 was an OOM command battle droid who served in the Separatist Droid Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Shortly after being promoted, the droid was deployed to the moon Rugosa under the command of Separatist Supreme Leader Asajj Ventress, where they led her army in an attempt to stop Jedi Grand Master Yoda from meeting with King Katuunko of Toydaria. After failing numerous attempts to kill Yoda, 224 and their forces were met with the Jedi Master, who came out of hiding to sit calmly in front of 224's Armored Assault Tank. The Jedi's ruse was not anticipated by the Separatist battle droids, however, as after 224 contacted Asajj Ventress for clarification, the commander gave an order to fire upon Yoda which was delayed by his units, leading to a clone trooper ambush of the Confederate forces and Yoda's dismantling of 224.
