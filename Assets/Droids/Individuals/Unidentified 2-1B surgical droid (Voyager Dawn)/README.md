# Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Voyager Dawn)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Voyager Dawn)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_2-1B_surgical_droid_%28Voyager_Dawn%29)

A medical droid was on the Voyager Dawn between 12 BBY and 9 BBY. The medical droid was of the 2-1B surgical droid model and was taking care of its master, Jedi Elan Rast. As the droid examined the Jedi, they were attacked by the Galactic Empire and The Grand Inquisitor. He decapitated the droid, before interrogating Elan, ultimately killing the Jedi.

- **Name:** Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid

- **Model:** 2-1B surgical droid
