# LA-R1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LA-R1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LA-R1)

LA-R1 was an astromech droid that was assigned to Fedbord during the Imperial Era. LA-R1 was abducted by Jawas in the city of Mos Gofti on the desert planet of Tatooine. He was rescued by Obi-Wan Kenobi from a Jawa sandcrawler.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LA-R1
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Plating:** Red
- **Equipment:**
  - Manipulator arm
  - Air-density sensors (Removed by Jawas)
