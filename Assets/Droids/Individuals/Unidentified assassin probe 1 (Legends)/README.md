# Unidentified assassin probe 1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified assassin probe 1/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_assassin_probe_1/Legends)

An assassin probe was smuggled onto the Coronet during its voyage to Coruscant, to assassinate Duchess Satine Kryze. It was the first to be defeated by Jedi General Anakin Skywalker and his unit of clone troopers.
