# ET-2T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [ET-2T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ET-2T)

ET-2T, pronounced Eetee Tootee, was a droid, or colony of four interconnected droids, active sometime between 8 ABY and 34 ABY. They were present in Hanna City, located on the planet Chandrila, specifically around the time of the annual Hanna City Flight Academy end-of-year family race.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ET-2T
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Gray and white
- **Equipment:**
  - Connector tubes
  - Elongated arms
  - Smoke emitters
