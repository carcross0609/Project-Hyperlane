# DM-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DM-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DM-7)

DM-7 was a droid whom the Toydarian junk dealer Watto hired to kill the Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn following the conclusion of the Boonta Eve Podrace on the planet Tatooine in 32 BBY. Along with four other hirelings, DM-7 ambushed Jinn in a deserted Mos Espa backstreet, but the Jedi Master roundly defeated his assailants, leaving the droid a shattered ruin.

- **Death:** 32 BBY; Mos Espa, Tatooine
