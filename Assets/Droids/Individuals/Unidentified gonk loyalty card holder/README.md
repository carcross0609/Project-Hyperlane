# Unidentified gonk loyalty card holder

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified gonk loyalty card holder](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_gonk_loyalty_card_holder)

A customer at the store Gonk-Stores Paradise signed up for a gonk loyalty card at some point after 1 BBY. The card recorded gonk stamps, which they could accumulate to get rewards, earned at the store Gonk-Stores Paradise. The store was located in Mos Eisley, a settlement on the planet Tatooine, and run by Gonk-Stores Paradise Trading Inc. The customer informed an individual at the store that they only needed two more gonk stamps before they received a free gonk-sicle. They were excited, but unsure what a gonk-sicle was.
