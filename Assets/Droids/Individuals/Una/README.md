# Una

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Una](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Una)

Una was a nurse droid at Reena University. Dean Ry Harket monitored the purported "symptoms" her student Axel Greylark was exhibiting of malongo pox, correctly—albeit without proof—assuming that he was faking it. She had an attending Una, repeatedly check his temperature in an attempt to prove his deception, though she never succeeded. During this process, Greylark hyperbolically urged her to save herself; Una aptly responded that aquatic beings such as Harket were immune to the disease he purportedly had.
