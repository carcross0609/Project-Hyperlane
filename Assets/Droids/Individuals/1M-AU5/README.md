# 1M-AU5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [1M-AU5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1M-AU5)

1M-AU5 was the designation of a golden MSE-6 series repair droid that was active during the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. As a MSE-series repair droid, it was produced by the company Rebaxan Columni, and like all units of the MSE-6 it stood twenty-five centimeters tall.
