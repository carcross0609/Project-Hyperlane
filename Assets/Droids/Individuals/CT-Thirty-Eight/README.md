# CT-Thirty-Eight

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CT-Thirty-Eight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CT-Thirty-Eight)

CT-Thirty-Eight was a droid that served onboard the Ataraxia while it was owned by the Twi'lek Lourna Dee during the High Republic Era. When Jedi Master Keeve Trennis boarded the Ataraxia by cutting through its hull with her lightsaber, CT-Thirty-Eight contacted the ship's flight deck to report the breach and activated energy fields to seal it. After she informed the flight deck that the breach had been sealed, she was destroyed by Trennis. Lourna Dee then attempted to contact CT-Thirty-Eight, but Trennis responded instead and told Dee of the droid's destruction.
