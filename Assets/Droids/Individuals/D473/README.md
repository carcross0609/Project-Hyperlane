# D473

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D473](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D473)

D473 was a droid who worked at Terpsichore Station, a mining facility on the planet Parnassos that belonged to the Con Star Mining Corporation. His task was to assign adequate tasks to new laborers according to their skills, which were determined in tests.
