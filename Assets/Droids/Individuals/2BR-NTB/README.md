# 2BR-NTB

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2BR-NTB](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2BR-NTB)

2BR-NTB, known as 2B, was a BB-series astromech droid who served the Resistance spy Norath Kev. 2B accompanied Kev to the planet Varkana, where he was discovered by the bounty hunter Ax Tagrin, working for the First Order. Tagrin shot 2B, capturing Kev. The droid had a distinct green and red paint scheme and sensors attuned to impending danger.
