# S3-R9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [S3-R9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S3-R9)

S3-R9 was an R-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton and owned by the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu. During the First Order's occupation of Batuu in 34 ABY, S3-R9 was displayed on a shelf of droid parts within the depot. It had silver and white paneling and a black sensor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S3-R9
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Silver and white
- **Affiliation:** Droid Depot
