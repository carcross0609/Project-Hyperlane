# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Rynn Zenat)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Rynn Zenat)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Rynn_Zenat%29)

A B1-series battle droid was a member of a Separatist crew that operated on the planet Loradil during the New Republic Era after their Providence-class carrier was shot down. Around 6 ABY, the B1-series battle droid encountered the humans Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, Beilert Valance and Rynn Zenat on the planet and fought them.
