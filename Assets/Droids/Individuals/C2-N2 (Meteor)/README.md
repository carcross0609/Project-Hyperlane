# C2-N2 (Meteor)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C2-N2 (Meteor)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C2-N2_%28Meteor%29)

C2-N2, also known as Seetoo Enntoo, was a Hollis-series steward droid owned by Meteor, Havoc Squad's commanding officer. Assigned to Meteor's BT-7 Thunderclap as its steward in 3643 BBY, C2-N2 was responsible for maintaining the starship and ensuring that the squad remained comfortable.
