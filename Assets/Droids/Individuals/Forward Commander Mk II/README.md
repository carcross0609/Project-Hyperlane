# Forward Commander Mk II

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Forward Commander Mk II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forward_Commander_Mk_II)

The Forward Commander Mk II was a droid produced on the planet Mustafar by the rogue assassin droid HK-47 as part of his droid army. Its design was based on that of HK-47.

- **Name:** Forward Commander Mk II
- **Homeworld:** Mustafar
- **Death:** 1.5 ABY, Mustafar
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** HK-47
