# 765

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [765](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/765)

765 was a B1-series battle droid who celebrated his retirement party aboard the Invisible Hand during the Battle of Coruscant. However, 765 and their companions were destroyed by Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi crashing their Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptors on the Invisible Hand's hangar deck.
