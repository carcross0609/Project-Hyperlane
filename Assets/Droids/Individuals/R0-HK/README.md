# R0-HK

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [R0-HK](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R0-HK)

R0-HK was a B1-series battle droid who fought alongside fellow B1 battle droid R0-GR in the Battle of Naboo. Years later, he was put on display at the Emperor Palpatine Museum.
