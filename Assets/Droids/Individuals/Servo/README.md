# Servo

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Servo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Servo)

Servo or SR-V0 was a Gonk droid who accompanied the nerf herder Sig Greebling. Servo was upgraded by Sig as a challenge from Jedi Master Bobarian Afol to test Sig's Force Builder abilities. He was given the ability to speak not only Gonkian but also Basic due to this upgrade. Servo was destroyed by Darth Devastator, but was later rebuilt by Sig.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Servo
- **Death:** During Mission to restore the galaxy, Dagobah (rebuild)
- **Model:** GNK-series power droid
- **Class:** Battery droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** He/Him
- **Sensor:** Green
- **Plating:** Blue and gray
