# Typhon (super tactical droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Typhon (super tactical droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Typhon_%28super_tactical_droid%29)

Typhon was a ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid of the Confederacy of Independent Systems who assisted the Dark side of the Force cult Infinite Coil. The droid was obsessed with their acquired plague with which they sought to infest the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Type:** CIS
- **Name:** Typhon
- **Model:** ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid
- **Class:** Tactical droid
- **Height:** 1.94 meters
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Black with yellow markings
- **Affiliation:**

  - Infinite Coil
