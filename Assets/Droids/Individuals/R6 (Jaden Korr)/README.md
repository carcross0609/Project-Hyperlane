# R6 (Jaden Korr)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R6 (Jaden Korr)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R6_%28Jaden_Korr%29)

R6, affectionately called Arsix by those who knew him, was a R6-series astromech droid owned by Jaden Korr in the time following the Second Galactic Civil War. He served as astromech droid to Korr's Z-95 Headhunter on his mission to Fhost in 41 ABY. When Korr traveled to the Frozen moon in the UR-2212-GR system of the Unknown Regions aboard Junker with its crew, Khedryn Faal and Marr Idi-Shael, R6 was ordered to remain behind, hidden in Fhost's system, and to report to the Jedi Council if Korr did not return within two weeks.
