# 50R-T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [50R-T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/50R-T)

50R-T, pronounced "Sortie," was a tactical droid whose origins stretched back to the time of the Old Republic. Bearing a resemblance to droids of the GEMINI class, 50R-T eventually found herself as scrap on a battlefield. She would go on to be reprogrammed as a croupier droid by new owners, who hoped that 50R-T's proclivity for tactics would give her an advantage in gambling activities. 50R-T wielded a blaster pistol, as well as a deck of modified Sabacc cards which she used to attack her enemies.
