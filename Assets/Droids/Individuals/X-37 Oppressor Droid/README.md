# X-37 Oppressor Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X-37 Oppressor Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-37_Oppressor_Droid)

The X-37 Oppressor Droid was a battle droid that served among the Sith Empire's forces on the Maelstrom Prison. It was destroyed by Republic forces that raided the space station early in the Galactic War.
