# 2BB-2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2BB-2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2BB-2)

2BB-2 was a blue-plated BB-series astromech droid with masculine programming in the service of the Resistance. The droid was present aboard the MC85 Star Cruiser Raddus during the evacuation of the Resistance base on the planet D'Qar in 34 ABY. After the destruction of the Raddus bridge, 2BB-2 attended a meeting during which command of the Resistance was granted to Vice Admiral Amilyn Holdo.
