# Bobbie

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bobbie](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bobbie)

Bobbie was a WA-7 waitress droid who worked aboard the Miss Fortune, a bar/starship based on Cyrkon. She featured a built-in order transmitter that kept her in touch with the kitchen at all times, and a paired set of repulsor stabilizers helped her maintain balance as she went around her customers.
