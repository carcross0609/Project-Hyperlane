# Unidentified wait staff droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified wait staff droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_wait_staff_droid)

A wait staff droid was serving food and drinks in a commissary on the planet Eriadu during the High Republic Era. In 228 BBY, the RDC officer Captain Pikka Adren ordered jogan juice and salted wuli nuts from the droid.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Galactic Republic
- **Name:** Unidentified wait staff droid
- **Model:** Waiter droid
