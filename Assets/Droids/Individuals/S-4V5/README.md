# S-4V5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S-4V5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-4V5)

S-4V5 was a shopkeeper droid who worked as a starship components merchant during Great Galactic War between the reconstituted Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic. During the war it ran a stall within the Republic-controlled Shipwright Auxiliary Spaceport on the planet Corellia. S-4V5 had yellow sensors and gold plating. While at its stall it made use of a holoprojector.
