# EEK-176

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EEK-176](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EEK-176)

EEK-176 was a B1-series battle droid manufactured to serve in the Trade Federation Droid Army. In 32 BBY, the Trade Federation invaded the planet Naboo, and EEK-176 participated in the initial landing on the world by leading a group of droids to assist command battle droid OOM-9 in the attack of the city of Vis and other nearby towns.
