# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Confederate space)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Confederate space)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Confederate_space%29/Legends)

A B1 battle droid was stationed on the bridge of General Grievous's Munificent-class star frigate and participated in the ambush on Jedi Master Anakin Skywalker in Separatist space around 21 BBY. The droid was ordered to move the cruisers into attack position. The Jedi, however, escaped with the help of Republic reinforcements and, adding insult to injury, caused the cruisers to collide. The droid then awkwardly reported their failure to Grievous, who responded by growling at the droid and smacking its head off.
