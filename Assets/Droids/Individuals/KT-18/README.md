# KT-18

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KT-18](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KT-18)

KT-18 (or "Kate") was a housekeeping droid that was purchased by Luke Skywalker at the Droidfest on Tatooine, intending to give it as a housewarming gift to his friend Han Solo.
