# T8-3R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [T8-3R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T8-3R)

T8-3R was a BB-series astromech droid that resided on The Island of a planet after being stranded there. The droid was one of the many inhabitants known as Castaways who called the island home. When picked up, the droid would flash green, then yellow, then red, before releasing electricity that shocked anyone nearby. T8-3R had yellow and blue plating, and a black sensor.
