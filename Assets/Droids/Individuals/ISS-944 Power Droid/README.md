# ISS-944 Power Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ISS-944 Power Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ISS-944_Power_Droid)

The ISS-944 Power Droid was a combat-prepared power droid owned by the reconstituted Sith Empire and used aboard the Harrower-class dreadnought Emperor's Glory during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY, it was destroyed by a boarding party from a Republic transport Esseles.
