# GXR-5 Sabotage Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GXR-5 Sabotage Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GXR-5_Sabotage_Droid)

GXR-5 Sabotage Droid was a battle droid of the Galactic Republic active during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY it was aboard the Thranta-class corvette Brentaal Star during the attack by the Gage-class transport Black Talon. GXR-5 boarded the vessel, but was destroyed by the Imperials aboard.
