# R2-X2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-X2/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-X2/Legends)

R2-X2 was the R2-series astromech droid used by Theron Nett, known as "Red Ten," during the Battle of Yavin. He was destroyed when Nett's X-wing starfighter was shot down by TIE pilot "Backstabber".
