# H-5LH

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [H-5LH](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H-5LH)

H-5LH was a shopkeeper droid posted at the Galactic Republic outpost in the Nightmare Lands on Voss during the Galactic War. While at the outpost, the droid sold various stimulants.
