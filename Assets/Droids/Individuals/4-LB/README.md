# 4-LB

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [4-LB](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/4-LB)

4-LB was a large, square labor droid who ran the Power Station at Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine, where locals came to power their speeders and equipment. It cared little about its customers and their whereabouts and was unenthusiastic about its role.
