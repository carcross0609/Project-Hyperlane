# TJ-912

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TJ-912](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TJ-912)

TJ-912 was a T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid with feminine programming who served the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. At the Battle of Sullust, TJ-912 commanded a Recusant-class light destroyer in a fleet led by Separatist Commander Asajj Ventress. The tactical droid was contacted by Separatist leader Count Dooku during the battle, who instructed her to destroy the Separatist command ship Ventress was aboard after being ordered by his own master, Darth Sidious, to kill her. TJ-912 complied, destroying the cruiser although unaware that Ventress had managed to escape the ship.
