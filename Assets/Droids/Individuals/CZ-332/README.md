# CZ-332

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CZ-332](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-332)

An inventory droid, CZ-332 worked at Farlander's Outfitting and Supply, an equipment store and pawn shop on the Wheel space station, which was open around the year 1 BBY during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It worked with the shop's owner, a Human named Nols, in selling a wide range of both new and used equipment.

## Specifications

- **Affiliation:** Farlander's Outfitting and Supply
