# Unidentified B1 battle droid (Pantora)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid (Pantora)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_%28Pantora%29)

This B1-series battle droid was stationed on the bridge of a Trade Federation Lucrehulk-class Battleship during the Blockade of Pantora. He tracked Ahsoka Tano and Riyo Chuchi as they moved through the ship's detention block in search for Chairman N. Papanoida's kidnapped daughters. The droid then informed Sib Canay of their presence.
