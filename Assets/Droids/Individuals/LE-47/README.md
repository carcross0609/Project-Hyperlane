# LE-47

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LE-47](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LE-47)

LE-47 was a LE-series droid who was owned by the Comet Runners. At some point during the High Republic Era, Corr Taldaan and the Comet Runners were present at Yarrum Tower when they came across the pirate Taborr Val Dorn who was showing off Jedi artifacts he stole from the droid 0G-LC. The Comet Runners stole the artifacts from Val Dorn and planned to sell them on Crescent Island, although they were thwarted by Val Dorn along with Jedi initiates Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay, and Nubs.
