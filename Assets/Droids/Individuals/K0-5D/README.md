# K0-5D

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [K0-5D](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K0-5D)

K0-5D. nicknamed Maximo, was an ASP-series labor droid that was heavily modified to participate in droid gladiator fights. During the Clone Wars, he served a band of Weequay pirates aboard the starship Marauder and was tasked with fighting captured droids, usually emerging victorious. His fighting skill earned the droid his nickname. He once ripped a droid apart, and then challenged Republic Senate protocol droid C-3PO, and 3PO's counterpart R2-D2 intervened on his behalf. However, their battle was ended prematurely by an attack by Separatist General Grievous's forces, which sucked the droids into the vacuum of space.
