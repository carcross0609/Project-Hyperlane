# Unidentified tactical droid (Serenno)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified tactical droid (Serenno)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_tactical_droid_%28Serenno%29)

A tactical droid served Count Dooku of the planet Serenno aboard his airship, the Windrunner. When Dooku, a Sith Lord, sent his apprentice Asajj Ventress to find his sister Jenza at Carannia Spaceport, he insisted Ventress take the airship, where she was accompanied by the tactical droid. Once the airship arrived at the spaceport, the droid notified Ventress, intending to land the Windrunner. Despite the tactical droid's protests, Ventress instead simply leapt off of the airship, landing on the ground below.
