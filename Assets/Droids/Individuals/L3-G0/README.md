# L3-G0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [L3-G0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L3-G0)

L3-G0 was an astromech droid with a black sensor and red, white, and yellow-colored plating. Serving in the Rebel Alliance, the droid was plugged into a rebel pilot's T-65B X-wing starfighter at one point.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Rebel
- **Name:** L3-G0
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Red, white, and yellow
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
