# Rod (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rod (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rod_%28droid%29)

"Hyper" Rod was a reprogrammed protocol droid who participated in Riot Racing at the Safa Toma Speedway on the planet Serolonis. Rod raced in a modified Seven Deuce Blaster. While racing on Serolonis's riot racing track, Rod got passed by TAY-0 while in first place. He later took part in the following race, but ultimately lost to Tech.
