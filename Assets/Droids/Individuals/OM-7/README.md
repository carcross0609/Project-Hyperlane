# OM-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [OM-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OM-7)

OM-7 was a B1-series battle droid who served within the Confederacy of Independent Systems' droid army during the Clone Wars. In 22 BBY, OM-7 participated in the Confederacy's invasion of the planet Ryloth, where he engaged a small Galactic Republic garrison led by Jedi General Ima-Gun Di and a resistance force of the native Twi'leks commanded by General Cham Syndulla.
