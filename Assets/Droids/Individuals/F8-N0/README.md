# F8-N0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [F8-N0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F8-N0)

F8-N0, or simply En-oh, was a droid that served as an assistant to Keikana Herot, the Science Commander of House Organa's forces at Wardpost Duvaal on the planet Alderaan. F8-N0 assisted Herot in her research into the Killiks.

## Specifications

- **Name:** F8-N0
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** House Organa
