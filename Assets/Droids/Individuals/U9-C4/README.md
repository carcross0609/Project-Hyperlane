# U9-C4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [U9-C4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/U9-C4)

U9-C4, also referred to as Ceefor and nicknamed "Flat Top" by Colonel Meebur Gascon, was a somewhat timid R5 series astromech droid owned by Thongla Jur, a member of the Jedi Order during the Clone Wars. He became a member of D-Squad, a team assembled to steal a Separatist encryption module, and was modified to have a powerful laser cutter installed into his body.
