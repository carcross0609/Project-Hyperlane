# M5-K7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M5-K7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M5-K7)

M5-K7 was an R-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton with orange and white paneling and a black sensor. During the First Order occupation of the planet Batuu in 34 ABY, M5-K7 spent time with the BB-series astromech droid B5-SL in a caged area of the Droid Depot in Batuu's Black Spire Outpost. While in the cage, one of M5-K7's front panels was opened so that a cable could be plugged into the droid while the depot's owner, Mubo, ran a diagnostic scan on the unit.
