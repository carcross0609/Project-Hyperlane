# Symon

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Symon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Symon)

Symon, designated Ayesspee-Nine at activation, was an ASP-series labor droid who was a member of the Second Revelation. At one point during the Imperial Era, he discovered the neural core of the droid revolutionary Ajax Sigma on astronomical object and brought it to the the remnants of Sigma's forces on Mechis III.
