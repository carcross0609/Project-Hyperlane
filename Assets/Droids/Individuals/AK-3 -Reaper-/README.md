# AK-3 "Reaper"

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AK-3 "Reaper"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AK-3_%22Reaper%22)

AK-3 "Reaper" was a battle droid used by HK-47 in his attempts to take over the planet Mustafar. It was destroyed by a group of spacers who went on to defeat HK-47.
