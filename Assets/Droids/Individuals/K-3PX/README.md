# K-3PX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K-3PX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-3PX)

K-3PX was a black 3PX-series protocol droid that belonged to the Sith Lord Darth Vader during the height of the Galactic Civil War, stationed at the Imperial citadel on Gamandar. K-3PX played a vital role in Admiral Griggor Tower's capture of Rebel agents Lando Calrissian and Chewbacca in 3 ABY—later, he would inform Vader of Tower's endangerment of Vader's coveted target Luke Skywalker on Iskalon, leading to Tower's execution.
