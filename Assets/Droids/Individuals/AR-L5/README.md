# AR-L5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AR-L5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AR-L5)

AR-L5 was a BB-series astromech droid with a blue sensor and blue and white paneling, covered in rust. As a member of the BB-series, it was manufactured by Industrial Automaton and stood 0.67 meters tall. In 34 ABY, during the First Order's occupation of the planet Batuu, AR-L5 was displayed among droid parts on one of the shelves inside Droid Depot, a store in Batuu's Black Spire Outpost.
