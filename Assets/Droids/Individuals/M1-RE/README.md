# M1-RE

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M1-RE](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M1-RE)

M1-RE, also referred to as Miree, was a Safari droid manufactured by Serv-O-Droid, Inc. for the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts. She was a specialized member of the Society who was tasked with caring for and studying several young creatures and aliens. During Miree's interactions with younglings on the Youngling Care Space Station, she was recorded by the cam droid CAM-E who broadcasted footage across the Holonet and sent their findings back to the Society. Miree was friends with SF-R3, also known as Aree, who was also a Safari droid affiliated with the Society. Miree cared for several different species of younglings, including Wookiees, Ewoks, Ortolans, Hutts, Gungans, Jawas, Gamorreans, Rodians, loth-cats, rancors, tauntauns, and porgs. With an optimistic attitude, Miree tackled every new case to teach viewers about the characteristics that made each youngling special.
