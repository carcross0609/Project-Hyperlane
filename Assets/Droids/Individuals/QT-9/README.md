# QT-9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [QT-9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QT-9)

QT-9 was a prototype therapy droid given to the former Rebel soldier Dade Hetkins after he was discharged from a New Republic vet hospital in Darropolis on the planet Hosnian Prime. QT-9 was approximately knee-high and had two ocular lenses. The droid had a square-like head and rolled around on a blue-and-gold spherical body.
