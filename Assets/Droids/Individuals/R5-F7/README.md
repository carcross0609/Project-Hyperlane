# R5-F7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R5-F7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-F7)

R5-F7 was an astromech droid serving onboard Gold Four Lieutenant Lepira's BTL Y-wing starfighter, flying point for Gold Squadron during the assault on the first Death Star at the Battle of Yavin and fending off Imperial fighters to clear a path for the Rebels to reach the Death Star's vulnerable exhaust port. R5-F7 was destroyed during the battle along with Lepira when their starfighter was shot down.
