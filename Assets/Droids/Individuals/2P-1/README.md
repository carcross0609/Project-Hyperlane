# 2P-1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2P-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2P-1)

2P-1 was a 2-1B surgical droid with yellow and white plating. During the First Order's occupation of the planet Batuu in 34 ABY, 2P-1 was kept on display in the Droid Depot, a store located in Black Spire Outpost. The medical droid was displayed in a cage next to the KX-series security droid K-7R1 near the depot's counter. Like all 2-1B surgical droids, 2P-1 was manufactured by Industrial Automaton and stood 1.77 meters tall.
