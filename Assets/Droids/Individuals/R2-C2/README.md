# R2-C2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-C2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-C2)

R2-C2 was an R-series astromech droid who served the Hutt crime lord Ziro Desilijic Tiure at his palace on Coruscant. The droid was painted in the blue-and-grey style of droids in service to Ziro.
