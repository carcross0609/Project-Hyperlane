# IT-O

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IT-O](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IT-O)

IT-O, also known as Ito, was a reprogrammed Imperial IT-O interrogation droid with masculine programming who served New Republic Intelligence agent Caern Adan. In 4 ABY, Adan tasked IT-O to help him figure out if Imperial defector Yrica Quell was committed to the New Republic. IT-O became Quell's therapist to help her work through the experiences she had to go through carrying out Operation: Cinder. After being damaged in a crash on a planetoid in the Cerberon system its memory gradually became corrupted and reverted to its original programing, torturing Yrica Quell for betraying the Empire before she discovered what had happened, it then attempted to kill her only to be destroyed by the New Republic pilot.
