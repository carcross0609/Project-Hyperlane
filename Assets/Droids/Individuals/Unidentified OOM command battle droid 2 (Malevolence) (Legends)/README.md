# Unidentified OOM command battle droid 2 (Malevolence)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid 2 (Malevolence)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_2_%28Malevolence%29/Legends)

This OOM command battle droid was stationed on board the Malevolence during the Battle of the Kaliida Nebula, serving under General Grievous, commander of the massive warship. In the ensuing battle, they were on the bridge of the warship to ascertain the status of the repairs to the hyperdrive. Moment's later, Senator Padmé Amidala and Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker entered the bridge and proceeded to destroy the OOM commander and the other battle droids stationed there.
