# Corruptor Zero

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corruptor Zero](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corruptor_Zero)

Corruptor Zero was a battle droid that commanded the droid armies of the Dread Masters at the Dread Fortress on Oricon during the Galactic War. During the Dread War, Corruptor Zero was destroyed while trying to repel invading forces.
