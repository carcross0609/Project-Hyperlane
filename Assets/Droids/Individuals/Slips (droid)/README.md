# Slips (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Slips (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slips_%28droid%29)

Slips was a droid used by former Emperor's Hand Mara Jade during the Caamas Document Crisis. In 19 ABY, in a mission to the Wild Space planet Nirauan, Jedi Master Luke Skywalker brought Jade's starship, the Personal Luxury Yacht 3000 Jade's Fire, along with Slips, to the planet in an attempt to rescue Jade, who had traveled to Nirauan while searching for the Hand of Thrawn fortress.
