# Proto Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Proto Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proto_Droid)

A Proto Droid was a specific kind of self-constructed junk droid. The rickety Proto One, who rebuilt his broken self with diverse replacement parts and resembled a strange mixture of many different droid types, was a Proto Droid.

- **Name:** Proto Droid
- **Creator:** Self-created
