# BD-72

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BD-72](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD-72)

BD-72 was a repurposed vintage BD explorer droid owned by Peli Motto on Tatooine during the New Republic Era. The droid assisted Din Djarin and Peli Motto with the construction of Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter. His motivational firmware had a deeply programmed curiosity meant for his task as an explorer droid. As a repair assistant he used it to eagerly find the best ways to use his varied equipment to help his fellow mechanics.
