# Unidentified assassin probe 3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified assassin probe 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_assassin_probe_3)

An assassin probe was one of two others smuggled onto the Coronet during its voyage to Coruscant, to assassinate Duchess Satine Kryze. It was the third and final one to be defeated by Jedi General Anakin Skywalker and his unit of clone troopers.
