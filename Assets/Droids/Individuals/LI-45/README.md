# LI-45

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LI-45](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LI-45)

LI-45 was a protocol droid stationed aboard the Imperial prototype Harrower-class dreadnought Javelin. As part of a trap laid by the Empire, most of the crew left the Javelin, leaving LI-45 behind and tasking him to rig the ship with detonite to kill the Galactic Republic strike team that was trying to capture the ship.
