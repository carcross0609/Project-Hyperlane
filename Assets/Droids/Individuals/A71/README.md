# A71

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A71](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A71)

A71 was the designation of an A-series assassin droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Battle of Jabiim. It was specially modified for service on Jabiim, presumably adapted to the planet's perpetual rainstorms.
