# Are Zero Are Zero

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Are Zero Are Zero](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Are_Zero_Are_Zero)

Are Zero Are Zero, nicknamed Roro, was a protocol droid. By 4 ABY, the droid was stationed out of commission inside the Holowan droid foundry on a terrestrial planet. Between 2 BBY and 4 ABY, Grand-engineer Lony Coleema was kidnapped by the IG-88 assassin droid IG-88B and brought into the foundry. After waking up, Coleema saw Are Zero Are Zero and reprogrammed it. After thanking them, the droid told the engineer where they were.
