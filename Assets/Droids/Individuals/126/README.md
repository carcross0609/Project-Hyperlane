# 126

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [126](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/126)

126 was a Confederate BX-series droid commando who held the rank of sergeant in the Separatist Droid Army and participated in the Battle of Khorm during the Clone Wars. Serving under Commander Asajj Ventress during the battle, 126 was responsible for activating Confederate stealth units during an assault on a Republic-occupied agrocite processing facility. Although the Confederate attack succeeded, the facility, along with the entire planet, soon fell back into Republic hands.
