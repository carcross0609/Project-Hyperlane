# HC-100

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HC-100](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HC-100)

HC-100 was the droid companion and homework checker of Ken in the Lost City of the Jedi. HC-100 had a humanoid body with silver plating and blue photoreceptor eyes. HC-100 was built by DJ-88. He was made to resemble C-3PO, and he walked and talked like a drill sergeant. He would often appear at random times to check Ken's homework, whether it was done or not.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HC-100

- **Creator:** DJ-88
