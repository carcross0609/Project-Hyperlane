# Mac-Vee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mac-Vee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mac-Vee)

Mac-Vee was a nanny and housekeeping droid who served the Erso family. After leaving for Lah'mu, Jyn Erso admitted that she missed Mac-Vee, and wondered what the droid would do without them.
