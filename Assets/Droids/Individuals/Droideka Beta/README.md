# Droideka Beta

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droideka Beta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droideka_Beta)

Droideka Beta was one of the few droidekas to survive the Clone Wars and the mass deactivation in 19 BBY. Around 15 BBY it joined the IG-88's Droid Army and became a part of the Droid Revolution led by the rogue assassin droid IG-88A, participating in the Mechis III Uprising.
