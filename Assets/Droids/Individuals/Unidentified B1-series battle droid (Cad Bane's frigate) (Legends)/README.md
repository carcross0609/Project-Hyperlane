# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Cad Bane's frigate)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Cad Bane's frigate)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Cad_Bane%27s_frigate%29/Legends)

This B1 battle droid was on board Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate, and worked on the torture and interrogation of the captured Jedi Knight, Bolla Ropal. Bane ordered the Jedi to continue to be tortured after he refused to unlock a holocron, but this killed Ropal. This B1-series battle droid controlled the interrogation levels, and the droid viewed the Jedi's vital signs. This battle droid had a much deeper voice than other B1 battle droids.
