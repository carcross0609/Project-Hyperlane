# Unidentified B1 battle droid 1 (Skytop Station)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 1 (Skytop Station)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_1_%28Skytop_Station%29)

This B1 battle droid was stationed aboard Skytop Station in 22 BBY. While chatting with three other droids, he gave a 631 model B1 battle droid an important object to carry to another level without dropping it. After witnessing the 631 droid fail to accomplish the task and drop the object, this droid expressed his disappointment towards the intelligence of the 631 model with a fellow droid.
