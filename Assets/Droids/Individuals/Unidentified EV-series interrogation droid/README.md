# Unidentified EV-series interrogation droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified EV-series interrogation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_EV-series_interrogation_droid)

An EV-series medical droid with feminine programing operated as an interrogation droid in the Citadel, a prison located on the planet Lola Sayu that was controlled by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Following the capture of Jedi Master Even Piell by the Separatist, she was tasked with extracting from him the coordinates of the Nexus Route, a hyperspace route that led into the heart of both the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy. However, while the droid was torturing Piell, she was shot through the head by Clone Captain CT-7567, who had infiltrated the Citadel along with a group of clone troopers and several Jedi to rescue Master Piell from the Separatists.
