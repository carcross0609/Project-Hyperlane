# Utooni's power droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Utooni's power droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Utooni%27s_power_droid)

This EG-6 power droid was one of many droids owned by the team of Jawa brothers known collectively as the Hunter Utooni. It featured an orange and blue exterior, with dark gray legs, gray and black components, as well as glowing green and red buttons and other elements. When Utooni wore their EG-6 Enthusiasts costume during the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition within the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara, they would strap this droid to the back of the upper Jawa.
