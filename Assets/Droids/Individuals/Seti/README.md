# Seti

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seti)

Seti was a fictional GV/3-series guardian droid who belonged to the young girl, Revella. He was one of the main characters of Revella's Journey: A Story of a Girl and Her Droid.
