# R4-K5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R4-K5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-K5)

R4-K5 was an R-series astromech droid that accompanied Sith Lord Darth Vader, formerly Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker, on missions for the Galactic Empire early during the Imperial Era. Once owned by a Jedi, R4-K5 was chosen for its dark hue by Vader to replace his former astromech droid, R2-D2.
