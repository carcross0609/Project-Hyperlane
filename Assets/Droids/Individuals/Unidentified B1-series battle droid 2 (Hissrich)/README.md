# Unidentified B1-series battle droid 2 (Hissrich)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid 2 (Hissrich)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_2_%28Hissrich%29)

A B1-series battle droid was stationed on the planet Hissrich in 22 BBY. They carried a comlink booster pack and an E-5 blaster rifle. As a member of a patrol squadron, they engaged in combat with the Jedi Master Mace Windu and Jedi Knight Rissa Mano in sector two zero nine. Windu bisected the battle droid, destroying them.
