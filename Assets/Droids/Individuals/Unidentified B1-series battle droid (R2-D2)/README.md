# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (R2-D2)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (R2-D2)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28R2-D2%29)

This B1-series battle droid was captured, reprogrammed and used by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. It was a member of a group of three battle droids placed under the command of the astromech droid R2-D2 during a mission to the Outer Rim planet Lola Sayu. This droid and their comrades flew the shuttle carrying a Republic strike team, the members of which had been carbon-frozen, onto the planet to rescue Jedi Master Even Piell from the Citadel prison.
