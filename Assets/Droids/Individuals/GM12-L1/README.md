# GM12-L1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GM12-L1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GM12-L1)

GM12-L1 was a blue L-1g general purpose droid that was repurposed as a labor droid and forced to work as the landing-pad operations droid in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, GM12-L1 was carrying a backpack full of kesselstone when it was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.
