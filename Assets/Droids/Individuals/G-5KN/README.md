# G-5KN

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-5KN](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-5KN)

G-5KN was a probe droid used by the Galactic Republic on Taris during the Cold War. He informed a passerby of a threat posed by the Locust and asked to eliminate him.
