# T4-Z4N

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [T4-Z4N](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T4-Z4N)

T4-Z4N was an astromech droid who served the Resistance on the planet Ajan Kloss during the First Order-Resistance War. The droid was one of five astromechs who worked under R5-2JE and who were all absent from their jobs at the landing pad one day. T4-Z4N was busy swinging through the trees instead of doing R5-2JE's work.
