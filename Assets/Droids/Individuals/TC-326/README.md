# TC-326

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TC-326](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TC-326)

TC-326 was a masculine TC-series protocol droid manufactured by Cybot Galactica that served in the Republic Navy's Open Circle Fleet during the Clone Wars. He was temporarily assigned as Archduke Poggle the Lesser's translator following his capture during the Second Battle of Geonosis. TC-326 was present for Anakin Skywalker's interrogation of the Geonosian leader, calmly carrying out his translation duties even as the Jedi Knight tortured Poggle for information.
