# EG-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EG-4/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EG-4/Legends)

EG-4 was a power droid owned by the Rebel Alliance. It was donated to the Alliance by the Bothan underground, and had a rough design and specially-modified power sockets mounted on top of its chassis.It was stationed at Echo Base around the time of the Battle of Hoth. EG-4 was present alongside other droids when Han Solo returned from his unsuccessful search for Luke Skywalker outside the base.
