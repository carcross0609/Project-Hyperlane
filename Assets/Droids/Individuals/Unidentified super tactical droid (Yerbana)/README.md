# Unidentified super tactical droid (Yerbana)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified super tactical droid (Yerbana)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_super_tactical_droid_%28Yerbana%29)

An ST-series super tactical droid served in the Confederacy of Independent Systems as a commander. The super tactical droid led Separatist forces during a battle on the planet Yerbana in 19 BBY. The tactical droid was Force-pulled out of cover and destroyed by Jedi General Anakin Skywalker after he led the Separatist droid forces into a trap.
