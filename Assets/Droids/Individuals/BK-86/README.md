# BK-86

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BK-86](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BK-86)

BK-86 was a IG-series assassin droid that worked as primary security droid for the Ithorian antiques dealer Dok-Ondar at his Den of Antiquities in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu. At some point following the Battle of Crait, it was destroyed by a member of the Finto Gang during an attempt to double cross Dok-Ondar by the gang's leader, Kase Finto. Dok-Ondar kept BK-86's remains in his store while looking for a replacement.
