# J-45

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [J-45](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-45)

J-45 was a gray 2-1B surgical droid employed by Jabba the Hutt on Tatooine. The droid offered Luke Skywalker medical treatment during his attempts to free a carbonite-frozen Han Solo from Jabba.
