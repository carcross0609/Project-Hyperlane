# TK-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TK-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TK-7)

TK-7 was a passive-aggressive droid who served as the Chief Editor Droid at the Galactic Digest magazine during the time of the Galactic Empire. The droid edited and left comments on an article written by the journalist Parazeen Parapa on the bounty hunters 4-LOM and Zuckuss after the Digest received a donation from the subjects' representative, Vap Tomulus. The Digest server was sliced before TK-7 had finished revising the writing, and, to TK-7's shock, the unedited article was published as it stood, with the droid's comments still present.
