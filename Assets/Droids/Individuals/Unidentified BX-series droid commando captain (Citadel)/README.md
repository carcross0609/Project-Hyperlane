# Unidentified BX-series droid commando captain (Citadel)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando captain (Citadel)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_captain_%28Citadel%29)

A droid commando captain was stationed at the Citadel under the command of Osi Sobeck where Even Piell was being being held. However, a team of Jedi and clone troopers managed to free Piell. When another commando droid entered and reported to Sobeck that the prisoners had escaped, Sobeck ordered this captain to shoot the other droid.
