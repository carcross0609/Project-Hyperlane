# R4-TR3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-TR3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-TR3)

R4-TR3 was an R4 astromech droid whose appearance was akin to a festive evergreen tree, even topped with a sparkling golden star. The droid was known to spend time on the forest moon of Endor.
