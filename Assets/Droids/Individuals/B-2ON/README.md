# B-2ON

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [B-2ON](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-2ON)

B-2ON was a protocol droid that served the twins Am and Karre, Dark Lords of the T-Empire. At some point after the Battle of Exegol of 35 ABY, B-2ON was with Am in her throne room aboard a Gemini-class Star Destroyer. He reported to her that the final adjustments to the ship and its hyper cannon superweapon were being made and that it would soon be operational. Just as Am gave the order to fire the weapon, they received an alert that the reactor core's power source had been stolen. Am realized that Karre had stolen it, and B-2ON accompanied her to a hangar, where they confronted him. With the help of his astromech droid R-DUO, Karre escaped the hangar and then dueled Am on the Star Destroyer's surface. After Karre defeated Am and left her floating unconscious, B-2ON used a jetpack to recover her.
