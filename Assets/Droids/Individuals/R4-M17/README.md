# R4-M17

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R4-M17](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-M17)

R4-M17 was a hardworking, but stubborn astromech droid. Her Arc welder had an intermittent short, and her third leg could no longer retract, but otherwise she was a skilled worker. The unit was owned by a moisture farmer, who bought it from the Jawa Traders shop in the center of Mos Eisley.
