# D-R3D

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D-R3D](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-R3D)

D-R3D was a battle droid used by the Dread Masters as a member of their defensive unit but was subsequently repaired and reprogrammed by the Eternal Alliance for offensive, defensive and medical uses.
