# G0-A1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G0-A1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G0-A1)

G0-A1 was an astromech droid that served the reconstituted Sith Empire. During the fighting in Section X on the planet Belsavis during the Galactic War, G0-A1 was sent by General Avrun back to Crag Outpost for aid.
