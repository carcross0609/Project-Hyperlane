# LM-7B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LM-7B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LM-7B)

LM-7B was an R4 astromech droid that was displayed on a shelf of droid parts within the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu during the First Order's occupation of the planet in 34 ABY. It was manufactured by Industrial Automaton and had blue, black, and white paneling and a black sensor.
