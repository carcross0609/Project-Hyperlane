# BL-R4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BL-R4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BL-R4)

BL-R4 was an astromech droid affiliated with the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts. A friend of fellow GSCR member Quinn, he had male programming and a shy personality. While on a flight to Batuu sometime after 34 ABY, BL-R4 was encharged with feeding some of the more dangerous creatures kept in the starship. During said flight, BL fed meat to a wampa, and while trying to feed a rathtar, it was attacked and sent flying out of the enclosure, while the rathtar was inadvertently set loose.
