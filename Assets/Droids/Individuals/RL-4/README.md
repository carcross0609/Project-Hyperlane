# RL-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RL-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RL-4)

RL-4 was a Protocol droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War as a special envoy in Office of the Supreme Chancellor Leontyne Saresh. In 3639 BBY RL-4 was visiting the Carrick Station, where he contacted an important figure, specifically selected by the Supreme Chancellor, to join Captain Hardin of the Outrider on a mission to the Vyron system.
