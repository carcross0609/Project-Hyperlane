# CZ-13

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CZ-13](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-13)

CZ-13, also known as Cees, was a friendly and efficient receptionist droid active during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. During the conflict the droid worked for IsoTech, a front-company for the black-market tech-salavaging operations of the Twi'lek siblings Reom and Shira. CZ-13 served as the receptionist at the company's offices on The Wheel space station.
