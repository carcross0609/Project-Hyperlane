# B8G-H0R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B8G-H0R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B8G-H0R)

B8G-H0R was a B8G labor droid that was forced to work in the spice mines owned by the criminal Pyke Syndicate on the planet Kessel. In the year 10 BBY, he was involved in a slave uprising on Kessel caused by a group of outlaws trying to steal coaxium fuel from the Pykes. During the uprising, B8G-H0R was part of a mob that charged out of the mine tunnels and onto a landing pad, where the rioters fought with their captors.
