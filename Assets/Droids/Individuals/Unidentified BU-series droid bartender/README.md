# Unidentified BU-series droid bartender

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified BU-series droid bartender](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BU-series_droid_bartender)

An unidentified droid was a BU-series droid bartender who served as a bartender at Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina on the planet of Tatooine during the Clone Wars. Around 21 BBY, this droid was serving drinks at the bar when several bounty hunters, including Asajj Ventress, were searching through a database of bounties. When he told the hunters to stop monopolizing all of the transmitters, a Weequay individual told the droid to shut its speakers.
