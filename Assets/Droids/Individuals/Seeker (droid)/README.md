# Seeker (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Seeker (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seeker_%28droid%29)

Seeker droids, also known as seeker remotes, were generally small spy droids that carried out scouting or spying missions for its owner. Models of seekers included the ID9 "parrot" droid, the ID10 seeker droid, the Prowler 1000 seeker droid, and the DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid.

- **Class:**

- **Armament:** Electro-shock prod
- **Equipment:** Antenna
- **Affiliation:**
