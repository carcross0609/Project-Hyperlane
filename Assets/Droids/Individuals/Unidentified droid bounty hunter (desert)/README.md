# Unidentified droid bounty hunter (desert)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid bounty hunter (desert)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_bounty_hunter_%28desert%29)

A droid bounty hunter attempted to capture R2-D2 and BB-8 for a bounty of 10,000 credits while they were traveling through a desert canyon to a junk shop to purchase a replacement part for the Millennium Falcon. He failed, destroying his speeder in the process, and the two droids paid him for a part of the wreckage, which happened to be exactly what they needed.
