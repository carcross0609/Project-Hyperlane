# Heavy Resource Recovery Machine 5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy Resource Recovery Machine 5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Resource_Recovery_Machine_5)

The Heavy Resource Recovery Machine 5 or HRRM-5 was a powerful battle droid used by the White Maw pirates on Hoth to guard their valuables stoled at the ice caves of Loramarr during the Galactic War. The droid was destroyed by a hero of the reconstituted Sith Empire who came to reclaim the stored valuables and cripple the White Maw.
