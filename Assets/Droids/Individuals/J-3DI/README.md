# J-3DI

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-3DI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-3DI)

J-3DI, also referred to as Jay-Threedeeaye, or simply Jay-Three, was a unique droid who had been programmed to believe that he possessed the Force and was a Jedi Knight. He had plating in the style of Jedi robes and wielded a blue-bladed lightsaber. He was created by fellow competitor Sprocket to uniquely match the Force-sensitive competitor Rieve. He possessed a databank of Jedi wisdom, and while not able to use the Force, he was still able to mimic its effects through technological means. Given the limits of information on the Jedi by 9 ABY, his programming often had mistakes in Jedi history, manifesting as incorrect phrasing of Jedi mantras. J-3DI competed in the Hunters of the Outer Rim tournament on the planet Vespaara.
