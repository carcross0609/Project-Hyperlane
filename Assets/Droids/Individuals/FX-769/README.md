# FX-769

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FX-769](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-769)

FX-769 was a FX-series medical assistant droid stationed on the planet Gavos and was part of the New Droids' Rights Movement, a group of droids that was focused on spreading violent rebellion against organic life forms throughout the galaxy.
