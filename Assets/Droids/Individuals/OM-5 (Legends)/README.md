# OM-5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OM-5/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OM-5/Legends)

OM-5 was a B1-series battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. During the Battle of Ryloth, OM-5 and OM-7 were sent by their Commander to check out a Galactic Republic Gunship that was blocking their path. OM-5 and OM-7 pulled the door open the gunship only to discover it was full of explosives. A clone trooper hidden in a nearby hill pressed the trigger, which caused a huge explosion and destroyed OM-5 and OM-7.
