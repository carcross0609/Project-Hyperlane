# 68-RKO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [68-RKO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/68-RKO)

68-RKO was a droid in the service of Bail Organa during the Clone Wars. He happened to be traveling on the same pleasure cruiser as Obi-Wan Kenobi and followed him in order not to be considered luggage. While accompanying Kenobi he witnessed the Jedi save the ship from Merson pirates, and defuse a hostile mob of passengers. He later told the story to Bail Organa, who in turn told the story to Leia Organa.
