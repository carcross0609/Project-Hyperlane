# Seezee (Gimm sisters)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Seezee (Gimm sisters)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seezee_%28Gimm_sisters%29)

Seezee was a CZ-series secretary/business communications droid employed as a tutor droid at the Gimm sisters' orphanage in the city of NiJedha, on the holy moon Jedha prior to the city's destruction in 1 BBY. Around that time, Seezee was already an old unit, with its servos whining as it moved about.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Seezee
- **Model:** CZ-series secretary/business communications droid
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Affiliation:** Gimm sisters' orphanage
