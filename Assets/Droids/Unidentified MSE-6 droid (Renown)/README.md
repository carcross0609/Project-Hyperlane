# Unidentified MSE-6 droid (Renown)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified MSE-6 droid (Renown)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_MSE-6_droid_%28Renown%29)

An MSE-6 droid was stationed aboard the Renown during the Clone Wars when the ship was captured by the Confederacy of Independent Systems. This droid survived the hijacking along with several other republic droids, including BNI-393, though the rest of the crew were killed.
