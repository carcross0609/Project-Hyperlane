# MK-1 Hazard Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MK-1 Hazard Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MK-1_Hazard_Droid)

The MK-1 Hazard Droid was a type of battle droid produced by the Colicoid species during the era of the Galactic War. A number of MK-1 Hazard Droids and the later models were deployed during the Colicoids' war games.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MK-1 Hazard Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoids
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Black
