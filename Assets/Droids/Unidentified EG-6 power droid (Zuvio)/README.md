# Unidentified EG-6 power droid (Zuvio)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified EG-6 power droid (Zuvio)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_EG-6_power_droid_%28Zuvio%29)

The droid was one of the supposedly rogue droids that attacked Constable Zuvio during his investigation of a banking ship robbery in Niima Outpost. As a part of Rikard Lovas's plot to steal the electronic credits of the citizens of Niima Outpost on Jakku, several droids were sent to an abandoned farmhouse to wait for Constable Zuvio. While waiting the gonk droid offered power to any of the other droids who needed it. During the skirmish at the abandoned Jakku moisture farm the droid was covered in grenades and triggered an explosion destroying itself and most of its other droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified EG-6 power droid
- **Death:** Sometime before the Escape from Jakku in 34 ABY
- **Model:** EG-6 power droid
