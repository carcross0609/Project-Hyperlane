# 9-A9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [9-A9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/9-A9)

9-A9, also known as Nynie, was a droid owned by the nobles of Eiattu 6. It looked after Princess Isplourrdacartha Estillo when she was a child. Seven months after the Battle of Endor, 9-A9 accompanied Grand Duke Gror Pernon and Poe to meet with Rogue Squadron.
