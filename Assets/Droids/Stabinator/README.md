# Stabinator

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stabinator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stabinator)

Stabinator was a modified Mark VII gladiator droid who worked for the Grakkus Arena around 0 ABY and had 15 clean kills on the Wumpo circuit using a power lance. Stabinator was manumitted after a slave uprising instigated by Wrollanppf. The droid later recounted the happenings in an interview included in Maz Kanata's Smuggler's Guide.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Hutt Cartel
- **Name:** Stabinator
- **Model:** Modified Mark VII
- **Class:** Gladiator droid
- **Affiliation:** Grakkus Arena
