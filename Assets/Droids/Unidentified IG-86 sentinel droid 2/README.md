# Unidentified IG-86 sentinel droid 2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified IG-86 sentinel droid 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_IG-86_sentinel_droid_2)

An IG-86 sentinel droid was stationed aboard the Vulture's Claw during the Clone Wars, being owned by Gha Nachkt. When Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano searched the ship for R2-D2 following the Battle of Bothawui, the sentinel droid along with another attacked the two Jedi. Though both droids demonstrated exceptional agility and firepower, they were ultimately no match for the Jedi, being destroyed during the ensuing skirmish.
