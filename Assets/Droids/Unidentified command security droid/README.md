# Unidentified command security droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified command security droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_command_security_droid)

A command security droid served Count Gora of the planet Serenno, leading a unit of other security droids. During the funeral of Gora's wife, Countess Anya, Gora had the droids stationed nearby. After a group of protestors arrived at the funeral to revolt over their jobs being taken by droids, Count Gora summoned his security. One of the lead protestors threw a rock at the commanding droid, who subsequently ordered his squad to open fire on the protestors. Before they could do so, the Jedi Padawan Dooku stepped in to protect the protestors from the droids.
