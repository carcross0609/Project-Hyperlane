# Science research droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Science research droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Science_research_droid)

Science research droids were a line of astromech droids that were produced during the early Galactic Civil War. As their name implied, these droids were designed to collect specimens and conduct scientific research. By 0 BBY, their design was considered top-notch. In order to be adaptable to any terrain, they could retract their wheels and use feet instead.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Science research droid
- **Line:** Astromech droid
- **Plating:** Shock-absorbent shell
- **Equipment:**
  - Set of wheels
  - Set of feet with long steel toes and spikes
