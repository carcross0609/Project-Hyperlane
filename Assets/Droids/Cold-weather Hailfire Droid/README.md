# Cold-weather Hailfire Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cold-weather Hailfire Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cold-weather_Hailfire_Droid)

Cold-weather Hailfire Droids were retrofitted Rebel Alliance IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tanks for increased effectiveness in sub-zero environments. They were able to launch a powerful missile volley, but had light armor and required constant protection on the battlefield.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cold-weather Hailfire Droid
- **Model:** Modified IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank
- **Class:** Droid tank
- **Armament:** Missile launcher pods (2)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Alliance Army
  -   Tatooine rebel cell
