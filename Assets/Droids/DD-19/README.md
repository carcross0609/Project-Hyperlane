# DD-19

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DD-19](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DD-19)

DD-19 was a masculine DD-19 "Overseer" labor pool droid assigned to the New Republic CR90 corvette FarStar. It was nicknamed "the slavemaster" due to its harsh treatment of other droids. DD-19 was primarily assigned to the droid repair bay on the ship, and had the authority to apply restraining bolts to unruly droids.
