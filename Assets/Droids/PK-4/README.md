# PK-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [PK-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PK-4)

PK-4 was a PK-series worker droid in service to the Trade Federation. It had white plating and a green light on their back and utilized a wrench. PK-4 was present during the Battle of Naboo in 32 BBY.
