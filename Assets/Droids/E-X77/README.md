# E-X77

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-X77](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-X77)

E-X77, also known as Ee-Exsevenseven or Exsev, was an RA-7 protocol droid head that was carried around by the Dowutin scavenger Koll Orvenk and was hired along by Tern Koyatta.
