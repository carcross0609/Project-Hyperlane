# OT-12 Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-12 Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-12_Battle_Droid)

The OT-12 Battle Droid was a large, six-legged battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. OT-12 Battle Droids were among the droids deployed within the Okara Droid Factory to defend the structure.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-12 Battle Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** Blaster cannon
