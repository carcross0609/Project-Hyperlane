# Surveillance droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Surveillance droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Surveillance_droid/Legends)

The surveillance droid existed in many eras. They were used to monitor houses, business and warehouses for any possible threat. They flew slowly in lighted or dark places monitoring with their sensors. Advanced models used infrared sensors for better results.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Surveillance droid
- **Homeworld:** Coruscant
- **Class:** Espionage droid
- **Equipment:** Sensor
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
