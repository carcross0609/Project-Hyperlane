# R5-D23

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-D23](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-D23)

R5-D23 was an R5 series astromech droid operating in marketplace of Lothal's Capital City. He was a typical R5, but also wore a blue cone-shaped hat with star and moon shapes in its design that served as auxiliary shielding. He included a Burtt acoustic signaller.
