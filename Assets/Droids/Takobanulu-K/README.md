# Takobanulu-K

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Takobanulu-K](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Takobanulu-K)

Takobanulu-K was a Gree research droid dispatched by the Gray Secant to study the planet Dromund Kaas. It was accompanied by a squad of more heavily-armed Gree droids.
