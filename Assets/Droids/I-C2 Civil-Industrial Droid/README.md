# I-C2 Civil-Industrial Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [I-C2 Civil-Industrial Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I-C2_Civil-Industrial_Droid)

The I-C2 Civil-Industrial Droid was a massive model of construction droid utilized by civilians. It contained a powerful fabrication system that was capable of producing slabs of building materials. The I-C4a combat construction droid was a smaller and militarized version of the I-C2 Civil-Industrial Droid that was manufactured by Veril Line Systems for the Galactic Empire's Imperial Corps of Engineers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** I-C2 Civil-Industrial Droid
- **Class:** Construction droid
- **Equipment:** Fabrication system
