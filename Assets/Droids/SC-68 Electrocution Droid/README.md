# SC-68 Electrocution Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SC-68 Electrocution Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SC-68_Electrocution_Droid)

The SC-68 Electrocution Droid was a model of four-legged battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. SC-68 Electrocution Droids were among the forces that defended the Shroud's factory on the moon Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SC-68 Electrocution Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Bronze
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
