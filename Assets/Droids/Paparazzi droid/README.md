# Paparazzi droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Paparazzi droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paparazzi_droid)

Paparazzi droids also referred to as Paparazzo droids were repulsorlift droids with built in cameras used to capture images of beings. They had a disc shaped body with two sets of three lenses that rotated on inner discs. The droids had five struts that extended from their bodies, with each ending in triangular light source that would repeatedly flash to illuminate their targets. A sixth struct extended down from the droid's body ending in two prongs.
