# Axel

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Axel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axel)

Axel was an R-series astromech droid that was purchased by the Twi'lek Pyya Bocc on the planet Malastare while broken. Bocc's friend Volo Bolus was not pleased to see the Twi'lek bring home the droid and suggested he be returned, but Bocc was keen not to hurt Axel's feelings. Bocc proved overconfident in his droid repair abilities and never fixed Axel prior to the Twi'lek's death, after which Bolus mused he might one day repair the droid around 10 BBY.
