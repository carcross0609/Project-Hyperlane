# Ulgo Siegebreaker

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ulgo Siegebreaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulgo_Siegebreaker)

The Ulgo Siegebreaker was a massively powerful battle droid during the time of the Cold War that was part of House Ulgo's forces. It patrolled the western end of the Glarus Valley, in the riverbed, during the Alderaan Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ulgo Siegebreaker
- **Homeworld:** Alderaan
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** House Ulgo
