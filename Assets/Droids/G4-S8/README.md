# G4-S8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [G4-S8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G4-S8)

G4-S8 was an R4 astromech droid, manufactured by Industrial Automaton. During the First Order's occupation of the planet Batuu in 34 ABY, G4-S8 was displayed amongst a variety of droid parts upon one of the upper shelves of the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on Batuu. The depot also sold action figures of the droid. It had green and white paneling and a black sensor.
