# Armx

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Armx](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armx)

Armx was a modified MerenData RM-2020 espionage droid turned bounty hunter known for his poetry, who often wrote epitaphs for his acquisitions. On one occasion he wrote such an epitaph about his capture of the target Gavron Noff.
