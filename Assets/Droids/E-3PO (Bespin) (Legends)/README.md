# E-3PO (Bespin)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-3PO (Bespin)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-3PO_%28Bespin%29/Legends)

E-3PO, mockingly referred to as E-Gregious by Imperial support teams, was an E-3PO protocol droid in the service of the Galactic Empire. Known for his gruff manners and vulgar demeanor, E-3PO's true allegiances lied with IG-88's Droid Army, a droid supremacy collective that had infiltrated the galaxy. Following the Battle of Hoth, the protocol droid acted as a liason between Imperial and Ugnaught technicians on Cloud City during the Empire's hunt for the Millennium Falcon.
