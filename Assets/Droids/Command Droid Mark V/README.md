# Command Droid Mark V

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Command Droid Mark V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Command_Droid_Mark_V)

The Command Droid Mark V was a model of humanoid-shaped battle droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. They carried personal blaster cannons, and were used in the factory during the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Command Droid Mark V
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Black
- **Armament:** Personal blaster cannon
