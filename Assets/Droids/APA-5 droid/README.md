# APA-5 droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [APA-5 droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/APA-5_droid)

APA-5 droid were droids used by the Galactic Empire to serve meals in military mess halls. They had limited intelligence and if put into a starfighter would be unable to do anything but keep it in a steady orbit. Kir Kanos took one of them to Yinchorr to keep his Scimitar assault bomber in the orbit.

## Specifications

- **Name:** APA-5 droid
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Sensor:** White
