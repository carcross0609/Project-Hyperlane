# B0-LT

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B0-LT](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B0-LT)

Manufactured by Veril Line Systems, B0-LT, nicknamed "Bolts," was an EGL power droid from the planet Batuu. During the First Order occupation of the planet in 34 ABY, there was a 525-credit bounty on B0-LT for reprogramming, evading arrest, and illegal self-modification with a blast shield. The bounty also stated that bounty hunters should not attempt to blast the droid and requested that B0-LT be returned whole or in parts to Mubo, proprietor of the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on Batuu.
