# J-6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-6)

J-6 was a protocol droid who existed during the High Republic Era, belonging to aspiring inventor Avon Sunvale. A repurposed bodyguard droid, she was given to Avon by her mother Ghirra Starros around three years before the girl was sent to Port Haileap. While on Haileap, Avon secretly uploaded self-actualization programming into J-6, which allowed the droid to rewrite and develop her own personality. J-6 had pink-gold plating, and was equipped with multiple implanted blasters, as well as a force field umbrella. Around 160 BBY, J-6 accompanied Jedi Master Vernestra Rwoh when she was a Jedi Wayseeker, piloting the Cantaros.
