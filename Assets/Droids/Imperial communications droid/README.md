# Imperial communications droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial communications droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_communications_droid)

Imperial communications droids were utilized by the Galactic Federation Triumvirate in 138 ABY. One particular model was the companion of junk trader Ania Solo and Imperial Knight Jao Assam during a series of their adventures.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial communications droid
- **Class:** Communications droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Light gray, green
- **Equipment:**
  - Repulsorlifts
  - Sublight drive
  - Holoprojector
  - Communications equipment
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Federation Triumvirate
