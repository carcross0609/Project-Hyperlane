# Hunter-killer droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hunter-killer droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter-killer_droid)

The hunter-killer droid was a type of security droid utilized by the government of the Mid Rim planet Ikkrukk. During the New Republic Era, hunter-killer droids were used in conjunction with orbital and ground-based cannons to form Ikkrukk's defenses. They, along with the rest of Ikkrukk's defensive systems, were disabled by Ikkrukkian sympathizers to the First Order in 34 ABY. This allowed the First Order to attack Ikkrukk during their conquest of the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hunter-killer droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Affiliation:** Ikkrukk's government
