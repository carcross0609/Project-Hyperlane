# SB-53 Incineration Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SB-53 Incineration Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SB-53_Incineration_Droid)

The SB-53 Incineration Droid was a model of three-legged battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. SB-53 Incineration Droids were among the forces stationed at the ruptured vault under the command of the Shroud's lieutenant Peej.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SB-53 Incineration Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Green
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
