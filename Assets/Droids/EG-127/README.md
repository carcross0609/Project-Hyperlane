# EG-127

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EG-127](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EG-127)

EG-127 was a green-plated GNK-series power droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. During the Galactic Civil War, it served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic and was stationed in Echo Base, the Alliance's headquarters on the planet Hoth. When the Galactic Empire attacked Echo Base in 3 ABY, it was the first battle EG-127 had participated in. The droid tried to power as much equipment as possible, as it believed the Alliance was depending on it. It saved an internal data record of the event, including an image of the base's interior.
