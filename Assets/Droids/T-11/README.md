# T-11

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-11](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-11)

T-11 was a JV-Z1/S DataBank Droid who served the Galactic Republic as a military tactics strategist. Following the rise of the Galactic Empire, T-11 was reassigned to controlling a garbage scow. Due to error with reprogramming the droid, T-11 began to suffer from a form of "psychoses" and paranoia. T-11 eventually wound up in the service of the Kaarenth Dissension.
