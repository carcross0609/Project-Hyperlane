# GX-99

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GX-99](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GX-99)

GX-99 was Cragus 12's security droid and was totally loyal to his owner. He had the basic skills and programming of his line of droids, but had gone through numerous modifications and upgrades to enhance his abilities.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GX-99
- **Manufacturer:** Aro
- **Model:** Aro-GX Security Droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Armament:** Wrist blasters
- **Affiliation:** Cragus 12
