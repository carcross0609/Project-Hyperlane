# B8-M2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B8-M2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B8-M2)

B8-M2 was a battle droid in the employ of the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Galactic War. During the invasion of Belsavis, B8-M2 was stationed deep in the Tomb guarding one of the Rakata teleporters, and served as a communications link to Commander Vorel.
