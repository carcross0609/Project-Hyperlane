# N6-BC

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [N6-BC](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N6-BC)

N6-BC was a astromech droid who served the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War against the Sith Empire. In 3640 BBY, the droid was stationed on the planet Ilum, during the battle that took place there. On Ilum the droid operated a stall in the Republic base camp, from which it sold various weapon modifications. N6-BC had red plating and yellow sensors.
