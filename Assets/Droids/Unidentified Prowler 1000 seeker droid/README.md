# Unidentified Prowler 1000 seeker droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Prowler 1000 seeker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Prowler_1000_seeker_droid)

This Prowler 1000 seeker droid was deployed at a Star Tours transport terminal during the Galactic Civil War to investigate the whereabouts of a Rebel spy. The droid scanned the cabin of a StarSpeeder 1000 through its viewport and identified one of the passengers as the spy, but the vessel took off, pushing the Prowler droid into deep space.
