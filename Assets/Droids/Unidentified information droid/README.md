# Unidentified information droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified information droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_information_droid)

During the Old Sith Wars, a droid was present on the Outer Rim world of Dantooine. It was used by the local authorities, the Galactic Republic, and the Jedi Order as an information droid. Several weeks after the destruction of Taris, the droid was taken by Jedi Knight Bolook to the scene of Calder Nettic's murder. The droid assisted Bolook and an amnesiac Revan in solving the murder.
