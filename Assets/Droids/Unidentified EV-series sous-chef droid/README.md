# Unidentified EV-series sous-chef droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified EV-series sous-chef droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_EV-series_sous-chef_droid)

An EV-series supervisor droid served as a sous-chef droid in the palace of Bib Fortuna, who had taken it over from its previous owner, Jabba the Hutt, following the crime lord's death. While the sous-chef droid worked in the palace kitchen alongside a COO-series cook droid, the two heard a noise from nearby. Though the EV-series assumed the disturbance was a rat, the source of the sound had been the former bounty hunter Boba Fett and Master Assassin Fennec Shand, who had infiltrated the palace to retrieve Fett's starship. The cook droid sent the EV-series to investigate, and the sous-chef droid came across an open grate that Fett and Shand had exited through. Realizing the noise had not been a rat, the sous-chef ordered its companion to call for security, but Fett knocked the EV-series droid down with his gaffi stick.
