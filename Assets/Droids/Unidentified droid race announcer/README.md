# Unidentified droid race announcer

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid race announcer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_race_announcer)

A blue protocol droid worked as a Podrace commentator around 22 BBY. The droid operated out of the circuits on the Mid Rim planet of Mon Gazza, and commentated on the Mon Gazza Maze event on its qualifier races. The droid was known to get excited as races drew to a close, and reminded pedestrians to not walk onto the track during a race. The droid had a comlink attachment augmented on his cranial unit.
