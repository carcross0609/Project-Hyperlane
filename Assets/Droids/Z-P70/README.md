# Z-P70

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-P70](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-P70)

Z-P70, also known as Zeppo, was a droid that helped Watto in his shop on Tatooine. When an extra-galactic spacer crashed on the planet in need of starship parts from Watto's shop, Zeppo provided the spacer with information about Jabba's Game Plaza and the people who could help them earn credits.
