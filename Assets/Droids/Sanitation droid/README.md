# Sanitation droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sanitation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sanitation_droid)

Sanitation droids were automatons used to collect garbage and waste. In 4015 BBY, sanitation droids were among those who rose up in revolt against their sentient masters, under the banner of the HK-series assassin droid HK-01.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sanitation droid
- **Affiliation:** HK-01's droid army
