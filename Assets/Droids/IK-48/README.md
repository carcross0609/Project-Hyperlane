# IK-48

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IK-48](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IK-48)

IK-48 was Darth Vader's personal probe droid on the planet Otavon XII. During the early years of the New Order, IK-48 accompanied Vader into the planet's forest when the latter learned that the Ovoni rebels, who were disrupting the All Terrain Armored Transport factory with their constant attacks, had Jedi with them. When Vader was attacked by a Sugati, IK-48 identified a sound emitter as the source of the Sugati's blind rage and aggression.
