# Scavenger droid (Koboh)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scavenger droid (Koboh)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scavenger_droid_%28Koboh%29)

Scavenger droids were originally created to bring scrap to their owners but started to work independently by 9 BBY. They were seen digging up scrap from the planet of Koboh and hoarding it for themselves. They were also found on Coruscant level 2046 and Jedha.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scavenger droid
- **Homeworld:** Koboh
