# D3-O9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D3-O9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D3-O9)

D3-O9, also spelled Deethree-Ohnine, was a droid who served aboard the Star Cruiser Halcyon. Built centuries prior to the First Order-Resistance War by the founder of Chandrila Star Line, D3's primary role aboard the Halcyon was running ship logistics. She also spoke with Halcyon guests on the droid link in the cabins and suites there.

## Specifications

- **Name:** D3-O9
- **Birth:** Centuries before the First Order-Resistance War
- **Class:** Logistics droid
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** White and gold
- **Affiliation:**
  - Chandrila Star Line
  - Hutt Clan
