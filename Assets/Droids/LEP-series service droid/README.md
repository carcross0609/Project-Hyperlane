# LEP-series service droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LEP-series service droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LEP-series_service_droid)

The LEP-series service droid, also known as the LEP servant droid, was a model of third class service droid that saw use during the Clone Wars and beyond. Nuvo Vindi, Whorm Loathsom, and Tan Divo were each known to possess one of these droids. They were capable of speech, either in Galactic Basic or a series of chirps and squeaks and usually in a high-pitched voice, and performed a variety of tasks including serving refreshments and holding datapads.
