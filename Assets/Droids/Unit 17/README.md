# Unit 17

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unit 17](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unit_17)

Unit 17 was a BX-series droid commando who served the Confederacy of Independent Systems as a sergeant during the Clone Wars. Around 22 BBY, the Confederacy engaged the Galactic Republic on the planet Khorm in a battle for the valuable agrocite mineral. Unit 17 participated in the battle, serving under the Confederate Commander Asajj Ventress.
