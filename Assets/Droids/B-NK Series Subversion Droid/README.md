# B-NK Series Subversion Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B-NK Series Subversion Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-NK_Series_Subversion_Droid)

The B-NK Series Subversion Droid was an espionage droid model produced by the Shroud, a freelance spy during the Galactic War. Manufactured in the Shroud's factory on Nar Shaddaa, the B-NK Series appeared to be an ordinary protocol droid, but possessed hidden vibroblades, missile launchers, and blasters. B-NK1, one of the Shroud's lieutenants, was a B-NK Series, and the Shroud planned to disseminate the B-NK droids throughout the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B-NK Series Subversion Droid
- **Homeworld:** Nar Shaddaa
- **Class:** Espionage droid
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Armament:**
  - Shoulder missile launchers
  - Extendable vibroblades in forearms
  - Blasters in hands
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
