# R4-G0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [R4-G0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-G0)

R4-G0 was an astromech droid who maintained Jek-14's stealth starfighter during the Clone Wars during the time when Jek-14 was on the run from the Separatists and Galactic Republic. The starfighter had earlier been maintained by a different yellow plated R-series astromech droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R4-G0
- **Line:** R-series
- **Model:** R5 series astromech droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Red, Gray, White
- **Affiliation:** Jek-14
