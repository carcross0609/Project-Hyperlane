# Unidentified OOM-series security droid (close)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM-series security droid (close)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM-series_security_droid_%28close%29)

A OOM-series security droid was a member of a Separatist crew that operated on the planet Loradil during the New Republic Era after their Providence-class carrier was shot down. Around 6 ABY, the OOM-series security droid and other battle droids attempted to eliminate the human Han Solo, but was destroyed in a collective effort by other individuals allied with Solo shortly thereafter.
