# DA worker droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DA worker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DA_worker_droid)

The DA worker droid was a model of bipedal labor droid with a flat disc-shaped head and long arms ending in large hands. They could have white plating and sensors of both red and yellow. One such droid was present in Coronet Spaceport on the planet Corellia in 13 BBY. The droid carried a large cylinder on its back and was stood next to an elevated duty station manned by Imperials when the criminals Han and Qi'ra entered the spaceport. After Qi'ra was captured by members of the White Worms gang, the droid was stood by one of the queues in the spaceport and turned and watched as she was dragged away.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DA worker droid
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Sensor:** Red and yellow
