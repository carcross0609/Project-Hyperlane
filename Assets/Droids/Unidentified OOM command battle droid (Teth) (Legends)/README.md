# Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Teth)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid (Teth)/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_%28Teth%29/Legends)

During the Battle of Teth, an OOM command battle droid commanded Separatist droid forces during the Galactic Republic's vertical assault on the B'omarr Monastery. Before the battle, the OOM command battle droid decided to fire on the republic transports despite another droid's objections. They also irritably had to visually point out where to concentrate fire when the same droid expressed confusion as to where they were to fire. When the sergeant accidentally fell off the mesa, the commander saw it as the droid leaving their post and sternly demanded that they return.
