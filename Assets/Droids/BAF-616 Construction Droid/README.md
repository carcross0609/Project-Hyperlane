# BAF-616 Construction Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-616 Construction Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-616_Construction_Droid)

The BAF-616 Construction Droid was a large six-legged model of construction droid that was produced by Balmorran Arms at the Balmorran Arms Factory on the planet Balmorra. BAF-616 Construction Droids were repurposed to support Galactic Republic forces in their defense of the factory near the end of the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-616 Construction Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Construction droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
