# Therapy droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Therapy droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Therapy_droid)

Therapy droids, also known as an emotional-support droid, were a class of droids used to service recovering hospital patients. One prototype therapy droid was QT-9; who was given to the former New Republic soldier Dade Hetkins after he was discharged from a New Republic veterans' hospital in Darropolis on the planet Hosnian Prime in 5 ABY.
