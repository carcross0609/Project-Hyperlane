# R2-T0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-T0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-T0)

R2-T0 was an R2-series astromech droid owned by the Toydarian junk dealer Watto, who made it available for sale from his junk shop in the spaceport of Mos Espa on the planet Tatooine. The Toydarian also rented the droid to mechanics around the port, and in 32 BBY, R2-T0 worked with the welding droid WEL-1708 in the streets of Mos Espa.
