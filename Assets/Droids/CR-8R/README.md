# CR-8R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CR-8R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR-8R)

CR-8R, also referred to as "Crater," was a prim, masculine-programmed droid who belonged to the Graf family. He was built by Rhyssa Graf from a mash-up of different droid models, including the lower half of a Viper probe droid, the right arm of a 3PO-series protocol droid, the left arm of a load lifter droid, the barrel-shaped shell of an astromech droid, and the head of an antique Mark IV architect droid. CR-8R participated in a mission to the Outer Rim.
