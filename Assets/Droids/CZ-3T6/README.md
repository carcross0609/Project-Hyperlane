# CZ-3T6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CZ-3T6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-3T6)

CZ-3T6, or 3T, was a CZ-series secretary/comm droid who worked in the Galactic Senate. After the formation of the Empire, 3T was reassigned to the Imeici Spire.
