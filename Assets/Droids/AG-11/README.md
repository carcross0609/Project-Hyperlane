# AG-11

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AG-11](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AG-11)

AG-11 was an astromech droid in service of the Galactic Republic during the Galactic War. A counterpart of D-2B8, Agee was onboard Carrick Station when he learned of his destruction along with the frigate Toron Voq.
