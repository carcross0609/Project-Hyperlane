# A2 Accounting/Business Unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A2 Accounting/Business Unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A2_Accounting/Business_Unit)

The A2 Accounting/Business Unit was an accountant droid produced by Genetech Corporation during the time of the Galactic Empire. Its market price was 8000 credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A2 Accounting/Business Unit
- **Manufacturer:** Genetech Corporation
- **Class:** Accounting droid
- **Degree:** Class one droid
- **Cost:** 8,000 credits
- **Width:** 1 meters
- **Height:** 0.6 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Holographic recorder and projector
  - Fine manipulators (2)
  - Repulsorlift unit
  - Speech synthesizer
