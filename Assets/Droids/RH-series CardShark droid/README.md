# RH-series CardShark droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RH-series CardShark droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RH-series_CardShark_droid)

The RH-series CardShark droid was a model of dealer droid made by the Corporate Sector–based LeisureMech Enterprises to act as a dealer for the card game sabacc. The model was one of only three notable products produced by the company, with the others being the BD-3000 luxury droid and the C5 bartender droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RH-series CardShark droid
- **Manufacturer:** LeisureMech Enterprises
- **Class:** Dealer droid
