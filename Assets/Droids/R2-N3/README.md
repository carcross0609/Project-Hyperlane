# R2-N3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-N3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-N3)

R2-N3 was an R2-series astromech droid that was stationed onboard the Naboo Royal Starship. During the Invasion of Naboo, Queen Padmé Amidala and others attempted to break through the blockade surrounding the planet. R2-N3 was deployed onto the hull of the ship when the shield generator was damaged, but was quickly shot down by Trade Federation battleships.
