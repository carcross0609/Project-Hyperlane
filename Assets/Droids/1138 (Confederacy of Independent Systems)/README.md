# 1138 (Confederacy of Independent Systems)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [1138 (Confederacy of Independent Systems)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1138_%28Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems%29)

1138 was the designation of a B1-series battle droid in the service of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the height of the Clone Wars. It was known to continually malfunction at which point it began repeating, "Roger, roger," over and over again.
