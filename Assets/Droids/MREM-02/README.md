# MREM-02

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [MREM-02](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MREM-02)

MREM-02, or Moreemohtwo was a kitchen droid owned by Corpo the Hutt. When Corpo enslaved famous chef Slabba Drewl, Jedi Masters Micah Giiett and Plo Koon came to his rescue. Corpo then bet Drewl in a cooking contest with MREM-02. While Giiett distracted the audience, Koon swapped the ingredients. When Micah's Squirmer Stew won the competition, Corpo started hitting Moreemohtwo, causing it to fall over.
