# Unidentified tour guide droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified tour guide droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_tour_guide_droid/Legends)

This tour guide droid was present on Mandalore during the Takeover of Mandalore. The hapless droid was welcoming a group of people to Mandalore moments before it was shot multiple times and destroyed by the Shadow Collective.
