# P-60

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [P-60](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-60)

P-60, and its superior, P-59, were the two droidekas summoned by Trade Federation Nute Gunray to keep Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi from penetrating the bridge of the Saak'ak. The droidekas ended up forcing the pair of Jedi Knights to escape into a vent as a result of their firepower.
