# Super battle droid rocket trooper

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Super battle droid rocket trooper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Super_battle_droid_rocket_trooper)

The super battle droid rocket trooper, also known as the B2 super rocket trooper or B2 Rocket Trooper, was an upgraded variant of the B2-RP battle droid used by the Separatist Alliance in the last stages of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Super battle droid rocket trooper
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Combat Automata
- **Designer:** Techno Union
- **Line:** B2-series super battle droid
- **Model:** B2 super rocket trooper
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.93 meters (6 ft, 4 in)
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Red, white
- **Armament:** 2 wrist-mounted laser cannons
- **Equipment:** Jetpack
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
