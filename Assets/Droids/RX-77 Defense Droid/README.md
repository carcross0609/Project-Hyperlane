# RX-77 Defense Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RX-77 Defense Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RX-77_Defense_Droid)

The RX-77 Defense Droid was a battle droid model used by Galactic Republic during the Cold War. The model was bipedal and armed with a vibrosword, possessing no ranged weapons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RX-77 Defense Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Gender:** Yellow
- **Armament:** Vibrosword
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
