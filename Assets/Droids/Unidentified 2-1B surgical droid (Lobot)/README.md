# Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Lobot)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Lobot)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_2-1B_surgical_droid_%28Lobot%29)

A 2-1B surgical droid was stationed aboard the Rebel Alliance flagship Home One during the Imperial Era. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, the droid carried out a medical assessment of the cyborg Lobot on the request of Lando Calrissian, after Lobot's cybernetic implants caused the weapons systems of Home One to go in battle mode. The droid concluded that Lobot had been running through old Imperial battle plans and that the cyborg was likely to die as a result of the implants taking over control of his autonomic nervous system, which would eventually cause his heart to stop. Not willing to see his friend die, Calrissian wiped the droid's memory and set out to find a way to save his friend.
