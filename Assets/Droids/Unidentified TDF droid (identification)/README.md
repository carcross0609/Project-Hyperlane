# Unidentified TDF droid (identification)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified TDF droid (identification)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_TDF_droid_%28identification%29)

A police droid served in Janix Civil Defense's Tactical Defense Force on the planet Janix. Between 17 BBY and around 13 BBY, the Tactical Defense Droid was at Janix Civil Defense HQ in Janix city when a fellow police droid brought in the Twi'lek Devon Izara, who had been arrested for theft. When asked, Izara refused to provider her name or any identification, causing the droid to tell the Twi'lek that she must comply. Brander Lawson, a captain with the Tactical Defense Force, overheard the interaction and had the police droid bring Izara to his desk. After Izara continued to refuse to identify herself, Lawson had the droid take the Twi'lek to a cell.
