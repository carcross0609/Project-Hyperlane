# Unidentified COO-series cook droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified COO-series cook droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_COO-series_cook_droid)

A COO-series cook droid served in the kitchen of Jabba's Palace after it was taken over by Bib Fortuna following the death of Jabba the Hutt. When the former bounty hunter Boba Fett and Master Assassin Fennec Shand infiltrated the palace through a grate in the kitchen, the COO-series cook droid was preparing food alongside a EV-series sous-chef droid. The two droids heard the sound of the grate, which the EV-series guessed was a rat. The cook droid sent the sous-chef droid to find the rat, contacting the LEP-series rat catcher, but the EV droid was instead knocked down by Fett. Stepping out from behind its counter to confront Fett, the chef droid began to spin the knives on its six arms. However, before the droid could attack, Shand crept up behind it, slicing off its head.
