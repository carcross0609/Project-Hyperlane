# Snooze

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Snooze](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snooze)

Snooze was a blue and silver astromech droid in service to the beings Yates and Oota. It assisted them in maintaining Yates's Podracer for use on the Mos Zabu racetrack on Tatooine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Snooze
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automation
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Blue and silver
