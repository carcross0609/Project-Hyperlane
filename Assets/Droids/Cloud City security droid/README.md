# Cloud City security droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cloud City security droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cloud_City_security_droid)

A model of security droid was utilized in Cloud City on Bespin during its time under the Baron Administrator Lando Calrissian. The droids were shaped like a humanoid torso with two three-clawed arms. They were also equipped with a holoprojector, allowing the Baron Administrator to project his face and voice through the droids. When the children Moxxin, Traff, and Vanraya stole the Cantonarive crown coin from him, Calrissian used several of the droids to chase after them, speaking to the children through a droid to convince them to return the coin to him.
