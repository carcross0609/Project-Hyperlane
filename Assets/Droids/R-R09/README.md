# R-R09

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-R09](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-R09)

R-R09 was a droid that resided on the planet of Tatooine during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. There, it managed a small shop, where it also provided the knowledge of cybernetics construction to others.
