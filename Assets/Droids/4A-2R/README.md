# 4A-2R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [4A-2R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/4A-2R)

4A-2R was a RA-7 protocol droid droid that was a member of Hondo Ohnaka's pirate gang by the Clone Wars. 4A-2R was a valued part of Ohnaka's operation due to the surveillance equipment in his head.

- **Type:** Criminal

- **Class:** Protocol droid

- **Sensor:** Black

- **Affiliation:** Ohnaka Gang
