# Deera

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Deera](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deera)

Deera was a droid who was a member of the Second Revelation. Prior to the outbreak of the Scourge threat, Deera spoke with the Revelation's leader, Ajax Sigma, about his history.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Deera
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Bronze
