# Unidentified EX droid (Driggit Parse)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified EX droid (Driggit Parse)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_EX_droid_%28Driggit_Parse%29)

An EX droid was sent to the planet Eriadu within the Occlusion Zone, a region of space controlled by the Nihil marauder organization, during the High Republic Era. In 228 BBY, the droid was found by the human Nihil Driggit Parse, who requested to be the one to decrypt the EX droid and obtain the message within.
