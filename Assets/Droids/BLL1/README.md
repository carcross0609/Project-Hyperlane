# BLL1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BLL1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BLL1)

BLL1 was a CLL-M2 binary loadlifter who worked for Oridelve Incorporated on the planet Gavos. During the Gavos Droid Revolution, EV-8D3, figurehead of the New Droids' Rights Movement, used protocols to control him.
