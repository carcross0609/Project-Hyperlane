# P4T-GM

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [P4T-GM](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P4T-GM)

P4T-GM was a red-and-white-plated P4T protocol droid that was overworked as a conflict resolver in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, P4T-GM was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.

## Specifications

- **Name:** P4T-GM
- **Model:** P4T protocol
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Height:** 1.75 meters
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Sensor:** Red and white
