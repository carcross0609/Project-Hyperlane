# R9-D9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R9-D9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R9-D9)

R9-D9 was an astromech droid who served King Sanjay Rash of Onderon during the Clone Wars. Late in the war, R9 showed Rash and General Kalani holographic images of Steela Gerrera and Lux Bonteri as Akenathen Tandin informed them the rebel leaders also included Steela's brother Saw.
