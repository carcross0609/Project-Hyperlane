# WRM-2456

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [WRM-2456](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WRM-2456)

WRM-2456, nicknamed Worm, was a protocol droid who served Thane Chulok in the city of New Xaxxis on the planet Metagos. He had a golden inverted pyramid body on treads. After Chulok's apparent defeat during Jedi Master Mace Windu's mission to Metagos, WRM-2456 offered to assist the surviving citizens.

## Specifications

- **Name:** WRM-2456
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Plating:** Golden
- **Affiliation:** Chulok's organization
