# F-23-DKMx

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [F-23-DKMx](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F-23-DKMx)

F-23-DKMx, also known simply as F-23, was a masculine F series repair droid with blue photoreceptors and silver plating, with a blue trim. By the year 4.3 ABY, he was employed by the Star Tours travel agency at their Tomorrowland Starport. Along with F-24-AMTx, F-23 performed the final assembly of StarSpeeder 3000 and serviced their ion engines. He did not mind having to work overtime as the extra credits helped pay for his expensive tuition at the University of the Universe.
