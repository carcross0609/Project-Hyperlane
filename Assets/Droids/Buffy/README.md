# Buffy

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Buffy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Buffy)

Buffy was the name of a CLE-004 window cleaning droid that worked on the planet Coruscant with the INS-444 Mick. The two droids replaced the bedroom window of Senator Padmé Amidala's apartment after the original window was destroyed through the actions of Zam Wesell's assassin droid and Obi-Wan Kenobi.
