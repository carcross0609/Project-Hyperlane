# B5-R8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B5-R8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B5-R8)

B5-R8 was a shopkeeper droid that operated during the time of the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the resurgent Sith Empire. The droid was present on CZ-198 as a supply vendor providing captured gear for Republic forces and the Adjudicators engaged in the Seizure of CZ-198.
