# Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Imperial-class Star Destroyer)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Imperial-class Star Destroyer)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RA-7_protocol_droid_%28Imperial-class_Star_Destroyer%29)

During the reign of the Galactic Empire, an RA-7 protocol droid served aboard an Imperial-class Star Destroyer. There, at one point the droid encountered a smuggler who had escaped from detention and wanted to retrieve their starship's impounded cargo. The droid begged the smuggler to spare his life and pointed them to a way to access the Star Destroyer's cargo bay.
