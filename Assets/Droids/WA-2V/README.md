# WA-2V

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [WA-2V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WA-2V)

WA-2V, also known as 2V or TooVee, was the personal attendant droid of Princess Leia Organa. She had bluish fingers, a roll ball for movement and a stiff apron that passed for her hips.
