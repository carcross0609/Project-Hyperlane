# Unidentified RX-series pilot droid (Garel)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RX-series pilot droid (Garel)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RX-series_pilot_droid_%28Garel%29)

An RX-series pilot droid flew a GX1 short hauler several years before the Battle of Yavin. During a routine trip to the planet Garel, his ship was hijacked by members of the Spectres rebel cell. After the rebels were cornered by an Imperial light cruiser, the pilot droid was tricked into powering his shuttle. Shortly later, his shuttle was destroyed by explosives that the rebels had planted aboard before escaping onto their ally Ketsu Onyo's ship Shadow Caster. The droid pilot was disintegrated during this explosion which enabled the Spectres to escape.
