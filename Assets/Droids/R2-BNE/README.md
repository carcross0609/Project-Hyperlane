# R2-BNE

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-BNE](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-BNE)

R2-BNE was an R2-series astromech droid who could often be found on the plains of Naboo. He was colored in pink, blue, and white, and wore a hat that resembled rabbit ears.
