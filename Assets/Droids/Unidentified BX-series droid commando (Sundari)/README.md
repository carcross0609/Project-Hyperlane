# Unidentified BX-series droid commando (Sundari)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando (Sundari)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_%28Sundari%29)

This BX-series droid commando was stationed in the Sundari Royal Palace with a fellow droid during the peace conference between the Galactic Republic and the Separatist Alliance there. When Lux Bonteri entered and delivered an embittered accusation against Count Dooku for his involvement in the murder of his mother, the Separatist delegates ordered the commando droids to bring him to the ship that transported them to Mandalore. At the ship, the holographic projection of Count Dooku denied any involvement in Mina's death and then ordered the droids to execute Lux. However, before Lux could be killed, he was saved by Ahsoka Tano, who had followed him out of the palace to the vessel and took out the commando droids.
