# Jo (bounty droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jo (bounty droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jo_%28bounty_droid%29)

Jo, known as the Cannibal, officially J0-WR1, was a bounty droid who worked for the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. She earned her moniker by building herself over the years by taking a part of every droid she found or destroyed. Sorc Tormo sent her as the champion to secure the bounty placed on the head of Jedi Knight Cal Kestis. Jo set up a series of bounty hunters and Bedlam Raiders to fight Kestis, mimicking the Haxion Fight Pit from which Kestis had escaped years prior. Jo held a grudge against Kestis since she first saw him on Ordo Eris.
