# XL-Lioness

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XL-Lioness](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XL-Lioness)

The XL-Lioness was a variant of the TDL nanny droid. Unlike the base model, the XL-Lioness incorporated blaster cannons into its design and was marketed towards celebrities and politicians who were concerned about their children's safety.

## Specifications

- **Name:** XL-Lioness
- **Manufacturer:** AccuTronics
- **Line:** TDL nanny droid
- **Class:** Nanny droid
- **Degree:** Class Three
- **Cost:** 9,000 credits
- **Height:** 1.9 meters
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Armament:** Two blaster cannons (hidden)
- **Equipment:**
  - AA-1 VerboBrain
  - TranLang III communications module
