# 8D smelter droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [8D smelter droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/8D_smelter_droid)

The 8D smelter droid (or 8D Smelting Operator) was a heavy industrial droid model manufactured by Roche Hive Mechanical Apparatus Design and Construction Activity for Those Who Need the Hive's Machines, a company founded by the Verpine species on the Roche Asteroids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 8D smelter droid
- **Manufacturer:** Roche Hive Mechanical Apparatus Design and Construction Activity for Those Who Need the Hive's Machines
- **Homeworld:** Roche asteroid field
- **Class:** Smelter droid
- **Degree:** Class five droid
- **Cost:** 7250 credits
- **Height:** 1.83 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Yellow
