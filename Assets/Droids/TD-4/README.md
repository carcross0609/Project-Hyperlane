# TD-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [TD-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TD-4)

TD-4 was a droid that was the constant companion of Lop on the planet Tao. The droid possessed a number of abilities and tools that included a recording functionality, capturing moments of Lop's life, from her time as an orphan through until becoming part of a family.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TD-4
- **Death:** Between 12 BBY and 1 BBY, Tao
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Red and white
- **Equipment:** Holoprojector
- **Affiliation:** Yasaburō clan
