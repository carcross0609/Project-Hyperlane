# 93D

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [93D](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/93D)

93D was a silver bipedal droid of unknown manufacture. He ran 93D's, one of the best known chop shops in Point Nadir. His services were costly, but considered worth the money. He had two extra arms and a scathing sense of humor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 93D
- **Homeworld:** Point Nadir
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Equipment:** Two secondary manipulator arms
- **Affiliation:** 93D's
