# SE4-10

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SE4-10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SE4-10)

SE4-10 was a modified Industrial Automaton SE4 servant droid. Following Qual'om Soach's attack on the Nalmar crime family, Eelien Kirat decreed that all top personnel required protection. SE4-10, alongside SE4-12, served as camouflaged security droids for Kirat and Elana Nalmar. SE4-10 was fully capable of performing their regular duties, but if needed could deploy a blaster rifle hidden in the left arm, which fired from the palm of the droid's hand.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SE4-10
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Model:** Modified SE4 servant droid
- **Height:** 1.6 meters
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Armament:** Internal blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:** Nalmar crime family
