# M5-BZ

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M5-BZ/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M5-BZ/Legends)

M5-BZ was an R4 astromech droid member of D-Squad during the Clone Wars. This droid was also referred to as Beezee. He was the droid of Tera Sinube. M5-BZ was lost in hyperspace after he opened an airlock on board a Venator-class Star Destroyer while the ship was in hyperspeed.
