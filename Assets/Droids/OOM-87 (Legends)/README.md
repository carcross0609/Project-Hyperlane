# OOM-87

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OOM-87/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OOM-87/Legends)

OOM-87, also known as OOM87, was a Confederacy of Independent Systems battle droid which reported to General Grievous during the Second battle of Florrum. It was sliced in half by the Jedi Youngling Petro during the report.
