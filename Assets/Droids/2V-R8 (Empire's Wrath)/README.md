# 2V-R8 (Empire's Wrath)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [2V-R8 (Empire's Wrath)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2V-R8_%28Empire%27s_Wrath%29)

2V-R8, also known as Twovee Areight, was a Seneschal-series factotum droid owned by the Empire's Wrath. Assigned to Empire's Wrath's Fury-class Imperial interceptor in 3643 BBY, 2V-R8 was responsible for maintaining the starship and ensuring that its crew remained comfortable.
