# N2-3P0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [N2-3P0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N2-3P0)

N2-3P0 was a gold and blue protocol droid who served the Friends of Paran resistance group on Derilyn during the Galactic Civil War. N2-3PO had a feminine programming and was known to add Gamorrese curses in her speech.

- **Class:** Protocol droid
