# 10-K8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [10-K8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/10-K8)

10-K8, also known as K8, and Ten-Kayt, was a droid with feminine programming that belonged to the bodyguard Alys "Crash" Ongwa, who led a security team during the High Republic Era. The droid was present with Ongwa when she was hired to protect Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh after the attack on Valo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 10-K8
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Tan and red
- **Affiliation:** Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection
