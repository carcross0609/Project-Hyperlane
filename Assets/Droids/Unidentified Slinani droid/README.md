# Unidentified Slinani droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Slinani droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Slinani_droid)

A droid was one of many droids that served the religious warrior clan known as the Slinani, protecting Red Mist, one of their Migration Shrines. When the rogue archaeologist Chelli Aphra was hired to steal an artifact from Red Mist, she sliced into the droid, altering its programming so that it protected them rather than attacked. With the help of the droid, Aphra and her young ward Vulaada Klam reached the artifact, and used the droid to retrieve it from the gravity trap it was held in. The gravity trap destroyed the droid, but allowed Aphra to take the artifact.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Slinani droid
- **Death:** 3 ABY, Red Mist
- **Sensor:** Red, later green
- **Plating:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Slinani
