# Exoh

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Exoh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exoh)

Exoh was an astromech droid who worked with Filson Bood during the High Republic Era. The droid was with the scavenger when they discovered Jedi Master Atticus Cale's ship in the Jagoth system.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Galactic Republic
- **Name:** Exoh
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Yellow and gray
