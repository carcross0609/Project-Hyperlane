# OT-09 Defender Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-09 Defender Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-09_Defender_Droid)

The OT-09 Defender Droid was a tripedal battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. OT-09 Defender Droids were among the droids that defended the Okara Droid Factory from attack.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-09 Defender Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Equipment:** Arm-mounted blasters
