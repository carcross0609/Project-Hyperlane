# Fastlatch-class defense droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fastlatch-class defense droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fastlatch-class_defense_droid)

The Fastlatch-class defense droid, also known simply as a fastlatch droid, was a model of defense/security droid developed by the Trade Federation and made illegal in most star systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fastlatch-class defense droid
- **Manufacturer:** Trade Federation
- **Class:** Defense/security droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Red and silver
- **Armament:** Laser cannons
- **Affiliation:**
  - Pressure pirates
  - Clode Rhoden
