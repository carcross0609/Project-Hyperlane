# YVH-8 S-series battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YVH-8 S-series battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YVH-8_S-series_battle_droid)

YVH 8 S-series battle droids also known as space assault models, were the latest versions of the YVH 1 battle droids. Lando Calrissian deployed several during an assault on Marvid and Craitheus Qreph's Base Prime.
