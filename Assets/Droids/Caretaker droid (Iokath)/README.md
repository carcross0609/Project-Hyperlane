# Caretaker droid (Iokath)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Caretaker droid (Iokath)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caretaker_droid_%28Iokath%29)

Caretaker droid was a type of droid designed by the unidentified Iokath species to tend to the artificial world of Iokath. The species went extinct following the Iokath Civil War, and their caretaker droids were repurposed by ARIES into the combat units, aggressive to any intruders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Caretaker droid
- **Creator:** Unidentified Iokath species
- **Homeworld:** Iokath
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Brown
- **Armament:** Blaster Pistol
- **Affiliation:**
  - Unidentified Iokath species
  - ARIES
