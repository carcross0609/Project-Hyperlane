# 7EX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [7EX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/7EX)

7EX was one of the many B2-series super battle droids deployed by the CIS during the Outer Rim Sieges. It was destroyed by Obi-Wan Kenobi on the Invisible Hand in 19 BBY, during the Battle of Coruscant, while acting as a security droid for Count Dooku.
