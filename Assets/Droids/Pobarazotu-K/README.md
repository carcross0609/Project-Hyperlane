# Pobarazotu-K

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pobarazotu-K](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pobarazotu-K)

Pobarazotu-K was a Gree research droid dispatched by the starship Gray Secant to study the planet Tatooine. Accompanied by a squad of Gree battle droids, it investigated Rakata technology left on the world, but the planet's shifting sands slowed its excavations.
