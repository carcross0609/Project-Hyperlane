# Unidentified OOM-series security droid (Luke Skywalker)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM-series security droid (Luke Skywalker)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM-series_security_droid_%28Luke_Skywalker%29)

A OOM-series security droid was a member of a Separatist crew that operated on the planet Loradil during the New Republic Era after their Providence-class carrier was shot down. Around 6 ABY, the OOM-series security droid attempted to locate the human Jedi Luke Skywalker under a Armored Assault Tank, but was unable to before being flung with the Force into another security droid.
