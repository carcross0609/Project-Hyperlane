# Unidentified IG droid (Morta Bota)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified IG droid (Morta Bota)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_IG_droid_%28Morta_Bota%29)

An IG droid was working for Morta Bota. During the Best Builder Competition on Mertes he attempted to distract Zander Freemaker, who was competing against Bota, but was stopped by Rowan Freemaker, Kordi Freemaker and R0-GR.
