# Mecha-Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Mecha-Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mecha-Droid)

The Mecha-Droid was a model of worker droid with brown plating and yellow cabling. Consisting of the body of a MSE-6 series repair droid, manufactured by Rebaxan Columni, and the two arms and two legs of a Serv-O-Droid, Inc. DUM-series pit droid. On the planet Tatooine, a group of Jawas sold Mecha-Droid blueprints from a Sandcrawler Shop near the Junkers Cantina. The default Mecha-Droid blueprint was sold at 24,000,000 credits, a golden design at 96,000,000 credits, a diamond-colored version at 192,000,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design at 384,000,000 credits.
