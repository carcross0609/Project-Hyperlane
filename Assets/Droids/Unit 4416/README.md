# Unit 4416

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unit 4416](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unit_4416)

Unit 4416 was a B1-series battle droid in service of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Stationed aboard Tambor Deep Space Centre, it assisted Gorgol the Handy in defense against the Republic siege. After the Republic forces seemingly broke for a run, Gorgol assigned two droid squads to investigate if their enemy was really gone. Unit 4416 led one such squad and reported the left flank to be clear, moments before being destroyed by the Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi.
