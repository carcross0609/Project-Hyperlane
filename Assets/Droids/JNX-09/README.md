# JNX-09

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JNX-09](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JNX-09)

JNX-09, also known as Jinx, was a droid partner to the master thief Ace Kallisto. She was considered to be a master codebreaker, being able to bypass any lock across the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** JNX-09
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** White and yellow
- **Affiliation:** Ace Kallisto's crew
