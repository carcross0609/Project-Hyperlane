# R2-Y9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-Y9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-Y9)

R2-Y9 was an astromech droid that served in Shadow Squadron in the early stages of the Clone Wars. It had and orange and white body with an orange and gray dome.
