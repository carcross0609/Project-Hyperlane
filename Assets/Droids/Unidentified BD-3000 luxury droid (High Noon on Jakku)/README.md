# Unidentified BD-3000 luxury droid (High Noon on Jakku)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified BD-3000 luxury droid (High Noon on Jakku)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BD-3000_luxury_droid_%28High_Noon_on_Jakku%29)

The droid was one of the supposedly rogue droids that attacked Constable Zuvio during his investigation of a banking ship robbery in Niima Outpost. As a part of Rikard Lovas's plot to steal the electronic credits of the citizens of Niima Outpost on Jakku, several droids were sent to an abandoned farmhouse to wait for Constable Zuvio. The BD-3000 droid was shot and destroyed at a distance by Zuvio.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified BD-3000 luxury droid
- **Homeworld:** Jakku
- **Death:** Sometime before the Escape from Jakku in 34 ABY
- **Model:** BD-3000 luxury droid
- **Plating:** Scarred and corroded chassis
- **Armament:** Czerka-93U hunting rifle
