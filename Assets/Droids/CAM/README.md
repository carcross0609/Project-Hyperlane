# CAM

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CAM](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CAM)

CAM was a cam droid affiliated with the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts who traveled the galaxy with the Safari droid SF-R3. Together, the two droids catalogued an abundance of wildlife across many planets, with CAM broadcasting footage across the HoloNet and sending findings back to the Society.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CAM
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Corporation
- **Class:** Holocam droid
- **Pronouns:** He/Him
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts
