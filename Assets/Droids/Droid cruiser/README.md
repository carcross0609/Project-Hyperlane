# Droid cruiser

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droid cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_cruiser)

The droid cruisers were a form of droid-operated combat units that were used to defend Tig Fromm's base on Ingo. In 15 BBY, droid cruisers were used to prevent Thall Joben, Jord Dusat, Kea Moll, R2-D2, and C-3PO from escaping from the base. However, C-3PO reactivated the Fromm Tower Droids who attacked the droid cruisers, allowing the group to escape in the confusion.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Droid cruiser
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Affiliation:** Fromm Gang
