# R6-H5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R6-H5/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R6-H5/Legends)

R6-H5, nicknamed Ar-Six, was the astromech droid of Jedi Master Kit Fisto. He accompanied his Master on many missions for the Jedi Council. He was known for being tentative, often worrying about his master, and for having a dislike of strange situations. Ar-Six has a domed R2-series head, typically R6-series astromechs had cone shaped heads similar to the R5-series. It is unknown why Ar-Six has this modification, possibly for use in Fisto's starfighter.
