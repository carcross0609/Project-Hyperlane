# Bartender droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bartender droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bartender_droid)

Bartender droids were a type of droid that existed in the galaxy. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, one such model of bartender droid, the C5 bartender droid, was known to serve in the suites of the Alisandre Hotel on the planet Coruscant. Bartender droids could also be repurposed individuals of different models, such as BU-11 who worked on Ziro's Palace, another droid who worked as the bartender of a cantina on Lau, a former DUM-series pit droid who worked as a bartender in the Suria space station and M0 who worked the droid cantina The Resistor on Plazir-15.
