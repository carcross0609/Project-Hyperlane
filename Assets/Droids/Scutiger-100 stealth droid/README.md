# Scutiger-100 stealth droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scutiger-100 stealth droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scutiger-100_stealth_droid)

The Scutiger-100 stealth droid, also known as the millicreep droid, was an insect-like assassin droid used by the Umbaran Militia. Crawling around on six electrified legs, millicreeps were able to use their tail to electrocute an organic being to death. The droids excelled at infiltration and their electrified legs enabled them to be effective at electroshock.
