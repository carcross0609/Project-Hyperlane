# Unidentified RQ protocol droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RQ protocol droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RQ_protocol_droid)

An RQ protocol droid served the Czerka Corporation aboard the cruiser Leverage. The RQ protocol droid processed the former Czerka slave Rahara Wick after she had been captured on the moon of the planet Pijal. Wick attempted to appeal to the protocol droid, but the droid dismissed her, sending her through to finish the process.
