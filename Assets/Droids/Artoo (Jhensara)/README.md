# Artoo (Jhensara)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Artoo (Jhensara)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Artoo_%28Jhensara%29)

Artoo was an R2-series astromech droid who belonged to the starship captain Jhensara. Artoo was one of three droids on the ship alongside Arthree and Arfive.
