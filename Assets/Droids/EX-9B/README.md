# EX-9B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EX-9B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EX-9B)

EX-9B, or Ninebee was an EX communication droid that was part of Jedi Master Silandra Sho's Republic Pathfinder team. The droid was given the nickname "Nibs" by Obik Dennisol, a Mirialan medic who served on the same team. In 382 BBY, EX-9B and the team traveled to twin planets Aubadas and Gloam in search of fellow pathfinder team Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two.
