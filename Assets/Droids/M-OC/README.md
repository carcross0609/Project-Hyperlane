# M-OC

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M-OC](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-OC)

M-OC was an Imperial hunter droid created by Emperor Palpatine and was tasked by the Emperor with locating a legendary weapon. At some point, M-OC encountered the Freemakers and relentlessly chased them through several planets. Eventually M-OC failed in his mission and was defeated along with the Empire. R0-GR, a veteran B1 battle droid of the Clone Wars, considered M-OC to wield his twin lightsabers with more skill and grace than most Jedi Masters.
