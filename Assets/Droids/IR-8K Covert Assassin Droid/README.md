# IR-8K Covert Assassin Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IR-8K Covert Assassin Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-8K_Covert_Assassin_Droid)

The IR-8K Covert Assassin Droid was a model of humanoid battle droid used by the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. Equipped with a blaster rifle and stealth field generator, IR-8K Covert Assassin Droids were among the forces guarding B-NK1's base in the back room of the Dealer's Den cantina on Coruscant

## Specifications

- **Name:** IR-8K Covert Assassin Droid
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Brown
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
- **Equipment:** Stealth field generator
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's syndicate
