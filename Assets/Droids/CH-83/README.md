# CH-83

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CH-83](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CH-83)

CH-83 was a BB-series astromech droid with a black sensor and green, gray, and white paneling. As a member of the BB-series, it was manufactured by Industrial Automaton and stood 0.67 meters tall. In 34 ABY, during the First Order's occupation of the planet Batuu, CH-83 was displayed among droid parts on one of the shelves inside Droid Depot, a store in Batuu's Black Spire Outpost.
