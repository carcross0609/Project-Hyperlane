# Black Sun gladiator droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Black Sun gladiator droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Sun_gladiator_droid)

The Black Sun gladiator droid was a gargatuan droid model used by Xizor as a sentry droid in his palace. The droid was divided into three parts: lower body, torso, and head. It could not be destroyed easily, as all the sections were capable of operating independently.
