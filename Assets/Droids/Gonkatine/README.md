# Gonkatine

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Gonkatine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gonkatine)

Gonkatine was a red GNK-series power droid that resided in the Unknown Regions planet of Exegol. He attempted to join the Knights of Ren; however, they were not allowed to join. After being denied his place in the Knights of Ren, Gonkatine attempted to create his own band of "mischiefs," the Knights of Gonk, which consisted of Gigagonk, Invisigonk and Mad Dog Gonkdo. The Knights of Gonk planned their first strike, destroying every remnant of the Battle of Exegol at the Sith Citadel. However, after planning their strike, they realized it was not really a bad and mischievous action, but instead a good one.
