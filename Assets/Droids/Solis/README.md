# Solis

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Solis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solis)

Solis was a Tac-Spec FIII Footman droid and counterpart to Fidelis. He was functional, but unlike his painted counterpart, he had bare, scratched metal showing, and was not programmed with extreme loyalty. Besides his original factory parts he apparently had upgrades made to himself such as the Rika/Moab mini rail cannon and possibly many others

## Specifications

- **Name:** Solis
- **Birth:** c. 900 BBY–425 BBY
- **Death:** 20 BBY
- **Manufacturer:** Tac-Spec Corporation
- **Model:** FIII Footman droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Armament:**
  - Mini rail cannon
  - Numerous other weapons
- **Equipment:**
  - Einblatz/Docker ultrahigh-fidelity auditory sensors
  - Numerous other tools and upgrades
