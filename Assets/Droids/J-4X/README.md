# J-4X

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-4X](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-4X)

J-4X was a droid that was active in the year 34 ABY. J-4X was present when Guavian Death Gang pirate Tara Rashin attacked a cargo vessel, and the droid communicated with a droid repair technician aboard the starship. The droid was shot by Rashin, but later recovered from the wreckage and repaired by the technician.
