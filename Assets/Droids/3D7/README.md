# 3D7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [3D7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3D7)

3D7 was a B1-series battle droid stationed on the planet Gavos and was part of the New Droids' Rights Movement, a group of droids that was focused on spreading violent rebellion against organic life forms throughout the galaxy.
