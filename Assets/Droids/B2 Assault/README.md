# B2 Assault

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B2 Assault](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2_Assault)

The B2 Assault was an enhanced B2-HA super battle droid that the Confederacy of Independent Systems deployed during the Third battle of Felucia to patrol the Separatist base. It was equip with tougher armor, built in laser cannon and cannon arm than the B2-HA series super battle droid. During the battle, the famed Clone Commando Boss commissioned a Jedi Knight to destroy this droid.
