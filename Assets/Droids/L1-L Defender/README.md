# L1-L Defender

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [L1-L Defender](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L1-L_Defender)

The L1-L Defender was a type of small battle droid available during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. It was used by the Gree on numerous planets, including Ilum.
