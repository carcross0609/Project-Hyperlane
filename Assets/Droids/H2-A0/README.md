# H2-A0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [H2-A0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H2-A0)

H2-A0 was an astromech droid that resided on Tatooine during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. There, it managed a small shop, where it also provided the knowledge of cybernetics construction to others.
