# Unidentified heavy battle droid model

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified heavy battle droid model](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_heavy_battle_droid_model)

A heavy battle droid model was a model of combat droid used by the Trade Federation during the Invasion of Naboo. Several of these droids were stationed on the streets of Theed as escorts of a Trade Federation aerial tank droid.
