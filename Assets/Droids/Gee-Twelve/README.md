# Gee-Twelve

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gee-Twelve](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gee-Twelve)

Gee-Twelve was a droid who worked at Y3-99's Bounty Services, a business operated by the droid Y3-99 on the Outer Rim Territories celestial body Halmad. The former towered above their employer and had gray-colored plating.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gee-Twelve(depicted standing behind Y3-99)
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Affiliation:** Y3-99's Bounty Services
