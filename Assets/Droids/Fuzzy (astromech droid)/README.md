# Fuzzy (astromech droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fuzzy (astromech droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fuzzy_%28astromech_droid%29)

Fuzzy was an R2-series astromech droid who served as Myri Antilles's navigator during Wraith Squadron's mission to the planet Kuratooine to apprehend corrupt Galactic Alliance General Stavin Thaal for his involvement with the Lecersen Conspiracy.
