# ZZ-10

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ZZ-10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZZ-10)

ZZ-10 was a droid who accompanied the Wookiee male Jedi Arkoff during the High Republic Era. He was buried on the planet Banchii with Azlin Rell's journal to protect it until he was discovered by the Nihil pirate organization.
