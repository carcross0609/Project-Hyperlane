# OHK-99

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OHK-99](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OHK-99)

The OHK-99 was a model of battle droid built by Grathan's scientists that were in fact Human soldiers converted into droids. Twenty soldiers under the command of Captain Sarnova, including Ensign Tholver Shan, were captured by Lord Grathan's forces and converted to OHK-99 units.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OHK-99
- **Creator:** Grathan
- **Class:** Battle droid
