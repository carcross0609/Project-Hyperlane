# CP-6 Refraction Probe

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CP-6 Refraction Probe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CP-6_Refraction_Probe)

The CP-6 Refraction Probe was a model of small probe droid used by the Chiss spy Maki'voro, a lieutenant of the spymaster known as the Shroud during the Galactic War. One of a number of probe models that Maki'voro used, the CP-6 was designed to hover near Maki'voro and deflect enemy attacks.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CP-6 Refraction Probe
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's syndicate
