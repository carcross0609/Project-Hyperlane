# PZ-80B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PZ-80B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PZ-80B)

PZ-80B, also known as Peezee-Eightybee or Eightybee, was a protocol droid who worked as a translator, referee and announcer at an underground fighting ring on Vespaara.
