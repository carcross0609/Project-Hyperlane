# MD-0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-0)

MD-0 was an MD-0 medical droid assigned to the Imperial Navy EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate. Its service carried over once the ship's crew mutined and took over control of the vessel. MD-0 was responsible for seeing crew members reporting illness so that they could be diagnosed. MD-0 also assisted MD-5 during physical examinations.
