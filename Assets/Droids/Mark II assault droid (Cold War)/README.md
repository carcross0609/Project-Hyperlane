# Mark II assault droid (Cold War)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark II assault droid (Cold War)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_II_assault_droid_%28Cold_War%29)

The Mark II assault droids were training droids used by the Jedi Liam Dentiri to test the physical capabilities of Padawans during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark II assault droid
- **Class:** Training droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Brown with yellow stripes
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
- **Firstmade:** By 3643 BBY
