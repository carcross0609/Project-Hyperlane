# HK-130

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HK-130](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HK-130)

HK-130 was a sentry droid of Balmorran manufacture. It consisted of a tiny metal ball propelled by microrepulsors and covered with silver studs that emitted powerful stun beams. Scanning each individual it encountered in order to determine its threat level, it was considered a troublemaker.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HK-130
- **Class:** Sentry droid
- **Armament:** Stun beams
- **Equipment:** repulsorlift
