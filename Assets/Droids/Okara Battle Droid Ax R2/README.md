# Okara Battle Droid Ax R2

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Okara Battle Droid Ax R2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Okara_Battle_Droid_Ax_R2)

The Okara Battle Droid Ax R2 was a model of three-legged battle droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. Equipped with arm-mounted blaster cannons, a number of them were used to defend the exterior of the factory during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Okara Battle Droid Ax R2
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Armament:** 2 blaster cannons
