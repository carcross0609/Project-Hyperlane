# Hologram VJ

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hologram VJ](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hologram_VJ)

Hologram VJ was a feminine BD-3000 luxury droid who hosted the Grand Army of the Republic broadcast during the Clone Wars. During the first year of the war, the clones serving at Rishi Station listened to the broadcast as Hologram VJ gave a shout out to the Mud Jumpers, a group of clones that were part of the 224th Clone division.
