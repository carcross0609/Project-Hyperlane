# Unidentified RIC labor droid (Zuvio)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified RIC labor droid (Zuvio)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RIC_labor_droid_%28Zuvio%29)

The RIC droid was one of the supposedly rogue droids that attacked Constable Zuvio during his investigation of a banking ship robbery in Niima Outpost. As a part of Rikard Lovas's plot to steal the electronic credits of the citizens of Niima Outpost on Jakku, several droids were sent to an abandoned farmhouse to wait for Constable Zuvio. During the skirmish at the abandoned Jakku moisture farm the droid was destroyed when an EG-6 gonk droid caused the farmhouse to explode.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified RIC labor droid
- **Death:** Sometime before the Escape from Jakku in 34 ABY
- **Model:** RIC-series general labor droid
