# Forre Musa

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Forre Musa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forre_Musa)

Forre Musa was waiter droid in Zerria's Bar on Drall in 40 ABY. Although he was only programmed to serve as a waiter, Musa offered patrons what he called "intelligent entertainment", ranging from political satire to poetry. Musa was a prompt waiter, even though few ever asked for his entertainment. His voicebox was shot by the Mandalorian Mirta Gev, much to the entertainment of the other Mandalorians present and Forre's owner, a Dabi.
