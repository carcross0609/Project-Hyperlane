# RO-Z67

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RO-Z67](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RO-Z67)

RO-Z67 was a heavily modified RO-series childcare and nanny droid employed by Darth Sidious, Dark Lord of the Sith, during the Clone Wars. Stationed at a secret facility on the planet Mustafar, RO-Z67 worked with several Force-sensitive children that were kidnapped by the bounty hunter Cad Bane on behalf of Sidious. Sidious planned to train the children to become dark side spies, but RO-Z67 was destroyed when Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker and his Padawan apprentice Ahsoka Tano rescued the children.
