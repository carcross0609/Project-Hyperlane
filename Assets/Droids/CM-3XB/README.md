# CM-3XB

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CM-3XB](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CM-3XB)

CM-3XB was a programmer droid that was forced to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, CM-3XB was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CM-3XB
- **Class:** Programmer droid
- **Plating:** White, Green
