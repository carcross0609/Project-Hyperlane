# Aquatic battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aquatic battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aquatic_battle_droid)

Aquatic battle droids were a type of battle droid used for underwater operations. When Jedi Grand Master Yoda, Jedi Master Mace Windu, Jedi Master Bant Eerin were testing Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi and Padawan Anakin Skywalker, a pair of aquatic battle droids were deployed against the mentor and apprentice on the planet Glee Anselm. Skywalker used animal control to make a whale attack them.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aquatic battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Energy harpoon
- **Equipment:** Jetpack
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order (secretly)
