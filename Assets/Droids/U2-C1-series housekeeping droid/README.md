# U2-C1-series housekeeping droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [U2-C1-series housekeeping droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/U2-C1-series_housekeeping_droid)

The U2-C1-series housekeeping droid was manufactured by Publictechnic and sold for commercial use as a janitor and maid. They were common sights throughout the galaxy and often fell beneath the notice of most individuals. This trait made the U2-C1 useful to criminal elements, who reprogrammed the droids to commit crimes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** U2-C1-series housekeeping droid
- **Manufacturer:** Publictechnic
- **Class:** Housekeeping droid
- **Degree:** Class three droid
- **Cost:** 750 credits
- **Height:** Under 1 meter
