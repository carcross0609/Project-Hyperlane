# Morp droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Morp droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morp_droid)

Morp droids were a series of small seeker droid that were developed by the Dark Jedi Asajj Ventress. They were named after the morp species of Dagobah.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Morp droid
- **Creator:** Asajj Ventress
- **Class:** Seeker droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:**
  - Electroshock armor
  - Pincers
- **Equipment:** Repulsorlift
