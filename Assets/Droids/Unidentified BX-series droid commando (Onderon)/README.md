# Unidentified BX-series droid commando (Onderon)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando (Onderon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_%28Onderon%29)

This BX-series droid commando was among the CIS reinforcements sent to Onderon with General Kalani during the Onderonian Civil War. The droid remained stationed at the palace with Kalani, King Sanjay Rash, and Okalin to protect them from the rebelling citizens of Iziz while the reinforcements were sent to the highlands to deal with the rebels. Later, the droid watched as Rash demanded more reinforcements from Count Dooku.
