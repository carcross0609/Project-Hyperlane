# ZX-10 Combat Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ZX-10 Combat Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZX-10_Combat_Droid)

The ZX-10 Combat Droid was an advanced model of battle droid developed by Czerka Corporation at their research moon CZ-198. Rasmus Blys had the line fitted with a tamper-proof security system that completely deactivated any sliced units, forcing the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire to destroy the ZX-10s still in their pods during the fighting at CZ-198.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ZX-10 Combat Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Corporation
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Yellow
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
