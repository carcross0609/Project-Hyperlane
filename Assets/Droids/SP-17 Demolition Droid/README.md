# SP-17 Demolition Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SP-17 Demolition Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SP-17_Demolition_Droid)

The SP-17 Demolition Droid was a model of demolition probe droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. Designed to self-destruct and injure enemies with the detonation, SP-17 Demolition Droids were among the forces that defended the Shroud's factory on the moon Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SP-17 Demolition Droid
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
