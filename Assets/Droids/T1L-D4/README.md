# T1L-D4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T1L-D4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T1L-D4)

T1L-D4, also referred to as "Tilda," was a droid who worked as a bounty hunter alongside Killena during the Imperial Era. The two hunted Cal Kestis and confronted him when the Stinger Mantis crew infiltrated a Haxion Brood base.
