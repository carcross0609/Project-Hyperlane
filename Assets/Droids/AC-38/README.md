# AC-38

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AC-38](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AC-38)

AC-38, or "Ace," was an AC-series pilot droid who had its StarSpeeder hijacked by a rogue protocol droid and taken on a dangerous trip across the galaxy. Because of the incident, all AC-series units were retired from service and replaced by RX-series droids, which proved to be only slightly more reliable. Rumors indicated that the tour company for which Ace worked had him demoted to janitorial duties.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AC-38"Ace"

- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Silver and blue, with orange detailing
