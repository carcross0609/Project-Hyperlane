# Droid laser turret (Primacy)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Droid laser turret (Primacy)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_laser_turret_%28Primacy%29)

The Bedlam Raiders made use of shielded droid laser turrets built into the wreckage of the Trade Federation Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter Primacy on the planet Koboh during the Imperial Era. The Jedi Cal Kestis worked his way past the turrets while fighting the Raiders on board the freighter wreckage in 9 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Droid laser turret
- **Class:** Turret-droid
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Affiliation:** Bedlam Raiders
