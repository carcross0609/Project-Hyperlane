# The Maestro

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [The Maestro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Maestro)

The Maestro was a Haxion Brood Bounty Droid that served the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. Once a loadlifter droid, the Haxion Brood reprogrammed the droid, who was enhanced with salvaged parts. They had silver plating, blue sensors, and a built-in wrist blaster.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Bounty hunter

- **Death:** 14 BBY
