# T-12

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-12)

T-12 was a service droid used by the Jedi to catalog Naddist artifacts being shipped to Ossus following the Freedon Nadd Uprising. During the Conclave at Deneba, T-12 was present, assisting Cay Qel-Droma and the other Jedi. When the Krath attacked Deneba during the conclave, T-12 was one of the droids subjected to the Krath programming trap, thus turning T-12 from a humble servant droid into a deadly assassin.
