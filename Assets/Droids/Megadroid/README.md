# Megadroid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Megadroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Megadroid)

The Megadroid was a type of battle droid and superweapon developed by the Confederacy of Independent Systems via Baktoid Combat Automata during the Clone Wars on the planet Golatha. At one point during the war, the Jedi Anakin Skywalker and Yoda undertook a mission to Golatha to destroy the factory where they were being built. One prototype survived the destruction of the factory but was taken down by the two Jedi after much difficulty. In 3 ABY or 4 ABY, the crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure had one Megadroid, which was then destroyed by the cyborg Beilert Valance with the help of T'onga, Losha Tarkon, Zuckuss, and 4-LOM.
