# IG-113

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IG-113](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG-113)

IG-113 was an IG-100 MagnaGuard serving the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars on board a Munificent-class star frigate, who was bigger than other MagnaGuards. At about 21 BBY, the Republic shot down the frigate, although IG-113 survived the crash. The Republic sent down Clone troopers to finish off the remaining battle droids, but they encountered Death Watch resistance. A Jedi Knight investigated the wreck and found IG-113, destroying him.
