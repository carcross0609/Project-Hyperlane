# Gonko

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Gonko](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gonko)

Gonko was a Power droid who resided on in the Jundland Wastes on Tatooine. He wanted to be escorted and protected from the Tusken Raiders, but fell over on the way to his destination
