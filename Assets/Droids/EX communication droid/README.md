# EX communication droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EX communication droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EX_communication_droid)

EX communication droids, also known as EX droids, Ex-droids, Ee-Ex comm droids, or Eeex, were a model of communications droid used during the High Republic Era to communicate across the galaxy. One such droid was used by a Republic Pathfinder team that discovered treasure; however, when the team was all killed during a pirate raid, the droid's whereabouts were unknown for centuries afterward until it surfaced again in the possession of a descendant of a member of the expedition, becoming sought-after as it pointed to the treasure.

## Specifications

- **Name:** EX communication droid
- **Model:** Communications droid
- **Height:** 97 centimeters (3 ft, 2 in)
- **Plating:** Beige with blue- and copper-colored plating on top
- **Armament:**
  - Photoreceptor
  - Several manipulator arms
  - An antenna
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
