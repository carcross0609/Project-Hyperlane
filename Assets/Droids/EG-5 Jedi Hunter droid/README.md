# EG-5 Jedi Hunter droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EG-5 Jedi Hunter droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EG-5_Jedi_Hunter_droid)

The EG-5 Jedi Hunter droid, also called the EG droid, was a model of battle droid created by the InterGalactic Banking Clan during the Clone Wars. Its sole purpose was to eliminate Jedi. Two members of the line were EG-05 and EG-07.

## Specifications

- **Name:** EG-5 Jedi Hunter droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** Lightsabers (2)
- **Affiliation:**
  - InterGalactic Banking Clan
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Retired:** c. 21 BBY
