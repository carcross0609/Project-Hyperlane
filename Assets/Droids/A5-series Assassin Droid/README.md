# A5-series Assassin Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A5-series Assassin Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A5-series_Assassin_Droid)

The A5-series Assassin Droid was a model of assassin droid operating during the Galactic Civil War. A reprogrammed unit, A5, served as a bouncer at the Farrimmer Cafe.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A5-series Assassin Droid
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Height:** 1.6 meters
- **Armament:** Internal blaster rifle
- **Equipment:**
  - Humanoid torso
  - Repulsorlift propulsion unit
  - Two audio sensors
  - Two photoreceptors
  - Broadband antenna
  - Vocabulator
