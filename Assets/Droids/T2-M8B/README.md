# T2-M8B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T2-M8B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T2-M8B)

T2-M8B was a haughty R-series astromech droid that had green and silver paneling. By the time of the blockade of the Anoat sector by the Galactic Empire, the astromech had operated the defenses of Cloud City during multiple local conflicts. Rather than a good cause, T2-M8B was mostly interested in a good fight.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T2-M8B
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Plating:** Green and silver
