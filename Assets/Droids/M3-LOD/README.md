# M3-LOD

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M3-LOD](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M3-LOD)

M3-LOD, nicknamed Melody, was an astromech droid that was created by Ca Isiora and Telem prior to the Battle of Endor. In 4 ABY, Melody took part in the battle and flew alongside Isiora during the conflict, but was taken by another pilot after the second Death Star was obliterated by the Rebel Alliance. As Isiora feared she and Telem were killed during the event, she was relieved after seeing the two on the Home One not long after.
