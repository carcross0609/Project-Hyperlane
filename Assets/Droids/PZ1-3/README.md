# PZ1-3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PZ1-3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PZ1-3)

PZ1-3 was a droid with masculine programming who served the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era, piloting the Padawan academic cruiser Star Hopper. He was present aboard the Star Hopper when the cruiser was redirected to assist the Trymant system after it fell victim to an Emergence of the Great Hyperspace Disaster. PZ1-3 later piloted the Star Hopper when it brought Lula Talisola, Zeen Mrala and Kantam Sy on a fact-finding mission back to Trymant IV.
