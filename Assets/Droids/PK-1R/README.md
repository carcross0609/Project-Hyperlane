# PK-1R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PK-1R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PK-1R)

PK-1R was a droid and a member of the Leisurenauts, an all-droid musical band that performed in Kaas City's Nexus Room cantina on Dromund Kaas during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire.
