# C2-N2 (Barsen'thor)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C2-N2 (Barsen'thor)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C2-N2_%28Barsen%27thor%29)

C2-N2, also known as Seetoo Enntoo, was a Hollis-series steward droid owned by the Jedi Master known as the Barsen'thor. Assigned to the Jedi's Defender-class light corvette as its steward in 3643 BBY, C2-N2 was responsible for maintaining the starship and ensuring that the Jedi's crew remained comfortable.
