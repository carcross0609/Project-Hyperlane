# Unidentified retired battle droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified retired battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_retired_battle_droid)

A battle droid equipped with full combat and defense systems worked at The Alcazar, a cantina in Myrra on Akiva. in 3 ABY. They had been grey plating and yellow photoreceptors. By 3 ABY, they had grown dissatisfied with their work at the cantina since it did not offer enough to utilized their combat abilities.
