# R-1 recon droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-1 recon droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-1_recon_droid)

The R-1 recon droid was a standard-issue reconnaissance droid used by Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. It was built by Arakyd Industries and was based on design of the DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid, originally created by the Sith. The R-1 was popular with snipers, planetary garrisons and shock troops. Some of them had also found a way into the hands of smugglers, bounty hunters, and other independent operations.
