# Kho Phon Farrus' droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kho Phon Farrus' droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kho_Phon_Farrus%27_droid)

A droid was owned by the Alcedian archaeologist Kho Phon Farrus, who sought to find artifacts of the Ascendant cult on behalf of the Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate during the Imperial Era. The droid was kept by Farrus on the Core Worlds planet Brentaal IV. In 3 ABY, the droid informed Farrus that they had received a transmission from "the Archivist" who had hired them.
