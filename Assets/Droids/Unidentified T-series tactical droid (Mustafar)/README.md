# Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Mustafar)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Mustafar)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_T-series_tactical_droid_%28Mustafar%29)

This T-series tactical droid was deployed alongside a second tactical droid on a Battlesphere above the planet Mustafar. The droid gave orders to B1 Battle Droids during Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano's fight with EG-07. Both tactical droids were destroyed by the Jedi.
