# FX-7 medical assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FX-7 medical assistant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-7_medical_assistant_droid)

The FX-7 medical assistant droid was a model of medical droid used in the galaxy. FX-7 monitored Luke Skywalker during his bacta treatment on the ice planet Hoth shortly before the battle. An FX-7 droid was station aboard the Tribunal when Ahsoka Tano used one of the arms to scan for an inhibitor chip within Clone Captain Rex's head but was unable to find it.
