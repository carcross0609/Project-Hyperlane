# ISS-7 Guardian Battledroid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ISS-7 Guardian Battledroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ISS-7_Guardian_Battledroid)

The ISS-7 Guardian Battledroid was a battle droid model in service of the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY it was stationed aboard the Emperor's Glory guarding the tractor beam control console. The Republic boarding party from the Esseles confronted the battle droid and destroyed it.
