# Bright Hope hacker droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bright Hope hacker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bright_Hope_hacker_droid)

This dedicated hacker droid was stationed aboard the GR-75 medium transport Bright Hope during the Battle of Hoth in 3 ABY. During the Bright Hope escape from Hoth during the battle, this droid helped Echo Base Chief Controller Toryn Farr erase the transport's computer records to prevent them from falling into Imperial hands.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bright Hope hacker droid
- **Class:** Hacker droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
