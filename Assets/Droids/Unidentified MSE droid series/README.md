# Unidentified MSE droid series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified MSE droid series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_MSE_droid_series)

This series of MSE droid ran on tracks, making their ability to travel limited. They were able to consume nuts, bolts and other spare parts, including rusted droid parts. This model was one of the earliest of the MSE series.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified MSE droid series
- **Line:** MSE-series
- **Class:** General purpose droid
- **Sensor:** Red
