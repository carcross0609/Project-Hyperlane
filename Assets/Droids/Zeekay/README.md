# Zeekay

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zeekay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeekay)

Zeekay was a droid that belonged to the child Yesula and her father. At some point no earlier than 227 BBY, Yesula and her father were rescued by Jedi Master Kelnacca and his Padawan, Yarzion Vell, after their home on the astronomical object Veek caught on fire. After safely escaping, Yesula told the Jedi that Zeekay was still inside and had holos of her dead mother in his databank. Vell then reentered the building to rescue Zeekay and returned him to Yesula.
