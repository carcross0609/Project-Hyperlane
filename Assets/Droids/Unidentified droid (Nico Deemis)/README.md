# Unidentified droid (Nico Deemis)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid (Nico Deemis)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_%28Nico_Deemis%29)

A droid was active on the planet Janix during the Imperial Era. After being followed by the Tactical Defense Force officer Captain Brander Lawson following a meeting with Head Boss Nico Deemis, the droid attempted to kill the undercover captain, but was shot in the chest by Two-Boots.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Criminal
- **Name:** Unidentified droid
- **Death:** During or after 19 BBY, Janix
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Yellow and gray
