# Homestead droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Homestead droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Homestead_droid)

This type of droid was used on a homestead used by a gang run by Saponza and his partner on Tatooine. The droids were used frequently to build and expand the base, as well as clearing debris. They were visually similar to an R6 series astromech droid, but had two clawed arms in place of treaded legs, and hovered as their primary method of locomotion.

- **Name:** Homestead droid
- **Affiliation:**
