# 3D-4K

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [3D-4K](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3D-4K)

3D-4K was a 3D-4 administrative droid who worked for Oridelve Incorporated on the planet Gavos. He was deactivated by R2-B7 before the Gavos Droid Revolution, a violent rebellion against organic life forms throughout the galaxy launched by EV-8D3, figurehead of the New Droids' Rights Movement.
