# Efrem LX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Efrem LX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Efrem_LX)

Efrem LX was a droid who was active during the time of the war between the First Order and the Resistance. In 35 ABY, Efrem LX was present in Kijimi City on the planet Kijimi at the same time as several Resistance operatives undertook a mission on the planet. Shortly afterward, Kijimi was destroyed as a show of force by the Sith Eternal.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Efrem LX
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Dark gray and bronze
