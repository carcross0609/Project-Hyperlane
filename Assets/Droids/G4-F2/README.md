# G4-F2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [G4-F2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G4-F2)

G4-F2 was an R-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. A day after arriving on the planet Batuu in 34 ABY, G4-F2 called into the radio show Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7 on station BSO 401.72 to ask in binary where it could find an oil bath as its joints were aching.
