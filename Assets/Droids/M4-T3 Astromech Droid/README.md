# M4-T3 Astromech Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M4-T3 Astromech Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M4-T3_Astromech_Droid)

The M4-T3 Astromech Droid was a model of astromech droid that was used for various tasks at Sobrik Spaceport, the main spaceport in the city of Sobrik on the planet Balmorra, while the city was under Imperial rule.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M4-T3 Astromech Droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Red and black
- **Plating:** Black
