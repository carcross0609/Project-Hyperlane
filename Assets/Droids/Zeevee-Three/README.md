# Zeevee-Three

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zeevee-Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeevee-Three)

Zeevee-Three was a laborer droid. Long before the Scourge infection of droids and cyborgs during the Galactic Civil War, Zeevee-Three was stationed on a faraway world and witnessed diamonds raining from the sky. He recorded his impressions of the event in his memory banks. Eventually, Zeevee-Three became inhabited by the Scourge, who found Zeevee's memory of the event, finding it beautiful.
