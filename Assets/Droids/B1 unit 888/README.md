# B1 unit 888

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B1 unit 888](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1_unit_888)

B1 Unit 888 was a B1-series battle droid that served on the planet Akiva, the site of a droid factory and battle during the Clone Wars. 888 was destroyed on Akiva. In 3 ABY, ND-5 was shot by an Imperial, causing his memory to malfunction and to call B1 unit 888 to report on their status upon encountering the droid's remains.
