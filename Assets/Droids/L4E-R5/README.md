# L4E-R5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L4E-R5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L4E-R5)

L4E-R5 was an R5 series astromech droid who served in the Resistance. L4E-R5 acted as the astromech droid of Lega Fossang, placed inside the astromech socket of her BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter.
