# Jedi training droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jedi training droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training_droid)

Jedi training droids were a type of training droid used by the Jedi Order. Used for combat training, the droids had two legs, two or four arms, and was capable of changing its appearance with a holoprojector. The training droids also had lightsabers for lightsaber combat. Less than three years after the death of Qui-Gon Jinn at the hands of Darth Maul, Padawan Anakin Skywalker programmed such a droid to mimic the appearance of the Sith during a training session.

- **Sensor:** White

- **Equipment:** Holoprojector
