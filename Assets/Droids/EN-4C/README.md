# EN-4C

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EN-4C](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EN-4C)

EN-4C was a battle droid that was part of the Cademimu V police force during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY, it was destroyed by a strike team seeking to disable the Cademimu missile stockpiles.

- **Name:** EN-4C
- **Death:** 3643 BBY, Cademimu V
- **Class:** Battle droid
