# Setar (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Setar (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Setar_%28droid%29)

Setar was a droid who was part of the Second Revelation, a group of sentient droids. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, Setar was present in the Colony of the Second Revelation and offered a piece of plating to the group's leader, Ajax Sigma, which was to be installed onto a new droid to join their number. Sigma accepted the gift, stating it would suit.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Setar
- **Plating:** White
- **Affiliation:** Second Revelation
