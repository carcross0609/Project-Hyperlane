# Flek (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flek (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flek_%28droid%29)

Flek was a droid bought cheap by a young Lando Calrissian, who used him for his (scant) knowledge. Flek couldn't swim, and accompanied Lando on a scam operation to the Meeks. Flek advised Lando that he would never become a scoundrel.
