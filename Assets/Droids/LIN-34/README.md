# LIN-34

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LIN-34](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LIN-34)

LIN-34 was a droid that operated during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, LIN-34 served Jabba the Hutt's criminal empire on Tatooine under Jabba's sadistic EV-series supervisor droid EV-9D9. In 1 ABY, BD-34 and three other droids, R3-B57, RA-316 and BD-34, escaped Jabba's Palace. EV-9D9 thus hired a spacer to track down the droids. Jabba's enemies of the Ay'Nat pirates assigned an AEG-77 Vigo yacht and an escort of X-wing starfighters to exfiltrate LIN-34 out of the Tatooine system. However, the yacht transporting LIN-34 was destroyed by Jabba's pilots, along with its escort.
