# V8-R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V8-R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V8-R)

V8-R was a masculine astromech droid who served in the Resistance Starfighter Corps during the Resistance's war against the First Order. Some members of the Resistance were superstitious about V8-R's name, and he was teased for it.
