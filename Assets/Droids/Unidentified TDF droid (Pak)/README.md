# Unidentified TDF droid (Pak)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified TDF droid (Pak)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_TDF_droid_%28Pak%29)

A police droid accompanied the human officer Pak during a raid on a bank tower on the planet Janix during the Imperial Era. He was destroyed by the former Lord Sith Maul.

- **Name:** Unidentified TDF droid

- **Model:** Tactical Defense Droid
- **Class:** Police droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Yellow, blue and gray
