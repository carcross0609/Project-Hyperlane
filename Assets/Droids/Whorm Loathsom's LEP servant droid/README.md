# Whorm Loathsom's LEP servant droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Whorm Loathsom's LEP servant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whorm_Loathsom%27s_LEP_servant_droid)

An LEP servant droid that belonged to General Whorm Loathsom of the Confederacy of Independent Systems came into use as his personal servant during the Battle of Christophsis. It was equipped with a holoprojector that was place on top of it's head and was detachable.
