# Scanning Patrol Detail series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scanning Patrol Detail series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scanning_Patrol_Detail_series)

The Scanning Patrol Detail series was a line of high-tech security droids manufactured by Industrial Automaton. The line was one of Industrial Automaton's proudest achievements, earning nearly-universal praise from the law enforcement community. One specific model of the series was the Hound-W2 SPD.
