# Unidentified HK-87 assassin droid (Batuu)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified HK-87 assassin droid (Batuu)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_HK-87_assassin_droid_%28Batuu%29)

An HK-87 assassin droid was present on the Outer Rim Territories planet Batuu between 3 ABY and 4 ABY. There, the droid was destroyed during a battle with the Sith Lord Darth Vader, ultimately allowing the Millennium Falcon and its crew to escape.
