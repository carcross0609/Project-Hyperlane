# DJ-88

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DJ-88](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DJ-88)

DJ-88, or Dee-Jay, was a class three droid affiliated with the old Jedi Order. A white, humanoid droid with red photoreceptors, DJ-88 was designed by its Jedi creator to protect the Lost City of the Jedi, a secret library and terraforming station located deep below the surface of Yavin 4.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DJ-88
- **Homeworld:** Yavin 4
- **Creator:** Jedi Order
- **Height:** Tall
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** White
- **Equipment:** Blinding smoke emitters
- **Affiliation:** Lost City of the Jedi
