# M5-BZ

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M5-BZ](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M5-BZ)

M5-BZ, also referred to as simply BZ or Beezee, was an astromech droid who had served the Jedi Master Tera Sinube and subsequently became a member of D-Squad. M5-BZ was lost in hyperspace after opening an airlock while aboard a Venator-class Star Destroyer, the Renown.
