# BILY

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [BILY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BILY)

BILY was a bear-like droid who was at one point present in Mos Eisley on Tatooine with the human Yuko. He had a microwave oven built into his chest. BILY was an acronym for "Baby I Love You."
