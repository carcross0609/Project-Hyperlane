# KD-36 Bioweapon Dispersal Drone

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KD-36 Bioweapon Dispersal Drone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KD-36_Bioweapon_Dispersal_Drone)

The KD-36 Bioweapon Dispersal Drone was a model of probe droid that was used by the freelance spy known as the Shroud. It was designed to deploy poisonous gases and bioweapons, and KD-36 drones were deployed to eliminate individuals who were intruding on the Shroud's business.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KD-36 Bioweapon Dispersal Drone
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
