# ZT-001D

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ZT-001D](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZT-001D)

ZT-001D was an elite Skytrooper, a type of battle droid used by the Eternal Empire of Zakuul. In 3630 BBY, it was protecting the GEMINI Prime in the factory on Darvannis. During the raid on Darvannis it attacked Torian Cadera and the Alliance Commander when they tried to reach GEMINI and was destroyed during the fight.
