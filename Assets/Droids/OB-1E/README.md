# OB-1E

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OB-1E](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OB-1E)

OB-1E was a OOM security battle droid owned by Gorn Vorrox during the Galactic Civil War to protect his store, Gorn's Emporium, located in the seedy Seelom District of Jyvus Space City, the largest space city in orbit over Duro.
