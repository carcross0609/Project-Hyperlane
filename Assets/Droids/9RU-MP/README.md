# 9RU-MP

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [9RU-MP](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/9RU-MP)

9RU-MP was a gruff red-plated BB-series astromech droid in the service of the Resistance. At some point 9RU-MP met B1-series battle droid R0-GR at the repair bay aboard the Raddus. R0-GR would later include 9RU-MP in his book Droidography.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Resistance
- **Name:** 9RU-MP
- **Model:** BB-series astromech droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Plating:** Red-Black
- **Equipment:** Antenna
- **Affiliation:** Resistance
