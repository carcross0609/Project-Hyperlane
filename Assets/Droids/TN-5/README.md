# TN-5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TN-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TN-5)

TN-5 was an ancient droid in Pau City on Utapau. It was rumored to have served Krynbalt Kyr, the first Grand Zigoth, over an eon before 13 BBY, continuing to remain functional. Fyzen Gor thought of TN-5 when contemplating the longevity of droids compared to organics.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TN-5
- **Homeworld:** Utapau
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Affiliation:** Krynbalt Kyr
