# Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Alderaan)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified 2-1B surgical droid (Alderaan)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_2-1B_surgical_droid_%28Alderaan%29)

A medical droid was on Alderaan during the Clone Wars. In 21 BBY, after an assassination attempt was made on Senator Padmé Amidala, this droid treated her and later informed a worried Ahsoka Tano that the senator would be fine as it was only a flesh wound.
