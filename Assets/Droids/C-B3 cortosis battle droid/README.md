# C-B3 cortosis battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C-B3 cortosis battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-B3_cortosis_battle_droid)

The C-B3 cortosis battle droid, or C-B3 for short, was a cortosis-armored model variation of the B2-series super battle droids which were produced in secret by the Confederacy of Independent Systems on the planet Mokivj near the end of the Clone Wars, in 19 BBY. They were manufactured in the Mokivj droid factory by Duke Solha, his brother and his sister.
