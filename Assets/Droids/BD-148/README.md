# BD-148

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BD-148](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD-148)

BD-148 was an elite Skytrooper, a type of battle droid used by the Eternal Empire of Zakuul. In 3636 BBY, it was one of many deployed on Darth Marr's flagship during a battle with the reconstituted Sith Empire. In the main engineering deck, it was destroyed by the enemy combatant who later became known as The Commander, and Darth Marr.
