# Vurrsk's translator droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vurrsk's translator droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vurrsk%27s_translator_droid)

A translator droid served Warlord Vurrsk, the leader of a Trandoshan tribe on Nameel, relaying Vurrsk's words in Galactic Basic Standard when speaking with outsiders. The translator droid was present for negotiations between Vurrsk's tribe and Dupei monks who had stolen credits from them. The droid translated for Vurrsk while speaking to the Jedi Eno Cordova and Cere Junda, who were sent to resolve the issue.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vurrsk's translator droid
- **Class:** Translator droid
- **Plating:** Gold
- **Affiliation:** Vurrsk's tribe
