# TRK-R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TRK-R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TRK-R)

TRK-R was a loader droid who worked in a settlement on the planet Serelia during the time of the Galactic Empire, transporting cargo using a landspeeder. The droid was present at a dock on Serelia to collect a cargo of fish from the resident Verla when Jedi trainee Luke Skywalker visited the planet, having been called there in a Force vision. Verla, believing she would be hunted down for being Force-sensitive, dropped the catch of fish to distract Skywalker, which also knocked down TRK-R.

- **Class:** Loader droid
