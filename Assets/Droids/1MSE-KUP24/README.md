# 1MSE-KUP24

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [1MSE-KUP24](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1MSE-KUP24)

1MSE-KUP24 was a KUP24 messenger droid that was forced to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spices mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, 1MSE-KUP24 was present in the mines' control room when it was taken over by a group of criminals who started a revolution against the Pykes. Joyful at his liberation, 1MSE-KUP24 joined the revolt.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 1MSE-KUP24
- **Model:** KUP24 messenger droid
- **Class:** Messenger droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Gray, black, orange
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Spice mines of Kessel
