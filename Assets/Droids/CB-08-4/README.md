# CB-08-4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CB-08-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CB-08-4)

CB-08-4 was a medical droid operating on Taris during the Galactic War. Its primary field of research and treatment was the rakghoul plague, and it ran the primary medcenter of Waypoint Station Draay.
