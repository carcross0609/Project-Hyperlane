# Khepi guardian droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Khepi guardian droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khepi_guardian_droid)

Khepi guardian droids were a type of sentry droid created by the Khepi people sometime during the High Republic Era to protect their Great Tomb in the Sayma Nebula. The droids were constructed using the same roxodium alloys that the Khepi used to construct the tomb itself. As such, the droids were made to last for thousands of years. The droids were sealed in cubic containers scattered around the tomb that were strong enough to prevent members of the Rokana Raiders from breaking them open. Combining the two pieces of the Architect's Scepter in the tomb's burial chamber activates the droids, who are programmed to kill any intruders in the tomb.
