# Q2-B3KO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Q2-B3KO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q2-B3KO)

Q2-B3KO, or Threekayo, was a feminine-programmed articulated holographic color separator. Originally a class five droid, she was mistakenly upgraded to class one status as a result of a requisition error. During the Clone Wars, Q2-B3KO created the propaganda poster Fighting for Your Independence, made in support of the Confederacy of Independent Systems' Droid Army. After the war came to an end Q2-B3KO was recruited by the Droid Gotra, an organization of repurposed battle droids discontented with the Galactic Empire for being cast aside after serving in the Clone Wars. As a member of the Droid Gotra, Q2-B3KO specialized in spreading messages of droid liberation.
