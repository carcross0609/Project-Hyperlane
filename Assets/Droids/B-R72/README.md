# B-R72

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B-R72](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-R72)

B-R72 was a heavy loader droid manufactured by JLD Mechanicals Consortium with yellow and red plating. The droid belonged to the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu. During the occupation of Batuu by the First Order in 34 ABY, B-R72 was stood on display outside the depot, placed on a plinth beside several other droids behind a railing. The loader droid stood 1.75 meters tall.

- **Manufacturer:** JLD Mechanicals Consortium
- **Model:** Heavy loader droid
