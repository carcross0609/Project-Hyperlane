# Vanguard probot

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vanguard probot](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard_probot)

The Vanguard probot was a second-degree probe droid designed and manufactured by Arakyd Industries during the final decades of the Galactic Republic. It was made to assist deep-space scouts and explorers in mapping out new hyperspace routes. The use of Vanguard droids ensured that the organic scouting crew did not have to perform jumps into unknown territory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vanguard probot
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Line:** Arakyd Probot Series
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Affiliation:** Republic Explorational Corps
