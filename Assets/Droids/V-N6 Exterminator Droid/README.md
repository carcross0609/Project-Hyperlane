# V-N6 Exterminator Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V-N6 Exterminator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-N6_Exterminator_Droid)

The V-N6 Exterminator Droid was a type of three-legged battle droid utilized by the Sith Empire. At least one was stationed aboard the Justice, and was defeated by Havoc Squad.
