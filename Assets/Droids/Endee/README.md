# Endee

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Endee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endee)

Endee was an R1-series astromech droid that was present on the planet Mon Cala one year into the reign of the Galactic Empire. After Jedi Padawan Ferren Barr revealed to his compatriots that Darth Vader had come to Mon Cala, he asked Endee to display files four-three-oh to four-five-nine, which contained information on Darth Sidious, Order 66, Skywalker, Maul, Darth Plagueis, Darth Tyranus, Jar Jar Binks, Padmé Amidala, Sifo-Dyas, and Kamino.
