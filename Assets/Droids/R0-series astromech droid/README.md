# R0-series astromech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R0-series astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R0-series_astromech_droid)

R0 astromechs were a series R-series astromech droids. The droids were used by the Resistance in starfighters such as the T-70 X-wing. The series included the drummer R0-1N6, Commander Poe Dameron's substitute R0-H2 and Jessika Pava's R0-4L0.
