# Todo 360

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Todo 360](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Todo_360)

Todo 360 was a techno-service droid owned by bounty hunter Cad Bane. During the Clone Wars, Todo served Bane on various missions despite being often overlooked and undervalued by him. Todo participated in the theft of a holocron from the Jedi Temple on the planet Coruscant, a job that Bane took from Darth Sidious himself. Although Todo was destroyed when Bane triggered a secret bomb inside of him, the faithful droid was later rebuilt. After, the droid was with Bane for another kidnapping on Coruscant and the subsequent mission to free the gangster Ziro the Hutt from Republic prison. As the Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi and Quinlan Vos attempted to hunt down Bane following the mission, Todo helped Bane battle the two Force-sensitives and eventually flee.
