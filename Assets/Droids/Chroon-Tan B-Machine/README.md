# Chroon-Tan B-Machine

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chroon-Tan B-Machine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chroon-Tan_B-Machine)

The Chroon-Tan B-Machine, also known as the Chroon-Tan midwife droid, was was a type of midwife droid designed to assist with the organic birthing process. The floating medical droid was designed to assist a variety of species with birth and newborn care. These droids had white faces inset with green and blue light sensors, rotund silver bodies, and thermal cushions to warm and cradle newborns. They were programmed to make soothing cooing sounds during childbirth. One of these medical units assisted with the birth of Senator Padmé Amidala's twin children, Luke and Leia, in the Polis Massa Base.
