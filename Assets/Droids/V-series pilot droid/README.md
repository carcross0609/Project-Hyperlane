# V-series pilot droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V-series pilot droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-series_pilot_droid)

The V-series pilot droid was a series of pilot droids manufactured by Industrial Automaton. Two models were produced in the line: the V1 pilot droid and the V6-series pilot droid.
