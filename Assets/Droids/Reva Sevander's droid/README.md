# Reva Sevander's droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Reva Sevander's droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reva_Sevander%27s_droid)

A droid was once owned by the Force-sensitive human female Reva Sevander, though it was later taken from her. In 9 BBY, when she interrogated Princess Leia Organa of Alderaan for information about the Hidden Path, Reva, now an Inquisitor known as the Third Sister, saw Organa's own droid, L0-LA59, and told the princess of her droid and what had happened to it.
