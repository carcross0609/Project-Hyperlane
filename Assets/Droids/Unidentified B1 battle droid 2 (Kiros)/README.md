# Unidentified B1 battle droid 2 (Kiros)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1 battle droid 2 (Kiros)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1_battle_droid_2_%28Kiros%29)

This B1 battle droid was stationed on the planet Kiros and participated in the Battle of Kiros. He informed Darts D'Nar about the Jedi destroying the bombs planted throughout the city, later informing him that all the bombs had been disarmed.
