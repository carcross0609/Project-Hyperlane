# Ba'ken's entourage droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Ba'ken's entourage droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ba%27ken%27s_entourage_droid)

Ba'ken's entourage droid served as a levitating guard droid for the alien Ba'ken on the planet Zenarita. It had red sensors on a cylindrical grey-plated head and body with an orange-colored sphere protruding from the bottom. The front of its body had two buttons and two compartment hatches. One of the hatches housed a three-pronged arm that could extend out and hold a blaster.
