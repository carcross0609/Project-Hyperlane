# Battle Droid C-10

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle Droid C-10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Droid_C-10)

Battle Droid C-10 was a model of battle droid that was deployed on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. Upgraded versions of the model included the Battle Droid C-11 and Battle Droid C-13.
