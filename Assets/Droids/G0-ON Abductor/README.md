# G0-ON Abductor

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G0-ON Abductor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G0-ON_Abductor)

G0-ON Abductor was a battle droid model used by Nocturno and Drake Raven, a pair of gladiators in the Arena Grand of Zakuul. Along with G0-ON Attackers, these droids assisted them during the fights. In 3630 BBY Nocturno and Drake were put up against a newcomer known as "the Mysterious Stranger," who defeated them along with their G0-ON Abductor in the seventh round of the Arena fights.
