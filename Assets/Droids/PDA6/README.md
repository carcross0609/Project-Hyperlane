# PDA6

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PDA6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PDA6)

PDA6 was a PD Lurrian protocol droid, a series of protocol droid designed by Cybot Galactica. PDA6 was stationed aboard the Ithorian Herdship of an Ithorian botanist named Master Zorneth. PDA6 partnered with fellow protocol droid C-3PO on a mission to bring down the force field which protected the fortress of Craw, the dictator of Targonn.
