# Pash Cracken's protocol droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pash Cracken's protocol droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pash_Cracken%27s_protocol_droid)

A silver protocol droid belonged to the Galactic Alliance Defense Fleet Major Pash Cracken by 29 ABY, the final year of the Yuuzhan Vong War. The droid and its owner were among a group of Galactic Alliance members held prisoner by the Yuuzhan Vong—an extra-galactic species of religious zealots waging war on the known galaxy—at a prison compound on the planet Selvaris, with Cracken having been captured during a battle at the Bilbringi Shipyards late in the previous year.
