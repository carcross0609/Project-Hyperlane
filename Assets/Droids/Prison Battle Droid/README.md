# Prison Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prison Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prison_Battle_Droid)

Prison Battle Droids were battle droids used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic as guards in its detention facilities during the Galactic Civil War. Their design was based on Cybot Galactica's CLL-8 binary load lifter, replacing its large hands with weapon systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Prison Battle Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
