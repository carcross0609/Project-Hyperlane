# FD3-MN fighting droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FD3-MN fighting droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FD3-MN_fighting_droid)

The FD3-MN fighting droid, also called FD3 fighting droid, was a model of gladiator droid with a white cylindrical body. They possessed two arms which could be equipped with a circular saw. FD3-MN was a modified FD3 fighting droid that was made to fight in the droid arena of the Lodge on the planet Vandor. In 10 BBY, FD3-MN fought against the power droid WG-22 and destroyed it before preparing to face a FD4 fighting droid. The match was interrupted by the droid L3-37 who tried to convince the FD4 not to fight while the arena manager Ralakili shouted at her. FD3-LT was also a droid of this model.
