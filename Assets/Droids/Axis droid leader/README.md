# Axis droid leader

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Axis droid leader](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axis_droid_leader)

The Axis droid leader was a reprogrammed K3 security droid that led the Vanguard Axis criminal cartel, an offshoot of the Droid Gotra, during the early Imperial Era. Between 19 BBY and 18 BBY, it encountered the Bad Batch when they delivered a case of forged chain code chips to the Axis Base on Ordo Tera Spaceport space station. After Omega discovered the Axis droids were trafficking the Wookiee Padawan Gungi and tried to free him, the Axis Leader intervened but was shot by Hunter. The Axis droid leader was a blue and gold plated droid, with red and blue sensors. It wore a bandolier with four thermal detonators and Gungi's lightsaber on the side. It also carried a blaster pistol for combat.
