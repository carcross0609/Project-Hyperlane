# UP-S2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [UP-S2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/UP-S2)

UP-S2, also known as Yupee-Estoo, was a hyperspace-capable courier droid used by Reekeene's Roughnecks. UP-S2 had a supercilious personality, and was assigned to assist Green squad in monitoring communications from the Imperial Communications Center on the planet Iyuta. Due to the mission requiring communications silence, information gathered by Green squad was loaded in UP-S2 and launched on a weekly basis to Home, the Roughnecks' command ship.
