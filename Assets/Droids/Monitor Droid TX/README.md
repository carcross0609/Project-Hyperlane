# Monitor Droid TX

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Monitor Droid TX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Monitor_Droid_TX)

The Monitor Droid TX was probe droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on Balmorra. A number of those droids were patrolling the area around the factory during the Battle of Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Monitor Droid TX
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Grey
