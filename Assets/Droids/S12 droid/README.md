# S12 droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S12 droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S12_droid)

The S12 droid was a droid series manufactured by Industrial Automaton by around 2 BBY. They possessed a cylindrical body. The body of the MdZ series droids was an inferior copy to that of the S12 droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S12 droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Firstmade:** By c. 2 BBY
