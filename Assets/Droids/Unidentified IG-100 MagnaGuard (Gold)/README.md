# Unidentified IG-100 MagnaGuard (Gold)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified IG-100 MagnaGuard (Gold)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_IG-100_MagnaGuard_%28Gold%29)

This MagnaGuard, alongside a green counterpart, were present alongside a few other Separatist-aligned battle droids in the caves of Utapau. However, several of the droids ended up devoured by Nos monsters. The attack was also such that both IG-100 MagnaGuards were temporarily deactivated while subtly entering repair mode due to their containing a self-repair unit.
