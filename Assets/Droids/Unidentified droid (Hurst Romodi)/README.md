# Unidentified droid (Hurst Romodi)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid (Hurst Romodi)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_%28Hurst_Romodi%29)

A droid served under Imperial General Hurst Romodi during the Galactic Civil War. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, the droid was present with Romodi in an office aboard the Executor, the flagship of the Sith Lord Darth Vader, where it was informed by the general to have members of Squadron D12 reassigned to Tac Team Blue. The droid began to reply to the order, but was destroyed by the Sith assassin Ochi, who had arrived to inform Romodi that Vader had gone rogue in his quest to destroy the Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate.
