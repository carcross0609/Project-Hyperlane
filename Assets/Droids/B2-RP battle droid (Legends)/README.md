# B2-RP battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B2-RP battle droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2-RP_battle_droid/Legends)

The B2-RP battle droid, more commonly known as the rocket droid, the super droid, or the jetpack droid, was a CIS jetpack-equipped B2 super battle droid variant used during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B2-RP battle droid
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Combat Automata
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Model:** B2-series super battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.93 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Armament:** Dual built-in wrist blaster (1-2)
- **Equipment:** Built-in rocket pack
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
