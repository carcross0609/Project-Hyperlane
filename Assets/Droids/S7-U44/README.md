# S7-U44

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S7-U44](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S7-U44)

S7-U44 is a vendor droid active during 1 ABY of the Galactic Civil War. A heavily customized protocol droid that had a red hull on its head and hands, and a heavily customized body patterned after a tuxedo, it frequently set up shop in a tent directly northeast of the Theed Hangar at Theed, Naboo, as well as at Coronet a short distance away from a spaceport.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S7-U44
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
- **Class:** Protocol droid
