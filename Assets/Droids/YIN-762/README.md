# YIN-762

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YIN-762](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YIN-762)

YIN-762 was a B1 battle droid who served as a commander in the Droid Army of the Trade Federation. In 32 BBY, YIN-762 was in command of a base outside the city Spinnaker on Naboo during the Trade Federation's invasion of the planet.
