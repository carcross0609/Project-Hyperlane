# Jabba Desilijic Tiure's Megadroid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jabba Desilijic Tiure's Megadroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jabba_Desilijic_Tiure%27s_Megadroid)

A Megadroid was owned by the Hutt Jabba Desilijic Tiure. The droid was ordered to fight the human cyborg Beilert Valance until it was destroyed by Beilert Valance with aid from 4-LOM, Losha Tarkon, T'onga, and Zuckuss.
