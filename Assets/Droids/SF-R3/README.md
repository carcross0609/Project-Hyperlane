# SF-R3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SF-R3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SF-R3)

SF-R3, also referred to as Aree, was a Safari droid manufactured by Serv-O-Droid, Inc. for the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts. Alongside fellow droid CAM, SF-R3 traveled across the galaxy, documenting and studying the various species of wildlife that inhabited the cosmos. From the hairy banthas of Tatooine to the immigrated porgs of Batuu, the Safari droid often found himself in complicated situations with various fauna, but always tried his best to learn as much as he could about the creatures of the galaxy.
