# Loadlifter droid (model)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Loadlifter droid (model)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loadlifter_droid_%28model%29)

Loadlifter droids or load lifters were a model of loader droid droids programmed for carrying their masters' cargo. One loadlifter droid, HURID-327, was the groundskeeper at the castle of the pirate Maz Kanata thirty years after the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Loadlifter droid
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Degree:** Class 5
- **Plating:**

  - Red
  - White
- **Equipment:**
  - Binary acoustic signaller
  - Repulsor anchor
