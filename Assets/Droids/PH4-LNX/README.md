# PH4-LNX

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PH4-LNX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PH4-LNX)

PH4-LNX, previously called Phalanx but preferring Fay, was a droid of the model designed by Czerka Corporation with the use of Iokath technology. Formerly a property of Godoba the Hutt, she escaped from him and joined the Gambling Authority and Management Entity. She later asked then Commander of the Eternal Alliance to join their side, so that she could hone her skills in battle.
