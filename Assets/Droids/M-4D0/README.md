# M-4D0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M-4D0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-4D0)

M-4D0, also known as Fourdee, was a golden protocol droid that served as a porter droid aboard the luxury liner Star of Empire. In 0 ABY, the Systems Infiltration Manager took over the liner and as a result many of the on-board droids attacked the passengers and crew. M-4D0 used a blaster to shatter a transparisteel viewport and was instantly sucked out.
