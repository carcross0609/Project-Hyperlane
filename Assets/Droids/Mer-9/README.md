# Mer-9

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mer-9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mer-9)

Mer-9 was a protocol droid assigned by the New Republic to observe the actions of the Corporate Sector Authority and the pro-Imperial forces in Verena City. To do this, Mer-9 worked as a translator and assistant to Bren-Aarica Rordan in her shop.
