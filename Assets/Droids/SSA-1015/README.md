# SSA-1015

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SSA-1015](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SSA-1015)

SSA-1015 was one of the OOM security battle droids serving the Trade Federation aboard the Lucrehulk-class battleship Saak'ak during the Federation's blockade of the planet Naboo in 32 BBY. SSA-1015 was a member of the squad that was dispatched to destroy the remains of two Galactic Republic ambassadors, Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn and his Padawan, Obi-Wan Kenobi, after it was presumed that they had been killed by poisonous dioxis gas that had been released into the ship's conference room. The Jedi managed to evade death by using the Force and destroyed SSA-1015, along with the rest of the squad.
