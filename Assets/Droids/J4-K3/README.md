# J4-K3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J4-K3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J4-K3)

J4-K3, also known as Jake, was an orange and white maintenance droid that moved around on a large rolling base. He was very dedicated to his work and communicated using binary. His standard sensor color was orange, but it would change color based on his emotions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** J4-K3"Jake"
- **Class:** Maintenance droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:**
  - Orange
  - Yellow
  - Blue
  - Red
  - Gray
- **Plating:** Orange and white
