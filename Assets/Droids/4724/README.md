# 4724

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [4724](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/4724)

4724 was the designation of a droid who served in the Separatist Droid Army during the Clone Wars between the Confederacy of Independent Systems and Galactic Republic. In 21 BBY, the droid was deployed to the world of Maridun as part of the battalion under the command of General Lok Durd, who had arrived to test the defoliator weapon against the planet's Lurmen colonists.

- **Death:** 21 BBY, Maridun
