# 6-POC

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [6-POC](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/6-POC)

6-POC was a LOM-series protocol droid who served for a project manager of GeneTech Syndicate during the time of the Galactic Civil War. Following the Battle of Yavin, 6-POC operated out of the GeneTech Syndicate Lab near the city of Dearic on the Double World of Talus. After a containment breach of violent genetically engineered arachne creatures wiped out his master and the other lab scientists, 6-POC reluctantly divulged a secret password to a spacer so that they could delete sensitive information regarding the dangerous Parasophic Arachne project.
