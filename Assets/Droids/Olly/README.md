# Olly

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Olly](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Olly)

Olly was a service droid that worked as an attendant at a refueling depot inside a lunar base. The pirate Phee Genoa used the depot and visited during or after 18 BBY to refuel her starship, the Providence. Asking Olly for the usual, she and the power droid MEL left him to refuel the vessel, which he did without noticing the Imperial operative CX-2 sneak aboard to steal flight data. Once the refueling was finished, Genoa paid Olly 1,600 credits and change before leaving.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Olly
- **Class:** Service droid
- **Pronouns:** He/him
