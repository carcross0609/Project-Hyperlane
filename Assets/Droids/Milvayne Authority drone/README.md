# Milvayne Authority drone

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Milvayne Authority drone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Milvayne_Authority_drone)

The Milvayne Authority utilized drones to assist them in throwing defective droids down into the undercity. A few of them threw the protocol droid 0-0-0 down there after he was reported to the Informahub. They were equipped with grappling cables to latch onto and carry the droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Milvayne Authority drone
- **Equipment:** Grappling cables
- **Affiliation:** Milvayne Authority
