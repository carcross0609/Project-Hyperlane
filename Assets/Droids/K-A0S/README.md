# K-A0S

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [K-A0S](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K-A0S)

K-A0S was an Imperial KX-series security droid who competed as a Hunter in Hunters of the Outer Rim on the planet Vespaara in 9 ABY. Having previously lost her original arms to the Wookiee Hunter Grozz, who wielded them as a pair of clubs, K-A0S possessed heavy plated Imperial riot shields grafted in their place and a shoulder mounted blaster. Wishing to find the individual who had stolen her limbs, she was drawn to the Arena after seeing the competition broadcast on ORSN. K-A0S joined the competition with the sole purpose of seeking vengeance on Grozz and recovering her original arms.
