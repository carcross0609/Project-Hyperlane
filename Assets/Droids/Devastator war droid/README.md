# Devastator war droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Devastator war droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devastator_war_droid)

The Devastator war droid was a class of android designed by Ubrikkian Steamworks as an upgrade to the sentinel droid around the time of the Old Sith Wars. A tough, well-built weapon, the Devastator contained redundant systems that minimized damage and allowed it to continue to fight at 85% combat efficiency even after taking significant damage. Due to its sophistication, the Galactic Republic restricted its sale to only military units, making it a rare sight in the galaxy. Nonetheless, most models ended up in possession of mercenaries and criminals.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Devastator war droid
- **Manufacturer:** Ubrikkian Steamworks
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Cost:** 12,000 credits
- **Armament:** Can be equipped with Blaster rifle or pistol
