# Lin-D

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lin-D](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lin-D)

Lin-D was a masculine-programmed entertainment droid designed to tell jokes and make wisecracks. With a humanoid design, and gray, silver, and orange plating, Lin-D's facial features sported a perpetual grin and a pair of adjustable, white photoreceptors—both qualities that underscored his flippant attitude and penchant for making light of any situation. Indeed, Lin-D's loquaciousness proved unbearable to some; the protocol droid C-3PO found him to be quite annoying.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lin-D
- **Class:** Entertainment droid
- **Height:** Slightly more than 1.7 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Gray, silver, and orange
