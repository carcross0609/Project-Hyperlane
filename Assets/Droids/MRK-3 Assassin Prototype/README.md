# MRK-3 Assassin Prototype

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MRK-3 Assassin Prototype](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MRK-3_Assassin_Prototype)

The MRK-3 Assassin Prototype droid was a more recent model of Assassin droid created by the Chromium Kings and presumably owned by Khundu. They resembled a Mataou Hutt security droid. The only known Prototype was destroyed by The Smuggler, who was sent by the Ivax Syndicate to stop the Chromium Kings from bragging about a recent sale of "specialized" droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MRK-3 Assassin Prototype
- **Manufacturer:** Chromium Kings
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Plating:** Silver and black
