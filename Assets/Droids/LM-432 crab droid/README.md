# LM-432 crab droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LM-432 crab droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LM-432_crab_droid)

The LM-432 "Muckraker" crab droid, commonly referred to as the crab droid or simply "Muckraker," was a six-legged droid walker of various sizes manufactured by the Techno Union. There were other models of this droid that had four legs. They were used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LM-432 crab droid
- **Manufacturer:** Techno Union
- **Class:** Droid tank
- **Degree:** Fourth-degree droid
- **Length:** 1.49 meters (4 ft, 11 in) (small variety)
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** Blaster cannons (2)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Techno Union
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
