# CD-2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CD-2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CD-2)

CD-2, or Ceedee-Too, or simply Ceedee, was a droid who worked in the communications hub of the Jedi Temple on the Core Worlds planet Coruscant during the High Republic Era.
