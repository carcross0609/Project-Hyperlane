# Mentor Assassin Zeta

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mentor Assassin Zeta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mentor_Assassin_Zeta)

Mentor Assassin Zeta was one of three assassin droids that were controlled by the droid consciousness Mentor. Alongside Beta and Theta it confronted the strike team sent to Zadd to halt Mentor's plans for droid revolution. Zeta and its fellows engaged in combat by sharing abilities, passing a set of defensive shields from one shell to another. In spite of this tactic, it was destroyed by the strike team.
