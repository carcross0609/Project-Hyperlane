# B3NK

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B3NK](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B3NK)

B3NK was a the navigator/tactical droid onboard Ki-Adi-Mundi's personal starship. He was also known to be very protective of the ship's computer, as well as his fellow crewdroids, especially J1M.
