# SBD series assistance and rescue droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SBD series assistance and rescue droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SBD_series_assistance_and_rescue_droid)

The SBD series assistance and rescue droid, or SBD, was a model of droid manufactured by Karflo Corporation. The SBD series was designed to accompany surveyors and explorers in areas far from civilization or dangerous environments. The droids had a rugged and durable design, and were programmed with intellect and a desire to assist.

- **Manufacturer:** Karflo Corporation
