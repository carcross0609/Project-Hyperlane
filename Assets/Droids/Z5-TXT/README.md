# Z5-TXT

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Z5-TXT](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z5-TXT)

Z5-TXT was a droid who worked on building a settlement in Quan-Shui and then worked in Cro-Akon. A group of Tectozin's went to slave droids building a settlement in Cro-Akon. Z5 was at a charging station but didn't have any energy to escape so Zeta sacrificed herself to give her energy to escape.
