# Pip-Squeak

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pip-Squeak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pip-Squeak)

Pip-Squeak was a MSE series maintenance droid that was given to an Akivan child who later passed away. Following his death, Pip-Squeak stopped turning on, and in 3 ABY, the child's mother traveled to the Akivan city Myrra hoping to get her repaired by the Rodian droidsmith known as "the Toymaker," who originally gave the family Pip-Squeak.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pip-Squeak
- **Line:** MSE series
- **Class:** Maintenance droid
- **Pronouns:** She/her
