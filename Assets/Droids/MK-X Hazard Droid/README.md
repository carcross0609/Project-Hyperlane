# MK-X Hazard Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MK-X Hazard Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MK-X_Hazard_Droid)

The MK-X Hazard Droid was a type of bipedal battle droid produced by the Colicoid species during the era of the Galactic War. A number of MK-X Hazard Droids were deployed alongside similar models during the Colicoids' war games.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MK-X Hazard Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoids
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Black
