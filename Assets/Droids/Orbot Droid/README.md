# Orbot Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orbot Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orbot_Droid)

Orbot Droids were droideka-like droids used by the Galactic Empire. They were carried aboard AT-ATs during the Battle of Hoth and also saw use during the occupation of Bespin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Orbot Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Green
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
