# UK2-B

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [UK2-B](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/UK2-B)

UK2-B was a droid that was active during the New Republic Era. In 9 ABY, the droid escorted two Gamorrean guards through the palace of Boba Fett and presented them to the newly-risen crime lord, watching intently as Fett chose to spare the prisoners' lives. UK2-B had brown and grey plating and a red sensor.
