# DV-4D4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DV-4D4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DV-4D4)

DV-4D4, or DVD-4D4, was a DV supervisor who worked in the control room in the spice mines of Kessel in 10 BBY. After L3-37 freed him and other droids, DV-4D4 freed every droid and slave in the mine.
