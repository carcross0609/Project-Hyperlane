# R5-U8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-U8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-U8)

R5-U8 was an R5 unit that served in Shadow Squadron as the astromech droid for Shadow Six. Their BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber was disabled during the Battle of the Kaliida Nebula.
