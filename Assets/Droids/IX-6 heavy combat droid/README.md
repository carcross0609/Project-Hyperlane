# IX-6 heavy combat droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IX-6 heavy combat droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IX-6_heavy_combat_droid)

The IX-6 heavy combat droid was a heavy battle droid manufactured by Roche, a Verpine corporation, based in the Roche asteroids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IX-6 heavy combat droid
- **Manufacturer:** Roche Hive Mechanical Apparatus Design and Construction Activity for Those Who Need the Hive's Machines
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Cost:** 13,500 credits
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** 2 arm-mounted blaster rifles
