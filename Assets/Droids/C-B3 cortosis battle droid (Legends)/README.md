# C-B3 cortosis battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C-B3 cortosis battle droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-B3_cortosis_battle_droid/Legends)

The C-B3 cortosis battle droid was a battle droid similar in design to the B2 super battle droid. The C-B3 was developed by the Techno Union to specifically combat Jedi during the Clone Wars.

- **Name:** C-B3 cortosis battle droid
- **Manufacturer:**

  - Techno Union
- **Homeworld:** Metalorn
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Cost:** 25,000 credits
- **Height:** 1.9 meters
- **Plating:** Black cortosis
- **Armament:** High-intensity double laser cannons (2)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Techno Union
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
