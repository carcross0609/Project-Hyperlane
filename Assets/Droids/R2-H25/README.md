# R2-H25

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R2-H25](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-H25)

R2-H25 was an R2-series astromech droid who was known to celebrate holidays. They had a black sensor, white and red plating, resembling a candy cane, and had a similarly striped candy cane on top of its dome. R2-H25 was a member of the Resistance and served alongside BB-H20 at the Battle of Crait in 34 ABY.
