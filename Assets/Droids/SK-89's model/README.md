# SK-89's model

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SK-89's model](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SK-89%27s_model)

A general-purpose model of droid was utilized by Kwikhaul Livery Corporation on the planet Kwenn. When the company's owner, Fraxa, replaced its organic drivers with droids due to low funds, multiple members of the model, including SK-89, were assigned to drive landspeeders. The drivers, angered by their replacement, rioted outside the Kwikhaul complex, destroying many of the droids, including at least one of SK-89's model.

- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:** Kwikhaul Livery Corporation
