# VT-211

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [VT-211](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VT-211)

VT-211 was an astromech droid used by Nyna Calixte in her Fury-class starfighter in 137 ABY. She used his assistance during her activities in Operation: Thunderstroke.
