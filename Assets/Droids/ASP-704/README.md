# ASP-704

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ASP-704](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ASP-704)

ASP-704 was a masculine ASP-7 labor droid imported by Eugene Talmont, the Imperial prefect of Mos Eisley spaceport on the planet Tatooine, shortly before the Battle of Yavin in the year 0 BBY. It was tasked with loading cargo aboard a GR-45 medium transport. After being taunted by IM4-099, ASP-704 simply slapped IM4-099, sending it to the ground.
