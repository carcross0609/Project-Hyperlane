# R5-Z0MB

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-Z0MB](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-Z0MB)

R5-Z0MB was an R5 series astromech droid whose appearance was akin to a droid zombie, and could be found wandering the swamps of Corvax Fen on Mustafar. The droid was various shades of green and pink and possessed a melted head with an exposed interior.
