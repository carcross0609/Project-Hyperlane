# Raz

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Raz](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raz)

Raz, nicknamed Razzy, was a droid that lived on the planet Cianap. She had witnessed the fall of the planet's civilization, and later became the companion of one of the Cianap native.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Raz
- **Pronouns:** She/her
- **Sensor:** Black
