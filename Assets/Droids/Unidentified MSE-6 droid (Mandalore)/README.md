# Unidentified MSE-6 droid (Mandalore)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified MSE-6 droid (Mandalore)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_MSE-6_droid_%28Mandalore%29)

An MSE-6 series repair droid served Moff Gideon on the hidden Imperial base on Mandalore during the New Republic Era. It was present when the Children of the Watch and the Mandalorian resistance launched an assault on the base, where it caught R5-D4 slicing into the base's computer to deactivate ray shields on the base and confronted the droid, before being shocked by the astromech and forced to retreat. Later, the droid returned with several other mouse droids to again confront the interloper, only for it to fly away, causing one of the mouse droid to fall off the ledge. All the mouse droids were equipped with lightbars and sirens.
