# Unidentified Temple droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Temple droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Temple_droid)

A Temple droid worked at the Temple of the Kyber during the High Republic Era until it was decommissioned. In 382 BBY, Keth Cerepath owned a droid that used the vocoder from the droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Temple droid
- **Equipment:** Vocoder
- **Affiliation:** Temple of the Kyber
