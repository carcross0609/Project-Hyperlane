# KDY-4 tech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KDY-4 tech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KDY-4_tech_droid)

The KDY-4 tech droid was designed by Kuat Drive Yards about 200 BBY for repairing and building starships. Nicknamed the "builder's buddy" because of its efficiency, usefulness, and easygoing manner, the KDY-4 was a second-degree droid standing roughly 1.5 meters high and equipped with four appendages (two retractable) and three legs. The three legs were equipped with magnets to provide stability while moving through damaged starships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KDY-4 tech droid
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Class:** Maintenance droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Cost:** 4400 credits
