# War-robot

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [War-robot](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/War-robot)

War-robots or war droids were a type of combat automaton used by Xim the Despot during his military campaigns. One of the first known combat droids used in the galaxy, the war-robots were heavily armed and armored and used to subjugate entire worlds by their master.

## Specifications

- **Name:** War-robot
- **Manufacturer:** Xim the Despot
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Height:** 2.78 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Battlefield pulse-wave cannon (Removable)
  - Heatbeam
  - Particle discharger
- **Equipment:** Kiiriium armor
- **Affiliation:**
  - Xim's empire
  - Hutt Cartel
