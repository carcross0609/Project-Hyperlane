# K0-29

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K0-29](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K0-29)

K0-29 was a battle droid in the employ of the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Galactic War. During the invasion of Belsavis, K0-29 was tasked with guarding the probe droid TA-350 deep in the Tomb on Belsavis, though TA-350 was of the opinion that K0-29 was unintelligent—it reacted threateningly to any organic that approached TA-350, regardless of whether they appeared to be Imperial.
