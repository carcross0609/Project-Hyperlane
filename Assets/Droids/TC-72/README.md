# TC-72

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TC-72](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TC-72)

TC-72 was a TC-series protocol droid in the employ of Jabba Desilijic Tiure. She once was assigned to the smuggler Han Solo on a voyage to Gamorr. The droid did not ingratiate herself to Solo, but was instrumental in the negotiations.
