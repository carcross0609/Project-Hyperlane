# Unidentified BX-series droid commando (crystals)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified BX-series droid commando (crystals)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_BX-series_droid_commando_%28crystals%29)

A BX-series droid commando was ordered to transport stolen lightsaber crystal to a ship in a ice mountains on the other side of the planet. However Clone Commander Gree had been assigned to retake the Crystals from the droid, who had hidden them in the canyon. The droid was attacked in a canyon by Gree's V-19 Torrent starfighter however used a J-1 proton cannon to repulse the starfighter. Although successfully landing a hit on the left wing the proton canon was also destroyed forcing the droid to flee to a wooded area on the planet.
