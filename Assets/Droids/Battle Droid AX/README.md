# Battle Droid AX

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle Droid AX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Droid_AX)

The Battle Droid AX was a model of humanoid-shaped battle droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. A number of them were used to defend the exterior of the factory during the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Battle Droid AX
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Tan
