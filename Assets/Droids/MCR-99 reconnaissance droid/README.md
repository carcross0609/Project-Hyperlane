# MCR-99 reconnaissance droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MCR-99 reconnaissance droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MCR-99_reconnaissance_droid)

MCR-99 reconnaissance droids were probe droids used by The Shroud to conduct surveillance on the Republic and reconstituted Sith Empire alike. They were repulsorlift powered droids secreted in remote locations on numerous worlds where they remained in place for long periods.
