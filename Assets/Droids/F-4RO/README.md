# F-4RO

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [F-4RO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F-4RO)

F-4RO was a labor droid who worked at Kublop Springs spaceport during the High Republic Era. Seeing him struggle to load a crate onto a starship, the Jedi younglings Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay and Nubs helped him but accidentally loaded the crate onto a pirate starship bound for Yarrum Tower. The Jedi younglings undertook a mission to recover the crate, bringing them into conflict with the Ganguls. Following the mission, F-4RO thanked the younglings for retrieving the crate.
