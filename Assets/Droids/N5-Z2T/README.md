# N5-Z2T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [N5-Z2T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N5-Z2T)

N5-Z2T was an N5 sentry droid who operated for the New Republic at the Karthon Chop Fields, a scrapyard where prisoners of the New Republic were sentenced to serve. In 9 ABY, the N5 sentry droid approached the prisoner Migs Mayfeld as he worked, ordering him to salute in the presence of New Republic Marshal Cara Dune. Dune remanded Mayfeld into her custody, and the security droid accepted, ordering the prisoner to follow her. Mayfeld was confused by the situation, and the droid repeated its order, threatening him to comply within three seconds. The prisoner relented, following Dune to her starship.
