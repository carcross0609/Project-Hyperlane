# HCT-10

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HCT-10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HCT-10)

HCT-10 was an excavation droid on the crew of the explorer Fionn Tucat during a mission to the uninhabited moon of Lupal. When the red light emitted by Lupal's dying star drove the Shistavanen crewmember Kordus Vrak mad, he began to attack the rest of the crew. HCT-10 became Vrak's first target, destroyed by the Shistavanen despite the industrial strength of its servos.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HCT-10
- **Death:** During the mission to Lupal
- **Class:** Excavation droid
