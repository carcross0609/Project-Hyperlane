# Wrench (R2 unit)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wrench (R2 unit)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrench_%28R2_unit%29)

Wrench was an R2-series astromech droid that belonged to Rhys Dallows, and was his most loyal companion. Wrench's serial number was unknown.
