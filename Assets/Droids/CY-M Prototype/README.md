# CY-M Prototype

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CY-M Prototype](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CY-M_Prototype)

The CY-M Prototype was a battle droid that was part of HK-47's droid army. It was similar in appearance to a Chirq Council Mechanized Guardian and was armed with a red-bladed lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CY-M Prototype
- **Homeworld:** Mustafar
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Armament:**
  - Lightsaber
  - Force pike
- **Affiliation:** HK-47's Droid Army
