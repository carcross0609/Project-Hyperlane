# YS-5755

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [YS-5755](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YS-5755)

YS-5755 was an R-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. The droid featured in the song "Goola Bukee" by Zano, during which YS-5755 had a solo performance. The droid DJ R-3X praised the R-unit's performance, citing it as the reason for the song's success. In 34 ABY, R-3X played "Goola Bukee" at Oga's Cantina in the town of Black Spire Outpost on the planet Batuu.
