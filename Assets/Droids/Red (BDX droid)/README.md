# Red (BDX droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Red (BDX droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Red_%28BDX_droid%29)

Red was a BDX droid who was active at Savi & Son Salvage in Black Spire Outpost on the Outer Rim planet Batuu by 34 ABY. The diminutive biped was a charismatic leader who enjoyed supervising his fellow BDX units and developing plans.
