# Unidentified GEMINI captain

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified GEMINI captain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_GEMINI_captain)

A GEMINI captain commanded an Eternal Fleet warship that was picked as a target by the Alliance seeking to take over the GEMINI frequency. Her ship was transferring prisoners from the Core Worlds to the Eternal Empire capital of Zakuul when it was intercepted and boarded. The GEMINI captain attempted to destroy and delay the intruders until SCORPIO granted her free will, allowing her to make her own choices beyond her programming. The captain then chose to upload her consciousness into the GEMINI frequency while setting her ship to self-destruct.
