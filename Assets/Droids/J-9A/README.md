# J-9A

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-9A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-9A)

J-9A was a female-programmed navigator droid assigned as an aid to Harra the Hutt. When the First Order arrived at Vodran and took over Harra the Hutt's stronghold, Harra and the droid retinue were exiled. J-9A led them to the ghost moon Kufs. The droids continued to protect Harra, and when members of J-Squadron arrived on the moon, J-9A captured them and held them until they escaped. The droid later accompaniend Harra when they returned to Vodran.
