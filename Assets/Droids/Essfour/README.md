# Essfour

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Essfour](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Essfour)

Essfour, also spelled as Essfor, was an R5 series astromech droid that served the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. He was assigned to Luke Skywalker, following R2-D2's mission to rescue a captured C-3PO.
