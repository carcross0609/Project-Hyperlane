# RC-2X

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RC-2X](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RC-2X)

RC-2X was a protocol droid who belonged to the Ithorian swooper Draknus during the Galactic Civil War. After the Battle of Yavin, RC-1Z, along with RC-1Z and RC-3T, helped Draknus to manage the Mos Entha race track on the planet Tatooine.
