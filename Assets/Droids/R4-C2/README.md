# R4-C2

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-C2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-C2)

R4-C2 was an R4 astromech droid in service to the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the early rebellion against the Galactic Empire. The droid relayed a holographic message from the extremist Saw Gerrera to members of the Massassi Group at the Great Temple on Yavin 4.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Rebel
- **Name:** R4-C2
- **Line:** R-series
- **Model:** R4 astromech droid
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** White and green
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Massassi Group
