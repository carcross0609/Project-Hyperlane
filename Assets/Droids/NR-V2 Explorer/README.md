# NR-V2 Explorer

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [NR-V2 Explorer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NR-V2_Explorer)

The NR-V2 Explorer was an exploration droid created by Arakyd Industries during the early years of the New Republic. It was based on the design of the Viper probe droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** NR-V2 Explorer
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
