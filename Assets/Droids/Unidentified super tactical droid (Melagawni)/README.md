# Unidentified super tactical droid (Melagawni)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified super tactical droid (Melagawni)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_super_tactical_droid_%28Melagawni%29)

A super tactical droid commanded droid forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems at a weapons depot during the Clone Wars. The Galactic Republic sent a squad of clone trooper deserters to destroy the weapons depot. The super tactical droid predicted the troopers' plan to destroy the facility, cornering them before they could do so, but was destroyed by the trooper Racetrack, who had not accompanied the squad inside the depot.
