# Dooba

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dooba](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooba)

Dooba was a MK 8001 Attendant Droid who looked after a young child. The child's plasticene doll, Branli, ended up with a broken finger after a fall from a grav slide. Dooba fixed the doll's finger for the child.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dooba
- **Manufacturer:** AccuTronics
- **Model:** MK 8001 Attendant Droid
- **Height:** 1.6 meters
