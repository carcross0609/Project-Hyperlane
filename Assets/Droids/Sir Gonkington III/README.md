# Sir Gonkington III

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Sir Gonkington III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sir_Gonkington_III)

Sir Gonkiton III was a GNK-series power droid hailing from the planet of Cantonica. He asked an stranger to escort him to a power socket across the city of Canto Bight. After he was successfully escorted, he attached himself to a power socket. He had brown plating and black sensors and stood at 1.1 meters.
