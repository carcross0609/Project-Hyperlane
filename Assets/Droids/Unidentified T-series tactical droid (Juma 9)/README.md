# Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Juma 9)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Juma 9)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_T-series_tactical_droid_%28Juma_9%29)

During the attack on Juma 9, a Tactical Droid led the battle droids on the space station. When Obi-Wan Kenobi and Plo Koon entered Juma 9 and made their way to the control bridge, he sent in most of his battle droids to kill the Jedi. They were quickly cut down. The Jedi destroyed the tactical droid and wiped out the other droids on the space station.
