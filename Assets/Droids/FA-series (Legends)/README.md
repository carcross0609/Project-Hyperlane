# FA-series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FA-series/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FA-series/Legends)

The FA-series was a line of service droids manufactured by the SoroSuub Corporation. The FA-4 pilot droid was one of the most famous models of the line.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FA-series
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:**
  - FA-4 pilot droid
  - FA-5 valet droid
- **Class:** Service droid
