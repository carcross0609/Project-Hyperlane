# Illumi-droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Illumi-droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Illumi-droid)

Illumi-droids were small hovering droids with three tentacle arms that acted like floating lanterns, illuminating dark places where their owners were. The droids levitated above the ground, and also had the capacity to walk using the trio of appendages on their underside. Several illumi-droids were used to illuminate Rish Loo's secret laboratory on Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Illumi-droid
- **Equipment:** Built-in light source
- **Affiliation:**
