# 2-ROB

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [2-ROB](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2-ROB)

2-ROB was the medical droid aboard Ki-Adi-Mundi's personal starship. Her databanks contained data on over 12 million species. She accompanied Ki-Adi-Mundi when he attempted to rescue his daughter, Sylvn, from the criminal Ephant Mon.
