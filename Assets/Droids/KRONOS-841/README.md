# KRONOS-841

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KRONOS-841](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KRONOS-841)

KRONOS-841 was a droid who bid by proxy with his cohorts at the Xrexus Cartel's auction of Jedi Padawan Eldra Kaitis in 33 BBY; however, they were outbid by the industrialist Jee Kra.
