# CZ-4X Assault Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CZ-4X Assault Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-4X_Assault_Droid)

CZ-4X Assault Droids were battle droids created by Czerka Arms during the era of the Galactic War. The droids were used to guard the Czerka facility CZ-198 during the conflict brought on by seizure of Czerka's assets by the Galactic Republic's Department of Adjudication and the reconstituted Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CZ-4X Assault Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Green and yellow
- **Affiliation:** Czerka Corporation
