# M4-Y10

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M4-Y10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M4-Y10)

M4-Y10 was a small astromech droid present on the planet Tatooine in 3626 BBY. The droid had black plating and antenna along with a red photoreceptor and stripes across its three legs and on the left side of its body. The right side had a white stripe. M4-Y10 had a cylindrical body and a wider round head.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M4-Y10
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Black, red, and white
- **Equipment:** Antenna
