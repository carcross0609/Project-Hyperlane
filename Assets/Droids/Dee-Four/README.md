# Dee-Four

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dee-Four](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dee-Four)

Dee-Four was a rebel protocol droid. Between 2 BBY and 4 ABY, it traveled to the asteroid outpost "Z-LOQ" with the rebel spy Alexsandr Kallus to receive information about Imperial technology from Grand-engineer Lony Coleema. After Coleema transferred the information to Dee-Four, the engineer was kidnapped by the IG-88 assassin droid IG-88. They were later rescued by Kallus and Dee-Four, who brought Coleema onboard the starship Glimmer of Hope.
