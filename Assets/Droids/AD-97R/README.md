# AD-97R

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AD-97R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AD-97R)

AD-97R was a Viper probe droid who resided in the Anoat sector at the time of the Iron Blockade. He was reprogrammed by scavengers to seek pillage on Hoth, AD-97R had developed an extreme attachment to and fondness for its controllers.
