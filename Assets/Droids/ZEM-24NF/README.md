# ZEM-24NF

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ZEM-24NF](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZEM-24NF)

ZEM-24NF was an elite Skytrooper, a type of battle droid used by the Eternal Empire of the planet Zakuul, that was among the Skytrooper forces deployed to board Darth Marr's flagship during a battle with the Eternal Fleet in 3636 BBY. Equipped with CryoBan grenades, the droid was destroyed in battle with an enemy combatant.
