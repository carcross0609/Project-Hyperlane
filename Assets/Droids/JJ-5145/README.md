# JJ-5145

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [JJ-5145](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JJ-5145)

JJ-5145, nicknamed Forfive, was a logistics droid that served the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era. The droid was sent to assist Jedi Master Stellan Gios aboard the Starlight Beacon by his friend, Knight Elzar Mann, after Gios had sent Mann on a retreat to work through a crisis but was unable to accompany him as he had promised. Gios, who did not desire having a droid follow him around, figured that an irked Mann had sent Forfive to him as a practical joke.
