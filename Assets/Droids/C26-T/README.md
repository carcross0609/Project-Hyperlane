# C26-T

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C26-T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C26-T)

C26-T was a sifter droid that was forced to work in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, C26-T was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C26-T
- **Class:** Sifter droid
- **Plating:** White, metallic (stained)
