# R7-D4

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R7-D4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R7-D4)

R7-D4 was an R-series astromech droid that served with the Kel Dor Jedi Master Plo Koon during the Clone Wars. After Koon lost his entire fleet to General Grievous and the Malevolence, R7 joined Koon as the copilot of his Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor, the Blade of Dorin, flying alongside Jedi General Anakin Skywalker and Shadow Squadron on a mission to destroy the warship before it could take out the Kaliida Shoals Medical Center in the Ryndellia system. R7 continued to serve with Koon as the war continued, flying with the Jedi Master at the First Battle of Felucia and during the Battle of Kadavo.
