# Mark V Commando Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark V Commando Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_V_Commando_Droid)

The Mark V Commando Droid was a model of battle droid that the Jedi Master Liam Dentiri used to test the combat prowess of Jedi students at the Jedi Temple on the planet Tython. The Mark V was the final of the five levels of testing.

- **Name:** Mark V Commando Droid
