# CGR-80

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CGR-80](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CGR-80)

CGR-80, also known as Seeger, was a Ceremony, Gala, and Ritual droid with yellow photoreceptors, and gold plating that was active during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Marriages through the Terms of Bonding, and Rodian harvest festivals were among the many rituals and ceremonies that the protocol droid was capable of performing.
