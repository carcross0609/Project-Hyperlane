# RF-U5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RF-U5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RF-U5)

RF-U5 was an excavation droid that served in the crew of the explorer Fionn Tucat during an expedition to the moon of Lupal. After the light from Lupal's dying star drove the Shistavanen Kordus Vrak to attack the rest of the crew, RF-U5 escaped aboard their starship with the two other survivors, Tucat's associate Romina Foss and the Lasat Borzul. As the ship escaped Lupal, the starlight from a solar flare shone into the ship, similarly turning Borzul feral.
