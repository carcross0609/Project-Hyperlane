# EG-01

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EG-01](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EG-01)

EG-01 was a GNK-series power droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton with green plating. During the occupation of the planet Batuu by the First Order in 34 ABY, the power droid was displayed alongside several other droids on a plinth behind railings located outside of the Droid Depot in Black Spire Outpost on Batuu.
