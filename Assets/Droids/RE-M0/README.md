# RE-M0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RE-M0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RE-M0)

RE-M0, also referred to as Remo, was a droid created by Doctor Nasan Godera during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. One of the doctor's assistants on Taris, RE-M0 aided an unknown Jedi Knight in finding and rescuing his master when Godera was kidnapped in 3643 BBY by the Imperial Agent Watcher One.
