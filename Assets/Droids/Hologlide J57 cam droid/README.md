# Hologlide J57 cam droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hologlide J57 cam droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hologlide_J57_cam_droid)

Hologlide J57 cam droids, also known as Hologlide J57 Podrace Cam Droids, were a type of floating droid capable of recording video. They were equipped with a repulsor wing, a transmission antenna as well as multiple cameras. The droids were powered by a mini-reactor. Several J57s were used on the planet Tatooine to record the Boonta Eve Classic podracing event.
