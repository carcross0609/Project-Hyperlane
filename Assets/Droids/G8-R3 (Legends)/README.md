# G8-R3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G8-R3/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G8-R3/Legends)

G8-R3, also known as R5-A7, was an R5-series astromech droid in the service of the Royal Naboo Security Forces in 32 BBY. He had the standard head of an R5 unit on the versatile body of an R2 unit. He was destroyed by a blaster bolt fired by the Trade Federation ships blockading Naboo while attempting to repair Queen Amidala's royal starship as it fled the planet.
