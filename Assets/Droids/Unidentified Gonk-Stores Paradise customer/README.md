# Unidentified Gonk-Stores Paradise customer

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified Gonk-Stores Paradise customer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Gonk-Stores_Paradise_customer)

A EG-6 power droid customer shopped at Gonk-Stores Paradise, a store in the town of Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine. They had a Trandoshan friend who bought a stabilizing coil from Worrt-o-mart, the store that Gonk-Stores Paradise replaced some time before 1 BBY. The Trandoshan paid for the stabilizing coil with two of their own limbs, one arm and one leg, and the EG-6 power droid thought this was an unreasonable price, despite the fact that the limbs eventually grew back.
