# DSD1 dwarf spider droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DSD1 dwarf spider droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DSD1_dwarf_spider_droid)

The DSD1 dwarf spider droid, or simply the dwarf spider droid, was a model of battle droid manufactured by Baktoid Armor Workshop. Favored by the Commerce Guild, it was first used against renegade miners in skirmishes that took place deep underground. The unit was also known as the burrowing spider droid for its ability to invade narrow spaces. It was armed with a powerful nose-mounted laser cannon capable of both rapid-fire and high-intensity burst. It was also shown to be used as an anti-air weapon, shown during the Battle of Teth and by shooting down the LAAT gunship carrying the Bad Batch, Cody, Rex, Jesse, and Kix on Anaxes. The four legs of the droid were designed and manufactured to allow attachment and movement on vertical terrain, such as cliffs, seen during the Battle of Teth, where dwarf spider droids affixed themselves to the cliff of the B'omarr Order Monastery to utilize a superior firing angle at incoming clone troopers and AT-TEs.
