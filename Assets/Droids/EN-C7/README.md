# EN-C7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EN-C7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EN-C7)

EN-C7 was a battle droid of the reconstituted Sith Empire stationed on the Harrower-class dreadnought Ziost Shadow during the Galactic War. During the outbreak of the rakghoul plague in the Tion Hegemony, they transported an Imperial strike team to Kaon.
