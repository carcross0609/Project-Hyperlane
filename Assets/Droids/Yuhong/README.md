# Yuhong

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yuhong](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yuhong)

Yuhong was a bounty droid who worked for the Haxion Brood crime syndicate during the Imperial Era. The droid was destroyed by Cal Kestis and Merrin on the moon Jedha. Yuhong had an unquenchable thirst for battle, and made a name for themself in the Mid Rim before a concerted effort to destroy the droid forced them to hunt in the more lawless Outer Rim.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Yuhong
- **Death:** 9 BBY, Jedha
- **Line:** Haxion Brood Bounty Droid
- **Pronouns:** They/them
- **Affiliation:** Haxion Brood
