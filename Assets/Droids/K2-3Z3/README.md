# K2-3Z3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K2-3Z3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K2-3Z3)

K2-3Z3 was a 3Z3 medical droid in the service of Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire. In 137 ABY, after the Battle of Borosk, K2-3Z3 saw to the medical treatment of Sergeant Ran Harkas of Joker Squad.
