# EV-series supervisor droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EV-series supervisor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EV-series_supervisor_droid)

The EV-series supervisor droid, also known as the EV-series droid overseer, was a model of supervisor droid manufactured by MerenData. The droid was gangly in form. One of the droids, EV-9D9, worked for Jabba Desilijic Tiure, as the chief of his palace's Cyborg Operations.
