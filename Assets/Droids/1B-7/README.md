# 1B-7

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [1B-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1B-7)

1B-7, pronounced One-Bee-Seven, was a medical droid owned by the Dark Lord of the One Sith, Darth Krayt. 1B-7 was assigned by Darth Wyyrlok to help the One Sith agent, Nyss Nenn, bring the Iteration of Jedi Knight Jaden Korr out of its slumber in a Spaarti cloning cylinder.
