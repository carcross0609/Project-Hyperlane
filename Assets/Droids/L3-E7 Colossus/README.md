# L3-E7 Colossus

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [L3-E7 Colossus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L3-E7_Colossus)

L3-E7 Colossus was a battle droid model used by the Sith Empire during the Galactic War. In 3640 BBY four Colossus droids were used to defend the approach to the Bastion in the Coronet City on Corellia, but were destroyed during the assault by the Galactic Republic forces.
