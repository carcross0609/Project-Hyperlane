# O6-D5

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [O6-D5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/O6-D5)

O6-D5 was an astromech droid that operated during the time of the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The droid was sent to CZ-198 as a mission courier carrying a call for recruitment into the Dread War for Republic strike forces operating in the area.

## Specifications

- **Name:** O6-D5
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Gold and gray
- **Affiliation:**

  - Jedi Order
