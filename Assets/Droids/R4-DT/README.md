# R4-DT

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-DT](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-DT)

R4-DT was an R4-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton that resided on the planet Daiyu. The droid was covered in multi-colored graffiti and had the words "RUN" and "So it goes" written on its plating in Aurebesh. The droid had a black sensor. In 9 BBY, R4-DT passed by the Daiyu marketplace in Daiyu City alongside a humanoid individual, and soon after stood by as the humanoid sat with another humanoid. It was at this time that the young Princess Leia Organa and the Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi walked by R4-DT while being hunted by the Galactic Empire's Inquisitors.
