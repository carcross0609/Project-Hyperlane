# IR-60 Aerial Commando Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IR-60 Aerial Commando Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-60_Aerial_Commando_Droid)

The IR-60 Aerial Commando Droid was a model of bipedal battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. The IR-60 was designed for aerial combat environments, and had built-in rockets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IR-60 Aerial Commando Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
