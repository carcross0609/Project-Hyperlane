# FX-1 medical assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FX-1 medical assistant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-1_medical_assistant_droid)

The FX-1 medical assistant droid was a medical droid manufactured by Medtech Industries, beginning the FX-series of medical assistant droids. Produced during the last decades of the Galactic Republic, it was ultimately succeeded by the FX-2 model.
