# DRN-592

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DRN-592](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DRN-592)

DRN-592 was a B1-series battle droid created by Baktoid Combat Automata who served as a commander in the Droid Army of the Trade Federation. As with all B1-series battle droids, DRN-592 was 1.91 meters (6 feet, 3 inches) tall, weighed 65 kilograms, and had masculine programming. The droid wielded an E-5 blaster rifle and had a backpack equipped with an antenna. The droid had light tan plating with brown markings.
