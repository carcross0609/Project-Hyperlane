# CE-3K

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CE-3K](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CE-3K)

CE-3K was a protocol droid serving the New Republic during the Galactic Civil War. In 10 ABY, CE-3K was assigned to Jedi Master Luke Skywalker's quarters on the planet New Alderaan, shortly before the world was attacked by the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CE-3K
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
