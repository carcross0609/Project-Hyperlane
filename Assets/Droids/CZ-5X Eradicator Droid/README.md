# CZ-5X Eradicator Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CZ-5X Eradicator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-5X_Eradicator_Droid)

CZ-5X Eradicator Droid was a battle droid manufactured by the Czerka Corporation. During the Seizure of CZ-198 in 3638 BBY the droid was used to opposed the forces trying to establish control over CZ-198, but was destroyed.
