# Uxiol Droid Manufacturing patrol drone

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uxiol Droid Manufacturing patrol drone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uxiol_Droid_Manufacturing_patrol_drone)

A type of patrol drone was produced by the Uxiol Droid Manufacturing company on the planet of Hallion. In 3954 BBY, when the Sith Lord Scourge along with Darth Nyriss' advisor Sechel infiltrated the droid manufacturing plant on the planet, the patrol droids were seen guarding the plant and were easily dispatched by the two Sith.

- **Manufacturer:** Uxiol Droid Manufacturing
