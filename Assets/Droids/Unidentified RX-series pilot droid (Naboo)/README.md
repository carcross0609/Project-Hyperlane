# Unidentified RX-series pilot droid (Naboo)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified RX-series pilot droid (Naboo)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_RX-series_pilot_droid_%28Naboo%29)

According to the Jawa trader Klikk, an RX-series droid experienced a spectacular malfunction while on the planet Naboo causing it to fly away with the starship on its own when its owner stepped down from the boarding ramp. Although the Naboo system observation post sent starfighters to stop the RX-series pilot droid, it eluded them and jumped to lightspeed, on course to the Mid Rim region of the galaxy housing the planets Bothawui, Nal Hutta, and Rodia.
