# Rheen

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rheen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rheen)

Rheen, styled as Rheen the Powderizer, was an enhanced gladiator droid. It was humanoid in shape with a narrow head topped with an antenna, a large armor plate over its lower torso and a single armor plate over its right shoulder. The droid's limbs were relatively thin, except for its forearms, which were far thicker.
