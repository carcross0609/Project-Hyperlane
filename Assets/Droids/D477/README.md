# D477

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D477](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D477)

D477 was a droid who worked at Terpsichore Station, a mining facility on the planet Parnassos that belonged to the Con Star Mining Corporation. Her task was to give uniforms to the miners.
