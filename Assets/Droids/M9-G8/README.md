# M9-G8

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M9-G8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M9-G8)

M9-G8 was an astromech droid belonging to Resistance X-wing pilot Yolo Ziff. He was stationed at the Resistance base on the planet D'Qar. He co-piloted with Ziff in his T-70 X-wing starfighter as part of Blue Squadron during the Battle of Starkiller Base.

- **Model:** R4 astromech droid
