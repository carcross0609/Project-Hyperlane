# BD-1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BD-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD-1)

BD-1 was a BD explorer droid that accompanied Jedi Master Eno Cordova during the last years of the Galactic Republic. After having a Force vision of the Jedi Order fall, Cordova hid a holocron with a list of Force-sensitive children and encrypted a series of messages about it inside BD-1, only revealed by someone the droid trusted. Following Order 66 and the rise of the Galactic Empire, the droid befriended Cal Kestis, a former Padawan who endeavored to rebuild the Jedi Order. Afterwards he accompanied Kestis on his adventures and his crusade against the Empire and subsequent quest for Tanalorr.
