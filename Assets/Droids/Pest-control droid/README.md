# Pest-control droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pest-control droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pest-control_droid)

Pest-control droids were a class of droids on the planet Tyed Kant during the Galactic Civil War. They were armed with insecticide spray and clipping shears, and were capable of maneuvering through the gas giant's atmosphere. Three droids infected by the Mynock virus malfunctioned and attacked a peaceful iagoin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pest-control droid
- **Equipment:**
  - Insecticide spray
  - Clipping shears
