# FD3-MN

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FD3-MN](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FD3-MN)

FD3-MN was a modified FD3-MN fighting droid made by Ralakili to be a gladiator droid to fight in his droid fighting pits inside Fort Ypso on Vandor. The droid's mechanical limbs could puncture metal with devastating might powered by electricity produced in her interior power cells. In 10 BBY, her fight with WG-22 was interrupted by the protests of L3-37 for droid rights, which cost FD3-MN dearly.
