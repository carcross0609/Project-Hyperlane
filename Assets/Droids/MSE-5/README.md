# MSE-5

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MSE-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MSE-5)

The MSE-5 was an experimental model of general, multi-purpose droid manufactured as part of the MSE series by Rebaxan Columni, a company based on the Chadra-Fan homeworld of Chad. Both the MSE-5 and the also experimental MSE-4 were inspired by the chak-chak, a small, six-limbed rodent native to Chad.

## Specifications

- **Manufacturer:** Rebaxan Columni
