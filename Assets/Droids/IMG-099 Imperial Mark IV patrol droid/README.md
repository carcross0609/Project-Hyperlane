# IMG-099 Imperial Mark IV patrol droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IMG-099 Imperial Mark IV patrol droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IMG-099_Imperial_Mark_IV_patrol_droid)

The IMG-099 Imperial Mark IV patrol droid, also known as Imperial Mark IV sentry droid, was a model of sentry droid developed by the Imperial Department of Military Research. They were used by the Galactic Empire for scouting missions during the Galactic Civil War. The low-flying droids were equipped with efficient sensors and could record holographic images.

- **Name:** IMG-099 Imperial Mark IV patrol droid

- **Height:** 0.3 meters
- **Equipment:**

  - Motion sensor
- **Affiliation:**
