# Mick

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mick)

Mick was a INS-444 that worked with the CLE-004 window cleaning droid Buffy on the ecumenopolis of Coruscant. The two droids replaced the bedroom window of Senator Padmé Amidala's apartment after the original window was destroyed through the actions of Zam Wesell's assassin droid and Obi-Wan Kenobi.
