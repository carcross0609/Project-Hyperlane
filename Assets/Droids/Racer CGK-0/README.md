# Racer CGK-0

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Racer CGK-0](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Racer_CGK-0)

Racer CGK-0 was a droid that was used in droid races on the Promenade of Nar Shaddaa during the Grand Acquisitions Race, a galaxy-wide scavenger hunt organized by the Chevin Conglomerate during the Cold War. Sapaaga the Hutt was a frequent better on the Promenade races and Racer CGK-0 was his favorite droid.
