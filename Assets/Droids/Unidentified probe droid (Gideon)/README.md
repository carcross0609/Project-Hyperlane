# Unidentified probe droid (Gideon)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified probe droid (Gideon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_probe_droid_%28Gideon%29)

A Viper probe droid served Moff Gideon during the New Republic Era. In 9 ABY, the droid was present on Coruscant to conduct a report between Gideon and Amnesty officer and spy Elia Kane.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Galactic Empire
- **Name:** Unidentified probe droid
- **Line:** Viper probe droid
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Height:** 1.6 m (5 ft 3 in)
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:**
  - Blaster
  - Self-destruct mechanism
- **Equipment:**
  - Holoprojector
  - Manipulator arms
- **Affiliation:** Gideon's Imperial remnant
