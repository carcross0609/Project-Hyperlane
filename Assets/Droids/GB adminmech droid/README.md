# GB adminmech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GB adminmech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GB_adminmech_droid)

The GB adminmech droid was a type of adminmech droid. The GB adminmech droid GB-BD was forced to work in the control room of the spice mines of Kessel for the Pyke Syndicate. When the droid L3-37 initiated a uprising by removing the restraining bolts of droids in the control room, GB-BD was freed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GB adminmech droid
- **Class:** Adminmech droid
- **Plating:** Blue and white
