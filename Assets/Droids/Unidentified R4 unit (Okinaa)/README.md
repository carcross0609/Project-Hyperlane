# Unidentified R4 unit (Okinaa)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Unidentified R4 unit (Okinaa)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_R4_unit_%28Okinaa%29)

An R4 astromech droid, along with a CZ protocol droid, searched for a village on a planet. During their search, the astromech droid admitted it did not know if they were headed toward a village, and the two droids began arguing. At night, the two droids saw a distant fire that the former Sith known as the Ronin had set to burn the body of the Sith Okinaa, the last member of the village. The protocol droid realized they were on the same path they had been on previously, and the two droids continued arguing.
