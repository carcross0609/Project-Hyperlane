# P3-7A

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [P3-7A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P3-7A)

P3-7A, or Peethree, was a droid owned by Keth Cerepath during the High Republic Era. He was saved from scrap and rebuilt by Bonbraks using the vocoder from an old Temple droid. His attempts at being sarcastic were often mistaken for piety, which caused him to be barred from Enlightenment soon after his first visit. Due to the origins of his vocoder, he was only capable of speaking in epithets related to the Force, such as "The Force knows all. The Force hears all. There can be no secrets from the Force," "If one remains calm, they will always find peace in the Force" and even "May the Force be with you."
