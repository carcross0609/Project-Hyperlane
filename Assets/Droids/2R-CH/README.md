# 2R-CH

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [2R-CH](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2R-CH)

2R-CH, also known as Torch, was a battle droid equipped with a flamethrower who worked with the Advozse Hegemony during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. Around 3643 BBY, 2R-CH occupied Hammer Station with other Hegemony forces and was eventually destroyed by a strike team that proceeded to destroy the space station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 2R-CH
- **Death:** c. 3643 BBY, Hammer Station
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Armament:** Flamethrower
- **Affiliation:** Advozse Hegemony
