# Tardi (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tardi (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tardi_%28droid%29)

The droid Viscount Tardi was designed to replace the Human Tardi who had died more than a month before the Rebel Alliance had intended to have him conduct a financial transaction for them. The Rebels decided to secretly replace Tardi with a droid to help Princess Leia Organa negotiate the sale of T-65 X-wing starfighters at the Bank of Aargau due to Tardi's impeccable reputation with the bank.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tardi
- **Birth:** 3 ABY
- **Death:** 3 ABY, Aargau
- **Class:** Human replica droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** He/him
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
