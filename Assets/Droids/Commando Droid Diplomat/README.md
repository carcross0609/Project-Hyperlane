# Commando Droid Diplomat

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Commando Droid Diplomat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commando_Droid_Diplomat)

Commando Droid Diplomats were specialized models of BX-series droid commandos specialized for diplomatic occasions. They differed from regular commando droids with their brown, yellow and blue colored plating and they wielded E-5 blaster rifles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Commando Droid Diplomat
- **Model:** BX-series droid commando
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Fourth-degree droid
- **Height:** 1.91 meters (6 ft, 3 in)
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:**
  - Blue
  - Brown
  - Yellow
- **Armament:** E-5 blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
