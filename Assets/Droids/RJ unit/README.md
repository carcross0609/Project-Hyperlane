# RJ unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RJ unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RJ_unit)

An RJ unit, also known as an RJ series, was a series of droid used during the High Republic Era. RJ-83 was a member of this series. RJ-21 was an old RJ series droid that lived with the Tallimarian scavenger Velorie Sarcon on Jakku at the time of the First Order-Resistance War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RJ unit
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Orange and white
