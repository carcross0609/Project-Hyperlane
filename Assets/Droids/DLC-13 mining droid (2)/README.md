# DLC-13 mining droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DLC-13 mining droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DLC-13_mining_droid)

The DLC-13 mining droid, also known as the Mustafar panning droid, droid lava collector or DLC-13 panning droid, was a mining droid used to extract valuable ores from inhospitable environments, like Mustafar and the Belgaroth asteroid field. The droids were made from heat-resistant carbonite.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DLC-13 mining droid
- **Homeworld:** Mustafar
- **Degree:** Class Five
- **Height:** 1.5 meters
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Equipment:**
  - Heat-resistant basket
  - Repulsorlift
  - Sensors
