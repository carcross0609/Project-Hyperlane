# JC series pilot droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [JC series pilot droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JC_series_pilot_droid)

The JC series was a pilot droid model produced by Industrial Automaton and derived from the V-series pilot droid. Unlike other pilot droids, they were programmed to be competitors and pushed starships to the maximum.

## Specifications

- **Name:** JC series pilot droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Cost:** 15,000 credits
- **Gender:** Female
