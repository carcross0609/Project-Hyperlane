# R5-T3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R5-T3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-T3)

R5-T3, also known as "Sparks" was an R5 astromech droid used by Dex Tiree. It was named after a saying Dex's mother said often, "Small sparks light big fires." Sparks and Dex perished in the Battle of Yavin.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Rebel
- **Name:** R5-T3
- **Death:** During the Battle of Yavin, Yavin system
- **Line:** R-series
- **Model:** R5 unit
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Affiliation:**
