# ID-75 Heavy Construction Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ID-75 Heavy Construction Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ID-75_Heavy_Construction_Droid)

The ID-75 Heavy Construction Droid was a model of construction droid that was created by the Gran cyborg Ayor-v9, who used them on Hoth during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. Ayor considered his droids to be his "family."

## Specifications

- **Name:** ID-75 Heavy Construction Droid
- **Creator:** Ayor-v9
- **Class:** Construction droid
