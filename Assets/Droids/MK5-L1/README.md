# MK5-L1

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MK5-L1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MK5-L1)

MK5-L1 was a black L-1g general purpose droid that was forced to work as the landing-pad operations droid in the criminal Pyke Syndicate's spice mines on the planet Kessel during the reign of the Galactic Empire. In 10 BBY, MK5-L1 was freed from slavery when its restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37.
