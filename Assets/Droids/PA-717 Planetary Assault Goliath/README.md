# PA-717 Planetary Assault Goliath

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PA-717 Planetary Assault Goliath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PA-717_Planetary_Assault_Goliath)

PA-717 Planetary Assault Goliath was a massive-sized Skytrooper, a battle droid used by the Eternal Empire of Zakuul. During the Invasion of Voss in 3630 BBY, it was deployed in the middle of Voss-Ka, where it was destroyed by Lana Beniko and the Alliance Commander.
