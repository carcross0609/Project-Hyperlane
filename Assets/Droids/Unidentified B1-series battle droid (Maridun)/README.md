# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Maridun)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Maridun)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Maridun%29)

A B1-series battle droid served under the command of General Lok Durd during the Clone Wars. While stationed on the planet Maridun, the battle droid was stationed in a Separatist command center and commanded his fellow units, only to be cut down by Jedi General Anakin Skywalker in a surprise attack.
