# I-82 intel droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I-82 intel droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I-82_intel_droid)

The I-82 intel droid was an intelligence droid model that resembled normal-looking utility droids in appearance. Such droids could be found in large numbers at the Upper Industrial Sector on Nar Shaddaa during the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** I-82 intel droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Rust
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
