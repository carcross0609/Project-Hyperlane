# 459

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [459](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/459)

459 was a Separatist B2-HA super battle droid who crashed on the planet Koboh aboard the Lucrehulk-class battleship Primacy, and was found and reprogrammed by the Bedlam Raiders along with the rest of the core ship's complement. In 9 BBY, 459 argued with fellow B2-HA droid 388 in the Primacy's wreckage over which of them was superior.
