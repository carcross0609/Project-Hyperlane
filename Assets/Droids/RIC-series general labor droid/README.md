# RIC-series general labor droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RIC-series general labor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RIC-series_general_labor_droid)

The RIC-series general labor droid, also known as the rickshaw droid or unipod droid, was a crude model of labor droid designed to pull rickshaws. Covered in brown plating, they stood 1.1 meters tall, had two arms that each ended in a hand with three clawed digits, and moved around on two wheels located at the base of their bodies. One of the screens in the Outlander Club, showed a Nuna-ball League game, where a team of RIC-series droids were competing against a team of Otoga-222 maintenance droids. A rickshaw droid known as RIC-920 worked in Mos Espa, a port city on the planet Tatooine, shortly before the First Battle of Geonosis.
