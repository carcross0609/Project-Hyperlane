# Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Cad Bane's frigate)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Cad Bane's frigate)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_B1-series_battle_droid_%28Cad_Bane%27s_frigate%29)

A B1 battle droid served aboard Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate during the Battle of Devaron, where he worked on the torture and interrogation of the captured Jedi Master Bolla Ropal. Cad Bane ordered for the Jedi's torture to continue, but this resulted in Ropal ultimately being tortured to death. As such, the B1-series battle droid, who controlled the interrogation levels and viewed the Jedi's vital signs, reported to Bane that Ropal was dead.
