# YVH-908

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YVH-908](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YVH-908)

YVH-908 was a YVH droid assigned by Jacen Solo to guard Allana. 908 went everywhere Allana went, and accompanied her several times on the Anakin Solo where 908 would be destroyed by Han and Leia Organa Solo during their rescue of Allana.
