# IR-9X Replicator Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IR-9X Replicator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-9X_Replicator_Droid)

The IR-9X Replicator Droid was a model of humanoid battle droid used by the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. IR-9X Replicators were capable of generating sophisticated holograms over their frames, allowing for highly-effective disguises, and a number of them were stored at the Shroud's factory on the moon Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IR-9X Replicator Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Pink
- **Plating:** Silver and black
- **Equipment:** Holographic disguise system
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
