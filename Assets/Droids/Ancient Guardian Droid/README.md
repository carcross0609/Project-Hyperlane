# Ancient Guardian Droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ancient Guardian Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Guardian_Droid)

The Ancient Guardian Droid was a large and ancient security droid that was used by Tenebrae to guard one of his secret vaults on Nathema. The droid was destroyed by the Eternal Alliance during the search for the Zildrog superweapon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ancient Guardian Droid
- **Death:** 3628 BBY, Nathema
- **Pronouns:** new
- **Affiliation:** Tenebrae
