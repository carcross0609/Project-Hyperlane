# Mark II Z3-NO landscaping droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark II Z3-NO landscaping droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_II_Z3-NO_landscaping_droid)

The Mark II Z3-NO landscaping droid was a model of gardener droid. One such droid was involved in the colonization of Ohma-D'un in 32 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark II Z3-NO landscaping droid
- **Class:** Gardener droid
- **Plating:** Orange
- **Equipment:**
  - Diggers
  - Grinders
  - Compressors
  - Hydrators
  - Vaporators
  - Pulverizers
- **Firstmade:** 32 BBY or before
