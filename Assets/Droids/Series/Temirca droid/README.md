# Temirca droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Temirca droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temirca_droid)

The Temirca droid was a droid used in Mos Espa as of 32 BBY. Its head was yellow with gray eyes and a long, cone-shaped object for its mouth.

- **Name:** Temirca droid
