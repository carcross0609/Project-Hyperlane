# Unidentified droid model (9-0-9)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified droid model (9-0-9)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_droid_model_%289-0-9%29)

The droids were a model of bipedal droid. By 229 BBY, one of these droids served onboard the Queen Xiri on the planet Eiram. Later, during the Galactic Civil War era, this model was employed by the Galactic Empire on some of their starships. LE-BO2D9 was a dry-witted droid that piloted a YT-2400 light freighter.
