# Administrative Droid AD-12

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Administrative Droid AD-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Administrative_Droid_AD-12)

The Administrative Droid AD-12 was a three-legged supervisor droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War. A number of AD-12 droids oversaw operations in the Okara Droid Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Administrative Droid AD-12
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Supervisor droid
- **Armament:** Arm-mounted blaster cannons
