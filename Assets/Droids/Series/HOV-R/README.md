# HOV-R

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [HOV-R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HOV-R)

The HOV-R was a model of hovering battle droid with red and yellow plating and black photoreceptors. As a type of monster droid, HOV-Rs had parts from two other droid models: the head of a Cybot Galactica Senate hovercam droid and the four limbs of a ID10 seeker droid. Blueprints for four variants of the droid were sold by the group of Jawas who ran the Sandcrawler Shop beside the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine. A blueprint for the HOV-R's default design cost 140,000 credits, a golden design cost 560,000 credits, a diamond-colored version cost 1,120,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design cost 2,240,000 credits.
