# R0-M3 Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R0-M3 Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R0-M3_Droid)

The R0-M3 Droid was a model of humanoid droid used by the Hutt Cartel on the moon Nar Shaddaa during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. A number of R0-M3 units were among the dozens of droids that malfunctioned in the Network Security District and went on a rampage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R0-M3 Droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Green
- **Affiliation:** Hutt Cartel
