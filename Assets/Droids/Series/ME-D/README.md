# ME-D

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ME-D](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ME-D)

The ME-D droids, pronounced Meedee, were specially-designed probe droids built by the Jedi Knight Leeha Narezz during the Cold War. They were taught Jedi history and philosophy by Leeha in hopes of proving that even droids could use the Force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ME-D
- **Creator:** Leeha Narezz
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Galactic Republic
