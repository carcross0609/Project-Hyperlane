# IT-S00.2 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IT-S00.2 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IT-S00.2_medical_droid)

The IT-S00.2 medical droid was a model of medical droid with a black spherical body and three delicate multiceps for injury treatment. In the year 34 ABY, the First Order operative Kylo Ren was treated by an IT-S00.2 on board the Mega-class Star Dreadnought Supremacy after he received a bowcaster wound to his abdomen and a lightsaber wound to his right shoulder and scar across one side of his chest and face during the Resistance attack on Starkiller Base. The droid managed to seal the wound using mechnosutures, but applied bacta therapy too late to prevent scarring.
