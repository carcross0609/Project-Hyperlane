# Krath war droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krath war droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath_war_droid/Legends)

The Krath war droid was a powerful battle droid series created by the Krath, an organization of darksiders founded by the corrupt nobles of the Empress Teta system in 4000 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Krath war droid
- **Manufacturer:** Cinnagar foundries
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Height:** 1.8 meters
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Armament:**
  - Pulse-wave bow
  - Melee combat short sword
- **Equipment:** Ablative plating
- **Affiliation:** Krath
