# HV-7

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HV-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HV-7)

HV-7s were a type of loader droid that was in use alongside CCLs and IW-37 pincer loader droids at a shipyard on the planet Corellia. The yard had been controlled by the Nightsister Morgan Elsbeth during the reign of the Galactic Empire, but had been taken over by the New Republic by 9 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HV-7
- **Class:** Loader droid
- **Affiliation:** Morgan Elsbeth's forces
