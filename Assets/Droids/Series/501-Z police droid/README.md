# 501-Z police droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [501-Z police droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/501-Z_police_droid)

501-Z police droids, also nicknamed Zeds, were police droids that were used by law-enforcement and police agencies to maintain order and apprehend criminals. These droids were SoroSuub Corporation's only advance into the police and law-enforcement market.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 501-Z police droid
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Class:** Police droid
- **Height:** 1.87 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine Programming
- **Equipment:**
  - Vocabulator
  - Rear movement sensors
  - Recharge coupling
  - Holographic projector/Recorder
  - Heavy servogrips
- **Affiliation:** Various police and law enforcement agencies
