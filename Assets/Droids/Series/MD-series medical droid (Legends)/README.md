# MD-series medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-series medical droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-series_medical_droid/Legends)

The MD-series medical droid was a line of medical droids manufactured by Industrial Automaton. The MD medical specialists were a common sight throughout the galaxy, and while not as advanced as the 2-1B, the MDs were highly respected on medical centers of less-civilized planets, where their low cost and high levels of specialization on specific medical fields were a big help for local physicians. They were also liked for their lower prices, as a standard unit would normally cost around 2,000 credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MD-series medical droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Degree:** Class one
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits
