# DL-2 line

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DL-2 line](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DL-2_line)

The DL-2 line was a type of B1-series battle droid. In 3 ABY, a Rodian droidsmith in Myrra, the capital city of Akiva, sold battle droids including at least one of the DL-2 line. They priced the DL-2 line at 500 credits each.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DL-2 line
- **Line:** B1-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
