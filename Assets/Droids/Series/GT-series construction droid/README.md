# GT-series construction droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GT-series construction droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GT-series_construction_droid)

The GT-series construction droid was a construction droid produced by Veril Line Systems. With a burly design, the GT-series possessed numerous tools and could undertake various tasks on construction sites. While GT-series droids were focused in their tasks, units could develop personalities and act overly talkative when not at work.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GT-series construction droid
- **Manufacturer:** Veril Line Systems
- **Class:** Construction droid
- **Degree:** Class five droid
- **Cost:** 17,500 credits
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Construction tools
  - Fusioncutter
  - I-C4a droid interface programming suite
