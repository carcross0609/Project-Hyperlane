# EW-39 War Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EW-39 War Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EW-39_War_Droid)

The EW-39 War Droid, also known as the AC-MKII Droid, was a battle droid model used for security by the Hutt Cartel on Nar Shaddaa during the Cold War. The droid moved on three legs and had its armaments inside the upper appendages.

## Specifications

- **Name:** EW-39 War Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Green
- **Affiliation:** Hutt Cartel
