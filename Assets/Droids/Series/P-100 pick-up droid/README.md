# P-100 pick-up droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [P-100 pick-up droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-100_pick-up_droid)

The P-100 pick-up droid, also known simply as the pick-up droid, was a droid used for manual relocation of large items. It could be deployed from the underside of P-100 Salvage Droids to retrieve valuable parts from crashed podracers and return them to the storage hold of the P-100.

## Specifications

- **Name:** P-100 pick-up droid
- **Equipment:** Manipulator arms
