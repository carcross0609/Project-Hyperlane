# Staircase droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Staircase droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Staircase_droid)

A staircase droid was a model of droid that acted as a staircase was used to allow a passenger to board a shuttle ferry at their respective stations on the planet Ferrix in 5 BBY. The droids had two photoreceptors and three white stair steps with red markings. The staircase droids moved with repulsorlifts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Staircase droid
- **Class:** Service droid
- **Equipment:**
  - Repulsorlift
  - Photoreceptors
