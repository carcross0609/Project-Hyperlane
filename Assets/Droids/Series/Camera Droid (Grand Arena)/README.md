# Camera Droid (Grand Arena)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Camera Droid (Grand Arena)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Camera_Droid_%28Grand_Arena%29)

Camera Droids were cam droids used to film and broadcast the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition live. They were deployed during and between matches, and would fly over the arenas, pausing at intervals to slowly pan and capture footage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Camera Droid
- **Class:** Cam droid
- **Plating:** White and yellow
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
