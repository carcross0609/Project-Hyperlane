# SO-1P autovalet droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SO-1P autovalet droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SO-1P_autovalet_droid)

SO-1P autovalet droids were a type of laundry droid. One was present on the Mega-class Star Dreadnought Supremacy when Rose Tico and Finn infiltrated it during the Battle of Oetchi. It was at work tending to First Order military uniforms in the laundry room that the two Resistance operatives stole officer disguises from for their mission.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SO-1P autovalet droid
- **Manufacturer:** Serv-O-Droid, Inc.
- **Model:** Laundry droid
- **Class:** Service droid
- **Degree:** Class five droid
- **Affiliation:** First Order
