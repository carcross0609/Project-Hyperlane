# CZ-2X Infiltrator Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CZ-2X Infiltrator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-2X_Infiltrator_Droid)

CZ-2X Infiltrator Droids were battle droids created by Czerka Arms during the era of the Galactic War. Smaller than their sister-models, the Infiltrator droids were utilized to augment the capabiliites of CZ-8X, an Eradicator droid guarding the Atrium during the conflict brought on by seizure of Czerka's assets by the Republic Adjucators and resurgent Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CZ-2X Infiltrator Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Gray and yellow
- **Affiliation:** Czerka Arms
