# DC5-1

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DC5-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DC5-1)

DC5-1 was the designation of a series of freight droids manufactured by Serv-O-Droid, Inc.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DC5-1
- **Manufacturer:** Serv-O-Droid, Inc.
- **Class:** Freight droid
- **Cost:** 4,500 credits
- **Length:** 2.8 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Cranial turret with audio and video sensors
  - Four extendable manipulator arms
  - Laser scanner
  - Pair of lifting claws
  - Remote directional transponder
