# Custodian droid (Iokath)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Custodian droid (Iokath)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Custodian_droid_%28Iokath%29)

Custodian droid was a type of droid designed by the unidentified Iokath species to tend to gardens and parks on their artificial world of Iokath. The species went extinct following the Iokath Civil War and custodian droids were repurposed by ARIES into the combat units, aggressive to any intruders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Custodian droid
- **Creator:** Unidentified Iokath species
- **Homeworld:** Iokath
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Brown
- **Armament:** Blaster Rifle
- **Affiliation:**

  - ARIES
