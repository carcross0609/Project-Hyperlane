# Haxion Brood Bounty Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Haxion Brood Bounty Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Haxion_Brood_Bounty_Droid)

Haxion Brood Bounty Droids were reprogrammed loadlifter droids used by the criminal organization Haxion Brood to hunt bounties along with their own bounty hunters. Assembled from smuggled and stolen parts, the heavily-built enforcers served the gang with brute force. The droids came in a variety of names, colors, and designs, with some brandishing antennas and others having padding along their shoulders. With a cocky and hostile attitude, they attacked as rugged brawlers, taunting their foes in combat. Occasionally, they would be accompanied by Haxion Brood commandos.
