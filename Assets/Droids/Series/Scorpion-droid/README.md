# Scorpion-droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scorpion-droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scorpion-droid)

The scorpion-droid was a droid found on a planet in Wild Space. The droids were programmed to destroy all life on the planet. They were stopped when a Energy projector was placed and were buried in the ground. The droids were reactivated when Captain Han Solo, his first mate Chewbacca, and the bounty hunter Zuckuss destroyed the projector's power source. The droids reactivated and attacked them. The scorpion-droids continued to attack Solo, Chewbacca, Zuckuss and 4-LOM. They were able to pursue the four of them and followed the group as they swam to Solo's starship, the Millennium Falcon. Solo was able to get them off the Falcon hull. When Solo tricked Zuckuss and 4-LOM, the droids resumed their attack on the two bounty hunters.
