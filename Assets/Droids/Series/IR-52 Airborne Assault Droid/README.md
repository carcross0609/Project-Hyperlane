# IR-52 Airborne Assault Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IR-52 Airborne Assault Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-52_Airborne_Assault_Droid)

The IR-52 Airborne Assault Droid was a model of bipedal battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. The IR-52 was designed for aerial combat environments, and had built-in rockets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IR-52 Airborne Assault Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Dark
- **Armament:** Vibroblade
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
