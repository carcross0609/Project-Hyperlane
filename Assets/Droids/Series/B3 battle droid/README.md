# B3 battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B3 battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B3_battle_droid)

B3 battle droids were battle droids manufactured by Baktoid Combat Automata and used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. B2-series super battle droids and B3 battle droids were favored over the expensive and complex E522 assassin droids. At one point, at least one was stationed near a listening station on the planet Vanqor. A curved piece of metal used in B3 repair was uncovered by Quinlan Vos when he and a Republic strike team attacked the listening post. The B3 battle droid was also mentioned in an intelligence report by Corporal WAC-47 describing E522 assassin droids, which was included in the survival compendium "Neyo's Almanac of Clankers."
