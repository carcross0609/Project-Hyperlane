# MD-15C medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MD-15C medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-15C_medical_droid)

The MD-15C medical droid was a model of MD-series medical droid with a humanoid body shape and white plating. Their two arms had modular appendage sockets at the elbow joint and their featureless faces were designed to be non-threatening. A newer model than the 2-1B surgical droid, it had a vastly larger database that allowed it to treat thousands of additional species.
