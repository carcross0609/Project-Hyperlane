# SD-54 Neutralization Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SD-54 Neutralization Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-54_Neutralization_Droid)

The SD-54 Neutralization Droid was a model of bipedal battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. SD-54 Neutralization Droids were among the droid forces that defended the Shroud's factory on the moon Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SD-54 Neutralization Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Bronze
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
