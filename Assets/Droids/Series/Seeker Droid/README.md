# Seeker Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seeker Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seeker_Droid)

The Seeker Droid was a specialized droid designed by Galactic Solutions Industries during the Cold War and the Galactic War to dig underneath the surface of a planet and extract treasures buried there.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Seeker Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Galactic Solutions Industries
