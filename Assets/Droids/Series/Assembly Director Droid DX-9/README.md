# Assembly Director Droid DX-9

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Assembly Director Droid DX-9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assembly_Director_Droid_DX-9)

The Assembly Director Droid DX-9 was a droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company that was used to oversee the assembly line of the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. Upgraded versions included the Director Droid DX-10 and the Prototype Director Droid DX-11.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Assembly Director Droid DX-9
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
