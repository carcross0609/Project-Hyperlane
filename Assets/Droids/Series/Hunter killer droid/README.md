# Hunter killer droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hunter killer droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_killer_droid)

Hunter killer droids were large, lumbering battle droids that were outdated by the year 5 ABY. Also known as a war droid, one hunter killer droid, 8-13, was stationed on the moon Sulon during 5 ABY.
