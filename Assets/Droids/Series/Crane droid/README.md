# Crane droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Crane droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crane_droid)

Crane droids also known as cargo lifter droids were industrial droids. They were employed by the Galactic Empire on their mobile mining operation on the planet Alabash under the command of Control, a Terreene Brainiac class of artificial intelligence. One model of crane droid was the 2V9 cargo lifter droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton.

- **Name:** Crane droid
- **Class:** Industrial droid
