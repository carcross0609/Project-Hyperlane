# Nightsister service droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nightsister service droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsister_service_droid)

Those droids, the head of which somewhat resembled the one of the EV-series supervisor droid, were used by the Nightsisters of Dathomir. Around 31 BBY, such droids were seen serving drinks to Queen Zalem and her fellow Sisters under a tent.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nightsister service droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Affiliation:** Nightsisters
