# Strike Droid SD

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Strike Droid SD](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strike_Droid_SD)

The Strike Droid SD was a battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on Balmorra. A number of those droids were used to defend the factory during the Battle of Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Strike Droid SD
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
