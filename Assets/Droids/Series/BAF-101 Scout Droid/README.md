# BAF-101 Scout Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-101 Scout Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-101_Scout_Droid)

The BAF-101 Scout Droid was a bipedal battle droid produced by Balmorran Arms on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. BAF-101 Scout Droids were armed with blaster rifles, and were among the forces used by the Galactic Republic to defend the Balmorran Arms Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-101 Scout Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Balmorran resistance
