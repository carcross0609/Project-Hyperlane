# Sublight survey droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sublight survey droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sublight_survey_droid)

The sublight survey droid was a survey droid used by the Galactic Republic. One of these droids visited the planet Sedri several hundred years before the Galactic Civil War; a report was logged but the planet was forgotten.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sublight survey droid
- **Class:** Survey droid
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
