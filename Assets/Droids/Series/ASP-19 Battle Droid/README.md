# ASP-19 Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ASP-19 Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ASP-19_Battle_Droid)

The ASP-19 Battle Droid was a model of battle droid developed from the ASP-series labor droid. It was popular among Jedi for sparring during the last years of the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ASP-19 Battle Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** ASP-series labor droid
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Cost:** 80,000 credits
