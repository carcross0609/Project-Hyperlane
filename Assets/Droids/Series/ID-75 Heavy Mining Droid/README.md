# ID-75 Heavy Mining Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ID-75 Heavy Mining Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ID-75_Heavy_Mining_Droid)

The ID-75 Heavy Mining Droid was a model of mining droid that was created by the Gran cyborg Ayor-v9, who used them on Hoth during the Galactic War and considered his droids to be his "family."

## Specifications

- **Name:** ID-75 Heavy Mining Droid
- **Creator:** Ayor-v9
- **Class:** Mining droid
