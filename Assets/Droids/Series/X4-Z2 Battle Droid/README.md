# X4-Z2 Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X4-Z2 Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X4-Z2_Battle_Droid)

The X4-Z2 Battle Droid was a model of three-legged battle droid used by the Exchange criminal organization on the moon Nar Shaddaa during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The Exchange used a number of X4-Z2 droids to patrol the Coronet Boulevard of the Corellian Sector.

## Specifications

- **Name:** X4-Z2 Battle Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Dark gray
- **Armament:** Blasters mounted to the arms and chin
- **Affiliation:** Exchange
