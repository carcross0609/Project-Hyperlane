# Mark VIII Enforcer Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark VIII Enforcer Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_VIII_Enforcer_Droid)

The Mark VIII Enforcer Droid was a model of three-legged battle droid that was used by the forces of the Specialists, a mercenary unit. Mark VIII Enforcer Droids were deployed under the control of QO-99 to the planet Makeb during the Conquest of Makeb.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark VIII Enforcer Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Affiliation:** Specialists
