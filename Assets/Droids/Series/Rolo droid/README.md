# Rolo droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rolo droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rolo_droid)

Rolo droids were a type of droid that rolled on a singular wheel. One rolo droid was present in Mos Espa on Tatooine in 32 BBY, and passed by the Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn and the young slave Anakin Skywalker.
