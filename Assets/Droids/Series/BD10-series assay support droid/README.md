# BD10-series assay support droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BD10-series assay support droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD10-series_assay_support_droid)

The BD10-series assay support droid was a class of droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton, designed to assist with cultures in less civilized areas of the galaxy. BD10-series droids were often used when negotiating with cultures in the Outer Rim.
