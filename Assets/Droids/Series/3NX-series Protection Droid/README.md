# 3NX-series Protection Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [3NX-series Protection Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3NX-series_Protection_Droid)

The 3NX-series Protection Droid was a model of security droid available during the Galactic Civil War. Despite their surprising sturdiness, the droid series was never a top seller, as they had a tendency to complain when shot. At least one 3NX-series Protection Droid joined the crew of a young smuggler at the time of the Iron Blockade.
