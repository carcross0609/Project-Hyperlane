# Shopkeeper droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shopkeeper droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shopkeeper_droid)

Shopkeeper droids, also known as sales droids, vendor droids or shopkeeping drones, were a type of droid designed to function as merchants. They were proprogrammed to be obsequious and desire to make customers happy. The GL vendor droid was a model of shopkeeper droid as was another model with a wide head. Jawas modified R1-series astromech droids into R1-type shopkeeping drones. They were able to show holograms of varying sizes. Their way of movement could be described as "rolling" and "tottering". A sales droid on Coruscant showed images of freighters to Affie Hollow and Scover Byne.
