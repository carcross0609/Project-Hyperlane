# CP-7 Anti-Cover Probe

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CP-7 Anti-Cover Probe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CP-7_Anti-Cover_Probe)

The CP-7 Anti-Cover Probe was a model of small probe droid used by the Chiss spy Maki'voro, a lieutenant of the spymaster known as the Shroud during the Galactic War. One of a number of probe models that Maki'voro used, the CP-7 probe was designed to force enemies out of cover.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CP-7 Anti-Cover Probe
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
