# Safeguard Mk II

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Safeguard Mk II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Safeguard_Mk_II)

The Safeguard Mk II was a model of three-legged battle droid produced by Holowan Laboratories on the moon Nar Shaddaa during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. Produced at their Holowan Labs facility in the Network Security District, a number of Safeguard Mk II units were used to patrol the facility.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Safeguard Mk II
- **Manufacturer:** Holowan Laboratories
- **Homeworld:** Nar Shaddaa
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Tan
- **Armament:** Arm- and chin-mounted blaster cannons
- **Affiliation:** Holowan Laboratories
