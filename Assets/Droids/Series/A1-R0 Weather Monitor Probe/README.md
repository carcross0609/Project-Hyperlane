# A1-R0 Weather Monitor Probe

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A1-R0 Weather Monitor Probe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A1-R0_Weather_Monitor_Probe)

The A1-R0 Weather Monitor Probe was a type of probe droid used by the Selkath species on the planet Manaan during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. The A1-R0 Probe was used to monitor the surface weather of the planet at surface installations such as the upper section of Genetics Laboratory G-1.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A1-R0 Weather Monitor Probe
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Sensor:** Green and red
- **Plating:** Black
