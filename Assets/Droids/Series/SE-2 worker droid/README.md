# SE-2 worker droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SE-2 worker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SE-2_worker_droid)

The SE-2 worker droid, also known as the farm droid, was a loose-jointed model of utilitarian menial labor droid featuring a monocular photoreceptor with dual-imaging plane. An SE-2 named "Essie" worked on the farm of Galen Erso on Lah'mu, and he alerted the Erso family when he spotted Director Orson Krennic's Imperial shuttle approaching the homestead in 13 BBY. T-1N8, nicknamed Tina, was another SE-2 worker droid who resided on the planet Koboh. SE-5 was a SE-2 droid that served as a pilot for the criminal Finto Gang on Batuu.
