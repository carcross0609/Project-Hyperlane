# G-2RD series guard droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-2RD series guard droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-2RD_series_guard_droid)

The G-2RD Series Guard Droid was a small, fourth-degree defense droid built by Arakyd Industries. The spherical, flat, black security droid had three evenly-spaced arms, sensor antennae studding its bottom, and multiple photoreceptors surrounding its surface. The G-2RD could manipulate locks and speak, read, and write Basic.
