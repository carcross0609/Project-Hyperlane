# R6 astromech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R6 astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R6_astromech_droid)

R6 astromech droids also known as the R6-series were a series of astromech droids. Much like the R5 series, the R6's head was shaped like a conical frustum, but its eye piece more closely resembled that of the R2 series.
