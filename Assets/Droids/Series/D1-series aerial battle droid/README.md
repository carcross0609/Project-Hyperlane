# D1-series aerial battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D1-series aerial battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D1-series_aerial_battle_droid)

The D1-series aerial battle droid, also known as the D-wing air support droid, the D-wing air security droid, D-wing security droid or simply the D-wing droid, was a D1-series aerial battle droid and a variant of the B-series droid line. The droids had wings that they used to pursue enemies to areas where normal battle droids could not. They were developed by the Techno Union, who operated out of a facility on the planet Skako Minor. They were active throughout the Clone Wars.
