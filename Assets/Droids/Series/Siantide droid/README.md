# Siantide droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Siantide droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Siantide_droid)

The Siantide droids were battle droids developed by the Galactic Republic's War Trust group, with Siantide technology. The prototype was destroyed by Darth Baras's apprentice during his hunt for General Faraire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Siantide droid
- **Manufacturer:** War Trust
- **Homeworld:** Taris
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
- **Firstmade:** c. 3642 BBY
