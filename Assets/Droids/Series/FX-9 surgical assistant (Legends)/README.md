# FX-9 surgical assistant

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FX-9 surgical assistant/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-9_surgical_assistant/Legends)

The FX-9 surgical assistant was a medical droid manufactured by Medtech Industries as part of the FX-series of medical assistant droids. Produced during the last decades of the Galactic Republic, it was ultimately succeeded by the FX-10 model.

- **Manufacturer:** Medtech Industries
