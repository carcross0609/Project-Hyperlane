# Millicreep

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Millicreep/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Millicreep/Legends)

The Millicreep was an assassin droid used by the Umbaran militia during the Battle of Umbara at 21 BBY. It was about a foot and a half long and was easy to transport when deactivated. Unleashed by a company of Umbaran Militia when they ambushed a 501st battalion led by Jedi General Anakin Skywalker, one of these droids killed numerous clones with its electrified tail before being destroyed by General Skywalker's lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Millicreep
- **Homeworld:** Umbara
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Armament:** Electrified tail
- **Affiliation:**
  - Umbaran militia
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
