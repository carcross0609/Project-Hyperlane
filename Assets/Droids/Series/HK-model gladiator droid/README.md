# HK-model gladiator droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HK-model gladiator droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HK-model_gladiator_droid)

The HK-model gladiator droid, also known as a HK assassin unit, was a type of assassin and gladiator droid. Hutt crime lord Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi maintained HK-models that had his medallion. They were later found disassembled in a crate during a raid conducted on a Razzi Syndicate warehouse by the Crimson Dawn organization. They were then reassembled to verify parts and presented to Crimson Dawn lieutenant Qi'ra as well as their leader Dryden Vos. The HK-87 assassin droid was a type of HK unit.
