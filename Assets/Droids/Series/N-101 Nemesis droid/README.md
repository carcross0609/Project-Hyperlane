# N-101 Nemesis droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [N-101 Nemesis droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N-101_Nemesis_droid)

The N-101 Nemesis droid was a model of battle droid that was produced by the company Trang Robotics. They were equipped with repulsor generators and laser arrays and they could collapse themselves into small units, roughly a meter in length. The droids could also assume a stealth posture, in which they resembled harvester beetles. Parts from an N-101 Nemesis droid were stored in the armory of the resistance base Mountain Home on the planet Toprawa and in about 18 BBY, the Rodian mech-tech Geri used them to augment the defenses of the droid I-5YQ.
