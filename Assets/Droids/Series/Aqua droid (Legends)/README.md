# Aqua droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aqua droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aqua_droid/Legends)

Aqua droids were a advanced model of a battle droid able to fight both on land and underwater. They were used in the Separatists' attack on Kamino. They also were used in the Separatist's attempt to let the Quarren gain political control of the planet Mon Calamari.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aqua droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Height:** 2.83 meters
- **Mass:** 130 kilograms
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** Retractable laser cannon
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
