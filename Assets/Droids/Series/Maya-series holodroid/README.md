# Maya-series holodroid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maya-series holodroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maya-series_holodroid)

Maya-series holodroids were a type of holodroid capable of morphing their appearance. Three Maya-series holodroids—Maya-8, Maya-12, and Maya-14—operated on the planet Metagos.
