# Ranger X-1

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ranger X-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_X-1)

The Ranger X-1 was an antiquated model of masculine defense droid programmed to automatically fight any act of hostility. It had green plating with "X-1" emblazoned in white on its chest, a pair of white photoreceptors, and morphed into a box when deactivated. The series was terminated when the Intergalactic Law Agency was founded during the last century of the Galactic Republic, and the last of the X-1s were collected and scrapped.

## Specifications

- **Class:** Defense droid
