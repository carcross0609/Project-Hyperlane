# DD-19 "Overseer" labor pool droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DD-19 "Overseer" labor pool droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DD-19_%22Overseer%22_labor_pool_droid)

The DD-19 "Overseer" labor pool droid, also known simply as the DD-19 "Overseer" Droid or DD-19 Overseer, was a model of supervisor droid manufactured by Ubrikkian Steamworks. A DD-19 Overseer was among the droids infected by the Iron Eclipse technovirus created by Iaco and Iaclyn Stark. Another designated DD-19 served aboard the New Republic CR90 corvette FarStar during the Galactic Civil War.
