# Mustafar Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Mustafar Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mustafar_Droid)

The Mustafar Droid, also known as a crystal collecting archeological droid, was a type of probe droid utilized within the Vader's Castle battlefield in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara. They were designed for archeological purposes, equipped with a laser cutter and multiple clawed arms used to unearth and collect crystals that they could store in the collection pack on their backs.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mustafar Droid
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Armament:** Laser cutter
- **Equipment:**
  - Collection pack
  - Repulsorlift
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
