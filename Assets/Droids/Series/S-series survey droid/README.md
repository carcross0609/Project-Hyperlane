# S-series survey droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [S-series survey droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-series_survey_droid)

The S-series survey droid was a model of civilian survey droid designed by TransTech Droidworks. It was used as the basis for their SR-series scout/observation droid.
