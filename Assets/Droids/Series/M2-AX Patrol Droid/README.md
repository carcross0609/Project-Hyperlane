# M2-AX Patrol Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M2-AX Patrol Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M2-AX_Patrol_Droid)

M2-AX Patrol Droid was a patrol droid model used by the Galactic Republic during the Cold War. These droids patrolled the Republic power generator in the Upper Industrial Sector on Nar Shaddaa.
