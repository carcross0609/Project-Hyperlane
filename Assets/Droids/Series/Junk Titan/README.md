# Junk Titan

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Junk Titan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Junk_Titan)

Junk Titans were bits and pieces of trash held together by Kazdan Paratus' Force powers. They were part of Paratus' private army of junk golems. Starkiller faced at least four of these on Raxus Prime before confronting Paratus himself.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Junk Titan
- **Creator:** Kazdan Paratus
- **Homeworld:** Raxus Prime
- **Retired:** 3 BBY (after the death of Kazdan Paratus)
