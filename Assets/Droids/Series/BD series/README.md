# BD series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BD series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD_series)

The BD series was a series of class three Nanny droids in use during the Old Republic era. Jedi Knight Kerra Holt encountered several of these in the stronghold of the Sith twins Dromika and Quillan on the planet Byllura. They were charged with taking care of the twins, as neither of them was capable of taking care of themselves.

- **Armament:** None
