# Rocket battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rocket battle droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rocket_battle_droid/Legends)

Rocket battle droids, also known as hunter battle droids or pod hunter droids, were modified B1-series battle droids, equipped with rocket packs, fusioncutters and floodlights, which scouted and destroyed life pods adrift in space.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rocket battle droid

- **Line:** B-series battle droid
