# GA-series servant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GA-series servant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GA-series_servant_droid)

The GA-series servant droid, or just GA servant droid for short, was a model of servant droid. It had collapsable legs for compact storage, contained a power plant, and featured hydraulic lines. By the year 34 ABY, GA units were used in Takodana Castle on the planet Takodana. One such servant droid was GA-97, a member of the Resistance spy droid network who informed the Resistance that one of their astromech droids, BB-8, was on Takodana.
