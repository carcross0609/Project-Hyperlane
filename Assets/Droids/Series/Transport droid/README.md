# Transport droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Transport droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transport_droid)

A transport droid, also known as a tow droid, was a type of droid used for transportation. They were comprised of a droid head built into a hauling platform.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Transport droid
- **Class:** Transport droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** White, light red
- **Affiliation:** Pixelito Port Hauling
