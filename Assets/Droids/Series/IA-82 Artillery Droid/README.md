# IA-82 Artillery Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IA-82 Artillery Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IA-82_Artillery_Droid)

The IA-82 Artillery Droid was a model of six-legged battle droid available during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The IA-82 generated massive ion blasts from its forward cannon, leaving ion fields in the wake of the blast. The Twi'lek arms dealer Olok the Shadow sold IA-82 Artillery Droids in his auction on Darvannis.
