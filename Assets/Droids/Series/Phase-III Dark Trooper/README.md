# Phase-III Dark Trooper

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phase-III Dark Trooper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phase-III_Dark_Trooper)

The Phase-III Dark Trooper, also known as the third-generation design Dark Trooper, was a type of elite, experimental third-generation battle droid manufactured by the Imperial Department of Military Research and used by the Imperial remnant of Moff Gideon. The black and silver-plated droids were humanoid in shape and featured a pair of red photoreceptors.
