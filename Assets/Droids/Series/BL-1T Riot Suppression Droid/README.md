# BL-1T Riot Suppression Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BL-1T Riot Suppression Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BL-1T_Riot_Suppression_Droid)

The BL-1T Riot Suppression Droid was a type of riot security droid used as security forces by the Selkath species on the planet Manaan during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. BL-1T Droids resembled other Selkath humanoid models, with white plating and a blue sensor, and they used a blaster pistol in combat along with a powerful riot shield—which could be transferred to others—and a riot grappling cable. Sairisi, the security chief at Genetics Laboratory G-1, used two BL-1Ts, and they were destroyed when Sairisi was killed during a covert raid on the facility.
