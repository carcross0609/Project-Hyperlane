# Plasma battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Plasma battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plasma_battle_droid)

The plasma battle droid was a gargantuan, 12-foot version of the standard B1-series battle droid used by the Trade Federation. They fired lightning-like plasma blasts that were powerful enough to overwhelm even a Jedi lightsaber. They were designed to disrupt enemy infrastructure, and as a result were poorly-defended.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Plasma battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 12 feet
- **Plating:** Tan with green markings
- **Armament:** Plasma
- **Affiliation:** Trade Federation
