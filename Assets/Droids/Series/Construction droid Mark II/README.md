# Construction droid Mark II

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Construction droid Mark II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Construction_droid_Mark_II)

The construction droid Mark II was a droid model used c. 3,951 BBY. It was a huge biped, stronger than the construction droid Mark I or construction droid foreman, but far less dexterous. It was used for heavy lifting that the smaller droids couldn't accomplish. A typical construction team consisted of one foreman, one Mark II, and multiple Mark I droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Construction droid Mark II
- **Class:** Class five droid
