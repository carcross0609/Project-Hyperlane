# Miner droid (Eos)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Miner droid (Eos)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miner_droid_%28Eos%29)

Miner droids were automatons used by the Trade Federation on the Outer Rim volcano planet Eos. The droids mined Eos for the droid factory there. The droids were oval-shaped and followed set routes. In 32 BBY, Vana Sage encountered the droids after escaping a Trade Federation contract infringement.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Miner droid
- **Class:** Miner droid
- **Affiliation:** Trade Federation
