# Spider-droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spider-droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spider-droid)

Spider-droids, also known as disc droids, or disk droids, were a model of droid used by the Scourge as a vector to corrupt other droids, assimilating them as part of the Scourge.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spider-droid
- **Sensor:** Pink
- **Affiliation:** Scourge (As vessels)
