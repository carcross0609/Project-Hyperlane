# Armor Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Armor Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armor_Droid)

Armor Droids were small flying droids found in the Ugnaught sublevels of Cloud City. They had nigh-impregnable outer armor. However, said armor was expanded outward when the droids are about to fire their pulse cannons, making them vulnerable temporarily as a result.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Armor Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Affiliation:** Ugnaughts
