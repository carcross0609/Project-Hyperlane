# OT-12 Pacification Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-12 Pacification Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-12_Pacification_Droid)

The OT-12 Pacification Droid was a large, multi-legged battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War. A number of OT-12 Pacification Droids were among the Imperial forces stationed on the Gorinth Plateau.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-12 Pacification Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Blaster cannon
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
