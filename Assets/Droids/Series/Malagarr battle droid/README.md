# Malagarr battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malagarr battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malagarr_battle_droid)

Malagarr battle droids were used as security droids on Yavin 4 to protect Sith treasure in the numerous Massassi temples. They had three arms, with each wielding a vibro-ax. In 0 ABY a Rebel soldier chanced upon several of them, and later, Chewbacca destroyed one.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Malagarr battle droid
- **Homeworld:** Yavin 4
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** 3 Vibro-axs
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
