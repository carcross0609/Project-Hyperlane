# GP-2 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GP-2 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GP-2_medical_droid)

The GP-2 medical droid was a class of medical droid that was used by the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances. At least one such droid was stationed aboard the Alliance Star Destroyer Anakin Solo in 41 ABY.
