# Imperial loader droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial loader droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_loader_droid)

Loader droids were a type of labor droids designed to load and handle cargo being transported on Imperial ships, such as the freighter Suprosa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial loader droid
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Sensor:** Red, with smaller supplementary blue and green sensors
- **Armament:** Telescoping arms (Not technically armed)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Imperial Remnant
  - Lumini Pirates
