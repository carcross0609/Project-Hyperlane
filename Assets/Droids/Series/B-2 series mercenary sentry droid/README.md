# B-2 series mercenary sentry droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B-2 series mercenary sentry droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-2_series_mercenary_sentry_droid)

B-2 series mercenary sentry droids were a variant of B2-series super battle droid that existed in the galaxy by the early New Republic Era. Several of these droids were members of the Droid Gotra who served the Hutt brother and sister duo known as the Twins on Nal Hutta, with one such mercenary droid standing sentry behind the Hutt brother and sister duo known as the Twins when the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin entered their throne room with his young apprentice Din Grogu.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B-2 series mercenary sentry droid
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Plating:** Dull and rusted silver
- **Armament:** Dual wrist blaster
- **Equipment:**
  - Bandolier
  - Cape
  - Machete
- **Affiliation:**
  - Droid Gotra
  - The Twins (As mercenaries)
