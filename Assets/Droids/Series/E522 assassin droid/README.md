# E522 assassin droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E522 assassin droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E522_assassin_droid)

The E522 assassin droid was an assassin droid model originally designed during the Separatist Crisis by Baktoid Combat Automata, at the time under the Techno Union. By the time of the Galactic Civil War, they were one of the most feared droids operating in the galaxy.
