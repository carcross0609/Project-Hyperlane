# Intruder (assassin droid)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intruder (assassin droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intruder_%28assassin_droid%29)

The Intruder was a simple model of assassin droid. A mobile weapons platform, the Intruder was not truly a droid as it was not intelligent or capable of learning and complex processing. Intruders were often featured in holomovies.

- **Name:** Intruder
