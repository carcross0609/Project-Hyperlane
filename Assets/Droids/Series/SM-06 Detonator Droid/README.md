# SM-06 Detonator Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SM-06 Detonator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SM-06_Detonator_Droid)

The SM-06 Detonator Droid was a model of small battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. Designed to approach a target and explode, SM-06 Detonator Droids were among the forces stationed at the ruptured vault under the command of the Shroud's lieutenant Peej.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SM-06 Detonator Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
