# ANT-621 droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ANT-621 droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ANT-621_droid)

ANT-621 droids, also known as ant droids, were six-legged droids with an appendage emerging from their head which they used to carry objects. A large number of ant droids assisted in the construction of the Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker in 9 ABY. The droids carried stones from the surrounding environment and placed them to form a dome-shaped structure as part of the temple.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ANT-621 droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Class:** Construction droid
- **Plating:** White and blue
- **Equipment:** Opposable appendage
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
