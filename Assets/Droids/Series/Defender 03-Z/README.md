# Defender 03-Z

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Defender 03-Z](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defender_03-Z)

The Defender 03-Z was a model of battle droid that saw use during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. Similar to the Defender 01-X but with the addition of a personal shield, the Defender 03-Z carried a blaster rifle, and a number of them were deployed to protect the satellite control tower on the Markaran Plains of the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Defender 03-Z
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Dull green-brown
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
- **Equipment:** Personal shield
