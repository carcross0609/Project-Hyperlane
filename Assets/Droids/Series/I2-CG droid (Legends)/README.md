# I2-CG droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I2-CG droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I2-CG_droid/Legends)

The I2-CG droid was an Imperial storage/transceiving droid carried aboard Tech 4 PDV. They were based on the ED4 droid model from Cybot Galactica. When intercepted, Plexus Droid Vessels could jettison their I2-CG droids before self-destructing, covering the droid's escape.
