# Unidentified large-sensored astromech series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified large-sensored astromech series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_large-sensored_astromech_series)

This astromech series was a model of astromech droid. One of these droids, KE4-N4, was assigned to New Republic Lieutenant Callahan in her E-wing starfighter on the planet Lothal, while another was present at Adelphi Base on the planet Adelphi.
