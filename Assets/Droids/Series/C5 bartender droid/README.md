# C5 bartender droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C5 bartender droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C5_bartender_droid)

The C5 bartender droid was a model of bartender droid manufactured by LeisureMech Enterprises, being one of the company's only notable products alongside the BD-3000 luxury droid and RH-series CardShark droid. C5s possessed two limbs and two white photoreceptors. The droids' plating was white.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C5 bartender droid
- **Manufacturer:** LeisureMech Enterprises
- **Class:** Bartender droid
- **Plating:** White
- **Affiliation:** Alisandre Hotel
