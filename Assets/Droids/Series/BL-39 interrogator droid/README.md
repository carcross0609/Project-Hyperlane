# BL-39 interrogator droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BL-39 interrogator droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BL-39_interrogator_droid)

The BL-39 interrogator droid was a Interrogation droid manufactured by Aratech, that was mainly used by the Galactic Empire. It was a direct successor of the IT-O Interrogator, which schematics were bought by Aratech after the Galactic Civil War. It could easily be converted into a medical assistant droid by making use of the droid's existing medical knowledge.

- **Name:** BL-39 interrogator droid

- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
- **Equipment:**

- **Affiliation:** Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire
