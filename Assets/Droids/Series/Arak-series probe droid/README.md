# Arak-series probe droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arak-series probe droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arak-series_probe_droid)

The Arak-series probe droid was a repulsorlift probe droid used by the Trade Federation shortly before the Battle of Hypori. The miners of Banvhar Station used three of them in a failed assassination attempt on General Grievous.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Arak-series probe droid
- **Line:** Arak-series
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
- **Equipment:**
  - Holocamera
  - Comlink
