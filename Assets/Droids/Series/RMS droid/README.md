# RMS droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RMS droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RMS_droid)

RMS droids were a type of droid that had cylindrical bodies with two legs at the bottom and a domed top. A tube ran from the top of the droid down one side of its body, and an antenna protruded from the top of the droid on the same side as the tube. RMS droids could have gray plating with a single band of red near the top of the droids' body.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RMS droid
- **Plating:** Gray and red
