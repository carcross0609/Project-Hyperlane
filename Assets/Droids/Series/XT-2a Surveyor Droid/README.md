# XT-2a Surveyor Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XT-2a Surveyor Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XT-2a_Surveyor_Droid)

XT-2a Surveyor Droid was a droid model in use during the Galactic War. White Maw pirate captain Ayor-v9 used several XT-2a Surveyor Droids for protection of his base in the tunnels of Hoth and considered them to be "his family".

## Specifications

- **Name:** XT-2a Surveyor Droid
- **Class:** Surveyor Droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Orange
- **Affiliation:** Ayor-v9
