# Compactor droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Compactor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Compactor_droid)

Compactor droid was a type of droid designed by the unidentified Iokath species to tend to gardens and parks on their artificial world of Iokath. The species went extinct following the Iokath Civil War and Compactor droids were repurposed by ARIES into the combat units, aggressive to any intruders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Compactor droid
- **Creator:** Unidentified Iokath species
- **Homeworld:** Iokath
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Yellow
- **Affiliation:**
  - Unidentified Iokath species
  - ARIES
