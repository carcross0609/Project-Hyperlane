# FX-8 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FX-8 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-8_medical_droid)

The FX-8 medical droid was a medical droid manufactured by Medtech Industries as part of the FX-series of medical assistant droids. Produced during the last decades of the Galactic Republic, it was ultimately succeeded by the FX-9 model.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FX-8 medical droid
- **Manufacturer:** Medtech Industries
- **Line:** FX-series medical assistant droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
