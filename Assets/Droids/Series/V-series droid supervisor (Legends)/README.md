# V-series droid supervisor

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V-series droid supervisor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-series_droid_supervisor/Legends)

The V-series droid supervisor was a model of supervisor droid manufactured by MerenData. A relaunch of the EV-series supervisor droid marketed towards the Imperial Military, its performance was unimpressive and the model was a financial failure.

## Specifications

- **Name:** V-series droid supervisor
- **Manufacturer:** MerenData
- **Class:** Supervisor droid
- **Degree:** Class three droid
- **Plating:** Black, gray, white
- **Equipment:**
  - Augmented monitor system
  - Logic processor
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
