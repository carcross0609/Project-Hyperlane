# Scout transmitter

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scout transmitter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scout_transmitter)

Scout transmitters were a type of minuscule spy droid that was in use during the Galactic Civil War. They were very small, able to fit into the palm of an analysis droid's hand. The devices were circular in shape with green plating, and could be attached to walls to silently observe, though they were capable of producing a type of audible speech to communicate with other droids.
