# TX7 Long-Range Probe

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TX7 Long-Range Probe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TX7_Long-Range_Probe)

The TX7 Long-Range Probe was a model of probe droid that was employed by the forces of House Thul during the Alderaan Civil War. A number of TX7 Long-Range Probes were deployed to the Alsakan Lowlands, a region of the planet Alderaan near the estate of Thul's rival House Organa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TX7 Long-Range Probe
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Plating:** Dark
