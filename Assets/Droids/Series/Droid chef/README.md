# Droid chef

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Droid chef](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_chef)

Droid chefs was a type of cooking droid found in the galaxy. During 3 ABY, droid chef operated street food stalls on the planets Akiva, Kijimi, and Tatooine, as well as the moon Toshara; serving Kurado fruit platter, Duradan Soup, Sketto Chuga and Che Mosskà respectively.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Droid chef
- **Class:** Cooking droid
- **Equipment:** Four arms
