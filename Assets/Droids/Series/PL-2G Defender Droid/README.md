# PL-2G Defender Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PL-2G Defender Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PL-2G_Defender_Droid)

The PL-2G Defender Droid was a type of small battle droid available during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** PL-2G Defender Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
