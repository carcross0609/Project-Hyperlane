# Flying attack droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Flying attack droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_attack_droid)

Flying attack droids were a type of floating, spherical droid that the merchant Tuut Orial sold from a stall on the asteroid Port Borgo around 9 ABY. During a demonstration of the droids, one poked an Aqualish observer in the eye and another chased the child KB. The droid SM-33 later punched a flying attack droid out of the air while defending KB and her friends.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flying attack droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** White and gray
- **Armament:**
  - Circular saw
  - Three blades
