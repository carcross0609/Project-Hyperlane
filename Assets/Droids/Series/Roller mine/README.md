# Roller mine

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Roller mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Roller_mine)

The roller mine was a droid model created on the planet Koboh. When the droid spotted a person or creature, a beam of light would appear and lock on to the target. When close enough, the beam would turn red, indicating the person or creature is in imminent danger of being blown up.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Roller mine
- **Homeworld:** Koboh
