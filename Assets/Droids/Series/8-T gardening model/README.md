# 8-T gardening model

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [8-T gardening model](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/8-T_gardening_model)

The 8-T gardening model, known as 8-T droids or Aytees, were a model of droid utilized for gardening. The droids had a similar appearance to that of an astromech droid dome, with magnetic treads and pincer claws that the droids used for pruning plants. The droids were programmed to attack any being that posed a threat to their garden. A number of 8-T droids were left aboard the Amaxine space station long after it was abandoned with several Drengir imprisoned within.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 8-T gardening model
- **Equipment:**

  - Pruning claws
