# Grenade droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grenade droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grenade_droid/Legends)

Grenade droids, also known as grenadier droids or B1 grenadier troopers, were a grenadier variant of the B1-series battle droid used by the Trade Federation and Confederacy of Independent Systems. Variants included the thermal detonator battle droid and B1 Grenadier Assault.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grenade droid
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:**
  - Grenades
  - Mortar
  -   Thermal detonators
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
