# AR-2B Utility Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AR-2B Utility Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AR-2B_Utility_Droid)

AR-2B Utility Droids, were fifth-degree menial labor droid manufactured by Karflo Corporation and were used in great number in most of the corporations on Goroth Prime during the rebellion era.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AR-2B Utility Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Karflo Corporation
- **Degree:** Class five droid
- **Cost:** 2,250 credits
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Equipment:** C3-DD NeuroNet brain
