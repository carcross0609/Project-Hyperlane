# IT-series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IT-series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IT-series)

The IT-series was model of small, spherical, floating droid that was developed on the droid-inhabited moon Uffel during the waning years of the Galactic Republic. In about 31 BBY, the droids of Uffel's Research and Development facility tasked various IT-series droids, including IT-R9, with following groups of tourists that were visiting the moon, to allow the droids to study the behavior of organic beings.

## Specifications

- **Manufacturer:** Uffel droid community
- **Homeworld:** Uffel
