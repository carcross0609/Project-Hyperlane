# P0 adminmech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [P0 adminmech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P0_adminmech_droid)

The P0 adminmech droid was a model of adminmech droid. During the Imperial Era, the P0 adminmech P0-03 was enslaved by the Pyke Syndicate on Kessel, working in their spice mines. P0-03's restraining bolt was removed during a droid revolt that was started by L3-37.

## Specifications

- **Name:** P0 adminmech droid
- **Class:** Adminmech droid
- **Plating:** White, red and blue
