# ACC-7 assassin droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ACC-7 assassin droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ACC-7_assassin_droid)

ACC-7 was a model of assassin droid produced by Arakyd Industries during the New Order. Their spherical body contained a standard repulsorlift engine for propulsion, and advanced vibroblades were attached to each of its four retractable arms for use in combat. The ACC-7 saw widespread usage among Imperial spies during the Galactic Civil War. The use of ACC-7's was later outlawed, however, by the New Republic. Many were brought back into commission for use against the Yuuzhan Vong.
