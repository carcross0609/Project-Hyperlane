# RH-series research droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RH-series research droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RH-series_research_droid)

The RH-series research droid was a model of science droid. Manufactured by Cybot Galactica, the droid was designed as a research assistant and mobile database. The RH-series proved to be unpopular, thus resulting in only a few thousand such droids being produced. The Alliance R&D division of the Rebel Alliance used many RH-series research droids during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RH-series research droid
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
- **Class:** Science droid
- **Cost:** 11,000 credits
- **Height:** Under 2 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Built-in science database
  - Scientific tools
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Alliance Military Research and Development
