# IG-1

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IG-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG-1)

IG-1 was the retroactive code name given to the first assassin droid design developed by Phlut Design Systems as part of Project Phlutdroid after the relative success of its IG lancer droid. However, the droid was ultimately left unproduced by PDS when the company's assets were seized by the InterGalactic Banking Clan shortly before the outbreak of the Clone Wars in the year 22 BBY. The assassin droid's unpublished blueprints, along with those of the rest of the IG-series, were later acquired by Holowan Mechanicals and relabeled IGs 1 through 99.
