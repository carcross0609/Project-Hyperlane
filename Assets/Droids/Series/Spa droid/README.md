# Spa droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spa droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spa_droid)

The spa droid, also known as the journalist droid, was a model of droid. They could be found within the Underworld of Coruscant, manning gates at locations like Terminal 24, as well as within recreations of the underworld in places like the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara. Additionally, instances of this model like SN-D1 and BO-N1 were employed at the Droid Spa on Coruscant.

## Specifications

- **Sensor:** Blue (Incandescent)
- **Affiliation:**
