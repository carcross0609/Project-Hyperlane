# Heavy Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heavy Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Battle_Droid)

The Heavy Battle Droid was a modified version of the B1-series battle droid that was equipped with firing programs to help in combat. They were used as heavy troopers by the Confederacy of Independent Systems military during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy Battle Droid
- **Line:** B1-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Gray and bone-white
- **Armament:** E-5C
- **Equipment:** Built-in shield emitter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
