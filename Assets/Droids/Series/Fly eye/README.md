# Fly eye

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fly eye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fly_eye)

The fly eye was a surveillance droid. It was invented by the Loronar Corporation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fly eye
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Corporation
- **Class:** Surveillance droid
- **Cost:** 1400 credits
- **Length:** 6 centimeters (diameter)
- **Height:** 3 centimeters
- **Equipment:**
  - Miniature repulsorlift engine
  - Holographic recorder
  - 4 Clawed gripper legs
  - Tightbeam antenna
  - Heat dispersal vents
