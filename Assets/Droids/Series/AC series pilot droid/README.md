# AC series pilot droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AC series pilot droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AC_series_pilot_droid)

AC Series pilot droids were the precursor to the RX-Series pilot droid that worked for the Star Tours company. An AC Series pilot droid piloted the StarSpeeder 1000 sometime between 19 BBY and 0 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AC series pilot droid
- **Manufacturer:** Star Tours
- **Class:** Pilot droid
- **Height:** 1.2827 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Silver, with blue and orange Star Tours markings
