# Salespeak 7 droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Salespeak 7 droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salespeak_7_droid)

The Salespeak 7 droid was a class of translator droid that became available around the time of the publication of Ebenn Q3 Baobab's Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide. The droids made use of new vocabulator technology to instantaneously translate the Wookiee language Shyrriwook.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Salespeak 7 droid
- **Class:** Translator droid
