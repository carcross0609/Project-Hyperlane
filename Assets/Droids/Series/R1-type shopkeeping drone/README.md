# R1-type shopkeeping drone

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R1-type shopkeeping drone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R1-type_shopkeeping_drone)

R1-type shopkeeping drones were R1-series astromech droids modified by Jawas, a species native to the planet Tatooine, to assist with shopkeeping. Created from an older model of astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton, they featured black plating, a red photoreceptor, and a grasping arm. Among the improvised equipment that could be found in his shop in Tatooine's port city of Mos Espa, the Toydarian junk dealer Watto owned an R1-type shopkeeping drone that handled most of the routine business operations of his parts dealership. The droid's dome was marked with a handprint, and it was pictured in a HoloNews advertisement for Watto's shop as an example of the droids available at the dealership.
