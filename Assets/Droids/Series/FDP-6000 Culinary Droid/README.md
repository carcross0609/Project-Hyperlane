# FDP-6000 Culinary Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FDP-6000 Culinary Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FDP-6000_Culinary_Droid)

FDP-6000 Culinary Droids were multi-limbed cooking droids equipped with a variety of kitchen utensils and enough culinary knowledge to be considered a four-star chef. An FDP-6000 was in the service of Nebula Consumables on Sprawl 41 during the Galactic Civil War. It contracted the Mynock virus and attempted to make meals out of Humans.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FDP-6000 Culinary Droid
- **Line:** FDB-6000
- **Class:** Cooking droid
- **Equipment:**
  - Tractor tread mobile unit
  - Cooking utensils
