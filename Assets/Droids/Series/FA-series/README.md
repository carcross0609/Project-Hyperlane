# FA-series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FA-series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FA-series)

The FA-series was a line of service droids manufactured by the SoroSuub Corporation, that included the FA-4 pilot droid and its successor, the FA-5 valet droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FA-series
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:**
  - FA-4 pilot droid
  - FA-5 valet droid
- **Class:** Service droid
