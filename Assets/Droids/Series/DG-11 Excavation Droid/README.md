# DG-11 Excavation Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DG-11 Excavation Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DG-11_Excavation_Droid)

DG-11 Excavation Droid was a droid model in use during the Galactic War. White Maw pirate captain Ayor-v9 used several DG-11 Excavation Droids for protection of his base in the tunnels of Hoth and considered them to be "his family".

- **Name:** DG-11 Excavation Droid
