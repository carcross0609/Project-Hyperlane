# CLL-M2 binary loadlifter

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CLL-M2 binary loadlifter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CLL-M2_binary_loadlifter)

The CLL-M2 binary loadlifter, also known as simply as the CLL-M2, ordnance lifter droid, or loader droid, was a model of heavy labor droid manufactured by Cybot Galactica as part of the CLL-series. Some of them were repurposed to assist Doctor Sionver Boll in studying the Zillo Beast.
