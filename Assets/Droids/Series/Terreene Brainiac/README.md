# Terreene Brainiac

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Terreene Brainiac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terreene_Brainiac)

The Terreene Brainiac was a model of control droid. The droid brain was rather naive when it came to organic interaction and could be conned, but it learned quickly that they were being deceived. Control, a control droid charged with running the Galactic Empire's mobile mining complex on Alabash was a Terreene Brainiac.
