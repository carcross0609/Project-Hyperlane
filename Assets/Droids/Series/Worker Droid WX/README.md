# Worker Droid WX

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Worker Droid WX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Worker_Droid_WX)

The Worker Droid WX was a battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on Balmorra. A number of those droids were used to defend the factory during the Battle of Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Worker Droid WX
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
