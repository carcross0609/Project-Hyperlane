# Hubdroid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hubdroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hubdroid)

Hubdroids were droid models used by the Galactic Empire. The droids were employed in Accresker Jail to ensure loyalty among the prisoners held there. If a convict strayed too far from or tried to destroy their designated hubdroid, bombs implanted in their uniforms would detonate.
