# Loader Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Loader Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loader_Droid)

Loader Droids were used in Trade Federation ships. During the Invasion of Naboo in 32 BBY, the two Jedi Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi fought a Loader Droid while trying to escape the Saak'ak.

- **Class:** Loader droid
