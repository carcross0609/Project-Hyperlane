# Imperial construction droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial construction droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_construction_droid)

A type of spider-like construction droid was utilized by the Galactic Empire for the assembly of large-scale construction projects. These construction droids were notably used for the assembly of the first Death Star's superlaser above Scarif in 5 BBY. They were also carried on board Imperial-class Star Destroyers, where they were used to repair damage to the ships' hull and perform modifications and upgrades.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial construction droid
- **Class:** Construction droid
- **Length:** About 1.78 meters
- **Height:** About 1.78 meters
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Black and gray
- **Equipment:** Opposable welding torches, back appendage
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Scourge (As a vessel)
