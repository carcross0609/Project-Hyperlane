# Imperial C-series war droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Imperial C-series war droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_C-series_war_droid)

The Imperial C-series war droid was a battle droid model that could be seen throughout Sith planets during the Cold War. Their main function was to maintain security in occupied territories, as it served as a portable turret.
