# CZ-series secretary/comm droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CZ-series secretary/comm droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CZ-series_secretary/comm_droid)

The CZ-series secretary/comm droid was a droid series produced by Serv-O-Droid, Inc. The CZ-series was released as Serv-O-Droid began to wane in its position as a dominant droid manufacturer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CZ-series secretary/comm droid
- **Manufacturer:** Serv-O-Droid, Inc.
- **Class:** 3rd-degree communications/business droid
- **Cost:** 8,280 or 9,000 credits
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Equipment:** Built-in comlink
