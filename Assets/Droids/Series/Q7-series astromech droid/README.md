# Q7-series astromech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Q7-series astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q7-series_astromech_droid)

The Q7-series astromech droid was a model of astromech droid that featured a spherical dome resembling that of R2 and R4-P astromechs. It was used to co-pilot the Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter and assisted with in-flight maintenance and navigation, giving pilots a larger margin of error when flying close to obstacles. Q7s were carried aboard V-wings flown by the Galactic Republic Navy during the Clone Wars.
