# Jedi training droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jedi training droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_training_droid/Legends)

The Jedi training droids were a model of droid used for various purposes in the Jedi Temple on Coruscant including verbally teaching a Padawan about Force-enhanced abilities and lightsaber practice. While a range of different forms and models existed, the droids were primarily skeletal bipeds, based on a humanoid frame.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jedi training droid
- **Manufacturer:** Upgrades and different models made by Kazdan Paratus
- **Homeworld:** Coruscant
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:**
  - Lightsabers
  - Blasters
  - Electrical pulses
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - New Jedi Order
  - Galactic Republic
