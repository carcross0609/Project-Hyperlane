# BAF-300 Heavy Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-300 Heavy Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-300_Heavy_Battle_Droid)

The BAF-300 Heavy Battle Droid was a bipedal battle droid produced by Balmorran Arms on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. BAF-300 Heavy Battle were armed with blaster cannons, and were among the forces used by the Galactic Republic to defend the Balmorran Arms Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-300 Heavy Battle Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Portable blaster cannon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Balmorran resistance
