# All-Terrain Exploration Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [All-Terrain Exploration Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/All-Terrain_Exploration_Droid)

The All-Terrain Exploration Droid (AT-ED) was a model of exploration droid developed in collaboration between Cybot Galactica and Kuat Drive Yards, designed to assist in the exploration and surveying of newly discovered planets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** All-Terrain Exploration Droid
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Cybot Galactica
  - Kuat Drive Yards
- **Line:** AT-series
- **Class:** Explorer droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Cost:** 8,900 credits
- **Height:** 2.5 meters
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Armament:** Forward-mounted blaster
- **Equipment:** Fabritech "Surveyor" mapping-and-surveying suite
