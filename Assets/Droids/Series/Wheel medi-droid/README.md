# Wheel medi-droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wheel medi-droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_medi-droid)

The medi-droids used on The Wheel had base platforms similar to those of Treadwell droids. Short, boxy, nonhumanoid models, their abilities included spraying narco-mist to dull pain and encourage healing. One such droid treated Han Solo while he fought in The Big Game.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wheel medi-droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Affiliation:** The Wheel
