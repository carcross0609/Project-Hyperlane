# FD4 fighting droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FD4 fighting droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FD4_fighting_droid)

The FD4 fighting droid was a model of gladiator droid with a brown cylindrical body topped by a disc shaped head with a large black sensor. A modified FD4 fighting droid was made to fight in the droid arena of the Lodge on the planet Vandor. In 10 BBY, it was set to fight against the FD3-MN fighting droid FD3-MN, but the match was interrupted by the droid L3-37 who tried to convince the FD4 not to fight while the arena manager Ralakili shouted at her.
