# Executive Droid EX-7

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Executive Droid EX-7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Executive_Droid_EX-7)

The Executive Droid EX-7 was a model of supervisor droid produced by the Okara Droid Company. A number of EX-7 droids worked in one section of the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra.
