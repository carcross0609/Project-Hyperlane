# Internal systems probe droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Internal systems probe droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Internal_systems_probe_droid)

The internal systems probe droid was a model of probe droid designed by Arakyd Industries. Measuring no more than seven centimeters tall, they floated throughout the air ducts of the Galactic Empire's first Death Star battle station, defending against intruders and reporting any unusual findings.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Internal systems probe droid
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Height:** 7 centimeters
- **Armament:** Defensive ports
- **Equipment:** Repulsorlift
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
