# KL-2B Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KL-2B Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KL-2B_Battle_Droid)

The KL-2B Battle Droid was a model of three-legged battle droid used by the Hutt Cartel on the moon Nar Shaddaa during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. A number of KL-2B units were among the dozens of droids that malfunctioned in the Network Security District and went on a rampage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KL-2B Battle Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Brown
- **Armament:** Arm- and chin-mounted blaster cannons
- **Affiliation:** Hutt Cartel
