# AS-M12-series droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AS-M12-series droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AS-M12-series_droid)

The AS-M12-series droid was a model of bespoke and familiar messenger droid used by Imperial Intelligence services of the Galactic Empire such as the Imperial Security Bureau. Because they used the AS-M12-series and MSE series messenger droids, the Imperial intelligence services rarely used Cybot Galactica's popular M4-series Messenger Droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AS-M12-series droid
- **Class:** Messenger droid
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Intelligence
