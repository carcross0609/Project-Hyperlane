# X2H-8 Overseer Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X2H-8 Overseer Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X2H-8_Overseer_Droid)

The X2H-8 Overseer Droid was a model of supervisor droid that saw use during the Cold War. A number of X2H-8 Overseer Droids oversaw the work of S7-H7 Slicing Droids, BB-22 Salvage Droids, and JT-65 Salvage Droids in the Barasin Refuse Yards on the planet Balmorra.
