# Commando droid captain

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Commando droid captain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commando_droid_captain)

Commando droid captains were BX-series droid commandos at the rank of captain tasked with commanding their fellow BX-series droids. Other commando droids with high ranks also appeared the same. The standard design of these droid captains possessed white head and chest markings. In practice, however, not all commando droid officers donned additional markings. Commando droid captains sometimes received edited programming in order to improve their leadership and improvisation.
