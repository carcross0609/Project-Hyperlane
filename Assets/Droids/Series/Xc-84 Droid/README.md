# Xc-84 Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xc-84 Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xc-84_Droid)

The Xc-84 Droid was a battle droid developed by the time of the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The bipedal droids armed with blaster cannons were used by the White Maw pirates on Hoth to guard the Tromper Crags Geothermal Plant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Xc-84 Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Grey
- **Armament:** Blaster cannon
- **Affiliation:** White Maw
