# Hound-W2 SPD

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hound-W2 SPD](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hound-W2_SPD)

The Hound-W2 SPD was a security droid from the Scanning Patrol Detail series developed by Industrial Automaton. They were rectangular shaped and about half the height of an astromech droid.
