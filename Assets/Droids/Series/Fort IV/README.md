# Fort IV

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Fort IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_IV)

The Fort IV (Japanese: フォートⅣ) was a wall-mounted autoturret used by the Galactic Empire to protect the corridors and walkways in their first Death Star. They were capable of detecting and blasting intruders.
