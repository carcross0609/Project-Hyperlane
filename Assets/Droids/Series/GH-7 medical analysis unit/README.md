# GH-7 medical analysis unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GH-7 medical analysis unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GH-7_medical_analysis_unit)

The GH-7 medical analysis unit was a model of medical droid in operation during the final years of the Galactic Republic. It was manufactured by the Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals Company in competition with Geentech's 2-1B, and designed by a group of Columi.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GH-7 medical analysis unit
- **Manufacturer:** Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals Company
- **Class:** Class 1 Medical droid
- **Height:** 0.7 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Armament:** None
- **Equipment:** Holoprojector, equipment tray
- **Affiliation:**

  - Polis Massa
