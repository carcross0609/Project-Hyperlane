# NON unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [NON unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NON_unit)

NON units, also known as NONs, were a model of protocol droids used by senators and other officials of the Galactic Republic. Padmé Amidala was assigned one NON unit, NON-3, which was reprogrammed to attempt to lead her into a death trap, but was saved by Bail Organa. Other senators assigned NON units included Rush Clovis and Organa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** NON unit
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Affiliation:**
