# Tagge prototype battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tagge prototype battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tagge_prototype_battle_droid)

The Tagge prototype battle droid, or prototype Tagge battle droid, was a prototype battle droid model designed by Lady Domina Tagge of the Tagge Corporation. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra was sent by Tagge to investigate a series of droid malfunctions and encountered the battle droids Tagge had designed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tagge prototype battle droid
- **Designer:** Domina Tagge
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:**
  - Red
  - Purple (Under Scourge infection)
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Armament:** Blaster cannon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Tagge Corporation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems (Intended)
  -   Separatist Droid Army (Intended)
  - Scourge (As a vessel)
