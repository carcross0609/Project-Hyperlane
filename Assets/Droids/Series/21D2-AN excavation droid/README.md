# 21D2-AN excavation droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [21D2-AN excavation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/21D2-AN_excavation_droid)

The 21D2-AN excavation droid, also referred to as the 21D2-AN excavator unit, was a boxy model of excavation droid. During the Clone Wars, Count Dooku of the Confederacy of Independent Systems requisitioned two of the droids for a mission to the Expansion Region planet Diso. The count used the two excavator units to dig up the Temple of Diso, which was buried beneath hundreds of tons of salt under a salt flat. The work was completed in a matter of hours, but the temple heavily damaged the two 21D2-AN excavation droids when it reacted to its exposure, as the structure was vergence in the Dark side of the Force.
