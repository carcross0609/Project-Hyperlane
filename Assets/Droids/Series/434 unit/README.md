# 434 unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [434 unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/434_unit)

The 434 unit was a type of kitchen droid. The Artiodac chef Strono Tuggs encountered an old 434 unit that knew a recipe for Cavaellin Spice Creams—a type of cookie from the Cavaell system—that Tuggs considered spectacular. In 34 ABY, Tuggs included the recipe as well as a mention of the droid in his first cookbook, The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook, claiming the recipe was to die for.
