# 87-RM Scout Collector

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [87-RM Scout Collector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/87-RM_Scout_Collector)

87-RM Scout Collectors were droids deployed by an unknown extinct population hundreds of years before the Galactic Civil War to Orellon II. Their base of operation was a fully automated starship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 87-RM Scout Collector
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Serv-O-Droid, Inc.
  - Later SoroSuub Corporation
- **Cost:** 4350 credits
- **Height:** 2.5 meters
