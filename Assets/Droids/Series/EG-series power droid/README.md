# EG-series power droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EG-series power droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EG-series_power_droid)

The EG-series power droid, also known as the EG power droid, was a model of power droid manufactured by Veril Line Systems. Like the very similar GNK-series power droid, it emitted low honking sounds, which earned it the nickname of "gonk droid."

## Specifications

- **Name:** EG-series power droid
- **Manufacturer:** Veril Line Systems
- **Model:** EG-series
- **Class:** Power droid
