# S-43 enforcer droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [S-43 enforcer droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-43_enforcer_droid)

The S-43 enforcer droid was a model of battle droid and enforcer droid developed by the Hutt Clan around the time of the Clone Wars. Based off of stolen battle droid plans from the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the S-43 was utilized by the Hutts in order to maintain their possessions as well as claim additional territory they were able to manage. During their service, they saw regular deployment from the Hutt Clan against underworld lowlifes and negligent debtors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S-43 enforcer droid
- **Class:**
  - Battle droid
  - Enforcer droid
- **Degree:** Class three droid
- **Sensor:** Upgraded processors
- **Armament:**
  - Blaster carbine
  - Integrated vibroblade
- **Affiliation:** Hutt Clan
