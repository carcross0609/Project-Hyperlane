# Defender 01-X

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Defender 01-X](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defender_01-X)

The Defender 01-X was a model of battle droid that saw use during the Cold War. Similar to the Defender 03-Z but lacking a shield, the Defender 01-X carried a blaster rifle, and a number of them were deployed to protect the satellite control tower on the Markaran Plains of the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Defender 01-X
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Dull green-brown
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
