# 44-CRB crab droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [44-CRB crab droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/44-CRB_crab_droid)

44-CRB crab droids were a model of droids utilized in the spice mines of Kessel. One participated in the uprising against the Pyke Syndicate during the raid by Tobias Beckett's crew.
