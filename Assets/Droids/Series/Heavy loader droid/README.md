# Heavy loader droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heavy loader droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_loader_droid)

The heavy loader droid, or the heavy loading droid, was a model of class five loader droid produced by JLD Mechanicals Consortium, and used for loading heavy objects, often working with starfighter ground crews. The Resistance droid B-U4D, known as "Buford," was a heavy loader droid. B-R72 was also a heavy loader droid that worked near the Droid Depot on the planet Batuu.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy loader droid
- **Manufacturer:** JLD Mechanicals Consortium
- **Class:** Loader droid
- **Degree:** Class five droid
- **Height:** 1.75 meters (5 ft, 8 in)
- **Sensor:** Red
