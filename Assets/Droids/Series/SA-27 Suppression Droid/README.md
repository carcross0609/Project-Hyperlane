# SA-27 Suppression Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SA-27 Suppression Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SA-27_Suppression_Droid)

The SA-27 Suppression Droid was a model of bipedal battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. SA-27 Suppression Droids were among the forces stationed at the ruptured vault under the command of the Shroud's lieutenant Peej.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SA-27 Suppression Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Green
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
