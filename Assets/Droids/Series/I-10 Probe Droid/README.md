# I-10 Probe Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I-10 Probe Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I-10_Probe_Droid)

The I-10 probe droid was a type of modified B1-series battle droid used during the Galactic Civil War. Droids of this kind was unarmed as it primarily filled a reconnaissance role. I-10 probe droids were used by the Rak'qua on Station Gamma above Ord Mantell following the Battle of Yavin. In 1 ABY, a number of spacers who explored Station Gamma fought and destroyed multiple I-10 probe droids.
