# FX-6 droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FX-6 droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-6_droid)

The FX-6 droid was a medical droid model that existed during the High Republic Era. The FX-6 had the ability to detect toxins and poisons, and was equipped with an alarm system. By 200 BBY, a modified unit was owned by President Jamie Brasson at his estate on Begamor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FX-6 droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Equipment:** Alarm system
