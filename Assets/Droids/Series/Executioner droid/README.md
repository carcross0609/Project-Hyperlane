# Executioner droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Executioner droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Executioner_droid)

The executioner droid was a very rare battle droid model. During the Clone Wars, there was only three of these deadly droids known to exist in the galaxy. One executioner droid, owned by the collector Kenna, faced Jedi Master Mace Windu aboard Kenna's starship when the Jedi went on a mission to an abandoned planetoid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Executioner droid
- **Armament:**
  - Front pincers (2)
  - Dual blaster cannons (2)
- **Equipment:**
  - Magnetically sealed armor
  - Force shields
- **Affiliation:** Kenna
