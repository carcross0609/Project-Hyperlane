# Bank guard droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bank guard droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bank_guard_droid)

Bank guard droids were a type of security droid employed by the Gran crime lord Nico Deemis at his bank tower, which was located in the banking district of Janix city on the planet Janix. The guards carried a blaster pistol, a similar model wielded by their Head Boss, Nico Deemis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bank guard droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Height:** Humanoid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Dark-gray
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
- **Affiliation:** Nico Deemis' syndicate
