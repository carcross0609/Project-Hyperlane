# Stealth droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stealth droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stealth_droid)

The stealth droid was a four-armed battle droid that was armed with a type of portable cloaking device. During the Clone Wars, General Grievous used a squad of stealth droids for an attack on Bandomeer. The stealth droid shared a similar design to a COO-series cook droid but had four arms instead of six.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stealth droid
- **Armament:**
  - Blasters
  - Buzz saws
  - Flamethrowers
- **Equipment:** Cloaking device
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
