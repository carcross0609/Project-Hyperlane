# Heavy Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy Battle Droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Battle_Droid/Legends)

The Heavy Battle Droid was a variant of the B1-series battle droid which was more heavily armored than the base model. They were armed with larger and more powerful blasters than the regular B1's E-5 blaster rifles, and wore larger "backpacks". It was used by the Trade Federation Droid Army during the time of the Invasion of Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy Battle Droid
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Model:** Heavy
- **Class:** B1-series battle droid
- **Armament:** Heavy Blaster Rifle
- **Affiliation:** Trade Federation Droid Army
