# Octoneedle

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Octoneedle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Octoneedle)

An Octoneedle (Japanese: オクトニードル) was a hovering droid used by the Galactic Empire to protect the corridors and walkways in their first Death Star. They appeared as spheres with eight needle-like protrusions which were at least partially retractable.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Octoneedle
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
