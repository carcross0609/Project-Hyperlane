# GLD-M General Labor Droid/Mining

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GLD-M General Labor Droid/Mining](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GLD-M_General_Labor_Droid/Mining)

The GLD-M was a general-purpose labor droid, specifically made for mining operation. Produced by Industrial Automaton, they were equipped with the RapidProgram module system, allowing them to be easily modified to provide several different services.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GLD-M General Labor Droid/Mining
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:**

  - Mining droid
- **Cost:** 4,000 credit
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Audio sensor
  - Infrared receptor
  - Photoreceptor
  - RapidProgram module system
  - Vocabulator
