# Terror Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Terror Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terror_Droid)

Terror Droids were small spider-like droids that were developed during the reign of the Galactic Empire under the direction of Darth Vader on the planet Kamino.
