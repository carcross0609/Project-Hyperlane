# Techno-service droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Techno-service droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Techno-service_droid)

Techno-service droids were diminutive droids designed for service. These small droids had feet that could combine into a wheel, making for fast movement and had rocket boosters built-in their legs. They were manufactured by Vertseth Automata on the planet Vertseth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Techno-service droid
- **Manufacturer:** Vertseth Automata
- **Homeworld:** Vertseth
- **Class:** Service droid
- **Degree:** Class three droid
- **Height:** 0.66 meters
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Armament:** Rocket boosters
