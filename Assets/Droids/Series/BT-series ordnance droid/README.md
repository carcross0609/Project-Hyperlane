# BT-series ordnance droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BT-series ordnance droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BT-series_ordnance_droid)

The BT-series ordnance droid, also known simply as BT droids, were a model of loader droid manufactured by Baktoid Fleet Ordnance. Designed to load and unload ordnance systems, the droids had extensive knowledge of the material they worked with. Although most BT-series droids had stiff and business-like personalities, some developed a more cavalier and daredevil attitude when dealing with explosives. During the Galactic Civil War, some BT-series droids served with the Alliance to Restore the Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BT-series ordnance droid
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Fleet Ordnance
- **Class:** Loader droid
- **Sensor:** Red and yellow
- **Plating:** Brown and silver
- **Armament:** None
- **Equipment:**

  - Built-in ordnance computer
