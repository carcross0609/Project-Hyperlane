# 2-1C medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [2-1C medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2-1C_medical_droid)

The 2-1C medical droid was a combat medic droid employed by the Galactic Empire. It was extremely similar in design to the FX-series medical assistant droid, but was designed for independent operation on the battlefield. It had a working vocabulator and a cruel, cynical attitude, a stark contrast to the eager, friendly IM-6 units used by the previous Grand Army of the Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 2-1C medical droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Height:** 1.7 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Equipment:** Vocabulator
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
