# SPD-R4 spider droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SPD-R4 spider droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SPD-R4_spider_droid)

The SPD-R4 spider droid was a model of service droid/maintenance droid commonly used in Imperial installations such as the Death Star I and Mount Tantiss; aboard automated ships such as the Another Chance; and by civilians.
