# Overseer droid (Death Star)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Overseer droid (Death Star)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Overseer_droid_%28Death_Star%29)

This model of overseer droid was an enhanced supervisor droid designed by the artificial intelligence team led by Doctor Gubacher. These units were intended to supervise the labor force working on the Death Star battle station for the Republic Special Weapons Group. The Iktotchi computer engineer Dagio Belcoze was a member of the team, and helped design these droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Overseer droid
- **Designer:** Dagio Belcoze
- **Class:** Supervisor droid
- **Retired:** Galactic Republic
