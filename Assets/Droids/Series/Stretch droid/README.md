# Stretch droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stretch droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stretch_droid)

Stretch droids were a type of droid with two brown legs, a black, ribbed cylindrical body and a conical head with a single black photoreceptor. In 32 BBY, a stretch droid was stood next to an RMS droid near the door of the Toydarian Watto's shop in the town of Mos Espa on the planet Tatooine when the Jedi Qui-Gon Jinn, Queen Padmé Amidala, the Gungan Jar Jar Binks and the astromech droid R2-D2 entered.

## Specifications

- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Brown and black
