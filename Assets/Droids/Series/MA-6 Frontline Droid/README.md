# MA-6 Frontline Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MA-6 Frontline Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MA-6_Frontline_Droid)

The MA-6 Frontline Droid was a type of battle droid used as security forces by the Selkath species on the planet Manaan during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Virtually identical to the MA-9 Frontline Droid, the MA-6 carried a blaster rifle in combat, and a number of them were part of the security forces of Genetics Laboratory G-1.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MA-6 Frontline Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Black, blue, and silver
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
