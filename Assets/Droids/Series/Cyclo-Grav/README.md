# Cyclo-Grav

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Cyclo-Grav](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyclo-Grav)

The Cyclo-Grav was a model of battle droid with gray plating and a black photoreceptor. It consisted of the head of a Serv-O-Droid, Inc. DUM-series pit droid with the four limbs and repulsorlift of an ID10 seeker droid, making it a monster droid. A group of Jawas sold blueprints for Cyclo-Gravs out of a Sandcrawler Shop that was located near the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine. The default Cyclo-Grav design cost 30,000,000 credits, a golden design cost 120,000,000 credits, a diamond-colored version cost 240,000,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design cost 480,000,000 credits.
