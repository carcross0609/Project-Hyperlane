# Safety droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Safety droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Safety_droid)

The safety droid was a type of security droid programmed to protect the inhabitants on the world of At Attin. During the early New Republic Era, they patrolled every sector of the planet including the streets of its city, At Attin Middle School Gamma, and government buildings.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Safety droid
- **Homeworld:** At Attin
- **Model:** Security droid
- **Class:** Class 4
- **Armament:**
  - Flashlight
  - Stun blaster rifles
- **Affiliation:** At Attin's government
