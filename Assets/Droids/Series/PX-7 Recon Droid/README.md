# PX-7 Recon Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PX-7 Recon Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PX-7_Recon_Droid)

The PX-7 Recon Droid was a model of probe droid available during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The PX-7 was designed to infiltrate enemy lines, using long-range sensors and laser defensive protocols. The Twi'lek arms dealer Olok the Shadow sold PX-7 Recon Droids in his auction on Darvannis.
