# Refuse droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Refuse droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Refuse_droid)

Refuse droids were a type of cleaning droid that was used for street cleaning in Kestro City on the planet Vardos during the reign of the Galactic Empire. After the city's refuse droids were struck down by a freak processor virus, the Vardos Star Scouts member Zan Videx regularly spent weekends sweeping the streets of Kestro City, having earned a litter picking badge the first time he volunteered for a mandatory weekend of street cleaning.
