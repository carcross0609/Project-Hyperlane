# FSD-6D flying surveillance droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FSD-6D flying surveillance droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FSD-6D_flying_surveillance_droid)

The FSD-6D flying surveillance droid was a small, gray, spherical camera droid, covered in sensors, commonly used by the early Galactic Empire on Coruscant. Manufactured by Arakyd Industries, it was equipped by a 1-meter lens-tipped extensible stalk, allowing it to look round corners. Able to see in low light and into the infrared spectrum, it came with a claw appendage, a blaster, a heuristic processor, locked access, an integrated comlink, sensor units, an improved sensor package, and a video recording unit. A single droid cost 19,500 credits.
