# SD-10 battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SD-10 battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-10_battle_droid)

The SD-10 battle droid was part of the SD-series of Balmorran Arms battle droids. Like preceding models in the series, the SD-10 was a humanoid fighting droid significantly larger than a human. The model was designed by Umak Leth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SD-10 battle droid
- **Creator:** Umak Leth
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Line:** SD-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Height:** 3.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 1 concussion missile launcher
  - 1 heavy repeating blaster
  - 1 explosive plasma cannon
- **Equipment:**

  - Fast-reaction servomotors
  - Point-of-impact shielding
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Balmorran Defense Force
  - Dark Empire
