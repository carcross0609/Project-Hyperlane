# Fast-moving Probe Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fast-moving Probe Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fast-moving_Probe_Droid)

Fast-moving Probe Droids were a type of Imperial probe droid. The cylindrical droids rolled back and forth over the ground in their assigned area, raising and lowering their bodies while moving. A number of these droids could be found on the first Death Star.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fast-moving Probe Droid
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
