# Battle Droid C-13

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle Droid C-13](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Droid_C-13)

The Battle Droid C-13 was a model of battle droid that saw use during the Cold War. Earlier versions included the Battle Droid C-10 and Battle Droid C-11. Equipped with a blaster rifle, a number of them were deployed to protect the satellite control tower on the Markaran Plains of the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Battle Droid C-13
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Black
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
