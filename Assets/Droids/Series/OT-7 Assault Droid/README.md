# OT-7 Assault Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-7 Assault Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-7_Assault_Droid)

The OT-7 Assault Droid was a bipedal battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War. A number of reprogrammed OT-7 Assault Droids were used by Imperial forces in the Jacent Valley north of the Okara Droid Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-7 Assault Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
