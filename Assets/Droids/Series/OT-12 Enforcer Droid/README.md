# OT-12 Enforcer Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-12 Enforcer Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-12_Enforcer_Droid)

The OT-12 Enforcer Droid was a large, six-legged battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. OT-12 Enforcer Droids were among the droids that defended the Okara Droid Factory from attack.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-12 Enforcer Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
