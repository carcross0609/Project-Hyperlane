# Battle droid (Graveyard)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle droid (Graveyard)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_droid_%28Graveyard%29)

Spaceworthy battle droids were utilized by the Galactic Empire in the Graveyard. They were a model of battle droid that was capable of moving in the vacuum of space. They were armed with ion cannons. The Empire used them, as well as Viper probe droids, as part of their trap for the Heroes of Yavin.

- **Name:** Battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
