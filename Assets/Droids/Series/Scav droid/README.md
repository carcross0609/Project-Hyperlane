# Scav droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scav droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scav_droid)

Scav droids were a type of droid utilized by the Nihil marauder organization during the High Republic Era. Designed by Zeetar, a Nihil and engineer who became one of the group's three Tempest Runners, scav droids were used in the pirates' raids to collect loot. The droids had crablike bodies and manipulator arms, with which they dismantled machinery. The Nihil employed scav droids on Rekelos, during an attack on the Cyclor Shipyards, and an attack on the Republic Fair on the planet Valo. Units deployed into ground operations were identified as ground scavs.
