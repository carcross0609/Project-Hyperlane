# Sniper droideka

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sniper droideka/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sniper_droideka/Legends)

The sniper droideka or droideka sniper, more commonly known as the sniper droid, was a sniper variation of droideka used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sniper droideka
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoids
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.87 meters
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** Heavy blaster rifle (1)
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
