# MCR-100 Miniprobe

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MCR-100 Miniprobe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MCR-100_Miniprobe)

The MCR-100 Miniprobe was a type of small probe droid during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Designed by the Shroud, it was an upgrade from the MCR-99 reconnaissance droid.
