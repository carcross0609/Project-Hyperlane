# LR-57 combat/retail droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LR-57 combat/retail droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LR-57_combat/retail_droid)

The LR-57 combat/retail droid, also known as the LR-57 combat droid, Retail Caucus droid, or retail droid, was a model of battle droid used by the Retail Caucus and the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars, most prominently at the Battle of Christophsis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LR-57 combat/retail droid
- **Manufacturer:** Retail Caucus
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 2.58 meters (8 ft, 6 in)
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Brown
- **Armament:** Double laser cannons (2)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Retail Caucus
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
