# A-DSD advanced dwarf spider droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A-DSD advanced dwarf spider droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-DSD_advanced_dwarf_spider_droid)

The A-DSD advanced dwarf spider droid was a more heavily armed and armored version of the DSD1 dwarf spider droid and the heavy dwarf spider droid, capable of operating in complete darkness.

- **Name:** A-DSD advanced dwarf spider droid
- **Manufacturer:** Commerce Guild Manufacturers
- **Line:** Spider droid family
- **Class:** Walker
- **Cost:** 25,000 credits
- **Armament:**

  - Fire-linked missile launchers (2) (36 frag missles)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Commerce Guild
