# TT-40 librarian droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TT-40 librarian droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TT-40_librarian_droid)

The TT-40 librarian droid was a library droid produced by the TelBrinTel Corporation for use in retrieving information. It was given little data storage capacity and no encryption capabilities, as it was designed to provide information on a by-request basis. The TT-40 was a common sight in public spaces such as corporate lobbies and municipal archives.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TT-40 librarian droid
- **Manufacturer:** TelBrinTel Corporation
- **Class:** Librarian droid
- **Height:** 1.5 meters
