# Architect Droid AD

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Architect Droid AD](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Architect_Droid_AD)

The Architect Droid AD was a model of humanoid-shaped supervisor droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on Balmorra. A number of them were used to defend the factory during the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Architect Droid AD
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Supervisor droid
- **Plating:** Rust
