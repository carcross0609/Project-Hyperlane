# 11-17-series mining droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [11-17-series mining droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/11-17-series_mining_droid)

11-17 mining droids were independent mole miners. They were made by Slayn & Korpil and Roche. 11-17s were equipped with a large plasma jet, a telescoping circular saw and a blow torch. To protect it against rockslides, the 11-17 had durasteel armor plating. Such droids were used on Hosk Station.
