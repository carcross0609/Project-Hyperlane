# DD-13 medical assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DD-13 medical assistant droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DD-13_medical_assistant_droid/Legends)

The DD-13 medical assistant droid, also known as the DD-13 cybernetic surgical droid, was a medical droid, designed by Ubrikkian Steamworks. Its primary purpose was the installation of cybernetic implants.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DD-13 medical assistant droid
- **Manufacturer:** Ubrikkian Steamworks
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Height:** 1.83 meters
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Equipment:** Various surgical tools on modular arms
- **Affiliation:**

  - Galactic Empire
