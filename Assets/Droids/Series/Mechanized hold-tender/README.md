# Mechanized hold-tender

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mechanized hold-tender](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanized_hold-tender)

Mechanized hold-tenders were repulsorlift-equipped droids. They lifted and moved cargo in the holds of blockade runners. One such droid was made to attack Luke Skywalker by 13-K when it infiltrated a Rebel blockade runner.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mechanized hold-tender
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Equipment:**
  - Repulsorlift generator
  - 2 large pincer-arms
