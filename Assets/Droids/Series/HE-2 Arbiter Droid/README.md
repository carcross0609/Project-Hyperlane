# HE-2 Arbiter Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HE-2 Arbiter Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HE-2_Arbiter_Droid)

The HE-2 Arbiter Droid was a model of droid that was used at the Balmorran Arms Factory on the planet Balmorra near the end of the Cold War. They carried blaster rifles.
