# R4-P Astromech

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R4-P Astromech](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R4-P_Astromech)

The R4-P Astromech was a model of R-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton. It was an R4 astromech droid that could be permanently integrated into the customized Jedi version of the Kuat Systems Engineering Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor, allowing the starfighter to interface with KSE's Syliure-31 hyperspace docking ring.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R4-P Astromech
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** R-series (R4 astromech droid)
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Maroon, with silver accent panels
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Galactic Republic
