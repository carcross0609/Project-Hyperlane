# MX-05 Constructor Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MX-05 Constructor Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MX-05_Constructor_Droid)

The MX-05 Constructor Droid was a model of four-legged construction droid used by the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. An MX-05 Constructor Droid repurposed as a battle droid was among the forces guarding B-NK1's base in the back room of the Dealer's Den cantina on Coruscant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MX-05 Constructor Droid
- **Class:** Construction droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
