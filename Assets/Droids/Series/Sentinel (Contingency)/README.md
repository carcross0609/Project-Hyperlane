# Sentinel (Contingency)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sentinel (Contingency)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentinel_%28Contingency%29)

Sentinels were a type of droid model used by the Galactic Empire which were programmed to serve the will of Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine. The droids were manufactured in the Imperial base on the planet Vetine, and were capable of playing pre-recorded messages from their head. After the death of Palpatine during the Battle of Endor, multiple sentinel droids known as the Messengers carried the late Emperor's posthumous messages to Imperial commanders with instructions to initiate Operation: Cinder.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sentinel
- **Homeworld:** Imperial base, Vetine
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Plating:** Red
- **Equipment:**
  - Glass face mask viewscreen
  - Syringe for blood testing
  - Palm holoprojector
  - Computer systems capable of interfacing with The Resource
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Sith
- **Firstmade:** In or prior to 20 BBY
