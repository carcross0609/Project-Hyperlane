# JT-65 Salvage Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [JT-65 Salvage Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JT-65_Salvage_Droid)

The JT-65 Salvage Droid was a model of humanoid-shaped salvage droid that saw use during the Cold War. A number of JT-65 droids worked with S7-H7 Slicing Droids and BB-22 Salvage Droids in the Barasin Refuse Yards on the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** JT-65 Salvage Droid
- **Class:** Salvage droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Blue and yellow
