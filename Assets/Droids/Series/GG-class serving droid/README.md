# GG-class serving droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GG-class serving droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GG-class_serving_droid)

GG-class serving droids were a type of service droid. In 11 BBY, a GG-class droid worked in the residence of the mayor of Gorse City on the planet Gorse. The droid carried a tray of drinks for guests during a celebration held at the residence following the death of Count Denetrius Vidian. Following the Battle of Yavin, a GG-class droid was present during the wedding of Moff Jaccun Pereez's daughter, Famma, to Commander Yendiv Bensek, where the droid acted as a spy for Inferno Squad.
