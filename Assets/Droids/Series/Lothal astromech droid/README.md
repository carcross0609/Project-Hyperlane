# Lothal astromech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lothal astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lothal_astromech_droid)

The Lothal astromech droid, also known as LAD astromech droid, or simply "LAD," was a model of astromech droid manufactured by Lothal Logistics Limited. Although they were not considered to be on par with the more advanced astromech models from Industrial Automaton, Lothal astromech droids were considered to be decent enough droids. The droids had mostly white bodies, and had transparent domes that revealed some of their inner mechanisms much like Industrial Automatons R3-series Astromech droid.
