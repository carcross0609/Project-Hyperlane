# RH7 CardShark

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RH7 CardShark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RH7_CardShark)

The RH7 CardShark was a sabacc-dealing droid made by LeisureMech Enterprises. They were installed in casinos across the galaxy as a replacement for organic dealers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RH7 CardShark
- **Manufacturer:** LeisureMech Enterprises
- **Height:** 1.5 meters (extended)
