# MD-11 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-11 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-11_medical_droid)

The MD-11 medical droid was a type of medical droid. Two were in use at the Aurora Medical Facility on Obroa-skai in 43 ABY, where they were used to help raise the Human Tobb Jadak out of a coma.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MD-11 medical droid
- **Line:** MD-series medical specialist droid
- **Class:** Medical specialist
