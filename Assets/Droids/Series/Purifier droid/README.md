# Purifier droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Purifier droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Purifier_droid)

Purifier droids were a type of droid designed by the unidentified Iokath species to serve as security on their artificial world of Iokath. The species went extinct following the Iokath Civil War and Purifier droids were repurposed by ARIES into the combat units, aggressive to any intruders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Purifier droid
- **Creator:** Unidentified Iokath species
- **Homeworld:** Iokath
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:**
  - Unidentified Iokath species
  - ARIES
