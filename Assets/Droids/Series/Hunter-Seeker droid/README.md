# Hunter-Seeker droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hunter-Seeker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter-Seeker_droid)

The Hunter-Seeker droid was an unusual droid starfighter employed by the Trade Federation as a defensive unit. Their primary aim was the protection of the C-9979 transports. Developed by the time of the Invasion of Naboo, the design was based upon the Droideka.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hunter-Seeker droid
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoid Creation Nest
- **Line:** Droideka variant
- **Class:** Droid starfighter
- **Armament:** Laser cannon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
