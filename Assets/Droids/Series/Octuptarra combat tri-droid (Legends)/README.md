# Octuptarra combat tri-droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Octuptarra combat tri-droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Octuptarra_combat_tri-droid/Legends)

The octuptarra combat tri-droids were advanced combat droids used by the Techno Union and the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. It was a smaller version of the titanic Octuptarra magna tri-droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Octuptarra combat tri-droid
- **Manufacturer:** Techno Union
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Cost:** 2,500 credits
- **Height:** 3.6 meters (at full height)
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:**
  - Laser turret cannons (3)
  - Gaseous virus (virus variant only)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Techno Union
