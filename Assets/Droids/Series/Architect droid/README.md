# Architect droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Architect droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Architect_droid)

Architect droid was a model of droid designed to help build things that dated back to the time of the Old Republic and the nascent days of the Jedi Order. One such droid was the Mark IV Professor Huyang, who specialized in the construction of lightsabers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Architect droid
- **Degree:** Class three
- **Height:** 1.80 meters
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Galactic Republic
  - New Republic
