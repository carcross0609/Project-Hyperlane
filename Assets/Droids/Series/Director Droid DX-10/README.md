# Director Droid DX-10

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Director Droid DX-10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Director_Droid_DX-10)

The Director Droid DX-10 was a model of supervisor droid produced by the Okara Droid Company, and an upgraded version of the Assembly Director Droid DX-9. A number of DX-10 droids oversaw the Research and Development section of the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Director Droid DX-10
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Factory
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
