# FX-4 medical assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FX-4 medical assistant droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-4_medical_assistant_droid/Legends)

The FX-4 medical assistant droid was a medical droid manufactured by Medtech Industries as part of the FX-series of medical assistant droids. Produced during the last decades of the Galactic Republic, it was ultimately succeeded by the FX-5 model.
