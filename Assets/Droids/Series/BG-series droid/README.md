# BG-series droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BG-series droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BG-series_droid)

BG-series droids were a model of assassin droid. An older model by the time of the New Republic, it was believed that all examples of the droid had been destroyed. At least one droid, BG-12, survived and was in the service of the criminal Themog.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BG-series droid
- **Model:** Assassin droid
- **Armament:** Two blaster pistols in arm recesses
- **Equipment:**
  - Armored chassis
  - Broadband antenna
  - Enhanced visual and audio sensors
  - Gerdoor GG-22 tactical decision-making node
  - Holographic recorder/projector
  - Verbo 212-38 vocabulator and language interpreter
