# Fun World Document Droids

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fun World Document Droids](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fun_World_Document_Droids)

Fun World Document Droids were a model of droid used for examining legal documents on Hologram Fun World. In 5 ABY, Han Solo and Leia Organa traveled to Hologram Fun World to get married. They met their friend Lando Calrissian there, who asked for their birth certificates to be taken to the Fun World Document Droids for inspection. However, neither Solo or Organa had their certificates available.
