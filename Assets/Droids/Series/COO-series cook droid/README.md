# COO-series cook droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [COO-series cook droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/COO-series_cook_droid)

The COO-series cook droid, also known simply as a COO Cook Droid, was a type of class three droid. The 6.5 or 7 feet Cooking droid, manufactured by Industrial Automaton, was designed to demands of places where large numbers of people all needed to be fed efficiently such as mining operations, military bases, civilian space stations, and scientific research complex. The droid had extendable arms for handing to multiple customers and magnetized feet that could lock onto deck plates. COO-series droids also had a photoreceptor that could see into the infrared spectrum in order to analyze heat distribution while cooking, and an olfactory sensor to determine if food was spoiled. Located inside the torso is a recipe storage unit. To upgrade this, the arms of the droid had to have been removed, which was a time-consuming and tedious process, so most didn't bother, leaving the COO droids simply to reheat pre-packed food. As a result of these, they became known for being rather grumpy.
