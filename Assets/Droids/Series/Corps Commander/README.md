# Corps Commander

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corps Commander](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corps_Commander)

The Corps Commander was a variant of Xim's war-robots. The Corps Commanders were designed with greater independence than their counterparts and could be distinguished by a golden death-head emblem on their breastplates in contrast to the white born by standard models. They saw action during the Xim Wars. Despite being outdated by modern era standards, they were still deadly opponents in combat. Han Solo encountered a Corps Commander during his treasure hunt on the planet Dellalt.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Corps Commander
- **Manufacturer:** Xim the Despot
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Height:** 2.78 meters
- **Armament:** Battlefield Pulse-wave cannons (removable)
- **Affiliation:** Xim's empire
