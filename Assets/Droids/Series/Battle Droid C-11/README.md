# Battle Droid C-11

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle Droid C-11](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Droid_C-11)

Battle Droid C-11 was a model of battle droid that was deployed on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. It was an upgraded version of the Battle Droid C-10 and was followed by the Battle Droid C-13.
