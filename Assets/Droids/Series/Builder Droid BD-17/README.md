# Builder Droid BD-17

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Builder Droid BD-17](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Builder_Droid_BD-17)

The Builder Droid BD-17 was a model of humanoid-shaped labor droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. They carried blaster pistols, and were used in the factory during the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Builder Droid BD-17
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Plating:** Tan
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
