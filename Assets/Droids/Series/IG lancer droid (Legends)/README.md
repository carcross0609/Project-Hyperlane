# IG lancer droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IG lancer droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IG_lancer_droid/Legends)

IG lancer combat droids, also known as IG-series battle droids or simply IG lancer droids, were a type of battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. The IG Lancer Droid first appeared in combat during the Battle of Muunilinst.

- **Name:** IG lancer droid
