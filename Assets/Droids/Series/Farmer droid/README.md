# Farmer droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Farmer droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Farmer_droid)

Farmer droid was a model of droid that was used by a human tribe on Sorgan. They assisted them in gathering the krill that were caught in the local village's ponds.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Farmer droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Equipment:** Baskets
- **Affiliation:** A human tribe
