# Scarab Mark VI assassin droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scarab Mark VI assassin droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scarab_Mark_VI_assassin_droid)

The Scarab Mark VI assassin droid was an assassin droid designed to look like a small insect. It was one of the eighteen variants that comprised Sienar Intelligence Systems' scarab droid line. They were designed to kill through stealth, not brute force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scarab Mark VI assassin droid
- **Manufacturer:** Sienar Intelligence Systems
- **Line:** Scarab droid
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Equipment:** Poison injector
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Dark Empire
