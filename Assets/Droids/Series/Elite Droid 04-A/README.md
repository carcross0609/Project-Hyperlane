# Elite Droid 04-A

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Elite Droid 04-A](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elite_Droid_04-A)

The Elite Droid 04-A was a model of battle droid that saw use during the Cold War between the reconstituted Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic. Equipped with a blaster pistol, a number of them were deployed to protect the satellite control tower on the Markaran Plains of the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Elite Droid 04-A
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Dull green
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
