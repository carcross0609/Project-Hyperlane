# B1 repeater blaster droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B1 repeater blaster droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1_repeater_blaster_droid)

The B1 repeater blaster droid was a variant of the B1-series battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems in its droid army during the Clone Wars. The droid used repeating blasters, which gave them superior firepower to the standard B1. The droids also had the same markings as the standard B1.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B1 repeater blaster droid
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Combat Automata / Baktoid Armor Workshop
- **Line:** B1-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.93 meters
- **Armament:** Repeating blaster
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
