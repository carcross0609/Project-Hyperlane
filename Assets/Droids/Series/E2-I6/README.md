# E2-I6

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E2-I6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E2-I6)

E2-I6 droids were instructional droids used by the Galactic Empire to assist in training Imperial Army recruits such as those trained on the planet Sirpar. The droids were also deployed at the Vernet Imperial Army Training Camp.

- **Name:** E2-I6
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
