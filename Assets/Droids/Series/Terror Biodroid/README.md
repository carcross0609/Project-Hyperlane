# Terror Biodroid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Terror Biodroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terror_Biodroid)

Terror Biodroids, also known as Terror Giants, were massive droid units used by the Galactic Empire. They were part of the Empire's new C-Class units and were created in Timira City on Kamino like the other units of this program. Terror Biodroids were known to fight with huge spiked arms and a variant of Force push.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Terror Biodroid
- **Armament:** Force generators
- **Equipment:** Razor titanium claws
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
