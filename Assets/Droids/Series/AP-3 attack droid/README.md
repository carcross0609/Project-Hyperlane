# AP-3 attack droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AP-3 attack droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AP-3_attack_droid)

The AP-3 attack droid (Assault Platform-3), also known as an AP-3 was a battle droid used by the Imperial Remnant. The AP-3 resembled an astromech droid, but it was armed with six missile launchers and micro-proton torpedoes. It also possessed a repulsorlift drive that enabled it to move quicker across the battlefield at the order of an AP-1-C.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AP-3 attack droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Armament:**
  - 6 missile launchers
  - micro-proton torpedoes
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
