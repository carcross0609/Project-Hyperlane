# GB-III

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [GB-III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GB-III)

The GB-III was a hovering droid used by the Galactic Empire to protect the corridors and walkways of the Death Star in 0 BBY. They were vaguely spherical and could move incredibly quickly.
