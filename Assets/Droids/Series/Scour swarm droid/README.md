# Scour swarm droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scour swarm droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scour_swarm_droid)

Scour swarm droid was a type of droid designed by the unidentified Iokath species to clean the Chromium Garden on their artificial world of Iokath. The species went extinct following the Iokath Civil War and Scour swarm droids were repurposed by the AI ARIES into the combat units, aggressive to any intruders.

## Specifications

- **Homeworld:** Iokath
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
