# BAF-1010 Artillery Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-1010 Artillery Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-1010_Artillery_Droid)

The BAF-1010 Artillery Droid was a six-legged heavy battle droid that was produced by Balmorran Arms at the Balmorran Arms Factory on the planet Balmorra. BAF-1010 Artillery Droids supported Galactic Republic forces in their defense of the factory near the end of the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-1010 Artillery Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Orange-brown
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Balmorran resistance
