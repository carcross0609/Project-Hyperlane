# KD-24 Auditory Provocation Drone

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KD-24 Auditory Provocation Drone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KD-24_Auditory_Provocation_Drone)

The KD-24 Auditory Provocation Drone was a model of probe droid that was used by the freelance spy known as the Shroud. It was equipped with sonic devices designed to attract and provoke local wildlife, and KD-24 drones were deployed to sic animals on individuals who were intruding on the Shroud's business.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KD-24 Auditory Provocation Drone
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
