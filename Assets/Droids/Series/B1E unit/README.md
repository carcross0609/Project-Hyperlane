# B1E unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B1E unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1E_unit)

The B1E unit was a model of medical droid. A B1E droid served aboard the Corporate Sector Authority Invincible-class Dreadnaught Heavy Cruiser Resolute and in 19 ABY, the unit treated the Pantoran spacer Reen Irana for injuries that she had received after being shot in the chest with a stun blast by the Jedi archivist Mander Zuma.
