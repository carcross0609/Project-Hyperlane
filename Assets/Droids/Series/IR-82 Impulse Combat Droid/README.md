# IR-82 Impulse Combat Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IR-82 Impulse Combat Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-82_Impulse_Combat_Droid)

The IR-82 Impulse Combat Droid was a model of three-legged battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. IR-82 Impulse Combat Droids were among the forces that defended the Shroud's factory on the moon Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IR-82 Impulse Combat Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Dark
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
