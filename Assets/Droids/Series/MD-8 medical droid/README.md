# MD-8 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-8 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-8_medical_droid)

The MD-8 medical droid was a model of medical droid and a part of the MD-series that was manufactured by Industrial Automaton. Like the company's MD-7 and MD-9 models, the MD-8 was a prototype medical droid used only in limited sectors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MD-8 medical droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** MD-series medical droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
