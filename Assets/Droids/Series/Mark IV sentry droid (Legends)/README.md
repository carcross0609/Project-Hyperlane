# Mark IV sentry droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark IV sentry droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_IV_sentry_droid/Legends)

The Mark IV sentry droid, also known as the IM4, was a small, multi-purpose sensor droid manufactured by the Imperial Department of Military Research and employed by the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark IV sentry droid
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Department of Military Research
- **Class:** Sentry droid
- **Degree:** Class five droid
- **Width:** 0.3 meters (diameter)
- **Equipment:**
  - Holorecording macrobinoculars
  - Broad-band comlink
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
