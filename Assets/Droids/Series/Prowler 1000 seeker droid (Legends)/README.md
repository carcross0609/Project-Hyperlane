# Prowler 1000 seeker droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prowler 1000 seeker droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prowler_1000_seeker_droid/Legends)

The Prowler 1000 seeker droid, also known as the Prowler 1000 exploration droid, Arakyd seeker droid, and retroactively dubbed the "Probot 1.0," was a compact probe droid manufactured by Arakyd Industries and deployed during the Clone Wars by the soldiers of the Republic. The first entry in the Arakyd Probot Series, it would be improved and refined into the Viper probe droid.
