# B1 Electrostaff droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B1 Electrostaff droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1_Electrostaff_droid)

B1 electrostaff droids were Separatist Droid Army units who often appeared with Trandoshan scavengers. They seemed to be a variant from a B1-series battle droid, as they are stronger and more agile. They carried electrostaffs and could knock down an opponent with a single blow and going as far as engaging force-sensitives who carried lightsabers.
