# ESPY E.R. Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ESPY E.R. Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ESPY_E.R._Droid)

The ESPY E.R. Droid was a type of modified TT-8L gatekeeper droid used during the Galactic Civil War. Droids of this kind was unarmed as it primarily filled a reconnaissance role. ESPY E.R. Droids were used by the Rak'qua on Station Gamma above Ord Mantell following the Battle of Yavin. In 1 ABY, a number of spacers who explored Station Gamma fought and destroyed multiple ESPY E.R. Droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ESPY E.R. Droid
- **Line:** TT-8L gatekeeper droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Plating:** Brown
- **Armament:** None
- **Affiliation:** Rak'qua
