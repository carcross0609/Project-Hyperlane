# Machinist Droid MC-12

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Machinist Droid MC-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Machinist_Droid_MC-12)

The Machinist Droid MC-12 was a model of labor droid produced by the Okara Droid Company. A number of MC-12 droids worked in one section of the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Machinist Droid MC-12
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Orange
