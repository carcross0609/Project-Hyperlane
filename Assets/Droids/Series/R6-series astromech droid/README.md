# R6-series astromech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R6-series astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R6-series_astromech_droid)

The R6-series astromech droid was the sixth astromech droid model made by Industrial Automaton under their R-series of droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R6-series astromech droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Cost:** 4,000—6,000 credits
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Equipment:**
  - Acoustic signaller
  - Arc welder
  - Circular saw
  - Extendible video sensor
  - Fire extinguisher
  - Holographic projector/recorder
  - Retractable fine worker arm
  - Retractable heavy grasper arm
  - Three wheeled legs
  - Video display screen
