# Wanderer Scout Surveyor

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wanderer Scout Surveyor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wanderer_Scout_Surveyor)

The Wanderer Scout Surveyor was a model of survey droid manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. They were often deployed with 87-RM Scout Collectors to capture and examine animals.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wanderer Scout Surveyor
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Class:** Class five droid
- **Cost:**
  - 17,000 credits—9,400 credits(new)
  - 8,000 credits (used)
- **Width:** 1 meter diameter
- **Height:** 2 meters
- **Armament:** Stun blaster
- **Equipment:**
  - Fine manipulators (3)
  - Legs (8)
  - Photoreceptor eye
  - Sensor pack
  - Surgical kit
