# T-41 Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-41 Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-41_Battle_Droid)

The T-41 Battle Droid was a model of cold-resistant battle droid designed by the Ugnaught Kerkarr for the Galactic Republic to use on the planet Hoth. However, Kerkarr's ship crashed, and his droids went rogue.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T-41 Battle Droid
- **Creator:** Kerkarr
