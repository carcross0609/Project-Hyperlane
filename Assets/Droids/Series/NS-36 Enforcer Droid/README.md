# NS-36 Enforcer Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [NS-36 Enforcer Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NS-36_Enforcer_Droid)

NS-36 Enforcer Droid was a model of battle droid in service of the Sith Empire during the Cold War. Some units could be found defending Imperial territory on Nar Shaddaa.
