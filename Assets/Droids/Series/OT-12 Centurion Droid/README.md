# OT-12 Centurion Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-12 Centurion Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-12_Centurion_Droid)

The OT-12 Centurion Droid was a large, three -legged battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War. A number of OT-12 Centurion Droids were among the Imperial forces defending the Balmorran Arms Factory before its capture by the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-12 Centurion Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Arm-mounted blaster cannons
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
