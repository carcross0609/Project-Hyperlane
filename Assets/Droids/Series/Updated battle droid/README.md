# Updated battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Updated battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Updated_battle_droid)

This battle droid was an advanced type of class four droid battle droid that was designed in an intent to replace the B1-series battle droid in the Droid Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems in 20 BBY. It was biped humanoid in shape and described as an unstoppable killing machine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Updated battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
- **Firstmade:** c. 20 BBY
