# Orb-Walker

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Orb-Walker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orb-Walker)

Orb-Walkers were a model of black-and-white plated astromech droid with a black photoreceptor. Consisting of an Industrial Automaton BB-series astromech droid with the two legs of a Veril Line Systems EG-6 power droid, Orb-Walkers were a type of monster droid. Their blueprints were sold at the Jawa-run Sandcrawler Shop beside the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine. The Jawas sold the default blueprint for an Orb-Walker for 1,500,000 credits, a golden design for 6,000,000 credits, a diamond-colored version for 12,000,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design for 24,000,000 credits.
