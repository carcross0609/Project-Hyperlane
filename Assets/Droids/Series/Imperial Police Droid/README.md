# Imperial Police Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial Police Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Police_Droid)

The Imperial Police Droid was a model of police droid that was in existence during the late Republic Era and went onto be used by the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era, earning the pre-existing droid its Imperial name.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Imperial Police Droid
- **Class:** Police droid
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Yellow and dark-blue
- **Armament:** E-5 blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:**
  - The Corner Shop
  - Galactic Empire
