# Command Droid Mark VII

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Command Droid Mark VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Command_Droid_Mark_VII)

The Command Droid Mark VII was a model of humanoid-shaped battle droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. They carried personal blaster cannons, and were used in the factory during the Cold War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Command Droid Mark VII
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Black
