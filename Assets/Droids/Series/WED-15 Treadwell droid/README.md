# WED-15 Treadwell droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [WED-15 Treadwell droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WED-15_Treadwell_droid)

WED-15 Treadwell droids were a model of maintenance droid used by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. One notable Republic WED-15 was 327-T, who served on board the Venator-class Star Destroyer Tranquility during the war. Another WED-15 worked in the Power control room at the Senate Office Building but was destroyed by one of Cad Bane's IG-86 sentinel droids. Another served on board a Venator-class Star Destroyer, the Renown, although it was destroyed while trying to escape the ship after Separatist forces took control.
