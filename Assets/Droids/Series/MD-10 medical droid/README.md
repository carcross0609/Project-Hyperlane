# MD-10 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-10 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-10_medical_droid)

The MD-10 medical droid was a model of medical droid and a part of the MD-series that was manufactured by Industrial Automaton. The design was a breakout success, and each droid was built to be a specialist within a particular medical field, including cardiology, neurology, obstetrics, and dentistry.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MD-10 medical droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** MD-series medical droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
