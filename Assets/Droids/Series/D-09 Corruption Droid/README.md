# D-09 Corruption Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D-09 Corruption Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-09_Corruption_Droid)

The D-09 Corruption Droid was a model of battle droid that was used by the Dread Host, the armies of the Dread Masters during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. D-09 Corruption Droids and similar models were stationed at the Dread Fortress under the command of the droid Corruptor Zero, and fought to defend the fortress during the Dread War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** D-09 Corruption Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** White-gold and black
- **Armament:** Blaster cannon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Dread Masters
  -   Dread Host
