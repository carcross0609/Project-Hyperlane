# Short-Range Transport droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Short-Range Transport droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Short-Range_Transport_droid)

The Short-Range Transport (SRT) droid, also known as the forklift droid, was a droid model utilized by the Geonosians of the planet Geonosis. Operating in the subterranean droid factories, SRT droids transported items across the vast chambers, and made use of a maintenance and recharge area within the foundry.

- **Name:** Short-Range Transport droid
- **Homeworld:** Geonosis
