# R5-series astromech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R5-series astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R5-series_astromech_droid)

The R5-series astromech droid, also known as the R2-5, was a line of low-cost astromech droids built by Industrial Automaton. Based upon the success of prior astromech models, such as the wildly popular R2-series, Industrial Automaton intended the R5-series to cater to budget buyers at the cost of some functionality.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R5-series astromech droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Cost:** 1,500—2,000 credits
- **Height:** 1 meter
- **Equipment:**
  - Acoustic signaller
  - Fire extinguisher
  - Holographic projector/recorder
  - Retractable fine worker arm
  - Retractable heavy grasper arm
  - Small circular saw
  - Three wheeled legs
