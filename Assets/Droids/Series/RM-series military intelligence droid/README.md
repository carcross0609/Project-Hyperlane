# RM-series military intelligence droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RM-series military intelligence droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RM-series_military_intelligence_droid)

The RM-series military intelligence droid was a military intelligence droid produced by MerenData. It was equipped with a heavy blaster rifle and an ion blaster as well as a built-in demolitions kit. The droid had sensory and recording equipment as well.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RM-series military intelligence droid
- **Manufacturer:** MerenData
- **Class:** Military intelligence droid
- **Cost:** 43,000 credits
- **Armament:**
  - Heavy blaster rifle
  - Ion blaster
  - Built-in Demolitions Kit
- **Equipment:**
  - Sensory equipment
  - Recording equipment
