# Sweeper droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sweeper droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sweeper_droid)

A sweeper droid was a type of cleaning droid that was used to clean the streets of the planet Coruscant, the capital of the Galactic Republic During the Clone Wars. In 3 ABY, Sweeper droids were employed across the Outer Rim, such as on the planet Tatooine and the space station of Renpalli Station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sweeper droid
- **Class:** Cleaning droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Varied
- **Firstmade:** By 21 BBY
