# BDX droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BDX droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BDX_droid)

BDX droids, also called BDX explorer droids, or BDX units, were a model of small explorer droids found throughout the galaxy. Similar to other explorer droid models, such as the BD unit, BDX droids were employed for gathering information, such as mapping terrain, geographic analysis, and scanning objects and structures.
