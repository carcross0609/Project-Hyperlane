# Scarecrow droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Scarecrow droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scarecrow_droid)

Scarecrow droids were mobile agrirobots designed to operate in crop fields, serving both as functional farm droids and as scarecrows. They featured a tall, straw-wrapped frame with short mechanical arms and a tracked base for movement. Twenty years after the Sith rebellion, scarecrow droids were present on a farm in a village on the Outer Rim planet Genbara, where an attack by the self-styled Dark Lord of the Sith Kouru and her Bandit Troopers was repelled by a former Sith known as the Ronin and a group of hired bodyguards.
