# SD-60 Personal Defense Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SD-60 Personal Defense Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-60_Personal_Defense_Droid)

The SD-60 Personal Defense Droid was a model of bipedal battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. SD-60 Personal Defense Droids were among the droid forces that defended the Shroud's factory on the moon Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SD-60 Personal Defense Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Dark
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
