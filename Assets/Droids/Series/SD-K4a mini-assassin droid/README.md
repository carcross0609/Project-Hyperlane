# SD-K4a mini-assassin droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SD-K4a mini-assassin droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-K4a_mini-assassin_droid)

The SD-K4a mini-assassin droid, also known as the probe killer, or the mini-assassin probe, was a model of assassin droid manufactured by Baktoid Combat Automata. Spider-like, the tiny, six legged droids could be carried inside the small pits on the head of the larger SD-K4 assassin droids. Additionally, a larger version of the SD-K4a also existed, featuring a central body that was about the same size as a human head. These droids were used on the Coronet in an attempt to assassinate Duchess Satine Kryze. Most of the droids were destroyed in the initial attempt, but Obi-Wan Kenobi found a survivor, which he used to identify the traitor aboard the ship.
