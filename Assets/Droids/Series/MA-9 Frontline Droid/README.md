# MA-9 Frontline Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MA-9 Frontline Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MA-9_Frontline_Droid)

The MA-9 Frontline Droid was a model of bipedal battle droid available during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The MA-9 was marketed as being obedient, adaptive, and deadly, and was equipped with a jet charge and a blade built into its bracer, as well as an optional carbonizing spray. The Twi'lek arms dealer Olok the Shadow sold MA-9 Frontline Droids in his auction on Darvannis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MA-9 Frontline Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Dark gray with white and orange markings
- **Armament:** Bracer vibroblade
- **Equipment:**
  - Jet charge
  - Optional carbonizing spray
