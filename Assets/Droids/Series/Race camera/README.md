# Race camera

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Race camera](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Race_camera)

Race cameras were a type of cam droid used to record podracing events. They were capable of high speeds to keep up with the fastest podracers.

- **Class:** Cam droid
