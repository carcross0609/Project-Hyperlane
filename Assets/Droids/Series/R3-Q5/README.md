# R3-Q5

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R3-Q5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R3-Q5)

R3-Q5 was the designation given to a series of R3-series astromech droids in service to the Galactic Empire shortly before the onset of the Galactic Civil War. They were employed by the Empire at various facilities, including a TIE fighter factory and the Death Star. Galen Marek, apprentice to Sith Lord Darth Vader, encountered several on his missions to these locales.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R3-Q5
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Black with bronze panels
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
