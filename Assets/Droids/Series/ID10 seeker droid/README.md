# ID10 seeker droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ID10 seeker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ID10_seeker_droid)

The ID10 seeker droid was a model of seeker droid that succeeded the ID9, with four legs instead of the ID9's five. Dio was an ID10 utilized by Inferno Squad following their formation after the Battle of Yavin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ID10 seeker droid
- **Class:** Seeker
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:**
  - Black (Imperial)
  - Yellow/white (New Republic)
- **Armament:** Electro-shock prod
- **Equipment:** Antenna
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Inferno Squad
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Danger Squadron
  - New Republic
  -   Inferno Squad
  - Resistance
