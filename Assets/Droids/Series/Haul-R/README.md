# Haul-R

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Haul-R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Haul-R)

The Haul-R was a model of battle droid with gold and silver colored plating and a black photoreceptor. As a monster droid, it was made from components of two other droid models: the head of a JLD Mechanicals Consortium heavy loader droid and the body of a Baktoid Combat Automata B2-RP. Blueprints for four variants of the droid were sold by the group of Jawas who ran the Sandcrawler Shop beside the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine. The Haul-R's default design cost 2,400,000 credits, a golden design cost 9,600,000 credits, a diamond-colored version cost 19,200,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design cost 38,400,000 credits.
