# Harvester (Confederacy of Independent Systems)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Harvester (Confederacy of Independent Systems)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harvester_%28Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems%29)

The harvester was a droid walker used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. They were used in a droid unit to mine the flora on the planet Hissrich due to the high amounts of energy the plants stored. The droids also fought against Jedi Master Mace Windu and his squad, using laser cannons mounted on their exterior.

- **Class:** Droid walker
