# G2 repair droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [G2 repair droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G2_repair_droid)

The G2 repair droid was a model of maintenance droid that was inclined to chatter. The Resistance used G2 repair droids such as G2-PI to perform memory wipes and G2-RX who performed repairs in the droid repair bay aboard the MC85 Star Cruiser Raddus.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G2 repair droid
- **Class:** Maintenance droid
- **Affiliation:** Resistance
