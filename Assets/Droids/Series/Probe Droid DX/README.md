# Probe Droid DX

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Probe Droid DX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Probe_Droid_DX)

The Probe Droid DX was a model of probe droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. During the Cold War, Imperial forces uploaded computer viruses to a number of probe droids outside of the factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Probe Droid DX
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Plating:** Black
