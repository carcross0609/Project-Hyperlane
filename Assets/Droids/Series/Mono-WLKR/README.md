# Mono-WLKR

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Mono-WLKR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mono-WLKR)

The Mono-WLKR was a model of worker droid with gray plating and a red photoreceptor. They were monster droids that had the head of an ID10 seeker droid and the body of a DUM-series pit droid, which was produced by Serv-O-Droid, Inc. Blueprints for Mono-WLKRs were sold at the Sandcrawler Shop, a Jawa-run business near the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine. The Jawas sold the default Mono-WLKR blueprint for 26,000,000 credits, a golden design for 104,000,000 credits, a diamond-colored version at 208,000,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design at 416,000,000 credits.
