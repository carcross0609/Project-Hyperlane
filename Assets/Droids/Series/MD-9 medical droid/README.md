# MD-9 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-9 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-9_medical_droid)

The MD-9 medical droid was a model of medical droid and a part of the MD-series that was manufactured by Industrial Automaton. The MD-9 model was a prototype medical droid used only in limited sectors, like the MD-7 and MD-8 models.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MD-9 medical droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** MD-series medical droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Degree:** Class one droid
