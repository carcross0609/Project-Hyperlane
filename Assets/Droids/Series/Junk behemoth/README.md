# Junk behemoth

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Junk behemoth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Junk_behemoth)

Junk behemoths were a type of junk golem found on Raxus Prime after the Clone Wars. They usually served as walking power plants or armor carriers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Junk behemoth
- **Creator:** Kazdan Paratus
- **Homeworld:** Raxus Prime
- **Retired:** 3 BBY (after the death of Kazdan Paratus)
