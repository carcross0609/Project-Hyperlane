# FX-series medical assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FX-series medical assistant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-series_medical_assistant_droid)

The FX-series medical assistant droid, also referred to as a FX-series medical droid, or as Fixits, was a medical droid that was part of ten-model series manufactured by Medtech Industries. They were often paired with 2-1B-series medical droids. The FX-series had a similar name to the FX-2 droid and FX-6 droid, both of which had been seen in the High Republic Era.

- **Name:** FX-series medical assistant droid

- **Model:** FX-series medical assistant droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Equipment:** Multiple arms and medical tools
- **Affiliation:**

  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
