# Opti-Pod

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Opti-Pod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Opti-Pod)

The Opti-Pod was a model of worker droid with brown and black plating and several black photoreceptors. Opti-Pods combined the head of an Arakyd Industries viper probe droid and the two legs of a Veril Line Systems EG-6 power droid. A group of Jawas sold blueprints for four variants of Opti-Pod from the Sandcrawler Shop beside the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine. A blueprint for the Opti-Pod's default design cost 3,600,000 credits, a golden design cost 14,400,000 credits, a diamond-colored version cost 28,800,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design cost 57,600,000 credits.
