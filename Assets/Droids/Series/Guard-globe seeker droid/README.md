# Guard-globe seeker droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Guard-globe seeker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guard-globe_seeker_droid)

The guard-globe seeker droid, also known simply as the guard globe, was a form of remote used by Dr. Arakkus to protect his fortress in the Graveyard of Lost Ships. They patrolled corridors and were programmed to intercept intruders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Guard-globe seeker droid
- **Class:** Remote
- **Armament:** Blaster
