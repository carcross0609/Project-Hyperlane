# Guardian NS-55 Enforcer Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Guardian NS-55 Enforcer Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian_NS-55_Enforcer_Droid)

Guardian NS-55 Enforcer Droid was a model of battle droids in service of the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. Some units could be found defending Imperial territory on Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Guardian NS-55 Enforcer Droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Plating:** Gold
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
