# I-7 Construct

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I-7 Construct](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I-7_Construct)

The I-7 Construct was a form of modified Viper probe droid used during the Galactic Civil War. Droids of this kind was unarmed as it primarily filled a reconnaissance role. I-7 Construct droids were used by the Rak'qua on Station Gamma above Ord Mantell following the Battle of Yavin. In 1 ABY, a number of spacers who explored Station Gamma fought and destroyed multiple I-7 Construct droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** I-7 Construct
- **Line:** Viper probe droid
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Plating:** Black
- **Armament:** None
- **Affiliation:** Rak'qua
