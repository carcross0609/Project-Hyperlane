# Municipal patrol droid Mark I

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Municipal patrol droid Mark I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Municipal_patrol_droid_Mark_I)

Municipal patrol droid Mark I was a droid designed by Automata Galactica as a law enforcement assistant for urban environments. It was also a popular way to boost firepower without adding personnel among private security companies and wealthy individuals. Even though small, Municipal Patrol Droid Mark I included more firepower than the average patrol droid.

- **Name:** Municipal patrol droid Mark I

- **Line:** Municipal patrol droid Mark I

- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Armament:** 2 blaster pistols
