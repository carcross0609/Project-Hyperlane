# SD-series battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SD-series battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-series_battle_droid)

The SD-series battle droid was a model of battle droid produced by Balmorran Arms. The company later loosely based the design of the TRD-2 sparring droid on the SD-series battle droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SD-series battle droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
