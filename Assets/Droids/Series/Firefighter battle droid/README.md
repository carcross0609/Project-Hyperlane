# Firefighter battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Firefighter battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firefighter_battle_droid)

Firefighter battle droids, also known as firefighting battle droids, or B1 firefighter droids, were specialized B1-series battle droids equipped with enhanced heat shielding that were programmed and equipped to fight fires. Notable for their distinctive paint and heavy backpacks, which featured the numbers 72066 running down either side, they carried fire hoses that sprayed fire-repellent foam. They also had small, built-in fire extinguishers. They could also be used for security.
