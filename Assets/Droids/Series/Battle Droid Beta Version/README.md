# Battle Droid Beta Version

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Battle Droid Beta Version](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Droid_Beta_Version)

The Battle Droid Beta Version was a model of B1-series battle droid used in the Trade Federation Droid Army. They were assembled at troop centers and were slower and less powerful than the standard B1 battle droid. Battle Droid Beta Versions were used in several battles in the war for the moon Alaris Prime, where many were destroyed by Wookiees.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Battle Droid Beta Version
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Combat Automata
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Model:** B1-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Height:** 1.91 meters
- **Armament:** E-5 blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
