# G-100 remote banking droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-100 remote banking droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-100_remote_banking_droid)

The G-100 remote banking droid was a model of banking droid produced by Cybot Galactica. The company focused on security over cosmetics to render it nearly invulnerable to assault. Many were used in Cloud City during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G-100 remote banking droid
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
- **Line:** G-100
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Equipment:** Built-in network uplink
