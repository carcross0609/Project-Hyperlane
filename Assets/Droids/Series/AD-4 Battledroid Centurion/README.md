# AD-4 Battledroid Centurion

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AD-4 Battledroid Centurion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AD-4_Battledroid_Centurion)

The AD-4 Battledroid Centurion was a type of battle droid used as security forces by the Selkath species on the planet Manaan during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. A number of AD-4 Centurions were among the security forces of Genetics Laboratory G-1, a genetic research facility on Manaan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AD-4 Battledroid Centurion
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Orange
- **Plating:** Dark blue and silver
- **Armament:** Arm-mounted blaster cannons
