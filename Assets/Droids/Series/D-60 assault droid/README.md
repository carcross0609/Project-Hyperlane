# D-60 assault droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D-60 assault droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-60_assault_droid)

The D-60 assault droid was a B2-series super battle droid variant used by the Separatists during the Clone Wars. Some of them were deployed during the First Battle of Geonosis, as well as the Attack on Cartao. They also featured blaster cannons on their arms and a slightly different appearance than the B2 super battle droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** D-60 assault droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Blaster cannons
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
