# I2-CG droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [I2-CG droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I2-CG_droid)

I2-CG droids were a model of droid. The plating on their barrel-shaped bodies was white, while their domed heads had silver plating with red detailing. In 34 ABY, the Abednedo laborer Roodown dragged a sled containing three non-functional I2-CG droids through Niima Outpost on the planet Jakku, passing the scavenger Rey as he did so. At some point Rey drew a picture of Roodown and the three droids in her survival guide.

## Specifications

- **Name:** I2-CG droid
- **Plating:** Red, white and silver
