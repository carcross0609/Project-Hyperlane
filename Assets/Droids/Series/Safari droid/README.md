# Safari droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Safari droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Safari_droid)

Safari droids were droids manufactured by Serv-O-Droid, Inc. designed for surveying wildlife across the galaxy. Commissioned by the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts, Safari droids were sent on particularly dangerous missions and programmed to report their findings on various creatures back to the Society. One such Safari droid, SF-R3, went on several adventures throughout the galaxy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Safari droid
- **Manufacturer:** Serv-O-Droid, Inc.
- **Sensor:** Pink
- **Plating:** Yellow and white
