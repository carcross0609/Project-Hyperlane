# Imperial nanny droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial nanny droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_nanny_droid)

The Imperial nanny droid, also known as the Kaminoan medical droid, was a model of nanny droid first used during the Clone Wars by the Death Watch Mandalorian splinter group, as well as by the Galactic Republic. It was used excessively by the Republic's successor state, the Galactic Empire, following the war and earning the nickname "Imperial nanny droid." The droid had two arms and floated in the air.
