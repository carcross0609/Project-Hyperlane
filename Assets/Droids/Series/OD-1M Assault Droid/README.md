# OD-1M Assault Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OD-1M Assault Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OD-1M_Assault_Droid)

The OD-1M Assault Droid was a model of battle droid that was created by the Gran cyborg Ayor-v9, who used them on Hoth during the Galactic War and considered his droids to be his "family."

## Specifications

- **Name:** OD-1M Assault Droid
