# Factory droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Factory droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Factory_droid)

A factory droid, colloquially known as a factory drone, was a common classification of labor droid that operated in industrial facilities. Many of these droids operated in Mos Eisley on Tatooine. Mazzoney used a factory droid permanently mounted at the entrance of his cantina, Mazzoney's on the planet Demophon, to collect the cover charge for entry.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Factory droid
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Degree:** Class 5
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Brown and gray
