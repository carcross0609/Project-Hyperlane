# Microdroid listener

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Microdroid listener](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Microdroid_listener)

Microdroid listeners were small spider-like droids used as audio bugs. The droids followed preprogrammed instructions and were difficult to detect because they didn't broadcast their surveillance data. They were equipped with self-destruct mechanisms.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Microdroid listener
- **Model:** Spy droid
- **Class:** Listening device
- **Cost:** 1,500 credits
- **Length:** <10 centimeters
