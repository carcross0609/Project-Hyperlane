# R1-series astromech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R1-series astromech droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R1-series_astromech_droid/Legends)

The R1-series astromech droid, also known as the R1 reactor drone, was Industrial Automaton's first production model of their new consumer-market R-series astromech droid line.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R1-series astromech droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** R-series
- **Class:** Astromech droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Cost:** 3,500 credits
- **Height:** 2.2 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Acoustic signaller
  - Intellex III
  - Retractable manipulator arms
  - Treaded unipod
