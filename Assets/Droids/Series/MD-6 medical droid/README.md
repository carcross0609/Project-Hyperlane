# MD-6 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-6 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-6_medical_droid)

The MD-6 medical droid was a model of medical droid and a part of the MD-series that was manufactured by Industrial Automaton. It was manufactured exclusively for use by the Empire.
