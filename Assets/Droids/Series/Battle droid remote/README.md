# Battle droid remote

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Battle droid remote](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_droid_remote)

One model of remotes were training droids designed to simulate battle droids. They shot blaster bolts from its sensor and had four extra blasters hidden inside. Many Jedi, including Padawan Ahsoka Tano, trained with these droids as part of a test.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Battle droid remote
- **Class:** Training droid
- **Sensor:** Green
- **Plating:** Gray and brown
- **Armament:** Blasters
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
