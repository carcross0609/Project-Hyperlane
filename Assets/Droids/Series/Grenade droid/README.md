# Grenade droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grenade droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grenade_droid)

Grenade droids were a type of B1-series battle droid in the Separatist Droid Army. In 22 BBY, a number of grenadiers served as part of the droid unit assigned to defend the Separatist droid factory on Thule.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grenade droid
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Model:** B1-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Thermal detonator
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
