# Trade Federation assassin droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trade Federation assassin droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trade_Federation_assassin_droid)

The Trade Federation assassin droid was a model of assassin droid used by the Trade Federation during their invasion of the planet Naboo in 32 BBY. The droids were humanoid in appearance and carried cortosis weapons. They fought the Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi during the invasion.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Trade Federation assassin droid
- **Class:** Assassin droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Red and black
- **Armament:** Cortosis blade or cortosis mace
- **Affiliation:** Trade Federation
