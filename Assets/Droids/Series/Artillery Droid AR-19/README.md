# Artillery Droid AR-19

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Artillery Droid AR-19](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Artillery_Droid_AR-19)

The Artillery Droid AR-19 was a model of battle droid manufactured by the Okara Droid Company. A number of AR-19 units were deployed to protect the Research and Development section at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Artillery Droid AR-19
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
