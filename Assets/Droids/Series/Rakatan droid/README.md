# Rakatan droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rakatan droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rakatan_droid)

A Rakatan droid was a model of droid used by the Infinite Empire at its height. These floating machines had two arms and acted as servants to the Rakata as well as their agents. One was stationed onboard Trill's Rakatan scout ship when the ship entered into the Tython system where it assisted in treating the wounded Je'daii Sek'nos Rath.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rakatan droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Affiliation:** Infinite Empire
