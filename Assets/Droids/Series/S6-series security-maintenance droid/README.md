# S6-series security/maintenance droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S6-series security/maintenance droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S6-series_security/maintenance_droid)

The S6-series security and maintenance droid was a common security observation droid used throughout the galaxy around the time of the Old Sith Wars. The small, spherical droid were seen in great numbers throughout the galaxy, though mostly unnoticed by the average sentients, making them excellent for surveillance and espionage.
