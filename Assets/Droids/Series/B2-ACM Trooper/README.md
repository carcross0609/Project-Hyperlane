# B2-ACM Trooper

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B2-ACM Trooper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2-ACM_Trooper)

The B2-ACM Trooper was a type of B2-series super battle droid used by the Separatist Droid Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Featuring a more powerful wrist blaster than the standard model, B2-ACMs were deployed when the firepower of a standard B2 was not enough.

- **Armament:** Wrist blaster
