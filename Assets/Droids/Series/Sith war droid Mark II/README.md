# Sith war droid Mark II

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith war droid Mark II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_war_droid_Mark_II)

The Sith war droid Mark II was utilized by the Sith Empire during the Great Galactic War. The Sith deployed these droids during the Sacking of Coruscant, first during the invasion of the Jedi Temple, and later during the occupation of the planet. These droids had three "feet" and four arm-mounted blasters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sith war droid Mark II
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Armament:** 4 arm-mounted blasters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sith Empire
  - Imperial Military
