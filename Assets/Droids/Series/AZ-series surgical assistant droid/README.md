# AZ-series surgical assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AZ-series surgical assistant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AZ-series_surgical_assistant_droid)

The AZ-series surgical assistant droid was a model of medical droid manufactured by Cybot Galactica and utilized during the Clone Wars. One such droid, AZI-3, served the Kaminoans in Tipoca City on the planet Kamino, caring for clones.

- **Model:** AZ-series
- **Class:** Medical droid
