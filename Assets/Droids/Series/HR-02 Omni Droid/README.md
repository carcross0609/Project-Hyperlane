# HR-02 Omni Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HR-02 Omni Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HR-02_Omni_Droid)

HR-02 Omni Droid was a droid model designed by Galactic Solutions Industries. It's purpose was to assist in holo-reconstructions, however their drawback was in increased power consumption. Such units were used by the GSI team working on the Emperor's Eye on Ziost during the Invasion of Ziost.
