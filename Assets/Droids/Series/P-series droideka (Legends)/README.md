# P-series droideka

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [P-series droideka/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-series_droideka/Legends)

The P-series droideka or P-series destroyer droid was a model of droideka produced by the Colicoids for the Trade Federation, and later for the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** P-series droideka
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoid Creation Nest
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.83 meters
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:** Twin built-in blaster cannons (2)
- **Equipment:** Shield generator
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
