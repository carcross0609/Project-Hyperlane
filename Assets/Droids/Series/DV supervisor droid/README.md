# DV supervisor droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DV supervisor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DV_supervisor_droid)

The DV supervisor droid was a model of supervisor droid. One DV supervisor, DV-4D4, worked in the control room of the spice mines of Kessel in 10 BBY. DV-4D4, along with the other droids in the control room, were freed in an uprising by L3-37.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DV supervisor droid
- **Class:** Supervisor droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
