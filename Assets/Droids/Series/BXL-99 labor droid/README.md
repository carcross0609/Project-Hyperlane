# BXL-99 labor droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BXL-99 labor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BXL-99_labor_droid)

BXL-99 labor droids, often referred to as Bee-Ex-Ell-Ninety-nines, were a type of labor droid. Standing about two meters tall, they were very heavy, and were equipped with a pair of three-pronged pincers. Three such droids were present in Coruscant's Factory District droid plant, until they were destroyed by Jedi-in-hiding Jax Pavan and the protocol droid I-5YQ.
