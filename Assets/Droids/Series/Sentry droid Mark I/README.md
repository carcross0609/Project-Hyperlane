# Sentry droid Mark I

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sentry droid Mark I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentry_droid_Mark_I)

The Sentry droid Mark I was a floating, spherical droid model equipped with a blaster. Others had flamethrowers and icebreakers, and were identical to G0-T0. G0-T0 used many of them as guards on his yacht, the Visionary.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sentry droid Mark I
- **Armament:**
  - Blaster (1)
  - Flamethrowers (some droids)
  - Icebreakers (some droids)
- **Affiliation:** G0-T0
