# LM-432 crab droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LM-432 crab droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LM-432_crab_droid/Legends)

The LM-432 crab droid, also simply referred to as a crab droid, was a battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Its name was derived from its six-legged scuttling movement and organic appearance, though it belonged to the spider droid family. These droids were also nicknamed "muckrackers" because of their extensive use in marshy terrain.
