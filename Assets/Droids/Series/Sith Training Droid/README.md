# Sith Training Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith Training Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Training_Droid)

Sith Training Droids were an advanced series of training droids used by the Order of the Sith Lords and were based on the design of the holodroid prototype unit known as PROXY. Constructed on the vast ocean-covered world of Kamino, the training droids were primarily used as instruments in a secret project conducted under the surveillance of the Sith Lord Darth Vader. Following the redemption and death of his former secret apprentice, Galen Marek, Vader was determined to create a "new Starkiller" by utilizing the experimental advanced cloning process.
