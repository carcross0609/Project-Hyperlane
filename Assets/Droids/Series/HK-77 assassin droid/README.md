# HK-77 assassin droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HK-77 assassin droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HK-77_assassin_droid)

The HK-77 assassin droid was an assassin droid developed by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the end of the Clone Wars, but they were not produced or deployed until the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HK-77 assassin droid
- **Line:** HK-series assassin droids
- **Class:** assassin droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Cost:** 19,865 credits
- **Armament:** (2) blaster rifle
- **Equipment:**

  - Quadanium steel battle armor
  - Vocabulator
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - HK-47's Droid Army
  - Various third parties
