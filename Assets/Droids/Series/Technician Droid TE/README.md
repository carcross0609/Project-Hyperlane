# Technician Droid TE

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Technician Droid TE](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Technician_Droid_TE)

The Technician Droid TE was a humanoid-shaped battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on Balmorra. A number of those droids were used to defend the factory during the Battle of Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Technician Droid TE
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
