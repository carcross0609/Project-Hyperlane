# 3DO protocol/service droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [3DO protocol/service droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3DO_protocol/service_droid)

The 3DO Protocol/Service Droid was an ancient series of protocol and service droids that was manufactured by Duwani Mechanical Products around 4000 BBY.

- **Name:** 3DO protocol/service droid
- **Manufacturer:** Duwani Mechanical Products
- **Class:** Protocol droid/Service droid
- **Height:** 1.6 m
- **Equipment:**
