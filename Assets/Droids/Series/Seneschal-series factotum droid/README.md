# Seneschal-series factotum droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seneschal-series factotum droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seneschal-series_factotum_droid)

The Seneschal-series factotum droid, also known as the 2V-series, or simply Toovee, was a model of service droid produced by Imperial DroidWorks during the Cold War. Four such units served the Emperor's Wrath, Darth Nox, Cipher Nine, and the Grand Champion of the Great Hunt during the Cold War, aboard their respective starships. One of the four droids came to serve the Outlander.
