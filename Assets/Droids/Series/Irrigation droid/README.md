# Irrigation droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Irrigation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Irrigation_droid)

Irrigation droids were a model of farming droid equipped with water cannons. Several irrigation droids at the Kemalo Farming Cluster on Kinyen were programmed to attack the farmers by the slicer Skaak Tippers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Irrigation droid
- **Equipment:** Water cannon
