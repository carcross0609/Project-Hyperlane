# Infiltrator probe droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Infiltrator probe droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltrator_probe_droid)

The Infiltrator probe droid was a probe droid produced in a collaboration between Arakyd Industries and Imperial technicians under Admiral Damon Krell following the Battle of Hoth. Augmented with superior armor, weapons and computer interface capabilities, an Infiltrator had the ability to tap into and completely control a starship. In 3 ABY, Krell attempted to use an Infiltrator droid to destroy the Rebel fleet, only to die when his plan backfired.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Infiltrator probe droid
- **Creator:** Damon Krell
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries/Imperial technicians
- **Line:** Arakyd Probot Series
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Armament:** 2 enhanced laser cannons
- **Equipment:**
  - Armor plating
  - Several retractable manipulator arms
  - Several computer interface probes
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
