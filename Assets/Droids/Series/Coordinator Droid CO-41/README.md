# Coordinator Droid CO-41

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coordinator Droid CO-41](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coordinator_Droid_CO-41)

The Coordinator Droid CO-41 was a model of supervisor droid produced by the Okara Droid Company. A number of CO-41 droids worked in one section of the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Coordinator Droid CO-41
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
