# UL-413 scaper droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [UL-413 scaper droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/UL-413_scaper_droid)

The UL-413 scaper droid was a model of scaper droid with an arched body with red, blue, and gray coloring and a gray treaded motility assembly beneath. They employed traction fields to smooth and dry out dirt and had a cognitive assembly module. During the High Republic Era, various scaper droids, including UL-413 scaper droids, were employed in Olega City on the planet Olega to flatten and groom terrain to a state that was suitable for pedestrians and vehicular traffic, as the city lacked paved roads.
