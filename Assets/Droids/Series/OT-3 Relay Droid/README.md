# OT-3 Relay Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-3 Relay Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-3_Relay_Droid)

The OT-3 Relay Droid was a probe droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War, when a number of OT-3 Relay Droids were stationed around the Okara Droid Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-3 Relay Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Probe droid
