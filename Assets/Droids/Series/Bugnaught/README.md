# Bugnaught

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bugnaught](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bugnaught)

Bugnaught was a type of droid gladiator used by the Rattataki as training adversaries. One in particular fought in the Cauldron on Rattatak. This Bugnaught burned Carl to death with a flamethrower installed in his arm, but was ripped to pieces by Blorga.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bugnaught
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:**
  - Retractable spikes
  - Arm flamethrower
- **Affiliation:** The Cauldron
