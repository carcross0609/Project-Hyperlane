# V-359 Guard Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V-359 Guard Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-359_Guard_Droid)

The V-359 Guard Droid was a model of battle droid in service of the Sith Empire during the Cold War. Some units were found defending Imperial territory on Nar Shaddaa.

- **Name:** V-359 Guard Droid
- **Class:** Security droid
