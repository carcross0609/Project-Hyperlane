# 1-1A medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [1-1A medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1-1A_medical_droid)

The 1-1A medical droid was a model of medical droid. During the High Republic Era, the Chadra-Fan doctor and Nihil member Kisma Uttersond utilized a rusty and old mechanical prosthetic that was once the leg of a 1-1A medical droid. As a result of the prosthetic, Uttersond had to hobble at a pace as fast as the leg would allow.
