# QR-unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [QR-unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QR-unit)

The QR-unit was a model of droid in use during the High Republic Era. Multiple QR-units, including Q-4 "Kew-For" and CT-Thirty-Eight, served Hutt Clan enforcer Lourna Dee aboard the starcruiser Lourna Dee, previously the Jedi starship Ataraxia. When attempting to reclaim the Ataraxia after Dee turned against the Hutts, Jedi Master Keeve Trennis destroyed the two QR-units. The Jedi Knight Ceret utilized the destroyed Kew-For to retrieve the access code for the Nihil's scav droids. They overwrote Kew-For's autonomy circuits and forced the droid to give them the code, claiming they did not have the time to negotiate with a QR-unit.
