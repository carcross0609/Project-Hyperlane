# Unidentified police droid model

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified police droid model](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_police_droid_model)

A model of police droid existed during the later Republic Era and was utilized by the police force of a city on Duro. They had a humanoid body shape and black and light-grey plating and no visible sensors. On the front of their head was written "Z|18" in Aurebesh writing, while on the back of their head was written "POLICE". On the left side of their chest was the emblem of the police force. They spoke Basic with a robotic tone. They wielded RG-4D blaster pistols and electro batons. They can also operate on patrol landspeeders.
