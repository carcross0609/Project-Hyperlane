# AAT Driver Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AAT Driver Battle Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AAT_Driver_Battle_Droid)

AAT Driver Battle Droids, also known as AAT Driver Droids, jungle battle droids, and as Kashyyyk Battle Droids, were a variant of the B1-series battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. They had tactical camouflage markings that both allowed them to blend into jungles and designated their role as tank operators. Droids of this model were deployed in Armored Assault Tanks.
