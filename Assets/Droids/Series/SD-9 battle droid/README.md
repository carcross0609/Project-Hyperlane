# SD-9 battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SD-9 battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-9_battle_droid)

The SD-9 battle droid was a battle droid that was part of Balmorran Arms's SD droid series. The SD-9 was humanoid like the earlier series, though it dwarfed even a Human.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SD-9 battle droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Line:** SD-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Cost:** 51,405 credits
- **Height:** 3.8 meters
- **Armament:**
  - 1 heavy repeating blaster
  - 1 explosive plasma cannon
- **Equipment:**
  - Ionization shielding
  - Laser-reflective armor
  - Pinpoint targeting sensors
- **Affiliation:**
  - Dark Empire
  - New Republic
