# IR-45 Eradicator Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IR-45 Eradicator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-45_Eradicator_Droid)

The IR-45 Eradicator Droid was a model of three-legged battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. The Shroud's lieutenant Stangg employed IR-45 droids among the forces that defended the Shroud's base on the planet Quesh.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IR-45 Eradicator Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Brown
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
