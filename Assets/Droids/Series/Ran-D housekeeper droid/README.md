# Ran-D housekeeper droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ran-D housekeeper droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ran-D_housekeeper_droid)

Ran-D housekeeper droids were hovering droids that maintained living spaces. Mama the Hutt kept at least two Ran-D housekeeper droids at her household on Nal Hutta. She had the droids feed her at dinner time. This type of droid also appeared in a cantina owned by an Ithorian bartender in the Bilbousa Bazaar on Nal Hutta.
