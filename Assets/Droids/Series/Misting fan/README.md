# Misting fan

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Misting fan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Misting_fan)

Misting fan was a model of small and mass-produced portable fan droids manufactured by the Resistance during their operations on Batuu. They were sold to the planet's locals at the Resistance Supply as a way to show support for the Resistance. Their designs were made to be reminiscent of moisture vaporators as they could also provide water with their cool breeze.
