# ASP-2

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ASP-2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ASP-2)

The ASP-2 was a model of ASP-series labor droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton that was less agile than the ASP-7 variant. Tayshin Maxa, a member of Enfys Nest's Cloud-Riders, wore a repurposed ASP-2 faceplate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ASP-2
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** ASP-series labor droid
- **Model:** ASP-2

- **Height:** more than 1.5 meters
