# MSE series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MSE series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MSE_series)

The MSE series, also known as the MSE-series Maintenance Droid and MSE-series, was a series of repair droid produced by the Rebaxan Columni company. Models included the MSE-4, MSE-5, and MSE-6 series repair droid. The MSE series was based on the chak-chak animal. However, some form of mouse droid had existed as far back as 229 BBY.
