# M2-AUX Foreman

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M2-AUX Foreman](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M2-AUX_Foreman)

The M2-AUX Foreman was a type of supervisor droid used by the Selkath species on the planet Manaan during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. At least two Foremans were employed at Genetics Laboratory G-1, a genetic research facility.

- **Name:** M2-AUX Foreman
