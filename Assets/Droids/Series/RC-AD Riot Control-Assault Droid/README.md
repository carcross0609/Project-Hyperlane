# RC-AD Riot Control/Assault Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RC-AD Riot Control/Assault Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RC-AD_Riot_Control/Assault_Droid)

The RC-AD was a model of repulsorlift-equipped riot control droids designed to operate as heavy security guards and was one of the more potent assets utilized by the Republic Correctional Authority. As such, it saw deployment in prisons such as the notorious asteroid prison on Oovo IV, where its capabilities to operate in the absence of gravity and atmosphere proved to be an asset to patrol beyond the boundaries of the prison walls.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RC-AD Riot Control/Assault Droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Armament:** Twin pulse cannons
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Republic Correctional Authority
