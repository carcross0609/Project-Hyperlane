# Mark Two seeker

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark Two seeker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_Two_seeker)

The Mark Two seeker was a seeker-type probe droid small enough to fit in a humanoid hand and travel through the life support ducts of an Imperial-class Star Destroyer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark Two seeker
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Degree:** Class two droid
