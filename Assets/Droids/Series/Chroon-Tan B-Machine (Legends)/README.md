# Chroon-Tan B-Machine

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chroon-Tan B-Machine/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chroon-Tan_B-Machine/Legends)

The Chroon-Tan B-Machine, also known as the EW-3 midwife droid, was a specialized medical droid capable of delivering and caring for newborn babies used in the Polis Massan medical facility. A midwife droid delivered the Skywalker twins on Polis Massa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Chroon-Tan B-Machine
- **Homeworld:** Polis Massa
- **Class:** Midwife droid
- **Degree:** Class one droid
- **Cost:** 5,870 credits
- **Height:** 1.16 meters
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Sensor:** Blue, green, white, and orange
- **Equipment:**
  - Thermal cushion
  - Paddle appendages
  - Warming cushion
- **Affiliation:** Kallidahin
