# Astronavigation droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Astronavigation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Astronavigation_droid)

The astronavigation droid was a model of droid produced by Golden Nyss Shipyards. NT 600 was the astronavigation droid of the Inferno, the starship of the smuggler Vilmarh Grahrk.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Astronavigation droid
- **Manufacturer:** Golden Nyss Shipyards
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Silver
