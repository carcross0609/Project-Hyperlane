# DD-13 medical assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DD-13 medical assistant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DD-13_medical_assistant_droid)

The DD-13 medical assistant droid, also known as the DD-13 tripedal medical droid or simply the DD-13 medical droid, was a model of medical droid designed to install cybernetic implants. Its tri-legged design granted it stability. A DD-13 worked at the Grand Republic Medical Facility, where it assisted in fitting the grievously injured Darth Vader with his life support armor.
