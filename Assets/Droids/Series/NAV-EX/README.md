# NAV-EX

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [NAV-EX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NAV-EX)

The NAV-EX was a model of battle droid with red plating, black panels, and a black photoreceptor. It was a type of monster droid, combining the head of an R-series droid, a product of Industrial Automaton, with the body of a viper probe droid, manufactured by Arakyd Industries. The Jawa-run Sandcrawler Shop near the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine sold blueprints for NAV-EXs. The default design was sold at 36,000 credits, a golden design at 144,000 credits, a diamond-colored version at 288,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design at 576,000 credits.
