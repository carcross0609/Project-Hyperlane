# ID-75 Heavy Assault Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ID-75 Heavy Assault Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ID-75_Heavy_Assault_Droid)

ID-75 Heavy Assault Droid was a droid model in use during the Galactic War. White Maw pirate captain Ayor-v9 used several ID-75 Heavy Assault Droids for protection of his base in the tunnels of Hoth and considered them to be "his family".

## Specifications

- **Name:** ID-75 Heavy Assault Droid
- **Model:** Surveyor Droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Yellow
- **Affiliation:** Ayor-v9
