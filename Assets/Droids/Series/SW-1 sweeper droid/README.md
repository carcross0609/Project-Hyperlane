# SW-1 sweeper droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SW-1 sweeper droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SW-1_sweeper_droid)

The SW-1 was a Cleaning droid used in the Aakaash system during the High Republic Era. By 220 BBY it was considered outdated and of low resale value. An individual in Fire accused one such droid of "eating" and damaging his shoes after wandering into his domicile.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SW-1 sweeper droid
- **Model:** SW-1
- **Affiliation:** Land & Sky Corporation
