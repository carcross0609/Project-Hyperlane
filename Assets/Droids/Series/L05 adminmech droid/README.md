# L05 adminmech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L05 adminmech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L05_adminmech_droid)

The L05 adminmech droid was a model of adminmech droid. One L05 adminmech droid, C00-L05C, was forced to work in the spice mines of Kessel for the Pyke Syndicate. C00-L05C, along with the other droids in the Kessel control room, were freed from their restraining bolts after L3-37 started a slave uprising in the mines.
