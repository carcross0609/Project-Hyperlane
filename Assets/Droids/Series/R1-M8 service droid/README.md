# R1-M8 service droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R1-M8 service droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R1-M8_service_droid)

The R1-M8 service droid was a model of service droid manufactured by the time of the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. By around 3643 BBY, several R1-M8 units malfunctioned and attacked their owners.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R1-M8 service droid
- **Manufacturer:** A company
- **Class:** Service droid
- **Degree:** Class three droid
