# R-4 recon droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-4 recon droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-4_recon_droid)

The R-4 recon droid was a reconnaissance droid manufactured by Arakyd Industries. It was used mostly during the Clone Wars and the Galactic Civil War by Rebel Marksmen and scout troopers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R-4 recon droid
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Black
- **Armament:** 1 Blaster
- **Equipment:**
  - Repulsorlift
  - Orbital strike beacon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Galactic Empire
