# Jedi droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jedi droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_droid)

A Jedi droid was a type of droid programmed with the capabilities of a Jedi. J-3DI was a Jedi droid who competed in the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition.
