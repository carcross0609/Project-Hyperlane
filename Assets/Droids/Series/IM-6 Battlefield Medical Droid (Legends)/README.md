# IM-6 Battlefield Medical Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IM-6 Battlefield Medical Droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IM-6_Battlefield_Medical_Droid/Legends)

The IM-6 Battlefield Medical Droid was a model of IM-series medical droid manufactured by Cybot Galactica. Widely employed, it was used by the Grand Army of the Republic and was perhaps the most versatile and advanced medical droid of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IM-6 Battlefield Medical Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
- **Line:** IM-series
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Degree:** Class one droid
- **Cost:** 11,800 credits (licensed availability)
- **Gender:** Feminine programming
- **Sensor:** Violet and green
- **Plating:** Silver and gold
- **Equipment:**
  - 2 hands
  - Heuristic processor
  - Hovering locomotion
  - Improved sensor package
  - Internal comlink
  - Medpac reservoir
  - Surgery kit
  - Vocabulator
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Grand Army of the Republic
