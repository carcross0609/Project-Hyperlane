# T-26G Torture Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-26G Torture Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-26G_Torture_Droid)

T-26G Torture Droid was an interrogation droid model used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Galactic War against the Galactic Republic. In 3640 BBY it was used by the Imperial forces during the Battle of Corellia.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T-26G Torture Droid
- **Model:** Interrogator droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Grey
