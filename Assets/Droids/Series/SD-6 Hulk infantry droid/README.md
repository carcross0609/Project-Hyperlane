# SD-6 Hulk infantry droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SD-6 Hulk infantry droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SD-6_Hulk_infantry_droid)

The SD-6 "Hulk" infantry droid was a predecessor to the Balmorran Arms SD-9 and SD-10 battle droid models, deployed during the last centuries of the Old Republic and the early years of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SD-6 Hulk infantry droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Line:** SD-Series
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Width:** c. 3.5 m
- **Height:** 3.5 m
- **Sensor:** Red or blue
- **Armament:**
  - 2 E-Web Blaster Cannons
  - 2 Merr-Sonn Ion gun rifles
  - 2 Grappling arms
- **Equipment:**
  - Starship-grade duranium armor
  - Telescopic-vision sensors
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  - Independent
