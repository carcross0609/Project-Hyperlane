# GL vendor droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GL vendor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GL_vendor_droid)

The GL vendor droid was a model of vendor droid with a bipedal black-plated body and a flat-topped head with red sensors. The Crimson Dawn criminal group owned a GL vendor droid designated GL-0T15 that served as the primiray turbolift operator on board the crime lord Dryden Vos's yacht, the First Light. It was present on the yacht's viewing platform amongst various party guests in 10 BBY, when the First Light traveled to the planet Vandor. GL-0T15 was equipped with a bank of weapon scanners including close-range sensor modules in its head that could detect chemical and energy weaponry.
