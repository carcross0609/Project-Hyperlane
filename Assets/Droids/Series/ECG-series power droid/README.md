# ECG-series power droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ECG-series power droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ECG-series_power_droid)

The ECG-series power droid was a model of power droid with a wide cylindrical body atop two short legs. The pirate Phee Genoa owned a modified ECG-series power droid named MEL-221 who was destroyed and rebuilt sometime between 19 BBY and 18 BBY.
