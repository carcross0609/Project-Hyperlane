# IT-3 Interrogator

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IT-3 Interrogator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IT-3_Interrogator)

IT-3 Interrogators were state-of-the-art interrogation droids used by the Galactic Empire. They were stationed at the headquarters of the Imperial Security Bureau on Coruscant. They succeeded the IT-O Interrogator in the line.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IT-3 Interrogator
- **Class:** Interrogation droid
- **Affiliation:**
