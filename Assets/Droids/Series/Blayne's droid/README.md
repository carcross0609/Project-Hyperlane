# Blayne's droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blayne's droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blayne%27s_droid)

Blayne's droids were battle droids designed by Doctor Blayne during the Clone Wars. The droid model consisted of a Human host covered by a mind-controlling liquid metal. The wearer of the armor served as a living bio-battery for the droid body.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blayne's droid
- **Creator:** Doctor Blayne
- **Homeworld:** Blayne's asteroid base
- **Plating:** Blue
- **Armament:** Wrist blaster
- **Equipment:** Blayne's liquid metal
- **Affiliation:** Doctor Blayne
