# I2F series manufacturing droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I2F series manufacturing droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I2F_series_manufacturing_droid)

The I2F series manufacturing droid was a model of industrial droid. It was used by the Galactic Empire, commonly assigned to staff the droid-industrial complex of Imperial garrisons.
