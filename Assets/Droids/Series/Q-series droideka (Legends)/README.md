# Q-series droideka

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Q-series droideka/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q-series_droideka/Legends)

The Q-series droideka was a model of destroyer droid employed by the Confederacy of Independent Systems. It was used mainly during the Clone Wars, and was produced by the Phlac-Arphocc Automata Industries. It served on various capital ships, such as the Invisible Hand. It also proved to be a lethal asset on the battlefield with its personal energy shield, but became vulnerable, like other droideka models, if rolling along on the battlefront, as their shield could not be activated during this time.
