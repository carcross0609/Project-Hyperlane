# OT-9 Sentinel Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-9 Sentinel Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-9_Sentinel_Droid)

The OT-9 Sentinel Droid was a four-legged security droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War. OT-9 Sentinel Droids were among the droids deployed by the reconstituted Sith Empire in defense of the Imperial Command Compound in the Markaran Plains.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-9 Sentinel Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Armament:** Arm-mounted blaster cannons
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
