# 631 model B1 battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [631 model B1 battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/631_model_B1_battle_droid)

The 631 model B1 battle droid was a model of B1-series battle droid used by the Separatist Droid Army during the Clone Wars. They were a more cheaply made droid than the standard B1. Like other B1s, they were created by the Geonosians, had masculine programming with black photoreceptors, bone-white plating, a comlink booster pack, and stood 1.93 meters in height. However, 631s were more clumsy and intellectually underpowered compared to other B1s, who regarded them as inferior.
