# SP-15 Surveillance Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SP-15 Surveillance Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SP-15_Surveillance_Droid)

The SP-15 Surveillance Droid was a model of surveillance probe droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. SP-15 Surveillance Droids were among the forces stationed at the ruptured vault under the command of the Shroud's lieutenant Peej.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SP-15 Surveillance Droid
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Green
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
