# Mark I training droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark I training droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_I_training_droid)

Mark I training droids were droids used for training by elements of the Jedi Order during the time of the Cold War, a proxy-conflict between the Galactic Republic and Sith Empire. Around 3643 BBY, the Jedi instructor Liam Dentiri used them to test the combat abilities of Padawans on the planet Tython.
