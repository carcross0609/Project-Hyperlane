# MSE-1T

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MSE-1T](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MSE-1T)

MSE-1T is one of a pair of a certain type of MSE-series droid that lived in Star Tours' Tomorrowland Starport. Both droids rode around a track and were said to consume spare metal parts. The second droid was in disrepair at one point.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MSE-1T
- **Line:** MSE-series
- **Class:** General purpose droid
- **Affiliation:** Star Tours
