# LB-series bulk-loading droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LB-series bulk-loading droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LB-series_bulk-loading_droid)

The T1-series bulk-loading droid, later known as the LB-series, was a type of bulk-loading droid used during the time of the Mandalorian Wars though it was originally produced long before the Sith War. The initials "LB" stand for "loader, bulk".

## Specifications

- **Name:** LB-series bulk-loading droid
- **Manufacturer:** Kellenech Technologies
- **Class:** Bulk-loading droid
- **Cost:** 6,000 credits
- **Sensor:** Yellow
