# PLNK-series power droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PLNK-series power droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PLNK-series_power_droid)

The PLNK-series power droid, nicknamed the plunk droid for its distinctive sound, was a heavy-duty power droid that held tibanna gas. It was manufactured by Industrial Automaton during the Clone Wars, where they saw widespread use in the Republic Navy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** PLNK-series power droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Class:** Power droid
- **Degree:** Class two
- **Height:** 1.37 meters
- **Plating:** Dark green, gray, brown, and gold
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Galactic Republic Navy
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Jabba's criminal empire
