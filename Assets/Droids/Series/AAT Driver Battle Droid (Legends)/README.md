# AAT Driver Battle Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AAT Driver Battle Droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AAT_Driver_Battle_Droid/Legends)

The AAT Driver Battle Droid was a specialized class of B1-Series battle droid designed for operating the Armored Assault Tanks (AAT) of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The droids were colored with dark green facial and torso markings.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AAT Driver Battle Droid
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Height:** 1.91 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Armament:** E-5 blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
