# OT-12 Hazmat Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-12 Hazmat Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-12_Hazmat_Droid)

The OT-12 Hazmat Droid was a large, six-legged battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. A number of OT-12 Hazmat Droids were deployed to the toxic lakes of Camp Traken-4.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-12 Hazmat Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Blaster cannon
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
