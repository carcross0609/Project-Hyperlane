# B-series battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B-series battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-series_battle_droid)

The B-series battle droid was a mass-produced line of battle droids prominently utilized by the Trade Federation, its allies, and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, most notably during the Clone Wars.
