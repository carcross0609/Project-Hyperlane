# DK-27 Guardian Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DK-27 Guardian Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DK-27_Guardian_Droid)

The DK-27 Guardian Droid was a model of battle droid that was deployed as security in Kaas City, the capital city of the planet Dromund Kaas and the reconstituted Sith Empire.
