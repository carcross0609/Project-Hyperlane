# FX-10 medical assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FX-10 medical assistant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FX-10_medical_assistant_droid)

The FX-10 medical assistant droid was a model of medical droid that was manufactured by Medtech Industries, as part of the FX-series of medical assistant droids. It was the successor of the FX-9 model. FX-10s were used in the Aurora Medical Facility on Obroa-skai.
