# Blaredroid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blaredroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blaredroid)

Blaredroids were a model of droid invented by the Nihil Tempest Runner Zeetar. Designed to intimidate and disorient enemies, the droids were outfitted with speakers which played a mixture of white noise and wreckpunk music. The droids saw their debut in the Nihil's attack on Valo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blaredroid
- **Creator:** Zeetar
- **Equipment:** Speakers
- **Affiliation:** Nihil
