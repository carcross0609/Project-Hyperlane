# Scorpenek annihilator droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scorpenek annihilator droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scorpenek_annihilator_droid)

The Scorpenek annihilator droid was a model of class four droid that saw utilization during the time of the New Republic. In 9 ABY, a pair of Scorpenek droids were utilized by the Pyke Syndicate in their efforts to claim control over the planet Tatooine from Daimyo Boba Fett and his gotra. However, during an engagement within the city of Mos Espa, the droids were destroyed by Fett and his allies.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scorpenek annihilator droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Armament:** Dual laser cannons (2)
- **Equipment:** Deflector shield generator
- **Affiliation:** Pyke Syndicate
