# Protodeka

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Protodeka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Protodeka)

The Protodeka was a prototype droid mobile ground siege weapon designed by a Colicoid engineer and manufactured by Geonosians on the planet Raxus Prime that was used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Dark Reaper crisis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Protodeka
- **Manufacturer:** Geonosians
- **Designer:** A Colicoid engineer
- **Homeworld:** Raxus Prime
- **Model:** Protodeka prototype
- **Class:** Mobile ground siege weapon
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:**
  - Claw
  - Homing concussion missiles
  - Laser cannons
  - Seismic wave emitter
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
