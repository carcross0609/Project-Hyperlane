# Unidentified serving droid (Black Sun)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified serving droid (Black Sun)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_serving_droid_%28Black_Sun%29)

A serving droid in the employ of Dark Prince Xizor and the Black Sun crime syndicate once tended to a meeting between Xizor and the Hutt crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure within Xizor's Palace in 3.5 ABY. The serving droid presented Jabba with a tray of insectoids and vile liquids that Hutts were known to favor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified serving droid
- **Class:** Service droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:** Black Sun
