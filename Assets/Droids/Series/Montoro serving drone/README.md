# Montoro serving drone

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Montoro serving drone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Montoro_serving_drone)

The Montoro serving drone was a service droid in use in Mos Espa, Tatooine as of 32 BBY. The body of the drone was yellow, and the droid rode on two wheels.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Montoro serving drone
- **Homeworld:** Presumably Tatooine
- **Equipment:** Rolling wheels for legs
- **Affiliation:** Mos Espa settlers
