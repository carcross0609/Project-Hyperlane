# B2-RP battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B2-RP battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2-RP_battle_droid)

The B2-RP battle droid, also known as the B2-RP super battle droid, rocket droid or jetpack droid, was a B2-series super battle droid model used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Engineered by the Techno Union, they were equipped with jetpacks which incorporated compact repulsorlifts, but were otherwise were the same as standard B2s. They were created as the Separatists response to clone jetpack troopers.
