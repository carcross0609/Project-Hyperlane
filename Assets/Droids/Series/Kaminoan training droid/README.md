# Kaminoan training droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaminoan training droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaminoan_training_droid)

The Kaminoan training droid was a model of training droid in use in the Tipoca City Training Facility on Kamino by 19 BBY. They were roughly human-sized and floated in the air using twin adjustable repulsorlift stabilizers. For combat simulations, the droids were equipped with two short retractable stun blasters, a pincer and a blue photoreceptor which could track and analyze clone trooper movements. They were fairly easy to disable with merely two training blaster bolts being enough to short-circuit them.
