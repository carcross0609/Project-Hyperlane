# JR-8 series maintenance droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [JR-8 series maintenance droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/JR-8_series_maintenance_droid)

JR-8 series maintenance droids were all-purpose janitor units constructed to clean, refurbish, and perform light repairs. They were equipped with an internal cleaning fluid reservoir and integrated spray nozzle, and they were known to spray detergent at individuals who interfered with their tasks.

## Specifications

- **Name:** JR-8 series maintenance droid
- **Manufacturer:** Publictechnic
- **Class:** Cleaning droid
- **Equipment:**
  - Low-light photoreceptors
  - 2 tool mounts
  - Rust inhibitor
  - internal cleaning fluid reservoir with integrated spray nozzle
