# C2-R4 Multipurpose Unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C2-R4 Multipurpose Unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C2-R4_Multipurpose_Unit)

The C2-R4 Multipurpose Unit was a droid series created and manufactured by a colony of Squibs. Despite the Squib's enthusiasm to release their creation onto the market, the C2-R4 was a disaster since they had no defined function or role, and were essentially a collection of household appliances attached to an R-series body shell.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C2-R4 Multipurpose Unit
- **Creator:** Squibs
- **Equipment:**
  - Bang-corn air popper
  - Chemical Composter
  - Enzymatic Converter
  - Food processor
  - Toaster oven
  - Vocabulator
