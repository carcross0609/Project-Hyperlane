# E-5 battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-5 battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-5_battle_droid)

The E-5 battle droid was a model of battle droid manufactured by Baktoid Combat Automata. Based on the E4 baron droid, it was an interim successor to the B1-series battle droid and was quickly made obsolete by the B2 super battle droid.
