# CLE-004 window cleaning droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CLE-004 window cleaning droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CLE-004_window_cleaning_droid)

CLE-004 window cleaning droids were a model of floating cleaning droid designed to clean the windows of buildings. They were used on the planet Coruscant to clean the windows of buildings many levels above the planet's surface. The droids would have their designations printed on their bodies, as seen on the unit Buffy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CLE-004 window cleaning droid
- **Manufacturer:** Publitechnic
- **Degree:** Class 5
- **Length:** 0.3 meters
