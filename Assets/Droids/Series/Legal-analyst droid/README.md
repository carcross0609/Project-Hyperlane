# Legal-analyst droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Legal-analyst droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Legal-analyst_droid)

Legal-analyst droids, also known as a litigation droid, were a model of analysis droid in service to the Galactic Empire during its reign. As a part of Imperial Navy law, one such droid, HM-12, was to defend Captain Iden Versio in a court-martial after she was accused of sedition.

- **Name:** Legal-analyst droid
- **Class:** Analysis droid
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
