# Slave Droid SD-9

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Slave Droid SD-9](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slave_Droid_SD-9)

The Slave Droid SD-9 was a model of humanoid-shaped labor droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. They carried blaster pistols, and were used in the factory during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Slave Droid SD-9
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Labor droid
- **Plating:** Tan
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
