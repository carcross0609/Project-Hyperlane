# LE-series droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LE-series droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LE-series_droid)

LE-series droids were a line of astromech droids used in the galaxy. Clink, was a LE-series droid who was owned by a Gran trader on Pantora. Along with the astromech droids 225 and DK-3, Clink was supervised by the protocol droid CG-67. LE-12, a LE-series droid who resided on the Colossus, was an older model in the line. LE-series droids had two clamps, two springy legs, and a head capable of rotating 360 degrees.
