# SR-series scout/observation droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SR-series scout/observation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SR-series_scout/observation_droid)

SR-series scout/observation droids were a droid model produced by TransTech Droidworks, based on their civilian S-series survey droid. SR-series droids were designed to seek out distant targets, relaying the information back to their base. They were equipped with an optical camouflage system that allowed them to blend in with their surroundings.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SR-series scout/observation droid
- **Manufacturer:** TransTech Droidworks
- **Cost:** 13,000 credits
- **Equipment:**
  - Long-range targeting systems
  - Optical camouflage system
  - Retractable manipulators
