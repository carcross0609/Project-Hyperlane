# B-1 series mercenary sentry droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B-1 series mercenary sentry droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B-1_series_mercenary_sentry_droid)

B-1 series mercenary sentry droids were a variant of B1-series battle droid that existed in the galaxy by the early New Republic Era. A number of these B1 units were members of the Droid Gotra. After the Gotra was hired to provide protection for the Twins, a number of these mercenary droids patrolled the Twins' palace with their gotra comrades. During the Battle of Nal Hutta, several ran past a series of hangar blast doors as they opened and HH-87 Starhopper starfighters launched from them, and several were destroyed when one of the fighters later crashed and exploded.
