# Fuel droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fuel droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fuel_droid)

Fuel droids, or refueling droids, were a type of droids used to refuel starship in place of an organic operator, to reduce the risk of explosion while manipulating explosive liquid metal fuel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fuel droid
- **Degree:** Class five droid
- **Height:** From building-sized to small
