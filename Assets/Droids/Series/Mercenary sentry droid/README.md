# Mercenary sentry droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mercenary sentry droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mercenary_sentry_droid)

Mercenary sentry droids were a type of droid that existed in the galaxy by the early New Republic Era. A number of the mercenary droids ran past a series of hangar blast doors as they opened and HH-87 Starhopper starfighters launched from them, and several were destroyed when one of the fighters later crashed and exploded.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mercenary sentry droid
- **Plating:** Rusted gray, blue, and black.
- **Equipment:**
  - Blaster
  - Cape
- **Affiliation:**
  - Droid Gotra
  - The Twins
