# Spaceport Luggage Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spaceport Luggage Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spaceport_Luggage_Droid)

The Spaceport Luggage Droid was a line of RX-series pilot droid that worked for Star Liner Travel during the New Republic Era. They were often used on commercial flights to carry luggage to the starliners and scan people to see if they were carrying weapons, since no weapons were permitted aboard a flight.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spaceport Luggage Droid
- **Model:** RX-series pilot droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:**
  - Blue and white
  - Yellow and white
- **Affiliation:** Star Liner Travel
