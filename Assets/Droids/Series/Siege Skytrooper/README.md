# Siege Skytrooper

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Siege Skytrooper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Siege_Skytrooper)

Siege Skytroopers were a skytrooper variant used by the Eternal Empire of Zakuul. Several times larger than a regular Human-sized version skytrooper, the Siege Skytroopers was powerful enough to go face-to-face with a walker. During the Assault on Odessen in 3630 BBY, a Siege Skytrooper was encountered by the Alliance Commander.
