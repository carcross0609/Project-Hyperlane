# MD-5 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-5 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-5_medical_droid)

The MD-5 medical droid, also called the MD-5 general practitioner medical droid, was a model of medical droid and a part of the MD-series that was manufactured by Industrial Automaton. It was slightly humanoid in appearance. They were used as multipurpose medical droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MD-5 medical droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Line:** MD-series medical specialist droid
- **Class:** Medical droid
- **Height:** 1.5 meters (approximately)
- **Sensor:** White (Normally)
- **Equipment:** Vocabulator
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  - Galactic Federation of Free Alliances
  - House Calipsa
