# Trade Federation maintenance droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trade Federation maintenance droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trade_Federation_maintenance_droid)

Trade Federation maintenance droids were bulky, primitive, treaded droids designed to remove dust and debris from the ventilation shafts of Trade Federation Lucrehulk-class battleships. Their electric shocks eradicated most unwanted material with enough power to injure Humans and presumably most other carbon-based organisms. They were also motion-sensitive, so that they zapped anything that moved.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Trade Federation maintenance droid
- **Manufacturer:** Trade Federation
- **Class:** Maintenance droid
- **Armament:** Electrostatic weapon
- **Affiliation:** Trade Federation
