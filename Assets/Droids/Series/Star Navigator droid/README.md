# Star Navigator droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Star Navigator droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Navigator_droid)

Star Navigator droids were droids that served on the Eye of Sion as part of Morgan Elsbeth's forces. Their number included a red sensored droid and a white sensored droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Star Navigator droid
- **Class:** Pilot droid
- **Sensor:**
  - Red
  - White
- **Plating:** Gold
- **Affiliation:**
  - Morgan Elsbeth's forces
  - Thrawn's forces
