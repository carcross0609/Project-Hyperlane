# Stone guardian

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stone guardian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stone_guardian)

The stone guardians, also known as the Zardossa droids, the Zardossa Stix statues, and the clay warriors of Zardossa Stix, were a model of droid which were used on the moon Zardossa Stix outside of the Temple of Malmourral. Despite their appearance, they were not made of stone but were instead sculpted and painted to appear as decorative statues or stone columns. They were also intended to be representations of an ancient fallen army, and according to Zardossan legend, were the only reason the warriors remained dead, and they would return to life if the statues were ever destroyed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stone guardian
- **Homeworld:** Zardossa Stix
- **Armament:** Heavy energy projector
- **Equipment:** Integrated SecureTech "Night-sight" passive detector suite
- **Affiliation:** Frangawl Cult
