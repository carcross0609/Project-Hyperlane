# FO-4 warden droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FO-4 warden droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FO-4_warden_droid)

The FO-4 warden droid was a model of warden droid designed by Ulban Arms and active during the High Republic Era, being utilized by the Corporate Sector Authority and Galactic Republic. one warden droid was stationed aboard the Palwick prison ship.
