# CP-9 Nanoshield Probe

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CP-9 Nanoshield Probe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CP-9_Nanoshield_Probe)

The CP-9 Nanoshield Probe was a model of small probe droid used by the Chiss spy Maki'voro, a lieutenant of the spymaster known as the Shroud during the Galactic War. One of a number of probe models that Maki'voro used, the CP-9 probe was designed to shield Maki'voro and rapidly heal him.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CP-9 Nanoshield Probe
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
