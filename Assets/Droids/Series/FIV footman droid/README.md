# FIV footman droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FIV footman droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FIV_footman_droid)

The FIV footman droid was a droid model manufactured by Tac-Spec. It had humanoid proportions with a reinforced chassis at the chest, a pelvic servomotor and precision-articulated limbs. It had blue plating and white sensors. A footman droid served Bo-Katan Kryze at Kryze Castle on the planet Kalevala.
