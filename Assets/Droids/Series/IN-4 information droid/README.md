# IN-4 information droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IN-4 information droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IN-4_information_droid)

The Veril Line Systems IN-4 information droid, also known as the IN-series information droid, was one of the most recognized droids to space travelers during the Rebellion era.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IN-4 information droid
- **Manufacturer:** Veril Line Systems
- **Class:** Information droid
- **Degree:** Class one droid
- **Cost:** 2,500 credits
- **Height:** 1.3 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Extendible I/O computer jack
  - Treadwell locomotion unit
  - Video sensor
  - Vocabulator speech/sound systeem
