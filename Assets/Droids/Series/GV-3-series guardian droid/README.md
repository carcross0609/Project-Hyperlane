# GV/3-series guardian droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GV/3-series guardian droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GV/3-series_guardian_droid)

GV/3-series guardian droids, also known as Guardian-class Droids and commonly referred to as Guardian droids, were droids that resembled canines. They were made by Cybot Galactica to act as security.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GV/3-series guardian droid
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
- **Class:** Guardian droid
- **Degree:** Fourth degree
- **Cost:**
  - 4,000 credit (new)
  - 2,00 credits (old)
- **Height:** 1.5 meters
- **Armament:** Retractable blaster
- **Equipment:**
  - Grasper jaw with retractable blade incisors
  - Two visual and auditory sensors (Human range)
