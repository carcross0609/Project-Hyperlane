# I-8 Black Widow

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I-8 Black Widow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I-8_Black_Widow)

The I-8 Black Widow was a form of modified BT-16 perimeter droid used during the Galactic Civil War. Droids of this kind was unarmed as it primarily filled a reconnaissance role. I-8 Black Widow droids were used by the Rak'qua on Station Gamma above Ord Mantell following the Battle of Yavin. In 1 ABY, a number of spacers who explored Station Gamma fought and destroyed multiple I-8 Black Widow droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** I-8 Black Widow
- **Line:** BT-16 perimeter droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Plating:** Black
- **Armament:** None
- **Affiliation:** Rak'qua
