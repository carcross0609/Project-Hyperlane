# GC-8

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GC-8](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GC-8)

The GC-8 was a model of droid with relatively humanoid features. Cator was a GC-8 droid who served as a librarian and guardian-protector within the Jedi Temple Archives, protecting the Jedi Order's most sensitive information until he was destroyed by Darth Vader in 19 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GC-8
- **Manufacturer:** ARO
- **Plating:** Gray (with gold markings)
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
