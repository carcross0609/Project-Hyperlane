# SP Eighty cleaner droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SP Eighty cleaner droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SP_Eighty_cleaner_droid)

SP Eighty cleaner droids, also called SP-80 cleaners, were a model of droids from the Single-Purpose series that were programmed with the single purpose of cleaning. When presented with something they considered a mess, they would attempt to clean it even if their limitations rendered them unable to do so. Though limited, the protocol droid C-3PO considered SP-80s and others of their series the core of droid operations.

## Specifications

- **Line:** Single-Purpose series
- **Plating:** Bronze
