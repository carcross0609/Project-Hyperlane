# Mark M Guard Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark M Guard Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_M_Guard_Droid)

The Mark M Guard Droid was a model of humanoid-shaped battle droid that was used by the forces of the Specialists, a mercenary unit. Mark M Guard Droids were deployed under the control of QO-99 to the planet Makeb during the Conquest of Makeb.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark M Guard Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
