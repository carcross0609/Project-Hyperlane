# BAF-1000 Assault Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-1000 Assault Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-1000_Assault_Droid)

The BAF-1000 Assault Droid was a bipedal battle droid produced by Balmorran Arms on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. BAF-1000 Assault Droids were armed with blaster rifles, and were among the forces used by the Galactic Republic to defend the Balmorran Arms Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-1000 Assault Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
