# Ii77

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ii77](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ii77)

The ii77 was one of many variants to the standard 3PO-series protocol droid that was developed and manufactured by Cybot Galactica. The ii77 droids were considered boutique droids, aimed at a specific market.

- **Name:** ii77
