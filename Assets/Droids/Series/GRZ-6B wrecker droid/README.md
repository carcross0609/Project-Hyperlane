# GRZ-6B wrecker droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GRZ-6B wrecker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GRZ-6B_wrecker_droid)

The GRZ-6B wrecker droid, also known as GRZ-6B demolition droid, was a wrecker droid developed by Serv-O-Droid, Inc.. Like the EVS Construction Droid, it was used for demolishing and recycling structures. However, it had a reputation of doing its job recklessly and creating large amounts of industrial waste to the like of rubble or slag.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GRZ-6B wrecker droid
- **Manufacturer:** Serv-O-Droid, Inc.
- **Homeworld:** Elshandruu Pica
- **Height:** 6 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Industrial-grade plasma torch
  - Laser cutter
  - Internal fusion furnace
