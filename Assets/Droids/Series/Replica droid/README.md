# Replica droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Replica droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Replica_droid)

Replica droids were droids layered with cloned skin to imitate humans. The feminine protocol droid named White had an exquisite outline similar to that of a replica droid's. Owned by Li Yu in the largely independent Aakaash system of the Oplovis sector in 200 BBY, White was observed by the human Jedi Sean to possess a simple coat of white paint that made her seem, unlike replica droids, disdainful of imitating humans.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Replica droid
- **Plating:** Cloned skin
- **Firstmade:** By 200 BBY
