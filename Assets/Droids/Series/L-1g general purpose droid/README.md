# L-1g general purpose droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L-1g general purpose droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L-1g_general_purpose_droid)

The L-1g general purpose droid, also known as the L-1 general purpose droid, L-1 general service droid, or L-1g worker droid, was a model of service droid commonly used for basic operational tasks. The droid was equipped with an AA-1 VerboBrain processor on par with that of a high-end protocol droid and versatile language processors. Due to its capacity to calculate complex scenarios, some referred to the model as the L-1 tactical droid. However, few bothered to use the L-1 unit to its full potential.
