# 5M-Sec Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [5M-Sec Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/5M-Sec_Droid)

The 5M-Sec Droid was a model of hulking, three-legged security droid used by the Galactic Empire. At the time of the Iron Blockade, one such droid guarded the site of a chromium mine near Wickridge, Burnin Konn, where the Jedi Khandra and Nuhj had once been killed by a member of the Imperial Inquisitorius.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 5M-Sec Droid
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Degree:** Class three droid
- **Equipment:** Deflector shield
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
