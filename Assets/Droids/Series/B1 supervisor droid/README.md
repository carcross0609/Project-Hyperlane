# B1 supervisor droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B1 supervisor droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1_supervisor_droid)

The B1 supervisor droid was a model of the B1-series battle droid created sometime after the end of the Invasion of Naboo. Around the time of the Clone Wars, the supervisor droid was used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems, and helped to bridge the gap between the B1-series' independent thinking. However, production of the model was later ceased as a result of its ability to confuse standard B1 units in battle, but they remained in Separatist service when the use of T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droids was unavailable.
