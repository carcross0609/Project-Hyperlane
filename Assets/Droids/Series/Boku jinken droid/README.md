# Boku jinken droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Boku jinken droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boku_jinken_droid)

Boku jinken droids were a type of droid that could be used as defense systems against opponents. During the lockdown of the planet Olcatz, the Anzellan Yakuza leader Que-Dama and his crew employed B1-Bokujin models as traps along Tkoku Bridge to defend Tkoku Island. When the Crusaders attempted to cross the bridge, the droids unleashed punches that knocked down some of the Crusaders.
