# Colossus droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Colossus droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Colossus_droid)

Colossus droid was a type of droid designed on Iokath after the species that created the world went extinct following the Iokath Civil War. The only remaining self-aware droid on Iokath ARIES used other droids as his slaves, weaponizing them into combat units regardless of their original purpose. The Colossus droid was developed as a massive war machine dozens of meters tall and capable of wiping out an entire army.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Colossus droid
- **Creator:** ARIES
- **Homeworld:** Iokath
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:** ARIES
