# DG-1B catering droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DG-1B catering droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DG-1B_catering_droid)

The DG-1B catering droid, also known as the baker droid, was a model of service droid, programmed to cater during social events. During the Clone Wars, Senator Padmé Amidala of the Galactic Republic made use of a baker droid to aid in the preparation of a jogan fruitcake for a gathering she would be holding in honor of Senator Aang. As the cake still lacked its jogan fruit garnish, Amidala dispatched her droids to purchase some. As the gathering began before their arrival with the jogans, the baker droid questioned as to what they were going to do.
