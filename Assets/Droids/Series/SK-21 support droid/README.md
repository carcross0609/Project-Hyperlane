# SK-21 support droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SK-21 support droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SK-21_support_droid)

SK-21 support droids were droids produced on the planet Mustafar by the rogue assassin droid HK-47 to support his droid army. Their design was based on that of HK-47. Several of them were destroyed in the crater of a volcano by a group of spacers that fought against HK-47 and his droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SK-21 support droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Affiliation:** HK-47
