# Mark IV recon droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark IV recon droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_IV_recon_droid)

The Mark IV recon droid was a reconnaissance droid manufactured by Arakyd Industries. It saw service during the Clone Wars with the Galactic Marines of the Galactic Republic and the Separatists' droid snipers and IG-100 MagnaGuards. During the Galactic Civil War, it was used by Imperial commandos and Wookiee warriors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark IV recon droid
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Height:** 0.75 meters
- **Sensor:** Red

  - Self-destruct mechanism

- **Affiliation:**

  - Galactic Empire
