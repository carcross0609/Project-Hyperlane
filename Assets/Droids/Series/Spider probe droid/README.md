# Spider probe droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spider probe droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_probe_droid)

The Spider probe droid, also known as First Order probe droid, was a model of probe droid used by the First Order around the time of the attack on Tuanul. One of these probes was on a moon in the Dassal system of the Unknown Regions when Resistance pilots Kazuda Xiono and Poe Dameron investigated the system. The droid could deploy a number of smaller remotes equipped with blasters if it came under attack. Another discovered the presence of the supertanker fuel depot Colossus at the planet Aeos Prime during the First Order-Resistance War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spider probe droid
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd-Harch Technologies
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Armament:** Probe remotes
