# S/D Decimator droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [S/D Decimator droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S/D_Decimator_droid)

The S/D Decimator droid, also identified as the Decimator Droid S/D Prototype and often simply called just the Decimator, Organic Decimator or decimator droid, was a droid manufactured by Baktoid Innovations and used by Skakoan scientists working with the Separatist Alliance. It used energy tentacles to search for organic matter. The droid had a small, circular frame, and a single red photoreceptor.
