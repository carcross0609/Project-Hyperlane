# Roving Eye observation droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Roving Eye observation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Roving_Eye_observation_droid)

The Roving Eye observation droid was a popular type of Holocam droid around 137 ABY – a recording device attached to a repulsor system. It was used by law enforcement agencies, corporations and even Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Krayt, who used the Roving Eye droids to monitor the training of his disciples in the Temple of the Sith.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Roving Eye observation droid
- **Class:** Cam droid
- **Degree:** Class two droid
- **Cost:** 3,200 credits
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire
