# Construction droid foreman

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Construction droid foreman](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Construction_droid_foreman)

The construction droid foreman was a droid model used c. 3951 BBY. It was more dexterous than the construction droid Mark I or construction droid Mark II, having five arms, and was apparently used to supervise the AI of the lesser droids. A typical construction team consisted of one foreman, one Mark II, and multiple Mark I droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Construction droid foreman
- **Equipment:** 5 arms
