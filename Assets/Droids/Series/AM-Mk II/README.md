# AM-Mk II

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AM-Mk II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AM-Mk_II)

The AM-Mk II was a model of construction droid utilized during the mid-Republic Era. During the time of the Galactic Civil War, the Veril Line Systems GT-series construction droid was the latest in a line of construction droids that dated back to the ancient AM-Mk II.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AM-Mk II
- **Class:** Construction droid
