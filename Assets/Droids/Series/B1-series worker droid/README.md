# B1-series worker droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B1-series worker droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1-series_worker_droid)

AccuTronics made a notable entry into the fifth-degree labor droid market with their B1 worker droid. While the CLL-8 binary load lifters were commonly employed in freight operations due to their substantial size, the B-1 worker droid served as a smaller counterpart to these massive machines.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B1-series worker droid
- **Manufacturer:** AccuTronics
- **Class:** Loader droid
- **Degree:** Class five droid
- **Height:** 1.5 meters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Radell Mining Corporation
