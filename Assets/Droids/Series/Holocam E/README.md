# Holocam E

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Holocam E](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holocam_E)

Holocam E-series cam droids were a series of small, friendly holocam droids, programmed with a female personality and seemingly at least basic level mechanical, technical, and scientific knowledge. Produced by Trang Robotics, Holocam E droids cheerfully dispensed advice and helpful hints as they became relevant.

## Specifications

- **Manufacturer:** Trang Robotics
