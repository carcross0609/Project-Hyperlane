# P-series droideka

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [P-series droideka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-series_droideka)

The P-series droideka was a series of droideka battle droid that was used by the Trade Federation Droid Army. During the Invasion of Naboo when the Trade Federation blockaded and conquered the planet, the P-series had been manufactured in the hundreds and participated in the invasion. Eventually by the time of the Clone Wars, the P-series droideka was too expensive for the level of production required, although some were still being made. The P-series was succeeded by the W-series droideka, but were both obsolete when the Q-series droideka was introduced late in the conflict.
