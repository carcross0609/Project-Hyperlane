# Constable droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Constable droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Constable_droid)

Constable droid was a type of law enforcement droid. The small constable droids of Plazir-15 were unarmed, befitting the planet's pacifistic society. Often working in groups, they were tasked with keeping the peace and moving people out of harm's way, such as when Din Djarin and Bo-Katan Kryze tangled with a droid rebel.
