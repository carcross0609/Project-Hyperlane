# Assembly Defender Droid AR-18

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Assembly Defender Droid AR-18](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assembly_Defender_Droid_AR-18)

The Assembly Defender Droid AR-18 was a model of security droid manufactured by the Okara Droid Company. A number of AR-18 units were deployed to protect the assembly line at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Assembly Defender Droid AR-18
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
