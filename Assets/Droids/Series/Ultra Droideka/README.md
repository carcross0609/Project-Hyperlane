# Ultra Droideka

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ultra Droideka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ultra_Droideka)

An Ultra Droideka was a larger variant of a droideka battle droid, used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. An oversized version of a regular droideka, it had considerably more powerful weapons, but required a tremendous amount of energy to sustain its shields. As a result, it often operated in the shieldless mode when there were other droids around to protect it.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ultra Droideka
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Cost:** 29,000 credits
- **Equipment:** Built-in shield projector
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
