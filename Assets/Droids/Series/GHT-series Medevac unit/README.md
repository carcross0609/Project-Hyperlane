# GHT-series Medevac unit

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GHT-series Medevac unit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GHT-series_Medevac_unit)

In an effort to capitalize on the post-Clone Wars era and Rebellion era wounded battlefield soldiers, TelBrinTel created the GHT-series Medevac unit, also known as the GHT medevac droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GHT-series Medevac unit
- **Manufacturer:** TelBrinTel Corporation
- **Degree:** Class one droid
- **Cost:** 5,200 credits
- **Length:** 1 meter
- **Equipment:**
  - Auditory receptor
  - Photoreceptors (2)(Human and macro-range)
  - Reinforced fine work appendages (2)
  - Reinforced hull
  - Repulsorlift
  - Retractable wheels (4)
