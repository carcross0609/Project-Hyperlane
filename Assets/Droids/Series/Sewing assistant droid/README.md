# Sewing assistant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sewing assistant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sewing_assistant_droid)

Sewing assistant droids were a type of droid with many arms, each specially designed for mending and sewing garments. The droids had a circular base and a tubular body covered in orange, black, and white plating where its arms were attached. Atop the body sat a domed head with a single black sensor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sewing assistant droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Orange, black, and white
