# QB-35

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [QB-35](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QB-35)

The QB-35 was a spherical droid model manufactured by the East Corner Heavy Industry during the High Republic Era. It was the export model of the WB-35 mechanic droid and was commonly found in the sectors around the Aakaash system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** QB-35
- **Manufacturer:** East Corner Heavy Industry
- **Line:** Worker Bee-type
- **Affiliation:** Land & Sky Corporation
