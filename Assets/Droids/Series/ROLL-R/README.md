# ROLL-R

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [ROLL-R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ROLL-R)

The ROLL-R was a model of worker droid with white plating, metallic gray panels, and a black photoreceptor. The model was a type of monster droid consisting of the head of a heavy loader droid—a JLD Mechanicals Consortium product on top of the four wheels of an ARG droid. Jawas sold blueprints for ROLL-Rs out of the Sandcrawler Shop near the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine, selling the default design for 62,000 credits, a golden design for 248,000 credits, a diamond-colored version for 496,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design for 992,000 credits.
