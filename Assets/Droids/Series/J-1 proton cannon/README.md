# J-1 proton cannon

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-1 proton cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-1_proton_cannon)

J-1 proton cannons were a form of droid anti-aircraft cannon. They were used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars against the Galactic Republic. The proton cannons were deployed by the Separatists on the planets Ryloth, Geonosis, and Anaxes.

## Specifications

- **Name:** J-1 proton cannon
- **Class:** Droid artillery
- **Equipment:** Scanners
- **Affiliation:**

  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
- **Firstmade:** c. 21 BBY
