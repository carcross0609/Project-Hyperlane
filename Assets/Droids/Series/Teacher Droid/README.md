# Teacher Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Teacher Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teacher_Droid)

A Teacher Droid was a type of service droid used on the planet At Attin that operated as teaching droids as well as home service droids. They were tall droid that move along on a large wheel. They had a short disc shaped head with long arms.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Teacher Droid
- **Homeworld:** At Attin
- **Class:** Teacher droid
