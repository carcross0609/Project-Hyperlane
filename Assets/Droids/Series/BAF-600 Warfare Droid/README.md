# BAF-600 Warfare Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-600 Warfare Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-600_Warfare_Droid)

The BAF-600 Warfare Droid was a bipedal battle droid produced by Balmorran Arms on the planet Balmorra during the Cold War. BAF-600 Warfare Droids were armed with blaster rifles, and were among the forces used by the Galactic Republic to defend the Balmorran Arms Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-600 Warfare Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** Bronze
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
