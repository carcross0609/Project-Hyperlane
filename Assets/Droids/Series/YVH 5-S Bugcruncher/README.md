# YVH 5-S Bugcruncher

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YVH 5-S Bugcruncher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YVH_5-S_Bugcruncher)

The YVH 5-S Bugcruncher was a spaceflight-capable variation of the highly successful YVH-series droid developed by Tendrando Arms during the Yuuzhan Vong War. Though they were built primarily to combat the Killiks, they retained the YVH designation and were first used by Han Solo to attack the Dark Nest on Kr, a moon in orbit over the planet Qoribu in 35 ABY.
