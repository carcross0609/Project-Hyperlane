# OT-9 Artillery Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-9 Artillery Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-9_Artillery_Droid)

The OT-9 Artillery Droid was a tripedal battle droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War. OT-9 Artillery Droids were among the droids deployed in the Markaran Plains around the Okara Droid Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-9 Artillery Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Plating:** White
- **Armament:** Arm-mounted blaster cannons
