# YI-5 Surveillance/Interrogation droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [YI-5 Surveillance/Interrogation droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YI-5_Surveillance/Interrogation_droid)

The YI-5 Surveillance/Interrogation droid was a droid shaped approximately like a remote, with a hovering, approximately spherical body. In addition to conducting interrogation semi-autonomously, a YI-5 droid could track down a subject in the manner of a seeker. Such droids were fitted with stealth fields that enabled them to blend in with the approximate color of their surroundings.
