# Gorax Hunt Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gorax Hunt Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gorax_Hunt_Droid)

Gorax Hunt Droids were utilized by the organizers of the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition to distribute "Ultimate Pickups" within certain battlefield configurations such as the "Gorax Hunt" arena. They used their four claw-like arms to suspend the pickup projectors upside down for Hunters from either team to collect. Utilizing their ability to float, these droids had a comprehensive view of their surrounding due to their numerous external sensors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gorax Hunt Droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** White and gray
- **Equipment:**
  - Antennae
  - Manipulator arms
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
