# Unidentified astromech droid series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified astromech droid series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_astromech_droid_series)

An unknown series of Astromech droid that was in service on several planets throughout the galaxy. On the mining colony of Tyne's Horky an orange and red colored unit was being sold at auction outside of Doodnik's Café, and a purple and gray colored unit was present on Biitu as part of the Great Heep's Droid harem.
