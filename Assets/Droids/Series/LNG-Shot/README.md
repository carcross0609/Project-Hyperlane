# LNG-Shot

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [LNG-Shot](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LNG-Shot)

The LNG-Shot was a model of battle droid with cyan and black plating and a cyan photoreceptor. They were monster droids, with a DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid for a head and the body of a Heavy Battle Droid, using a DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle. A group of Jawas who ran the Sandcrawler Shop beside the Junkers Cantina on the planet Tatooine sold blueprints for LNG-Shots. The default LNG-Shot blueprint cost 4,200,000 credits, a golden design cost 16,800,000 credits, a diamond-colored version cost 33,600,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design cost 67,200,000 credits.
