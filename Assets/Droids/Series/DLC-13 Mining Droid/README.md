# DLC-13 Mining Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DLC-13 Mining Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DLC-13_Mining_Droid)

The DLC-13 Mining Droid was a model of mining droid used on Mustafar by the Klegger Corporation during the Clone Wars and later by the Mensix Mining Company during the Galactic Civil War. This droid was made of heat-resistant carbonite and was equipped with low-powered deflector shield generators.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DLC-13 Mining Droid
- **Class:** Mining droid
- **Equipment:** Deflector shield generator
- **Affiliation:**
  - Klegger Corporation
  - Mensix Mining Company
