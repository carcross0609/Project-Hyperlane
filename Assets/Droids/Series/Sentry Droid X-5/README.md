# Sentry Droid X-5

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sentry Droid X-5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentry_Droid_X-5)

The Sentry Droid X-5 was a model of sentry droid produced by the Okara Droid Company at the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra. Armed with blaster rifles, a number of them were used to defend the assembly line of the factory during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sentry Droid X-5
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Sentry droid
- **Plating:** Dull green-brown
- **Armament:** Blaster rifle
