# Redkihl Rokk's spider droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Redkihl Rokk's spider droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Redkihl_Rokk%27s_spider_droid)

Redkihl Rokk's spider droids were droids used by Redkihl Rokk to attempt to kill Han Solo, Leia Organa, C-3PO, and Chewbacca while they were aboard his ship. Before the Empire, Redkihl Rokk used these to stun opponents on a ship so he could loot the ship of valuable items.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Redkihl Rokk's spider droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Armament:** Laser cannon (1)
- **Affiliation:** Redkihl Rokk's Pirates
