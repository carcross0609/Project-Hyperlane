# B1-Bokujin

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [B1-Bokujin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1-Bokujin)

B1-Bokujin droids were a model of boku jinken droids that could be used as defense systems against opponents, taking the shape of mechanical fists. During the lockdown of the planet Olcatz, the Anzellan Yakuza leader Que-Dama and his crew employed B1-Bokujin models as traps along Tkoku Bridge to defend Tkoku Island. When the Crusaders attempted to cross the bridge, the droids unleashed punches that knocked down some of the Crusaders.
