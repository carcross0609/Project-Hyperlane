# Patrol droid (First Order)

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Patrol droid (First Order)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Patrol_droid_%28First_Order%29)

The First Order's Starkiller Base superweapon was patrolled by many units of a spherical, white plated model of patrol droid around thirty years after the Battle of Endor. These droids were equipped with a long-range communications antenna, which protruded from the top of their spherical bodies. While infiltrating Starkiller Base during the Battle of Starkiller Base, the Resistance fighters Han Solo, Chewbacca and Finn avoided a patrol droid moving through the exterior regions of the base. The droid's sensors failed to pick them up due to the heavy snow and forest they were hidden within and a thermal distorter Chewbacca was carrying which masked their heat signatures.
