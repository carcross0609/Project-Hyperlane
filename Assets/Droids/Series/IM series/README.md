# IM series

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [IM series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IM_series)

The IM series was a model of security droid manufactured by Droid Security Systems, a division of Sacul Industries Group, and was present on their logo. Similar in appearance to the DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid, they possessed a multitude of sensors and had blue and silver plating. By about the year 1 BBY, the IM series had undergone improvements and were used in Spaceport THX1138.

## Specifications

- **Name:** IM series
- **Manufacturer:** Droid Security Systems
- **Class:** Security droid
- **Sensor:** Black, red, and white
- **Plating:** Blue and silver
- **Affiliation:** Spaceport THX1138
