# AMP Walker

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [AMP Walker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AMP_Walker)

The AMP Walker was a model of worker droid with gray plating and yellow cabling. A type of monster droid, they had the body of an EG-series power droid, a Veril Line Systems product, and the legs of an R-series astromech droid, a product of Industrial Automaton. On the planet Tatooine, the Jawa-run Sandcrawler Shop near the Junkers Cantina sold AMP Walker blueprints. The default design was sold at 5,400,000 credits, a golden design at 21,600,000 credits, a diamond-colored version at 43,200,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design at 86,400,000 credits.
