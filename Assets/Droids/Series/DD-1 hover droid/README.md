# DD-1 hover droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DD-1 hover droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DD-1_hover_droid)

The DD-1 hover droid was a type of training droid, sometimes customized and utilized by the Jedi Order to teach Jedi Initiates on Tenoo. Younglings training at the Tenoo Jedi Temple dueled against these fast-moving droids to hone their acrobatics and fighting skills against the droids' quadruple electroblade training swords.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DD-1 hover droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Class:** Training droid
- **Armament:** 4 electroblade training swords (Jedi customizations)
- **Equipment:** Repulsorlift
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
