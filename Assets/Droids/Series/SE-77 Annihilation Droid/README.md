# SE-77 Annihilation Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SE-77 Annihilation Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SE-77_Annihilation_Droid)

The SE-77 Annihilation Droid was a model of six-legged battle droid used by the the Shroud, a freelance spy active during the Galactic War. SE-77 Annihilation Droids were among the droid forces that defended the Shroud's factory on the moon Nar Shaddaa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SE-77 Annihilation Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
