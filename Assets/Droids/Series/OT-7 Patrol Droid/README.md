# OT-7 Patrol Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [OT-7 Patrol Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OT-7_Patrol_Droid)

The OT-7 Patrol Droid was a tripedal patrol droid model produced by the Okara Droid Company on the planet Balmorra during the Galactic War. OT-7 Patrol Droids were among the droids deployed in the Markaran Plains around the Okara Droid Factory.

## Specifications

- **Name:** OT-7 Patrol Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Company
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Patrol droid
- **Plating:** Bronze
- **Armament:** Arm-mounted blaster cannons
