# SEC-M droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SEC-M droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SEC-M_droid)

SEC-M droid was a Mouse droid model used by the Imperial Intelligence of the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Cold War. In 3643 BBY Cipher Nine used one SEC-M droid to plant a tracker onto the ship of the Eagle's network.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SEC-M droid
- **Model:** Mouse droid
- **Plating:** Grey
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Intelligence
