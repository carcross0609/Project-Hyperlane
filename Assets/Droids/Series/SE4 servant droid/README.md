# SE4 servant droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SE4 servant droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SE4_servant_droid)

The SE4 servant droid, also known as the SE4 Series Protocol Droid, was a droid line constructed by Industrial Automaton to help with the planning of social occasions and parties.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SE4 servant droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Class:**
  - Servant droid
  - Protocol droid
- **Degree:** Class three droid
- **Cost:**
  - 2,600 credits (new)
  - 1,300 credits (used)
- **Height:** 1.6 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Auditory sensors
  - Olfactory sensors
  - Photoreceptors
  - Simulated tastebuds
  - Vocabulator speech system
