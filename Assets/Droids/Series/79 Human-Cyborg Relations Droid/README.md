# 79 Human-Cyborg Relations Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [79 Human-Cyborg Relations Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/79_Human-Cyborg_Relations_Droid)

The 79 Human-Cyborg Relations Droid was produced by Duorq. It was designed to be an entry-level droid, equipped with a TranLang I Communication module and an H9 Droid Brain. H-9PO, a server at the Farrimmer Cafe, was a 79 Human-Cyborg Relations Droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 79 Human-Cyborg Relations Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Duorq
- **Equipment:**
  - Humanoid body
  - Two audio sensor
  - Two photoreceptors
  - Vocabulator
  - Broadband antenna
  - H9 Droid Brain
  - TranLang I Communication module
