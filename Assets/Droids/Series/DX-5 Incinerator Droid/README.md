# DX-5 Incinerator Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DX-5 Incinerator Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DX-5_Incinerator_Droid)

DX-5 Incinerator droids were a model of battle droid used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Galactic War against the Galactic Republic. Several DX-5s were deployed to the planet Voss, where they were sent to the Dark Reliquary.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DX-5 Incinerator Droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Armament:** 2 Flamethrowers
- **Equipment:** Grappling hook
- **Affiliation:**
  - Sith Empire
  -   Imperial Army
