# CP-3 Attack Drone

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CP-3 Attack Drone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CP-3_Attack_Drone)

The CP-3 Attack Probe was a model of small probe droid used by the Chiss spy Maki'voro, a lieutenant of the spymaster known as the Shroud during the Galactic War. One of a number of probe models that Maki'voro used, the CP-3 probe was designed as a small attack droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CP-3 Attack Drone
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Plating:** Dark
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
