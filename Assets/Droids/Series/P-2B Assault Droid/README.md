# P-2B Assault Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [P-2B Assault Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-2B_Assault_Droid)

The P-2B Assault Droid was a model of three-legged battle droid available during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The P-2B was designed solely to dominate the battlefield, and could be upgraded to use plasma cell ammo. The Twi'lek arms dealer Olok the Shadow sold P-2B Assault Droids in his auction on Darvannis.
