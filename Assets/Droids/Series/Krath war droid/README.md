# Krath war droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Krath war droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krath_war_droid)

The Krath war droid was an ancient type of droid. When creating the ED-V8-series envoy droid, the Rseikharhl Droid Group built it around the chassis of a Krath war droid. It shared its name with the Krath, an ancient group from the Deep Core. The IG-series 100 MagnaGuard that was produced by Holowan Laboratories for the Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies, General Grievous, took inspiration from the Krath war droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Krath war droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Degree:** Class four droid
- **Equipment:** Ablative plating
