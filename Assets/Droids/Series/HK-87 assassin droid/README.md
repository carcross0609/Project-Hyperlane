# HK-87 assassin droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HK-87 assassin droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HK-87_assassin_droid)

The HK-87 assassin droid was a model of HK assassin droid. During the time of the Galactic Empire and New Republic, they served as bodyguards and enforcers. Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth utilized such droids in her army while ruling over the city of Calodan on the forest planet Corvus. After Elsbeth's defeat defeat against the former Jedi Ahsoka Tano and the Mandalorian Din Djarin, the droids continued to serve her during her search for Grand Admiral Thrawn who had been trapped at the planet Peridea in the Far galaxy.
