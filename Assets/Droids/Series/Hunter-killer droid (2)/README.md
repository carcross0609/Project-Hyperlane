# Hunter/killer droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hunter/killer droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter/killer_droid)

Hunter/killer droids were spherical floating droids equipped with a single red sensor and three blasters. They were used to track down targets and guard sensitive areas. Xucphra Corporation used them on Thyferra as of 44 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hunter/killer droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Affiliation:** Xucphra Corporation
