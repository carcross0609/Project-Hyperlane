# BAF-379 Industrial Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BAF-379 Industrial Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BAF-379_Industrial_Droid)

The BAF-379 Industrial Droid was an industrial droid model that was produced by Balmorran Arms at the Balmorran Arms Factory on the planet Balmorra. BAF-379 Industrial Droids were used in the factory near the end of the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** BAF-379 Industrial Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Homeworld:** Balmorra
- **Class:** Industrial droid
- **Plating:** Orange
