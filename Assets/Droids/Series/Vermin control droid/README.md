# Vermin control droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vermin control droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vermin_control_droid)

Vermin control droids were used at the Imperial mining complex on the planet Alabash. Armed with blasters and poison gas, they were employed by Control in an attempt to kill a team of Alliance operatives.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vermin control droid
- **Armament:** Blaster
- **Equipment:** Poison gas
