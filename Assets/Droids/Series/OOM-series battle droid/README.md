# OOM-series battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [OOM-series battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OOM-series_battle_droid)

The OOM-series battle droid, also known as the OOM-mark, was a mark of the B1-series battle droid. OOM command battle droids, OOM pilot battle droids, and OOM-series security droids were part of the line. They saw use in the Invasion of Naboo. They were also used throughout the Clone Wars. B1-268 was an OOM command battle droid under the command of General Kalani in the Separatist holdout on Agamar during the early rebellion against the Galactic Empire.
