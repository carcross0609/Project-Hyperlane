# Mining Droid MK3

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mining Droid MK3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mining_Droid_MK3)

The Mining Droid MK3 was a model of mining droid used on Mustafar by the Klegger Corporation during the Clone Wars and later by the Mensix Mining Company during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mining Droid MK3
- **Class:** Mining droid
- **Affiliation:**
  - Klegger Corporation
  - Mensix Mining Company
