# SN-1F4 miniature sifter droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SN-1F4 miniature sifter droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SN-1F4_miniature_sifter_droid)

SN-1F4 miniature sifter droids, or RT sifter droids, were a model of sifter droid. M6-15R, who lived in Niima Outpost on the planet Jakku, was a SN-1F4 droid. In the year 34 ABY, shortly before the outpost was attacked by the First Order. Their function was to filter through sand or other loose substances to locate objects such as valuable minerals or salvage.
