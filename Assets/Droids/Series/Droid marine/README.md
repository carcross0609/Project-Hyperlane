# Droid marine

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droid marine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_marine)

The droid marine was a variant of the B1-Series battle droid variant deployed by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. They specialized in boarding enemy ships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Droid marine
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Combat Automata
- **Line:** B-series battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.91 meters
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Armament:**
  - E-5 blaster rifle
  - E-60R missile launcher
  - V-1 thermal detonators
- **Equipment:** Missile Launcher, E-5 Blaster Rifle, Thermal Detonator
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Separatist holdouts
  -   Gizor Dellso's Navy
