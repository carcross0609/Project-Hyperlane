# Techno Union HoloDroid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Techno Union HoloDroid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Techno_Union_HoloDroid)

The Techno Union HoloDroid was a droid equipped with a holoprojector and the ability of momentum. Similar to that of the Trade Federation mechno-chair, the HoloDroid was used for intergalactic communication for important personnel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Techno Union HoloDroid
- **Equipment:** Holoprojector
- **Affiliation:** Techno Union
