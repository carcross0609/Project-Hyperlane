# DX-6 Ravager

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DX-6 Ravager](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DX-6_Ravager)

The DX-6 Ravager was a model of battle droid used by the reconstituted Sith Empire during the Galactic War against the Galactic Republic. Several DX-6s were deployed to the planet Voss, where they were sent to the Dark Reliquary.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DX-6 Ravager
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Affiliation:** Sith Empire
