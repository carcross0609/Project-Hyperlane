# WA-2 waitress droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [WA-2 waitress droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WA-2_waitress_droid)

The WA-2 waitress droid was a line of serving droids. One such model was employed as a waitress at Panky's diner on the planet Barlok in 27 BBY.
