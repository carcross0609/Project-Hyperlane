# DTS-series dismantler droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DTS-series dismantler droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DTS-series_dismantler_droid)

DTS-series dismantler droids, or simply dismantler droids, were a type of demolition droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton and used by the Galactic Empire for dismantling BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bombers at Reklam Station.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DTS-series dismantler droid
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Class:** Demolition droid
- **Cost:** 40,000 credits
- **Height:** 3.1 meters
- **Plating:** Orange, red
- **Armament:**
  - Blaster
  - Flamethrower
  - Mechanical claws
  - Razor cutter
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
