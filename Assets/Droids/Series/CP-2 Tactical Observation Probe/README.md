# CP-2 Tactical Observation Probe

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CP-2 Tactical Observation Probe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CP-2_Tactical_Observation_Probe)

The CP-2 Tactical Observation Probe was a model of small probe droid used by the Chiss spy Maki'voro, a lieutenant of the spymaster known as the Shroud during the Galactic War. It was one of a number of probe models that Maki'voro used.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CP-2 Tactical Observation Probe
- **Class:** Probe droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Silver
- **Affiliation:** The Shroud's organization
