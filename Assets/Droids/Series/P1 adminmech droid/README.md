# P1 adminmech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [P1 adminmech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P1_adminmech_droid)

The P1 adminmech droid was a model of adminmech droid that could have white or orange colored plating. They had two stocky legs supporting a frustum-shaped body with a flat, tapered head. In 10 BBY, one P1 adminmech droid was forced to fight in the droid arena of the establishment known as the Lodge on the planet Vandor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** P1 adminmech droid
- **Model:** Adminmech droid
- **Plating:** Orange or white
