# Gyrowheel 1.42.08-series recycling droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gyrowheel 1.42.08-series recycling droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gyrowheel_1.42.08-series_recycling_droid)

The Gyrowheel 1.42.08-series recycling droid was used onboard ships to collect and recycle garbage. They used enzymes to break garbage down and turn it into edible meals. They were designed to be used on ships that had to go long periods between restocking.
