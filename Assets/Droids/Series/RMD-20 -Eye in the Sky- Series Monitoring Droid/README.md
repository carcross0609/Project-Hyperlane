# RMD-20 "Eye in the Sky" Series Monitoring Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RMD-20 "Eye in the Sky" Series Monitoring Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RMD-20_%22Eye_in_the_Sky%22_Series_Monitoring_Droid)

The RMD-20 "Eye in the Sky" Series Monitoring Droid was a cheap, small droid that could follow a target, patrol an area, or be guided by commands sent to its built-in comlink. It was spherical with cameras arranged around it. It had no advanced sensors, and was basically a floating audio and visual transmitter. It was frequently used by civilians, but some cheaper police and military units would also field them.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RMD-20 "Eye in the Sky" Series Monitoring Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Kystallio Detection Plus
- **Cost:** 800 credits
- **Equipment:**
  - Comlink
  - micro-repulsorlift nodes
  - sensors
