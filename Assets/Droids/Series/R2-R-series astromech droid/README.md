# R2-R-series astromech droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R2-R-series astromech droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R2-R-series_astromech_droid)

The R2-R-series astromech droid was an astromech droid model produced by Industrial Automaton. It was used primarily with X-wing and Y-wing fighters that were on reconnaissance missions. The R2-R could also download recon data. An R2 unit cost anywhere from 4,000 to 10,000 credits based on the R2 unit.
