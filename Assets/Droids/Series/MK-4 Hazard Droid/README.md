# MK-4 Hazard Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MK-4 Hazard Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MK-4_Hazard_Droid)

The MK-4 Hazard Droid was a type of six-legged battle droid produced by the Colicoid species during the era of the Galactic War. A number of MK-4 Hazard Droids were deployed during the Colicoids' war games.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MK-4 Hazard Droid
- **Manufacturer:** Colicoids
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Plating:** Dark
