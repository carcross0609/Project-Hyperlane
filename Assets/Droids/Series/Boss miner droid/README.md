# Boss miner droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Boss miner droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boss_miner_droid)

The Boss miner droid, also called the Super miner droid was a mining droid that had a dangerous weapon on its back. It helped the Confederacy of Independent Systems and the Nightsisters dig up Force crystals on Christophsis during the Clone Wars. The super miner droid noticed C-3PO and R2-D2, and attempted to attack the two droids, but Artoo closed the door in time to save them.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Boss miner droid
- **Class:** Class five mining droid
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Equipment:** Mining equipment
- **Affiliation:**

  - Nightsisters
