# Alien droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Alien droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alien_droid)

A googly-eyed species utilized a number of droids. They were armed with stun weapons, and could render the crew of starships unconscious with beams of shimmering light.
