# B2-HA super battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B2-HA super battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2-HA_super_battle_droid)

The B2-HA super battle droid was a heavy weapons variant of the B2-series super battle droid used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars, even fighting in the First Battle of Geonosis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B2-HA super battle droid
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Combat Automata
- **Line:** B2-series super battle droid
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Height:** 1.93 meters (6 ft, 4 in)
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Sensor:** Red
- **Armament:**
  - Wrist blaster (right arm)
  - Integrated rocket launcher (left arm)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
  - Bedlam Raiders
