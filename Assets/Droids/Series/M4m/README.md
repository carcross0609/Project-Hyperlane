# M4m

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M4m](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M4m)

The M4m was the military model of Cybot Galactica's M4 Series Messenger Droid. It was almost identical to the civilian version, but included a small internal sporting blaster to protect the information it carried. The M4m cost 4,000 credits, double the cost of a standard M4.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M4m
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
- **Cost:** 4,000 credits
- **Height:** 0.3 meters
- **Armament:** Small internal sporting blaster
- **Equipment:**
  - Body armor
  - Holographic projector/recorder
  - Repulsorlift drive unit (4-meter flight ceiling)
