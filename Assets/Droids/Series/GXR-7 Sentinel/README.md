# GXR-7 Sentinel

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GXR-7 Sentinel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GXR-7_Sentinel)

The GXR-7 Sentinel was a battle droid model used by the Galactic Republic during the Cold War with the reconstituted Sith Empire. Several were aboard the Thranta-class corvette Brentaal Star during an attack by the Imperial Gage-class transport Black Talon and were destroyed by an Imperial boarding party.
