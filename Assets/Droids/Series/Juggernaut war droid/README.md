# Juggernaut war droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Juggernaut war droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Juggernaut_war_droid)

The Juggernaut war droid was a battle droid constructed by Duwani Mechanical Products and designed specifically for use by the Republic rocket-jumper corps, serving as a combat unit to complement their existing R-EAU Linemaster droids that the Juggernauts were designed off of.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Juggernaut war droid
- **Manufacturer:** Duwani Mechanical Products
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Cost:** 20,000 credits
- **Height:** 1.95 meters
- **Armament:**
  - Pulse-wave rifle
  - Shatter beam
  - Wide-beam sonic stunner
- **Equipment:** Outrigger flame for jets
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - HK-01's droid army
  - Czerka Corporation
- **Firstmade:** 4800 BBY
- **Retired:** 4015 BBY
