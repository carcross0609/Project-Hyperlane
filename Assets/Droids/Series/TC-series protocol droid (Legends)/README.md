# TC-series protocol droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TC-series protocol droid/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TC-series_protocol_droid/Legends)

The TC-series protocol droid, also known as the TC 3PO protocol droid, was the first protocol droid line produced by Cybot Galactica to feature the TranLang III communications module. They were nearly identical to another Cybot product, the 3PO-series protocol droid. Most TC series protocol droids were made to serve aboard space stations. They were usually given a silver or red coat of paint. The TC series also somewhat lacked the eccentric behavior the 3PO units were known to develop. Due to his hodgepodge reconstruction, the famous protocol droid C-3PO had more mechanical similarities to the TC-series than to his own model.
