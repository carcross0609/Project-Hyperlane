# Finance Retrieval and Net Investigations droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Finance Retrieval and Net Investigations droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Finance_Retrieval_and_Net_Investigations_droid)

The Finance Retrieval and Net Investigations droid was manufactured by Investa Arts. They were used by the Galactic Empire as part of their Special Investigations squads because of their access to the FiNet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Finance Retrieval and Net Investigations droid
- **Manufacturer:** Investa Arts
- **Height:** 1.6 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - AA-1 VerboBrain
  - Audio and electronic recording system
  - Broadband antenna
  - FiNet interface
  - Three information retrieval jacks
  - Two audio sensor
  - Two photoreceptors
