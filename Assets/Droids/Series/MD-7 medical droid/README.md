# MD-7 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MD-7 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MD-7_medical_droid)

The MD-7 medical droid was a model of medical droid and a part of the MD-series that was manufactured by Industrial Automaton. MD-7s were experts in emergency trauma. The line was a prototype model used only in limited sectors, like the MD-8 and MD-9 units. At least one MD-7 was permanently assigned to the executive section of the Imperial Palace.
