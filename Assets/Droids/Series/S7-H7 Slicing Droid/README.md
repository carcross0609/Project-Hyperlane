# S7-H7 Slicing Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S7-H7 Slicing Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S7-H7_Slicing_Droid)

The S7-H7 Slicing Droid was a model of slicer droid that saw use during the Cold War. A number of S7-H7 droids worked with BB-22 Salvage Droids and JT-65 Salvage Droids in the Barasin Refuse Yards on the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** S7-H7 Slicing Droid
- **Class:** Slicer droid
- **Sensor:** Blue
- **Plating:** Yellow and silver
