# KUP24 messenger droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KUP24 messenger droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KUP24_messenger_droid)

The KUP24 messenger droid was a type of messenger droid. 1MSE-KUP24 was a KUP24 messenger droid that was enslaved by the Pyke Syndicate at the spice mines of Kessel. 1MSE-KUP24 was freed when L3-37 began a droid revolt in the spice mine control room.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KUP24 messenger droid
- **Class:** Messenger droid
- **Plating:** Gray
