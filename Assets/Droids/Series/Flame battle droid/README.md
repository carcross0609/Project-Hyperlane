# Flame battle droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flame battle droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flame_battle_droid)

The flame battle droid was a flamethrower-equipped variant of the B1-series battle droid produced by the Trade Federation that was used during the Invasion of Naboo and the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flame battle droid
- **Manufacturer:** Trade Federation
- **Class:** Battle droid
- **Sensor:** Black
- **Plating:** Tan with red markings
- **Armament:** Flame jet
- **Equipment:** Gas tanks
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  -   Separatist Droid Army
