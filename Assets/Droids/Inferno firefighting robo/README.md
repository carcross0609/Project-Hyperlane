# Inferno firefighting robo

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Inferno firefighting robo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inferno_firefighting_robo)

The Inferno firefighting robo was developed by the Corporate Sector Authority to put out fires. They were very tough and were able to stand even the hottest of flames. They used chemical foam to extinguish fires, and also had a searchlight and a loudspeaker.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Inferno firefighting robo
- **Manufacturer:** Corporate Sector Authority
- **Class:** Firefighter droid
- **Height:** 1.06 meters
- **Equipment:**
  - Audio/Echolocation Scanner
  - Hose
  - Laser cutter
  - Two gripper arms
- **Affiliation:** Corporate Sector Authority
