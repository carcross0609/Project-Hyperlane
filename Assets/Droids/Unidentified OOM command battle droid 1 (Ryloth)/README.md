# Unidentified OOM command battle droid 1 (Ryloth)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified OOM command battle droid 1 (Ryloth)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_OOM_command_battle_droid_1_%28Ryloth%29)

An OOM command battle droid served as a commander during the Battle of Ryloth. They were responsible for leading the Armored Assault Tank Mk Is on Ryloth against attacking AT-TEs that were commanded by Jedi General Mace Windu. During the skirmish, the droid commander was destroyed along with the AATs by Windu and Lightning Squadron.
