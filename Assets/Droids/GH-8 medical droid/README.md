# GH-8 medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GH-8 medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GH-8_medical_droid)

GH-8 medical droids were a type of medical droid used by the medcenters on the planet Vashka. They were white and silver in color, and they had the ability to hover.
