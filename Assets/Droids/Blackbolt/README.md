# Blackbolt

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blackbolt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blackbolt)

Blackbolt was a droid pirate captain who operated on the starship Terror of Tenoo during the High Republic Era, plundering the planet Tenoo, including Kaliah Kublop's staff. However, he eventually vanished; the Terror crashed on an asteroid near the planet and both Blackbolt and his vessel were left deactivated. The pilot Nash Durango, unaware Blackbolt was a droid, accidentally reactivated him when she discovered his lost starship. After being sent through hyperspace to the far reaches of the galaxy, Blackbolt would later return to Tenoo with his crew in search for Tanglevine Base.
