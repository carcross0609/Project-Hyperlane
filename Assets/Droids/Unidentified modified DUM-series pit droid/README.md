# Unidentified modified DUM-series pit droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified modified DUM-series pit droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_modified_DUM-series_pit_droid)

A modified DUM-series pit droid worked at the bar at Tanda's Cantina aboard a space station above the planet Suria. Sometime around 18 BBY, the droid interacted with the rogue Clone Force 99 members Hunter and Wrecker as they searched for bounty hunter Fennec Shand.
