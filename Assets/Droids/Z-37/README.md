# Z-37

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-37](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-37)

Z-37 was an RX-series pilot droid owned by Galactic Republic Commander Griebs Kishpaugh in 22 BBY during the Clone Wars. Z-37 was destroyed when the clone trooper Banks boarded Kishpaugh's shuttle and accused the Commander of treachery against the Republic. In the encounter, Kishpaugh inadvertently shot and destroyed Z-37 with his blaster. He had gold plating.
