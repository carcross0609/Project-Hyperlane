# Unidentified Wookiee medical droid

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Wookiee medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Wookiee_medical_droid)

Members of the Wookiee species built a medical droid on the moon Alaris Prime between 39 BBY and 36 BBY for the purpose of saving Attichitcuk, a Wookiee Chieftain who had been shot and gravely injured by B1-series battle droids of the Trade Federation. The medical droid traveled to Attichitcuk's location along with a party of Wookiees led by his son, Chewbacca, where the droid was able to give enough medical aid to save the Chieftain's life.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified Wookiee medical droid
- **Homeworld:** Alaris Prime
- **Birth:** Between 39 BBY and 36 BBY, Alaris Prime
- **Model:** Wookiee medical droid
- **Class:** Class one medical droid
- **Affiliation:** Alaris Prime colonists
