# FIII Footman droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FIII Footman droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FIII_Footman_droid)

The FIII Footman Droid was a highly-advanced model of combat and servant droid that was manufactured by the Tac-Spec Corporation for the House of Tund on the planet Vjun several centuries prior to the outbreak of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FIII Footman droid
- **Manufacturer:** Tac-Spec Corporation
- **Class:** 4th-degree security droid/servant droid
- **Height:** 1.9 meters
- **Armament:** miniaturized rail cannon
- **Equipment:**
  - durasteel plating
  - encrypted internal comlink
  - vocabulator
- **Affiliation:**
  - House Malreaux
  - House of Tund
