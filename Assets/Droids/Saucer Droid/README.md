# Saucer Droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saucer Droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saucer_Droid)

Saucer Droids were droids used in the detention levels of the first Death Star. The dome-shaped droids moved back and forth across the floor and featured red and green lights. A small sensor could be seen on the top as they moved. When the droids stopped a top hatch opened to allow more sensors and lights to raise up.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Saucer Droid
- **Plating:** Gray
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
