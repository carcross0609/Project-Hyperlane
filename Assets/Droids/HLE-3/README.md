# HLE-3

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HLE-3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HLE-3)

HLE-3, also known as Ellee, was a cobbled together droid, made with a PIP droid and a GNK-series power droid, that was owned by the female Besalisk Emm'jai. In 28 ABY, the droid was repaired by the human scavenger Rey, which delighted Emm'jai.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HLE-3"Ellee"
- **Model:** Cobbled together PIP droid, GNK-series power droid, and other droids
- **Pronouns:** He/him
