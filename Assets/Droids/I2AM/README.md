# I2AM

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [I2AM](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/I2AM)

The I2AM, also known as the I2-AM3 droid was an Imperial captain/astrogator droid carried aboard the Tech 4 PDV. They were based on the R2-series astromech droid produced by Industrial Automaton.

## Specifications

- **Name:** I2AM
- **Manufacturer:** Industrial Automaton
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
