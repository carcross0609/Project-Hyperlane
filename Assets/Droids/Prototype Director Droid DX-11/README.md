# Prototype Director Droid DX-11

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prototype Director Droid DX-11](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prototype_Director_Droid_DX-11)

The Prototype Director Droid DX-11 was an advanced version of the Director Droid DX-10 produced by the Okara Droid Company. A number of DX-11 droids were in storage in the Research and Development section of the Okara Droid Factory on the planet Balmorra.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Prototype Director Droid DX-11
- **Manufacturer:** Okara Droid Factory
- **Armament:** Blaster pistol
