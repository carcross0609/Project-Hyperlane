# 2R-series medical droid

**Kind:** Series  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [2R-series medical droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2R-series_medical_droid)

The 2R-series medical droid was a medical droid manufactured by Cybot Galactica. The RH-series research droid, a science droid also produced by Cybot Galactica, was a variant of the 2R-series medical droid.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 2R-series medical droid
- **Manufacturer:** Cybot Galactica
