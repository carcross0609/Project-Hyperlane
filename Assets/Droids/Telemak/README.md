# Telemak

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Telemak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telemak)

Lord Telemak was a droid who served as the guardian of the station known as The Haven by the year 22 BBY. By allying with the Scourge, Tarr Kligson supplanted Telemak's place as the guardian of the station and destroyed it by ripping off the unit's head. Kligson turned the droid into his puppet, whom he would speak directly through. Telemak was officially Kligson's assistant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Telemak
- **Death:** 3 ABY or 4 ABY, The Haven
- **Gender:** Masculine programming
- **Pronouns:** It/its
- **Sensor:** White
- **Plating:** Blue and silver
- **Affiliation:** The Haven
