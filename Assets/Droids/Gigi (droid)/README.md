# Gigi (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gigi (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gigi_%28droid%29)

Gigi was an astromech droid who operated during the High Republic Era. She served under the droid mechanic Rek Minuu, where she was upgraded with a flying drone head. Sometime between 232 BBY and 230 BBY, after Jedi Initiates Kai Brightstar, Nubs, and Lys Solay revealed Minuu was stealing parts in order to upgrade droids, Gigi—along with fellow astromechs Dozer and Beepers—left Minuu; the droids opted to work for Dotti at their workshop after the incident.
