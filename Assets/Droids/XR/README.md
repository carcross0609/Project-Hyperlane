# XR

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XR](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XR)

XR was the personal droid assistant of Dr. Akary Smith. However, it ended up reprogrammed and armed by General Koong, and was tasked with capturing Smith, Jann Tosh, C-3PO, and R2-D2. However, R2 managed to outsmart the droid.
