# Copper (droid)

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Copper (droid)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Copper_%28droid%29)

"Copper" was the nickname of a protocol droid owned by the Galactic Empire. As an experiment, Imperial technicians equipped the droid with deflector shield generators to provide defense for ground troops. However, the project was abandoned, and Copper was recruited by a young smuggler.

## Specifications

- **Name:** "Copper"
- **Class:** Protocol droid
- **Sensor:** Yellow
- **Plating:** Light blue
- **Equipment:** Deflector shield generators
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - The Smuggler
