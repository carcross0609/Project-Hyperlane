# Sterdic IV

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sterdic IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sterdic_IV)

Sterdic IV, also known as Sterdic Four, or simply as Sterdic, was a planet located in the Ryndellian sector of the Mid Rim Territories, on the edge of the Outer Rim Territories. Cawa City, located on Sterdic IV, was liberated from Imperial rule by the Alliance to Restore the Republic seventeen days after the Battle of Endor.
