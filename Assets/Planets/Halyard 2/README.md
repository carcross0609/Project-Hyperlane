# Halyard 2

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Halyard 2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halyard_2)

Halyard 2 was a planet known for its starship factory operated by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. During the Imperial Period, the factory produced light transports. Several hundred prototypes models of HWK-1000 light freighters once disappeared from the factory due to a clerical error.
