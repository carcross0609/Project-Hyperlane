# Panisia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Panisia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panisia)

Panisia was a planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. In 3 ABY, the Rebel Alliance operated a base on Panisia until they were forced to evacuate it when the Crimson Dawn assassin Deathstick informed the Galactic Empire of the base's location. During the ensuing battle, Deathstick was able to capture Cadeliah, heir to the Mourner's Wail Syndicate and Unbroken Clan criminal syndicates, who was under the protection of the rebels at the base.
