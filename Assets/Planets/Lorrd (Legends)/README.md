# Lorrd

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lorrd/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lorrd/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lorrd`, Outer Rim, grid N-4 — map position (4118.6, 8164.5, -167.6)

Lorrd was located in the Kanz sector and was home to the unique Lorrdian culture of baseline Humans. Lorrd was close to Bimmiel and its capital city was Lorrd City.
