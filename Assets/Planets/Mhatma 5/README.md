# Mhatma 5

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mhatma 5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mhatma_5)

Mhatma 5 was a cold terrestrial world that occupied the fifth orbital position in the Mhatma system, a part of the freestanding subsectors portion of the Mid Rim. During the Galactic Civil War, Mhatma 5 was situated on the galactic frontier.

At some point after the popular holovid celebrity Shantee Ren launched her campaign to have the stohl—elusive, furred serpent-like predators native to the Sombure sector ice planet Ichtor 8—declared an endangered species, a large preserve of transplanted wild stohls was established on Mhatma 5. Ren herself also took up permanent residence on the world, and an entry of a holobrochure for the Tagge Holographic Museum of the planet Chandrila dedicated to the stohl later noted that the holovid star still resided there.
