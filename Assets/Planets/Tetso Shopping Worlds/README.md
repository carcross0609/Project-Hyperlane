# Tetso Shopping Worlds

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tetso Shopping Worlds](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tetso_Shopping_Worlds)

The Tetso Shopping Worlds were a group of planets located within the Tetso system, in the Slice portion of the Core Worlds that was known as the Arrowhead. In 2 ABY, Princess Mi expressed interest in visiting the Tetso Shopping Worlds.
