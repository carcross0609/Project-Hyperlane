# Etti IV

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Etti IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Etti_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Etti IV`, Outer Rim, grid S-4 — map position (10953.7, 8972.0, 714.3)

Etti IV was a planet located in the Etti system that served as the headquarters of the droid manufacturing company Cybot Galactica during the early Imperial Era. Cybot Galactica operated out of a city-headquarters known as the Arcoplex, and in 19 BBY they were actively expanding the city by burning the surrounding forests, clearing debris with construction droids, and laying down golden metal foundation. During the New Republic Era, Sabodor's was an exotic pet shop located on Etti IV, and Han Solo and Chewbacca ferried pets from there aboard their bulk freighter Eravana.
