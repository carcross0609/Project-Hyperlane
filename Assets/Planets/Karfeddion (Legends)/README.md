# Karfeddion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Karfeddion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karfeddion/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Karfeddion`, Mid Rim, grid L-18 — map position (576.6, -12087.4, 20.5)

Karfeddion was a planet in the Senex sector. It was the homeworld of House Vandron. A large bountiful world, rich with plantations and ranches, it was used as a breeding ground for slaves of various species, including Ossans, Balinaka, and Humans.

At some point during one of the conflicts between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire, prior to the Invasion of Ossus, a military confrontation took place on Karfeddion in which the Jedi Dazh Ranos and another Gran Jedi were a part of. They had met in a trench and shared their rations.

Outsiders were banned in order to quell abolitionist discourse against slavery.
