# Solarine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Solarine/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solarine/Legends)

Solarine was a location in the galaxy. Ziro the Hutt and Sy Snootles had planned to go there to share the rest of their lives together, but Snootles killed Ziro as an act of revenge for abandoning her.

- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
