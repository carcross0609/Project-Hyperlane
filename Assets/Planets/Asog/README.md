# Asog

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Asog](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asog)

Asog was a terrestrial planet located in the Asog system, a part of the Esstran sector of the Outer Rim Territories and the New Territories. It was situated in grid square Q-4 of the Standard Galactic Grid.

At one point, Asog was a fertile habitat. Sometime prior to the composition of the Jedi book Rammahgon at least five thousand years before the Battle of Yavin, adherents of the Sith tradition of Force-users rendered Asog barren, which was subsequently noted by the Jedi Kli the Elder in a chapter of the Rammahgon.
