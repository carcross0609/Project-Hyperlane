# Lahag Erli

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lahag Erli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lahag_Erli)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lahag Erli`, Expansion Region, grid J-15 — map position (-2326.1, -8666.5, -49.3)

Lahag Erli was a planet located in the Lahag Erli system, a part of the Expansion Region's Cantons of Lahag sector. It was the homeworld of a social group of the same name, the forces of which invaded and occupied the worlds of the Har system early in the history of the Galactic Republic, which was founded in 25,053 BBY.
