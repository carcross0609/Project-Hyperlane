# Dawk

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dawk)

Dawk was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories that was orbited by a moon. During the Republic Era, between around 40 BBY and 32 BBY, the Devaronian criminal Coir Cion operated on the moon of Dawk. The Sith Lord Darth Sidious dispatched his Sith apprentice, Darth Maul, to kill Cion and his associates after the Devaronian threatened to blackmail Senator Sheev Palpatine—Sidious's public persona—with information on his underworld dealings.
