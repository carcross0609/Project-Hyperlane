# Xibariz

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Xibariz](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xibariz)

Xibariz was a planet located in the Xibariz system of the Inner Rim Territories and the Slice. It was situated in grid square O-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid. At some point, the author and historian Eloc Throno wrote the book The Traveler's Guide to Xibariz, subtitled "A Journey Through Perpetual Twilight," as a guide for visitors to the planet. The position of Xibariz was also noted on a galactic map included in Traveler's Guide to Batuu, a similar book by Throno detailing the planet Batuu that was published in 34 ABY.
