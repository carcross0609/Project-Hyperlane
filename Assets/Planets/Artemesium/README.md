# Artemesium

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Artemesium](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Artemesium)

Artemesium was a terrestrial planet located in the Mid Rim Territories and the Slice regions of the galaxy, within the Lifh sector's Artemesium system. It was situated in grid square R-8 of the Standard Galactic Grid. Artemesium had a a breathable atmosphere. A future Inquisitor of the Galactic Empire's Inquisitorius known as the Fifth Brother lived on the planet with his family until he was taken in by the Jedi Order.

When the Fifth Brother was young, the Galactic Senate decided that his homeworld would be stripped of its resources in order to save lives, and the resulting exploitation, which itself also cost lives, left Artemesium ruined. Sometime between 12 BBY and 11 BBY, as the Fifth Brother received a mission from The Grand Inquisitor to travel to Murkhana City on the Outer Rim Territories planet Murkhana and retrieve the schematics for a cloaking device known as the Shroud, he reflected on his past on Artemesium and the planet's fate.
