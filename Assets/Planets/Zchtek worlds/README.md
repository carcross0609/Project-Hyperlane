# Zchtek worlds

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zchtek worlds](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zchtek_worlds)

The Zchtek worlds of the Mid Rim were home to the Jin. Subjugated by the Galactic Empire, they were the site of a genocide perpetrated by that regime.
