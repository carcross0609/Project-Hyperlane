# Raxus Prime

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Raxus Prime/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raxus_Prime/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Raxus Prime`, Outer Rim, grid S-5 — map position (11550.2, 6165.6, -361.3)

Raxus Prime, once known as the Circlet of the Tion and Nikato's Shining Gem, was a waste-covered planet in the Raxus system in the Tion Hegemony sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Once a beautiful world of lagoons and islands, over many centuries it became heavily-industrialized, until by the time of the Clone Wars it had become totally covered with toxic debris and had a poisonous surface as a result of the Commerce Guild and Corporate Alliance dumping endless waste.
