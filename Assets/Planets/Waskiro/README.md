# Waskiro

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Waskiro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Waskiro)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Waskiro`, Outer Rim, grid R-7 — map position (9479.8, 3425.3, -266.4)

Waskiro was a planet located in the Waskiro system in the Jospro sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Despite being located in the northern half of the galaxy, Waskiro recognized the sovereignty of the Nihil in 228 BBY, and a gala was held there celebrating the Nihil's leader, Marchion Ro. A Republic team consisting of Avar Kriss, Keeve Trennis, and Tey Sirrek, as well as former Nihil members Lourna Dee and Quin Carree, infiltrated this gala in order to discover the location where Baron Boolan and the captive Jedi Sskeer were being held. As a consequence of Ro discovering their party, and Dee attacking Ro, the entire palace was bombed by Ro's ship the Gaze Electric.
