# Yamradi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yamradi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yamradi)

Yamradi was a planet located in the galaxy. During the High Republic Era, SF-R3 from the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts was sent to Yamradi to research the Chylaroo species.
