# Chagar IX

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chagar IX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chagar_IX)

Chagar IX, also known as Chagar, was a world located in the Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld of stormtrooper Sergeant Kreel, and fell under the control of the Galactic Empire in Kreel's youth.
