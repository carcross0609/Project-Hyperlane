# Temesher

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Temesher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temesher)

Temesher was a planet in the galaxy. During the Cold War between the reconstituted Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic, a large amount of cortosis was discovered on Temesher underneath a graveyard used by the natives' warriors.
