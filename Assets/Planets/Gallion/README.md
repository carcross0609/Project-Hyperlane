# Gallion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gallion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gallion)

Gallion was a planet that was located in the Grumani sector. Gallion was one of the largest rocky planets in the sector and had low gravity, allowing lifeforms on the world to develop to titanic sizes.
