# Caldus

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Caldus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caldus)

Caldus was a tropical planet located in the galaxy. In 9 ABY, Mandalorian Din Djarin and his son Grogu visited the planet to relax on one of its beaches, where they were attacked by Moff Gideon's Imperial forces.
