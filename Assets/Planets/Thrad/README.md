# Thrad

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thrad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thrad)

Thrad was a dry desert planet which was represented by Nadea Tural in the Imperial Senate. In 4 BBY, Tural was rescued by the crew of the Ghost after her shuttle crashed.
