# Ord Lonesome

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Lonesome](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Lonesome)

Ord Lonesome was a world located in the Ord Lonesome system, a part of the Albanin sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Ord Lonesome served as an Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost for the Galactic Republic during the Pius Dea Era, a period of time that lasted between approximately 12,000 BBY and 11,000 BBY, despite it being located outside the borders of Republic space. When the Republic launched the Thirty-Fourth Pius Dea Crusade against the Hutt species in 11,057 BBY, Republic forces moved from Ord Lonesome to the Riileb system, in the vicinity of Hutt Space.
