# Mountain

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mountain (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mountain_%28planet%29)

Mountain was a planet of the Aakaash system, which was ruled by the Land & Sky Corporation. It was noted to be a polluted world with many areas being particularly high in deadly toxins or Radiation. The environment was unstable with weather-based natural disasters being relatively common, and the planet was inhabited by ferocious fauna.
