# Clabron

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clabron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clabron)

Clabron was a planet in the Clabron system in the Core Worlds that was the homeworld of the Clabronian species. During the Clone Wars, many Clabronians wished to stay out of the conflict, but Grand Minister Stin planned to side with the Galactic Republic. The Confederacy of Independent Systems, who had spies on the world, sent the assassin Lis Mohles to kill Stin, along with Padmé Amidala, who had went to visit Clabron and meet the Grand Minister. While Stin's successor, Second Minister Tarmin, was more reluctant to openly fight in the war, Amidala managed to convince him to consider providing other aid to the Republic.
