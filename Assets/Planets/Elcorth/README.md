# Elcorth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Elcorth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elcorth)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Elcorth`, Core Worlds — map position (-317.6, 420.0, 11.7)

Elcorth was one of two mining colonies founded by the Morath on the edge of the Koornacht Cluster closest to Coruscant. The other was Kojash. Both were overtaken by the Yevetha during the Great Purge.

Enara claimed to have been a pholikite miner here. In fact, she was a Fallanassi from J't'p'tan.
