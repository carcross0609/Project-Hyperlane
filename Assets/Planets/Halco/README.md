# Halco

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Halco](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halco)

Halco was the third planet of the Bestine system located in the Inner Rim Territories. It had two moons and, along with Bestine IV, was one of the two terrestrial worlds in the star system.
