# Armath

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Armath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armath)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Armath`, Other, grid N-15 — map position (3951.1, -7651.7, -31.0)

Armath was a terrestrial planet located in the Mikaster sector's Armath system, which was situated in the Trailing Sectors portion of the Expansion Region. In 15 BBY, Ambassador Zell of the planet Majoor went on an important peace mission to Armath, accompanied by a convoy of starships.
