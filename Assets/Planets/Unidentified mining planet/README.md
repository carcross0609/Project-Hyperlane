# Unidentified mining planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified mining planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_mining_planet)

A terrestrial mining planet was the site of a chemical mine during the High Republic Era. The human Raf Thatchburn grew up on the planet and worked in the mine, which ultimately resulted in his lungs becoming shredded, forcing Thatchburn to wear a breathing apparatus. By 382 BBY, Thatchburn had left the world to work as an inspector in the Galactic Antipiracy Command.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified mining planet
- **Class:** Terrestrial
- **Interest:** A chemical mine
