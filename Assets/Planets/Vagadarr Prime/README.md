# Vagadarr Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vagadarr Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vagadarr_Prime)

Vagadarr Prime was a planet located in the Vagadarr system of the Inner Rim Territories. It was the homeworld to a race of giants of living stone. Its terrain included deserts, forests, and mountains.
