# Kuna's Tooth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kuna's Tooth/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuna%27s_Tooth/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kuna's Tooth`, Outer Rim, grid H-16 — map position (-5024.1, -10370.9, -169.2)

Kuna's Tooth, also known as the Tooth, was a cool Outer Rim Territories mining planet located in the Moddell sector. The site of an orbital shipyard called Doestes High Port, the planet was governed by a bureaucracy and inhabited by members of the Ayrou, Human, and Vashan species. Years after the Battle of Endor, Kuna's Tooth hosted backalley cookhouses where smuggled-in Rokna tree fungus from the Endor system was processed into the Rokna Blue drug before being transported into the wider galaxy.
