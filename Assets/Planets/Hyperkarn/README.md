# Hyperkarn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hyperkarn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperkarn)

Hyperkarn was a forested planet in the Outer Rim Territories with a twilight forest where the human female Miramir lived for years with her grandmother before giving up her life there to travel with her husband Dathan, who had also lived on Hyperkarn for a period of days. By 21 ABY, she missed her home, while Dathan knew he had felt something for the place while living there, but never truly considered it home.
