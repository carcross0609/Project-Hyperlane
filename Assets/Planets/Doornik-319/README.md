# Doornik-319

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Doornik-319](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doornik-319)

Doornik-319 (also Morning Bell, Morning's Bell and Preza) was a planet in the Doornik-319 system. The planet was known to the Kubaz as Morning's Bell and to the Yevetha as Preza. Originally a Kubaz colony world, it was taken over by the Yevetha during the Great Purge, during which the Yevetha wiped out the Kubaz colonists.
