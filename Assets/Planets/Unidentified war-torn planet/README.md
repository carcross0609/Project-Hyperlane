# Unidentified war-torn planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified war-torn planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_war-torn_planet)

A planet existed in the galaxy. During an intergalactic event known as the Clone Wars, which had begun by 22 BBY, the planet had aligned itself with the Confederacy of Independent Systems, a major player in the war. It was eventually subjugated to an invasion from the Galactic Republic, the Confederate adversary, that saw the world covered in continual fighting. The on-going war eventually prompted a civilian named Rayt to disguise himself a a clone trooper and try to escape the planet. Eventually he boarded a Republic vessel and was carted offworld. The planet's terrain consisted of heavy pine forests in some areas, most notably in the area surrounding Rayt's town.
