# Storm

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Storm (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_%28planet%29)

Storm was a gas giant, and the fifth planet in the Naboo system, located within the Chommell sector on the galaxy's Mid Rim. Its thirty-two moons included its third moon and its tenth moon, which housed a penal colony and Tagge Company outpost respectively.
