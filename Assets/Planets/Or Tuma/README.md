# Or Tuma

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Or Tuma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Or_Tuma)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Or Tuma`, Outer Rim — map position (9843.6, -9838.3, -76.3)

Or Tuma was the outermost planet orbiting the star Ryloth in the Ryloth system. It was a frozen rock located in the Outer Rim Territories.

A small colony of Twi'leks settled the planet in an attempt to get away from the scorching hot temperatures of Ryloth.
