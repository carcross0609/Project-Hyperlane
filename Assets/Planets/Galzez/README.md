# Galzez

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Galzez](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galzez)

Galzez was a planet located in the Galzez system, in the Dustig sector of the Mid Rim Territories. Its surface was covered rocky mountains and many orange crystals. It was home to a base operated by the Syvik Delegation.
