# Yamime

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yamime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yamime)

Yamime was a gas giant located in the Felucia system, which in turn was found in the Outer Rim Territories' Thanium sector. During the Clone Wars, this area of space was contested over by the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems.
