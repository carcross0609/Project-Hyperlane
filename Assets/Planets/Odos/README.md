# Odos

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Odos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Odos)

Odos was a planet located in the Odos system, one of the Odos systems situated within the Meridian sector of the Outer Rim Territories.

Odos was located near the Chorios systems. It was also located near the Exodo system and the Spangled Veil Nebula. The planet Odos was surrounded by gas clouds.

In 13 ABY, while at Exodo II, Han Solo, Chewbacca and Lando Calrissian were attacked by an Imperial fleet from the nearby Antemeridian sector. They lost their pursuers by flying the Millennium Falcon through the gas clouds of Odos and the fringes of the Spangled Veil Nebula.
