# Smarck

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Smarck](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Smarck)

Smarck was a planet in Outer Rim Territories, Cademimu sector, Smarck system.

For several years, Macus Kayniph was in charge of Black Sun operations on Smarck. Black Sun kept a storehouse of Spaarti cloning cylinders on the planet.
