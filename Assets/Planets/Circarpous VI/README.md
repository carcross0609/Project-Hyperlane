# Circarpous VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Circarpous VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Circarpous_VI)

Circarpous VI was the sixth planet orbiting the star Circarpous Major in the Circarpous system. Located in the Circarpous sector of the Expansion Region, it was a gas giant.
