# Colonial Station Chaf

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Colonial Station Chaf](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Colonial_Station_Chaf)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Colonial Station Chaf`, Unknown Regions, grid F-9 — map position (-8701.6, 1005.5, 27.0)

Colonial Station Chaf was a terrestrial planet within the territory of the Chiss Ascendancy in the Unknown Regions. It shared its name with the Chaf family, one of the Nine Ruling Families of the Chiss Ascendancy. The communications triad of Colonial Station Chaf was used for relaying communication through to the Xodlak family task force led by Senior Captain Xodlak'in'daro on their way to the planet Hoxim.

The Kilji Illumine government's Illumine Kilhorde battle fleet long-range triad transmitter located on Colonal Station Chaf while they conquered several nations to the south and southeast of the Ascendancy to communicate with its leader, Generalirius Nakirre. Through the transmitter, Nakirre received reports of victory from his field commanders; however, one day the reports ceased, leading him to fear that his generals had foolishly extended their conquests to Colonial Station Chaf, which would have suspended the long-range triad transmitter's operation. In fact, however, the Kilhorde had been destroyed by the Paataatus Hiveborn government, which deemed the fleet's operations near their borders unacceptable and sent a Paataatus attack fleet to end them.
