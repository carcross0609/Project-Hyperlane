# Molos

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Molos/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Molos/Legends)

Molos was a planet in the galaxy that was ruled by King Amand and controlled by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. The Rebel Alliance sent aid to the planet after the civilians rose up against the Empire. They formed an army and fought off the Imperial reinforcements in the city Molopolis.
