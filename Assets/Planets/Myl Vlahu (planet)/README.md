# Myl Vlahu

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Myl Vlahu (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Myl_Vlahu_%28planet%29)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Myl Vlahu`, Mid Rim — map position (1840.5, -10971.6, -560.4)

Myl Vlahu was a gas giant planet in the Ruusan system. It was the sixth planet orbiting the star Hoth's Brand. It was named after Jedi Myl Vlahu who fought in the Ruusan campaign during the New Sith Wars.
