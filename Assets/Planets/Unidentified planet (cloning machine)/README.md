# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (cloning machine)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28cloning_machine%29)

This planet was a snowy world that was the location of a cloning machine left over from the Clone Wars era. Luke Skywalker went to the planet to find and destroy the device. The machine was operated by a scientist who created a number of defective clones of himself, then forced Skywalker to do the same. After those clones were killed, Luke then destroyed the machine. Shortly after, he was taken to the planet Xantar by an alien, and met a fellow human guest.
