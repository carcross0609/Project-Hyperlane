# Ossiathora

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ossiathora](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossiathora)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ossiathora`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (215.6, -11842.0, 237.2)

Ossiathora was an agriworld located in the Ossiathora system of the Juvex sector, a part of the Senex-Juvex region in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. The Senex-Juvex Loop hyperlane linked it to the Deminol and Kassido systems. Ossiathora was the origin point of Ossiathoran noodles and was controlled by House Kassido, a noble house based on the world of Kassido.
