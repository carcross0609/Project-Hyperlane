# Praesitlyn

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Praesitlyn/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Praesitlyn/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Praesitlyn`, Outer Rim, grid M-19 — map position (2426.8, -13699.3, 584.9)

Praesitlyn was a planet in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim. It was the location of the Intergalactic Communications Center, a major HoloNet hub in the galaxy. The Communications Center became a strategic asset during the Clone Wars, leading to the fierce Battle of Praesitlyn.
