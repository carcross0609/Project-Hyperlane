# Ubrikkia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ubrikkia/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ubrikkia/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ubrikkia`, Mid Rim, grid Q-10 — map position (8933.6, -970.3, 33.2)

Ubrikkia was a Mid Rim industrial world situated in the Kastolar sector. Located on the Ootmian Pabol hyperlane, for millennia it fell under the influence of Hutts from nearby Hutt Space, directly constituting a part of that domain around 5000 BBY. Ubrikkia was the site of the headquarters of the company Ubrikkian Industries and its subsidiaries and shared its name with the ornament known as the Ubrikkian tail spiral ring.
