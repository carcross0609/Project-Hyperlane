# Aquaris

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aquaris](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aquaris)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Aquaris`, Expansion Region, grid N-7 — map position (4484.8, 3305.2, -238.2)

Aquaris was a oceanic planet located in the Aquaris system in the Sumitra sector of the Expansion Region. It and Thustra were the closest principal planets to the location of the stolen Imperial corvette Carrion Spike while it was at the location designated as LCC-4-4-7. Aquaris had a notable criminal presence, and was located near the Perlemian Trade Route. The former pirate Queen Maz Kanata marked it as the location of an outlaw garage on a map that she included in the book that became known as the Smuggler's Guide. The planet was the origin point of the Aquaris Freeholders, who forced the Artiodac chef Strono Tuggs to make them a gourmet meal after his freighter and finding no spice aboard; Tuggs used a variety of ingredients he had found on Aquaris to create the Aquaris Broth.
