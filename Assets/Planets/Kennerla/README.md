# Kennerla

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kennerla](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kennerla)

Kennerla was a planet where Affie Hollow had traveled during the High Republic Era while serving aboard a starship, at some point before she became the co-pilot of the transport Vessel for the Byne Guild. Kaleesh Captain Dumas Mar'Ti went to Kennerla, visiting the planet's grungiest cantina, and stood at the end, holding her grudge against Affie Hollow, before she discovered her there.
