# Test environment WL428T73

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Test environment WL428T73](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Test_environment_WL428T73)

Test environment WL428T73 was a designation given by Iokath creators to a planet located in the Unknown Regions of the galaxy. Z17AX558 was chosen by the species as a test environment for their weapons of mass destruction and a new fully-integrated, and optimized planetary assault platform completely eradicated the local sentient population. The invaders discovered an underground vault filled with artifacts, but were unable to determine their exact value.
