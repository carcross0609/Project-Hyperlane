# Bellis IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bellis IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bellis_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bellis IV`, Outer Rim, grid P-7 — map position (6877.8, 3212.3, -264.5)

Bellis IV, also known as Bellis Four, was a planet in the Bellis system of Demetras sector. Prior to the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire, Bellis IV was the sight of a conflict but was ultimately freed, in large part due to the actions of the Jedi Ngani Zho.
