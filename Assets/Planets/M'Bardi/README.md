# M'Bardi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M'Bardi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M%27Bardi)

M'Bardi was a water planet located in the Kalamith sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was also the site of the M'Bardi Prison.
