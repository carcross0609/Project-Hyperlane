# Tentator

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tentator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tentator)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tentator`, Colonies, grid L-9 — map position (1163.8, 1122.7, -9.7)

Tentator was a planet located within the Tentator system of the Colonies, used as a prison by the Galactic Republic. The Slam gang escaped from the Greylands Security Complex on-planet, but were recaptured and returned to the poorly-maintained high-security prison, along with their ship, an Ubrikkian star yacht.

Jedi Masters Obi-Wan Kenobi and Siri Tachi, along with their respective apprentices, Anakin Skywalker and Ferus Olin, visited Tentator to pick up the Slams' yacht after its recapture.
