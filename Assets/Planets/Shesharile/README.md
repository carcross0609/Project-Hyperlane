# Shesharile

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shesharile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shesharile)

Shesharile was a gas giant world that was located within the Shesharile system, in the Minos Cluster.

A number of moons orbited the large planet, only two of which were able to support life. Known as the Twin Planets, Shesharile 5 and Shesharile 6 were ruled by the same system-wide government. The societies of both worlds collapsed when the Galactic Empire terminated his business deals with the two moons to focus the use of its resources on building the Death Star II.
