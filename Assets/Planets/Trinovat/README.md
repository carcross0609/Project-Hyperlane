# Trinovat

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trinovat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trinovat)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Trinovat`, Outer Rim, grid P-6 — map position (6831.9, 5492.8, -89.5)

Trinovat was a sunny, verdant world located in the Trinovat system. It was situated in the Loop Worlds area of the Gordian Reach sector within the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The hyperlane known as the Junction-Tierell Loop connected it to the Durgen's Star and Near Indosa systems. Trinovat was the homeworld of the sentient Trinovate species and featured small farms and mines.
