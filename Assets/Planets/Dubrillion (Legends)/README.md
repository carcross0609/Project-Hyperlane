# Dubrillion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dubrillion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dubrillion/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dubrillion`, Outer Rim, grid K-3 — map position (-498.6, 9366.5, -403.2)

Dubrillion was a planet in the Dubrillion system with beautiful oceans and lush, green continents, a sky dotted with fluffy, white clouds. It was the sister-world of Destrillion.

Dubrillion was located in the Outer Rim Territories near Dantooine and Muunilinst, in the heart of CIS space during the Clone Wars. Dubrillion would duly become a world of the Galactic Empire after the Clone Wars; when that government fell, entrepreneur Lando Calrissian would take-over the planet and serve as its administrator. There was an asteroid belt situated near the planet, which was used by Calrissian as the basis for a large operation some time after the Empire's end. In addition, Dubrillion hosted many hotels and apartments with luxury suites, located in its massive high-rise cities. Leia Organa Solo was its ambassador for a short time. During the Yuuzhan Vong War, the planet was attacked, and eventually conquered, by the invading Yuuzhan Vong. Following the Yuuzhan Vong's defeat, Dubrillion later became a world of the Fel Empire.
