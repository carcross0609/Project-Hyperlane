# Q'Maere

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Q'Maere](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q%27Maere)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Q'Maere`, Outer Rim, grid M-21 — map position (2796.6, -16691.5, -138.6)

Q'Maere was a planet located within the Kathol Rift. The planet had a deadly, poisonous atmosphere, full of chlorine, and the sky was a sickly green. The majority of the surface was a caustic ammonia ocean interspersed with volcanic salt-caked islands. The planet was the location of the Q'Maere Research Facility.
