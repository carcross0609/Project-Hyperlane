# Speco

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Speco](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Speco)

Speco was a planet aligned with the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War, located in the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was ruled by an unnamed Imperial governor, but it was rumored that his mistress, a Falleen named Miaxi, really had control by manipulating him with her charms.
