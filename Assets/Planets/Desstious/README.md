# Desstious

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Desstious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Desstious)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Desstious`, Mid Rim — map position (-3221.4, -9163.2, 262.7)

Desstious was a world located in the Desstious system, a part of the Lambda sector in the Trailing Sectors portion of the Mid Rim. It was the homeworld of the Human male Par Lankin, who was born in 50 BBY. Lankin's homeworld was noted in Wanted by Cracken, a datafile that served as a compilation of reports on various threats to the New Republic and was published in 7 ABY.
