# Maal

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Maal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maal)

Maal was a gas giant located at the intersection of the Authority Guardian Corridor and the Trianii Shunt, in space annexed by the Corporate Sector in 5 BBY. The planet was orbited by Hiit, a moon that was the home of the avian Hiitians.

- **Name:** Maal
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
- **Sector:** Corporate Sector
- **System:** Hiit system

- **Moons:** Hiit
