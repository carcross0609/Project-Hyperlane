# Myomar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Myomar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Myomar/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Myomar`, Expansion Region, grid J-7 — map position (-1894.3, 3085.7, -9.6)

Myomar was a planet in the Deadalis sector of the Expansion Region, and the site of Imperial starship maintenance facilities. After the inception of the Galactic Empire, Grand Admiral Ishin-Il-Raz conducted a massacre on the planet. Years later, Han Solo and Chewbacca stole the Millennium Falcon's deflector shield generators from an Imperial facility on Myomar.
