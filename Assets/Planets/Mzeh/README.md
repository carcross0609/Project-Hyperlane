# Mzeh

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mzeh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mzeh)

Mzeh was a gas giant located in the Fondor system in the Freeworlds Territory portion of the Tapani sector, within the Southern Core portion of the Colonies. The planet occupied the sixth orbital position around the star Fondor and had eleven moons. Along with the rest of its star system, Mzeh was located on the Gandeal-Fondor Hyperlane, with another hyperlane also connecting it to the Sefon system.
