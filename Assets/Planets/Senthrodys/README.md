# Senthrodys

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Senthrodys](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senthrodys)

Senthrodys was a terrestrial planet located in the Senthrodys system of the Inner Rim and the Slice. It was situated in grid square O-10 of the Standard Galactic Grid. In 4 ABY, Senthrodys was targeted during Operation: Cinder, the Galactic Empire's campaign to destroy worlds that both supported and opposed its regime. As part of Operation: Cinder, the Empire used tectonic devices to shatter the crust of Senthrodys.
