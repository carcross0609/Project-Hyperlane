# Zilka home planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zilka home planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zilka_home_planet)

The insectoid Zilka sentient species originated from a planet. When the gangster Chulok analyzed two unconscious Zilka enforcers who had been incapacitated by the incognito Jedi Master Mace Windu, the Rodian Lok—one half of Chulok—noted that Windu had been familiar with Zilka anatomy, suggesting he had either been to their planet or had studied them.
