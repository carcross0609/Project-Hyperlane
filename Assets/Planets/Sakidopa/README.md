# Sakidopa; (The Bootana Hutta region, with Sakidopa represented by the number 20)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sakidopa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sakidopa)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Sakidopa`, Hutt Space, grid S-11 — map position (11696.6, -2019.5, 364.3)

Sakidopa was a planet of the Sakidopa system, situated in the Bootana Hutta area in the Hutt Space portion of the Slice. A secret hyperlane linked it to the Saki system.

The Sakiyans of the planet Sakiya—also known as Saki—settled Sakidopa—the name of which, meaning "Second Saki," was shared with "dopa," the Huttese word for "two." Sakidopa was established as a mining colony to support the repulsorlift manufacturing industry that developed on Sakiya and was considered a part of the group known as the Sakiyan Collective.

On Sakidopa, the Sakiyans mined the exotic element graxitium, which they shipped off for enrichment at a facility near the Godsheart pulsar and ultimately used to produce repulsorlift circuitry. As with other Sakiyan colony worlds, the fate of Sakidopa in the aftermath of Hutt Space's invasion by the extra-galactic Yuuzhan Vong species in 26 ABY was not widely known.
