# Uveron

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Uveron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uveron)

Uveron was a planet located in the Colonies. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, Rebel Alliance leader Mon Mothma undertook a funding mission for the Alliance to the world. During her time on Uveron, Mothma was kidnapped by the Scarabi.
