# Hallion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hallion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hallion)

Hallion was a planet located in the Sith Worlds of the Outer Rim. The planet featured lava fields that produced obsidian, and decorative spheres made of polished obsidian from the lava fields were prized for their dark luster. Hallion struggled during the transition from a private enterprise to adjust to the imperial economy of the Sith Empire.
