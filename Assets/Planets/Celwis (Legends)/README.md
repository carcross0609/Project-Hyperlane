# Celwis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Celwis/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Celwis/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Celwis`, Unknown Regions, grid F-8 — map position (-7855.4, 1608.8, -240.0)

Celwis was a planet of the Chiss Ascendancy in the Celwis system of the Unknown Regions. It was located on the Vagaari Corridor, a hyperlane that ran through the Unknown Regions. Celwis was once regularly attacked by alien aggressors, but it eventually became a demonstration of Chiss military strength shortly after the formation of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Force.
