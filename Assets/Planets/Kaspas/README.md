# Kaspas

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaspas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaspas)

Kaspas was a terrestrial ice world located in the Kaspas system, a part of the Hevvrol sector in the Mid Rim Territories. It was positioned at the coordinates 0-15 on the Standard Galactic Grid. Some time after the fall of the Galactic Empire in 5 ABY, the scoundrel Misook backed out of a deal with a notorious crime boss on Kaspas and escaped to the Outer Rim planet Batuu, but was pursued by the bounty hunter Emim'Ai.
