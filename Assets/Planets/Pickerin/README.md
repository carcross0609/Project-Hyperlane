# Pickerin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pickerin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pickerin)

Pickerin was a planet under the control of the Galactic Empire. In 0 ABY, Pickerin was visited by the Hand of Judgment stormtrooper team after hearing word that the government there had enslaved local artisans.
