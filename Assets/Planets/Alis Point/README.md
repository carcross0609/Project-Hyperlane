# Alis Point

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Alis Point](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alis_Point)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Alis Point`, Outer Rim, grid N-18 — map position (4042.7, -12860.8, 71.4)

Alis Point was a planet in the Tamarin sector of the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy and the site of a Rebel base during the Galactic Civil War. Shortly after the Battle of Yavin, the base had to be evacuated. During the evacuation, Zev Senesca single-handedly held off three TIE/LN starfighters, while the base's unarmed transport fled into hyperspace. Senesca's heroics earned him a spot in the newly founded Rogue Squadron.
