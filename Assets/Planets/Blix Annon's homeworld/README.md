# Blix Annon's homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blix Annon's homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blix_Annon%27s_homeworld)

A planet was the homeworld of Blix Annon. Annon represented the planet in the Galactic Senate. While en route to his homeworld, Annon died of a heart attack during a kidnapping attempt.
