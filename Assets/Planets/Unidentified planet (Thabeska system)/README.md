# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Thabeska system)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Thabeska_system%29)

A planet was located in the Outer Rim Territories, in the same system as Thabeska. It had mountainous terrain, and was sparsely populated. It had a few small moons. Ahsoka Tano, after leaving the Fardi clan for the last time, went to this planet to hide out for a few days, but was accosted by a Black Sun agent. While being pursued by the agent in the planet's upper atmosphere, Tano was intercepted by another ship belonging to Chardri Tage and Tamsin, a pair of pilots working for Bail Organa who had been sent to find her. After knocking out Tage and Tamsin, and being reunited with R2-D2, Tano hid on one of the planet's moons.
