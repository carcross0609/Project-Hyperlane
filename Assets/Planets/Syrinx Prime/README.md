# Syrinx Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Syrinx Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syrinx_Prime)

Syrinx Prime was a planet in the Syrinx system in the Outer Rim Territories that was mostly covered in rocky hills and mountains. Syrinx Prime was also the homeworld of the Syrinxians. At some point in the late Republic Era, there was a war between Syrinx Prime and the neighboring planet Vekura that later had the Jedi evolved which helped end the conflict between the two worlds. During the war, Syrinx Prime was lead by the President Hoss.
