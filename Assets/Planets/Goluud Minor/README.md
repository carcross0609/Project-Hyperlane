# Goluud Minor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Goluud Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Goluud_Minor)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Goluud Minor`, Deep Core — map position (-1466.9, -2528.2, 19.5)

Goluud Minor was a mining world located within the Goluud Minor system of the Core Worlds. By 5000 BBY, a new mining colony had been established on the planet, and merchant lord Ssk Kahorr sent the drone ship Zeta Five to deliver much-needed mining technology and equipment to the colony and sell them to the wealthiest miners. However, the Zeta Five never arrived at Goluud Minor, as it was destroyed by a solar flare from the unstable red supergiant star Primus Goluud during a stopover in the Primus Goluud system.
