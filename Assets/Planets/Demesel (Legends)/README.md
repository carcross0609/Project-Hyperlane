# Demesel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Demesel/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Demesel/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Demesel`, Outer Rim — map position (10380.7, 3759.1, 1.6)

Demesel was a planet of grassy plains in the Meram sector, presumably in the Demesel system. Its local day was 22 standard hours long; its year was 285 local days long. It was a trading hub of the Outer Rim settled by a consortium of Galactic Core businesses. With the beginning of the Galactic Civil War, the planet lost its importance, becoming a backwater. It became the headquarters of the Glasfir Ring, which took over the local underworld, eliminating Hutt competition. The local government was coerced by Glasfir'a'lik to leave the crime ring alone.
