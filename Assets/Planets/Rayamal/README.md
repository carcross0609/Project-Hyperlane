# Rayamal

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rayamal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rayamal)

Rayamal was the sixth planet orbiting the star of Julevia in the Julevian system. Located in the Dalonbian sector of the Outer Rim Territories, it was a gas giant.
