# Rocedila

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rocedila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rocedila)

Rocedila was a planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. During her time at the University of Bar'leth, eventual rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra wrote her third-year paper about the planet. Sometime later, during the scourge of the droids, Aphra planned to drop the Tagge Corporation flagship, the Acquisitor, into the planet to destroy the ship and end the scourge threat aboard the vessel.
