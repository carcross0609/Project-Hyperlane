# Eufornis Major

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eufornis Major](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eufornis_Major)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Eufornis Major`, Core Worlds — map position (-103.5, -3823.3, -27.4)

Eufornis Major, sometimes referred to as simply Eufornis, was a prestigious ecumenopolis planet located in the galaxy's Core Worlds. Due to their similar names, Eufornis Major was occasionally confused with Eufornis Minor, a remote Outer Rim colony world. In 5 BBY, Telgordo Travel operated a transport service from Coruscant that stopped at Eufornis Major, as well as Plexis and Hosnian Prime.
