# Exegol

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Exegol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exegol)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Exegol`, Unknown Regions, grid F-7 — map position (-8968.6, 3559.2, -67.3)

Exegol (also spelled Ixigul in ancient texts) was a dark desert planet located within the Unknown Regions that, according to legend, was the hidden redoubt world of the Sith. It was the throneworld of the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, publicly known as Emperor Palpatine, and the Sith Eternal by 35 ABY. It was also the birthplace of Supreme Leader Snoke, whom Sidious created to rule the First Order while concealing himself in the shadows. Exegol was used to hide the Final Order, which was a fleet amassed by the Sith Eternal, from where they planned to deploy their Star Destroyers and establish a new Sith Empire for Darth Sidious. Exegol was the site of a battle during the war between the First Order and the Resistance, which saw the defeat of the Sith Eternal.
