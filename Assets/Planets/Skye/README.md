# Skye

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Skye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skye)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Skye`, Outer Rim, grid H-19 — map position (-4836.0, -14881.5, 306.9)

Skye was a small terrestrial planet located in the Marat system of the Varada sector, within the Outer Rim and the Western Reaches. Around 394 BBY, the human Axel Greylark—the son of the Galactic Republic Senator Kyong Greylark—visited several places around the galaxy before eventually traveling to Skye.

In 382 BBY, Path of the Open Hand cult members Binnot Ullo and Goi Ganok transported a load of the klytobacter bioweapon to the Outer Rim's Eiram system and, when caught, lied that they had instead been hauling protein concentrates from a station near Skye to the planet Shuraden when they were attacked by pirates. When being questioned on the Eiram system's moon Eirie, Ullo continued reinforcing his prior story, though the Jedi and royals sent to question the pair remained suspicious of their claims. Eventually, Gamok inadvertently revealed that he and Ullo hailed from the planet Dalna instead, bringing their ruse story about Skye to an end.
