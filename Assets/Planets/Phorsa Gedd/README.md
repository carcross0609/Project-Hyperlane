# Phorsa Gedd

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phorsa Gedd](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phorsa_Gedd)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Phorsa Gedd`, Mid Rim, grid L-10 — map position (780.4, -1040.3, -44.2)

Phorsa Gedd was a Mid Rim Territories planet that had factory-deserts. The Imperial soldier Vryant served in the planet's trenches for two years, and the Rebel Alliance's 61st Mobile Infantry also fought on Phorsa Gedd as a part of the Alliance's campaign into the Mid Rim, which spanned nine months from around 1 ABY. The engineer Vifra also joined the 61st off of the world between 2 ABY and 3 ABY. Members of the company remembered and spoke about the battle in the time afterward. In 4 ABY, when the New Republic pilots Wyl Lark and Nath Tensent discussed their worst nights in the wilderness, one of them mentioned spending a night on Phorsa Gedd.
