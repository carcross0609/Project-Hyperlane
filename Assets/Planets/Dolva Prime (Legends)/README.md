# Dolva Prime

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dolva Prime/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dolva_Prime/Legends)

Dolva Prime was a planet located in the Semagi sector of the Mid Rim. Boba Fett ambushed Edo and Rolu Strom in the Old Hallet prairie of Dolva Prime in 2 ABY in order to recover a fake Arachedron sculpture.
