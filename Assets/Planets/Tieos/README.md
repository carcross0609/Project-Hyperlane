# Tieos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tieos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tieos)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tieos`, Outer Rim, grid S-15 — map position (10630.9, -8493.8, 239.8)

Tieos was an agriworld in the Tieos system, one of hundreds located within the Abrion sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Sometime after the outbreak of the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and Separatist Alliance, Tieos and the rest of the sector were led in defection from the Republic to the Confederacy by Senator Esu Rotsino, who had represented the sector in the Galactic Senate. Following its defection, Rotsino went on to represent the planet alongside the rest of the Abrion sector in the Separatist Senate, while its supply of foodstuffs were desired to be utilized by the Confederacy in order to force starving star systems into joining them.
