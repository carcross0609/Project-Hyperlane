# Three Ellas

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Three Ellas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Ellas)

The Three Ellas was the collective name given to the Freeworlds of Dorella, Pernella and Sorella in the Tapani sector. They were situated along the Three Ellas Run. These three ore-rich systems were heavily settled with mining installations. The Three Ellas were receptive to fringe communities.
