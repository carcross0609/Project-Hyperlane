# Barcaria

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Barcaria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barcaria)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Barcaria`, Mid Rim, grid K-16 — map position (-1282.5, -10040.8, 360.3)

Barcaria was a planet located in the Barcaria system, a part of the Sombure sector of the Mid Rim. It was the origin point of the commodity known as Barcarian claw salt.

The parents of the Human Jace Malcom had a ranch on Barcaria where he spent his youth. Upon coming of age at some point by 3681 BBY, Malcom, wishing to see the rest of the galaxy, enlisted with the Republic Military and was posted off-world. At one point by 0 BBY, the smuggler Han Solo visited Barcaria on a mission for members of the Hutt species.
