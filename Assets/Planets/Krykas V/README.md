# Krykas V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krykas V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krykas_V)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Krykas V`, Mid Rim — map position (668.3, -11865.4, 307.3)

Krykas V was an uninhabited planet in the Mid Rim, covered in mud, briers, and thorns. The planet was blanketed a thick, tan fog consisting of clouds of particulate matter with strong electromagnetic charges strong enough to knock out most ship's sensors. The planet was located in the middle of an asteroid field.
