# Fedalle

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fedalle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fedalle/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Fedalle`, Core Worlds, grid M-10 — map position (2097.2, -817.1, -103.5)

Fedalle was a powerful and wealthy Core Worlds industrial planet located on the Hydian Way. It was home to a population known as the Fedalleans. Located in space explored between 27,500 BBY and 25,000 BBY, Fedalle had become a part of the Galactic Republic by around 22,000 BBY and was aligned with the Republic's capital world, Coruscant, during the early Alsakan Conflicts. By the time of the Mandalorian Wars, Fedalle had been visited by the Republic Hammerhead-class cruiser Sanctuary. Millennia later, the planet was a part of the Galactic Empire, eventually serving as an Imperial fortress world. By 11 ABY, Fedalle was represented in the New Republic by Senator Hrekin Thorm.
