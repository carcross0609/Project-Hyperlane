# Maateen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Maateen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maateen)

Maateen was a planet in the galaxy. During the Cold War between the Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic, the strong repression of Maateen authorities forced the Exchange to recruit new personnel to assure weapons shipment delivery to the planet.
