# Oslumpex V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oslumpex V/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oslumpex_V/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Oslumpex V`, Outer Rim, grid S-4 — map position (11085.2, 8715.7, 138.3)

Oslumpex V was a planet in the Corporate Sector's Oslumpex system, located at the crossroads of the hyperlanes known as the Authority Arc and the Shaltin Tunnels. Situated near the Corporate Sector's border with the Wyl sector, Oslumpex V served as a convenient entrance point for outside goods, and was a rich world as a result. The Corporate Sector Authority's largest shipyard was located on Oslumpex V, along with a headquarters for starshipwrights' unions and drydocks that serviced many smuggler and tramp starships. One of the companies that called Oslumpex V home was Starshipwrights and Aerospace Engineers Incorporated, which worked on Han Solo's Millennium Falcon during his smuggling days—and were left stiffed on a bill for 2,500 credits.
