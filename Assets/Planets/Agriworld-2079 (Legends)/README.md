# Agriworld-2079

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Agriworld-2079/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agriworld-2079/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Agriworld-2079`, Mid Rim, grid L-7 — map position (1459.2, 4272.2, -84.9)

Agriworld-2079 was a planet located on the spinward edge of the Mid Rim. Originally, it was the ancestral homeworld of a species of primitive nomadic herders. The M'shinn from Genassa later colonized the planet. The newcomers fenced in the nomadic herders, claimed their land, and left the peaceful aliens to figure out how to survive. Since that time, the original names of the inhabitants and their homeworld were lost to history, and the M'shinni imposed their own names instead. The planet became one of several vital M'shinni agriworlds, producing a vast array of agricultural products that were shipped throughout the galaxy.
