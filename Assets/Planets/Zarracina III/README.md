# Zarracina III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zarracina III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zarracina_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Zarracina III`, Expansion Region, grid O-14 — map position (5183.2, -6250.1, -231.2)

Zarracina III was a planet located in the Expansion Region's Zarracina sector. It was the homeworld of the Zarracine species. When a stable hyperspace jump point was discovered in the Zarracina system in 11,660 BBY, the Pius Dea–controlled Galactic Republic attempted to acquire Zarracina III.

However, the Zarracines rejected Republic envoys, and their entire ruling council committed suicide when the Republic attempted to conquer the planet. In response, Zarracina III was subjected to an orbital bombardment that devastated the planet and forced the few surviving Zarracines to adopt a nomadic existence.
