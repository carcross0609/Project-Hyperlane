# Gattering

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gattering](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gattering)

Gattering was a desert planet located in the Gattering system in the Noolian sector of the Mid Rim Territories. In 19 BBY, a group of enslavers operated from a base on the planet until they were killed by the Sith Lord Darth Vader.
