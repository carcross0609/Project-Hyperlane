# Forscan IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Forscan IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forscan_IV)

Forscan IV was a planet located in the Forscan system within the Auril sector. At some time prior to his mission to Ord Cestus, Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi traveled to Forscan IV for a mission. He had several close calls there, during one of which his padawan Anakin Skywalker saved his life.
