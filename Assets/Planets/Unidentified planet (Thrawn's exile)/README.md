# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Thrawn's exile)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Thrawn%27s_exile%29)

A planet in Wild Space was used by the Chiss Thrawn, following his battle against the Grysk Hegemony, to fake his exile to eventually join the Galactic Empire. Around 15 BBY, Imperial Captain Voss Parck came to the planet in search of smugglers, only to instead come across Thrawn's hut.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified planet
- **Region:** Wild Space
- **Class:** Terrestrial
- **Atmosphere:** Breathable
- **Terrain:** Forests
- **Flora:** Pyussh berry
- **Fauna:** A small animal
- **Population:** At least 1: Thrawn
