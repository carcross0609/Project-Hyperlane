# Drearia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Drearia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drearia)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Drearia`, Inner Rim, grid L-8 — map position (1190.8, 2137.5, -32.5)

Drearia was a world located in the Drearia system of the Inner Rim and the Interior. It was situated in grid square L-8 of the Standard Galactic Grid, and the super-hyperroute known as the Hydian Way linked Drearia to the Paqualis system as well as the world Champala. Drearia shared its name with the company Drearian Defense Conglomerate.

In 9 ABY, Drearia fell within the borders of the New Republic. Around the same year, while the astronomer Kh'ymm was searching her archive of worlds on the Observatory Moon in order to determine the potential location of the lost planet At Attin, Drearia's name was listed on her computer screen among other worlds.
