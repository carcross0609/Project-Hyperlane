# Yetoom Na Uun

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yetoom Na Uun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yetoom_Na_Uun)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Yetoom Na Uun`, Mid Rim, grid L-18 — map position (475.2, -12236.5, -26.7)

Yetoom Na Uun was a planet located within the Yetoom system of the Senex sector. Positioned at the sector's edge, Yetoom was a shadowport controlled by gangs of Senex-Juvex slave species, and was the headquarters of Fargednim P'taan. It was often visited by disreputable nobles from the Senex and Juvex Houses.
