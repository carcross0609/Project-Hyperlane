# Mobetta

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mobetta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mobetta)

Mobetta was a world located in the Mobetta system, within the Airam sector of the Outer Rim Territories. A space battle between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire took place near Mobetta during the Galactic Civil War.
