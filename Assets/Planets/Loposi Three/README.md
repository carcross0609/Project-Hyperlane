# Loposi Three

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Loposi Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loposi_Three)

Loposi Three was a planet in the Loposi system of the Mid Rim's Dustig sector. Loposi Three's moon possessed an artificial atmosphere of nutrient-rich gases that were derived from liquid kolto, a medicinal plant. The Loposi system was later a member of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars.
