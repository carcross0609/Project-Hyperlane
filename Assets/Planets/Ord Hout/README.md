# Ord Hout

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Hout](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Hout)

Ord Hout was a world located in the Ord Hout system. It was situated in the Brema sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Ord Hout served as an Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost for the Galactic Republic during the Pius Dea Era, a period of time that lasted between approximately 12,000 BBY and 11,000 BBY.
