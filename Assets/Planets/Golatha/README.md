# Golatha

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Golatha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golatha)

Golatha was a small planet located in the Golatha system in the Dalicron sector of the Outer Rim Territories, near the Golatha Nebula. During the Clone Wars the Confederacy of Independent Systems produced Megadroids on the planet.
