# Artus Five

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Artus Five](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Artus_Five)

Artus Five was a lifeless gas giant in the Artus system of the Kwymar sector in the Outer Rim Territories. Following the destruction of Dominator, Darth Jadus faked his death and choose the orbit of Artus Five as a place to hide his secret dreadnought.
