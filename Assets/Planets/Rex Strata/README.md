# Rex Strata

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rex Strata](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rex_Strata)

Rex Strata was a planet and the homeworld of the technician Faraz. Jedi Master Zia Zaldor Zanna trained on this planet during her time as a Jedi trainee.
