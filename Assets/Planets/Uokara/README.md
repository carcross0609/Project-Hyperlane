# Uokara

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Uokara](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uokara)

Uokara was a fertile class IV planet in the Unknown Regions. For millions of years, it was inhabited by the pathogen Ghymnon, who covered it entirely from sea to sea and killed thousands of would-be conquerors and despots. In the Imperial Era, the Sith Lord Darth Vader surveyed the world for the Galactic Empire and incorrectly reported it uninhabited.
