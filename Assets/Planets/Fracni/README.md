# Fracni

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fracni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fracni)

Fracni was a small planet located near the Juinkwy Triangle, a confluence of hyperspace routes. The Jedi Wayseekers Ty Yorrick and Vernestra Rwoh once stopped pirates from scamming Fracni's populace with bootleg bacta, and Rwoh later reminisced that their conversation while resting in one of Fracni's cities afterwards was when Yorrick first told Rwoh that she was considering leaving the Jedi Order.
