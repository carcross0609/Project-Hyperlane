# At Achrann

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [At Achrann](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/At_Achrann)

At Achrann was a planet within the galaxy that was one of the nine Jewels of the Old Republic. During the New Republic Era, At Achrann was a war-torn world that greatly resembled At Attin, with two tribes—the Troik and Hattan—fighting each other. The Myykian Neel encountered the human Hayna on At Achrann.
