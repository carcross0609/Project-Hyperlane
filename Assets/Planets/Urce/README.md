# Urce

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Urce](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Urce)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Urce`, Mid Rim, grid L-6 — map position (1107.5, 4646.4, 198.9)

Urce was a Mid Rim planet located in the Lesser Plooriod Cluster. A member of the Galactic Republic since at least the Clone Wars, during the Galactic Civil War Urce served the Galactic Empire as a regional seat of power and was the site of the palace of High Inquisitor Mox Slosin. In 12 ABY, the Imperial Remnant was victorious over the New Republic at a confrontation at Urce.
