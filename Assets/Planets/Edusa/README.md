# Edusa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Edusa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Edusa)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Edusa`, Outer Rim, grid O-5 — map position (5445.6, 7247.3, 61.2)

Edusa was an industrial planet located in the Outer Rim Territories' Morshdine sector. During the Great Galactic War, the reconstituted Sith Empire enslaved the Twi'lek girl Leontyne Saresh there, and she eventually incited a revolt of enslaved beings on the planet before departing for Galactic Republic space.

Edusa was located within the borders of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars, and, during the Galactic Civil War, pirates displaying the Blazing Claw emblem were frequently encountered there. The Yuuzhan Vong eventually conquered the Edusa system during their invasion of the galaxy.
