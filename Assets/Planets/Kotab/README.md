# Kotab

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kotab](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kotab)

Kotab was a planet located in the Kotab system of the Inner Rim Territories and the Slice. It was situated in grid square N-10 of the Standard Galactic Grid. The world was one of the homeworlds of the sentient Kotabi bond-twins Ceret and Terec, alongside the planet Sagamore where the twins originated from.
