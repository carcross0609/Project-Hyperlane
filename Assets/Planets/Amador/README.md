# Amador

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Amador](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amador)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Amador`, Expansion Region, grid L-8 — map position (750.7, 1757.8, 65.3)

Amador was a planet located in the Chalenor system of the Expansion Region's Immalia sector, and was orbited by at least one moon named Mayvitch 7. Situated in the Northern Dependencies, Amador lay at the junction of a hyperlane that connected the Coth Fuuras system and the planet Nessem and a second route that linked it to the Drearia system. Sometime between 3665 and 3653 BBY, a battle was fought on Amador during the Great Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire, where the Chagrian soldier Fideltin Rusk defended the entrance of a fortress on Amador from a much larger force of Imperial soldiers. Over three thousand years later, a group of Human colonists on Mayvitch 7 were wiped out by the Yinchorri species during the Yinchorri Uprising of 33 BBY.
