# Hazun

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hazun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hazun)

Hazun was a planet located in the Astal sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Hazun was the homeworld of Princess Inaya and her royal family.
