# Excarga

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Excarga/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Excarga/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Excarga`, Outer Rim, grid T-16 — map position (12494.9, -9873.8, -399.6)

Excarga was a mining world in the Outer Rim Territories. The planet had ties to the Mining Guild.

Excarga was the site of the First and Second Battles of Excarga in 22 BBY, during the Clone Wars; in the former, the planet was captured by the Confederacy of Independent Systems; in the latter, liberated by the Galactic Republic.
