# Gon Tiek

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gon Tiek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gon_Tiek)

Gon Tiek was a world located within the Gon Tiek system, a part of the Atravis sector in the Western Reaches portion of the Outer Rim Territories. At some point after the establishment of the Galactic Empire in 19 BBY, the 501st Legion, an elite Imperial stormtrooper unit, fought on Gon Tiek.
