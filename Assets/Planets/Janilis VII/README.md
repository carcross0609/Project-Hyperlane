# Janilis VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Janilis VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Janilis_VII)

Janilis VII was an Outer Rim Territories planet situated in the Cronese Mandate sector of the Tion Cluster. It was one of the cradles of the Cronese Human civilization and was part of the Kingdom of Cron. Janilis VII hosted several archives that were still in existence twenty-five millennia later, although the planet itself had become rundown and its society was engaged in subsistence economy.
