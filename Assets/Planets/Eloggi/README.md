# Eloggi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eloggi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eloggi)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Eloggi`, Outer Rim — map position (-3257.5, -12698.8, -166.0)

Eloggi was the second planet in the Endor system, on the Outer Rim of the galaxy. It was a frozen, barren rock that possessed no moons. The Galactic Empire established a mining colony on the planet. In the wake of the Battle of Endor, the New Republic allowed a number of Sullustans to take over the operations there.
