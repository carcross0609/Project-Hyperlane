# Kizan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kizan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kizan)

Kizan was a world that housed a number of academics, including students who traveled to Kizan's glass towers to study the philosophies of other cultures. Pundith, a key city on Kizan, was destroyed by the Sith Lord Darth Caldoth in order to lure the Jedi Master Bran Ath'Morath to the world.
