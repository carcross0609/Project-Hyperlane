# Daronak

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Daronak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daronak)

Daronak was a terrestrial planet located in the Daronak system, a part of the Phelleem sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was the site of Channoga Boulevard.

At one point during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, a team of Alliance agents were making contact with a Rebel cell on Daronak. However, an individual they had dealt with was in reality an informer who reported them to the Empire, leading to Imperial stormtroopers pursuing the agents who in turn ran down Channoga Boulevard. Eventually, several bounty hunters employing thermal detonators also engaged the team, which ultimately made its way off-planet.
