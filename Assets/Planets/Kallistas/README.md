# Kallistas

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kallistas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kallistas)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kallistas`, Outer Rim, grid O-18 — map position (4650.7, -12919.5, 55.9)

Kallistas was a mining colony next to the Revkinn system in the Parmel sector. Its low mountains covered radioactive elements and rare gemstones. Numerous pirate bases were located on the planet. Grand Admiral Osvald Teshik spent part of his childhood, especially during the summers, on this planet.
