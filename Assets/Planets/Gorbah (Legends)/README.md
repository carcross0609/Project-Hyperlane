# Gorbah

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gorbah/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gorbah/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gorbah`, Outer Rim — map position (-4908.0, -10478.0, 25.8)

Gorbah was a planet and the location of four secret fighter bases under control of a rogue group attempting to take out invading Imperial forces. When they realized there was no hope in beating the Empire, they fled their bases immediately.
