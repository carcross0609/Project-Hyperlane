# Kylah Lohmata's homeworld

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kylah Lohmata's homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kylah_Lohmata%27s_homeworld)

A world in Hutt Space that had never seen peace or justice was the homeworld of the human girl Kylah Lohmata. Lohmata's father died in a blaster fight when she was young, leading her and her mother to leave the world and travel for some time, eventually settling for several months in a migrant community on Chalacta.
