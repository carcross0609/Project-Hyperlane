# Metagos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Metagos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Metagos)

Metagos was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories. Its capital city, New Xaxxis, was situated in underground caverns derisively known as the Glass Abyss. Jedi Master Mace Windu went on a mission to the planet after the death of Qui-Gon Jinn to free the Sa'ad clan from crime lords.
