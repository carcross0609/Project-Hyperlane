# Ramook

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ramook](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ramook)

Ramook was an agriworld located just beyond the far end of the Chiloon Rift.

In 45 ABY, Jedi Knight Ben Skywalker was investigating the planet for signs of Ship, a Sith Meditation Sphere controlled by Vestara Khai, a former member of the Lost Tribe of Sith.
