# Elbriade

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Elbriade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elbriade)

Elbriade was a planet where the narcotic Blue could be found. It was possible that a drug trafficking ring headed by the Galactic Empire could be traced there. The planet was annexed into the Empire during the Galactic Civil War. During this time, a group of Rebel investigators went under cover on Sanod to find the source of the drug trafficking.
