# Aeos Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aeos Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aeos_Prime)

Aeos Prime, also simply known as Aeos, was an ocean planet in the Aeos system of the Outer Rim Territories, home to the aquatic Aeosian species. A group of Aeosians allowed the Rebel Alliance to construct an outpost on the planet, which was later attacked by the First Order during its war against the Resistance.

Some time after the attack, the space station Colossus attempted to find refuge on Aeos Prime, but a tribe of Aeosians initially did not welcome them due to various misunderstandings. The Aeosians eventually allowed the Colossus and its inhabitants to stay on the planet after they discovered that the Colossus was not intentionally hostile, and shortly after a mutually beneficial agreement was made between the Aosians and the Colossus. Later they were discovered by the First Order, who returned to Aeos to attack and/or capture the Colossus. With the help of the Aeosians, the space station managed to escape into hyperspace. The First Order later initiated orbital bombardment on an area of the planet in retaliation for aiding the Colossus.
