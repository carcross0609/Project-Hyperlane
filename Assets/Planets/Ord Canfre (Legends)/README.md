# Ord Canfre

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Canfre/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Canfre/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ord Canfre`, Outer Rim, grid K-5 — map position (-393.4, 6212.1, -27.5)

Ord Canfre was an Outer Rim Territories world located in the Cassander sector. Having served as a Galactic Republic Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost during the Pius Dea Era, millennia later Ord Canfre hosted a facility of the Jedi Order's Exploration Corps. While there, the Jedi Bowspritz first encountered akk dogs. During the Clone Wars, the Republic surrendered the world to the Confederacy of Independent Systems after the Battle of Ord Canfre. Ord Canfre came under the control of the Pentastar Alignment and Imperial Remnant decades later, and its star system was conquered by the Yuuzhan Vong during their invasion of the galaxy.
