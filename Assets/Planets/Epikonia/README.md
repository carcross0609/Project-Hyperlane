# Epikonia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Epikonia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Epikonia)

Epikonia was a terrestrial tech-focused planet located in the Ojoster sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was a communications hub that relayed and boosted signals from its sector to other parts of the galaxy.
