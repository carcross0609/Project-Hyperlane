# Andando

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Andando](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andando)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Andando`, Mid Rim, grid Q-15 — map position (8475.6, -8257.9, 174.3)

Andando was the innermost planet of the Ando system, a star system located within the Lambda sector of the galaxy's Mid Rim and the Slice region. Ando's sister planet, it orbited the star Ando and had no moons. Andando was ravaged in the aftermath of an Aqualish war several decades after the species discovered space travel. They systematically stripped the planet of its resources and blasted its surface into molten rock, rendering Andando uninhabitable.
