# Reaper's World

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Reaper's World/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reaper%27s_World/Legends)

Reaper's World was a planet in the Nilgaard sector. It was the sector capital, colonized c. 5000 BBY; at the time it was idyllic, with green oceans and blue mountains. Nilgaard Bioprocessing used up the planet's natural resources, leaving it in an ice age of glaciers. The planet had urban cityplexes.
