# Skustell

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skustell/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skustell/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Skustell`, Outer Rim, grid N-19 — map position (3923.1, -14549.4, -601.6)

Skustell was an humid planet in the Skustell Cluster of the Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld of the Ongree, a bipedal, humpbacked, amphibious sentient species. Even after Skustell became part of galactic society, Ongree were not widely seen away from their homeworld.

During the Old Republic Skustell was rumored that a golden waterfall on the planet hid the entrance of a cave, which contained treasures.
