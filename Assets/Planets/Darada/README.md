# Darada

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darada](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darada)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Darada`, Core Worlds, grid K-9 — map position (-390.6, 375.4, 32.6)

Darada was a planet on the edge of the Core Worlds. Jev Sunrider was a Jedi Watchman of Darada. His grandson, Andur Sunrider, spent the first nineteen years of his life there.

During the Cold War the Exchange delivered black market weapons to their allies on Darada.
