# Hirin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hirin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hirin)

Hirin, also known as OM814, was a barren rock planet with two moons. It occupied the fourth and outermost orbit around the red giant star Thrantin Major in the Thrantin OM81-A system, a part of the Ollonir Boundaries in the Negs portion of the Colonies.
