# Mytus V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mytus V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mytus_V)

Mytus V was the fifth planet in the Mytus system. It was unoccupied, being barren and inhospitable to humanoid life. Mytus V was the planet assigned to Pakka during Han Solo's scheme to determine Rekkon's assassin.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Mytus V
- **Region:** Wild Space
- **System:** Mytus system
- **Coordinates:** S-3
- **Suns:** 1: Mytus
- **Terrain:** Barren rock
