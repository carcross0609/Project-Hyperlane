# Sarvchi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sarvchi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarvchi)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Sarvchi`, Unknown Regions, grid F-9 — map position (-8493.6, 1470.7, 345.9)

Sarvchi was a planet in the Sarvchi system, a star system that lay in the eastern part of Chiss Space in the Unknown Regions. A number of Chiss migrated to Sarvchi after the climate of the species' homeworld, Csilla, grew less habitable.

Sarvchi was a location of significance to the Chiss Pommrio family. When Pommrio military personnel received an emergency summons relating to a supposed unclaimed nyix mine on the world of Hoxim, they were asked to rendezvous at Sarvchi.

During the Chiss Ascendancy crisis, Sarvchi was the site of a standoff between Chaf and Ufsa Chiss families.
