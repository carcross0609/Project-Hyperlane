# Zayron

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zayron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zayron)

Zayron was an agricultural planet located at the outer edge of the galaxy. During the Imperial Era, the Jedi Knight Tensu Run, a survivor of Order 66, led a group of Jedi who resided on Zayron.
