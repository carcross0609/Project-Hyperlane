# Plexis

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Plexis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plexis)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Plexis`, Core Worlds, grid L-13 — map position (131.4, -4667.6, 224.4)

Plexis was a relatively small planet located in the Plexis system in the Core Worlds region of the galaxy. During the Age of the Empire, it housed a number of spice gangs, all of which were under the authority of the Zenowai Crime Society via an overseer named Shoja Ko. Telgordo Travel ran travel services from Coruscant to Plexis, Hosnian Prime, and Eufornis Major from the Coruscant Eastern Spaceport.

By 3 ABY, a viper probe droid designated Viper 071-V-8852 had been sent to Plexis where it undertook an eight week mission to observe the spaceport and recorded any activity of spice or weapons smuggling.
