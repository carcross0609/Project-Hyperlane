# Arbran III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arbran III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arbran_III)

Arbran III was the third planet in orbit around the star Arbran. It was located in the Arbran system in the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector, a part of the Trailing Sectors portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Arbran III's surface consisted of ice, and it was orbited by two moons.
