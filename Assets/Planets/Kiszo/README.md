# Kiszo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kiszo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kiszo)

Kiszo was a small planet located in the Kiszo system of the Outer Rim's Lol sector. During the High Republic Era, members of the Church of the Force religious group lived there by the time between around 170 BBY and around 160 BBY. At that time, the Mirialan Jedi Master Vernestra Rwoh visited Kiszo during her travels as a Jedi Wayseeker and learned the common phrase "let us partake of the bounty of the Force." Around 160 BBY, Rwoh briefly spoke about her time on Kiszo with Jedi Master Yaddle, who was also familiar with the refrain from the planet.
