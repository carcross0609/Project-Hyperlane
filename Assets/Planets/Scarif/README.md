# Scarif

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scarif](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scarif)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Scarif`, Outer Rim, grid S-15 — map position (11100.4, -8160.1, -266.3)

Scarif was a remote, tropical planet located in the Abrion sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Although a small and idyllic world, Scarif played an important part in the Galactic Empire's military-industrial complex, becoming the center for top-secret research beyond inspection distance of the prying eyes of the Imperial Senate. Among the projects stored here was the Death Star battle station, a construction project so important that the entire planet was covered by a planetary deflector shield that could only be entered via the Shield Gate, a Golan M3185 space station.
