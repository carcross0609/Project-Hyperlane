# Brynom

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Brynom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brynom)

Brynom was a terrestrial planet located in the galaxy. During the Clone Wars, Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker and his Padawan Ahsoka Tano followed a distress call from Brynom and were temporarily captured by a squad of Separatist B1-series battle droids.
