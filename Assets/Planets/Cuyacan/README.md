# Cuyacan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cuyacan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cuyacan)

Cuyacan was a planet located in the Cuyacan system within the Sepan sector of the Slice and Expansion Region, in grid square P-13 on the Standard Galactic Grid, that had a princess some time after the fall of the Galactic Empire. It was said that the smuggler Ana Tolla had stolen a diamond choker from the princess while she was still wearing it.

At some point after the Garsea family left the planet Batuu in 21 ABY, the Frinn Mak Traveling Circus was heading to Cuyacan when they rescued the smuggler Izal Garsea aboard her starship, the Meridian, after it ran out of fuel and became stranded in space. The historian Eloc Throno included the planet on a map in his book, Traveler's Guide to Batuu, which was published in 34 ABY.
