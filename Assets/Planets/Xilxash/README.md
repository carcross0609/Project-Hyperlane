# Xilxash

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xilxash](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xilxash)

Xilxash was an agricultural planet that applied for and was granted Galactic Alliance membership in 44 ABY. It was a small backwater world, known for its delicious brul fruit.
