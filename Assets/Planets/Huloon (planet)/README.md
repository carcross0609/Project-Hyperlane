# Huloon; The Bootana Hutta region, with Huloon represented by the number 13

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Huloon (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huloon_%28planet%29)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Huloon`, Hutt Space, grid S-11 — map position (11555.9, -1578.9, 196.3)

Huloon was a planet located in the Huloon system within Bootana Hutta near the center of Hutt Space. A part of Hutt Space, it was formerly the home to a slave species also called Huloons before it was depopulated by the Hutts and turned into a nature preserve. After they had lost their home, small populations of Huloons resided on many worlds outside the Bootana Hutta and yearned to return. However, those desperate for a glimpse of their stolen home would often suffer ridicule and torment at the hands of the Hutts.
