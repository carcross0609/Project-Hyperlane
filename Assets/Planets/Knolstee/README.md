# Knolstee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Knolstee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knolstee)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Knolstee`, Outer Rim, grid R-3 — map position (10205.7, 9077.1, -104.1)

Knolstee was a near-virgin planet in the Corporate Sector owned by Park Holdings Inc. Park Holdings used the planet as a layover for traveling tourists. Sightseers were welcome to stay in small luxurious orbital stations before traveling to Knolstee's surface, where they could take tours lead by Park Holdings that consisted primarily of Ithorians.
