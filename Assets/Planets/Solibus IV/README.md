# Solibus IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Solibus IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solibus_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Solibus IV`, Expansion Region, grid K-15 — map position (-772.7, -8559.3, 55.0)

Solibus IV was a planet visited by Aayla Secura during her apprenticeship to Quinlan Vos. It was there that the two Jedi would open the Shu-Wang Prism and solve the Mystery of the Lodi.
