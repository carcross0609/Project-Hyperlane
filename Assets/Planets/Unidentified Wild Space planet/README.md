# Unidentified Wild Space planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Wild Space planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Wild_Space_planet)

Centuries before the Great Galactic War, Jedi Master Raniah and her followers found refuge on an uninhabited planet in Wild Space after their were expelled from the Jedi Order. Unfortunately for them, they were soon discovered by the Sith Emperor and were captured and enslaved by the Sith Empire. They eventually became servants of the Emperor and Raniah became Darth Nul. Centuries later, during the Third Galactic War, the ruins of Raniah's camp were discovered by Sana-Rae and Arcann of the Eternal Alliance, revealing the fate of Master Raniah and her followers.
