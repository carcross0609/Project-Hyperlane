# Kobal's World

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kobal's World/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kobal%27s_World/Legends)

Kobal's World was a planet in the Koradin sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Kobal's World was the site of significant Imperial atrocities during the era of the Galactic Civil War, including the slaughter of a number of women and children by stormtroopers. One of those stormtroopers later accompanied Darth Vader to Cheelit, where the Guild of Vindicators assassin Clat the Shamer used the memories to compel the soldier to commit suicide.
