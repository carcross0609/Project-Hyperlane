# Danos V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Danos V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Danos_V)

Danos V was an astronomical body in the Danos system of the Outer Rim Territories' Shadola sector. Sting insects that were said to be large in comparison to other insects in the galaxy could be found on Danos V.
