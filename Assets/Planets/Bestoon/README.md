# Bestoon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bestoon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bestoon)

Bestoon was a planet located in the Bestoon system in the Colonies region of the galaxy in square J-9 on the Standard Galactic Grid, which also fell in the Interior. It was the homeworld of Ochi, a Bestoonian Sith assassin affiliated with the Sith Eternal cult. The assassin was often addressed as Ochi of Bestoon and wielded a ornate knife known as the Blade of Ochi of Bestoon. He also captained a ship named the Bestoon Legacy, which was left with his droid D-O after the hunter's demise in a vexis burrow on Pasaana. The planet also lent its name to the Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon, a pirate group that worked with Ochi during the Galactic Civil War and New Republic Era.
