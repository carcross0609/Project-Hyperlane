# Tallaan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tallaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tallaan)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tallaan`, Colonies, grid L-13 — map position (512.3, -5197.1, 30.2)

Tallaan was a planet in Tapani sector's Freeworlds Territory at the confluence of the Giju Run, the Procopian Shipping Lane, and the Shapani Bypass. It was best known for its starship production.

Tallaan was a voting member of the League of Tapani Freeworlds.
