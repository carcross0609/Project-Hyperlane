# Huulia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Huulia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huulia)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Huulia`, Colonies — map position (-361.9, -5349.1, 23.6)

Huulia was a planet located in the Huulia system of the Colonies. In 32 BBY, the nacelle from a Huulian starfighter was among the junk stored in the scrapyard of the Toydarian Watto's junkshop in the city of Mos Espa on the planet Tatooine. Huulia was the homeworld of Kit Valent, a pilot that perished flying for the Alliance to Restore the Republic's Rogue Squadron during the Battle of Hoth in 3 ABY.
