# Polarca VII

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Polarca VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Polarca_VII)

Polarca VII was a small planet located in a neighboring star system – approximately two hours hyperspace travel – to Foerost. 10,000 kilometers above the surface, Station 14 geosynchronously orbited the world.
