# Ord Mantell

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Mantell/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Mantell/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ord Mantell`, Mid Rim, grid L-7 — map position (71.1, 3824.5, 137.2)

Ord Mantell was a terrestrial planet which was located into the Bright Jewel system of the Bright Jewel sector in the Mid Rim Territories. It was located near Anobis and was originally settled by the Galactic Republic circa 12,000 BBY as an Ordnance/Regional Depot.

The planet was primarily known as a former industrial world, where large portions of the planet's surface was covered in immense junkyard fields, which served as the perfect lawless haven for many smugglers, mercenaries and bounty hunters of all stripes. Although a dangerous place, its strategic location on the Celanon Spur between the Expansion Region and the Outer Rim Territories gradually made the planet as the leading commercial hub not only of the Bright Jewel sector, but of the Dohu sector and Qiilura sector as well.
