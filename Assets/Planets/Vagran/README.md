# Vagran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vagran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vagran)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Vagran`, Core Worlds, grid M-11 — map position (2401.8, -1946.8, -35.9)

Vagran was a planet located in the Corellian sector with different temperate zones all varying in temperature and precipitation. Its climates and its favorable sunlight enabled Vagran to support a great deal of life.

During the Thrawn campaign, Talon Karrde's team obtained three GAT-12 Skipray Blastboats from Vagran.
