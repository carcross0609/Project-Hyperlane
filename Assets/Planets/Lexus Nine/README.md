# Lexus Nine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lexus Nine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lexus_Nine)

Lexus Nine was a planet in the galaxy that underwent an orbital bombardment sometime prior to the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. The bombardment created a rift under the planet's surface that caused massive cataclysms and wiped out the entire population.
