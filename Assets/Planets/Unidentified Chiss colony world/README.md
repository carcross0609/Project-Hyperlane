# Unidentified Chiss colony world

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Chiss colony world](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Chiss_colony_world)

A colony world was part of the Chiss species' Chiss Ascendancy nation. The planet was inhabited by several Chiss families. In 2 BBY, the Grysk warlike species' interstellar state known as the Grysk Hegemony kidnapped several young Chiss Force-sensitive girls who lived on the colony world, which they used as their navigators. Shortly after a battle between the same years against a group of Grysk war vessels, during which Galactic Empire Grand Admiral Thrawn's forces rescued the girls Admiral Ar'alani promised Thrawn that she would strengthen the colony world's security.
