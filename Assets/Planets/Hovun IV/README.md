# Hovun IV

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hovun IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hovun_IV)

Hovun IV, also known simply as Hovun, was a planet covered in light purple oceans, where the criminal Dvorad operated. The thief Tobias Beckett and his crew stole a supply of blank identichips from Dvorad whilst on the planet.
