# Genetia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Genetia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Genetia)

Genetia was a planet in the Genetia system in the Expansion Region, in grid square I-14 of the Standard Galactic Grid that was orbited by seven moons. It was inhabited by the Genetians, a human race whose Force-sensitive members tended to have powerful empathic abilities. Genetia was the homeworld of Imri Cantaros, as well as his father, and as a child he divided his time between Genetia and his mother's homeworld of Hynestia before he eventually ended up at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant where he spent his youngling years. The seven Genetian moons were used as an exclamation by the Nihil pirate Klinith Da after she saw the large cruiser Steady Wing, which she and fellow pirate Gwishi had been sent to destroy.
