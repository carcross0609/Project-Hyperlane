# Toledian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Toledian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Toledian)

Toledian was a planet located in the Toledian system within the Narrant sector of the galaxy's Mid Rim and Western Reaches, positioned in grid square I-16 on the Standard Galactic Grid. In 34 ABY, a mining accident destroyed Toledian's main city. The incident, and the chaos that followed, came to be known on the planet Batuu, where it was discussed by residents of Black Spire Outpost. Julen Rakab, a Batuuan resident, overheard a discussion about the accident while he was with the smuggler Izal Garsea at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo. The same year, the historian Eloc Throno included Toledian on a map in his book, Traveler's Guide to Batuu.
