# Shuldene

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shuldene/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shuldene/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Shuldene`, Outer Rim, grid J-18 — map position (-1720.5, -12465.0, 113.1)

Shuldene was a planet in the Yarith sector of the Outer Rim Territories' Greater Javin region. Formerly a waterworld with abundant aquatic life, Shuldene froze solid after undergoing an orbital shift. Tourists occasionally visited the planet to amaze at the carcasses of massive sea creatures poking through the glassy ice surface.
