# Schesa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Schesa/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Schesa/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Schesa`, Unknown Regions, grid F-8 — map position (-8397.4, 2499.6, -206.2)

Schesa was a planet located in the Schesa system of the Unknown Regions' Chiss Space. The hyperlane known as the Path of the Houses linked it to the Noris system, and it was also situated at the endpoint of the Way of Schesa hyperspace route, which connected it to the Pesfavri system and the Redoubt. Schesa was an outlying colony of the sentient Chiss species and was home to one of the Ruling Families that ruled over the Chiss Ascendancy, the government of the Chiss.

At some point by 28 ABY, Schesa's resident Ruling Family employed hives of the sentient Killiks as a work force. As a result, the Family fell out of power that year and became a casualty of a civil conflict against rival Ruling Families over the former's continued reliance on the Killiks.
