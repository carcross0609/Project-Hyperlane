# Mechis II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mechis II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mechis_II)

Mechis II was an industrial planet located in the Mechis system of the Inner Rim. By the time of the Cold War, the world's plant life had adapted to grow inside droids and other machinery.
