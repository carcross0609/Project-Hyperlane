# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Ilum system)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Ilum_system%29)

A planet was the outermost of the Ilum system. It was considered a black rock, and had no atmosphere and little gravity. In 18 BBY, after witnessing the Galactic Empire's mining of the planet Ilum, and needing to get out of range of the Star Destroyers present before they spotted her, Ahsoka Tano landed on this planet.
