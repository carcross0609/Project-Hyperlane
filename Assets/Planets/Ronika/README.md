# Ronika

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ronika](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ronika)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ronika`, Other, grid L-10 — map position (376.8, -210.3, -95.7)

Ronika was a terrestrial prison planet located in the galaxy. During the Galactic Civil War, Eara Lota, the heir to the monarchy of the planet Empress Teta, frequently protested the living conditions on Ronika or the working conditions for miners elsewhere in the system, with her protests being covered in local holovids.
