# Gerrenthum

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gerrenthum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gerrenthum)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gerrenthum`, Outer Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-1353.6, -12610.6, 262.6)

Gerrenthum was a planet located in the Gerrenthum system of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories, serving as the Anoat sector's capital after the previous capital, Anoat, was rendered uninhabitable. For many years, Imperial Governor Ubrik Adelhard governed the sector with an iron fist and absolute loyalty to the Galactic Empire. Orbited by a security station, many business owners and politicians on Gerrenthum openly bribed Imperials to ignore their illegal activities.

By 5 ABY, Gerrenthum was no longer affiliated with the Empire's loyalist forces. When listing a fake scenario to her commanding officer Soran Keize, Yrica Quell of the Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing suggested that Gerrenthum and Fedovoi End, another planet which had split from the loyalist faction, would pledge allegiance to a reformed Imperial High Command.
