# Bengely Four

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bengely Four](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bengely_Four)

Bengely Four was a world in the galaxy that was settled by a group of Kitonaks. During the Cold War, the Kitonak colonists began to rapidly undergo a series of benign genetic mutations, the source of which was traced to a viral infection augmented by a rare chemical in the colony's air supply.
