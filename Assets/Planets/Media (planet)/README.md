# Media

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Media (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Media_%28planet%29)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Media`, Outer Rim, grid S-4 — map position (10837.5, 8863.0, -320.6)

Media was an urban world located in the Media system, a part of the Outer Rim Territories' Corporate Sector. It was connected to the Lucaya system by the hyperlane known as the Lucaya Cross. Media's economy relied on light industry, and several prominent manufacturers of repulsorlift units and related accessories were based there. A substantial portion of the world's population consisted of immigrants of the Tiss'shar species.
