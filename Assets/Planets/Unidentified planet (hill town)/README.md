# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (hill town)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28hill_town%29)

A planet that contained hills and rivers was controlled by the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era. Between 18 BBY and 15 BBY, the human rebels Luthen Rael and Kleya Marki were present on the planet.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified planet
- **Class:** Terrestrial
- **Atmosphere:** Type I (breathable)
- **Terrain:**

  - Rivers
- **Interest:** A hill town
- **Otherspecies:**

  - A long-legged species
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
