# Allst Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Allst Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Allst_Prime)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Allst Prime`, Other, grid S-7 — map position (11480.7, 3341.0, -11.7)

Allst Prime was a backwater planet in the Mid Rim Territories where Governor Magé rule over an impoverished population during the Galactic Empire's reign. Between 2 BBY and 1 BBY, the Rebel Alliance's 61st Mobile Infantry was deployed into Allst Prime to destroy the Imperial outpost there. During the battle, the rebel soldier, secretly a bounty hunter, Brand rescued one of her comrades and then joined Captain Micha Evon to incapacitate Magé at her headquarters. Brand then intended to collect the bounty on Evon, but the captain convinced her to stay with the 61st.
