# Telerath

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Telerath](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Telerath)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Telerath`, Inner Rim, grid N-7 — map position (3356.5, 3160.6, -15.7)

Telerath was a planet located in the Nouane sector of the Inner Rim. It was allegedly a resort world but had problems with gang violence. Disputes on Telerath were settled through sword combat, in a convention known as Sword Law. In 23 BBY, the bounty hunter Jango Fett and his son, Boba, met with fellow hunters Neelda, Rinn, and Tiver, who Fett had hired for an upcoming mission, in a cantina on Telerath.
