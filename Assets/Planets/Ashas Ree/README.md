# Ashas Ree

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ashas Ree](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ashas_Ree)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ashas Ree`, Outer Rim, grid R-5 — map position (9328.0, 7379.4, -67.4)

Ashas Ree was a forested planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. Its sole inhabited outpost was situated next to a Jedi temple that had been built to seal the power of a Sith temple beneath it. During the First Order-Resistance War, traveler Mika Grey came to Ashas Ree seeking a relic hidden in the Sith temple, intending to keep it out of the First Order's hands, but became trapped inside the temple as a result until she was found and released by Colossus resistance members Kazuda Xiono, CB-23, Eila, and Kel two days later.
