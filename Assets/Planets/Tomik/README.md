# Tomik

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tomik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tomik)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tomik`, Expansion Region, grid O-14 — map position (5238.3, -7110.6, 249.3)

Tomrik was the largest gas giant in the Bacrana system. The planet consisted of swirling bands of gasses, colored bright orange and red. Sixteen satellites orbited the planet, most of them originating as asteroids captured from the Carba Asteroid Belt. Lant Mining Corporation conducted mining operations on three of Tomik's moons. The moons were strip-mined and abandoned.
