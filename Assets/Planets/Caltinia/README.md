# Caltinia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Caltinia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caltinia)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Caltinia`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (497.4, -11869.9, 333.2)

Caltinia was a lush planet that was the homeworld of House Elegin, famous for its hundreds of dueling fields surrounded by numerous mausoleums, temples, and museums celebrating and mourning epic duels of the past. It was located in the Senex-Juvex's Senex sector in the Mid Rim and Western Reaches of the galaxy. Caltinia was once visited by Lando Calrissian.
