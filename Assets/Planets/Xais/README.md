# Xais

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Xais](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xais)

Xais was a planet situated in the Rocun Cluster of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet was home to a single settlement, a former gas refinery. Following the Battle of Galov, the Nihil marauder organization operated on Xais, occupying the gas foundry there. When Jedi Knights Keeve Trennis and Terec infiltrated the Nihil's ranks by posing as marauders and setting the Nihil up to rescue them from a fake skirmish against the Jedi Order, they were brought to Xais, where they were met by Tempest Runner Zeetar.
