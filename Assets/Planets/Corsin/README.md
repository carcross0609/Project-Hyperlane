# Corsin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Corsin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corsin)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Corsin`, Expansion Region, grid M-7 — map position (2848.0, 3501.2, -58.8)

Corsin was a terrestrial world located in the Corsin system in the Greater Plooriod sector of the Expansion Region. It was located along the Hydian Way, and was the western endpoint of the Vaathkree Trade Corridor. The luxury liner Stellar Vista departed from Corsin before being pulled out of hyperspace in the Obroa-skai system as part of an attempt by the Galactic Empire to capture the stolen Imperial corvette Carrion Spike.
