# Manforgon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Manforgon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Manforgon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Manforgon`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (231.2, -11777.3, -181.2)

Manforgon was a planet located in the Mid Rim's Senex sector. It was used for centuries as a penal colony of the Senex-Juvex region. Eventually becoming a lawless world fought over by warlords, Manforgon was turned over to the Galactic Empire by the noble houses of the Senex-Juvex. The Empire used the planet for military purposes, and during the Galactic Civil War, Imperial Customs Officer Tellix served there.
