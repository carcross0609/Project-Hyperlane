# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Kouru)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Kouru%29)

A planet was located in the Outer Rim and was the site of shanty distilleries and a spaceport. The Sith Kouru landed on the planet after the end of the Sith rebellion and eventually made her way to the spaceport.
