# Pacara

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pacara](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pacara)

Pacara was a terrestrial planet located in the Pacara system in the galaxy's Mid Rim, in grid square J-15 on the Standard Galactic Grid. By the time of the mission to Batuu during the First Order-Resistance War, Pacara housed a secret base for the Resistance, where General Leia Organa was once stationed.

In 34 ABY, an Intersystem Transport Ship commanded by Lieutenant Bek lifted off from Batuu carrying new Resistance recruits intending to fly to Pacara. The ship was instead captured by forces of the First Order, eventually leading to a battle in space. After the battle, the recruits returned to the Batuu base.
