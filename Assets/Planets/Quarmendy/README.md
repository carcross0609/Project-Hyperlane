# Quarmendy

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quarmendy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quarmendy)

Quarmendy was a rocky planet in the Mid Rim Territories. The Nexus, a floating city and trading outpost, was situated above Quarmendy. During the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the latter group controlled the Nexus. The Republic would undertake a mission to Quarmendy in 22 BBY. Later, the mission to Quarmendy was mentioned in an intelligence report by Clone Commander Wolffe describing D1-series aerial battle droids, which was included in the survival compendium "Neyo's Almanac of Clankers."
