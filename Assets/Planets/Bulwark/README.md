# Bulwark

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bulwark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bulwark)

Bulwark was a planet located in the Bulwark system of Wild Space. During the Galactic Civil War, Bulwark served as a Alliance to Restore the Republic safe world—a designated planet where people connected with the Alliance could live free of oppression from the Galactic Empire. It was also among the few sites to which the Rebel Alliance allocated one of their v-150 Planet Defender ion cannons.
