# Fhost

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fhost](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fhost)

Fhost was a planet located in the Wild Space of the galaxy which was close to the edge of the Unknown Regions. Mostly covered by deserts, mountains, and a single ocean, Fhost had its beauty. The largest population center, Farpoint, was built on the remains of a spaceship that crashed there long before 25 ABY. Fhost was so far out from the main hyperlanes that all HoloNet shows that were available were at least four months old.
