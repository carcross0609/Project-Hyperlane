# Simoth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Simoth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Simoth)

Simoth was a forest world home to a camp of Simothian enslavers. After taking Ry Nymbis as his apprentice, the Sith Lord Darth Caldoth abandoned him in the camp on Simoth. For seven years, Nymbis fought for the slavers, becoming stronger. Darth Caldoth returned and slaughtered the slavers, and Nymbis attacked him in anger, but was subdued. Caldoth was satisfied with his apprentice, taking him away from Simoth to train him.
