# Harloff Minor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Harloff Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harloff_Minor)

Harloff Minor was a planet in the Harloff Minor system of the Core Worlds, near Coruscant. The heavily-traveled world, with its large equatorial cities, was considered a center of commerce and culture in the galaxy.
