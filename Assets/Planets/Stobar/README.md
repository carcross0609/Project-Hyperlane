# Stobar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stobar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stobar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Stobar`, Expansion Region, grid M-15 — map position (2104.5, -7951.4, 88.3)

Stobar was a planet located in the Stobar system in the Boeus sector of the Expansion Region that was the site of the Stobar Spaceport. Savage Opress would visit Stobar during his search for his long-lost brother, the former Sith Lord Maul. After a confrontation between Opress and several individuals at a local diner, Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker and his Padawan Ahsoka Tano would visit the diner to see what had happened, with the former sensing a disturbance in the Force shortly after they arrived.
