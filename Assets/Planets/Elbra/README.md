# Elbra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Elbra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elbra)

Elbra was a world located in the Airam sector of the Outer Rim. During the Galactic Civil War, the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Rage intercepted a exchange of supplies between the Airam Clans and the Rebel Alliance near Elbra.
