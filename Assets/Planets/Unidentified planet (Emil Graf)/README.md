# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Emil Graf)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Emil_Graf%29)

A planet located in Wild Space was orbited by at least two pink moons. At some point after the Galactic Civil War, Emil Graf, BB-00, and CR-8R discovered the planet.
