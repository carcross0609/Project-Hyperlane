# Bofa II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bofa II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bofa_II)

Bofa II was a planet that housed an Imperial Cargo Transfer Area during the Galactic Civil War and was the distribution site of Imperial surveillance droids. The BFF-1 bulk freighter Libom was captured there.
