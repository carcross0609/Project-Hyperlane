# Sinsara

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sinsara](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sinsara)

Sinsara was a dangerous and minor planet in the Anoat sector within the Outer Rim. It had a mysterious connection with Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn's past. In the years before the Invasion of Naboo, the assassin Corlis Rath attempted to kill Jinn for a reason involving Sinsara.
