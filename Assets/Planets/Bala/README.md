# Bala

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bala](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bala)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bala`, Expansion Region, grid O-14 — map position (5492.5, -6461.7, -109.1)

Bala, a ball of searing rock, was the innermost planet of the Aramand system. Despite its inhospitable environment, the Aramandi managed to establish a colony on the surface of the planet.
