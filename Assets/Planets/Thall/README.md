# Thall

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thall](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thall)

Thall was a planet that was inhabited by a civilization who followed a religion of the Force. By the High Republic Era, its original civilization was long extinct, though new settlements had been established on the planet. The former Jedi Master Cohmac Vitus visited Thall in search for information on the Tolemites, a people who had encountered the Nameless creatures. On Thall, Vitus managed to locate a map leading to the Tolemite homeworld of Tolis. The former Jedi was pursued by the Nihil Child of the Storm Sicarus and several Nameless, but managed to escape.
