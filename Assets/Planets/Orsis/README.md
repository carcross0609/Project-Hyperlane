# Orsis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orsis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orsis)

Orsis was a planet that had jungles, oceans and at least one volcano. It was home to the Orsis Academy, which was famous for training trackers, spys and assassins; among its most famous graduates was a young Darth Maul.
