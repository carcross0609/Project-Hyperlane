# Delrian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Delrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delrian)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Delrian`, Inner Rim — map position (422.6, -6462.5, -29.9)

Delrian was a world in the Inner Rim Territories which hosted an Imperial penal work camp. Prisoners incarcerated there during the Imperial Era were tasked with installing electronics on the durasteel restraints used on the Oovo 4 prison.

During the New Republic Era the prison came under the control of the New Republic and N5 sentry droids were deployed as prison guards by the New Republic Correctional Corps there. The convicted alien criminal Vorg Alsum served a 35 year sentence at Delrian for speeder theft. The sentence was listed on Alsum's profile, which was found on a database seen by Marshal Cara Dune when she searched for the profile of the criminal Migs Mayfeld in 9 ABY.
