# Embaril

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Embaril](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Embaril)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Embaril`, Outer Rim, grid S-6 — map position (11034.4, 5545.5, -82.2)

Embaril was a world located in the Embaril system. It was a part of the Allied Tion sector of the Tion Cluster, situated within the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Embaril lay on the Cadinth Run hyperlane between the Cadinth and Voss systems, and its surface featured green hills and crystalline lakes. It was among the Tion Cluster's least-spoiled worlds, and affluent aristocrats from the cluster and beyond were drawn to Embaril by its scenery.

For centuries after the death of the Tionese ruler Xim at some point around 25,100 BBY, Embaril was under the rule of Jaminere Marches, a state centered on the world Jaminere. By the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, Embaril's hills and lakes were represented as a faded holograph at the booths of the Orman's Sky Palace bar on the planet Lianna, one of the establishment's several such recreations of sights from the Allied Tion sector worlds.
