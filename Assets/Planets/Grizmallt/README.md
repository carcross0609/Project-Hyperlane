# Grizmallt

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grizmallt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grizmallt)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Grizmallt`, Core Worlds, grid L-9 — map position (425.8, 265.6, -31.7)

Grizmallt was a war-torn planet in the Grizmallt system of the Core Worlds, and the origin of the humans who crash-landed on and colonized Naboo. Around 3900 BBY, a civil war on Grizmallt caused a group Grizmallti to flee their home in colony ships and settle on Naboo. Commissar was a position of authority on the planet.
