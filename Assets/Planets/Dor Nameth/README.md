# Dor Nameth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dor Nameth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dor_Nameth)

Dor Nameth was a distant planet located in the Dor Nameth system, a part of the Bright Jewel sector in the New Territories portion of the Mid Rim. The planet was situated on the Celanon Spur hyperlane, which connected it to the Qiilura and Korvaii systems. Dor Nameth was the homeworld of Brangus Glee, an elder of a dexterous species of renowned travelers. By 12 ABY, the planet fell within the borders of the New Republic.
