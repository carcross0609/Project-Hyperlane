# Sorella

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sorella](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorella)

Sorella was a planet in the Sorella system in Tapani sector. Sorella was also one of the Freeworlds Territory. Also, it was a member of the Three Ellas, with a liberal government looser about crime and smuggling than most Tapani worlds.
