# Serenno

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Serenno/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Serenno/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Serenno`, Outer Rim, grid P-5 — map position (6128.8, 6945.0, -175.4)

Serenno was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories. It was known for its aristocratic Counts, the most famous of them being Dooku. It was also well known for its long history of resisting authority.
