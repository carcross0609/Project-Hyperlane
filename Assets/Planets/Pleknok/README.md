# Pleknok

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pleknok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pleknok)

Pleknok was a terrestrial planet located in an Unknown Regions star system of the same name. Located along the border of the territory of the Chiss Ascendancy and the Paataatus Hiveborn, Pleknok was used as a waypoint by smugglers conducting black market trade between the Ascendancy and neighboring nations. It was also a site of at least two border skirmishes between the Chiss and Paataatus species.
