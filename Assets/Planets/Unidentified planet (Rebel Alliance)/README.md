# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Rebel Alliance)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Rebel_Alliance%29)

During the Imperial Era, an uninhabited planet existed within the Outer Rim Territories. While the Rebel Alliance was stationed on the planet to repair an Imperial fortress for their own purposes, the Sith Lord Darth Vader managed to thwart their efforts and use the fortress on the planet which destroyed the terrain.
