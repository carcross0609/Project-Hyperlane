# Unidentified craggy planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified craggy planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_craggy_planet)

A craggy planet was located somewhere in the galaxy between the planets Arbooine and Eriadu. Near the end of the Clone Wars, the Jedi Knight Suljo Warde stopped at the planet and left his holocron on the planet's surface.
