# Mithra

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Mithra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mithra)

Mithra was a rocky planet of tormented landscapes, full of rare elements including Ytterbium. Between 3–4 ABY, the planet, along with Gilkahande, Vélane and the Industrial Monad of Fenzy, was linked to Müller Menskovar, a failed Jedi and later senator, who supported Palpatine in his dark projects.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Mithra
- **Terrain:** rocky
- **Exports:** Ytterbium
