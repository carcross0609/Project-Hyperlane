# Kaltrais

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaltrais](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaltrais)

Kaltrais was a planet located in the Fenril sector of the Inner Rim Territories. By around 6 ABY, Tusvein Shoirin served as the president. Following his death, the Fenril Consortium took control of the planet.
