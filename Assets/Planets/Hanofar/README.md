# Hanofar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hanofar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hanofar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Hanofar`, Colonies — map position (1221.3, 1878.4, -19.4)

Hanofar was a terrestrial world located in the Hanofar system, a part of the Southern Core portion of the Colonies. During the early years of the Galactic Civil War, a conflict fought between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, Hanofar was held by the Empire.

At some point during that time, a rebel group on Hanofar engaged in protests against Imperial taxation. The protests grew violent, leading to skirmishes with Imperial forces and several innocent bystanders getting injured. Among the hurt was the mother of the Human male Vess Kogo.
