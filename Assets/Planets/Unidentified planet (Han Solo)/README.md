# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Han Solo)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Han_Solo%29)

A red planet was located near the Rikmania system in the Steniplis sector of the Outer Rim Territories. While running a job for Jabba Desilijic Tiure, the smugglers Han Solo and Chewbacca crashed their freighter the Millennium Falcon on the planet after running an Imperial blockade, and discovered a shipyard run by Rekias Nodo.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified planet
- **Moons:** At least 10
