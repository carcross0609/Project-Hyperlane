# Bardram Scoft

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bardram Scoft](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardram_Scoft)

Bardram Scoft was a planet located in the Unknown Regions. It was home to the Scofti, whose government changed frequently, often with a violent transition. On one occasion where the previous Scofti Prefect was assassinated, various outside groups, including the Chiss Ascendancy, sent emissaries to Bardram Scoft. While at Bardram Scoft, the Chiss Junior Commander Mitth'raw'nuru "Thrawn" learned that the Lioaoin Regime was supporting pirates that had recently been attacking at the edges of the Ascendancy.
