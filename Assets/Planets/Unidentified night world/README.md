# Unidentified night world

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified night world](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_night_world)

A night world was located in the galaxy, with ashen swamp terrain covered in electric mist. The Sith Lord Darth Caldoth took his apprentice Ry Nymbis to the night world so that he could hunt down and kill a knight and take their lightsaber. Nymbis did so, using the saber he acquired to create his own.
