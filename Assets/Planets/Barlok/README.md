# Barlok

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Barlok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barlok)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Barlok`, Inner Rim, grid L-8 — map position (684.1, 1988.0, -137.5)

Barlok was a terrestrial planet in the Barlok system of the Marcol sector in the Inner Rim Territories and the Interior. It fell within grid square L-8 of the Standard Galactic Grid. The world was known for its advanced horticultural techniques. Prisht-fruit was native to the world and its most prized produce. Other edible plants like taba leaf and shi-shok could also be purchased there.

Having had enough of hot worlds like Li-Toran and Jakku, the Artiodac chef Strono Tuggs visited Barlok during a culinary tour of the galaxy that he made between 34 ABY and 35 ABY. Tuggs used the opportunity to stock up on fruit and vegetables, including two bushels of prisht-fruit, which he thought he would have been a fool to miss. He later mentioned Barlok as part of the recipe for prisht parfait in his third cookbook, The Ultimate Cookbook, which was published in 35 ABY.
