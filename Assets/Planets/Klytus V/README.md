# Klytus V

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Klytus V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Klytus_V)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Klytus V`, Wild Space — map position (11318.5, 9723.6, -479.2)

Klytus V, was a gas giant in the fifth orbital position of the Klytus system located on the edge of Wild Space in grid square H-15 of the Standard Galactic Grid. The planet had at least one prominent storm in its atmosphere, and its eye was crimson in color and visible from orbit. Klytus V was orbited by the space station Klytus V Station, which the Duro Interstellar Freight and Shipping company made deliveries to.

Approximately one year after the proclamation of the New Order, Captain Visler Aqorian Korda III of the Galactic Empire was tasked with cataloging gas giants on the edge of Wild Space. While cataloging Klytus V, an ensign informed Korda that they were scheduled to orbit the planet for an additional seven hours.
