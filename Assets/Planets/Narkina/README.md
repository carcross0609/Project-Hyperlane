# Narkina

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Narkina](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Narkina)

Narkina was a gas giant located in the Abrion sector's Narkina system within the Outer Rim, specifically at Standard Galactic Grid space S-15. The planet also lay in the section of space known as The Slice. Appearing brown from space, Narkina had at least five moons, one of which was the water moon Narkina 5, on which the Galactic Empire maintained a prison complex during the Imperial Era.
