# Lan Tundi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lan Tundi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lan_Tundi)

Lan Tundi was an ore-rich planet in the Outer Rim's Lan system. Though its barren surface could not support life, underground mining colonies had been constructed on the planet by the time of the Galactic Empire. During that time, the export of ore from mines on Lan Tundi and the other inner planets was the system's only real source of income.
