# Vexar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vexar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vexar)

Vexar was a gas giant of the Vexar system located in the Corporate Sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was orbited by at least three moons, including the third moon of Vexar.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Vexar
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
- **Sector:** Corporate Sector
- **System:** Vexar system
- **Coordinates:** S-4
- **Moons:** At least 3, including the third moon of Vexar
- **Class:** Gas giant
- **Terrain:** Gaseous
