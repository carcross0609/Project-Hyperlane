# Aricho

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aricho](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aricho)

Aricho was a planet located in the Outer Rim behind the Nihil pirates Stormwall in the Occlusion Zone during the High Republic Era. After the destruction of Starlight Beacon in 230 BBY, Jedi Padawan Imri Cantaros hid on the planet. In 228 BBY, after the aspiring inventor Avon Sunvale learned that Nihil forces led by the Quarren Kara Xoo intended to set up operations on the planet because of a valuable herb on Aricho and fearing that Nihil General Abediah Viess would also make her way to the world with Nameless creatures which fed on Force-sensitives, Sunvale set out to rescue her friend.

Later, the Hutt Dahara conspired with President Orenna Sala to set up glaka root production facilities on Aricho before it was discovered that the planet and its glaka root fields had been afflicted with the blight and the planet was evacuated.
