# Talcene

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Talcene](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talcene)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Talcene`, Mid Rim, grid Q-8 — map position (8672.0, 2910.7, 67.1)

Talcene was a planet located in the Talcene system, a part of the Talcene sector of the Mid Rim. It was connected to the Orleon and Salvara systems by the Perlemian Trade Route super-hyperroute, which served as a dividing line between the regions known as the Slice and the Trans-Hydian. Talcene shared its name with the Talcene Transit, a hyperspace route linking the Perlemian Trade Route to the Suolriep sector's Saleucami system. The planet's surface featured cityscape.

Talcene was home to notoriously competitive industrial guilds. The Human Shea Hublin, who was born around 35 BBY, grew up on Talcene. As a boy, he raced swoop bikes, and, during his teenage years, Hublin made a good living as a courier for Talcene's industrial guilds. Around 19 BBY, at the age of sixteen, he joined the navy of the Galactic Republic, and his eventual service in the naval forces of the Republic's successor, the Galactic Empire, involved assignments offworld.
