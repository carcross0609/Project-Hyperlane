# Ord Mirit

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Mirit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Mirit)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ord Mirit`, Colonies, grid K-9 — map position (-710.1, 1112.1, 36.0)

Ord Mirit was originally one of the Republic's Ordnance/Regional Depots. After the Battle of Endor, the planet's Imperial garrison relocated to Corellia. During the Yuuzhan Vong War, the planet was a strategic world of the New Republic. It was the homeworld of Habeer Zignean.
