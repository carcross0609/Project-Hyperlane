# Uba V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uba V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uba_V)

Uba V was a planet located within the Uba system of the Mid Rim. It was a home to an Ubese colony, which was destroyed by the Jedi in the Battle of Uba IV during the New Sith Wars.
