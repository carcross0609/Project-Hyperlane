# Rafa IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rafa IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rafa_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rafa IV`, Outer Rim, grid T-8 — map position (13079.8, 2236.7, -117.6)

Rafa IV was a temperate planet in the Rafa system of the Centrality, located in the Outer Rim Territories. It was a colony world of the Sharu species.

Before the Sharu uprising in 4 BBY, its main city Teguta Lusat was the capital of the Rafa system. Rafa IV was governed by Duttes Mer, colonial governor of the Rafa.
