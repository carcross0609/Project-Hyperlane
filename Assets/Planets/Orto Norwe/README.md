# Orto Norwe

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orto Norwe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orto_Norwe)

Orto Norwe was an ice world located in the Trans-Vulta sector of the Mid Rim. It was home to a settlement known as Yellowblade's Landing. Around 20 BBY, Darth Maul and Savage Opress landed there shortly after leaving Raydonia.
