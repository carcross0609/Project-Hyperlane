# Soronia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Soronia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soronia)

Soronia was the seventh planet in the Corellian system. It was a frozen, rocky world and had no moons. Soronia and the other lifeless world in the star system, Crollia, were both located on the fringes of the system, and were believed to have naturally formed in orbit around the star Corell—unlike the other five worlds, which were placed in the system through the use of Centerpoint Station.
