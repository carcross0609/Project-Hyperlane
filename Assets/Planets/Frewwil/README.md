# Frewwil

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Frewwil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frewwil)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Frewwil`, Expansion Region, grid I-14 — map position (-3660.1, -7228.2, 8.7)

Frewwil was a planet located in the Frewwil system, a part of the freestanding subsectors portion of the Expansion Region. An inhospitable world, Frewwil was explored by the Republic Survey Corps scout Barosa Warren. More than fifty years later, by 2 ABY, Frewwil was controlled by the Galactic Empire and was considered to be one of the most dangerous planets under the Empire's jurisdiction.
