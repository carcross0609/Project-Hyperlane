# Hebekrr Minor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hebekrr Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hebekrr_Minor)

Hebekrr Minor was an inhabited terrestrial planet in the Outer Rim Territories, the second habitable planet in its solar system after the geologically unstable Hebekrr Major. In 22 BBY, during the first weeks of the Clone Wars, Hebekrr Minor's major population centres in its southern hemisphere came under siege from forces of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, and a company of the Grand Army of the Republic led by Jedi Master Sivad arrived to defend the population.
