# Jendar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jendar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jendar)

Jendar was a planet located in the Nembus sector of the Outer Rim Territories. During the Galactic Civil War, it was the site of a campaign between the forces of the Rebel Alliance and the Galactic Empire for the control of the planet.
