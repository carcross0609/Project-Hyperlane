# Panna

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Panna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panna)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Panna`, Outer Rim, grid S-6 — map position (10732.3, 5578.1, -113.2)

Panna was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories' Cronese Mandate sector. Situated at the coordinates S-6 on the Standard Galactic Grid, the world was connected to the celestial body Rampa II by the Bubble Wink, a hyperspace route favored by smugglers. It was orbited by at least one moon, Panna's Redwater moon moon. The former pirate Queen Maz Kanata marked it as the location of an outlaw garage on a map that she included in the book that became known as the Smuggler's Guide.
