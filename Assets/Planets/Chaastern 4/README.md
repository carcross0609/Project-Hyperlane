# Chaastern 4

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chaastern 4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chaastern_4)

Chaastern 4 was a planet in the Shelsha sector. By 0.5 ABY it was home to one of the only two remaining Imperial army garrisons in the sector, the other being located on Minkring.
