# Anthan Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anthan Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anthan_Prime)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Anthan Prime`, Outer Rim, grid R-7 — map position (9406.5, 4333.9, 309.3)

Anthan Prime, also known as Anthan, was a gas giant located in the Anthan system of the Outer Rim Territories' Braxant sector. It was connected by hyperspace route to the planets Bastion and Bescane. Above the planet floated the Anthan Spire, a city used as a vacation resort for the incredibly rich.

Anthan Prime was orbited by several moons. Anthan 1 was close enough to sit within the Thanteen Substrata, a part of Anthan's own gaseous atmosphere.
