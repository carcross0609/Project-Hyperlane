# Corvis Minor V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corvis Minor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corvis_Minor_V)

Corvis Minor V in the Corvis Minor system was a planet orbited by six moons, including Distna. It was a gray-orange gas giant located near an asteroid belt.
