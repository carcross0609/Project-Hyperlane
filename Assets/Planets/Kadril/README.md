# Kadril

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kadril](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kadril)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kadril`, Outer Rim, grid R-6 — map position (9216.3, 5500.2, -125.1)

Kadril was an inhospitable, irregularly-shaped world colonized by the reptilian Kadrillians in 6 BBY. It was the source of kunda stones. It was here that Darth Vader developed pacifog. The reptilian Kadrillians were the only inhabitants of the planet to survive the planet's bombardment during the Stenax Massacres of 4 ABY.
