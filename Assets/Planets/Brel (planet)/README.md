# Brel

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Brel (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brel_%28planet%29)

Brel was a planet located in the Tsevuka system in the Cademimu sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The Merchant Uprising was massacre that took place on the planet during the New Republic Era.
