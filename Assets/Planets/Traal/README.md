# Traal

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Traal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Traal)

Traal was a remote planet. At some point by approximately 180 BBY, the Twi'lek Jedi Master Skia Ro famously survived on Traal for two years while equipped with nothing but a towel, a fact that was known to her apprentice Christina Starspeeder.
