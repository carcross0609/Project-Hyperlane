# Sarvchi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sarvchi/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarvchi/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Sarvchi`, Unknown Regions, grid F-9 — map position (-8493.6, 1470.7, 345.9)

Sarvchi was a planet located in the Sarvchi system, a part of the Unknown Regions' Chiss Space. It was situated on the hyperlane known as the Path of the Houses, which connected it to the Cioral and Copero systems.

Constituting a part of the core territory of the Chiss Ascendancy, the interstellar state of the Chiss species, Sarvchi was the homeworld of the Chaf family, one of the Ascendancy's Ruling Families. The planet was under Chaf control and was a neutral world of representation for the non-Chiss species inhabiting the Ascendancy. When, around 22 ABY, Aristocra Chaf'orm'bintrano of the Chaf family sent a message addressed to the New Republic's Jedi Master Luke Skywalker, the sender of the communication was styled as "Chaf'orm'bintrano, Aristocra of the Fifth Ruling Family, Sarvchi."
