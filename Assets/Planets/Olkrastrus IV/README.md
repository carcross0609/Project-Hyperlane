# Olkrastrus IV

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Olkrastrus IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Olkrastrus_IV)

Olkrastrus IV was a terrestrial forest planet in the Olkrastrus system in the Numesira sector of the Mid Rim Territories. A massive number of Separatist holdouts remained on world during the earliest months of the Galactic Empire. The main Separatist force hid within the canyons and attempted to get their factories back online while secondary groups existed in scattered pockets across the whole planet. The latter groups ranged from limited numbers of guerrilla fighters to entire armies equipped with hundreds of soldiers.
