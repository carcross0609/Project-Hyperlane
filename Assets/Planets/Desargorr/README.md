# Desargorr

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Desargorr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Desargorr)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Desargorr`, Outer Rim, grid S-6 — map position (10858.4, 5585.2, -98.1)

Desargorr was a mountainous world located in the Desargorr system. It was a part of the Allied Tion sector of the Tion Cluster, situated within the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories.

The surface of Desargorr, which had abundant deposits of minerals, was pitted by ruthless mining over the span of millennia. For centuries after the death of the Tionese ruler Xim at some point around 25,100 BBY, Desargorr was under the rule of Jaminere Marches, a state centered on the world Jaminere. By the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, the mountains of Desargorr were represented as a faded holograph at the booths of the Orman's Sky Palace bar on the planet Lianna, one of the establishment's several such recreations of sights from the Allied Tion sector worlds.
