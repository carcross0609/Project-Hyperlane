# Qi Lozar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Qi Lozar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qi_Lozar)

Qi Lozar was a desert world that was located in the Grumani sector, in the Outer Rim Territories. It served as a haven for hermits, refugees and other individuals who were trying to escape their pasts.
