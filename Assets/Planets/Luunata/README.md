# Luunata

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Luunata](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luunata)

Luunata was a planet. At some point, the female human Jae Mordant the noble House Mordant from the mining planet Shu-Torun went to Luunata where she established her own mining cartel and ruled it as a Ore-Baroness. Mordant lost her rule over Luunata when it was captured for the Confederacy of Independent Systems by the Infinite Coil, a dark side-aligned cult led by Kundri Fathom. During the Clone Wars, Mordant sought to take back Luunata and, together with a Republic intelligence officer, tasked the independent covert special forces group Zero Company with the investigation of Infinite Coil, which who sought to create a galaxy-wide plague.
