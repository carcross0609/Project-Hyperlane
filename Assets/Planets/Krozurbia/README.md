# Krozurbia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krozurbia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krozurbia)

Krozurbia was a planet located in the Outer Rim's Shadola sector. It was the homeworld of the Krozurbian species. They were engaged in two hundred years of war until peace was brought about due to the intervention of the Ithorian Zorneth.
