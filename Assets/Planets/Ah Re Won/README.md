# Ah Re Won

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ah Re Won](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ah_Re_Won)

Ah Re Won was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories. During the Republic Era, between around 40 BBY and 32 BBY, the Sith Lord Darth Sidious sent his Sith apprentice Darth Maul to meditate in stillness and quiet in the Sith temple on Ah Re Won.
