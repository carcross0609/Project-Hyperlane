# Ag'gor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ag'gor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ag%27gor)

Ag'gor was a hothouse planet with no moons that occupied the second closest orbital position to the star Opoku. It was located within the Opoku system, a part of the Galov sector within the galactic regions known as the Outer Rim Territories and the Slice. The Triellus Trade Route hyperlane connected Ag'gor to the star systems of Arami and Lyran.
