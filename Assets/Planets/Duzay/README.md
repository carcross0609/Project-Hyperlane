# Duzay

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Duzay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duzay)

Duzay was a world in the galaxy. In 1 ABY, a group of tourists from Duzay sojourned at the Lake Retreat Hotel during their stay on Naboo. An employee of the hotel named Rovim Minnoni recruited a spacer to rescue these tourists when they got lost during a walk in the wilderness.
