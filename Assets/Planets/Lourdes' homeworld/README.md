# Lourdes' homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lourdes' homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lourdes%27_homeworld)

Lourdes' homeworld was a small planetoid located in the galaxy. A desert world, there was at least one city on the planet, dotted with small stone buildings. Around 31 BBY, the world was conquered by the Yahk-Tosh warlord Gar-Oth and his droid army. Killing the King, the warlord ruled the people of the world with fear. When Jedi Knight Yoshi Raph-Elan crash-landed on the world and befriended Princess Lourdes, the duo were able to confront the warlord and assassinate him, liberating the world.
