# Unidentified jungle planet (Blue Star Pirates)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified jungle planet (Blue Star Pirates)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_jungle_planet_%28Blue_Star_Pirates%29)

An island cove on this unidentified jungle planet was once used by the Blue Star Pirates as a base of operations. At one time, Supreme Chancellor Palpatine assigned a team, made of former Royal House of Learning students, to investigate the planet and discover the source of the Blue Star Pirates' military grade weapons. The team infiltrated the island cove and defeated the pair of guards stationed there. Investigating the base, the team discovered clues that lead them to the Hyperdrive Cantina on Vorusku.
