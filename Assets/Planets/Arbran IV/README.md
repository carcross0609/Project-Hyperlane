# Arbran IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arbran IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arbran_IV)

Arbran IV was the fourth planet orbiting the star Arbran in the Arbran system. A gas giant with eight moons, it was located in the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector, a part of the Trailing Sectors portion of the Outer Rim Territories.
