# Marleyvane

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Marleyvane](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marleyvane)

Marleyvane was a terrestrial astronomical object that hosted an Imperial Academy and Imperial shipyards. It was located in the Marleyvane system in the Tharin sector of the Outer Rim Territories, along the Triellus Trade Route just north of the intersection with another hyperspace route at the nearby Junkfort Station. Lieutenant Chiron and Sergeant Currahee were stationed at Marleyvane before being transferred to the Academy for Young Imperials on Lothal.

A member of the Rokana Raiders pirate group ran stolen starship parts out of Marleyvane sometime before 3 ABY.
