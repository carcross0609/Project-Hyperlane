# Endovar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Endovar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endovar)

Endovar was a terrestrial planet located in the Endovar system within the Terrabe sector of the Mid Rim and the Interior. It was situated in grid square M-10 of the Standard Galactic Grid.

During the High Republic Era, a Jedi Temple of the Jedi Order was situated on Endovar. The Jedi Masters Torban Buck and Kantam Sy both spent time on Endovar as Padawans. On Endovar, the two participated in an event known as the Great Jedi Rumble Race, though the tradition of hosting the race eventually stopped. Grand Master Yoda watched after a group of Padawans on the object for some time. At some point, Sy's future Padawan, Lula Talisola, was present alongside them and Yoda at the Jedi temple on Endovar. The Jedi also had a pen where they looked after Happabores on Endovar, including one named Monsalmo. Endovar was also known to have very frequent seasonal festivals.
