# Ord Gertoth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Gertoth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Gertoth)

Ord Gertoth was a planet located somewhere in the galaxy. Shortly before 15 BBY, the droids C-3PO and R2-D2 stowed away on a starship traveling from R-Duba to Ord Gertoth, but the duo were kicked off at a one-woman refueling station.
