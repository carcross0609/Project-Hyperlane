# Verdusia

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Verdusia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verdusia)

Verdusia was a grassy planet located in the galaxy that was visited by General Grievous during the Clone Wars for a vacation. His stay was interrupted by Jedi Aayla Secura, Anakin Skywalker, and Ahsoka Tano.
