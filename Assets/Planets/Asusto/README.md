# Asusto

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Asusto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asusto)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Asusto`, Outer Rim — map position (-2841.1, -13362.4, 52.6)

Asusto was a small forested planet located within the Tamsis Nebula in the Outer Rim Territories, strong with the dark side of the Force and often visited by smugglers. The last of the Presagers of Hakotei hid on the planet. The Presagers led Jedi Master Lene Kostana and her Padawan Sifo-Dyas into a trap on Asusto by tricking them into following a supposed trail for Sith artifacts from Karazak.
