# Seswenna

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Seswenna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seswenna)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Seswenna`, Outer Rim, grid M-18 — map position (1931.9, -12703.7, -616.6)

Seswenna was a planet located in the Seswenna system of the Outer Rim Territories. During the High Republic Era, the planet was a stronghold of the Galactic Republic. When the Nihil marauders expanded the Stormwall that surrounded their Occlusion Zone territory, Seswenna suddenly fell within Nihil space.

Admiral Conan Antonio Motti, a human male who was a member of the Imperial Military's Joint Chiefs and head of naval operations aboard the first Death Star, was from Seswenna.
