# Ooo-temiuk

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ooo-temiuk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ooo-temiuk)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ooo-temiuk`, Outer Rim, grid R-16 — map position (9777.7, -10009.0, 409.2)

Ooo-temiuk was a forested world located in the Ooo-temiuk system, a part of the Arkanis sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was situated on the Triellus Trade Route hyperlane, which linked it to the C-Foroon and Tatoo systems.

Ooo-temiuk was settled around 75 BBY by members of the Rodian species as part of a migration wave from their violence-plagued homeworld, the Savareen sector planet Rodia, and shared its name with Ooo-sek, another Rodian colony in the Mid Rim. In the years leading up to 43 ABY, the Greel Wood Logging Corporation of the Arkanis sector's Pii system planted stands of greel tree saplings on Ooo-temiuk.
