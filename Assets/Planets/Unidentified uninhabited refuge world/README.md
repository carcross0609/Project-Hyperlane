# Unidentified uninhabited; refuge world

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified uninhabited refuge world](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_uninhabited_refuge_world)

An uninhabited refuge world was the location where Chiss Syndic Mitth'ras'safis and a human female wished to crash a starship hailing from Lesser Space, the portion of the galaxy lying beyond the Unknown Regions, at some point around 27 BBY. By bringing it to the world, the pair would have been able to share the Galactic Republic technology within the starship with the Chiss Ascendancy nation's Defense Hierarchy Council. However, the ship was too damaged to reach its destination, forcing them to crash it in a stone-filled valley instead.
