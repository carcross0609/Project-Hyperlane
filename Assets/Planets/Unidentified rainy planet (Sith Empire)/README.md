# Unidentified rainy planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified rainy planet (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_rainy_planet_%28Sith_Empire%29)

During the Dark Lord of the Sith Naga Sadow's preparations to attack the Galactic Republic in the year 5000 BBY, he gathered his military forces from all across the Sith Empire, including from a planet on whose surface a large, light-gray-colored building had been built. In that year, it rained in the vicinity of the building, and Sadow's Sith soldiers of the Massassi caste loaded pieces of military technology—pulled over the muddy ground by quadrupedal beasts of burden—onto waiting Sith warships.
