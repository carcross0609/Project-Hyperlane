# Muntro VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Muntro VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Muntro_VI)

Muntro VI was a world in the Outer Rim Territories. The world was known for its famous Otherworld Arts Festival, an arts festival held every ten years by the Deftoi of Muntro VI.
