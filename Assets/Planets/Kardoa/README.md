# Kardoa

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kardoa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kardoa)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kardoa`, Other, grid P-19 — map position (6970.4, -13964.6, 147.5)

Kardoa was a rocky planet located in the Atrivis sector with the Outer Rim Territories. Three weeks after the bombing at the Jedi Temple, Jedi Master Depa Billaba and Padawan Caleb Dume went to Kardoa to confirm or deny a Separatist presence. When the Jedi and their clone trooper battalion encountered Separatist forces, a skirmish ensued.
