# Kiddi

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Kiddi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kiddi)

Kiddi was a desert planet located in the galaxy. In 1 BBY, smuggler Han Solo was hired by Garre to deliver gemstones from Tatooine to his partner Trumblo on Kiddi.
