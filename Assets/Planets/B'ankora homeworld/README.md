# B'ankora homeworld

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [B'ankora homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B%27ankora_homeworld)

The homeworld of the B'ankora species was a planet in the galaxy. At some point before around 1300 BBY, the planet suffered a cataclysmic collision with a meteor. In response the surviving B'ankora were given a piece of grass land on the Galactic Republic's capital Coruscant and were ultimately granted the land by by Supreme Chancellor Chasen Piian to use in perpetuity so they could resume their sedentary lifestyle with humble homes, fields, and trails, which became known as the B'ankor Refuge.
