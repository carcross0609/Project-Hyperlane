# Gandalla

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gandalla](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gandalla)

Gandalla was a planet located in the Boeus sector of the Expansion Region.

In 8 ABY Gandalla was the site of an attempted assassination of a Sector Ranger by Brahle Logris.
