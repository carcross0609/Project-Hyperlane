# Alpinn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Alpinn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alpinn)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Alpinn`, Outer Rim, grid O-8 — map position (5264.2, 2071.6, -184.9)

Alpinn was a planet located in the Outer Rim Territories where Lyra Erso searched for kyber crystals. Alpinn reflected light in realspace due to the remnants of ancient mineral seas. When these seas dried up eons before the Imperial Era, they left extensive areas of sedimentary rock and crystallized elements, which covered much of the planet's primary landmass in white.
