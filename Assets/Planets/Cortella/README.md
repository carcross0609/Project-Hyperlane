# Cortella

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cortella](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cortella)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Cortella`, Core Worlds, grid K-9 — map position (-1231.9, 780.9, 58.9)

Cortella was a Core Worlds planet ruled by a monarchy. In 25 BBY, the Human Jedi Knights Obi-Wan Kenobi and Siri Tachi impersonated the king and queen of Cortella in order to infiltrate the elite Leadership School on the planet Andara. During a meeting with the school's president, in which they negotiated a permission to carry out a tour of the establishment, the Jedi pretended to be interested in sending their son to attend the school.
