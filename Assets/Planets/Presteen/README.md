# Presteen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Presteen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Presteen)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Presteen`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (475.8, -11786.3, -181.1)

Presteen was a trade world located in the Presteen system of the Senex sector, a part of the Senex-Juvex region in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. The Senex-Juvex Loop hyperlane linked it to the Caltinia and Veron systems. Presteen was under the control of the lesser nobles of House Araba, based on the nearby planet of Cyimarra.

By 3 ABY, Queen Sula of Presteen traveled to the Cloud City, a floating metropolis located in the clouds of the Outer Rim Territories planet Bespin. There the queen participated in a high-stakes sabacc game against players including Cloud City's Baron Administrator, Lando Calrissian; the Hutt Corugg; and Imperial Moff Elior.
