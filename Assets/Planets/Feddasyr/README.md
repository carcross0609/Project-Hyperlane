# Feddasyr

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Feddasyr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Feddasyr)

Feddasyr was a planet located in the Elus sector of the Inner Rim. The planet was known for being a drug and gambling hub. During the Galactic Civil War, the planet had a neutral status.

Rebel Agent Sarin operated undercover for 5 years on Feddasyr without her cover ever being blown.
