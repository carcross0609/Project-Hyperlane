# Phothrell

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phothrell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phothrell)

Phothrell was a planet in the Inner Rim Territories where the Palace of the High Games Masters was located. Around 19 BBY, the guerrilla fighter Saw Gerrera and Senators Mon Mothma and Bail Organa met in the ruins of the palace to discuss Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine and his new regime.
