# Nomlis III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nomlis III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nomlis_III)

Nomlis III was a planet in the Kurdin system where the Bretie Facility, a production center for the TIE Experimental Project, orbited. Nothing else is known about the planet, other than the fact that it was the site of the battle in which Rebel Alliance pilots, one of them being Ace Azzameen, raided and destroyed the Bretie Facility in their quest to disrupt the TIE Experimental Project being carried out by the Galactic Empire.
