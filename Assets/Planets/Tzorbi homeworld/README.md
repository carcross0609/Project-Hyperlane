# Tzorbi homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tzorbi homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tzorbi_homeworld)

A planet was the homeworld of the Tzorbi, a species that was one of the first to join the Galactic Republic but went extinct thousands of years before the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire.
