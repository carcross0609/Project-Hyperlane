# Armorer's planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Armorer's planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armorer%27s_planet)

The "Armorer's planet", also known as the Covert, was an unknown planet in the Outer Rim Territories that hosted a Mandalorian covert in 9 ABY. The planet was discovered by a scout from the Children of the Watch. When the Mandalorian Din Djarin saved the Children of the Watch covert using his N-1 starfighter while Paz Vizsla's son, Ragnar took the Mandalorian Creed; Djarin and fellow Mandalorian Bo-Katan Kryze traveled to the planet after Kryze's castle on Kalevala was destroyed by an Imperial remnant after they left Mandalore. When Ragnar was taken by a canyon raptor on the planet, he was rescued by Djarin, Kryze, Vizsla, and the Shriek-hawk Training team.

The planet had no name, was uncharted, and was only identified by a catalogue number in the Imperial registry. The Children of the Watch referred to it as their latest Veheh'yaim, Mando'a for a temporary settlement.
