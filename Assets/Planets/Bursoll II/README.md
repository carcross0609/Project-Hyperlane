# Bursoll II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bursoll II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bursoll_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bursoll II`, Wild Space, grid H-17 — map position (-5673.6, -11434.3, -362.5)

Bursoll II was a Wild Space planet whose flora included the rurylis flower. Upon the planet's discovery, the SoroSuub Corporation wished to use in manufacturing a rare radioactive mineral bountifully present on Bursoll II.

To that end, SoroSuub attempted to appease the native Prince Geben Arrulis during his visit to the planet Sullust. The corporation tasked a group of individuals with bringing rurylis specimens from Bursoll II to Sullust, which it successfully did, although the plant's newly discovered psychoactive effect on beings spending time with it caused further difficulties with the prince's visit.
