# Alistan Nor

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Alistan Nor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alistan_Nor)

Alistan Nor was a remote planet located in the galaxy where the ancient Force Builders' city of Alistan Nor was located. Alistan Nor historically was covered in grassy plains, but over time, it became a lush jungle. Alistan Nor was also surrounded by three asteroid rings. By the time of the quest to build the Arrowhead, Alistan Nor had been abandoned and became a lush jungle.
