# Brembo

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Brembo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brembo)

Brembo was a terrestrial planet whose surface consisted of volcanic rock. It was located in the Thanium sector's Felucia system, found in the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Brembo occupied the second orbital position around the star Felix and had no moons.
