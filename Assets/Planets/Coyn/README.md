# Coyn

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coyn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coyn)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Coyn`, Outer Rim, grid M-20 — map position (2585.4, -15399.2, -204.8)

Coyn was a planet in the Coyn system and the homeworld of the Coynites. It was located in Elrood sector, where the Coyn Route met the Elrood-Derilyn Trade Route. The capital was the city of En'Tra'Tal, or "King-City". Forests, marshes, and plains broken by simple mountain ranges covered this beautiful planet. Coyn was defended by their space fleet, Coyn'Skraj'Ha.
