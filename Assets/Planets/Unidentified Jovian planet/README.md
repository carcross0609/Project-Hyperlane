# Unidentified Jovian planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Jovian planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Jovian_planet)

During the Galactic Civil War, the smuggler Roark Garnet fought an Imperial for control of a bathysphere in the atmosphere of a Jovian gas giant.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified Jovian planet
- **Class:** Gas giant
- **Gravity:**
  - Heavier than standard for Humans
  - Pressure levels of 1,000 atmospheres
- **Fauna:** Flying life form
