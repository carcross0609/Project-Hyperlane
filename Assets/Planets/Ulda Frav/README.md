# Ulda Frav

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ulda Frav](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ulda_Frav)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ulda Frav`, Expansion Region, grid P-11 — map position (6600.4, -2599.6, -133.8)

Ulda Frav was a tropical Expansion Region planet located in the Hangshan sector. Located in widely explored space by 5000 BBY, by 3998 BBY the world was visited by both a group of Jedi who arbitrated a treaty there and the Nazzar Crown Prince Qrrrl Toq. On the planet, the Jedi informed Toq of his potential for becoming a Jedi, which he eventually did. Millennia later, Vlaydm Churhee served as a sharpshooter on Ulda Frav before forming the Churhee's Riflemen mercenary unit.
