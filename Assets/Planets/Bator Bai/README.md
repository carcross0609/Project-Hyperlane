# Bator Bai

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bator Bai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bator_Bai)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bator Bai`, Mid Rim, grid L-18 — map position (399.6, -12072.6, -197.3)

Bator Bai was an ice world located in the Bator Bai system of the Senex sector, a part of the Senex-Juvex region in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. It was linked by a hyperlane to the Kalgo and Suliana systems.

Bator Bai featured ice cliffs and caverns, the remarkable clarity of which it was known for. Like the other worlds in the Senex-Juvex, it was under the control of the aristocratic Ancient Houses that ruled there.
