# Maryx Minor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maryx Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maryx_Minor)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Maryx Minor`, Outer Rim, grid O-19 — map position (5488.3, -14365.2, 85.9)

Maryx Minor was a planet located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Many eruptions, explosions, and spurts of radiation were common on the planet. These conditions made it difficult to maintain shelter for very long, a reason the Galactic Empire passed over it. Somepoint between 5 BBY and 4 BBY, the Spectres looked to establish a base on the planet, but quickly left after an eruption caused a lot of damage to the Ghost.
