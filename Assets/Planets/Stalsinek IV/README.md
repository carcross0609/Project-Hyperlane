# Stalsinek IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stalsinek IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stalsinek_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Stalsinek IV`, Inner Rim, grid O-9 — map position (5126.0, 704.6, -116.2)

Stalsinek IV was an Inner Rim jungle planet located in the Hapes Cluster. It was the site of the Temple of Stalsinek, which in turn contained a fountain that was the source of legendary healing waters. During the Galactic Civil War, two groups of offworlders visited Stalsinek IV in order to acquire a sample of the healing waters, with one of the groups coming into contact with a local tribe of beings that worshiped at the temple.
