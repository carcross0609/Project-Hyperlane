# Heptalia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heptalia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heptalia)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Heptalia`, Inner Rim, grid M-14 — map position (2710.6, -6789.0, 23.7)

Heptalia was a planet located in the Heptalia system, a part of the Trailing Sectors portion of the Inner Rim. Heptalia's artisans produced furniture coverings and embroidered rugs that were considered to be exquisite and lent renown to the planet.

Shula Pakasj, an employee of GalactiSend on the Core Worlds planet Coruscant, owned a Heptalian embroidered throw by 41 ABY and kept it at her boyfriend Lon Shevu's Coruscant apartment.
