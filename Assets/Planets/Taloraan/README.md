# Taloraan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Taloraan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taloraan)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Taloraan`, Expansion Region, grid I-15 — map position (-3634.1, -7637.2, -210.8)

Taloraan was a gas giant that contained a life zone. Located in the Taloraan system in the Expansion Region and the Interior in grid square I-15 of the Standard Galactic Grid, it was home to the Wind Raiders. While living as a hermit on the planet Pasaana, Lando Calrissian wore a Taloraan Wind Raider's helmet, given to him by Komat. He also wore a coat with a collar insulated with Fleft-wauf pattern.
