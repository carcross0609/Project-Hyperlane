# Charon homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Charon homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charon_homeworld)

The homeworld of the Charon species was located in otherspace. It was located near a massive black hole.

The planet was the first to fall victim to the Charon Death Cult, who cleansed the world of all non-believers before turning their attention to the rest of otherspace.
