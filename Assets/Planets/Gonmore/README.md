# Gonmore

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gonmore](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gonmore)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gonmore`, Outer Rim, grid K-6 — map position (-419.9, 5420.3, -83.6)

Gonmore was a Mid Rim planet located in the Irishi sector and on the Entralla Route hyperlane. At one point a part of the New Republic, by 22 ABY it hosted a sector clearinghouse for the smuggling organization led by Talon Karrde. That year, the smuggler Jerf Huxley unsuccessfully attempted to force Karrde's former second-in-command, Mara Jade Skywalker, to arrange for a large sum of money to be transferred to him from the Gonmore clearinghouse as severance pay for Karrde ending the employment of Huxley and his own smuggling group.
