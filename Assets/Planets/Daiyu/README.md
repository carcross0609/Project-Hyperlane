# Daiyu

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Daiyu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daiyu)

Daiyu was a temperate planet located in the Hocatar sector of the galaxy's Expansion Region. It was under the control of the Galactic Empire during its reign.
