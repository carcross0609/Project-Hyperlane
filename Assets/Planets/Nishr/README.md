# Nishr

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nishr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nishr)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Nishr`, Outer Rim — map position (5184.0, 5296.7, 209.1)

Nishr was a planet in the Outer Rim, home to a near-Human species known as the Nish. It was the scene of a conflict between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War.
