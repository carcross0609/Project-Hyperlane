# Sterna IV

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sterna IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sterna_IV)

Sterna IV was a gas giant in the galaxy that, during the reign of the Galactic Empire, was the site of an Imperial factory facility. The bounty hunter Boba Fett raided the prison to capture a prisoner as part of a bounty.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Sterna IV
- **Class:** Gas giant
- **Interest:** Imperial factory facility
