# Polis Massa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Polis Massa/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_Massa/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Polis Massa`, Outer Rim, grid K-20 — map position (-465.4, -16097.4, 85.6)

Polis Massa was once an arid but habitable planet in the Subterrel sector of the Outer Rim Territories; and the homeworld of the little-known Eellayin species. Polis Massa was later struck by a mysterious cataclysm. This natural disaster destroyed the planet and formed a field of asteroids. The largest fragment of the planet remained known as Polis Massa and was colonized by the Kallidahin who dedicated their entire species to excavate the remains of the now-vanished Eellayin civilization.
