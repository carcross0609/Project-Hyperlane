# Bothawui

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bothawui/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothawui/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bothawui`, Mid Rim, grid R-14 — map position (9333.2, -6510.0, 393.1)

Bothawui () was a cosmopolitan planet, located in the center of Bothan Space in the Mid Rim. It was the homeworld of the Bothan race as well as a major hub for information trafficking. It was also the center of the Bothan Spynet, widely agreed upon to be the best network of information gathering in the galaxy. The Imperial name for the planet was Bothawui Proper.
