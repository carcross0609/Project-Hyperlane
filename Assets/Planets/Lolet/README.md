# Lolet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lolet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lolet)

Lolet was a planet located in the Lolet system. The Galactic Empire's Apprentice Legislature was chosen to decide a punishment for the planet after its government stood in violation of Regulation 16-ME regarding supplying fuel to Imperial pilots.
