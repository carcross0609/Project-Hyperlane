# Skine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skine)

Skine was a planet located in the Skine sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was the location of the Skine Bounty Hunter College.

During the Cold War, the xenophobic and expansionist faction known as the Advozse Hegemony used the prototype Hammer Station battle station to bombard Skine's capital city when the planet refused to surrender to the Hegemony.
