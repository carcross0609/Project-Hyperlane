# Tralfin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tralfin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tralfin)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tralfin`, Outer Rim, grid Q-3 — map position (7670.0, 9890.3, 350.2)

Tralfin was tropical resort world located in the Outer Rim Territories' Xappyh sector. In 28 BBY, the Human Qan Gindoch was born on Tralfin. Shortly after the Battle of Yavin, Moff Crin Harson of the Galactic Empire was murdered on Tralfin. A group of Rebel Alliance agents with which Harson had been acquainted was implicated in the crime, but eventually the real killer was discovered and the Rebels were cleared of the murder charges.
