# G'rho

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G'rho](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G%27rho)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `G'rho`, Wild Space — map position (-3796.0, 5543.8, -316.0)

G'rho was a planet in Wild Space on the Shiritoku Spur and was where forests of juvica grew. A remote colony, its colonists raised duncows and built Boku Settlement near a rich vein of crimson ore. Dev Sibwarra's family moved there shortly after his birth in 11 BBY when they fled Chandrila.
