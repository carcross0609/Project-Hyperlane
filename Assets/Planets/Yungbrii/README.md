# Yungbrii

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yungbrii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yungbrii)

Yungbrii was a cold and rocky planet in the Opalok system of the Far Xandil sector in the Mid Rim. It had a sparse population, including humans, who were mostly unconcerned with galactic affairs. The planet also had a meager defense force. Yungbrii was the homeworld of New Republic Security Bureau officer Sela Trune, whose family were killed during a skirmish that involved the Spice Runners of Kijimi one month after she departed.
