# Torost

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Torost](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torost)

Torost was a terrestrial world located in the Torost system within the Bryx sector of the Mid Rim and the Slice. It was situated in grid square R-7 of the Standard Galactic Grid.

By 35 ABY, Torost was not entirely under First Order control during their war against the Resistance, and as such was one of several contested locations where the First Order deployed ground forces under the direction of General Bellava Parnadee.
