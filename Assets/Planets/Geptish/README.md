# Geptish

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Geptish](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geptish)

Geptish was a terrestrial planet located in the Mid Rim and Western Reaches. During the High Republic Era, Jedi Master Silandra Sho and her Padawan, Rooper Nitani, returned to Geptish's Jedi temple while not on missions on the Galactic Frontier. In 382 BBY, after Nitani performed a pincer movement on the planet Aubadas, she remembered the times she had practiced the maneuver on Geptish. Multiple locations on Geptish were favored meditation spots of Sho, which Nitani recalled while searching similar spots on the world Batuu around 379 BBY.
