# Wizar II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wizar II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wizar_II)

Wizar II was a planet located within the Wizar system of the Wild Space. It was the homeworld of a species of squid-like sentients known as Uroths. It was a terrestrial planet, largely unknown to the outside galaxy.
