# Okyaab 6

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Okyaab 6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Okyaab_6)

Okyaab 6 was a supposed Outer Rim Territories planet located in the Ojoster sector. According to a 3963 BBY Galactic Republic propaganda broadcast by the "Captain Goodvalor" persona, Okyaab 6 had been conquered by the Mandalorian Neo-Crusaders but, after less than a week of Mandalorian rule, the local Okyaabi residents had risen up against them and reclaimed the planet, subsequently joining the Republic.
