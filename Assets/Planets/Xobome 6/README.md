# Xobome 6

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xobome 6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xobome_6)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Xobome 6`, Colonies, grid N-10 — map position (3510.9, -479.4, 117.8)

Xobome 6 was a planet located within the Xobome system of the Colonies, between Commenor and Doldrums. Its high plains were blasted by freezing winds. Wraith Squadron captured the Night Caller over Xobome 6.
