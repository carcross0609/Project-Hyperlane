# Umax

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Umax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umax)

Umax was a planet located somewhere in the galaxy, on which a detention center had been established. Sonko Fromm, a male of the Annoo-dat Blue species, was briefly imprisoned on Umax before he was freed by his boss Sise Fromm.
