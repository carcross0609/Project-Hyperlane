# Aestilan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aestilan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aestilan)

Aestilan was a world located in the Demetras sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Following the Battle of Yavin, the world was scouted by Rebel Alliance forces seeking for a new base. Aestilan was known for its rock worms making the planetary mantle very fragile.
