# Novor 18

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Novor 18](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Novor_18)

Originally the 18th planet of the Novor system, Novor 18 was the the most inner planet that was not destroyed when the system's star, Novor Major, expanded into its red giant phase.
