# Magar's World

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Magar's World/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Magar%27s_World/Legends)

Magar's World was a brutal, inconsequential backwater planet that did not appear on many star-charts. It was said there were only two real reasons why someone might know of it: that individual either went to see the gladiatorial games, or they were among the unlucky beings forced to fight in them.

The despot Magar controlled the illegal games, and life for the slave gladiators and exotic beasts they fought was often extremely short. Successful escapes were rare.
