# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Glastis 3)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Glastis_3%29)

A planet was located in the Glastis system in Chiss Space within the Unknown Regions. Located in the south-nadir part of the Ascendancy, the planet was midway between the capital world Csilla and Copero. The planet was marginally habitable, but was not developed itself; the Chiss Defense Force emergency repair blackdock Glastis 3 was situated in its orbit. Syndic Mitth'ras'safis "Thrass" contemplated that the fact that none of the Ascendancy's families had deemed the star system worth developing indicated that "marginally habitable" was somewhat of an exaggeration.
