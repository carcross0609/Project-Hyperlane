# Malian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Malian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malian)

Malian was a planet located in the Malian system in the Expansion Region. At one point after the Battle of Yavin, Killaen Chamat had a force vision, leading Chamat, Codo Keburr, and Omi Vass to search a temple on Malian for a holocron.
