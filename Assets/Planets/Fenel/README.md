# Fenel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fenel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fenel)

Fenel was an Outer Rim Territories world located in the Demetras sector. It was the homeworld of the Fenelar species. A powerful world possessing starshipwrights and technologists, it was eventually raided by the Mandalorian Crusaders, resulting in the Fenelar's extinction. Fenel subsequently became a Mandalorian world and was bombarded by the Galactic Republic during the Mandalorian Excision.
