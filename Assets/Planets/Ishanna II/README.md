# Ishanna II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ishanna II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ishanna_II)

Ishanna II was the second planet in the Ishanna system. A barren world with no natural atmosphere, Ishanna II was important for its mineral deposits, which were mined by various independent companies. The planet had three moons.
