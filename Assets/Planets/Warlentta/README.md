# Warlentta

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Warlentta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Warlentta)

Warlentta was a world in the Warlentta system that was the homeworld of Larma D'Acy, a commander in the Resistance. After Warlentta refused to join the New Republic, its independent culture made an impression on Senator Leia Organa. Around 28 ABY, after Organa founded the Resistance, she personally visited the world and recruited D'Acy.
