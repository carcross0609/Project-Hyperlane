# Cadannia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cadannia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cadannia)

Cadannia was a planet in the Mid Rim region of the galaxy presumably completely, or mostly covered in dense jungles or forests that produced some of the galaxy's strongest toxins.

Senflax, which secreted a powerful neurotoxin, could only be found on Cadannia. This plant was capable of causing muscle and neural paralysis, and temporarily disrupted one's connection to the Force.
