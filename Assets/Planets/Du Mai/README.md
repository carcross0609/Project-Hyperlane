# Du Mai

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Du Mai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Du_Mai)

Du Mai was a desert world located in the Du Mai system, a part of the Bryx sector in the Slice portion of the Mid Rim. It featured a mountain cave.

At some point by 21 ABY, a holy man known as the Lost Prophet resided in the cave and attracted pilgrims by giving them pieces of gnomic advice. That year, the journalist Cindel Towani revealed that the utterances made by the Lost Prophet consisted of scrambled bits of conditioning and flash-training that clone troopers of the Galactic Republic had undergone on Kamino, the extragalactic planet that was the origin point of the clone army that had served the Republic during the Clone Wars of 22 BBY–19 BBY. Furthermore, Towani gave compelling evidence that the holy man had been a clone trooper pilot who had crashed his ARC-170 starfighter on Du Mai during the final days of that conflict, in the process sustaining head injuries.
