# Parcelus Minor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Parcelus Minor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Parcelus_Minor/Legends)

Parcelus Minor was a planet in the Expansion Region. Its small amount of landmass was almost entirely covered with swamp jungles; the natives of the planet spent much time simply pushing the edge of the swamp back. All plant life on Parcelus Minor exuded tzeotine, a combustible resin used by the native population for heating and fuel.
