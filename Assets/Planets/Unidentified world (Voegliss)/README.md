# Unidentified world (Voegliss)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified world (Voegliss)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_world_%28Voegliss%29)

At one point during the reign of the Galactic Empire, the Imperial scientist Devlor Samt and his team surveyed a supposedly uninhabited world. There, the team came under a ferocious attack by two members of a sentient species that was not native to the world.
