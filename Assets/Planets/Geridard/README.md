# Geridard

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Geridard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geridard)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Geridard`, Mid Rim — map position (-3305.8, -9310.6, -230.3)

Geridard was a Mid Rim world in the Geridard system of Doldur sector. It was known for its retirement and medical facilities, catering to renowned corporate officials. Augusta I'att was to enter the Geridard Convalescent Center, though she was executed before ever attending.
