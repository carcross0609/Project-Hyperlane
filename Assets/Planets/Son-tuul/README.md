# Son-tuul

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Son-tuul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Son-tuul)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Son-tuul`, Outer Rim — map position (10634.7, 5176.8, 406.2)

Son-tuul was a jungle planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. It was the base of the Son-tuul Pride, a criminal organization and one of the Five Crime Syndicates. Darth Vader attacked the Son-tuul Pride and liberated its cache of credits. A group of mercenaries then met at a cantina on the planet to steal those credits from Vader's forces. Later on, a new crew of mercenaries, led by Chelli Aphra, was formed by the Son-tuul Pride to search for their leader 0-0-0's memories. Shortly after they were recovered, the Empire attacked and destroyed the Son-tuul Pride. 0-0-0 survived and left the planet to search for Aphra.
