# Hypori

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hypori](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hypori)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Hypori`, Outer Rim, grid S-16 — map position (10868.0, -9678.2, -227.0)

Hypori was a remote planet located in the Hypori system in the Ferra sector of the Outer Rim Territories that had a hydrothermal landscape that was host to a Techno Union droid factory. The hulk of a crashed Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship stood as a grim memorial of a battle during the Clone Wars in which a group of Jedi Knights were taken by surprise. Hypori had a Geonosian colony, and Geonosian warriors protected Baktoid Armor factories on the planet. During the Imperial Era, Commander Orson Krennic's engineering team was based at an Imperial facility on the planet where they worked in weapon development for the Galactic Empire. In 18 BBY, the research station was destroyed by Star Destroyer turbolasers. At some point, Sheev Palpatine ordered for the Cosinga-class Heavy Corvette Imperialis to be constructed over Hypori. Engines sourced from the Sedapard Cluster were brought to Hypori for the construction project. In the last weeks of the Galactic Civil War, TK-4610 and his Imperial unit was forced to leave Hypori. Rather than be captured like the rest of his unit, TK-4610 retreated to the planet Nevarro.
