# Atloran

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Atloran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atloran)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Atloran`, Core Worlds, grid K-9 — map position (-577.2, 1096.9, 6.4)

Atloran was the third planet in the Panto system. It was a barren, lifeless sphere of a world, but nevertheless was known for its zero-gravity campgrounds and amusement parks. There were seven zero-gee camps around the spaceport and they catered to aliens who had special gravity needs or humanoid species who wanted to enjoy the challenge and benefits of no gravity.
