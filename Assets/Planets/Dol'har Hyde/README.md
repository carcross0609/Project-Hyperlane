# Dol'har Hyde

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dol'har Hyde](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dol%27har_Hyde)

Dol'har Hyde was a forest planet situated in the Outer Rim Territories. During the High Republic Era, a cell of the Nihil marauder organization that was led by Krix Kamerat was based on Dol'har Hyde. The site of a battle centuries before the Imperial Era that involved the Nihil, the planet became strewn with the wreckage of starships. Despite the damage that had been done to the planet, its forests grew back around the wreckage.

The smuggler Remy acquired a Nihil Path engine from the remains of a marauder vessel, selling it to Beol De'Rruyet on Corellia. While investigating the Path engine and its history, the rogue archaeologist Doctor Chelli Aphra traveled to Dol'har Hyde with the smuggler Sana Starros, where they climbed the large tree that had swallowed the wreckage.
