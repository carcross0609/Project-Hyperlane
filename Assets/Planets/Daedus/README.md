# Daedus

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Daedus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daedus)

Daedus was a small planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. After the Nihil pirates created their Stormwall, the planet became trapped within Nihil territory known as the Occlusion Zone.

Creighton Sun and Porter Engle flew a shuttle to Daedus and were shot down by pirates. He managed to crash-land the ship and fought the marauders who had downed them. Years later, Engle returned to the planet to search the wreckage in hopes of finding an intact EX droid.
