# Chita's homeworld

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Chita's homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chita%27s_homeworld)

A planet was the homeworld of the human smuggler Chita. At some point between 19 BBY and 0 ABY, the Galactic Empire arrived to the world and, according to Chita, "made a mess" of the planet and her family, viewing the event as awful. Later, at some point between 2 BBY and 0 ABY, Chita told the former Jedi Gleenu of her homeworld when talking of the planet Zenarita's struggle under Imperial rule, mentioning they were not the only ones that held a grudge against the Empire.
