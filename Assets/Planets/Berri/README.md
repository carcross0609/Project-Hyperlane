# Berri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Berri](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Berri)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Berri`, Inner Rim, grid N-9 — map position (3365.4, 995.0, 125.2)

Berri was a planet in the Inner Rim. It was a hot, smog-shrouded world with high gravity and a dense atmosphere. It was the homeworld of the Berrite species, which evolved to become master foragers due to its few natural food sources. There was a lack of galactic trade with Berri, and its inhabitants cultivated a heavy, hand-crafted industry that harnessed power from natural geothermic energies.
