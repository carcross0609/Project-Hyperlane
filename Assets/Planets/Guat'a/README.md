# Guat'a

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Guat'a](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guat%27a)

Guat'a was a barren planet located in the Outer Rim Territories near the Torch Nebula, and a haven for many criminal types. The Spice Runners of Kijimi undertook a heist on Guat'a, stealing from the warlord Smaatku.
