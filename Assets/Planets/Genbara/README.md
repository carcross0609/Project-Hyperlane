# Genbara

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Genbara](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Genbara)

Genbara was a planet located at the fringe of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. It was blanketed with pines and villages nestled in mountainous valleys. Twenty years after the failed Sith rebellion, the former Sith known as the Ronin came to Genbara. There, he dueled a bandit leader named Kouru who claimed to be a Dark Lord of the Sith.
