# Arbooine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arbooine/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arbooine/Legends)

Arbooine was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories, inhabited by the Sathari species. Quolas was a tree-city on the planet. During the Galactic Civil War, the Galactic Empire believed there were Rebel Alliance bases located among the forest.

The planet was named by a Galactic Republic explorer after he found the native name unpronounceable.
