# Ahlenn

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Ahlenn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahlenn)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ahlenn`, Outer Rim — map position (1782.6, -11314.3, -424.0)

Ahlenn was a planet in the Ahlenn system. During the Galactic Civil War, a prolonged conflict between Rebel Alliance and Galactic Empire, the Imperial Admiral Kohrin's second fleet bombarded the planet thinking that it was home to an active Rebel base, in retaliation for Luke Skywalker's attack on the Executor-class Star Dreadnought Enforcer at the nearby planet of Bespin. The base was in fact empty, abandoned two months previously.
