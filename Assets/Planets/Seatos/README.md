# Seatos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Seatos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seatos)

Seatos was a planet in the Denab system in the Outer Rim Territories that was inhabited in the past by a species from a different galaxy. They constructed a mechanism on it to locate their galaxy powered by a spherical star map.
