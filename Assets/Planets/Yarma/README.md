# Yarma

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yarma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yarma)

Yarma was a planet in the Yarma system within the Tharin sector of the Outer Rim Territories where Reklam Station, an Imperial salvage yard, was located. In 2 BBY, a mission was undertaken by members of Phoenix Cell to steal a squadron of Y-wing starfighters for their fleet.
