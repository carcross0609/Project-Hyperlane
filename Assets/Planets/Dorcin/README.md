# Dorcin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dorcin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorcin)

Dorcin was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories, on or near the Rimma Trade Route. It was a Dorcin trader who traded the Squibs starship technology in exchange for mineral rights to a frozen waste on Skor II.
