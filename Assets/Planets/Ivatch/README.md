# Ivatch

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ivatch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ivatch)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ivatch`, Outer Rim, grid M-21 — map position (2224.0, -16850.5, -115.7)

Ivatch was a planet within the Kathol sector's Ivatch system, located within the Outer Rim Territories. Connected to the neighboring planets of Shintel and Peirs by hyperlanes, Ivatch supported a small colony whose economy was based around supplying ore and natural resources to Gandle Ott.
