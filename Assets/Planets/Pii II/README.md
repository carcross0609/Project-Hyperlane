# Pii II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pii II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pii_II)

Pii II was a planet in the Pii system. A fiery ball of rock, the planet was the second to orbit the Pii sun. Pii II, along with the rest of the Pii system, was located in the Arkanis sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Sometime between 1 ABY and 3 ABY, a battle between the Rebel Alliance's Red Squadron and Imperial Assault Gunboats occurred near Pii II.
