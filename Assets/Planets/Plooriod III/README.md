# Plooriod III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Plooriod III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plooriod_III)

Plooriod III, the third planet of the Plooriod system, was an agricultural territory of the Expansion Region. Plooriod III had a vast ocean with small spire-like islands dotting the surface.
