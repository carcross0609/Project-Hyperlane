# Malsuum

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malsuum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malsuum)

The desert planet Malsuum was the site of a battle during the Clone Wars. Clone trooper Hock Malsuum was marooned on the planet and was taken in by farmers. More than two years later, he left to seek out Darth Vader.
