# Ballum

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ballum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ballum)

Ballum was a planet located within the Mid Rim near the Nihil Occlusion Zone during the High Republic Era. A year after the destruction of Starlight Beacon, Ballum was the base of operations for Skarabda the Wise.
