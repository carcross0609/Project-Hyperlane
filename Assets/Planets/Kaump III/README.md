# Kaump III

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Kaump III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaump_III)

Kaump III was a Mid Rim planet located in the Bruanii sector. Its high gravity led to its native species being short-statured and with a dense body structure. The mercenary and pirate Chanchaz Iryt was born on Kaump III and eventually left his homeworld after committing a series of lucrative robberies in an affluent district of a local city.
