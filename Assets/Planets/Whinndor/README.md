# Whinndor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Whinndor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whinndor)

Whinndor was a world that was located in the Grumani sector. It had a sulphuric atmosphere and seas that were abundant with organic molecules. During the Republic Dark Age the planet was located in a Sith-contested space, with the mercenary Rusher's Brigade once fighting on its surface. The Whinndorian gorsk-plant was a plant species native to Whinndor.
