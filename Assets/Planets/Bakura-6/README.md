# Bakura-6

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bakura-6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bakura-6)

Bakura-6, also known simply as Planet 6, was a barren, ice-covered planet in the Bakura system. It was the site of a small Bakuran colony that was destroyed by the Ssi-ruuk during the battle of Bakura.
