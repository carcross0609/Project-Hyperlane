# Carbos Thirteen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Carbos Thirteen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carbos_Thirteen)

Carbos Thirteen was a planet around which a space battle between Admiral Nek Bwua'tu and the Corellian and Bothan fleets of the Confederation was conducted during the weeks that followed the Second Battle of Fondor. Admiral Bwua'tu managed to ensnare both opposing fleets near the planet, eliminating a large percentage of the warships that supported the Confederation.
