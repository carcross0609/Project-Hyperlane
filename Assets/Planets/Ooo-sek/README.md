# Ooo-sek

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ooo-sek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ooo-sek)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ooo-sek`, Mid Rim — map position (-2806.2, 3154.5, 45.9)

Ooo-sek was one of the first Rodian colony worlds. Its swamps were home to a carnivorous species of plant known as yo'uqiol, Rodese for "Hand of Death." Ooo-sek also boasted vast jungles rife with flora and fauna, making it an ideal hunting ground for adventurous Rodians to train as bounty hunters.
