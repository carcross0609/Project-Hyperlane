# Nameless planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nameless planet (distress signal)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nameless_planet_%28distress_signal%29)

An unremarkable planet located far from inhabited worlds. The outskirts of the planet were rarely visited by starships, so anyone who found themselves on its surface without a way to leave was doomed to a lonely death from depleted supplies or the dangerous wildlife.
