# Rishos

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rishos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rishos)

Rishos was the fourth planet orbiting the star Rish in the Rishi system. Located in the Abrion sector of the Outer Rim Territories, it was a gas giant. Promising stores of gases were found on Rishos that stood waiting to be harvested by enterprising mining corporations.
