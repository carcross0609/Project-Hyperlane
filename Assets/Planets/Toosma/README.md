# Toosma

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Toosma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Toosma)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Toosma`, Outer Rim, grid R-16 — map position (10304.3, -9298.2, -214.8)

Toosma was the fourth and largest planet orbiting the star Tyrius in the Tyrius system. Located in the Savareen sector of the Outer Rim Territories, it was a yellow-orange gas giant and had a total of 18 moons.

The moons of the planet were occasionally mined by Rodian arms manufacturers.
