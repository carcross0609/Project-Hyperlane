# Nightsend

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nightsend](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsend)

Nightsend was a planet in the Nightsend system of the Phelleem sector in the Outer Rim Territories and the Slice. It was far from its star and had a slow rotation. This led to long hot days and cold nights. It was the site of a meeting of the Five Crime Syndicates hosted by Verelea Xhorrun.
