# Fleyars IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fleyars IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fleyars_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Fleyars IV`, Expansion Region, grid N-14 — map position (3346.8, -6698.5, -49.0)

Fleyars IV was the fourth planet in its system. It was the location of the Battle of Fleyars IV, in which the Galactic Empire subjugated the planet. The hero of the Battle was Akal Zed, whom took over command of the Seswennan Nightcloak when his captain was killed.
