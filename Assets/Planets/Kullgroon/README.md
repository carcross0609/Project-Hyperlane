# Kullgroon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kullgroon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kullgroon)

Kullgroon was a planet located in the Expansion Region where droids were repaired at an outpost. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, the Empire enslaved the Petrusians who worked at the outpost, including Rythus, who was the father of freedom fighter Kristiss. Kristiss later hired the smuggler Lando Calrissian to smuggle weapons to the Petrusians on Kullgroon so they could earn their freedom. Lando traveled through the Wastelands of Kullgroon where he was captured by outlaws of the planet. He managed to escape and lead the revolting Petrusians.
