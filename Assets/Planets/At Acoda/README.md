# At Acoda

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [At Acoda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/At_Acoda)

At Acoda was a planet in the galaxy that was one of the Jewels of the Old Republic. The Fallen Sanctum on At Achrann, another of the Jewels, had a pillar with the hyperspace coordinates to At Acoda, along with one for each of the other Jewels, considered destroyed by the wider galaxy.
