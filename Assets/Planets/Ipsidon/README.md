# Ipsidon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ipsidon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ipsidon)

Ipsidon was a remote planet home to violent sandstorms that could occur creating lightning and provoking the Ibisidon bucks into stampeding through its canyons. During or after 18 BBY, the mercenary squad Clone Force 99 became stranded on Ipsidon during a mission to extract ipsium from a mine owned by Ciddarin Scaleback, during which the thief and miner Benni Baro stole their starship, the Marauder. They were able to reclaim it by tracking their GNK-series power droid, Gonky, who was left aboard.
