# Doaskin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Doaskin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doaskin)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Doaskin`, Mid Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-674.9, -12162.7, -185.7)

Doaskin was a planet located in the Doaskin system, a part of the Bruanii sector in the Western Reaches portion of the Outer Rim Territories. During the early years of the reign of the Galactic Empire, which began in 19 BBY, it served as the Tagge Company's primary base for operations in the Greater Javin region. By the time not long after the Battle of Hoth of 3 ABY, Planet Dreams, Incorporated had custom-developed and sold to the House of Tagge a compound at Doaskin, with the company's primary saleswoman Treva Horme being credited with seeing the deal through.
