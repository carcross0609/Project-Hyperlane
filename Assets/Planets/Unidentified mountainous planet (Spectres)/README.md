# Unidentified mountainous planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified mountainous planet (Spectres)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_mountainous_planet_%28Spectres%29)

A mountainous planet was visited by the Spectres rebel cell during the Imperial Era. Rebels Sabine Wren, Hera Syndulla, and Chopper attempted to steal a medical kit from a Lambda-class shuttle on the planet; however, Galactic Empire stormtroopers captured the shuttle while pursuing the Sprectes. Wren and Syndulla then stole another shuttle while Chopper captured the stormtroopers' before fleeing.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified mountainous planet
- **Terrain:** Mountainous
- **Interest:** A Imperial base
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
