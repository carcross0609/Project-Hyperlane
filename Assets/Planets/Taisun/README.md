# Taisun

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Taisun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taisun)

Taisun was a temperate planet located within the galaxy that was the site of Taisun Isle, which was ruled by the Taisun Isle Royal Court. During the New Republic Era, the archaeologist Chelli Lona Aphra and the former smuggler Chewbacca were sent on a mission to Taisun to protect the coronation of the island's king, which brought them into conflict with Krrsantan.
