# Blaudu Octus

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blaudu Octus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blaudu_Octus)

Blaudu Octus was a planet in the Regulan system, near Blaudu Sextus. It was home to the Octusi species, a semi-sentient species who lived as willing servants to the people of Blaudu Sextus until Freedom Flight stirred a revolt amongst the Octusi in 44 ABY.

## Planetary data

- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
- **Sector:** Albanin sector
- **System:** Regulan system

- **Species:** Octusi
