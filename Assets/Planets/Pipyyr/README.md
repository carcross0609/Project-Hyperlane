# Pipyyr

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pipyyr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pipyyr)

Pipyyr was a gray and white colored planet located in the galaxy that was the homeworld of the Pipyyrian species. It was located near the planet of Bakura. The atmospheric pressure of the planet caused problems for some, making them sick. The waters of the planet were poisonous and had to be filtered through carbon before it would become drinkable. Because of that Pipyyrians did not live near water or went outside when it rained. It was not unusual for communication buyos surrounding the planet to burnout because of heavy sunflares.
