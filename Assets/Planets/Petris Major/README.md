# Petris Major

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Petris Major](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Petris_Major)

Petris Major was a planet located in the Outer Rim Territories that had at least one city. During the High Republic Era, Jedi Tar'ak Borran lived on Petris Major, who had an Echo Stone. Later during the High Republic Era, Barnabas Vim traveled to Petris Major in search for the Echo Stone which later Borran gave him. A Oolorian settlement was also located on the planet, which many of the settlement's people died from many small floods from a nearby ocean.
