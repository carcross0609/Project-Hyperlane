# Kalandis Seven

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kalandis Seven](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalandis_Seven)

Kalandis Seven was a desolate planet in the Kalandis system, located in the Pallis sector within the Inner Rim. In 3640 BBY, Kalandis Seven was invaded and occupied by the reconstituted Sith Empire sometime after the Battle of Corellia. The Galactic Republic's military forces on the surface struggled to keep a foothold while the Republic and Imperial Navies fought above the planet. During the battle, a Republic squad on the planet's surface, including Sergeant Shanra Immel and Colonel Jace Malcom, was chosen to destroy the local spaceport. The squad's mission was ultimately successful.
