# Herrik

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Herrik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Herrik)

Herrik was a planet in the remote Herrik system that orbited a star. The star emitted a unique form of stellar interference that caused numerous equipment failures and droid malfunctions among a research team in the system.
