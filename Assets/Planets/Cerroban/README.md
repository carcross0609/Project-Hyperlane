# Cerroban

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cerroban](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cerroban)

Cerroban was a world located in the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Following the Battle of Yavin, the world was scouted by Rebel Alliance forces seeking for a new base. Cerroban was a waterless and airless rocky world that was pounded by asteroids regularly.
