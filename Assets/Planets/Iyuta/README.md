# Iyuta

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iyuta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iyuta)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Iyuta`, Colonies, grid K-9 — map position (-644.6, 1345.1, -87.0)

Iyuta was an underdeveloped planet in the Fakir sector. Controlled by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War, it was the location of several important governmental facilities and was well protected by spy satellites and large cruisers. The small city of Takari was situated on the planet, served by the Iyuta spaceport. An Imperial Communications Center was built outside the city over an old sewer system.
