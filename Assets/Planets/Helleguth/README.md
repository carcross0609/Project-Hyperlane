# Helleguth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Helleguth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Helleguth)

Helleguth was a planet located in the Helleguth system of the Inner Rim. Obi-Wan Kenobi intended to bring the criminal T'mott Zoat there, to stand trial for his crimes. One of those crimes was the murder of a woman named Jesef in 41 BBY, who Obi-Wan Kenobi failed to protect.
