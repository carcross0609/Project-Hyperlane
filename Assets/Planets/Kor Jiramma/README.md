# Kor Jiramma; (The Bootana Hutta region, with Kor Jiramma represented by the number 17)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kor Jiramma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kor_Jiramma)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kor Jiramma`, Hutt Space, grid S-11 — map position (11668.7, -1695.7, 325.5)

Kor Jiramma was a world located in the Kor Jiramma system, a part of the Bootana Hutta area in the Hutt Space portion of the Slice. A hyperlane linked it to the Huloon system. Kor Jiramma was the throneworld of the Jiramma clan of the Hutt species, and its proximity to the throneworld Kor Vanderijar was a reason for partnership between the Jiramma and the Vanderijar clan.
