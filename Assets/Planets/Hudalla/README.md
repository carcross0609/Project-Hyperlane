# Hudalla

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hudalla](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hudalla)

Hudalla was a large red and violet gas giant found in the Hudalla system. Its planetary rings were some of the largest in the entire galaxy. Sifo-Dyas referenced Hudalla's size when joking about the ego of fellow Jedi Initiate Dooku. A type of onion ring wrapped in puffer pig bacon was named the Rings of Hudalla in the planet's honor, and the Artiodac chef Strono Tuggs included the recipe in his cookbook.
