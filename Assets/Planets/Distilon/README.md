# Distilon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Distilon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Distilon)

Distilon was a world that was the site of a battle between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire between 2 BBY and 3 ABY. Rebel pilots Wedge Antilles and Derek Klivian, who had previously defected from the Empire, participated in the fighting at Distilon.
