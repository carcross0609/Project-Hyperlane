# Bastatha

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bastatha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastatha)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bastatha`, Inner Rim, grid L-13 — map position (751.2, -4909.6, 155.8)

Bastatha was a planet in the Bastatha system of the Inner Rim. Independent from the New Republic in the years following the Galactic Civil War, Bastatha became a haven for organized crime, and the Kajain'sa'Nikto crime lord Rinnrivin Di ran his cartel on the planet. Bastatha's surface was superheated by its red giant star, forcing all life on the planet, except for some bacteria, to live in underground caverns and tunnels. Due to the heat, residents of Bastatha preferred chilled foods.
