# Gaurick

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gaurick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gaurick)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gaurick`, Outer Rim, grid S-4 — map position (11318.2, 8770.7, -51.4)

Gaurick was a planet in the Corporate Sector. It was controlled by a religious cult, with leaders known as muftis, with the cult being known as the Renouncers.

Han Solo and his partner Chewbacca were hired to deliver chak-root to workers on Gaurick; the smugglers were almost taken captive when some of the workers had religious objections to the substance.
