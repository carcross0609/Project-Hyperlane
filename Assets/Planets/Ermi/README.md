# Ermi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ermi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ermi)

Ermi was a planet located within the Sertar sector. Many years prior to the Clone Wars, a number of Jedi were from Ermi, and were distinguished by crafting lightsabers with multifaceted gems that caused stray energy tendrils to spiral off from their lightsaber blades. During the Clone Wars, distinct equipment inspired by the Jedi of the planet Ermi were available to Jedi Knights.
