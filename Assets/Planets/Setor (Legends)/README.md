# Setor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Setor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Setor/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Setor`, Colonies, grid L-8 — map position (270.2, 2098.9, 30.1)

Setor was a Colonies world. A group of colonists were persecuted on, and then exiled from, Setor nearly a decade before the Battle of Yavin. The colonists intended to settle the Outer Rim Territories world Wetyin's Colony but changes in financial support from the Galactic Empire left them virtually abandoned. The colonists then searched for a new homeworld, and the scout Lile Gorsek eventually became convinced that the moon Yavin 4 would over time allow the Wetyin's Colony's inhabitants to rival and even surpass their original home, Setor.
