# Vir Aphshire

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vir Aphshire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vir_Aphshire)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Vir Aphshire`, Other, grid P-18 — map position (7103.5, -13272.7, -231.1)

Vir Aphshire was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories' Tharin sector which had honey fields, orchards, and clay hives. Around 0 ABY, the Rebel Alliance's 61st Mobile Infantry drove the Galactic Empire's forces from the orchards and clay hives, during which news came about the destruction of the Empire's Death Star. After the fighting, the rebel Sergeant Fektrin led a scout party to observe the nearest settlement on the planet and had Private Hazram Namir pass his report on to Captain Micha Evon. Upon receiving the report, Evon spoke with Namir about the private's accomplishments in the company as well as their philosophies on war.
