# Kamasto

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kamasto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kamasto)

Kamasto was a frigid world that was located in the Grumani sector, along the Hydian Way hyperlane, where it orbited an orange dwarf. The world was the site of a number of monasteries that were operated by the Children of Mani, before the religion was divided by a schism. The world was later the site of a number of unsuccessful acts of diplomacy. Between 3701 BBY and 3699 BBY the Hydian Way was charted through Kamasto as part of the Seswenna Expansion.
