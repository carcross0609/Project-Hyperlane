# Lao-mon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lao-mon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lao-mon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lao-mon`, Colonies — map position (3235.5, -1987.2, -4.7)

Lao-mon was the tropical homeworld of the shape-shifting Shi'ido species, it was known in their language as Sh'shuun. Their world remained isolated, its exact location unknown to any outlanders. It was the homeworld of the Senior Anthropologist Mammon Hoole, who was famous during the New Republic.
