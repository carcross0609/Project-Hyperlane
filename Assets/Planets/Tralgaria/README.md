# Tralgaria

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tralgaria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tralgaria)

Tralgaria was a planet that had massive amounts of rhydonium. Between 10 BBY and 9 BBY, Jabba the Hutt had drilling platforms on Tralgaria to mine rhydonium.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Tralgaria
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
- **Sector:** Parmic sector
- **System:** Tralgaria system
- **Coordinates:** P-18
- **Exports:** Rhydonium
