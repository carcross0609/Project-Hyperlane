# Zissh

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zissh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zissh)

Zissh was a planet located within the Zissh system of the Expansion Region. It was a frozen world where Jedi Knight Aven Rolk planned to establish a new Jedi Temple and rebuild the Jedi Order. However, his proclamation drew the attention of the Galactic Empire, and Rolk was later found dead in an ice cave.
