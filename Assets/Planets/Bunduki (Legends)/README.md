# Bunduki

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bunduki/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bunduki/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bunduki`, Outer Rim, grid H-16 — map position (-5625.2, -9455.2, 55.8)

Bunduki was a planet in the Pacanth Reach. It was home to the Followers of Palawa, who developed the martial art of Teräs Käsi. Many of the Followers, including Arden Lyn, fell to the dark side of the Force and joined the Legions of Lettow. The planet was conquered by the Epicanthix.

The mother of Zan and Zu Pike was an important noble of Bunduki.

Young mineral heir Yun was sent to Bunduki to learn patience from the Followers of Palawa, though he returned to his father when they refused to train him in teräs käsi. Following his seduction by Sariss—and the dark side—Yun, now a Dark Jedi, returned to Bunduki with an Imperial force to revenge himself on the Followers. There, the group encountered Arden Lyn, who was likewise seeking vengeance against the monks.
