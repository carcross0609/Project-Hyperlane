# Renatasia III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Renatasia III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Renatasia_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Renatasia III`, Outer Rim, grid U-8 — map position (14435.2, 2530.4, 39.0)

Renatasia III, or simply Renatasia, was an agriworld that was brutally conquered by the Galactic Empire.

Renatasia III was a lost Human colony world located in the Open Sea. It was one of fourteen colonization projects sponsored by Queen Tasia of Grizmallt in 3950 BBY.

By the time of rediscovery, the local inhabitants were using Steel, Titanium, and Organoplastics, were capable of wringing useful amounts of energy from the core of an atom; and had also profitably colonized six bodies in their system. They had not yet made basic discoveries that would inevitably lead to shields, tractor beams, disruptors, and disintegrators.
