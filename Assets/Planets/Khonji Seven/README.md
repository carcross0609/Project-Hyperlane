# Khonji Seven

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Khonji Seven](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khonji_Seven)

Khonji Seven was a world that was located just outside the Brunet system.

It was the site of an Imperial testing base and in 1 BBY, the Star Destroyer Vector was sent to transport a virus to the testing base. However, the Star Destroyer never arrived there due to an outbreak of the virus which decimated the crew.
