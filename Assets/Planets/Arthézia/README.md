# Arthézia

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Arthézia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arth%C3%A9zia)

Arthézia was a planet and the homeworld of Professor Adazian Loh'Khar. His family lived on the planet while he worked for the Flight Unlimited Group, an Imperial-aligned corporation. The corporation threatened to injure Loh'Khar's family if he stopped working for them.
