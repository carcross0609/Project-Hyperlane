# Heiklet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heiklet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heiklet)

Heiklet was a storm planet located in the Occlusion Zone during the High Republic Era. Its people and culture had been destroyed at some point in its history by the planetwide storm that was still ongoing in 228 BBY, but at least one fortress still survived. Marchion Ro brought Baron Boolan to this fortress in order to complete his research into a cure for the blight, along with the captive Jedi Sskeer who was to be used by Boolan as a test subject.
