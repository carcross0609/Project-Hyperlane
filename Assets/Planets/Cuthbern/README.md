# Cuthbern

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cuthbern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cuthbern)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Cuthbern`, Outer Rim, grid J-18 — map position (-2087.0, -12590.8, -209.3)

Cuthbern was a desolate wasted moon in the Wazta sector of the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy. It was known for its factories that produced extremely high quality weapons, including the Star Anvil blaster rifle. It was annexed by the Empire and its population was enslaved. It was later abandoned after a Rebel attack.
