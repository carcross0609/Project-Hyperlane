# Bront

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bront](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bront)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bront`, Outer Rim, grid O-19 — map position (5089.1, -14093.5, -148.7)

Bront was a planet located within the Bront system, a part of the Outer Rim Territories' Svivreni sector. At some point by 0 ABY, the Human male Graf Yonna and his family lived on Bront.

During that time, the Galactic Empire committed atrocities on Bront that led to the planet's destruction as well as the death of Yonna's family. The resultant psychological trauma caused Yonna to lose his sanity, and he assumed the personality of Parsol d'Ahndole, an ancient adventurer and hero.
