# Kor Gejalli; (The Bootana Hutta region, with Kor Gejalli represented by the number 18)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kor Gejalli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kor_Gejalli)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kor Gejalli`, Hutt Space, grid S-11 — map position (11668.7, -1844.3, -233.5)

Kor Gejalli was a world located in the Kor Gejalli system, a part of the Bootana Hutta area in the Hutt Space portion of the Slice. It was connected by hyperlanes to the Groth and Kor Trinivii systems. Kor Gejalli served as the throneworld of the Gejalli clan of the Hutt species; however, at some point by the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, the Anjiliac clan defeated the Gejalli in a clan war and took over its holdings, including Kor Gejalli.
