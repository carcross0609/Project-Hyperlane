# Daxam IV

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Daxam IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daxam_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Daxam IV`, Outer Rim — map position (9539.0, 9248.8, -119.6)

Daxam IV, also referred to simply as Daxam, was a cold, desert planet in the Outer Rim Territories, at the edge of the binary star system of the same name. It was connected by hyperspace route to Rior and Azzameen Station, and was also near the planet Pamarthe. Consequently, many Pamarthen pilots were recruited to work there.

By 28 ABY, Daxam IV had joined the New Republic. Despite the planet's self-reliance and limited off-planet commerce, it was aligned with the Centrists, which favored a stronger galactic government and idealized the old Galactic Empire. During this time, the world was also home to the Amaxines, a paramilitary and terrorist group that was secretly aligned with the First Order.
