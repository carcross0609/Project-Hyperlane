# Iotra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iotra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iotra)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Iotra`, Outer Rim, grid S-8 — map position (11210.2, 1659.1, 425.8)

Iotra was the fifth planet in the Iotran system and the temperate, industrial homeworld to the militant Iotran species. The planet was ruled by the Joint Council, eight top-ranking leaders of the Iotran Police Force (IPF).

The southern latitudes of the planet were plagued by the seasonal East Vacaliv wind storms; the IPF often needed to provide rescue operations during these storms.
