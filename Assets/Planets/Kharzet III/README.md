# Kharzet III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kharzet III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kharzet_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kharzet III`, Outer Rim — map position (10701.8, 8564.0, 242.4)

Kharzet III was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories' Mortex sector that was uncharted as of 3 ABY. The planet's lush forests were home a wide variety of plant life, and also to a unique species of gundark known as the long-necked gundark. Princess Leia Organa and C-3PO scouted the planet for possible new location for the Rebel base after the Battle of Hoth, but they they quickly fled after coming under attacked by long-necked gundarks.
