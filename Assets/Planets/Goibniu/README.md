# Goibniu

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Goibniu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Goibniu)

Goibniu was a planet located within the Videnda sector of the Outer Rim Territories. A space battle between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire took place near the planet during the Galactic Civil War.
