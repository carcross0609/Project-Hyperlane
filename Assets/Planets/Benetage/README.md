# Benetage

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Benetage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Benetage)

Benetage was a planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. It was abandoned almost 30,000 years before the Galactic Civil War between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire. It was believed that the Crown of Verity was used as a symbol of the planet's magistrates before its abandoning, and the crown eventually came into the possession of the Jedi Order. That crown was later taken to Auratera by Jedi Master Tobar Ka-Teen.
