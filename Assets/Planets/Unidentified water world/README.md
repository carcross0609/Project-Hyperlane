# Unidentified water world

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified water world](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_water_world)

An inhabited water world existed in the galaxy. In 382 BBY, the EX droid EX-8C saw the world, with artificial lights illuminating its inhabited areas, on its journey from the Outer Rim planet Gloam while carrying a distress message from stranded Jedi Master Rok Buran.
