# Bendii

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bendii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bendii)

Bendii was a planet located in the Seitia sector of the Outer Rim. From space, Bendii appeared as a blue orb. Around six months after the Battle of Yavin, Han Solo, Leia Organa and Chewbacca fled Imperial and bounty hunter pursuit above Bendii while ferrying supplies for the Rebel Alliance aboard the Millennium Falcon.
