# Soullex

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Soullex](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soullex)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Soullex`, Outer Rim, grid P-3 — map position (6090.0, 9741.5, -44.2)

Soullex was a planet in the Fardon system in Wild Space of the galaxy. After the Battle of Tantive V, it was garrisoned as the headquarters of Fleet Admiral Gaen Drommel. Drommel was betrayed and killed by Colonel Niovi, who surrendered the Guardian to the New Republic.
