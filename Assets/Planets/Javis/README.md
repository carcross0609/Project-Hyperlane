# Javis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Javis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Javis)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Javis`, Colonies — map position (1538.2, 1820.5, -60.4)

Javis was a planet in located in the Tapani sector's Javis system.

Tampson Consolidated, Incorporated moved its headquarters from the Mid Rim to Javis and took a loan from House Mecetti. House Mecetti ensured Tampson defaulted on the loan, thus gaining control of the company.
