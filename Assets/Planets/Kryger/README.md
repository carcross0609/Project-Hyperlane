# Kryger

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kryger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kryger)

Kryger was a terrestrial planet in the Kryger system in the Ado sector of the Mid Rim Territories where the Jedi Order operated a Jedi temple during the High Republic Era. Around 161 BBY, the planet experienced an unprecedented rain season, which was followed by a mudslide, leaving Kryger in the midst of environmental collapse. The Devaronian Jedi Master Quintana, who was stationed at the Kryger Jedi temple, assisted evacuating those who wanted to leave the planet, and was eventually reassigned to serve at the Jedi temple on the Colonies planet Hynestia.
