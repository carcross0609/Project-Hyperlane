# Kholes

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kholes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kholes)

Kholes was a planet in Imperial space and the home of a Rebel outpost. In 1 ABY, soon after an outbreak of Bledsoe's disease on the nearby planet Tay'ah'loo, the Rebel outpost was wiped out by Imperial forces.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Kholes
- **Region:** Mid Rim
- **Sector:** Elbaran sector
- **System:** Kholes system
