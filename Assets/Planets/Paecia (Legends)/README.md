# Paecia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Paecia/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paecia/Legends)

Paecia was a world located in the Quelii sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The world was inhabited by Humans known as the Paecians. During the Old Republic era, they developed a great civilization and founded an empire named the Paecian Empire. The Humans from Paecia then colonized neighboring worlds such as Dathomir. The Paecian Empire eventually vanished around 3000 BBY.
