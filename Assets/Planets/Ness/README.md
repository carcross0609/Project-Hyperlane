# Ness

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ness](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ness)

Ness was a terrestrial planet in the Dagobah system of the Sluis sector in the Outer Rim Territories. Along with the rest of the star system, the planet lay on the hyperlane known as the Rimma Trade Route, and it occupied the first orbital position around the star Darlo. Its surface consisted of molten rock, and the planet featured a searing hot environment.
