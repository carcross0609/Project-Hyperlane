# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Vagaari pirate base)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Vagaari_pirate_base%29)

An Unknown Regions planet located within a star system was the location of the fictitious Vagaari pirate base according to Watith Captain Fsir. Around 18 BBY, the captain was hired to attack the Chiss heavy cruiser Springhawk by the Grysk operative "Jixtus," Fsir brought the cruiser to the world by telling its commander, Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo "Thrawn," that the Vagaari base was located there.

After arriving over the world, Watith gunboats attacked the Springhawk in hopes of delaying or destroying it. Although Fsir had informed Thrawn that an orbital weapons platform was located on the world's far side, and that they would alert it of the heavy cruiser's arrival, the Chiss knew that the Watith was lying, and disabled Fsir's Saltbarrel freighter. After disabling it with ship–disabling plasma spheres, the gunboats went dormant, confirming Thrawn's theory that Captain Fsir was controlling them. The Springhawk left the planet after capturing all Watith warships and the Saltbarrel.
