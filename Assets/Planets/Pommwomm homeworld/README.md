# Pommwomm homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pommwomm homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pommwomm_homeworld)

The inner planet of the Sif'kric system was located in the Mezeg sector of the Mid Rim.

The planet was the homeworld of the pommwomm plant, used by the Sif'krie to produce food and medicine. Export of pommwomm plants made up about 20% of the Sif'kric economy.
