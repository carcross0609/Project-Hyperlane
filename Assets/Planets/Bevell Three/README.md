# Bevell Three

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bevell Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bevell_Three)

Bevell Three was the third planet in the Bevell system. Jai Raventhorn led a Rebel team to this planet to capture four Imperial agents, but the mission was compromised: only Jai escaped. The four Infiltrators commandos who died included Leong. As a propaganda measure rather than a reward, Jai was promoted to Master Sergeant.
