# Revery

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Revery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revery)

Revery was a planet known for being inhabited by rich beings, as well as its beaches, mountains, and privacy. It appeared blue from space, and on the planet, there were aquamarine seas, green and gold land, and pink clouds could be seen in the sky. Astri Oddo and Clive Flax visited this planet looking for Eve Yarrow's estate.
