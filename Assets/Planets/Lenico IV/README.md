# Lenico IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lenico IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lenico_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lenico IV`, Outer Rim, grid L-6 — map position (942.1, 5803.6, 122.3)

Lenico IV was a planet in the Lenico system of the Outer Rim Territories. Its barren surface presented ruins and others desolated places, like the Ash Plains. Numerous space station colonies such as Lenico Colony Blue remained in its orbit.

While the surface of the planet was supposedly dead, several life forms found refuge on the planet around the time of the Great Galactic War, including a small tribe of Ortolans, before they where massacred by the passing Sith apprentice Exal Kressh. Later during the war, members of the Jedi Order took part in a battle on the Ash Plains.
