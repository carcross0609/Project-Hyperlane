# Dahgee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dahgee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dahgee)

Dahgee was a planet where unique lightsaber crystals were mined. A small group of Jedi with unique robes also lived there. The lightsaber crystals on Dahgee gave their lightsabers a strange, zigzagging blade with a black core, almost like a tree branch.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Dahgee
- **Region:** Mid Rim
- **Sector:** Thusa sector
- **System:** Dahgee system
- **Coordinates:** O-7
- **Exports:** Dahgee Lightsaber crystals
