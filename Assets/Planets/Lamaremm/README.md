# Lamaremm

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lamaremm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lamaremm)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lamaremm`, Outer Rim — map position (5631.0, 9478.7, -47.6)

Lamaremm was the fourth planet in the Lamaro system.

Small and covered in ice, it developed a native life made of microbes and lichen. Around five hundred years before the Battle of Naboo, the Outer Rim Oreworks Company analyzed the planet and concluded that it had no commercial value for them.
