# Nagi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nagi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nagi)

Nagi was a planet that was the homeworld of the Nagai species. During the New Republic Era, a number of Nagai were forcefully removed from their homeworld to the planet Gadrilam by other members of the species. Later, the Zantarrk Gang were informed a few hundred more Nagai were en route from Nagi to them.
