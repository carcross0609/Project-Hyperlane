# Capella

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Capella](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capella)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Capella`, Outer Rim, grid Q-6 — map position (8329.9, 5579.9, 152.8)

Capella was a mining world located in the Capella system. It was situated in the Back Reach area of the Gordian Reach sector within the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Capella was linked via hyperlane to the Arda and Little Capella systems. It was the origin point of Capellan turg-roots. Mining operations there were mostly carried out by droids and robominers manufactured by the inhabitants of the Arda system.
