# Unidentified city planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified city planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_city_planet)

A city planet was located in the galaxy. During the rule of the Galactic Empire, a rebel cell known as the Spectres came in their starship Ghost to the city planet, and picked up some supplies, destroyed machines and a com array, liberated prisoners of the Empire, and destroyed an All Terrain Armored Transport.
