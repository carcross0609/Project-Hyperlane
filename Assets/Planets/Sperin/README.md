# Sperin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sperin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sperin)

Sperin was a planet in the Bajic sector of the Outer Rim Territories, near the Vergesso Asteroids.

It was the secret headquarters of the Natori Association, which was a regional agency of the Tenloss Syndicate. Worked from a base on Sperin, the Natori Association constituted a loose collective of ex-military personnel who banded together to train mercenaries for hire. During the Galactic Civil War, this company had a large proportion of non-humans in its ranks, making it a suspicious entity in the eyes of the Galactic Empire, but the company was tolerated since it had not yet come into conflict with Imperial forces in a significant way. Unlike other elements of the Tenloss Syndicate, Natori maintained neutrality toward the Alliance in an attempt to keep the Imperials from focusing attention on the Association.
