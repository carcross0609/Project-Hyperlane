# Andowyne

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Andowyne](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andowyne)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Andowyne`, Outer Rim, grid M-21 — map position (2774.3, -16938.5, -544.6)

Andowyne was a Force-imbued planet located in the Kathol Rift. It was the home planet of the Brownie, Daikini, Nelwyn and possibly midi-chlorian life forms. The planet was forgotten for many years by the galaxy at large, but was later rediscovered by Jedi historians in the Jedi Archives.
