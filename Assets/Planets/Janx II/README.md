# Janx II

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Janx II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Janx_II)

Janx II was a gas giant located in the Janx system in the Prackla sector of the Expansion Region near the Inner Rim Territories. In 21 ABY, Boxer Point Station, a space station that functioned as a huge multipurpose port facility, was located in high orbit above Janx II.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Janx II
- **Region:** Expansion Region
- **Sector:** Prackla sector
- **System:** Janx system
- **Coordinates:** O-8
- **Class:** Gas giant
