# Lenahra

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lenahra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lenahra)

Lenahra was a terrestrial planet on the most distant reaches of the Outer Rim Territories in the Unknown Regions. The planet was strong with life energy and had a degree of sentience, with its entire biosphere strongly connected to each other through a substance known as the Power.
