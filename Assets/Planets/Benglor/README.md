# Benglor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Benglor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Benglor)

Benglor was a planet in the Onatos sector in the galaxy's Mid Rim Territories. It had rocky terrain and a hot climate. During the Clone Wars, Jedi General Anakin Skywalker investigated supposed Confederacy of Independent Systems activity on Benglor with a force of clone troopers of the 501st Legion. On Benglor, Skywalker and his troopers were attacked by a creature, which Skywalker managed to defeat with the help of Clone Captain CT-7567 "Rex."
