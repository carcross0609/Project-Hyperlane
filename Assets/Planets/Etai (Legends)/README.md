# Etai

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Etai/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Etai/Legends)

Etai was a planet located in the Mid Rim of the galaxy. Miners plumbed the depths of the world's deep caverns in search of the rare, resonant etaan crystals, a powerful gemstone that was utilized to power lightsabers.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Etai
- **Region:** Mid Rim
- **Coordinates:** Canon-only
- **Terrain:** Deep caverns
