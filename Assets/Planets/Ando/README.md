# Ando

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ando](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ando)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ando`, Mid Rim, grid Q-15 — map position (7887.5, -8964.5, 140.7)

Ando was a water-covered planet in the Ando system of the Lambda sector in the galaxy's Mid Rim, located in grid square Q-15 on the Standard Galactic Grid, that was the homeworld of the amphibious Aqualish species. At the time of the Trade Federation's Invasion of Naboo, it was a member of the Galactic Republic and was represented in the Galactic Senate by Senator Po Nudo. During the Separatist Crisis, turmoil occurred on Ando that left the galaxy staggered and it seceded from the Republic, joining the Confederacy of Independent Systems, which promised it the support of the InterGalactic Banking Clan and Techno Union. A type of wine known as Andoan wine was produced on Ando. It was the homeworld of Senator Po Nudo, the weapons manufacturer Amda Wabo, and the smuggler Ponda Baba.
