# Hisseen

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hisseen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hisseen)

Hisseen was a planet in the Mid Rim Territories that was home to the Hisseenians. During the Clone Wars, the Confederacy of Independent Systems attempted to capture the planet in 22 BBY, and the Galactic Republic sent Jedi Generals Obi-Wan Kenobi, Plo Koon, and Anakin Skywalker to protect the planet and its inhabitants.
