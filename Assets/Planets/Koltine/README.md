# Koltine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Koltine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Koltine)

Koltine was a Wild Space world located within the star system of the same name as part of the Jahilid Drift region. More than a decade after the establishment of the Galactic Empire, the Kaleesh Imperial General Bentilais san Sk'ar led a strike force in conquering Koltine and other worlds along the Jahilid Drift. Sk'ar's victories there led to a boost in his reputation among Imperial admirals.
