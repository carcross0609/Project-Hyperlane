# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Mother)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Mother%29)

A planet was located in the Unknown Regions. It was orbited by the Rakatan entity Mother who lived inside an abandoned space station, connected to it by an organic tether. The planet was in tidal lock with another planet of the system.
