# Shador

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shador](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shador)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Shador`, Mid Rim, grid Q-13 — map position (8056.7, -4721.3, -237.2)

Shador was a Mid Rim planet on the Gamor Run. It was the Shadorian homeworld and had such a foul atmosphere, that it was painful for Humans to breathe. The planet was dominated by swamps, and the various towns were built on pylons rising above the wet terrain.

Its residents were known as Shadorians, and were known as assassins. Han Solo visited the planet at least twice, the second time with a comatose Darth Vader in his hold shortly after the Battle of Yavin.
