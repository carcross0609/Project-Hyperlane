# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Live islands)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Live_islands%29)

A planet existed in the galaxy that was home to a scrap eating worm species. In 4 ABY, Nien Nunb and Shriv Suurgav landed on the planet to look for two TIE fighters.
