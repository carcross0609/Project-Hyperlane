# Ottethan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ottethan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ottethan/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ottethan`, Outer Rim — map position (-1902.9, 9041.6, -86.2)

Ottethan was a planet located in the Outer Rim Territories's Ottethan system, in the Ottethan Empire. It was ruled by a warlord who married Neema-Da-Boda, but later fed her to the rancors that roamed the planet.
