# Destrillion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Destrillion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Destrillion)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Destrillion`, Outer Rim — map position (-4837.7, -9972.6, -535.9)

Destrillion was a planet in the Dubrillion system of the Outer Rim Territories, in close proximity to Dubrillion and the asteroids of Lando's Folly. It was a barren world with a turbulent atmosphere, and appeared brown and dirty from space. It also contained various energy tunnels as well as an energy field containing a tibanna gas platform. In 3 ABY, during the Galactic Civil War, Rogue Squadron, led by Wedge Antilles, was sent there to investigate an Imperial research facility, but found nothing but several TIE Hunters defending an Imperial supply convoy; the Alliance would later discover the facility on Dubrillion.

Twenty-one years later, when Lando Calrissian came to Dubrillion to find a new way to mine asteroids, he built several ore processing plants in the upper atmosphere of Destrillion. It was conquered by the Yuuzhan Vong during their invasion of the galaxy, forcing the inhabitants to evacuate to Dantooine.
