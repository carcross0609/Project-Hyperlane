# Casfield 6

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Casfield 6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Casfield_6)

Casfield 6 was a planet in the Casfield system. Incoming starship traffic was managed by droids. A six-ship collision resulted in the death of the 6,000 year old Queen of the Offens.
