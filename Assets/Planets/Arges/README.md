# Arges

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arges](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arges)

Arges was a planet that was believed to be the homeworld of the arges frog, a type of frog that had come to inhabit the forests and deserts of several other worlds, including Nevarro, Ossus, and Arvala-7, by the New Republic Era. Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts Director of Creature Cartography Ardis San Tekka mentioned Arges when writing about arges frogs in her journal at some point after the Battle of Exegol in 35 ABY.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Arges
- **Fauna:** Arges frog
