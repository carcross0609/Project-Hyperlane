# Runaway Prince

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Runaway Prince](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Runaway_Prince)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Runaway Prince`, Hutt Space, grid R-11 — map position (9718.8, -2090.8, 72.3)

Runaway Prince, called Lordunkee Wanpolla in Huttese, was an uninhabited planet in Hutt Space which the Hutts gave to the Yuuzhan Vong.

The planet was a minor Hutt colony, and got its name due to some event or story, sometime before modern recorded history. The peculiar name was the only thing that remained of that otherwise forgotten tale.

When the Hutts first arrived, they squeezed the planet dry of all of its natural resources in a matter of months, making it a bog world like Nal Hutta. During the Yuuzhan Vong War, the planet was one of the many given as a token of goodwill to the extragalactic invaders. The Yuuzhan Vong then terraformed Runaway Prince to suit themselves and turned the planet into a yorik coral production facility. Villips were also grown on the planet.
