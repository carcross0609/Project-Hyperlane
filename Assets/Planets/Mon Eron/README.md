# Mon Eron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mon Eron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Eron)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mon Eron`, Outer Rim — map position (10935.9, -10509.9, -373.5)

Mon Eron was the fifth planet in the Calamari system. It was settled by members of the Mon Calamari species around 4400 BBY. The colonists eventually terraformed the planet's oceans. It was orbited by two moons. and contained forests.
