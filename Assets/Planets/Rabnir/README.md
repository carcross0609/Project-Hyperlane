# Rabnir

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rabnir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rabnir)

Rabnir was a planet located in Wild Space. During the Galactic Civil War, the Twi'lek female Daesha, a disciple of Hitanaga, the queen of the Nightlands, resided on the planet and lured the Jedi Luke Skywalker to the planet so he could be offered to Hitanaga to house her spirit. However, Princess Leia Organa had followed Skywalker and helped to foil Hitanaga's attempts.
