# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (dead red moon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28dead_red_moon%29)

A planet was the third orbit of a star in the Sixela system, located in the Outer Rim's Thuris sector. The planet had a single dead red moon. The moon held a civilization which had been extinct millennia before 1 ABY, when it was settled by the rogue Imperial Commander Rezi Soresh.

Soresh's machinations led to a battle in the system. The planet, along with everything else within a few billion kilometers, were turned into radiation and dust when the sun became supernova as a result of Soresh's resonance torpedoes.
