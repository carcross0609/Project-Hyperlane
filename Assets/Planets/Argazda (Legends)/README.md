# Argazda

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Argazda/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Argazda/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Argazda`, Outer Rim, grid N-4 — map position (3754.1, 7873.9, -280.4)

Argazda was the Argazdan homeworld. Around the time of the Mandalorian Wars, local Provisional Governor Myrial and her followers the Myrialites rebelled against the Galactic Republic, establishing the Argazdan Redoubt. They conquered neighboring worlds throughout the Kanz sector, including Lorrd. These events were known as the Kanz Disorders, lasting from 3970 BBY–3670 BBY. This conflict resulted in the deaths of more than five billion until combined Republic/Jedi efforts under Lorrdian Jedi Master Mari-Elan Nora overthrew the Myrialites.

During the Clone Wars the planet was located within Separatist space. Later, during the Yuuzhan Vong War, Argazda surrendered to the extragalactic Yuuzhan Vong invaders as they swept through the Outer Rim.
