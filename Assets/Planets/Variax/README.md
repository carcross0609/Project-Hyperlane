# Variax

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Variax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Variax)

Variax was a rocky planet in the Variax system in the Aldino sector of the Mid Rim Territories that hosted a shipyard during the High Republic Era. After taking on several new recruits and capturing Jedi Padawan Wes Vinik, the pirate Sellaccc Orryak and the Ganguls attempted to steal starships from the Variax shipyard. The Jedi Initiates Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay, and Nubs, assisted by the pilot Nash Durango and former pirate Chigg, rescued Vinik and stopped the Ganguls, leaving them to be confronted by Variax security.
