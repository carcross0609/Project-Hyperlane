# Dybrin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dybrin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dybrin)

Dybrin was a planet located in the Dybrin system, in the Deep Core and the Interior region, in grid square L-12 on the Standard Galactic Grid. In 34 ABY, historian and author Eloc Throno included a map with the location of Dybrin in his book Traveler's Guide to Batuu, a guide that detailed the planet Batuu and its inhabitants. Dybrin was located in the same star system as the moon Dybrin 12, homeworld of the Dybrinthe species.
