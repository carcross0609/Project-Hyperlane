# Garen IV

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Garen IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Garen_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Garen IV`, Outer Rim — map position (3932.4, -13333.9, 125.9)

Garen IV was a terrestrial prison world in the Garen system in the Thuldin's Grant sector of the Expansion Region, in grid square J-15 of the Standard Galactic Grid, that operated during the Imperial and New Republic Eras. The convicted Gamorrean criminal Chargutt Lo served a 15-year sentence on Garen IV for aggravated assault. The sentence was listed on Lo's profile, which was among a number of profiles seen by Marshal Carasynthia Dune when she searched for that of the criminal Migs Mayfeld in 9 ABY.
