# Urajab

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Urajab](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Urajab)

Urajab was a jungle planet located in the Urajab system within the Vulcusion sector of the Expansion Region and Trailing Sectors, in grid square L-16 on the Standard Galactic Grid. In the first week of the Lee Skillen's job as a supervisor at the company Ohnaka Transport Solutions in Black Spire Outpost on the Outer Rim planet Batuu, she accidentally sent a crate of worms to Urajab instead of its intended destination. According to Skillen, the worms became an invasive species on Urajab, and destroyed the planet's jungles, reducing them to weeds.

Sometime later, in 34 ABY, Skillen told the tale to the farmer Julen Rakab, a resident of Black Spire Outpost who mistakenly delivered the wrong parcel to Ohnaka Transport Solutions. The same year, the historian and author Eloc Throno included Urajab on a map in his Batuu guidebook, Traveler's Guide to Batuu.
