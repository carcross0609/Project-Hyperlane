# Saracor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Saracor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saracor)

Saracor was a planet that had massive amounts of rhydonium. Between 10 BBY and 9 BBY, Jabba the Hutt had drilling platforms on Saracor to mine rhydonium.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Saracor
- **Region:** Mid Rim Territories
- **Sector:** Oriolan sector
- **System:** Saracor system
- **Coordinates:** J-16
- **Exports:** Rhydonium
