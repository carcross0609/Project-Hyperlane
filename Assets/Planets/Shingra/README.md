# Shingra

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Shingra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shingra)

Shingra was the only inhabitable planet in the Ordali system. It is covered in snowy mountains, with an average temperature of -15°C. Rarely, gems renowned for their size and purity can be found by mining the rock here. The primary spaceport is a small town of Kommba, mainly constructed of local conifer wood. Atmospheric flying is discouraged due to constant turbulence, and repulsorlifts are unreliable due to magnetic materials in the ground.
