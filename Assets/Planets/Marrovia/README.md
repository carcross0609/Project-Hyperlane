# Marrovia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Marrovia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marrovia)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Marrovia`, Outer Rim, grid P-5 — map position (6953.0, 6360.3, -22.8)

Marrovia was an agriworld located in the Marrovia system, a part of the Gordian Reach sector in the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The Pinooran Spur hyperlane connected the world to the Kli'aar and Ladarra systems.

Marrovia was known for its type of hardy, fatty pigs. The animals were fed in giant farms and then processed in rendering stations, of which there were many on the world's surface. Beings on Marrovia experienced a stench that was hard to believe.
