# Firrhana

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Firrhana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firrhana)

Firrhana was a planet located in the Firrhana system in the Ojoster sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was once controlled by the Sith, who conducted sacrifice rituals in a temple they built there. It was abandoned after an ecological collapse caused by a volcanic eruption.
