# Praya

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Praya](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Praya)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Praya`, Outer Rim, grid K-4 — map position (-167.9, 8116.7, -407.6)

Praya was an ocean planet located in the Praya system, within the Clacis sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. By the year 4.3 ABY, it was home to the luxurious Sea Castle Resort Hotel. The Star Tours travel agency facilitated transit to Praya through their Deluxe Undersea Package.
