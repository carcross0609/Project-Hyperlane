# B'nish

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B'nish](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B%27nish)

B'nish was a planet in the galaxy. The Human girl First was held there as a slave until her escape around 41.5 ABY. The B'nishi were native to the world.
