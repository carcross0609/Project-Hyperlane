# Stindaron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stindaron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stindaron)

Stindaron was a gas giant in the Dathomir system, located in the Quelii sector in the New Territories portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was orbited by twenty moons and occupied the seventh orbital position around the star Domir.
