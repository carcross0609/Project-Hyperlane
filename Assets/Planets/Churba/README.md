# Churba

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Churba](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Churba)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Churba`, Mid Rim, grid P-14 — map position (6904.5, -6372.9, -7.0)

Churba was the cosmopolitan sector capital of the Churba sector, known for its cutting-edge technology. It was headquarters of Sencil Corporation.

Within the first 20 years of the Galactic Empire, an old dreadnaught was left in the planet's orbit serving as a museum until, sometime prior to 1 BBY, the Rebel Alliance sent deep-space tugs that stole the ship from orbit and transferred to a secret location; it was repurposed and renamed the New Hope as a move of psychological warfare.

The Imperial Intelligence agent Kirtan Loor and Rogue Squadron pilot Dar Keyis were from the planet.

Tetengo Noor, a Human male in the New Republic military, was also from Churba.
