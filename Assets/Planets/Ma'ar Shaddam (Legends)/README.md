# Ma'ar Shaddam

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ma'ar Shaddam/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ma%27ar_Shaddam/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ma'ar Shaddam`, Outer Rim, grid N-20 — map position (4110.6, -15287.6, 365.9)

Ma'ar Shaddam was a remote Outer Rim planet, home to the armorers who served Boba Fett. The planet was settled by Galactic Republic Master Weaponsmiths not only for the quality of its ore, but because its remoteness made it difficult for the authorities to police the sale of illegal weaponry.

Firmus Kett's family had investments on Ma'ar Shaddam.
