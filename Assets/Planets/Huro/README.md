# Huro

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Huro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huro)

Huro was a planet in the Huro system within the Ciutric sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Trever Flume stopped at an exclusive florist on Coruscant who sold exotic blooms and bought a small plant with vibrant yellow leaves native to the planet Huro. It was the cheapest option they had.
