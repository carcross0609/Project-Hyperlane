# Yn

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yn)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Yn`, Inner Rim, grid K-14 — map position (-584.1, -6316.9, -303.3)

Yn was a planet located within the Yn system of the Inner Rim. Around 5300 BBY, the Jedi laid siege to an Imperial stronghold at the battle of Yn. Tulak Hord, Ortan Cela, Khem Val, and Veshikk Urk broke the siege with their army of dark side warriors. Hord and Khem Val fed on the energies of the fallen Force-users using an ancient Sith ritual. Millennia later, Yn was represented in the New Republic Senate by Wwebyls, a tiny humanoid.
