# Thracior

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thracior](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thracior)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Thracior`, Core Worlds, grid K-12 — map position (-179.6, -4139.7, 176.9)

Thracior was a Core Worlds planet that was home to a clan-based culture. In 32 BBY, the planet's warlords began arguing over territory, leading to raids between the local tribes. As a result, the parents of the Human siblings Dane and Floria, who were married despite being from different clans, were killed. Dane and Floria escaped death and subsequently moved to a space station. 27 BBY, Floria told Jedi Padawan Anakin Skywalker about her and Dane's past at Thracior.
