# Cimarosa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cimarosa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cimarosa)

Cimarosa was the homeworld of the Human Von Longo and his two sons, Kale Longo and Trig Longo. They lived on the world until sometime in 1 BBY, when they were arrested by the Galactic Empire, and placed on the prison barge Purge.
