# Dles IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dles IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dles_IV)

Dles IV was a Rebel Alliance penal world in the Dles system of the Periphery. Although the colony was under direct jurisdiction of Alliance High Command, the nearby Sulorine Sector HQ contributed supplies and manpower when needed.
