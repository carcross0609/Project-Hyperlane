# Pesfavri

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pesfavri/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pesfavri/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Pesfavri`, Unknown Regions, grid G-8 — map position (-6350.9, 2831.2, -73.2)

Pesfavri was an Unknown Regions planet located in Chiss Space. A colony of the Chiss species, it housed a base of the Defense Fleet of the Chiss Ascendancy. As Defense Fleet Admiral Ar'alani was escorting the detained Chiss Force Commander Mitth'raw'nuruodo off the Galactic Republic exploration and colonization vessel Outbound Flight after it had been crippled by Mitth'raw'nuruodo's forces in 27 BBY, the trader Jorj Car'das and Syndic Mitth'ras'safis of the Chiss Eighth Ruling Family informed the admiral of their intent to remain aboard the ship.

Shortly before that, the group had formulated a plan to take the vessel to the Defense Fleet so as to deny the Fifth Ruling Family the possibility to acquire the ship and the technology aboard it. Ar'alani, believing that Mitth'ras'safis intended to go through with the plan, noted that the installation at Pesfavri was the closest to their location. In reality, however, the Syndic planned to scuttle the Outbound Flight in a nearby star, although the vessel was ultimately flown into the Redoubt star cluster instead.
