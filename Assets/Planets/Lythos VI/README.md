# Lythos VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lythos VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lythos_VI)

Lythos VI was the sixth planet of the Lythos system, located in the Corporate Sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet was one of four major agriworlds in the Lythos system, the others being Lythos VII, VIII, and IX. Like the rest of the system, Lythos VI was located on the confluence of the Hydian Way and Trianii Shunt trade routes.
