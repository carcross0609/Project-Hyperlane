# Kol Iben

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kol Iben](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kol_Iben)

Kol Iben was a gas giant located in a star system in the Outer Rim Territories' Arkanis sector. It had at least one moon, the estuary moon of Trask. The planet was situated in the Outer Rim Territories.
