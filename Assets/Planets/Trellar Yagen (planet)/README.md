# Trellar Yagen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trellar Yagen (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trellar_Yagen_%28planet%29)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Trellar Yagen`, Expansion Region, grid O-13 — map position (5698.6, -5123.3, 193.4)

Trellar Yagen was a planet in the Bovo Yagen system. It was covered in jungles and had a hot climate. It hosted a wide range of flora and fauna, much of it carnivourous. The most famous predator on the planet was the trell.

Due to the Bovorian's sensitive hearing, heavy industry was banned on their home planet. This lead to Trellar Yagen being the base of operations for the mining and processing activity in the system.
