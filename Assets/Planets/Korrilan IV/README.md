# Korrilan IV

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Korrilan IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korrilan_IV)

Korrilan IV was a planet located in the Mid Rim Territories. The Zalpertine Cup was a Podracing competition held on the world, catering to podracers piloting CR-17 Incendias.
