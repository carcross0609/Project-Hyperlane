# Sarahwiee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sarahwiee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sarahwiee)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Sarahwiee`, Mid Rim, grid P-7 — map position (6471.7, 3810.2, -182.1)

Sarahwiee was the third planet orbiting the white dwarf Bseto in the Mid Rim. A frozen planet, with a local day of 28 standard hours long, and a year of 389 local days. The planet was discovered shortly after the declaration of the New Order. Emperor Palpatine kept its location a secret.

The planet was the site of a research base. After the Battle of Bilbringi in 9 ABY, Luke Skywalker and Alexandra Winger led agents of the New Republic to the base on an infiltration mission. The mission, part of a contracted supply run, allowed the New Republic operatives to free captive scientists, including Carl Barzon.
