# Tebru

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tebru](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tebru)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tebru`, Outer Rim, grid P-17 — map position (6595.7, -11207.2, 128.9)

Tebru was a rocky planet that was located within the Tebru system, a part of the Outer Rim Territories' Juris sector. By the year 3 ABY, the droid inventor and historian Toria Tell maintained a large residence with a workshop and a massive library on the planet. Tell kept her extensive droid collection, which contained many of the galaxy's oldest and rarest robots, at her home. When not traveling off-world aboard her stock light freighter in order to expand her droid collection, the inventor quietly tinkered at her Tebru home.
