# Magravia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Magravia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Magravia)

Magravia was a world located in the Magravia system, a part of the Karthakk sector in the Trailing Sectors portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was situated on the hyperlane known as the Llanic Spice Run, which connected it to the Llanic system as well as the Socorro system in the Kibilini sector.

Magravia was a notable spice-production site, being the origin point of the spice variant known as the Magravian cat-spice. As a result, the world was well-known to smugglers.
