# Vetna Mooncrest's homeworld

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vetna Mooncrest's homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vetna_Mooncrest%27s_homeworld)

A terrestrial world was the homeworld of the Force-sensitive human Vetna Mooncrest. According to Mooncrest, it was said on her world that peace did not exist without war. At some point by the time between 40 BBY and 34 BBY, Mooncrest had been taken from her homeworld to be trained as a Jedi in the Jedi Order, eventually becoming a Jedi Knight.

At some point between those two years, Mooncrest and several other members of the Order took part in a defense of the Outer Rim world Syrinx Prime, which had been invaded by its neighboring world Vekura. After Jedi Master Mace Windu joked with Grand Master Yoda about how he thought peace talks between the two worlds were progressing, Mooncrest noted what was said on her world about peace not existing without war. In response, Jedi Master Ki-Adi-Mundi characterized it as a "dark" perspective for viewing the galaxy.
