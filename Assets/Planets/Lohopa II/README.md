# Lohopa II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lohopa II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lohopa_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lohopa II`, Expansion Region, grid M-15 — map position (2362.7, -8437.8, -49.6)

Lohopa II was a mining colony in the Lohopa system of the Expansion Region, on the Harrin Trade Corridor. The local mining operations managed to resist Imperial takeover attempts. The planet developed a growing travel business due to its location on the Corridor. The local day was 25 standard hours long; its year was 414 local days long.
