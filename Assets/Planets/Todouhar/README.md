# Todouhar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Todouhar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Todouhar)

Todouhar was a small Outer Rim world located in the Seswenna sector's Todouhar system. At some point between 235 BBY and 230 BBY, the pilots Affie Hollow and Leox Gyasi visited the world's spaceport. Gyasi's nemesis, the pilot Koley Linn, encountered them and bought out the spaceport's fuel stores, stranding Gyasi and Hollow on Todouhar for three days.
