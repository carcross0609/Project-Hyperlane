# Allard

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Allard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Allard)

Allard was a world located in the Occlusion Zone in the Outer Rim Territories. Avar Kriss visited a town on Allard shortly after the fall of Starlight Beacon, as the Nihil explained to the planet's citizens the nature of their rule, and the Stormwall.
