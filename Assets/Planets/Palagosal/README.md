# Palagosal

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Palagosal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palagosal)

Palagosal was a planet located in the Arkanis sector. After signs of the blight were found on the planet, the Galactic Republic ordered an evacuation of any individuals on the planet. After the message was sent out to the galaxy, a mission to the planet occurred after a distress signal was sent out from it. The team managed to successfully find the individual who sent out the call and five others who were trapped under Marl's Good Enough Eats.
