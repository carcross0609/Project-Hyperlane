# Oor VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oor VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oor_VII)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Oor VII`, Outer Rim — map position (-1451.5, 7158.0, -17.6)

Oor VII was a planet located within the Cronese Mandate, on the Outer Rim of the galaxy. In ancient times, Oor VII was a humming factory world, but millennia of environmental degradation had left it parched and thinly populated. The planet was also known for its annual spelling bees.
