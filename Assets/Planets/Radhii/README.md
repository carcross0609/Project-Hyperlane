# Radhii

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Radhii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Radhii)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Radhii`, Other, grid P-15 — map position (6532.5, -8321.1, 12.9)

Radhii was a planet located in the Radhii system of Wild Space that was part of the Galactic Empire by the time of the Battle of Endor. In Tavuu the capital of the planet, the Segenka prison was located there. Also Tavuu was located on a large plateau, in the western part, which cut off into the jungle of Zoess. The deschene and ghoma were a clawed creatures native to the Zoess.
