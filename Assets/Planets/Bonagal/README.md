# Bonagal

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bonagal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bonagal)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bonagal`, Outer Rim, grid O-7 — map position (4765.9, 3298.9, -159.0)

Bonagal was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories' Mandalore sector. Located within the Mandalore system, Bonagal occupied the seventh orbit around the system's star, Mandalore, between the orbits of Mandallia and Shukut. A gas giant, Bonagal was surrounded by thirty-four moons.

After the Taung forces of Mandalore the First journeyed from Roon to conquer the uninhabited planet that became Mandalore, the newly renamed Mandalorians spread out to conquer the worlds of the surrounding system, including Bonagal.
