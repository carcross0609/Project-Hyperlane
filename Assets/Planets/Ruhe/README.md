# Ruhe

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ruhe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruhe)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ruhe`, Outer Rim, grid Q-17 — map position (8072.1, -11385.3, -346.2)

Ruhe was a planet that was allied with the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. Dooku maintained a citadel on Ruhe, situated on the edge of a massive volcanic or meteoric crater. Half a month after the Battle of Boz Pity, a strike team including Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Jenks was shot down on Ruhe while trying to capture Dooku; it was later revealed that it was a trap set by Dooku in the hopes of capturing Skywalker and Kenobi.
