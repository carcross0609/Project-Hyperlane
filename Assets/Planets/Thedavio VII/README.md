# Thedavio VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thedavio VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thedavio_VII)

Thedavio VII was a world located in the Expansion Region. Prior to the Battle of Hoth, a fleet of unidentified black starships was spotted near the planet. It was unknown whether they were some kind of new Imperial design.
