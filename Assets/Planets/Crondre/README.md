# Crondre

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Crondre](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crondre)

Crondre was an astronomical object that was part of the Dufilvian sector's Crondre system, which fell within square R-15 of the Standard Galactic Grid as part of the Mid Rim Territories and the Slice. It was the homeworld of the human stormtrooper designated TK-7919, who was born around 22 BBY. TK-7919 served the Galactic Empire and then went on to also serve under the Imperial Remnant, eventually dying on the planet Nevarro in 9 ABY. During that year, Crondre fell within the borders of the New Republic.
