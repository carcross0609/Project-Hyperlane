# Retta

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Retta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Retta)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Retta`, Wild Space, grid Q-11 — map position (8412.2, -2765.7, 84.7)

Retta was a jungle planet located in the Retta system in Wild Space. During the Clone Wars, Kreeda hid a group of individuals who wished to escape the war on Retta, using her ability to become invisible. The Confederacy of Independent Systems general Flebek was ordered to find the secret to the invisibility, but were tricked into leaving by Jedi Generals Anakin Skywalker and Yoda.
