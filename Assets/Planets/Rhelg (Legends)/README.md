# Rhelg

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rhelg/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rhelg/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rhelg`, Outer Rim, grid R-5 — map position (9828.2, 7216.6, 219.3)

Rhelg was one of the only agricultural worlds in the Sith Worlds region of the Outer Rim Territories' Esstran sector. Falling between Khar Delba and the Sith homeworld of Korriban on the looping Nache Bhelfia hyperlane, Rhelg was considered one of the five sacred Sith planets, along with Ziost, Khar Delba, Krayiss II, and Korriban. A primitive world covered in vast expanses of arable land, Rhelg was the private world of the Sith Lord Ludo Kressh as well as the site of his palace during the Great Hyperspace War.
