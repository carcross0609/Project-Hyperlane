# Imroosia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imroosia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imroosia)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Imroosia`, Other, grid L-9 — map position (1331.9, 702.1, -0.0)

Imroosia was a volcanic planet located in the Imroosia system of the Inner Rim Territories in grid square N-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid. It was the homeworld of the sentient Imroosian species, which had evolved heat-resistant skin in order to cope with the planet's searing hot temperatures and members of which spoke the Imroosian language.

The Imroosian Margo detested the hot environment of her homeworld. After becoming the concierge of Dryden Vos, the public face of the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate, by 10 BBY, she enjoyed the cool interior of Vos's yacht, the First Light, having no intention of returning to Imroosia. Agira, the Imroosian handmaiden of the House of Organa, also hailed from Imroosia.
