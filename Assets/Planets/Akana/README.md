# Akana

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Akana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Akana)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Akana`, Outer Rim, grid M-20 — map position (2077.4, -15101.1, -141.3)

Akana was the dry and barren fourth world within the Elrood system, located in the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy in the Elrood sector. The surface itself only boasted life in the microbial range, although the Professiona Artists Guild maintained an artistic colony in a small, sealed outpost there. Its only satellite, Akanala was a source of inspiration for those artists.
