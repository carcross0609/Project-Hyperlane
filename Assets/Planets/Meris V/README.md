# Meris V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Meris V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meris_V)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Meris V`, Outer Rim — map position (10479.0, 8839.9, 267.5)

Meris V was a chilly, barren wasteland in the Meris system of the Elrood sector. The landscape of Meris V was mostly composed of rocky wastes, and was orbited by a single moon. Meris V remained unexplored, and there remained many undiscovered ore deposits on the rocky world.
