# Dezeke

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Dezeke](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dezeke)

Dezeke was a terrestrial planet located in the galaxy. During the reign of the Empire, three stormtroopers stole two chairs from the royal palace on Dezeke for Maul, believing him to be Darth Vader.
