# Fitca Prime

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fitca Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fitca_Prime)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Fitca Prime`, Mid Rim, grid L-6 — map position (1302.6, 5472.1, 38.9)

Fitca Prime was a terrestrial planet that was the primary world of the Fitca system, a part of Urce Space in the Lesser Plooriod Cluster, which was in turn situated in the New Territories portion of the Mid Rim. At some point by 2 ABY, a Galactic Empire force led by High Inquisitor Mox Slosin subjugated the Fitca system and Fitca Prime. During the first Rebel Alliance support wave during that conflict, Alliance Colonel Shanda Dok was captured, and she was subsequently interrogated by Slosin. Between 12 ABY and 13 ABY, Fitca Prime fell within the borders of the New Republic.
