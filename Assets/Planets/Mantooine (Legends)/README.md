# Mantooine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mantooine/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mantooine/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mantooine`, Outer Rim, grid L-5 — map position (837.1, 7090.0, -73.4)

Mantooine was a planet in the Atrivis sector, on the Relgim Run. The people of Mantooine distrusted outsiders and government, particularly the totalitarian Galactic Empire. After the Mantooine Liberators were decimated by Imperial forces, the Fest Resistance Group united with the Liberators to form the Atrivis Resistance Group, which allied with the pan-galactic Rebel Alliance and became its Atrivis Sector Force.
