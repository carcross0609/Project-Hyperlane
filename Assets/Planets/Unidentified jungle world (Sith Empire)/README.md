# Unidentified jungle world

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified jungle world (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_jungle_world_%28Sith_Empire%29)

A jungle world had rainforests on its surface, featuring gnarled trees and long grass. The world was home to a reptavian species. During the reign of the Sith Empire, the Sith Lords of the Empire transported Sith war behemoths—a species of large, quadrupedal herbivores native to the Sith Worlds planet Khar Delba—to several harsh jungle worlds, including the world with the gnarled trees, where the creatures were subjected to Sith alchemy and centuries-long breeding programs in order to turn them into violent war beasts for the Sith armies.

At the time of the Dark Lord of the Sith Naga Sadow's preparations to attack the Galactic Republic in 5000 BBY, his Sith soldiers maneuvered several of the war behemoths of the jungle world into the troop containers of a Sith personnel carrier waiting to take them to the Sith invasion fleet.
