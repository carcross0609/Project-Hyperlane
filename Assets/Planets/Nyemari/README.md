# Nyemari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nyemari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyemari)

Nyemari was a planet. By 1 ABY, the planet's capital city Anem had the most modern, sophisticated spaceport in the Meridian sector. The planet was also host to Galactic Empire military installation. The indigenous species was the Nyemarians. It was visited by a team of Rebel Alliance operatives to steal the Duchess' access codes to the installation. After the mission, the Rebel's encounter with a mysterious messenger led them to Rezi Soresh in the Sixela system.
