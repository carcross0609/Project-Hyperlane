# Bresallis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bresallis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bresallis)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bresallis`, Outer Rim, grid T-8 — map position (12725.0, 1946.5, -114.4)

Bresallis was a planet located within the Bresallis system, which was located in the Cadma sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was plagued by a criminal underworld run by the Hutts. Ambassador Walads opposed the Hutts, trying to disrupt their businesses. He was about to make a speech to the government on the subject when the Hutts hired Brahle Logris to assassinate him.
