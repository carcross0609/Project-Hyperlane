# Noctralis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Noctralis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Noctralis)

Noctralis was a planet where Trachta was grievously injured during the Clone Wars. He and a group of clone troopers were hunting a Jedi Padawan who defected to the Confederacy of Independent Systems when the Padawan threw a thermal detonator, killing the troopers and injuring Trachta. The planet was dark and had spindly-looking dead trees.
