# Skorii-Lei

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Skorii-Lei](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skorii-Lei)

Skorii-Lei was an aquatic planet within the galaxy that Princess Leia Organa traveled to sometime after the Battle of Yavin. Sometime before her arrival on the planet, the Rebellion destroyed the primary industry of the world, causing Pash Davane to go from being an engineer to lifting crates.
