# Tchuukthai homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tchuukthai homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tchuukthai_homeworld)

The Tchuukthai homeworld was a planet in the Unknown Regions. It was home to the sentient Tchuukthai species, which, although discovered multiple times, was generally assumed to be primitive and dangerous. The Tchuukthai carefully guarded the identity and location of their homeworld. Eventually, the Tchuukthai Thon departed the planet after meeting a Jedi Master, ultimately becoming a Jedi himself.
