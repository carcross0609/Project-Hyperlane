# Mirak Major

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mirak Major](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mirak_Major)

Mirak Major was a planet. During the Clone Wars, the Confederacy of Independent Systems triggered this planet's mega-volcanos. The planet's population of at least six billion, however, was safely evacuated by General Gentis.
