# Ebruchi homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ebruchi homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ebruchi_homeworld)

The Ebruchi homeworld was a terrestrial planet that was the homeworld of the sentient Ebruchi species. The Vagaari species, members of which were slavers that operated in the Unknown Regions, were believed to have devastated the Ebruchi homeworld, whose remaining inhabitants fled the planet, only to ironically become pirates over time. As the Ebruchi did not admit failure, they erased their homeworld's devastation from their common memory.
