# Avon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Avon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Avon`, Mid Rim, grid Q-16 — map position (8147.2, -10011.6, 58.5)

Avon was a planet in the same system as Radnor. Its inhabitants were the Avoni. Avon was, for the most part, distrusted by the Radnorans. In the years following the Battle of Naboo, Avon sent a fleet of warships to invade and occupy Radnor, in revenge for the Radnorans not selling them a deadly new bioweapon that they developed. However, with intervention from the Jedi, their attempts were short-lived.
