# Aubadas

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aubadas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aubadas)

Aubadas was a planet in the Aubadas system, in the Tunka sector of the Outer Rim Territories, and the twin planet of Gloam. It was originally a desolate, desert planet with limited resources, however overtime was transformed into a lush marshy planet with many valleys, gullies, and mountains. In 382 BBY, Jedi Master Silandra Sho and her Republic Pathfinder team visited the planet in search of pathfinder team Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two and made contact with the Katikoot species and entered their capital city of Diurna
