# Uncharted planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uncharted planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uncharted_planet)

This uncharted planet was located in Wild Space and was home to a small colony of Barundi. When a ship belonging to Illyan Webble, the president of Seraphan Industries, crashed on the planet, Webble discovered the fabled Yavin Vassilika in the Barundi village. Several treasure hunters later visited the planet in an effort to retrieve the Vassilika.
