# Baffop

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Baffop](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baffop)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Baffop`, Outer Rim, grid O-19 — map position (5119.6, -13885.9, -100.2)

Baffop was a planet located in the Baffop system, a part of the Tamarin sector in the Outer Rim Territories. In 32 BBY, the Human male Anselm was born at Baffop. At some point before the Battle of Yavin, the forces of the Galactic Empire ravaged Baffop, leading to extensive civilian casualties. After witnessing the devastation of his homeworld, Anselm became a member of the Rebel Alliance, which opposed the Empire. By the time of Imperial Grand Admiral Thrawn's campaign against the New Republic in 9 ABY, Baffop was situated in space either conquered by or allied with Thrawn's confederation.
