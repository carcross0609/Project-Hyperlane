# Dendro IV

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Dendro IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dendro_IV)

Dendro IV was a planet that appeared green from space. In 34 ABY, the First Order attacked the planet, causing a region of its surface to become devastated and burned.
