# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Kenobi)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Kenobi%29)

This unidentified planet was a planet in the galaxy. During the Clone Wars, Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi went on a mission to this planet, thinking he would find a Separatist traitor with information to sell. The mission was actually a trap set up by six bounty hunters who attempted to assassinate Kenobi, as a means of gaining admission to the Bounty Hunters' Guild.
