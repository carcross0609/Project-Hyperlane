# Boothi XII

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Boothi XII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boothi_XII)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Boothi XII`, Other, grid N-7 — map position (3367.2, 3737.9, 46.4)

Boothi XII was a planet located in the galaxy. During her time at the University of Bar'leth, Chelli Lona Aphra explored a subterranean vault on Boothi XII, hoping to discover artifacts that would further her career as an archaeologist. However, when exploring the vault, she found nothing of value.
