# Ban-Satir II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ban-Satir II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ban-Satir_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ban-Satir II`, Outer Rim, grid S-4 — map position (11271.4, 8842.9, 558.2)

Ban-Satir II was a planet located within the Ban-Satir system in the Corporate Sector of the Outer Rim region of the galaxy. Through Ban-Satir II ran the Tingel Route hyperspace lane, which connected the planet with the worlds of Issagra and Gaurick.

A lush but dull agriworld, Ban-Satir II is famous for its Novanian grog, an expensive and potent intoxicant. It is produced and marketed from the isle of N'van in the planet's northern hemisphere. The best Norvanian vintages are reserved for the Authority's elite, making Norvanian Reserve a popular cargo for smugglers. At the same time, the distributors on Ban-Satir II sometimes encountered problems that hindered trade.

Female Human Voralla Morbo grew up on the corporate retreat of the Isle of N'Van. Spending years in the service industry, working around executives from across the galaxy, she developed a head for business and a keen ear.
