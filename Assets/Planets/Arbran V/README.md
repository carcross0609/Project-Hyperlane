# Arbran V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arbran V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arbran_V)

Arbran V was the fifth planet in orbit around the star Arbran. It was the second of two gas giants in the Arbran system, located in the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector, a part of the Trailing Sectors portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet was orbited by eleven moons.
