# Aplooine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aplooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aplooine)

Aplooine was an ash-covered world in the Outer Rim Territories' Grumani sector. Its surface only featured a limited number of stable landing points on mountaintops; nevertheless, the world served as one of the origin points of the spread of exploration of surrounding space during the Kymoodon Era. During the millennia following that time, Aplooine continued to harbor colonists.
