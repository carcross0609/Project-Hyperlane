# Harix

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harix)

Harix was a planet in the Colonies that was a strong center of Rebel activity during the Galactic Civil War. In 1 ABY, Imperial Major Stafuv Rahz captured a Harix schoolteacher and a number of children to serve as bait for Rebel leaders. However, Rebels Luke Skywalker and Han Solo led a rescue mission that saved them before fleeing the planet.
