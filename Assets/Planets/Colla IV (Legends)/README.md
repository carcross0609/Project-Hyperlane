# Colla IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Colla IV/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Colla_IV/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Colla IV`, Inner Rim, grid N-9 — map position (3635.9, 1395.8, 69.2)

Colla IV, or simply Colla, was the home planet of the insectoid Colicoid species, makers of the droideka, Annihilator droid, buzz droid, and the droid tri-fighter. Its starship engineers were known for ingenuity rather than style.
