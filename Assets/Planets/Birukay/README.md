# Birukay

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Birukay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Birukay)

Birukay was a barely-populated planet located at the farthest edge of the galaxy in the Outer Rim Territories. Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra would stash the assassin droids BT-1 and 0-0-0 on Birukay, where they remained until they were discovered by Sana Starros, Aphra's companion and ex-lover, and her crew.
