# Tellion

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tellion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tellion)

Tellion was a densely populated planet in the galaxy that was a well-known manufacturing and recycling world. It was part of the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era, and was afflicted by the Blight in the final weeks of the Nihil conflict.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Tellion
- **Population:** Densely populated
- **Exports:** Manufacturing and recycling
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
