# Melnea's World

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Melnea's World/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Melnea%27s_World/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Melnea's World`, Outer Rim, grid R-16 — map position (10078.3, -10235.8, -612.0)

Melnea's World was a murky, swampy planet located in the Melnea's World system, a part of the Arkanis sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Melnea's World was located on the Cranan-Excarga Route.

Melnea's World was situated on the Hyperlane known as the Cranan-Excarga Route. It was settled by miners from Tatooine after local mining operations failed and the arrival of the Hutts, subsequent to which the world was transformed into a smugglers' hideout. The smuggler traffic encountered by Vor Deo came from the worlds beyond Cirus and Melnea's World.
