# Reerez

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Reerez](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reerez)

Reerez was one of four planets located in the Mygeeto system of the Albarrio sector, which was located in both the Outer Rim and New Territories regions. It was an airless rock that had moons and was in closer orbit of the star Malola, and lay at K-5 on the Standard Galactic Grid.
