# Hol Kinto

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hol Kinto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hol_Kinto)

Hol Kinto was the fifth and outermost planet of the extra-galactic Nagi system. It was located in a dense star cluster within the satellite galaxy Companion Besh that the Galactic Empire classified as part of the Unknown Regions. It was a gas giant that had five moons and orbited the star Nagi Prime.
