# Banas

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Banas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Banas)

Banas was a rugged planet with a cold environment that was located in the Outer Rim Territories and on the frontier of the Galactic Empire's territory during the Galactic Civil War. Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa traveled to Banas to investigate if the planet was suitable for a new base for the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Banas was also the homeworld of a sentient species.
