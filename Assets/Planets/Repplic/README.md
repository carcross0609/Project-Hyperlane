# Repplic

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Repplic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Repplic)

Repplic was a gas giant that orbited the star Csilla in the Csilla system. Repplic occupied the third orbital position and had twenty-two moons. The gas giant was located in Chiss Space, a sector of the Unknown Regions controlled by the Chiss Ascendancy.

Along with the rest of the Csilla system, Repplic was linked by the hyperlanes known as the Path of the Houses to the Cioral and Sposia systems. The Cressus Route and Vagaari Corridor hyperspace routes connected the system to the Avidich and Cormit systems, respectively, while other hyperlanes linked it to the Jamiron, Rentor, and Rhigar systems.
