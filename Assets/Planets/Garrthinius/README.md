# Garrthinius

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Garrthinius](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Garrthinius)

Garrthinius was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories' Steniplis sector that was under the control of the Galactic Empire by the time of the Battle of Endor. Although the population was largely sympathetic to anti-Imperial activity, the insurgents' efforts to resist Imperial occupation were disorganized. Several Rebel Alliance agents traveled to the planet, where they eventually turned the various revolutionary groups into a force capable of overthrowing the Imperial government.
