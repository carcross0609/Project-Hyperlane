# Kol Huro I

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kol Huro I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kol_Huro_I)

Kol Huro I was the innermost planet of the Kol Huro system. Like other planets in the system, Kol Huro I was harvested for its natural resources during the years leading up to the Battle of Naboo, as the tyrant who controlled the system began mass-producing weapons and battle droids for his own protection.
