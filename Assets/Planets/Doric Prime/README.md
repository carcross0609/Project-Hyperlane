# Doric Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Doric Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doric_Prime)

Doric Prime was a planet that existed in the the galaxy. It was a mostly oceanic world, with few islands that harbored forests. A type of settlement was located near one of the islands and was supplied by a nearby hydro power plant. At some point during the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Separatists, the elite Republic clone commando squad Clone Force 99 the "Bad Batch", aswell as the Kyuzo bounty hunter Embo, traveled to the planet in pursuit of the Separatist Dark Acolyte Asajj Ventress who met at the power plant with the bounty hunter Aurra Sing to hand over a list of undercover Republic Ghost Agents she had stolen from the Republic capital Coruscant.
