# Orellon II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orellon II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orellon_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Orellon II`, Mid Rim — map position (1420.9, 4808.5, 261.1)

Orellon II was a planet in the Mid Rim, covered in jungles, plains and volcanic mountains. Thirty-five point nine percent of the planet's surface was covered with water.

Jedi Knight Michael Tandre accidentally discovered the planet, when he crashed on Orellon II over two hundred years before the Galactic Civil War. The native species, known as the Kentra, would go on to create an entire religion based on the Jedi's teachings.
