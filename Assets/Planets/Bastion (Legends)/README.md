# Bastion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bastion/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bastion/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bastion`, Outer Rim, grid K-3 — map position (-1339.3, 9699.8, -169.1)

Bastion, originally Sartinaynian, was an industrialized planet located in the Sartinaynian system in the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy, and was the capital world of the Imperial Remnant, a statelet established from the former Galactic Empire. Bastion was located in the Braxant sector at the terminus of the trade route known as the Braxant Run.

Originally, "Bastion" was just a term for the worlds on which the Council of Moffs were temporarily located. Its last location remained a closely guarded secret by the time of the Caamas Document Crisis, where the Imperial Remnant was located on Sartinaynian shortly before peace with the New Republic in 19 ABY.

During the Yuuzhan Vong invasion, Bastion was attacked, resulting in the loss of the world to the Yuuzhan Yong, who took control and almost destroyed Bastion as they took a hold of the Outer Rim.

It was later rebuilt and became the capital world of the Fel Empire, a successor state, and later the headquarters of Roan Fel's Empire-in-exile.
