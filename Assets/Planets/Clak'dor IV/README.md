# Clak'dor IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Clak'dor IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clak%27dor_IV)

Clak'dor IV was a Bith colony world. Most of the Y'bith left Clak'dor VII and resettled here, in the city of New Nozho.
