# Gal Milnor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gal Milnor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gal_Milnor)

Gal Milnor was a planet that housed the main supply base used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic to store food, munitions and fuel. The base, along with a sizeable fleet of Alliance freighters, was destroyed by the Galactic Empire shortly before the Battle of Endor. This setback forced the Alliance to seek supplies from Dunari and the Hurrim.
