# Latt

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Latt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Latt)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Latt`, Expansion Region — map position (5727.4, -2835.2, 42.0)

Latt was the third planet in the Demar system. By the Battle of Yavin, it had been reduced to a nearly barren rock by the Lant Mining Corporation. A few workers had remained on the planet to seal the deep mine shafts and salvage the leftover mining equipment and buildings. Latt had one moon.
