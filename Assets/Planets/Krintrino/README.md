# Krintrino

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Krintrino](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krintrino)

Krintrino was a planet. During the early stages of the Galactic Civil War, Imperial forces were responsible for the destruction of an Rebel Alliance resistance cell that was based there, about five years prior to the Battle of Yavin.
