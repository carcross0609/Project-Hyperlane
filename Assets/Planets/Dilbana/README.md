# Dilbana

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dilbana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dilbana)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dilbana`, Outer Rim, grid U-12 — map position (13680.3, -3981.1, 31.7)

Dilbana was a planet in the Baxel sector of the Outer Rim. Located along the Triellus Trade Route, the planet was under a de facto rulership overseen by Jiliac Desilijic Tiron. This form of rule ended with the Hutt Lord's death at the hands of Durga Besadii Tai.
