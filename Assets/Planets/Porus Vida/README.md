# Porus Vida

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Porus Vida](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Porus_Vida)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Porus Vida`, Inner Rim, grid O-9 — map position (5782.3, 557.1, -304.5)

Porus Vida was a planet located in the Inner Rim and famous for its beauty and its culture. Despite its reputation as a peaceful world of museums, Porus Vida saw conflict throughout its history, including several sieges during the Alsakan Conflicts, and was devastated by the United Warlord Fleets in 12 ABY.
