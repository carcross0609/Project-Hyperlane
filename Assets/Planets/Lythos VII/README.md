# Lythos VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lythos VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lythos_VII)

Lythos VII was an agriworld located in the Lythos system of the Outer Rim Territories' Corporate sector. The seventh planet in the system, Lythos VII was one of four agricultural worlds in the system, the others being Lythos VI, Lythos VIII, and Lythos IX. The Lythos system, and therefore Lythos VII, was located at the crossroads of the Hydian Way and Trianii Shunt hyperlanes.
