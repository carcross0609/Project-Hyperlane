# Gulhadar III

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gulhadar III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gulhadar_III)

Gulhadar III, also known simply as Gulhadar, was a prison planet in the Gulhadar system of the Inner Rim Territories that was under the control of the Benelex Marshal Service. Between around 8 BBY and 2 BBY, the Wookiee Chewbacca was imprisoned on Gulhadar alongside the pirate Maz Kanata, Doctor Cornelius Evazan, and Ponda Baba.
