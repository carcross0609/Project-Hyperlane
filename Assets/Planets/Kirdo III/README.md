# Kirdo III

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kirdo III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kirdo_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kirdo III`, Outer Rim, grid N-19 — map position (3988.4, -14104.0, -156.3)

Kirdo III, also referred to simply as Kirdo, was a planet located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories, that was the homeworld of the Kitonak species On the way back from Rodia, Luke Skywalker forged a new hyperspace lane between Kirdo and Orto Plutonia.

Chindinkalu plants were native to Kirdo III. The Kitonak musician Droopy McCool used a hollow chindinkalu plant to create his Chindinkalu flute which he played at Jabba's Palace in 4 ABY during the Rescue of Han Solo.
