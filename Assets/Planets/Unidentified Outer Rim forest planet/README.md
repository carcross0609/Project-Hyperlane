# Unidentified Outer Rim forest planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Outer Rim forest planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Outer_Rim_forest_planet)

A forested planet was located in the Outer Rim Territories. The Human male clone Irol hailed from this planet. He learned how to fly a speeder bike on his homeworld. During the Galactic Civil War, a conflict between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic, Irol became an Imperial scout trooper sergeant by 4 ABY.
