# Aris V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aris V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aris_V)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Aris V`, Outer Rim, grid O-18 — map position (5242.6, -12808.0, 346.9)

Aris V was a planet in Parmel sector chosen as a possible base of operations for the Laramus Base Irregulars after they left Laramus Base.

The Rebel Alliance struck an Imperial military target on Aris V before the Battle of Turak IV, leading to an increase in operations in the Parmel sector.
