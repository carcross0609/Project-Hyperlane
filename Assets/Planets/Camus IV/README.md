# Camus IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Camus IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Camus_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Camus IV`, Other, grid Q-4 — map position (8752.1, 8795.1, -84.4)

Camus IV was the fourth planet in the Camus system of the Illisurevimurasi sector, located within the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Sometime prior to the Battle of Hoth in 3 ABY, the smugglers Han Solo and Chewbacca once vacationed on Camus IV, but Solo became seasick while floating among the planet's ocean waves.
