# Kuthic Worlds

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kuthic Worlds/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuthic_Worlds/Legends)

The Kuthic Worlds were a part of the Tharin sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The hyperlane known as the Talcene Transit connected the group of worlds to the larger Perlemian Trade Route hyperlane as well as the Saleucami system of the Suolriep sector. Starships traveled along the Talcene Transit in order to deliver cargo from the Perlemian to the Kuthic Worlds.
