# Endymion

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Endymion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endymion)

Endymion was a planet in the Pleiades region of the Kalamith sector in the Outer Rim Territories. Around 3660 BBY it was involved in the Great Galactic War. According to Darth Malgus in his personal journal, Sith troops were moved from Endymion, but its exact role in the war is unknown, as this part of the journal was cut out.
