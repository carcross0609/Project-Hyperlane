# Unidentified Nikto colony world

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Nikto colony world](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Nikto_colony_world)

A colony world of the Nikto species was ruled by a grand chieftain during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire, and the chieftain allowed his people to freely prey on passing starships until intimidated by offworlders.
