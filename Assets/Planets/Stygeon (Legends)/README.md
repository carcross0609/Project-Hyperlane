# Stygeon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stygeon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stygeon/Legends)

Stygeon was a snowy, mountainous planet that was home to a secret Separatist prison. Dark Lord of the Sith Sidious imprisoned his former apprentice Darth Maul after his excursion as a crime lord. Maul escaped with the help of his Mandalorian super commandos.
