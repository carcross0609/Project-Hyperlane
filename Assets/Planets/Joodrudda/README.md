# Joodrudda

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Joodrudda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Joodrudda)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Joodrudda`, Outer Rim, grid S-4 — map position (11347.1, 8741.4, 36.7)

Joodrudda was a barren, nearly uninhabited world located on the Tingel Route, just outside the borders of the Corporate Sector. Its only point of interest was a sprawling palace constructed hundreds of years before the Battle of Yavin by a clan of eccentric Hutts.
