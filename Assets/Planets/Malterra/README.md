# Malterra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malterra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malterra)

Malterra was a minor planet that was located within the Tython system of the galaxy's Deep Core region. The small world experienced violent storms when it passed the planet Sunspot in its orbit, and it had an extremely hot climate. More than thirty-six thousand years before the Battle of Yavin, the non–Force-sensitive settlers of the planet Tython colonized Malterra. Millennia later, mining operations had been established on the planet.
