# Kronas

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kronas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kronas)

Kronas was the first planet of the Kattellyn system, located in the Rolion sector of the Outer Rim. It was a ball of searing rock that orbited too close to its sun.
