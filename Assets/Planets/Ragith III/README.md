# Ragith III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ragith III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ragith_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ragith III`, Expansion Region, grid N-15 — map position (3463.4, -7887.4, 76.7)

Ragith III was a minor world in the Expansion Region, that had been settled by Humans for several generations by the time of the Dissolution of the Imperial Senate. The Ragithian Human inhabitants were descendant from colonists genetically bred and selected to adapt to the high gravity of the planet. Rodo the bouncer came from Ragith III.
