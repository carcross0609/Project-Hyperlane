# Loradil

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Loradil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loradil)

Loradil was a desert planet in the Fenril sector of the Inner Rim Territories. During the Clone Wars, a Providence-class dreadnought on its way to Agamar crashed on Loradil, stranding a complement of battle droids on the world. In the New Republic Era, the CR90 corvette Nechrim's Wake was forced down on Loradil after being attacked by pirates during the Fenril crisis. Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker, Rynn Zenat, Han Solo, and Beilert Valance later came to rescue the crew of the Wake, donning Phase II Clone trooper armor to face off with the robotic forces. The group then met the secret passenger of the Wake, the Nagai Marntiv, and departed successfully.
