# Valahari

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Valahari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valahari)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Valahari`, Outer Rim, grid N-5 — map position (3797.9, 7219.1, -50.1)

Valahari was a terrestrial planet located in grid square N-5 of the Standard Galactic Grid, in the Valahari system of the Valahari sector, a part of the D'Astan sector in the Outer Rim Territories and the New Territories. Around the time of the Clone Wars, a conflict fought between 22 BBY and 19 BBY by the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the planet achieved fame for its native starfighter aces and engineers.

Tofen's Raiders, an elite starfighter unit, was rumored to be headquartered on Valahari. Valahari was mentioned as their rumored homeworld in an entry that was written by ARC trooper Muzzle and published in the survival compendium "Neyo's Almanac of Clankers." Valahari shared its name with the Valahari flight harness, a type of flight harness that saw use during the Clone Wars as well as by the smuggler Corus Kapellim during the war between the First Order and the Resistance.
