# Unidentified agricultural planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified agricultural planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_agricultural_planet)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Unidentified agricultural planet`, Other, grid K-18 — map position (-362.4, -12452.8, 457.1)

Following the events of the Gorse Conflict, Zaluna Myder settled on an agricultural world. By the Age of the Empire, most of the planet's settlers had moved to places like Gorse to look for work. Zaluna settled in an abandoned house that had been sold at a cheap rate and had a back garden. The planet had a sun and was covered in trees and flowers. It was located several sectors away from Gorse.
