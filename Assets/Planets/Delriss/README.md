# Delriss

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Delriss](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delriss)

Delriss was a rocky planet located in the Expansion Region. Thousands of years before the Battle of Yavin, the planet was mined for rare ores, though they eventually were exhausted. It was orbited by the aquatic moon of Delriss.
