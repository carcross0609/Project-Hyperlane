# Mortis

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mortis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mortis)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mortis`, Wild Space, grid K-2 — map position (-839.6, 11000.8, 403.5)

Mortis was an ethereal realm within the Force that resembled a planet. Its existence was whispered over in legends and stories passed down through the millennia by Jedi, Sith, and the average spacer alike. It was believed to have existed outside any star system, and potentially the galaxy or even the universe. Some believed that the origins of the Force came from Mortis.
