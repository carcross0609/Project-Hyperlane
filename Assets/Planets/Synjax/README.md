# Synjax

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Synjax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Synjax)

Synjax was a paradise planet renowned for its pristine beaches. In 4 ABY, Synjax was the final destination of the first voyage of the star cruiser Halcyon after it had been liberated from the Galactic Empire. The individual Kelad was supposed to meet a buyer for his Thermal oscillator design on the planet.
