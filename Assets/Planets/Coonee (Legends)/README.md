# Coonee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coonee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coonee/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Coonee`, Expansion Region, grid O-15 — map position (5179.6, -7799.2, 10.9)

Coonee was a planet located in the Expansion Region. Han Solo, who was in his teens, and Chewbacca traveled to Coonee where they herded coldppedas, fifteen-footed scaled domestic animals, for three years.
