# Primea

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Primea](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Primea)

Primea was a planet located in the Primea system, and the capital of the Vak Combine. It was home to the Vak species. General Yiv of the Nikardun Destiny sought to conquer the planet, leaving warships in orbit and meeting with the planetary leaders.

Primea was the site of two battles during the Nikardun conquests: the attack at Primea by Lioaoin forces ordered by Yiv, and the battle over Primea between the Nikardun on one side and the Chiss Ascendancy and Vak Combine on the other.
