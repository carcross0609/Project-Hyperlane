# Shramar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shramar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shramar)

Shramar was a planet that was controlled by the Imperial Remnant during the time of the Yuuzhan Vong War.

]] At some point prior to the war, advance scouts of the Yuuzhan Vong seeded the planet with both lambents and muur with the intention of harvesting them at a later date. During the war, it was the site of the Battle of Shramar.
