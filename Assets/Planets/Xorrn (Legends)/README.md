# Xorrn

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xorrn/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xorrn/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Xorrn`, Outer Rim — map position (-4929.1, -9924.8, -334.1)

Xorrn was a mineral rich planet located in the Ferra sector within the Outer Rim Territories. Xorrn was a young planet. As the surface of the planet had only recently begun to cool, the natural resources of the planet were easily accessed. However the unstable crust and prohibitive start-up costs prevented nearly all mining operations.
