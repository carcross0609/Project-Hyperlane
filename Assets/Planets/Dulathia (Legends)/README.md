# Dulathia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dulathia/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dulathia/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dulathia`, Mid Rim, grid P-8 — map position (6816.5, 2212.7, -289.6)

Dulathia was an Imperial planet, until it was retaken by the New Republic around 8 ABY. The Star Destroyer Tempest was one of the few ships to survive, and brought its contents to Garos IV.
