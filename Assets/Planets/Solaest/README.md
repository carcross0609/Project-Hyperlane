# Solaest

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Solaest](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solaest)

Solaest was a planet located in the Solaest system, a part of the Belsmuth sector in the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The Salin Corridor hyperspace route linked it to the Belsmuth, Katraasii, New Bornalex, and Shadren systems as well as the Phindar system of the Demetras sector.

At some point between 2 BBY and 0 BBY, Solaest was the site of the Solaest Uprising, which was suppressed by the forces of the Galactic Empire as part of its wider attempts to crack down on the activities of its enemy during the Galactic Civil War, the Rebel Alliance. The uprising resulted in extensive civilian casualties. Solaest also shared its name with the Imperial Solaest Fleet Long Range Patrol Wing.
