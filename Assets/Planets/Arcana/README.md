# Arcana

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arcana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arcana)

Arcana was a planet that held a stronghold constructed by the Nightsister witches of Dathomir thousands of years before the Battle of Endor. In 9 ABY, Ahsoka Tano took a map from Arcana which was believed to lead to the Imperial Grand Admiral Thrawn.
