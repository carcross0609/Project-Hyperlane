# Unidentified fenti bean colony

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified fenti bean colony](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_fenti_bean_colony)

A planet was home to a colony of fenti bean and nerf farmers. A carnivorous plant grew in a ravine on the world. The colony was once visited by Jedi Master Ortraag and Jedi Watchman Shalavaa.
