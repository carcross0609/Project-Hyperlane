# Glauheim

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Glauheim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glauheim)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Glauheim`, Outer Rim — map position (9365.2, 5745.3, 12.1)

Glauheim was a gas giant that was orbited by three moons. It was the second planet in the Marcellus system, which resided in the Marcellus Nebula. Glauheim circled the yellow sun Marcellus. The gas giant, along with the rest of the its star system, was located in the Meerian sector of the New Territories portion of the Outer Rim Territories.
