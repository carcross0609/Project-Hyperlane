# Tiikae

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tiikae](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tiikae)

Tiikae was a small desert planet located between the Starlight Beacon and Coruscant. Controlled by the San Tekka clan under the permission of the Galactic Republic, it housed a settlement inhabited by San Tekka vassals and a Jedi temple during the High Republic Era. When the Nihil attacked Tiikae, the Jedi Vernestra Rwoh, Imri Cantaros, Reath Silas, and Cohmac Vitus responded to assist.
