# Uhltenden

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uhltenden](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uhltenden)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Uhltenden`, Other, grid P-6 — map position (6694.5, 5380.9, -5.2)

Uhltenden was a planet located within the Thrasybule sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Surrounded by a debris ring from many wrecked starships, the planet had an atmosphere toxic to life, and no natural life resided on the planet.
