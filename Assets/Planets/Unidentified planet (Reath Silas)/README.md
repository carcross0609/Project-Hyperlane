# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Reath Silas)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Reath_Silas%29)

During the High Republic Era, the human Jedi Reath Silas visited a planet where insects was considered typical foodstuff and were toasted and oiled before being eaten out of bags. During his time on the planet, Silas ate a bug for the first time. In 228 BBY, the Jedi recalled his visit to the planet when he and the scientist Avon Starros were served grub gruel for dinner on the Mid Rim planet Kashyyyk.
