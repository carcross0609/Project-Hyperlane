# Kijimi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kijimi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kijimi)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kijimi`, Mid Rim, grid R-8 — map position (9738.4, 2547.9, 9.0)

Kijimi was a frigid mountainous planet located in the Bryx sector of the galaxy's Mid Rim, in grid square R-8 on the Standard Galactic Grid. Once famed for its marked spirituality, the planet became known as a haven for criminals and lawlessness. It was the homeworld of Zorii Bliss, leader of the smuggling gang known as the Spice Runners of Kijimi. During their journey around the galaxy to uncover Jedi artifacts, Karr Nuq Sin and RZ-7 briefly stopped at Kijimi City to refuel the yacht Avadora. While on Kijimi, Sin was questioned by the First Order as they believed he had a clue to finding Jedi Master Luke Skywalker. In 35 ABY, Kijimi was obliterated by the Xyston-class Star Destroyer Derriphan of the Sith Eternal's fleet, the Final Order.
