# Ena

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ena](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ena)

Ena was a planet home to the Enami sentient species. During the High Republic Era, the planet was situated within Galactic Republic space. After the destruction of Starlight Beacon, the Hutt Clan, encroaching on Republic space, sent a force led by Tregarra the Hutt to occupy Ena, stealing food from the Enami. Republic and Jedi Order forces commanded by Jedi Master Keeve Trennis eventually liberated the planet and rescued the Enami.
