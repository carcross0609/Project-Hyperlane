# Groth; (The Bootana Hutta region, with Groth represented by the number 16)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Groth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Groth)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Groth`, Hutt Space, grid S-11 — map position (11573.2, -1771.4, -34.8)

Groth was a terrestrial world located in the Groth system, a part of the Bootana Hutta area in the Hutt Space portion of the Slice. Hyperlanes linked it to the Kor Desilijic, Kor Gejalli, and Saki system. At one point, Groth served as a throneworld of one of the clans of the Hutt species, but eventually it became a resort where Hutts spent their time gambling and watching gladiatorial fights.
