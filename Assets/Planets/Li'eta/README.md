# Li'eta

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Li'eta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Li%27eta)

Li'eta, as known by its inhabitants, or Polroth Five in star charts, was a planet in the Polroth system in the Bryx sector of the Mid Rim Territories. It was visited by the guerillas of the Onderonian resistance fighter Saw Gerrera during the early weeks of the Galactic Empire where they raided a medical facility.
