# Kira I

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kira I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kira_I)

Kira I was a planet in the Kira system, a part of the Expansion Region's Kira sector. It was an uninhabitable world with no apparent resources of value. Kira I was surveyed by Galactic Republic scouts visiting the Kira system years before 70 BBY. At some point following the initial survey, Kira I and two other planets of the system were destroyed by a cataclysm of unknown origin, leaving behind an asteroid field.
