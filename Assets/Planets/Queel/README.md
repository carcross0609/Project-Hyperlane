# Queel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Queel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Queel)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Queel`, Mid Rim, grid P-15 — map position (6346.6, -8548.1, 285.3)

Queel was a swamp-covered Mid Rim planet. The planet was one of the many worlds to see action in the early months of the Clone Wars. In 22 BBY it was the site of a battle between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. During the battle, Jedi General Anakin Skywalker, Jedi Commander Ahsoka Tano, and Clone Captain CT-7567 "Rex" received a new mission, and General Obi-Wan Kenobi continued the command of the engagement.
