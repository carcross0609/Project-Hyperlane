# Enujut

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Enujut](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enujut)

Enujut was a rocky world in the galaxy. Evor Haris served as a peacekeeper on the world, welcoming visitors. After a group of Zemerin, telepaths that fed on negative emotions, arrived on Enujut, they began to induce negativity within the world's inhabitants in order to feed on it. Haris managed to trick the Zemerin into leaving with the help of a protocol droid.
