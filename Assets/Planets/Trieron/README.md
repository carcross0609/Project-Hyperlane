# Trieron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trieron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trieron)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Trieron`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (1349.5, -10735.9, 21.9)

Trieron was a Mid Rim world and the third planet in the Veron Minor system. It was a research planet covered in cool oceans and glacial continents. Several learning institutions had oceanographic research bases there, including Academy at Sab Rufo, Metharg's University, and UnitedChem/SoroSuub University.
