# Orimanther

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orimanther](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orimanther)

Orimanther was a world in the Quelii sector that was visited by the Chevin slaver Phylus Mon during the waning years of the Galactic Republic. According to a rumor, Mon attempted to capture a flock of Orimanther's battlebirds, but was initially unsuccessful, so he meditated and created a Force storm, to knock the birds from out of the sky.
