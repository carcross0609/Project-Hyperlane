# Horn Station

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Horn Station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Horn_Station)

Horn Station was a backwater world in the Lahara sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was neglected by the Galactic Republic until a Jedi Knight, nicknamed "Pinky," liberated the planet from Shoto Eyefire, a Defel crime lord and blastfighter. Horn Station was the homeworld of a species of hulking, green-skinned, reptilian humanoids.
