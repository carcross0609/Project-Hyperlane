# Planet of the Red Nebula

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Planet of the Red Nebula](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planet_of_the_Red_Nebula)

The Planet of the Red Nebula was the sole inhabited planet in the Red Nebula, located in Wild Space. It was bombarded with comets when the nebula was severed from the rest of the galaxy by an unknown cataclysm.
