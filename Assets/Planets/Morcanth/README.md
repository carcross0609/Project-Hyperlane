# Morcanth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Morcanth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Morcanth)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Morcanth`, Unknown Regions — map position (-9217.8, 2498.5, 146.6)

Morcanth was an Unknown Regions planet the galactic neighborhood of which was the subject of a centuries-long war between three spacefaring alien species. At one point, the corrupted Jedi Winslau Da'k imprisoned his former Jedi Master at a fortress on Morcanth, where Da'k resided in wealth and comfort. After the Jedi Master telepathically sought out help from a group of agents of the Rebel Alliance, the rebels ultimately orchestrated an assault on Da'k's fortress and successfully rescued the Jedi.
