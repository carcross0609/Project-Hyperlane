# Rekkana

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rekkana](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rekkana)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rekkana`, Other, grid Q-15 — map position (8267.3, -8582.4, -130.0)

Rekkana was a shipyard planet home to the Rekk, who were known for their skills in creating automatic systems. Shortly after the Battle of Endor, Lando Calrissian picked up a distress call from Rekkana while on his way to return the Millennium Falcon to Han Solo, making a detour to investigate the mention of Imperial forces. Upon arriving above Rekkana, he was attacked by TIE fighters that showed signs of being computer-controlled.
