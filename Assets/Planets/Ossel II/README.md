# Ossel II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ossel II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ossel_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ossel II`, Outer Rim, grid L-18 — map position (657.9, -12255.8, 445.2)

Ossel II was the swampy, high-gravity homeworld of the Ossans, in Western Reaches and the Outer Rim Territories. Its main export was syp wood, though traders trying to purchase syp wood from Ossans were often required to take Ossans on as part of their crew. Many Ossans ended up slaves on Karfeddion.
