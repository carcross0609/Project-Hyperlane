# Algara II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Algara II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Algara_II)

Algara II, also referred to simply as Algara and Algar, was a planet in the Algaran system of the Lambda sector, located in the Mid Rim with a population of approximately 1 billion. It was also the homeworld of the Xan. Algara II was orbited by four moons: Kevron, Omakaton, Radeon and Tallakron.
