# Terr'skiar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Terr'skiar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terr%27skiar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Terr'skiar`, Mid Rim, grid P-10 — map position (7191.8, -484.1, 284.2)

Terr'skiar was a trade world located in the Terr'skiar system, a part of the Kashyyyk Region's Terr'skiar sector in the Slice portion of the Mid Rim. The hyperlanes known as the Nightroad and the Ootmian Pabol linked it to the Pizilis system and the Coachelle and New Apsolon systems, respectively, while the Terr'skiar Pass route connected it to the Trax sector's Bissillirus system. Terr'skiar served as the capital of the Terr'skiar sector.
