# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Graballa the Hutt)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Graballa_the_Hutt%29)

An uninhabited mining planet was used by Graballa the Hutt to mine for Kyber crystals under instruction by Darth Vader in order to power the Death Star II. Graballa and his gang strip-mined the entire planet but found no Kyber Crystals. When Vader checked in on their progress via Hologram, Graballa was forced to admit they had found nothing, to which Vader replied that his time and patience was growing thin. Lacking in options, Grablla's Toydarian henchman Yeppau suggested using Rowan Freemaker and his connection to Kyber Crystals in order to find the Crystals they need.
