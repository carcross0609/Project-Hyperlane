# Jabiim

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jabiim/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jabiim/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Jabiim`, Outer Rim, grid S-7 — map position (11327.8, 3413.7, 393.4)

Jabiim , known as Gwynhes Minor during the reign of Xim, was a small, muddy, Outer Rim world. It was most notably the site of three fierce Battles of Jabiim.
