# Choah Belt

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Choah Belt/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Choah_Belt/Legends)

Choah Belt was an asteroid belt in the Mortex sector of the Outer Rim Territories. During the Galactic War, it was rumored that the long-dead crime lord Shisha Mor had used one of the asteroids as a hideout. The Alliance to Restore the Republic maintained a fuel refinery in the asteroid field until shortly after the Battle of Yavin. The Galactic Empire discovered its location and destroyed it, forcing the Alliance to try and hijack the Black Ice.
