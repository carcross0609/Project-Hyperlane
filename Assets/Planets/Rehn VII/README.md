# Rehn VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rehn VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rehn_VII)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rehn VII`, Expansion Region, grid O-14 — map position (4863.4, -7094.1, 260.2)

Rehn VII was a planet located within the Rehn system of Brak sector, situated in the Expansion Region. The world was capable of foodstuff production and had manufacturing capabilities.

During the Galactic Civil War, the Rehn system was one of four systems heavily restricted by the Galactic Empire as it was the location of an Imperial Navy staging zone. Despite the increased security, the Alliance established a listening post on the planet to spy on Imperial activities in the staging area.
