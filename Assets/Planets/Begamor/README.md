# Begamor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Begamor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Begamor)

Begamor was a planet of the galaxy with an irregular orbit around a binary star. The light of the planet's suns appeared to have a green tint upon the surface. The world was once led by a corrupt president, Jamie Brasson. In 200 BBY, Jedi Master Mostima and her Padawan Sean went to deal with Jamie Brasson. In orbit of the planet were tens of thousands of artificial satellites and more than thirty space stations.
