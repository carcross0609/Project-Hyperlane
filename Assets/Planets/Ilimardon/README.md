# Ilimardon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ilimardon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ilimardon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ilimardon`, Mid Rim, grid L-7 — map position (482.3, 3549.4, -151.1)

Ilimardon was a planet located in the Mid Rim's Nicandra sector. It was governed by the Regal Hierarchy, which maintained a wildlife division and a fleet of starships. When the Regal Hierarchy became interested in the use of domesticated mynocks as weapons against starships, it employed the Human Gres Ople, former mynock trainer of the Trandoshan pirate Chorssk's organization, to train a new flock of the creatures. By 12 ABY, Ilimardon was a member of the New Republic.
