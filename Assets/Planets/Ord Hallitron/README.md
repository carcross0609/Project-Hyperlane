# Ord Hallitron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Hallitron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Hallitron)

Ord Hallitron was a planet in the Mid Rim. An Ordnance/Regional Depot, Ord Hallitron was used by the Pius Dea-controlled Galactic Republic as a staging point for their assault on the nearby world of Glee Anselm during the Great Northern Crusade in 11,884 BBY.
