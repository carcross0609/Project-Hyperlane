# Clariv

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Clariv](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clariv)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Clariv`, Other, grid S-6 — map position (11257.8, 4694.3, 251.4)

Clariv was a world located in the Clariv system. It was a part of the Back Spiral area of the Tion Hegemony sector, which was situated in the Tion Cluster within the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Clariv lay between the Erediss and Rudrig systems on the hyperlane known as the Tion Trade Route.

Clariv was the site of the bustling Clariv Shipyards, with a large portion of the business there being conducted for interests in the nearby Keldrath and Pakuuni sectors. Several years prior to 2 ABY, the bounty hunter Milacass was injured in an explosion at the shipyards. Clariv was sympathetic to the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War, and when the Galactic Empire learned of the fact shortly before the Battle of Yavin, the world became the target of a brutal Imperial subjugation.
