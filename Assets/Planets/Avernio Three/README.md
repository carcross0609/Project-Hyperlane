# Avernio Three

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Avernio Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avernio_Three)

Avernio Three was a world located in the galaxy. At some point before roughly 6000 BBY, the planet was chosen by the unidentified Iokath species as one of the testing sites for their weapons and Avernio Three's unique civilization was assaulted with the destructive force of Iokath weaponry.
