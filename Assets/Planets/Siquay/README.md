# Siquay

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Siquay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Siquay)

Siquay was a gas giant located in grid square S-8 of the Standard Galactic Grid, in the Sriluur system located in the Periphery subsector of the Tharin sector within the Outer Rim Territories and the Slice. Siquay had a space dock orbiting it. At some point no earlier than 379 BBY, the Evereni siblings Vika and Velya Faer refueled their starship at Siquay's dock and stayed there long enough for Marda Ro—the Evereni founder of the group of pirates who would eventually become the Nihil—to catch up with them in her hunt for other Evereni.

After starships under her command surrounded the siblings' ship, Ro sent the two Faers an invitation to come aboard her vessel, the Gaze Electric, and they obliged. Ro later recorded a message for her descendants and mentioned Siquay and her encounter with the Faers in it. By 252 BBY, during his youth, Ro's great-great-grandson Marchion Ro had listened to her journal, including the message that mentioned Siquay.
