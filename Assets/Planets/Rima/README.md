# Rima

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rima](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rima)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rima`, Expansion Region, grid O-14 — map position (5139.0, -6455.8, -29.4)

Rima was the first planet of the Demar system. Its closeness to the system's sun caused it to be a lifeless, super-hot ball of rock. Though the entire Demar system was owned by the Lant Mining Corporation, Rima was never mined due to a lack of resources and the difficulty of working so close to the supergiant sun.
