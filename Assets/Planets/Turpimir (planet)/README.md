# Turpimir

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Turpimir (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Turpimir_%28planet%29)

Turpimir was the fourth planet orbiting the star Hoth's Brand in the Ruusan system. Located in the Teraab sector of the Mid Rim, it was a barren world.

It was named after the Jedi Knight Turpimir.

During Jerec's Quest for the Valley of the Jedi on Ruusan and his invasion of the planet, most ships of his Vengeance Battle Group were still centered around the fourth planet of the system.
