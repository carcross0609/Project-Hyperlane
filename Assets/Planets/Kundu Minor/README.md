# Kundu Minor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kundu Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kundu_Minor)

Kundu Minor, also known as Kundu, was an arid planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. It was the home of the Besalisks Braygh and Slaygh.
