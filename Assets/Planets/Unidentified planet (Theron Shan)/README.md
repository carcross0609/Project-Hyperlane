# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Theron Shan)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Theron_Shan%29)

In 3666 BBY, the Jedi Knight Satele Shan bore her child, Theron, on a planet far from the reach of the Great Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. With the help of Jedi Master Ngani Zho, Shan gave birth to her son in a cave on the planet's surface, though she gave up Theron to be raised by Zho while she returned to the battlefront. The planet also featured a breathable atmosphere as well as trees and other foliage, and the sky was an orange color.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified planet
- **Atmosphere:** Type I (breathable)
- **Terrain:**
