# Kip

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kip](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kip)

Kip was a planet in the Outer Rim's Chorlian sector and the homeworld of the Kip species. It was a source of ulikuo gemstones. It was a damp mud-ball riddled with caves. Zorba Desilijic Tiure was imprisoned on Kip in 22 BBY, shortly before the Clone Wars. After the Battle of Endor, in 5 ABY, Ysanne Isard arranged for pirates to take over Kip and free Zorba. Kip years were roughly equal to three standard years.
