# Rondai-2

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rondai-2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rondai-2)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rondai-2`, Inner Rim, grid J-8 — map position (-1914.5, 2591.7, -23.1)

Rondai-2 was a cosmopolitan planet of mild weather and mild landscapes, with rolling hills but no mountains. It was two days travel by hyperspace from Haven and less than a day from Coruscant. It was a planet for top-security, low-profile business meetings in the Ulta Center.
