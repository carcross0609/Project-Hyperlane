# Intuci

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intuci](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intuci)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Intuci`, Outer Rim, grid S-15 — map position (11015.9, -8819.3, -456.7)

Intuci was a planet in the Abrion sector of the Outer Rim Territories under the control of the Galactic Empire.

The war criminal Gaar Suppoon, under the alias of Sonopo Bomoor, sacked the city of Bonaka Nueno and massacred its citizens.
