# Skärtis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skärtis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sk%C3%A4rtis)

Skärtis, or Skartis, was a resort world located in the Senex sector that was maintained by House Araba. Its seven moons were famous for their complicated orbits. Seeing all seven in the sky at once was said to be a lucky omen.
