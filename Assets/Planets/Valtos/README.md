# Valtos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Valtos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valtos)

Valtos was a rocky planet located in the Valtos system of Wild Space. In 9 BBY, Han Solo was at Valtos space port before being captured by the bounty hunters Zuckuss and 4-LOM. Amaiza Foxtrain had multiple safehouses on the planet, and hid in one from the Galactic Empire after she stole transit chips. She was tracked down by the Empire who launched a raid on the hideout while the Lepi smuggler Jaxxon T. Tumperakki attempted to take the transit chips for the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Tovey's Cantina was located near the hideout.
