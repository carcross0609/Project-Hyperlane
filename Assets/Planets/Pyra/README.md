# Pyra

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pyra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pyra)

Pyra was a world located in the Pyra system within the Fei Hu sector of the Mid Rim and the Slice. It was situated in grid square P-13 of the Standard Galactic Grid.

During its reign, the Galactic Empire mined Pyra for its resources until its fertile soil had been turned into desert. In 4 ABY, during a meeting of Rebel Alliance personnel in Bright Tree Village on the Outer Rim moon Endor, Princess Leia Organa used Pyra as an example of worlds that had been destroyed by the Empire in its attempts to acquire resources from them.
