# Luzalite

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Luzalite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luzalite)

Luzalite was a planet located in the Luzalite system of the Core Worlds and the Interior, situated in grid square L-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid. A hyperspace route connected it to the Pantolomin and Coruscant systems. Luzalite was little-known by the time of the High Republic Era. Kozmo Sundrel IV was a Luzalite lord hailing from Luzalite, and a variety of diamond—from which Sundrel's Luzalite diamond medallion was fashioned—was also from the planet. By 396 BBY, Sundrel had become active in the wider galaxy. Around that same year, when Reena University student Axel Greylark spoke to his father—Galactic Republic Senator Lexxir Greylark—about his improved friendship with Sundrel, Lexxir referred to Sundrel's homeworld.
