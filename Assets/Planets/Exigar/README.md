# Exigar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Exigar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exigar)

Exigar was a polluted planet in the galaxy. During the Cold War, surveys of Exigar detected unusual mutations in the planet's flora, and further investigation revealed that the pollution was causing abnormal variations in native lifeforms.
