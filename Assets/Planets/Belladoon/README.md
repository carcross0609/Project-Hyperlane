# Belladoon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Belladoon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Belladoon)

Belladoon was a world located in the Myto sector's Belladoon system within the Outer Rim Territories and the New Territories regions. Situated within grid square K-3 of the Standard Galactic Grid, it lay along the Veragi Trade Route hyperlane between the astronomical objects Dantooine and Dubrillion. Belladoon contributed to the Galactic Empire's military by 5 ABY, during the Galactic Civil War between the Empire and the New Republic. In a meeting with his new Imperial Shadow Council that year, Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax spoke about the Empire using its Outer Rim holdings to its advantage, listing Belladoon, Korrus, and Zhadalene as worlds there that were already under Imperial sway.
