# At Attin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [At Attin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/At_Attin)

At Attin was a forested planet in the Outer Rim Territories with an orderly population that was isolated from the wider galaxy as part of the Great Work of the Old Republic. Citizens worked diligently as analysts with the help of service droids of various types on the world beneath the Barrier, which hid the world and blocked any view of the stars from the surface. By the New Republic Era, At Attin was known to the wider galaxy as a legendary treasure planet among the Jewels of the Old Republic.

The children of the Skeleton Crew hailed from At Attin, and their adventures aboard the formerly long-lost starship Onyx Cinder culminated in an invasion of the planet by Captain Silvo's pirates to plunder the At Attin Mint. The invasion was foiled by the heroics of Skeleton Crew and the intervention of the New Republic, which was thus made aware of the planet's existence.
