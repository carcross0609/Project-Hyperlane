# Boneworld

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Boneworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boneworld)

The Boneworld was a far-off terrestrial planet in the Boneworld system in the Toblain sector of the Outer Rim Territories with rocky terrain that was covered in massive skeletons. It was usually visited mainly for its supply of ambercryst and fossilized bone marrow, both of which were very valuable. In 382 BBY, Jedi Masters Porter Engle and Barash Silvain visited the planet, and Engle cooked stew for some of its inhabitants using ingredients he found there. But after settling a dispute over ambercryst between a Twi'lek individual and Nautolan named Ralen, the two Jedi left the world.
