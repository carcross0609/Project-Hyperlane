# Zebitrope VIII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zebitrope VIII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zebitrope_VIII)

Zebitrope VIII was a terrestrial planet located in the Zebitrope system, a part of the Centrality sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It occupied the eight orbital position in its star system, which was connected to the Cadma system via the Cadma Conduit hyperlane.

Zebitrope VIII was home to a rare species of lizard that originated on the planet Zebitrope IV. The lizards had a symbiotic relationship with a type of mold that only grew on the reptiles' backs. The mold was the only source of the valuable and illegal drug lesai, and as a result, Zebitrope VIII was regularly visited by smugglers after they had completed the spice-trafficking route known as the Kessel Run.
