# Poln Minor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Poln Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poln_Minor)

Poln Minor was a planet in the Poln system, of the Outer Rim Candoras sector. Although once a center of mining and manufacturing, by 0 ABY its roles had diminished in both capacities. Poln Minor was rich in natural resources, and had a deep network of underground caverns. AF-two-two-seven-five was an intersection located below Poln Minor's surface.
