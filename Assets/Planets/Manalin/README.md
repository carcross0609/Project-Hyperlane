# Manalin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Manalin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Manalin)

Manalin was a rocky planet located in the Joralla system, a part of the Mektrun sector in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. Manalin occupied the closest orbital position around its sun, the red giant–type star Jaska, and had no moons. The searing-hot planet had a poisonous atmosphere and was considered to have no commercial value.
