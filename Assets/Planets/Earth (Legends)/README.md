# Earth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Earth/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Earth/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Earth`, Outer Rim, grid K-6 — map position (-852.2, 5590.7, -14.1)

Earth was a planet the Star Tours travel agency operated on at some point between 1 and 0 BBY. From space, one could see that a major proportion of Earth's surface was covered by oceans, with the remainder consisting of landmasses, and that a web of clouds enveloped the planet in a white veil. Through the Star Tours spaceline, Earth was connected to other worlds such as Coruscant, Kashyyyk, Naboo, Hoth, Geonosis and Tatooine. Around 4.3 ABY, Earth also acted as a hub for flights to Endor, prior to Star Tours being shut down. It was composed of various nation-states, with the Intergalactic Passport being useless as a planetary passport for any of these nation-states.
