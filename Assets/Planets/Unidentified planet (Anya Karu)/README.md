# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Anya Karu)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Anya_Karu%29)

This unidentified planet was the location of a downed Imperial Star Destroyer, the Anya Karu. Aron Harcourt, the captain of the ship, once sent the bounty hunter Boba Fett to the planet to retrieve a holo-recording of Harcourt's wife, Janelle, from the wreckage.

Upon his arrival, Fett's ship, the Slave I, engaged several patrolling TIE/LN starfighters in battle. After he landed, Fett explored the downed Anya Karu and was attacked by mynocks who had taken refuge within the wreckage. He barely escaped the wreck when it was destroyed by the Adjudicator.
