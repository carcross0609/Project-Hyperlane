# Toskhowwl VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Toskhowwl VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Toskhowwl_VI)

Toskhowwl VI was a planet located within the Toskhowwl system of the Mid Rim. Around 5 ABY, the Jaltiri tribals lived on Toskhowwl VI and were known beyond their world for their custom of building stacks of severed heads.
