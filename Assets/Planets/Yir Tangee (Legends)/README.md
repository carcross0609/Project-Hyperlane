# Yir Tangee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yir Tangee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yir_Tangee/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Yir Tangee`, Colonies, grid L-8 — map position (296.6, 2520.0, -153.7)

Yir Tangee was a planet in the Yir Tangee system of the Colonies. In 22 BBY, Senator Havriso Looruya was appointed to the Galactic Senate's Loyalist Committee—a committee to assist with the crisis of Separatists' attempts to secede from the Galactic Republic—as a representative of Yir Tangee. Looruya was discovered funding Separatist territories after being accused by Senator Onaconda Farr of the planet Rodia, leading to Looruya's stepping down from the committee.
