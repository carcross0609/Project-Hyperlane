# PL-40112-CE-021105

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PL-40112-CE-021105](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PL-40112-CE-021105)

PL-40112-CE-021105 was a planet located in the Salfrem system, within the forbidden Wild Space sector known as the Wastes. The planet was the home of Bertroff Hissa, a Human-Sephi hybrid who ended up becoming Grand Moff of the Galactic Empire. PL-40112-CE-021105 remained uncharted until the Empire launched a survey. However, the surveyors awoke an ancient horde of Rozzum, a species of inter-dimensional monsters. The Imperials were forced to ally with the Near-Humans who lived on PL-40112-CE-021105 in order to vanquish the creatures. Ultimately, only two survived the encounter: Hissa, and the ranking Imperial officer from Naboo, Commander Quarsh Panaka.
