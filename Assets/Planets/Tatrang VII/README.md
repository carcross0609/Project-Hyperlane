# Tatrang VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tatrang VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tatrang_VII)

Tatrang VII was a ringed gas giant–type planet with eleven moons that was located within the Tatrang system, a part of the Tammuz sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It occupied the seventh orbital position around the system's star, the red giant Tatrang. The planet was considered to hold little economic value.
