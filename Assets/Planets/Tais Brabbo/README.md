# Tais Brabbo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tais Brabbo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tais_Brabbo)

Tais Brabbo was a planet in the Tais Brabbo system in the Shadola sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Crime-ridden, Tais Brabbo's corruption was such that the Hutts were rumored to have decided against moving some of their operations there. Described as a good place to get lost, on any given day millions of individuals who wished to avoid attention from authorities were present there. The planet did have local marshals, but they were ineffective. Because it possessed communications equipment which allowed quick contact with Coruscant and was far away from the Starlight Beacon space station, Tais Brabbo was one of four planets Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro chose as targets for attacks intended to lure Starlight's Jedi away from the station.
