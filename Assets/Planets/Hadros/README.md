# Hadros

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hadros](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hadros)

Hadros was a planet of the Outer Rim Territories that was rich in ore deposits. Supposedly, at some point after the year 29 ABY, Ambassador Slokin blackmailed the ambassador of Hadros into providing him access to the ore deposits of Hadros, which had not yet been claimed by the First Order.
