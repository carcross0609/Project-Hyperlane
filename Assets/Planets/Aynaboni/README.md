# Aynaboni

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aynaboni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aynaboni)

Aynaboni was a tropical planet located in the galaxy. Covered largely in ocean that housed some sea life, the planet also had at least one jungle island and was inhabited by aggrocrabs. During the early Imperial Era, the Bad Batch took a job retrieving a crate from an island on the planet for the pirate Phee Genoa.
