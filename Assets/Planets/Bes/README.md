# Bes

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bes)

Bes was a world located in the Bes system, within the Airam sector of the Outer Rim Territories. A space battle between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire took place near Bes during the Galactic Civil War.
