# Tay'ah'loo

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tay'ah'loo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tay%27ah%27loo)

Tay'ah'loo was a planet in Imperial Space. In 1 ABY, an unscheduled capsule carrying Bledsoe's disease landed on the planet. The plague was released and decimated the population of Tay'ah'loo within days. Stormtroopers soon arrived to cart away the dead and disinfect the planet.
