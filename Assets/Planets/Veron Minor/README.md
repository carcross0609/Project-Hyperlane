# Veron Minor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Veron Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veron_Minor)

Veron Minor, also known as simply Veron, was a planet located within the Mektrun Cluster; it was the second planet in the Veron Minor system. The native Gazaran were ruled by the ancient noble house of Araba. The planet attracted many tourists. Ferlem Kie'hintack was at one time Imperial Governor of the planet.
