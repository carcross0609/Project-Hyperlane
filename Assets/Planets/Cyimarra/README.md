# Cyimarra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cyimarra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyimarra)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Cyimarra`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (391.3, -11712.3, 282.1)

Cyimarra was a terrestrial planet located in the Mid Rim's Senex sector. The homeworld of the the aristocratic House Araba, its surface featured cities interspersed among woodlands and mountains as well as a sea.

Cyimarra was famous for the Crystal Spires, a series of ancient towers of uniform height that were evenly spaced across the planet's surface. By 0 ABY, the planets Corellia and Corulag sent delegations to Cyimarra, and it was speculated that they would present a recreation of the Crystal Spires on the planet Coruscant during the festivities of that year's New Year Fete Week.
