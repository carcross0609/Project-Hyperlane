# Cybloc

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cybloc](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cybloc)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Cybloc`, Outer Rim, grid R-7 — map position (9648.7, 4434.0, 226.8)

Cybloc, also known as Cybloc Prime, was a greenish planet orbiting the star Erg Es 992 in the Meridian sector. During the Clone Wars there was a space station in orbit over the planet. Darth Maul and his brother Savage Opress broke into the space station and stole some credits. One of its moons, Cybloc XII, housed a New Republic trade base. However, the region was purged during the Death Seed incident.
