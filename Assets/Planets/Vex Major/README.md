# Vex Major

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vex Major](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vex_Major)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Vex Major`, Outer Rim, grid H-16 — map position (-4942.7, -10162.1, -133.3)

Vex Major, also known as Far Gent, was a swampy Outer Rim Territories planet located in the Moddell sector. Originally settled by a sect of the Order of the Ffib, by the time of the Yuuzhan Vong War decades later Vex Major was inhabited by insular clans of Human fishers.
