# Attahox

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Attahox/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Attahox/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Attahox`, Expansion Region, grid P-12 — map position (7120.7, -3253.5, -337.4)

Attahox was a world in the Expansion Region near Daalang.

During the Great Sith War, Krath came from Ulda Frav and conquered the planet before continuing on to Nixor.

By the time of the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and Sith Empire, Attahox was known as a garbage planet, and had been used for centuries by criminal gangs as a hideout and cache. Archaeologists sifted through the refuse in search of lost secrets.

During the Clone Wars, Attahox was located within Separatist space.

As of the Galactic Civil War, Attahox was known as a stinking, impoverished, and corrupt planet that served as the base of operations for the Exalted Torgaigne crime syndicate. The Torgaigne controlled the local trade in phosovane salts, which were used in painkillers on Rim worlds.

As part of its efforts to economically revitalize Expansion Region planets in The Slice region, the Galactic Empire funded a colony on Attahox to search for resources previous strip miners had overlooked.
