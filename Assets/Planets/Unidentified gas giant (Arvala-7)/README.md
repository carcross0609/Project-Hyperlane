# Unidentified gas giant

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified gas giant (Arvala-7)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_gas_giant_%28Arvala-7%29)

A gas giant was part of the Arvala system in the Sevetta sector within the Outer Rim Territories and the Slice. It fell within grid square S-15 of the Standard Galactic Grid and had dozens of moons orbiting it until they were shaken loose by a cometary calamity. By 9 ABY, one of theses moons, Arvala-7, had settled into its own stable orbit as a planetoid at the system's seventh orbital position, closer than the gas giant to the star system's sun.
