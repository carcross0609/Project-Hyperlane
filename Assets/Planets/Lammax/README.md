# Lammax

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lammax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lammax)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lammax`, Colonies — map position (-912.4, -5549.4, 64.9)

Lammax was a shadowport planet in the Danju system in the Colonies, near a nebula that made travel to the planet dangerous. Port Bianco was established on Lammax by Lamuu Bianco during the last years of the Galactic Republic as a place for his pirate ships to dock between tourist seasons. It was at its height during the Galactic Civil War. Former bacta pirate Shanto Dhil served as mayor of Port Bianco.
