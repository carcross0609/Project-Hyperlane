# Unidentified Outer Rim planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Outer Rim planet (Tasiele Shan)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Outer_Rim_planet_%28Tasiele_Shan%29)

When the Jedi Knight Tasiele Shan was exiled by the Jedi Order for challenging their views on marriage and attachment, she was sent to an unnamed planet in the Outer Rim Territories in order to meditate on her controversial views. When the Jedi attempted to retrieve her years later, all they found was Shan's cliffside dwelling and a set of journals written to her young daughter Satele.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified Outer Rim planet
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
- **Terrain:** Cliffs
