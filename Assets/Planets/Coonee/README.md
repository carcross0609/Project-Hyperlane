# Coonee

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Coonee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coonee)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Coonee`, Expansion Region, grid O-15 — map position (5179.6, -7799.2, 10.9)

Coonee was a planet in the Brevost sector of the Expansion Region. According to a theory from smuggler Tregga, the Wookiee Chewbacca raised an orphaned Han Solo after the end of the Clone Wars, and before being captured by the Galactic Empire on the planet Mimban they made their living on planets such as Coonee and Saberhing.

Tregga told the bartender Tivoche Bilure his theory who wrote it his journal. Bilure did not believe the theory since Tregga's implants were known to malfunction. Despite Tregga's theory, Han and Chewbacca first met on Mimban after they were captured by the Empire.
