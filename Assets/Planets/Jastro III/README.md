# Jastro III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jastro III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jastro_III)

Jastro III was a Core Worlds planet that was under the administration of a governor of the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. A Rebel Alliance underground group operated on the planet, and the governor's daughter, who was in love with the Rebel Selas Ferr, put the group in contact with "Alexan," a mysterious, influential individual who was secretly the governor himself in disguise. At one point, Alexan had Ferr's group take the governor's daughter away with them while helping them rescue two of their captured members. Alexan was subsequently killed in a firefight with Imperial troopers, leading to Ferr and his companions learning his true identity.
