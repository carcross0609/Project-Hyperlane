# Throffdon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Throffdon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Throffdon)

Throffdon was a planet located in the Throffdon system of the Inner Rim and the Interior regions, in grid square J-9 on the Standard Galactic Grid. The planet orbited at least one sun, which went supernova during the Imperial Era. In 34 ABY, the historian Eloc Throno included a map with the location of Throffdon in his book Traveler's Guide to Batuu, a book that detailed the Outer Rim planet of Batuu.
