# Kasiol 3

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kasiol 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kasiol_3)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kasiol 3`, Outer Rim, grid R-18 — map position (9709.9, -12573.2, 163.8)

Kasiol 3, also known as Kasiol's third planet, was a world located in the Outer Rim Territories' Pelgrin sector. Its surface featured forests and numerous swamps. The local fauna included the herlixx, a species of amphibian omnivore that hunted fish and smaller amphibians. Kasiol 3 had been colonized by the time of the reign of the Galactic Empire.
