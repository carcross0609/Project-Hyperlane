# Veelo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Veelo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veelo)

Veelo was a planet in the Veelo system in the Vorzyd sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It had a Type III atmosphere which made it lethal for humans without protective breath masks. The surface consisted of severely polluted waters and swamps which could be traversed via hydroskiffs offered to travelers for a price in the Veelo docks, and teemed with dangerous gryzards. During or after 18 BBY, the bounty hunter Sylar Saris took refuge in a hideout on the planet after being targeted by the Haxion Brood, prompting fellow hunter Fennec Shand to come to the planet and hunt him down along with genetically defective clones Hunter and Wrecker.
