# Unidentified gas giant

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified gas giant (Ciaox Verith system)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_gas_giant_%28Ciaox_Verith_system%29)

A gas giant was located in the Ciaox Verith system, within the Trailing Sectors portion of the Inner Rim Territories. In 5 ABY, the Galactic Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing ran combat drills above the gas giant until it was attacked by a New Republic battle group led by General Hera Syndulla.
