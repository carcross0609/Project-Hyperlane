# Korphir

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Korphir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korphir)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Korphir`, Outer Rim, grid Q-6 — map position (7839.1, 5512.4, -61.3)

Korphir was an Imperial planet during the Galactic Civil War.

It served as a temporary home to fugitive Havet Storm in 1 BBY. The young boy found employ there acting as a mind reader in a circus, until his fellow performers grew suspicious of his strange abilities and failed an attempt on his life.

Understandably, this caused Storm to leave the planet.
