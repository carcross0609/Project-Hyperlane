# At Arissia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [At Arissia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/At_Arissia)

At Arissia was a planet within the galaxy that was one of the Jewels of the Old Republic. The Fallen Sanctum on At Achrann, another of the Jewels, had a pillar with the hyperspace coordinates to At Arissia, along with one for each of the other Jewels. By the time of the New Republic, At Arissia and seven of the other Jewels were believed to have been destroyed by the wider galaxy.
