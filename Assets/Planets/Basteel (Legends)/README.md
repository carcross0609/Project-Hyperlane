# Basteel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Basteel/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Basteel/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Basteel`, Outer Rim, grid P-3 — map position (6746.5, 9883.2, 91.8)

Basteel was a temperate, bleak planet where Slique Brighteyes hid from Admiral Mir Tork and Doctor Leonis Murthé. Not long after the Battle of Endor, Slique hired Boba Fett to kill Mir and Leonis for 100 credits. Fett returned with their heads just before Slique died.
