# Shumari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shumari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shumari)

Shumari was a planet in the Parmel sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The Tusken Raider KkH'Oar'Rrhr was rumored to have traveled to this planet to confront the Demon Outlander who wiped out his village, otherwise known as Darth Vader.
