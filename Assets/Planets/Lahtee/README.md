# Lahtee

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lahtee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lahtee)

Lahtee was a distant snow-covered planet located in the Mid Rim Territories of the galaxy. In 34 ABY, Supreme Leader Kylo Ren traveled to Lahtee with Vaneé to hunt down a Theelin Jedi survivor known as Grandea. After Ren seemingly killed Grandea, the Jedi seemed to rise into the air to taunt Ren, to which Ren responded by striking her down again. However, her body vanished and the village around them transformed suddenly from a lively community to a dilapidated husk of itself.
