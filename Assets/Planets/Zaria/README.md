# Zaria

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zaria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zaria)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Zaria`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (200.6, -11926.9, -280.4)

Zaria was a terrestrial world located in the Zaria system of the Juvex sector, a part of the Senex-Juvex region in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. The Senex-Juvex Loop hyperlane linked it to the Anstares and Malador systems.

An industrial world, Zaria was the homeworld of the sentient, frog-like Zarian species. Although the Zarians themselves were free, their homeworld, like the rest of the Senex-Juvex worlds, was under the control of the region's aristocratic Ancient Houses.
