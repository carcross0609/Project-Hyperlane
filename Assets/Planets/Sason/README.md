# Sason

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sason](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sason)

Sason was a backwater gas giant in the Outer Rim Territories that was the site of a Jedi prison known as the Sason Temple. During the Clone Wars, the Jedi Masters Shaak Ti and Aayla Secura visited the Sason Temple and, many years later, during the Imperial Era, the rogue archeologist Chelli Lona Aphra and the Jedi Luke Skywalker traveled to Sason in search of the temple's secrets.
