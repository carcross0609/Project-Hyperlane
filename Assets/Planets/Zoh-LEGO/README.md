# Zoh

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Zoh/LEGO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zoh/LEGO)

Zoh was an unhabited junk planet where Jek-14 settled during the Imperial Era. The Freemakers found Jek-14 in Zoh, and requested him to train Rowan Freemaker in the ways of the Force.
