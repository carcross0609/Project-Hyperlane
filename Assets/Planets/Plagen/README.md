# Plagen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Plagen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plagen)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Plagen`, Expansion Region, grid N-16 — map position (3915.4, -10287.0, -359.5)

Plagen was a planet in the Expansion Region, on the Enarc Run, with high plateaus covered in lakes and grasslands of lush yellow flora; its lowlands were dry plains.

At the junction of the Hydian Way and the Enarc Run the spaceport of TreeLeaf on the Celdas plateau is a modern city bustling with activity. The native short and furry Chikarri live in trees with spherical buildings connected by walkways.
