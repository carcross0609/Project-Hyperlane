# Tansyl 5

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tansyl 5](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tansyl_5)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tansyl 5`, Other, grid K-9 — map position (-922.2, 383.0, 0.9)

Tansyl 5, also known simply as Tansyl, was a planet that was the homeworld of the Frigosian species. Due to its atmosphere being different from that of Takodana, the Frigosians Thromba and Laparo wore breathing apparatuses around where a humanoid nose would be during their time on Takodana.
