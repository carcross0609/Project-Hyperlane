# Arzid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arzid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arzid)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Arzid`, Other, grid T-9 — map position (12211.1, 1145.8, -165.8)

Arzid was a planet in the Calaron sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was inhabited by forests of giant macaab mushrooms, tentacle-bushes, and by the dangerous arachnor that feasted on the mushrooms as part of their diet.

It was also the site of a small Imperial outpost. Death rates among sentries at this outpost, due to arachnors or tentacle bushes, were high enough that being sent there as a sentry was seen as a death sentence.

In 16 ABY, a squad of E4 baron droids overthrew the administration of the only settlement on the planet, setting themselves up as warlords over its population.
