# Hon-Tallos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hon-Tallos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hon-Tallos)

Hon-Tallos was a terrestrial planet in the Inugg system. During the High Republic Era, the Jedi Order operated a temple on Hon-Tallos. Jedi Knight Lily Tora-Asi made several supply runs to Hon-Tallos while she and several other Jedi were stationed on the planet Banchii, assisting the local settlers. Residents of Ta'klah who were evacuated after an Emergence of the Great Hyperspace Disaster were taken to Banchii from Hon-Tallos by Tora-Asi and Jedi Master Arkoff.
