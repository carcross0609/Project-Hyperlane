# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Kenobi and Skywalker)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Kenobi_and_Skywalker%29)

A planet was the site of a battle early in the Clone Wars. Galactic Republic forces led by Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker clashed with the Separatist Droid Army on the world.
