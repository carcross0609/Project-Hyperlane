# Sri Karna

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sri Karna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sri_Karna)

Sri Karna was a planet located in the Cularin system in the Thaere sector of the Expansion Region. Sri Karna was also the homeworld of a humanoid species with antennae, of which Ru Annlord was a member.
