# Shiffrin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shiffrin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shiffrin)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Shiffrin`, Outer Rim, grid T-15 — map position (12646.4, -7643.7, 5.0)

Shiffrin was an Outer Rim Territories planet located in the Tammuz sector. Constable Kallin Bremen maintained law and order in a small village there. At one point, a Shiffrin farmer discovered what turned out to be large deposits of the valuable ore hfredium, and the Galactic Empire subsequently established a garrison there and began confiscating the properties of local landowners. Bremen was eventually executed after planning a peaceful demonstration against the confiscations, and his son, Jak Bremen, went on to leave the planet.
