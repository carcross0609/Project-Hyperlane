# Cianap

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cianap](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cianap)

Cianap was a terrestrial planet in the galaxy. At some point, an advanced society existed on Cianap, but it was destroyed, leaving ruins of cities behind. During the Galactic Civil War, a battle between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance occurred above Cianap, in which Darth Vader was forced to land his TIE Advanced due to a system malfunction.
