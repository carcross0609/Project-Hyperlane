# Indoumodo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Indoumodo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Indoumodo)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Indoumodo`, Wild Space, grid S-18 — map position (10921.0, -13015.0, 718.2)

Indoumodo was a jungle planet located in the Indoumodo system in Wild Space, in grid square S-18 of the Standard Galactic Grid. Venomous insects known as kouhuns were native to the world's swamps, where they hunted prey as large as dogs. Assassins would capture kouhuns to add to their arsenal. The planet's northern hemisphere comprised forests known for their hanava trees and large populations of greddleback termites.
