# Zoh

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zoh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zoh)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Zoh`, Other, grid O-10 — map position (4852.8, -825.4, -105.2)

Zoh was a isolated junk planet located in the Sarka sector of the Mid Rim Territories where Jek-14 took refuge. R0-GR visited the world and recorded his time there in his autobiography From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story.
