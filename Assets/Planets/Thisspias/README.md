# Thisspias

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thisspias](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thisspias)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Thisspias`, Expansion Region, grid O-8 — map position (5267.1, 2611.7, 189.3)

Thisspias was a rocky, temperate planet located in the Thisspias system in the Farstey sector of the Expansion Region. It was the homeworld of the Thisspiasian species, including the Jedi Master Oppo Rancisis. The planet had been a member of the Galactic Republic for a very long time, and was also the site of many important battles, particularly during the Great Manifest Period.

Thisspias was led by the Blood Monarchy, and a Blood Monarch—one Blood Monarch joined the Galactic Republic in the hopes of both securing her reign and expanding Thisspias' economy.
