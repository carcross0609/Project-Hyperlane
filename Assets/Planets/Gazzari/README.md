# Gazzari

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gazzari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gazzari)

Gazzari was a planet that was located in the Grumani sector. Gazzari had a breathable atmosphere, but the planetary surface was inhospitable, due to the presence of volcanoes, tectonic activity and acid rain.
