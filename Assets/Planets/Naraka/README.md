# Naraka

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Naraka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naraka)

Naraka was a rocky and hazy planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. During the Imperial Era, it was controlled by the Galactic Empire, who maintained a prison there. In 2 BBY, the rebel team known as the Spectres traveled to Naraka to rescue the pirate Hondo Ohnaka from the prison.
