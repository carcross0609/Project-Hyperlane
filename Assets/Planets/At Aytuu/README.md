# At Aytuu; (Drawing of the Jewels of the Old Republic, including At Aytuu, depicted)

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [At Aytuu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/At_Aytuu)

At Aytuu was an Outer Rim Territories planet of the Trilon sector that was one of the Jewels of the Old Republic, a group of worlds that were hidden by the Galactic Republic long before approximately 9 ABY. It was depicted on a scroll in a drawing of the Jewels of the Old Republic, and its and the other Jewels' names and coordinates were engraved on pillars in a tower-like structure on the planet At Achrann.
