# Maill

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Maill](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maill)

Maill was a planet located within the Maill system of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet was well-known for having produced one of the rarest coins in the galaxy; the world's King had commissioned the coin in honor of his wife. The coin was minted from a special alloy to have the color of a flaming sunset—to match the color of the lady's hair. The alloy ended up destroying the minting machines and only a few hundred coins were produced.
