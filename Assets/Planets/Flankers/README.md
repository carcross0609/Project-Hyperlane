# Flankers

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flankers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flankers)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Flankers`, Colonies, grid K-9 — map position (-638.3, 718.5, -54.9)

Flankers was a remote planet in Fakir sector in the Colonies. Its star went supernova shortly after the Battle of Yavin, destroying the entire system. Just prior to the explosion, the Imperial garrison on the planet was abandoned.
