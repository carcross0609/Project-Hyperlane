# Bosph

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bosph/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bosph/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bosph`, Outer Rim, grid Q-3 — map position (7792.3, 9746.9, 206.9)

Bosph was the homeworld of the Bosph species. Located in the Bosph sector, it was discovered by the Republic several decades before the rise of the Empire. Due to the Bosphs' Force-sensitivity, Moff Harsh bombarded the planet. The Bosphs went into denial, pretending the bombardment had never occurred; though in reality, the planet was almost completely destroyed. After this, the planet itself was covered in grassy plains broken by the scars of turbolaser fire.
