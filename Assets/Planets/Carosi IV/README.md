# Carosi IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Carosi IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carosi_IV)

Carosi IV was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories destroyed by the expansion of the star Carosi into a red giant. The indigenous Carosites had fled the planet beforehand to Carosi XII.
