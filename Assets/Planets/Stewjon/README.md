# Stewjon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stewjon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stewjon)

Stewjon was a planet in the Deep Core that was the homeworld of Obi-Wan Kenobi, a male human Jedi Master. Between 32 and 29 BBY, Kenobi told his Padawan, Anakin Skywalker, that he did not remember leaving his homeworld. Stewjon was also the homeworld of Tadd Marteens, a farmer who joined the Alliance to Restore the Republic after the Battle of Yavin.
