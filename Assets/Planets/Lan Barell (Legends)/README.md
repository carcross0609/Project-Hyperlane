# Lan Barell

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lan Barell/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lan_Barell/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lan Barell`, Outer Rim — map position (-372.5, 5444.9, 174.8)

Lan Barell was a dusty, dry neutral backwater planet located on the Outer Rim of the galaxy near Breshkall. Heavy iron content in the soil gave the planet a faint blue sheen, and when combined with the red glare of its sun, gave the planet a garish and gloomy atmosphere.
