# Sedri Minor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sedri Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sedri_Minor)

Sedri Minor was an arable farming planet located in the Sedri system of the Periphery, situated in the Outer Rim Territories. An independent colony was founded on Sedri Minor around 244 BBY during the High Republic Era, growing Vratixian barley, a component of the medical substance bacta. After a Nihil attack on a Hutt starship that was carrying Vratixian barley from Sedri Minor, the Jedi Order investigated the planet.
