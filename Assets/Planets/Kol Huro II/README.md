# Kol Huro II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kol Huro II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kol_Huro_II)

Kol Huro II was the second planet of the Kol Huro system. Like other planets in the system, Kol Huro II was harvested for its natural resources during the years leading up to the Battle of Naboo, as the tyrant who controlled the system began mass-producing weapons and battle droids for his own protection.
