# Povanaria

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Povanaria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Povanaria)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Povanaria`, Outer Rim, grid P-6 — map position (7389.1, 5318.2, -332.7)

Povanaria was a world located in the Povanaria system. It was situated in the Loop Worlds area of the Gordian Reach sector within the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The hyperlane known as the Junction-Tierell Loop connected it to the Bronsoon and Durgen's Star systems. Povanaria was a windswept world of low grasses and moss, and it was the homeworld of the Povanarian species, members of which had already traveled offworld centuries prior to 43 ABY.
