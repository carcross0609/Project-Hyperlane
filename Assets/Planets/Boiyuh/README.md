# Boiyuh

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Boiyuh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boiyuh)

Boiyuh was a terrestrial planet in the Boiyuh system within the Colonies and the Interior regions, situated at the coordinates J-9 on the Standard Galactic Grid. It was the homeworld of the human twins Lyttan Dree and Tamu Dree, who were so hungry that they chose to enlist into the Galactic Empire's navy, joining the Imperial Naval Academy on the planet Carida by 10 BBY.
