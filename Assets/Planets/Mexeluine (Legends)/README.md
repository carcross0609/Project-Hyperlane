# Mexeluine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mexeluine/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mexeluine/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mexeluine`, Outer Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-1345.6, -12537.0, 203.9)

Mexeluine was a thriving trade world lying along the Corellian Trade Spine in the Anoat sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Originally settled during the Galactic Republic's efforts to harvest the economic potential of the Greater Javin region centuries before the Galactic Civil War, Mexeluine grew wealthy as a result of Figg & Associates' commercial development of the region. Mexeluine successfully positioned itself as a cheaper alternative to neighboring Gerrenthum, the region's commercial capital.
