# Hettitite

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hettitite](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hettitite)

Hettitite was a planet that was located within the star system of the same name, a part of the Dalonbian sector in the Outer Rim Territories region of the galaxy. It was the homeworld of a sentient Near-Human species that had pale skin, white hair, and red eyes.

At some point, Hettitite became threatened by a supernova, and in 365 BBY, members of the native species colonized the planet Sernpidal, eventually becoming known as Sernpidalans.
