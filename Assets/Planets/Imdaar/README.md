# Imdaar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imdaar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imdaar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Imdaar`, Outer Rim, grid J-6 — map position (-1986.9, 5394.7, 234.9)

Imdaar was a terrestrial world located in the Imdaar system within the Dantus sector of the Outer Rim. It was situated on grid square J-6 on the Standard Galactic Grid. During the Imperial Era, Imdaar housed a supply base of the Galactic Empire.

In 0 ABY, after the Rebel Alliance had destroyed the Empire's Death Star superweapon, the Alliance carried out a bombing of the supply base on Imdaar. Later that year, while in a meeting of Rebel Alliance leaders, Admiral Gial Ackbar used the bombing on Imdaar as an example of the Alliance's actions following the Death Star's destruction after Princess Leia Organa stated her belief the Rebel Alliance was not doing enough in their fight against the Empire.
