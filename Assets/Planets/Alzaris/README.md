# Alzaris

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Alzaris](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alzaris)

A small and ingrate planet, Alzaris had a very low population, concentrated around a very small open field which served as a spaceport and some massive greenhouses. The planet is constantly bathed in the reddish light of its dying star.

Although the atmosphere is extremely wet but breathable, there is arid portions on the planet, which are used for crops.

A big part of the production is sold to the Imperial artificial satellite in orbit around Rama.
