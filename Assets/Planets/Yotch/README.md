# Yotch

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yotch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yotch)

Yotch was a gas giant orange-red in color located in the Esseveya system of the Mid Rim Territories that, according to Captain Oswin Dicto, was a failed star. The planet was covered in rheoscopic fluid that gave Yotch its colorful effect from space. By 4 ABY, the planet had begun to die and the star cruiser Halcyon circled over the planet for a few hours during its first voyage after being liberated from the Galactic Empire.
