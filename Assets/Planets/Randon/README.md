# Randon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Randon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Randon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Randon`, Mid Rim, grid Q-10 — map position (7896.9, -523.3, 213.1)

Randon was a planet located in the Randon system in the Mytaranor sector of the Mid Rim Territories, at the confluence of a number of hyperspace routes. The Ootmian Pabol ran through Randon, and three routes began at the world: the Trax Tube, the Pabol Sleheyron and the Randon Run.

In 33 BBY, the Idiot Three were dropped off on Randon by Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi after attempting to hijack the Regal Zephyr.
