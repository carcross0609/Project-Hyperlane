# Onuf

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Onuf](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Onuf)

Onuf was a gas giant–type planet with five moons. It was located in the Rannon system, a part of the Instrop sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet occupied the fourth and outermost orbit around its star, the yellow-white Rannon.
