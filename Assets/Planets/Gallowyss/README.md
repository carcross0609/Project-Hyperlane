# Gallowyss

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gallowyss](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gallowyss)

Gallowyss was a planet located in the Anoat sector. After Grand Moff Ubrik Adelhard was betrayed by his advisor, Reyna Oskure, he escaped alongside Commander Bragh and several stormtroopers aboard an escape pod that crashed onto Gallowyss. Adelhard and his forces survived, remaining on the planet awaiting rescue by a Lambda-class shuttle. The Jedi Luke Skywalker and the New Republic pilot Rynn Zenat visited Gallowyss in search of Adelhard, though he managed to escape.
