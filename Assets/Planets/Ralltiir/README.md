# Ralltiir

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ralltiir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ralltiir)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ralltiir`, Core Worlds, grid L-9 — map position (1116.4, 657.5, -18.2)

Ralltiir was a terrestrial planet located in the Core Worlds' Darpa sector, in the Ralltiir system. During the Clone Wars, Lieutenant Commander Orson Krennic of the Galactic Republic financed a few weeks of vacation on the planet for the smuggler Has Obitt's former crew members as compensation for replacing them.

Ralltiir later served as a safe world for Senator Mon Mothma's organized rebellion against the Galactic Empire, which was eventually formalized as the Rebel Alliance. In 1 BBY, the Empire began an extended occupation of the planet, often using violence against civilians. The planet was also the homeworld of General Pharl McQuarrie and the pilot Derek Klivian, who were human members of the Alliance.
