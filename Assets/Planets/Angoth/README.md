# Angoth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Angoth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Angoth)

Angoth was a small planet that featured woods and a ring of scattered asteroids. During the High Republic Era, a settlement existed on Angoth, and the Nihil marauders also established a base in a former detention center for smugglers. The Jedi Order and Republic Defense Coalition embarked on a mission to capture one of the Nihil's Nameless creatures from the base on Angoth.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Angoth
- **Class:** Terrestrial
- **Atmosphere:** Type I (breathable)
- **Terrain:** Woods
- **Interest:**

  - A settlement
- **Flora:** Trees
