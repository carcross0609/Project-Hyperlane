# Ciutric IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ciutric IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ciutric_IV)

Ciutric IV, commonly known just as Ciutric, was the capital planet of the Ciutric Hegemony. The planet had at least two moons, and was subject to frequent yet brief periods of torrential downpour.

It was an industrialized world with a small shipyard, and was located at a hub of trade routes.
