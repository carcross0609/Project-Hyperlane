# Megalox Beta

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Megalox Beta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Megalox_Beta)

Megalox Beta was a planet in the Expansion Region that housed a prison known as Megalox, which Poe Dameron and Black Squadron visited during their search for Lor San Tekka. The planet had a high gravity and was considered inhospitable, except for a small gravity field-covered zone that housed the Megalox prison. The planet was the site of a brief skirmish between the Resistance and the First Order.
