# Ningoth

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Ningoth/LEGO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ningoth/LEGO)

Ningoth was a remote ocean planet inhabited by Opee sea killers. The bottom of Ningoth's oceans were covered in rock spires. During the Imperial Era, one of the Kyber Saber crystals was hidden there until it was recovered by Rowan Freemaker, who then lost it to the Imperial agent Naare.
