# Uviuy Exen

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Uviuy Exen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uviuy_Exen)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Uviuy Exen`, Colonies, grid L-9 — map position (829.2, 1337.8, -114.3)

Uviuy Exen was a world located in the Uviuy Exen system of the Shwuy sector, a part of the Colonies and the Interior. It was situated in grid square L-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid. The super-hyperroute known as the Hydian Way linked Uviuy Exen to the Brentaal system in the Core Worlds as well as to the Inner Rim Territories' Champala system. In addition, it was connected to Palanhi system by the Shwuy Exchange hyperspace route.

In 9 ABY, Uviuy Exen fell within the borders of the New Republic. Around the same year, while the astronomer Kh'ymm was searching her archive of worlds on the Observatory Moon in order to determine the potential location of the lost planet At Attin, Uviuy Exen's name was listed on her computer screen among other worlds.
