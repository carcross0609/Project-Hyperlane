# Quian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quian)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Quian`, Core Worlds, grid L-9 — map position (1205.8, 1193.2, 44.9)

Quian was a terrestrial planet located in the Quian system of the Core Worlds. It was home to a Human population known as the Quians.

The Quian Benald Orlan was born at some point no earlier than 20 BBY in a family that included his Quian parents, Karin Orlan and her husband, as well as several younger siblings. By the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, Quian was under the control of the Empire, and the Orlan family resided at a household there.

At some point, Benald sold out his family to the local Imperial authorities and he went on to study at the off-world Imperial University of Marngar III. Quian's role in Benald's biography was eventually noted by the New Republic General Airen Cracken in an entry of the datafile Wanted by Cracken—the initial version of which was published in 7 ABY—dedicated to Benald.
