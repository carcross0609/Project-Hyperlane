# Ceitia Five

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ceitia Five](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ceitia_Five)

Ceitia Five was a planet located within the Mid Rim. In 138 ABY, Darth Wredd attacked and killed a One Sith member who had infiltrated the legislature of the Galactic Federation Triumvirate on the planet.
