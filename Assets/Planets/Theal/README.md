# Theal

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Theal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Theal)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Theal`, Mid Rim, grid J-15 — map position (-1741.3, -7997.1, 274.3)

Theal was a planet situated in the Theal system, a part of the freestanding subsectors portion of the Mid Rim. It was an inhospitable world that was explored by the Republic Survey Corps scout Barosa Warren. More than fifty years later, by 2 ABY, Theal was controlled by the Galactic Empire and was considered to be one of the most dangerous planets under the Empire's jurisdiction.
