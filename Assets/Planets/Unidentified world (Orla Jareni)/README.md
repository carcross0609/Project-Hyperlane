# Unidentified world (Orla Jareni)

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified world (Orla Jareni)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_world_%28Orla_Jareni%29)

During her time as a Jedi Padawan Orla Jareni undertook a mission to a world known for its beautifully realistic scuplture. The worlds artists crafted statues that were so lifelike they seemed to have softness, texture, and spirit. Jareni recalled the statues when she looked upon the husked remains of fellow Jedi Knight Regald Coll after he had been killed aboard Starlight Beacon by a Nameless creature.
