# Tinoon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tinoon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tinoon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tinoon`, Outer Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-991.0, -13113.7, -12.8)

Tinoon was a planet located in the Anoat sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Months into the blockade of the sector by the Galactic Empire, a Rebel cargo filled with foodstuffs and armor was impounded at the Tinoon spaceport. With the help of the Smuggler, the Rebels sliced through the Imperial security system to recover their ship and their supplies.
