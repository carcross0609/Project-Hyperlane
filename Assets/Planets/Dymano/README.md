# Dymano

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Dymano](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dymano)

Dymano was a planet on which a circus was held. The planet was habitable for humans. At some point between 19 BBY and 2 BBY, the former Sith Lord Maul—disguised as the Sith Lord Darth Vader—took a squad of Imperial stormtroopers to the planet. On Dynamo, the squad stole string lights and red lipstick from the circus while Maul held a clown with pie on its face in the air using the Force. One of the stormtroopers, who desired to visit the planet Felucia, commented in disappointment that Dymano was not Felucia.
