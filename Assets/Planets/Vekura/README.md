# Vekura

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vekura](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vekura)

Vekura was a planet in the Syrinx system in the Outer Rim Territories that was mostly covered in rocky floor and covered only with a sparse vegetation of colorful trees and grasses and simple stone buildings. It was also the homeworld of the Vekurans. At some point in the late Republic Era, there was a war between Vekura and the neighboring planet Syrinx Prime and Vekura that later had the Jedi evolved which helped end the conflict between the two worlds. During the war, Vekura was seemingly lead by the General Lafco.
