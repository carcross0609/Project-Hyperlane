# Plin Minor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Plin Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plin_Minor)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Plin Minor`, Other, grid L-13 — map position (196.9, -5506.0, -21.8)

Plin Minor, also known as Plin, was a terrestrial planet in the Plin Minor system of the Inner Rim Territories that was home to the Plinovian species. The Solodoe wished to terraform the planet, which would damage the Plinovians' natural habitat. The two species entered into negotiations on Station Zeta, which ended when the Jedi diplomat Arath Tarrex stole the seal of office of the Solodoe ambassador Ketas, who accused the Plinovian ambassador of the crime. Angered, the Plinovian ambassador self-combusted, causing an explosion. Ketas refused to continue the negotiations, determined to continue terraforming.
