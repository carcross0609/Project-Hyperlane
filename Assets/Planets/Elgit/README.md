# Elgit

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Elgit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elgit)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Elgit`, Hutt Space, grid T-10 — map position (12081.8, -1161.4, -89.9)

Elgit was a minor trade world that was located within the Elgit system of the galaxy's Hutt Space region and lay on the hyperlane called the Dead Road, which connected it to the planets of Zisia and Usk. The Elgit–M'Hanna Corridor linked Elgit to the world of Sakifwanna, and the trade planet was also joined by a hyperspace route to the Gos Hutta system.

Elgit served as a clearinghouse for goods that were reserved for the Bootana Hutta region of the Hutt Space, and the hyperlanes leading to Elgit were periodically patrolled by the fleet of Troonol, a member of the Hutt species.
