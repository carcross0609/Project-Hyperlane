# Sorgal 12

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sorgal 12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorgal_12)

Sorgal 12 was an Outer Rim mining planet located in the Mieru'kar sector. The engineer Vharn and his family hailed from Sorgal 12, with the family becoming enslaved by the occupying Galactic Empire. Vharn joined the Rebel Alliance but was killed in 3 ABY by bounty hunter Beilert Valance following a plot to betray the rebels. Valance instructed for half his payment to be used to free Vharm's family on Sorgal 12.
