# Pesitiin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pesitiin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pesitiin)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Pesitiin`, Expansion Region, grid I-8 — map position (-3231.3, 2455.8, -111.0)

Pesitiin was an Expansion Region gas giant in the Pesitiin system with perpetual atmospheric storms. Several years before 400 BBY its upper atmosphere housed a Bosken & Bosken mining center which attempted to harvest the planet's gases for profit, but it proved to be an unsustainable business due to the high cost of maintaining a floating mining center, leading to the abandonment of the outpost less then a decade after it was built.
