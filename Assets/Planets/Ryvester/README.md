# Ryvester

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ryvester](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ryvester)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ryvester`, Outer Rim, grid R-7 — map position (10344.2, 4090.0, 181.4)

Ryvester was a planet in the Meridian sector. Imperial Admiral Kosh Teradoc was presumed dead on the planet during a Wraith Squadron, a New Republic commando unit, mission in 13 ABY. Teradoc kept a personal yacht in the bay to the east of his naval base which the Wraiths stole after their mission.
