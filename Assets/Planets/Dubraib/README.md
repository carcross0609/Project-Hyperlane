# Dubraib

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dubraib](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dubraib)

Dubraib was a waterworld in the Cegul sector's Dubraib system, located in the Outer Rim Territories and the Western Reaches, It was situated in grid square M-20 of the Standard Galactic Grid. During the High Republic Era, the Galactic Republic designated a section of the Outer Rim—which included Dubraib—as the Galactic Frontier, worlds considered far removed enough from the galaxy's center to send explorers to. In 231 BBY, the Jedi Master Gil Jaretto traveled to the Starlight Beacon space station from Dubraib.
