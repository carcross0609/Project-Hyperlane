# Disim

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Disim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disim)

Disim was a planet in the Epidimi system, discovered 5000 BBY by the Republic, which crossbred its native avians with those of Iri, resulting in the Tarongs, whom drove their mother species on both worlds extinct.
