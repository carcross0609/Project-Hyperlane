# Aria Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aria Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aria_Prime)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Aria Prime`, Other, grid P-16 — map position (6851.6, -9412.4, 82.3)

Aria Prime was a planet in the Aria Prime system of the Inner Rim Territories that was where Lyra Erso was born around 48 BBY. Lyra's mother lived and worked on Aria Prime as an artist. Due to the Clone Wars and her mother's failing health, Lyra suggested to her husband Galen Erso that they relocate to Aria Prime from Coruscant for the duration of the conflict.
