# Tyan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tyan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tyan)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tyan`, Mid Rim, grid K-7 — map position (-68.9, 4442.5, 92.2)

Tyan was a Mid Rim planet located in the Tyan sector. By 76 BBY, it was represented in the Galactic Republic by Senator Galim Eddawan, whose cruiser was captured by the pirate Lorian Nod, with Eddawan not being seen again afterward. In 12 ABY, Tyan was a member of the New Republic and was the site of a battle of the Galactic Civil War.
