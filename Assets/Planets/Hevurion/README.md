# Hevurion

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hevurion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hevurion)

Hevurion was an member world of the New Republic about thirty years after the Battle of Endor. Located in the Hevurion system in the Inner Rim Territories, it was situated along the Hydian Way southwest of Bogden. Erudo Ro-Kiintor was the senior senator from Hevurion to the Galactic Senate but used his position to collude with the First Order. The snowtrooper commander TX-8080 hailed from Hevurion.
