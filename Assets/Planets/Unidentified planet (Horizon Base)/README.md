# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Horizon Base)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Horizon_Base%29)

A planet located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories was covered in a shallow sea of crystal clear water that featured coral growth. Conquered by the Galactic Empire, it was the site of Horizon Base, a vital Imperial shipping port and refueling station.
