# Argus

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Argus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Argus)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Argus`, Mid Rim, grid P-16 — map position (7230.0, -9236.2, 612.7)

Argus was a Human-inhabited planet in the Argus system. When the New Order was instituted, Argus's Celestial School of Spaceflight Engineering was closed down. At least one of the school's professors, Maggis, became a teacher at the Imperial Naval Academy on Imperial Center, rather than be blacklisted from ever teaching again.
