# Ord Dalet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Dalet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Dalet)

Ord Dalet was an Outer Rim Territories planet located in the Sertar sector. It was a Galactic Republic Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost established no earlier than the time of the Old Sith Wars and was named after the letter "dalet" from the Common Sith writing system. Around 3638 BBY, a member of the Shi'ido species became hospitalized on Ord Dalet.
