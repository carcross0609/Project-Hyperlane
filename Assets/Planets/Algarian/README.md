# Algarian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Algarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Algarian)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Algarian`, Mid Rim, grid Q-15 — map position (8704.7, -8912.1, -31.5)

Algarian was a planet in the Mid Rim's Dufilvian sector. It was raided by the Galactic Empire, which was attempting to defeat all Alliance support on-planet.

Algarian was well known for its huge and complex governing bureaucracy. No other information was publicly available without submission of a Form AA-23-7022 and a 150 credit processing fee to the Algarian Bureau of Planetary Information and Tourism.
