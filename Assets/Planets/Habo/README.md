# Habo

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Habo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Habo)

Habo was an inhabited planet situated in the Outer Rim Territories. The residents of Habo had a reputation for being shy, and visitors were not common to the planet. After an elderly dark-side Force user arrived on the planet, the Jedi Master Tajin Crosser and his Padawan, Dan G'vash, investigated the source of a disturbance in the Force on Habo. While Crosser entered Habo village to talk with the locals, G'vash eventually ventured into the mountains, where he encountered the elder. Master and apprentice then defeated him in a lightsaber duel.

The planet's inhabitants were mostly engaged in agriculture, and some had converted the mountains surrounding their villages completely into ziggurat-shaped farm terraces. The typical farmers on the planet lived in pit-houses of wooden and earthen construction covered in a tent-like canopy of cloth. One local dish was called Oon-Doon.
