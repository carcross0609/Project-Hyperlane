# Ovise V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ovise V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ovise_V)

Ovise V was a planet in the Ovise system of the Moddell sector. A gas giant and a known source of tibanna gas, Ovise V was orbited by several inhabited moons, including Midakos and Midakinos.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Ovise V
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories; Inner Zuma Region
- **Sector:** Zuma sector; Moddell sector; Inner Moddell systems
- **System:** Ovise system
- **Coordinates:** H-16
- **Moons:**
  - Midakos
  - Midakinos
