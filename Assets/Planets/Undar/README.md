# Undar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Undar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Undar)

Undar was an airless terrestrial planet in the Dagobah system, located in the Outer Rim Territories' Sluis sector. It occupied the third orbital position around the star Darlo and had two moons. Along with the rest of the star system, Undar was situated on the hyperlane known as the Rimma Trade Route.
