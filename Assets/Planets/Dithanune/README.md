# Dithanune

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dithanune](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dithanune)

Dithanune was a planet located in the Dithanune system, a part of the Bortele sector in the Slice portion of the Mid Rim. It was home to the lethal predator known as the needle beast, which the Defel miner and undercover Rebel Alliance spy and saboteur Thar'quan once encountered by the time of the Galactic Civil War.
