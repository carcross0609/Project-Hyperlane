# Atorra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Atorra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atorra)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Atorra`, Outer Rim, grid Q-5 — map position (8098.9, 6760.8, -495.1)

Atorra was a planet located in the Atorra system. It was situated in the Back Reach area of the Gordian Reach sector, within the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet was connected by the Ardan Cross hyperlane to the Troos and the Chenowei systems.

At some point, members of the sentient B'trillan species immigrated to Atorra, giving rise to a sparsely populated agricultural colony. By 43 ABY, however, decades of drought had ruined the colony. By that time, it was rumored that an ancient battle had resulted in the crash-landing of alien starships in Atorra's badlands.
