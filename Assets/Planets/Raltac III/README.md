# Raltac III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Raltac III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raltac_III)

Raltac III was a planet located within the Raltac system of the Inner Rim Territories. A band of criminals under the leadership of Grintlok had a safe house located in on the planet.
