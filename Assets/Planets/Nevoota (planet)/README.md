# Nevoota

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nevoota (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nevoota_%28planet%29)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Nevoota`, Colonies — map position (1902.6, 441.8, 20.3)

Nevoota was a planet of the Balmorra system, in the Colonies. It was the homeworld of the Nevoota, a species of sentient bees wiped out by the Mandalorians in 4024 BBY.
