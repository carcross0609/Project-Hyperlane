# Joetz

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Joetz](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Joetz)

Joetz was a snowy planet where, during the Imperial Era, rebels were based. The rebel princess Crane and her droid Tor-Tu investigated the Galactic Empire's movements on the planet.
