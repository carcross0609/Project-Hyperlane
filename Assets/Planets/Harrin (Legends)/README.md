# Harrin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Harrin/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harrin/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Harrin`, Inner Rim, grid L-15 — map position (1223.7, -7532.4, -140.7)

Harrin was a planet located in the Inner Rim sector of the same name. It was situated on the Corellian Trade Spine super-hyperroute and served as the endpoint of the Harrin Trade Corridor hyperlane.

Harrin was a member of the Galactic Republic since approximately 12,000 BBY; however, it was aligned with the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars. By 17 BBY, Harrin was a member world of the Galactic Empire.
