# Hyrol Preen Beta

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hyrol Preen Beta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyrol_Preen_Beta)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Hyrol Preen Beta`, Outer Rim — map position (2825.6, -12115.2, -172.5)

Hyrol Preen Beta was a planet in the Outer Rim. Its populace was wiped out by Imperial Storm commandos and ZQ infantry droids because of suspected Rebel activity. A spacer, Johin, brought news of the devastation.
