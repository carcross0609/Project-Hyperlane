# Stieg

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stieg](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stieg)

Stieg was a planet in the Outer Rim. It was populated by the Stieg-Fan, who ran a peaceful government. During the planet Kegan's isolationist period, The Learning taught that Stieg was a dangerous place to visit, surrounded in war and without any governing body.
