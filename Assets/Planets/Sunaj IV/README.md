# Sunaj IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sunaj IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sunaj_IV)

Sunaj IV was a planet in the Sunaj system. The Relentless patrolled the vicinity of Sunaj IV prior to the Battle of Hoth.

It was while receiving service near Sunaj IV that the Juvaini was destroyed by Rebel starfighters.
