# Merisee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Merisee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merisee/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Merisee`, Outer Rim — map position (-685.8, -17309.2, -265.4)

Merisee, was an agriworld in the Elrood sector, located along the Elrood-Derilyn Trade Route. It was known as the "Breadbasket of Elrood Sector."

First contact between the indigenous Meris and Teltiors occurred in an event called the Joining, and the two races fought a war for most of the next millennium. The planet was eventually discovered by the Galactic Republic.

Tozzin Foods and the Utozz Prime Brewery operated on the planet. The Loag, an ancient assassins' cult that produced the Loag Dagger, once flourished on Merisee.
