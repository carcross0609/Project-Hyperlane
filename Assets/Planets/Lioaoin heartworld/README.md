# Lioaoin heartworld

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lioaoin heartworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lioaoin_heartworld)

The Lioaoin heartworld, also known as Lioaoi, was a planet home to the Lioaoi and the capital of the Lioaoin Regime. It was situated near a gravity well. From space, it appeared green, blue, and white. It was active with freighters, docking and repair stations, couriers, and several space stations were situated in its orbit, including two bluedocks. The entire system was guarded by military patrol ships.
