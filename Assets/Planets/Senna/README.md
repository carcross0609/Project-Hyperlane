# Senna

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Senna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senna)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Senna`, Outer Rim, grid T-10 — map position (12435.1, -666.7, 420.6)

Senna was a planet known for its lava and heat. It was located in the Kessel system within the Kessel sector of the Outer Rim Territories and The Slice in grid square T-10 of the Standard Galactic Grid. In 9 ABY, during an interview session for the competitive sports show Hunters of the Outer Rim, the human Hunter Sentinel shared his past as a stormtrooper of the Galactic Empire. In his reminiscing, he claimed to have fought and prevailed over a Fireworm on a lava planet—although he was unsure if it was on Senna or another lava planet, Mustafar—by shoving a Permafrost crystal in the creature's maw. Later Imara Vex, another competitor in the show, came in and warned fans of the dubious legitimacy of Sentinel's stories.
