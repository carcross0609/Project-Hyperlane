# Tantive V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tantive V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tantive_V)

Tantive V was a planet in the Tantive system of Kwymar sector. It was the location of the Battle of Tantive V, after the Battle of Endor. The New Republic won the battle, causing the retreat of Gaen Drommel to Soullex.
