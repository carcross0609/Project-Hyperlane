# Arbooine

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arbooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arbooine)

Arbooine was an Outer Rim Territories celestial body located in the Arboo sector. It was home to the venomous arboreal forest predator known as the bearsloth, which posed a danger to any visitors to Arbooine. Nevertheless, the creatures and their venom led to several individuals traveling to the celestial body.
