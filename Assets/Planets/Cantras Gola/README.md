# Cantras Gola

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cantras Gola](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cantras_Gola)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Cantras Gola`, Outer Rim, grid J-4 — map position (-2307.4, 7589.1, 116.9)

Cantras Gola was a planet in the Perinn sector. It was a corporate planet controlled by PowerOn Conglomerate. During the Thrawn campaign, it decided to leave the Pentastar Alignment and join the New Republic. The Alignment threatened to kill the children of the ambassador of Cantras Gola if he had any sort of meeting with the New Republic. The children had been taken by slavers to Gabredor III. They were rescued by a Red Moons mercenary grup, allowing Cantras Gola to resume joining the New Republic.
