# Gloon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gloon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gloon)

Gloon, also known as Gloova, was a planet located in the Gloon system. The hot, humid world was the homeworld of the Gloovan species, including the pirate Muglan Tarantyne and her husband Straan Valgar.
