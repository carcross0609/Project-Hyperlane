# Abelor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Abelor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abelor)

Abelor was a terrestrial planet located in the Abelor system, within the Yushan sector of the Mid Rim and the Western Reaches regions, along the Corellian Trade Spine. It was situated in grid square K-17 of the Standard Galactic Grid.

By 3 ABY, the Abelor survivalist pants, Abelor survivalist belt, and Abelor survivalist vest were produced on the planet and available for sale throughout the galaxy. At some point between 33 ABY and 34 ABY, the smuggler Damar Olin encountered the smuggler Ana Tolla and her crew after he and his partner Izal Garsea became stranded with no fuel, food, or contacts in a dusty port on Abelor. In 34 ABY, historian and author Eloc Throno included a map depicting the location of Abelor in his book Traveler's Guide to Batuu, which detailed the planet Batuu.
