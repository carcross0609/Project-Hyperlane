# Unidentified Outer Rim planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified Outer Rim planet (Jenra)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Outer_Rim_planet_%28Jenra%29)

A planet was located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. In order to get away from the service of the Galactic Empire, Jenra had spent two decades sending money in small amounts, so it wouldn't attract attention, to an untraceable account on this planet.
