# Yulant

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yulant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yulant)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Yulant`, Core Worlds, grid L-10 — map position (516.3, -243.7, -10.4)

Yulant was an agricultural planet in the Core Worlds owned by the Salliche Agricultural Corporation who sued Dasobo Meats, a meat shipping company based on the planet. It was one of the worlds on the Ag Circuit. Approximately six months after the Battle of Endor, workers here revolted against increased Imperial control.
