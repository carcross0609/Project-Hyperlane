# Tierell

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tierell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tierell)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tierell`, Outer Rim, grid O-6 — map position (5332.0, 5658.0, -1.7)

Tierell was a planet located in the Tierell system within the Thesme sector of the Outer Rim Territories.

Located at the intersection of the Hydian Way and the Junction-Tierell Loop, Tierell and the planet Feriae Junction were the two ports that serve most traffic coming and going from the Loop Worlds of the Gordian Reach.
