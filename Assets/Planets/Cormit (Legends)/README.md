# Cormit

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cormit/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cormit/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Cormit`, Unknown Regions, grid F-8 — map position (-8366.0, 1677.6, 424.1)

Cormit was a planet of the Chiss Ascendancy in the Unknown Regions. Cormit was located near the border of the Ascendancy's core territory in the Cormit system and at the intersection of two hyperlanes, the Vagaari Corridor and the Phalanx Route. The planet was the location of one of the two major Chiss Expansionary Defense Force bases.
