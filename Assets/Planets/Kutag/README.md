# Kutag

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kutag](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kutag)

Kutag, also known as Doornik-881, was an Imperial factory farm world that was maintained by droids. It was in the Doornik-881 system in the Koornacht Cluster. Its inhabitants were killed in the Great Purge by the Yevetha, who took over the planet.
