# Helikoprion

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Helikoprion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Helikoprion)

Helikoprion was a waterworld in the Helikoprion system in the Nilgaard sector of the Outer Rim Territories that was home to the Heliko species. After the planet was engulfed in a civil war, the Delik family, who resided on Helikoprion, moved off-world.
