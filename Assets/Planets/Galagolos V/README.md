# Galagolos V

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Galagolos V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galagolos_V)

Galagolos V, also known simply as Galagalos, was a swampy and stinky planet located in the galaxy's Colonies region, in grid square J-9 on the Standard Galactic Grid, that was the homeworld of the Cloddogran Bargwill Tomder. The pirate Hondo Ohnaka once crash-landed a ship in a spaceport on Galagolos V during the reign of the Galactic Empire.
