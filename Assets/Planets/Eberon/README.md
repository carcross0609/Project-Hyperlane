# Eberon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eberon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eberon)

Eberon was a planet located in the Eberon system of the Mid Rim's Mytaranor sector. Around the time of the Great Galactic War, Eberon was engaged in the Eberon–Kashyyyk War with the nearby planet Kashyyyk, but the conflict was eventually ended by the personal intervention of the Jedi Satele Shan.
