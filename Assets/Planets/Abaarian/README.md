# Abaarian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Abaarian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abaarian)

Abaarian was a planet in the Abaarian system in the Mid Rim. The world joined the Galactic Alliance in the wake of the Yuuzhan Vong War, shipping bottled water to needy planets. Several of these water shipments were hijacked during the Dark Nest Crisis. The Jedi High Council dispatched Jedi Knights Tesar Sebatyne and Lowbacca to investigate.
