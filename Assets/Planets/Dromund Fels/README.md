# Dromund Fels

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dromund Fels](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Fels)

Dromund Fels was an arid planet that occupied the fourth orbit around the star Dromund in the Dromund system, a star system in the Sith Worlds within the Outer Rim Territories's Esstran sector. Orbited by one moon, the planet was under the control of the Old Sith Empire before its collapse, and the reconstituted Empire carried out a number of archaeological excavations on Dromund Fels thousands of years later.
