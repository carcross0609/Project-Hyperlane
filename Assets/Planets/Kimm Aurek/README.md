# Kimm Aurek

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kimm Aurek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kimm_Aurek)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kimm Aurek`, Mid Rim, grid L-18 — map position (300.7, -12055.0, 529.4)

Kimm Aurek was a dingy industrial world located in the Kimm Aurek system, one of the Kimm systems of the Juvex sector, which was itself a part of the Senex-Juvex region in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. It lay on the hyperlane known as the Senex-Juvex Loop, which connected it to the Juvex and Kimm Besh systems. Similarly to its Senex-Juvex Loop neighbors Kimm Besh and Kimm Cresh, Kimm Aurek was under the control of House Sreethyn, one of the aristocratic Ancient Houses of the region.
