# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Dona Laza)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Dona_Laza%29)

This unidentified planet was located in the Dona Laza sector of the Expansion Region, at a distance of approximately twenty-eight hours of hyperspace travel away from Tangrene. It hosted the Dona Laza tapcafe and the Mumbri Storve cantina.
