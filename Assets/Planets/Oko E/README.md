# Oko E

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oko E](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oko_E)

Oko E was a resort world in the Oko system. It was known for its "wild-water" rafting on rivers of molten sulfur. The cyborg Lobot partook in this activity, likening it to maneuvers he performed on the elusive Teljkon Vagabond ship with Lando Calrissian in 16 ABY.
