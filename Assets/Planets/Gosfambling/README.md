# Gosfambling

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gosfambling](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gosfambling)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gosfambling`, Mid Rim — map position (7083.6, -856.8, 45.4)

Gosfambling was a planet in the Mid Rim, and was the homeworld of the Gosfamblings. C-Gosf represented the world in the New Republic Senate.

Cybot Galactica stopped the production of their LIN demolitionmech autonomous minelayer after an incident on Gosfamnling. A detonite charge exploded while the LIN unit was handling it, causing a cave-in. Six hundred miners were trapped and suffocated to death.
