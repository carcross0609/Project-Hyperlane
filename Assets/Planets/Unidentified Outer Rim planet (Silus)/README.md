# Unidentified Outer Rim planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Outer Rim planet (Silus)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Outer_Rim_planet_%28Silus%29)

A planet was located in the Outer Rim Territories, far from the galactic capital, Coruscant. It hosted a gladiatorial ring, in which fighters competed to the death, with the winner collecting credits. Spectators gambled over the outcome. The Drovian Silus killed several challengers, including the Aleena Tabor and his brother, before he was in turn defeated by Darth Maul, Sith apprentice to Darth Sidious.
