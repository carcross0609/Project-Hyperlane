# Cyrkon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cyrkon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyrkon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Cyrkon`, Outer Rim, grid O-12 — map position (5708.9, -3437.8, -283.9)

Cyrkon was a toxic planet located in the Outer Rim Territories, on the edge of Hutt Space. In the aftermath of the Battle of Yavin, Lieutenant Caluan Ematt of the Alliance to Restore the Republic went into hiding on Cyrkon after escaping an ambush by the Galactic Empire on Taanab.
