# Tatrang V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tatrang V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tatrang_V)

Tatrang V was the fifth planet of the Tatrang system, located in the Tammuz sector of the Outer Rim. It was a desolate mountainous world with two moons.
