# Gryphon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gryphon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gryphon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gryphon`, Inner Rim — map position (3551.5, 2726.8, 89.6)

Gryphon was a mountainous planet located in the Inner Rim, where war among its clans had long been a way of life. During the rise of the Galactic Empire, the native Brock Lar had been the highest-ranking warrior in his clan, but after the death of his youngest son, he realized that there was no hope for peace. Around 0 BBY, he chose to escape the clutches of death and seek out a peaceful world where his family could begin a new life. The people of Alderaan listened to Brock’s story and welcomed his family with open arms.
