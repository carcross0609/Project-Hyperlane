# Unidentified blue marble planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified blue marble planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_blue_marble_planet)

A blue-hued planet was located near the Hydian Way, not far from the planet Bandomeer. A stormy world, the planet appeared to be blue in color from space, while its atmosphere had white and gray clouds. It had five moons and was the homeworld of the silver-scaled draigon.
