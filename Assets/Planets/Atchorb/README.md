# Atchorb

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Atchorb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atchorb)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Atchorb`, Outer Rim, grid S-3 — map position (11011.3, 9170.7, -611.4)

Atchorb was a world located in the Atchorb system of the Corporate Sector, a part of the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The hyperlane known as the Shaltin Tunnels linked Atchorb to the D'ian and Ulicia systems, while the Authority Guardian Corridor connected it to the Hiit and Tothis systems.

Atchorb was a trade world, albeit one of middling importance despite vast prior investments. That changed, however, when the Corporate Sector Authority began shipping military units onto the world, which used it as staging grounds for operations in Wild Space areas northward of the Corporate Sector's borders, such as Trianii Space. As a result, Atchorb experienced an economic boom.
