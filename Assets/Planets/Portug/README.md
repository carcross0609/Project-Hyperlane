# Portug

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Portug](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Portug)

Portug was a planet located in the Outer Rim region of the galaxy. The Tusken Raider KkH'Oar'Rrhr and his followers once visited this planet in search of his revered leader A'sharad Hett, after his exile from Tatooine.
