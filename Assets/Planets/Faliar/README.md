# Faliar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Faliar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Faliar)

Faliar was a terrestrial planet in the Fondor system, located in the Freeworlds Territory portion of the Tapani sector, within the Southern Core portion of the Colonies. Along with the rest of its star system, Faliar was located on the hyperspace route known as the Gandeal-Fondor Hyperlane, with a hyperlane also connecting it to the Sefon system. The planet occupied the first orbital position around the star Fondor and had no moons. Faliar featured a hot climate.
