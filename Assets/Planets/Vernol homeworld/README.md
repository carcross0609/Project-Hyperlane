# Vernol homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vernol homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vernol_homeworld)

The homeworld of the Vernol species was rendered uninhabitable by a meteor strike fifty years before the Galactic Civil War. Because of this, the Vernol moved off planet, establishing colonies across the galaxy. The largest concentration of Vernols settled on the arctic planet of Garnib.
