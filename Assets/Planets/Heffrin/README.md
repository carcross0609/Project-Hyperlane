# Heffrin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heffrin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heffrin)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Heffrin`, Outer Rim, grid R-17 — map position (9689.8, -10779.4, 474.8)

Heffrin was a planet located in the Arkanis sector of the Outer Rim. This mountainous world was a small mining colony maintained by the Galactic Empire. The planet held important ruins from a vanished civilization, including the Heffrin Aqueducts, which spanned Heffrin's continents; research during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire identified the Aqueducts' builders as early Ithorian explorers.
