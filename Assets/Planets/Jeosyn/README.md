# Jeosyn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jeosyn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jeosyn)

Jeosyn was a planet in the Weneen sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It had an extremely slow rotation period, about three months, which meant that days and nights were unusually long and that both caused temperature extremes. The side of the planet experiencing night was called the Shadow Side; the side experiencing day was the Sun Side. The Dreamers' headquarters was located on the Shadow Side of the planet.
