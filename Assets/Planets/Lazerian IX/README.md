# Lazerian IX

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lazerian IX](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lazerian_IX)

Lazerian IX was a planet that occupied the ninth orbital position in the Lazerian system. It was located within the Kira sector, in the Trailing Sectors portion of the Expansion Region. The planet was a frigid, rocky world with no moons, and orbited the yellow star known as Lazerian. Lazerian IX was found along the trade routes known as the Harrin Trade Corridor and Kira Run. At some point, it was surveyed by the Lazerians of the planet Lazerian IV.
