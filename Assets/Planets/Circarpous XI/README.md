# Circarpous XI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Circarpous XI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Circarpous_XI)

Circarpous XI was the eleventh planet orbiting the star Circarpous Major in the Circarpous system. Located in the Circarpous sector of the Expansion Region, it was a gas giant.
