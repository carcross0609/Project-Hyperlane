# Trasse

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Trasse](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trasse)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Trasse`, Mid Rim, grid Q-8 — map position (8371.8, 2179.3, -191.1)

Trasse was a Mid Rim Territories planet, located in the Near Perlemian area, that fell under the control of the Rebel Alliance during its offensive into the Mid Rim, later being evacuated after the campaign collapsed due to the Galactic Empire's counterpush in 2 ABY. In 4 ABY, when rumors spread of an Alliance chief traveling to the Perlamian, Cynabar's InfoNet made a news report listing Trasse and three other Mid Rim worlds as potential sites of rebel activity.
