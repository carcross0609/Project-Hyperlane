# Katiz Xemir's homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Katiz Xemir's homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Katiz_Xemir%27s_homeworld)

The Human Katiz Xemir settled on a remote colony planet and called it home. However, after Xemir refused to relocate offworld to make way for a mining facility on the planet, the Galactic Empire killed his family. That caused Xemir to hire himself out as a mercenary against Imperial targets.
