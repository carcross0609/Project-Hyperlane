# Gammalin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gammalin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gammalin)

Gammalin was a planet in the Vish sector of the Mid Rim. Its populace was wiped out by the Emperor's Plague. Unknowingly released by Fonterrat, he had brought it from an Imperial storehouse and booby-trapped by Nolaa Tarkona. After poisoning the populace of Gammalin, he was arrested and died of malnutrition, as his captors died of the plague.
