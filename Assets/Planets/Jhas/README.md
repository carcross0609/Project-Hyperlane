# Jhas

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jhas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jhas)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Jhas`, Outer Rim — map position (12267.4, -230.3, 439.2)

Jhas was one of two gas giants located in the Hoth system of the Outer Rim Territories. Jhas would shrink as a result of pressure on its surface, causing gravitational energy to convert to heat. Due to this phenomenon, the eleventh of Jhas's twenty-four moons, Jhas Krill, had a warm, swampy jungle terrain.
