# Rilias II

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rilias II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rilias_II)

Rilias II was a planet located in the galaxy. In 228 BBY, it was located with the Occlusion Zone created by the Nihil marauders. That year, the anti-Nihil operative Cair San Tekka broadcasted a message recorded by Jedi Master Avar Kriss from the central water garden.
