# Osseriton

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Osseriton/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Osseriton/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Osseriton`, Unknown Regions — map position (-8288.8, 1655.6, 319.7)

Osseriton was the only planet in the Osseriton system of the Unknown Regions. The Hemes Arbora settled there after abandoning their homeworld of Carrivar.

Chiss Expansionary Defense Force teams queried the world and its inhabitants and recorded the data in their library.
