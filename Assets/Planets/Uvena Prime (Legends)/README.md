# Uvena Prime

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uvena Prime/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uvena_Prime/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Uvena Prime`, Outer Rim, grid M-18 — map position (2612.2, -12160.9, -155.1)

Uvena Prime, or Uvena I, was the home planet of the Shistavanens in the Uvena system. The species, capable of hyperspace-level technology, emigrated to other uninhabited worlds of the system, including Uvena III, to prevent strangers from outlying systems from arriving and settling them. As of at least the Galactic Civil War, the planet was led by a monarchy, which made use of the royal Shistavanen Star Cruiser known as the Malachite Scent.
