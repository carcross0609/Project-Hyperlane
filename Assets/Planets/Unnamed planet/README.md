# Unnamed planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unnamed planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unnamed_planet)

A rocky unnamed planet that had two moons and orbited a red sun was home to a settlement. After the fall of the Jedi Order and rise of the Galactic Empire, a Jedi who survived the Order's destruction went into hiding on the planet, working alongside a local rebel cell. The Empire's Inquisitorius dispatched two Inquisitors, the Fourth Sister and Barriss Offee, to hunt down the Jedi. As a result of the local populace sympathising with the rebel cell, the settlement and surrounding area was severely damaged, impoverished, and neglected by the Empire. After the Inquisitors arrived at the settlement, its governor claimed there were no Jedi on the planet, although Offee learned from a girl that the Jedi was hiding in the mountains. In retaliation, the Fourth Sister slaughtered the gathered citizens, and the two Inquisitors went to find the Jedi. During the ensuing duel, Offee eventually convinced the Jedi to surrender, but the Fourth Sister struck them down regardless. Offee, discovering the Jedi was still alive, turned against the Fourth Sister when she refused to help them, throwing her fellow Inquisitor off the cliff before renouncing her allegiance to the Empire and returning to save the Jedi.
