# Keldooine

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Keldooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keldooine)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Keldooine`, Hutt Space, grid R-10 — map position (9853.8, -1260.7, -495.5)

Keldooine was a planet and shadowport in the Keldooine system on the edge of Hutt Space. During the Republic Era, Keldooine was a key world in the region, though by 33 BBY, the piracy rampant in the Slice had taken hold on the planet. The former pirate Queen Maz Kanata marked it as a point of interest on a map she included in the book that would become known as the Smuggler's Guide.
