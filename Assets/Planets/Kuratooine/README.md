# Kuratooine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kuratooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuratooine)

Kuratooine was a planet in Wild Space. Though it was home to several Galactic Alliance Army bases and a Galactic Alliance embassy, it was not officially part of the Alliance and continued to maintain its own system of laws and its own government. Its capital was Kura City. Turman Durra and Myri Antilles traveled to the city, disguised as honeymooners, as the other members of Wraith Squadron in different disguises traveled in pairs or threes, in order to set up their new temporary HQ for their operation.
