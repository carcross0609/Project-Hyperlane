# Oseon VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oseon VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oseon_VII)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Oseon VII`, Outer Rim, grid T-8 — map position (12909.2, 2263.5, -202.5)

Oseon VII was a planet located in the Oseon system that was a major tourist attraction and a center of exploration for the whole Centrality. Trouvee family owned a gambling complex on this planet. At one time, Han Solo and Leia visited the planet.
