# Dagu

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dagu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dagu)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dagu`, Outer Rim, grid S-16 — map position (11747.2, -10078.2, 232.4)

Dagu was a terrestrial celestial body located in the Dagu system, a part of the Instrop sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. During the Clone Wars, nine months after the conflict's opening Battle of Geonosis, it was the site of a revolt and a battle between the forces of the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. On the date 14:3:22, the HoloNet News Core Edition reported on a public ceremony held at the Jedi Temple on the Core Worlds planet Coruscant, in which the Republic's Supreme Chancellor Palpatine commended the Togruta Jedi Master Shaak Ti for her valor in, among other actions, protecting the populace of Dagu from the forces of the Confederacy.
