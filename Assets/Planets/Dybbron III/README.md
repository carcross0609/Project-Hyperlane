# Dybbron III

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dybbron III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dybbron_III)

Dybbron III, or just Dybbron, was a planet in the Inner Rim Territories' Dybbron system which hosted a breakaway faction of the Galactic Empire during the later Galactic Civil War. In 5 ABY, it was targeted by the loyalist 204th Imperial Fighter Wing, which engulfed the planet as part of its campaign against Imperial breakaway worlds. After learning of the attacks, New Republic General Hera Syndulla sent some of her forces to Dybbron III to check for survivors.
