# Dwi'lar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dwi'lar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dwi%27lar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dwi'lar`, Mid Rim — map position (6464.3, -396.3, 64.5)

Dwi'lar was a gas giant, the fifth planet of the Kothlis system.

Along with the other gas giant of the system, Dwi'kar, the two were known as "siblings" or "the twins" by the locals of Kothlis that orbited between them. Dwi'kar was the cooler of the two and its surface consisted of green and blue gases.

Botha Ammunitions Corporation harvested blaster gases from the two planets.
