# Tiisheraan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tiisheraan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tiisheraan/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tiisheraan`, Core Worlds, grid L-13 — map position (700.9, -5158.9, 58.0)

Tiisheraan was a planet located in the Tiisheraan system, a part of the Southern Core portion of the Core Worlds. In 3638 BBY, the Hutt scientist and droid inventor Doctor Juvard Illip Oggurobb believed that Tiisheraan would be perfectly suited for hosting a private laboratory that he wished to have.

Tiisheraan was the homeworld of the Galactic Republic Senator Galen Derlin and the birthplace of his Human son, Bren. After the Republic was replaced by the Galactic Empire in 19 BBY, former Senator Derlin returned to Tiisheraan, and was eventually murdered by an Imperial assassin sent by Emperor Palpatine. Following his father's death, Bren, as well as his fellow Human native of Tiisheraan, Beryann Raleg, joined the Rebel Alliance.
