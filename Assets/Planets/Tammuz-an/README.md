# Tammuz-an

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tammuz-an](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tammuz-an)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tammuz-an`, Outer Rim, grid T-14 — map position (13135.2, -6725.1, -38.0)

Tammuz-an was a planet located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Shortly after the end of the Clone Wars, the kingdom of Tammuz-an purchased a batch of R-22 prototypes from Kuat Systems Engineering designers for their planetary defense fleet, after the Galactic Empire rejected the ship for mass production. Tammuz-an was responsible for nicknaming the R-22 as "Spearheads" after their resemblance to local spears.
