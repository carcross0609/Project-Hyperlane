# Didyma V

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Didyma V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Didyma_V)

Didyma V, was a terrestrial world that was located in the Didyma system in the New Territories region and Wild Space, in grid square J-5 on the Standard Galactic Grid. It was the homeworld of the Didynon Engi Golba. Lommite, a type of ore used in transparisteel, was mined on the world.

By 35 ABY, the First Order, the successor state of the Galactic Empire, had annexed Didyma V, taking control of the lommite production. Golba escaped from the mines after its annexation to join the Resistance, a paramilitary group led by former New Republic senator Leia Organa.
