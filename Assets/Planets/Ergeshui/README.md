# Ergeshui

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ergeshui](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ergeshui)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ergeshui`, Inner Rim — map position (5666.4, 687.9, -99.8)

Ergeshui was a world that existed within the galaxy. Between 2 BBY and 4 ABY, the bounty hunter Boba Fett went to the world to deliver a human bounty to the alien criminal Tusk, who subsequently executed the human for backstabbing them sometime in the past.
