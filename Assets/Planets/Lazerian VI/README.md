# Lazerian VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lazerian VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lazerian_VI)

Lazerian VI was the sixth planet in the Lazerian system, in orbit around the yellow star named Lazerian. The planet was a gas giant and had eight moons. It was located within the Kira sector in the Trailing Sectors portion of the Expansion Region. Lazerian VI was found along the trade routes known as the Harrin Trade Corridor and the Kira Run. At some point, it was surveyed by the Lazerians of Lazerian IV, another planet in the Lazerian system.
