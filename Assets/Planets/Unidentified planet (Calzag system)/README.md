# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Calzag system)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Calzag_system%29)

A planet located in the Calzag system was the homeworld of a clan of Gamorreans. Believing he was pursuing the Millennium Falcon, bounty hunter Boba Fett landed on the planet, where he was attacked by the clan.
