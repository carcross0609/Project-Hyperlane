# Babbadod

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Babbadod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Babbadod)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Babbadod`, Inner Rim, grid N-14 — map position (3510.9, -6091.3, 33.2)

Babbadod was a colony world of the Inner Rim located on the Hydian Way between the planet Chardaan and the Itani Nebula. In the early years of the New Republic, the planet Babbadod was visited by the Koqus liner Star Morning, which was owned by the fleeing Fallanassi religious order.
