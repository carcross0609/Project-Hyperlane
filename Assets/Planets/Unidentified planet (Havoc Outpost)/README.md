# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Havoc Outpost)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Havoc_Outpost%29)

A planet located in Wild Space was home to the trading and supply base Havoc Outpost. A terrestrial world situated in Standard Galactic Grid square S-19, it appeared bluish from space. The planet had a Type I atmosphere that was breathable to humans and featured mountainous terrain, including a lake. Havoc Outpost was built into the cliffside overlooking the lake. The planet was allied with the Rebel Alliance, who used Havoc Outpost for operations. In 4 BBY, during the Galactic Civil War between the rebels and the Galactic Empire, the human rebel Sabine Wren was tasked with traveling to the planet to deliver the courier droid EG-86 to rebel forces representing Senator Bail Organa. With the help of her former associate, the human mercenary Ketsu Onyo, Wren arrived at Havoc Outpost and delivered the droid.
