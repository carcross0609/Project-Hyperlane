# Jiroch-Saloch

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jiroch-Saloch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jiroch-Saloch)

Jiroch-Saloch, also known as Saloch, was a world in the Mid Rim's Yushan sector. At some point after receiving galactic-level technology, the hunting-oriented Tunroth species colonized the planet. Spacers referred to Jiroch-Saloch, together with the Jiroch system's other worlds Jiroch-Reslia and Jiroch-Kalok, as the Triumvirate. A number of Duros also settled on the planet, leaving a strong influence on its architecture.
