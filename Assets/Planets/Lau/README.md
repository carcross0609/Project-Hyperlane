# Lau

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lau)

Lau was a planet in the Meerian sector of the Outer Rim Territories. After Omega and Crosshair fled Imperial facility on Wayland, they ended up on Lau before they managed to steal and ship and leave the planet.
