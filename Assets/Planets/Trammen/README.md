# Trammen

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trammen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trammen)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Trammen`, Expansion Region, grid O-11 — map position (5481.5, -2409.1, -143.7)

Trammen was a heavily populated Expansion Region world located in the Harron sector. Situated on the Ootmian Pabol hyperspace route, around the time of the beginning of the Alsakan Conflicts, it was a part of the Galactic Republic as well as the Exploitation Region that was controlled by the trading conglomerates known as the Grand Companies. During the Galactic Civil War, Trammen served as a Galactic Empire staging area.
