# Shrokaan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shrokaan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shrokaan)

Shrokaan was a planet located in the Baroli sector of the Expansion Region. During the Great Jedi Purge, Darth Vader executed a Jedi Master on the twelve-bladed satellite of Shrokaan by exposing him to the void of space, as he was too dangerous to capture alive.
