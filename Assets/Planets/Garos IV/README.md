# Garos IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Garos IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Garos_IV)

Garos IV was a planet in the Mid Rim, the fourth world in the Garos system. Located near the Nyarikan Nebula, it was somewhat isolated from outside contact and its inhabitants were a tough, self-reliant people. It was the homeworld of Imperial officers Brenn and Dellis Tantor, and the adopted homeworld of New Republic Captain Alexandra Winger.

In the Post-Imperial era, the planet was best known for the University of Garos, whose database of secret Imperial files were available for academic study.
