# Derra IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Derra IV/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Derra_IV/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Derra IV`, Expansion Region, grid N-15 — map position (3075.0, -7789.7, 376.1)

Derra IV, or Derra Four, also known simply as Derra, was a planet in the Derra system of the Mikaster sector, located in the Expansion Region. It was a minor world of little value and had been relatively overlooked by the Empire early in the Galactic Civil War. This made it an ideal site for Rebel Alliance operations.
