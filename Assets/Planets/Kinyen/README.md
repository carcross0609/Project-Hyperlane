# Kinyen

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kinyen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kinyen)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kinyen`, Expansion Region, grid L-15 — map position (161.2, -8909.5, 132.1)

Kinyen was an agriworld located in the Kinyen system in the Bes Ber Bikade sector of the Expansion Region, on a junction with the Corellian Trade Spine and the Great Gran Run. It was the original homeworld of the Gran species. Malastare , which was colonized by Gran hundreds of years before the Imperial Era, was also considered a homeworld of the Gran.

Kinyen was the homeworld of the Gran senators Philo and Kharrus, who represented Kinyen in the Galactic Republic Senate during the Clone Wars.
