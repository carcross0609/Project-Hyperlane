# Batuv

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Batuv](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Batuv)

Batuv was a planet in the Expansion Region that was occupied and blockaded by the Galactic Empire. Prior to his meeting with Tobias Beckett, Captain Lando Calrissian, and his co-pilot L3-37 took the Millennium Falcon to Batuv where Calrissian picked up a cape from Borkus.
