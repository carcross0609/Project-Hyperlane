# Marfa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Marfa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marfa)

Marfa was a planet in the Core Worlds that housed Jedi AgriCorps facilities during the Cold War. Hestizo Trace and the Murakami orchid she had created a bond with were kidnapped here by the Whiphid bounty hunter Tulkh. Later, Jedi Knight Rojo Trace came to the planet in search of Hestizo.
