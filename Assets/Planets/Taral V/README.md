# Taral V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Taral V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taral_V)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Taral V`, Outer Rim, grid U-7 — map position (13679.7, 4359.7, -20.6)

Taral V was a planet controlled by the Sith Empire during the Cold War. During the Cold War, a Galactic Republic strike team was sent to Taral V to retrieve information on a missing Jedi. The planet was home to many dangerous species.
