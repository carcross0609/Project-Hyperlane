# Neskar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neskar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neskar)

Neskar was a planet orbited by at least 3 moons that was located in the Neskar system in the Tashtor sector of the Mid Rim Territories. From space, Neskar was banded with streaks of red and purple. Neskar Station, a space station that hosted battle-racing, was situated in orbit of Neskar. Around 4 ABY, Rebel Alliance members Lando Calrissian and Chewbacca flew the Millennium Falcon past Neskar to meet the racer Sebulba, an informant who possessed details regarding pirates that were robbing rebel supply lines.
