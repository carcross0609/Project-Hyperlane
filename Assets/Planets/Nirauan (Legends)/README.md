# Nirauan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nirauan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nirauan/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Nirauan`, Wild Space, grid I-5 — map position (-3295.6, 6058.5, -116.4)

Nirauan was a planet located in second of three inner planets in the Nirauan system in Wild Space. It was inhabited by the indigenous avian Qom Jha and Qom Qae species, and was the capital of the Empire of the Hand, where it housed a large garrison of Chiss and human personnel.
