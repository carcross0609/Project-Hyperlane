# Vo

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vo)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Vo`, Core Worlds, grid M-11 — map position (2450.8, -2140.4, 96.6)

The seventh and outermost planet in its system, Vo was a frozen, moonless, lifeless rock. It was located in the Sacorrian system, one of the Outlier systems of the Corellian sector, in the Arrowhead portion of the Galactic Core. In 18 ABY, the government of Vo's system-mate Sacorria attempted to secede from the New Republic.
