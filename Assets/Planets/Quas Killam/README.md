# Quas Killam

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quas Killam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quas_Killam)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Quas Killam`, Mid Rim, grid P-10 — map position (6710.4, -26.5, 303.9)

Quas Killam was a planet on the edge of the Mid Rim. Its people were the Killam. Quas Killam was a world of weapons manufacturing factories and trinium mines, and so was conquered by the Trade Federation early in the Clone Wars. Throughout the war the planet changed sides several times.
