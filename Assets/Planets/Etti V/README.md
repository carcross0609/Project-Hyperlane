# Etti V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Etti V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Etti_V)

Etti V was a planet in the Etti system in the Corporate Sector of the Outer Rim Territories. By the time of the Battle of Yavin, the Corporate Sector Authority moved the Etti species off Etti IV to the planet and did not allow anyone else access to Etti V.
