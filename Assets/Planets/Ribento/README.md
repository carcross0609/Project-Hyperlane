# Ribento

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ribento](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ribento)

Ribento was a planet that existed in the galaxy. The planet was located in the Ribento system and had two inhabitable moons, Morlas and Tho. During the High Republic Era, the planet was bombarded by the Nihil.
