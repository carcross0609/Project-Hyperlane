# Miraluka homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Miraluka homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miraluka_homeworld)

This planet was the original homeworld of the Near-Human Force-sensitive Miraluka species. At some point in time, however, the planet was rendered uninhabitable because it was unstable and began losing its atmosphere to space. As a result of the cataclysm, the Miraluka were forced to leave their homeworld and ultimately settled on the planet Alpheridies.
