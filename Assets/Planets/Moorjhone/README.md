# Moorjhone

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moorjhone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moorjhone)

Moorjhone was a planet located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories, within the Jospro sector and was the homeworld of felinoid Moorjhoni. It was in a trinary star system. It was normally lit and heated by two of the three suns at a given time. Once every ten years, however, all three suns would rise on the same side of the planet, resulting in the Day of Three Suns.
