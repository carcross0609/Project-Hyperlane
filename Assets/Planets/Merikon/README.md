# Merikon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Merikon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merikon)

Merikon was a planet located in the Fath sector of the Outer Rim Territories in the galaxy. During the Imperial Era, the Galactic Empire operated a training facility on the planet. Ivhin Jayme, a member of the Imperial Military, received intensive training by the Merikon instructors while at the facility.
