# Ruweln

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ruweln](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruweln)

Ruweln was a Bothan colony world located in Bothan space. Ruweln was among the roughly 150 settled systems in Bothan Space dominated by Bothan colonists, and was ruled by the Bothan Council, which was made up of representatives from hundreds of Bothan clans. It was the homeworld of Galactic Alliance Admiral Nek Bwua'tu.
