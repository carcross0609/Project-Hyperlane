# Mikkia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mikkia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mikkia)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mikkia`, Expansion Region — map position (5763.9, -2510.8, 136.4)

Mikkia was a planet with a variety of climates located in the galaxy's Expansion Region, and home to the Mikkian species. Mikkia was a member of the Galactic Republic for thousands of years. During the High Republic Era, Jedi Master Mirro Lox and his Padawan Amadeo Azzazzo helped assist and rescue people during a large groundquake on the planet. The destruction of the Jedi Order contributed to a high level of anti-Imperial sentiment on Mikkia.
