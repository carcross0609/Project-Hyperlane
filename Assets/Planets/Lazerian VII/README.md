# Lazerian VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lazerian VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lazerian_VII)

Lazerian VII was the seventh planet in the Lazerian system. It was located within the Kira sector, in the Trailing Sectors portion of the Expansion Region. The planet was a gas giant with ten moons, and orbited the yellow star known as Lazerian. Lazerian VII was found along the trade routes known as the Harrin Trade Corridor and Kira Run. It was once surveyed by the Lazerians of Lazerian IV, another planet in the Lazerian system.
