# Unidentified planet (A-rack)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (A-rack)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28A-rack%29)

A planet, which was liberated by Empire of the Hand Grand Admiral Thrawn, was the origin point of the A-rack rolling vehicles the Grand Admiral used during a battle against alien warlord Nuso Esva on the Unknown Regions planet Quethold in 8 ABY. Imperial Stromma contingent stormtrooper Lhagva remembered where the A-racks originated during the conflict.
