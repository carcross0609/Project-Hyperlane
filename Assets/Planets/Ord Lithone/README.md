# Ord Lithone

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Lithone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Lithone)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ord Lithone`, Inner Rim, grid L-8 — map position (139.3, 2441.7, -111.0)

Ord Lithone was an Inner Rim world that served as a Galactic Republic outpost during the Pius Dea Era. Millennia later, it hosted a Baktoid Armor Workshop factory. In 22 BBY, citing the increased Republic regulations on battle droid manufacture and sales as a reason, Baktoid announced the closure of several of its factories, including that on Ord Lithone.
