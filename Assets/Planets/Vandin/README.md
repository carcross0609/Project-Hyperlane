# Vandin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vandin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vandin)

Vandin was a planet with at least one floating tibanna gas platform that housed tibanna gas miners. The planet utilized at least some form of organized security force, consisting of a number of probe droids. The Brekken Gang maintained some presence on the planet.
