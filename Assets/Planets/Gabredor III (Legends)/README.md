# Gabredor III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gabredor III/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gabredor_III/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gabredor III`, Outer Rim, grid K-4 — map position (-668.8, 8538.2, -110.6)

Gabredor III was a planet situated between Dantooine and Obtrexta sector, in Myto's Arrow. It had a Karazak Slavers Cooperative transfer center under the authority of slave master Greezim Trentacal. Trentacal kidnapped the children of the Ambassador of Cantras Gola to force the PowerOn Conglomerate to remain with the Alignment; in exchange, the Alignment agreed not to interfere with Karazak operations. The center was destroyed by the Red Moons, who rescued the children and other slaves.
