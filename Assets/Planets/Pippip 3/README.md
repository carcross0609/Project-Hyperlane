# Pippip 3

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pippip 3](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pippip_3)

Pippip 3 was a planet located in grid square Q-4 on the Standard Galactic Grid, in the Pippip system within the Illisurevimurasi sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim and New Territories regions. It was the homeworld of the human Resistance pilot Tallissan Lintra, and was populated by creatures such as dire hounds and whellays. The planet was connected by the Listehol Run hyperspace route to the Mirial and Gigor systems.

Sometime after the Galactic Civil War, Lintra's parents established a farm on Pippip 3, where the young pilot used her father's old RZ-1 A-wing interceptor as a cropduster. Later, during the evacuation of D'Qar amidst the First Order-Resistance War, Lintra remembered Pippip 3's fauna while comparing First Order TIE fighters going through the Resistance defensive formation to dire hounds loose among whellays.
