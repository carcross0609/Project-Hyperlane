# Nilrebmah IV

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Nilrebmah IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nilrebmah_IV)

Nilrebmah IV was a frozen planet in the Nilrebmah system. The planet's atmosphere was methane-based, and no life had ever evolved on its frigid surface.
