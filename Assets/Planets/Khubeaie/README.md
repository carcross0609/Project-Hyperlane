# Khubeaie

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Khubeaie](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khubeaie)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Khubeaie`, Outer Rim, grid R-17 — map position (9851.1, -10731.8, -667.7)

Khubeaie was a nondescript industrial planet located in the Khubeaie system, a part of the Arkanis sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was situated at the endpoint of the Vaschean Way hyperlane, which linked it to the Heffrin system, while the hyperspace route known as the Old Corellian Run connected it to the Vor Deo system as well as the Ryloth system of the Gaulus sector.

Khubeaie was a misty world that was more widely known as a stopover point for smugglers transporting spice from the planet Ryloth to the Arkanis sector world Tatooine as well as the super-hyperroute known as the Triellus Trade Route. Members of the Human, Hutt, Rodian, and Twi'lek species could all be found on Khubeaie.
