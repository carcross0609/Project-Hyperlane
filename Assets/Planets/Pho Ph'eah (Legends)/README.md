# Pho Ph'eah

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pho Ph'eah/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pho_Ph%27eah/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Pho Ph'eah`, Outer Rim, grid P-5 — map position (6461.3, 7129.8, 21.7)

Pho Ph'eah was a mountainous world in the Outer Rim Territories, that served as the homeworld for the four-armed Pho Ph'eahian species. Located far from its star, Pho Ph'eah relied on its heavy natural geothermal activity to provide heat to its native lifeforms. Pho Ph'eahians were naturally adept with technology, and turned Pho Ph'eah into a hub of technological development in the years after contact with the Galactic Republic.
