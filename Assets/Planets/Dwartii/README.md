# Dwartii

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dwartii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dwartii)

Dwartii was a planet located in the Dwartii system within the Nouane sector of the Inner Rim, in grid square N-8 on the Standard Galactic Grid. It was home to the eponymous Four Sages of Dwartii—Sistros Nevet, Faya Rodemos, Yanjon Zelmar, and Braata Danlos—who were controversial philosophers and lawgivers from the early days of the Galactic Republic, as well as the philosopher Cowus Roont, often called the 'Fifth Sage' of Dwartii.

Dwartii also shared its name with a social group that had a set of glyphs associated with it. A map showing Dwartii's location in the galaxy was included in Traveler's Guide to Batuu, a guide book for the planet Batuu written by the historian Eloc Throno in 34 ABY. A book describing objects relating to galactic history included an entry on the Four Sages of Dwartii's statues, which mentioned their homeworld of Dwartii.
