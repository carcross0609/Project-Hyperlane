# Maldroth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maldroth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maldroth)

Maldroth was a planet in the Maldroth system in the Grumani sector of the Outer Rim Territories with a vast archipelago surrounded by a glittering sea. According to legend a group of colonists settled on Maldroth after a great war that left the people with a lasting guilt.
