# Galator III

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Galator III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galator_III)

Galator III, also known simply as Galator, was a casino planet in the galaxy where Kes Dameron and Shara Bey first met. Around 4 BBY, Dameron traveled to the betting houses on Galator III with his friend, who was due to get married. Once there, Dameron bet on a race that Bey participated in.
