# Wevno

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Wevno](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wevno)

Wevno was a terrestrial planet located in the galaxy. During the reign of the Empire, Maul, disguised as Darth Vader, stole bottles of oil from a hardware shop on Wevno with the help of three stormtroopers, who were tricked by his disguise.
