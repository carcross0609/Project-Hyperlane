# Jondora

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jondora](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jondora)

Jondora was a terrestrial planet in the Jondora system of the Taris sector, a subsector of the Ojoster sector in the Outer Rim Territories, where the salyard boss Salman Paak attended a separatist meeting in 7 BBY. At the meeting, Paak met a woman that convinced him to act as a liaison for his planet, Ferrix, upon his return. Paak was thus provided with a fractal radio unit that he and the mechanic Bix Caleen used to stay linked to a shadowy anti-Imperial network. Paak was caught for it during the Empire's occupation of Ferrix, and after some interrogation, he retold his encounter on Jondora for Dedra Meero, a supervisor in the Empire's Imperial Security Bureau.
