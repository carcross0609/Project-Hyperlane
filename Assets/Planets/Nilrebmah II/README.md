# Nilrebmah II

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Nilrebmah II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nilrebmah_II)

Nilrebmah II was a planet in the Nilrebmah system. The atmosphere of the world was fluorine-based. It's desert-covered surface was cratered, due to the fact that its atmosphere was filled with asteroids which made frequent impact on the surface. It was the homeworld of an unidentified civilization.
