# Rinn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rinn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rinn)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rinn`, Outer Rim, grid U-12 — map position (13963.9, -3163.6, 215.4)

Rinn was a frozen planet located in the Baxel sector of the Outer Rim. Inhabitants of Rinn depended on heaters to live in the freezing conditions. In 4 BBY, the planet experienced an energy crisis when they ran low on power generators, causing the rebels of Phoenix Cell to assist them.
