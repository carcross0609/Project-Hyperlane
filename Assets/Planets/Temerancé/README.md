# Temerancé

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Temerancé](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Temeranc%C3%A9)

Temerancé was a forested planet located in the Rekai system. After the Nihil marauders established the Occlusion Zone, Temerancé fell behind its Stormwall barrier. The Nihil undertook several raids on Temerancé, during which they also kidnapped Force-sensitive children, including Lene. The monster hunter Ty Yorrick arrived on Temerancé for a job, but intended to depart after learning that the target was the Nihil. However, the parent of Lene told Yorrick about the kidnapped children, asking her to save Lene and the others.
