# Isis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Isis/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Isis/Legends)

Isis was a terrestrial planet in the Anoat sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The homeworld of the Gutretee, it was a secret Rebel Alliance safe world during the Galactic Civil War, and later became one of the Mining Worlds under the control of the Figg conglomerate.
