# Tenupe

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tenupe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenupe)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tenupe`, Wild Space, grid I-8 — map position (-3935.3, 2210.2, 102.6)

Tenupe was a mountainous and tropical planet located within the Tenupe system that was located in the Utegetu Nebula of Wild Space and was on the Sparkle Run. It was orbited by the Shattered Moon. It had multiple inland seas and was controlled by the Killiks; however the planet was captured by the Chiss during the Battle of Tenupe of the Swarm War in 36 ABY. Jagged Fel and Alema Rar spent two years marooned on the planet after the Dark Nest Crisis taking away a Twi'lek story-chain from there.
