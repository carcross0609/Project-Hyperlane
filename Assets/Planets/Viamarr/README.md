# Viamarr

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Viamarr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Viamarr)

Viamarr was a world in the Viamarr system of the Inner Rim Territories that was controlled by the Galactic Empire. The Leonis family once lived on this planet and during Zare Leonis's fifteenth birthday, laughed over stories of their time on this world and other worlds half-forgotten.
