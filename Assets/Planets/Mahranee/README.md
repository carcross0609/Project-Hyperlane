# Mahranee

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mahranee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mahranee)

Mahranee was a planet in the Mahranee system in the Taldot sector of the Mid Rim Territories that was the homeworld of the Mahran species. Known for being rich in resources, Mahranee was attacked and taken by the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars in order to secure the planet's riches for the Separatist cause.
