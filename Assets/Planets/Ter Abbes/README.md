# Ter Abbes

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ter Abbes](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ter_Abbes)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ter Abbes`, Expansion Region, grid O-8 — map position (5117.3, 2167.7, -336.3)

Ter Abbes was a grim industrialized planet located within the Ter Abbes system of the Expansion Region, just off the Perlemian Trade Route. Its government was democratically elected, but prone to instability, as was proven when Nom Anor contracted Mandalorian mercenary Goran Beviin to assassinate Tholote B'Leph, an opposition politician just prior to an election. The civil war ignited by his death was still in progress in 25 ABY.

At the time of the meeting of Boba Fett and Nom Anor on a Yuuzhan Vong warship, there was a male Human from Ter Abbes on board. He was taken as a slave and implanted by an experimental Yorik-Kul.
