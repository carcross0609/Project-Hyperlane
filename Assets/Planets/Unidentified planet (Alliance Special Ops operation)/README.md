# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Alliance Special Ops operation)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Alliance_Special_Ops_operation%29)

During the Galactic Civil War in 4 ABY, the Alliance to Restore the Republic attempted to infiltrate this planet controlled by the Galactic Empire. The mission was a failure, and the infiltration team was forced to retreat.
