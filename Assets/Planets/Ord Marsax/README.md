# Ord Marsax

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Marsax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Marsax)

Ord Marsax was an Outer Rim Territories world located in the Belderone sector. During the Pius Dea Era, it served as a Galactic Republic outpost. Millennia later, according to a story relayed by Bounty Hunters' Guild member Arack Dostryt, the researcher Doctor Nuvo Vindi carried out gas attacks on every bounty posting agency on Ord Marsax.
