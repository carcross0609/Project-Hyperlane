# Quintil

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quintil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quintil)

Quintil was a frigid planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. Tomasso, an operative of the Spice Runners of Kijimi, was stranded on Quintil after successfully eliminating the high commander of the Zualjinn Syndicate. A cell of the Spice Runners were sent to rescue him, briefly being captured by the Zualjinn themselves.
