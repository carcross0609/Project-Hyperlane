# Fillata

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fillata](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fillata)

Fillata was the third planet orbiting the star Prael in the Japrael system. Located in the Japrael sector of the Inner Rim Territories, it was not able to sustain any life due to it being a toxic world.
