# Unidentified jungle world

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified jungle world (Torch Nebula)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_jungle_world_%28Torch_Nebula%29)

A planet was located on the edges of the Torch Nebula, a nebula in the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy. A nameless jungle world, the planet was believed by biologists serving the Hutt Cartel to be the homeworld of the lurker, an amphibious bipedal creature that was first identified in the larger galaxy at some point by 3840 BBY.

- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
