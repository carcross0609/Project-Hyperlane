# Unidentified fringe planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified fringe planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_fringe_planet)

A terrestrial planet existed on the fringe of the galaxy. When the Jedi Bruhn began to succumb to the dark side of the Force at some point by 3643 BBY, he decided to sacrifice himself in order to protect the Jedi Order from the effects of his transformation. To that end, Bruhn imprisoned himself in a tomb on the planet, where he eventually died.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified fringe planet
- **Class:** Terrestrial
- **Interest:** Bruhn's tomb
