# Rutan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rutan/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rutan/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rutan`, Outer Rim, grid L-20 — map position (163.1, -15238.0, -119.8)

Rutan was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories, on the Hydian Way.

A Rutanian tradition was to go hunting most days of the week, and after the hunts, the Rutanians would have a huge meat feast.
