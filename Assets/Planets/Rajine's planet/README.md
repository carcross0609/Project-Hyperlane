# Rajine's planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rajine's planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rajine%27s_planet)

Rajine's planet was a rocky planet used by the Jedi Knight Samuro to imprison the Jedi and energy vampire Rajine and her victims. He imprisoned them in a city and raised a Force barrier over the planet to ensure that Rajine could not escape. During the Clone Wars, the Jedi Master Mace Windu defeated Rajine in the city on the planet.
