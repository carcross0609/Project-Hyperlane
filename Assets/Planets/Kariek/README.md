# Kariek

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kariek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kariek)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kariek`, Wild Space, grid I-6 — map position (-3545.2, 5431.5, -317.9)

Kariek was a planet located in Wild Space. Its sole indigenous sentient lifeform were the Eickarie people, who were divided into various tribes. The Eickaries were a humanoid reptilian race with green and orange skin, with patterns of black scales around their eyes and across the back of their heads.

Its cities were dominated by towering fortresses and watchtowers which were interconnected via a network of underground tunnels.
