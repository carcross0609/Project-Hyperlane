# Rafa III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rafa III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rafa_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rafa III`, Outer Rim, grid T-8 — map position (13200.6, 2032.2, -518.5)

Rafa III was a terrestrial planet located in the Rafa system, a part of the Centrality sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It occupied the third orbital position around the Rafa system's sun, and along with the rest of the Rafa was situated on the Arleen Loop hyperlane, which connected it to the Arleen and Dela systems.

Rafa III was the site of deep-bore hardrock mining operations that utilized giant laser drill-bits. By 3 BBY, a pair of the planet's drill-bits had been gifted as souvenirs to the Poly Pyramid, a bar in the system's capital settlement Teguta Lusat on the planet Rafa IV. There, the drill-bits were used to prop open the inner doors of the establishment, which was frequented by vacationing miners from Rafa III.
