# Dremulae

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dremulae](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dremulae)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dremulae`, Deep Core, grid M-10 — map position (1526.5, -1102.9, 56.9)

Dremulae was a terrestrial planet in the Deep Core and was known for the natural beauty of its Sea of Translucency and for its popular wealthy resort.

Dremulae was a popular galactic tourist attraction, even for those luxury travelers opting for a holographic simulation experience—a sim-vacation, or sim-voyage.

The Dreamul'er plant, which could heal a lightsaber cut, was native to Dremulae.
