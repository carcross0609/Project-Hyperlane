# Koru Neimoidia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Koru Neimoidia/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Koru_Neimoidia/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Koru Neimoidia`, Colonies — map position (3103.0, -829.1, -183.2)

Koru Neimoidia was a Colonies planet that was one of the most famous purse world colonies of the Neimoidian species. It was a more desirable destination than the Neimoidians' homeworld, Neimoidia, itself and hosted opulent residences of affluent and powerful Neimoidians. By 3643 BBY, Koru Neimoidia was the site of the headquarters of the Opal Foundation, which was in reality a front for an influential crime lord. Millennia later, during the Clone Wars, the Galactic Republic laid siege to the planet, which at the time was a possession of the Trade Federation and a stronghold of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The Republic forces, led by the Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, eventually devastated and captured the world.
