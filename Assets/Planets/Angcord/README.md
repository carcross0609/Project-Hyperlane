# Angcord

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Angcord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Angcord)

Angcord was a planet in the galaxy mostly covered in water, with numerous rocky islands blanketed with green trees. During the High Republic Era, a pair of Jedi traveled to Angcord to search for ancient artifacts. The legendary Echo Stone was hidden on Angcord at some point before the High Republic Era.
