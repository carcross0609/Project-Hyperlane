# Bhuna Sound

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bhuna Sound/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bhuna_Sound/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bhuna Sound`, Mid Rim, grid K-17 — map position (-767.3, -11273.6, -88.4)

Bhuna Sound was a Mid Rim planet which joined the New Republic shortly after the Battle of Endor. To help in the planet's rebuilding efforts, the New Republic sent a freighter, the Dewback's Burden, to bring supplies to Bhuna Sound. However, the vessel was attacked by the TIE Pirates, who captured the transport.
