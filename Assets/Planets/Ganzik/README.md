# Ganzik

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ganzik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ganzik)

Ganzik was a world located in the Ganzik system, a part of the Mid Rim's Maldrood sector. It was situated near the hyperlane known as the Perlemian Trade Route. The primary shipyards of the starship manufacturing company Mesens Corporation were located in orbit around Ganzik.
