# Kerilt; (background)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kerilt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kerilt)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kerilt`, Mid Rim — map position (-306.9, -11803.8, -139.1)

Kerilt was the innermost planet of the Algaran system, covered by large rainforests. Many surviving Caamasi fled to Kerilt after the bombardment of Caamas by the Galactic Empire, founding the planet's first city, Morymento.

The planet was plundered by the Invids in 11 ABY, although they left supplies for the colony to grow.

From Kerilt, the Caamasi later relocated to Susevfi.
