# Havath Prime

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Havath Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Havath_Prime)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Havath Prime`, Outer Rim — map position (-3971.2, -10147.8, 176.4)

Havath Prime was the fourth planet in the Muunilinst system, a part of the Obtrexta sector in the New Territories portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was a gas giant orbited by thirty-two moons, itself circling the star Muunilinst. The entirety of the Muunilinst system was located along the hyperlane known as the Braxant Run.
