# Gamandar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gamandar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamandar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gamandar`, Outer Rim — map position (9246.5, -10138.6, 136.5)

Gamandar was a planet in the Mid Rim's Iskalon system, considered the twin planet of the nearby waterworld of Iskalon. The homeworld of a humanoid sentient species also known as the Gamandars, the planet was brutally conquered and its people enslaved by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. Afterwards, Admiral Griggor Tower established his base of operations in the system in a massive citadel on Gamandar.
