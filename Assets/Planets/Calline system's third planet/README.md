# Calline system's third planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Calline system's third planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Calline_system%27s_third_planet)

The third planet of the Calline system was a gas giant planet. It had no moons. Trace amounts of industrial grade gases were in its atmosphere.

- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
