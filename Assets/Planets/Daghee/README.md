# Daghee

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Daghee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daghee)

Daghee was a backwater planet in the Daghee system in the Thusa sector of the Mid Rim Territories, in grid square O-7 of the Standard Galactic Grid. The Soh family—including the human Lina Soh and her human son Kitrep Soh—hailed from Daghee. During the High Republic Era, Lina reprensented the planet in the Galactic Senate of the Galactic Republic until she was elected as Supreme Chancellor around 234 BBY. In 229 BBY, the Vurk Jedi Master Harli Cogra wrote a book titled Chronicles of the Jedi, which detailed the Jedi of the High Republic Era, and included a mention of Daghee in the fourth chapter of the book within a section dedicated to Lina.
