# Delassin VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Delassin VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delassin_VI)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Delassin VI`, Outer Rim — map position (10342.5, 8371.0, -182.1)

Delassin VI was an Outer Rim Territories planet located in the Calaron sector. It had oceans on its surface, and primitive seagoing vessels plied the waters of the planet's Diniz and Zehr Seas. The Vodran Xenon Nnaksta found work as a longshoreman at the docks servicing the Delassin VI sea vessels by 22 BBY and enjoyed handling cargo there until his career path took him off-world again.
