# Godo

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Godo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Godo)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Godo`, Outer Rim, grid O-18 — map position (5743.3, -12159.3, 39.4)

Godo was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories. It was the source of flame gems. The world was saved by Lando Calrissian and Han Solo. The Godoans refused to save Lando from a deadly disease in return until the Alliance of Free Planets threatened to attack Godo.

Jabba Desilijic Tiure once stole a valuable statue from the Godoans, the Dancing Goddess, and the Minstrel, both of which were unexplainable connected to the planet's lifeforms. His former servant Doda Bodonawieedo sold it back to them after the Hutt's death, demanding an exorbitant price, which was paid in order to regain the planet's vitality.
