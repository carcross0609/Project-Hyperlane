# Uaua

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uaua/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uaua/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Uaua`, Outer Rim, grid U-8 — map position (13867.3, 2274.4, 299.8)

Uaua was an Outer Rim Territories planet located in the Centrality sector. It was the homeworld of the Quor'sav, a species of tall, flightless sentients with avian and monotreme traits. After a virus accidentally introduced by off-world colonists nearly eliminated the Quor'sav, they developed a distrust for members of non-avian species.

The Besalisk chef Dexter Jettster once obtained a recipe from Uaua for pan-frying a nerf steak, and as part of a tribute paid to the Galactic Republic by Uaua's star system, the Quor'sav Waywa Fybot left his homeworld to become a law enforcement officer. Decades later, Fybot revisited Uaua in a dream induced by the Flamewind phenomenon of the Oseon system.
