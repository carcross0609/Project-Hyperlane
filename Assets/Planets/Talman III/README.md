# Talman III

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Talman III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Talman_III)

Talman III was a hot desert planet known for its mining activities and relative lawlessness. Some time during the Galactic Civil War, the planet contained a warehouse that supplied the Imperial logistics station in the Rama system with air filters as well as a spaceport and a nightclub.
