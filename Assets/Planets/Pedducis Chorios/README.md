# Pedducis Chorios

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pedducis Chorios](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pedducis_Chorios)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Pedducis Chorios`, Outer Rim, grid R-7 — map position (9769.6, 4032.4, -33.4)

Pedducis Chorios was a planet in the Meridian sector in the Pedducis Chorios system. After her initial retirement from the Imperial Starfleet, Admiral Daala led a group of Imperial Remnant settlers on the world. It was also the world were Warlord K'iin resided and was home to several pirate bases.
