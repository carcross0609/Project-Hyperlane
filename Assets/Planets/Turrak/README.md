# Turrak

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Turrak](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Turrak)

Turrak was a planet in the Turrak system within the Outer Rim Territories and Galactic Frontier that was the homeworld of the Scalvi species. During the High Republic Era, it was visited by Jedi Grandmaster Yoda after the Scalvi sent out a call for help.
