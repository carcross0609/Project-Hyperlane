# Caloria

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Caloria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caloria)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Caloria`, Colonies, grid L-13 — map position (777.9, -5060.3, 185.8)

Caloria was a backwater planet in the Caloria system in the Tapani sector of the Colonies that was governed by House Reena and was at the end of the Caloria Run. After Lyxas Sor won the Tapani Open, she was awarded a noble title and Sor's estate on Caloria.
