# Velmor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Velmor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Velmor/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Velmor`, Mid Rim, grid P-7 — map position (6989.9, 3113.3, 102.3)

Velmor was the third, terrestrial planet in the Velm system in the Mid Rim near Myrkr, Obroa-skai and Aquaris.

Its diameter was 11,788 kilometers, and its gravity was slightly below standard, at 0.92. A Velmorian day was 26 standard hours, and its year 297 local days.

Velmor was home to two sentient species: native insectoid Velmoc and Basic-speaking Human colonists known as Velmorians, although the former were nearly extinct by 3 ABY, making up a scant third of a percent of the population.
