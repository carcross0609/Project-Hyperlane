# Ba'Har

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ba'Har](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ba%27Har)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ba'Har`, Outer Rim, grid M-20 — map position (2263.7, -16005.9, -131.4)

Ba'Har was a planet located within the Coyn system of the Outer Rim Territories. The name "Ba'Har" meant "Battle Blade" in the Coynite language. Ba'Har was a multicolored gas giant with a small band of rings, the remains of a moon torn apart by gravitational stresses.
