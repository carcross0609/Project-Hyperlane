# Unidentified tiny planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified tiny planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_tiny_planet)

A tiny planet in an unnamed sector of the Outer Rim orbited at a distance from its tiny sun, resulting in its atmosphere and oceans being unsettled. During the High Republic Era, the planet was located within the Occlusion Zone territory of the Nihil marauders. In 228 BBY, Nihil General Abediah Viess escaped to the planet in a yacht, leaving the vessel trapped alongside a message for Jedi Master Porter Engle, who was following her.
