# Petrakis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Petrakis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Petrakis)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Petrakis`, Inner Rim, grid O-11 — map position (4966.3, -2268.2, -97.8)

Petrakis was the site of the first engagement between Rebel Alliance B-wing starfighters and Imperial forces. During an attack on an Imperial convoy that carried a squadron of Fighters, the escorting TIEs were so panicked when they first encountered the attacking B-Wings that they left formation and fled. So the rebel starfighters managed to destroy the whole convoy consisting of several Freighters, Transports and Shuttlecraft despite the arrival of imperial reinforcements, a full wing of assault gunboats.
