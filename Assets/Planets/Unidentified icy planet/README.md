# Unidentified icy planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified icy planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_icy_planet)

During the Clone Wars, an icy mountainous planet was the site of the battle between Jedi Knights Aayla Secura, Voolvif Monn, Agen Kolar, Anakin Skywalker and several LM-432 crab droids.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified icy planet
- **Moons:** At least 4
- **Atmosphere:** Type I (breathable)
- **Terrain:**

  - Mountains
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
