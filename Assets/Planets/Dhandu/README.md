# Dhandu

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dhandu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dhandu)

Dhandu was a planet located in the Dhandu system, within the Interior and the Expansion Region, in grid square I-8 on the Standard Galactic Grid. It was orbited by at least three moons, including Dhandu 3, whose low-gravity grassland was home to the Dhanduese, a species of avian sentients.
