# Wayyl

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wayyl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wayyl)

Wayyl was a planet that was aligned with the Galactic Republic and was represented in the Galactic Senate decades prior to 32 BBY. During that time, it was represented in the Senate by Senator Dagonet, one of the longest-standing senators of the Republic. The world was the site of a mission where Jedi Master Dooku and his Padawan Qui-Gon Jinn were tasked with retrieving Dagonet's kidnapped son.

Wayyl's surface, appearing gray and dark green from orbit, featured large open fields of grayish dirt and trees, with little other vegetation. The planet also had a large ocean and many massive lakes and rivers.
