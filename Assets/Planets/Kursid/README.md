# Kursid

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kursid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kursid)

Kursid was a planet in Esstran sector of the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy. It was inhabited by a species of primitive humanoids whose sole contact with the greater galaxy was through training missions conducted to the planet by the Order of the Sith Lords.
