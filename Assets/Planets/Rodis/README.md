# Rodis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rodis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rodis)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rodis`, Outer Rim, grid H-16 — map position (-5547.8, -9701.1, -62.1)

Rodis was a primitive and war-torn planet located in the Rodis system of the Sugai sector, one of the subsectors making up the Outer Zuma region within the Outer Rim Territories' Zuma sector. It was the homeworld of the Rodisar species, members of which waged seasonal winter wars.

Nilo was a Rodisar from Rodis who climbed the corrupt military ranks of his homeworld by bribing politicians, eventually attaining the rank of general. At some point by 21.95 BBY, Zygerrian enslavers kidnapped Nilo and his command staff, and the general was ultimately sold to war barons on the planet Rattatak.
