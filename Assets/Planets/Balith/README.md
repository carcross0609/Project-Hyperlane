# Balith

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Balith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balith)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Balith`, Other, grid N-7 — map position (3459.0, 4099.2, -84.6)

Balith was an inhabited planet located in the Balith system that was in the midst of a civil war in 21 BBY. That year, at the request of Balith's people, the Galactic Republic dispatched its Third Legion, under the command of Jedi General Anakin Skywalker, to join the conflict.
