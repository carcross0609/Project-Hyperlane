# Olega

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Olega](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Olega)

Olega was a planet located far from the Core World Coruscant that was home to Olega City where the Jedi Order operated a Jedi temple during the High Republic Era. The Force-sensitive assassin Mae Aniseya visited Olega to kill Jedi Master Torbin, who had taken the Barash Vow at the Olega Jedi temple. Jedi Master Sol, his Padawan, Jecki Lon, Jedi Knight Yord Fandar, and Mae's sister, Osha, confronted her on Olega, although she managed to escape.
