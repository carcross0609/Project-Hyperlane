# Lok Trul

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lok Trul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lok_Trul)

Lok Trul was a gas giant planet located in the Opoku system, a part of the Galov sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet occupied the fifth orbital position around the star Opoku and had thirteen moons. Along with the rest of its system, Lok Trul was located along the Triellus Trade Route.
