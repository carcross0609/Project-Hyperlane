# Unidentified planet (Tholme and Ventress)

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Tholme and Ventress)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Tholme_and_Ventress%29)

A planet was the site of a battle between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems early on in the Clone Wars. During the battle, Jedi Master Tholme fought the assassin Asajj Ventress, and died by her hand.
