# Gepparin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gepparin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gepparin)

Gepparin was a planet in the Gepparin system of the Shelsha sector. It was the headquarters of the BloodScar pirate gang, who had taken control of the large mining outpost on planet's surface during the Galactic Civil War, while the smaller farming outposts remained free. The base was attacked in 0.5 ABY by the Galactic Empire, and was completely destroyed.
