# Bal'demnic

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bal'demnic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bal%27demnic)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bal'demnic`, Outer Rim, grid R-7 — map position (10058.0, 4064.0, -196.0)

Bal'demnic, also known simply as the Unknown Planet, was an ocean planet in the Bal'demnic system in the Auril sector of the Outer Rim Territories. During the later years of the High Republic Era, the planet was yet to be explored by the galaxy at large, with the star system known simply as XA-1958JHR under the Columi catalog system. By the time of the Galactic Empire, the planet had been given the proper name of Bal'demnic.

The planet was the home of "the Stranger," a former Jedi turned Sith. In 132 BBY, Verosha Aniseya was brought to the planet by the Stranger after the Mission to Khofar during the High Republic Era. The world was known for its high tides and was secluded from other planets, which in turn made it the perfect hiding spot for one to train in the arts of the dark side. Darth Plagueis watched from a cave as the Stranger and Aniseya departed from the island and left the planet on the Exile II.
