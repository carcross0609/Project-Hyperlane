# Vesla Two

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vesla Two](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vesla_Two)

Vesla Two was a planet located in the Vesla system of the Outer Rim's Corosi sector. It could originally support life, but the Sith Lord Darth Mekhis tested one of her new superweapons on the planet and caused catastrophic atmospheric damage—enough to draw a colony of shyrack broodlings.
