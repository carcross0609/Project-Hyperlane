# Fyodos

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fyodos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fyodos)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Fyodos`, Outer Rim, grid U-14 — map position (14371.1, -7045.5, -495.5)

Fyodos, also known as Tatrang IV, was an Outer Rim planet that was located within the Tatrang system. The world's surface was devastated during the Great Cleansing, a war of mass destruction that took place in 350 BBY. Prior to the cataclysm, the surface possessed two main seas, one of which was called the Othlorian Sea.
