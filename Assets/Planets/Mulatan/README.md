# Mulatan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mulatan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mulatan)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mulatan`, Hutt Space, grid S-10 — map position (11386.5, -1261.6, -315.2)

Mulatan was a world located in the Mulatan system, a part of the Bootana Hutta area in the Hutt Space portion of the Slice. It was connected to the Gos Hutta and Sleheyron systems by the hyperlane known as the Pabol Hutta.

Mulatan was a guardian world that served as a gateway into the Bootana Hutta, which contained the throneworlds of the clans of the Hutt species. Mulatan was dedicated to the defense of those worlds, and a number of Hutt warships, a rare sight elsewhere in Hutt Space, always surrounded Mulatan, with outsiders being advised not to linger there. Mulatan was also regularly patrolled by the fleet of Troonol, a commander of the Hutt Qunaalac clan.
