# Unidentified planet (Outreachial)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Outreachial)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Outreachial%29)

A planet was the site of the settlement of Outreachial, and another settlement which hosted a bazaar. The planet was home to lizardflies, who lived in cliffs above Outreachial.
