# Soruus

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Soruus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soruus)

Soruus was an Outer Rim Territories world located in the Tion Cluster's Cronese Mandate sector. Originally a part of the Kingdom of Cron, it was a successful trade world during the reign of Cronese King Xim and was also associated with a tradition of gladiatorial spectacles that persisted over the subsequent millennia. The merchant, philosopher, and historian Bleys Harand at one point visited a gladiatorial arena on Soruus in search of a contemporary visual depiction of Xim.
