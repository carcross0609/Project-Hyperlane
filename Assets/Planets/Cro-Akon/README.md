# Cro-Akon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cro-Akon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cro-Akon)

Cro-Akon was a world located in the Thuldin's Grant sector within the Expansion Region of the galaxy. Prior to being taken to the Deep in the Unknown Regions, a Z7 series construction droid was part of a crew that built a settlement in the tundra of Cro-Akon.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Cro-Akon
- **Region:** Expansion Region
- **Sector:** Thuldin's Grant sector
- **System:** Cro-Akon system
- **Coordinates:** J-15
- **Terrain:** Tundra
