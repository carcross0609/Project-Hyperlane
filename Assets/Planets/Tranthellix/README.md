# Tranthellix

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tranthellix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tranthellix)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tranthellix`, Mid Rim, grid L-18 — map position (390.7, -12138.4, 26.6)

Tranthellix was a Mid Rim world situated in the Senex sector. It was the homeworld of the large carnivorous birds known as the Tranthebar mountain rippers. The world was owned by House Petro of the Senex-Juvex region and was the site of sky cities. Eventually racing using the Tranthebar mountain rippers as mounts became established on Tranthellix. Both the rippers and the races were restricted to their homeworld due to House Petro wishing to profit off them, although they became popular in the Core Worlds due to live HoloNet broadcasts.
