# Tatrang II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tatrang II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tatrang_II)

Tatrang II was a searing rock planet with no moons that was located within the Tatrang system, a part of the Tammuz sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It occupied the second closest orbit around the system's star, the red giant Tatrang. The planet was lifeless and was considered to be commercially worthless.
