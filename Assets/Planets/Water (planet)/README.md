# Water

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Water (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Water_%28planet%29)

Water was a planet of the Aakaash system, which was ruled by the Land & Sky Corporation and used for agricultural activity. It exported the Water chickens, which was an ingredient in dishes such as the Water chicken meat pie and Water chicken egg fried rice.
