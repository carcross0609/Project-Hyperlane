# Fibuli

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fibuli](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fibuli)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Fibuli`, Wild Space, grid S-3 — map position (10673.4, 9506.3, 216.1)

Fibuli was a planet in Trianii Space annexed by the Corporate Sector Authority.

It was once the site of a major Trianii colony before three years of fighting turned it into a wasted neutral land. After the Battle of Yavin it served as the location for peace talks between the Trianii and the CSA.

One of the planet's major cities was Knanan.
