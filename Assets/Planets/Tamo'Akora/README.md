# Tamo'Akora

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tamo'Akora](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tamo%27Akora)

Tamo'Akora was a gray-green terrestrial planet in the Amosu system. The largest of the star system's three worlds, it was the only habitable one, though it was constantly wracked with thunderstorms. Major Gohl and a unit of First Order stormtroopers stopped on Tamo'Akora during a training tour, though their shuttle crashed on the planet's surface.
