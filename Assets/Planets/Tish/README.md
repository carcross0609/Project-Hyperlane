# Tish

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tish](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tish)

Tish was a mountainous planet with a stormy climate home to a lightning mining platform crewed by Ugnaughts. A creature native to Tish that fed on the planet's lightning attempted to feed on the lightning collected by the platform, and the Ugnaughts sent out a distress signal, believing the creatures were simply attacking the platform. The Jedi Chareg Yim answered the call and discovered why the creatures had been harassing the platform, and informed the Ugnaught crew.
