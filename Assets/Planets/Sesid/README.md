# Sesid

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sesid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sesid)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Sesid`, Outer Rim, grid P-4 — map position (6479.0, 8350.0, 61.4)

Sesid, the homeworld of the Draedan species, was an aquatic planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. It was a popular tourist destination, and many vacationers traveled across the galaxy to visit its tropical islands. The Galactic Empire conducted pharmaceutical research on Sesid, and deployed a military garrison on the scenic world to protect their assets.
