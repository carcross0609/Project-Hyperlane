# Tel IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tel IV (Minor)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tel_IV_%28Minor%29)

Tel IV was the fourth and outermost planet of the Tel Minor system, located in the Brak sector of the Expansion Region. It was a ball of rock.
