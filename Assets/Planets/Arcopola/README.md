# Arcopola

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arcopola](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arcopola)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Arcopola`, Outer Rim, grid Q-5 — map position (8592.5, 7235.5, -102.8)

Arcopola was a world located in the Arcopola system of the Spadja sector, a part of the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was the sister world of the terrestrial planet Gambolla and was the site of the location Greccia. In 2 ABY, the famous jatz musician Fitz Roi gave a rare Outer Rim concert during the Arcopola Baas Music Festival at Arcopola. Later that week, on the date 37:8:14, the Galactic Resorts correspondent Tanda Marelle reported on the concert from Greccia.
