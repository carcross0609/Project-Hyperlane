# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (space crystals)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28space_crystals%29)

A terrestrial planet was the site of a cave with a deposit of space crystals. At one point, the protocol droid C-3PO and the astromech droid R2-D2 visited the planet while traveling on a single-pilot starship. After obtaining space crystals from the cave, the droids traveled to Tatooine.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified planet
- **Class:** Terrestrial
- **Terrain:** Mountains, slimy swamps and dark woods
- **Interest:** A deposit of space crystals
- **Flora:** A clawed plant
- **Fauna:** A giant serpent
A four-armed creature
- **Otherlife:** An orange fungus
