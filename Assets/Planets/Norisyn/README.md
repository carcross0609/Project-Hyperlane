# Norisyn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Norisyn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Norisyn)

Norisyn was a planet that orbited a blue sun and had at least five moons, including its fifth moon. During the High Republic Era, Norisyn was located within the Nihil marauders' Occlusion Zone territory.
