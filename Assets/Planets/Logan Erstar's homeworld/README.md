# Logan Erstar's homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Logan Erstar's homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Logan_Erstar%27s_homeworld)

A world at one point by the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance had royal families and governing ministers. Logan Erstar, Baron of Filby, was born there.
