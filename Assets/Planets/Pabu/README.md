# Pabu

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pabu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pabu)

Pabu was a small planet dotted with small islands, including a remote island that was a haven for refugees displaced during the Clone Wars and others after. During or after 18 BBY, Clone Force 99 traveled to the location to reach Pabu, which was recommended by Phee Genoa as a place where Cid could not find them and where they could settle down with Omega. Genoa described the location as her "home away from home." It had no spaceports or major industries, and most of the residents were refugees seeking safety from other worlds. Clone Force 99 took a liking to the planet, and decided it would be a good place to settle down.

After rescuing the clone cadets Deke, Stak, and Mox from the planet Setron, Hunter decided to drop the cadets off on Pabu. When the Bad Batch finally were reunited with Omega and Crosshair, they returned to Pabu.
