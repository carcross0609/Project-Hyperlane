# Taskeed

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Taskeed/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taskeed/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Taskeed`, Outer Rim, grid S-7 — map position (11381.3, 3125.1, 234.3)

Taskeed was a planet located within the Outer Rim Territories. Originally known as Taskored, it was absorbed into Xim's empire as he expanded his borders from the Kingdom of Cron. During his campaigns, Xim tested a powerful weapon on Taskeed, leaving behind a massive crater that became known as the Pentakus Crater.

During the Clone Wars, the planet was located within Separatist space.
