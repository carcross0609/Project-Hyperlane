# Uba IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Uba IV/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uba_IV/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Uba IV`, Mid Rim, grid J-6 — map position (-1631.8, 5066.3, -24.1)

Uba IV, a planet in the Mid Rim, was the home of the Ubese people following the destruction of Uba III. At the end of the first Battle of Uba IV, it was heavily poisoned and irradiated.

A millennium later, in 22 BBY, it joined the Confederacy of Independent Systems and was the site of a droid manufacturing plant. Clone Commander Gree faced virus-loaded Octuptarra combat tri-droid and regular tri-droids on the planet. Even after the Clone Wars under the Galactic Empire, the Ubese guarded their world with both droids.
