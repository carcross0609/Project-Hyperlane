# Ueda

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ueda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ueda)

Ueda was a remote planet located in Xappyh sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Ueda was home to Point Ueda, located on a small green island connected via a landbridge to further land, where a landing pad was located. Within Point Ueda was the Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop by the late High Republic Era. Ueda was considered a haven for wanderers, many of whom would frequent the village of Point Ueda. It was inhabited by a diverse range of sentient species, such as humans and aliens including Duraceph, Clarodians, and Drezar.
