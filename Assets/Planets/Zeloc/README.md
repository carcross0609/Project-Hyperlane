# Zeloc

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zeloc](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zeloc)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Zeloc`, Mid Rim, grid P-7 — map position (7344.6, 3766.9, 89.6)

Zeloc was a planet in the fifth orbit around the star Velm. It was the second of two gas giants in the Velm system, located in the Halori sector in the Trans-Hydian portion of the Mid Rim. Zeloc was orbited by eleven moons, and it was also encircled by a ring system.
