# Gonda

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gonda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gonda)

Gonda was a terrestrial planet located in the Gonda system, within the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector of the Outer Rim Territories, in grid square N-18 on the Standard Galactic Grid. Gonda was where smuggler Lando Calrissian nearly lost his droid companion L3-37 to a member of the Hutt species after making a bad bet in a game of sabacc. In 34 ABY, Gonda was included on a map in the book Traveler's Guide to Batuu, written by the historian Eloc Throno.
