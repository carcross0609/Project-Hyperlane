# Regulgo

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Regulgo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Regulgo)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Regulgo`, Mid Rim, grid P-17 — map position (6651.6, -11623.0, -493.6)

Regulgo was a planet located in the Regulgo system, a part of the Ryndellian sector within the Mid Rim and Trailing Sectors regions. Months after Han Solo departed Kamar in approximately 2 BBY, Kamarians tracked him down on Regulgo. While there, the Kamarians attacked Solo and took him captive; however, Solo eventually escaped.
