# Hrasskis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hrasskis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hrasskis)

Hrasskis was a planet located in the Hrasskis system of the Expansion Region. It was the homeworld of the Hrasskis species.

Hrasskis was member of the New Republic at the time of the Black Fleet Crisis and represented at the New Republic Senate by Cian Marook.
