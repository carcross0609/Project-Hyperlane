# Estarvera

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Estarvera](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Estarvera)

Estarvera was a planet in the galaxy that was home to at least one city. During the final weeks of the Nihil conflict, Estarvera was afflicted by the Blight, and much of its population unsuccessfully attempted to flee the world.
