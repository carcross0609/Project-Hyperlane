# Karlinus

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Karlinus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karlinus)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Karlinus`, Mid Rim, grid O-16 — map position (4830.8, -10394.0, -84.6)

Karlinus was a prosperous planet located in the Karlinus system in the Chommell sector of the galaxy's Mid Rim, known for its agricultural industry and its workforce with a high turnover rate. When Padmé Amidala wished to rescue slaves from Tatooine, she and Sabé chose to let them live a new life on Karlinus. Sabé managed to rescue twenty-five slaves, giving them jobs on Karlinus with the help of the planet's governor, Kelma.
