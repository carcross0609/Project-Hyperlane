# Avidich

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Avidich](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avidich)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Avidich`, Unknown Regions, grid E-8 — map position (-9151.2, 1805.6, 71.7)

Avidich was a terrestrial planet of the Chiss Ascendancy, located in the Unknown Regions. After a majority of the population of the Chiss capital of Csilla were forced offworld by changes in the planet's climate, Avidich was one of the planets they were sent to.
