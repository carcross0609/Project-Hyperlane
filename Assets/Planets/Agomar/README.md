# Agomar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Agomar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agomar)

Agomar was a planet in the Agomar system of the Seswenna sector. During the Battle of Agomar, the Confederacy of Independent Systems pitted its Droid Army against the Grand Army of the Republic, with the world eventually falling back onto Republic control. Whaladons were found on the planet during the Clone Wars.
