# Ool

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ool](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ool)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ool`, Unknown Regions, grid E-8 — map position (-9302.6, 1736.8, -180.7)

Ool was a terrestrial planet in the Unknown Regions located in the nation of the Chiss Ascendancy. In 18 BBY, it was inhabited by over two billion Chiss residents and was a fairly popular destination for tourists. Ool's landscape consisted of beaches and mountains, and it was also a farming world with a rich and diverse agricultural base and large and small manufactory groups. Mining operations took place in its mountain spines, including in one of the Ascendancy's three pure nyix metal mines. The city of Pomprey was located on Ool, where a skybus depot was found.

The Ardok Ranch was located in the "wilds of Ool," which belonged to Cohbo'rik'ardok, core name "Borika," and her husband, Bomarmo. The Ardok Ranch was also home to the Seekers' Shadehouse, a facility of the Seekers program that worked with sky-walker navigators. In 18 BBY, Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet caregiver Mitth'ali'astov, core name "Thalias," once visited the ranch to speak to Borika about problems her sky-walker, Che'ri, was having. They spoke about the sky-walker's training process, but Thalias was soon called away to the planet Csilla. She returned to Ool some weeks later, accompanied by Che'ri so the Shadehouse could learn from her problems.
