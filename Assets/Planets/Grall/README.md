# Grall

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grall](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grall)

Grall was a planet located in the Joralla system, a part of the Mektrun sector in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. Grall occupied the second orbital position around its sun, the red giant–type star Jaska, and had no moons. The planet had a poisonous atmosphere and a temperate climate, and was considered to be commercially worthless.
