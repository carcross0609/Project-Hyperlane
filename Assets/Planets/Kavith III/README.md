# Kavith III

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kavith III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kavith_III)

Kavith III was an icy planet in the Kavith system in the Corva sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet was flat, with no mountains to obstruct the sky, and covered with black lakes and rivers. Inhabitants of the planet would shelter underground during blizzards. After a blizzard, the sky would turn green, purple, and pink.
