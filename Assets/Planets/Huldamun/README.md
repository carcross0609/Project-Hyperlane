# Huldamun

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Huldamun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huldamun)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Huldamun`, Outer Rim, grid R-17 — map position (9376.5, -10621.2, -306.1)

Huldamun was a relatively prosperous industrial planet located in the Huldamun system, a part of the Arkanis sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was situated on the Vaschean Way hyperlane, which linked it to the Arkanis and Vasch systems. Huldamun was a part of the informal grouping of planets known as the Regency Worlds, and it chiefly exported mining equipment.
