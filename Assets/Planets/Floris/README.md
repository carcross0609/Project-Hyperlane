# Floris

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Floris](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Floris)

Floris was an uninhabited planet located far on the Outer Rim that was distant from the Outer Rim planet Eiram. In 228 BBY, during the High Republic Era, the sentient carnivorous plants known as the Drengir, believing the blight plague was coming to Eiram, made an attempt to leave the planet. After the Jedi Order discovered this, they agreed to give the Drengir their starship, the Innovator, and set it for a distant world so the plants could escape the blight until it had been stopped. Before departing, however, the Great Progenitor—the Drengir's matriarch—had Republic Defense Coalition Petty Officer Orlis change their destination from Floris to the Mid Rim planet Kashyyyk, where she thought they would flourish.
