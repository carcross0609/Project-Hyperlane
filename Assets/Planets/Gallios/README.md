# Gallios

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gallios](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gallios)

Gallios was a terrestrial planet in the Outer Rim Territories where the droids 0-0-0 and BT-1 traveled to between 3 ABY and 4 ABY and caused mayhem. The rebel astromech droid C1-10P heard on the HoloNet about a homicidal droid, referring to 0-0-0, on Gallios and passed the news onto fellow rebel droid R2-D2, who was looking to recruit a team to help his friend, the protocol droid C-3PO who had become corrupted by the Scourge. Following this, R2 traveled to Gallios where he caught up with 0-0-0.
