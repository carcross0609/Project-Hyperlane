# Reventine

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Reventine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reventine)

Reventine was a planet in the Seswenna sector of the Outer Rim Territories, two star systems away from the planet Eriadu. In 228 BBY, Republic Defense Coalition forces led by Jedi Knights Bell Zettifar and Burryaga Agaburry theorized that Reventine would be the next planet targeted by Nihil marauders led by Storm Melis Shryke. However, Defense Coalition forces instead encountered Jedi Master Avar Kriss, who had escaped the Nihil-controlled region of space known as the Occlusion Zone using Shryke's starship, the Cacophony.
