# Disney Parking Planet

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Disney Parking Planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disney_Parking_Planet)

Disney Parking Planet was a terrestrial planet with a breathable atmosphere, typically used by visitors to the Disney Playground Planet as parking space. Heavily built-out for spacecraft storage, the planet was orbited by at least five variously-colored moons, which were used for wayfinding. While mostly urbanized, the planet featured mountaintops and greenery, though to a limited extent.

The human mother Marge Simpson at one point brought several mothers and their children to the planet on the Jedi Ahsoka Tano's T-6 shuttle in preparation for a visit to the Disney Playground Planet, an occasion during which the alien Mickey skipped in line ahead of them to park in the human Bob Iger's spot.
