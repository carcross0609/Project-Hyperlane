# Ruhnuk

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ruhnuk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruhnuk)

Ruhnuk was a planet located in Wild Space surrounded by an ionic storm. Due to the world's dry, arid climate it was prone to duststorms. Heta Kol and the Hidden Chain had established a base of operations there, sometime after their attack on the Spirit of Vengeance II. The planet was contested between the Hidden Chain and the Mandalorian Trat'ade during the Showdown on Ruhnuk around 3625 BBY.
