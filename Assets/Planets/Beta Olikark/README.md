# Beta Olikark

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Beta Olikark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beta_Olikark)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Beta Olikark`, Outer Rim, grid S-4 — map position (11663.1, 8655.3, 209.1)

Beta Olikark was a planet in the Outer Rim, at the tip of a galactic arm on the other side of the galaxy from the Rayter sector. Its natural stones had intricate patterns and were exported to other worlds for flooring. On one occasion, a diplomat for the New Republic, Kare Fontin was escorted to the planet from Coruscant by New Republic agents.
