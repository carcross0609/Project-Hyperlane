# Squamatan homeworld

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Squamatan homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Squamatan_homeworld)

The homeworld of the Squamatan species was a planet located in the Squamat system in the Mayagil sector of the Outer Rim Territories. At some point during the reign of Emperor Palpatine, the Galactic Empire seized the planet's abundant thorilide deposits and forced its natives into slavery.
