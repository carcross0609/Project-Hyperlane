# Miv'rah

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Miv'rah](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miv%27rah)

Miv'rah was a world located in the Miv'rah system within the Gaulus sector of the Outer Rim and the Slice. It was situated in grid square R-17 of the Standard Galactic Grid, and was linked to the Ab Dalis and Wrea systems by the Corellian Run super-hyperroute. By the time of the Galactic Empire's reign, which began in 19 BBY, Miv'rah was inhabited by omnivorous reptiles known as blurrgs.
