# Vernost

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Vernost (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vernost_%28planet%29)

Vernost was a volcanic planet and the only place in the galaxy where the "miracle liquid" Ceela was found. In 2 ABY, the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire battled over the Ceela in the canyons of the planet.
