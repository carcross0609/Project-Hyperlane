# Heptooine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heptooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heptooine)

Heptooine was a planet located in the south quadrant of the galaxy. During the Kymoodon Era, areas of space grew around Heptooine, as well as Aplooine, Mantooine and Sestooine.
