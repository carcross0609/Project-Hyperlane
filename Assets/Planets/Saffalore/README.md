# Saffalore

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saffalore](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saffalore)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Saffalore`, Outer Rim, grid S-4 — map position (11048.6, 8917.0, -436.4)

Saffalore was a planet in the Corporate Sector, at one end of the Lucaya Cross. It was one of the Sector's most recent boomworlds.

Saffalore's surface was largely covered by hard, uncultivated ground with sickly green grass, hills, and forests. The headquarters of Binring Biomedical Product was located there. Other successful corporations with impressive stock performance were also headquartered there.
