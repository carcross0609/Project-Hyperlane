# Jagga II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jagga II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jagga_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Jagga II`, Colonies — map position (3459.7, -2231.4, -6.0)

Jagga II was a planet located in the Venjagga system of the Colonies. Even after the Battle of Endor, Jagga II produced missiles for the Imperial Navy. It was later a strategic world of the New Republic during the Yuuzhan Vong War.
