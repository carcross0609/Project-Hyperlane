# Natinati

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Natinati](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Natinati)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Natinati`, Wild Space — map position (10745.7, 9704.9, 537.9)

Natinati was a planet in the Natinanti system in Wild Space region of the galaxy. The sacred temple Poborandurannum was located on the planet. At a time prior to 5 BBY, Jabba Desilijic Tiure, a Hutt crime lord, came to the planet to explore Poborandurannum and sacked it during his visit. When Jabba returned to his palace the planet Tatooine in 5 BBY, the Hutt mentioned the planet and the sacking to his Twi'lek majordomo Bib Fortuna.
