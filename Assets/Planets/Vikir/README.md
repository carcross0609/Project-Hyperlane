# Vikir

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vikir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vikir)

Vikir was a snowy planet within the galaxy that, during the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, was a strategic waypoint. The Separatists sent a weapon to Vikir in an attempt to force its people to join their side. It was home to mountains and also a hyperfuel refinery. During the war, Clone Force 99 was sent on a Mission to Vikir in search of the weapon.
