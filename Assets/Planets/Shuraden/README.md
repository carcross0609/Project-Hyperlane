# Shuraden

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shuraden](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shuraden)

Shuraden was a terrestrial planet on the Galactic Frontier in the Outer Rim Territories. It sat in grid square I-18 of the Standard Galactic Grid and was connected by a hyperspace route to the Virgillia system and Sump, homeworld of the Nuknog species. Shuraden was inhabited by the Ximpi, a diminutive winged sentient species. During the High Republic Era, Jedi Master Sskeer took his Padawan Keeve Trennis to Shuraden so she could undergo her Jedi Trials.
