# GUHL-J03870

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GUHL-J03870](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GUHL-J03870)

GUHL-J03870 was an Outer Rim Territories planet located in the Trilon sector. It had a rocky surface and was home to a type of six-limbed creature. At one point between 28 ABY and 34 ABY, the First Order Star Destroyer Finalizer visited the planet, with one of its troop transports bringing back from the surface one of the creatures as a stowaway. That led to a First Order stormtrooper squad being dispatched to GUHL-J03870, where they encountered multiple other members of the species, resulting in the unit departing the planet.
