# Meris II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Meris II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meris_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Meris II`, Outer Rim — map position (3017.8, -11258.5, 299.5)

Meris II was the second planet in the Meris system of the Elrood sector.

A hot jungle world, Meris II was home to primitive, heat-resistant life forms—some corporations investigated the possibility of genetically engineering these bacteria to produce molecules that could be incorporated into heat shielding. It was believed the bacteria could be used for commercial uses, though no specific products were marketed.
