# Tepasi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tepasi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tepasi)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tepasi`, Core Worlds, grid L-9 — map position (1342.2, 271.1, 19.1)

Tepasi was a planet located in the Tepasi system in Tagge Space, a region of the Core Worlds. Tepasi was the homeworld of Grand General Cassio Tagge, a member of the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War who was born into the noble House of Tagge. It shared its name with Tepasi taffy.
