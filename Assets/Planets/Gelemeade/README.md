# Gelemeade

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gelemeade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gelemeade)

Gelemeade was a planet in the galaxy with at least four moons. During the Cold War, a routine scan of Gelemeade's fourth moon detected signs of simple organic life beneath its uninhabitable surface, and further investigation found a variety of exotic life underground.
