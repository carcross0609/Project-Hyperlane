# Jhensrus

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jhensrus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jhensrus)

Jhensrus was a jungle frontier world in the Trax sector. The inhabitants of the planet were often confronted by the native creatures, hence a population of fighters and survivors was shaped.
