# Uvena Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Uvena Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uvena_Prime)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Uvena Prime`, Outer Rim, grid M-18 — map position (2612.2, -12160.9, -155.1)

Uvena Prime, also known as Uvena, was a planet that was the homeworld of the Shistavanen species. Located in the Uvena system in the Seswenna sector of the Outer Rim Territories, it was a world of forested continents and vast oceans. Shistavanens most often remained on their homeworld, distrusting outsiders, though some natives left for the wider galaxy.
