# Monta

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Monta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Monta)

Monta was a planet in the Mid Rim Territories. Located in the Monta system within the Daimar sector, the planet was situated in grid square Q-16 of the Standard Galactic Grid. Between 22 BBY and 19 BBY, many people including a Bith were killed during the Monta Massacre on Monta.
