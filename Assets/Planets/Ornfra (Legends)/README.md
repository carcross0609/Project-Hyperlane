# Ornfra

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ornfra/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ornfra/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ornfra`, Unknown Regions, grid F-8 — map position (-8351.4, 2290.1, -66.1)

Ornfra was a planet located in the Ornfra system of the Unknown Regions' Chiss Space. The hyperlane known as the Path of the Houses linked the world to the Naporar and Sharb systems, and a smaller hyperspace route also connected it to the Noris system. Ornfra was an outlying colony of the sentient Chiss species and was home to one of the Ruling Families that ruled over the Chiss Ascendancy, the government of the Chiss.

At some point by 28 ABY, Ornfra's resident Ruling Family employed hives of the sentient Killiks as a work force. As a result, the Family fell out of power that year and became a casualty of a civil conflict against rival Ruling Families over the former's continued reliance on the Killiks.
