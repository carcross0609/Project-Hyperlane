# Unidentified green planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified green planet (Wookiees)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green_planet_%28Wookiees%29)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Unidentified green planet`, Other, grid K-18 — map position (-469.4, -12320.0, 11.4)

There existed a green planet in the vicinity of the desert world Tatooine, in the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy. At some point during the Galactic Civil War, the heroes of the Rebel Alliance Han Solo and Chewbacca were ambushed by the enemy Galactic Empire on that planet. Although outnumbered, the Rebels were saved at the eleventh hour by a contingent of Wookiee warriors who had made their home on the planet.
