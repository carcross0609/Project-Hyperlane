# Vadran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vadran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vadran)

Vadran was a planet in the Vadran system in the Rago sector of the Mid Rim Territories that was the homeworld of the Gergilla species. The surface of the planet was covered in colorful jungles and forests of purple plants where gergillas roam free. The planet also seems to possess mountains and a single settlement in the form of a building near them. From space, a planetary ring could be seen encircling the planet. At some point after or in 34 ABY, the Safari Droid Aree and his cambot crash-landed on Vadran and came in contact with a gergilla.
