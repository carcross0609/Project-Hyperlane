# Teptixii

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Teptixii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teptixii)

Teptixii was a blue-green planet located in the Teptixii system of the Unknown Regions. In 0 ABY, the Galactic Empire Task Force Admonitor commanded by Captain Voss Parck engaged alien Warlord Nuso Esva's eight Firekiln warships and twenty-seven escort ships alongside Captain Calo Drusan's Imperial Star Destroyer Chimaera over the planet. Nuso Esva—posing as Imperial Court member Lord Odo aboard the Chimaera—advised Drusan to divide his ships by coming behind them, which was successful in prompting them to retreat to hyperspace.
