# Abtin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Abtin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Abtin)

Abtin was a planet in the Abtin system in the Danjar sector of the Outer Rim Territories. When the Nihil marauders expanded the Stormwall barrier that surrounded their Occlusion Zone territory, Abtin was one of the worlds that fell into the Nihil's new borders. Kitrep Soh, the son of Galactic Republic chancellor Lina Soh, and his boyfriend Jom Lariin, who had been sightseeing on the Outer Rim prior to the Stormwall's initial formation, visited Abtin, where they learned about its annexation by the Nihil.
