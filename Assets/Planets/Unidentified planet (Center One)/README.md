# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Center One)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Center_One%29)

A planet was the location of the Galactic Empire's Center One, a reeducation facility for political prisoners. The Partisans Karama and Nankry traveled to the planet to plant bombs at Center One and destroy it.
