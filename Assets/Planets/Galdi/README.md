# Galdi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Galdi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galdi)

Galdi was a planet located in an eponymous star system within the Western Reaches portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Situated in the Reithcas sector, a subsector of the Sullust sector, at the coordinates M-18 on the Standard Galactic Grid, it was the homeworld of the human Tuck Hybecks. In 5 BBY, a convicted Hybecks had to state their homeworld while being processed by an Imperial shoretrooper on the planet Niamos. The shoretrooper, in response to this information, directed Hybecks to a transport destined for the astronomical object Belsavis, in order for them to serve their sentence.
