# Xeron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xeron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xeron)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Xeron`, Inner Rim, grid M-14 — map position (2007.4, -7196.8, -18.6)

Xeron was a planet located within the Xeron system, a part of the Trailing Sectors portion of the Inner Rim. Xeron was the site of a battle between the Rebel Alliance and Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War circa 3 ABY, where the Imperial Army attacked Rebel ground troops with a new warbot to devastating effect.
