# Lantillies

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lantillies/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lantillies/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lantillies`, Mid Rim, grid P-8 — map position (6464.8, 1829.2, 230.2)

Lantillies was a planet in the Lantillies system and the sector capital of the Lantillian sector in the Mid Rim. Located along the Perlemian Trade Route and at the head of the Randon Run, Lantillies was heavily involved in the shipping and shipbuilding industries, and its Human natives were well-known for their emphasis on reliability over elegance. Lantillies was conquered by the Honorable Union of Desevro & Tion early in galactic history, though the planet was a member of the Galactic Republic through much of the government's history. Lantillies later became a stronghold of the New Republic during the Yuuzhan Vong War of 25–29 ABY.
