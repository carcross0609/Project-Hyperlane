# Tatrang VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tatrang VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tatrang_VI)

Tatrang VI was a ringed gas giant–type planet with twenty-three moons that was located within the Tatrang system, a part of the Tammuz sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It occupied the sixth orbital position around the system's star, the red giant Tatrang. The planet was considered to hold little economic value.
