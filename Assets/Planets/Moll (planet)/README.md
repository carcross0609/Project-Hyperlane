# Moll

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moll (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moll_%28planet%29)

Moll was a hot rock planet with no moons. It was located in the Rannon system, a part of the Instrop sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet occupied the closest orbit to its star, the yellow-white Rannon.
