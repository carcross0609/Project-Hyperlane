# Indupar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Indupar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Indupar/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Indupar`, Mid Rim, grid M-17 — map position (2133.8, -10944.4, 401.7)

Indupar was a Mid Rim world in Ado sector. It was the capital of the Induparan Crown Worlds. It was ruled by the Induparan Monarchy and House Indupar, including King Dahon Indupar during the New Order. During the Clone Wars, Indupar was located within Separatist space. The Imperial Governor of the Induparan Crown Worlds was Mirash Peet. Imperial Commodore Soleric commanded the 16th Escort Force, escorting merchant convoys through the Induparan to protect them from the Free Lance.
