# Ord Tellarom

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Ord Tellarom/LEGO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Tellarom/LEGO)

Ord Tellarom was a desert planet located in the galaxy that was home of a Clone Wars battle site. During the Imperial Era, Ord Tellarom was the home of a Zillo Beast, and the location where the B1-series battle droid R0-GR was reactivated by the Freemakers.
