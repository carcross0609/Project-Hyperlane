# Lyran IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lyran IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lyran_IV)

Lyran IV was an Outer Rim Territories farming planet located in the Quiberon sector. It was home to the parasites known as Lyran bloodslugs. The planet served as a notable trans-shipment point of illegal spice, with the local crime lords investing into the local agricultural communities in order to maintain cover for their illicit operations.
