# Jofoger

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jofoger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jofoger)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Jofoger`, Outer Rim — map position (11398.2, 1933.3, -149.3)

Jofoger was a planet located in the Jofoger system of the Periphery, a region in the Tharin sector of the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was situated on the Sisar Run hyperlane.

Risiev Credal was a Human born around 28 BBY who hailed from Jofoger and had the appearance common among the population of Humans that had long lived in the area of the Sisar Run. Credal eventually joined the Rebel Alliance and traveled offworld.
