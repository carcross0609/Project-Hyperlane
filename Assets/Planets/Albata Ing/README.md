# Albata Ing

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Albata Ing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Albata_Ing)

Albata Ing was a terrestrial world located in the third position of the Felucia system, a star system located in the Outer Rim Territories' Thanium sector. During the Clone Wars, this area of space was contested over by the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems.
