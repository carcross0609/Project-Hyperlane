# Dur Sabon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dur Sabon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dur_Sabon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dur Sabon`, Outer Rim — map position (-1879.1, -12136.2, 247.7)

Dur Sabon was a planet inhabited by an eel-like species of the same name. Above the planet’s ocean level rose cities on large rocky platforms supported by descending pillars. In the sky above the cities, a series of spheres of unknown purpose hovered. Around 33 BBY, the young Ootoolan princess Foolookoola was escorted to Dur Sabon for her safety from the nearby planet Ootoola. Friends of the late royal family gave her shelter and expressed hope that she would one day return home safely.
