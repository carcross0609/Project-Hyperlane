# Ord Dorlass

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Dorlass](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Dorlass)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ord Dorlass`, Mid Rim — map position (-1585.4, 4931.9, -82.7)

Ord Dorlass was a Mid Rim world located in the Iskin sector. Serving as a Galactic Republic Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost during the Pius Dea Era, around 3643 BBY it was affected by an epidemic of a dangerous disease. During the Galactic Civil War it served as a stopping point in the popular interstellar race known as the Dahvil-Fodro Hyperspace Promenade.

Immediately prior to one such race, when handing a data module over to the racer and undercover Rebel Alliance operative Siro Simito for secret delivery to the celestial body Fait d'Fait, his fellow Rebel agent Kaini posed as a holovid reporter from Ord Dorlass. When Simito and his competitor Seeg stopped on the world during the race itself, they staged a fistfight in a local nightclub that resulted in a boost to their popularity.
