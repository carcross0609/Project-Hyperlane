# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Sith Empire)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Sith_Empire%29)

During the Dark Lord of the Sith Naga Sadow's preparations to attack the Galactic Republic in the year 5000 BBY, he gathered his military forces from all across the Sith Empire, including from a planet that orbited a yellow sun and had at least two moons. The world had rust-colored mountains on its surface. A stone fortress had been built near those mountains, and during Sadow's military buildup, a large number of Sith soldiers of the Massassi caste embarked on waiting Sith personnel carriers in the vicinity of the structure. The amassed Sith ground forces also included several bipedal war beasts with artillery emplacements on their backs.
