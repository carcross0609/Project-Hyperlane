# Kor Nasirii; (The Bootana Hutta region, with Kor Nasirii represented by the number 1)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kor Nasirii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kor_Nasirii)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kor Nasirii`, Hutt Space, grid S-10 — map position (11120.7, -858.4, -139.5)

Kor Nasirii was a world located within the Kor Nasirii system, a part of the Bootana Hutta region of Hutt Space, and was part of the Slice section of the galaxy. It was connected by a hyperlane to the Kor Vosadii system.

Kor Nasirii was the throneworld of the Nasirii kajidic. During the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the resurgent Sith Empire in 3643 BBY, Kor Nasirii housed databases belonging to members of the Hutt species. The information contained within the databases was used to blackmail prominent figures throughout the galaxy.
