# Tanthior

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tanthior](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tanthior)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tanthior`, Core Worlds, grid M-11 — map position (2595.7, -2575.1, -64.2)

Tanthior was a planet located in the Corellian sector of the Core Worlds.

Tanthior was a jungle planet, populated with a variety of dangerous fauna and flora.

The atmosphere of Tanthior was poisonous to most species not native to the planet.

Small groups of scientists lived on Tanthior in biologically-sealed habitats, studying the planet's unique ecology.
