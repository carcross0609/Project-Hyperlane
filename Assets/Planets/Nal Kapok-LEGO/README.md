# Nal Kapok

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Nal Kapok/LEGO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nal_Kapok/LEGO)

Nal Kapok was a jungle planet where the Kyber Saber hilt was hidden by followers of the Jedi Master Baird Kantoo. During the Clone Wars, a battle between the Confederacy of Independent Systems and Galactic Republic was fought on Nal Kapok. During the Imperial Era, following the Battle of Hoth, the planet was home to a small Imperial base. Darth Sidious and Darth Vader dispatched the Sith agent Naare on a mission to recover the Kyber Saber's hilt from Nal Kapok. While there, she encountered the Force-sensitive Rowan Freemaker and convinced the boy that she could train him in the ways of the Force and that they could find the Kyber Saber together.

Nal Kapok was fully covered in jungles and seas. The planet was also home to many living organisms, such as dianogas.
