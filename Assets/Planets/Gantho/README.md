# Gantho

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gantho](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gantho)

Gantho was a planet located in the Gantho system. The planet was represented in the New Republic Senate by Senator Arastide. In 13 ABY the New Republic was in the middle of an investigation into Loronar Corporation and their supposed depletion of the world's resources.
