# Jarvanam

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jarvanam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jarvanam)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Jarvanam`, Outer Rim — map position (2941.5, 4655.2, 232.7)

Jarvanam was a planet located within the star system of the same name, a part of the Outer Rim Territories' Astal sector. Around the year 10 ABY, it served as the homeworld of the Baradis School of Technology, a covert criminal training organization and member of the Tenloss Syndicate.
