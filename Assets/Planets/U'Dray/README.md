# U'Dray

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [U'Dray](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/U%27Dray)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `U'Dray`, Outer Rim, grid O-18 — map position (4921.0, -12291.8, 41.3)

U'Dray was a planet situated in the U'Dray system of the Hook Nebula, a part of the Trailing Sectors portion of the Outer Rim Territories. As part of the Galactic Civil War's Hook Nebula campaign waged at some point by the time shortly after the Battle of Yavin, the Qeimet fleet of the Galactic Empire carried out an assault at U'Dray. The Rebel Alliance initially achieved a rare victory over the fleet's Scimitar assault wing, although the Empire nevertheless ultimately prevailed in the engagement.

The assault wing's commanding officer, Captain Tomax Bren, mentioned the ongoing assault of U'Dray in a datamemo sent to the wing's squadron commanders. The message was subsequently included in a compilation of Imperial documents made by Rebel historian Major Arhul Hextrophon for Alliance Supreme Commander Mon Mothma and other Rebel officers.
