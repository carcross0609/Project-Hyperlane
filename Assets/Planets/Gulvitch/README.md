# Gulvitch

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gulvitch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gulvitch)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gulvitch`, Outer Rim, grid Q-6 — map position (7578.5, 5536.2, -320.1)

Gulvitch was a mining world located in the Gulvitch system. It was situated in the Loop Worlds area of the Gordian Reach sector within the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The hyperlane known as the Junction-Tierell Loop connected it to the Arkuda and Bronsoon systems.

Several centuries prior to 43 ABY, Gulvitch was colonized by members of the Bagmim and Povanarian species, which originated on the nearby worlds of Xochtl and Povanaria, respectively. By that year, Gulvitch was a struggling colony that was only visited by spacers who had been experiencing misfortunes and were looking for inexpensive cargoes to pick up.
