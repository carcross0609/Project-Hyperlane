# Targusian

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Targusian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Targusian)

Targusian was a terrestrial planet located in the Targusian system within the Rayter sector of the Outer Rim Territories and Western Reaches. It was situated in grid square J-19 of the Standard Galactic Grid and was inhabited by both individuals and fire lizards during the High Republic Era.

Between around 170 BBY and around 160 BBY, the Mirialan Jedi Master Vernestra Rwoh visited Targusian as she traveled the galaxy as a Jedi Wayseeker and gathered together a bloom of fire lizards. Around 160 BBY, while Rwoh was on the planet Cerifisis, she mentally recalled the time she had corralled the fire lizards on Targusian, noting that while it was one small and simple act she had carried out within the past few years, it had made the lives of the planet's inhabitants better.
