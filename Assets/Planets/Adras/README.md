# Adras

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adras](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adras)

Adras was a Galactic Empire–held world located in the Outer Rim Territories' Seswenna sector. Shortly before the Battle of Endor, a team of Sullustan Alliance Intelligence officers was tasked with meeting another group of Rebel Alliance agents in order to lead a group of combat starships to join the main Rebel Fleet.

Before the two teams could meet outside the city of Nezan on Adras, the Sullustans were arrested by Imperial authorities and held at a detention center near Nezan. The other Rebel group eventually discovered their allies' fate and helped them escape the detention facility and leave Adras, allowing them and the combat vessel group to rendezvous with the Rebel Fleet.
