# Diab

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Diab](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Diab)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Diab`, Wild Space, grid J-6 — map position (-2839.1, 5765.4, -337.5)

Diab was a planet located within the Diab system of the Wild Space. Its sixth moon was orbited by the secret Jedi prison known as the Prism. Diab's electrical storms wreaked havoc with sensors and no transmissions were allowed in or out of the prison.
