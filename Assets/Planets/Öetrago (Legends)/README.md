# Öetrago

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Öetrago/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%C3%96etrago/Legends)

Öetrago, or Oetrago, was a terrestrial planet located within the Öetrago system, a part of the Mayagil sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was covered with what could almost be objectively viewed as unattractive plant life.

At some point by 14 ABY, Han Solo visited Öetrago.

After the end of the Yuuzhan Vong War in 29 ABY, many Ithorian refugees settled on Öetrago following the devastation of their homeworld of Ithor at the hands of the Yuuzhan Vong.
