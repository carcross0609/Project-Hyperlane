# Clak'dor II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Clak'dor II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clak%27dor_II)

Clak'dor II was the second-innermost planet within the Colu system, on the Outer Rim of the galaxy. After the destruction of Clak'dor I, the world became the star system's innermost.
