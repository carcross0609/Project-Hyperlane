# Unidentified lava planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified lava planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_lava_planet)

A lava planet was located in the galaxy. Under the rule of the Galactic Empire, a rebel cell known as the Spectres, came in their starship Ghost, and picked up some supplies, destroyed machines and a com array, liberated prisoners of the Empire, and destroyed a TIE fighter.
