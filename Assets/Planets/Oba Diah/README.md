# Oba Diah

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oba Diah](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oba_Diah)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Oba Diah`, Outer Rim, grid T-10 — map position (13272.4, -216.6, 463.6)

Oba Diah was a planet located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories that was the homeworld of the Pyke species. Located on the end of the Kessel Run, the Pyke Syndicate managed the distribution of spice from Kessel as it was delivered throughout the galaxy from the high fortress of the Pyke Syndicate on Oba Diah.
