# Neijaia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neijaia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neijaia)

Neijaia was a planet in the Neijaia system of the Expansion Region covered in swamps was home to many wild species, including a native species who spoke the Neijaian language. Poe Dameron and BB-8 visited the planet, but were chased by the natives. They were assisted by Kori Ong, who helped them leave the planet.
