# Aq Vetina

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aq Vetina](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aq_Vetina)

Aq Vetina was a planet inhabited by a number of humans. During the Clone Wars, the Confederacy of Independent Systems attacked a small settlement on the planet, killing the population. The Mandalorians of Death Watch intervened in the attack, fighting back against the Separatist droid forces. Aq Vetina was the homeworld of the young human Din Djarin, who was rescued from the attack by a Mandalorian warrior and raised as a foundling in the Mandalorian Creed.
