# Gargon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gargon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gargon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Gargon`, Outer Rim, grid O-7 — map position (4830.2, 4320.6, -4.5)

Gargon was a mountainous planet in the Gargon system within the Mandalore sector of the Outer Rim and New Territories, in grid square O-7 of the Standard Galactic Grid. It was settled by Old Mandalorians exiled from their homeworld Mandalore by the pacifistic New Mandalorians after a civil war between around 41 BBY and around 39 BBY. On Gargon, they lived as mercenaries working for the highest bidder, as Mandalorians had done for millennia.

In 7966 C.R.C., the marauder Enfys Nest and her Cloud-Riders raided an Imperial medical spice cache on Gargon, stealing medicinally processed spice while leaving the potentially more valuable raw spice. Afterward, the Galactic Empire began to piece together information that Nest was amassing resources to fund a rebel uprising. In 34 ABY, a file on the Gargon raid was included in Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious, a collection of case files compiled by Commissioner Exantor Divo, head of law enforcement on the planet Hosnian Prime.
