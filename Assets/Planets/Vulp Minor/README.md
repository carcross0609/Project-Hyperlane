# Vulp Minor

**Continuity:** Legends

Vulp Minor was a terrestrial planet located in the Vulpter system of the Deep Core. The planet featured a frozen environment, and its surface consisted of rock. Vulp Minor occupied the fifth orbital position around its star, Vulpter, and it had three moons.
