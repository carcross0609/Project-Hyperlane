# Watassay

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Watassay](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Watassay)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Watassay`, Outer Rim, grid R-7 — map position (9662.8, 3644.5, 419.5)

Watassay was a small, inhabitable planet located in the Five Points system of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. At some point, the Galactic Empire enacted a blockade around it, wanting to claim the resources there. Furthermore, they attempted to starve the citizens of the planet. In 1 BBY, Blue's partisans traveled to Watassay with crates of foodstuffs for the people, with the crew helping to unload the supplies before distributing it though a small makeshift town.
