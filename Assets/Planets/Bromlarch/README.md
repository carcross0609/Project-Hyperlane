# Bromlarch

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bromlarch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bromlarch)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bromlarch`, Mid Rim, grid O-16 — map position (5296.1, -9284.2, -129.1)

Bromlarch was a planet located in the Vish sector of Mid Rim. It was represented in the Galactic Senate by Senator Caelor Gaans. Bromlarch had a planetary aqueduct system that was damaged during a series of groundquakes. Galactic Republic Senators Padmé Amidala and Rush Clovis both had three nights to rescue Bromlarch's aqueduct system, thus saving the planet from famine, by creating the Mid Rim Cooperation Motion. Senator Mina Bonteri and Jedi Master Depa Billaba accompanied them.
