# Tabor V

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Tabor V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tabor_V)

Tabor V was a planet located in the galaxy. It was the homeworld of the Xyrass. The planet was controlled by a pirate organization known as the Snake's Guild. The Galactic Empire knew its coordinates, but the Imperial authority thought it was unoccupied.

The only city on the planet was Karthren.
