# Coccia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coccia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coccia)

Coccia was a terrestrial planet, located in the Felucia system of the Thanium sector, part of the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Coccia occupied the first orbital position around the star Felix and had one moon. The planet featured a hot environment and had a surface of scorched rock.
