# Sakifwanna

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sakifwanna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sakifwanna)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Sakifwanna`, Hutt Space, grid T-11 — map position (12050.8, -1771.2, -112.0)

Sakifwanna was a planet that was located within the Sakifwanna system in Hutt Space. The planet lay on the Elgit–M'Hanna Corridor hyperlane that connected it to the worlds of Elgit and M'Hanna. A smaller hyperspace route linked Sakifwanna to the Cyax system, and another hyperlane connected the world to the planet of Saki.

Sakifwanna, the name of which meant "Fourth Saki" and was shared with "fwanna," the Huttese word for "four," was a colony world of the sentient Sakiyan species that was native to Saki. Sakifwanna served as a nursery world for the Sakiyan Collective, with Sakiyan shepherds breeding creatures for the repopulation of Saki's game preserves. The fate of Sakifwanna following the invasion of the Hutt Space by the extra-galactic Yuuzhan Vong species was unclear to the wider galaxy.
