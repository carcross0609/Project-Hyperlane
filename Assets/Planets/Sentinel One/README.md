# Sentinel One

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sentinel One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentinel_One)

Sentinel One was a toxic volcanic planet with dark rocky terrain located in the galaxy. Covered in bright yellow rivers of acid and once plundered for minerals, Sentinel One was the location of huge antennas that littered the planet's landscape, collectively making up a planet-wide listening station used by Skakoans.

During the New Republic Era, the racing organization Galactic League held unsanctioned racing matches utilizing various repulsorcraft including speeders on the planet.
