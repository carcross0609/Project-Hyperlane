# Eiattu 6

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eiattu 6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eiattu_6)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Eiattu 6`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (1330.9, -11308.9, -533.4)

Eiattu 6 was a planet in the Ado sector of the Mid Rim. Commonly called simply Eiattu by its inhabitants, it was ruled by a monarchy.
