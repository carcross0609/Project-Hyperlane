# Malenko

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Malenko](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malenko)

Malenko was a planet located in the Mid Rim Territories. Part of the Malenko system in the Eidoloni sector, the planet appeared to be bathed in green mists and covered in bleak water bodies with crystalline shards sprawling through its surface. Long before the Imperial Era it was inhabited by the Andon Host species. During the Clone Wars the Separatist general Kazameer established a secret base on Malenko.
