# Yalln

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yalln](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yalln)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Yalln`, Outer Rim, grid R-5 — map position (9905.7, 6221.4, -1.1)

Yalln was a terrestrial industrial world situated in the Yalln system, a part of the Mortex sector in the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It featured corporate complexes, outside of which stretched vast unsettled territories.

The Human Clenna grew up on Yalln, spending a lot of time during his youth exploring the wilderness outside the corporate complexes with his trusty tal. At some point, Clenna traveled offworld and served in the Imperial Army of the Galactic Empire, but he eventually returned to Yalln. He grew both restless and tired of his life there, however, and ultimately joined the offworld Galactic Outdoor Survival School as a staff member at some point by 2 ABY.
