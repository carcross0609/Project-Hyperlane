# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Rhydonium heist)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Rhydonium_heist%29)

A planet in the galaxy that was many parsecs away from Markez Moon. The planet had a pipeline that transported Rhydonium during the Imperial Era. In 3 BBY Wilmon Paak, Saw Gerrera and his Partisans used a a piece of equipment to steal an amount of Rhydonium from the pipeline.

## Planetary data
