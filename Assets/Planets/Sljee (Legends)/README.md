# Sljee

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sljee/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sljee/Legends)

Sljee was an Expansion Region planet located in the Ehosiq sector. Species living on it, which included the sentient Sljee, primarily relied on the sense of smell. Between 12 ABY and 13 ABY, Sljee was part of the New Republic.
