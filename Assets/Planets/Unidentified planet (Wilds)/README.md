# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Wilds)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Wilds%29)

This planet was the location of the Wilds, a swampy overgrown jungle. A settlement was located seventeen kilometers from the Wilds. The planet had a native predator, the corvaj. During a training patrol, Ranna Gorjaye and Calson crashed on the planet after Calson crowded the formation and clipped Gorjaye's wing. Calson was killed by a corvaj after refusing to follow Gorjaye's commands on the ground.
