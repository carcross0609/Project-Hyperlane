# Ord Pentala

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Pentala](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Pentala)

Ord Pentala was a world located in the Ord Pentala system of the Inner Rim's Western Reaches portion. Ord Pentala served as an Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost for the Galactic Republic during the Pius Dea Era, a period of time that lasted between 12,000 BBY and 11,000 BBY, despite it being located just outside the borders of Republic space.
