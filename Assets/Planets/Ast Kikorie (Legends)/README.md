# Ast Kikorie

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ast Kikorie/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ast_Kikorie/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ast Kikorie`, Outer Rim, grid H-16 — map position (-5215.5, -10203.9, 28.9)

Ast Kikorie, also called "Down Below," was a planet in the Moddell sector located in the Outer Rim Territories of the galaxy. It was a Galactic Republic astrocartographic research outpost with a ring of orbiting telescopes connected to Kikorie City by a skyhook. The planet was abandoned by the Republic at the onset of the Clone Wars in 22 BBY.
