# Unidentified triple-ringed gas giant

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified triple-ringed gas giant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_triple-ringed_gas_giant)

A gas giant planet was located in the Unknown Regions and had three sets of orbital planetary rings and a thick, roiling atmosphere. By 19 BBY, an excursion liner of the Chiss Ascendancy's Boadil family ventured too close to the gas giant, and the starship's captain ignored the engineers' warnings of electronic interference from the planet's magnetic fields.

The liner was caught in the planet's gravity well, and the Chiss patrol ship Boco under the command of Senior Commander Mitth'raw'nuru, core name "Thrawn," attempted to rescue it. With the assistance of the patrol cruiser Parala under the command of Senior Captain Irizi'ar'alani, ("Ziara"), Thrawn lightened the liner by shooting off its luxury wings, pulling the ship out of the gravity well with tractor beams and saving all eight thousand passengers aboard.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified triple-ringed gas giant
- **Region:** Unknown Regions
- **Class:** Gas giant
- **Interest:** Three sets of planetary rings (in orbit)
