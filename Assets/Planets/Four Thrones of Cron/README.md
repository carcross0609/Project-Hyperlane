# Four Thrones of Cron

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Four Thrones of Cron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Thrones_of_Cron)

The Four Thrones of Cron was a term used in the Kingdom of Cron around 25,130 BBY, near the end of Xer VIII's reign. It referred to the worlds Chandaar, Cadinth and Raxus Prime of the Kingdom of Cron, and the still-independent Desevro of the Livien League.

The first three worlds were conquered by Xer. When Xer retired, his son Xim finally conquered the Livien League in 25,127 BBY. With the Four Thrones under one crown, the Kingdom of Cron became the Empire of Xim.
