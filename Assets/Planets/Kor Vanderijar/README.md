# Kor Vanderijar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kor Vanderijar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kor_Vanderijar)

Kor Vanderijar was a planet located in the Bootana Hutta region of Hutt Space. It was the throneworld of the Vanderijar kajidic.

Kor Vanderijar was located in close proximity to Kor Jiramma, the throneworld of the Jiramma kajidic. This proximity led the Vanderijar to often partner with the Jiramma.
