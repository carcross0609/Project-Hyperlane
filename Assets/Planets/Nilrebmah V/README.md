# Nilrebmah V

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Nilrebmah V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nilrebmah_V)

Nilrebmah V was a terrestrial world planet in the Nilrebmah system. Covered in seas filled with fish and crustaceans, the planet's atmosphere was oxygen-rich and breathable by the majority of species; though the planet never evolved sentient life.
