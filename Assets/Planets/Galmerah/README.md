# Galmerah

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Galmerah](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galmerah)

Galmerah was a graveyard planet located in the galaxy. During the time of the Galactic Empire, a number of bounty hunters visited Galmerah while hunting down Nakano Lash, a fellow bounty hunter who disappeared after an apparent betrayal years prior. While on Galmerah, hunters Bossk and Beilert Valance entered into a skirmish after the latter discovered map coordinates that supposedly led to Lash.
