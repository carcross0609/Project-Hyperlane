# Sorotarr VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sorotarr VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sorotarr_VI)

Sorotarr VI was a planet located in the Sorotarr system, a part of the Yushan sector in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. It was the site of the establishment known as the Game and was a favorite destination for gamblers. Similarly to worlds such as Adarlon and Harloen, by the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, Sorotarr VI was commonly frequented by people such as corporate executives; Imperial Moffs, Fleet Admirals, and nobles; and dishonest individuals, who were all traveling incognito and were seeking out entertainment.
