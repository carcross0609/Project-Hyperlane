# Pii system's seventh planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pii system's seventh planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pii_system%27s_seventh_planet)

The Pii system's seventh planet was a frozen ball of ice that orbited the Pii sun. The cold planet was located in the Arkanis sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Like the rest of the Pii system, it was a seven-day hyperspace journey away from the Perlemian Trade Route and lay along the Old Corellian Run and the Trans Gulf Route hyperlanes.

The planet, along with the rest of the Pii system, was originally located on the super-hyperroute known as the Corellian Run. When that hyperspace route changed its course at some point between around 11,000 BBY and 4000 BBY and as a result no longer ran through the Pii system, the system's seventh planet remained on the Old Corellian Run.
