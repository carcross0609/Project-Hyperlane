# Iceberg Four

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iceberg Four](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iceberg_Four)

Iceberg Four was the eighth planet in the Calamari system and also the farthest from its sun. It was used as a Galactic Alliance staging area during the Defense of Mon Calamari.
