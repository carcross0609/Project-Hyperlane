# Elliirad

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Elliirad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elliirad)

Elliirad was a world located within the Airam sector of the Outer Rim Territories. A space battle between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire took place near the planet during the Galactic Civil War.
