# Takobo

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Takobo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Takobo)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Takobo`, Other, grid R-5 — map position (9715.5, 6632.4, -129.1)

Takobo was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories that was home to a number of emigrated species, including Ithorians. The peaceful world boasted colorful architecture with a mix of modern and classical designs. During the Galactic Civil War, two Imperial Inquisitors, the Seventh Sister and the Fifth Brother, captured a Force-sensitive Ithorian named Pypey from the Ithorian settlement known as Hammertown. The infant was rescued by Ezra Bridger and other members of the rebel crew of the starship Ghost.
