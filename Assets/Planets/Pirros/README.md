# Pirros

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pirros](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pirros)

Pirros was a planet in the Fenril sector of the Inner Rim Territories that was once a member of the Galactic Republic. After the end of the Galactic Civil War, Pirros was among the worlds of the sector that formed the Fenril Consortium in opposition to the New Republic.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Pirros
- **Region:** Inner Rim Territories
- **Sector:** Fenril sector
- **Coordinates:** I-13
- **Demonym:** Pirrosi
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
