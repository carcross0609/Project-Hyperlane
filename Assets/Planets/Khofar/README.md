# Khofar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Khofar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Khofar)

Khofar was a planet in the Outer Rim Territories that featured forest and jungle terrain. During the High Republic Era, the Wookiee Jedi Master Kelnacca lived a solitary life in the jungles of Khofar.
