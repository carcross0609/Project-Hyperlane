# Ingae

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ingae](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ingae)

Ingae was a terrestrial planet located in the Ingae system in the Rseik sector of the Outer Rim Territories, on the edge of Wild Space. The planet was the home of the Collective, a secret organization of scientists. It appeared on few maps, and on the maps it did appear on, it was shown to be a gas giant.
