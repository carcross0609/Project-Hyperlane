# Gadrilam

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gadrilam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gadrilam)

Gadrilam was a little-known forested planet. A workforce of indentured Nagai was forcefully relocated to the world, overseen by members of the same species. Around 6 ABY, Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker traveled to Gadrilam in search of the Crown of Verity, a powerful Jedi artifact that had fallen into the hands of the Nagai collector Ibo Truddispal. Skywalker became entangled in local affairs during his mission, combating the forces of Gallana Venk and the Zantarrk Gang. The Jedi Knight retrieved the Crown but was unable to stop Zantarrk from burning a Nagai village to the ground.
