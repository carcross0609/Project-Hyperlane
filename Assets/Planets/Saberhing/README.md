# Saberhing

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Saberhing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saberhing)

Saberhing was a planet in the galaxy. The smuggler Tregga theorized that before being captured by the Galactic Empire on Mimban, the Wookiee Chewbacca raised an orphaned Han Solo, and they together made their living on planets such as Coonee and Saberhing.

When bartender Tivoche Bilure heard the theory on Vandor, he wrote it down in his journal. Bilure did not believe it, however, given that Tregga's implants were known to malfunction. Despite Tregga's theory, Solo and Chewbacca met for the first time on Mimban after they were captured by the Empire.
