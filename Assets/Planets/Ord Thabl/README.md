# Ord Thabl

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Thabl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Thabl)

Ord Thabl was a world located in the Ord Thabl system. It was situated in the Halla sector, a part of the Kashyyyk Region in the Slice portion of the Mid Rim. Ord Thabl served as an Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost for the Galactic Republic during the Pius Dea Era, a period of time that lasted between approximately 12,000 BBY and 11,000 BBY. When the Republic launched the Third Pius Dea Crusade against the Hutt species in 11,939 BBY, Republic forces moved from Ord Thabl toward Hutt Space, including the Cyborrea system in the region's vicinity.
