# Randon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Randon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Randon/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Randon`, Mid Rim, grid Q-10 — map position (7896.9, -523.3, 213.1)

Randon was a planet from which the Randoni originated. It was a large business world with crowded bazaars and elegant corporate towers. It was at the intersection with several major trade routes, including the Trax Tube, Ootmian Pabol, Pabol Sleheyron, and the Randon Run.

During the Clone Wars, the planet was located within Separatist space.

The world was overrun by the Yuuzhan Vong shortly after they arrived in Hutt Space.
