# Kwenn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kwenn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kwenn)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kwenn`, Mid Rim, grid R-10 — map position (9785.2, -1242.6, -457.1)

Kwenn was a planet located in the Mid Rim and the Slice. The Kwenn Space Station was situated above the planet. During the High Republic Era, Kwenn was saved from the brink of destruction by the Jedi Order, and as part of the Grand Renewal, its Gem Cities of Kwenn were transformed into an island paradise.
