# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Holowan droid foundry)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Holowan_droid_foundry%29)

The Holowan droid foundry of Holowan Mechanicals was located on a pink terrestrial planet. Between 2 BBY and 4 ABY, Grand-engineer Lony Coleema was kidnapped by the IG-88 assassin droid IG-88B and brought onto the world inside the foundry.
