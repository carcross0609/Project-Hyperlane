# Endoraan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Endoraan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endoraan)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Endoraan`, Outer Rim, grid K-4 — map position (-1340.1, 8037.8, 127.1)

Endoraan was a planet that was an endpoint of the Relgim Run, opposite Mantooine.

After the Battle of Endor and the defeat of the Galactic Empire, the world remained under the control of Grand Moff Ardus Kaine and his Pentastar Alignment and later the Imperial Remnant under Admiral Gilad Pellaeon.
