# Hinda

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hinda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hinda)

Hinda was a world located in the Hinda system, within the Airam sector of the Outer Rim Territories. A space battle between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire took place near Hinda during the Galactic Civil War.
