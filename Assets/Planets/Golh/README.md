# Golh

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Golh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Golh)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Golh`, Outer Rim — map position (-1236.4, -15470.2, -164.5)

Golh was a planet located within the Golh system of the Outer Rim Territories. An aquatic planet, it was owned by Hydrospeare Corporation, who used Golh as a test site for its aquatic projects, commonly aquatic weapons and military vehicles.

A group of Sedrian Rebel operatives managed to reach Golh and ambush a test team. Only Dr. Sere Lure, a designer supervising operations, managed to escape, and she has since recommended any other aquatic world as test site.
