# Dolstan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dolstan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dolstan)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dolstan`, Outer Rim — map position (4782.6, 4114.6, 22.9)

Dolstan was one of the planets of the Pimbrellan League. It was the planet originally settled by the Pinacists, and was dominant in League politics. It was a dry planet, covered in savannas, deserts and land-locked salt lakes. The land was fertile, and Dolstan provided much of the food for the League.
