# Boro-borosa III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Boro-borosa III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boro-borosa_III)

Boro-borosa III was a planet in the Boro-borosa system with three suns. The system was a minor Garonnin possession with mining operations in three asteroid belts and the planetary ring. It was rumored to be home to a fabled lost treasure left by Hutt Royalty or the Rakatan Infinite Empire.

Boro-borosa III's prisoners would be sent to the nearby planet of Atron, which was known throughout the galaxy for its prisons.
