# Shadda-Bi-Boran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shadda-Bi-Boran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadda-Bi-Boran)

Shadda-Bi-Boran was a planet located within the Toblain sector in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. By 228 BBY, it was located within the Occlusion Zone created by the Nihil marauders. In 39 BBY, the planet's sun was dying. Padmé Amidala, her father Ruwee Naberrie, and Onaconda Farr visited the planet as part of a relief group and helped evacuate the entire populace. The evacuees, however, proved unable to adapt to life elsewhere because they were unable to replicate Shadda-Bi-Boran's environment properly and died. In 28 BBY, Amidala told Senator Rush Clovis about her visit to the planet and her attempt to help its populace. The planet's sun soon expanded into a nebula, with the system becoming known as the Shadda Nebula.
