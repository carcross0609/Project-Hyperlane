# Atoa

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Atoa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atoa)

Atoa was an ice planet with frigid temperature. It was located in the Atoa system within the Expansion Region's Ghost Nebula sector in grid P-10 of the Standard Galactic Grid. During the Clone Wars, between 22 to 19 BBY, the Confederacy of Independent Systems set a prison camp on Atoa. Karama, a former reporter of the Galactic Republic, was one of the prisoners before eventually joining the Partisans after getting rescued by the resistance fighter Saw Gerrera.

In 9 ABY, during the competitive sporting show Hunters of the Outer Rim, the human Hunter Sentinel did an interview where he reminisced on his time as an Imperial stormtrooper when he was deployed on an ice planet, though he could not recall if it was Atoa or other ice planets Hoth and Gigor. On that ice planet, Sentinel claimed to have fought victorious against a bonzami or a wampa and also found a Permafrost crystal, which he later used to supposedly defeat a Fireworm during his next deployment.
