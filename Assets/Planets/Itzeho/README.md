# Itzeho

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Itzeho](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Itzeho)

Itzeho was one of the planets located within the Mygeeto system of the Albarrio sector, situated in both the Outer Rim Territories and New Territories regions. An ice ball, Itzeho was the sister world of the planet Mygeeto, which was often mistaken for a simple world like the former. Itzeho was also the last planet in the Mygeeto system, and lay at K-5 on the Standard Galactic Grid.
