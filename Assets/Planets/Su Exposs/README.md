# Su Exposs

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Su Exposs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Su_Exposs)

Su Exposs was the fourth planet orbiting the star Bright Jewel in the Bright Jewel system. Located in the Bright Jewel sector of the Mid Rim, it was a gas giant.
