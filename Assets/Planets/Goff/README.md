# Goff

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Goff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Goff)

Goff was a world located in the Goff system, within the Airam sector of the Outer Rim Territories. A space battle between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire took place near Goff during the Galactic Civil War.
