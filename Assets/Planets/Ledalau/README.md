# Ledalau

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ledalau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ledalau)

Ledalau was an oceanic planet located in the Ledalau system in the Core Worlds. In its past it had continents, but over time the water level rose leaving only a few thousand square meters of land which was all located within one tiny archipelago in the upper latitude of the planet where it was cold and foggy. By 230 BBY it had already been more than a millennium since the water level had risen and swallowed most of the planets land. Few relics of ancient civilizations remained and the planet possessed few resources and even less infrastructure and thus was left mostly alone.

Elzar Mann visited the planet with Orla Jareni when he took a retreat from the Jedi Order.
