# Garen IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Garen IV/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Garen_IV/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Garen IV`, Outer Rim — map position (3932.4, -13333.9, 125.9)

Garen IV was a planet near Hargeeva in the Expansion Region. It was the location of an Imperial penal colony.

Stevan Makintay was imprisoned at Garen IV by his father, who disapproved of his son's proposed marriage to Ketrian Altronel.
