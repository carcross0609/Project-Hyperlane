# Exo (planet)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Exo (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exo_%28planet%29)

Exo was a legendary planet that may or may not have existed. The philosopher Deamos Na-Coth believed in the existence of the planet, and founded a series of expeditions known as The Cabal to search beyond the Outer Rim Territories to find the planet.
