# Arporatal-Lanin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arporatal-Lanin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arporatal-Lanin)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Arporatal-Lanin`, Mid Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-27.5, -12166.5, -11.9)

Arporatal-Lanin was a rugged world located in the Arporatal-Lanin system of the Juvex sector, a part of the Senex-Juvex region in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. A hyperlane connected it to the Thull's Vault system. Arporatal-Lanin's surface featured jagged, tumbled ranges of mountains as well as deep, forested valleys.

Arporatal-Lanin was under the control of the aristocratic Ancient Houses of the Senex-Juvex and was known for its native sentient species known as the Arpor-Lans, who excelled as hunters, trackers, and scouts. The Rebel Alliance scout Lieutenant Deeve was an Arpor-Lan from Arporatal-Lanin, where he learned numerous valuable skills related to scouting and tracking.
