# Balagash

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Balagash](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Balagash)

Balagash was a gas giant in the Expansion Region that was orbited by seventeen moons, including Ma'a, Rhoremone, and Balumbra. During the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, which lasted from 22 BBY to 19 BBY, battles were fought on all of Balagash's moons. In 19 BBY, Imperial Intelligence operative Haki Zeophrine accompanied Senator Bail Organa to Balumbra and noticed Balagash burning with a pustulant green light.
