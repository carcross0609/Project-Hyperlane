# Ridley

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ridley](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ridley)

Ridley was a planet in the Inner Rim Territories that the bounty hunter Boba Fett visited to undertake a job. Whilst on the planet, he was forced to defend his client from Bossk.
