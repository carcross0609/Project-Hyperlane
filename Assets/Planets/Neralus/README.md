# Neralus

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neralus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neralus)

Neralus was a gas giant in the Haileap system, part of a binary planet along with Nixus, which was also a gas giant. Both planets were orbited by the jungle moon Wevo. One of the two planets was brown and orange striped in appearance.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Neralus
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
- **Sector:** Tunka sector (Dalnan)
- **System:** Haileap system
- **Coordinates:** I-19
- **Suns:** 2
- **Moons:** Wevo
- **Class:** Gas giant
