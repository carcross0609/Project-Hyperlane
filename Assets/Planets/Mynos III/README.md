# Mynos III

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mynos III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mynos_III)

Mynos III, or Mynos Three, was an agricultural planet in the Dalchon sector of the Outer Rim Territories, under the protectorate of the Pacithhip of Shimia. The planet was red in color, possessing two planetary rings that crossed at the equatorial lines. During the High Republic Era, Mynos III was occupied by the Nihil marauders, despite not being within the group's Occlusion Zone territory, leading the Pacithhip Lord Salk to petition the Galactic Republic and Jedi Order for help.
