# Nothoiin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nothoiin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nothoiin)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Nothoiin`, Outer Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-1128.3, -12646.4, 174.0)

Nothoiin was a planet in the Nothoiin system in the Anoat sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It had a moon on which ur-diamonds were mined. During the Iron Blockade, a makeshift medical center on Nothoiin had to resort to smugglers to acquire supplies from Gerrenthum. The planet's native sentients species, the gold-skinned Nothoiins, once ruled the entire Anoat sector.

Baron Shkan-Shkan Yew, one of the richest men in the galaxy by 19 BBY, was the ruler of an obscure Nothoiin colony world.
