# Nilrebmah VI

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Nilrebmah VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nilrebmah_VI)

Nilrebmah VI was once a planet in the Nilrebmah system. After some cataclysm in the galaxy's ancient past, the planet was reduced to rubble, forming a debris cloud where the planet once was. An exogorth was recorded as making its abode deep within the asteroid cloud.
