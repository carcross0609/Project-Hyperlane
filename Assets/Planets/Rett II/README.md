# Rett II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rett II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rett_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rett II`, Mid Rim, grid Q-11 — map position (8475.9, -2408.7, 106.9)

Rett II was the second planet in the Rettna system, and was the site of a Jante mining colony. It was attacked and captured by the Jante's sector neighbors, the Freda. During the New Order, the decades-old feud between Jante and Freda erupted into full-scale war as each sabotaged the other's mining operations. Imperial forces were called in to put an end to the hostilities.
