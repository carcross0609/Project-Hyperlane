# Adega

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adega](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adega)

Adega was a planet located in the Auril sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was the source of the earliest lightsaber crystals, known as Adegan crystals, and was also home to venomous creatures known as Adegan eels.
