# Technology-deficient planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Technology-deficient planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Technology-deficient_planet)

The "technology-deficient planet" was a technologically primitive planet inhabited by Humans, or Near-Humans. Their technology had not developed beyond the use of horses, slugthrowers, and metal swords. They had no advanced technology whatsoever.

Han Solo once crash-landed there and ended up solving a national crisis of one of the kingdoms of the planet.

## Planetary data

- **No Grid Check:** 1
- **Name:** Technology-deficient planet
- **Atmosphere:** Type I (breathable)
- **Fauna:** A four-eared horse
- **Species:** Human/Near-Human
