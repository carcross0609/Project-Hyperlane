# Necropolis

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Necropolis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Necropolis)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Necropolis`, Core Worlds, grid J-13 — map position (-1584.2, -4721.9, 51.5)

The Necropolis was a junk planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. It was connected to the worlds of Rattatak and Ponemah Terminal by a hyperspace route. During their journey to find Jedi Master Luke Skywalker, Rey, Chewbacca, and R2-D2 were forced to land on Necropolis after a mechanical issue.
