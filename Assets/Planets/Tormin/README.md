# Tormin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tormin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tormin)

Tormin was a planet in the galaxy. During the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire, Colonel Frellder went missing there, and investigation found that Frellder's own men had killed him due to his harsh style of command.
