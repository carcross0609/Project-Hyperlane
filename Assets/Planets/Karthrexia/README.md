# Karthrexia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Karthrexia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karthrexia)

Karthrexia was a world in the Kriz sector of the Outer Rim. Around 5 ABY, a thick survival tent was necessary to withstand the harsh conditions on the Karthrexian glaciers.
