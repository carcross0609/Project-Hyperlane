# U-Tendik

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [U-Tendik](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/U-Tendik)

U-Tendik was a planet in the Mid Rim used as a refuge by the Rodian Tetsu Clan. The landscape was dominated by tall Tendril trees, which bore large nuts of the same name. The Rodians Nok and Teeku herded arboreal creatures known as "Tree-Botts" for milk and meat. Other native creatures included bo-toads and manka cats.
