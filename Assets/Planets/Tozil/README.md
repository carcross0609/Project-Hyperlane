# Tozil

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tozil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tozil)

Tozil was a terrestrial planet located in the Mid Rim Territories. Around 149 BBY, at a Jedi temple on Tozil, the Jedi Yarzion Vell was dying. His Padawan, Ordea, was worried about who would complete their training, so Vell told them about a similar experience he had in which Jedi Master Kelnacca completed his training after his previous Master, Lulad, had died. Kelnacca then arrived on Tozil and Vell died soon after. After his death, Ordea and Kelnacca held a funeral for Vell, and Kelnacca agreed to complete Ordea's training.
