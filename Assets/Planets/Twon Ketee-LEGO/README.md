# Twon Ketee

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Twon Ketee/LEGO](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twon_Ketee/LEGO)

Twon Ketee was a planet where Han Solo, Chewbacca and Varond Jelik were sent to capture Rathtars. It was inhabited by frogs, spiders and carnivorus plants.
