# Unidentified swamp planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified swamp planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_swamp_planet)

A swamp planet was the home of Jedi Master N'Kata Del Gormo resided during the Golden Age of the Old Republic when a young Yoda and his human friend crashed near where he was living, the Jedi Master then took them in for training.
