# Lehon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lehon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lehon/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lehon`, Unknown Regions — map position (-8507.8, 1811.6, -173.1)

Lehon, also referred to as Rakata, Rakata Prime, and the Unknown World, was the homeworld of the Rakata species and the capital of their Infinite Empire.
