# Tinopai

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Tinopai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tinopai)

Tinopai was a terrestrial planet located in the galaxy. During the reign of the Empire, three stormtroopers stole portraits of Darth Vader and Darth Sidious from an art gallery on Tinopai for Maul, whom they believed to be Vader.
