# ZeHeth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ZeHeth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZeHeth)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `ZeHeth`, Mid Rim, grid N-16 — map position (3184.5, -10022.4, -396.5)

ZeHeth was an Mid Rim world located in the ZeHeth system of the Dustig sector in grid square N-16 of the Standard Galactic Grid. The super-hyperroute known as the Hydian Way connected it to the Malastare system as well as the Expansion Region's Pax system. Around 9 ABY, while the astronomer Kh'ymm was searching her archive of worlds on the Observatory Moon in order to determine the potential location of the lost planet At Attin, ZeHeth's name was listed on her computer screen among other worlds.
