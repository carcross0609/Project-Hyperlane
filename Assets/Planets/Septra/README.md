# Septra

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Septra](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Septra)

Septra was a small farming planet located in the Septra system in the Kathol sector on the edge of the Outer Rim Territories. During the High Republic Era, the arms dealer Nilsson Summach illegally manufactured 47 lightsaber nullifiers in the Kolnare Mine on Septra. The planet was known for having abundant mineral deposits, including alercite and hynaxium.
