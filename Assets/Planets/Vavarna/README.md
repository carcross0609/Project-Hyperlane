# Vavarna

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vavarna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vavarna)

Vavarna was a planet where the Lerct Historical Institute, including Beaumont Kin, undertook an archaeological dig. During the dig, Kin was almost eaten by plant life on the planet. During his next dig with the institute on the Mid Rim Territories planet Yoturba in 21 ABY, Kin recalled his experiences on Vavarna, and even shuddered at the mention of the name of the planet.
