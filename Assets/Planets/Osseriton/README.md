# Osseriton

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Osseriton](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Osseriton)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Osseriton`, Unknown Regions — map position (-8288.8, 1655.6, 319.7)

Osseriton was a penal world located not far into the Unknown Regions in the Osseriton system within grid square G-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid. It shared its name with Osseriton Assemblages, the manufacturer of the Oubliette-class transport, which was originally designed with the singular purpose of ferrying criminals from surrounding star systems to a hard labor camp in the penal colonies of Osseriton, where convicts were sent to be forgotten about while extracting resources from the harsh landscape.

At some point by 4 ABY, the dark side mercenaries known as the Knights of Ren were hired on a no-questions-asked assignment to raid the prison and free its occupants. As there were no specific restrictions in the assignment, the knights also raided the hangars of the prison's security forces and stole one of the prison transports, the Oubliette-class vessel Night Buzzard. The group then modified the starship for personal use, removing any livery of the Osseriton correctional facilities.
