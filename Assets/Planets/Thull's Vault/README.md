# Thull's Vault

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thull's Vault](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thull%27s_Vault)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Thull's Vault`, Mid Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-15.0, -12121.3, 368.7)

Thull's Vault was a planet located in the Thull's Vault system of the Juvex sector, a part of the Senex-Juvex region in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. It was connected via hyperlane to the Arporatal-Lanin and Talhovi systems. The planet's surface featured luminescent sands.

Thull's Vault shared its name with Thull Vandron, a wealthy entrepreneur from the Core Worlds planet Axum who spearheaded the settlement of the Senex-Juvex around 1000 BBY. The former world eventually came under the control of the region's aristocratic Ancient Houses, and, according to legend, a noble of House Carbinol once scuttled an ancient fleet of treasure ships under the glowing sands of Thull's Vault.
