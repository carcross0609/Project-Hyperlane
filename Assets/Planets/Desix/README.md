# Desix

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Desix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Desix)

Desix was a planet that seceded from the Galactic Republic prior to the Clone Wars and joined the Confederacy of Independent Systems. After the reorganization of the Galactic Republic into the Galactic Empire, Imperial forces initially attempted to peacefully oust the governor of the planet, Tawni Ames. She refused and took the Imperial-picked governor deployed to the planet, Grotton, hostage. As a result, the Empire deployed a squad of clone stormtroopers led by Clone Marshal Commander Cody, which successfully quelled the resistance, eliminated Ames, and installed Imperial rule onto the planet.
