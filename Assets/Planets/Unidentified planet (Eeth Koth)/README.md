# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Eeth Koth)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Eeth_Koth%29)

A planet covered in deserts was located somewhere in the galaxy. It was known for the alcoholic beverage known as dust juice. Eeth Koth hid on the planet during the Great Jedi Purge, but was hunted down and killed by Darth Vader.
