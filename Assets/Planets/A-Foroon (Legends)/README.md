# A-Foroon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A-Foroon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-Foroon/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `A-Foroon`, Outer Rim, grid R-16 — map position (9900.2, -9732.7, -31.4)

A-Foroon was a windy world of scraggly forests located in the A-Foroon system, a part of the Arkanis sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. It was situated on the Triellus Trade Route hyperlane, which linked it to the B-Foroon and Piroket systems.

A-Foroon, B-Foroon, and C-Foroon together constituted the Foroon Worlds, which were remade by members of the Hutt species into throneworlds during the century immediately preceding the dissolution of the Galactic Republic in 19 BBY. A-Foroon was specifically controlled by the the Hutt Jahibakti kajidic, in the name of which gangs of the Yahk-Tosh aliens operated on the throneworld.
