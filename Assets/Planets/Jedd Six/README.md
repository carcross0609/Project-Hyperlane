# Jedd Six

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jedd Six](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedd_Six)

Jedd Six was a planet that occupied the sixth orbital position in the Jedd system of Wild Space. During the last years of the Galactic Republic, Lexi Fernandin became the first individual to scout the planet. While the Human and his companions were exploring Jedd Six, an incident occurred. Later, at some point following the Battle of Yavin, Fernandin experienced another, similar incident that made him recall his time on Jedd Six.
