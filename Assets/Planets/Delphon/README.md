# Delphon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Delphon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delphon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Delphon`, Outer Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-1012.0, -13024.4, -77.0)

Delphon was a planet in the Anoat sector of the Outer Rim Territories. During the Galactic Empire's Iron Blockade, the criminal Trade Spine League wanted to quietly move a shipment of crystals from the planet Burnin Konn to Delphon, but had to first deal with the Black Hole Gang. At another point during the blockade, an anonymous party hired a young smuggler to break into Delphon's senatorial compound and convince one of the aides to secretly work against the Empire. One notable settlement on Delphon was Delphon City, which housed at least one cantina, at least one docking bay, and Cage's Tech Emporium. The planet was also described as having more than one moon.
