# The Graveyard of the Dragons

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [The Graveyard of the Dragons](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Graveyard_of_the_Dragons)

The Graveyard of the Dragons was the putative planet where all Duinuogwuin traveled to die. The Graveyard of the Dragons of Xuaquarres was believed to be The Graveyard of the Dragons.
