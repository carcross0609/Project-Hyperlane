# Havricus

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Havricus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Havricus)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Havricus`, Colonies, grid N-12 — map position (3148.4, -4370.6, 12.4)

Havricus was a world located in the Havricus system of the Colonies. It was situated on the super-hyperroute known as the Corellian Run, which served as the dividing line between the regions known as the Slice and the Southern Core and linked it to the Pencael system as well as the Inner Rim's Iseno system.

During the years leading up to the beginning of the Clone Wars in 22 BBY, Havricus became one of the several worlds of the galaxy that experienced an infestation of stone mites—arthropods feeding on silicates and complex metals. An issue of the CoCo District Edition of the HoloNet News released on the date 13:5:9 noted that Havricus reported damage to the world's structures from the infestation.
