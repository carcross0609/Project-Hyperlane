# Hebekrr Major

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hebekrr Major](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hebekrr_Major)

Hebekrr Major was a geologically unstable terrestrial planet in the Hebekrr system within the Ojoster sector of the Outer Rim Territories' and New Territories. It was situated in grid square N-7 of the Standard Galactic Grid. It was the first of two habitable planets in its star system, Hebekrr Minor being the second. The planet was inhabited long before 22 BBY, but its developing geological instability led Hebekrr Major to be abandoned. Expeditions occasionally visited to retrieve ancient technology and cultural artifacts from Hebekrr Major's broken surface, but it was unsafe to remain on the planet for long periods of time.
