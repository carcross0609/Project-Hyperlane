# Unidentified planet (old Hamma's place)

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (old Hamma's place)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28old_Hamma%27s_place%29)

A planet was the location to where Jyn Erso fled after her visit to Tamsye Prime went awry. Akshaya Ponta met Erso on that planet as Erso was looking to escape around 5 BBY.

Old Hamma's place, a location owned by Hamma, was located on the planet.
