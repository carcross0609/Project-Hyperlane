# Tharnaka

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tharnaka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tharnaka)

Tharnaka was a planet that housed the artifact Tharnakan Story Stone. It was the native planet of the Tharnakan species. Long before 233 BBY, the climate on Tharnaka changed, causing a flood to descend on the entire planet. As a result, the vast plains and hilly landscapes of the planet disappeared without a trace.
