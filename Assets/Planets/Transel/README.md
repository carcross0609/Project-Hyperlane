# Transel

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Transel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Transel)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Transel`, Colonies, grid L-13 — map position (881.7, -5808.9, 113.5)

Transel was an obscure, backwater terrestrial planet located in the Transel system, a part of the Southern Core portion of the Colonies. It was situated on the hyperlane known as the Shipwrights' Trace, which connected it to the Bassadro, Fondor, and Surric systems.

A small starship engineering company was based on Transel. At a single manufacturing plant, the firm, which at one point was acquired by the larger Core Galaxy Systems corporation, for many years produced the Dynamic-class freighter, which had been designed by 4000 BBY. By 3956 BBY, Transel was a member of the Galactic Republic.
