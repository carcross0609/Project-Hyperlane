# Maun Digitalis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Maun Digitalis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maun_Digitalis)

Maun Digitalis was a world located in the Maun Digitalis system, a part of the Northern Dependencies portion of the Colonies. At some point after the establishment of the Galactic Empire in 19 BBY, the elite Imperial stormtrooper unit 501st Legion fought on Maun Digitalis.
