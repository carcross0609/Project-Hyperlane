# Japeal

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Japeal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Japeal)

Japeal was a terrestrial planet with a temperate climate in the Japeal system in the Kanz sector of the Outer Rim Territories, situated along the Veragi Trade Route. During the High Republic Era, its climate and plentiful water made it attractive to settlers, drawing in members of dozens of species who began constructing their own settlements, businesses, and infrastructure, including three orbital space stations. Because Japeal possessed communications equipment which allowed it to reach Coruscant quickly and was far away from the Starlight Beacon space station, Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro chose it as one of four planets he targeted for attacks intended to lure away the Jedi stationed at Starlight.
