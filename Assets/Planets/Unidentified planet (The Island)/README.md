# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (The Island)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28The_Island%29)

A planet surrounded by a maelstrom was covered in oceans and contained an island. The inhabitants of The Island, who were stranded on the planet, referred to themselves as Castaways.
