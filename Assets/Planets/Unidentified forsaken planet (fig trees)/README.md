# Unidentified forsaken planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified forsaken planet (fig trees)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_forsaken_planet_%28fig_trees%29)

An unidentified planet had fig trees in its native flora. In the last decades of the Galactic Republic, that planet was mostly forgotten, and the Ithorian Tendau Bendon took interest in the preservation of its "mourning" fig trees.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Unidentified forsaken planet
- **Flora:** Fig trees
