# Hindasar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hindasar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hindasar)

Hindasar was a planet in the Hindasar system of the Expansion Regions' Corpheli sector. After escaping the prison on Delrian, Doctor Cornelius Evazan purchased a forged medical license and established himself as a personal surgeon. Charging exorbitant fees with the promise of droid-free surgery, Evazan scarred hundreds of beings, with several dozen dying of their wounds.
