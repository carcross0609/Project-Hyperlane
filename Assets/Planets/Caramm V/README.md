# Caramm V

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Caramm V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Caramm_V)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Caramm V`, Other, grid R-8 — map position (9193.9, 2221.8, -66.3)

Caramm V was a planet in the Caramm system within the Talcene sector of the Mid Rim Territories and the Slice. It was situated in grid square R-8 of the Standard Galactic Grid.

The world was ruled by the silken-tongued individual Ado Eemon and occupied the fifth orbital position in its star system. Around the time of the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and Confederacy of Independent Systems, in which Eemon allied with the Confederacy, the planet had become ravaged as its ruler deployed a personal fleet of gunships to eliminate threats to his power.
