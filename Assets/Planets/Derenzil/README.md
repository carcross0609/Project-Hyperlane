# Derenzil

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Derenzil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Derenzil)

Derenzil was a swamp planet located in the Wild Space Derenzil system in grid square S-18 of the Standard Galactic Grid. A short-lived civil war occurred there in which both sides were supplied with highly specialized, aquatic sonic weapons by Geonosians from the Outer Rim planet Geonosis. The weapons were created in a factory near the Stalgasin hive on Geonosis that was equipped with a fighter hangar due to the risk of the project; however, by 22 BBY, the factory was abandoned.
