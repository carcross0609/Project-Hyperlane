# Artesia

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Artesia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Artesia)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Artesia`, Expansion Region, grid P-11 — map position (7020.3, -2464.6, 227.5)

Artesia was a planet located within the Artesia system, a part of the Hangshan sector in the galactic regions known as the Slice and the Expansion Region. The Artesian space collage art form originated on the planet.
