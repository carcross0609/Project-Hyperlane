# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Depatar)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Depatar%29)

A planet was the site of the black-market spaceport Depatar. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, the Imperial Special Forces unit Inferno Squad traveled to the planet from the Lost Souls asteroid field in preparation for the arrival of the crew of the bounty hunter T'onga after they damaged her ship's hyperdrive, knowing that Depatar was the closest spaceport the crew could travel to for repairs and a new hyperdrive. Once the crew arrived at the spaceport, T'onga and 4-LOM set out to purchase a new hyperdrive but were ambushed by Inferno Squad. After T'onga was knocked out by Agent Gideon Hask, Commander Iden Versio posed as T'onga, and returned to the Edgehawk with 4-LOM, though the crew believed it was actually T'onga. As she approached the ship, Versio informed Agent Del Meeko to prepare to fire on the crew.
