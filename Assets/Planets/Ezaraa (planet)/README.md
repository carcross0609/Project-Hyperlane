# Ezaraa

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ezaraa (planet)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ezaraa_%28planet%29)

Ezaraa was a planet that was the homeworld of the Ezaraa species. The Dominion of the Ezaraa, a group of Ezaraa who wished to overthrow the Galactic Empire and reign across the galaxy, were based on the planet, but the land they possessed was limited to only two continents.
