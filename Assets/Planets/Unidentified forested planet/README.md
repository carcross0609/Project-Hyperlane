# Unidentified forested planet

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Unidentified forested planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_forested_planet)

Three months after the Battle of Hoth, Darth Vader led an operation to this forested planet in search of his son, Luke Skywalker, although Skywalker had in fact died on Hoth, of which Vader was unaware. Vader's forces left behind a path of fiery destruction among native settlements, but their efforts turned up no signs of Skywalker.
