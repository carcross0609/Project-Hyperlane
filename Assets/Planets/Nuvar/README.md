# Nuvar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nuvar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nuvar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Nuvar`, Mid Rim, grid N-17 — map position (3267.9, -10677.6, -379.0)

Nuvar was a jungle planet in the Mid Rim near the modern Hydian Way. In 3701 BBY, Freia Kallea's scoutship Hope crash-landed on uncharted Nuvar after it was crippled by the Neimoidians Thoax and Farge Osaax, leaving her stranded for six standard years. Banu Hydia located her on the planet and found her in a wild state.

Kallea's experiences on Nuvar were dramatized in Acts II and III of Trebian Shullos's epic opera The Kallea Cycle, and the presentation of the opera on Brentaal in 38 GrS included a rotating Jungle of Nuvar set on Votrad Stadium's 500-meter-long stage.
