# Qualai

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Qualai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qualai)

Qualai was a small, low-gravity, terrestrial planet located in the Qualai system within the Kallea sector on the edge of the Outer Rim Territories and in the Western Reaches. It was situated in grid square K-20 of the Standard Galactic Grid. In 232 BBY, a garden module on the Starlight Beacon space station had soil native to Qualai filling its base, which Jedi Knight Elzar Mann mentally noted as he and Jedi Master Avar Kriss walked through the module.
