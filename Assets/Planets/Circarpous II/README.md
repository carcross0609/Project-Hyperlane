# Circarpous II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Circarpous II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Circarpous_II)

Circarpous II was the second planet orbiting the star Circarpous Major in the Circarpous system. Located in the Circarpous sector of the Expansion Region, it was a hostile hothouse.
