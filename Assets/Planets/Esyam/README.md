# Esyam

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Esyam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Esyam)

Esyam was a planet of molten rock that was located within the Y'Toub system of the galaxy's Hutt Space region. The planet occupied the first orbital position around its star, Y'Toub, and lay on the junction of four hyperlanes called the Oktos Route, the Ootmian Pabol, the Pabol Hutta, and the Shag Pabol.
