# Foran Tutha

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Foran Tutha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Foran_Tutha)

Foran Tutha was a terrestrial world located in the Tion Cluster's Cronese Mandate in the Foran Tutha system, within the Outer Rim Territories. Like other nearby worlds such as Belderone, Foran Tutha was host to a cottage industry that supported treasure hunters for the ancient Jedi planet Ossus, located nearby Cron Drift.

After the Kanjiklub gang defeated forces of the Guavian Death Gang during the skirmish in the Cronese Mandate, the gang member Razoo Qin-Fee claimed that the train of freighters containing items they stole stretched back to Foran Tutha when writing about the events in the book known as the Smuggler's Guide.
