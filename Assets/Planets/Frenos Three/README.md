# Frenos Three

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Frenos Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frenos_Three)

Frenos Three was an uninhabited planet in the galaxy that hosted massive deposits of lommite. The Czerka Corporation to use shell companies to buy up land rights on the planet and pay off key Galactic Republic officials in order to acquire the lommite.
