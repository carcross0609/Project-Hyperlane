# Rirsack

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rirsack](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rirsack)

Rirsack was a planet located in the Belsmuth sector of the Outer Rim. Lieutenant Lar Ndigo of the New Republic bravely fought there sometime during or prior to 13 ABY.
