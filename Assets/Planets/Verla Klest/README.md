# Verla Klest

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Verla Klest](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verla_Klest)

Verla Klest was an gas giant–type planet with nine moons that occupied the seventh orbital position around the star Abo. It was located within the Lehon system, a part of the Unknown Regions' area known as the Tempered Wastes.
