# Carosi V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Carosi V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carosi_V)

Carosi V was a planet in the Carosus system. It was considered a prime candidate for colonization, until it was destroyed in the expansion of the star Carosi into a red giant.
