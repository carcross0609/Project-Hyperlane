# Klatooine

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Klatooine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Klatooine)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Klatooine`, Hutt Space, grid S-9 — map position (11616.0, 1443.8, 194.7)

Klatooine was a desert planet located within the Klatooine system in Hutt Space that was the homeworld of the Klatooinian species. The former pirate Queen Maz Kanata marked it as a point of interest on a map she included in the book that would become known as the Smuggler's Guide. The xenolinguist Jaiyna Sibinth, who studied the language of Pasaana's Aki-Aki, saw similar recurring symbols and themes to the native cultures of other desert planets, including Klatooine's.
