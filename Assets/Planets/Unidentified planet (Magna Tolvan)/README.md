# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Magna Tolvan)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Magna_Tolvan%29)

A planet was located in a distant region of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet's terrain was comprised of mountains as well as plains covered with purple and green grass, and it had a type I atmosphere breathable to humans.

Around 0 ABY, following an encounter with Imperial Captain Magna Tolvan at the space-based fortress known as the Citadel of Rur, the rogue archaeologist Chelli Aphra took Tolvan to the planet in her Lambda-class T-4a shuttle. Aphra and her father, Korin, left Tolvan on the planet, along with her RA-7 protocol droid. Tolvan eventually found her way off the planet, but was demoted to the rank of lieutenant for the losses she suffered at the Citadel of Rur.
