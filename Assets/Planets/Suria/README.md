# Suria

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Suria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Suria)

Suria was a planet located in the Suria system in the Bajic sector of the Outer Rim Territories and the Trailing Sectors. It was situated in grid square P-17 of the Standard Galactic Grid. Appearing blue from space, Suria was orbited by the Suria space station, a common locale for mercenaries, assassins, and bounty hunters alike. During or after 18 BBY, the bounty hunter Fennec Shand met the rogue clone commandos Hunter and Wrecker on the space station above Suria to negotiate a deal.
