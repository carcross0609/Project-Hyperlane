# Baraan-Fa

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Baraan-Fa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baraan-Fa)

Baraan-Fa was a small, beautiful planet in the Grumani sector of the Outer Rim Territories that was known for its trade and farming, inhabited by members of the human species. It was the homeworld of Sari Nadle, a cadet in the Resistance's J-Squadron.
