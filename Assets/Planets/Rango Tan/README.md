# Rango Tan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rango Tan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rango_Tan)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rango Tan`, Other, grid Q-6 — map position (8569.9, 5604.2, 86.8)

Rango Tan was a planet located in the galaxy, home to an indigenous pre-hyperdrive species of bipedal sentients known as the Rangan. It had a population of 45 million, which was made up of three major civilizations. Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin of the Galactic Empire ran a simulation of the Death Star firing at the planet to test the reactions of the Imperial gunners.
