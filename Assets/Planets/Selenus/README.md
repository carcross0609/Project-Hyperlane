# Selenus

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Selenus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Selenus)

Selenus was, according to legend, a planet located in Wild Space whose moon was rumored to have the favorite residence of the princess of a vast wealthy empire. In actuality, the name referred to the planetoid Salaniss, where the Sith Lord Darth Siberus was entombed in carbonite, and the legend of Selenus had been created by Siberus to lure victims to her.
