# Clerion

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clerion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clerion)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Clerion`, Other, grid O-8 — map position (5681.1, 2054.0, -125.8)

Clerion was an uninhabited gas giant located in the Lower Tion. It was situated in the Clerion system in the Palamut sector of the Expansion Region. The former pirate Queen Maz Kanata marked it as a point of interest on a map she included in the book that would become known as the Smuggler's Guide. Gallandro and his crew visited Clerion to steal an item from the Varcinius Repository, located in geostationary orbit of the planet.
