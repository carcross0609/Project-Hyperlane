# Lennax

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Lennax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lennax)

Lennax was an Imperial planet in the Carpurnal system, coreward of Celanon. It was a hot, arid planet covered in grass plains and near desert. The Human Lennaxians lived in a simple, nomadic society. There were numerous trade settlements on the planet, primarily Port Lennax. A world of no particular influence, removed from major trade routes, it nonetheless attracted spacers of various species.
