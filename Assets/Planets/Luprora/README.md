# Luprora

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Luprora](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luprora)

Luprora was a planet located in the galaxy that had unstable environment conditions. The planet was the homeworld of the aquatic Tsw'ells and sentient R'ora species. Prior to the First Order-Resistance War, Luprora was settled by a humanoid species known as the Lupr'or. Following the Battle of Starkiller Base, Captain Phasma, TN-3465, and BB-K8 pursued the fugitive Lieutenant Sol Rivas to Luprora, whom Phasma framed for lowering Starkiller Base's shields. Phasma then killed Rivas and TN-3465, and destroyed BB-K8 in order to conceal the secret that she had destroyed Starkiller Base.
