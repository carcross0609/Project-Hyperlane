# Jardeen IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jardeen IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jardeen_IV)

Jardeen IV was a planet in the Expansion Region's Trans-Jardeen sector. It was the site of the Imperial Army training center, used by the Galactic Empire for teaching the tactics of utilizing walkers in combat. Shortly after the Battle of Endor, Captain Iolan Gendarr of the Imperial Star Destroyer Reliance and General Arndall Lott, both stationed on Jardeen IV, fled the planet before the arrival of New Republic forces.
