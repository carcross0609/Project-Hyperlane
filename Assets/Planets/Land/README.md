# Land

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Land](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Land)

Land was the capital planet of the Aakaash system and the world from which the unique culture of the system originated. In addition to the Xia capital, the planet had 10 major cities of comparable grandeur.
