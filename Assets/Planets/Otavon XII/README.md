# Otavon XII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Otavon XII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Otavon_XII)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Otavon XII`, Mid Rim — map position (-1080.7, -11887.3, -275.4)

Otavon XII, also written Otavon 12 and sometimes called simply "Otavon", was a forested planet inhabited by the Ovoni.

During the early years of the New Order, the Galactic Empire established Tremor Base, an AT-AT production facility, on the planet. However, Tremor base was destroyed in 19 BBY by Ovoni rebels.
