# Kamar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kamar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kamar/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kamar`, Wild Space, grid S-4 — map position (11488.5, 8984.1, 591.8)

Kamar was a dry and arid planet located in Wild Space, home to the insectoid Kamarian species and the howlrunner. It had deserts and mountains across its surface that scorched under the hot-white sun. Various plant life such as the miser-plant, barrel-scrub, and sting-brush sprouted up.

Other creatures native to Kamar included digworms, stingworms, bloodsniffers, and nightswifts.
