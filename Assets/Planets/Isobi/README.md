# Isobi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Isobi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Isobi)

Isobi was a terrestrial world that was the homeworld of Kehal Kehouris, who lead the Second Isobian Revolution to overthrow its noble families and was imprisoned as a result.
