# Skeressa

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skeressa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skeressa)

Skeressa was a planet in the galaxy. During the Cold War between the Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic, Skeedo the Hutt hired individuals to hijack arms shipments to the local militia of Skeressa.
