# Rynmar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rynmar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rynmar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Rynmar`, Inner Rim, grid O-9 — map position (5303.9, 936.9, 5.3)

Rynmar was a world located in the Rynmar system. It was situated in the Hapan Rim Worlds, an area of the Hapes Cluster in the Slice portion of the Inner Rim. Rynmar lay at the junction of the Great Rim Route and Rynmar Trail hyperlanes, while another hyperspace route linked it to the Rainboh system.

Rynmar was home to the rare hover beak creature. During the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, it was one of a number of select worlds whose fauna was showcased in the Atrium, a public building in the only spaceport on the Outer Rim Territories planet Ord Mynock.
