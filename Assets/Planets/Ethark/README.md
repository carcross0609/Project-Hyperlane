# Ethark

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Ethark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ethark)

Ethark was a tropical planet in the Epsilon 69.02 system. The surface consisted of vast steppes of photosynthetic plants, forests, and mineral reliefs. An estimated two-thirds of the surface was covered in forests.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Ethark
- **System:** Epsilon 69.02 system
- **Moons:** Lahn
- **Atmosphere:** Breathable
- **Climate:** Tropical
- **Flora:** Photosynthetic plants
