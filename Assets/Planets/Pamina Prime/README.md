# Pamina Prime

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pamina Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pamina_Prime)

Pamina Prime was a planet in the Inner Rim. It was home to shellfish that were responsible of a deadly disease when eaten, the Borotavi syndrome. Jedi Master Taria Damsin was diagnosed with this disease after eating a local blue-shelled mollusk with a green stripe.

- **System:** Pamina Prime system
