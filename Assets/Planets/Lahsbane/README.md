# Lahsbane

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lahsbane](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lahsbane)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lahsbane`, Mid Rim, grid R-15 — map position (9550.5, -8228.4, -428.6)

Lahsbane was a planet located in the Lahsbane system in the Onatos sector in the Mid Rim Territories and the Slice, and was situated in grid square R-15 on the Standard Galactic Grid. During his time as a Padawan of the Jedi Order, Dooku and his Jedi Master, Yoda, traveled to Lahsbane, which the Serennian later documented in his personal journal.
