# Ertrax

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ertrax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ertrax)

Ertrax was a Wild Space planet located within the star system of the same name. Ertrax was home to the Ertraxi, a peaceful species of multiple-eyed humanoids who were eager to make contact with other species and cultures.

At some point during the reign of the Galactic Republic, a Republic starship crash-landed on Ertrax, leaving no survivors. The Ertraxi found the wrecked vessel, repaired it over the span of several years, and eventually flew it off-planet in search of other species.
