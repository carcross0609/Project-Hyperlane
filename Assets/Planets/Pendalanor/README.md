# Pendalanor

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Pendalanor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pendalanor)

Pendalanor was a jungle planet in the galaxy. It took three days of hyperspace to travel from the planet Hoth to Pendalanor. The planet was the home of the Pendalanorians, which were a species of tall, skeletal humanoids. The Ho'Din Ho-Jon lived on Pendalanor.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Pendalanor
- **Class:** Terrestrial
- **Terrain:** Jungles
- **Species:** Pendalanorian
