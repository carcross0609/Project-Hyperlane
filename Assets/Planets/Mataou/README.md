# Mataou

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mataou](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mataou)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mataou`, Outer Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-1330.8, -12924.7, 518.1)

Mataou was a hot desert planet located in the Mataou system, in the Anoat sector of the Outer Rim Territories, on the edge of the Ivax Nebula. Long before the Galactic Civil War, Mataou was ruled by Hutts, including the Gnuda Kajidic. In their heyday, the Hutts of Mataou established vast settlements in the classical architectural Hutt style. A force of security droids existed on world to take out any threat.

However, by the time of the Galactic Empire's reign, the planet had been abandoned by its Hutt masters, who left behind the security droids. It was primarily inhabited by farmers of a serpentine creature known as ro hypa. Additionally, some of the former Hutt-ruled habitations had been restored by criminal groups, who used them as headquarters. The only real value Mataou held for the Empire was to act as a primary entry point for starships leaving the Corellian Trade Spine to enter the Ison Trade Corridor.

With the assistance of an individual known as the Smuggler, the Trade Spine League reactivated the Hutts' old sensor system to probe weaknesses in the Iron Blockade, successfully encountering a way to bypass the Imperials. Later, the Empire sought to maintain control over Mataou as a force of insurgents known as the Uprising established a strong foothold there. This resulted in the Battle of Mataou.
