# Dandrian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dandrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dandrian)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dandrian`, Outer Rim, grid R-6 — map position (10254.8, 4586.0, 213.0)

Dandrian in the Dandrian system was called the "crossroads of the galaxy". It had a single sun and was orbited by the Dandrian's Ring of asteroids, the remnants of a moon that struck the planet during formation. It had a thick atmosphere, flat marshes, and tropical grass, but no trees. It was located between Tatooine and Yavin, and between Bespin and Dantooine. It was inhabited by indigenous bonecrushers as well as Humans and Gamorreans.
