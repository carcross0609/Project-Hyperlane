# Jiroch-Kalok

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jiroch-Kalok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jiroch-Kalok)

Jiroch-Kalok, also known as Kalok, was a world situated within the Mid Rim's Yushan sector. The planet was colonized by the hunting-oriented Tunroth species at some point after they had received galactic-level technology. Jiroch-Kalok, together with the Jiroch system's other worlds Jiroch-Reslia and Jiroch-Saloch, were referred to by spacers as the Triumvirate. A number of Duros also settled on the planet, leaving a strong influence on its architecture.
