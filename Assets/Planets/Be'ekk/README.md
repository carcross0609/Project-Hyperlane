# Be'ekk

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Be'ekk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27ekk)

Be'ekk was a planet in the Cholganna system of the Outer Rim Territories. It orbited the star Chol, with three smaller planets orbiting more closely than it, Ryien, Cholganna and Chol. It was brown in color and had a ring. The Cholganna system was partially engulfed by the Chol Nebula, and the planets in the Star system passed through the nebula during their orbit.
