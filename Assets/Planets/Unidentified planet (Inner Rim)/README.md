# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Inner Rim)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Inner_Rim%29)

This planet was located in the Inner Rim and it was held by the Galactic Empire prior to 5 ABY. The Empire hired Mandalorians to hold the planet's system, which they did by taking control of several trineutronium power plants on the planet and threatening to detonate them—which would sterilize the planet and kill all 3.5 billion beings who lived there—if New Republic ships began landing, essentially taking the entire world hostage.
