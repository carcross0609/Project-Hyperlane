# Cholganna

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cholganna/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cholganna/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Cholganna`, Outer Rim, grid T-5 — map position (12010.2, 7013.7, -596.1)

Cholganna was a planet best known for its cool forests and dangerous creatures. It was home to the nexu, a predatory feline. Animals such as the arboreal octopus and the bark rat were also plentiful on the planet. Both were known prey of the nexu.
