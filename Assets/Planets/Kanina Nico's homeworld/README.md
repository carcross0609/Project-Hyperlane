# Kanina Nico's homeworld

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kanina Nico's homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kanina_Nico%27s_homeworld)

A planet was located in the Huru system, and was rich in farium, which was mined by its human inhabitants. It was the homeworld of Kanina Nico, who returned to the planet after learning the Galactic Empire planned to enslave the inhabitants.
