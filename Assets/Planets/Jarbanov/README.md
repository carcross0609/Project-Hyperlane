# Jarbanov

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jarbanov](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jarbanov)

Jarbanov was a barren planet located in the Slice portion of the Expansion Region, in the Serathrace sector in grid square P-11 on the Standard Galactic Grid. Situated in the inner section of the Jarbanov system, in outskirts of the junker systems, the world surrounded by rings of junk and was home to a civilian outpost that processed the junk to supply the New Republic.
