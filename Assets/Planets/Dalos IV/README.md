# Dalos IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dalos IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dalos_IV)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dalos IV`, Outer Rim, grid S-5 — map position (11850.0, 6726.1, -284.7)

Dalos IV was a hot, humid planet of shallow oceans in the Outer Rim's Nilgaard sector. It had a single rainforest continent, Kavila, and the planet was 7,000 kilometers in diameter. It was originally a penal colony. Due to the planet being largely ignored by the Galactic Empire, it became home to many beings fleeing persecution. The local architecture was old-fashioned, but the technology was of modern levels. Voyager's Rest was located on Kavila. A popular local sport was Trophy Chase, in which the locals rode nephs. The planet's Governor-General was Lira Rimrunner.
