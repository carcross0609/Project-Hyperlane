# Muugrah

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Muugrah](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Muugrah)

Muugrah was a planet that was closer to the fringe of the galaxy than it was to the Galactic Core. Its capital was Slaibo, which was close to Squarn's Junkyard. It was a planet of open grasslands inhabited by prospectors and ranchers.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Muugrah
- **System:** Muugrah system
- **Suns:** 2
- **Terrain:** Open grasslands
- **Interest:** Squarn's Junkyard
- **Cities:** Slaibo (capital)
