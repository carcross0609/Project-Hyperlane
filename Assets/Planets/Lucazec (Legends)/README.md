# Lucazec

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lucazec/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucazec/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Lucazec`, Outer Rim, grid Q-6 — map position (8459.0, 4932.3, 35.8)

Lucazec was a planet located within the Outer Rim Territories. Originally known as Far Thanium, it was absorbed into Xim's Empire as he expanded his borders from the Kingdom of Cron. Lucazec was a potential candidate for one of the lost thrones of Xim. After the fall of the empire in 25,100 BBY, it would eventually join the Galactic Republic. Lucazec was the Fallanassi world of origin. During the Clone Wars the planet was located within Separatist space. The Fallanassi fled the planet when Cassio Tagge tried to recruit them to the Empire.

The Yuuzhan Vong conquered the planet while invading the galaxy.
