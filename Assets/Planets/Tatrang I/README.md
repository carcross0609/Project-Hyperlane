# Tatrang I

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tatrang I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tatrang_I)

Tatrang I was a hot rock planet with no moons that was located within the Tatrang system, a part of the Tammuz sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet occupied the closest orbit to its star, the red giant Tatrang. Tatrang I was lifeless and was considered commercially worthless.
