# Sauwecer

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sauwecer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sauwecer)

Sauwecer was a terrestrial planet in the Sauwecer system in the Videnda sector of the Outer Rim Territories that was the origin point of Sauweceran tea. In 5 ABY, Sauwecer was visited by a group of individuals including New Republic pilot Rynn Zenat and the Jedi Luke Skywalker to speak with the spice runner Tomasso.
