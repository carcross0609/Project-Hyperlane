# Kergans

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kergans](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kergans)

Kergans was a urban planet located in the Kergans system within the Inner Rim Territories that was protected by the New Republic. It was the homeworld of Lorica Demaris. A hotel owned by Lorica's mother and father was located on Kergans. At some point before joining the Resistance, Lorica watched podracing on her homeworld.
