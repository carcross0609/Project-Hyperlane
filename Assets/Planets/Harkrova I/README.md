# Harkrova I

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Harkrova I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Harkrova_I)

Harkrova I was a terrestrial planet located in the Harkrova system in the Inner Rim Territories. The planet was situated at the coordinates N-9 on the Standard Galactic Grid. A barren, sulfurous wasteland, it was the only planet in the star system, holding the first orbital position around a dim yellow star. Harkrova I had a singular, unnamed moon that was home to a former Jedi temple. Both the planet and its star system were named after the ancient astronomer Harkrova, who discovered them long before 4 ABY. In 9 ABY, Harkova I fell within the borders of the New Republic.
