# Ripurni

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ripurni](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ripurni)

Ripurni was a lifeless planet located near the edges of the Anoat sector. Eventual Imperial Grand Moff Ubrik Adelhard was born on this planet. At some point during the Imperial Era, Ripurni was used as a mining planet. An Imperial patrol passed a mining mission where a miner insulted the Empire and was executed by a TK trooper.
