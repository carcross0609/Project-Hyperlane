# Bardelberan 7

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bardelberan 7](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardelberan_7)

Bardelberan 7, also known simply as Bardelberan, was a planet located in the Bardelberan system, within the Churnis sector in the Mid Rim. It was situated in grid square J-6 on the Standard Galactic Grid. During the Imperial Era, Bardelberan 7 had an Imperial Garrison, where the pirate Hondo Ohnaka was once involved in an incident.

In 10 BBY, upon leaving the planet Galagolos V the light freighter Millennium Falcon was contacted by an Imperial TIE/sh shuttle, which informed the smugglers Han Solo and Chewbacca that they were harboring a suspect wanted for the Bardelberan 7 incident. Solo believed Mahjo Reeloo was the suspect, but she informed him that she had never visited Bardelberan 7 before, and Solo later deduced that it was actually Ohnaka who was the suspect. The position of Bardelberan 7 was eventually noted on a map included in the book Traveler's Guide to Batuu, which was written by the historian Eloc Throno and published in 34 ABY.
