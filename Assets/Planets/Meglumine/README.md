# Meglumine

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Meglumine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meglumine)

Meglumine, originally known as Ord Meglumine, was a planet in the Meglumine system of the Inner Rim. An Ordnance/Regional Depot, Ord Meglumine was used by the Pius Dea-controlled Galactic Republic as a staging point for their assault on Iridonia during the Great Northern Crusade in 11,884 BBY.

In 1 BBY, Imperial stormtrooper TK329 was serving on the Imperial Star Destroyer Vector, which had a stopover near Meglumine. A small number of the ship's crew went planetside, while the rest enjoyed some recreation time aboard the ship.
