# Hetnagaro

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hetnagaro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hetnagaro)

Hetnagaro was a world located in the Hetnagaro system within the Andirma sector of the Expansion Region and Trailing Sectors. It was situated in grid square L-15 of the Standard Galactic Grid.

By 4 ABY, Hetnagaro was devastated by the Galactic Empire during its reign. Along with planets such as Alderaan and Nacronis, Hetnagaro was one of the worlds that the New Republic U-wing pilot Kairos wished to avenge through the destruction of the Empire.
