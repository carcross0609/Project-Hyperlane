# Blutopia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blutopia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blutopia)

Blutopia was a planet in the Vensensor sector's Blutopia system, in the Expansion Region and the Trailing Sectors. The watery world was home to the Blutopian species. Blutopia pearls were a dish native to the planet and were considered by the Blutopian Oga Garra one of her favorites.

In 10 BBY, the scoundrel Jaylen Vrax visited the Renner Family Winery to meet with its owner, Bischt Renner. Following a scuffle, Vrax's BX-series droid commando ND-5 killed Renner, prompting the scoundrel and his partner to depart Blutopia shortly afterward.
