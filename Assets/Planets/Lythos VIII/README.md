# Lythos VIII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lythos VIII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lythos_VIII)

Lythos VIII was the eighth planet of the Lythos system of the Outer Rim Territories' Corporate sector, which was located at the confluence of the Hydian Way and Trianii Shunt hyperlanes. Along with Lythos VI, Lythos VII, and Lythos IX, Lythos VIII was one of the major agriworlds in the Lythos system.
