# Duro

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Duro](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duro)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Duro`, Core Worlds, grid M-11 — map position (2356.5, -2802.0, 4.7)

Duro was a temperate planet in the Duro system of the Core Worlds's Duro sector. The planet was located in grid square M-11 of the Standard Galactic Grid, and was situated along the Corellian Trade Spine. The planet was the homeworld of the Duros species, including the duro Bounty hunter Cad Bane, who spent his childhood in a crime-ridden city on Duro with his friend Niro.
