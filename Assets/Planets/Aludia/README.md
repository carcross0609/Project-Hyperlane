# Aludia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aludia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aludia)

Aludia was a forest planet located in the galaxy. During 232 BBY, a starship thief named Sty Trune crash-landed a starship on Aludia's surface after getting into a fight with bounty hunter Rhugo Tar. Aludia was also the homeworld of the Mardorsh, Skriffle, and Aludian trumble pig species.
