# Stic

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stic)

Stic was a planet located in the Stic system of the Xappyh sector, a part of the Outer Rim Territories and the New Territories. It was situated in grid square Q-3 of the Standard Galactic Grid. Stic was the homeworld of the Xi'Dec species of sentient insectoids. It was mentioned in "Species From A to Z," a survival compendium compiled by New Republic Captain Carson Teva during the New Republic Era.
