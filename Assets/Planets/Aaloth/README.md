# Aaloth

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aaloth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aaloth)

Aaloth was a Twi'lek colony planet in the Gaulus sector of the Outer Rim Territories. During its history, Aaloth was once occupied by the Sith Empire. By the High Republic Era, Aaloth was nominally a member of the Galactic Republic, as a part of the Clan Assembly on Ryloth and was ruled by a Keeper. It was the homeworld of the Nihil Tempest Runner Lourna Dee and her family.
