# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (Lexim)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28Lexim%29)

A forest planet was located in the galaxy. A hunting ground owned by Lexim was located on this planet. Sometime around 4 BBY, Lexim, Alyson, Greve, and Wad were all attacked by gundarks whilst at the hunting ground. Garazeb Orrelios picked up their distress call and went to investigate.
