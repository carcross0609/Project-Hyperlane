# Oisin

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oisin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oisin)

Oisin was a planet inhabited by the Oisinians. During the High Republic Era, the Oisinians traded with the Malorins of the planet Malorin. However, at some point after the Nihil marauders established their Occlusion Zone territory, the Stormwall barrier surrounding Nihil space placed Oisin just outside its border, with Malorin trapped behind it. Jedi Master Eve Byre and her Padawan, Zaph Mora, embarked on a mission to Oisin to deliver supplies aboard the Byne Guild transport Vessel.
