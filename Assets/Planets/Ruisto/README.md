# Ruisto

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ruisto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruisto)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ruisto`, Outer Rim, grid U-6 — map position (13504.3, 5041.3, -286.8)

Ruisto was a planet located in the Calamari sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Ruisto was the first world settled by the Mon Calamari and the site of their initial meeting with the Republic scouts. During the early years of the Galactic Civil War, the Empire bombarded Ruisto as a training exercise for its planned attack on the Mon Calamari homeworld.
