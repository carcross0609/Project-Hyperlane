# Mon Gazza

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mon Gazza](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Gazza)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mon Gazza`, Mid Rim, grid Q-16 — map position (7742.2, -9217.1, -113.9)

Mon Gazza was a Mid Rim Territories planet located on both the Corellian Run and the Llanic Spice Run. It was the site of spice mining and trading. A significant portion of the spice trade was under the reign of the kingpin Ravi, who was murdered by the mercenary droid AD-W4 when he attempted to hire the droid to kill a rival. The Pyke Syndicate spice cartel controlled the bulk of Mon Gazza's spice mines by the time of the Clone Wars.

During the Galactic Civil War in 4 ABY, the New Republic began establishing a planetary outpost on Mon Gazza, although spice gang warfare continued in the meantime. The Galactic Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing destroyed the outpost in 5 ABY. During the New Republic's reign, the Spice Runners of Kijimi carried out a heist on Mon Gazza and outran New Republic patrols using the local Zugga Challenge podracing course.
