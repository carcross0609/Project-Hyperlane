# Öetrago

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Öetrago](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%C3%96etrago)

Öetrago was a planet situated in the Öetrago system in the Mayagil sector of the Outer Rim Territories. During the High Republic Era, the planet's forests were ravaged by fires. Jedi Master Sskeer and his Padawan, Keeve Trennis, were sent to combat the forest fires, holding back the flames to allow the creatures of the forest to escape. While on Öetrago, Sskeer left Trennis to hold back the flames on her own while he rescued a drowan calf caught in the blaze.
