# Chenowei

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chenowei](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chenowei)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Chenowei`, Outer Rim, grid Q-5 — map position (8162.4, 6591.5, 301.9)

Chenowei was a terrestrial world located in the Chenowei system. It was situated in the Back Reach area of the Gordian Reach sector, within the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The hyperlane known as the Ardan Cross connected it to the Atorra and B'trilla systems, while a smaller hyperspace route linked it to the Elamposnia system. A simple, rustic world, Chenowei was dominated by Human members of the anti-technological Church of the Light religion.
