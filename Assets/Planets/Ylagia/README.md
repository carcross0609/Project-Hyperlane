# Ylagia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ylagia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ylagia)

Ylagia was a planet in the Ylagia system, a trinary star system in the Rocantor sector of the Expansion Region. It was orbited by a vortex moon, and for the last decade of the Galactic Republic, it was the home to one of the few remaining Alvadorjian clans.

Featuring iron jungles in its eastern continent, the planet smelled of rust, and was home to creatures such as metal-eaters and steel-boring beetles. It also possessed cities such as Ologage, in which art, despite the conservation costs, played a prominent role.
