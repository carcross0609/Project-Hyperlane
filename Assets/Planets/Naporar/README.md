# Naporar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Naporar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naporar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Naporar`, Unknown Regions, grid F-8 — map position (-8727.6, 2256.8, -182.3)

Naporar was a planet in the Chiss Ascendancy and was also the headquarters of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet. Naporar contained a medcenter where Mitth'raw'nuruodo underwent physical therapy after the Vagaari pirate operations. Mitth'ali'astov received her sky-walker training at the Expansionary Fleet complex there. After Csaplar was targeted by three alien warships, four Expansionary Fleet warships were sent from Naporar to support defenses on Csilla.

During the civil unrest between Chiss families, Supreme Admiral Ja'fosk ordered ships from the Expansionary Fleet to meet at Naporar when the Syndicure demanded they keep their distance from other Chiss worlds.
