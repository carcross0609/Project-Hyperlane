# Skorrupon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Skorrupon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skorrupon/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Skorrupon`, Mid Rim, grid N-7 — map position (3387.5, 3785.1, 44.6)

Skorrupon was a planet located in the Trans-Vulta sector of the Mid Rim. Skorrupon was the site of several notable battles: during the early Imperial Era, anti-Imperial agents Arani Korden, Rorworr, and Deel Surool intercepted High Inquisitor Volytar's shuttle outside of his personal Star Destroyer over the world. The three thus became known as the Heroes of Skorrupon. Later, in 0 ABY, Rebel forces attempted to ambush the newly christened Super Star Destroyer Executor above Skorrupon, in one of the Rebellion's first encounters with the powerful flagship. The Rebel force was utterly destroyed, with fleeing pilot Vrad Dodonna the only survivor. Skorrupon was also attacked by the Yuuzhan Vong during the Yuuzhan Vong War.
