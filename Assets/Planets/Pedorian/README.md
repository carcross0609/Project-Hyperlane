# Pedorian

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pedorian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pedorian)

Pedorian was a planet that experienced a conflict known as the Pedorian Civil War during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The Hutt Cartel supplied weapons to both factions in the war.
