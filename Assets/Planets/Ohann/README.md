# Ohann; Occupying the second orbit in a diagram of the Tatoo system

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ohann](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ohann)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ohann`, Outer Rim — map position (-1359.0, -12008.3, 188.1)

Ohann was the second planet orbiting the stars Tatoo I and Tatoo II in the Tatoo system. Located in the Arkanis sector of the Outer Rim Territories, it was a gas giant. It was the largest out of the three planets in the Tatoo system.

Drone barges were used to ferry ice blocks from Ohann's ice rings. The free-trader Garouf Lafoe imported ice from Adriana's rings to Tatooine.
