# Iego

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Iego](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iego)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Iego`, Outer Rim, grid T-7 — map position (12414.7, 3743.4, -346.7)

Iego was a mysterious planet located in the Outer Rim Territories on the fringes of the known galaxy that was surrounded by a thousand moons collectively known as the moons of Iego, at least one of which was populated by a race of beings known as Angels. During the Clone Wars, the Confederacy of Independent Systems constructed a laser field that prevented anyone from leaving the planet, before the field was destroyed by Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, freeing the Iegoans.
