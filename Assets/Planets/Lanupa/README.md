# Lanupa

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lanupa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lanupa)

Lanupa, once known as The Demon's Rest or Battle World, was a mountainous neutral planet located in the galaxy. It was home of the Skull Ridge Mountain, atop which was located the Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa, formerly a fearsome pirate stronghold that boasted torturous execution acid baths and fighting pits. SM-33 was outraged at how they had "ruined it!" with visiting pleasure yachts, spa, mud baths (fed by natural hot springs), bacta soaks and buffets ("food for weaklings!"). Lanupa was also the homeworld of the Trash crab, a species of crab that wore pieces of technological scrap on their back.

During the New Republic Era, as the luxury spa did not admit children, the crew of the Onyx Cinder (Fern, Wim, Neel, KB, the droid SM-33 and Jod Na Nawood) had to adopt new identities: the children as the "Most Wise Elders" from the Bratric System, and Jod Na Nawood as the probable Muun Professor Umiam Gorelox.

The Force-sensitive human Jod Na Nawood studied Ur-Kittât writing on a wall as the Myykian Neel watched, before they discovered the abandoned Captain Rennod's Lair and found the coordinates to At Attin.

Nawood's former pirate crew invaded Lanupa in search of the Nawood after being tipped off by the bounty hunter Pokkit. They managed to capture Nawood and SM-33, before fleeing the planet as a squadron of New Republic X-wing starfighters chased them off, resulting one of the two pirate vessels being shot down.

Meanwhile, the Skeleton Crew managed to recover the Onyx Cinder, but as the ship was pinned by a garbage machine, they were forced to purge the hull, allowing them to break free and depart Lanupa.
