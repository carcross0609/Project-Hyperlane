# Gatalenta

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gatalenta/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gatalenta/Legends)

Gatalenta was a planet located in the galaxy. By the third Galactic War, Gatalentans were known for their tea and their long tradition of supporting the Jedi. Gatalenta was considered a beautiful planet by Ardinondu Dipolus Daeruun, a general in the Galactic Republic.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Gatalenta
- **Coordinates:** Canon-only
- **Demonym:** Gatalentan
- **Exports:** Gatalentan tea
