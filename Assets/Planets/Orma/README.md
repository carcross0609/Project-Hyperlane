# Orma

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Orma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orma)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Orma`, Expansion Region — map position (-3746.3, -7449.2, 56.6)

Orma was a junk planet in the Aramand Cluster of the Brak sector. Junkers had covered the world in scrapyards which contained many cultural artifacts including some toys and tech. Further within the planet were items of amazing value. Around the time of the Cold War, an individual was sent to look for the valuable artifacts, discovering a relic from the height of the Tion Hegemony. Several hundred years before the Galactic Civil War, the Orma akia colonized the planet.
