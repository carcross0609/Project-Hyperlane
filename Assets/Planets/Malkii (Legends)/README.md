# Malkii

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malkii/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malkii/Legends)

Malkii was a remote planet. It commonly served as a refuge for mercenaries, insurrectionists and terrorists, but perhaps it was most notorious for its reputation as the training ground of the infamous assassins known as Malkite Poisoners.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Malkii
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
