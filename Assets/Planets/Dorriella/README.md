# Dorriella

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dorriella](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dorriella)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dorriella`, Outer Rim — map position (8021.6, 5607.8, -276.2)

Dorriella was a terrestrial world located in the Dorriella system, a part of the Outer Rim Territories' Wazta sector. A family with three children, including the Human girl Shanna Kinn, lived there, with Kinn spending time with her two older brothers and learning to repair starships as a result.

At some point, military forces of the Galactic Empire arrived at Dorriella and occupied it. During an Imperial raid on a suspected Rebel Alliance hideout, Kinn's family was killed, leaving the girl an orphan. Subsequently, in order to survive on Dorriella's streets, Kinn resorted to thievery and eventually stowed away aboard a freighter that took her off-world.
