# Mussubir Three

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mussubir Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mussubir_Three)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mussubir Three`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (297.6, -11712.7, -244.8)

Mussubir Three, or simply Mussubir, was a planet located in the Mussubir system of the Senex sector, in the Mid Rim of the galaxy. It was an important industrial world controlled by House Araba.

The governor was allied with Roganda Ismaren. He disappeared on 'vacation' in 12 ABY.
