# Circarpous VII

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Circarpous VII](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Circarpous_VII)

Circarpous VII was the seventh planet orbiting the star Circarpous Major in the Circarpous system. Located in the Circarpous sector of the Expansion Region, it was a gas giant.
