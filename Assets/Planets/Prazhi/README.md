# Prazhi

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prazhi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prazhi)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Prazhi`, Expansion Region, grid O-12 — map position (5655.4, -3983.0, 285.2)

Prazhi was an Expansion Region planet located in the Cyrillian Protectorate. It was the homeworld of the Zexx species. A member of the galactic community since at least 5000 BBY, a millennium later Prazhi was situated on an important hyperspace trade route. By the time of the Galactic Empire's reign, Prazhi had become a trade world the economy of which was revitalized by Imperial efforts to resettle the Expansion Region.
