# Ord Klina

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ord Klina](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Klina)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ord Klina`, Mid Rim — map position (8062.8, -664.5, 228.9)

Ord Klina was a world located in the Ord Klina system, a part of the Klina sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. Ord Klina served as an Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost for the Galactic Republic during the Pius Dea Era, a period of time that lasted between approximately 12,000 BBY and 11,000 BBY, despite it being located outside the borders of Republic space. When the Republic launched the Thirty-Fourth Pius Dea Crusade against the Hutt species in 11,057 BBY, Republic forces moved from Ord Klina toward Hutt Space.

At some point by 2 ABY, Tyrimm Wyln, a Togorian farmer and the mate of bladesmith Mlatar Thon Gra, used the credits she and her sisters had saved from managing a herd of bists on her native Togoria to tour a portion of the galaxy that included Ord Klina.
