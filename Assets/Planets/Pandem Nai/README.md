# Pandem Nai

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pandem Nai](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pandem_Nai)

Pandem Nai was a terrestrial planet in the Outer Rim Territories and New Territories with a tibanna gas industry. It, along with its tibanna-mining space station, Orbital One, was taken over by the Galactic Empire's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing shortly after the Battle of Endor. The 204th established fortifications on the planet, after which their presence was detected by the New Republic, which attacked Pandem Nai. Its capital city Induchron was endangered by falling debris from the Orbital One during the assault.
