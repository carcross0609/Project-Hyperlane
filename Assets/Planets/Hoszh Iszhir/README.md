# Hoszh Iszhir

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hoszh Iszhir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoszh_Iszhir)

Hoszh Iszhir was a desert planet in the Core Worlds and was the homeworld of the reptilian Gektl species. Half of Hoszh Iszhir was covered in waterless deserts, while the other half was shrouded in a poisonous atmosphere. There were rich lodes of guerrerite on planet, forcing the Gektl to build planetary defenses. It wasn't until Major Stafuv Rahz traded the shield codes to the Galactic Empire which then subjugated Hoszh Iszhir.
