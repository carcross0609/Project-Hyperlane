# Daxan Beta

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Daxan Beta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Daxan_Beta)

Daxan Beta was a planet situated near a main trade route used by the Galactic Empire. General Jan Dodonna of the Alliance to Restore the Republic briefly studied it as a potential location for a new Rebel base after the evacuation of the Great Temple on Yavin 4, but quickly rejected it due to its proximity to the Imperial trade route.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Daxan Beta
- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
- **Sector:** Thesme sector
- **System:** Daxan Beta system
