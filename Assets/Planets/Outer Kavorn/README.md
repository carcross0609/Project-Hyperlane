# Outer Kavorn

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Outer Kavorn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Kavorn)

Outer Kavorn was a terrestrial world. By 228 BBY, an infection known as the Blight that drained worlds of life, was found on Outer Kavorn during its spread across the galaxy. Around that year, the Blight's spread was stopped.
