# Hetzal Prime

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hetzal Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hetzal_Prime)

Hetzal Prime, also known simply as Hetzal, was a planet located in the Savareen sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet and its system were within the territory of the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era, containing a total population of forty billion. It was also said that every crop in the galaxy could grow somewhere on the worlds of Hetzal. During that time, Hetzal Prime and the rest of its system were threatened by anomalies from hyperspace. The Jedi Master Avar Kriss, who was in charge of the combined Galactic Republic and Jedi Order effort to save the system, later became known as the "Hero of Hetzal."
