# Hapor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hapor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hapor)

Hapor was a terrestrial planet that was located in the Inner Rim's Hapor system and featured the Griren Province. In 0 ABY, the Hand of Judgment, a group of five Galactic Empire stormtrooper deserters, participated in a minor water dispute in the province.

Within three months of the event, Emperor's Hand Mara Jade read a report on the Hand of Judgment's involvement in the dispute and subsequently used Hapor as a center point when trying to locate the group. Shortly after, Jade tracked down the Hand of Judgment to the planet Elegasso.
