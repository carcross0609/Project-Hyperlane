# Polmanar

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Polmanar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Polmanar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Polmanar`, Outer Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-1003.7, -12948.6, -356.4)

Polmanar was a planet located in the Anoat sector of the galaxy. At the time of the Galactic Empire's Iron Blockade of the sector, Polmanar was a base of the Trade Spine League. When Leeka Pali of the Trade Spine League suspected double-dealings from one of their own, she covertly sent a young smuggler to the planet to see if her suspicions had any merit.
