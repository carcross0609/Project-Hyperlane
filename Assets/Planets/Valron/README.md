# Valron

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Valron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Valron)

Valron was a planet that contained the Sacred Garden used by Jedi to train their apprentices. It was visited by Ady Sun'Zee and her apprentice Nooa during the High Republic Era.
