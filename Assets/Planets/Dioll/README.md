# Dioll

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dioll](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dioll)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dioll`, Mid Rim, grid L-17 — map position (60.8, -11887.4, -178.9)

Dioll was a rocky planet located in the Dioll system of the Juvex sector, a part of the Senex-Juvex region in the Western Reaches portion of the Mid Rim. The Kassido Bypass hyperlane linked it to the Kardura and Thermon systems.

Dioll's climate contradictorily featured both strong winds and deep fogs, and in that way it resembled a miniature version of a gas giant–type planet. Dioll was the homeworld of the sentient Diollan species and, like the rest of the Senex-Juvex worlds, was under the control of the region's aristocratic Ancient Houses.
