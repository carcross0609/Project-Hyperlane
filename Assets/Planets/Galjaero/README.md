# Galjaero

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Galjaero](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galjaero)

Galjaero was a planet located in the Tungra sector within the Outer Rim Territories. In 232 BBY, the Abednedo Chigg and other former pirates got their starship stuck in the mud on the planet.
