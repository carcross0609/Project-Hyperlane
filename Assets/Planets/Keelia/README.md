# Keelia

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Keelia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keelia)

Keelia was a terrestrial planet in the Outer Rim Territories. It had lush forests, rivers, and large rock formations.

The Force, known locally as "Magina," was an important concept among its inhabitants.
