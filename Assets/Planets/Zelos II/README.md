# Zelos II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zelos II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zelos_II)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Zelos II`, Mid Rim, grid N-16 — map position (3696.7, -9623.7, -316.7)

Zelos II was the second planet of the Zelos system. It was aligned with the Galactic Empire and was an important source of ores for the Imperial war machine. It was also the homeworld of the Zelosians. The Bare Forest, otherwise known as the Forest of the Walking Dead, was located in the mountains at the far north of the northern continent. The planet was orbited by four moons.
