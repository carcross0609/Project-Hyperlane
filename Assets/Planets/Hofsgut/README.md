# Hofsgut

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hofsgut](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hofsgut)

Hofsgut was a world with energy-rich resources that was located in the Hofsgut system within the Mid Rim Territories' Hewett sector. By 4 ABY, Princess Leia Organa visited Hofsgut and established a connection with the world's president. That year, Organa reached out to the president, hoping to persuade Hofsgut to side with the forming New Republic and reasonably confident she could convince the president due to their connection.
