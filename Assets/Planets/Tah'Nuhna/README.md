# Tah'Nuhna

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tah'Nuhna](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tah%27Nuhna)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tah'Nuhna`, Mid Rim, grid R-14 — map position (10284.2, -6879.0, 235.6)

Tah'Nuhna was an ice planet in the Mid Rim, home to the species of the same name. Tah'Nuhna and its people had been neutral in every conflict, welcoming to all, and they allowed the Resistance to briefly stay on their planet shortly after the Battle of Crait, leading the First Order to destroy their civilization.
