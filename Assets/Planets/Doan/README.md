# Doan

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Doan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doan)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Doan`, Outer Rim, grid M-6 — map position (1752.8, 5300.0, 210.7)

Doan was a small planet in the Outer Rim Territories whose sole economy was focused on mining. It was ruled by a group of nobles, the Doan Royal House, while most of its population were miners who lived in poverty.
