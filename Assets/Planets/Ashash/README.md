# Ashash

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ashash](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ashash)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ashash`, Other, grid N-14 — map position (4165.6, -6575.9, -90.0)

Ashash was a waterworld located in the Ashash system, a part of the Zarracina sector in the Trailing Sectors portion of the Expansion Region. It was home to a species of creature that could emit a harmful gas from its body—an aspect in which the creature was similar to the stinkfish of the Outer Rim Territories planet Sedri.
