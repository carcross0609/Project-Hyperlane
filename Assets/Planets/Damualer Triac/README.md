# Damualer Triac

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Damualer Triac](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Damualer_Triac)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Damualer Triac`, Inner Rim, grid O-10 — map position (4754.4, -959.8, 103.7)

Damualer Triac was a planet located in the Damualer Triac system of the Inner Rim of the galaxy. The Millennium Oak grew on the world. Jedi Master Haaran Balmor retired to Damualer Triac with his second wife, Hannah Balmor. Haaran and Jedi Hart Daele, who was seven kiloparsecs away in the Samarine Province, played several games of Divoran Holochess over a forty-year period via Projective Telepathy.
