# Vornax

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vornax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vornax)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Vornax`, Outer Rim, grid S-5 — map position (11740.8, 6284.3, -590.9)

Vornax was a barren planet located within the Vornax system of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet itself was covered in abandoned droid factories and piles of rusted scrap and garbage. The factories had been left disused for centuries. The only beings who ever visited the abandoned world were those attempting to escape law, or bounty hunters looking to collect a bounty.
