# Yabosta

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yabosta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yabosta)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Yabosta`, Expansion Region, grid L-15 — map position (1030.2, -8072.0, 79.6)

Yabosta was a planet located in the Yabosta system of the Expansion Region. It was orbited by at least two moons, the second of which was called Skalokor. Port Kalla was one of the largest cities on the planet, and its primary spaceport.
