# Riggs Ashar's homeworld

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Riggs Ashar's homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Riggs_Ashar%27s_homeworld)

A planet was the homeworld of Riggs Ashar, who worked for the planet's militia by guarding the landing area at a spaceport. Near the spaceport, there was a bar that Ashar frequented.
