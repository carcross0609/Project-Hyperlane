# Dwi'kar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dwi'kar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dwi%27kar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dwi'kar`, Mid Rim — map position (-392.5, 4181.9, 264.0)

Dwi'kar was a gas giant and the third planet of the Kothlis system, located within the Bothan sector on the Mid Rim of the galaxy.

Along with the other gas giant of the system, Dwi'lar, the two were known as "siblings" or "the twins" by the locals of Kothlis that orbited between them. Dwi'kar was the hotter of the two and its surface consisted of orange and red gases.

Botha Ammunitions Corporation harvested blaster gases from the two planets and maintained an orbital refinery on Dwi'kar's fifth moon.
