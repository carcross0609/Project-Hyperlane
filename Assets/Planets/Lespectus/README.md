# Lespectus

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lespectus](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lespectus)

Lespectus was a Core Worlds planet located in the Lespectus system in grid square M-10 of the Standard Galactic Grid. A hyperspace route connected it to the Fedalle and Kuat systems.

Lespectus was the homeworld of the Moldwarp males Draiven Bosh, who lived during the High Republic Era, and Edmo Ectacle, who was born around 22 BBY. In 34 ABY, historian Eloc Throno noted the planet's position on a galactic map included in his book Traveler's Guide to Batuu. By the time of the First Order occupation of the planet Batuu the same year, the pirate Hondo Ohnaka had obtained a stolen identity profile of Ectacle which mentioned Lespectus, as did a bounty posting for Ectacle that was outstanding around the same time.
