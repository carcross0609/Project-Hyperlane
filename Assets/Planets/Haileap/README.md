# Haileap

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Haileap](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Haileap)

Haileap was a terrestrial planet in the Haileap system in the Dalnan sector, a subsector of the Tunka sector in the Outer Rim Territories. Its primary spaceport was the outpost Port Haileap. During the High Republic Era, Port Haileap was watched by a Jedi marshal. The starship Steady Wing departed from Haileap before it was destroyed further into the system in an attack by Nihil pirates.
