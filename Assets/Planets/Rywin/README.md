# Rywin

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rywin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rywin)

Rywin was a planet in the galaxy. It was the homeworld of the tromonids. Mere months after a scientific expedition to the planet resulted in the discovery of the species, they had infested five other planets due to their high reproduction rate.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Rywin
- **Species:** Tromonids
