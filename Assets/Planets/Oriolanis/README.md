# Oriolanis

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Oriolanis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oriolanis)

Oriolanis was a terrestrial planet located in the Oriolanis system, a part of the Mid Rim's Oriolan sector. The minor technology company Oriolanis Defense Systems, which existed by 27 BBY, was based on Oriolanis. Shortly before the Battle of Endor of 4 ABY, Oriolanis was the site of a Class II military shipyard used by the Galactic Empire. The output of the facility was limited to Imperial-class Star Destroyers.
