# Basarais

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Basarais](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Basarais)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Basarais`, Expansion Region, grid O-11 — map position (4782.4, -1797.5, -167.7)

Basarais was a planet in the Expansion Region. During the Black Fleet Crisis, outside Basarais, a children’s counting rhyme was known that went, «If I had a fat toe, a short toe, a black toe, a new toe, I would know where to go».
