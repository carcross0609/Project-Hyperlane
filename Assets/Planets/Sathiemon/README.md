# Sathiemon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sathiemon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sathiemon)

Sathiemon was an arid desert world located in the galaxy, where Boba Fett was presumed dead by a hit squad during the Imperial Period, while the latter was hunting a bounty. Sathiemon had numerous holes and burrows, that the natives used.
