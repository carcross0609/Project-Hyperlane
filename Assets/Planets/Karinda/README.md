**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Karinda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karinda)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Karinda`, Outer Rim, grid J-18 — map position (-2233.8, -12313.0, -16.9)

Karinda was an arid, dusty Outer Rim Territories planet that lay in the Wazta sector. During the Galactic Civil War, the Galactic Empire maintained a small garrison there, while the Rebel Alliance also constructed a hidden listening outpost on the planet. At one point, the Rebel light freighter Arion Star delivered supplies to the outpost, with the starship's crew experiencing in the process both a rough landing on Karinda and pursuit by an Imperial force from the garrison.
