# Drexel II

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Drexel II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drexel_II)

Drexel II, also called the "water world of Drexel II," lacked a proper name. It was an ocean-covered planet. The sentient Inleshat species were once indigeonous to the planet. C-3PO aided the Rebel Alliance on Drexel II during the period of 0 ABY and 3 ABY. During the Yuuzhan Vong invasion, a group of Gluss'sa'Nikto established a colony on Drexel II.
