# Torrenoteer Minor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Torrenoteer Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torrenoteer_Minor)

Torrenoteer Minor was a desert planet located in the Torrenoteer system. It had three moons, two which were green, and the third being violet. In 21 ABY, Dathan, Miramir, and their daughter Rey traveled to Torrenoteer Minor aboard the Goldstone where they hoped to meet with Lando Calrissian and Jedi Master Luke Skywalker. When the two arrived, Calrissian made his way to the Goldstone, but was captured by forces of the Jedi hunter Ochi before he could make contact with the family, and the Goldstone fled. Calrissian was able to escape, and Skywalker defeated the bounty hunters. After they searched the ships of the bounty hunters, they realized they were not able to locate the Goldstone, and so Skywalker suggested they head to the Aubreeyan Cluster where he knew someone who could help them find the location of the Goldstone and the family.
