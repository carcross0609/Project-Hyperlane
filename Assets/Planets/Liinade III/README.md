# Liinade III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Liinade III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liinade_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Liinade III`, Outer Rim — map position (13713.6, -4925.2, -345.6)

Liinade III was an agriworld in the Ciutric Hegemony. Its development began near the end of the Galactic Republic and continued under the Galactic Empire. It had light industry which produced comlinks and caf synthesizers. It fell to the New Republic in 9 ABY during the Battle of Liinade III.
