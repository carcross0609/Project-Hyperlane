# Askaj

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Askaj](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Askaj)

Askaj, also known as Askaji, was a planet located in the Askaj system in the Cegul sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld of the Askajian species. On Askaji, the native Askajians lived in tribes led by a chief. By 5 ABY, it had joined the New Republic and was represented in the Galactic Senate by Senator Grelka Sorka.
