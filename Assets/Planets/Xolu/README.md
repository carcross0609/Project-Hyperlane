# Xolu

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xolu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xolu)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Xolu`, Hutt Space, grid S-13 — map position (11308.7, -4910.7, -33.4)

Xolu was a planet that was located within the Xolu system in Hutt Space, a region of the galaxy controlled by the Hutt species. It was connected to the worlds of Hollastin and Far Pando by the hyperlane called the Pando Spur.

Xolu was the homeworld of the sentient Yahk-Tosh species that had pledged fealty to the Hutt Grand Council, the ruling political body of the Hutt species. The planet was considered important to the Hutt slavery culture.
