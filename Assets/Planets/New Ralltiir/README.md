# New Ralltiir

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [New Ralltiir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Ralltiir)

New Ralltiir was a planet on which Han Solo, Leia Organa Solo, and Chewbacca rested after the first defeat of the clone of Palpatine. While there, Voren Na'al interviewed Han about his exploits in the Corporate Sector years before.
