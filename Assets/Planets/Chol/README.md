# Chol

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chol)

Chol was a planet in the Cholganna system of the Outer Rim Territories. It orbited the star Chol, with three larger planets orbiting further away than it, Cholganna, Ryien and Be'ekk. It was brown in color. The Cholganna system was partially engulfed by the Chol Nebula, and the planets in the Star system passed through the nebula during their orbit.
