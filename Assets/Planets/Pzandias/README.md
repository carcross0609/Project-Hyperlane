# Pzandias

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pzandias](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pzandias)

Pzandias was a planet located in the Pzandias system within the Bryx sector of the Mid Rim and the Slice. It was situated in grid square S-7 of the Standard Galactic Grid.

Sometime between 22 BBY and 19 BBY, amid the Clone Wars between the Confederacy of Independent Systems and Galactic Republic, Pzandias became affiliated with the Confederacy along with its sector. Under Separatist control, Head of State Count Dooku stationed General Horn Ambigene on the planet, where he was titled the Commander of Organic Training for the Separatists, tasked with training biological individuals in defending their homeworlds from the Republic.
