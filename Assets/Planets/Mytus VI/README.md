# Mytus VI

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mytus VI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mytus_VI)

Mytus VI was the sixth planet in the Mytus system. It was unoccupied, being barren and inhospitable to humanoid life. Mytus VI was the planet assigned to Torm Dadeferron during Han Solo's scheme to determine Rekkon's assassin.

## Planetary data

- **Name:** Mytus VI
- **Region:** Wild Space
- **System:** Mytus system
- **Coordinates:** S-3
- **Suns:** 1: Mytus
- **Terrain:** Barren rock
