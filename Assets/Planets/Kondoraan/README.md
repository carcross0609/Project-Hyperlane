# Kondoraan

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kondoraan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kondoraan)

Kondoraan was a planet within the galaxy. The inhabitants used sky skimming boards as their main method of transportation. In 232 BBY, Zia Zaldor Zanna took a group of Jedi younglings to Kondoraan, where they learned how to surf.
