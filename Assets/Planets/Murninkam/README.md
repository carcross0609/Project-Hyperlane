# Murninkam

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Murninkam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Murninkam)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Murninkam`, Outer Rim — map position (5354.3, 7864.1, -147.0)

Murninkam was a tropical world far from the space lanes. The Kajiin Swamp can be found there.

Spinda Caveel chose that planet for his hideout, both because of its isolated astrographic location, and the dangerous natural environment. His fortress/laboratory stood out in the jungle, but inside a powerful Full-globe class-one Imperial shield. The Pike Sisters, under Caveel's employ, came to the planet to deliver him A-OIC, a medical droid they had stolen. The planet was protected by their Robotic picket ships, which engaged Guri's Stinger, and fell into Kajiin, soon followed by Kar Yang's ship, being after Guri. Guri left the planet with A-OIC, secretly followed by Kar Yang.
