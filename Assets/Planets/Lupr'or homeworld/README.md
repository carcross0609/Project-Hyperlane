# Lupr'or homeworld

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lupr'or homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lupr%27or_homeworld)

Many years before the Battle of Starkiller Base, the Lupr'or settled on the planet Luprora after they escaped a planet that had been spoiled by technology. After realizing that technology would be the end of them if they did not change, the Lupr'or buried and destroyed the technology that had brought them to Luprora.
