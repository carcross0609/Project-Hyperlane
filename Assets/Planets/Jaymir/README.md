# Jaymir

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jaymir](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaymir)

Jaymir was a frontier world located on the edges of the Mid Rim Territories, in grid square I-16 of the Standard Galactic Grid. It was the homeworld of the human siblings Aarton and Nimi Chireen, who became members of the Resistance.
