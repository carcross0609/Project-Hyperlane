# Solu Five

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Solu Five](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solu_Five)

Solu Five was a planet located in the Outer Rim Territories along several profitable hyperlanes. During the Galactic War, the planet was represented in the Galactic Senate of the Galactic Republic by Senator Grom Zian.
