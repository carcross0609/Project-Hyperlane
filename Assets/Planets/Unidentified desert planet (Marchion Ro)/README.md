# Unidentified desert planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified desert planet (Marchion Ro)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_desert_planet_%28Marchion_Ro%29)

An unnamed desert planet was located near Wild Space. During the High Republic Era, the planet was one of the worlds where Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro of the Nihil marauders seeded the Drengir carnivorous plant species, intending to experiment whether they would thrive in a place with little traditional vegetation. The blight eventually began to spread on the desert planet, and Ro visited the world during his investigations of the affliction, using the Rod of Power to safely walk into the blighted desert.
