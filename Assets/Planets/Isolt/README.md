# Isolt

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Isolt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Isolt)

Isolt was a pastoral planet that fell within the Nihil marauders' Occlusion Zone territory during the High Republic Era. The Nihil, attempting to trigger a volcanic eruption similar to the event that devastated the planet Dalna, bombed Isolt repeatedly. The bombing destroyed nearly all life on Isolt and caused at least one major volcanic eruption. Nihil General Abediah Viess established a base on Isolt, retreating there after escaping from the planet Naboo. Jedi Master Porter Engle, having been led by Viess, followed her to Isolt to fight her.
