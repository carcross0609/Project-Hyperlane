# Chrona

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chrona/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chrona/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Chrona`, Inner Rim, grid O-8 — map position (5699.8, 2027.3, 10.8)

Chrona was an agriworld in the Inner Rim, near the Vaathkree Trade Corridor. Banthas were raised and primacale crops were grown on the planet. During the final days of the Galactic Republic, chronamite was discovered on the planet.
