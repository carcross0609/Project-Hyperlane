# Kogan 6

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kogan 6](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kogan_6)

Kogan 6 was a planet in the Kogan system of the Outer Rim Territories. Dr. Bromial led an Obroan Institute archeological expedition onboard the Penga Rift to Kogan 6 after the Black Fleet Crisis.
