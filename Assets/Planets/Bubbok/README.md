# Bubbok

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bubbok](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bubbok)

Bubbok was a terrestrial planet in the Dagobah system, located in the Outer Rim Territories' Sluis sector. It had three moons and occupied the fourth orbital position around the star Darlo. Along with the rest of the star system, Bubbok was situated on the hyperlane known as the Rimma Trade Route. Bubbok's surface consisted of ice, and it featured a frigid environment.
