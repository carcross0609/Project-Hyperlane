# Zaadja

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zaadja](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zaadja)

Zaadja was a planet located within the Savareen sector of the region known as the Outer Rim Territories. During the Clone Wars between the Galactic Republic and Separatist Alliance, Rodian Senator Onaconda Farr, who represented Zaadja and the entire sector in the Republic Senate, contracted with the Confederacy, which allowed for the Savareen sector to receive foodstuffs from the confederate government's allies, the Trade Federation, in exchange. Furthermore, as a result of Farr's deal with the Confederacy, the government would also be provided exploitation rights to Zaadja and the rest of the planets in the sector, such as Nelvaan and Christophsis.
