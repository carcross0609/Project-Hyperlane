# Athus Klee

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Athus Klee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Athus_Klee)

Athus Klee was a world in the Athus Klee system in the Kalamith sector of the Outer Rim Territories. The Besalisk Dexter Jettster traveled to the world during the reign of the Galactic Empire and discovered an ore hub operated by the Crimson Dawn criminal syndicate where workers had been cybernetically altered by Doctor Cornelius Evazan. The Besalisk managed to rescue one Rodian worker from the hub and reported it to the Imperial Senate's Council of Labor Abuses.
