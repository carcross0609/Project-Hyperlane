# Vodran

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vodran](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vodran)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Vodran`, Hutt Space, grid S-9 — map position (11598.3, 1068.9, 314.0)

Vodran was a swampy, murky jungle planet located in the Vodran system in the Si'Klaata Cluster of Hutt Space, in grid square S-9 on the Standard Galactic Grid. It was the homeworld of the sentient Vodran species, along with dianogas, carnivorous cephalopods that spread themselves across the galaxy by climbing aboard starship bilges and stowing away in their garbage tanks. It was conquered by the Hutt Cartel, which was controlled by the Hutts from the neighboring world of Nal Hutta, who turned the Vodrans into slaves or vassals. Sometime later the Hutts had abandoned the planet and the swamp had taken back much of it.
