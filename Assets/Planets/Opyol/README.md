# Opyol

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Opyol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Opyol)

Opyol was a terrestrial planet located in the Opoku system, located in the Galov sector as part of the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet occupied the fourth orbital position around the star Opoku and had a single moon. Along with the rest of its system, Opyol was situated along the Triellus Trade Route.
