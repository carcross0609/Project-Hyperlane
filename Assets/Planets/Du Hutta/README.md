# Du Hutta

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Du Hutta](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Du_Hutta)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Du Hutta`, Hutt Space, grid S-11 — map position (10696.9, -2577.9, 430.9)

Du Hutta was a terrestrial world located in the Du Hutta system, a part of the Hutt Space portion of the Slice. It was connected by the Ootmian Pabol hyperlane to the Irith and Y'Toub systems.

Du Hutta was a colony world of the Hutt species and featured numerous private retreats owned by Hutts from the species' adopted homeworld, Nal Hutta. In addition, less prominent members of several Hutt clans maintained estates there. In the years following the Battle of Endor of 4 ABY, the crime lord Jocoro Desilijic Tiure operated a small criminal enterprise from a base on Du Hutta, and the Klatooinian former bodyguard Umpass-stay eventually came to serve the Hutt faithfully there.
