# Nelgenam

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nelgenam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nelgenam)

Nelgenam was a planet in the Tashtor sector of the Mid Rim Territories that was home to the ancient Lost Library of Nelgenam. Centuries prior to the era of the First Order, Nelgenam was a paradise world, hosting many visitors from across the galaxy. However, the planet was eventually torched by Nihil raiders, and was left a desolate wasteland, with the library falling into ruin.

The cartographer Emil Graf managed to recover Nelgenam's coordinates from the First Order, and ventured to the planet to investigate the library. On Nelgenam, Graf encountered a Tarinna, a member of a species that fed on memories. The Tarinna tried to feed off of the many tales Graf had heard, but was defeated with the help of the cartographer's companions.
