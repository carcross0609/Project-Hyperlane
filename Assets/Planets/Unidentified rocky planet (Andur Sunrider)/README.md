# Unidentified rocky planet (Andur Sunrider)

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified rocky planet (Andur Sunrider)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_rocky_planet_%28Andur_Sunrider%29)

This rocky planet was visited by Jedi Knight Andur Sunrider and his family in 3999 BBY. While on their way to deliver Adegan crystals to Jedi Master Thon, the Sunriders were forced to land on the planet when their ship, the Lightside Explorer, had a malfunction in its hyperdrive. However, the problem was quickly fixed, and the Sunriders departed.
