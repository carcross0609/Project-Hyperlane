# Cabarria

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cabarria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cabarria)

Cabarria was a planet in the Colonies and the Interior regions where the Sith Lord Darth Vader and the Inquisitor Ninth Sister traveled to investigate a reported Jedi sighting at a bar in 19 BBY. However, Vader instead walked into a trap set by the Chas, a bounty hunter family that had been hired to kill him. The Chas were able to disarm Vader, but the Sith Lord instead used patrons as both shields and projectiles, soon forcing the bounty hunters to flee.

Vader pursued the Chas in a speeder chase until he was able to ground the family, making the parents Bhada and Ramat Cha help him find out who hired them by threatening the life of their daughter, Chanath Cha. Chanath was made to swear to forget the encounter and flee in order to survive while her parents located the origin of the kill order on Vader. Once given the information he needed, Vader slew Bhada and Ramat, Chanath bearing witness from afar, and left for the planet Coruscant.
