# Hypotria

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hypotria](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hypotria)

Hypotria was a Mid Rim planet situated in the Bortele sector. During the Galactic Civil War, a deep-cover Rebel Alliance operative and their astromech droid, R2z-DL, were posted on Hypotria, where the latter, posing as a maintenance droid, was stealing valuable data off starships arriving for repairs and then placing it in the memory banks of Rebel Alliance vessels.

At one point, the Laboi crime lord Qa'till, whose employees killed the Rebel operative, assumed control over the planet's starship maintenance operations. R2z-DL, however, continued his Alliance assignment, eventually passing on information that facilitated the liberation of the planet Vilosoria from the Galactic Empire's control.
