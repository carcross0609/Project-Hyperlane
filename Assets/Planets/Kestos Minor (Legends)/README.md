# Kestos Minor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kestos Minor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kestos_Minor/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kestos Minor`, Outer Rim — map position (5535.1, 7612.4, -417.0)

Kestos Minor was a planet. It was represented directly in the Galactic Senate by Ivor Drake.

The planet was targeted by the Galactic Empire during the Kwymar Suppressions. During the Imperial assault on the planet, Modigal Glave sustained facial damage.
