# Bathoris

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bathoris](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bathoris)

Bathoris was a frozen planet located in the Bathoris system in the Inner Rim Territories, situated in grid square N-8 of the Standard Galactic Grid. According to legend, it was home to a temple of the Jedi Order, where the Sith Lord Darth Caldoth was once defeated by the Jedi Master Bran Ath'Morath. Around 362 BBY a team of Republic Pathfinders visited Bathoris as part of their investigation into the death of a Jedi Master, and discovered that a comms team had also been murdered on the world.
