# Tieos

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tieos/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tieos/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tieos`, Outer Rim, grid S-15 — map position (10630.9, -8493.8, 239.8)

Tieos was a planet located in the Tieos system, a part of the Abrion sector within the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet featured several oceans on its surface, and its landmasses were completely covered in grasslands. Shortly following the Battle of Yavin, Tieos held massive future potential as an agriworld.

The father of the Galactic Empire TIE pilot T. Alvak at one point served as senior port master of the Imperial base on Tieos. At some point during the Galactic Civil War, the Rebel Alliance pilot Wedge Antilles was shot down over Tieos. In an annotation he later made in a captured copy of the Imperial Military manual Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide, Antilles mentioned the incident, further noting that downed starfighter pilots should put maximum effort into avoiding being discovered by repulsorlift companies of the Imperial Army.
