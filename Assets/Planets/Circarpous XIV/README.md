# Circarpous XIV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Circarpous XIV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Circarpous_XIV)

Circarpous XIV was the fourteenth and outermost planet in the Circarpous system. Its fifth moon had extensive volcanic activity which kept it warm and habitable.

During the Galactic Civil War, the Rebel Alliance maintained a secret base on the moon which served as nexus for operations on Circarpous III and Circarpous IV.
