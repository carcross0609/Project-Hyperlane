# Unidentified gas planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified gas planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_gas_planet)

A large gas planet was located in the same system as the ocean planet Arli within the Outer Rim Territories. Situated closest to its sun, the gas planet was also the star system's namesake.
