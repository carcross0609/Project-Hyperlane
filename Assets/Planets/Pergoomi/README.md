# Pergoomi

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pergoomi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pergoomi)

Pergoomi was one of several planets in the Mygeeto system of the Albarrio sector, which was located in the Outer Rim Territories and New Territories regions. In close orbit to the star of Malola, Pergoomi was an airless rock that was orbited by several moons. It also lay at grid square K-5 on the Standard Galactic Grid.
