# Qotile

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Qotile/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qotile/Legends)

Qotile was a world located within the Qotile system, in the Colundra sector of the Outer Rim Territories. Ravaged by the Stark Hyperspace Conflict, a deserter fled here and established himself king, though it would not be long before the bounty hunter Bossk claimed the bounty and inherited the title.
