# Pygorix

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pygorix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pygorix)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Pygorix`, Outer Rim, grid Q-6 — map position (8789.0, 5778.3, -222.5)

Pygorix was a world located in the Pygorix system. It was situated in the Back Reach area of the Gordian Reach sector within the Trans-Hydian portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The hyperlane known as the Ardan Cross connected it to the Feldwes system as well as the Spintir system of the Spadja sector. A smaller hyperspace route linked Pygorix to the Karsten's World system.

Pygorix served as a religious retreat known for centuries-long study of the religious beliefs and experiences of various species. Fees collected at an orbital station that served as a customs port made Pygorix a wealthy world, and access to the nearby Karsten's World system was restricted to the high abbots of Pygorix.
