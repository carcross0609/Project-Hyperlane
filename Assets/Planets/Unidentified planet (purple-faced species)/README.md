# Unidentified planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified planet (purple-faced species)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_planet_%28purple-faced_species%29)

A planet was located in the Outer Rim, near the border of the Galactic Republic's territory. The world was conquered by Mandalorian forces under control of Rohlan Dyre, a Mandalorian Crusader who was trying to escape the army so he could discover the true motivations behind Mandalorian expansion into Republic space.
