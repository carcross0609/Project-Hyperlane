# Zhadalene

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zhadalene](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zhadalene)

Zhadalene was a world located in the Kanz sector's Zhadalene system within an intersecting portion of the Outer Rim Territories and the New Territories. Situated within grid square N-4 of the Standard Galactic Grid, it lay along the Veragi Trade Route hyperlane between the Argazda system and the Sh'ung-tesk system. By 5 ABY, during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the New Republic, Zhadalene contributed to the Imperial Military. In a meeting with his new Imperial Shadow Council that year, Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax spoke about Imperial holdings in the Outer Rim being vital to the Empire's progress in the war, listing Zhadalene, Belladoon, and Korrus as worlds in that region that were already under their sway.
