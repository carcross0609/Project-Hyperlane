# Hensara III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hensara III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hensara_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Hensara III`, Colonies, grid N-11 — map position (3274.2, -2114.3, -68.8)

Hensara III, also known simply as Hensara, was a planet in the Colonies. The New Republic's stolen Imperial Customs Frigate Battle of Yavin was sunk in a deep lake on this planet to hide it from an Imperial patrol. The Eridain and Rogue Squadron retrieved the ship's crew and defeated the Empire in the Rout of Hensara.
