# Kich

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kich](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kich)

Kich was a hot terrestrial planet with no moons. It was located in the Rannon system, a part of the Instrop sector in the Slice portion of the Outer Rim Territories. The planet occupied the second closest orbit to its star, the yellow-white Rannon.
