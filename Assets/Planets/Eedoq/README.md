# Eedoq

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eedoq](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eedoq)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Eedoq`, Outer Rim — map position (11246.9, -7788.7, -72.6)

Eedoq was a world in the Outer Rim's Tantra sector. Eedoq was the homeworld of the Camblian Order, which, shortly before the Battle of Yavin around in 1 BBY, sent 309 prospective students to be enrolled at the Clarion Scholars Academy on the planet Daemen. However, their transport ship, the Lark, was destroyed during the journey by the Imperial pilot Tars Nandy, resulting in the deaths of all 309 children.
