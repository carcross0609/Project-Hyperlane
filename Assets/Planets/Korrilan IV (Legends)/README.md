# Korrilan IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Korrilan IV/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Korrilan_IV/Legends)

Korrilan IV was a planet in the galaxy. The Zalpertine Cup was held on the world, a Podracing series that catered to racers piloting CR-17 Incendias.
