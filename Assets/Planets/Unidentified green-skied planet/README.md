# Unidentified green-skied planet

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified green-skied planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_green-skied_planet)

The Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi and Senator Dewell Bronk had to change transports on a planet with green colored skies while trying to flee Coruscant following the end of the Clone Wars. Bronk encountered a pair of clone troopers who tried to arrest him on his way to his transport at Pad 560, but Kenobi saved him, allowing him to successfully leave the world.
