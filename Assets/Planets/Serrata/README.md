# Serrata

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Serrata](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Serrata)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Serrata`, Outer Rim, grid M-19 — map position (2387.5, -13936.0, 167.3)

Serrata was the eighth planet in the Derilyn system. It was a gas giant and had 5 moons. Serrata was a near-star, its mass falling just below the necessary level to make the transition. In the sky of Derilyn, Serrata was a reddish disk as wide as the length of a man's index finger.
