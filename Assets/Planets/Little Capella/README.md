# Little Capella

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Little Capella](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Little_Capella)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Little Capella`, Outer Rim, grid Q-6 — map position (8397.4, 5429.1, 42.0)

Little Capella was a planet in the Little Capella system in the Gordian Reach, a region in the Outer Rim Territories. In 2 ABY, the company Rapora Mining operated the Rapora Mining Center on Little Capella as one of many new facilities throughout the Outer Rim.
