# Molos

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Molos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Molos)

Molos was a planet located in the Stensen sector within the Outer Rim Territories that was under the rule of King Amand during the Galactic Civil War and controlled by the Galactic Empire. Following an uprising against the Empire, the Alliance to Restore the Republic dispatched aid, leading to the creation of a civilian army. In the following Battle of Molopolis, the army fought against the Empire's reinforcements and defeated them.
