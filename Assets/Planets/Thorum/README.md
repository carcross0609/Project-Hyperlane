# Thorum

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thorum](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thorum)

Thorum was a minor jungle planet located in the Thorum system of the Vendusii sector in the Mid Rim Territories of the galaxy. It had a substantial Crimson Dawn presence. Calda was the planet's largest city and a business hub. The Hammers syndicate were also located on the world.
