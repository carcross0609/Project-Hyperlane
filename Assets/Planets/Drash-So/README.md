# Drash-So

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Drash-So](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drash-So)

Drash-So was a planet located in the Selvatas system of the Outer Rim Territories. During the Second Imperial Civil War, the One Sith ran a prison labor camp on the planet. Ania Solo and Ramid were two of the inmates there. The camp was liberated by the Empire-in-Exile at the end of the war.
