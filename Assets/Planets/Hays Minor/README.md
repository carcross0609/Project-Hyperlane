# Hays Minor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hays Minor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hays_Minor)

Hays Minor was a planet in the Otomok system that was the homeworld of Rose and Paige Tico. Prior to the pair joining the Resistance, the First Order used the world to test weapons after stripping it of its Haysian smelt and also stole children to become stormtroopers. After the First Order's arrival, the once quiet and unspoiled planet became dark and polluted.
