# Kendamar

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kendamar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kendamar)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Kendamar`, Outer Rim — map position (4563.1, 6918.7, 114.9)

Kendamar was a terrestrial planet located in the Kendamar system, a part of the Outer Rim Territories' Lol sector. Around 10 ABY, it served as the homeworld of Kendamari Casinos, a chain of casinos that constituted a part of the Tenloss Syndicate.
