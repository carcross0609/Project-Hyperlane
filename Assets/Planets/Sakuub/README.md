# Sakuub

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sakuub](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sakuub)

Sakuub was a world located in the Sakuub system of Pelgrin sector in the Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld of the Sakuubians.

The planet contained Old Town and the Sky Temple of Karsol, and was a tourist spot for its sector.

In 44 ABY, after the defeat of Abeloth, Jaina Solo and Jagged Fel honeymooned there.
