# Unidentified forest planet

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified forest planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_forest_planet)

A forest planet was located in the galaxy. Under the rule of the Galactic Empire, a rebel cell known as the Spectres, came in their starship Ghost and navigated the planet, before they picked up some supplies, destroyed machines and a com array, liberated prisoners of the Empire, and destroyed an All Terrain Defense Pod.
