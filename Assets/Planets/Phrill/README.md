# Phrill

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phrill](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phrill)

Phrill was a planet located in the galaxy. Melis Shryke, a member of the Nihil had launched an attack on Phrill, leaving the planet in devastation. Bell Zettifar and Burryaga Agaburry traveled to the planet's main town, Perseverance alongside other Republic Defense Coalition relief ships. Here, they rescued 12 citizens stuck under a building, giving Bell Zettifar newfound hope towards his cause of breaking through the Stormwall.
