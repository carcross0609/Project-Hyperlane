# Nag Ubdur

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nag Ubdur](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nag_Ubdur)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Nag Ubdur`, Outer Rim — map position (-3649.6, -11094.5, 240.1)

Nag Ubdur was a planet located in the Ubdur system of the the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. It was inhabited by Ubdurians. Binjai-Tin was considered to be a large merchant city on the planet.

Up until a few months after the Battle of Endor, the planet was under the control of the Galactic Empire, who strip-mined the world for zersium. Sometime after the Rebellion on Akiva, the New Republic attempted to liberate the world, but was met with an increasingly-desperate Imperial defense.
