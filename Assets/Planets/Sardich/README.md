# Sardich

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sardich](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sardich)

Sardich was a terrestrial planet located in the Sardich system of the Unknown Regions. It was the location of Cluost City as well as a Senator's mansion.

The Resistance General Finn and Jannah followed a lead there after the Battle of Exegol to find First Order Major Chetachi Gohl there after rescuing a ship full of young passengers who had been kidnapped by the First Order. During their search in the abandoned Senator's mansion, one of the rescued children, Niila, revealed herself as the mysterious Lieutenant Shroud whom Finn and Jannah had both encountered years earlier.
