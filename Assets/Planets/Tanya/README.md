# Tanya

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tanya](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tanya)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Tanya`, Colonies, grid L-13 — map position (821.5, -5302.8, -198.6)

Tanya was a planet located in the Tapani sector's Tanya system. The rebel cell known as Shimper Ale maintained a manor house on Tanya. The planet produced vast harvests of grain and vegetables each year and was an important agricultural producer of the sector.
