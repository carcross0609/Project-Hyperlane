# Homon

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Homon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Homon/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Homon`, Outer Rim — map position (10055.5, 5642.4, -151.9)

Homon was a Mid Rim planet located in the Homon sector. While the Galactic Empire was carrying out an offensive in Homon's vicinity during the Galactic Civil War, the Rebel Alliance Chief of Sector Command suggested during a meeting of the Alliance High Command that the Rebel starfighter base at Homon could be stripped to form a fighter wing for a planned assault on an Imperial base over the planet Mantooine.
