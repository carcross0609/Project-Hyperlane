# Darlyn Boda

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darlyn Boda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darlyn_Boda)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Darlyn Boda`, Outer Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-1325.2, -13102.6, -454.4)

Darlyn Boda was a planet in the Anoat sector of the Outer Rim Territories. During the Galactic Empire's Iron Blockade, Darlyn Boda had a criminal network which included the Trade Spine League and the Ivax Syndicate.

By the time of the Iron Blockade, a group of New Republic pilots were stranded in the planet pursued by Moff Ubrik Adelhard's forces. Adelhard personally joined his stormtroopers on the planet where he berated the Rebels' commitment to the New Republic, personally executing a pilot who stood up to him.
