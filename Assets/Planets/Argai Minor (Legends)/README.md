# Argai Minor

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Argai Minor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Argai_Minor/Legends)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Argai Minor`, Colonies, grid M-9 — map position (2114.6, 787.4, -69.3)

Argai Minor, also known as Argai, was a Colonies planet located in the Haldeen sector. When the Galactic Republic exploration and colonization vessel Outbound Flight was launchd in 27 BBY, Argai Minor was one of the stops on its shakedown cruise through Republic space.
