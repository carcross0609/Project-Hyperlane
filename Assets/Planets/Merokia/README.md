# Merokia

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Merokia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merokia)

Merokia was a desert planet located in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories near the Unknown Regions, in grid square J-18 on the Standard Galactic Grid. It was inhabited during the era of the New Republic. Merokia was the homeworld of the Force-sensitive human Karr Nuq Sin.

During the High Republic Era Merokia was allied to E'ronoh but cut ties after the Eiram and E'ronoh War reignited again.
