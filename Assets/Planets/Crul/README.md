# Crul

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Crul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crul)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Crul`, Mid Rim, grid M-11 — map position (2170.7, -2717.1, -61.7)

Crul was a planet located in the galaxy's Mid Rim that was covered in warm, shallow, and salty seas and lagoons. It was the homeworld of two sentient species: the Crolutes, who were exclusively male, and the Gilliands, who were exclusively female.
