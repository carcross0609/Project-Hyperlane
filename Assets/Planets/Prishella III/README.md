# Prishella III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Prishella III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prishella_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Prishella III`, Outer Rim, grid T-10 — map position (13224.0, -361.9, -260.9)

Prishella III was an Outer Rim Territories planet located in the Kessel sector. It hosted several garrisons of the Galactic Empire. During the Galactic Civil War, a Rebel Alliance Special Forces team was air-dropped on Prishella III, where it kidnapped Imperial General Evar Orllincus during his inspection tour of the garrisons.
