# Perimako Major

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Perimako Major](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Perimako_Major)

Perimako Major was a world where the Alliance to Restore the Republic fought against the Galactic Empire between 2 BBY and 3 ABY. Wedge Antilles and Derek Klivian, former Imperial cadets who had defected to become starfighter pilots in the Rebellion, were present during the battle at Perimako Major.
