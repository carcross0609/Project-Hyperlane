# Gargolyn IV

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gargolyn IV](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gargolyn_IV)

Gargolyn IV was a planet known to be a garden paradise that was lush with animal life and vegetation. It was the homeworld of the Cor species. The land of the Cors was guarded by a huge beast that destroyed any settlement the scouting Karflo Corporation initiated.
