# Jaresh

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jaresh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaresh)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Jaresh`, Outer Rim — map position (5847.8, -13745.5, 311.9)

Jaresh was a planet in the Corva sector of the Outer Rim Territories. A key member of the Galactic Republic during the High Republic Era, Jaresh received a sky-island at the Republic Fair on Valo which contained a representation of its bustling markets. It was the homeworld of the Kyuzo Grimshaw, who was the cousin of Nyessa.
