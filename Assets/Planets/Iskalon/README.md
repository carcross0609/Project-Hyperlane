# Iskalon

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Iskalon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iskalon)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Iskalon`, Mid Rim, grid R-16 — map position (9533.8, -9417.2, -315.8)

Iskalon was a planet located in the Iskalon system in the Trans-Nebular sector of the Mid Rim. The pirate Queen Maz Kanata hid a portion of the spoils from her reign there in a buried vault equipped with a antitamper sub-space alarm. As of when she began writing the book that would become known as the Smuggler's Guide, the alarm protecting the cache of treasure on Iskalon had not sounded. The Iskalon uprising occurred during the Imperial Era.
