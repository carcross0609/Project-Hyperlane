# Riah

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Riah](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Riah)

Riah was a planet located in the Nihil Occlusion Zone during the High Republic Era. In 228 BBY, Riah was struck by the blight and its populace evacuated by the Republic Defense Coalition.
