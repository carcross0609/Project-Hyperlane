# Bridin Anchorage

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bridin Anchorage](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bridin_Anchorage)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Bridin Anchorage`, Outer Rim, grid P-18 — map position (7316.1, -12463.8, -225.7)

Bridin Anchorage, originally called Gallienis IV, was once a lush, tourist world in the Portmoak sector. It was renamed Quarlo Bridin, a disgraced politician who took over the world shortly after Thrawn's death. Once his planet's immediate safety was secured, Bridin used his Hutt friends to coerce the passage of a battery of ordinances making a variety of formerly criminal operations legal. This, in turn, resulted in a flood of criminal enterprises to the planet, including Manliss Enterprise.
