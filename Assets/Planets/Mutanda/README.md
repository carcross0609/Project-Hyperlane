# Mutanda

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mutanda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mutanda)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Mutanda`, Mid Rim, grid K-17 — map position (-838.6, -11564.3, 283.1)

Mutanda was a planet in the Killaniri system. The planet was a rolling land of grasslands, jungles, and natural wonders. In addition to the sentient Horansi, the world had many different species of big game animals, resulting in a booming legalized safari trade. Primarily, Mutanda was a planet for exotic hunting vacations and was easily accessible because of the spaceport on the satellite of Justa. However, the encroachments of poachers resulted in the extinction of many species of Mutandan wildlife.
