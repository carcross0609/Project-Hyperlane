# Ominia

**Continuity:** Unknown  
**Source:** [Ominia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ominia)

Ominia was a glacial planet in the Epsilon 69.02 system. The planet's surface was completely covered in ice and had no indigenous life. A mining station owned by Yohn Stelly was present on the planet.
