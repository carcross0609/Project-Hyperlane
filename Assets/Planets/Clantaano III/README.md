# Clantaano III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Clantaano III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clantaano_III)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Clantaano III`, Outer Rim, grid U-12 — map position (13679.9, -4080.8, 38.5)

Clantaano III, also known as Clantaano, was a desert planet located within the Outer Rim Territories' Albanin sector. It was the homeworld of the nomadic and violent Clantaani species. The Clantaani gunman Longo Two-Guns and his cousins were born on Clantaano III, and they gave rise to a gang of Clantaani criminals that moved offworld by 32 BBY.
