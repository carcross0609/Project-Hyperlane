# Ragmar V

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ragmar V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ragmar_V)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Ragmar V`, Outer Rim, grid Q-17 — map position (8024.7, -10786.0, 170.4)

Ragmar V was a barren, desert-coated world in the Outer Rim Territories. Its population center was an outpost of outcasts of various species, who were opposed to the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars.
