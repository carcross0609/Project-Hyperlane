# Jamiron

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jamiron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jamiron)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Jamiron`, Unknown Regions, grid F-8 — map position (-8811.5, 2004.6, 109.3)

Jamiron was a planet in the Jamiron system in Chiss Space within the Unknown Regions. Mitth'ali'astov undertook her formal schooling on Jamiron. It was home to several major manufactories, at least one of which was owned by the Ascendancy's Clarr family. As such, there was a large amount of space traffic to and from Jamiron.

In 18 BBY, Jamiron was the site of the Attack at Jamiron and the Skirmish at Jamiron, part of the Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war.
