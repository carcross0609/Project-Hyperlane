# Nigel III

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nigel III](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nigel_III)

Nigel III was a planet in Expansion Region situated along the Enarc Run hyperspace route. It had an Imperial presence during the reign of the Galactic Empire. The world's port authority seized the Kazellis-class light freighter Longrunner after its pilot, Duan Zorn, was arrested for stealing the starship and failing to pay docking fees.
