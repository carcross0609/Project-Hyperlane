# Carnth

**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Carnth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carnth)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Carnth`, Mid Rim, grid M-7 — map position (1969.9, 3748.0, -84.1)

Carnth was a Mid Rim world located in the M'shinni sector. During the Galactic Civil War, the Galactic Empire's prefect on Carnth promised the farmers of a local village substantial rewards for remaining loyal to the Empire, which hampered the Rebel Alliance's efforts to establish a small base at the settlement. Eventually, Airen Cracken led a Rebel force in an assault on the village that ultimately resulted in the settlement aligning itself with the Alliance.
