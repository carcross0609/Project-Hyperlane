# Dasoor

**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dasoor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dasoor)
**Galaxy atlas:** system `Dasoor`, Mid Rim, grid K-18 — map position (-89.3, -12211.6, 137.9)

Dasoor was a planet located in the Dasoor system in the Agarix sector of the Mid Rim Territories. It was known as a world overrun by thieves, and gangs of enslavers. Some months after the Battle of Endor, the smuggler Han Solo and his Wookiee co-pilot Chewbacca were headed to this planet, before changing course to the Wookiee's home planet of Kashyyyk.
