# Boiler rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Boiler rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boiler_rifle)

The boiler rifle was a model of short-range blaster rifle that could be modified with an underbarrel spin-sealed bottle of tibanna gas that illegally doubled the weapon's fire power. In 1 BBY, the Rebel Alliance Special Forces commando Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek used a black market boiler rifle during the Battle of Scarif.

- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Range:** Short-range
