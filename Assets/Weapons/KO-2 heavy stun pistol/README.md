# KO-2 heavy stun pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KO-2 heavy stun pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KO-2_heavy_stun_pistol)

The KO-2 heavy stun pistol was a type of stun pistol manufactured by Palandrix. It was a favored weapon of law enforcement agencies throughout the galaxy's Core Worlds, and although it was large and intimidating, the KO-2 was manufactured from lightweight but durable alloys and plastics, which gave its frame a sleek, stream-lined appearance. In the hands of a trained professional, the KO-2 was powerful enough to put down a Wookiee in one or two blasts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KO-2 heavy stun pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Palandrix
- **Type:** Stun pistol
- **Cost:** 650 credits
- **Material:** Lightweight but durable alloys and plastics
- **Range:** Short-ranged
