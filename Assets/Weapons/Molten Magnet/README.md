# Molten Magnet

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Molten Magnet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Molten_Magnet)

A Molten Magnet was a grenade-like device. Developed by Vurk Rebel cells, keen on weaponizing the planet Sembla's lava in their fight for its liberation, a breakthrough was made after the Vurk Rebel cells obtained Imperial research. Imperial technology was used to contain and stabilize Sembla's magma, which had unique magnetic properties, attracted to metallic surfaces. Early attempts cooled and congealed too fast, so Vurk sages skilled in alchemical arts imbued the containment units with the Force to prevent the magma from cooling.
