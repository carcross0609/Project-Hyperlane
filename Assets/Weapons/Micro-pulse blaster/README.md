# Micro-pulse blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Micro-pulse blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Micro-pulse_blaster)

Micro-pulse blasters were a form of blaster weapons used during the time of the Great Sith War. The weapon worked by firing short controlled bursts of coherent energy rather than a fully powered energy blast. The weapons were usually created from thoranium, allowing them to release more powerful discharges than standard blasters, even larger rifles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Micro-pulse blaster
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Range:** 23 meters
