# Arkanian heavy pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arkanian heavy pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arkanian_heavy_pistol)

The Arkanian heavy pistol was a type of heavy blaster pistol manufactured by the Arkanians at some point around the year 5956 BBY, which was before heavy blaster pistols became popular. Roughly two thousand years later, during the Jedi Civil War and Dark Wars fought between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire, the weapons were still superior to many newer designs. However, the Arkanian heavy pistols were incompatible with new weapon modifications at that point. During that time, the weapons were marketed for sale on planets such as Manaan and Taris.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Arkanian heavy pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Arkanians
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Culture:** Arkanians
- **Created:** c. 5956 BBY
- **Locations:**
  - Manaan
  - Taris
- **Cost:** Between 750 and 1,300 credits
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Range:** 23 meters (Effective range)
- **Purpose:** Ranged combat
