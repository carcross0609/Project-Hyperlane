# Fierce Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fierce Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fierce_Clubs)

The Fierce Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs.
