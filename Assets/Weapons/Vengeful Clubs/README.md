# Vengeful Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vengeful Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vengeful_Clubs)

The Vengeful Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. These clubs consisted of chunks of viper probe droid bodies bound together by loose wiring.
