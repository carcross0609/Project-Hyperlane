# S86k full-gauge carbonite armor upgrade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [S86k full-gauge carbonite armor upgrade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S86k_full-gauge_carbonite_armor_upgrade)

The S86k full-gauge carbonite armor upgrade was a carbonite projector armor attachment that was used by bounty hunters during the Cold War. Crysta Markon gave the bounty hunter she was working with during the Great Hunt a S86k for use in capturing bounties alive.

- **Name:** S86k full-gauge carbonite armor upgrade
