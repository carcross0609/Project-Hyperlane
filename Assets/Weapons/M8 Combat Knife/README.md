# M8 Combat Knife

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M8 Combat Knife](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M8_Combat_Knife)

The M8 Combat Knife was produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc., with one issued to every clone trooper within the Galactic Republic's clone army as a simple melee weapon. The knife also gained wide renown for its uses as both a weapon and a tool. Its handle was constructed from flat, black polycarbonate from a textured material for extra grip and comfort, and was based off another Merr-Sonn product, the Treppus-2 vibroknife. The cost of an M8 Combat Knife was 150 credits.
