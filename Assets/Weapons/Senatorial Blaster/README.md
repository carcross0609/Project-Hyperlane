# Senatorial Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Senatorial Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senatorial_Blaster)

The Senatorial Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This blaster pistol featured a black and gray frame, which was covered in white and gray painted art, including various depictions of stars, as well as a Rebel Alliance starbird stamped on the grip.
