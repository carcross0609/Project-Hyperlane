# Jareni's double-hilted lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jareni's double-hilted lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jareni%27s_double-hilted_lightsaber)

Jareni's double-hilted lightsaber was a white-bladed hinged double-bladed lightsaber utilized by the Jedi Master and Wayseeker Orla Jareni during the High Republic Era. The lightsaber was equipped with a hinge in the middle of the hilt which allowed the weapon to fold to make two parallel blades.
