# SB-920 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SB-920 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SB-920_laser_cannon)

The SB-920 laser cannon was manufactured by Borstel Galactic Defense. They were manufactured exclusively for Imperial starships and the Death Star. It was manned by one gunner who was stationed in a pressurized enclosure. They fired through magnetic Force field ports.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SB-920 laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Borstel Galactic Defense
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Navy
