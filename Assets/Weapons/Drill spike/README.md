# Drill spike

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Drill spike](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drill_spike)

Drill spikes, also called Drill Beaks, were a type of weaponized drill that was used to drill through the hulls of starships or buildings. The Trident-class assault ship manufactured by the Colicoid Creation Nest were armed with a drill spike and the weapons were used in the Battle of Kamino during the Clone Wars.
