# Rallying Arms

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rallying Arms](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rallying_Arms)

The Rallying Arms was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. These black, red, and gold pistols featured small tassels dangling from the bottom of the barrel.
