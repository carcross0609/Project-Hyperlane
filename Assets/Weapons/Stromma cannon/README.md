# Stromma cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Stromma cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stromma_cannon)

A type of cannon powerful enough to destroy a TIE/LN starfighter with a single shot was given to the Quesoth species by the Stromma species years before 8 ABY. The weapons were used as anti-air defenses, with their massive forward shield plates protecting them from destruction along their fire-lines. The cannons only had manual targeting.
