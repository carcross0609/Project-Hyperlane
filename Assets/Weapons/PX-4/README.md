# PX-4

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PX-4](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PX-4)

The PX-4 was a type of proton torpedo first produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. during the Galactic War between the Republic and the Sith Empire. The PX-4 was capable of locking onto targets at an extreme range and dealing massive damage to enemy starships, as it ripped through both shields and armor. The torpedo's drawbacks included a narrow lock-on targeting cone, a lengthy lock-on period, and a limited ammunition capacity for its launcher.

## Specifications

- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Proton torpedo
