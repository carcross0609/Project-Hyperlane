# Explosive micro-rocket

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Explosive micro-rocket](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_micro-rocket)

Explosive micro-rockets were a class of micro-rocket that were designed to destroy groups of infantry. The rockets typically sold for 150 credits and were the most popular class of micro-rocket on the market. Explosive micro-rockets used a chepatite impact explosive to create a blast of energy. The rockets were not powerful enough to break through full body armor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Explosive micro-rocket
- **Type:** Micro-rocket
- **Cost:** 150 credits
