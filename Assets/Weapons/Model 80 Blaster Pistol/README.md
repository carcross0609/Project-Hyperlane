# Model 80 Blaster Pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Model 80 Blaster Pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_80_Blaster_Pistol)

The Model 80 Blaster Pistol was a type of blaster pistol manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc., and was an old standby for planetary defense forces, militias, and mercenary units. It traded on its stopping power, rugged durability, and customizability, and was among the heaviest and bulkiest blaster pistols in its class.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model 80 Blaster Pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 550 credits
- **Range:** Medium-ranged
