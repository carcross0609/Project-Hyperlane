# Glimmerghost Prototype

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Glimmerghost Prototype](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glimmerghost_Prototype)

The Glimmerghost Prototype was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle consisted of a black, silver, and brown frame, with white and red painted elements.
