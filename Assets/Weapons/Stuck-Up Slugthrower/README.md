# Stuck-Up Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stuck-Up Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stuck-Up_Slugthrower)

The Stuck-Up Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This slugthrower resembled a large DL-44 heavy blaster pistol like the one used by Han Solo.
