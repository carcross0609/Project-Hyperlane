# Obratuk Glii's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Obratuk Glii's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obratuk_Glii%27s_lightsabers)

A set of at least six single-bladed lightsabers were owned and wielded by the Parwan Jedi Master Obratuk Glii during the High Republic Era. Due to his Parwan physiology, Glii possessed numerous prehensile tentacles that allowed him to wield several lightsabers simultaneously. Each of his weapons were constructed by Glii in memory of his former Padawans who had died over his millennium of life.
