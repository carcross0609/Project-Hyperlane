# WP-19 incendiary grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [WP-19 incendiary grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WP-19_incendiary_grenade)

The WP-19 incendiary grenade manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. was a type of incendiary grenade that, upon detonation, released a flaming adhesive gel that clung to surfaces and merely spread if wiped or hosed. The gel burned at very high temperatures, and the white-hot flames spread quickly across structures and forested areas. However, in light of alleged atrocities, most Imperial units were equipped with the far less dangerous D-24 Inferno Grenade—another Merr-Sonn-manufactured grenade—in situations that once called for the WP-19. The cost of the WP-19 was 125 credits.
