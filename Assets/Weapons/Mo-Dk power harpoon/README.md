# Mo/Dk power harpoon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mo/Dk power harpoon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mo/Dk_power_harpoon)

The Mo/Dk power harpoon was a weapon mounted at the rear of the T-47 airspeeders modified by the Rebel Alliance for use at Echo Base on Hoth. The harpoon was used with great effect during the Battle of Hoth to trip the legs of the Galactic Empire's AT-ATs, exposing the vulnerable neck joint to laser fire from the T-47 speeders.
