# Blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blaster_rifle)

Blaster rifles were weapons more powerful and larger than other types of blasters, such as blaster pistols. It was used in various factions. Stormtroopers of the Imperial Army were armed with E-11 medium blaster rifles. The Alliance to Restore the Republic used many different types of blasters, as they could not afford to mass produce weapons. However, the A280 blaster rifle was the standard-issue Alliance rifle.
