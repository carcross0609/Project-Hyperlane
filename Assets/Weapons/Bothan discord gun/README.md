# Bothan discord gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bothan discord gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothan_discord_gun)

The Bothan discord gun was a last resort weapon that was used by Bothan dignitaries when off world. These weapons fired sonic blasts at their targets and were used 4,000 years before the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bothan discord gun
- **Type:** Sonic rifle
- **Culture:** Bothan
