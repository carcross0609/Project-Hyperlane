# Destructive Electromagnetic Pulse 2 ion carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Destructive Electromagnetic Pulse 2 ion carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Destructive_Electromagnetic_Pulse_2_ion_carbine)

The Destructive Electromagnetic Pulse 2 ion carbine, or DEMP 2, fired high power ion bursts capable of disabling electrical systems and stunning organic targets. It had a range of three to 120 meters.

- **Name:** Destructive Electromagnetic Pulse 2 ion carbine

- **Model:** Destructive Electromagnetic Pulse 2

- **Range:** 120 meters
