# BE-29 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BE-29 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BE-29_blaster_rifle)

The BE-29 blaster rifle was a blaster rifle with a black barrel, a black scope, a black trigger, and a brown, petrified rochiawood shoulder stock. The moisture farmer Beru Whitesun Lars owned a sawed off BE-29 blaster rifle that acted as a shotgun blaster that she kept hidden behind a false pipe in the homestead she lived in with her husband Owen Lars and their adopted son, Luke Skywalker.
