# Freedom One

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Freedom One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedom_One)

The Freedom One was a popular blaster manufactured by the SoroSuub Corporation. It was a technological clone of BlasTech Industries's E-11 medium blaster rifle, the standard-issue blaster rifle of the Galactic Empire's Stormtrooper Corps. The Freedom One was the most successful blaster out of many attempts by various different corporations to copy the success of the E-11. It was formerly known as the Stormtrooper One, but the corporation renamed it to the Freedom One after changing their allegiance to the Rebel Alliance.
