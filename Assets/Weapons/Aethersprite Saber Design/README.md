# Aethersprite Saber Design

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aethersprite Saber Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aethersprite_Saber_Design)

The Aethersprite Saber Design was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber. It was designed in a way that was meant to invoke the aesthetics of the Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptors used by members of the Jedi Order shortly before its collapse.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aethersprite Saber Design
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
