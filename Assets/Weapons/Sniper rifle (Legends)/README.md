# Sniper rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sniper rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sniper_rifle/Legends)

Sniper rifles were long-range weapons, either projectile or, more commonly, blaster-based (these were also called sniper blaster rifles). The purpose of a sniper rifle was to kill a target at extreme distances. A sniper blaster rifle fired a long beam of energy, capable of killing with a single headshot.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sniper rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 8 kg
- **Capacity:** 10 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 90 m
  - Maximum: up to 450 m
- **Purpose:** Sniping
