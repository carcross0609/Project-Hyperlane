# Lah Kara's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Lah Kara's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lah_Kara%27s_lightsaber)

A lightsaber was created by the sabersmith Lah Zhima and was used by his daughter, Lah Kara. It was part of a batch of lightsabers ordered by Margrave Juro, who sought to rebuild the Jedi Order. The lightsaber's length was adjustable and its kyber crystal was tempered so that its color would reflect the user's connection to the Force. After Zhima was kidnapped by Jedi Hunters, Kara took the lightsabers to deliver them to the Hy Izlan Aerial Temple. She used the lightsaber to defend herself from a pursuing Jedi Hunter and to fight Sith acolytes in the temple. As she fought, the blade's color changed from clear to green.
