# Pantoran Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pantoran Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pantoran_Blaster)

The Pantoran Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This ornate blaster pistol featured a bronze base and plating, as well as elements painted a dark blue, with worn cream and light blue patterns displayed overtop, as well as repeated symbols inscribed into the barrel, all resembling an outfit once worn by the Pantoran Senator Riyo Chuchi when she visited the planet Orto Plutonia.
