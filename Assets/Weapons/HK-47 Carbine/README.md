# HK-47 Carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HK-47 Carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HK-47_Carbine)

The HK-47 Carbine, also known as HK-47 blaster, was a custom-crafted model of blaster carbine was developed during the final years of the Galactic Republic. It was unusual in that it was equipped with a bio-link module that allowed the user to interface directly with the weapon during combat. One such weapon was owned by the droid HK-47 during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Owners:** HK-47
