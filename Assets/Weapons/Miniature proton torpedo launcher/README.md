# Miniature proton torpedo launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Miniature proton torpedo launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Miniature_proton_torpedo_launcher)

The miniature proton torpedo launcher was a portable missile launcher that fired miniaturized proton torpedoes. It was originally designed as an integrated weapon for the Zero-G assault stormtroopers of the Galactic Empire. The launcher was fitted into the Zero-G stormtrooper's left gauntlet which enabled them to rip through the hull of a starship in boarding missions. However, Rebel engineers adapted the weapon into a portable shoulder-mounted launcher.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Miniature proton torpedo launcher
- **Type:** Portable missile launcher
- **Creators:** Rebel engineers
- **Cost:** 1,500 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Large
- **Weight:** 8 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 4 miniature proton torpedoes
  - 3-meter blast radius
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 75 meters
  - Maximum: up to 750 meters
- **Purpose:**
  - Anti-personnel weapon
  - Anti-vehicle weapon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Zero-G assault stormtrooper
