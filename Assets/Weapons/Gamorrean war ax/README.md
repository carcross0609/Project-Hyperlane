# Gamorrean war ax

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gamorrean war ax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamorrean_war_ax)

The Gamorrean war ax, also called the Gamorrean battle-ax, was a type of ax used by Gamorreans. It was similar to the Gamorrean Arg'garok vibro-ax. The guards, who were under the employment of Jabba the Hutt, used that type of ax.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gamorrean war ax
- **Type:** Ax
- **Purpose:** Melee combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Hutt Clan
  -   Jabba's criminal empire
  - Fett gotra
