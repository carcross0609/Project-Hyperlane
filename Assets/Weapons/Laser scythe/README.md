# Laser scythe

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Laser scythe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Laser_scythe)

The laser scythe was an energy weapon that equipped the scythe harvester. It resembled a long lightsaber mounted on a mechanized arm. The laser scythe was used to clear a path for the harvester and could also cut down enemy troops.
