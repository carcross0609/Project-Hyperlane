# Sentry gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Sentry gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentry_gun)

Sentry guns were small automatic weapons. They were used aboard an abandoned ship. The Jedi Lah Kara triggered the ship's sentry guns and deflected their blaster shots with her lightsaber. The Jedi Hunters Unda and Anda later used kunai to destroy the ship's sentry guns.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sentry gun
- **Type:** Blaster
- **Locations:** An abandoned ship
