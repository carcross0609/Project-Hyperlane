# Loden Greatstorm's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Loden Greatstorm's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loden_Greatstorm%27s_lightsaber)

Loden Greatstorm's lightsaber was a yellow-bladed lightsaber owned by the Jedi Master Loden Greatstorm. The Twi'lek Jedi used the lightsaber during the Great Hyperspace Disaster while fighting pirates at the Ranoraki compound. Greatstorm later wielded his blade while rescuing the Blythe family from the Nihil on Elphrona. However, he was captured during the raid, and Tempest Runner Lourna Dee seized Greatstorm's lightsaber and turned it over to Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, who wore it on his belt as a trophy.
