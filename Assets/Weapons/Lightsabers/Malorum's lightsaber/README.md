# Malorum's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Malorum's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malorum%27s_lightsaber)

The Inquisitor Malorum owned and wielded a red lightsaber. He used this lightsaber to duel Ferus Olin in the Theed power generator room. Eventually, Ferus Olin knocked the lightsaber from Malorum's hands with his own lightsaber given to him from Garen Muln. Malorum leapt into the air to grab the lightsaber and summon it to him with the Force, but Malorum was blinded by the need of the moment, and fell to the core. The lightsaber was most likely destroyed when the Theed Hangar was blown up, as the explosion took the hangar, Imperial Headquarters and Malorum with it.
