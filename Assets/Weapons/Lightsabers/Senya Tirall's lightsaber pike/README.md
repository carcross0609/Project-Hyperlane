# Senya Tirall's lightsaber pike

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Senya Tirall's lightsaber pike](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senya_Tirall%27s_lightsaber_pike)

Senya Tirall's lightsaber pike was a blue-bladed lightsaber pike wielded by Senya Tirall. Senya used this traditional Knight of Zakuul weapon when she investigated the site of the Ord Mantell massacre and was ambushed by her daughter Vaylin.
