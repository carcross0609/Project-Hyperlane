# Dagan Gera's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dagan Gera's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dagan_Gera%27s_lightsaber)

Dagan Gera's lightsaber was a yellow-bladed split lightsaber wielded by the Jedi Knight Dagan Gera during the High Republic Era. After being released from stasis during the Imperial Era in 9 BBY, Gera bled the kyber crystal and the blade turned red.
