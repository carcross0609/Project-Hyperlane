# Lola's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Lola's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lola%27s_lightsaber)

The former Sith apprentice Lola wielded a unique double-bladed lightsaber. Lola's personal weapon featured curved blades that were each a different color; one red, the other yellow. At some point Lola used her lightsaber to duel her former Sith Master Kutu.
