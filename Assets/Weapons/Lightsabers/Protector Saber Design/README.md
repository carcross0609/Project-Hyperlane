# Protector Saber Design

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Protector Saber Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Protector_Saber_Design)

The Protector Saber Design was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber. It was covered in bone, feathers, cloth, and beads reminiscent of those found on the Forest Moon of Endor with the Ewok species.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Protector Saber Design
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
