# Tan Yuster's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tan Yuster's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tan_Yuster%27s_lightsaber)

Tan Yuster's lightsaber was the personal weapon of Tan Yuster. Yuster was the Padawan learner of Agen Kolar and a part of the Jedi assault team in the Petranaki Arena. During the battle he was overwhelmed by B2-series super battle droids and killed. Kolar salvaged his fallen apprentice's lightsaber and kept its kyber crystal, which he added to his own weapon in tribute to Yuster. Kolar used his lightsaber with Yuster's kyber crystal when he and Saesee Tiin were killed by Darth Sidious alongside Kit Fisto and Mace Windu in an attempt to arrest him.
