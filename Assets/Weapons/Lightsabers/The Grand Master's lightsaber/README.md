# The Grand Master's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [The Grand Master's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Grand_Master%27s_lightsaber)

A blue-bladed lightsaber was wielded by the Grand Master, leader of the Crusaders. He used it to duel the Ronin and Aneé-san on the planet Olcatz.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Grand Master's lightsaber
- **Type:** Lightsaber
- **Owners:** The Grand Master
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Crusaders
