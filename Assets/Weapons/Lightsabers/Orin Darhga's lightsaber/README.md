# Orin Darhga's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Orin Darhga's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orin_Darhga%27s_lightsaber)

Orin Darhga's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber used by Orin Darhga during the High Republic Era. After Darhga's death on Dalna, Enya Keen took the lightsaber. Its kyber crystal was later added to the Kyber Arch.

- **Name:** Orin Darhga's lightsaber

- **Owners:**
