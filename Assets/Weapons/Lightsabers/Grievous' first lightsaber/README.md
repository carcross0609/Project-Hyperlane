# Grievous' first lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grievous' first lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grievous%27_first_lightsaber)

A lightsaber was constructed and used by an old Jedi of the Jedi Order. After the Jedi's death, their lightsaber fell into the possession of Count Dooku and was given to the Kaleesh cyborg General Grievous as his first lightsaber, and then the general added to his collection from his time as a warlord on Kalee.
