# Taron Malicos's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Taron Malicos's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taron_Malicos%27s_lightsabers)

Taron Malicos's lightsabers were two red single-bladed lightsabers used by fallen Jedi and leader of the Nightbrothers of Dathomir, Taron Malicos. The hilt of the sabers used the Magus model. Malicos made use of them in his duel against Cal Kestis on Dathomir.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Taron Malicos's lightsabers
- **Model:** Single-bladed lightsabers
- **Owners:** Taron Malicos
- **Hilt Model:** Magus
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Red
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**

  - Nightbrothers
