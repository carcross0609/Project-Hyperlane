# Nubs's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nubs's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nubs%27s_lightsaber)

Nubs's lightsaber was a blue-bladed training lightsaber utilized by the Poobian Jedi youngling Nubs during the High Republic Era. It had a design that represented Nubs' love of nature.
