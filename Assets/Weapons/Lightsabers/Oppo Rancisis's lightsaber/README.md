# Oppo Rancisis's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oppo Rancisis's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oppo_Rancisis%27s_lightsaber)

Oppo Rancisis's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber that belonged to the Thisspiasian Jedi Master Oppo Rancisis. Though Rancisis was adept with his lightsaber, he preferred to use his highly developed Force powers in combat. In 229 BBY, Rancisis ignited his lightsaber along with the rest of the Jedi High Council and assembled Jedi Order inside the Galactic Senate Chamber on Coruscant, after the Council finished announcing the implementation of the Guardian Protocols in response to the Destruction of Starlight Beacon by the Nihil.
