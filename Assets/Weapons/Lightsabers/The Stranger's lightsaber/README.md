# The Stranger's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [The Stranger's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Stranger%27s_lightsaber)

The Stranger's lightsaber was a red-bladed lightsaber used by the Sith known as "the Stranger" during the High Republic Era. It had the ability to be split into two lightsabers, one of which was a shoto.

## Specifications

- **Name:** The Stranger's lightsaber
- **Model:** Lightsaber
- **Type:**

  -   Single-bladed lightsaber
  -   Shoto lightsaber (when separated)
- **Culture:** Sith
- **Created:** During or prior to 132 BBY
- **Destroyed:** 132 BBY, Brendok
- **Owners:** "The Stranger"
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Red
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:** Sith
