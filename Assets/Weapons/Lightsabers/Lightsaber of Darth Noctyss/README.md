# Lightsaber of Darth Noctyss

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lightsaber of Darth Noctyss](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_of_Darth_Noctyss)

The lightsaber of Darth Noctyss was a ancient red-bladed lightsaber owned by the Sith Lord Darth Noctyss. At some point, the lightsaber came into the possession of the Sith Lord Viceroy Exim Panshard who wielded it for a time. Eventually the saber was stored in a bronze box. After the fall of the Galactic Empire, Jedi Master Luke Skywalker and Church of the Force member Lor San Tekka attempted to find Noctyss' lightsaber, as well as other relics that belonged to her, but were unsuccessful. In 21 ABY, the box that contained the weapon was aboard a core ship on Kiza's world, and after he destroyed her other lightsaber, the spirit of Panshard instructed the Pantoran female Kiza to take Noctyss' lightsaber and wield it as he had done.
