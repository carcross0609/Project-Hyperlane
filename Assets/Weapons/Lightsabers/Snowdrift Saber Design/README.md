# Snowdrift Saber Design

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Snowdrift Saber Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snowdrift_Saber_Design)

The Snowdrift Saber Design was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber. Its design was intended to resemble the aesthetic of the snowspeeders utilized by the Rebel Alliance during the Battle of Hoth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Snowdrift Saber Design
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
