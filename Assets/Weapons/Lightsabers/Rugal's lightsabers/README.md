# Rugal's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Rugal's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rugal%27s_lightsabers)

Rugal's lightsabers were the personal lightsabers of the human Jedi Rugal, which was a cane lightsaber—a specialized device that was also a walking stick—due to her advanced age. Designed to be as much of a weapon as a reinforcement for her gnarled frame, Rugal only exposed the duplicity of her stick when armed combat was the necessary course of action. When needed, she would activate her lightsaber by separating the handle and collar from the shaft to produce two pale blue beams of plasma. Rugal used her saber during the Imperial Era, when she wielded it to great effect against a Inquisitor.
