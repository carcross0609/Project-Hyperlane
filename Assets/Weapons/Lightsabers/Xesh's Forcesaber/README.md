# Xesh's Forcesaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xesh's Forcesaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xesh%27s_Forcesaber)

Xesh's Forcesaber was the personal Forcesaber of the Force Hound, Xesh. It was purple bladed and Xesh used it while in service to his Predor master Tul'kar. This weapon was present when Xesh traveled to Tython and he used it to break the Madog steel blades of Sek'nos Rath and Hawk Ryo in his duel between the three Je'daii Journeyers that encountered him. This Forcesaber was later confiscated by the Je'daii Order, and Je'daii Ranger Hawk Ryo was given the blade to use as his own.
