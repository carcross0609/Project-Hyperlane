# Tenth Brother's shoto lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tenth Brother's shoto lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tenth_Brother%27s_shoto_lightsabers)

The Tenth Brother's shoto lightsabers were two shoto lightsabers used by the Tenth Brother, an Inquisitor in the Galactic Empire's Jedi-hunting organization, the Inquisitorius. Previously using a green-bladed lightsaber during his time as a Jedi in 22 BBY, he wielded the shoto lightsabers as an Inquisitor in 18 BBY during a mission to the planet Mon Cala, where he met his end. The lightsabers featured a handguard and produced red-colored blades from their kyber crystals.

- **Name:** Tenth Brother's shoto lightsabers
- **Type:** Two shoto lightsabers
- **Owners:** Tenth Brother
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
