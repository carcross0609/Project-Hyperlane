# Omen Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Omen Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omen_Saber_Pattern)

The Omen Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. It featured an ornate design, with a black, white, and red color scheme.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Omen Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
