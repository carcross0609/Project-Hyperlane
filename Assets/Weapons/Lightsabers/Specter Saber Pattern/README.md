# Specter Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Specter Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Specter_Saber_Pattern)

The Specter Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Specter Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
