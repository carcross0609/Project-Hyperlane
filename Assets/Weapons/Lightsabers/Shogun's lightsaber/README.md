# Shogun's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Shogun's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shogun%27s_lightsaber)

A purple-bladed lightsaber was used by a blind Jedi of the Jedi clans known as Shogun. The lightsaber was a sabercane that they utilized both in combat and as a cane. Shogun used the cane while walking on the snowy mountain Mount Hakamori on an astronomical object, where they met the former Sith known as the Ronin in a hot spring. Before the two parted ways the next morning, the Ronin had a vision of Shogun using their lightsaber to defeat him and destroy his own lightsaber. However, when the vision ended, Shogun informed the Ronin they did not intend to kill him.
