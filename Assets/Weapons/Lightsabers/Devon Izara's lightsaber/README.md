# Devon Izara's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Devon Izara's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devon_Izara%27s_lightsaber)

Devon Izara's lightsaber was the personal blue-bladed lightsaber of the Twi'lek Jedi Padawan Devon Izara. The saber's hilt was made of black and silver colored metal, and covered by a leather grip.
