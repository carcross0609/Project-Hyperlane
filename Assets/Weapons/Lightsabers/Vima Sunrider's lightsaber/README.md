# Vima Sunrider's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vima Sunrider's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vima_Sunrider%27s_lightsaber)

Vima Sunrider's lightsaber was a lightsaber created in 3986 BBY by the Jedi apprentice Vima Sunrider during her tutelage under Jedi Master Ulic Qel-Droma. Sunrider focused her lightsaber with Adegan crystals that once belonged to her late father, Andur Sunrider. Upon its completion, the weapon produced a brilliant green blade. She continued to use her newly-created lightsaber for the duration of her apprenticeship under Qel-Droma.
