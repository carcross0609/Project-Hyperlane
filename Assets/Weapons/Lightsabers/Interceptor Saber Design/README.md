# Interceptor Saber Design

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Interceptor Saber Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Interceptor_Saber_Design)

The Interceptor Saber Design was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber. It resembled the design of both the Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor and Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptors used by Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker shortly before the collapse of the Jedi Order.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Interceptor Saber Design
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
