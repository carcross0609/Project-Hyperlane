# Dark Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dark Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Saber_Pattern)

The Dark Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. It served as a replica of one of Darth Maul's lightsabers when not connected to form a double-blade.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dark Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
