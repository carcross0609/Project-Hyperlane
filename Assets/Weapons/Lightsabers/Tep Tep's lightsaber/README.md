# Tep Tep's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tep Tep's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tep_Tep%27s_lightsaber)

Tep Tep's lightsaber was a lightsaber owned by the Alcedian trans female Jedi Initiate Tep Tep. She used it during her time on the planet Valo in 229 BBY.
