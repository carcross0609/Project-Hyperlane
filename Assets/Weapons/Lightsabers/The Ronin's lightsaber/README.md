# The Ronin's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [The Ronin's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ronin%27s_lightsaber)

The Ronin's lightsaber was a lightsaber that belonged to the former Sith known as the Ronin. During a skirmish at a village on the Outer Rim planet of Genbara, the Ronin used this lightsaber to fight and kill the Sith Kouru. The lightsaber was broken, and could not be retracted, so the Ronin used a specially shielded scabbard.
