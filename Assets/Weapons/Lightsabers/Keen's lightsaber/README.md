# Keen's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Keen's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Keen%27s_lightsaber)

Keen's lightsaber was a yellow-bladed lightsaber utilized by the Jedi Enya Keen during the High Republic Era. Keen's lightsaber a metal knuckle guard sculpted into the hilt, a feature rarely seen in the sabers of other Jedi. A flared fitting was set just below the blade emitter to protect Keen from injury during lightsaber practice.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Keen's lightsaber
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Owners:** Enya Keen
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
