# Pirate Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pirate Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pirate_Saber_Pattern)

The Pirate Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. The hilt featured a decorative red and gold wrap around the handle portion.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pirate Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
