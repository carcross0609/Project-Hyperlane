# Aneé-san's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Aneé-san's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ane%C3%A9-san%27s_lightsaber)

The Twi'lek Aneé-san possessed a red-colored lightsaber decades after the Sith rebellion. When she dueled the Sith-hunting former Sith known as the Ronin on the planet Olcatz, she surmised the man was after her lightsaber.
