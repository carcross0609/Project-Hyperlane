# The Traveler's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [The Traveler's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Traveler%27s_lightsaber)

The Traveler's lightsaber was the Jedi lightsaber and scion's blade wielded by the Traveler. They did not construct it themselves, instead inheriting it as the bloodline heir of a Jedi clan. The lightsaber hilt had a dark leather strip wound around it and metal shards of ornamentation, with wooden pegs securing the sides, and a durasteel core.
