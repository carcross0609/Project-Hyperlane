# Jaro Tapal's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jaro Tapal's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaro_Tapal%27s_lightsaber)

This lightsaber was first used by Jedi Master Jaro Tapal. Just before Tapal's death, he passed the weapon to his padawan, Cal Kestis, who used the weapon until its crystal broke while Kestis was on Dathomir.
