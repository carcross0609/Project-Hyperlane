# Maverick Saber Design

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maverick Saber Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maverick_Saber_Design)

The Maverick Saber Design was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber. It was designed to resemble the lightsaber that belonged to Jedi Master Quinlan Vos.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Maverick Saber Design
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
