# Kai's first lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kai's first lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kai%27s_first_lightsaber)

Kai's first lightsaber, also known as Kai's lightsaber was a training lightsaber that was wielded by Jedi Initiate Kai Brightstar during the High Republic Era. The saber was a generic brass cylinder design issued for younglings.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kai's first lightsaber
- **Model:** Lightsaber
- **Type:**
  - Single-bladed lightsaber
  - Training lightsaber
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Owners:**
  - Kai Brightstar
  - Taborr Val Dorn
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Blue
- **Succeed:** Brightstar's second lightsaber (temporarily due to Kai recovering the first and deciding to keep both before returning the second to Yoda)
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
