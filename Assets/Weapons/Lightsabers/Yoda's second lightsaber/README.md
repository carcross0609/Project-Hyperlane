# Yoda's second lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yoda's second lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda%27s_second_lightsaber)

Yoda kept a second lightsaber, which he previously built as a backup blade, on Dagobah inside his hut in 3 ABY after his previous one was lost during a duel in the Galactic Senate against Darth Sidious and then incinerated during a celebration marking the eradication of the Jedi Order in 19 BBY.
