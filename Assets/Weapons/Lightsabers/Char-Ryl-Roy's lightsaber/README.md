# Char-Ryl-Roy's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Char-Ryl-Roy's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Char-Ryl-Roy%27s_lightsaber)

Char-Ryl-Roy's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber wielded by Jedi Master Char-Ryl-Roy during the High Republic Era. After Roy's death during the Night of Sorrow, his lightsaber was kept by his Padawan Enya Keen after she spoke with Jedi Master Yoda and he agreed with the idea.

- **Name:** Char-Ryl-Roy's lightsaber
- **Type:** Single bladed lightsaber

- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
