# Darth Shaa's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darth Shaa's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Shaa%27s_lightsabers)

A pair of red-bladed Sith lightsabers were used as personal weapons by Darth Shaa during her career as a Sith. At least a millennium before the rise of the Galactic Empire, Lady Shaa used them to free the Sculptor Momin from his prison, killing the guards and melting the lock on the cell door. She later used them against Momin during his lightsaber combat training, until her death at the hands of her apprentice during one such training.
