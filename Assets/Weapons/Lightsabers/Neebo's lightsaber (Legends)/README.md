# Neebo's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Neebo's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neebo%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

This lightsaber was used by the Jedi Master Neebo. After Neebo went missing on a mission to the moons of Sanjin, the lightsaber was found in the collection of the General Grievous.
