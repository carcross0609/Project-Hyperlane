# Adi Gallia's first lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Adi Gallia's first lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adi_Gallia%27s_first_lightsaber/Legends)

A lightsaber was built by 32 BBY and used by Jedi Master Adi Gallia, who served in the Jedi Order and the Galactic Republic. The lightsaber was destroyed during a mission sometime before the Invasion of Naboo. Gallia had constructed a new lightsaber by the invasion's end in 32 BBY.
