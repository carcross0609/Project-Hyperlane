# Petro's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Petro's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Petro%27s_lightsaber)

Petro's lightsaber was the personal lightsaber of the human Jedi youngling Petro. The weapon was constructed out of various materials and emitted a blue blade. Petro built the weapon on the Paladin-class corvette the Crucible in the year 20 BBY.
