# Fifth Brother's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fifth Brother's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Brother%27s_lightsaber)

The Fifth Brother's lightsaber was a double-bladed spinning lightsaber wielded by the Jedi hunter known as Fifth Brother, similar to other double-bladed spinning lightsabers wielded by Imperial Inquisitors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fifth Brother's lightsaber
- **Model:**
  - Double-bladed spinning lightsaber
  - Split saber
- **Type:**
  - Double-bladed spinning lightsaber (disc mode)
  - Single-bladed lightsaber (crescent mode)
- **Creator:** Unknown
- **Owners:** Fifth Brother
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical
- **Hilt Material:** Metallic
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Red
- **Blade Length:** 1 meter (fully extended, crescent mode)
- **Mods:** Extendable hand guard, can spin around central handle with detachable emitters locked to hand guard (disc mode)
- **Protection:** Large circular hand guard
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Inquisitorius
