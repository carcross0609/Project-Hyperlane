# Master Lodaka's Lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Master Lodaka's Lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Master_Lodaka%27s_Lightsaber)

Master Lodaka's Lightsaber was one of the finest lightsabers ever created by Lodaka, a Rodian Jedi Master who could manipulate crystals on a molecular level. Its blade was resistant to the shut-down effect of cortosis, which partially addressed a problem that had vexed lightsaber duelists for millennia. The lightsaber was laid to rest with Master Lodaka following her death, within a chamber of the Lothal Jedi Temple.
