# Satele Shan's green-bladed lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Satele Shan's green-bladed lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan%27s_green-bladed_lightsaber)

During the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire, the Jedi Knight Satele Shan utilized a green-bladed lightsaber to replace the double-bladed lightsabers that she used primarily through the Great Galactic War with the Empire. The weapon was used by Shan during the Cold War, but she eventually returned to using a double-bladed saber by the time of the Galactic War with the Empire.
