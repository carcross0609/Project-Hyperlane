# Asajj Ventress' Padawan lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Asajj Ventress' Padawan lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress%27_Padawan_lightsaber)

Asajj Ventress wielded a green-bladed lightsaber during her time as the Padawan of Jedi Knight Ky Narec. When Narec was killed by Weequay pirates, Ventress used both her and her fallen master's lightsabers to kill the raiders in revenge.
