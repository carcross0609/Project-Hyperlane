# Tahl's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tahl's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tahl%27s_lightsaber)

A blue-bladed lightsaber was constructed and wielded by the Noorian Jedi Master Tahl. The lightsaber was wielded on many missions by the Jedi, most significantly on Telos IV, Centax-2, and on New Apsolon.
