# Darth Malak's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Malak's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Malak%27s_lightsaber)

Darth Malak's lightsaber was the personal weapon of the Sith Lord Darth Malak. Malak constructed his Sith lightsaber some time after meeting the Sith Emperor. During the Jedi Civil War, he would use it against the Jedi he once fought alongside. Eventually, he would duel both his former Master Revan and Bastila Shan aboard the Leviathan. He would once again use the weapon for the final time on the Star Forge during the Battle of Rakata Prime where he was slain by Revan.
