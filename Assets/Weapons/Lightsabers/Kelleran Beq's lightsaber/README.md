# Kelleran Beq's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kelleran Beq's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kelleran_Beq%27s_lightsaber)

Kelleran Beq's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber wielded by the Jedi Master Kelleran Beq, who was nicknamed "The Sabered Hand" due to his skill with the Jedi weapon. Beq wielded this lightsaber during the implementation of Order 66 on Coruscant, Operation: Knightfall, while attempting to defend the Jedi Initiate Grogu from the attacks of the clone troopers of the 501st Legion.
