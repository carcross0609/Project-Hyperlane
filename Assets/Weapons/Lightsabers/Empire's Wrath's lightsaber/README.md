# Empire's Wrath's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Empire's Wrath's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empire%27s_Wrath%27s_lightsaber)

The Empire's Wrath's lightsaber was a red-bladed lightsaber wielded by the Sith Lord known as the Empire's Wrath. They acquired it from Naga Sadow's tomb on Korriban as part of their trials under Darth Baras.
