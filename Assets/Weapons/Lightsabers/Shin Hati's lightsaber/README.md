# Shin Hati's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shin Hati's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shin_Hati%27s_lightsaber)

Shin Hati's lightsaber was a lightsaber wielded by Shin Hati. It had an orange-red blade. Hati employed her lightsaber to attack the crew of a New Republic ship Vesper when she and her master Baylan Skoll freed Morgan Elsbeth during the search for Ezra Bridger. Soon after, she dueled Sabine Wren at LothalNet comm tower E-272, and ultimately won the fight by stabbing Wren through her torso with the saber.
