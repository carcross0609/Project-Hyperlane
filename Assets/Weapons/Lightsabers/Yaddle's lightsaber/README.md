# Yaddle's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yaddle's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yaddle%27s_lightsaber)

Jedi Master Yaddle wielded a green plasma-bladed shoto lightsaber during her service to the Jedi Order. Despite her skill in lightsaber combat, Yaddle lost a duel with the fallen Jedi Count Dooku, who killed his former colleague in the aftermath of the Invasion of Naboo.
