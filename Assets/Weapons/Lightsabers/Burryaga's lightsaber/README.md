# Burryaga's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Burryaga's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Burryaga%27s_lightsaber)

Burryaga's lightsaber of Wookiee Jedi Padawan Burryaga Agaburry was a single-bladed crossguard lightsaber with a blue blade powered by a kyber crystal. The lightsaber's hilt was plated in meryx, the amber of the white wroshyr tree which had been extinct for millennia, wrapped in the leather of a tach, and was finished off with an pure electrum crosspiece. It was specially crafted to be used by a tall Wookiee, and as such was one of the longest lightsabers used by the Jedi Order.
