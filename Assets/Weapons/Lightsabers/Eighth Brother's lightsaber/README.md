# Eighth Brother's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eighth Brother's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eighth_Brother%27s_lightsaber)

The Eighth Brother's lightsaber was a double-bladed spinning lightsaber wielded by the Jedi hunter known as the Eighth Brother, similar to other double-bladed spinning lightsabers wielded by Imperial Inquisitors, though it uniquely included a buzzsaw-like feature. It was destroyed by Kanan Jarrus on Malachor.
