# Nari's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nari's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nari%27s_lightsaber)

Nari's lightsaber was owned by the Jedi Nari during the Great Jedi Purge. In 9 BBY, while being hunted by the Inquisitors, Nari pleaded the former Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi for his help and presented the lightsaber to him. Kenobi urged Nari to abandon the lightsaber and to live a normal life, much to Nari's dismay.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nari's lightsaber
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Owners:** Nari
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical, straight
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
