# Ty Yorrick's purple-bladed lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ty Yorrick's purple-bladed lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ty_Yorrick%27s_purple-bladed_lightsaber)

Ty Yorrick's purple-bladed lightsaber, also known as Ty's lightsaber, or as Yorrick's lightsaber, was a purple-bladed lightsaber owned by former Jedi Padawan-turned-mercenary Ty Yorrick during the High Republic Era. Built according to instructions given to her by her former Jedi Master Cibaba, Yorrick wielded the weapon in service to the Jedi Order until she departed after an incident with fellow Padawan Klias Teradine at the Yallow Shrine. Yorrick kept the lightsaber as she became a mercenary, adding four long spikes around the top of the hilt which she knew Cibaba would have disapproved of.
