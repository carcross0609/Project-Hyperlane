# Alema Rar's first lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Alema Rar's first lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alema_Rar%27s_first_lightsaber)

Alema Rar's first lightsaber was constructed by Jedi Padawan Alema Rar, alongside her sister, Numa Rar. It used by Alema during the Yuuzhan Vong War until it was destroyed in 27 ABY aboard the Nebula Chaser when it was thrown into a disintegration chute alongside Numa's lightsaber. The two Twi'lek sisters recovered their lightsabers' silver Adegan crystals and used then to power their second lightsabers.
