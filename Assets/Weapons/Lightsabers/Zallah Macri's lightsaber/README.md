# Zallah Macri's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zallah Macri's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zallah_Macri%27s_lightsaber)

Zallah Macri's lightsaber was a blue-bladed lightsaber owned by Jedi Zallah Macri during the High Republic Era. After Macri's death in the Path of the Open Hand compound, the Path's Mother kept the lightsaber, using it to kill a rampaging wargaran on Jedha and later, the Herald of the Path.
