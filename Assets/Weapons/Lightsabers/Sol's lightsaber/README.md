# Sol's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sol's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sol%27s_lightsaber)

Sol's lightsaber was the personal single-bladed royal blue lightsaber of Jedi Master Sol. In 132 BBY, the weapon was damaged after Sol was disarmed by former Sith assassin Mae-ho Aniseya, after a duel on Brendok.
