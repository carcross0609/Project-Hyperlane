# Lord Corvax's Lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lord Corvax's Lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_Corvax%27s_Lightsaber)

Lord Corvax's Lightsaber, often referred to as a Lightsword, was an ancient lightsaber that was wielded by Lord Dorwin Corvax, the husband of Lady Corvax.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lord Corvax's Lightsaber
- **Model:** Lightsaber
- **Owners:**
  - Lord Corvax
  - Descendant of Lady Corvax
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Blue
- **Design:** Ancient; like a regular sword. Has an exposed crystal
- **Markings:** Multiple gold accents
- **Purpose:** Symbol of office for Corvax's family
