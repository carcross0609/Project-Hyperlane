# Yaddle's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yaddle's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yaddle%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

After escaping her prison on Koba, Jedi Padawan Yaddle returned to Coruscant after many years of captivity and was promoted immediately to the rank of Jedi Master on the Jedi High Council. Master Yaddle then constructed an orange-bladed lightsaber which she wielded until her death on Mawan in 26 BBY.
