# Even Piell's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Even Piell's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Even_Piell%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

When he was a Jedi Padawan, Lannik Jedi Master Even Piell constructed a small green bladed lightsaber, to match his small size. As a Jedi Guardian, Piell would frequently use the lightsaber in battle.
