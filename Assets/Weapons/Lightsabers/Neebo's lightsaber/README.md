# Neebo's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neebo's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neebo%27s_lightsaber)

A lightsaber was used by Neebo, a female Jedi Master in the Jedi Order. After Neebo went missing on a mission to the moons of Sanjin, the lightsaber was found in the collection of General Grievous.
