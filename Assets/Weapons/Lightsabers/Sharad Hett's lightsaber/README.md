# Sharad Hett's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sharad Hett's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sharad_Hett%27s_lightsaber)

Jedi Knight Sharad Hett used this lightsaber during his career as a Jedi Knight, and it remained at his side in his self-imposed exile on Tatooine. A'Yark recalled the blade being green, but the blade was red during Sharad's time as a warlord.
