# Asajj Ventress's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Asajj Ventress's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress%27s_lightsaber)

Asajj Ventress's lightsaber, or simply Ventress's lightsaber, was a yellow-bladed lightsaber acquired by the bounty hunter Asajj Ventress on the black market some time after fallen Jedi Barriss Offee stole the lightsabers that Ventress had used as the apprentice of Count Dooku. She viewed getting a new lightsaber as a way to distance herself from what had been her last physical link to her time as his apprentice. In 19 BBY, during which the lightsaber had a straight cylindrical-shaped hilt, Ventress used the lightsaber for her bounty hunting career until it was laid to rest with her on her homeworld of Dathomir after her apparent death in the second battle of Christophsis. After her revival, Ventress continued to wield it as a bounty hunter during the Imperial Era, during which it had a curved hilt.
