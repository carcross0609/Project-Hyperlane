# Kiza's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kiza's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kiza%27s_lightsaber)

A red-bladed lightsaber was used by the Pantoran female Kiza. The lightsaber was originally kept within the basement of the Peace and Security Headquarters in Coronet City on Corellia. In 5 ABY, the weapon was recovered by the Acolytes of the Beyond member Remi during a revolution in the city staged by the Acolytes. Remi kept the lightsaber, though prior to the Acolytes attack on a New Republic outpost on Devaron, it was taken from him by Yupe Tashu, the leader of the Acolytes and given to Kiza. During the attack, Remi attempted to take back the lightsaber, but Kiza instead ignited it through Remi's head and killed him. Kiza continued to use the lightsaber until 21 ABY when it was destroyed on a core ship on the asteroid known as Kiza's world by the spirit of the Sith Lord Viceroy Exim Panshard.
