# Vintage Saber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vintage Saber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vintage_Saber)

The Vintage Saber was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vintage Saber
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
