# Drya Thornne's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Drya Thornne's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drya_Thornne%27s_lightsaber)

Drya Thornne's lightsaber was an orange-bladed lightsaber with retractable crossguards. It belonged to Drya Thornne, a lieutenant of the Bedlam Raiders, though it was initially a weapon wielded by an unknown member of the Jedi Order before they were killed and their weapon taken as a trophy by Rayvis, who entrusted several lightsabers in his personal collection to his most trusted lieutenants.

- **Name:** Drya Thornne's lightsaber
- **Model:** Lightsaber
