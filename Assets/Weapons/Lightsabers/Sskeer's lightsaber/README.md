# Sskeer's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sskeer's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sskeer%27s_lightsaber)

Sskeer's lightsaber was a blue-bladed lightsaber utilized by Jedi Master Sskeer during the High Republic Era. The unconventional design of the hilt featured a talon-shaped knuckle guard that projected out of the emitter assembly. The hilt's color pattern was made from complementary segments of bronze and gold.
