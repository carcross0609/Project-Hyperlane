# Metal Gonkrocker

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Metal Gonkrocker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Metal_Gonkrocker)

The Metal Gonkrocker was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. The hilt was covered in sharp points, some of which may have consisted of bronzium according to fellow Hunter Dizzy of Team Slingshot.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Metal Gonkrocker
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
