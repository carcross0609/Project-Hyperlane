# Oppressive Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oppressive Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oppressive_Saber_Pattern)

The Oppressive Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. It was designed to resemble the overall appearance and aesthetic of the renegade Sith Lord Savage Opress.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Oppressive Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
