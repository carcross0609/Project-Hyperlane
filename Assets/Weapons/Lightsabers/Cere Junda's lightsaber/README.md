# Cere Junda's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cere Junda's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cere_Junda%27s_lightsaber)

Cere Junda's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber originally owned by Cere Junda. Sometime after escaping torture, she removed the kyber crystal and sold it to help pay off Greez Dritus' debts with the Haxion Brood, but kept the hilt. She later gave it to Cal Kestis on Ilum for him to create a new lightsaber with its parts. During the five years separated from Cal, Cere created a new lightsaber which produced a blue blade.
