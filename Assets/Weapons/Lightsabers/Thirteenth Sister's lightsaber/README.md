# Thirteenth Sister's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thirteenth Sister's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thirteenth_Sister%27s_lightsaber)

A double-bladed spinning lightsaber was utilized by the Thirteenth Sister during her time as a member of the Inquisitorius.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thirteenth Sister's lightsaber
- **Model:** Double-bladed spinning lightsaber
- **Type:**
  - Double-bladed spinning lightsaber (disc mode)
  - Single-bladed or double-bladed lightsaber (crescent mode)
- **Owners:** Thirteenth Sister
- **Crystal:** Two Kyber crystals
- **Color:** Red
- **Protection:** Large circular hand guard
- **Capacity:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Inquisitorius
