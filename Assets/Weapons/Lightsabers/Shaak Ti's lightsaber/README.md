# Shaak Ti's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shaak Ti's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shaak_Ti%27s_lightsaber)

Shaak Ti's lightsaber was a blue-bladed lightsaber carried by the Togruta Jedi Master Shaak Ti prior to and during the Clone Wars. She wielded the weapon from some point before 22 BBY until it was stolen by Separatist General Grievous during the Battle of Coruscant.
