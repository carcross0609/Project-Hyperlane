# Cohmac's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cohmac's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cohmac%27s_lightsaber)

Cohmac's lightsaber was a blue-bladed lightsaber used by Jedi Master Cohmac Vitus. The weapon's hilt was equipped with a golden emitter and a gold-accented end cap. Before departing the Jedi Order, Vitus used the saber to knight his Padawan Reath Silas and then gave him the weapon.
