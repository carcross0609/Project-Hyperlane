# Hennix's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hennix's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hennix%27s_lightsaber)

A blue-bladed lightsaber belonged to Hennix, a Force-sensitive male Quarren who joined the Jedi Order under the tutelage of Jedi Master Luke Skywalker. Hennix used the lightsaber while he and his fellow Padawans hunted Ben Solo. After his death at the hands of Solo, her fellow padawan Voe took his saber.
