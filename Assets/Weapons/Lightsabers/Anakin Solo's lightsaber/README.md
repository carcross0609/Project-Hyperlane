# Anakin Solo's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Anakin Solo's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Solo%27s_lightsaber)

Anakin Solo's lightsaber was a violet-bladed lightsaber that was created and wielded by the Jedi Knight Anakin Solo during the Yuuzhan Vong War. It played an important role in the outcome of the war while in the hands of Anakin and many others after his death.
