# Bounty Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bounty Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bounty_Saber_Pattern)

The Bounty Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. Its design was intended to resemble the aesthetic of the infamous Theelin bounty hunter Latts Razzi.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bounty Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
