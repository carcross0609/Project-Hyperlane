# Karbin's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Karbin's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karbin%27s_lightsabers)

A set of at least five single-bladed lightsabers were owned and wielded by the Mon Calamari cyborg Commander Karbin during the Imperial Era. Just as his predecessor General Grievous did, with his split arms, Karbin could wield up to four lightsabers simultaneously. Karbin used the lightsaber against the Sith Lord Darth Vader on the Outer Rim planet Vrogas Vas.

- **Name:** Karbin's lightsabers
