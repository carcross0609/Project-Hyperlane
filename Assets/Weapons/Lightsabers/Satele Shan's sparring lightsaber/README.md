# Satele Shan's sparring lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Satele Shan's sparring lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satele_Shan%27s_sparring_lightsaber)

The Jedi Grand Master Satele Shan utilized a yellow-bladed lightsaber for sparring during the Cold War and the Galactic War. It shared the same design as her second double-bladed lightsaber that was destroyed in the Battle of Alderaan.
