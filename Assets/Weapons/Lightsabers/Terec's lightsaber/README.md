# Terec's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Terec's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terec%27s_lightsaber)

Terec's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber wielded by Jedi Knight Terec during the High Republic Era. Its hilt was cylindrical with golden bands on each end, and it featured an s-curve emitter. It was stolen by Tempest Runner of the Nihil marauder organization Lourna Dee, but was reclaimed by the Jedi and eventually returned to Terec after her defeat at the Battle for No-Space.
