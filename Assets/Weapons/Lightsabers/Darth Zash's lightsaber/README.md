# Darth Zash's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Zash's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Zash%27s_lightsaber)

A red-bladed lightsaber that belonged to the Sith Lord Darth Zash was in use during the era of the Great Galactic War and the Cold War. She used it during her apprenticeship, but later exchanged it in favor of a purple-bladed saber. When Zash accepted an apprentice of her own, she provided the young Sith Inquisitor with her old lightsaber.
