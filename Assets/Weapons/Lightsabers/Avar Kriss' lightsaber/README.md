# Avar Kriss' lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Avar Kriss' lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avar_Kriss%27_lightsaber)

During the High Republic Era, the Jedi Master Avar Kriss utilized a green-bladed crossguard lightsaber. The weapon's hilt was sheathed in silver-white electrum, with a spiraling grip of incised green seastone, and had a physical crossguard at one end. The hilt was long-handled for a dual-handed grip.
