# Jecki Lon's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jecki Lon's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jecki_Lon%27s_lightsaber)

Jecki Lon's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber owned by the Theelin-human hybrid Jedi Padawan Jecki Lon. She briefly used this lightsaber in a duel along with the Wookiee Jedi Master Kelnacca's lightsaber after his death until her own demise at the hands of the mysterious Sith Lord known as "The Stranger" on the planet Khofar in 132 BBY. The weapon had a brown handgrip.
