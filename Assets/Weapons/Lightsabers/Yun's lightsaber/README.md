# Yun's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yun's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yun%27s_lightsaber)

This yellow-bladed lightsaber was created by the Dark Jedi Yun. The weapon came equipped with a so-called "modified pistol grip" meaning that carefully cast projections echoed the Human hand and gave his index finger a place to rest. Not only that, but the grip was made from a highly malleable "live" polymer that explored the user's hand and morphed into a solid, highly customized grip.
