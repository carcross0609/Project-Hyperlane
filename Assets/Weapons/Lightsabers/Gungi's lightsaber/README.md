# Gungi's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gungi's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gungi%27s_lightsaber)

Gungi's lightsaber was the personal lightsaber of the Wookiee Jedi Padawan Gungi. The weapon was constructed out of the wood from a Brylark tree and emitted a green blade. It was built aboard the Paladin-class corvette Crucible in the year 20 BBY.
