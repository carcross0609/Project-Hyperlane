# Grandea's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grandea's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grandea%27s_lightsaber)

A lightsaber was utilized by the Theelin Jedi Knight Grandea during the Clone Wars. Grandea destroyed the weapon after Order 66 in order to blend in and survive the Great Jedi Purge.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grandea's lightsaber
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Destroyed:** No earlier than 19 BBY
- **Owners:** Grandea
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Green
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
