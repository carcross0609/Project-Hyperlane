# Vildar Mac's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vildar Mac's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vildar_Mac%27s_lightsaber)

Vildar Mac's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber owned by the Kiffar Jedi Master Vildar Mac during the High Republic Era. After Mac's death, his lightsaber came into the possession of his husband, Tey Sirrek.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vildar Mac's lightsaber
- **Type:** Single-blade
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Owners:**
  - Vildar Mac
  - Tey Sirrek
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylinder with curved hand guard
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Green
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Convocation of the Force
