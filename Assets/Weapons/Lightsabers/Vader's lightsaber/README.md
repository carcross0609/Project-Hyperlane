# Vader's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vader's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vader%27s_lightsaber)

Vader's lightsaber, formerly known as Kirak Infil'a's lightsaber was a green-bladed curved-hilt lightsaber owned by Jedi Master Kirak Infil'a and briefly Darth Vader during the early stages of the Great Jedi Purge.
