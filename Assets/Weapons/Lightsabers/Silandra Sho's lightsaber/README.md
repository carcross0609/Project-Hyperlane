# Silandra Sho's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Silandra Sho's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silandra_Sho%27s_lightsaber)

Silandra Sho's lightsaber, also known as Silandra's lightsaber, was a blue-bladed lightsaber utilized by Jedi Master Silandra Sho, though she preferred to use her shield in combat. Keth Cerepath used the lightsaber to protect Sho from the Brother of the Ninth Door Mytion during the Battle of Jedha.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Silandra Sho's lightsaber
- **Type:** Single bladed lightsaber
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Owners:** Silandra Sho
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Blue
