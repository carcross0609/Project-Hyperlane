# Aneé-san's leg lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Aneé-san's leg lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ane%C3%A9-san%27s_leg_lightsaber)

The Twi'lek Aneé-san possessed a red-colored lightsaber attached to a leg prosthetic decades after the Imperial unification. When she dueled the Jedi known as The Grand Master on the planet Olcatz, she fatally pierced his head with her leg lightsaber.
