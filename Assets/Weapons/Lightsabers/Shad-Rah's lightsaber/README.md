# Shad-Rah's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Shad-Rah's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shad-Rah%27s_lightsaber)

Shad-Rah's lightsaber was a magenta-bladed lightsaber used by the former Jedi Shad-Rah Varcanella, who served the Galactic Empire under the name Zero. Between 19 BBY and 0 ABY, Zero wielded his lightsaber to duel his former Padawan F aboard the Imperial-class Star Destroyer Oppressor. Using the lightsaber, Zero cut off F's right arm and feet but was frozen by F using a capsule of carbonite.
