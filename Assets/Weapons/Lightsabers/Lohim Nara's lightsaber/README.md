# Lohim Nara's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lohim Nara's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lohim_Nara%27s_lightsaber)

Lohim Nara built a weapon that resembled a blue-bladed lightsaber from junk he found on the streets of Coruscant during the High Republic Era.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lohim Nara's lightsaber
- **Model:** Imitation lightsaber
- **Owners:** Lohim Nara
- **Hilt Material:** A drainpipe
- **Crystal:** None
- **Color:** Blue
- **Purpose:** Imitate a Jedi lightsaber
