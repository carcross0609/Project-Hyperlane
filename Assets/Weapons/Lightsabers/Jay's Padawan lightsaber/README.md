# Jay's Padawan lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Jay's Padawan lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jay%27s_Padawan_lightsaber)

Jay's Padawan lightsaber was a blue single-bladed lightsaber that belonged to former Jedi Padawan Jee Ken Tsokee, better known as Jay. After the Clone Wars, Jay became a singer in the band Star Waver and converted his lightsaber into a microphone, which he used in his performances.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jay's Padawan lightsaber
- **Type:** Single-bladed
- **Owners:** Jee Ken Tsokee
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Blue
- **Mods:** Converted into a microphone
- **Purpose:**
  - Lightsaber combat
  - Music
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Star Waver
