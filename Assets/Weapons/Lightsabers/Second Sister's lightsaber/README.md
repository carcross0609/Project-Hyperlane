# Second Sister's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Second Sister's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Sister%27s_lightsaber)

A double-bladed spinning lightsaber was utilized by the Second Sister during her time as a member of the Inquisitorius.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Second Sister's lightsaber
- **Model:** Double-bladed spinning lightsaber
- **Type:**
  - Double-bladed spinning lightsaber (disc mode)
  - Single-bladed or double-bladed lightsaber (crescent mode)
- **Owners:**
  - Second Sister
  - Cere Junda
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical
- **Crystal:** Two Kyber crystals
- **Color:** Red
- **Mods:** Extendable hand guard, can spin around central handle with detachable emitters locked to hand guard (disc mode)
- **Protection:** Large circular hand guard
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Inquisitorius
  - Stinger Mantis crew
