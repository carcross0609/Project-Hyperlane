# Sifo-Dyas's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sifo-Dyas's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sifo-Dyas%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

This blue-bladed lightsaber was a weapon that had once belonged to Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas, who also owned another blue-bladed lightsaber during his lifetime. Sifo-Dyas carried the lightsaber at the time of his death, and it was abandoned on the desert moon of Oba Diah where it was discovered by Master Plo Koon.
