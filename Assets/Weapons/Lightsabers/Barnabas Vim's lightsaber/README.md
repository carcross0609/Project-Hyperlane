# Barnabas Vim's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Barnabas Vim's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barnabas_Vim%27s_lightsaber)

Barnabas Vim's lightsaber was a green-bladed curved-hilt lightsaber wielded by Jedi Master Barnabas Vim. In 228 BBY, during the High Republic Era, the weapon was discovered by fallen Jedi Azlin Rell, who bled its kyber crystal and kept the lightsaber for himself.
