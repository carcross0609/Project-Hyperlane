# Dooku's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dooku's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

A curved-hilt dual-phase lightsaber was constructed by and served Dooku during his career as both a Jedi Master and as the Sith Lord Darth Tyranus. During the Battle of Coruscant, his lightsaber would fall into the hands of Anakin Skywalker, and he used it to kill Dooku with by slicing his head off.
