# Viv'Nia's training lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Viv'Nia's training lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Viv%27Nia%27s_training_lightsaber)

Viv'Nia's training lightsaber was a training lightsaber used by Jedi Initiate Viv'Nia Nia'Viv during the High Republic Era. The lightsaber was supplied by an armory pool and was incapable of inflicting lethal damage. It had a standard basic appearance with a metallic silver hilt and shiny black segments around its midsection. It was optimally balanced for the repeated execution of basic defensive forms.
