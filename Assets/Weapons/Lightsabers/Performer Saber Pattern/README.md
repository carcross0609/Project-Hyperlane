# Performer Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Performer Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Performer_Saber_Pattern)

The Performer Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. This hilt featured exposed wires, straps, and buckles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Performer Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
