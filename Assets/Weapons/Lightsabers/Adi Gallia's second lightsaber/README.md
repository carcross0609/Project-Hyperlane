# Adi Gallia's second lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Adi Gallia's second lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adi_Gallia%27s_second_lightsaber)

This blue-bladed lightsaber was the personal lightsaber of Jedi Master Adi Gallia. It replaced her first lightsaber after it was destroyed on a mission. She used it during many campaigns in the Clone Wars against foes, such as General Grievous, Savage Opress, and many others. Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi used it briefly along with his own saber to hold his own against Darth Maul and his brother, Savage Opress after Gallia's death at the horns of the latter, using it to cut off Opress' left arm.
