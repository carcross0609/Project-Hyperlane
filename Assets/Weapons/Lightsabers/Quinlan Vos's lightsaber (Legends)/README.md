# Quinlan Vos's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Quinlan Vos's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

Quinlan Vos constructed his green-bladed lightsaber while under the tutelage of Jedi Master Tholme. Vos wielded his lightsaber aggressively, After becoming a Jedi Knight, Vos continued to use the lightsaber, even during the Clone Wars.
