# Komat's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Komat's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Komat%27s_lightsaber)

Komat's lightsaber was a white-bladed lightsaber owned by the former Acolytes of the Beyond member Komat. The saber fell into Komat's possession during her time with the Acolytes, where the blade was red. After Komat escaped from the Acolytes with the help of Jedi Master Luke Skywalker, the Jedi purified the weapon's kyber crystal, leaving the blade white, and symbolizing the purging of evil from Komat's mind and soul. In 21 ABY, Komat used the weapon in a duel with the Sith Lord Exim Panshard, whose spirit had inhabited the body of the Pantoran Kiza, another former Acolyte.
