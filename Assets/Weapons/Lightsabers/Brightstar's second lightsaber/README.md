# Brightstar's second lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Brightstar's second lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brightstar%27s_second_lightsaber)

Brightstar's second lightsaber was a green-bladed training lightsaber formerly wielded by Jedi Grand Master Yoda and later gifted to Jedi Initiate Kai Brightstar during the High Republic Era. The blade had a silvery finish with a compact profile and a prominent activation button. The lower grip of the blade had a woven cloth wrapping. The blade, being scaled for Yoda's use, was useful in the hands of the human trainee. Brightstar originally lost his lightsaber to save his friend and fellow youngling Nubs from falling off the Crimson Firehawk. Kai would use his lightsaber during a training session against a training droid. He later developed Jar'Kai with his green and blue training lightsabers to fight off sentient droid Apexx before he repented. Kai later returned the green training lightsaber to Yoda.
