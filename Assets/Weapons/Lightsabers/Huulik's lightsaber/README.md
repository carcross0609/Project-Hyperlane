# Huulik's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Huulik's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huulik%27s_lightsaber)

Huulik's lightsaber was a amethyst-bladed lightsaber wielded by the Rodian Jedi Knight Huulik during the Clone Wars. After his demise following the execution of Order 66, the weapon was entombed along with its owner in an unmarked mausoleum on Rodia. Almost twenty years later, the late Rodian's niece Taneetch Soonta retrieved the lightsaber from Huulik's tomb and offered it to the aspiring Jedi Luke Skywalker. However, the weapon's new owner accidentally rendered it useless while trying to deconstruct it.
