# Torban Buck's blue-bladed lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Torban Buck's blue-bladed lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torban_Buck%27s_blue-bladed_lightsaber)

Torban Buck's blue-bladed lightsaber, also known as Torban's lightsaber was a curved-hilt lightsaber owned by Jedi Master Torban Buck during the High Republic Era. Possessing a wooden hilt and blue blade, Buck was relatively proficient in the lightsaber's use, but preferred not to fight since he was a Jedi healer.
