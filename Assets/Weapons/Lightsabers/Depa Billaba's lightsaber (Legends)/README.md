# Depa Billaba's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Depa Billaba's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Depa_Billaba%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

A lightsaber was constructed and wielded by Chalactan Jedi Master Depa Billaba by the time of her ascension to the Jedi High Council. The single-bladed Jedi weapon had a cylindrical gray hilt, a flared blade emitter, and a single blade, alternatively of red or green hue.
