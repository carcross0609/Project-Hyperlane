# Rana Kant's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rana Kant's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rana_Kant%27s_lightsaber)

Rana Kant's lightsaber, also known as Kant's lightsaber, was a green-bladed lightsaber utilized by the Jedi High Council member Rana Kant during the High Republic Era, until her death after the Dedication of the Starlight Beacon.
