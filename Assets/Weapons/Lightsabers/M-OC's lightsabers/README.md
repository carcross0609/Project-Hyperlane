# M-OC's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M-OC's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-OC%27s_lightsabers)

M-OC's lightsabers were lightsabers used by the hunter droid M-OC. According to R0-GR, a veteran B1 battle droid of the Clone Wars, M-OC wielded his twin blades with more skill and grace than most Jedi Masters.
