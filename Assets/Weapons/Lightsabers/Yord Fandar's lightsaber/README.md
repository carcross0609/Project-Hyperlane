# Yord Fandar's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yord Fandar's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yord_Fandar%27s_lightsaber)

Yord Fandar's lightsaber was the personal yellow-bladed lightsaber owned by Jedi Knight Yord Fandar. The Jedi carried this lightsaber until his death at the hands of the mysterious Sith Lord known as "The Stranger" on the planet Khofar in 132 BBY. In the aftermath, the assassin Mae-ho Aniseya used the lightsaber to chop off her long hair in order to impersonate her twin sister, Verosha Aniseya.
