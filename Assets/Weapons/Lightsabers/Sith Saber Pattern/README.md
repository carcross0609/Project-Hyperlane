# Sith Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sith Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Saber_Pattern)

The Sith Saber Pattern, or Rieve's lightsaber was a red-bladed lightsaber utilized by the Force-sensitive Hunter Rieve during her fights in the Grand Arena. The organizer of the Hunters of the Outer Rim, Balada the Hutt, gave Rieve the lightsaber out of her own collection to complete Rieve's Hunter persona of a Sith Lord.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sith Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single blade
- **Owners:**
  - Balada
  - Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
