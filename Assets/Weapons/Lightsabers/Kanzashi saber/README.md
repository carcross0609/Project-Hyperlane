# Kanzashi saber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Kanzashi saber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kanzashi_saber)

Kanzashi sabers were small, red-bladed lightsabers used by the Twi'lek Aneé-san after the Jedi known as the Grand Master cut off her left arm on the planet Olcatz. Multiple kanzashi sabers were fitted onto an arm prosthetic known as senju saber by her boss, the Anzellan Que-Dama. The sabers used kyber crystals previously stolen by the former Sith known as the Ronin.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kanzashi saber
- **Creator:** Que-Dama
- **Owners:** Aneé-san
- **Crystal:** The Ronin's stolen kyber crystals
- **Color:** Red
- **Blade Length:** Short
