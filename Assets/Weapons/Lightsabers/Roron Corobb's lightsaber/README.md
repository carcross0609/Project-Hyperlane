# Roron Corobb's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Roron Corobb's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Roron_Corobb%27s_lightsaber)

This green-bladed lightsaber was the personal weapon of the Jedi Roron Corobb. After Confederate General Grievous killed Corobb during the Battle of Coruscant, the lightsaber fell into the cyborg's possession.
