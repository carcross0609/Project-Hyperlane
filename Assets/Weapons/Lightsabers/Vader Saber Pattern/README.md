# Vader Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vader Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vader_Saber_Pattern)

The Vader Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. It served as a replica of Darth Vader's lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vader Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
