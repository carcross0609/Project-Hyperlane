# Imri's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imri's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imri%27s_lightsaber)

Imri's lightsaber was the first lightsaber owned and built by Jedi Padawan Imri Cantaros. It had a pale blue blade, and was powered by a kyber crystal. During his time at Port Haileap with his Master, Douglas Sunvale, Cantaros disassembled the lightsaber, and had difficulty in resembling it exactly as it had previously been, resulting in a weaker blade. Cantaros had the lightsaber with him when he was stranded on the jungle moon Wevo alongside Jedi Knight Vernestra Rwoh, inventor Avon Starros, Dalnan Honesty Weft and Starros' droid J-6 after an attack on the starship Steady Wing that had resulted in the death of Master Sunvale.
