# Greelwood Saber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Greelwood Saber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Greelwood_Saber)

The Greelwood Saber was crafted by the Ithorian Oorrl Morm to emphasize the Jedi maxims of defense and protection, rather than attack. With a rare pontite crystal mined from the crystal caves in the Eocho Mountains and a heavily lacquered greel-wood hilt, the lightsaber was made on the planet Ossus. After Morm's death, the lightsaber was interred within a vault in the Chamber of Antiquities.
