# Jedi Saber Design

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jedi Saber Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Saber_Design)

The Jedi Saber Design was a custom lightsaber hilt utilized by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jedi Saber Design
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
