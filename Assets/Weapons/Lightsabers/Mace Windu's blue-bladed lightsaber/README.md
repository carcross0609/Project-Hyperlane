# Mace Windu's blue-bladed lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mace Windu's blue-bladed lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mace_Windu%27s_blue-bladed_lightsaber)

A blue-bladed lightsaber was carried by Jedi Master Mace Windu during his membership of the High Council of the Jedi Order. An unexceptionally constructed weapon, the lightsaber had a metallic gray ribbed hilt with a conical segment of radiator casing on its pommel, and a red length adjuster for its blue blade.
