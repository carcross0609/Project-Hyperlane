# Eleventh Brother's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eleventh Brother's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eleventh_Brother%27s_lightsaber)

A double-bladed spinning lightsaber was utilized by the Eleventh Brother during his time as an Inquisitor of the Galactic Empire. It was similar in appearance to other double-bladed spinning lightsabers wielded by Inquisitors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Eleventh Brother's lightsaber
- **Model:** Double-bladed spinning lightsaber
- **Type:** Double-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Eleventh Brother
- **Hilt Shape:** Disc-hilt
- **Crystal:** Two kyber crystals
- **Color:** Red
- **Mods:** Filigree near both emitters
- **Protection:** Emitter ring
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Inquisitorius
