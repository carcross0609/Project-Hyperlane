# Yoda's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yoda's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda%27s_lightsaber)

Yoda's lightsaber was one of the personal lightsabers of Yoda, the last Grand Master of the Jedi High Council. In keeping with its owner's small stature, the green-bladed weapon had a shorter hilt and shorter blade, making it a shoto.
