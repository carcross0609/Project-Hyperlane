# Obi-Wan Kenobi's second lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Obi-Wan Kenobi's second lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi%27s_second_lightsaber)

Obi-Wan Kenobi's second lightsaber was the second lightsaber constructed and wielded by Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi following the loss of his first one during the battle of Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Obi-Wan Kenobi's second lightsaber
- **Model:** Lightsaber
- **Type:** Single-blade
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Creator:** Obi-Wan Kenobi
- **Created:** Prior to the Mission to Dallenor
- **Owners:** Obi-Wan Kenobi
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical, straight
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Blue
- **Design:** Similar design to his first lightsaber, which was based on Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber
- **Precede:** Obi-Wan Kenobi's first lightsaber
- **Succeed:** Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**

  - Jedi Order
  - Galactic Republic
