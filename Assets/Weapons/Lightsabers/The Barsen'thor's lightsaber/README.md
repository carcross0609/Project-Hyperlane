# The Barsen'thor's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [The Barsen'thor's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Barsen%27thor%27s_lightsaber)

The Barsen'thor's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber that was created and wielded by the Jedi Master known as the Barsen'thor. After his death, the lightsaber was placed in the Barsen'thor's tomb.
