# Darth Atrius' lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darth Atrius' lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Atrius%27_lightsabers)

A pair of crossguard lightsabers were once owned by Darth Atrius during a time when the Sith were plentiful and powerful. Sometime after the Battle of Yavin, the sabers were recovered by the smuggler Sana Starros and she sold one of them to a crime lord on Hradreek, and the other to the Galactic Empire. Both were later destroyed by Luke Skywalker and Darth Vader in 0 ABY during the Galactic Civil War.
