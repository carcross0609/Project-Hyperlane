# Stellan Gios's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stellan Gios's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stellan_Gios%27s_lightsaber)

Stellan Gios's lightsaber was a blue-bladed crossguard lightsaber owned by Jedi Master Stellan Gios. The lightsaber had a cylindrical silver-and-black hilt with golden accents. Just below the blade emitters, a physical crossguard was mounted, which was pushed out to support the smaller quillon beams when the lightsaber was ignited, but collapsed when the weapon was deactivated for greater portability.
