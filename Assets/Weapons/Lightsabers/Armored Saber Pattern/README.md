# Armored Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Armored Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armored_Saber_Pattern)

The Armored Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. It was supposedly of an ancient design.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Armored Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
