# Sabine Wren's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sabine Wren's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sabine_Wren%27s_lightsaber)

Sabine Wren's lightsaber, formerly Ezra Bridger's second lightsaber, was a green-bladed lightsaber constructed by Ezra Bridger, a Padawan and rebel prior to the Battle of Yavin, sometime after his original lightsaber was destroyed by the Sith Lord Darth Vader during the mission to Malachor.
