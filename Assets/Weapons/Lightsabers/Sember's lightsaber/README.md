# Sember's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sember's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sember%27s_lightsaber)

Sember's lightsaber was a blue-bladed lightsaber constructed and infrequently used by the human Jedi Master Sember Vey. She wielded the weapon until her death at the First Battle of Geonosis in 22 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sember's lightsaber
- **Owners:** Sember Vey
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
