# First Blade

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [First Blade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Blade)

The First Blade was an ancient weapon that served as the foundation for all modern lightsabers. It was designed by an ancient Jedi Master known as the Weapon Master and kept on Tython by droids for millennia.

## Specifications

- **Name:** First Blade
- **Model:** Lightsaber
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Creator:** Weapon Master
- **Owners:**
  - Weapon Master
  - Rajivari
  - Scorekeeper's Herald (The Barsen'thor's lightsaber)
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
