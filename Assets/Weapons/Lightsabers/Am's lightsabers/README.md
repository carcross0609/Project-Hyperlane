# Am's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Am's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Am%27s_lightsabers)

Am's lightsabers were the flexible, whip-like, red-bladed lightsabers used by Am, a Dark Lord of the T-Empire. Am's armor included attachments to allow her to wield multiple blades at once.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Am's lightsabers
- **Model:** Lightwhip
- **Owners:** Am
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** T-Empire
