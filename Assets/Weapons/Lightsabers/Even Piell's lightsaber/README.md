# Even Piell's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Even Piell's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Even_Piell%27s_lightsaber)

A green-bladed lightsaber was the personal weapon of Jedi Master Even Piell during the Clone Wars. It was shorter than average to account for the Lannik Jedi Master's diminutive size, which would make wielding a full-size lightsaber impractical, and had a chrome-like sheen to its hilt.
