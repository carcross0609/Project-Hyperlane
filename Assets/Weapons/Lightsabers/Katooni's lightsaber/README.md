# Katooni's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Katooni's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Katooni%27s_lightsaber)

Katooni's lightsaber was the personal lightsaber of the Tholothian Jedi youngling Katooni. The weapon was constructed out of various materials and emitted a blue blade. It was built on the planet Florrum in the year 20 BBY.
