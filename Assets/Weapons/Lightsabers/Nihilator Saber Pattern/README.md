# Nihilator Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nihilator Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nihilator_Saber_Pattern)

The Nihilator Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. It was designed to resemble the overall disheveled aesthetic of the Nihil, anarchistic marauders who rose to prominence during the High Republic Era.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nihilator Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
