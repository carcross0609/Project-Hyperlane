# Vix Fonnick's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vix Fonnick's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vix_Fonnick%27s_lightsaber)

Vix Fonnick's lightsaber was an indigo-bladed lightsaber utilized by the Jedi Vix Fonnick during the early High Republic Era. She built the lightsaber with the help of her Jedi Master, Barnabas Vim.

- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
