# Luminara's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Luminara's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luminara%27s_lightsaber)

Luminara's lightsaber was the personal green-bladed lightsaber of Jedi Master Luminara Unduli during the Clone Wars. The lightsaber was of a similar design to Plo Koon's lightsaber, bearing a finned radiator casing and ribbed gripping surface. The hilt of the weapon bore marks of wear and tear that attested to its wielder's extended operations in the field.
