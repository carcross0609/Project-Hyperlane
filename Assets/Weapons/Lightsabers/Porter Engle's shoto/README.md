# Porter Engle's shoto

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Porter Engle's shoto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Porter_Engle%27s_shoto)

In his younger days, the Jedi Porter Engle used a blue-bladed shoto lightsaber alongside his regular handguard lightsaber, wielding both blades at the same time. He utilized it in a sparring match against Helion Volte. Engle also used the shoto with his handguard lightsaber during his mission on Gansevor.
