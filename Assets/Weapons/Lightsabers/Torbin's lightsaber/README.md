# Torbin's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Torbin's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Torbin%27s_lightsaber)

Torbin's lightsaber was a yellow-bladed lightsaber owned by Torbin. He used it during his time as the Padawan of Jedi Master Indara. The lightsaber hung from Torbin's belt while he tested Verosha Aniseya's blood which prompted her to ask whether all members of the Jedi Order had their own lightsabers. Torbin later used his lightsaber to defend himself from attacks by Aniseya's coven of witches.
