# Galen Marek's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Galen Marek's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galen_Marek%27s_lightsaber)

This red-bladed lightsaber was the Sith weapon of the Dark Lord Darth Vader's secret apprentice, Galen Marek, a Force-sensitive assassin codenamed Starkiller. The apprentice's lightsaber was of a skeletal and utilitarian design. Intended for both training and combat purposes, it served multiple purposes such as the many sparring matches between the apprentice and his holodroid PROXY, as well as meditation sessions in which he concentrated on telekinetically re-assembling his lightsaber for modifications.
