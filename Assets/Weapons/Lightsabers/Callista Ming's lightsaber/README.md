# Callista Ming's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Callista Ming's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Callista_Ming%27s_lightsaber)

Callista Ming's lightsaber was a single-bladed lightsaber created by the Human Altisian Jedi, Callista Ming. While the lightsaber was predominantly used by Callista Ming, it briefly fell into the hands of the Jawa known as Shorty and later Jedi Master Luke Skywalker when Callista had transferred her consciousness into the Eye of Palpatine. Callista, however, reclaimed ownership of her lightsaber after she was freed from the Dreadnaught in 12 ABY.
