# Naare's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Naare's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naare%27s_lightsaber)

Naare's lightsaber was a red lightsaber used by the Sith agent Naare during the Quest for the Kyber Saber Crystals. While posing as a Jedi in an attempt to manipulate Rowan Freemaker into finding the Kyber Saber, Naare kept her Sith lightsaber hidden. Rowan eventually discovered the Sith blade during Naare's rescue mission on Tatooine to rescue the Freemakers. He stole the Sith lightsaber and wielded it during a mission to recover golden orbs from the third moon of Takodana. Naare reclaimed the Sith lightsaber from Rowan during a duel on Naboo but the boy stole her blue lightsaber. Naare later wielded the Sith lightsaber during her encounters with the Freemakers on Zoh, Hoth, the Freemaker Garage, and Coruscant.
