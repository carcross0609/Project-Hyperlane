# X-Wing Saber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [X-Wing Saber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-Wing_Saber)

The X-Wing Saber was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber. Its design was intended to resemble the aesthetic of the X-wing starfighter utilized by the Rebel Alliance.

## Specifications

- **Name:** X-Wing Saber
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
