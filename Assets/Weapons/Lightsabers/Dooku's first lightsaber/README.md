# Dooku's first lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dooku's first lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku%27s_first_lightsaber)

This lightsaber was built by Dooku, future leader of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and Sith Lord, during his time as a Jedi Padawan. At some point after becoming a Jedi Knight, Dooku set aside his first weapon and built a new and superior lightsaber with a personalized design that he used throughout his years as both a Jedi Master and Sith.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dooku's first lightsaber
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Creator:** Dooku
- **Created:** After Dooku's Initiate Trials in 86 BBY
- **Owners:** Dooku
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Succeed:** Dooku's lightsaber
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
