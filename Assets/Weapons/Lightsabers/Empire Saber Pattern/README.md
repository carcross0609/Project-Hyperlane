# Empire Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Empire Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Empire_Saber_Pattern)

The Empire Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. Unlike most of her hilts, this one was shorter, featuring minimal flair.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Empire Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
