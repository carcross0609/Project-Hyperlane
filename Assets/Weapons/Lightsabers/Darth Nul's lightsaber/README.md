# Darth Nul's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Nul's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Nul%27s_lightsaber)

A lightsaber was owned by Jedi Master Raniah centuries before the Great Galactic War. Raniah conserved her lightsaber after she became Darth Nul. Following her death, the lightsaber was kept in Nul's secret archive. Darth Nul's lightsaber was discovered by Jedi Knight Tau Idair during the Third Galactic War. Testing performed by Doctor Juvard Illip Oggurobb on Odessen revealed that Darth Nul had been a Jedi before becoming a Sith.
