# Eeko-Dio Daki's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eeko-Dio Daki's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eeko-Dio_Daki%27s_lightsaber)

A blue-bladed lightsaber was the personal lightsaber of the Mosyk Jedi Master Eeko-Dio Daki. While in hiding on the Mid Rim Territories planet of Janix during the Imperial Era, Daki kept his saber hidden in order to conceal his identity as a Jedi. He would however take up arms once more alongside his Padawan Devon Izara in order to combat the former Sith turned crime lord Maul when the Zabrak reemerged on Janix.
