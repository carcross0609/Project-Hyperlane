# Bobarian Afol's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Bobarian Afol's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bobarian_Afol%27s_lightsaber)

Bobarian Afol's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber owned by Force Builder Jedi Master Bobarian Afol. At some point, he lent the lightsaber to Sig Greebling in order for him to fight his brother Darth Devastator.
