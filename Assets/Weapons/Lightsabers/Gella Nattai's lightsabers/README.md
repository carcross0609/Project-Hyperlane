# Gella Nattai's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gella Nattai's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gella_Nattai%27s_lightsabers)

During the High Republic Era, Jedi Knight Gella Nattai used two purple-bladed lightsabers. She used them on many occasions throughout her Jedi career. Shortly after the ending of the Eiram-E'ronoh War in 382 BBY, Axel Greylark temporarily took one of Gella's Lightsabers, which she later retrieved.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gella Nattai's lightsabers
- **Model:** Lightsaber
- **Type:** 2 single-bladed lightsabers
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Owners:** Gella Nattai
- **Hilt Material:** Black moonstone
- **Crystal:** Twin kyber crystal
- **Color:** Purple
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
