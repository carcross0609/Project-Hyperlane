# Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi%27s_third_lightsaber/Legends)

Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber was the weapon created and wielded by Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi during the Clone Wars and the Great Jedi Purge after he lost his second lightsaber Following his death in combat against Darth Vader, the weapon passed into Vader's possession before being lost on Vjun after the destruction of the Death Star. It was found by the imposter Darksider Orloc before being recovered by Anakin Solo and returned to the Jedi Order.
