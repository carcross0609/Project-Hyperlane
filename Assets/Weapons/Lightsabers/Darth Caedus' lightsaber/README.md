# Darth Caedus' lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Caedus' lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caedus%27_lightsaber)

Darth Caedus constructed a red-bladed lightsaber to compensate for the loss of his first lightsaber, one that he had constructed in 22 ABY while still a Jedi Knight. Caedus wielded his Sith lightsaber until his death in 41 ABY where he used it in a duel with his twin sister Jaina Solo.
