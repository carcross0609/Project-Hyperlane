# Tiplee's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tiplee's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tiplee%27s_lightsaber)

A blue-bladed lightsaber was owned by the Mikkian Jedi Master Tiplee and used during the Clone Wars, including the Battle of Ringo Vinda in 19 BBY until her death at the Assault on Vizsla Keep 09 later that year.
