# Dawnriser Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dawnriser Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dawnriser_Applicator)

The Dawnriser Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. It featured a gold, silver, and dark gray exterior, with white cloth wrapped around part of the stock, as well as a glowing pink core.
