# Beskar MT-97

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beskar MT-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beskar_MT-97)

The Beskar MT-97 was a pair of beskar MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. These ornate pistols were gold, black, silver, red, and pale dark green in coloration, with circular grip ornaments, and yellow tassels dangling from the bottoms of the barrels.
