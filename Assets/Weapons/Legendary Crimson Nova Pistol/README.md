# Legendary Crimson Nova Pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Legendary Crimson Nova Pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Legendary_Crimson_Nova_Pistol)

The Legendary Crimson Nova Pistol was an energy blaster pistol in use during the time of the Galactic Civil War. The weapon was of unknown origin, and according to legend, it had been wielded by various heroes in battles for centuries. Although it was very old, by around 1 ABY, the pistol was still in perfect condition, as if time had no effect on it.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Legendary Crimson Nova Pistol
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Range:** 35 meters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
