# Huttballer Set

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Huttballer Set](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huttballer_Set)

The Huttballer Set was the name given to a set of two weapons, an Arc Caster and a Pulse Blaster, utilized by the team of Jawa brothers known collectively as the Hunter Utooni that they could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to their standard set. These weapons featured a black, gray, white, and orange color scheme, commonly associated with the sport Huttball, with a red, yellow, and white decal of a huttball displayed on the upper side of the Arc Caster.
