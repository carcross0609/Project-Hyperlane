# EP-750X EMP missile launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EP-750X EMP missile launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EP-750X_EMP_missile_launcher)

The EP-750X EMP missile launcher was a type of missile launcher produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc., where they were used by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. As an alternative to the standard dorsal-mounted twin mass-driver missile launchers, the Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry gunship could be equipped with an EP-750X. They fired guided missiles that were tipped with EMP warheads that delivered a powerful ion blast upon detonation, and were, as a result, effective against massed droid formations, as well as effective anti-ship and anti-vehicle weapons.
