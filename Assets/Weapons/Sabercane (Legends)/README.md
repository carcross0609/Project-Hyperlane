# Sabercane

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sabercane/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sabercane/Legends)

Sabercanes were a simple variation on the standard lightsaber, merely being a hilt concealed as the handle of a cane.
