# Sifo-Dyas's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sifo-Dyas's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sifo-Dyas%27s_lightsaber)

Sifo-Dyas's lightsaber was a blue-bladed lightsaber belonging to Jedi Master Sifo-Dyas. Powered by a kyber crystal, the lightsaber was used by Sifo-Dyas during his time as a Jedi Master. Around 32 BBY, Sifo-Dyas was sent to the planet Oba Diah by Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum on a secret mission to negotiate with the Pyke Syndicate, accompanied by Valorum's personal aide Silman. However, before negotiations could begin, the Jedi High Council, which Sifo-Dyas had recently been removed from, ordered him to Felucia to deal with a tribal dispute. While departing Oba Diah, Sifo-Dyas' shuttle was then shot down by the Pykes, who had been paid by a man they knew as Tyranus to assassinate the Jedi Master. The T-6 shuttle crashed on Oba Diah's moon, where the Pykes found Sifo-Dyas' corpse and a still-living Silman. Taking the aide prisoner, the spice dealers turned Sifo-Dyas' body over to Tyranus, but left the Jedi's lightsaber at the crash site.
