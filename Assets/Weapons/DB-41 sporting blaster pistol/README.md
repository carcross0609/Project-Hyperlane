# DB-41 sporting blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DB-41 sporting blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DB-41_sporting_blaster_pistol)

The DB-41 sporting blaster pistol, or DB-41 blaster pistol was a model of blaster pistol. The Abyssin gangster Gor Koresh carried one that was modified for Abyssin sight. Boba Fett also carried one in his right holster while fighting Stormtroopers on Tython, causing a power generator of an E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon to explode and fighting the Pyke Syndicate enforcers on Tatooine.
