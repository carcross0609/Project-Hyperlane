# Jawa ionization blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jawa ionization blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jawa_ionization_blaster)

Jawa ionization blasters were ion blasters used by the Jawas of Tatooine to stun droids and bring them on their sandcrawlers. When R2-D2 wandering through the desert of Tatooine, the Jawa Dathcha used a Jawa ionization blaster on him to capture him.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jawa ionization blaster
- **Type:** Ion blaster
- **Creators:** Jawas
- **Purpose:** Droid immobilization
