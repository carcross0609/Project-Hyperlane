# Extended lariats

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Extended lariats](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Extended_lariats)

Extended lariats were a weapon used by Mandalorians. During the New Republic Era, the Armorer packed extended lariats for the war party that rescued Ragnar. Din Djarin and Bo-Katan Kryze used the lariats to free Ragnar from a canyon raptor.
