# Model 134a "Buzzsaw" rotary cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Model 134a "Buzzsaw" rotary cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_134a_%22Buzzsaw%22_rotary_cannon)

The Model 134a "Buzzsaw" rotary cannon was a portable slugthrower produced by Czerka Arms. The cannon was able fire thousands of rounds per second due to its six barrels, but its weight and recoil made it a difficult weapon to use, often needing the support of a bipod or sling.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model 134a "Buzzsaw" rotary cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Type:** Slugthrower
- **Cost:** 4,500 credits
- **Range:** Long
