# Nakime's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Nakime's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nakime%27s_lightsaber)

A lightsaber was owned by the human Jedi Padawan Nakime. At some point, she used it in a lightsaber duel with her Jedi master against the Sith Aman. It broke after the Sith incapacitated Nakime, sending the Jedi down a cliff side.
