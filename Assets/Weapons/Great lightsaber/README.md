# Great lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Great lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_lightsaber)

The great lightsaber was a type of oversized lightsaber built for use by large individuals. As such, they were heavy, often awkward and difficult to use, and they featured reinforced hilts with heavy-duty power and emitter systems which made a blade that were longer than two meters in length.
