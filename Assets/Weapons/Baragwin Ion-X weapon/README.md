# Baragwin Ion-X weapon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Baragwin Ion-X weapon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baragwin_Ion-X_weapon)

The Baragwin Ion-X weapon was an ion rifle of an experimental Baragwin design that had been extensively modified by the Rodian Suvam Tan. The most significant alteration was the incorporation of a shaped-energy delivery system that delivered concussive force in addition to normal energy discharge.
