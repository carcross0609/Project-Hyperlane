# Concealed dart launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Concealed dart launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Concealed_dart_launcher)

The concealed dart launcher was a type of dart shooter usually worn on the wrist and concealed by clothing. In 137 ABY while on a bounty hunting mission, Deliah Blue used her dart launcher during an encounter with a Bothan Jedi named Hosk Trey'lis.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Concealed dart launcher
- **Type:** Poison dart shooter
- **Cost:** 1,900 credits (Illegal)
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg
- **Capacity:** 6 darts
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 120 m
- **Purpose:**
  - Concealed weapon
  - To stun or sedate a target
