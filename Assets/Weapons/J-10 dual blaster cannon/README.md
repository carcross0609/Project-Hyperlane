# J-10 dual blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [J-10 dual blaster cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/J-10_dual_blaster_cannon)

The J-10 dual blaster cannon was a model of blaster cannon used by the Separatist Droid Army during the Clone Wars. Essentially a pair of scaled-up reciprocating E-5 blaster rifles, it was rarely deployed due to its many drawbacks. During the Galactic Civil War, Alliance to Restore the Republic outposts were commonly equipped with pristine J-10s found in abandoned Separatist weapon caches to cover evacuations.
