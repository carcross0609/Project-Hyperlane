# Prairie Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Prairie Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prairie_Slugthrower)

The Prairie Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This blocky slugthrower consisted of dark red wood-like components, as well as bronze, black, and scuffed green metal elements.
