# Super Battle Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Super Battle Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Battle_Clubs)

The Super Battle Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. These clubs consisted of a pair of B2-series super battle droid arms.
