# Galactic Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Galactic Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Rifle)

The Galactic Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This ornate blaster rifle consisted of a weathered black and brown frame, appearing to consist of both metal and wood. Overlaid were purple and green painted portions, with golden ringed patterns detailed overtop of the body of the weapon, as well as purple strips of cloth wrapped around the stock and grip. Imara Vex also referred to this blaster as a sniper rifle.
