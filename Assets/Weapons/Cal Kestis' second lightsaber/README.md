# Cal Kestis' second lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cal Kestis' second lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cal_Kestis%27_second_lightsaber)

This lightsaber was created by Jedi Padawan Cal Kestis when he went to Ilum after damaging his previous weapon. The weapon was created by combining parts from Cere Junda's lightsaber and his former deceased master Jaro Tapal's lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cal Kestis' second lightsaber
- **Model:** Handcrafted Lightsaber
- **Type:**
  - Split saber
  -   Double-bladed lightsaber
  -   Single-bladed lightsaber (when separated)
  - Crossguard lightsaber
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Creator:** Cal Kestis
- **Created:** 14 BBY, Ilum
- **Owners:** Cal Kestis
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical, straight
- **Hilt Material:** Salvaged materials from Cere Junda's lightsaber and Jaro Tapal's lightsaber
- **Crystal:** Two kyber crystals
- **Color:** Blue
- **Mods:** Hilt allows user to switch between single-bladed Jar'Kai, double-bladed, or crossguard
- **Precede:** Jaro Tapal's lightsaber
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Mantis crew
  - Partisans
  - Hidden Path
