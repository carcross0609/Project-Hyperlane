# SKZ Sporting Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SKZ Sporting Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SKZ_Sporting_Blaster)

The SKZ Sporting Blaster was a civilian sporting rifle manufactured by Corellian Arms. Commonly used for hunting or self-defense, the SKZ was a popular model of sporting blaster for centuries. It was not as powerful as military-grade blaster rifles, but it was powerful enough to bring down medium-sized quarries up to all but the largest game. They had a modular design to allow for attachments, and could be easily broken down into three pieces for easy transport and storage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SKZ Sporting Blaster
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Arms
- **Type:** Sporting blaster
- **Cost:** 600 credits
