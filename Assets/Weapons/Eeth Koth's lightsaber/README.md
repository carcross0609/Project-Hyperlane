# Eeth Koth's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Eeth Koth's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eeth_Koth%27s_lightsaber)

Eeth Koth's lightsaber was the lightsaber belonging to Zabrak Jedi Master Eeth Koth during the final years of the Galactic Republic. It produced a green-blade when ignited. During the Clone Wars, Eeth Koth used his lightsaber when he fought Separatist General Grievous. Koth was briefly relieved of his weapon during his brief tenure as Grievous' prisoner but was able to recover it after he was freed.
