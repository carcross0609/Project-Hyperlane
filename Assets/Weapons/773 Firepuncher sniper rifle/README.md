# 773 Firepuncher sniper rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [773 Firepuncher sniper rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/773_Firepuncher_sniper_rifle)

The 773 Firepuncher sniper rifle, also known as the 773 Firepuncher rifle or simply 773 Firepuncher, was a model of a versatile sniper rifle manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. that featured ablative coating and a wide-beam "burning" mode. The rifle could also fire bolts that could reflect off of surfaces. The 773 Firepuncher rifle was a customizable weapon, but even a standard-issue 773 Firepuncher rifle was a powerful weapon. It could act as a blaster rifle, grappling hook launcher, and homing-beacon dispatch. The weapon was capable of firing many types of ammo, including electro-darts and tripwires.
