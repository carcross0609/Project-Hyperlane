# "Snubble special" pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** ["Snubble special" pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%22Snubble_special%22_pistol)

The "snubble special" pistol, also known as just the "snubble", was a black-colored model of double-snub nosed blaster pistol. The blaster had limited ammunition but packed a powerful punch and included a stun setting. The Grindalid Moloch, a lieutenant of the White Worms gang, favored using a "snubble" that was equipped with a scope with built in luma-compensators. He also removed the stun setting on his pistol to ensure he always used lethal force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** "Snubble special" pistol
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Locations:** Coronet City, Corellia
- **Affiliation:** White Worms
