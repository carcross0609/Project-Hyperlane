# Gun runner's pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gun runner's pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gun_runner%27s_pistol)

Gun runner's pistols were a make of blaster pistol available during the Cold War. They were silver in color with yellow patterning on and several blue lights built in. Sergeant Blyes, a member of the Republic Military fighting in a civil war on the planet Ord Mantell during the Cold War owned one as part of his personal supply of weapons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gun runner's pistol
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Locations:** Ord Mantell
