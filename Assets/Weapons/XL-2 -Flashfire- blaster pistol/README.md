# XL-2 "Flashfire" blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [XL-2 "Flashfire" blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XL-2_%22Flashfire%22_blaster_pistol)

The XL-2 "Flashfire" blaster pistol was a type of blaster pistol manufactured by Talus Ironworks. Its creation was accidental after Talus test-fired a prototype with a faulty focusing aperture. While the blaster could still fire, it could also emit a bright flash of light, and instead of replacing the aperture, the engineers decided to study it, and pushed to see how bright the flash could be while it also maintained its lethal beam cohesion. The result was the XL-2 Flashfire.
