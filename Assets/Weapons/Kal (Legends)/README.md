# Kal

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kal/Legends)

A kal was a traditional Mandalorian dagger. Forged of beskar iron, Mandalorian Crusaders were known to wield a kal into battle in their off hand, often in addition to another weapon. Most often, the kal was wielded alongside a beskad, the traditional Mandalorian saber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kal
- **Manufacturer:** Mandalorian metalsmiths
- **Model:** Dagger
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Culture:** Mandalorian
- **Material:** Beskar
- **Range:** Close range
- **Purpose:** Stabbing
- **Affiliation:** Mandalorians
