# Pre-shaped Detonite Charge

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pre-shaped Detonite Charge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-shaped_Detonite_Charge)

Pre-Shaped Detonite Charges were manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. Costing two hundred credits, the charges were capable of penetrating materials such as duracrete, plasteel, and durelium.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pre-shaped Detonite Charge
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Shaped charge
- **Cost:** 200 credits
