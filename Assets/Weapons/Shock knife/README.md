# Shock knife

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shock knife](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shock_knife)

A shock knife was a type of knife. In 228 BBY, Nihil Minister of Information Ghirra Starros owned a miniature shock knife, which she had with her when she visited a café on the Core Worlds planet Coruscant. As she drank cream tea, Starros mentally noted that if anything were to happen to her while at the café, she had the shock knife for defense, though she ended up not needing to use it.
