# Unidentified lightsaber (Fido Blick)

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Unidentified lightsaber (Fido Blick)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_lightsaber_%28Fido_Blick%29)

A yellow-bladed lightsaber was owned by a Jedi who died during the destruction of Starlight Beacon. After their passing, their lightsaber came into possession of the Duros Fido Blick who used the weapon to save Jedi Padawan Cam Lindon during the battle in Arovia park.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Unidentified lightsaber
- **Type:** Lightsaber
- **Owners:**

  - Fido Blick
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Yellow
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
