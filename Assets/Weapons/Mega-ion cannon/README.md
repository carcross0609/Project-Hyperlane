# Mega-ion cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mega-ion cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mega-ion_cannon)

A mega-ion cannon, also known as a superheavy ion cannon or an ion pulse-cannon, was a large ion cannon used by the Separatists. It could fire a large circular energy blast that could disable the power systems of entire fleets. A Subjugator-class heavy cruiser, the Malevolence was a superweapon equipped with two mega-ion cannons, on each port and starboard sides of the vessel. However, the Malevolence was destroyed when it crashed into the Dead Moon of Antar in the Prindaar system. A new mega-ion cannon was being constructed within a Separatist weapons factory on Abrion Major until it was discovered by the Republic, leading to the Battle of Abrion Bridge.
