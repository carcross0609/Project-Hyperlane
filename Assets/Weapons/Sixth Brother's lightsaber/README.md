# Sixth Brother's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sixth Brother's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sixth_Brother%27s_lightsaber)

The Sixth Brother's lightsaber was utilized during his time as an Inquisitor of the Galactic Empire. The weapon would be destroyed slightly over a year after the formation of the Empire on the Outer Rim moon Raada, causing the Sixth Brother's death, when the Inquisitor fought the former Jedi Padawan Ahsoka Tano. Tano claimed the kyber crystals from the Sixth Brother's lightsaber for use in her own newly-constructed pair of lightsabers, after purifying them of the taint of the dark side.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sixth Brother's lightsaber
- **Model:**
  - Double-bladed spinning lightsaber
  - Split saber
- **Type:**
  - Double-bladed spinning lightsaber (disc mode)
  - Single-bladed lightsaber (crescent mode)
  - Two lightsabers (disconnect mode)
- **Destroyed:** 18 BBY, Raada (remains incorporated into Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers)
- **Owners:** Sixth Brother
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical
- **Hilt Material:** Metallic
- **Crystal:** Two kyber crystals
- **Color:** Red
- **Blade Length:** 1 meter fully extended (crescent mode)
- **Mods:**
  - Extendable hand guard, can spin around central handle with detachable emitters locked to hand guard (disc mode)
  - Detachable hilt allows the user to fight with two single sabers (disconnect mode)
- **Protection:** Large circular hand guard
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Inquisitorius
