# Mouth-operated gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mouth-operated gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mouth-operated_gun)

The mouth-operated gun was a ranged weapon intended for operation by the Laboi, a species of limbless mammals native to the planet Laboi II. The mouth-operated weapon required no hands to fire and was useful to Laboi who left their homeworld for the greater galaxy. Nevertheless, such technology was incredibly rare and extremely expensive; during the Galactic Civil War, it cost at least ten times the price of a comparable hand-operated weapon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mouth-operated gun
- **Type:** Gun
- **Culture:** Laboi
- **Cost:** At least ten times that of a comparable hand-operated weapon
- **Purpose:** Allow limbless beings to use gun weapons
