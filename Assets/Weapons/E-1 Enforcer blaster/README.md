# E-1 Enforcer blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-1 Enforcer blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-1_Enforcer_blaster)

The E-1 Enforcer blaster was a model of Imperial blaster pistol used around the time of the Third Galactic War. E-1 Enforcer blasters were used by Imperial officers and medics during the mission to Mek-Sha.
