# Vernestra Rwoh's purple-bladed lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vernestra Rwoh's purple-bladed lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vernestra_Rwoh%27s_purple-bladed_lightsaber)

Vernestra Rwoh's purple-bladed lightsaber, also known as Vernestra Rwoh's lightsaber, or as Vernestra's lightsaber, was a purple-bladed lightsaber wielded by Jedi Master Vernestra Rwoh during the High Republic Era. Originally an ordinary single-bladed weapon, Rwoh modified it to be able to switch between lightsaber and lightwhip modes after the design came to her in a dream. She kept the modification secret for several weeks, practicing with the whip in private, until after she had become stranded on the jungle moon Wevo with several others, revealing the modification in order to clear brush to escape acid rain. Switching between blade modes was activated by twisting a ring just below the blade emitter.
