# Halsey's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Halsey's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halsey%27s_lightsaber)

This lightsaber was the personal green-bladed lightsaber of Jedi Master Halsey. In 20 BBY, Halsey used his lightsaber against the Sith warrior Savage Opress on Devaron, but the Zabrak grabbed his hand and squeezed it so hard causing Halsey to drop his blade, which fell to the ground. Even so, Halsey was proficient in hand-to-hand combat and was able to hold off Opress for a moment, but that wasn't enough and the Jedi Master was killed.
