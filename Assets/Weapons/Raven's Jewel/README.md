# Raven's Jewel

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Raven's Jewel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raven%27s_Jewel)

The Raven's Jewel was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. It featured a number or protruding metal spikes, with a red cord wrapped around the handle, and a purple gemstone situated at the bottom.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Raven's Jewel
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
