# DT-57 "Annihilator" blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DT-57 "Annihilator" blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DT-57_%22Annihilator%22_blaster)

The DT-57 "Annihilator" blaster was a model of blaster rifle that could be wielded with one hand, much like a blaster pistol. Once such Annihilator, named the Grievance Striker, was owned by General Grievous during the Clone Wars, though it was ultimately used by Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi to kill the dreaded cyborg during the Battle of Utapau.
