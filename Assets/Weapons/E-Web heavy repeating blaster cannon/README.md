# E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-Web_heavy_repeating_blaster_cannon)

The E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon, also known as the E-Web heavy repeater, Emplacement Weapon, Heavy Blaster, or simply as the E-Web, was the most powerful heavy repeating blaster cannon in the Imperial arsenal and commonly used by Imperial stormtroopers and clones of the Galactic Republic that came before it.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon
- **Manufacturer:**
  - BlasTech Industries
  - Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** E-Web
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Cost:** 8000 credits
- **Range:**

  - 500 meters (maximum range)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Grand Army of the Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Army
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
