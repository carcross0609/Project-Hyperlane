# Ceremonial sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ceremonial sword/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ceremonial_sword/Legends)

The ceremonial sword was an object used in treaties and ceremonies by Toydarians. King Katuunko once presented Jedi Master Yoda with his sword. This action represented the king entrusting the safety and defense of his people to Yoda and the Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ceremonial sword
- **Type:** Sword
- **Culture:** Toydarian
- **Destroyed:** c. 21 BBY
- **Locations:** Toydaria
- **Cost:** Not for sale
