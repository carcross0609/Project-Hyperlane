# AG-2G quad laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AG-2G quad laser cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AG-2G_quad_laser_cannon/Legends)

The Corellian Engineering Corporation AG-2G quad laser cannon was a powerful quad laser cannon, two of which were installed on the top and bottom of the Millennium Falcon by Lando Calrissian and modified by Han Solo, the latter of whom also added the ventral cannons. Imperial Lancer-class frigates mounted twenty of these quad laser cannons, for use against fast-moving starfighters.
