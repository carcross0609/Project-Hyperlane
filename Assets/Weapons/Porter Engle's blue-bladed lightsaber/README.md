# Porter Engle's blue-bladed lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Porter Engle's blue-bladed lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Porter_Engle%27s_blue-bladed_lightsaber)

Porter Engle's blue-bladed lightsaber was a blue-bladed lightsaber utilized by Jedi Porter Engle during the High Republic Era. In his younger days, he also had a shoto, which was also blue-bladed.
