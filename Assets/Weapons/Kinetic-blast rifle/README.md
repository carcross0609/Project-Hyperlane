# Kinetic-blast rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kinetic-blast rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kinetic-blast_rifle)

The kinetic-blast rifle, also known as Mustafarian Blaster Rifle, or Mustafar rifle, was a type of long blaster rifle that fired bolts of kinetic energy. They were developed by the Mustafarian species, natives of the planet Mustafar whose skin and exoskeletons could withstand the heat radiated by most common blasters.

- **Type:** Blaster rifle
