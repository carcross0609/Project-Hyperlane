# The Elder's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [The Elder's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Elder%27s_lightsabers)

Two red-bladed lightsabers were utilized by a Force-sensitive warrior known as the Elder. Between 832 BBY and 32 BBY, the Elder traveled to the planet Habo, where he used his lightsabers to kill a Vamga predator. The Elder then encountered Jedi Padawan Dan G'vash and challenged him to a lightsaber duel. After defeating him, the Elder waited near G'vash's unconscious body for his master, Tajin Crosser, to appear and provide a challenge. The Elder again used his lightsabers to duel Crosser, who grabbed one of the lightsabers and destroyed it. With the help of G'vash, Crosser then killed the Elder.
