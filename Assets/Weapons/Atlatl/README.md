# Atlatl

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Atlatl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Atlatl)

An atlatl was a one-handed Gungan throwing device consisting of a short stick of non-conductive hardwood with a cradle in one of its ends. It acted as an extension of the arm, enabling a booma to travel at a greater speed and force than possible with the unaided arm. It had optimal range of 30 meters, and a maximum range of 100 meters. It could also be used without an energy ball, as a blunt weapon.
