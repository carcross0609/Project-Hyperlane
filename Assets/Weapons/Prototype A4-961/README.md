# Prototype A4-961

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Prototype A4-961](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prototype_A4-961)

Prototype A4-961 was an experimental grenade developed by the Tagge Corporation. Between 3 ABY and 4 ABY, Lady Domina Tagge—the company's leader—utilized the prototype against a pair of KX-series security droids after they had been infected by the Scourge and began to attack her. As she threw the grenade, Tagge referred to it as a premature test of the prototype, though noted as it destroyed the droids that it still provided her with data.
