# Sticky mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sticky mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sticky_mine)

Sticky mines were a type of explosive device. Corporal Purpruff of the Alliance to Restore the Republic's Special Forces was equipped with a sticky mine when she traveled to the planet Hoth while scouting for a new location for the Alliance's headquarters. After discovering huge subterranean caverns on Hoth, the corporal tested their structural stability by attaching a sticky mine to a stalactite and detonating it; however, the stalactite did not even shudder during the explosion. Purpruff later included the results of this test as part of a report on Hoth she submitted, which ultimately ended up being included in The Rebel Files, a collection of physical records documenting the history of the Alliance.
