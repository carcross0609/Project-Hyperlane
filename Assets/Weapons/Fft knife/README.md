# Fft knife

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fft knife](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fft_knife)

A fft knife was a ceremonial knife used by the Ssi-ruuk to cut open fft. It was approximately 20 centimeters long with three prongs that ended in a serrated blade. They were very durable and required little maintenance. A large amount were captured from the Shriwirr and kept as trophies by Alliance troops.
