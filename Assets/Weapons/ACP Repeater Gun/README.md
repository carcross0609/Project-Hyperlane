# ACP Repeater Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ACP Repeater Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ACP_Repeater_Gun)

The ACP Repeater Gun was a heavy repeating blaster produced by Arakyd Industries and sold for 1,000 credits. Initially designed for planetary defense forces who deemed it too expensive and underpowered, it found popularity with mercenaries and criminals as an effective suppression weapon. Among mercenaries and enslavers, the repeater became known as the Trandoshan Chopper, while members of the Gank species called it the Nar Shaddaa Grinder.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ACP Repeater Gun
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits
- **Range:** Medium
- **Purpose:** Usage by planetary defense forces
