# P-20 seeker missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [P-20 seeker missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-20_seeker_missile)

The P-20 seeker missile was a missile model used during the Galactic War. Powerful enough to bring down small starfighters, the forces of InterStellar Regulators founder Warhound used such missiles during the Conquest of Makeb.

## Specifications

- **Name:** P-20 seeker missile
- **Type:** Missile
- **Purpose:** Anti-starfighter missile
- **Affiliation:** Warhound's forces
