# DF.12 Com Turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DF.12 Com Turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DF.12_Com_Turret)

The DF.12 Com Turret, also known as a Resistance Turret, was a ground-based turret emplacement that served as a reliable first line of defense. It was used at Resistance encampments and temporary air bases. One such turret could be found at Black Spire Outpost on Batuu, specifically at the Resistance encampment at the ancient ruins.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DF.12 Com Turret
- **Type:** Defensive ground-based turret
- **Affiliation:** Resistance
