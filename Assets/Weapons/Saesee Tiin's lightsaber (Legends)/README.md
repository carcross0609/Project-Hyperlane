# Saesee Tiin's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saesee Tiin's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saesee_Tiin%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

A single-bladed green lightsaber belonged to Jedi Master Saesee Tiin. Though he preferred missions in the cockpit of his starfighter, he wielded the lightsaber with precision and great skill. The color of the blade switched from violet, cobalt and emerald.
