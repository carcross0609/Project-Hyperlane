# Kantam Sy's green-bladed lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kantam Sy's green-bladed lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kantam_Sy%27s_green-bladed_lightsaber)

Kantam Sy's green-bladed lightsaber, also known as Kantam's lightsaber was a green-bladed lightsaber owned by Jedi Master Kantam Sy during the High Republic Era. Sy wielded the lightsaber during a battle against the Nihil on the moon Quantxi.
