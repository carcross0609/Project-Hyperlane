# VX Vibro-greatsword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [VX Vibro-greatsword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VX_Vibro-greatsword)

The VX Vibro-greatsword, also known as the Czerhander, was a type of vibrosword produced by the weapon-manufacturer Czerka Arms, and available during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. The sword was extended in both blade and hilt length compared to other models of vibrosword, and as such were also heavier. The hilt allowed for the weapon to be wielded with two hands, giving the user greater control and resulting in many skilled swordsman favoring the weapon. The sword equalled a vibro-ax in terms of hitting power, and despite its weight and size was well balanced enough to parry with. The weapon cost 900 credits and during the war was rare enough that it was hard to find.
