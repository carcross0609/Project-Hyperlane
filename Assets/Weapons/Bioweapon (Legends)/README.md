# Bioweapon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bioweapon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bioweapon/Legends)

A bioweapon was a genetically engineered pathogen developed and used as a military weapon. It was often, although not always, designed to affect only a specific species or number of species.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bioweapon
- **Creators:**
  - Nuvo Vindi (Blue Shadow Virus)
  - General Evir Derricote (Created various biological agents)
  - Vul Isen
  - Darth Maladi (Omega Red)
- **Purpose:** Biological warfare
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Galactic Empire
  - Chiss Ascendancy
  - Imperial Remnant
  - One Sith
