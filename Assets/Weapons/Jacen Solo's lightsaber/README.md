# Jacen Solo's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jacen Solo's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jacen_Solo%27s_lightsaber)

Jacen Solo's lightsaber was a weapon constructed by Jedi Knight Jacen Solo in 23 ABY. He used it until his fall to the dark side in 40 ABY, at which point it was confiscated by Luke and Ben Skywalker. As a result of losing his first lightsaber, he constructed a red-bladed lightsaber to compensate for the loss of his first one and wielded this second lightsaber until his death in 41 ABY where he used it in a duel with his twin sister Jaina Solo.
