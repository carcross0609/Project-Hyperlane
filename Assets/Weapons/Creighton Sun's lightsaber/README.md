# Creighton Sun's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Creighton Sun's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Creighton_Sun%27s_lightsaber)

During the High Republic Era, Jedi Master Creighton Sun utilized a blue-bladed crossguard lightsaber. He used the lightsaber to help remove Morton San Tekka's body from rubble that he was trapped under after the Attack on the peace summit at the Second Spire on the moon Jedha. He later used it during the Battle of Jedha and the Night of Sorrow.
