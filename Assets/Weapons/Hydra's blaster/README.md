# Hydra's blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hydra's blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hydra%27s_blaster)

The Grand Inquisitor, Hydra owned a blaster which she kept on her utility belt. While interrogating Clive Flax, Astri Oddo stole the blaster and locked her in the detainment cell that Hydra was interrogating Clive in.
