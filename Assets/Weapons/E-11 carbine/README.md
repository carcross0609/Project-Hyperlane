# E-11 carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-11 carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-11_carbine)

The E-11 carbine was the carbine version of the popular E-11 blaster rifle. It was popular among Alliance soldiers, and also utilized by Imperial recruit trainees. It was more lightweight than E-11 blaster rifle with a higher rate of fire, but it also had less range and stopping power.
