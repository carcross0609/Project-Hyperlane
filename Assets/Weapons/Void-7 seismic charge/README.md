# Void-7 seismic charge

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Void-7 seismic charge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Void-7_seismic_charge)

The Void-7 seismic charge was a type of seismic charge equipped on the Slave I, a Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft owned by the bounty hunter Jango Fett and later his son, Boba Fett. The elder Fett utilized Void-7s in 22 BBY in an attempt to dispatch Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi during a dogfight over the planet Geonosis, just prior to the outbreak of the Clone Wars.
