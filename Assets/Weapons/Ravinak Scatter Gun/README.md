# Ravinak Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ravinak Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ravinak_Scatter_Gun)

The Ravinak Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. Blue fur or feathers protruded from the surface of this gun.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ravinak Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
