# Reva's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Reva's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reva%27s_lightsaber)

Reva's lightsaber also known as Reva's double-bladed Inquisitor Lightsaber, and the Third Sister's lightsaber, was a lightsaber wielded by the Jedi hunter known as the Third Sister, also known as Reva, during her service to the Galactic Empire. It was similar in appearance to other double-bladed spinning lightsabers wielded by Imperial Inquisitors. As the hunt for Obi-Wan Kenobi came to an end, Reva would lay down her lightsaber, seeking to turn her back on the dark side of the Force.
