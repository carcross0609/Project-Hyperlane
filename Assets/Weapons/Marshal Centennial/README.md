# Marshal Centennial

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Marshal Centennial](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marshal_Centennial)

The Marshal Centennial was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle featured a black and silver base showing some wear, with an indented symbol on the sides, as well as an orange glowing core, and worn, brown wooden components on the stock and at the base of the barrel. According to Imara Vex, the rifle was old and had seen much use against outlaws.
