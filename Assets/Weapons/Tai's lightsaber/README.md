# Tai's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tai's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tai%27s_lightsaber)

This blue-bladed lightsaber belonged to Tai, a Force-sensitive male human who joined the Jedi Order under the tutelage of Jedi Master Luke Skywalker. Ben Solo once wielded the lightsaber in conjunction with his own and used it to fatally stab the dark-side user Ren.

- **Name:** Tai's lightsaber
