# BWDL19 rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BWDL19 rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BWDL19_rifle)

The BWDL19 rifle was a model of blaster rifle manufactured during the Galactic Civil War. This variant of the DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle was developed by the Galactic Empire to protect the Imperial Research and Prison Facility on Dathomir. A BWDL19 rifle was discovered in the Quarantine Zone of Dathomir during the Blackwing virus outbreak in 1 ABY.

- **Type:** Blaster rifle.
