# Outpost Sweeper

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Outpost Sweeper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outpost_Sweeper)

The Outpost Sweeper was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle consisted of a worn black, gray, brown, and tan metal frame, covered in blue, yellow, and orange painted elements, as well as dark brown strips of cloth bound around the grip, stock, and base of the barrel.
