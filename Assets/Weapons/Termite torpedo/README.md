# Termite torpedo

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Termite torpedo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Termite_torpedo)

Termite torpedos were a type of torpedo that deployed termite droids upon impact. They cost 900 credits on the market and were launched out of termite torpedo launchers. The Arakyd Industries Dismantler Torpedo and the Baktoid BT-30 Termite Droid Launcher both launched termite torpedos.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Termite torpedo
- **Type:** Torpedo
- **Cost:** 900 credits
- **Purpose:** Deploy termite droids
