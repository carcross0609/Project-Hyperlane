# M-300 hunting blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M-300 hunting blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-300_hunting_blaster)

The M-300 hunting blaster, nicknamed the "Hunting Blaster," was a long-range stun rifle manufactured by BlasTech Industries to survive the rigors of the outdoors. Popular with hunters, it had unparalleled range and accuracy for its class, and due to trade-secret technologies it provided an intense and coherent blaster beam that could stun even the toughest creatures.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M-300 hunting blaster
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** M-300
- **Type:** Stun rifle
- **Cost:** 1,600 credits
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Range:** Long-ranged
