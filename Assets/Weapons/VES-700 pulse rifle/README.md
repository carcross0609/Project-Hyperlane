# VES-700 pulse rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [VES-700 pulse rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VES-700_pulse_rifle)

The VES-700 pulse rifle was a model of pulse rifle manufactured by Corondexx. Equipped with a stun setting, the rifles fired a rapid series of micro-blasts across a wide blast-pattern at a medium range and were particularly effective in closed spaces, such as starship corridors. As such, the weapon could be found on board many light freighters, and was also often used by riot police and bounty hunters. During the Galactic Civil War the weapon cost 950 credits and was hard to find for purchase.
