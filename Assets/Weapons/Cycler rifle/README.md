# Cycler rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cycler rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cycler_rifle)

Cycler rifles were crude yet reliable slugthrower rifles designed to fire solid projectiles. They could hit targets at extreme ranges and penetrate energy shields. Used primarily by primitive cultures and hunters, they were strong enough to tear whole limbs from a body.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cycler rifle
- **Type:** Slugthrower rifle
- **Cost:** 200–400 credits
- **Weight:** 4–6 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 12–15 shots
- **Range:** 200–1,000 meters
- **Purpose:** Long-range combat
- **Affiliation:** Tusken Raiders
