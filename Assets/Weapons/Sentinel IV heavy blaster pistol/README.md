# Sentinel IV heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sentinel IV heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentinel_IV_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The Sentinel IV heavy blaster pistol was manufactured by the Caelli-Merced Syndicate. A highly prized weapon, they were generally only sold on Socorro. Members of the Black Bha'lir considered carrying a Sentinel IV to be a status symbol.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sentinel IV heavy blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Caelli-Merced Syndicate
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 1,200 credits
- **Capacity:** 100
- **Range:** 60 meters
