# MegaCaliber Six turbolaser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MegaCaliber Six turbolaser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MegaCaliber_Six_turbolaser_cannon)

The MegaCaliber Six turbolaser cannon was an extremely powerful turbolaser cannon mounted on the back of the First Order's All Terrain MegaCaliber Six walker. It saw use during the Battle of Crait and was tasked to destroy the Resistance's artillery of Crait outpost. It was designed to penetrate the strong shields on enemy bases. Due to its extreme power, it was installed on the back of a specially designed walker and had its own dedicated power generator and fuel cells that allowed it to fire faster.
