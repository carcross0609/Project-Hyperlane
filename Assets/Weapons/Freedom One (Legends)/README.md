# Freedom One

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Freedom One/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedom_One/Legends)

The Freedom One was a blaster rifle manufactured by the SoroSuub Corporation. It was formerly known as the Stormtrooper One prior to SoroSuub changing its allegiance and throwing its support to the Rebel Alliance and New Republic. The Stormtrooper One was a clone of the BlasTech E-11 rifle, and saw far less use than that weapon, though the Imperial war machine did use both products.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Freedom One
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,000 credit
