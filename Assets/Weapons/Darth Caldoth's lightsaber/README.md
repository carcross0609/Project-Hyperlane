# Darth Caldoth's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darth Caldoth's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Caldoth%27s_lightsaber)

A crimson-bladed lightsaber was used by the Duros Sith Lord Darth Caldoth. He made use of it when he traveled to Simoth and was reunited with his Sith apprentice Ry Nymbis.
