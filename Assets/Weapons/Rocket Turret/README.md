# Rocket Turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rocket Turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rocket_Turret)

Rocket Turrets, also known as Rocket Towers, were weapon emplacements utilized by a number of factions, including the Galactic Republic, the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the Galactic Empire, and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. They were most effective against vehicles, and were highly customizable and easily upgradable.
