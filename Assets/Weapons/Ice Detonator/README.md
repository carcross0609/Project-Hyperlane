# Ice Detonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ice Detonator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Detonator)

The Ice Detonator, also known as the Ice Spike, was a fragmentation grenade used and popularized by the fishermen of Neftali. It was manufactured by the Caelli-Merced Syndicate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ice Detonator
- **Manufacturer:** Caelli-Merced Syndicate
- **Type:** Fragmentation grenade
- **Culture:** Neftali fishermen
- **Locations:** Neftali
- **Cost:** 800 credits
- **Shape:** Oval
- **Size:** Fist-sized
- **Range:** 8 meter radius
