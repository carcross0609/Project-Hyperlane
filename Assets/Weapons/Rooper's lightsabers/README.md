# Rooper's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rooper's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rooper%27s_lightsabers)

Rooper's lightsabers, also known as Nitani's dual lightsabers, were two blue-bladed twin-lightsabers utilized by Jedi Padawan Rooper Nitani.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rooper's lightsabers
- **Type:** Two single bladed twin lightsabers
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Owners:** Rooper Nitani
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
