# Darth Sion's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Sion's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sion%27s_lightsaber)

A red-bladed lightsaber belonged to the Lord of Pain, Darth Sion. Sion used the lightsaber to amputate Kreia, who was formerly his master Darth Traya, in 3951 BBY.
