# Darth Momin's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darth Momin's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Momin%27s_lightsabers)

A pair of Sith lightsabers were the personal weapons of the Sith Lord Darth Momin. Sometime before the rise of the Galactic Empire, Momin utilized these lightsabers while training under Darth Shaa. Eventually he would use his sabers to cut down his Sith Master.

- **Name:** Darth Momin's lightsabers
