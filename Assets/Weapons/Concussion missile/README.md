# Concussion missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Concussion missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Concussion_missile)

A concussion missile was a type of missile in which energy warheads would release clouds of high-velocity proton particles upon impact. They were commonly mounted on starfighters such as the Rebel Alliance RZ-1 A-wing interceptor and the Imperial TIE/sa bomber, and fired through special launchers to provide hull-piercing capability.
