# Bloodhammer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bloodhammer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodhammer)

Bloodhammer was a large weapon owned by Vin-Vin Sunfall, the bartender of Vin-Vin's Wheelhouse on the planet Metagos. He kept the weapon under the bar, within reach as he served customers. When the undercover Jedi Master Mace Windu entered the establishment, Sunfall, sensing signs of danger, brushed his fingers against Bloodhammer, but hoped he would not need to use it.
