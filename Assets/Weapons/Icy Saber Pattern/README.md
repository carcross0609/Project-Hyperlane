# Icy Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Icy Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Icy_Saber_Pattern)

The Icy Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. The saber's hilt appeared to be covered in ice.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Icy Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
