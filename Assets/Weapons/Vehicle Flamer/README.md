# Vehicle Flamer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vehicle Flamer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicle_Flamer)

Czerka Arms' Vehicle Flamer was a flamethrower that could be mounted on a vehicle such as a speeder. They cost around 2,000 credits and had a range of twenty meters. Vehicle Flamers were used to take out buildings and eliminate full squads.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vehicle Flamer
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Type:** Flamethrower
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits
- **Capacity:** 50
- **Range:** 20 meters
