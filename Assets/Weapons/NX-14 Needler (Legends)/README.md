# NX-14 Needler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [NX-14 Needler/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/NX-14_Needler/Legends)

The NX-14 Needler was a type of needler designed to penetrate personal energy shields by firing a sharp, ferroceramic dart. The dart could also be laced with contact poison, delivering the poison to its targets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** NX-14 Needler
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Model:** NX-14
- **Type:** Needler
- **Cost:** 650 credits
- **Size:** Palm-sized
- **Material:** Fibermesh polycarbonate
- **Range:** Short
- **Purpose:**
  - To penetrate personal energy shields
  - To poison targets
