# C-5B plasburst cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C-5B plasburst cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-5B_plasburst_cannon)

The C-5B plasburst cannon was a model of plasburst laser cannon that could be equipped to starships. The canon would charge up and launch a powerful laser. Equipping an EML-850 light freighter with a C-5B plasburst cannon cost seven hundred and fifty credits and required ten units of quadranium, and four 4XB Calculation Units.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C-5B plasburst cannon
- **Model:** C-5B
- **Type:** Plasburst laser cannon
- **Cost:** 750 credits
