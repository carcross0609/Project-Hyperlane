# Charlin Plaka's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Charlin Plaka's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charlin_Plaka%27s_lightsaber)

Charlin Plaka's lightsaber was a lightsaber used by the Twi'lek Charlin Plaka as she served the Jedi Order as a Padawan and Knight during the Republic Era. The weapon's hilt made of shining chrome. After she killed Plaka, the Inquisitor Iskat Akaris took the Jedi's lightsaber with her as proof that she was worthy of the title Thirteenth Sister.
