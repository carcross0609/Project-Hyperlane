# 17kv antipersonnel twin blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [17kv antipersonnel twin blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/17kv_antipersonnel_twin_blaster)

The 17kv antipersonnel twin blaster was a type of twin blaster cannon equipped onto the Multi-Troop Transports of the Trade Federation and its private droid army. Following the outbreak of the Clone Wars, in which the Multi-Troop Transport found its way into the droid armies of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the 17kv antipersonnel twin blaster was used by the Confederacy during the war.
