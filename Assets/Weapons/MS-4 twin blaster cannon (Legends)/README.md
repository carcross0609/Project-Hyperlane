# MS-4 twin blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MS-4 twin blaster cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MS-4_twin_blaster_cannon/Legends)

The MS-4 twin blaster cannon was the primary weapon of the Galactic Empire's AT-ST walker. Manufactured by Taim & Bak, the blaster cannon was powerful enough to blast apart trees and destroy other walkers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MS-4 twin blaster cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
- **Type:** Blaster cannon
- **Range:** 2 kilometers
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
