# Matty's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Matty's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Matty%27s_lightsaber)

Matty's lightsaber was a blue-bladed lightsaber wielded by the the Jedi Padawan Matthea Cathley during the High Republic Era. The saber was used by Cathley both during the Battle of Jedha and during the Battle of Dalna.
