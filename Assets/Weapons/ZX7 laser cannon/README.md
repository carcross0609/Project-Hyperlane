# ZX7 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ZX7 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZX7_laser_cannon)

The ZX7 laser cannon was a model of medium laser cannon. Botajef Shipyards-produced SS-54 assault ships, such as the Halo, were equipped with twin-mounted ZX7 cannons hanging below their cockpits.

- **Name:** ZX7 laser cannon
