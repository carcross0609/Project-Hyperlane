# T-345

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-345](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-345)

The T-345 was a type of blaster wielded by a Gran hunter during the Great Hunt of Malastare in 3 ABY. During the hunt, the Gran was killed by the Trandoshan bounty hunter Bossk who wished to use the blaster, but discarded it after realizing that the blaster's trigger was too small for his fingers.
