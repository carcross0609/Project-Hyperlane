# V-59 concussion grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V-59 concussion grenade/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-59_concussion_grenade/Legends)

The V-59 Concussion grenade was a type of grenade-sized sapper charge often used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War. After rappelling up to and cutting through its belly armor with his lightsaber, Luke Skywalker used one of these to destroy Blizzard 4 during the Battle of Hoth.
