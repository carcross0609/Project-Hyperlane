# Savage's spear

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Savage's spear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Savage%27s_spear)

Savage's spear was a spear owned by the Zabrak Sith assassin Savage Opress. It was said to be enchanted by Nightsister magick. He swung and spun this weapon with ease, using both the pike on the top of the weapon and the spear on the bottom. Savage also used it to kill Jedi Master Halsey and Padawan Knox during the massacre at the Temple of Eedit on the planet Devaron. However, this weapon would be superseded by Savage's lightsaber, another double-bladed weapon.
