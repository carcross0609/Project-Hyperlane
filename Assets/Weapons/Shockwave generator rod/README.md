# Shockwave generator rod

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shockwave generator rod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shockwave_generator_rod)

Shockwave generator rods were weapons mounted on Basilisk war droids. A cluster of rods were mounted in the hose of the war droid. When activated, the rods combined their charges to release a plasma burst capable of puncturing a starship's hull.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shockwave generator rod
- **Type:** Plasma weapon
