# Verpine shatter pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Verpine shatter pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verpine_shatter_pistol)

The Verpine shatter pistol was a variant of the shatter gun created by the insectoid Verpine. The weapon was designed to be light and easy to wield, and while it dealt less damage than the rifle variants, it costed significantly less at 15,000 credits and still packed a punch.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Verpine shatter pistol
- **Type:** Shatter gun
- **Creators:** Verpine
- **Cost:** 15,000 credits
