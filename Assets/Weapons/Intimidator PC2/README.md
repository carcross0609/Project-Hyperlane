# Intimidator PC2

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Intimidator PC2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Intimidator_PC2)

The Intimidator PC2, also known as the PC2 pulse wave light cannon was a pulse-wave cannon manufactured by Greff-Timms Industrial. A two-man weapon, it cost 7,000 credits, had an ammunition capacity of 100 shots, and a range of 300 meters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Intimidator PC2
- **Manufacturer:** Greff-Timms Industrial
- **Type:** Pulse-wave cannon
- **Cost:** 7,000 credits
- **Crew:** 2
- **Capacity:** 100
- **Range:** 300 meters
