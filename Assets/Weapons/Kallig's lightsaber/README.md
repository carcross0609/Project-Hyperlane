# Kallig's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kallig's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kallig%27s_lightsaber)

Kallig's lightsaber was the weapon used by the ancient Sith Lord Aloysius Kallig, a contemporary of Tulak Hord. After the Sith Lord's demise, the lightsaber was entrusted to the Escalus family. Those wardens had to keep the weapon until an heir of Aloysius Kallig came to recover it.
