# Bowcaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bowcaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bowcaster/Legends)

The bowcaster (sometimes called the Wookiee Crossbow) was a Wookiee projectile weapon that fired a metal quarrel enveloped in energy. Some bowcasters were modified to fire pure energy. Some variants had a capacity of twenty-four shots.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bowcaster
- **Manufacturer:** Wookiees
- **Type:** Exotic weapon
- **Culture:** Wookiees
- **Cost:**
  - 900 to 1500 credits for an off-worlder
  - Wookiees received theirs as free gifts
- **Shape:** Crossbow
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 8 kg
- **Capacity:** From 6 shots per magazine to 35 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 50 m
  - Maximum with kthysh replacement bowstring: up to 30 m
- **Markings:** Often decorated with clan markings
