# Hutt Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hutt Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt_Applicator)

The Hutt Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This otherwise generic-looking gray, white, and tan applicator featured a large blade attached to the bottom of the barrel.
