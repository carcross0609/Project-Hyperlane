# Kith Kark's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kith Kark's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kith_Kark%27s_lightsaber)

Kith Kark's lightsaber was a lightsaber owned by the Jedi Kith Kark. It was lost on Onderon following Kark's death. About 370 years later, it was considered a valuable historical artifact, being recovered and taken to Nar Shaddaa.
