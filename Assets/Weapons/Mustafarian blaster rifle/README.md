# Mustafarian blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mustafarian blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mustafarian_blaster_rifle)

The Mustafarian blaster rifle was a model of blaster rifle carried by the Confederacy of Independent Systems' Northern Mustafarian guards on the planet Mustafar. Members of Kkkt's clan also utilized this weapon during a scouting mission.
