# Echani foil

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Echani foil](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_foil)

The Echani foil was a sword crafted to honor Raskta Fenni, the greatest Echani duelist of her time. Many of these weapons were sold but the difficulties in the lightsaber-resistant cortosis weave allowed only a few to survive.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Echani foil
- **Type:** Vibrosword
- **Culture:** Echani
