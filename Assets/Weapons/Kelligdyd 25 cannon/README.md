# Kelligdyd 25 cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kelligdyd 25 cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kelligdyd_25_cannon)

The Kelligdyd 25 cannon was an artillery piece used during the Republic Dark Age, the last century of the New Sith Wars. It was used by mercenary factions like Brigadier Jarrow Rusher's Rusher's Brigade. Kelligdyd 25 cannons were attached to the troopship Diligences cargo ramps and could be quickly deployed in battle. They saw action during the Battle of Gazzari and the Battle of Byllura.
