# Super Blaster 920 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Super Blaster 920 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Blaster_920_laser_cannon)

The Super Blaster 920 laser cannon was a model of laser cannon capable of destroying a starfighter with a single shot. The Galactic Empire's Death Star was equipped with 10,000 such weapons, which were all linked to a tactical computer system throughout the battle station, and had a faster recharge rate than most cannons. Each cannon was manned by one standing gunner, a seated targeting technician, and a seated energy technician. The gunners were stationed in pressurized chambers with open ports that were protected by invisible force fields.
