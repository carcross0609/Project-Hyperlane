# Plo Koon's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Plo Koon's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plo_Koon%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

Plo Koon's lightsaber was the lightsaber constructed and used by the Kel Dor Jedi Master Plo Koon during the decades leading up to his death in 19 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Plo Koon's lightsaber
- **Model:** Lightsaber
- **Type:** Single-blade
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Creator:** Plo Koon
- **Created:** before 32 BBY
- **Owners:** Plo Koon
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical, straight
- **Hilt Material:** Alloy metal
- **Color:**
  - Orange (19.43 BBY)
  - Yellow (32 BBY)
  - Blue (22 BBY)
  - Green (20 BBY)
- **Design:** Based on Lacunas Subartuk's lightsaber
- **Markings:** Blue radiator grooves
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jedi Order
  - Galactic Republic
