# TU-B4 laser turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TU-B4 laser turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TU-B4_laser_turret)

A TU-B4 laser turret was a model of laser turret that could be equipped to starships. By 3 ABY, the Jawa Teeka had salvage a TU-B4 laser turret and stored it inside their sandcrawler on Tatooine, a desert planet in the Outer Rim Territories.
