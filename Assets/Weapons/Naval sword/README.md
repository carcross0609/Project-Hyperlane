# Naval sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Naval sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Naval_sword)

A Naval sword, also known as a ceremonial sword, was a ceremonial weapon used by the Galactic Empire. Between 19 BBY and 9 BBY, the Imperial General Irrv was in charge of the Imperial occupation of the planet Culroon III and planned a ceremony to arrange the transition to Imperial control with the planet's leader, the Culroon Kloff. The ceremony would involve the exchange of the Naval sword and a ceremonial blaster to symbolize the peaceful transition of power.
