# Pirate Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pirate Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pirate_Clubs)

The Pirate Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They resembled crude axes with handles made of branches, containing metal joints and covered in decorative red ribbon wrappings.
