# Yarael Poof's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Yarael Poof's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yarael_Poof%27s_lightsaber)

A blue-bladed lightsaber was wielded by the Quermian Jedi Master of the Jedi Order, Yarael Poof. It was powered by a kyber crystal. Poof wielded his lightsaber with significant dexterity—thanks to his spineless anatomy—and expert-level skill, though the height disparity between him and opponents rendered him impartial in resorting to its use.
