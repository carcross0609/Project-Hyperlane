# R-9 flash canister

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-9 flash canister](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-9_flash_canister)

The R-9 flash canister was a flash canister used in the training of Jedi apprentices. This explosive device would cause dozens of tiny shells within it to burst out and detonate in a flash of bright light, temporarily blinding those within the 4-meter blast radius.

## Specifications

- **Name:** R-9 flash canister
- **Type:** Grenade
- **Cost:** 100 credits
- **Shape:** Canister-shaped
- **Size:** Hand-held
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg
- **Capacity:** Single-use
- **Range:**
  - Thrown weapon range
  - 4-meter blast radius
- **Purpose:**
  - To create a blinding flash of light
  - Jedi apprentice combat training
- **Affiliation:** Jedi
