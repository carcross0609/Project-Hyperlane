# Spraystick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spraystick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spraystick)

The spraystick was a handheld weapon that saw use during the time of the Galactic Civil War. The weapon was capable of lethally spraying a chemical at the target. The spraystick was given the designation of a rifle though it resembled a lightsaber hilt, both in size and in shape.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spraystick
- **Range:**
  - 30 meters (optimal)
  - 64 meters (maximum)
