# Second item

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Second item](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_item)

The second item, also known as the second Starflash, was a superweapon similar to the previous Starflash superweapon that the Chiss Ascendancy used in a final battle against an unknown enemy around 4300 BBY. The second Starflash superweapon was acquired by the Stybla family around 285 BBY, and was unknown to almost all Chiss.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Second item
- **Owners:**
  - Unknown alien species
  - Stybla family
- **Size:** Small enough to be loaded into a ship, but large enough to be operated by 20 people
- **Purpose:** To send a massive blast of energy at a star, which would then cause an even larger blast back
- **Affiliation:** Chiss Ascendancy
