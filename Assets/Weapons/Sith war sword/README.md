# Sith war sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith war sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_war_sword)

The Sith war sword was a melee weapon used by Sith and Sith minions during the Dark Wars. Their double-edged staff-like appearance may have been the forebearer of the double-bladed lightsabers preferred by many Sith warriors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sith war sword
- **Type:** Vibro double-blade
- **Material:** Cortosis
- **Purpose:** Melee combat
- **Affiliation:** Sith
