# Agen Kolar's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Agen Kolar's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agen_Kolar%27s_lightsaber)

The lightsaber wielded by the Zabrak Jedi Master Agen Kolar had the particularity of using dual kyber crystals, which allowed the color of the plasma blade to be either green or blue. The second kyber crystal was from the lightsaber of his Padawan, Tan Yuster, who was killed at the First Battle of Geonosis. One time he used the weapon was when he died at the hands of Darth Sidious alongside fellow masters Saesee Tiin, Kit Fisto, and Mace Windu. It appeared to be the same design as Adi Gallia's second lightsaber.
