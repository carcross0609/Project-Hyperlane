# Fourth Sister's double-bladed spinning lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fourth Sister's double-bladed spinning lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Sister%27s_double-bladed_spinning_lightsaber)

The double-bladed spinning lightsaber was wielded by the Inquisitor known as the Fourth Sister. It was similar in appearance to the double-bladed spinning lightsabers wielded by other Imperial Inquisitors. She utilized the lightsaber during her mission to capture Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi, on Daiyu. The lightsaber was abandoned in a cave.
