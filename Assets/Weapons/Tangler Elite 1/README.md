# Tangler Elite 1

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tangler Elite 1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tangler_Elite_1)

The Tangler Elite 1 was a tangle gun which was popular among bounty hunters because it could be attached to personal battle armor. The gun fired a triple strand of durawire with weighted ends.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tangler Elite 1
- **Manufacturer:** Salus Corporation
- **Type:** Tangle gun
- **Cost:** 900 credits
- **Capacity:** 5
- **Range:** 60 meters
