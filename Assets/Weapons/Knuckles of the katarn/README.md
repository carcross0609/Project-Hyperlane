# Knuckles of the katarn

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Knuckles of the katarn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Knuckles_of_the_katarn)

The knuckles of the katarn was a Wookiee melee weapon made from the claws of a katarn, a savage predator from the planet Kashyyyk. Custom-made by Krepauk—the Huntmaster of the Rryatt Trail on Kashyyyk—the knuckles of the katarn was once given to a spacer who had killed a katarn during a hunt shortly after the Battle of Yavin.
