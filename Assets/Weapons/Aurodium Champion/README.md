# Aurodium Champion

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aurodium Champion](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurodium_Champion)

The Aurodium Champion was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle featured a gold, silver, and gray frame, with a golden illustration of the Hypernova on the stock.
