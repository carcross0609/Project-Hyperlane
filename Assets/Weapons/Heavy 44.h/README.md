# Heavy 44.h

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy 44.h](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_44.h)

The Heavy 44.h was a model of double laser cannon manufactured by Atgar SpaceDefense Corporation. It was designed for anti-vehicle use. Several were installed in armored turrets at Fortress Keremark.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy 44.h
- **Manufacturer:** Atgar SpaceDefense Corporation
- **Type:** Heavy anti-vehicle laser cannon
- **Locations:** Fortress Keremark
- **Cost:**
  - 35,000 credits (new)
  - 18,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 4
- **Capacity:** Unlimited (power generator)
- **Range:** Ten kilometers
