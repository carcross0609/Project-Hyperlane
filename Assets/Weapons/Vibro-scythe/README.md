# Vibro-scythe

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vibro-scythe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vibro-scythe)

The vibro-scythe was a curved vibro weapon used by Vicrul, a member of the Knights of Ren, who saw himself as a harvester or reaper of the fallen. The blade of Vicrul's scythe was forged from annealed phrik.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vibro-scythe
- **Type:** Vibro weapon
- **Material:** Annealed phrik
- **Affiliation:** Knights of Ren
