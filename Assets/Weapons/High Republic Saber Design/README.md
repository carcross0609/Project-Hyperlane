# High Republic Saber Design

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [High Republic Saber Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Republic_Saber_Design)

The High Republic Saber Design was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber. It featured the white and gold color scheme commonly associated with the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era.

## Specifications

- **Name:** High Republic Saber Design
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
