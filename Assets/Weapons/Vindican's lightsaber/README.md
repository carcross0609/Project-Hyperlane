# Vindican's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vindican's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vindican%27s_lightsaber)

The Sith Lord Vindican owned a double-bladed lightsaber during the Great Galactic War. He wielded this saberstaff when he fought with his apprentice Darth Malgus against the Jedi Master Kao Cen Darach and his Padawan Satele Shan during the Fall of Korriban. After falling in battle, his apprentice forced the lightsaber from Vindican's hand and utilized it, to lethal effect, against the Jedi Battlemaster Darach of the Jedi Order.
