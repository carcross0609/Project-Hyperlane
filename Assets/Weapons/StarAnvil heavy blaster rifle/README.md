# StarAnvil heavy blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [StarAnvil heavy blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/StarAnvil_heavy_blaster_rifle)

The StarAnvil was designed as the ultimate blaster rifle. It took the scope technology of a sporting blaster and combined it with the power of a long blaster to create a nasty and powerful heavy blaster rifle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** StarAnvil heavy blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Munitions
- **Model:** StarAnvil
- **Type:** Heavy blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 2,000 to 5,000 credits
- **Size:** Large
- **Weight:** 9 kg
- **Capacity:** 50 shots
- **Range:** 500 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
