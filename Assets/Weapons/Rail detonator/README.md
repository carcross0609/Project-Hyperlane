# Rail detonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Rail detonator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rail_detonator)

A rail detonator or rail detonator gun was a rifle-shaped weapon that used powerful electromagnets to launch rail charges or explosive canisters at a target at incredible speed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rail detonator
- **Type:** Explosive projectile launcher
- **Cost:**
  - 1,900 credits (Available for military use only)
  - Magazine of 10 explosive canisters costs 50 credits
- **Shape:** Rifle-like
- **Size:** Large
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 10 shots
  - Collateral damage to adjacent targets
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 meters
  - Maximum: up to 450 meters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial jumptroopers
  - Kota's Militia
