# Heartbreaker

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heartbreaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heartbreaker)

The Heartbreaker was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. The slugthrower featured a gold, silver, gray, and blue coloration, with ornate plating and a golden skull-like attachment around the end of the barrel. It also had a pouch around stock for holding extra ammunition.
