# Kaminoan great staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kaminoan great staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaminoan_great_staff)

The Kaminoan great staff was a melee weapon in the form of a staff polearm used by the Kaminoans to detain clones during the Clone Wars, but saw combat usage during the time of the Galactic Civil War.
