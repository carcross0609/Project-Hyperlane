# Alliance disruptor

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Alliance disruptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance_disruptor)

The Alliance disruptor was a type of energy-based disruptor pistol commonly used by medics. It was was standard issued to members of the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the time of the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Alliance disruptor
- **Type:** Disruptor pistol
- **Size:** Handheld
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
