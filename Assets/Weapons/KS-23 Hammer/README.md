# KS-23 Hammer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KS-23 Hammer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KS-23_Hammer)

The KS-23, unofficially known as the Hammer, was a model of slugthrower produced by Czerka Arms during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. The weapons fired explosive pellets over a short range and were primarily used by big-game hunters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KS-23 Hammer
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Model:** KS-23
- **Type:** Slugthrower
- **Cost:** 1500 credits
- **Capacity:** 5 pellets
- **Range:** Short
