# PLX-1

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PLX-1/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PLX-1/Legends)

The Merr-Sonn PLX-1, or Plex, was a powerful missile launcher that was in service almost from the beginning of the Clone Wars and continued to be used in different militaries well after the conclusion of that cataclysmic conflict.

## Specifications

- **Name:** PLX-1
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** PLX-1
- **Type:** Portable Missile Launcher
- **Capacity:** 2 Missiles
- **Range:** 40 kilometers
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Garos Resistance
