# Armor-piercing grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Armor-piercing grenade/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armor-piercing_grenade/Legends)

Armor-piercing grenades, also known as AP grenades, were a type of grenades available during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Anti-Material grenades and Model 9 armor piercing grenades were both types of AP grenades manufactured by Merr-Sonn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Armor-piercing grenade
- **Type:** Grenade
- **Cost:** 100 credits
- **Capacity:** Single use
- **Range:** Thrown weapon
