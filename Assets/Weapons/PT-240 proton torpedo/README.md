# PT-240 proton torpedo

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PT-240 proton torpedo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PT-240_proton_torpedo)

A PT-240 proton torpedo was a model of proton torpedo that could be equipped to starships. Equipping an EML-850 light freighter with PT-240 proton torpedoes cost two thousand and five hundred credits and required a X-FP-C28 ship computer, twenty five units of quadanium steel, and twelve enhanced durasteel hulls.

- **Model:** PT-240
- **Type:** Proton torpedo
