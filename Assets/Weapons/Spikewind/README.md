# Spikewind

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spikewind](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spikewind)

The spikewind was a trap used by Jedi Master Kirak Infil'a and his droid, Arex. When newly-christened Sith Lord Darth Vader hunted Infil'a on the river moon of Al'doleem, the Jedi and Arex set many traps for Vader, including the spikewind. However, when the Sith overcame the obstacles that preceded the spikewind, Infil'a decided to fight Vader in lightsaber combat, and he told Arex to disable the remaining traps.
