# Penetrator MB-450

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Penetrator MB-450](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Penetrator_MB-450)

The Penetrator MB-450, also colloquially called a Luxan Penetrator, was a high powered sporting blaster designed by Luxan Personal Armaments, Ltd. A Luxan Penetrator was the favored weapon of the Ithorian bounty hunter and gunslinger Bulduga.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Penetrator MB-450
- **Manufacturer:** Luxan Personal Armaments, Ltd.
- **Type:** Sporting blaster
- **Cost:** 550—600 credits (50 credits per power cell)
- **Weight:** 1 kilogram
- **Capacity:** 100 shots
- **Range:** 60 meters
