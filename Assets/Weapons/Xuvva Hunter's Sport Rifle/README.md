# Xuvva Hunter's Sport Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Xuvva Hunter's Sport Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xuvva_Hunter%27s_Sport_Rifle)

Xuvva Hunter's Sport Rifle was a make of sporting blaster rifle available during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. During the war, the Imperial Intelligence agent codenamed Cipher Nine acquired one while in the palace of the Hutt crime lord Suudaa Nem'ro on the planet Nal Hutta. Selling for 25 credits, parts of the black-colored blaster rifle could be modified, including its color crystal. The weapons were designed for use hunting Xuvva, a species of winged predators native to Nal Hutta.
