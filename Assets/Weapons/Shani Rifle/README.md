# Shani Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shani Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shani_Rifle)

The Shani Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle consisted of a black, gray, and red frame, with a glowing yellow core, splashes of blue, pink, and yellow paint covering a portion of the top, green strips of cloth circling the grip, and additional multicolored cloth bound to the barrel, resembling the general aesthetic commonly showcased by Shani bounty hunter Deva Lompop.
