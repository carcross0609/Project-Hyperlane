# Clan Eldar MT-97

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clan Eldar MT-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_Eldar_MT-97)

The Clan Eldar MT-97 was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. Though part of Clan Tal himself, Aran brandished these weapons out of respect for members of Clan Eldar, displaying their lion sigil on the grips of each pistol.
