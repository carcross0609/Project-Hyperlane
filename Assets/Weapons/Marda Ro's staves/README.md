# Marda Ro's staves

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Marda Ro's staves](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marda_Ro%27s_staves)

A pair of staves wrapped in white mynock leather came into the possession of the Evereni pirate leader Marda Ro between 382 BBY and around 381 BBY. Around the latter year, she drew one of the staves to threaten the Twi'lek Fori Nagor in the street of a settlement on the planet Dantooine after Nagor had been following Ro. At some point no earlier than 379 BBY, Ro used the staves while sparring with the Evereni Vika Faer. She later mentioned the two encounters where she used her staves in a recording she made for her descendants, which her great-great-grandson Marchion Ro had listened to by 252 BBY.
