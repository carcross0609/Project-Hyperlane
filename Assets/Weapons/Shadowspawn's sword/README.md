# Shadowspawn's sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadowspawn's sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadowspawn%27s_sword)

The character of Lord Shadowspawn created by Lord Cronal owned and used a sword imbued with Sith alchemy. Shadowspawn led the Legions of Shadow and his physical presence was often portrayed through a Pawn.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shadowspawn's sword
- **Owners:**
  - Cronal
  - Nick Rostu
- **Material:** Meltmassif
- **Length:** Half a hand longer than Luke's lightsaber.
- **Width:** A hand wide.
- **Affiliation:** Legions of Shadow
