# Tulrus sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tulrus sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tulrus_sword)

A tulrus sword was, as its name suggested, a type of two-handed sword that was made from the body of a tulrus, a species native to the planet Mustafar. They appeared to be constructed from one of the many spikes that protruded from the creature's body. Despite their size, the swords were known to be well balanced, as if they had been made out of a lighter material.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tulrus sword
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Locations:** Mustafar
- **Material:** Parts of a Tulrus
