# Marauder sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Marauder sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marauder_sword)

The Marauder sword was a type of sword which was used by the Sanyassan Marauders during the time of the Galactic Civil War. This heavy sword was capable of inflicting terrible wounds. The Sanyassan Marauders known as Terak one during his in his dual against Noa Briqualon during the skirmish known as the Battle of Endor.
