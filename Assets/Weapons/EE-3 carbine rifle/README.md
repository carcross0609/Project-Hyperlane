# EE-3 carbine rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [EE-3 carbine rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EE-3_carbine_rifle)

The EE-3 carbine rifle was a model of blaster carbine manufactured by BlasTech Industries. The most notorious user of the EE-3 was the bounty hunter Boba Fett during and after the Galactic Civil War, with the weapon being considered his signature weapon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** EE-3 carbine rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** EE-3
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Cost:** 1,500 credits
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Range:** Medium
- **Affiliation:**
  - Jabba's criminal empire
  - Galactic Empire
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Fett gotra
  - Children of the Watch
