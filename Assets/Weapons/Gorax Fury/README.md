# Gorax Fury

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gorax Fury](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gorax_Fury)

The Gorax Fury was an E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon owned by the human Hunter Sentinel that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard E-Web.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gorax Fury
- **Model:** E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon (Sentinel's E-Web)
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Owners:** Sentinel
- **Affiliation:**
