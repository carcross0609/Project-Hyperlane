# Huntsman vibrospear

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Huntsman vibrospear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huntsman_vibrospear)

The Huntsman vibrospear was a model of two-handed vibrospear produced by LaserHone. Costing 950 credits, the two-meter-long weapon was created for use in the hunting of large animals. It was designed to be quiet and to vibrate at frequencies that most animals could not hear. The spear was able to block incoming attacks and was able to use its steel alloy blade to cut through steel and stone.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Huntsman vibrospear
- **Manufacturer:** LaserHone
- **Model:** Huntsman
- **Type:** Vibrospear
- **Cost:** 950 credits
- **Material:** Steel alloy
- **Length:** Over two meters
