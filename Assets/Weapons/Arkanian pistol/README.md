# Arkanian pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arkanian pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arkanian_pistol)

The Arkanian pistol was a blaster pistol that was made by the Arkanians during the time of the Jedi Civil War. A variant of it was the Arkanian heavy pistol.
