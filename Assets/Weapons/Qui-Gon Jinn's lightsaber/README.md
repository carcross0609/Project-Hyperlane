# Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Qui-Gon_Jinn%27s_lightsaber)

Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber was a standard single-bladed lightsaber wielded by the human Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn. Jinn used this lightsaber until the Battle of Naboo, where he was mortally wounded by the Sith Lord Darth Maul. Qui-Gon's apprentice, Obi-Wan Kenobi, then used the same lightsaber to defeat Maul after losing his own. After the battle, the weapon was later returned to the Jedi Temple. With the advent of the Great Jedi Purge, the blade again fell into the possession of Kenobi, who went into hiding on the planet Tatooine. At one point, Kenobi treaded through the desert with his master's lightsaber and that of his former apprentice, Anakin Skywalker, attached to his cloak.
