# Lightclub

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lightclub](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightclub)

The lightclub, also known as a great lightsaber or greatsaber, was a weapon similar to a lightsaber. However, special focusing crystals and power systems enabled this rare lightsaber to project a blade up to three meters in length. These large lightsabers were generally used only by beings of immense stature. Gorc, a mutated Gamorrean Dark Jedi, used such a weapon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lightclub
- **Manufacturer:** Self-made by owners
- **Model:** Custom-made weapon
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Size:** Varied; 5 kg on average
- **Affiliation:** Force users in general
