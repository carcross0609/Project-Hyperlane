# Exchange negotiator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Exchange negotiator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exchange_negotiator)

Exchange negotiators were a type of energy baton used by the criminal organization known as the Exchange. They were presumably used to "negotiate" prices with buyers, or perhaps the name was just a cover-up of the weapon's real use. The negotiator delivered a more potent shock than other stun batons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Exchange negotiator
- **Type:** Stun baton
- **Affiliation:** Exchange
