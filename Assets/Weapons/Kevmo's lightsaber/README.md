# Kevmo's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kevmo's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kevmo%27s_lightsaber)

Kevmo's lightsaber was a yellow-bladed lightsaber with a red gold-plated hilt owned by Jedi Padawan Kevmo Zink during the High Republic Era. After Kevmo's death in the Path of the Open Hand compound, Marda Ro kept the lightsaber. It was damaged during the Battle of Jedha by a wargaran, which caused the blade to become jagged and spluttering. The saber eventually ceased functioning after Marda used it to kill one of the Nameless.
