# ZX miniature flame projector

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ZX miniature flame projector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZX_miniature_flame_projector)

The ZX miniature flame projector, also known as the ZX miniature flamethrower, was a miniature flamethrower often attached to the gauntlet of its user. Bounty hunter Jango Fett used one of these devices, which would cast fire in an arc five-meters long and 1 meter in diameter. Fett's son, Boba used the same type of flamethrower to escape from the Sarlacc after the death of Jabba Desilijic Tiure and the collapse of his criminal empire and to fight the Mandalorian warrior Koska Reeves, and his former mentor Cad Bane. Commander Gar Saxon of the Shadow Collective Mandalorian super commandos utilized one during the Siege of Mandalore against Lady Bo-Katan Kryze of the Mandalore resistance.
