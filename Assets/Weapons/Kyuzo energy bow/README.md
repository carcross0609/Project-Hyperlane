# Kyuzo energy bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kyuzo energy bow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyuzo_energy_bow)

The Kyuzo energy bow was a massive energy bow that fired terrifyingly large, charged bolts. Though designed to be fired from a mount or mobile platform, the skilled Kyuzo archers of the Gyaryi clovoc were able to use their considerable might to draw the bows and their elite training to hit targets at incredible distance. The Kyuzo energy bow cost 2,400 credits.
