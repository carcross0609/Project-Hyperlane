# Thermal detonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thermal detonator/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thermal_detonator/Legends)

A thermal detonator, also known as a thermal grenade, was a highly unstable grenade-like weapon popular among military personnel, criminals, bounty hunters, and mercenaries. The thermal detonator was utilized since at least as early as 4000 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thermal detonator
- **Manufacturer:**
  - BlasTech Industries
  - Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
  - Tarascii Explosives
- **Type:** Nuclear Fusion Explosive
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits
- **Shape:** Spherical
- **Size:** Handheld
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:** 1 detonation
- **Range:**
  - Thrown weapon range (up to 20 meters)
  - 6-meter blast radius
- **Affiliation:** Various
