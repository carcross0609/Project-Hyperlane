# Urai Fen's blades

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Urai Fen's blades](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Urai_Fen%27s_blades)

The Talortai Urai Fen wielded two large blades that allowed him to cut through infantry like grass. The weapons could also bypass the shields of vehicles and physically damage them.
