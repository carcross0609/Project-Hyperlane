# Concussion disc

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Concussion disc](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Concussion_disc)

A concussion disc was a type of concussion grenade carried by the assassin droid IG-88. A hollowed space in cyborg bounty hunter Beilert Valance's right forearm allowed space for potential upgrades, including the possibility of a concussion disc launcher.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Concussion disc
- **Type:** Concussion grenade
