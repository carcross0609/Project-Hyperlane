# Diiro

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Diiro/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Diiro/Legends)

A diiro was a weapon commonly used by the Sathari of the planet Arbooine. The weapons consisted of a hollow alloy blade at the end of a wooden handle. The blade was often filled with poison.
