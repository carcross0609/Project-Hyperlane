# Freedom Three

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Freedom Three](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Freedom_Three)

The Freedom Three was a model of heavy blaster rifle manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. The Stormtrooper Three was issued to Imperial desert stormtroopers during the Galactic Civil War. Originally designated as the Stormtrooper Three, the weapons were later rebranded by SoroSuub.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Freedom Three
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Heavy blaster rifle
