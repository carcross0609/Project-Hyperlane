# Ren (lightsaber)

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ren (lightsaber)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ren_%28lightsaber%29)

The Ren was a red-bladed lightsaber wielded by a Force-sensitive human male, also known as Ren, who gained the lightsaber and his name from his predecessor. The Ren served as a symbol of leadership of the Knights of Ren, a band of marauders that used the dark side of the Force. It was discarded on the Minemoon after Ren's death by the Jedi Ben Solo, who wielded a red-bladed crossguard lightsaber after taking control of the Knights as Kylo Ren.
