# V-1 Thermal Detonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V-1 Thermal Detonator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-1_Thermal_Detonator)

The V-1 Thermal Detonator was a type of thermal detonator, a powerful, grenade-like explosive device that could be carried in one hand. It had a spherical gray shell, with small ridges along its surface. At one point, a V-1 Thermal Detonator was submerged underwater, in a location surrounded by algae.

## Specifications

- **Name:** V-1 Thermal Detonator
- **Type:** Thermal detonator
- **Shape:** Spherical
