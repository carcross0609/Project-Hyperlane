# Argazdan riot buster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Argazdan riot buster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Argazdan_riot_buster)

The Argazdan Riot Buster was a powerful type of sonic rifle that was used by the Argazdan during their rule over the Lorrdians as a part of the Kanz Disorders; at this time in history, non-lethal technology was a profitable commodity. This gun knocked its targets over and stunned them; it had a range of twenty-eight meters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Argazdan riot buster
- **Manufacturer:** Argazdans
- **Type:** Sonic rifle
- **Range:** 28 meters
- **Affiliation:** Argazdan Redoubt
