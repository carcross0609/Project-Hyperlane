# Discord missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Discord missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Discord_missile)

Discord missiles, also known as buzz droid missiles were jet-powered missiles carried by droid tri-fighters, vulture droids, and droid gunships that contained up to seven Pistoeka sabotage droids. Once fired, they dodged their way through enemy defenses with maneuvering thrusters until they reached their target and hatched open, releasing a swarm of buzz droids that used their mechanical arms and cutting tools to dismantle enemy ships and droids and inflict as much damage as possible.
