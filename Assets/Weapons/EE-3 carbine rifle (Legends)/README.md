# EE-3 carbine rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EE-3 carbine rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EE-3_carbine_rifle/Legends)

The EE-3 carbine rifle was a blaster that was modeled after the E-11 blaster rifle later used by Imperial stormtroopers. The rifle consisted of a small handle attached to a long circular barrel. At least two versions of the EE-3 were known to exist, with one featuring a hand grip covering the length of the barrel.

- **Model:** EE-3
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
