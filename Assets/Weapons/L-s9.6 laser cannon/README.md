# L-s9.6 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L-s9.6 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L-s9.6_laser_cannon)

The L-s9.6 laser cannon was a model of laser cannon produced by Sienar-Jaemus Fleet Systems. It was the standard armament for Sienar-Jaemus's TIE/fo space superiority fighter, which was equipped with two black L-s9.6 cannons, one on either side of the cockpit's underside. It was also used on the TIE/sf space superiority fighter. The vehicles and their weaponry were heavily used by the First Order in their conflict with the Resistance around thirty years after the Battle of Endor.
