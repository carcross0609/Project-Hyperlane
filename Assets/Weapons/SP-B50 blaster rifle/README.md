# SP-B50 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SP-B50 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SP-B50_blaster_rifle)

The SP-B50 blaster rifle was utilized by the Sovereign Protectors of the Sith Eternal in protection of their leader, the Dark Lord of the Sith, Darth Sidious. They were ceremonial weapons which mixed a blaster and a vibro-active force blade together. In 35 ABY, they were used against the Jedi Rey when she and the former Kylo Ren, Ben Solo, turned on Sidious. The pair successfully killed all members of the Sovereign Protectors, ending the usage of their rifles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SP-B50 blaster rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle/vibroblade hybrid
- **Locations:** Sith Citadel, Exegol
- **Shape:** Two-pronged blaster
