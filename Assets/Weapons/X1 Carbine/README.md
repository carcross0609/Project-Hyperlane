# X1 Carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [X1 Carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X1_Carbine)

The X1 Carbine, also known as the Wookiee disruptor rifle, was a disruptor rifle utilized by Wookiee sharpshooters and Chieftain Merumeru during the Battle of Kashyyyk. Its circular grip design came from the tree-lined villages in the southern-hemisphere of Kashyyyk.
