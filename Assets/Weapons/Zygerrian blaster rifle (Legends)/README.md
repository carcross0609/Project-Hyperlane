# Zygerrian blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zygerrian blaster rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zygerrian_blaster_rifle/Legends)

The Zygerrian blaster rifle was a model of blaster rifle used by the Zygerrian slavers during the Clone Wars. It was one of the two favored weapons of the Zygerrians, the other being the shock whip. On one setting, the blaster rifle was capable of generating a large explosion on contact with solid objects. The Ohnaka Gang also made use of these weapons.
