# Ripper (weapon)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ripper (weapon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ripper_%28weapon%29)

The ripper was an Old Republic weapon designed to bypass personal energy shields by firing physical projectiles. The ripper fired many pieces of shrapnel at a target, in a similar manner to the flechette weapons later used during the Galactic Civil War. The shrapnel fired by the ripper penetrated deep into the target's body, causing additional damage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ripper
- **Type:** Shrapnel-shooting pistol
- **Cost:** 750 credits
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 10 shots
  - Additional embedded shrapnel damage
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 meters
  - Maximum: up to 90 meters
- **Purpose:** To bypass personal energy shields
