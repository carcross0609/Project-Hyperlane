# Cere Junda's second lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cere Junda's second lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cere_Junda%27s_second_lightsaber)

Sometime during the five years that Cere Junda was separated from the Stinger Mantis crew, she constructed a new blue-bladed lightsaber to replace her previous one from which she'd sold the kyber crystal to help pay off Greez Dritus' debts with the Haxion Brood and given the hilt to Cal Kestis for him to create a new lightsaber with its parts.
