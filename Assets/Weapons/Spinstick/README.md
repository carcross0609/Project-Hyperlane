# Spinstick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spinstick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spinstick)

Spinsticks were a type of weapon that were viewed by some individuals as esoteric by the year 19 BBY. The weapons were utilized by some members of the Force-sensitive Gray Paladins—an offshoot of the Teepo Paladins—who were themselves a part of the Jedi Order. The Gray Paladins used weapons such as spinsticks to avoid relying on the Force or lightsabers, the traditional weapons of the Jedi.
