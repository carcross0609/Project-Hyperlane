# Rancor Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rancor Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rancor_Applicator)

The Rancor Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This ornate applicator included a dark gray, yellow, and gold metal frame, with various elements of bone, potentially from a rancor, attached. This included most of the stock, the back of the body, and both on top and below the barrel.
