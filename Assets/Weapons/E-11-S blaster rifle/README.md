# E-11/S blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-11/S blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-11/S_blaster_rifle)

The E-11/S blaster rifle was a BlasTech blaster rifle which was custom made and had a very limited market. Combining E-11 blaster technology with Slugthrower technology, the E-11/S was primarily used by assassins, terrorists, and other criminals. It had a selection switch which allowed it to either fire the blaster, the slugthrower, or both at the same time. It could be adjusted to change the simultaneous firing time of both weapons from a tenth of a second to several seconds.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-11/S blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Modified blaster rifle/slugthrower combination
- **Cost:** 7,000 credits
- **Capacity:**
  - 25 (blaster)
  - 6 (slugthrower)
