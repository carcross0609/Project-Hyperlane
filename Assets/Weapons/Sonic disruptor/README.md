# Sonic disruptor

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sonic disruptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_disruptor)

A sonic disruptor was a disruptor weapon used in the years following the Great Sith War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sonic disruptor
- **Type:** Disruptor pistol
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits (Illegal)
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 10 shots
  - Cannot be deflected by a lightsaber
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 meters
  - Maximum: up to 120 meters
- **Purpose:** To disintegrate a target
