# Officer's Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Officer's Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Officer%27s_Blaster)

The Officer's Blaster was a modified A-180 heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This worn blaster pistol featured a black, silver, and white frames, with a vented barrel, side-mounted scope, and blue and yellow painted elements, including a blue Rebel Alliance starbird displayed on the side of the grip.
