# HOB heavy repeating blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HOB heavy repeating blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HOB_heavy_repeating_blaster/Legends)

The HOB heavy repeating blaster, also known as the High-Output repeating blaster, was a model of heavy repeating blaster produced by SoroSuub Corporation and available in the period following the Battle of Yavin during the Galactic Civil War between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire. Mostly used by military organizations, the weapon was mounted on a tripod and required two crew to operate while deployed as an emplaced weapon, although it was also possible for an individual of extreme strength to carry the weapon. Due to its robust, reliable nature and simple construction the HOB was fairly easy to deploy and take down or repair in the field. The weapon was considered powerful and had a high fire rate and energy draw, costing 6500 credits to purchase.
