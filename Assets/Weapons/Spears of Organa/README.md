# Spears of Organa

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spears of Organa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spears_of_Organa)

The Spears of Organa were an array of defensive turrets built on the planet Alderaan by House Organa. The three turrets were built into the bedrock of the mountains near Castle Organa. While not up to weapon standards at the time, the Spears were still more powerful than any contemporary walker.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spears of Organa
- **Manufacturer:** House Organa
- **Type:** Defensive turrets
- **Creators:** House Organa
- **Locations:** Apalis Coast, Alderaan
- **Purpose:** Defense of Castle Organa
- **Affiliation:** House Organa
