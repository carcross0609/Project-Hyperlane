# Heavy assault blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy assault blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_assault_blaster_rifle)

The heavy assault blaster rifle was a large blaster rifle. It was designed to give a single person enough firepower to take out a whole squad of enemies. It was restricted to military use under Imperial law.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy assault blaster rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 3,000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 7 kg
- **Capacity:** 50 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 225 m
