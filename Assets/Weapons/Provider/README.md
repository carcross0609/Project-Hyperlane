# Provider

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Provider](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Provider)

The Provider was a slugthrower rifle produced by Barimoq Industries. With a range of one hundred and fifty meters and a capacity of four slugs, the rifle was traditionally used by the Tasari for hunting.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Provider
- **Manufacturer:** Barimoq Industries
- **Type:** Slugthrower rifle
- **Culture:** Tasari
- **Cost:** 450 credits
- **Capacity:** 4 slugs
- **Range:** 150 meters
