# Anti-Material grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Anti-Material grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-Material_grenade)

Anti-Material grenades were a type of armor-piercing grenades available during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. They were produced by Merr-Sonn and cost 100 credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Anti-Material grenade
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Armor-piercing grenade
- **Cost:** 100 credits
- **Capacity:** Single use
- **Range:** Thrown weapon
