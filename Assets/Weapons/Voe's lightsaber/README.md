# Voe's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Voe's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Voe%27s_lightsaber)

A green-bladed lightsaber belonged to Voe, a Force-sensitive female human who joined the Jedi Order under the tutelage of Jedi Master Luke Skywalker. Voe used this lightsaber before it was destroyed during her hunt for Ben Solo following the destruction of the Jedi Temple. She replaced it with the lightsaber of her friend Hennix, who was killed by Solo during a conflict on Elphrona.
