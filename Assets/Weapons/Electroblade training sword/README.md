# Electroblade training sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Electroblade training sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electroblade_training_sword)

Electroblade training swords, or electroblades, were a type of sword whose tapered blades could deliver a nasty shock and were able to deflect blaster bolts, though only to a limited extent. The Jedi Order used them as training weapons, along with wooden staves. During the High Republic Era, the Jedi Order utilized DD-1 hover droids equipped with electroblade training swords to train Jedi Initiates at the Tenoo Jedi Temple.
