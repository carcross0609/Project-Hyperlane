# Han's heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Han's heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Han%27s_heavy_blaster_pistol)

Han's heavy blaster pistol was the modified heavy blaster pistol owned by Tobias Beckett before being given to the future Rebel Alliance General and then smuggler Han Solo. The heavy blaster pistol was a DL-44 model manufactured by BlasTech Industries and, despite newer models having come and gone, Solo preferred only his own modified DL-44 as his primary weapon. Solo carried the weapon with him since before the Galactic Civil War between the Rebel Alliance and the Galactic Empire, and had it by his side at the time of his death on Starkiller Base over 30 years later.
