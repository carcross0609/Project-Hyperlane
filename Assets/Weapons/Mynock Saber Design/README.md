# Mynock Saber Design

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mynock Saber Design](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mynock_Saber_Design)

The Mynock Saber Design was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber. The hilt appeared to display some damage, exposing the central cylinder of the saber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mynock Saber Design
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
