# Stakes Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stakes Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stakes_Slugthrower)

The Stakes Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This dark gray and gold slugthrower included a large scope and red and white painted patterns.
