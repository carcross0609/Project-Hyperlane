# Contact stunner

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Contact stunner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Contact_stunner)

A contact stunner was a small personal protection device. It had a dozen contact disks on one end that ensured it would deliver a concentrated jolt of energy. It operated on a single energy cell. It was easy to conceal due to its slender and compact design.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Contact stunner
- **Type:** Stun weapon
- **Cost:** 700 credits
- **Size:** Hand-held weapon
- **Weight:** 1.1 kg
- **Purpose:** Self-defense
