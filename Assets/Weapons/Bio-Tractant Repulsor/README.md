# Bio-Tractant Repulsor

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bio-Tractant Repulsor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bio-Tractant_Repulsor)

The Bio-Tractant Repulsor was a personal defense weapon manufactured by Brisallon Chemical Consolidated. It was a chemical weapon that caused a sense of calm, peace, or confusion on the affected victim.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bio-Tractant Repulsor
- **Manufacturer:** Brisallon Chemical Consolidated
- **Type:** Anti-personnel weapon
- **Cost:** 200 credits
- **Capacity:** 6
- **Range:** 5 meters
