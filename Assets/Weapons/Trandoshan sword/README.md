# Trandoshan sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trandoshan sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan_sword)

While most cultures abandoned the primitive sword in favor of vibroblades or other weapons, the Trandoshan sometimes used swords as a badge of honor. This variant was made of the rare ore Chalon, and was sharper and heavier than the typical blade. Trandoshan swords were not suitable for weapon upgrades.
