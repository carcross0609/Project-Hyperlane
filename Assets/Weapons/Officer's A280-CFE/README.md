# Officer's A280-CFE

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Officer's A280-CFE](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Officer%27s_A280-CFE)

The Officer's A280-CFE was a modified A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This blaster pistol featured a silver and gray frame, with red and yellow painted elements.
