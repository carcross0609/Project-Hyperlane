# Jannah's energy bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Jannah's energy bow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jannah%27s_energy_bow)

Jannah's energy bow was a custom-built energy bow used by the human female Jannah during the war between the First Order and the Resistance. Jannah made several of these bows and distributed them to the heads of Company 77, which promptly distinguished them as a symbol of leadership.
