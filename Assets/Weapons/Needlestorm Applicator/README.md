# Needlestorm Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Needlestorm Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Needlestorm_Applicator)

The Needlestorm Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. It featured a glowing pink core, silver, black, and orange plating, and a rectangular guard around the barrel with pink paint splatters towards the end.
