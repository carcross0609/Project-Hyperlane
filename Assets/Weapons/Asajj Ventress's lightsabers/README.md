# Asajj Ventress's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Asajj Ventress's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Asajj_Ventress%27s_lightsabers)

Asajj Ventress's lightsabers were a pair of red-bladed curved-hilts used by Sith assassin Asajj Ventress. The lightsabers joined as a split saber, as they could be wielded separately or joined together to form a unique S-shaped double-bladed lightsaber.
