# Donderbus blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Donderbus blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Donderbus_blaster_pistol)

The Donderbus blaster pistol was a model of blaster pistol favored by members of the Kanjiklub. However, members of the criminal organization often preferred rifles due to the rifles' superior firepower. As a result, this was not the primary weapon of choice for the members of Kanjiklub who confronted Han Solo in 34 ABY.
