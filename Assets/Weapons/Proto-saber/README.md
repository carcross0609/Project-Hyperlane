# Proto-saber

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Proto-saber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proto-saber)

The proto-saber, known to the Mustafarians as the laser sword, was a type of ancient prototype lightsaber locked away in an Imperial base near Fortress Vader on Mustafar. In 1 BBY, members of the Rebel Alliance undertook a mission to Mustafar, and discovered the proto-saber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Proto-saber
- **Model:** Lightsaber
- **Type:** Prototype lightsaber
- **Discovered:** 1 BBY
- **Locations:** Imperial base, Mustafar
