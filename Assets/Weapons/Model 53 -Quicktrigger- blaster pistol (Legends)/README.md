# Model 53 "Quicktrigger" blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Model 53 "Quicktrigger" blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_53_%22Quicktrigger%22_blaster_pistol/Legends)

The Model 53 “Quicktrigger” blaster pistol was a blaster pistol produced by Merr-Sonn, popular among those living on the fringes of society. While it was not the most powerful blaster available, the Quicktrigger could easily be customized with various upgrades and attachments to meet the specific needs of its users. Thanks to its straightforward design and sturdy construction, the Model 53 was easy to maintain and repair, and it rarely incurred more than minor damage from continuous use.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model 53 "Quicktrigger" blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 450 credits
- **Range:** Medium-ranged
