# Breacher One

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Breacher One](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Breacher_One)

Breacher One was a breacher missile launcher used by the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet Nightdragon man-of-war Vigilant. In 18 BBY, the warship's technicians had trouble fixing it after it was damaged, but they were ultimately successful. Senior Captain Kiwu'tro'owmis, core name "Wutroow," reported Breacher One's return to status to the ship's commanding officer, Admiral Ar'alani, who commended the repair team on their work. Shortly after, during a battle, Ar'alani ordered Junior Captain Evroes'ky'mormi "Oeskym" to disable the thrusters in Breacher One so they could drop the missiles into space without firing them.
