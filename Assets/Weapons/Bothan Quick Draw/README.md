# Bothan Quick Draw

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bothan Quick Draw](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothan_Quick_Draw)

The Bothan Quick Draw was a fast firing weapon designed to kill off an opponent before they had the chance to react. Used 4,000 years before the Galactic Civil War, these hold-out blasters were used to kill a target without causing serious damage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bothan Quick Draw
- **Type:** Hold-out blaster
- **Culture:** Bothan
