# Steward's Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Steward's Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Steward%27s_Saber_Pattern)

The Steward's Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the droid Hunter J-3DI that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Steward's Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** J-3DI
- **Color:** Blue
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
