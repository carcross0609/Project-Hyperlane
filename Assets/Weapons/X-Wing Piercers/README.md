# X-Wing Piercers

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [X-Wing Piercers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-Wing_Piercers)

The X-Wing Piercers was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. These pistols were designed to resemble the X-wing starfighters utilized by pilots of the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War.
