# Ion rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ion rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ion_rifle)

An ion rifle was a type of blaster rifle that sent ion energies. A weapon capable of disabling droids, ion rifles were among the arsenal of Captain Brutus' crew during the New Republic Era. Around 9 ABY, the crew utilized ion rifles twice to disable the pirate droid SM-33, first on Borgo Prime and then on Lanupa.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ion rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Culture:** Space pirates
- **Shape:** Three barrels
- **Purpose:** Disabling droids
- **Affiliation:** Captain Brutus' crew
