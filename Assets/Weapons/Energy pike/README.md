# Energy pike

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Energy pike](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Energy_pike)

The energy pike was a two meter long melee weapon with a blade at one end. It had an energy sheath and the blade vibrated during combat. The energy pike was the traditional weapon wielded by S'krrr warriors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Energy pike
- **Culture:** S'krrr
- **Locations:** S'krrr
- **Length:** 2 meters
