# Lightning gloves

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lightning gloves](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_gloves)

Lightning gloves were a rare type of glove in use during the Dark Wars, a series of conflicts fought between the remnants of the Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic. The gloves produced strong electrical pulses when they came in contact with another object, a property which provided the user an advantage in unarmed combat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lightning gloves
- **Type:** Gloves
- **Created:** By 3951 BBY
- **Cost:** 29,600 credits
- **Range:** Unarmed combat
- **Purpose:**
  - Delivering damaging electrical pulses in unarmed combat
  - Protecting against electricity
