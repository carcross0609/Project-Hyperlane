# Scout trooper blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Scout trooper blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scout_trooper_blaster_pistol/Legends)

This hold-out blaster pistol, sometimes known as a scout blaster but commonly referred to as a scout trooper blaster pistol, was a standard-issue weapon used by the Imperial Navy commandos and scout troopers of the Galactic Empire. Clone scout troopers of the 41st Elite Corps and Clone shadow troopers were also known to carry these weapons during the latter stages of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Scout trooper blaster pistol
- **Type:** Hold-out blaster
- **Range:** Short
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  - Corellian Security Force
