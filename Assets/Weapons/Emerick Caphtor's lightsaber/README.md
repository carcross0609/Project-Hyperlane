# Emerick Caphtor's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Emerick Caphtor's lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emerick_Caphtor%27s_lightsaber)

A lightsaber was used by the Jedi Master Emerick Caphtor. It had a yellow blade. The lightsaber was wielded by Caphtor during his investigation of Jedi Master Loden Greatstorm's death and the destruction of Starlight Beacon.
