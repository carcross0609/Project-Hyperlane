# Alema Rar's third lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Alema Rar's third lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alema_Rar%27s_third_lightsaber)

Alema Rar's third lightsaber was a single-bladed lightsaber constructed by ex-Jedi Alema Rar after her second lightsaber was confiscated by Jedi Grand Master Luke Skywalker on the Moon of Kr.
