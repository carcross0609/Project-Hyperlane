# Terentatek Saber Pattern

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Terentatek Saber Pattern](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Terentatek_Saber_Pattern)

The Terentatek Saber Pattern was a custom lightsaber hilt owned by the human Hunter Rieve that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard lightsaber. Its design was meant to resemble the appearance of a terentatek, armored creatures native to the planet Kashyyyk that fed on the blood of Force-sensitives.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Terentatek Saber Pattern
- **Type:** Single-bladed lightsaber
- **Owners:** Rieve
- **Color:** Red
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
