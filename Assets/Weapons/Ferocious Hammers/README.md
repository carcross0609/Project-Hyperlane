# Ferocious Hammers

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ferocious Hammers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ferocious_Hammers)

The Ferocious Hammers were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They resembled traditional Ferrixian hammers such as those wielded by the Time Grappler of Ferrix City on the planet Ferrix.
