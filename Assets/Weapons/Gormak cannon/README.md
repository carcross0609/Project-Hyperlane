# Gormak cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gormak cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gormak_cannon)

The Gormak cannon was a massive cannon constructed by the Gormak species in their city of Gorma-Koss on the orders of their king, Jokull. The weapon was aimed at the mountaintop city of Voss-Ka, the home of the planet Voss' other native species, the Voss.
