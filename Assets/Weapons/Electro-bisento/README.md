# Electro-bisento

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Electro-bisento](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electro-bisento)

The electro-bisento was a shaft with a blade placed on the top. It was wielded by the First Guard, one of eight Elite Praetorian Guards who served as the protectors of Supreme Leader Snoke of the First Order.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Electro-bisento
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Owners:** First Guard
- **Locations:** Snoke's throne room, Supremacy
- **Purpose:** Close combat
