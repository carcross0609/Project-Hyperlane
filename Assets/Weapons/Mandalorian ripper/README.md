# Mandalorian ripper

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mandalorian ripper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_ripper)

The Mandalorian ripper was an energy-propelled slugthrower pistol that was produced in great numbers during the Great Sith War. It was a common sidearm for Mandalorian officers during the Mandalorian Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mandalorian ripper
- **Manufacturer:** Mandalorians
- **Type:** Energy-propelled slugthrower pistol
- **Culture:** Mandalorians
- **Created:** circa 4000 BBY
- **Locations:**
  - Kashyyyk
  -   Shadowlands
- **Purpose:** To bypass personal energy shields
- **Affiliation:** Mandalorians
