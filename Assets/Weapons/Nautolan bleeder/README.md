# Nautolan bleeder

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nautolan bleeder](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nautolan_bleeder)

Nautolan bleeders were a type of ceremonial dagger forged by the ancestors of the Nautolan species. By 1 BBY, only four of the ancient daggers had been found. Axis network leader Luthen Rael had one of uncertain provenance in his gallery, Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest, on the planet Coruscant. He used it to stab himself when Imperial Security Bureau Supervisor Dedra Meero visited the gallery and revealed she knew he was a rebel.
