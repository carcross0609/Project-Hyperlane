# Zabrak Disruptor Cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zabrak Disruptor Cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak_Disruptor_Cannon)

The Zabrak Disruptor Cannon was a disruptor-type weapon that was created by the Zabrak race of Iridonia during the Great Sith War. They were large weapons and designed for heavy use in front-line combat.
