# Flamethrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flamethrower/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flamethrower/Legends)

A flamethrower or flame projector was a weapon which fired a burning stream of liquid or gas.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flamethrower
- **Type:** Flame projector
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Shape:** Resembles a bulbous blaster rifle
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 7 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 5 shots
- **Range:** A conical area 9 meters long and 9 meters wide at its terminus
- **Affiliation:**
