# Shoto lightsaber

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shoto lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shoto_lightsaber/Legends)

The shoto lightsaber or lightsaber shoto, commonly referred to as shotos, but also known as short lightsabers or lightdaggers, were essentially lightsabers with a shorter blade, whose purpose was primarily to serve as an off-hand weapon for those duelists seeking to employ the twin-bladed technique of Jar'Kai, but were also utilized by those Jedi and Sith whose stature made wielding full sized lightsabers impractical, or, in some cases, impossible.
