# Plasma mace

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Plasma mace](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plasma_mace)

A plasma mace was a type of weapon. The former Jedi Ty Yorrick wielded such a mace during the High Republic Era. Yorrick used the weapon while fighting in the Valo Atrocity and while fighting a Trilark alongside the help of Drewen Qweebjillan.
