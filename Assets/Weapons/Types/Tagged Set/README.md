# Tagged Set

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tagged Set](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tagged_Set)

The Tagged Set was the name given to a set of two weapons, an Arc Caster and a Pulse Blaster, utilized by the team of Jawa brothers known collectively as the Hunter Utooni that they could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to their standard set. These colorful yet weathered weapons featured a brown, gold, silver, dark gray, and white base color scheme, with an overlay of various pieces of graffitied artwork ranging from bright green, pink, yellow, cyan, blue, and white, displaying stars, arrows, splatters, and at least one Jawa face. Fellow Hunter Sprocket feared these weapons may have shot paint as a projectile despite being told they did not.
