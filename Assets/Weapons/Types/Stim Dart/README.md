# Stim Dart

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stim Dart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stim_Dart)

Stim Darts were a type of darts used by the Rodian Hunter Skora as she competed in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara. These stims were loaded into her specialized Dart Gun, and could be quickly swapped for poison darts due to a tricky mechanism on the gun, though these darts were fired at allies, injecting them with a unique stimulating concoction that healed them.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Stim Dart
- **Type:** Stim dart
- **Creators:** Skora
- **Locations:** Vespaara
- **Purpose:** Healing
