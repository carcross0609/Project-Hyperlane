# Seeker-torpedo

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Seeker-torpedo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seeker-torpedo)

A seeker-torpedo was a type of ground-to-air missile, used by the Beast Riders of Onderon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Seeker-torpedo
- **Type:** Homing missile
- **Culture:** Onderonian
- **Creators:** Beast Riders
- **Discovered:** 4000 BBY
- **Owners:** Commander Gobee
- **Locations:** Wilderness of Onderon, perimeter of Fortress Kira
- **Shape:** Six-barreled missile turret
- **Range:** Ground-to-air
- **Purpose:** Defend Fortress Kira from aerial attacks
- **Affiliation:** Beast Riders of Onderon
