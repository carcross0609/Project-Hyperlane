# Aurodium Bullseye

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Aurodium Bullseye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurodium_Bullseye)

The Aurodium Bullseye was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It was a valuable weapon to Diago who worked hard to obtain it.
