# H-7 "Equalizer" blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [H-7 "Equalizer" blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H-7_%22Equalizer%22_blaster_pistol)

The H-7 "Equalizer" blaster pistol was a type of blaster pistol manufactured by Coronet Arms, and was considered to have been a masterpiece of blaster pistol design. It was thought of as both a work and a functional weapon, and featured clean lines and bronzium plating, with a streamlined, tubiform aesthetic. The Equalizer teetered on the edge of a heavy blaster pistol classification, as a result of its accuracy, energy output, and space for aftermarket kits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** H-7 "Equalizer" blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Coronet Arms
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 1,200 credits
- **Range:** Medium-ranged
