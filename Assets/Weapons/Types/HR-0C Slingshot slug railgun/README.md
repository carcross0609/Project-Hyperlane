# HR-0C Slingshot slug railgun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HR-0C Slingshot slug railgun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HR-0C_Slingshot_slug_railgun)

The HR-0C Slingshot slug railgun was a type of slug rail gun produced by Balmorran Arms during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. A relatively simple rail gun, the Slingshot had incredible range and fired metal projectiles that partially bypassed a target's shield, dealing severe damage to enemy starfighters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HR-0C Slingshot slug railgun
- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
- **Type:** Projectile rail gun
