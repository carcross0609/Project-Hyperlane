# PLX-2M portable missile launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PLX-2M portable missile launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PLX-2M_portable_missile_launcher)

The PLX-2M portable missile launcher, sometimes referred to as the Plex-Twoem, or Plex, was a shoulder-mounted missile launcher produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. and based on their earlier PLX-1 model. The sheer explosive power of the PLX-2M made it dangerous to fire blindly in close quarters, but was extremely effective when used as a 'smart' tracking weapon. Once locked on to a target, the PLX-2M's missiles had a range of 50 kilometers.
