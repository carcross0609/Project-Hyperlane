# E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-HOB_heavy_repeating_laser_cannon)

E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon was a heavy repeating blaster cannon. It was based on the Imperial Era E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon and acted as its successor, with Moff Gideon identifying it as part of the overall "E-Web" series. An Imperial remnant led by Gideon utilized the blaster on Nevarro in 9 ABY. During the battle Din Djarin took control of the E-HOB cannon until Gideon caused it to explode with his D-16 blaster pistol. The cannon was able to be utilized while detached from its turret body. Another E-HOB was used on Tython where it was destroyed by Boba Fett's DB-41 sporting blaster pistol.
