# Fleet Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fleet Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fleet_Blaster)

The Fleet Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This blaster pistol resembled a DH-17 blaster pistol like those commonly utilized by some members of the Rebel Alliance and New Republic.
