# ATA Pulse-wave Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ATA Pulse-wave Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ATA_Pulse-wave_Blaster)

The ATA Pulse-wave Blaster was a pulse-wave blaster pistol produced by Greff-Timms Industrial and belonged to category of ancient blasters. It fired devastating pockets of coherent energy, but had lackluster range and no option to stun enemies.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ATA Pulse-wave Blaster
- **Manufacturer:** Greff-Timms Industrial
- **Type:** Pulse-wave blaster
- **Cost:** 600 credits
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 1 kilogram
- **Capacity:** 50
- **Range:** 100 meters
