# Mustafar bandit sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mustafar bandit sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mustafar_bandit_sword)

The Mustafar bandit sword was a bladed weapon that was crafted and used by the criminals on the lava world of Mustafar. The base of the sword was cut into jagged triangles, likely to allow the wielder to better damage their enemy in close quarter fighting. The blade itself was not very lengthy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mustafar bandit sword
- **Type:** Sword
- **Locations:** Mustafar
- **Purpose:** Melee combat
- **Affiliation:** Mustafarian bandits
