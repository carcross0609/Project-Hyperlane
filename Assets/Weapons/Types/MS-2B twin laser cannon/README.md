# MS-2B twin laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MS-2B twin laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MS-2B_twin_laser_cannon)

The MS-2B twin laser cannon was a model of laser cannon produced by the company Taim & Bak. The short-range VCX-series auxiliary starfighter Phantom and the modified VCX-100 light freighter Ghost were armed with MS-2Bs.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MS-2B twin laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
- **Type:** Laser cannon
