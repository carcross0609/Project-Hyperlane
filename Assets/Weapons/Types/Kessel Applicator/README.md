# Kessel Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kessel Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kessel_Applicator)

The Kessel Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This worn applicator featured a black, white, and silver base, with yellow and gray wear and tear, red patterns, and a blocky end of the barrel.
