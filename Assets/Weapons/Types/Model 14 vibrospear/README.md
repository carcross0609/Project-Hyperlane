# Model 14 vibrospear

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Model 14 vibrospear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_14_vibrospear)

The Model 14 vibrospear, sometimes known as the "Stalker," was a two-meter-long plasteel vibrospear that was designed for hunting large animals. The two-handed weapon was created by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. in response to LaserHone's Huntsman vibrospear, and although it was cheaper, it was much noisier, meaning that prey could be alerted by its vibrations.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model 14 vibrospear
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Vibrospear
- **Material:** Plasteel
- **Length:** 2 meters
