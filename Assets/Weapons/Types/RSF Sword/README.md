# RSF Sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RSF Sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RSF_Sword)

The RSF Sword was a single-handed, bladed melee weapon commonly used by fencers during the time of the Galactic Civil War. As indicated by its name, the sword was affiliated with the Royal Security Forces of Naboo, otherwise known as the RSF. Despite being decorated with insignias of the Galactic Empire, the weapon was actually most often carried by members of the Alliance to Restore the Republic. A B1-series battle droid named Colonel OR-5 also possessed a variation of the weapon known as "OR-5's Sword."
