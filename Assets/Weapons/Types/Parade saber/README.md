# Parade saber

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Parade saber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Parade_saber)

A parade saber, also known as a ceremonial officer's saber, was a type of sword utilized by the Galactic Empire. They were worn by Imperial officers for ceremonial purposes, and were not meant to be used for warfare. Han Solo, a former Imperial lieutenant, once dreamed that he was publicly read out of the military as a cadet. The ceremony involved another officer snapping the ceremonial saber—the blade of which had been weakened by a laser score in advance—over their knee.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Saber
- **Range:** Melee weapon
- **Purpose:** Ceremonial
