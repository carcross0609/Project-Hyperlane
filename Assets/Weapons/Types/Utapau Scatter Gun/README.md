# Utapau Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Utapau Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Utapau_Scatter_Gun)

The Utapau Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. A pair of curved blades flanked the barrels of this gun.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Utapau Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
