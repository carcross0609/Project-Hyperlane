# Ultra Turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ultra Turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ultra_Turret)

Ultra Turrets were a type of custom automated defensive emplacement designed by the Mon Calamari Hunter Sprocket for use in the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition hosted on the planet Vespaara. These autoturrets were considered Sprocket's ultimate defense within the Grand Arena, and could target multiple enemies with a rapid succession of blaster fire. They could only operate for a short time after activation, and once their time had expired, they would self-destruct. Sprocket could also deploy a smaller version of this turret, which were less durable and could only target one individual at a time.
