# Saber

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Saber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saber)

A saber was a type of sword favored by pirates. However, many pirates wore them mainly for show, although they were fully capable of being used as weapons. Sabers were a type of rapier, often made of flexisteel and used in the sport of fencing.
