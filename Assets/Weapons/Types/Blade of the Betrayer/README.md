# Blade of the Betrayer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blade of the Betrayer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blade_of_the_Betrayer)

The Blade of the Betrayer was a single-handed blade owned by the female Near-Human Dark Jedi Vansk, who served as a member of the Force-using organization known as the Blackguard during the Galactic Civil War. Vansk did not often use the blade in combat, preferring instead to use her red-bladed lightsaber, but she carried the blade as a secondary weapon. The blade was taken from Vansk after her death in 1 ABY at the hands of spacers, when a Sullustan archaeologist named Mi Fon Lu hired a group of spacers to kill all the Blackguard in the Southern Jedi Ruins on the planet Mustafar, as he wished to research in the area.
