# Vera Beroya's mythosaur axe

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vera Beroya's mythosaur axe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vera_Beroya%27s_mythosaur_axe)

During the Imperial Era, the Mandalorian chieftain Vera Beroya of Clan Awaud wielded a massive mythosaur axe that previously belonged to her father, Nam Beroya. In combat, Beroya was known to switch to her mythosaur axe after expending the ammunition of her SHUKUR-05 light repeater.
