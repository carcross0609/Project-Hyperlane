# M9 "Boomer" heavy pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M9 "Boomer" heavy pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M9_%22Boomer%22_heavy_pistol)

The M9 "Boomer" heavy pistol, also known colloquially as the "hand cannon," was a model of slugthrower pistol manufactured by Czerka Arms. It was designed to cram as much killing power as possible into as small a package as possible, and became a standout in the heavy pistol market. The weapon was thick and squat with a large caliber and sold for one thousand credits.
