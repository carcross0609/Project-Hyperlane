# LJ-40 concussion carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LJ-40 concussion carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LJ-40_concussion_carbine)

The LJ-40 concussion carbine was a model of short-range blaster carbine produced by Arakyd Industries and available during the Galactic Civil War. It was originally created as a modification of the LJ-50 concussion rifle, then put into production by Arakyd after they learned of its undeniable effectiveness at breaching walls and scattering enemy forces.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LJ-40 concussion carbine
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Model:** LJ-40
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Cost:** 2,500 credits

  - Scattering enemy troops
