# Narglatch Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Narglatch Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Narglatch_Slugthrower)

The Narglatch Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This dark blue and gray slugthrower featured a narglatch decal, as well as yellow markings and the letters "OPN" in aurebesh upside down on the stock.
