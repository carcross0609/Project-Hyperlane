# Anti-orbital cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Anti-orbital cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-orbital_cannon)

The Anti-orbital cannon was a weapon emplacement constructed by the Confederacy of Independent Systems that was used against orbiting starships, particularly capital ships. They saw use on the planet Geonosis at the beginning of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Anti-orbital cannon
- **Locations:** Geonosis
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
