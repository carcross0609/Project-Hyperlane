# Baze's repeating cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Baze's repeating cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baze%27s_repeating_cannon)

Baze's repeating cannon was an MWC-35c "Staccato Lightning" repeating cannon used by the freelance assassin and Rebel soldier Baze Malbus. It weighed 30 kilograms and was scarlet and silver in color. The weapon contained the firepower of five laser rifles in one and could hold up to 35,000 blaster shots. Its ability to fire both quickly and accurately led to the name "Staccato Lightning."
