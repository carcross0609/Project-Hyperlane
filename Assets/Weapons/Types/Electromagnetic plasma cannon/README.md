# Electromagnetic plasma cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Electromagnetic plasma cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electromagnetic_plasma_cannon)

The electromagnetic plasma cannon was a cannon utilized by the Umbaran Militia on its mobile heavy cannon, hover tank, and Zenuas 33 Umbaran starfighter. They were noted for their power and destructive ability and were a unique technology that the clone troopers of the Grand Army of the Republic had never encountered before.
