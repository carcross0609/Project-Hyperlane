# COMPNOR stun blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [COMPNOR stun blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/COMPNOR_stun_blaster)

The COMPNOR stun blaster was a non-lethal weapon developed for the elite members of COMPNOR SAGroup.The weapon was symbolic, meant to ensure them of their importance and to win their loyalty.

## Specifications

- **Name:** COMPNOR stun blaster
- **Type:** Stun blaster
- **Cost:** 300 credits
- **Capacity:** 10 shots
- **Affiliation:** COMPNOR
