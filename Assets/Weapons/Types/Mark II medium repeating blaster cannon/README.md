# Mark II medium repeating blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mark II medium repeating blaster cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_II_medium_repeating_blaster_cannon)

The Mark II medium repeating blaster cannon was a blaster cannon manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc., and was one of the weapons used to defend the Rebel Alliance's Echo Base from invading Imperial forces during the Battle of Hoth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark II medium repeating blaster cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** Mark II
- **Type:** Medium repeating blaster
- **Weight:** 23 kg (50.7 lb)
- **Range:** 400 m (1312 feet)
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
