# Grievance Striker

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grievance Striker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grievance_Striker)

The "Grievance Striker," also known as the "Annihilator," was a custom DT-57 "Annihilator" blaster owned by General Grievous, the Supreme Commander of the Separatist Droid Army during the Clone Wars. During the battle of Utapau, Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi shot Grievous with the Grievance Striker, ironically killing the cyborg owner and general with his own weapon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grievance Striker
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** Modified DT-57 "Annihilator" blaster
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Owners:** General Grievous
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
