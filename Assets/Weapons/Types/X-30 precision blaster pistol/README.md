# X-30 precision blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [X-30 precision blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-30_precision_blaster_pistol)

The X-30 precision blaster pistol was a type of blaster pistol manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation, and was used for recreational shooting throughout the galaxy. The X-30 was adequate in terms of its accuracy and rate of fire, and its energy output was light, even for a target blaster. However, competition shooters were fond of how easy the X-30 was to customize. Scopes, upgraded Galven-pattern barrels, hair triggers, and a slew of aftermarket kits were available from SoroSuub to custom fit an X-30 to any need.

## Specifications

- **Name:** X-30 precision blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits
- **Range:** Long-ranged
