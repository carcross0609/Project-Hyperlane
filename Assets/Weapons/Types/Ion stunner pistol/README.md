# Ion stunner pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ion stunner pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ion_stunner_pistol)

An ion stunner pistol, also known as an ion flare gun, was a small weapon, identifiable by a focusing dish on its barrel. When operated, it would fire a short ion flash capable of temporarily disrupting electronic devices. This made it particularly adept at disabling security doors and droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ion stunner pistol
- **Locations:** Coruscant
- **Purpose:** To shut down electrical devices
