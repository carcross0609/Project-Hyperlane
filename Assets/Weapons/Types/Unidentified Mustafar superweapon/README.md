# Unidentified Mustafar superweapon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified Mustafar superweapon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_Mustafar_superweapon)

A superweapon capable of releasing a deadly virus to the galaxy. By the time of the Clone Wars it had been buried on Mustafar. After learning of the existence of the weapon, General Grievous planned to use the weapon in a plan to rebel against Count Dooku. EG-07 a battle droid employed by Grievous located the weapon on Mustafar. By the time the Jedi Order had learned of Grievous's plans and tracked the weapon to Mustafar, Grievous had drilled down to the weapon and placed a battlesphere in Mustafar's atmosphere. Grievous planned to use the battlesphere's lasers to charge the weapon's long depleted power source.
