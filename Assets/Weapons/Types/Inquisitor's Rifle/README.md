# Inquisitor's Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Inquisitor's Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitor%27s_Rifle)

The Inquisitor's Rifle was a two-handed blaster rifle used by Imara Vex, a contestant of the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition. Atop the blaster sat a half of an emitter ring, a distinct feature of the double-bladed spinning lightsabers utilized by the Galactic Empire's Inquisitorius. Vex's weapon was black and gray, which matched Vex's Imperial Inquisitor costume. The rifle fired red blaster bolts. In 9 ABY, Vex used the Inquisitor's Rifle for ranged combat while participating in the Hunters of the Outer Rim on the planet Vespaara, though she normally used a customized A280 blaster rifle, the Firestorm Rifle.
