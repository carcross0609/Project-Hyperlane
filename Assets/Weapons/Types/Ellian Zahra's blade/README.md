# Ellian Zahra's blade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ellian Zahra's blade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ellian_Zahra%27s_blade)

A blade was owned by and the favorite weapon of Commander Ellian Zahra of the Galactic Empire. Zahra intended to use the blade to kill Princess Leia Organa of the Rebel Alliance, who Zahra held responsible for the death of her mentor Governor Wilhuff Tarkin, during the destruction of the Death Star.
