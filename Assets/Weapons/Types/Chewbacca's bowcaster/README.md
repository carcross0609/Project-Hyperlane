# Chewbacca's bowcaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chewbacca's bowcaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chewbacca%27s_bowcaster)

Chewbacca's bowcaster was a traditionally hand-crafted bowcaster weapon belonging to the Wookiee Chewbacca. Chewbacca built the bowcaster sometime prior to using it during the Battle of Kashyyyk in the Clone Wars, in which the Wookiees of Kashyyyk and the Galactic Republic made a joint effort against the Separatist Alliance. After Chewbacca pledged a life debt to Han Solo and joined the smuggler, Chewbacca continued to use his bowcaster throughout the Galactic Civil War between the Rebel Alliance and the Galactic Empire, having crafted several bowcasters until then.
