# SH-9 slugthrower pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SH-9 slugthrower pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SH-9_slugthrower_pistol/Legends)

The SH-9 slugthrower pistol, also known as the whisper pistol, was a magazine fed slugthrower pistol with an inbuilt silencer manufactured by Czerka Arms. The weapon was favored to covert ops for silent takedowns due to its ability to pierce laminate armors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SH-9 slugthrower pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Type:** Slugthrower pistol
- **Cost:** 250 credits
- **Capacity:** 5—10 Slugs
- **Range:** 60 meters
