# War cloud

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [War cloud](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/War_cloud)

The war cloud, also known as fog of war, was a Nihil gas weapon capable of knocking individuals unconscious and disabling starship systems. War clouds could be launched from gas torpedoes to puncture ship hulls. Nihil masks protected the marauders from the gas' effects.

## Specifications

- **Name:** War cloud
- **Type:** Weaponized gas
- **Creators:** Zadina Mkampa
- **Purpose:**
  - Harming respiration
  - Disabling starship electronics
- **Affiliation:** Nihil
