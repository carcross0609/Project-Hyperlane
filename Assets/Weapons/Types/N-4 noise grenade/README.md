# N-4 noise grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [N-4 noise grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N-4_noise_grenade)

The N-4 noise grenade was a type of grenade manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. for crowd dispersal and riot control. When detonated, the N-4 emitted a sharp blast of broad-spectrum sound, causing intense pain to those near the epicenter. While the sound only lasted for a few seconds, nearby targets experienced lingering effects for long periods of time afterwards, which resulted in some covering their ears or running from the grenade unless they were wearing a helmet equipped with appropriate reactive hearing protection. The N-4 was also favorited by burglars, who utilized them to discourage pursuits if chased, while bounty hunters used them to disable security forces around well-protected targets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** N-4 noise grenade
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Grenade
- **Cost:** 150 credits
- **Purpose:** Crowd dispersal and riot control
