# Mandalorian assault rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mandalorian assault rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_assault_rifle)

Mandalorian assault rifles were powerful weapons. They were so powerful that they were considered almost overpowered for their size. However, this wouldn't bother their Mandalorian users who, in general, were not known for subtlety during war. The weapon was short and had a thumb through stock with a small scope. This design was further modified by Czerka Corporation to create an Assault blaster rifle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mandalorian assault rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Mandalorians
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Capacity:** 500 shots
- **Range:**
  - 130 meters (optimum)
  - 300 meters (maximum)
- **Affiliation:** Mandalorian Neo-Crusaders
