# Lightspeed torpedo

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lightspeed torpedo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightspeed_torpedo)

A lightspeed torpedo, also referred to as a Galaxy Gun Missile, was a type of missile that could travel through hyperspace. It was the main weapon of the Galaxy Gun and was used to carry the superweapon's payload through hyperspace to its targets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lightspeed torpedo
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Department of Military Research
- **Type:**
  - Missile
  - Torpedo
