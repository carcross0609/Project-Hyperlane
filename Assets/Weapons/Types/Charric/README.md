# Charric

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Charric](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charric)

A charric was a handheld weapon used by the Chiss Defense Force. It had at least two settings. Charric discharges were brighter and made a higher-pitched sound than Galactic Republic blasters. Mitth'ali'astov and Mitth'raw'nuruodo were both trained in the use of charrics.
