# Flame Carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flame Carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flame_Carbine)

Czerka Arms' Flame Carbine was a type of personal flamethrower. They generally cost around 500 credits apiece. The weapon was used by Alliance SpaceOps and Pathfinders, and was the favored weapon of choice for Urban Combat Specialists in street-fighting operations.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flame Carbine
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Type:** Flamethrower
- **Cost:** 500 credits
- **Capacity:** 10
- **Range:** 7 meters
