# Imperial heavy repeater

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial heavy repeater](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_heavy_repeater)

The Imperial heavy repeater was a type of weapon manufactured by BlasTech Industries and used by the Imperial Army. The repeater fired ionized bursts of metallic bolt at an incredibly rapid rate. At some point during the reign of the Galactic Empire, Masse Goskey and his associate Ephant Mon, who ran Masse Goskey's Arms Emporium and Explosives Mart in Mos Eisley spaceport on the planet Tatooine, purchased an Imperial heavy repeater for 2,700 credits. Mon recorded the transaction in the Smuggler's Guide, a book that was owned by various criminals, with each adding information to the guide.
