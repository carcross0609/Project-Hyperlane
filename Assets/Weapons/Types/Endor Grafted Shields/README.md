# Endor Grafted Shields

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Endor Grafted Shields](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endor_Grafted_Shields)

The Endor Grafted Shields were a pair of shields owned by the KX-series security droid Hunter K-A0S that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard Grafted Twin Shields. A depiction of the Battle of Endor was crudely scribbled across the shields, and a number of leaves, twigs, and arrows protruded from its surface.
