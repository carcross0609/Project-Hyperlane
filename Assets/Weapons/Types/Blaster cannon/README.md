# Blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blaster cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blaster_cannon)

A blaster cannon was a weapon that utilized gas to produce a visible short-range beam of intense energy. They were less powerful than laser cannons. While blaster cannons were commonly fitted to starships or ground vehicles like the Flash speeder, there also existed handheld models like the Z-6 rotary blaster cannon.
