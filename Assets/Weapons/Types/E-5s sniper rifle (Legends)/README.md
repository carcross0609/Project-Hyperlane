# E-5s sniper rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-5s sniper rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-5s_sniper_rifle/Legends)

The BAW E-5s sniper rifle was a four-shot rifle produced by Baktoid Armor Workshop and used by the Battle droid assassins of the Trade Federation and the Separatist Droid Army. When the rogue Geonosian Gizor Dellso reactivated an army, the sniper rifle was used by his battle droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-5s sniper rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Armor Workshop
- **Model:** E-5s
- **Type:** Sniper rifle
- **Capacity:** 4 shots
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Gizor Dellso's army
