# Blade-breaker

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blade-breaker/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blade-breaker/Legends)

A blade-breaker was a type of melee vibroblade used concurrently with a larger vibroweapon, such as a vibrosword. It was equipped with a powerful ultrasonic generator in the hilt designed to shatter the material of an opponent's weapon trapped in the blade's grasp.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blade-breaker
- **Type:** Vibroweapon
- **Cost:** 250 credits
- **Purpose:** Melee weapon
