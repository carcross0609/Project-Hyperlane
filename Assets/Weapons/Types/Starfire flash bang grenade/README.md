# Starfire flash bang grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Starfire flash bang grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starfire_flash_bang_grenade)

Starfire flash bang grenades were a type of flash-bang grenades available during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. They were produced by Imperial Arms and cost 75 credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Starfire flash bang grenade
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Arms
- **Model:** Starfire
- **Type:** Flash-bang grenade
- **Cost:** 75 credits
- **Capacity:** Single use
- **Range:** Thrown weapon
