# Vibro-voulge

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vibro-voulge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vibro-voulge)

The vibro-voulge was a staff with a axe-like blade placed on the top used by two of the First Order's Elite Praetorian Guard who served Supreme Leader Snoke aboard his flagship the Supremacy. They were high-tech weapons resistant to lightsaber blows.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vibro-voulge
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Owners:** Eighth Guard
- **Locations:** Snoke's throne room, Supremacy
- **Purpose:** Close combat
