# Model VX Vibro-greatsword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Model VX Vibro-greatsword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_VX_Vibro-greatsword)

The Model VX Vibro-greatsword, sometimes known as a "Czerhander," was a massive vibrosword produced by the Czerka Arms corporation. Unlike most vibroswords, it was designed to be wielded with two hands. Despite its weight, the sword was well-blanaced enough that it could be used to parry opponents' weapons. The weapons were popular with members of larger species, and could match a vibro-ax's power. Model VX Vibro-greatswords cost 900 credits.
