# V-6 Haywire Grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V-6 Haywire Grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-6_Haywire_Grenade)

The V-6 Haywire Grenade was a type of single-use ion grenade created and produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. As an ion grenade, the V-6 Haywire released powerful electromagnetic pulses upon detonation, which could destroy or incapacitate any electrical and computer systems within range of the blast radius, including droids, vehicles, deflector shields, automated turrets, and other pieces of technology that were unprotected from the blast. The cost of a V-6 Haywire Grenade was sixty-five credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** V-6 Haywire Grenade
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Ion grenade
- **Cost:** 65 credits
- **Capacity:** Single-use
- **Purpose:** Damage or incapacitate electrical and computer systems
