# Kay's blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kay's blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kay%27s_blaster)

Kay's blaster was a VM-19 heavy blaster pistol owned by the slicer Riko Vess who later gave it to her daughter Kay Vess. It was highly customizable and capable of switching blaster modules during combat.

- **Name:** Kay's blaster
- **Model:** VM-19
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
