# DL-12 heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DL-12 heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DL-12_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The DL-12 heavy blaster pistol was a model of heavy blaster pistol that was slightly more powerful than a standard heavy blaster. At some point, the bounty hunter brothers Irol and Nevo Faunor each carried a DL-12 heavy blaster pistol on the Mid Rim planet Krykas V while attempting to collect a bounty on Min Erethen, a droid and weapons designer who was stranded on the world.

## Specifications

- **Model:** DL-12
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Purpose:** Ranged combat
