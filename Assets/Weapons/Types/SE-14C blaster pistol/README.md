# SE-14C blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SE-14C blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SE-14C_blaster_pistol)

The SE-14C blaster pistol saw use during the reign of the Galactic Empire. The gun also had two scopes that were used for different firing options. Rebel troopers, Imperial officers, and stormtroopers sometimes used it despite its poor recoil and because of its high rate of fire. It was related to the SE-14r light repeating blaster, which was similar in design.
