# KT6 Heavy Blaster Cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KT6 Heavy Blaster Cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KT6_Heavy_Blaster_Cannon)

The KT6 Heavy Blaster Cannon was created by Taim & Bak and equipped on Gamma-class assault shuttles. Control of the weapons was split between two of the assault shuttle's three gunners, and they were sometimes used during boarding actions to cripple the target spacecraft.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KT6 Heavy Blaster Cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
- **Type:** Heavy blaster cannon
