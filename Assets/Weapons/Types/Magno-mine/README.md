# Magno-mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Magno-mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Magno-mine)

A magno-mine was a magnetized, deep-space explosive that could be attached to a ship and detonated remotely. With a large blast radius, the Galactic Empire utilized magno-mines to cause maximum damage during battles involving multiple enemy ships. One was used during a skirmish over Mykapo, deployed by a TIE/sa bomber and attached to the captured rebel vessel Sato's Hammer.

- **Type:** Explosive
