# Ashla Staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ashla Staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ashla_Staff)

An Ashla Staff was a type of staff, often created from wooden branches gifted by Tholothian dignitaries, used by the Revered Masters of Lasat culture. These staffs held a spiritual purpose in Lasat culture, and had a special connection to the Force. A survivor of the Siege of Lasan named Chava carried one such staff on her journeys to return to Lira San.
