# "Subduer-9" Riot Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** ["Subduer-9" Riot Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%22Subduer-9%22_Riot_Blaster)

The "Subduer-9" Riot Blaster was a type of long-ranged scatterblaster manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation, and was one of the most common law enforcement scatterblasters in the galaxy. Although it was heavier at six kilograms and not as manageable as standard blaster rifles, riot blasters could subdue large groups of civilians. The Subduer-9 could emit a wide cone of stun blasts, which rendered targets unconscious and made it easy to place them in binders and awaken in prison cells hours later. The cost of the Subduer-9 was 1,250 credits.
