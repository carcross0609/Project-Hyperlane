# Lb-14 dual heavy laser turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lb-14 dual heavy laser turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lb-14_dual_heavy_laser_turret)

The Lb-14 dual heavy laser turret was a model of laser turret produced by Sienar-Jaemus Fleet Systems for the First Order's TIE/sf space superiority fighter. The turret was controlled by the starfighter's gunner and located on the underside of the vehicle. During his attempt to escape the Resurgent-class Star Destroyer Finalizer the defecting stormtrooper Finn used the Lb-14 on a TIE/sf he and the Resistance pilot Poe Dameron stole to cause mayhem in Hangar Six onboard the Star Destroyer.
