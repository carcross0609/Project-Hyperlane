# SH-9 slugthrower pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SH-9 slugthrower pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SH-9_slugthrower_pistol)

The SH-9 slugthrower pistol was a model of slugthrower produced by Czerka Arms and sold for 250 credits. The pistol was composed of lightweight composite alloys and was often referred to as a "whisper-pistol" because it was equipped with a built-in noise suppressor. The SH-9 could hold five rounds and was popular with Rebel Alliance SpecOps because it was incredibly unlikely to jam and could pierce laminate armors. The weapon was capable of function even when wet or submerged.
