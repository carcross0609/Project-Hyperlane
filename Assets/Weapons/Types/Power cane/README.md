# Power cane

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Power cane](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Power_cane)

The Palandrix Personal Protection Gear power cane was a deadly vibroblade disguised as a cane. Manufactured from high-density plastoids, and covered with exceptionally strong hardwoods, the power cane had the appearance of a simple walking cane. Hidden inside a spring-loaded compartment however, was a 30-centimeter stiletto blade, which was attached to a power source and vibro generator. When desired, the handle of the cane could be released by the spring, sending the hidden short sword into waiting hands. It also had a power point on top, disguised as an ornamental gemstone-like top, that could be used to deliver a shock as well.
