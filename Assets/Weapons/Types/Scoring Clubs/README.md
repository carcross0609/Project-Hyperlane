# Scoring Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scoring Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scoring_Clubs)

The Scoring Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They feature depictions of Huttballs on the shoulder areas of each.

- **Name:** Scoring Clubs
- **Model:** Grozz's Clubs
- **Type:** Club
- **Owners:** Grozz
- **Purpose:** Combat
