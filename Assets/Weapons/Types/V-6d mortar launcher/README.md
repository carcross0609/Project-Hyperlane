# V-6d mortar launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V-6d mortar launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-6d_mortar_launcher)

The V-6d mortar launcher was a support weapon manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.. Used by the Galactic Empire's TIE pilots and Imperial Army pilots, and Imperial commandos during a number of battlefronts throughout the Galactic Civil War, the mortar launcher was capable of firing volleys of mortars into enemy lines, with a capacity of 5 grenades. The grenades could be configured to explode on contact or on a timer.
