# Blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blaster cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blaster_cannon/Legends)

A blaster cannon, also known as a flash cannon and assault cannons, was a limited-range heavy artillery variant of the handheld blaster. Blaster cannons were commonly fitted aboard atmospheric vehicles; however, a portable shoulder-fired version was also used to inflict devastation on vehicles, troops, and structures.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blaster cannon
- **Type:** Heavy weapon
- **Cost:** 3000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:**
  - Two-handed portable shoulder-fired weapon
  - Vehicle-mounted weapon
  - Built-in droid weapon
- **Weight:** 18 kg
- **Capacity:** 6 shots to 10 shots
- **Range:**
  - Portable version:
  -   Optimum: up to 150 m
  -   Maximum: up to 375 m
  - Vehicle-mounted version:
  -   Optimum: up to 360 m
  -   Maximum: up to 1800 m
- **Purpose:**
  - Anti-personnel weaponry
  - Anti-vehicle weaponry
