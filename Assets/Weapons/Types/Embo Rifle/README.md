# Embo Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Embo Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Embo_Rifle)

The Embo Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle consisted of a black, gray, and silver frame, with yellow, orange, and red painted elements, and strips of brown cloth wrapped around the stock and grip, resembling the clothing commonly worn by the Kyuzo bounty hunter Embo.
