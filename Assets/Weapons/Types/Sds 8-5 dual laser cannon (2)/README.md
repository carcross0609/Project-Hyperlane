# Sds 8/5 dual laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sds 8/5 dual laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sds_8/5_dual_laser_cannon)

The Sds 8/5 dual laser cannon was a model of laser cannon manufactured by Taim & Bak. Two Sds 8/5 dual laser cannons were equipped on Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptors, a model of interceptor prevalently utilized by the Jedi Order.

- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
