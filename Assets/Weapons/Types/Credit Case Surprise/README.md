# Credit Case Surprise

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Credit Case Surprise](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Credit_Case_Surprise)

The Credit Case Surprise was a custom weapon produced by Synnt-ovations, Inc. Disguised as a briefcase filled with credits, the device was mainly employed by wealthy businesspeople. When attackers would attempt to steal from the individual, the Credit Case Surprise could be activated, causing a large flash of light which would temporarily blind the attacker, giving the owner a chance to escape.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Credit Case Surprise
- **Manufacturer:** Synnt-ovations, Inc.
- **Type:** Personal defense item
- **Cost:** 75 credits
- **Range:** 12 meters
