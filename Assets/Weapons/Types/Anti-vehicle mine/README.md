# Anti-vehicle mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anti-vehicle mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-vehicle_mine)

Anti-vehicle mines were large, heavy explosive devices that were capable of destroying walkers, speeders, and other terrestrial vehicles. When a vehicle passed over a mine, the mine's sensors would detect its weight, specific alloys, or repulsorfields, triggering the device. It would not detonate if troopers were to step on one, as their most sensitive settings required far more weight than even that of the most encumbered trooper and a larger repulsorfield than repulsor boots or packs were capable of generating.
