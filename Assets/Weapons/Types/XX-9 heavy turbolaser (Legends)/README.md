# XX-9 heavy turbolaser

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [XX-9 heavy turbolaser/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XX-9_heavy_turbolaser/Legends)

The XX-9 heavy turbolaser was a model of turbolaser manufactured by Taim & Bak. During the Galactic Civil War, the Galactic Empire commonly used the XX-9 aboard its fleet of Imperial-class Star Destroyers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** XX-9 heavy turbolaser
- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
- **Type:** Heavy turbolaser
- **Protection:** Quadanium steel
- **Range:**
  - 15 kilometers (optimum)
  - 100 kilometers (maximum)
