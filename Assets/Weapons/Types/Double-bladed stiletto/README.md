# Double-bladed stiletto

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Double-bladed stiletto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_stiletto)

A double-bladed stiletto was a weapon that contained two long, thin blades attached to a wrist sheathe, so the twin blades extended over the back of the hand. The blades could be retracted into the wrist sheathe and concealed under long sleeves, then extended with a flick of the forearm. A miner named Jake attacked Luke Skywalker with two double-bladed stilettos and double-bladed boots on Mimban.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Double-bladed stiletto
- **Locations:**

  - Mimban
- **Shape:** Twin stilettos attached to a wrist guard
- **Material:** Metal
- **Length:**
