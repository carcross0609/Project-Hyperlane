# Firefont 04 mass driver cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Firefont 04 mass driver cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firefont_04_mass_driver_cannon)

The Firefont 04 mass driver cannon was a type of mass-driver cannon used by the AT-TE and the AT-AP. Manufactured by Gazac, the projectile cannon was turret-mounted to the walker's dorsal surface. The mass-driver cannon fired sonic charges, bunker busters, heat-seekers, as well as a variety of other projectile ammunition from its rotating turret.
