# Stardrop

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Stardrop](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stardrop)

Stardrops were a type of torpedo that were capable of annihilating a massive base from orbit. They were known to be unstable and volatile. Agents of the Rebel Alliance in the Anoat sector owned several, which they used for blowing up asteroids. Most of those were confiscated by the Galactic Empire, but one was deliberatly abandoned in the ice of the planet Hoth.
