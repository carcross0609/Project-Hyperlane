# Kuruk's blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kuruk's blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kuruk%27s_blaster_rifle)

Kuruk's blaster rifle was the primary weapon of the Knight of Ren Kuruk. The rifle was used against Ben Solo during the battle of Exegol. The rifle had three modes across two barrels: rapid firing, sniping, and a pump-action mode designed to fire plasma bolts.
