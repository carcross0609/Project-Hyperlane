# Gilded Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gilded Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gilded_Applicator)

The Gilded Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This thick applicator was chrome blue, gold, and silver in coloration, with a pink barrel tip and pink, glowing core, as well as a stock painted with brown stripes, almost resembling the appearance of wood.
