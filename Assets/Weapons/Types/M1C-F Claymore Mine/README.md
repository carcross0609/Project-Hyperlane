# M1C-F Claymore Mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M1C-F Claymore Mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M1C-F_Claymore_Mine)

The M1C-F claymore mine was a type of fragmentation land mine produced during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. Simple yet powerful, its detonation was triggered by motion sensors, and ejected razor-sharp pieces of metals.
