# Chepatite impact explosive

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Chepatite impact explosive](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chepatite_impact_explosive)

Chepatite impact explosives were an explosive substance utilized in explosive micro-rockets. The rockets were reliant on the explosives, which provided them with their powerful kinetic force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Chepatite impact explosive
- **Type:** Explosive
- **Purpose:** Providing kinetic force for explosive micro-rockets
