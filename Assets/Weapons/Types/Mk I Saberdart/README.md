# Mk I Saberdart

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mk I Saberdart](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mk_I_Saberdart)

The Kamino saberdart, also called Mk I Saberdart, was a type of toxic dart created by the Kaminoans of the planet Kamino. One of these darts was used by the bounty hunter Jango Fett to kill Zam Wesell, who had failed in her mission to assassinate the Senator Padmé Amidala and later captured by Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi and his Padawan Anakin Skywalker, to prevent her from telling said Jedi who hired her. The dart had little cuts on its side that made it instantly identifiable to Dexter Jettster when Obi-Wan brought it to him at his diner for identification, having been unable to find any information about it in the Jedi Archives.
