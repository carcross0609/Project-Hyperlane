# GLX Firelance blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GLX Firelance blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GLX_Firelance_blaster_rifle)

Developed during the Clone Wars, the GLX Firelance blaster rifle was a type of compact blaster rifle manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation that marketed to "freelance law enforcement agents." The GLX was immensely popular among bounty hunters and law enforcement agencies for its select-fire modes and powerful stun setting, which made it one of SoroSuub's top-selling carbines. And although the GLX was short on lethality and was pricey, bounty hunters and law enforcement officers enjoyed its extended range, versatility, and dizzying rate of fire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GLX Firelance blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,600 credits
- **Range:** Long-ranged
