# E-11 medium blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-11 medium blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-11_medium_blaster_rifle)

The E-11 medium blaster rifle, more commonly known as the E-11 blaster rifle was a light but powerful blaster rifle manufactured by BlasTech Industries. Although a Nihil of Krix Kamerat's cell used a similarly-designed blaster during the Battle of Takodana of the High Republic Era, the E-11 was only created during the Imperial Era. The DAS-430 Neural Inhibitor was also of a similar design. The SoroSuub Corporation-produced Freedom One blaster was a technological clone of the E-11.
