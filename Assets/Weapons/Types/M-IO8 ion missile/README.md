# M-IO8 ion missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M-IO8 ion missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-IO8_ion_missile)

A M-108 ion missile was a model of ion missile that could be equipped to starships. Equipping an EML-850 light freighter with a M-108 ion missile cost two thousand and five hundred credits and required a X-FP-C28 ship computer, twenty five units of quadranium, and twelve 4XB Calculation Units.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M-IO8 ion missile
- **Model:** M-108
- **Type:** Ion missile
- **Cost:** 2500 credits
