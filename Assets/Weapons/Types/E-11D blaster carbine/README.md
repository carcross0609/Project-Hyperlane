# E-11D blaster carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-11D blaster carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-11D_blaster_carbine)

The E-11D blaster carbine was a blaster carbine manufactured by BlasTech Industries. It was used by the Galactic Empire's death troopers and Purge Troopers. Very similar in design to the E-11 medium blaster rifle, it featured a stock and a large-bore reinforced barrel in order to maximize its rate of fire and intensity.
