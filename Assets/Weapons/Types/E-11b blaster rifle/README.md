# E-11b blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-11b blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-11b_blaster_rifle)

The BlasTech E-11b blaster rifle was an expertly and expensively modified version of the standard issue E-11 blaster rifle used by the Imperial stormtroopers during the Galactic Civil War, which were modeled after the DC-15 series used in the Clone Wars. These weapons were often found in the hands of Rebel troops, namely those in the Battle of Endor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-11b blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** E-11b
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Length:** 0.438 Meters (with stock folded)
- **Weight:** 2.6 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 500 shots
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
