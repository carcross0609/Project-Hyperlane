# Arena Heroics

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Arena Heroics](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arena_Heroics)

The Arena Heroics was a pair of decorative K-16 Bryar Pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. Sporting a gold, brown, and black color scheme, each of these Bryar blaster pistols featured a raised golden insignia of Balada the Hutts Hypernova-class control ship, the Hypernova, on each side, as well as two curved golden points at the bottom of the grip.
