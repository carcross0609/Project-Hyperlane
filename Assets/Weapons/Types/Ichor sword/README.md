# Ichor sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ichor sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ichor_sword)

Ichor swords were a type of sword made of magical ichor. Mother Talzin owned one which she used to duel with Jedi Master Mace Windu. Talzin's ichor blade was later gifted by the Great Mothers to Morgan Elsbeth and was used by her to duel Ahsoka Tano.
