# Defender sporting blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Defender sporting blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defender_sporting_blaster_pistol/Legends)

The Defender sporting blaster pistol was made by the Drearian Defense Conglomerate. The DDC Defender was a short range and low-powered weapon, meant for civilian self-defense and small-game hunting. It was also a popular weapon for use in honor duels among the nobility.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Defender sporting blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Drearian Defense Conglomerate
- **Model:** Defender
- **Type:** Sporting blaster
- **Cost:** 300 to 650 credits
- **Size:** Small
- **Length:** 43.9 cm (17.3 inches)
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:** 100 shots
- **Range:** 30 meters (optimal), 60 meters (maximum)
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
