# High-explosive missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [High-explosive missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High-explosive_missile)

High-explosive missiles, or HE missiles, were powerful long-range missiles. Designed against armored vehicles, they were also effective against fortifications or weapon emplacements. Types of high-explosive missiles included the Kirgo Blastworks AA4 Light Anti-Armor Missile and the SoroSuub Mk VIII Lancer AA Missile.
