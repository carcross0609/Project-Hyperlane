# A295 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [A295 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A295_blaster_rifle)

The A295 blaster rifle was a blaster rifle used by Emon Kref while he was stationed at the Rebel Alliance's Echo Base. Kref used the rifle in the Battle of Hoth, though its energy pack was held in place by only a cord and a piece of tape.

- **Type:** Blaster rifle
