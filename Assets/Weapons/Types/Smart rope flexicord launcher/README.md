# Smart rope flexicord launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Smart rope flexicord launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Smart_rope_flexicord_launcher)

The smart rope flexicord launcher was a model of whipcord launcher that could be installed into a vambrace. The Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin used a smart rope flexicord launcher that was concealed behind a panel on the left vambrace of his Mandalorian armor during the reign of the New Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Smart rope flexicord launcher
- **Type:** Whipcord launcher
