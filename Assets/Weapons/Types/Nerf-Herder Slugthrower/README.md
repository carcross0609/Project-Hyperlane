# Nerf-Herder Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nerf-Herder Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nerf-Herder_Slugthrower)

The Nerf-Herder Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This dark red and gray slugthrower featured a nerf decal, as well as orange markings and the letters "NERF" in aurebesh upside down on the stock.
