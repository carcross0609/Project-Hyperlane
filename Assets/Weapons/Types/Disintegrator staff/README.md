# Disintegrator staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Disintegrator staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disintegrator_staff)

A disintegrator staff was a type of staff with a brown shaft and clubbed ends. The Toydarian junk dealer Watto kept a disintegrator staff with the ends wrapped on the trophy shelf of podracing memorabilia at his junkshop in the city of Mos Espa on the planet Tatooine when Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn led a group into the store looking for a T-14 hyperdrive generator in 32 BBY. The items on the shelf included trophies gifted to him by famous podracers and pit crews, items stolen from museums, and purchases made by his agents at the illegal auctions traditionally held after podraces with spectacular crashes.
