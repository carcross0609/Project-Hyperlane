# Sonic rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sonic rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_rifle)

Sonic rifles were the sonic analog to the blaster rifles, capable of striking targets from a greater range than the heavy sonic blaster while still maintaining the same damage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sonic rifle
- **Type:** Sonic weapon
- **Cost:** 900 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 50 shots
  - Autofire mode available
  - Cannot be deflected by a lightsaber
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 meters
  - Maximum: up to 450 meters
- **Purpose:**
  - To counter lightsaber-wielding Jedi and Sith
  - To disorient an opponent
