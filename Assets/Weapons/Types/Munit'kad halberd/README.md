# Munit'kad halberd

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Munit'kad halberd](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Munit%27kad_halberd)

The munit'kad halberd also known as the Mandalorian executioner's vibro-ax, was an ancient type of Mandalorian vibro-ax. It had a beskar ax-head that vibrated at ultrasonic speeds. One such halberd was stolen from the Scabbard Monument of Krownest in the year 7966 C.R.C. The Knight of Ren Ap'lek used a munit'kad halberd as his primary weapon.
