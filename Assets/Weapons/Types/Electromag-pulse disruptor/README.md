# Electromag-pulse disruptor

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Electromag-pulse disruptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electromag-pulse_disruptor)

Electromag-pulse disruptors were a type of ion melee weapon and tool that was designed to short-out electronic equipment. Consisting of a slim stylus and costing 180 credits, the disruptor was used as a tool in the daily work of some technicians, who kept it handy in order to deal with computers or droids that they lost control of.

- **Name:** Electromag-pulse disruptor
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Cost:** 180 credits
