# V-35 Spoiler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V-35 Spoiler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-35_Spoiler)

The v-35 Spoiler was a model of ion cannon produced by the Kuat Drive Yards for the purpose of planetary defense. One of three popular models from KDY's v-series it was considered a light ion cannon, with a surface-to-air capability useful against starfighters, and was a common weapon emplacement used to defend various strongholds. The Spoiler required a crew of four to operate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** v-35 Spoiler
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Medium surface-to-air ion cannon
- **Cost:**
  - 25,000 credits (new)
  - 4,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 4
- **Capacity:** Unlimited (power generator)
- **Range:** 5 kilometers
