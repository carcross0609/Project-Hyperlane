# MM-XT Mini-Missile Tube

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [MM-XT Mini-Missile Tube](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MM-XT_Mini-Missile_Tube)

The MM-XT Mini-Missile Tube was a single-shot compact missile launcher produced by Sienar Fleet Systems during the Galactic Civil War and cost 700 credits. It was designed for small vehicles, such as speeder bikes, landspeeders or airspeeders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** MM-XT Mini-Missile Tube
