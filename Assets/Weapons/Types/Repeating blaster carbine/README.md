# Repeating blaster carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Repeating blaster carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Repeating_blaster_carbine)

The repeating blaster carbine was a short-range autofire weapon similar to a light repeating blaster. It could only shoot in autofire mode, firing multiple shots in rapid succession at a greater area instead of the more accurate single-shot mode. It had difficulty hitting targets at longer ranges, thus making it ineffective against distant targets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Repeating blaster carbine
- **Type:** Light repeating blaster
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 6 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 30 shots
  - Autofire-only mode
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 225 m
