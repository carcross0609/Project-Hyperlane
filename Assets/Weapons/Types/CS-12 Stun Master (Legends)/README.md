# CS-12 Stun Master

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CS-12 Stun Master/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CS-12_Stun_Master/Legends)

The CS-12 Stun Master was a contact stunner sold by the SoroSuub Corporation as a personal protection device. Due to its innocuous appearance and small size—only fifteen centimeters long and five centimeters in diameter—it was easily smuggled past authorities. It required an energy cell to be effective.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CS-12 Stun Master
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Contact stunner
- **Cost:** 575 credits—700 credits; 15 credits per power cell
- **Size:** Small
- **Length:** 15 centimeters
- **Diam:** 5 centimeters
- **Weight:** 1.1 kilograms
