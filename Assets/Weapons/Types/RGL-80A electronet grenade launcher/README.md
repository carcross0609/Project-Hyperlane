# RGL-80A electronet grenade launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RGL-80A electronet grenade launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RGL-80A_electronet_grenade_launcher)

The RGL-80A electronet grenade launcher was a type of stun net launcher manufactured by Golan Arms. A larger version of the company's RL-40 stun net launcher, the weapon held a magazine of five electronet grenades, and had a maximum range of five hundred meters. Similar to the pistol-sized RL-40, the RGL-80A could vary the intensity of the electrical shock delivered by means of a remote control.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RGL-80A electronet grenade launcher
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Type:** Stun net launcher
- **Capacity:** 5 electronet grenades
- **Range:**
  - 350 meters (optimum)
  - 500 meters (maximum)
