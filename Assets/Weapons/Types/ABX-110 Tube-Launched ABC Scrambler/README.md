# ABX-110 Tube-Launched ABC Scrambler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ABX-110 Tube-Launched ABC Scrambler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ABX-110_Tube-Launched_ABC_Scrambler)

The ABX-110 Tube-Launched ABC Scrambler was an ABC scrambler modified from an Imperial Army anti-ordnance EM probe. It was employed by bounty hunters to disorient targets and by Imperial forces in order to suppress large unruly crowds.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ABX-110 Tube-Launched ABC Scrambler
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Corporation
- **Type:** ABC scrambler
- **Cost:**

  - 350 (ammo pod)
- **Length:** 1.2 meters (launcher)
- **Capacity:** 1
- **Range:** 500 meters
