# 84-U hunting rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [84-U hunting rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/84-U_hunting_rifle)

The Czerka Arms 84-U hunting rifle was a blaster rifle produced around the time of the Galactic Civil War. One 84-U hunting rifle was dropped into the rancor pit of Jabba the Hutt's palace on Tatooine prior to 4 ABY, and the Yuzzum Wam Lufba later utilized this rifle to escape from the pit after falling into it. Following his exit from the rancor pit, Lufba became the henchman of the gun-runner Pote Snitkin and would continue to often carry the 84-U blaster rifle.
