# Mounted MPTL 17

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mounted MPTL 17](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mounted_MPTL_17)

The Mounted MPTL 17 was a compact model of proton torpedo launcher manufactured by Bryn & Gweith. It was designed for anti-vehicle use. Several were installed in armored turrets at Fortress Keremark.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mounted MPTL 17
- **Manufacturer:** Bryn & Gweith
- **Type:** Anti-vehicle proton torpedo launcher
- **Locations:** Fortress Keremark
- **Cost:**
  - 18,000 credits (new)
  - 9,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 4
- **Capacity:** 25
- **Range:** 16 kilometers
