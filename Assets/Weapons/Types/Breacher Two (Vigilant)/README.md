# Breacher Two (Vigilant)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Breacher Two (Vigilant)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Breacher_Two_%28Vigilant%29)

Breacher Two was a breacher missile launcher used by the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet Nightdragon man-of-war Vigilant. In 18 BBY, during a battle against the forces of the Grysk operative "Jixtus" at Listening post KR21, Ar'alani ordered Junior Captain Evroes'ky'mormi, core name "Oeskym," to disable the thrusters in Breacher Two so the Vigilant could drop the missiles into space without firing them.
