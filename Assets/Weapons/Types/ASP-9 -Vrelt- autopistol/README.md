# ASP-9 "Vrelt" autopistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ASP-9 "Vrelt" autopistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ASP-9_%22Vrelt%22_autopistol)

The ASP-9 "Vrelt" autopistol was a type of slugthrower pistol manufactured by Talus Ironworks, and was a common weapon used throughout the Corellian system, intended for pest control in rural areas. Engineers built the ASP-9 specifically to hunt vrelts: large, hairless rodents that scurried and hopped around on two large hind legs, and scavenged for crops and were known to even gnaw on sleeping campers. The ASP-9 fired slugs in short bursts to improve the odds of hitting the evasive creatures.
