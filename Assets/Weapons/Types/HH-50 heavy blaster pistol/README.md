# HH-50 heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HH-50 heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HH-50_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The HH-50 heavy blaster pistol was a large-framed heavy blaster pistol made by BlasTech Industries. Nearly the size of a blaster carbine, the HH-50 was the largest, heaviest, and most high-output blaster pistol produced by BlasTech as of the Galactic Civil War, boasting a double-barreled design that shared various parts and systems with the Clone Wars-era DC-15 blaster carbine.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HH-50 heavy blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 1,300 credits
- **Size:** Blaster carbine-sized
- **Range:** Short-ranged
