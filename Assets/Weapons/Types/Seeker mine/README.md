# Seeker mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Seeker mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seeker_mine)

A seeker mine was a type of space mine that was sold for 1,300 credits. Equipped with a powerful explosive charge, sensors, a sublight drive, and a guidance system, a seeker mine could hunt down and destroy starships that entered its radius.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Seeker mine
- **Type:** Space mine
- **Cost:** 1,300 credits
- **Purpose:** Seek out and destroy starships
