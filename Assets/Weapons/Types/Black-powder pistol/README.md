# Black-powder pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Black-powder pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Black-powder_pistol)

The black-powder pistol was an archaic slugthrower pistol that used a combustible powder to launch a physical projectile. It was commonly used by pre-industrial civilizations, and had a slow reload time.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Black-powder pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Yctor Arms
- **Model:** Black Powder
- **Type:** Slugthrower pistol
- **Cost:** 200 credits
- **Shape:** Pistol-shaped
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 1.4 kg
- **Capacity:** 50 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 90 m
- **Markings:** Decorated grip often inlaid with semiprecious stones
