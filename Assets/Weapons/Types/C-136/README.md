# C-136

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C-136](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-136)

The C-136 was a land-based ion cannon produced by Speizoc Arms. Intended for use against targets such as cities and other infrastructure, the C-136 was also capable of striking targets in low-orbit. The C-136 was effective against the less refined deflector shield designs of the time it was designed in, but the weapon became obsolete as the effectiveness of shield technology increased.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C-136
- **Manufacturer:** Speizoc Arms
- **Type:** Heavy surface-to-surface bombardment cannon
- **Cost:** 100,000 (used)
- **Crew:** 7
- **Capacity:** 40 power generator
- **Range:**
  - 50–600 meters
  - 601–5,000 meters
  - 5,001–25,000 meters
  - 100 meter blast radius
