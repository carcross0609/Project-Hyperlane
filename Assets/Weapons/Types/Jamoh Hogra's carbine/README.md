# Jamoh Hogra's carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jamoh Hogra's carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jamoh_Hogra%27s_carbine)

Jamoh Hogra, a male Zabrak Mercenary who lived around the time of the Jedi Civil War, made his own personal carbine that was supposed to protect him against the many enemies he earned in his lifetime, particularly with his raid on a Sith dreadnought. However he was killed in a bath instead. His carbine rifle eventually found its way to Mic'tunan'jus Orgu, a merchant in Anchorhead's spaceport on Tatooine.
