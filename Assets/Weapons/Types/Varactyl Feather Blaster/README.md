# Varactyl Feather Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Varactyl Feather Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Varactyl_Feather_Blaster)

The Varactyl Feather Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This colorful blaster pistol featured black and silver components, as well as elements with red-to-yellow or blue-to-green-to-yellow gradients. Much of the blaster was worn, and a varactyl feather protruded from one side.

- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
