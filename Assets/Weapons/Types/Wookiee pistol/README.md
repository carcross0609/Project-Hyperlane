# Wookiee pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wookiee pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wookiee_pistol)

The Wookiee pistol, also known as the Kashyyyk Pistol, was a blaster pistol manufactured and used by the Wookiees of Kashyyyk. It had a lot of design similarities with the Wookiee projectile rifle, including its bronzium frame. It was characterized by its short range.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wookiee pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Wookiees
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Culture:** Wookiee
- **Locations:** Kashyyyk
- **Material:** Bronzium
- **Range:** Short
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Wookiee Army
