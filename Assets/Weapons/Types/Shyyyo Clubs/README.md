# Shyyyo Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shyyyo Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shyyyo_Clubs)

The Shyyyo Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They feature depictions of shyyyo bird heads on the shoulder areas of each.

- **Name:** Shyyyo Clubs
- **Model:** Grozz's Clubs
- **Type:** Club
- **Owners:** Grozz
- **Purpose:** Combat
