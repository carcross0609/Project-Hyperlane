# DH-17 blaster carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DH-17 blaster carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DH-17_blaster_carbine)

The DH-17 blaster carbine was a model of blaster carbine, and a heavier version of the DH-17 blaster pistol. It was heavily used during the Galactic Civil War by the Imperial Navy and the Rebel Alliance Navy's marines during shipboard skirmishes. It was known for its versatility, durability, and simplicity.
