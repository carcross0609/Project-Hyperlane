# C-K12B rapid fire cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C-K12B rapid fire cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-K12B_rapid_fire_cannon)

The C-K12B rapid fire cannon was a model of laser cannon that could be equipped to starships and could fire bursts at multiple targets. Equipping an EML-850 light freighter with a C-K12B rapid fire cannon cost two thousand credits and required a X-FP-C28 ship computer, twenty units of quadanium steel, and eight heuristic processors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C-K12B rapid fire cannon
- **Model:** C-K12B
- **Type:** Laser cannon
- **Cost:** 2000 credits
