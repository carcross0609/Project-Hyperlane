# Luma grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Luma grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Luma_grenade)

Luma grenades were a model of grenade that released an intense blast of light. They were used primarily as flares or to illuminate target areas, but were also effective at blinding opponents when detonated at close range.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Luma grenade
- **Type:** Grenade
- **Purpose:**
  - Blind opponents
  - Mark target areas
