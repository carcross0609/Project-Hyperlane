# Mercybringer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mercybringer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mercybringer)

The Mercybringer was a type of ion starfighter missile produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. during the Galactic War between the Republic and the Sith Empire. The Mercybringer was capable of downing most enemy shields and short onboard power systems in one hit, despite the minimal hull damage, and the missile possessed a moderate lock-on period and a long range.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mercybringer
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Ion missile
