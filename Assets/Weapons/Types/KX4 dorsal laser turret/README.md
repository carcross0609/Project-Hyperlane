# KX4 dorsal laser turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KX4 dorsal laser turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KX4_dorsal_laser_turret)

The KX4 dorsal laser turret was a model of laser turret produced by the company Taim & Bak. The short-range Corellian shuttle fighter Phantom was armed with a KX4.
