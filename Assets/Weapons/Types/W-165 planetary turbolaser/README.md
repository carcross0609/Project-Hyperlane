# W-165 planetary turbolaser

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [W-165 planetary turbolaser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/W-165_planetary_turbolaser)

The w-165 planetary turbolaser was a heavy turbolaser manufactured by Kuat Drive Yards. At the time of its introduction, it was the most powerful planetary turbolaser ever built, and was four times as powerful as the v-150 Planet Defender .

## Specifications

- **Name:** w-165 planetary turbolaser
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Planetary turbolaser
- **Cost:** 10 million credits
- **Diam:**
  - 150 meter diameter support platform
  - Over 50 meter diameter reactor core
  - Nearly 25 meter diameter barrel
- **Protection:**
  - Permacite armor
  - Deflector shield generator
- **Crew:** 50 (soldiers, technicians, computer operators, engineers, and gunners)
- **Range:**
  - 1,100 kilometers (optimum)
  - 9,600 kilometers (maximum)
- **Affiliation:** New Republic
