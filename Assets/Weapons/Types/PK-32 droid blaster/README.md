# PK-32 droid blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PK-32 droid blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PK-32_droid_blaster)

The PK-32 droid blaster was a model of droid blaster capable of shooting 820 rounds at 32 KHz. Weighing 4 kilograms, it measured 24 cubits in length and 4 cubits in width. It was available for purchase at a price of 220.40 (unit of currency undetermined).
