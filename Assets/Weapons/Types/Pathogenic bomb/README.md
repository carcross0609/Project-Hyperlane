# Pathogenic bomb

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pathogenic bomb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pathogenic_bomb)

A pathogenic bomb was a chemical warhead that mixed two prime components together to create a poison cloud that was capable of spreading disease among targeted populations. The resulting mixture was highly volatile, which necessitated its storage in independent receptacles until just prior to deployment. In 8 ABY, the population of the planet Kolatill in the Kathol sector of the Outer Rim Territories was targeted by Imperial warlord Moff Kentor Sarne. The assault was in response to Kolatill's overthrow of their Imperial governor in the wake of Sarne's ousting from his own capital, Kal'Shebbol, by New Republic forces. The attack—which was to feature the dropping of pathogenic bombs on major cities across the surface of the planet—was thwarted by the combined forces of the Kolatill Defense Force and the starfighter squadron assigned to the New Republic CR90 corvette FarStar.
