# Beastly Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beastly Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beastly_Scatter_Gun)

The Beastly Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It was designed to loosely resembled the large creatures known as Reeks, including a real Reek horn attached to the barrel of the gun as a trophy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Beastly Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
