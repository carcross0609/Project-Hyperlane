# Variable holdout blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Variable holdout blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Variable_holdout_blaster)

A unique, locally produced holdout blaster manufactured on Ord Gimmel, variable holdout blasters could switch between seven different power levels, which potentially put them on par with military holdout blasters. The short, squat design was built for the long fingers of the native Deaons, who manufactured a variety of different blaster models. Over the years, interest in Ord Gimmel's brewing civil war had grown galaxy-wide, and as a result, many collectors and enthusiasts discovered the utility of the variable blaster design, which made it a popular export.
