# Boonta Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Boonta Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boonta_Blaster)

The Boonta Blaster was a Huttese weapon named for the infamous and deified Hutt who allegedly commissioned the first of its kind: Boonta Hestilic Shad'ruu. They were bejeweled weapons that cost 1,000 credits and were often custom designed as ornamental scepters for a specific Hutt or passed down as family heirlooms.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Boonta Blaster
- **Type:** Blaster
- **Culture:** Hutt
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits
- **Range:** Short-ranged
- **Affiliation:** Hutt Clan
