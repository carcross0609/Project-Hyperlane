# L7 Liquidsilver light blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L7 Liquidsilver light blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L7_Liquidsilver_light_blaster_pistol)

The L7 Liquidsilver light blaster pistol was a type of light blaster pistol manufactured by BlasTech Industries, and was a favored weapon by smugglers and outlaws. It shot straight, hit hard, and had a clean, tubular design that made it aesthetically pleasurable in minimalist ways. The design made it particularly easy to conceal within piping and conduits, and smugglers were known to stash them aboard their ships for emergency purposes. Despite being just weak enough to be classified as a light blaster pistol, the L7 shared roughly the same power output as the Model 53 and Model 80 blaster pistols. The L7 made up for this lack of raw power with the high accuracy and exceptional armor penetration of its blaster bolts.
