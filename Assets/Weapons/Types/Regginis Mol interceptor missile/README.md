# Regginis Mol interceptor missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Regginis Mol interceptor missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Regginis_Mol_interceptor_missile)

In 20 BBY, the Reginnis Mol interceptor missile was discontinued. In 0 ABY, in the planet Poln Minor's Capperling's Cantina, the leader of a squadron of Chosen warriors, Ranquiv, asked Rebel operatives Leia Organa and Han Solo if they could calibrate Caldorf VII and Regginis Mol interceptor missiles while offering them a job to calibrate them. Organa then confirmed that they could calibrate the former and wished luck to Ranquiv, as she believed it would be unlikely that he could find anyone who could calibrate the latter, remarking that they had been discontinued. The Chosen—who Solo suspected has asked if they could calibrate Regginis Mol missiles to test the pair's programming knowledge—then gave the pair the job.
