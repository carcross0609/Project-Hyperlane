# GH-44 blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GH-44 blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GH-44_blaster)

The GH-44 blaster was a type of weapon used in the days of the Old Republic. In the year 3653 BBY, the Human Zeerid Korr, a former smuggler, wore twin GH-44 blasters in hip holsters while delivering a cargo of weaponry to the planet Ord Mantell. He sometimes replaced these with his single E-11 blaster pistol.
