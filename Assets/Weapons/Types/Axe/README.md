# Axe

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Axe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axe)

An axe (alternatively spelled ax) was a melee weapon that consisted of a heavy edged head fixed to a handle. Some axes had two blades. Although regular axes were intimidating weapons in themselves, their lethality and cutting ability could be increased by the addition of a vibration generator, which produced a variant known as a vibro-ax.
