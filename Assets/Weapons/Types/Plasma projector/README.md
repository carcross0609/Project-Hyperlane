# Plasma projector

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Plasma projector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plasma_projector)

The plasma projector was a massive and unwieldy weapon that fired bolts of plasma energy at its target. Only highly trained marksmen were capable of handling this weapon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Plasma projector
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Range:** 28 meters
