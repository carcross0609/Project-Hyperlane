# Purrgil Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Purrgil Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Purrgil_Scatter_Gun)

The Purrgil Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It was colored to resemble the appearance of a purrgil.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Purrgil Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
