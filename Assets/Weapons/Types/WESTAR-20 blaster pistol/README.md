# WESTAR-20 blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [WESTAR-20 blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WESTAR-20_blaster_pistol)

The WESTAR-20 was a short blaster pistol and the predecessor to the WESTAR-34 blaster pistol. Omega Squad's Fi Skirata carried a custom WESTAR-20 during his stay on Mandalore, after being pronounced brain dead and leaving the Grand Army of the Republic.
