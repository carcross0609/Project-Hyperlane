# Hand Baton CPD-12

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hand Baton CPD-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_Baton_CPD-12)

The Hand Baton CPD-12 was a personal defense weapon manufactured by Bacnor Armaments. The rod shaped weapon emitted a stream of plasma that liquified the outer casing of the weapon's ammunition canister. The combination create a blasting beam that was capable of stopping several attackers at once.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hand Baton CPD-12
- **Manufacturer:** Bacnor Armaments
- **Type:** Anti-personnel defense weapon
- **Cost:** 50 credits (5 credits per ammo canister)
- **Capacity:** 1
- **Range:** 5 meters
