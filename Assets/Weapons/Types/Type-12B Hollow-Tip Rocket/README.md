# Type-12B Hollow-Tip Rocket

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Type-12B Hollow-Tip Rocket](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Type-12B_Hollow-Tip_Rocket)

The Type-12B Hollow-Tip Rocket, also known as Type-12B capsules and Type-12B rockets, was an ammunition type manufactured by Locris Syndicates and Locris Syndicated Securities.A hollow-tip rocket, Type-12B rockets were typically loaded with FGA-583 nerve agent, although other compounds could be used such as Accudrop Stun Gas. Some bounty hunters were known to "hot load" their rockets with exotic poisons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Type-12B Hollow-Tip Rocket
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Locris Syndicates
  - Locris Syndicated Securities
- **Type:** Hollow-tip rocket
- **Cost:**
  - 15—600 credits (single rocket)
  - 250 credits (clip)
- **Range:** 60 meters
