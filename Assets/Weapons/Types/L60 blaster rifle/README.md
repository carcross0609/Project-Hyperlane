# L60 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [L60 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L60_blaster_rifle)

The L60 blaster rifle, also known more simply as a Toydarian Royal Guard blaster rifle, was a model of blaster rifle that was utilized by the Toydarian Guard during the Clone Wars. The L60 was manufactured through a contract specifically for the Toydarian Guard. It featured an ornate wood-veneered stock with a modified trigger guard for Toydarian physiology. The L60 was considered to be a light weapon, but were simple, accurate and had a small rate of fire, though the guardsmen were trained to make every shot count. Additionally, the L60 had a long range telescopic sight and an energy clip slotted on one side.
