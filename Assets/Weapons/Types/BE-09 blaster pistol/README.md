# BE-09 blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BE-09 blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BE-09_blaster_pistol)

The BE-09 blaster pistol was a model of blaster pistol owned by the moisture farmer Beru Whitesun Lars. Her BE-09 had minor carbon scorching on its barrel as a result of minimal use. In 9 BBY, Lars used her BE-09 to protect her nephew Luke Skywalker from the former Inquisitor Reva Sevander.
