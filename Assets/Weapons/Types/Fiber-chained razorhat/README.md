# Fiber-chained razorhat

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fiber-chained razorhat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fiber-chained_razorhat)

The fiber-chained razorhat was a saucer-shaped article both worn as a garment and wielded as a thrown weapon by the go-zeki gangsters of the Kyuzo species. A fiber-chain linked to the hat allowed it to return to the user after an attack. In the year 19 BBY, three razorhat-armed go-zeki were active on Nar Shaddaa, the moon of the planet Nal Hutta, until they were slain by the Bpfasshi Dark Jedi Fomadu.
