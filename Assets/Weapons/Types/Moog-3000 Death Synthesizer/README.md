# Moog-3000 Death Synthesizer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Moog-3000 Death Synthesizer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moog-3000_Death_Synthesizer)

The Moog-3000 Death Synthesizer, also known simply as the Trade Federation cannon, was a model of double-barreled repeating blaster used by the Trade Federation in the year 32 BBY during the Invasion of Naboo. They were mounted on tri-pods, and have a small targeting computer on top. They have a long-range and possess strong firepower. They were placed throughout the city of Theed on Naboo. On multiple occasions during the invasion, cannons were taken over by the Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi and turned against the Trade Federation B1-series battle droid army.
