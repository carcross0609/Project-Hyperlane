# Kelligdyd 5000 cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kelligdyd 5000 cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kelligdyd_5000_cannon)

The Kelligdyd 5000 cannon was an artillery piece used during the Republic Dark Age, the last century of the New Sith Wars. It was used by mercenary factions like Brigadier Jarrow Rusher's Rusher's Brigade. Kelligdyd 5000 cannons were attached to the troopship Diligence's cargo ramps and could be quickly deployed in battle. They saw action during the Battle of Gazzari, the Battle of Byllura, and the Siege of Calimondretta.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Kelligdyd 5000 cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Kelligdyd
- **Material:** Sarrassian iron
- **Weight:** Multiple tons
