# Blast synchronizer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blast synchronizer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blast_synchronizer)

A blast synchronizer was a weapon for sale at Kitak Hu's weapons shop on the planet Vorzyd V. It fit over the faceplate of a droid. R2-D2 once suggested C-3PO wear one to become a weapon droid.
