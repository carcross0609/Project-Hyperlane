# Hold-out blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hold-out blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hold-out_blaster/Legends)

Hold-out blasters (also holdout blaster or hold-out blaster pistol) were small, palm-sized blasters that could be easily concealed. Underpowered and short-ranged, hold-out blasters were the weapon of choice for undercover agents and criminals since they were illegal in many systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hold-out blaster
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 200 to 300 credits (Illegal)
- **Size:** Handheld
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg
- **Capacity:** 6 shots
- **Range:** Optimum: up to 3 m
- **Purpose:**
  - Concealed weapon
  - Backup weapon
  - Self-defense
