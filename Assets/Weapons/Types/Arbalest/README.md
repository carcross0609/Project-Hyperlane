# Arbalest

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arbalest](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arbalest)

The arbalest was a projectile weapon typically carried by the militiagungs of the Gungan Grand Army on the planet Naboo. The Gungan General Tobler Ceel established a training center in the Scrumma Jawbone, an arm of Lake Paonga, where militiagungs could practice with arbalests alongside other weapons such as the cesta and atlatl.
