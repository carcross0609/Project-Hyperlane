# Collector-repulsor array

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Collector-repulsor array](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Collector-repulsor_array)

The collector-repulsor array, also referred to simply as the repulsor ray, was a superweapon under development by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. It would focus the G-waves of hundreds of repulsor projectors to distort planetary crusts, with cataclysmic results.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Collector-repulsor array
- **Manufacturer:** Galactic Empire
- **Type:** Gravitic superweapon
- **Range:** None; transported by a small starship
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
