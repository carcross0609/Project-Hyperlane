# Bryar rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bryar rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bryar_rifle)

The Bryar rifle was a type of blaster rifle that could fire small and fast shots, although it could also fire a 'charge,' one large and stronger shot. The Bryar rifle was heavy, and less accurate than other types of blaster rifles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bryar rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,350 credits
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 3 kg
- **Capacity:** 50 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 225 m
