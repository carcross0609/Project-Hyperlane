# Protector PRP-500 Hold-Out Dart Pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Protector PRP-500 Hold-Out Dart Pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Protector_PRP-500_Hold-Out_Dart_Pistol)

The Prax Arms Protector PRP-500 Hold-Out Dart Pistol was a palm-sized personal defense weapon. Since it required no power cells or energy emitters, it could be easily concealed from scanning equipment.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Protector PRP-500 Hold-Out Dart Pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Prax Arms
- **Type:** Anti-personnel weapon
- **Cost:** 250 credits (40 credits per ammunition clip)
- **Capacity:** 6 rounds per clip
- **Range:** 25 meters
