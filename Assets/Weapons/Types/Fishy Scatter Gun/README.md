# Fishy Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fishy Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fishy_Scatter_Gun)

The Fishy Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This gun was covered in a fishnet print, with a fish skeleton attached to the top.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fishy Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
