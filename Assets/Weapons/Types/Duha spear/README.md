# Duha spear

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Duha spear](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duha_spear)

Duha spears were a type of spear that had a metal point at the end. In a 26 ABY speech, Duros anti-Jedi agitator and Yuuzhan Vong Ducilla Brarun used the point of a duha spear as an example of strength when expressing her conviction that the people of Duro could survive the Yuuzhan Vong invasion only by standing apart from the rest of the galaxy and New Republic.

- **Type:** Spear
