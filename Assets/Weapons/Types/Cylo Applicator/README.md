# Cylo Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cylo Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cylo_Applicator)

The Cylo Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This ornate applicator featured a gold, blue, and gray color scheme, with a patterned stock and red cloth wrapped around certain components.
