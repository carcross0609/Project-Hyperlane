# Zhaboka

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zhaboka/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zhaboka/Legends)

The Zhaboka was a double-bladed sword that originated from Iridonia. It was a Zabrak ceremonial weapon that originated as a plain wooden quarterstaff and later evolved into a frightening bladed weapon. It was similar to a double-bladed sword but with lighter and smaller blades allowing the wielding of the weapon with greater speed. It was an ancient weapon, dating back to before the Mandalorian Wars, and incorporated a detachable central grip.
