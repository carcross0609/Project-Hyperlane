# DL-19C blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DL-19C blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DL-19C_blaster_pistol)

The DL-19C blaster pistol was a model of blaster pistol manufactured by BlasTech Industries. Equipped with a stun setting, it cost 450 credits. Unlike its predecessor, the extremely popular and successful DL-18 blaster pistol, the DL-19C was designed as a specialized and highly adjustable weapon aimed at a much more niche market. The design team chose to elevate customizability above all else, including the power, accuracy, durability, and capacity that most weapon engineers strove for.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DL-19C blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DL-19C
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 450 credits
