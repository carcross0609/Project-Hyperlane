# M-40 Thunderbolt light repeating blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [M-40 Thunderbolt light repeating blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-40_Thunderbolt_light_repeating_blaster)

The M-40 Thunderbolt light repeating blaster was a type of light repeating blaster produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. By 10 BBY, as part of an arms exchange on the planet Bonadan, arms dealers Masse Goskey and Ephant Mon of Masse Goskey's Arms Emporium and Explosives Mart bought one such blaster for 3,100 credits. Mon recorded the purchase in the logbook that would become the Smuggler's Guide.
