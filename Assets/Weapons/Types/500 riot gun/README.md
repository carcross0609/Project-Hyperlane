# 500 riot gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [500 riot gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/500_riot_gun)

The BlasTech 500 "ESPO" riot gun was manufactured specifically for the Corporate Sector Authority's brutal Espo Troops.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 500 riot gun
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** 500 "ESPO"
- **Type:** Riot gun
- **Cost:** 1,000 to 1,200 credits (available for military use only)
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 2.2 kg to 4.5 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 50 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
  - Autofire mode available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 450 m
- **Affiliation:** Corporate Sector Security
