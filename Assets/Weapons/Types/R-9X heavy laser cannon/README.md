# R-9X heavy laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [R-9X heavy laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-9X_heavy_laser_cannon)

R-9X heavy laser cannon, also known as the R-9X fire-linked laser cannon and the R-9X laser cannon, was a model of laser cannon developed by Gyrhil. It was mounted on A/SF-01 B-wing starfighters. The Resistance used it to arm its transports, often paired with a precision targeting laser.

- **Name:** R-9X heavy laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Gyrhil
