# Shockprod staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shockprod staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shockprod_staff)

The shockprod staff was an electrical staff used by Gand findsmen. As per ancient tradition, new findsmen were presented with a shockprod staff when they were initiated.
