# Heatbeam

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heatbeam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heatbeam)

A heatbeam was a weapon that fired yellow jets of plasma. Lasers were used to create the plasma, and the weapon produced a superheated exhaust when fired. Soldiers in Xim the Despot's armed forces who carried heatbeams were known as "heatbeamers", and wore fire-proof padded gloves and kamas. Xim's war-robots were armed with heatbeams built into the droid's right gauntlet.
