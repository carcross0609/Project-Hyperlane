# FC-1 flechette launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FC-1 flechette launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FC-1_flechette_launcher)

The FC-1 flechette launcher was a model of flechette launcher produced by Golan Arms that used a plasma-based firing mechanism. The weapon held six anti-personnel canisters containing hundreds of tiny, razor-sharp flechettes made of durasteel. When fired, its projectiles were capable of penetrating up to ten centimeters, even through the armor of an Imperial stormtrooper. The design of the FC-1 inspired the creation of the DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle at the hands of Salus Corporation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FC-1 flechette launcher
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Model:** FC-1
- **Type:** Flechette launcher
- **Cost:** 2,500 credits
- **Capacity:** 6 flechette canisters
- **Range:** Medium-ranged
