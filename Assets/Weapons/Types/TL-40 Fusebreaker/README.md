# TL-40 Fusebreaker

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TL-40 Fusebreaker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TL-40_Fusebreaker)

The TL-40 Fusebreaker was a model of heavy blaster rifle that was used by the Rokana Raiders during the Imperial Era. It was capable of firing ten powerful shots, A personal force field could be installed on the barrel of the rifle to protect the wielder.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TL-40 Fusebreaker
- **Model:** TL-40
- **Type:** Heavy blaster rifle
