# Jurgan Kalta's assault rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jurgan Kalta's assault rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jurgan_Kalta%27s_assault_rifle)

Jurgan Kalta's assault rifle was the favorite weapon of the Zabrak mercenary Jurgan Kalta, who was active before the Great Sith War. The weapon was capable of dealing both ion damage and the standard damage dealt by a blaster. Sometime after Kalta's death, this accurate, high-powered assault rifle came into the possession of the Twi'lek businessman Crattis Yurkal.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Jurgan Kalta's assault rifle
- **Type:** Assault blaster rifle
- **Created:** By 3956 BBY
- **Owners:**
  - Jurgan Kalta
  - Crattis Yurkal
- **Cost:** 8,000 credits
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Range:** 28 meters (Effective range)
- **Purpose:** Ranged combat
