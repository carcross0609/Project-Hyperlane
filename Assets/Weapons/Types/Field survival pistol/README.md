# Field survival pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Field survival pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Field_survival_pistol)

The Field survival pistol was considered to be a good choice as a back-up weapon due to its versatility, though it was modest in performance, it was fully upgradable. If upgraded well enough, it can become stronger than a non-upgraded lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Field survival pistol
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Range:** 23 meters
