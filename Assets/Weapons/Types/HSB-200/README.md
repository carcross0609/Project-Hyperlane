# HSB-200

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HSB-200](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HSB-200)

The BlasTech Industries HSB-200 was a small blaster that was barely considered a hold-out blaster. Design decisions by BlasTech made it almost a blaster pistol instead of a holdout. It was not particularly effective, and was considered more for show than actual combat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HSB-200
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Small blaster
- **Cost:** 300—360 credits
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 1 kilogram
- **Capacity:** 12
- **Range:** 40 meters
