# Rockfall Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rockfall Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rockfall_Rifle)

The Rockfall Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle featured a metal frame, with tan and silver base elements, as well as portions painted brown and gray, a faded red, yellow, and black decal of a falling meteor displayed on the side, and brown strips of cloth bound around the grip, stock, and base of the barrel.
