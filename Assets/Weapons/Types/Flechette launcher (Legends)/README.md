# Flechette launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flechette launcher/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flechette_launcher/Legends)

A flechette launcher was an anti-personnel weapon that saw extensive use during the last half of the Clone Wars. This weapon fired canisters containing hundreds of razor-sharp flechettes — durasteel darts that spread out over the large area. The projectiles would explode, releasing flechettes at high velocity in a wide spread.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flechette launcher
- **Type:** Projectile weapon
- **Cost:** 1,100 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 4 shots
  - Collateral damage to adjacent targets
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 225 m
- **Purpose:** Antipersonnel weapon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Stormtrooper Corps
  -     EVO troopers
  -     Swamptroopers
