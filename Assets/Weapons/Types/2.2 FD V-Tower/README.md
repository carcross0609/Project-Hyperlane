# 2.2 FD V-Tower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [2.2 FD V-Tower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2.2_FD_V-Tower)

The 2.2 FD V-Tower was a model of anti-vehicle laser cannon that was manufactured by Atgar. It was crewed by four beings, but could be controlled from a command center if the crew was disabled. Several of them were deployed in defense of Fortress Keremark.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 2.2 FD V-Tower
- **Manufacturer:** Atgar SpaceDefense Corporation
- **Type:** Anti-vehicle laser cannon
- **Locations:** Fortress Keremark
- **Cost:**
  - 15,000 credits (new)
  - 8,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 4
- **Capacity:** Unlimited (power generator)
- **Range:** 10 kilometers
