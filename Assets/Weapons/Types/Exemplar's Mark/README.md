# Exemplar's Mark

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Exemplar's Mark](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exemplar%27s_Mark)

The Exemplar's Mark was a carbine weapon used by Exemplar Zheus at the time of the Galactic Civil War. It was his most trusted weapon and named after him for his bravery in battle. Many of the Outcasts wielded such weapons in honor of Zheus.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Exemplar's Mark
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Owners:** Exemplar Zheus
