# Tomral RM-76 heavy laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tomral RM-76 heavy laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tomral_RM-76_heavy_laser_cannon)

The Tomral RM-76 heavy laser cannon was a single barreled heavy laser cannon used on starships. Lando Calrissian had two installed in the dorsal and ventral turrets of the Millennium Falcon when he owned the ship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tomral RM-76 heavy laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Type:** Heavy laser cannon
