# Particle blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Particle blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Particle_blaster)

A particle blaster was a type of weapon owned by the Galactic Empire on the planet Crucival. In 1 BBY, they supplied these to civilian volunteers who were willing to help defend them against the Rebel Alliance.
