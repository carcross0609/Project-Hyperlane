# TTT-54 "Thumper"

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TTT-54 "Thumper"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TTT-54_%22Thumper%22)

The TTT-54 "Thumper", also known as the Tees, was a low tech grenade launcher originally designed by the Rebel Alliance and later licensed to SoroSuub for full production. It had an adjustable grenade housing that allowed it to use a wide variety of munitions, including thermal detonators, though the system required recalibration for each new grenade type and could jam if different-sized grenades were stored in the magazine. Unlike comparable models such as the MobileMortar-3 , its range was shorter and it lacked an attached repulsorlift to ease the weight burden.
