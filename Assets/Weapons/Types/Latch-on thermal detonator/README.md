# Latch-on thermal detonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Latch-on thermal detonator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Latch-on_thermal_detonator)

A latch-on thermal detonator was a type of custom thermal detonator that was utilized by the human former Nihil Krix Kamerat during the High Republic Era. In 228 BBY, Kamerat told members of the Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection that he wished to involve one such detonator in an assassination attempt on the Evereni Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, which was not favored by the members.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Latch-on thermal detonator
- **Type:** Thermal detonator
- **Shape:** Spherical
- **Purpose:** Demolition
