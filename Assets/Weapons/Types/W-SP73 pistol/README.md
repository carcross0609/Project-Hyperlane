# W-SP73 pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [W-SP73 pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/W-SP73_pistol)

The W-SP73 pistol was a model of blaster pistol. Former Imperial sharpshooter-turned-mercenary Migs Mayfeld carried one in his right holster on his hip. Din Djarin had a W-SP73 pistol at the bottom of the right column in his weapon locker aboard the Razor Crest.
