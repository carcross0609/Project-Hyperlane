# Bardiche

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bardiche](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardiche)

A bardiche was a type of bladed polearm, a traditional weapon of the Massassi. During the Seventh Battle of Ruusan, the member of the Brotherhood of Darkness who captured Jedi Lord Berethon had a lanvarok with a bardiche blade.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bardiche
- **Type:** Glaive polearm
- **Culture:** Massassi
- **Affiliation:** Sith
