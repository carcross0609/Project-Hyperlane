# Corpo rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Corpo rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corpo_rifle)

Corpo rifles were a type of long-range blaster rifle produced by BlasTech Industries but branded for the Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation, who supplied them to their Corporate Tactical Forces as a form of heavier firepower. Tac-Corpos used the weapons during special operations including a mission to the planet Ferrix in 5 BBY. Corpo rifles physically resembled a carbine variant of the SE-14C blaster pistol.
