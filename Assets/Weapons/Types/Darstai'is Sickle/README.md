# Darstai'is Sickle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darstai'is Sickle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darstai%27is_Sickle)

The Darstai'is Sickle was a curved blade wielded by the Sayormi Cyrans the Unfeeling. Cyrans had honed the blade to perfection, so it cut with only the slightest bit of pressure. He took great care to keep his weapon in the best condition possible at all times.
