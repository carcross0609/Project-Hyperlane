# Sporting blaster carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sporting blaster carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sporting_blaster_carbine)

The sporting blaster carbine was a similar to a blaster carbine. This weapon could be wielded in one hand for medium range, but wielding the weapon with two hands enabled the wielder to fire at long range.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sporting blaster carbine
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 2.6 kilogram
- **Capacity:**
  - 100 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 450 m
