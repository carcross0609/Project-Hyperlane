# Snap shot blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Snap shot blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snap_shot_blaster)

The snap shot blaster was an easily concealed blaster pistol that was favored by intelligence agents, undercover law enforcement officers, and criminals. A variant of a hold-out blaster, it was often used as a last resort. Morrigan Corde used a snap shot blaster pistol in emergency situations.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Snap shot blaster
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 250 credits (Illegal)
- **Shape:** Pistol-shaped
- **Size:** Hand-held
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:** 1 shot
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 120 m
- **Purpose:** Concealed weapon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Intelligence agents
  - Undercover law enforcement
  - Criminals
