# CX-55 scatterblaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CX-55 scatterblaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CX-55_scatterblaster)

The CX-55 scatterblaster was a model of scatterblaster used by the Alazmec of Winsit, a group of Alazmec cultists that was drawn to the Corvax Fen on the planet Mustafar. The blaster had a brown stock and a long black barrel, with sections of silver-colored paneling around the muzzle and just in front of the trigger. The weapon fired yellow blaster bolts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CX-55 scatterblaster
- **Type:** Scatterblaster
- **Affiliation:** Alazmec of Winsit
