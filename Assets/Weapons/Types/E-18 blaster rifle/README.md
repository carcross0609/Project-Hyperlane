# E-18 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-18 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-18_blaster_rifle)

The E-18 Blaster rifle was a blaster rifle and the successor of the E-11 blaster rifle. It was utilized by the Imperial Elite Guard in 40 ABY during the Second Galactic Civil War and Galactic Exploitation Technologies security service aboard Ormni.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-18 blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Remnant
