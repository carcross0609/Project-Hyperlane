# Imperial Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Applicator)

The Imperial Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This applicator features a black and white appearance, with an Imperial aesthetic, added scope, white cloth wrapped around the top of the stock, and splattered pink and blue paint across it.

- **Type:** Long-range dart gun
