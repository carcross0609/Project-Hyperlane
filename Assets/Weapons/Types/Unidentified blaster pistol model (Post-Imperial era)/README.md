# Unidentified blaster pistol model (Post-Imperial era)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Unidentified blaster pistol model (Post-Imperial era)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_blaster_pistol_model_%28Post-Imperial_era%29)

A blaster pistol model existed in the galaxy by the year 5 ABY. The blaster's body was a simple box shape, with a wooden grip lacking a trigger guard, and a long skinny barrel. The blaster pistol fired green lasers and was very accurate. The pistol was favored among Rodian bounty hunters and some Lumini Pirates. In addition, the weapon found it's way into the service of the officers of the Galactic Empire, as well as Imperial vehicle pilots and TIE pilots, and found some use in the hands of scout troopers. The weapon also found its way into the hands of New Republic officers by the year 10 ABY.
