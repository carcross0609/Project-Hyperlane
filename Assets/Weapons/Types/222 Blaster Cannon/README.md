# 222 Blaster Cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [222 Blaster Cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/222_Blaster_Cannon)

The 222 Blaster Cannon was an anti-vehicle blaster artillery emplacement used by the Imperial Military to defend important installations. Built by Jakan Arms, the 222 drew energy from a dedicated power generator, allowing it to fire an unlimited number of shots. The cannon normally required a crew of four to operate but could make due with half that number.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 222 Blaster Cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Jakan Arms
- **Type:** Medium anti-vehicle laser cannon
- **Cost:** 12,500 credits (7,500 used)
- **Range:** 12 kilometers
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
