# Beti (rifle)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beti (rifle)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beti_%28rifle%29)

Beti was a modified snub-barrelled blaster rifle owned by Sylvestri Yarrow. A gift from her mother Chancey, Sylvestri named the rifle after her childhood doll as a joke. The blaster rifle resembled the E-5 blaster rifle later used by the Trade Federation and Confederacy of Independent Systems in the late Republic Era.

- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Owners:** Sylvestri Yarrow
