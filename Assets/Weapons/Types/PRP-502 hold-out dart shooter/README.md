# PRP-502 hold-out dart shooter

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PRP-502 hold-out dart shooter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PRP-502_hold-out_dart_shooter)

The Protector PRP-502 hold-out shooter, was a small, light, hold-out dart shooter manufactured by Prax Arms. This weapon had a range of only about twenty-five meters, but could be broken down into four pieces for easy concealment and transportation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** PRP-502 hold-out dart shooter
- **Manufacturer:** Prax Arms
- **Type:** Dart shooter
- **Cost:** 300 credits
- **Weight:** 0.5 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 6 darts
- **Range:**
  - 10 meters (optimum)
  - 25 meters(maximum)
