# Life Day Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Life Day Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Life_Day_Clubs)

The Life Day Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They resembled ornate branches, with gold, crimson, and emerald handles, as well as thorns spread across and a spherical object situated within.

- **Model:** Grozz's Clubs
