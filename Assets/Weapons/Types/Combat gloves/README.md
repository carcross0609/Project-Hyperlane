# Combat gloves

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Combat gloves](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Combat_gloves)

Combat gloves were a pair of padded gloves that provided the wearer with additional impact power with every punch because of their weight and materials used in their construction. They were usually carried by anyone who expected to get into a brawl or fistfight, such as practicers of martial arts, Mandalorians, and Mercenaries.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Combat gloves
- **Type:** Gloves
- **Cost:** 250 credits
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg
- **Range:** Unarmed combat
- **Purpose:** Increasing unarmed damage
