# Dagger of Mortis

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dagger of Mortis/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dagger_of_Mortis/Legends)

The Dagger of Mortis, also known as the Mortis Dagger and Force-imbued dagger, was a blade that was kept in the underground Altar of Mortis. It was capable of killing the Ones; namely the Father, Son, Daughter, and Mother/Abeloth.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dagger of Mortis
- **Locations:** Mortis
- **Affiliation:** The Ones
