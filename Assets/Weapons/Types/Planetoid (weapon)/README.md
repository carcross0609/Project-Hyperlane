# Planetoid (weapon)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Planetoid (weapon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planetoid_%28weapon%29)

Planetoids were spherical hazards scattered throughout the zero-gravity arena in the final round of the Big Game, a gladiatorial competition held on The Wheel. The planetoids would explode with deadly force upon contact with a contestant or the blast from a needle ray. They also had force fields that detected nearby contestants and responded by erupting with vaporizer beams.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Planetoid
- **Type:** Trap/hazard
- **Locations:** The Wheel
- **Purpose:** Hazards during the Big Game
- **Affiliation:** The Wheel
