# HK8 Sawtooth

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HK8 Sawtooth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HK8_Sawtooth)

The HK8 Sawtooth was a blaster pistol with low ammunition capacity and a tendency to overheat during rapid fire. Despite being considered ugly, it was popular with criminal enforcers prior to entering mass production. The arms dealer Masse Goskey owned HK8 Sawtooths that he argued over with his partner Ephant Mon before Mon fatally shot him with one of the pistols. The criminal Razzi Syndicate also managed to acquire the weapon prior to it entering mass production.
