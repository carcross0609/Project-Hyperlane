# Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blurrg-1120_holdout_blaster)

The Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster was a hold-out blaster pistol manufactured by Eirriss Ryloth Defense Tech. The weapon was named after the blurrg, a creature native to Ryloth, the home of its manufacturer. The Blurrg-1120 had a primary blaster barrel, a secondary blaster barrel, a small sight, a "lekku" trigger, an ergonomic grip handle, and a gas and power cell cartridge. The Blurrg-1120 was customizable, as it could be modified to fire explosive bolts. In addition, it sported nine unique firing modes, including a standard single-shot mode and a two-blast burst mode. Its final firing mode consumed a lot of energy, and didn't register how many shots were fired, forcing its user to check the indicator located under the pistol to know how many shots were left. The pistol was favored by the Twi'lek rebel Hera Syndulla, who kept it in a holster on her right boot.
