# Heavy blaster arm

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy blaster arm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_blaster_arm)

The heavy blaster arm was a commercial heavy blaster-equipped mechanical limb for protocol droids. Commonly sold in pairs, the arms were cylindrically shaped and possessed a dark gray color. The product was traded during the Clone Wars, a major galactic conflict fought between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems from 22 to 19 BBY.

- **Type:** Droid part
