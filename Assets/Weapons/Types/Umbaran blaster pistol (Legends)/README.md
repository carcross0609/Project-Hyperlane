# Umbaran blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Umbaran blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umbaran_blaster_pistol/Legends)

The Umbaran blaster pistol also known as Umbaran blaster rifle was used by the Umbaran militia during the Clone Wars. It was an advanced version of blaster technology and was not commonly used outside of Umbara. It fired green blaster bolts.
