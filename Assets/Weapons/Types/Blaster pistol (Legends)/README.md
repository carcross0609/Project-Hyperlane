# Blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blaster_pistol/Legends)

A blaster pistol was a small, hand-held blaster.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Blaster pistol
- **Type:** Blaster
- **Cost:** 500 credits
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 100 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 120 m
