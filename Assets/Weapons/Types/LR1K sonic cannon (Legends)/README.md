# LR1K sonic cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LR1K sonic cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LR1K_sonic_cannon/Legends)

The LR1K sonic cannon, also known as the Geonosian sonic cannon or the Geonosian defense platform, was a high-powered artillery emplacement weapon produced by the Gordarl Weaponsmiths on Geonosis prior to and during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LR1K sonic cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Gordarl Weaponsmiths
- **Model:** LR1K
- **Type:** Sonic cannon
- **Cost:** 9,000 credits (5,000 used)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Geonosians
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
