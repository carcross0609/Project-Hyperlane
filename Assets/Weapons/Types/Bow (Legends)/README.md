# Bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bow/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bow/Legends)

Bows were primitive weapons that fired arrows used for hunting, sport, or combat. Advanced versions were also made for high-tech worlds.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bow
- **Type:** Simple ranged weapon
- **Culture:** Ewoks
- **Cost:** 300 credits
- **Material:**
  - Wood
  - Durable and lightweight composite materials
- **Weight:** 1.4 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 1 arrow
  - Quiver can hold 10 arrows
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 120 m
- **Purpose:**
  - Hunting
  - Sport
  - Combat
