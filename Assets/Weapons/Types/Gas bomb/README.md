# Gas bomb

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gas bomb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_bomb)

Gas bombs were explosives filled with poisonous gas. The Grand Army of the Republic deployed gas bombs made by Doctor Sionver Boll with Malastarian fuel to kill the Zillo Beast.
