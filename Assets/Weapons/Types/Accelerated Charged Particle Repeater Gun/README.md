# Accelerated Charged Particle Repeater Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Accelerated Charged Particle Repeater Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Accelerated_Charged_Particle_Repeater_Gun)

The Accelerated Charged Particle Repeater Gun, commonly known as the ACP repeater gun, Trandoshan Chopper or Nar Shaddaa Grinder, was a medium-range blaster manufactured by Arakyd Industries. It was popular amongst criminals, most notably the Trandoshan mercenary group. These guns were not at all advanced and had built-in extinguishers in case of overheating.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Accelerated Charged Particle Repeater Gun
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Capacity:** 40 rounds
- **Affiliation:**
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Trandoshans
