# Separatist flagship deck cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Separatist flagship deck cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_flagship_deck_cannon)

The Separatist flagship deck cannon was a large cannon used on the Providence-class carrier/destroyer Invisible Hand. It consisted of a large mass-driver barrel and a smaller ion cannon barrel on an rotating platform and an ammunition feed channel belt. It also had a heat dissipater for higher energy blast at the top.

- **Type:** Mass-driver cannon
