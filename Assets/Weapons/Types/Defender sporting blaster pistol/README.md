# Defender sporting blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Defender sporting blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defender_sporting_blaster_pistol)

Defender sporting blaster pistols, also known simply as a Defender Pistol, were a type of blaster pistol favored and used as a sidearm by Leia Organa. She first learned to shoot the Defender as a pre-teen, and displayed a rare talent for shooting. She stunned a mutinous Poe Dameron with one aboard the Raddus. Amilyn Holdo, whose command Dameron had overthrown, carried a Defender pistol.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Defender sporting blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Drearian Defense Conglomerate
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Weight:** Lightweight
- **Protection:**

- **Capacity:** 100 shots
