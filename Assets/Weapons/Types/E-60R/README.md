# E-60R

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-60R](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-60R)

The E-60R was a model of missile launcher utilized by B1-series battle droids of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. They could be used to pierce armor, even when fired at long-range. Rebel Vanguards made use of the E-60R during the Galactic Civil War.
