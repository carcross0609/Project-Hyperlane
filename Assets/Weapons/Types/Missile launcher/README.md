# Missile launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Missile launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Missile_launcher)

Missile launchers, also known as rocket launchers or warhead launchers, were a class of weapon which launched explosive projectiles. Launchers ranged in size and function, from small weapons usable by a single individual to larger launchers which were built into starships such as the Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry, or ground vehicles like the All Terrain Missile Platform. They saw use during the Clone Wars and the subsequent Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Missile launcher
- **Purpose:** Launch missiles or torpedos
