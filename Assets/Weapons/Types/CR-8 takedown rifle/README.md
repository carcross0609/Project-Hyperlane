# CR-8 takedown rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CR-8 takedown rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR-8_takedown_rifle)

The CR-8 takedown rifle was a high-performance blaster rifle produced by Corellian Arms. Designed to arm Corellian Security Forces snipers and secret agents, the CR-8 was built to exacting standards; long, deadly, and made for long-range pinpoint accuracy, measuring just shy of two meters. The CR-8's modified firing system used a supercharged actuator, upgraded XCiter, and a high-output proprietary blaster gas to improve ranged accuracy by reducing beam refraction and degradation and increase firepower. However, the CR-8 produced inordinate amounts of heat, which was offset by a low rate of fire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CR-8 takedown rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Arms
- **Cost:** 3,250 credits
- **Material:** Composite

- **Range:** Extreme
- **Affiliation:** Corellian Security Forces
