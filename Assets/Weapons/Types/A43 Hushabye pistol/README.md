# A43 Hushabye pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A43 Hushabye pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A43_Hushabye_pistol)

The A43 Hushabye pistol was a blaster pistol owned by Coruscant Security Force Lieutenant Tan Divo. He wore the blaster on the left side of his police utility belt with the handle facing forward. The criminal Lauli Wahlo also owned a A43 Hushabye pistol, although he added a silencer.
