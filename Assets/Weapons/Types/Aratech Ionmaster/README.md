# Aratech Ionmaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aratech Ionmaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aratech_Ionmaster)

The Aratech Ionmaster was the most advanced ion blaster produced by the Aratech Company as of the Jedi Civil War. This weapon was designed for multiple-purpose use, though it was generally less damaging against organic opponents. However, ion weapons were able to penetrate some defenses that would have blocked typical blaster fire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Aratech Ionmaster
- **Manufacturer:** Aratech Repulsor Company
- **Type:** Ion pistol
