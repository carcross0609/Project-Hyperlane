# RGL-80 electronet grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RGL-80 electronet grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RGL-80_electronet_grenade)

The RGL-80 electronet grenade, also known as an electronet grenade was a type of grenade designed by Golan Arms to stun and capture targets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RGL-80 electronet grenade
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Type:** Stun net deploying antipersonnel wire-guided grenade
- **Culture:** Bounty hunters
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits (magazine)
- **Shape:** Grenade-style canister
- **Size:** Small to medium
- **Material:**
  - Inside made up of a webbing of sticky, current-conducting
  - fibers
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:** Five grenades in force-fed magazine
- **Range:**
  - 2 meter diameter (net size)
  - 500 meters (maximum)
- **Purpose:** To stun and capture targets
