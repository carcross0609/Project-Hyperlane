# Plum Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Plum Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plum_Rifle)

The Plum Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle consisted of a worn black and gold frame, with a brown gradient around the tip of the barrel, as well as black, light gray, and plum-colored elements around the center, and brown strips of cloth bound to the grip, stock, and base of the barrel.
