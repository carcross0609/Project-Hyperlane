# Missile launcher (Trade Federation)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Missile launcher (Trade Federation)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Missile_launcher_%28Trade_Federation%29)

A missile launcher turret model was used by the Trade Federation. The turrets fired seeker missiles from their dual barrels.In 32 BBY, during the invasion of the planet Naboo, several launchers were attached to the communications relay satellite Comm 4 for defense. They were also part of the defenses at a Trade Federation base. Another launcher was found near Borvo's base at Porso Hill. The Trade Federation prison Camp Four also had a turret placed in a valley.
