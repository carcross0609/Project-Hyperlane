# Shock stick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shock stick/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shock_stick/Legends)

The shock stick was a larger and more powerful version of the stun baton. It was a large two-handed weapon that was able to incapacitate creatures by delivering stunning energy in a rapid, overwhelming bursts. It could also be mounted on a rifle as a bayonet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shock stick
- **Type:** Stun baton
- **Cost:** 450 credits
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 1.4 kg
