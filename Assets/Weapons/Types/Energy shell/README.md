# Energy shell

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Energy shell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Energy_shell)

Energy shells were projectiles fire from launch tubes equipped on Armored Assault Tanks. The shells were either armor-piercing antivehicle shells or explosive "bunker busters", and were sheathed in plasma which increased the projectile's speed and penetration. AATs could carry a payload of up to fifty-five shells. To reload, the tank withdrew to a reload station where the lower portion of the AAT was removed and a fresh, fully-loaded replacement was put in its place.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Energy shell
- **Type:** Plasma-encased high-energy shell
