# Snap baton

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Snap baton](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snap_baton)

A snap baton was a collapsible baton that expanded from 12 centimeters to one meter. They were easily concealable or carried in a pocket or utility belt. Some custom upgrades (which doubled the price) that were sometimes incorporated included a panic button or a built-in glowrod. Many models existed, but the Merr-Sonn model was covered in a thin layer of dark sound absorbing material to help reduce the noise when the baton was deployed or used.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Snap baton
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Cost:** 100—200 credits
- **Length:**
  - 12 centimeters (collapsed)
  - 1 meter (expanded)
- **Weight:** 1 kilogram
- **Purpose:**
  - Melee weapon
  - Personal protection
