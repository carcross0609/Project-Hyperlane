# DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DF-D1_Duo-Flechette_Rifle/Legends)

The DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle was produced by Salus Corporation and was essentially a scaled-down version of the Golan Arms FC-1 flechette launcher. The design was stolen and modified to be a smaller, rifle-sized weapon. It could be mounted on personal battle armor, firing shrapnel flechettes out a stubby double barrel and was particularly effective at close range. While effective, it was very inaccurate and eventually removed from service.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Salus Corporation
- **Type:** Flechette launcher
- **Cost:** 1,000—1,200 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 4.5 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 5 or 6 flechette canisters
- **Range:** 60 meters
