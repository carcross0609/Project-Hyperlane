# Proton bomb

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Proton bomb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proton_bomb)

Proton bombs were powerful explosive weapons carried by bomber craft such as BTL-A4 Y-wing starfighters and TIE/sa bombers. The bombs themselves were spherical in appearance, although when stored in cylindrical containers, they could also be used as high-yield explosives capable of producing great damage to heavily armored vehicles such as AT-ATs.
