# Longswat-44 sniper blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Longswat-44 sniper blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Longswat-44_sniper_blaster_rifle)

The Longswat-44 sniper blaster rifle was a model of sniper rifle manufactured by Czerka Arms. It had a long barrel, a scope, and a handle under the body. The Delphidian pirate Sidon Ithano owned a red Longswat-44 and used the rifle while fighting for the Resistance against the Sith Eternal forces of the Final Order on the surface of the Resurgent-class Star Destroyer Steadfast during the Battle of Exegol in 35 ABY. He then carried the weapon with him while attending a celebration at the Resistance base on the moon Ajan Kloss following their victory in the battle.
