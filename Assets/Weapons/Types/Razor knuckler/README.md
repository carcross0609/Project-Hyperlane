# Razor knuckler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Razor knuckler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Razor_knuckler)

Razor knucklers, also amalgamated as razorknucklers, or known simply as razors, were a bladed weapon used for close combat. The polished steel blades and edges gleamed when held in the light. Razor knucklers were favored by Black Sun thugs and assassins.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Razor knuckler
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Affiliation:** Black Sun
