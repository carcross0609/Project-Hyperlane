# DLT-20A blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DLT-20A blaster rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DLT-20A_blaster_rifle/Legends)

The DLT-20A (also called a "longblaster" or "longbarrel") was a blaster rifle manufactured by BlasTech Industries sometime before the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DLT-20A blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DLT-20A
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,300 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 6.7 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 30 shots
  - Autofire mode available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 450 m
- **Affiliation:**
  - Grand Army of the Republic
  -   Republic recon patrols
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial garrisons
  - Twi'lek freedom fighters
