# SWE/2 sonic rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SWE/2 sonic rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SWE/2_sonic_rifle)

The SWE/2 sonic rifle was a type of sonic rifle manufactured by Telex-Delcor as a follow-up to the initial SWE/1 design used during the Clone Wars. It could incapacitate even the toughest, most persistent malcontent, equipped with a powerful acoustic generator to fire a concentrated beam of sound waves that were similar in power and performance to particle-beam packets fired by other blasters. When used on sentient beings, it was capable of affecting a target's hearing and sense of balance, staggering them and shorting out their fine-motor centers.
