# Endor A280C Custom

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Endor A280C Custom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endor_A280C_Custom)

The Endor A280C Custom was a custom A280C blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This modified blaster rifle featured a sleek, metallic, gray and silver frame, with vertically stacked twin barrels, tube-like stock, and glowing orange components.
