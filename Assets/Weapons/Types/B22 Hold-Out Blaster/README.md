# B22 Hold-Out Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [B22 Hold-Out Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B22_Hold-Out_Blaster)

The B22 Hold-Out Blaster was a hold-out blaster designed by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. for Imperial political agents. Its special handgrip allowed easy concealment and quick draws from a pocket.

## Specifications

- **Name:** B22 Hold-Out Blaster
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Hold-out blaster
- **Cost:** 300 credits; 25 credits per power pack
- **Weight:** 0.5 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 10 shots
- **Range:** 12 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
