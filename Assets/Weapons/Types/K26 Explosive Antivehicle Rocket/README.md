# K26 Explosive Antivehicle Rocket

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [K26 Explosive Antivehicle Rocket](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K26_Explosive_Antivehicle_Rocket)

The K26 Explosive Antivehicle Rocket was manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc., for use in wrist rocket launchers such as the Kelvarek Consolidated Arms MM9 rocket system. It was capable of a reaching a target within 100 meters, and had a blast radius of two meters, within which it caused shrapnel damage upon impact. The wrist rockets cost 500 credits each and were slightly more powerful than standard fragmentation grenades and capable of destroying a speeder bike. The K26 Explosive Aantivehicle Rocket was in use at some point before the year 0 ABY, however it was restricted to military usage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** K26 Explosive Antivehicle Rocket
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Wrist rocket

- **Range:** 100 meters
