# Quirky Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quirky Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quirky_Slugthrower)

The Quirky Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It featured a standard purple and silver base, with ornate designs on top, including lines, patterns, and decals of white, purple, red, yellow, and orange, some being gradients.
