# Splatter Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Splatter Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Splatter_Applicator)

The Splatter Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This colorful applicator featured a black, gray, silver, and white frame, with glowing pink core, and various pink and blue paint splatters scattered across.
