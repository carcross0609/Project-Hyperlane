# DC-15S

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DC-15S/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DC-15S/Legends)

The DC-15S, also referred to as the DC-15 blaster and DC15 carbine, was the blaster carbine variant of BlasTech's DC-15A blaster rifle, not to be confused with the DC-15A itself. This weapon was carried by the clone troopers of the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars. It was also in service in the earlier years of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DC-15S
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DC-15S
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Capacity:** Approx. 500 shots
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  - Various others
