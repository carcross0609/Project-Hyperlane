# MS-1 fire-linked heavy laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MS-1 fire-linked heavy laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MS-1_fire-linked_heavy_laser_cannon)

The MS-1 fire-linked heavy laser cannon was a model of Class II heavy laser cannon manufactured by Taim & Bak. The laser cannons were powered by two external power cells. The Imperial All Terrain Armored Transport had a pair of MS-1 heavy laser cannons on the vehicle's "chin."

- **Name:** MS-1 fire-linked heavy laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
- **Type:** Heavy laser cannon
