# High Republic Twin Shields

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [High Republic Twin Shields](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Republic_Twin_Shields)

The High Republic Twin Shields were a pair of shields owned by the KX-series security droid Hunter K-A0S that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard Grafted Twin Shields. The surface of these combined shields formed a mural featuring legendary Jedi, including Jedi Master Yoda, all posed behind a large High Republic crest.
