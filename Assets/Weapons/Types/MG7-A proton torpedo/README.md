# MG7-A proton torpedo

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [MG7-A proton torpedo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MG7-A_proton_torpedo)

The MG7-A proton torpedo was a type of proton torpedo produced by the weapons manufacturing company Krupx. A miniaturized version of the torpedo was used by the Resistance during their conflict with the First Order, with each of their T-70 X-wing starfighters able to carry eight torpedoes at a time. During the Battle of Starkiller Base, the Resistance used many MG7-A torpedoes to bomb Starkiller Base's thermal oscillator in the hope of crippling the superweapon.
