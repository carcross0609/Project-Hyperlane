# Righteous MT-97

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Righteous MT-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Righteous_MT-97)

The Righteous MT-97 was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. These blasters resembled the aesthetic of the armor worn by lawman-turned-bounty hunter Tam Posla.
