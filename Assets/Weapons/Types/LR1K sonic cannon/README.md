# LR1K sonic cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LR1K sonic cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LR1K_sonic_cannon)

The LR1K sonic cannon also known as the Geonosian sonic cannon, or the Geonosian sonic defense platform, was a sonic cannon weapon. This weapon was employed by the Geonosians for the Confederacy of Independent Systems at both the First Battle of Geonosis and the Second Battle of Geonosis. The weapon employed a powerful blast of sonic energy that proved powerful in the Confederacy's efforts in the Clone Wars, as it could kill most clones.
