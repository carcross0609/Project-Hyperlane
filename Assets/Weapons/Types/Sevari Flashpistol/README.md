# Sevari Flashpistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sevari Flashpistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sevari_Flashpistol)

Sevari Flashpistols were used and manufactured by the Sevari on Sevarcos II where ordinary blaster weapons were less effective, and sometimes even prone to power pack detonations, due to the flying sand particles that would become electrically charged during the violent sand storms of Sevarcos II.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sevari Flashpistol
- **Type:** Flashpistol
- **Culture:** Sevari
- **Cost:** 50—500 credits (depending on model, barrel number, and design work)
- **Capacity:** 1
- **Range:** 60 meters
