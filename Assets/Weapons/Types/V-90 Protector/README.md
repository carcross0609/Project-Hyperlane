# V-90 Protector

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [V-90 Protector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-90_Protector)

The v-90 Protector was a model of ion cannon produced by the Kuat Drive Yards for the purpose of planetary defense. One of three popular models from KDY's v-series it was considered a medium ion cannon, with a surface-to-low orbit capability useful against capital ships, and was a common weapon emplacement used to defend various strongholds. The Protector required a crew of ten to operate at full capacity but could get by with a skeleton crew of four.

## Specifications

- **Name:** v-90 Protector
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Medium ion surface-to-space cannon
- **Cost:**
  - 100,000 credits (new)
  - 30,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 10
- **Capacity:** Unlimited (Power generator)
- **Range:** Low orbit
