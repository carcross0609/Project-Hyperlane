# Amaranthine Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Amaranthine Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amaranthine_Blaster)

The Amaranthine Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Amaranthine Blaster
- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
- **Type:**
  - Heavy blaster pistol
  -   Veteran Blaster
- **Owners:** Zaina
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
