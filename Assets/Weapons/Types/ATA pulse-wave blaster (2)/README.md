# ATA pulse-wave blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [ATA pulse-wave blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ATA_pulse-wave_blaster)

The ATA pulse-wave blaster was a vicious short-ranged plasma disruptor pistol capable of maiming or disintegrating targets. It was produced by Greff-Timms Industrial and was considered state-of-the-art millennia before the Galactic Civil War. The weapon functioned on the same basic principle as modern blasters during the war but fell under the auspices of disruptor weapon bans and control acts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ATA pulse-wave blaster
- **Manufacturer:** Greff-Timms Industrial
- **Type:** Disruptor pistol
- **Cost:** 750 credits
- **Range:** Short
