# Whistling bird

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Whistling bird](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whistling_bird)

A whistling bird was a type of weapon used by Mandalorians. They were small guided munitions placed in Mandalorian vambraces which, when deployed, flew through the air while making a whistling noise before striking the target and killing them with a small explosion.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Whistling bird
- **Type:** Explosive projectile
- **Culture:** Mandalorian
- **Affiliation:**
  - Mandalorian
  -   Clan Mudhorn
