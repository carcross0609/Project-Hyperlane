# Firestar 10-K

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firestar 10-K](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firestar_10-K)

The Firestar 10-K was a carbine weapon. Oddly, it was once found in the stomach of a creature known as SteelClaw in the Dead Forest on Kashyyyk. After the Firestar 10-K was found, they went through mass production.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Firestar 10-K
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Range:** 60 meters
