# DH-X heavy blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DH-X heavy blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DH-X_heavy_blaster_rifle)

The DH-X heavy blaster rifle was a cumbersome model of long-range heavy blaster rifle that was designed to punch a hole the size of a smashball through any target within sight. The weapon had a short and squat barrel with a durasteel finish and a long, rectangular emitter nozzle. This was unusual for a heavy blaster, as most had designs with long, thin barrels. DH-Xs came standard with a buttstock made of solid composite alloy and a monocular targeting optics suite.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DH-X heavy blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DH-X
- **Type:** Heavy blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,900 credits
- **Material:**
  - Composite alloy (Buttstock)
  - Durasteel (Barrel)
- **Range:** Long
