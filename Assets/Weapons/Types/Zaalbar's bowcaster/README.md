# Zaalbar's bowcaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zaalbar's bowcaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zaalbar%27s_bowcaster)

Zaalbar's bowcaster was one of Zaalbar's only personal possessions. Over many years of exile, he made many modifications but it conformed greatly to the traditions of the Wookiee village of Rwookrrorro. When Zaalbar was captured by Gamorrean slavers in the Undercity of Taris, his bowcaster was confiscated but later retrieved after he had sworn a life debt to Revan.
