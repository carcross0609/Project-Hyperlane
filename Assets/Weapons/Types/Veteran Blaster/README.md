# Veteran Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Veteran Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veteran_Blaster)

The Veteran Blaster was a modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Veteran Blaster
- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Owners:** Zaina
- **Affiliation:**
