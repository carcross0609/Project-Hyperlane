# Ion pike

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ion pike](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ion_pike)

The Ion pike was a pike used to disable droids who have attempted to escape or were malfunctioning. The pikes were flexible enough to fit around armor and to power sources.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ion pike
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Purpose:** To disable escaped or malfunctioning droids
