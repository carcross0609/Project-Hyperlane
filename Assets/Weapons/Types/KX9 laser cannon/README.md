# KX9 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KX9 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KX9_laser_cannon)

The KX9 laser cannon, also known as the KX-9 Laser Cannon, was a laser cannon manufactured by the Taim & Bak company. The KX9 laser cannon consisted of four parts; the laser cannon, laser barrel, flashback suppressor, and the laser tip. T-65B X-wing starfighters utilized four KX9 laser cannons, one at the end of each S-foil, and the Delta-class T-3c shuttle was equipped with two twin KX-9s.
