# Deathhammer 17

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Deathhammer 17](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deathhammer_17)

The Deathhammer 17 was a model of blaster pistol that was manufactured by BlasTech Industries. In about 0 BBY, the holostar Javul Charn wielded a Deathhammer 17 after falling under attack from the Anomid assassin Edge on the planet Falleen.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Deathhammer 17
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
