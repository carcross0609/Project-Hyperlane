# LL-30 blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LL-30 blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LL-30_blaster_pistol/Legends)

The LL-30 was a blaster pistol manufactured by BlasTech Industries. A pair of these weapons were also used by the infamous bounty hunter Cad Bane, who modified them to his own personal specifications, dubbing them "Persuaders". Hondo Ohnaka's pirate gang also made use of this weapon.

## Specifications

- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** LL-30
