# Fotia's Fire launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fotia's Fire launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fotia%27s_Fire_launcher)

The Fotia's Fire launcher, also known as the Fotia's Fire cannon, was a type of cannon that fired Fotia's Fire projectiles, which burnt a starship's hull. The Tof Kingdom's Royal Fortune-class light clipper capital ships had one Fotia's Fire launcher amidships.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fotia's Fire launcher
- **Type:** Cannon
- **Culture:** Tof
- **Purpose:** Fire Fotia's Fire projectiles
- **Affiliation:** Tof Kingdom
