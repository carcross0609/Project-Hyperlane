# Imperial Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperial Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Rifle)

The Imperial Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blocky blaster rifle featured a worn black and silver base, with dark gray plating resembling the aesthetic of many Imperial vessels. The large barrel featured glowing white rectangles on the top and bottom of each side, sandwiching longer rectangular glowing green elements. Additionally, a white Imperial crest was stamped on the barrel, and the grip had brown strips of cloth bound around it.
