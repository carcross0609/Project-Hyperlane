# D-29 repulsor rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [D-29 repulsor rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D-29_repulsor_rifle)

The D-29 repulsor rifle was a type of repulsor rifle manufactured by SakTek. Able to knock large targets across a room or ocasionally simuluate high- and low-gravity environments, the large repulsor weapon was tuned to narrow, longer-range areas of effect to maximize effectiveness while reducing power drain, rarely utilized by bounty hunters, law enforcement, and pranksters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** D-29 repulsor rifle
- **Manufacturer:** SakTek
- **Cost:** 1,550 credits
- **Size:** Large
