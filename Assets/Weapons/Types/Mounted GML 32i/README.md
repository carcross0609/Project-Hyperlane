# Mounted GML 32i

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mounted GML 32i](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mounted_GML_32i)

The Mounted GML 32i was a type of missile launcher manufactured by Bryn & Gweith. They were often installed on Rapid Deployment stations.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mounted GML 32i
- **Manufacturer:** Bryn & Gweith
- **Type:** Anti-vehicle missile launcher
- **Cost:**
  - 8,000 credits (new)
  - 5,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 1
- **Capacity:** Five missiles
- **Range:** Two kilometers
