# KX9 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KX9 laser cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KX9_laser_cannon/Legends)

The KX9 laser cannon was a powerful laser cannon produced by the Taim & Bak weapons company and could operate both in and out of space. They were considerably more powerful than those on the various TIE models.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KX9 laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
- **Type:** Laser cannon
- **Range:**
  - 8 kilometers (optimum)
  - 80 kilometers (maximum)
