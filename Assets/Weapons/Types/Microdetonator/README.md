# Microdetonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Microdetonator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Microdetonator)

Microdetonators were detonators that were used as a low profile explosives. They were a discreet way of triggering small, targeted explosions, and were equipped with a timer to add to their effectiveness. One such microdetonator was used by the former bounty hunter Brand, by then a soldier of the Rebel Alliance's 61st Mobile Infantry, to destroy a chemical barrel inside the Galactic Empire's Distillery on the Mid Rim jungle planet Coyerti in 3 ABY. This caused a significant distraction, redirecting the attention of Imperial personnel inside to a potential saboteur and the chemical leak. After the detonation, Brand's squad was able to destroy the facility.
