# Model 12 repulsor mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Model 12 repulsor mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_12_repulsor_mine)

The Model 12 repulsor mine was a model of anti-vehicular mine produced by SoroSuub Corporation. It was available in the period following the Battle of Yavin during the Galactic Civil War between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire and cost 1400 credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model 12 repulsor mine
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** Model 12
- **Type:** Anti-vehicular mine
- **Cost:** 1400 credits
