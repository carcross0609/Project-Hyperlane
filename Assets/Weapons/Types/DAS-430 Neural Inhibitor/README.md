# DAS-430 Neural Inhibitor

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DAS-430 Neural Inhibitor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DAS-430_Neural_Inhibitor)

The DAS-430 Neural Inhibitor, also known as the Neural Inhibitor Projectile Launcher, was a projectile launcher used by the bounty hunter IG-88 that fired green blasts which slowed down and damaged its targets. It had a very similar design to the E-11 medium blaster rifle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DAS-430 Neural Inhibitor
- **Model:** DAS-430 Neural Inhibitor
- **Type:** Projectile launcher
- **Purpose:** Slowing down and damaging its targets
