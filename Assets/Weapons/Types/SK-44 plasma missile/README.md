# SK-44 plasma missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SK-44 plasma missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SK-44_plasma_missile)

The SK-44 was a model of plasma missile manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. during the Galactic Civil War and cost 160 credits. Its detonation is like the impact of a blaster bolt, with the power of a missile.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SK-44 plasma missile
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** SK-44
- **Type:** Plasma missile
- **Cost:** 160 credits
