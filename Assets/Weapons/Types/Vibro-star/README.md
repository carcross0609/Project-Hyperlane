# Vibro-star

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vibro-star](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vibro-star)

Vibro-stars were a type of throwing vibro weapon utilized by Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, who kept several in a sheath along his wrist. The stars were incredibly sharp and able to cut effortlessly through many materials, including bone and metal. During a fight in the Great Hall of the Nihil, Ro used a star on Tempest Runner Kassav Milliko when the Weequay attempted to use a blaster he was carrying in contravention of the Great Hall's rules regarding weapons, slicing off half of Milliko's hand and the butt of the blaster.
