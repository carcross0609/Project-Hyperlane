# AD-616K6KA

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AD-616K6KA](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AD-616K6KA)

During his tenure as a Deputy Inspector with Preox-Morlana's security service, Syril Karn carried a blaster pistol, of a model commonly carried by Pre-Mor personnel, stamped with the serial number AD-616K6KA. After Karn lost the weapon, it exchanged hands several times and became peripherally involved in certain central events of the early rebellion against the Galactic Empire.
