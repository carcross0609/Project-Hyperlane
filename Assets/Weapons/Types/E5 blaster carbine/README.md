# E5 blaster carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E5 blaster carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E5_blaster_carbine)

The E5 blaster carbine, or E-5 blaster carbine, was a blaster carbine used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems throughout the Clone Wars and later by criminal, paramilitary, or Rebel groups during the Galactic Civil War. It was a shortened version of the B1 battle droid's famous E-5 blaster rifle manufactured by Baktoid Armor Workshop.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E5 blaster carbine
- **Manufacturer:** Baktoid Armor Workshop
- **Model:** E5
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Created:** 22 BBY
- **Cost:**
  - 900 credits (Original price)
  - 550 credits (Galactic Civil War price)
- **Weight:** 2.2 kg
- **Range:** 20 meters
- **Affiliation:**
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - HK-47's Droid Army
