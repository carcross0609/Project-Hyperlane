# Huttballer Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Huttballer Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huttballer_Clubs)

The Huttballer Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They consisted of a pair of B1-series battle droid legs, with the ankles bound in brown cloth to serve as hand grips.
