# Jicoln Cadera's beskad

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jicoln Cadera's beskad](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jicoln_Cadera%27s_beskad)

During his time as a champion of the Mandalorian warrior clans, Jicoln Cadera owned and wielded a beskad. The beskar sword was decorated with the skulls of felled warriors, and bore a hilt adorned with the skull of a ferocious Krayt dragon, symbolizing the hunger for war and destruction prized by Mandalorians of the era.
