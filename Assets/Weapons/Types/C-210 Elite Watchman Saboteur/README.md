# C-210 Elite Watchman Saboteur

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C-210 Elite Watchman Saboteur](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-210_Elite_Watchman_Saboteur)

The C-210 Elite Watchman Saboteur was a make of blaster pistol available on worlds such as Coruscant and Dromund Kaas during the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. They were silver in color and sold for 135 credits. Parts of the weapon were modifiable, including its color crystal, though the unmodified version fired orange blaster bolts.

- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 135 credits
