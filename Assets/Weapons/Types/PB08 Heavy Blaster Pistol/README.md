# PB08 Heavy Blaster Pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PB08 Heavy Blaster Pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PB08_Heavy_Blaster_Pistol)

Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.'s PB08 Heavy Blaster Pistol was one of the most sturdy and reliable blaster pistols on the market. It featured a short ventilated barrel and a heat management system. The internal gas was circulated in heat sinks in the barrel, causing a unique energy management that economized the tibanna stores. It cost 800 credits.
