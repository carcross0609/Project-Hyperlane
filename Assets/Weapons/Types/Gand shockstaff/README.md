# Gand shockstaff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gand shockstaff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand_shockstaff)

Used by the famous Gand findsmen to herd their prey, the Gand shockstaff was a powerful weapon in which its stunning capability was backed up by its sharp tip. While not too powerful in a combat situation, it did deliver an electrical bonus.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gand shockstaff
- **Type:** Electrostaff
- **Culture:** Gand
- **Creators:** Gand findsmen
- **Purpose:** To herd the Gand Findsman Prey
