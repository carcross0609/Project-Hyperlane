# Rancor Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rancor Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rancor_Scatter_Gun)

The Rancor Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It appeared to be adorned with rancor claws, teeth, and other bones.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rancor Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
