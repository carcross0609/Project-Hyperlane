# RT-97C heavy blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RT-97C heavy blaster rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RT-97C_heavy_blaster_rifle/Legends)

The RT-97C heavy blaster rifle was a designation given to certain model of offensive blaster weaponry that produced more devastating results than its lesser cousin, the blaster rifle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RT-97C heavy blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** RT-97C
- **Type:** Heavy blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits
- **Size:** 108 cm
- **Length:**
  - 42.4 inches
  - Barrel: 27 inches
- **Weight:** 8.1 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 1500 shots of gas, 500 shots from power cells
- **Range:** 1500 meters
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
