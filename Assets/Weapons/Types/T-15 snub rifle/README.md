# T-15 snub rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [T-15 snub rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-15_snub_rifle)

The T-15 snub rifle was a powerful infantry weapon produced by BlasTech Industries under the New Order. Heavier than a standard E-11 blaster rifle, it was sometimes carried by stormtrooper squad leaders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** T-15 snub rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
