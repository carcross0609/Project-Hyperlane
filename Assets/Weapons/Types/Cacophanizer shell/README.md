# Cacophanizer shell

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cacophanizer shell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cacophanizer_shell)

Cacophanizer shells were artillery weapons used by the Galactic Republic during the Great Galactic War. They were utilized at the Invasion of Begeren and the Battle of Ord Radama. Their effects could only be overcome through the use of a sonic damper.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cacophanizer shell
- **Type:** Artillery
- **Locations:**
  - Begeren
  - Ord Radama
- **Purpose:** Sonic damage
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Republic
