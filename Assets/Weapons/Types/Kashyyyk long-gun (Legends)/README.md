# Kashyyyk long-gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kashyyyk long-gun/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kashyyyk_long-gun/Legends)

The Kashyyyk long-gun, also known as Long-gun, or simply as the Wookiee Blaster Rifle, was a model of rifle weapon. Used almost exclusively by the Wookiees, the long-gun's bronzium alloy barrel offered no scope or rangefinder, limiting the weapon to use in short-range combat. The weapon used a 10-shot ammunition clip, and each shot would incur a powerful recoil. The Wookiee leader Tarfful wielded a long-gun during the defense of Kachirho. It weighed 8 kilograms and cost 2,000 credits.
