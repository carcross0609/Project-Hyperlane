# Ewok Festival of Love Crossbow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ewok Festival of Love Crossbow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ewok_Festival_of_Love_Crossbow)

The Ewok Festival of Love Crossbow was a unique weapon, offered to spacers during the Festival of Love in exchange for Chak hearts. If used on an enemy, the arrows would cause the enemy's hate of you to lessen.

- **Name:** Ewok Festival of Love Crossbow
- **Type:** Crossbow
