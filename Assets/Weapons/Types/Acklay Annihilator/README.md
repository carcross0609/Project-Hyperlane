# Acklay Annihilator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Acklay Annihilator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acklay_Annihilator)

The Acklay Annihilator was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle consisted of a black, gray, and silver frame, with a bony fragment covered in spikes, potentially once belonging to an acklay, strapped to the top.
