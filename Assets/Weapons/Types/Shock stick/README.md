# Shock stick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shock stick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shock_stick)

A shock stick was a small stick-weapon that could deliver an electric shock. Members of the Kintan Striders Gang wielded shock sticks. The scavenger Devi wore one such weapon tied to her left leg.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shock stick
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Affiliation:** Kintan Striders Gang
