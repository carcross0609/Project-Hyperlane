# Eradicator (weapon)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Eradicator (weapon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eradicator_%28weapon%29)

Eradicators, partially developed by the Imperial Science Bureau of the Sith Empire under the codename Special Project 62991A, were exotic satellite based weapons developed during the years of the Cold War.
