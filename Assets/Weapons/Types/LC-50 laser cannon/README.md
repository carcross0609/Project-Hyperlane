# LC-50 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LC-50 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LC-50_laser_cannon)

The LC-50 laser cannon was a model of laser cannon that could be equipped to starships. In 3 ABY, the Trailblazer, an EML-850 light freighter, was equipped with a LC-50 laser cannon when the thief Kay Vess stole it from the Imperial Security Bureau Director Sliro Barsha's Westhill Palace in the city of Canto Bight on the planet Cantonica.

- **Manufacturer:** Dymek
- **Model:** LC-50
