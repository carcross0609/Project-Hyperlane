# Concordia WESTAR-35

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Concordia WESTAR-35](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Concordia_WESTAR-35)

The Concordia WESTAR-35 was a pair of WESTAR-35 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. Unlike his usual MT-97s, these WESTARs harkened back to the Mandalorian warriors exiled to Concordia, who would eventually lead the Death Watch.
