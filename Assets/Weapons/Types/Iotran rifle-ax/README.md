# Iotran rifle-ax

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Iotran rifle-ax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Iotran_rifle-ax)

The Iotran rifle-ax was an exotic weapon traditional to the Iotran Guard employed by the Muuns of Muunilinst. It consisted of a vibro-ax with a blaster pistol embedded in the handle. Iotrans were well trained in both melee and ranged combat styles.
