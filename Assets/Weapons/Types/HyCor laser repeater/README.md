# HyCor laser repeater

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HyCor laser repeater](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HyCor_laser_repeater)

A HyCor laser repeater was a type of vent-barreled laser repeater. By 4 ABY, the human Corwin Ballast owned one of the weapons and carried it strapped to his body, under his jacket. While visiting the Inner Rim planet Jakku that year, Ballast was threatened by a rag-man and brandished his laser repeater to wave off the man's threat.
