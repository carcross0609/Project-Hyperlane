# AG-2G quad laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AG-2G quad laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AG-2G_quad_laser_cannon)

The AG-2G quad laser cannon was a vintage quad laser cannon model and the primary defensive weapons of the Millennium Falcon when Finn and Rey commandeered the ship thirty years after the Battle of Endor. AG-2Gs were manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AG-2G quad laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Model:** AG-2G
- **Type:** Quad laser cannon
