# PCC-64 blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [PCC-64 blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PCC-64_blaster_pistol)

The PCC-64 blaster pistol was a model of blaster pistol. In 9 ABY,, the Devaronian Burg carried a pair of PCC-64 blaster pistols to rescue the prisoner Qin from the Bothan-5. Din Djarin had a PCC-64 blaster pistol at the bottom in the right column in his weapon locker aboard the Razor Crest. Around that year, while escaping with the Skeleton Crew, Jod Na Nawood on Borgo Prime before being captured by his old pirate crew on Lanupa.
