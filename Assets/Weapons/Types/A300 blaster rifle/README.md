# A300 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [A300 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A300_blaster_rifle)

The A300 blaster rifle, also known as the A-300 blaster rifle, was a blaster rifle manufactured by BlasTech Industries, as part of the A-series. It was notably used by many Rebel Alliance Special Forces members of Rogue One during the Battle of Scarif.
