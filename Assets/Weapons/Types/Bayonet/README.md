# Bayonet

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bayonet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bayonet)

A bayonet was a sharp blade that could be mounted on the end of a blaster rifle or other long-barreled rifle. Bayonets could be purpose-made to attach to a weapon, be regular knives attached to rifles by improvised means, or be built into the weapon itself, as with certain models of bo-rifle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bayonet
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Purpose:** Use rifle as spear-like melee weapon
