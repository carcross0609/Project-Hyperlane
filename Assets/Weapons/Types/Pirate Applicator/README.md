# Pirate Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pirate Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pirate_Applicator)

The Pirate Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This colorful applicator featured a dark gray silver, and purple frame, with various glowing elements, including an orange button, pink core, greenish barrel lights, and white pattern on the stock. Other white and yellow patterns could be found drawn on the gun as well, in addition to a curved, pointed bayonet attachment underneath the barrel.
