# KX5 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KX5 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KX5_laser_cannon)

The KX5 laser cannon was a laser cannon developed by the Taim & Bak. It could be used as an alternative to IX4 laser cannons on BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bombers. They were also used on Z-95 Headhunter starfighters.

- **Name:** KX5 laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
