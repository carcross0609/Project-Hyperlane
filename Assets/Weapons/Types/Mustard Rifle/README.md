# Mustard Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mustard Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mustard_Rifle)

The Mustard Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle consisted of a worn black, silver, and light gray frame, with a metallic red gradient around the tip of the barrel, mustard and grayish-brown colored painted portions, and mustard colored strips of cloth bound around the grip, stock, and base of the barrel. The name "Vex" was also printed on the sides of the blaster in aurebesh.
