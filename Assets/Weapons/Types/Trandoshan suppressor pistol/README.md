# Trandoshan suppressor pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Trandoshan suppressor pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trandoshan_suppressor_pistol)

The Trandoshan suppressor pistol was a a pistol that fired electric charges, used by Trandoshans at the time of the Galactic Civil War. It was manufactured with the intent of subduing individuals without causing too much damage. Much to the Trandoshan's delight, the weapon caused excruciating pain when hit.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Trandoshan suppressor pistol
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Affiliation:** Trandoshans
