# Thunderbolt Shock Prod

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thunderbolt Shock Prod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thunderbolt_Shock_Prod)

The Thunderbolt Shock Prod was a type of shock prod produced by SoroSuub Corporation. A nearly two meter solid alloy staff, the weapon had an insulated, two-handed grip on one end and sharp prongs on the other that generated an arc of powerful, non-lethal electricity able to tame a krayt dragon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thunderbolt Shock Prod
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Shock prod
- **Cost:** 875 credits
- **Size:** Nearly two meters
