# Model 9 armor piercing grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Model 9 armor piercing grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_9_armor_piercing_grenade)

Model 9 armor piercing grenades were a type of armor-piercing grenades available during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. They were produced by Merr-Sonn and cost 100 credits.

- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** Model 9
- **Type:** Armor-piercing grenade
