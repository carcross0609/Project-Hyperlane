# Firespray DL-87 stun rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firespray DL-87 stun rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firespray_DL-87_stun_rifle)

The BlasTech Industries Firespray DL-87 stun rifle was an unusual blaster rifle. It was primarily designed to knock out opponents rather than killing them. It had a wide muzzle, with two transceptors mounted in the corners of the barrel. It could deliver normal damage, but it was short-ranged and did very little actual damage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Firespray DL-87 stun rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DL-87
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 4.5 kg
- **Capacity:** 10 shots
- **Range:** 200 meters (maximum)
