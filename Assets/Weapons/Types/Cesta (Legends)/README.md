# Cesta

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cesta/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cesta/Legends)

The cesta was a wooden military staff produced by the Otoh Gunga Defense League and employed by the Gungan Grand Army on Naboo.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cesta
- **Manufacturer:** Otoh Gunga Defense League
- **Culture:** Gungans
- **Cost:** 100 credits
- **Size:** two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 1.8 kg
- **Capacity:** 1 booma
- **Range:** hurling boomas up to 120 meters
- **Purpose:**
  - hurling boomas or
  - staff-like melee weapon
- **Affiliation:** Gungan Grand Army
