# Infiltrator Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Infiltrator Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltrator_Blaster)

The Bespin Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster.

- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
- **Type:**

  -   Veteran Blaster
- **Owners:** Zaina
