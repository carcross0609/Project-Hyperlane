# Savage's spear

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Savage's spear/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Savage%27s_spear/Legends)

A spear was owned by Zabrak assassin Savage Opress. He used it to kill Jedi Master Halsey and Padawan Knox at the Temple of Eedit on the planet Devaron. It was said to be enchanted by Nightsister magic.
