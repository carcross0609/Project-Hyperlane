# Mounted CGL 55ci

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mounted CGL 55ci](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mounted_CGL_55ci)

The Mounted CGL 55ci was a type of concussion grenade launcher manufactured by Bryn & Gweith. They were often installed on Rapid Deployment stations.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mounted CGL 55ci
- **Manufacturer:** Bryn & Gweith
- **Type:** Concussion grenade launcher
- **Cost:**
  - 8,000 credits (new)
  - 5,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 1
- **Capacity:** 25 concussion grenades
- **Range:** 200 meters
