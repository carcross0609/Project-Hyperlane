# Model CR-28 flame cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Model CR-28 flame cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_CR-28_flame_cannon)

The model CR-28 flame cannon manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. was a larger version of the regular flamethrower with a longer range, hotter temperature, and greater fuel capacity.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model CR-28 flame cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** CR-28
- **Type:** Flamethrower
- **Cost:** 2,500 to 3,000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:**
  - Too large to use as personal weapon
  - Must be mounted on vehicle or tripod
- **Weight:** 25 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 20 shots or 50 shots
  - Shoots a large cone of fire 18 meters long and 12 meters wide at the terminus
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 10 m
  - Maximum: up to 20 m
- **Purpose:**
  - Clearing out bunkers
  - Shock and terror
- **Affiliation:** Corporate Sector Authority
