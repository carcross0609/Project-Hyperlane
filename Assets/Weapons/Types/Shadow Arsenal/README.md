# Shadow Arsenal

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shadow Arsenal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Arsenal)

The Shadow Arsenal was the term given to a large quantity of superweapons developed by the Galactic Republic by the end of the Great Galactic War. It was orchestrated by Colonel Laren Omas of Republic Special Forces Division who was involved with weapon research projects and brought together some of the galaxy's greatest scientists who were led by Doctor Nasan Godera. Their mission was to create warheads designed to bring about complete destruction to the restored Sith Empire.
