# Grievous Grafted Shields

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grievous Grafted Shields](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grievous_Grafted_Shields)

The Grievous Grafted Shields were a pair of shields owned by the KX-series security droid Hunter K-A0S that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard Grafted Twin Shields. When combined, the front of this shield formed the face of the cyborg General Grievous.
