# Vicrul's scythe

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vicrul's scythe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vicrul%27s_scythe)

Vicrul's scythe was the primary melee weapon of Knight of Ren Vicrul. It had a ultrasonic vibro-tech settings that increased the lethality such that even glancing blows could produce deadly lacerations. It was made of tempered steel impregnated with phrik filaments, making it incredibly strong. Though a lightsaber blade could cut through the weapon, it required a greater application of pressure to do so. The scythe was used against Ben Solo during the Battle of Exegol.
