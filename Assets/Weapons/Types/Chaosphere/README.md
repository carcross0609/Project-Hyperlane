# Chaosphere

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Chaosphere](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chaosphere)

Chaospheres were weapons developed by the Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Mekhis during the Great Galactic War. Mekhis deployed the chaospheres during the Battle of Rhen Var.
