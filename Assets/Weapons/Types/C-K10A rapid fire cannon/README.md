# C-K10A rapid fire cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C-K10A rapid fire cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-K10A_rapid_fire_cannon)

The C-K10A rapid fire cannon was a model of laser cannon that could be equipped to starships and could fire bursts at multiple targets. Equipping an EML-850 light freighter with a C-K10A rapid fire cannon cost seven hundred and fifty credits and required ten units of quadranium, and four 4XB Calculation Units.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C-K10A rapid fire cannon
- **Model:** C-K10A
- **Type:** Laser cannon
- **Cost:** 750 credits
