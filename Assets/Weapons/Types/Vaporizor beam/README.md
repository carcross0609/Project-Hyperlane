# Vaporizor beam

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vaporizor beam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vaporizor_beam)

Vaporizer beams were equipped on the planetoids that served as hazards in the final round of the Big Game. While the planetoids exploded on impact, anything close enough to hit the proximity force field would cause the planetoid to launch deadly vaporizer beams.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vaporizor beam
- **Locations:** The Wheel
- **Purpose:** Vaporization
- **Affiliation:** The Wheel
