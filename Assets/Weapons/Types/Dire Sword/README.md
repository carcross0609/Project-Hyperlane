# Dire Sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Dire Sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dire_Sword)

The dire sword was an archaic weapon used by duelists and other students of classical fighting styles. Crafted with tradition rather than technology, the weapon was basically a large two-handed longsword.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dire Sword
- **Type:** Sword
- **Cost:** 100 credits
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Range:** Melee weapon
