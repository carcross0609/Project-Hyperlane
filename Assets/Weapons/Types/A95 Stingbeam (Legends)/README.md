# A95 Stingbeam

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [A95 Stingbeam/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A95_Stingbeam/Legends)

The A95 Stingbeam, produced by Czerka Arms, was a compact and easily concealable energy weapon, among the smallest blasters available. Small enough to fit in the palm of a hand, it operated similarly to a traditional blaster but focused its blast along a needle-like rod between the user’s fingers. Effective at close range, the A95 Stingbeam was capable of lethal force, though its blasts quickly lost cohesion over distance.
