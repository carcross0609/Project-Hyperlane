# Primeval E-Web

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Primeval E-Web](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Primeval_E-Web)

The Primeval E-Web was an E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon owned by the human Hunter Sentinel that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard E-Web. Sentinel seemed to have a storied history with this weapon, supposedly relying on it heavily during his early days in the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Primeval E-Web
- **Model:** E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon (Sentinel's E-Web)
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Owners:** Sentinel
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Hunters of the Outer Rim
