# Mandalorian training darts

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mandalorian training darts](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_training_darts)

Mandalorian training darts were a harmless weapon used for training. During the New Republic Era, the Mandalorian foundlings Ragnar and Grogu used the training darts to duel each other. The dart splattered when it hit a target. Later, Grogu, accompanied by several Anzellans in a sewer tunnel, shot an aggressive rat-like creature in its one eye, scaring it off and prompting praise from one of the youngling's diminutive companions.
