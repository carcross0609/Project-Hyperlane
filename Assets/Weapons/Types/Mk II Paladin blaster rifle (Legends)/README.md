# Mk II Paladin blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mk II Paladin blaster rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mk_II_Paladin_blaster_rifle/Legends)

The Mk II Paladin blaster rifle, also simply just known as the Coruscant Senate Guard Blaster Rifle, was a model of blaster rifle that was used by the Senate Guards of the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mk II Paladin blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** Mk II
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:**
  - 4,000 credits (c. 32 BBY)
  - 3,250 credits (c. 0 BBY)
  - 7,500 credits (c. 5 ABY)
- **Size:** Large
- **Weight:** 7 kilogram
- **Range:**
  - 30 meters (optimum)
  - 300 meters (maximum)
- **Affiliation:** Senate Guard
