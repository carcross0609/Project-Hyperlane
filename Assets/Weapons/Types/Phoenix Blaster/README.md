# Phoenix Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phoenix Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phoenix_Blaster)

The Phoenix Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This multicolored blaster pistol was black, purple, white, yellow, orange, red, green, and gold in coloration, resembling the general aesthetic of the famous rebel starship, the Ghost.
