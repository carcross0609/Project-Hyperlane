# Ap'lek's Mandalorian executioner vibro-ax

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ap'lek's Mandalorian executioner vibro-ax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ap%27lek%27s_Mandalorian_executioner_vibro-ax)

Ap'lek's Mandalorian executioner vibro-ax was an antiquated Mandalorian executioner's vibro-ax that was the weapon of choice for the Knight of Ren Ap'lek. Ap'lek fought with the vibro-ax against Ben Solo on Exegol, but it availed him naught and he perished at the hands of his former leader.
