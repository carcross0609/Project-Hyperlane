# TU-C6 laser turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TU-C6 laser turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TU-C6_laser_turret)

A TU-C6 laser turret was a model of laser turret that could be equipped on a starship. It was more powerful than the TU-B4 laser turret. Equipping an EML-850 light freighter with a TU-C6 turret cost two thousand and five hundred credits and required a X-FP-C28 ship computer, thirty units of quadanium steel, and fifteen heuristic processors.
