# Wampa's Nightmare Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wampa's Nightmare Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wampa%27s_Nightmare_Rifle)

The Wampa's Nightmare Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This worn blaster rifle featured the standard Imperial aesthetic, with a metallic black frame and vented barrel, as well as a white casing over the body featuring gray designs and a faded red Imperial crest stamped on each side.
