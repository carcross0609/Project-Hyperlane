# Battleworn Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Battleworn Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battleworn_Applicator)

The Battleworn Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. It featured a glowing pink core, bluish-green plating, light blue wiring, and white and pink straps wrapped around a basic black and silver frame.
