# Sonic Imploder

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sonic Imploder](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_Imploder)

A Sonic Imploder was an explosive device used by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War, though it also saw use by the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It used sound waves to temporarily blind and weaken enemy units within its vicinity, though it could also be lethal if said units were in close proximity to it upon detonation. It bore a strong resemblance to the C-25 fragmentation grenade.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sonic Imploder
- **Type:** Explosive
- **Shape:** Near-cylindrical
- **Purpose:** Anti-infantry
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
