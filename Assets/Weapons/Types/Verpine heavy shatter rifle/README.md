# Verpine heavy shatter rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Verpine heavy shatter rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verpine_heavy_shatter_rifle)

The Verpine heavy shatter rifle was a variant of the shatter weaponry created by the insectoid Verpine. The heavy shatter rifle exchanged critical rating for increased damage and accuracy, and shots fired from the weapon could pierce the armor more effectively, a favorable trade-off for the increased weight. The cost of the Verpine heavy shatter rifle was 45,000 credits.
