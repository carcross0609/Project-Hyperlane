# Disruption gloves

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Disruption gloves](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disruption_gloves)

Disruption gloves, also known as disruptor gloves, were a rare type of glove in use during a series of conflicts fought between the remnants of the Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic known as the Dark Wars. During unarmed combat, the gloves allowed a user to penetrate many types of defenses, including deflector shields.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Disruption gloves
- **Type:** Gloves
- **Created:** By 3951 BBY
- **Cost:** 26,000 credits
- **Range:** Unarmed combat
- **Purpose:** Penetrating deflector shields in unarmed combat
