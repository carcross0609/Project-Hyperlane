# LJ-50 concussion rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LJ-50 concussion rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LJ-50_concussion_rifle)

The LJ-50 concussion rifle was a model of powerful and deadly blaster rifle produced by Arakyd Industries. After a shipment of LJ-50s was accidentally sent to a squad in the 92nd Coruscant Engineers, the squad's armorer saw their potential for breaching walls and scattering enemy troopers and so stripped them down into blaster carbines.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LJ-50 concussion rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Model:** LJ-50
