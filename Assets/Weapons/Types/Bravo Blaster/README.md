# Bravo Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bravo Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bravo_Blaster)

The Bravo Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This blaster pistol featured a black body, brown wood-like grip, gray plating, silver scope, and a silver and golden cylinder attached to the underbarrel, all covered in worn red markings.

- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
