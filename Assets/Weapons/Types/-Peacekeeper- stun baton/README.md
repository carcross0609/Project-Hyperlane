# "Peacekeeper" stun baton

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** ["Peacekeeper" stun baton](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%22Peacekeeper%22_stun_baton)

The "Peacekeeper" stun baton, also known simply as the "Peacekeeper" baton, was a model of stun baton manufactured by Czerka Arms and sold for 500 credits. It was a heavy, lengthy baton made out of metal and had powerful stun capabilities that could still be felt after getting hit by it. The weapon's shock capabilities led to multiple conscientious groups blacklisting it, as well as discontinuing its use. Some of the Galactic Empire's governors mass ordered the weapon during the Galactic Civil War to keep their populations in check. The weapon was also popular among bounty hunters, who used the baton to circumvent laws and their client's request for non-lethal weaponry.
