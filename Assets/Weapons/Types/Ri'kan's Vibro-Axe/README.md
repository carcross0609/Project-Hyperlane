# Ri'kan's Vibro-Axe

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ri'kan's Vibro-Axe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ri%27kan%27s_Vibro-Axe)

By the time of the Third Galactic War, Ri'kan's Vibro-Axe was a vibro-axe utilized by Ri'kan Kateen, a Force-sensitive Twi'lek who served as a Mandalorian of the Hidden Chain. The vibro-axe conducted an open electric current that could be either yellow or blue in coloration.
