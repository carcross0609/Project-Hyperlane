# Sonic cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sonic cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_cannon)

Sonic cannons were a type of weapon. The LR1K sonic cannon was used by the Geonosians during the First and Second Battle of Geonosis. During the Imperial Era, cyborg human Beilert Valance travelled to the Outer Rim planet of Arvina to acquire sonic cannons from the arms dealer Fetya.
