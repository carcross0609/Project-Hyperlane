# Electromagnetic pulse grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Electromagnetic pulse grenade/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electromagnetic_pulse_grenade/Legends)

The electromagnetic pulse grenade, also known as an EMP grenade, haywire grenade, Electrostatic Charge Detonator, ECD, and often referred to by Republic clone troopers as droid poppers, was an anti-droid weapon often used by the Grand Army of the Republic—specifically by the Army's elite soldiers, the clone commandos—during the Clone Wars against battle droid opponents.

- **Name:** Electromagnetic pulse grenade
- **Manufacturer:**

- **Type:** Electrostatic Charge Grenade
- **Cost:** 500 credits
- **Size:** Hand-held
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg
- **Capacity:** Single-use
- **Range:**

  - 3-meter blast radius
- **Purpose:** Anti-droid weapon
- **Affiliation:**

  -   Grand Army of the Republic
  -     Clone troopers
  -     Clone jet troopers
