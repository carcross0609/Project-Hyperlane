# LD-1 target blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LD-1 target blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LD-1_target_blaster_rifle)

The Merr-Sonn LD-1 target blaster rifle (also called a targeting blaster rifle) was technically a sporting blaster rifle, but in actuality it was nearly as powerful as a military-grade blaster rifle like the E-11. This was intentional - the goal of the project was to create a weapon that approaches the damage of a blaster rifle without the pesky legal restrictions that come with such weapons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LD-1 target blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** LD-1
- **Type:** Sporting blaster rifle
- **Culture:** Assassins
- **Cost:** 1,000 to 1,275 credits
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 200 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 90 m
  - Maximum: up to 620 m
- **Purpose:** Long-range shooting
