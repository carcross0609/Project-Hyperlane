# Ap/11 double laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ap/11 double laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ap/11_double_laser_cannon)

The Ap/11 was a model of laser cannon designed for use on vehicles produced by the Corellian Engineering Corporation during the Galactic Civil War. The Rebel Alliance fitted one Ap/11 double laser cannon to each of a number of Incom T-47 airspeeders as part of their modification into combat-worthy "snowspeeders" shortly before the Battle of Hoth. It could destroy an AT-ST with three shots but proved to have no effect on AT-AT's.
