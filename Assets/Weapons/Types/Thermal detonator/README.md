# Thermal detonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Thermal detonator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thermal_detonator)

Thermal detonators, also known as thermal charges or thermal devices, were palm-sized, spherical devices that were used as extremely deadly explosive weapons. Different models of thermal detonators saw use across galactic history, resulting in their wide-spread usage by militaries and third party actors alike.
