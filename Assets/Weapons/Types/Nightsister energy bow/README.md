# Nightsister energy bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nightsister energy bow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightsister_energy_bow)

The Nightsister energy bow was a type of handcrafted energy bow used by the Nightsisters and Nightbrothers of Dathomir. Its string and arrows were made from plasma that emitted a bright pink or orange glow. During the Clone Wars, a special group of Nightsisters known as the Hunters used those bows to kill targets designated by Mother Talzin.
