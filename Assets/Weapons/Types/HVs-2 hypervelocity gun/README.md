# HVs-2 hypervelocity gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HVs-2 hypervelocity gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HVs-2_hypervelocity_gun)

The HVs-2 hypervelocity gun, or simply hypervelocity gun, or sometimes referred as an electromagnetic gun, was a fixed planetary defense weapon used against orbiting space-craft such as capital ships by the Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HVs-2 hypervelocity gun
- **Manufacturer:**

  - Taim & Bak
- **Model:** HVs-2 hypervelocity gun
- **Type:** Projectile cannon
- **Locations:** Various worlds
- **Protection:**
  - Ray shield generator
  - Particle shield generator
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
