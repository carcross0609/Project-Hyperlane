# Wookiee projectile rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wookiee projectile rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wookiee_projectile_rifle)

The Wookiee projectile rifle was a primitive slugthrower rifle used and produced by Wookiees on Kashyyyk. The rifle utilized the explosive force produced by a chemical product or compressed gas to propel its projectiles at high speeds. The weapon had an effective range of 300 meters and was made in-part with bronzium.
