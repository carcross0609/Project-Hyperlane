# M-40 "Thunderbolt"

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M-40 "Thunderbolt"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-40_%22Thunderbolt%22)

The M-40 "Thunderbolt" was a model of light repeating blaster. Produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc., the blaster rifle could fire a long-ranged distance. As a light repeating blaster, the weapon had a high rate of fire fed from a power magazine or a backpack-mounted power generator, was over two meters long, and also possessed a stun setting. Often carried by a support gunner with the required tools and training, legal purchase of such blasters was restricted to the Imperial Army, planetary defense forces, and private military contractors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M-40 "Thunderbolt"
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Light repeating blaster
- **Cost:** 2,250 credits
- **Size:** Over two meters
- **Range:** Long
