# L04 Silent Thunder concussion missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [L04 Silent Thunder concussion missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/L04_Silent_Thunder_concussion_missile)

The L04 Silent Thunder concussion missile was a type of concussion missile produced by Balmorran Arms during the Galactic War between the Republic and the Sith Empire. Too large for any typical starfighter to carry in large numbers, the Silent Thunder was designed to lock onto a target and use its onboard fuel supply to travel long distances and destroy the enemy starship. Only two L04 missiles were typically required to destroy a single enemy craft.

- **Manufacturer:** Balmorran Arms
