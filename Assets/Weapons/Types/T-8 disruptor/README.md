# T-8 disruptor

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [T-8 disruptor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/T-8_disruptor)

The T-8 disruptor was a model of disruptor used by the Galactic Empire. In the year 4 BBY, Tseebo, an Imperial Information Office worker, used his AJ^6 cyborg construct to steal the then-new T-8's specifications before defecting to the rebellion.
