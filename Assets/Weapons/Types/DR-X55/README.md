# DR-X55

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DR-X55](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DR-X55)

The DR-X55 was a lift-mine made by Norsam Corp. The mine could detect enemy vehicles 100 meters away, and alter its altitude to intercept the target. It had a maximum flight ceiling of five hundred meters, and detonated when a target was within three meters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DR-X55
- **Manufacturer:** Norsam Corp.
- **Type:** Lift-mine
- **Diam:** 1 meter
- **Purpose:** Fortification
