# Garrote

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Garrote](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Garrote)

Garrotes were a type of quiet melee weapon used for assassinations. They were extremely simple weapons, being composed out of a length of cord or cable, with a handle attached to each end. They were easy to conceal and could be made out of readily available materials on the field. Garrotes worked by being tightened around a target's neck to stop airflow. They typically cost 50 credits on the market and could either kill or knockout their targets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Garrote
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Cost:** 50 credits
- **Purpose:** Assassinations
