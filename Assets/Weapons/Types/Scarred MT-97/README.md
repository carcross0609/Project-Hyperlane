# Scarred MT-97

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Scarred MT-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scarred_MT-97)

The Scarred MT-97 was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. These pistols resembled Tal's aforementioned standard twin blasters, but with much more visible wear.
