# Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" blaster cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ax-108_%22Ground_Buzzer%22_blaster_cannon)

The Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" blaster cannon was a blaster cannon model produced by BlasTech Industries that Han Solo mounted on the Millennium Falcon for defense while the freighter was on the ground. It had a dedicated generator so that it could work even if the ship's electrical system was down. The blaster cannon had a fire rate of 12 blasts per second and had a range of power settings from stun to armor-piercing. The cannon could be controlled manually or by the ship's computer, which would prevent it from hitting friendlies or parts of the ship itself.
