# Mark II EWHB-10

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mark II EWHB-10](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_II_EWHB-10)

The Mark II EWHB-10, also known as the Mark II medium repeating blaster cannon or simply the Mark II Medium Blaster, was a blaster cannon produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.. It was a variant of the E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon. Rebel Alliance forces used the Mark II EWHB-10 at Echo Base during the battle of Hoth.

- **Name:** Mark II EWHB-10
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** E-web (variant)
- **Type:** Blaster cannon
