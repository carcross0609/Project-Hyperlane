# Tasari Crossbow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tasari Crossbow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tasari_Crossbow)

The Tasari Crossbow was a traditional weapon among Tasari warriors, and a particular favorite of blood cultists. Each weapon was individually manufactured as dictated by tradition, and they were never mass-produced. The construction of a crossbow was a significant event in a Tasari's life, and marked the passage from childhood to becoming an adult.

- **Manufacturer:** Individual manufacturers
- **Type:** Crossbow
