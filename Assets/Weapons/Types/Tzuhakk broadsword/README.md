# Tzuhakk broadsword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tzuhakk broadsword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tzuhakk_broadsword)

The Tzuhakk broadsword was a Sith sword recovered by the Jedi Master Mace Windu from the double planet Bpfassh by 19 BBY, during the waning years of the Galactic Republic. Subsequently, Master Windu wrote on a section of Sorzus Syn's chronicle—later assembled into the Book of Sith—explaining that he placed the sword in the Jedi Temple's Black Vault, the Jedi Order's dedicated storage place of Sith weaponry located on the Republic capital world of Coruscant.
