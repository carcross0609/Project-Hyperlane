# "Renegade" heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** ["Renegade" heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%22Renegade%22_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The "Renegade" heavy blaster pistol was a type of heavy blaster pistol manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. It occupied a niche similar in design to that of the T-6 "Thunderer" of BlasTech Industries and the C-10 "Dragoneye Reaper" of Czerka Arms, and it featured the highest energy output of any SoroSuub blaster pistol model. More lightweight and better priced than its competition, the Renegade dominated the super-heavy blaster pistol market, however, most enthusiasts eschewed the mass-market weapon due to its poor beam cohesion and awkwardly angeled grip, and preferred pricier but higher-quality options.
