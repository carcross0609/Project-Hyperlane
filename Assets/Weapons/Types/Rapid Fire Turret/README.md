# Rapid Fire Turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rapid Fire Turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rapid_Fire_Turret)

The Rapid Fire Turret was a weapon that was used by both the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. In defense, it excels at taking out support units, and has no specific weaknesses against any other units, playing a generalist role.

- **Name:** Rapid Fire Turret
- **Type:** Turret
