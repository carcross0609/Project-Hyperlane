# Laser trip mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Laser trip mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Laser_trip_mine)

Laser trip mines, also known as personnel mines, were powerful explosive devices used for infantry combat during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance. Projecting a laser trigger upon activation, the mine would detonate when enemy soldier crossed the tripwire. The laser trip mine also featured a built-in scrambler, jamming nearby enemy scanners.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Laser trip mine
- **Type:** Mine
- **Shape:** Triangular, with circular back
- **Size:** Handheld
- **Purpose:**
  - Infantry combat
  - Signal jammer
