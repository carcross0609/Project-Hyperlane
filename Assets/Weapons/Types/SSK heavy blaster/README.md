# SSK heavy blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SSK heavy blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SSK_heavy_blaster)

The SoroSuub SSK heavy blaster was a model of a blaster pistol manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation before the Cold War. One such pistol was owned by Corso Riggs, who gave it the name "Flashy," like he did to all of his weapons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SSK heavy blaster
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
