# Repulsor throwing razor

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Repulsor throwing razor/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Repulsor_throwing_razor/Legends)

The repulsor throwing razor, most often called a Rodian throwing razor by those who used them, was a ranged blade weapon produced by Rheshalva Interstellar Armaments and most often used by Rodian bounty hunters. These weapons were equipped with a repulsor engine to further increase their range. This sometimes allowed for the installation of retrieval devices, endowing them with a "Boomerang effect."

## Specifications

- **Name:** Repulsor throwing razor
- **Manufacturer:** Rheshalva Interstellar Armaments
- **Type:** Throwing weapon
- **Cost:** 3,000 credits (pair)
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 1 kilograms
