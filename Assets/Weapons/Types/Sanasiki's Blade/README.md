# Sanasiki's Blade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sanasiki's Blade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sanasiki%27s_Blade)

This Short sword was once used by Sanasiki to kill Nelinik, a Zabrak assassin. It was made with a cortosis-weave to defend against lightsabers and energy cells to disrupt droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sanasiki's Blade
- **Type:** Vibroblade
