# Sith dagger

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sith dagger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_dagger)

A Sith dagger or Sith blade was type of blade that resonated with the power of the dark side of the Force was wielded by Sith assassins. The assassin Ochi of Bestoon utilized one such blade.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sith dagger
- **Manufacturer:** Ancient Sith
- **Model:** Custom
- **Type:** Ceremonial Sith dagger
- **Purpose:**
  - Inflict agony by draining the blood of living beings
  - Lead the wielder to Sith temples and forgotten treasures
- **Affiliation:** Sith
