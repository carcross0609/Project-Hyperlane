# Halberd

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Halberd](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Halberd)

A halberd was a bladed weapon. A Zilka enforcer who worked for the gangster Chulok on the planet Metagos wielded a halberd that had a smear of dried blood on its blade.
