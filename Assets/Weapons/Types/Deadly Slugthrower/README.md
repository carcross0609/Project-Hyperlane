# Deadly Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Deadly Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deadly_Slugthrower)

The Deadly Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It featured a sleek gold, silver, red, and black color scheme.
