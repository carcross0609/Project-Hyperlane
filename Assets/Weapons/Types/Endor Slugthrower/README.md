# Endor Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Endor Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endor_Slugthrower)

The Endor Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower.
