# C-16 fragmentation grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [C-16 fragmentation grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-16_fragmentation_grenade)

The C-16 fragmentation grenade was a model of fragmentation grenade manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. The grenade contained a detonite core surrounded by a prestressed chrome shell. When detonated, the C-16 sent out a flurry of shrapnel into the target area. The C-16 had a low mass, and incorporated a detonator switch with a present delay of four seconds. The timer could be altered by twisting a dial. The C-16 was popular with Alliance to Restore the Republic soldiers during the Galactic Civil War, as well as New Republic troops.
