# Heavy turbolaser turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heavy turbolaser turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_turbolaser_turret)

A series of six total dual heavy turbolaser turrets, also referred to as heavy turbolaser defence turrets or simply heavy turbolaser turrets were the heaviest shipboard armament installed aboard Imperial I-class Star Destroyers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy turbolaser turret
- **Type:** Turbolaser
- **Length:** 50 meters
- **Range:** Less than 1,200 kilometers
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Navy
