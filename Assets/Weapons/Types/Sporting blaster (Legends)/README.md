# Sporting blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sporting blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sporting_blaster/Legends)

The sporting blaster pistol was a light, thin-barrelled blaster variant often used by those of noble heritage, who preferred a more elegant alternative to the uncivilized standard blaster.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sporting blaster
- **Type:** Blaster
- **Cost:** 300 credits
- **Shape:** Pistol-shaped
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 6 shots with energy cell
  - 100 shots with power pack
  - Close-range stun setting available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 60 m
  - Maximum: up to 120 m
- **Affiliation:**
  - Royal Handmaidens of Naboo
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
