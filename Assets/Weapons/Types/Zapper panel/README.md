# Zapper panel

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zapper panel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zapper_panel)

Zapper panels were anti-surveillance devices that could be built into walls. When activated they attracted small spy drones, such as AC1 surveillance droids, and then destroyed them. A zapper panel was installed in the storefront of a miraponics shop on Vorzyd V.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Zapper panel
- **Locations:** Vorzyd V
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
