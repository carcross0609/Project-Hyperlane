# Vulkar shock stick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vulkar shock stick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vulkar_shock_stick)

The Vulkar shock stick was a basic stun baton, charged at a higher frequency to make it cause decent physical damage. It was carried primarily by members of the Black Vulkars on Taris around the time of the Jedi Civil War. It focused electrical pulses around the baton, thereby causing intense wounds.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vulkar shock stick
- **Type:** Stun baton
- **Affiliation:** Black Vulkars
