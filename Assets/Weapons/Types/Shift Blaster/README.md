# Shift Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shift Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shift_Blaster)

The Shift Blaster was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This sleek blaster rifle bore a black, gray, silver, and tan frame, as well as small red and blue detailing.
