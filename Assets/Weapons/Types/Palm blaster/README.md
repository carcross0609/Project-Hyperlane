# Palm blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Palm blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Palm_blaster)

A palm blaster, also known as a hand cannon or a hand blaster, was a type of blaster that was built into a cybernetic arm. Beilert Valance, a cyborg bounty hunter, had a palm blaster built into his left arm that had been boosted by banned Trandoshan technology. The palm blaster produced blasts proportional in power to the length of recharging, taking 30 seconds to fully recharge.
