# E-Web (Pike Trooper)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [E-Web (Pike Trooper)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-Web_%28Pike_Trooper%29)

An E-Web was an E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon owned by the human Hunter Sentinel that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to his standard E-Web. It featured a dirty frame with a white, red, and dark gray color scheme, as well as brown cloth straps wrapped around at least some of the handles.

- **Model:** E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon (Sentinel's E-Web)
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Owners:** Sentinel
