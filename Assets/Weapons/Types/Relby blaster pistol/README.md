# Relby blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Relby blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Relby_blaster_pistol)

The Relby blaster pistol was a blaster pistol that was effective but uncommon in the galaxy. It was often overlooked for other pistols, like the DL-44. The Smuggler used a couple of these pistols before. They came in 3 models (Rough, Retooled, and Sporting) and 4 variants (Carbine, Repeating, Target, and Assault). Blastech had a series of similar looking pistols, under their own name.
