# Incinerator rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Incinerator rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Incinerator_rifle)

The incinerator rifle was a type of weapon that was often used by Bothan spies of the Rebel Alliance to hide the evidence of their misdeeds.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Incinerator rifle
- **Type:** Disintegrating energy rifle
- **Culture:** Bothan spies
- **Cost:** 3,500 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Medium (two-handed weapon)
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:** 20 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 meters
  - Maximum: up to 450 meters
- **Purpose:** To disintegrate a target
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Renegade Squadron
  -   Bothan Spynet
