# Omega's Zygerrian energy bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Omega's Zygerrian energy bow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omega%27s_Zygerrian_energy_bow)

Omega's Zygerrian energy bow was a Zygerrian energy bow that was found by Omega on the planet Ord Mantell shortly after the formation of the Galactic Empire. After seeing it fit, she picked up the bow, later using it on a mission to retrieve a tactical droid's head on the planet Corellia. She lost it while dueling with Rafa Martez but found it and used it to fight against police droids. She later tried using it to fend off Cad Bane after he shot Hunter, but was disarmed by the droid Todo 360 and captured by Bane. Wrecker retrieved the bow and gave it back to Omega after she was rescued from Bora Vio.
