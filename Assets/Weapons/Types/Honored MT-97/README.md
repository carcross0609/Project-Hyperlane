# Honored MT-97

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Honored MT-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Honored_MT-97)

The Honored MT-97 was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. The design of these pistols had some kind of connection to the Royal Academy of Government that had once operated on Mandalore.
