# Old Besi

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Old Besi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Besi)

Old Besi was a SWE/2 Riot Suppression Gun owned by Roy Harlison, a member of the Swoop gang Rabid Mynocks. He used it to defend the Mynocks' turf in Gallisport on Shesharile 5.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Old Besi
- **Manufacturer:** Telex-Delcor
- **Model:** SWE/2 Riot Suppression Gun
- **Type:** Sonic stun effect rifle
- **Cost:** 800 credits
- **Capacity:** 2
- **Range:** 10 meters
