# Paint bomb

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Paint bomb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paint_bomb)

Paint bombs, also known as color bombs, were miniaturized explosives which spread clouds of paint and were created by the young Mandalorian weapons expert Sabine Wren. She used one during a diversion on Lothal, planting it on a TIE fighter. It went off, knocking down several stormtroopers and spreading paint on their armor. The diversion allowed the starship the Ghost to escape.
