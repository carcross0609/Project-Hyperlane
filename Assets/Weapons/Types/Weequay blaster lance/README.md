# Weequay blaster lance

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Weequay blaster lance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Weequay_blaster_lance)

The Weequay blaster lance, also known as an long-barreled blaster rifle, was a scoped blaster rifle whose lengthy barrel could be fitted with a bayonet. It was used by several members of Hondo Ohnaka's pirate gang and carried by several bounty hunters, including Cato Parasitti and Twazzi. The IG-86 sentinel droids HELIOS-3D and HELIOS-3E also carried these rifles.
