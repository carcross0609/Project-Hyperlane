# Grysk lightning gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grysk lightning gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grysk_lightning_gun)

Grysk warriors utilized a type of lightning gun against the Galactic Empire shortly after the Battle of Atollon. A close-in shot from the gun could scramble muscles and the nervous system of a human, leaving them temporarily unable to function.

- **Type:** Lightning gun
