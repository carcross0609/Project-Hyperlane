# Model VIII Animal Shock Prod

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Model VIII Animal Shock Prod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_VIII_Animal_Shock_Prod)

The Persuader, officially known as the Model VIII Animal Shock Prod, was a shock prod produced by SoroSuub Corporation. A long, narrow rod with a multi-pronged tip, the Persuader had a thick haft on its opposite end that housed its power cell and controls. Equipped with non-slip handgrips on its haft, the prod measured barely over two meters in length and required two hands to wield.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model VIII Animal Shock Prod
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Shock prod
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits
- **Length:** Just over two meters
