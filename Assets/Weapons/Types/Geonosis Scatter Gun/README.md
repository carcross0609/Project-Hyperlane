# Geonosis Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Geonosis Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geonosis_Scatter_Gun)

The Geonosis Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It was designed to resemble sonic weaponry such as the sonic blasters deployed by the Geonosians before and during the Clone Wars.

- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
