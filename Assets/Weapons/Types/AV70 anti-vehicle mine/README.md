# AV70 anti-vehicle mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [AV70 anti-vehicle mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AV70_anti-vehicle_mine)

The AV70 anti-vehicle mine was a model of anti-vehicular mine produced by Merr-Sonn. It was available in the period following the Battle of Yavin during the Galactic Civil War between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire and cost 1400 credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** AV70 anti-vehicle mine
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** AV70
- **Type:** Anti-vehicular mine
- **Cost:** 1400 credits
