# Gungan catapult

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gungan catapult](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gungan_catapult)

Gungan catapults, or Gungan Energy Catapults, were mobile weapons that were used by the Gungans in the Battle of Naboo against invading Trade Federation droids. The catapults launched large boomas that deactivated the enemy droids.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gungan catapult
- **Type:** Catapult
- **Culture:** Gungan
- **Locations:** Naboo
- **Capacity:** 1 Booma
- **Purpose:** Providing fire with boomas from a distance
- **Affiliation:** Gungan Grand Army
