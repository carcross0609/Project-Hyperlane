# Mortar

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mortar/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mortar/Legends)

A mortar, also known as a mortar launcher or mortar cannon, was an indirect fire weapon that lobbed explosive projectiles known as mortar bombs or shells in a high arc.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mortar
- **Cost:** 2,500 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Large
- **Weight:** 20 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 5 mortar shells
  - 3-meter blast radius
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: 76 to 150 meters
  - Maximum: up to 750 meters
- **Purpose:** To drop shells on the enemy from behind cover and at a distance
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Trade Federation
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Galactic Empire
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Royal Naboo Security Forces
  - Wookiee
