# Reciprocating quad blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Reciprocating quad blaster cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reciprocating_quad_blaster_cannon)

The reciprocating quad blaster cannon, also known simply as the "cip-quad," was a model of blaster cannon manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.. Developed during the Clone Wars, it was essentially a downscaled anti-starfighter emplacement. During the reign of the Galactic Empire, the Heavy Shoretroopers of the Imperial Army underwent specialized training to utilize cip-quads.
