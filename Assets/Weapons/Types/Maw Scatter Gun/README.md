# Maw Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Maw Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maw_Scatter_Gun)

The Maw Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This rather ordinary looking black gun was accentuated by what appeared to be seaweed sprouting glowing orbs wrapped around it, similar in appearance to the vegetation found among the swamps of the planet Rodia.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Maw Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
