# Villainous Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Villainous Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Villainous_Rifle)

The Villainous Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle featured a complex frame with silver, gray, black, and tan metal elements, including a side-mounted scope and vented magazine, as well as a worn grip with a wood-like appearance, and a stock that had the appearance of a clubbed, carved bone.
