# Pulse mass generator tube

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pulse mass generator tube](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pulse_mass_generator_tube)

Pulse mass generator tubes were starship-mounted weaponry developed by the Hapes Consortium. They were used to deploy pulse mass mines. Each Hapan Battle Dragon was equipped with four tubes, each carrying a magazine of four mines.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pulse mass generator tube
- **Creators:** Hapes Consortium
- **Purpose:** Deployment of pulse mass mines
