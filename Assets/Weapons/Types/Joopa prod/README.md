# Joopa prod

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Joopa prod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Joopa_prod)

Joopa prods, also known as electro-poles, were a type of electrostaff used by Gregor, Kanan Jarrus and his Padawan Ezra Bridger to help them catch the elusive joopa that Gregor called the Big Bongo. After Big Bongo swallowed the Lasat Garazeb Orrelios whole, Gregor assigned the Jedi to use Joopa prods to hit the AT-TE's regulator laser line, which led into the monster's gut. Their hits through the regulator line charged the Joopa prods, sending bolts of energy down to the joopa, eventually forcing the Big Bongo to burst through Seelos's salty surface. Once the joopa emerged above the surface, Rex was able to fire one shot from the walker's main mass-driver cannon, killing the Big Bongo.
