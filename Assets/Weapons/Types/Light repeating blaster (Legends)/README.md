# Light repeating blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Light repeating blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_repeating_blaster/Legends)

The light repeating blaster or light repeating blaster rifle was a heavy rifle-type blaster weapon carried by military personnel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Light repeating blaster
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,200 to 2,250 credits (available for military use only)
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 6 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 30 shots with power pack
  - More when attached to power generator
- **Range:**
  - Up to 150 meters (optimum)
  - Up to 300 meters (maximum)
