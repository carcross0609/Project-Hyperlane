# Gormak rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gormak rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gormak_rifle)

Gormak rifles were weapons constructed by the Gormak species native to the planet Voss. The Gormak were a tech-savvy species that came into contact with the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire in 3653 BBY. The Gormak were known to convert stolen Republic and Imperial technology for their own uses, and were adept at constructing weapons and machinery from salvaged parts.
