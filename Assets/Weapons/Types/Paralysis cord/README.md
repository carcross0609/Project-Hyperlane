# Paralysis cord

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Paralysis cord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Paralysis_cord)

A paralysis cord, also known as stun cord, was a weapon used by IG-88 to paralyze a target for live capture. The cord was similar in function to a whipcord launcher, in that when fired it unspooled a large amount of animated cord, that proceeded to wrap itself around a target. Unlike a whipcord however, a paralysis cord kept on unspooling, until it had completely bound an enemy in its coils. Essentially, this paralyzed the victim, as the tight coils disabled a sentients ability to move. Some models of paralysis cord had conductive metals interwoven through its fabric, in which after enveloping an enemy, a powercell would electrify the cord, and further incapacitate the target.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Paralysis cord
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Owners:** IG-88
- **Purpose:** Paralyze targets
