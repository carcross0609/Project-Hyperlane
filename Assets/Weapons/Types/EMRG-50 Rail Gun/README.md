# EMRG-50 Rail Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [EMRG-50 Rail Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EMRG-50_Rail_Gun)

The EMRG-50 Rail Gun was an Arachnoid built rail gun. It boasted a magazine of four shots, and had a minimum range of a hundred meters, and a maximum range of four thousand meters. However, the weapon had a single severe drawback; recoil. The EMRG-50 boasted such a high recoil that it was all but unusable by sentients without a strength factor nearly double that of a standard human. The Arachnoid Ptak-Sok owned a EMRG-50 during the early years of the New Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** EMRG-50 Rail Gun
- **Type:** Rail gun
- **Cost:** 10,000 credits
- **Capacity:** 4 shots
- **Range:** 3–4000 meters
