# Soldier's Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Soldier's Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soldier%27s_Blaster)

The Soldier's Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This worn blaster pistol featured a black and gray metal base, with painted yellow elements, as well as a brown, wood-like portion for the grip with a checkered pattern carved into it. This was supposedly an old pistol from Zaina's time as a soldier within the Rebel Alliance.
