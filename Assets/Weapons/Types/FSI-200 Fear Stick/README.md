# FSI-200 Fear Stick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [FSI-200 Fear Stick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FSI-200_Fear_Stick)

The FSI-200 Fear Stick, or simply the fear stick, was a sharp weapon that was similar in size to a stylus or hypodermic injector and caused a powerful sensation of fear in a person stabbed with one. The device, which cost 1,500 credits, was popular with con artists and interrogators and were undetectable to scanners when powered down.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FSI-200 Fear Stick
- **Cost:** 1,500 credits
