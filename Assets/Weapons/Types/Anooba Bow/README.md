# Anooba Bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Anooba Bow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anooba_Bow)

The Anooba Bow was a composite bow utilized by the Zabrak Hunter Nox that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard bow. It appeared to consist primarily of the bones of an anooba.
