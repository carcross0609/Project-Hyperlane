# Needler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Needler/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Needler/Legends)

A needler was a type of pistol designed to penetrate personal energy shields by firing a sharp, tiny dart. The dart could also be laced with contact poison, delivering the poison to its targets. The NX-14 was a popular model.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Needler
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Type:** Dart-shooting pistol
- **Locations:**
  - Darkknell
  -   Xakrea
  -     Daimanate Dynamic Testing Facility
- **Cost:** 650 credits
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 1 kilogram
- **Capacity:** 10 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 meters
  - Maximum: up to 90 meters
- **Purpose:**
  - To penetrate personal energy shields
  - To poison targets
