# WESTAR-35 blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [WESTAR-35 blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WESTAR-35_blaster_pistol/Legends)

The WESTAR-35 blaster pistol was a model of blaster pistol from the WESTAR series of blasters. As a product of the WESTAR series of blasters, the WESTAR-35 was the successor to the WESTAR-20 and WESTAR-34 blaster pistols. The WESTAR-35 was also capable of firing yellow or blue bolts of plasma. It was the favored weapon of numerous Mandalorians.
