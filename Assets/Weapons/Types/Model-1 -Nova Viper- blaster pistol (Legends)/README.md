# Model-1 "Nova Viper" blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Model-1 "Nova Viper" blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model-1_%22Nova_Viper%22_blaster_pistol/Legends)

The Model-1 "Nova Viper" blaster pistol was a blaster pistol produced by Raider Arms. It was perfectly balanced, innovative, and crafted with precision down to a fraction of a micrometer. Raider Arms customized each Nova Viper sold, from the grip to the finish. Consequently, Nova Viper blasters were highly sought after and cost nearly as much as a new speeder. This blaster’s exceptional precision came with a hidden cost, requiring meticulous maintenance and extraordinary skill to repair. Nonetheless, a fringer who acquired a Nova Viper would almost always consider themselves fortunate and regarded it as one of their most prized possessions.
