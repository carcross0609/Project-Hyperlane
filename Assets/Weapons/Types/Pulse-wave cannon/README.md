# Pulse-wave cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pulse-wave cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pulse-wave_cannon)

Pulse-wave cannons were powerful energy weapons that fired packets of coherent energy. The cannons were used in the Great Sith War and the Xim Wars. They were used by the Mandalorians who mounted them on their Basilisk war droids.
