# Dueling pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dueling pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dueling_pistol)

An ornate, long-bodied blaster pistol of impeccable craftmanship, the Coronet Arms model was a typical design of dueling pistols. Often ordered in pairs, dueling pistols possessed a stopping power that made it equal to carbines and rifles, with a long frame that made them accurate. However, dueling pistols lost beam cohesion quickly, which limited their range and preventing them from carrying anymore than enough energy for a single blast.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Dueling pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Coronet Arms
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 750 credits
