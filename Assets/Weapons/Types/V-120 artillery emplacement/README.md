# V-120 artillery emplacement

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [V-120 artillery emplacement](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/V-120_artillery_emplacement)

The V-120 artillery emplacement was an anti-vehicle weapon used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic, and later by the Resistance on the planet Crait. The emplacements were armed with two heavy laser cannons for use against heavy armor, alongside another two medium laser cannons for additional firepower. The heavy laser cannons were mounted on a large semicircle rail that allowed the weapons to articulate left or right to a limited degree.
