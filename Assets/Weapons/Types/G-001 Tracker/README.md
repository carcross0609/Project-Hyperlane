# G-001 Tracker

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [G-001 Tracker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/G-001_Tracker)

The G-001 Tracker was a laser cannon produced by CoMar Combat Systems. It was the predecessor of the G-003 Tri-Tracker. Two versions were used: a double turret medium laser and a single turret light laser. The Mortex Sector HQ on Fangol was equipped with nine G-001; three double turret weapons and six single turret weapons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** G-001 Tracker
- **Manufacturer:** CoMar Combat Systems
- **Type:** Laser cannon
- **Range:**
  - 10—300 meters (short range)
  - 301—1,500 meters (medium range)
  - 1,501—8,000 meters (long range)
