# XX-9 heavy turbolaser battery

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [XX-9 heavy turbolaser battery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XX-9_heavy_turbolaser_battery)

The XX-9 heavy turbolaser battery was a turbolaser weapon emplacement manufactured by Taim & Bak. This type of turbolaser battery was equipped on the Imperial I-class Star Destroyers of the Galactic Empire, as well as the Death Star.
