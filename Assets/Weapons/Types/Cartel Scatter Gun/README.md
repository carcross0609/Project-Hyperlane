# Cartel Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Cartel Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cartel_Scatter_Gun)

The Cartel Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It's color scheme resembled the overall aesthetics of the White Worms criminal organization, including featuring the word worm inscribed in Aurebesh on the side.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cartel Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Inscription:** Worm (In Aurebesh)
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
