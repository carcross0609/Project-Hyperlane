# Carbonite gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Carbonite gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Carbonite_gun)

Carbonite guns, also known as carbonite rifles, were weapons that fired a short stream of liquid carbonite that solidified on contact with a target. It was a very formidable weapon capable of immobilizing everything from small infantry such as a stormtrooper to extremely large opponents such as a rancor. They could also be used to disable mechanical devices such as tanks.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Carbonite gun
- **Type:** Heavy weapon
- **Creators:** Empress Teta scientists
- **Cost:** 1,200 credits
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 6 kg
- **Capacity:** 20 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 450 m
- **Purpose:** To immobilize targets
