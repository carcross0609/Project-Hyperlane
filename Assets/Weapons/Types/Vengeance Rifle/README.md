# Vengeance Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vengeance Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vengeance_Rifle)

The Vengeance Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle featured a sandy silver, gray, and black frame, as well as black, orange, and teal angular designs painted over top.
