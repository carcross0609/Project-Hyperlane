# Separatist commando blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Separatist commando blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_commando_blaster_rifle)

The Separatist commando blaster rifle was a blaster rifle used by the Nimbus commandos during the Clone Wars. The weapon was capable of both long-range sniping and semiautomatic rapid fire as well as of launching concussive sonic blasts.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Separatist commando blaster rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Capacity:**
  - Long-range sniping
  - Semiautomatic rapid fire
  - Concussive sonic blasts
- **Affiliation:** Nimbus commandos
