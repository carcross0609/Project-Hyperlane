# Point-defense laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Point-defense laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Point-defense_laser_cannon)

A Point-defense laser cannon, also called a Point-Defense Battery, was a type of starship laser cannon weapons emplacement specifically designed to destroy incoming missiles, objects, torpedoes, or even fighters. The Venator-class Star Destroyer had fifty-two of such weapons, the Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought had twenty-six such cannons, and the MC85 Star Cruiser had a total of twelve point-defense turrets.
