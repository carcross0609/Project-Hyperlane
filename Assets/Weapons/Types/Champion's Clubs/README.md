# Champion's Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Champion's Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Champion%27s_Clubs)

The Champion's Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They resembled morning star-style maces, with black and crimson handles, with golden heads decorated in spikes, aurebesh engravings, and emerald jewels.
