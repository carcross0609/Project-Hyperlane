# Violet Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Violet Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Violet_Applicator)

The Violet Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This applicator resembled Ziro Desilijic Tiure in coloration, with a base consisting of dark gray and primarily violet to purple gradients, as well as glowing pink and yellow elements, and yellow markings resembling Ziro's tattoos.

- **Type:** Long-range dart gun
