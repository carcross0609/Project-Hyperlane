# Vibroknuckler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vibroknuckler/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vibroknuckler/Legends)

A vibroknuckler or vibro knuckler was a weapon that was worn on an individual's fist and provided the wielder's punches with extra power. The Sayormi possessed a variant of this weapon known as the Sayormi Heartstriker.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vibroknuckler
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Type:** Vibro-weapon
- **Cost:** 200 to 350 credits
- **Size:** Hand-held
- **Weight:** 0.5 kilograms
- **Purpose:**
  - Increase unarmed combat damage
  - Concealed weapon
