# "Precision-X" Marksman Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** ["Precision-X" Marksman Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%22Precision-X%22_Marksman_Rifle)

The "Precision-X" Marksman Rifle, known simply as the "Precision-X" blaster, was a type of specialized, long-ranged blaster rifle manufactured by Cadregg Armory. The blaster utilized a series of cycling chambers to excite the blaster gas used in the rifle, in order to produce extraordinarily intense beams of energy. These beams were only slightly more powerful than those from similar weapons, but maintained coherence over long distances. This allowed the rifle to maintain its stopping power far past a typical blaster's range limitations. The cycling chamber's effects also extended to the weapon's stun setting, allowing targets to be stunned at medium ranges. The blaster could be equipped with a single hard point.
