# Grenade launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Grenade launcher/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grenade_launcher/Legends)

A grenade launcher was a portable weapon used to shoot grenades over longer distances than they could normally be thrown.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grenade launcher
- **Type:** Heavy weapon
- **Cost:** 500 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:** 4 grenades
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 75 m
  - Maximum: up to 375 m
- **Purpose:** Firing grenades at greater range
- **Affiliation:**
  - Mandalorians
  -   Mandalorian Supercommandos
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Grand Army of the Republic
  -     Clone jet troopers
  - Galactic Empire
  -   Imperial Military
  -     Zero-G assault stormtroopers
