# Frontier Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Frontier Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frontier_Slugthrower)

The Frontier Slugthrower, also known as a Diago's slugthrower, was a slugthrower rifle utilized by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara.
