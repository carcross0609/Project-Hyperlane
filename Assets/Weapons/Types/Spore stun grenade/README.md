# Spore stun grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spore stun grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spore_stun_grenade)

The spore stun grenade manufactured by Speizoc was a type of stun grenade that dispersed a cloud of spores harvested from a carnivorous plant that acted as a powerful soporific for most species. The spores were fast acting upon inhalation or contact with the skin, and caused intense drowsiness and sleep. Spore grenades were expensive in comparison to other stun grenades, costing 100 credits, but their effectiveness was second to none, and performed better against more resilient species and creatures, which made them popular for zoological excursions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spore stun grenade
- **Manufacturer:** Speizoc
- **Type:** Stun grenade
- **Cost:** 100 credits
