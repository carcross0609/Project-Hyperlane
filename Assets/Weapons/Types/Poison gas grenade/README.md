# Poison gas grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Poison gas grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poison_gas_grenade)

Poison gas grenades were a type of grenade that stored concentrated gases of either dioxis, trion, or Fex-M3. Upon detonation, the grenade used several emitters to spray aerosolized neurotoxin, anesthetic, or neuroparalytic poisons. While most military forces eschewed at chemical warfare, assassins, bounty hunters, and Rebel partisans made use of poison gas grenades.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Poison gas grenade
- **Type:** Grenade
- **Cost:** 50 credits
