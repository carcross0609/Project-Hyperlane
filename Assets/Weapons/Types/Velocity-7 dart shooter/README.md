# Velocity-7 dart shooter

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Velocity-7 dart shooter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Velocity-7_dart_shooter)

The Velocity-7 was a dart shooter produced by Prax Arms during the final years of the Galactic Republic. The bounty hunter Jango Fett had a Velocity-7 unit integrated into the right wrist gauntlet of his Mandalorian battle armor. This was used when Jango Fett shot Zam Wesell to prevent Obi-Wan Kenobi from getting information out of him/her.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Velocity-7 dart shooter
- **Manufacturer:** Prax Arms
- **Model:** Velocity-7
- **Type:** Dart shooter
- **Capacity:** 1 dart
- **Range:**
  - 10 meters (optimum)
  - 100 meters(maximum)
- **Affiliation:** Mandalorians
