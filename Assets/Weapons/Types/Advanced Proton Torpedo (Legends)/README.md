# Advanced Proton Torpedo

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Advanced Proton Torpedo/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Proton_Torpedo/Legends)

An Advanced Proton Torpedo was a type of torpedo used by starfighters during the Galactic Civil War. They had a higher blast rating and exit velocity than standard proton torpedoes, though they were capable of tracking a target for less time.
