# Dawn Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dawn Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dawn_Rifle)

The Dawn Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle featured a black frame with golden stripes, resembling a common color scheme worn by many members of the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate, such as the Hylobon Enforcers.
