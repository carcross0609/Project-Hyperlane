# Nacht-5 smoke grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nacht-5 smoke grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nacht-5_smoke_grenade)

The Nacht-5 smoke grenade, also known as a smoker, was a smoke grenade produced by BlasTech Industries. They were primarily used as a means of obscuring vision and as a low-tech signaling device. Different colors of smoke were available as well, such as red or blue.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Nacht-5 smoke grenade
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Smoke grenade
- **Cost:** 25 credits
