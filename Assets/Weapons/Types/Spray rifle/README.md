# Spray rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spray rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spray_rifle)

The spray rifle was a ranged weapon manufactured by Loronar Corporation. The rifle's ammunition consisted of a highly-pressurized stream of fluid, a powerful sedative suspended in an organic solvent, stored in transparent metallic canisters attached as a clip-on. The substance was easily absorbed through the skin of almost every known species, a process that carried the sedative directly into the bloodstream. It could also penetrate many armors, including environmentally-sealed armor, proving useful against targets that wore equipment that granted immunities against airborne sedatives.
