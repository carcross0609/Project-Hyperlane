# Satine's Lament

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Satine's Lament](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Satine%27s_Lament)

Satine's Lament, also known as Leia's blaster, was a model of the Defender sporting blaster pistol produced by Drearian Defense Conglomerate. The blaster pistol model was named after the planet Mandalore's former Duchess, Satine Kryze, a devout pacifist who hated violence. Its alternative name came from its usage by General Leia Organa, a famous revolutionary who fought for both the Alliance to Restore the Republic and Resistance.
