# LD-1 target rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LD-1 target rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LD-1_target_rifle)

The LD-1 target rifle was a blaster rifle produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. during the Galactic Civil War that was officially classified as a sporting blaster, despite its significant power. Its performance and build quality, and the fact that its classification allowed it to circumvent most weapon restrictions throughout the galaxy, led to it being extremely popular, consistently outselling the SoroSuub Corporation's X-45.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LD-1 target rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** Blaster rifle
- **Type:** Sporting blaster (officially)
- **Cost:** 1,275 credits
