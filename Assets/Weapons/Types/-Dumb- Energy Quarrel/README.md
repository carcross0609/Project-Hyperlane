# "Dumb" Energy Quarrel

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** ["Dumb" Energy Quarrel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%22Dumb%22_Energy_Quarrel)

The "Dumb" Energy Quarrel was a type of explosive energy quarrel produced by Kelvarek Consolidated Arms that was used in the company's MM9 rocket system. Produced at some point before 0 ABY, the quarrels were much less effective than a blaster pistol and had a range of sixty meters when fired from the rocket system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** "Dumb" Energy Quarrel
- **Manufacturer:** Kelvarek Consolidated Arms
- **Type:** Quarrel
- **Created:** Before 0 ABY
- **Cost:** 50 credits
- **Range:** 60 meters
