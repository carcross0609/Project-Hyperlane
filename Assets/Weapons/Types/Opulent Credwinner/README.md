# Opulent Credwinner

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Opulent Credwinner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Opulent_Credwinner)

The Opulent Credwinner was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It was an ornate slugthrower with an angular, metal, light blue, white, and gray stock, a wooden center, and a curved gray cover over top. It was seemingly a relatively pricey weapon.
