# Captain Ithano's blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Captain Ithano's blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Captain_Ithano%27s_blaster_rifle)

A blaster rifle was owned by pirate captain Sidon Ithano. The heavy bore rifle was used by the "Crimson Corsair" during the time of the Cold War in the New Republic Era, after he had stolen the weapon from a member of the Kanjiklub gang. During the same time, the Kanjiklubber Crokind Shand also used a visually identical weapon. Like other weapons used by the gang, both blaster rifles utilized makeshift components, with the shoulder stocks were made out of bone (Shand's using that of a Roggwart) and hand-made bayonets being attached to the barrels with brown cord. Ithano also owned a blaster pistol with a similar design. During the Battle of Exegol in 35 ABY, Ithano instead used a Czerka Arms Longswat-44 sniper blaster rifle in the same crimson color as his armor.
