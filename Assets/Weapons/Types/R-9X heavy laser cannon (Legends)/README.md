# R-9X heavy laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-9X heavy laser cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-9X_heavy_laser_cannon/Legends)

The R-9X heavy laser cannon was a type of laser cannon manufactured by Gyrhil. Slayn & Korpil's B-wing starfighter and the Tenloss Syndicate's Hornet-class interceptors incorporated R-9X heavy laser cannons as part of their weapons systems. With modifications, the R-9X heavy laser cannon could be upgraded, overcharging them to deal twice the amount of damage. However, this came at the cost of them momentarily overheating. R-9X's installed on Hornet-class interceptors were upgraded in that manner.
