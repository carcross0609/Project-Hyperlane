# Wrist blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wrist blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrist_blaster)

A wrist blaster was a blaster weapon worn and fired from a wrist. B2-series super battle droids, built by Baktoid Combat Automata, had built-in wrist blasters as their main weapon. The bounty hunter C-21 Highsinger had a wrist blaster built into his arm. A Dur-24 wrist laser was favored by the bounty hunter Boba Fett.
