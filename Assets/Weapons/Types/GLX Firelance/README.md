# GLX Firelance

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [GLX Firelance](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GLX_Firelance)

The GLX Firelance, also called the SoroSuub Firelance blaster rifle or simply SoroSuub Firelance, was a lightweight blaster rifle manufactured by SoroSuub. It was first released during the last years of the Old Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** GLX Firelance
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,200 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 2.5 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 50 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
  - Autofire mode available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 meters
  - Maximum: up to 450 meters
- **Affiliation:** Bounty hunters
