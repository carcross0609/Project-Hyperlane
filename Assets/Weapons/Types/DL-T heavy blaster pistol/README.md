# DL-T heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DL-T heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DL-T_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The DL-T heavy blaster pistol was a pistol undergoing tests and should not be available in general public. It is named "DL-T" due to its status as a testing weapon. Its appearance is a DL-44 heavy blaster pistol but has gold color on it. During the Galactic Civil War, these pistols were found used by smugglers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DL-T heavy blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DL-T heavy blaster pistol
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Value:** Not available for public sale
- **Range:** 35—46 meters
