# Binnot Ullo's poison glove

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Binnot Ullo's poison glove](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Binnot_Ullo%27s_poison_glove)

Binnot Ullo's poison glove was a glove worn by Binnot Ullo that contained multiple types of poison. In 382 BBY, it contained an unidentified droid poison as well as a poison that could kill humanoid species in less than a minute. It was a dark glove that reached to the elbow of the wearer. During the Eiram and E'ronoh War, it was intended to carry Erami poison, which was substituted after Axel Greylark stole the poison. The glove was embedded with tiny needles that would imbue the poison stored in vials in its sleeve. The needles extended when the owner's hand squeezed inside the glove, and retracted when the fingers were extended.
