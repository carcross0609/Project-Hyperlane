# Duo blaster carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Duo blaster carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duo_blaster_carbine)

The Duo blaster carbine was a dual-barreled blaster carbine produced by the SoroSuub Corporation and sold for 1,200 credits. The barrels of the 2.5-kilogram weapon were positioned vertically with an upper and lower barrel. The weapon had a maximum range of 200 meters, and users could fire the barrels separately or together. The weapon lacked a stun setting and used power pack that cost 100 credits and held fifty shots.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Duo blaster carbine
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Cost:** 1,200 credits
- **Weight:** 2.5 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 50 shots
- **Range:** 200 meters
