# Flame Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flame Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flame_Rifle)

Czerka Arms' Flame Rifle was a hand-held weapon that operated on the same principle as a flame projector. The bell-mouthed weapon ejected a spontaneously ignited liquid that adhered to the intended object, destroying anything it touched. The Flame Rifle was used by Alliance Heavy Weapons Specialists and Urban Guerrillas.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flame Rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Type:** Flamethrower
- **Cost:** 700 credits
- **Capacity:** 25
- **Range:** 10 meters
