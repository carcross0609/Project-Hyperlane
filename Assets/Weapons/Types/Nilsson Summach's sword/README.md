# Nilsson Summach's sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nilsson Summach's sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nilsson_Summach%27s_sword)

A sword was built by the arms dealer Nilsson Summach during the High Republic Era. The sword had a Karagin design and a 47 lightsaber nullifier embedded in its hilt. Summach used the sword to fight Jedi Master Vernestra Rwoh in the Kolnare Mine on the planet Septra, but was defeated.
