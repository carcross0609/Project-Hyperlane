# Heavy blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heavy blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_blaster_rifle)

Heavy blaster rifles were a larger and more powerful variant of the blaster rifle. They were often only distributed to one or two infantry members per squad, and inflicted more damage from greater distances than anything else used within a squad. With barrels that were often exceptionally long, heavy rifles tended to be very accurate. They could easily pierce plastoid and heavy combat armor, and could even pose a threat to lightly armored speeders.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy blaster rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 1,500 credits
