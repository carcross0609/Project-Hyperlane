# Endorian Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Endorian Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endorian_Scatter_Gun)

The Endorian Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. Much of the exterior of this gun consisted of bone and cloth bound by string.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Endorian Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
