# Wrist rocket launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wrist rocket launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrist_rocket_launcher)

Wrist rocket launchers were miniaturized missile launchers capable of being worn on the wrist. They fired various types of wrist rockets, including darts and small missiles. They were a favorite of bounty hunters due to their discreet nature, versatility, and power. Bounty hunter Mira used a wrist rocket launcher as her trademark weapon. Another model of wrist rocket launcher was the Kelvarex Consolidated Arms MM9 rocket system, which was used by bounty hunter Boba Fett.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wrist rocket launcher
- **Type:** Miniaturized rocket launcher
- **Cost:** 2,500 credits
- **Shape:** Molded to fit over wrist and forearm
- **Length:** A person's forearm
- **Width:** A person's wrist
- **Weight:** 1 kilogram
- **Capacity:** 1 wrist rocket
- **Range:** Up to 60 meters
- **Purpose:** Firing various wrist rockets
- **Affiliation:** Bounty hunters
