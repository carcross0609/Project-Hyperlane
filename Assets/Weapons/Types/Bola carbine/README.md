# Bola carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bola carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bola_carbine)

Bola carbine was an individually handcrafted, unique energy weapon that ensnared and tripped up targets. Bounty hunters often fielded a bola carbine as their primary weapon to both take out hostiles and capture targets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bola carbine
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Cost:** 1,600 credits
