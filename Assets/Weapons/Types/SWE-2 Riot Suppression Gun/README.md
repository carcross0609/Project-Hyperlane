# SWE/2 Riot Suppression Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SWE/2 Riot Suppression Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SWE/2_Riot_Suppression_Gun)

The SWE/2 Riot Suppression Gun, colloqiually known as a Popper, was a breach-loading, dual barrel rifle. It was primarily used by law enforcement agencies for crowd control purposes. Each barrel held one ammunition shell, and could be fired independently or at the same time. Each shell emitted an expanding wall of over-pressurized air and chemical reactants that could knock down doors, peoples, and barricades.
