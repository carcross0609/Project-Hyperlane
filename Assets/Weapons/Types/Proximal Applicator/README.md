# Proximal Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Proximal Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proximal_Applicator)

The Proximal Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This plain light applicator featured various types of ware to its plating.
