# Double-bladed vibrosword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Double-bladed vibrosword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_vibrosword)

The double-bladed vibrosword was a melee weapon that consisted of two full-length vibroblades mounted at the ends of a hilt. Wielding the vibrosword required the use of two hands, and was difficult and dangerous for untrained users, but could be a deadly weapon when held by a skilled combatant.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Double-bladed vibrosword
- **Type:** Vibrosword
- **Cost:** 1,300 credits
- **Length:** Up to 3 meters
- **Range:** Close
