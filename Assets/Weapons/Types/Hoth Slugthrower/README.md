# Hoth Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hoth Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoth_Slugthrower)

The Hoth Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This plain looking slugthrower was silver, gray, and black in coloration, with simulated frosted effects and a large scope.
