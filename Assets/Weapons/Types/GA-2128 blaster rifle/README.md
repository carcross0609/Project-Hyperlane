# GA-2128 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [GA-2128 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GA-2128_blaster_rifle)

The GA-2128 blaster rifle was a model of blaster rifle. One modified GA-2128 blaster rifle was owned by Forten, a member of Company 77, a group of former stormtroopers who defected from the First Order.
