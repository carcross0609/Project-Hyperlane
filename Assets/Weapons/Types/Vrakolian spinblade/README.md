# Vrakolian spinblade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vrakolian spinblade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vrakolian_spinblade)

Vrakolian spinblades were a variety of small razor-edged throwing discs that were produced by the Vrakolian. The thick-skinned sentient species typically used these blades as part of certain games of skill, while other species adopted them for use as deadly weapons. A skilled thrower could make the blades curve in flight, allowing them to hit targets under cover.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vrakolian spinblade
- **Type:** Thrown weapon
- **Culture:** Vrakolian
- **Cost:** 10 Credits
- **Range:** 20 meters
