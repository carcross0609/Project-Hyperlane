# M-12

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [M-12](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/M-12)

The M-12 was an Imperial-class sweep bomb used for heavy demolition and leveling for the Galactic Empire or, in some cases, their manufacturer's director, Kuat of Kuat. The high-pressure explosive originally used a removable atmospheric phase-change detonator.

## Specifications

- **Name:** M-12
- **Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards
- **Type:** Sweep bomb
- **Capacity:** 20 millibar differential
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Kuat Drive Yards
