# Fotia's Fire

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fotia's Fire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fotia%27s_Fire)

Fotia's Fire was a projectile weapon mounted on the warships used by the Tof Kingdom, the monarchy of the Tof species. The projectile splashed superheated glowing slime and burned a starship's hull on impact. The Tof Royal Fortune-class light clipper capital ship possessed a Fotia's Fire launcher.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fotia's Fire
- **Type:** Projectile
- **Culture:** Tof
- **Material:** Superheated glowing slime
- **Purpose:** Burn a starship's hull
- **Affiliation:** Tof Kingdom
