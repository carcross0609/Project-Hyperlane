# Flashstick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Flashstick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flashstick)

Flashsticks were nonlethal weapons used by the Drall constabulary. The weapons cost 375 credits and emitted a bright flash of light that could blind or stun an opponent. Species that did not rely on eyesight or that could not see at all were not particularly affected by the weapon. Many users wore protective eye gear to prevent the flashes from disorienting themselves, and some used two of the devices at once.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flashstick
- **Cost:** 375 credits
- **Affiliation:** Drall constabulary
