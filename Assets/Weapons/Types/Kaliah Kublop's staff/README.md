# Kaliah Kublop's staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kaliah Kublop's staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaliah_Kublop%27s_staff)

The explorer Kaliah Kublop owned a staff. Sometime after the founding of Tenoo, the staff was stolen by the droid Pirate Blackbolt. The statue of Kublop in Kublop Springs depicted her holding her staff.
