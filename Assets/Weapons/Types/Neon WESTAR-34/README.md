# Neon WESTAR-34

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neon WESTAR-34](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neon_WESTAR-34)

The Neon WESTAR-34 was a pair of WESTAR-34 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. Unlike his usual MT-97s, these WESTAR pistols featured sleek curves and a chrome finish.
