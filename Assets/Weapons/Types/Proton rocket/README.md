# Proton rocket

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Proton rocket](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proton_rocket)

A proton rocket was a type of ballistic missile used by the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. The rocket could be launched from batteries, and shot several shells with splash damage, causing the most impact to scattered groups of units.

- **Name:** Proton rocket
- **Purpose:** Defense
