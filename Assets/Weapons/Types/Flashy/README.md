# Flashy

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Flashy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flashy)

Flashy was the SSK heavy blaster owned by Corso Riggs during the Cold War. Corso later gave Flashy to the smuggler known as the Voidhound to aid said smuggler's search for Skavak in a Separatist stronghold located in a volcano. According to Corso, Flashy is one of Corso's named weapons, along with Hewey, Sparkles and Torchy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Flashy
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Model:** SSK heavy blaster
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Owners:** Corso Riggs
