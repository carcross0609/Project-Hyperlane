# Explosive briefcase

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Explosive briefcase](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_briefcase)

The explosive briefcase was an explosive device that was disguised to look like a briefcase. They were used by the Wayfar spies and in 1 ABY, a spacer obtained one of them.

- **Name:** Explosive briefcase
- **Type:** Bomb
