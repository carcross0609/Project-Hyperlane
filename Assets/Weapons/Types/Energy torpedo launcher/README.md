# Energy torpedo launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Energy torpedo launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Energy_torpedo_launcher)

The energy torpedo launcher was a projectile launcher that fired energy torpedoes. The Trade Federation's Vulture-class starfighter were equipped with two energy torpedo launchers.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Energy torpedo launcher
- **Type:** Torpedo launcher
- **Capacity:** 4 energy torpedoes
