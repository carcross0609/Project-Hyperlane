# AP-M1 armor piercing grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [AP-M1 armor piercing grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AP-M1_armor_piercing_grenade)

The AP-M1 armor piercing grenade was a model of grenade manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.. It was designed to provide light anti-armor capabilities to basic foot soldiers, detonating in a highly focused blast that can crack open even the toughest armor. The grenade cost two hundred credits and was the size of a human fist and consisted of a small shaped charge core encased in a plasteel casing.
