# Imperious Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Imperious Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperious_Rifle)

The Imperious Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle was weathered, with its black frame showcasing silver elements underneath. It was covered in white cloth bindings, and had part of the Imperial crest stamped in red on the side.
