# Quarterstaff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Quarterstaff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quarterstaff)

A quarterstaff was a type of staff which could be used as a melee weapon. The scavenger Rey used a salvaged quarterstaff as her weapon when living on the planet Jakku. Her staff was battered, but durable, and had handle wrappings made from scraps of uniform.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Quarterstaff
- **Type:** Staff
- **Purpose:** Melee combat
