# Duskblade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Duskblade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duskblade)

The duskblade was a short sword wielded by members of the Nightsisters. Used both in battle and for ceremonial purposes, the duskblade had a curved single-edged blade and a one-handed hilt. Being lightweight, flexible and easy to handle, these swords were lethal in the hands of a trained user.

- **Type:** Sword
- **Culture:** Dathomirian
- **Cost:** 1,800 credits
- **Purpose:** Hand-to-hand combat
