# DXR-6b disruptor rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DXR-6b disruptor rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DXR-6b_disruptor_rifle)

The DXR-6b disruptor rifle, sometimes improperly named DXR-6 disruptor rifle, was a disruptor rifle variant of the DXR-6 disruptor produced by the Tenloss Syndicate during the Galactic Civil War. This weapon possessed an oversized XCiter that permitted the actuating blaster module to process great volumes of blaster gas to produce powerful shots.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DXR-6b disruptor rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Tenloss Syndicate
- **Type:** Disruptor rifle
