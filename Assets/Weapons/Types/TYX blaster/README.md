# TYX blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [TYX blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TYX_blaster)

The TYX blaster was a model of small blaster, one of which was owned by the droid 4-LOM. 4-LOM once brought his TYX blaster to Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel, where he concealed it from the diner's bouncer before firing a volley of shots toward the crime lord Mozeen Parapa.

- **Type:** Blaster
