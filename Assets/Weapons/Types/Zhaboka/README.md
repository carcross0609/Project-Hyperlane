# Zhaboka

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Zhaboka](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zhaboka)

The zhaboka was a traditional Zabrak melee weapon, similar to a spear but possessing a blade at either end of the weapon's long staff. Kes Dameron once encountered a Zabrak who possessed a zhaboka and claimed to be a Jedi. The Zabrak mercenary Ledesmar owned a zhaboka, which was used to kill her by Spice Runner of Kijimi Zorii Bliss during a skirmish aboard a stolen cruiser.
