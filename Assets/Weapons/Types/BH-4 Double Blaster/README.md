# BH-4 Double Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BH-4 Double Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BH-4_Double_Blaster)

The BH-4 Double Blaster, also known as the Black Sun foot soldier blaster, or just Black Sun Blaster, was a model of double-barreled blaster pistol used by Black Sun enforcers as well as members of the Black Sun Guard and Captain of the Guard Ziton Moj.

- **Type:** Blaster pistol
