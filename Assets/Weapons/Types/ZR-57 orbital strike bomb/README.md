# ZR-57 orbital strike bomb

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ZR-57 orbital strike bomb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ZR-57_orbital_strike_bomb)

The ZR-57 orbital strike bomb was a weapon designed to destroy major urban centers and was used by the Galactic Republic during the time of the Cold War, most notably in the Separatist War. One of these bombs was stolen by the Mantellian Separatist Movement on the island of Avilatan, Ord Mantell, from a crashed transport, around 3643 BBY. Its firepower would have been enough to level the island, and Havoc Squad was dispatched to recover the bomb. When it was ascertained that the bomb was taken inside the separatists' mountain fortress, Havoc, excluding the newly transferred sergeant, departed to retrieve it but suddenly dropped off the grid. Thus, Lieutenant Aric Jorgan sent the sergeant into the volcano and transmitted the deactivation code, rendering the bomb a radioactive paperweight. When the sergeant discovered that the rest of Havoc was ready to defect to the Sith Empire, they already had the ZR-57 loaded onto their shuttle, unaware that the sergeant disarmed it.
