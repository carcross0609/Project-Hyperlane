# HH-15 projectile launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HH-15 projectile launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HH-15_projectile_launcher)

The Golan Arms HH-15 projectile launcher was a shoulder-fired missile launcher widely used by Rebel Alliance ground forces, notably Rebel vanguard divisions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HH-15 projectile launcher
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Model:** HH-15
- **Type:** Portable missile launcher
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Large
- **Weight:** 12 kg
- **Capacity:** 1; 2; 7 or 8 missiles in reserve
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 75 meters
  - Maximum: up to 750 meters
- **Purpose:**
  - Anti-vehicle
  - Blasting through armored fortifications
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Rebel vanguards
