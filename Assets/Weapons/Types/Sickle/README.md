# Sickle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sickle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sickle)

A sickle was a farming tool that could also be used as a Melee weapon. During the Clone Wars, the Nightsister Selena and her human daughter Morgan Elsbeth wielded a pair of sickles on Dathomir, which Selena enhanced her's with magick to fight General Grievous. During the New Republic Era, the bounty hunter Embo wielded two sickles to fight Mandalorian mercenary Din Djarin, who wielded two machetes on Nal Hutta.

- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Purpose:** Farming
