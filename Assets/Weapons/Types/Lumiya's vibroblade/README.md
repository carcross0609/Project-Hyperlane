# Lumiya's vibroblade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lumiya's vibroblade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lumiya%27s_vibroblade)

In 40 ABY, Dark Lady of the Sith Lumiya carried a vibroblade in her boot. While entertaining the Twi'lek Dark Jedi Alema Rar at her asteroid home, Lumiya produced the vibroblade and rammed it into her own leg. To Rar's astonishment, the blade had absolutely no effect on the Dark Lady, as all of her limbs were prosthetic.
