# Lawman's Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Lawman's Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lawman%27s_Rifle)

The Lawman's Rifle was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This slugthrower was white, silver, and dark gray, with red buttons, a glowing, light blue core, and a vented barrel.
