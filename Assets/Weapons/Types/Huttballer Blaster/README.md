# Huttballer Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Huttballer Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Huttballer_Blaster)

The Huttballer Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This streamlined blaster pistol was tan, black, orange, and gray in coloration, the general color scheme associated with the sport huttball, even featuring a huttball decal on the side of the grip.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Huttballer Blaster
- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
- **Type:**

- **Owners:** Zaina
