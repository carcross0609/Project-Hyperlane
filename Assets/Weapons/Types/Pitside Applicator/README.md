# Pitside Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pitside Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pitside_Applicator)

The Pitside Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This bright applicator was yellow, red, silver, white, and tan in coloration.
