# Desert Sandviper

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Desert Sandviper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Sandviper)

The Desert Sandviper was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle's frame was primarily dark gray, though weathering had exposed a silver underlayer. It also featured a foldable brown foregrip, and was wrapped with various tan strips of cloth.
