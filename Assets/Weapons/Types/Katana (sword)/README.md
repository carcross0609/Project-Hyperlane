# Katana (sword)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Katana (sword)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Katana_%28sword%29)

A katana, also known as curved sword, was a type of sword characterized by a slightly curved blade. This kind of weapon was popular among members of the Cult of Lord Nyax during the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Katana
- **Type:** Sword
- **Affiliation:**

  - Cult of Lord Nyax
  - Valarian Syndicate
