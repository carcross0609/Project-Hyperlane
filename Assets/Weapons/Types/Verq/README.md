# Verq

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Verq](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verq)

The Verq was a model of close-assault blaster rifles produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions. Verqs were powerful weapons with short barrels but had a standard range well below three hundred meters. In 45 ABY, several Nargon biots serving the corporation Galactic Exploitation Technologies on the installation Brink Station wielded Verqs in a shootout with the Humans Han Solo and Leia Organa Solo. A recording of the fight was played for Organa Solo's brother, Jedi Grand Master Luke Skywalker, at the Jedi Academy on the world Shedu Maad; the combat instructor Jagged Fel observed that the the Nargons had operated the Verqs very proficiently.
