# Firearc49 Speargun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Firearc49 Speargun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firearc49_Speargun)

The Firearc49 Speargun was a model of blaster speargun manufactured by BlasTech Industries and marketed to the Galactic Empire as an underwater assault weapon. The weapon was a long metal pipe capable of shooting small spears underwater to a distance as far as fifty meters. A small blaster was located beneath the spear barrel. The speargun held one spear at a time, with room for two more to be clipped on the side. The blaster's ammunition pack lasted fifty shots. A quiver could be carried by the wielder to carry additional spears. The seatrooper garrison on the planet Spira was equipped with this model of weapon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Firearc49 Speargun
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Blaster speargun
- **Cost:** 300 credits
- **Capacity:**
  - Speargun: 3 spears (one loaded, two side-mounted)
  - Blaster: 50 shots
- **Purpose:** Underwater personal weapon
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
