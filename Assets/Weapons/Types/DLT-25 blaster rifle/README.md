# DLT-25 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DLT-25 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DLT-25_blaster_rifle)

The DLT-25 blaster rifle was a type of blaster rifle utilized by the Galactic Empire during the Imperial Era. In 9 BBY, one such blaster was ring mounted on a CSL-430 hover transport on the planet Mapuzo.
