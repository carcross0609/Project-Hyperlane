# A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A280-CFE_convertible_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The A280-CFE (covert field edition) convertible heavy blaster pistol, also known as an A280-CFE blaster rifle or simply a A280-CFE blaster, was a modular version of the A280 blaster rifle, as part of the A-series. It featured a core pistol that could be reconfigured into an assault rifle or sniper rifle. Captain Cassian Andor used an A280-CFE during the Battle on Jedha, the mission to Eadu, and the Battle of Scarif.
