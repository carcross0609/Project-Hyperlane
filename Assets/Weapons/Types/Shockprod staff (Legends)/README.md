# Shockprod staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Shockprod staff/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shockprod_staff/Legends)

A shockprod staff was an electrical stun weapon used by Gand Findsmen. The staff ended in a V-shaped twin prod. The stunning electrical jolt was delivered whenever organic material passed between the prods. The staff had an effective range of ten feet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shockprod staff
- **Cost:**
  - 500 credits (on Gand)
  - 1000 credits (in other locations)
- **Weight:** 4 kilograms
