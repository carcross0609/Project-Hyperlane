# Assault blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Assault blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_blaster_rifle)

The assault blaster rifle was a Mandalorian assault rifle modified by the Czerka Corporation. A precision weapon used by military units, it was one of the most precise weapons of its time.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Assault blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Corporation
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Creators:** Mandalorians (original design)
- **Cost:** 1,750 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 50 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
  - Autofire mode available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 90 m
  - Maximum: up to 450 m
- **Affiliation:** Military units
