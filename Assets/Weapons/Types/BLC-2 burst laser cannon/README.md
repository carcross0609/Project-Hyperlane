# BLC-2 burst laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [BLC-2 burst laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BLC-2_burst_laser_cannon)

The BLC-2 burst laser cannon was a type of laser cannon developed by SoroSuub Corporation by the time of the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the Sith Empire. BLC-2 burst cannons were considered to be the starfighter equivalent of a scattergun, as it fired a cluster of high-damage bolts in a wide arc with a slow rate of fire. The BLC-2s were used primarily at close range, as they conserved large amounts of power and were ineffective at greater distances.
