# Sonic pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sonic pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_pistol)

A sonic pistol was a type of sonic weapon similar to a blaster pistol. It created a high-intensity sonic blast powerful enough to shatter solid objects and cause devastating injuries.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sonic pistol
- **Type:** Sonic weapon
- **Cost:** 900 credits
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 100 shots
  - Cannot be deflected by a lightsaber
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 meters
  - Maximum: up to 120 meters
- **Purpose:**
  - To counter lightsaber-wielding Jedi and Sith
  - To disorient an opponent
