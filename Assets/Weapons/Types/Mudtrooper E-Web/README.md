# Mudtrooper E-Web

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mudtrooper E-Web](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mudtrooper_E-Web)

The Mudtrooper E-Web was an E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon owned by the human Hunter Sentinel that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard E-Web. It resembled the Z-6 rotary blaster cannons like those utilized by Imperial Imperial swamp trooper during the the Mimban Campaign.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mudtrooper E-Web
- **Model:** E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon (Sentinel's E-Web)
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Owners:** Sentinel
- **Affiliation:**

  - Hunters of the Outer Rim
