# Dulok Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dulok Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dulok_Rifle)

The Dulok Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This ornate blaster rifle featured a black, weathered frame covered in green, yellow, and red markings. Attached to the rifle were also a spiky, club-like nob for a stock, what appeared to be part of a spine over the scope, a small creature skull over the barrel, and green cloth bound around the grip.
