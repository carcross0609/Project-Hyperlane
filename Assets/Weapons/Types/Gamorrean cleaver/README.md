# Gamorrean cleaver

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gamorrean cleaver](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamorrean_cleaver)

The Gamorrean cleaver was an unwieldy melee weapon created by the Gamorreans that was more likely to stun an enemy after the first blow, rather than killing outright. Nonetheless, the target of such a weapon did not have much of a chance of escaping. It could have also been used as an off-hand weapon by a skilled or strong fighter
