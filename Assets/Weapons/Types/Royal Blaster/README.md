# Royal Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Royal Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Royal_Blaster)

The Royal Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This blaster pistol consisted of silver, dark gray, light gray, and pale purple components, with metallic pink painted portions, as well as sandy weathering.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Royal Blaster
- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
- **Type:**

  -   Veteran Blaster
- **Owners:** Zaina
