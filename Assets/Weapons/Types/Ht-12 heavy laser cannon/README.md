# Ht-12 heavy laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ht-12 heavy laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ht-12_heavy_laser_cannon)

The Ht-12 heavy laser cannon was a model of laser cannon manufactured by Taim & Bak. Dark Prince Xizor of the Black Sun crime syndicate utilized a pair of double Ht-12 laser cannons on his personal starfighter, the Virago.
