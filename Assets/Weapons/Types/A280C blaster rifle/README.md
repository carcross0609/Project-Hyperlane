# A280C blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [A280C blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A280C_blaster_rifle)

The A280C blaster rifle, also designated as the A280 C heavy blaster rifle, was a modified variant of the popular A280 blaster rifle and was the preferred weapon of Rebel Alliance Special Forces. It featured a high rate of fire and was known for its excellent firepower and sturdy design.

## Specifications

- **Name:** A280C blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** A280C
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Created:** Nearly a century before 4 ABY
- **Cost:** 1,800 credits
- **Affiliation:**

  - Haxion Brood
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - New Republic
