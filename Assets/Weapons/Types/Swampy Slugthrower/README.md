# Swampy Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Swampy Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Swampy_Slugthrower)

The Swampy Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This slugthrower featured a pale green, silver, and black design, with a large barrel reminiscent of many Imperial weapons, red markings, and a silver Imperial crest stamped onto the top of the stock.
