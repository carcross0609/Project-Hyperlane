# High Republic Branch

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [High Republic Branch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Republic_Branch)

The High Republic Branch were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They resembled ornate branches with blue cloth handle wraps and the High Republic crest painted on the face of each in gold.

- **Model:** Grozz's Clubs
