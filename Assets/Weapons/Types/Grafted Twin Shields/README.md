# Grafted Twin Shields

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grafted Twin Shields](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grafted_Twin_Shields)

The Grafted Twin Shields were the primary weapon used by the KX-series droid Hunter K-A0S during the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition. Serving both defensive and offensive tools, these shields could be linked together to form a greater area of protection, or separated to be used as individual melee weapons, and were highly customizable. These shields replaced K-A0S' previous limbs after they were torn off and stolen by fellow Wookiee Hunter Grozz, resulting in a fierce rivalry between the two.
