# Pulsed energy projectile laser

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pulsed energy projectile laser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pulsed_energy_projectile_laser)

The Pulsed energy projectile laser, commonly shortened to PEP laser, was a non-lethal form of weaponry. Originally designed for police units tasked with controlling riots, the PEP laser made use of a deuterium fluoride laser to incapacitate targets without causing serious, long-lasting injury. Effective against most medium-sized humanoids, the PEP laser emitted a loud shockwave of sound and light when discharged, capable of knocking an individual off of their feet and inflicting pain significant enough that further resistance would be almost physically impossible. Those familiar with the weapon noted that a shot from a PEP laser was similar to being struck with a flash-bang grenade and hit with several plastoid rounds in the chest at the same time. Victims of a PEP laser strike were subject to severe bruising at and around the point of impact. Despite their unique makeup, PEP laser rounds could be deflected by a lightsaber as easily as normal blaster bolts.
