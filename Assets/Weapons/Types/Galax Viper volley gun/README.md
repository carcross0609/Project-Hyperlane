# Galax Viper volley gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Galax Viper volley gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galax_Viper_volley_gun)

The Galax Viper volley gun was an anti-vehicle gun produced by Kurtough during the early years of the New Republic. A large weapon, the Galax Viper employed a collection of missile launchers in a single unit, each designed to fire shortly after the previous one. This produced a "curtain effect," with a continuous volley of projectiles striking the vehicle and eliminating most deflector shields.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Galax Viper volley gun
- **Manufacturer:** Kurtough
- **Type:** Anti-air vehicle weapon
- **Cost:** 2,500–4,000 credits
- **Crew:** 2
- **Capacity:** 80; unlimited with power generator
- **Range:** 1,000 meters
