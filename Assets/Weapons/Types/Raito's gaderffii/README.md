# Raito's gaderffii

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Raito's gaderffii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raito%27s_gaderffii)

This gaderffii may have once belonged to Raito, a Tusken Raider who was said to have slain a Jedi. His tribe died out sometime around the Great Sith Wars, and his gaffi stick came into the possession of another tribe's Storyteller, who may have given it to Revan.
