# KY12 medium laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KY12 medium laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KY12_medium_laser_cannon)

The KY12 medium laser cannon was a medium laser cannon that was produced by Taim & Bak. A turret-mounted KY12 was the primary weapon of the BTS-A2 Long-Range Strike Fighter/Bomber.
