# K5 "Ion Storm" Defense Battery

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [K5 "Ion Storm" Defense Battery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K5_%22Ion_Storm%22_Defense_Battery)

The K5 "Ion Storm" Defense Battery was a model of repeating ion cannon manufactured by Oriolanis Defense Systems. It could be equipped to starfighters and utilized ion pulses to disable enemy starships. At one point, it cost 6,000 credits on the market.

## Specifications

- **Name:** K5 "Ion Storm" Defense Battery
- **Manufacturer:** Oriolanis Defense Systems
- **Type:** Repeating ion cannon
- **Cost:** 6,000 credits
- **Purpose:** Disable starships
