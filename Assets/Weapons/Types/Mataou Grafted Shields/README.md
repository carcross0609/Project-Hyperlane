# Mataou Grafted Shields

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mataou Grafted Shields](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mataou_Grafted_Shields)

The Mataou Grafted Shields were a pair of shields owned by the KX-series security droid Hunter K-A0S that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard Grafted Twin Shields. These shields bore the same general color scheme as the security droids once utilized by the Hutts who had settled on the planet Mataou, and when combined, they prominently displayed the Hutt Clan insignia.
