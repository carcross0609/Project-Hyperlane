# LC-60 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LC-60 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LC-60_laser_cannon)

The LC-60 laser cannon was a model of laser cannon that could be equipped to starships. Upgrading an EML-850 light freighter previously equipped with LC-50 laser cannons to LC-60s cost five hundred credits and required five units of Durasteel, and two heuristic processors. The LC-60s had an increased fire rate over the LC-50.
