# Pulverizer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Pulverizer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pulverizer)

The "Pulverizer" was a rocket pod manufactured by Elsinore-Cordova Engineering during the Galactic War between the Republic and the Sith Empire. Designed to destroy enemy objectives and to be loaded onto starfighters in large quantities, Pulverizers and other rocket pods lacked guidance systems.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pulverizer
- **Manufacturer:** Elsinore-Cordova Engineering
- **Type:** Rocket pod
