# Mag-pulse grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mag-pulse grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mag-pulse_grenade)

Mag-pulse grenades were a small cylindrical type of grenade. The Teedo who captured the astromech droid BB-8 on the planet Jakku around thirty years after the Battle of Endor wore a mag-pulse grenade on his bandolier while out scavenging.
