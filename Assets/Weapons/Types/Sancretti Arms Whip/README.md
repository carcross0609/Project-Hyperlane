# Sancretti Arms Whip

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sancretti Arms Whip](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sancretti_Arms_Whip)

The Sancretti Arms Whip was a standard whip produced by Sancretti Arms & Munitions, Interstellar. Consisting of spun cables of bantha hide, the whip measured four meters in length, and could deliver slashing attacks at range; a skilled user could also disarm or trip his opponent, though the weapon proved useless against even light armor.
