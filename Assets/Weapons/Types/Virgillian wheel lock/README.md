# Virgillian wheel lock

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Virgillian wheel lock](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Virgillian_wheel_lock)

The Virgillian wheel lock was a type of archaic slugthrower produced by the Virgillians. Due to a lack of access to blasters, the wheelock was used by the Virgillian Free Alignment in their efforts during the Virgillian Civil War within the Virgillia system. Captain Tessa Manchisco was skilled in the use of the weapon.
