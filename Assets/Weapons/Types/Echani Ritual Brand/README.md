# Echani Ritual Brand

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Echani Ritual Brand](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echani_Ritual_Brand)

The Echani Ritual Brand was a type of double-bladed sword used in ritual combat by Echani Firedancers. The Echani used the blade in a graceful pattern of dodging and slicing, causing the combat to appear similar to a dance. The melee weapon consisted of two Cortosis-weave blades joined in the center by a colored hilt. It was capable of withstanding a lightsaber.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Echani Ritual Brand
- **Type:** Double-bladed sword
- **Culture:** Echani
- **Locations:** Taris
- **Cost:** 280 credits
- **Material:** Cortosis-weave
- **Purpose:** Ritual combat
- **Affiliation:** Echani Firedancers
