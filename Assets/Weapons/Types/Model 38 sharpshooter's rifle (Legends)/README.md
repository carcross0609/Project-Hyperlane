# Model 38 sharpshooter's rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Model 38 sharpshooter's rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_38_sharpshooter%27s_rifle/Legends)

The Model 38 was one of the most popular models of Slugthrower rifle produced by Czerka Arms during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. The weapons were primarily used by big-game hunters, snipers and assassins. Each rifle was unique and adjusted to its owner.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model 38 sharpshooter's rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Model:** Model 38
- **Type:** Slugthrower
- **Cost:** 3,000 credits
- **Range:** Very long
