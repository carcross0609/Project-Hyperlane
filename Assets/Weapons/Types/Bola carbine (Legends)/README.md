# Bola carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bola carbine/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bola_carbine/Legends)

Bola carbines were specially-handcrafted weapons that were originally made by members of the Bothan species. A variant of the carbine was known as the Special Ops Bola carbine. They were in use after the Battle of Yavin during the time of the Galactic Civil War. Bola Carbines were known to be quieter than average blasters and always had a purple blaster bolt.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bola carbine
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Culture:** Bothan
- **Cost:** 1,600 credits
- **Range:** 35 meters
