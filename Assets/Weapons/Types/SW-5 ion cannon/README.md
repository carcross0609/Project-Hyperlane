# SW-5 ion cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SW-5 ion cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SW-5_ion_cannon)

The SW-5 ion cannon was a ion cannon manufactured by ArMek. It was used by Quarrie in his Prototype B6 starfighter with one cannon mounted at each of the two S-foils and one under the cockpit. They were part of the composite-beam laser weapon. Also, the SW-5 was utilized by some Koensayr BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bombers as a twin turret mounted behind the cockpit. Some BTL-A4s used SW-4 ion cannons instead.
