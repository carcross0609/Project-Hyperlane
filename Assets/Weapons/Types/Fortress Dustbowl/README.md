# Fortress Dustbowl

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Fortress Dustbowl](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fortress_Dustbowl)

The Fortress Dustbowl was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle featured a metallic gray and brown frame, with white and yellow paint, and a glowing yellow element on the scope.
