# Bryar pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bryar pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bryar_pistol/Legends)

The Bryar pistol was a modified Bryar rifle with its stock and muzzle sawed down.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Bryar pistol
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 1,350 credits
- **Shape:** Pistol-shaped
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 3 kg
- **Capacity:** 100 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 60 m
  - Maximum: up to 120 m
