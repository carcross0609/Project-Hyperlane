# Mini rocket

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mini rocket](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mini_rocket)

Mini rockets were employed as a weapon by Mandalorian warriors as of 43.5 ABY. These rockets were small, capable of homing in on individual or even multiple targets, and were launched in clusters from the Mandalorian's gauntlet. Mandalorians employed mini rockets when Galactic Alliance Chief of State Natasi Daala hired them for a raid on the Jedi Temple in 43.5 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mini rocket
- **Culture:** Mandalorians
- **Size:** Small
- **Purpose:** Small explosive
- **Affiliation:** Mandalorians
