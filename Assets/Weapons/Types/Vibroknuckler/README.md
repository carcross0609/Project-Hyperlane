# Vibroknuckler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vibroknuckler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vibroknuckler)

The vibroknuckler was a model of vibro weapon manufactured by Czerka Arms. It consisted of a four centimeter-long blade fixed to a piece of metal that a hand could slide through. The vibroknuckler's user was able activate it by clenching their fist. While the blade did not increase its user's range, it did make its attacks almost impossible to block by an unarmed opponent. It was also extremely difficult to remove the weapon from its user by force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vibroknuckler
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Type:** Vibroweapon
- **Size:** Hand-held
- **Length:** 4 centimeters (blade)
