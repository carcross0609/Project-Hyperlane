# QuasarFire Turbolaser Turret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [QuasarFire Turbolaser Turret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/QuasarFire_Turbolaser_Turret)

The QuasarFire Turbolaser Turret was a ground-based emplacement weapon produced by Taim & Bak. These turbolasers were produced with their own power generators. Six QuasarFires were used by the Corporate Sector Authority to defend the Stars' End prison.

- **Name:** QuasarFire Turbolaser Turret
