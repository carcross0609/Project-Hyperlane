# KX3 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [KX3 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KX3_laser_cannon)

The KX3 laser cannon was a laser cannon manufactured by the Taim & Bak company. The Delta-class T-3c shuttle had a KX3 laser cannon affixed on the top of its central stabilizer foil, along with a multi-spectrum ranging laser.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KX3 laser cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Taim & Bak
- **Model:** KX3
- **Type:** Laser cannon
