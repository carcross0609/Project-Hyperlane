# Adventurer slugthrower rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Adventurer slugthrower rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adventurer_slugthrower_rifle)

An Adventurer slugthrower rifle, also known as the Czerka Adventurer rifle was a projectile rifle with great accuracy over long distances, and was manufactured by Czerka Arms. The weapon could be easily dismantled for ease of transport. Aurra Sing was known to carry one with her. Unlike most other weapons, it shot a solid projectile.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Adventurer slugthrower rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Type:** Slugthrower rifle
- **Range:** 450 meters
- **Purpose:** Assault weapon
