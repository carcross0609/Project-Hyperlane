# CR-2 heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CR-2 heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR-2_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The CR-2 heavy blaster pistol, also known more simply as a CR-2 blaster pistol, was a heavy blaster pistol manufactured by Corellian Arms that was utilized by the Royal Naboo Security Forces. Small and agile, it had an extreme rate of fire and shot ionized bolts. It also could be configured with night vision scopes or have an extended stock for reduced recoil.
