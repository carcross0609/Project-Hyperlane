# Gi/9 antipersonnel laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gi/9 antipersonnel laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gi/9_antipersonnel_laser_cannon)

The Gi/9 antipersonnel laser cannon, also known as the Gi/9 antipersonnel blaster cannon, was a model of laser cannon manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. The Hutt crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure's LO-KD57 luxury barge, the Khetanna, was equipped with Gi/9 cannons mounted on the rails of the sail barge's top deck. As the weapons were removable, the exact number of cannons on the ship varied, but there were typically twenty mounted.
