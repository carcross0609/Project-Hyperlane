# Direct Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Direct Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Direct_Slugthrower)

The Direct Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It was a basic silver slugthrower with a wooden stock.
