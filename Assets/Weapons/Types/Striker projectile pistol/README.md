# Striker projectile pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Striker projectile pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Striker_projectile_pistol)

The Striker projectile pistol was a typical slugthrower pistol manufactured by Oriolanis Defense Systems. Considered to be durable but otherwise unimpressive, the Striker had an ammunition clip of eight slugs. Only a direct hit on a vital area was likely to kill a target. The Striker was readily available, even on planets with strict weapons regulations, and was favored by those in the Outer Rim Territories.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Striker projectile pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Oriolanis Defense Systems
- **Type:** Slugthrower pistol
- **Cost:** 275 credits
- **Weight:** 1.4 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 8 slugs
- **Range:**
  - 30 meters (Optimum)
  - 60 meters(Maximum)
