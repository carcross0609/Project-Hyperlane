# Hammer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hammer/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hammer/Legends)

The Hammer was a Z-6 rotary blaster cannon that was operated by clone trooper commander Thorn during the Clone Wars. It doubled as a melee weapon, as demonstrated by Thorn on Scipio. Though he fought fiercely and took down many droids, he was finally overwhelmed by droids, and he was shot to death in the chest.
