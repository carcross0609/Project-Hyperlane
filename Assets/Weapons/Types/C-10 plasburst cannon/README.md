# C-10 plasburst cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [C-10 plasburst cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/C-10_plasburst_cannon)

The C-10 plasburst cannon was a model of plasburst laser cannon that could be equipped to starships. The cannon would charge up and launch a powerful laser. Equipping an EML-850 light freighter with a C-10 plasburst cannon cost two thousand credits and required a X-FP-C28 ship computer, twenty units of quadanium steel, and eight heuristic processors.

## Specifications

- **Name:** C-10 plasburst cannon
- **Model:** C-10
- **Type:** Plasburst laser cannon
- **Cost:** 2000 credits
