# Microwave stunner

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Microwave stunner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Microwave_stunner)

The microwave stunner produced by Jer'N Weapons Systems fired a bolt of microwave energy that interfered with the neural processes of living creatures. It was about the size of a hold-out blaster.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Microwave stunner
- **Manufacturer:** Jer'N Weapons Systems
- **Type:** Specialized energy weapon
- **Cost:** 175 credits (25 credits per power pack)
- **Capacity:** 10
- **Range:** 20
