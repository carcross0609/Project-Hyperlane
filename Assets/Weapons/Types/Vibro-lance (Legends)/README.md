# Vibro-lance

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vibro-lance/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vibro-lance/Legends)

The vibro-lance or vibrolance was a large powered spear-like weapon that vibrated rapidly upon impact, dramatically increasing the amount of damage delivered while minimizing the effort required by the wielder.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vibro-lance
- **Type:** Vibroweapon
- **Cost:** 500 credits
- **Shape:** Spear-like
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 2 kg
