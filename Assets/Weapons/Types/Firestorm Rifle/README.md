# Firestorm Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Firestorm Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Firestorm_Rifle)

The Firestorm Rifle was a customized A280 blaster rifle utilized by the human bounty hunter and competitive Hunter Imara Vex, both during her time as a member of the Orphans, as well as for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Firestorm Rifle
- **Model:** A280 blaster rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Owners:** Imara Vex
- **Affiliation:**
  - Orphans
  - Crimson Dawn
  - Hunters of the Outer Rim
