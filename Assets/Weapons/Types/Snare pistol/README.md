# Snare pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Snare pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Snare_pistol)

The snare pistol was cord-shooting weapon designed to ensnare a target so that they can be captured alive. The pistol fired a length of synthcord which wrapped around the target, hindering its ability to move or escape. The snare pistol was a smaller version of the snare rifle and the snare gun.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Snare pistol
- **Type:** Cord-launching pistol
- **Cost:** 600 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 2 kg
- **Capacity:** 2 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 meters
  - Maximum: up to 90 meters
- **Purpose:** To capture targets alive
- **Affiliation:**
  - Blujay
  -   B4J4 security droids
