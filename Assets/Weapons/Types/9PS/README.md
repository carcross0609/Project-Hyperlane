# 9PS

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [9PS](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/9PS)

The 9PS, also known as the "Gundark Fist" 9PS, was a scattergun produced by Speizoc Arms during the Cold War. Weighing 2.75 kilograms with a barrel of 400 millimeters and a rancor-hide stock grip, the gun carried 50 plasma shells and had an effective range of 10 meters but a maximum range of 35 meters.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 9PS
- **Manufacturer:** Speizoc Arms
- **Type:** Scattergun
- **Material:** Rancor-hide grip
- **Length:** 400 millimeters barrel
- **Weight:** 2.75 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 50 plasma shells
- **Range:** 10 to 35 meters
