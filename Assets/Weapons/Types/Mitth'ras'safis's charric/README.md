# Mitth'ras'safis's charric

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mitth'ras'safis's charric](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mitth%27ras%27safis%27s_charric)

Syndic Mitth'ras'safis of the Eighth Ruling Family owned a charric that was adorned with Chiss lettering. When Mitth'ras'safis boarded the Outbound Flight with Jorj Car'das in 27 BBY, he carried the weapon concealed in his tunic. In 22 ABY, his weapon was recovered by the Jedi Master Luke Skywalker and his wife Jedi Knight Mara Jade Skywalker.
