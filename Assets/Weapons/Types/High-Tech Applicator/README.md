# High-Tech Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [High-Tech Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High-Tech_Applicator)

The High-Tech Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This complicated looking applicator featured an exterior of yellow and varying shades of gray, with patterns on the barrel, multiple areas for projectile storage, and a foldable stock.
