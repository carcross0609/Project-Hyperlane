# Silver Lined Sword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Silver Lined Sword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silver_Lined_Sword)

The silver lined sword was a long and straight blade that saw use during the Jedi Civil War. It sparkled with a silver sheen that became brighter as the hilt was grasped. The blade was warm and appeared to become white-hot when held. A flame insignia was impressed upon the hilt. One of these swords resided in the Tomb of Ajunta Pall.
