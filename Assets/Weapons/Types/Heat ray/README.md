# Heat ray

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Heat ray](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heat_ray)

A heat ray was a type of weapon used by Darth Voord in the puppet show "The Legend of the Noble Bonbrak". In the story, the devastating weapon was used to boil oceans.
