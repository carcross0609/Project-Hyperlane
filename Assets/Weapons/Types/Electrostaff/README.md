# Electrostaff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Electrostaff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electrostaff)

An electrostaff, also known as a shock staff, an electro-pole or a Force Lance, was a staff-like melee weapon manufactured by Baktoid Armor Workshop and Holowan Mechanicals. They were used by the Confederacy of Independent Systems' IG-100 MagnaGuard by the Galactic Republic's clone shock troopers during the Clone Wars, and by Imperial Purge, riot, and Heavy Troopers leading up to and during the Galactic Civil War.
