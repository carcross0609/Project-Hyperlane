# Relby K-25 blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Relby K-25 blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Relby_K-25_blaster)

The Relby K-25 blaster was a reconfigurable police blaster notably used by the Police Force in the city of Canto Bight. Canto Bight Police Department personnel wielded such blasters during the arrest of Rose Tico and Finn for their illegal parking on the beach.
