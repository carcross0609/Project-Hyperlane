# Hutt Cartel Bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hutt Cartel Bow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt_Cartel_Bow)

The Hutt Cartel Bow was a composite bow utilized by the Zabrak Hunter Nox that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard bow. This bow came equipped with multiple backup combat options, including those of the melee weapon variety.
