# DE-10 blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DE-10 blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DE-10_blaster_pistol)

The DE-10 blaster pistol was a stylish and reliable heavy blaster pistol that was manufactured by Antrech Arms, a subsidiary of BlasTech Industries. It was vaunted for its reliability and ease of maintenance, and heavily resembled a slugthrower revolver.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DE-10 blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Antrech Arms
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 1,400 credits
- **Range:** Long range
