# Plasma charge

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Plasma charge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plasma_charge)

Plasma charges were explosives that emitted shockwaves of plasma. The charges cost 200 credits per unit. Though the heat released by a plasma charge was much less than the amount released by a baradium charge, they were useful for incinerating construction materials and overloading personal shields.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Plasma charge
- **Type:** Explosive
- **Cost:** 200 credits
