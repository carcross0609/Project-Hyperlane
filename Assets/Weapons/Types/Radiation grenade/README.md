# Radiation grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Radiation grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Radiation_grenade)

Radiation grenades were small explosives that were either thrown or launched from a grenade launcher. On impact, a radiation grenade detonated in a burst of light smoke, releasing a cloud of short-lived radioactive particles that immediately sickened and weakened targets. The radiation grenade had a 3-meter blast radius and bypassed most conventional armors and shields; because of this they were considered highly illegal and were used by only the most depraved or desperate armies. Radiation sickness would persist and progressively worsen until it was cured.
