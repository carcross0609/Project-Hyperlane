# E-Web(15) Heavy Repeating Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-Web(15) Heavy Repeating Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-Web%2815%29_Heavy_Repeating_Blaster)

The E-Web(15) Heavy Repeating Blaster was manufactured by BlasTech Industries, and was a modified version of their standard E-Web heavy repeating blaster. Released the year after Palpatine's death, the E-Web(15) was a two-man weapon that could be "quick-mounted" on a tripod, allowing it to be set up and operational in under five minutes using the attached generator's "autoconfigure" setting.

## Specifications

- **Name:** E-Web(15) Heavy Repeating Blaster
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Cost:** 6,300 credits
- **Crew:** 2; skeleton: 1
- **Capacity:** Power generator
- **Range:** 500 meters
