# SW-7a ion cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SW-7a ion cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SW-7a_ion_cannon/Legends)

The SW-7a ion cannon was a model of ion cannon manufactured by ArMek. The Slayn & Korpil B-wing starfighter was armed with three of the cannons as part of its weapons loadout. They could fire singly, or linked to fire all three cannons simultaneously.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SW-7a ion cannon
- **Manufacturer:** ArMek
- **Type:** Light ion cannon
