# Yaret-Kor

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Yaret-Kor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yaret-Kor)

The Yaret-Kor, more commonly known as a plasma cannon, volcano cannon or lava cannon, was the primary inter-ship weapon of the Yuuzhan Vong fleet. As with all other Yuuzhan Vong bio-technology, the Yaret-Kor was an organic creation.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Yaret-Kor
- **Manufacturer:** Yuuzhan Vong shapers
- **Model:** Yaret-Kor
- **Type:** Plasma cannon
- **Range:**
  - 5 kilometers (optimum)
  - 60 kilometers (maximum)
- **Affiliation:** Yuuzhan Vong
