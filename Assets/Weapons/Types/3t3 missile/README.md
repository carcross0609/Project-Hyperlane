# 3t3 missile

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [3t3 missile](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/3t3_missile)

The Arakyd Industries 3t3 missile was a small guided missile armed with a miniature proton warhead and used in portable launchers, such as Merr-Sonn's PLX-2M portable missile launcher. A PLX-2M's missiles cartridge was typically loaded with six of these missiles. Often used against airborne threats, the launchers themselves and the weapons they fired were nicknamed "chip missiles" by New Republic starfighter pilots.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 3t3 missile
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Model:** 3t3
- **Type:** Missile
- **Cost:** 350 credits per pack of six
- **Weight:** 8 kg per pack of six
- **Purpose:** Often against airborne threats
