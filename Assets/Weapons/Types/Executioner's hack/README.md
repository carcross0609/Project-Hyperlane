# Executioner's hack

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Executioner's hack](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Executioner%27s_hack)

The executioner's hack was a melee weapon which saw use during the Galactic Civil War, sometime after the Battle of Yavin. A massive axe, it was a favorite weapon among operatives of the Black Sun, a criminal organization active at the time. Although it was balanced as a two-handed weapon, its low weight gave it the possibility to be used as a one-handed one. Black Sun operatives active within the Death Watch bunker on the forest moon of Endor were known to carry schematics of the weapon.
