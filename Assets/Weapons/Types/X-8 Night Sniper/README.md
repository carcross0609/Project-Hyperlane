# X-8 Night Sniper

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [X-8 Night Sniper](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-8_Night_Sniper)

The X-8 Night Sniper was a model of blaster pistol manufactured by BlasTech Industries that featured a computerized scope and silent mode operation. The scope featured a thermal vision that can reveal humanoids in most weather conditions like smoke and dioxis, and different times of day. X-8 Night Snipers were used by both the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War.
