# Force pike

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Force pike](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_pike)

Force pikes were a type of staff-like weapon with a vibro-edged head. They also had a stun setting, and were capable of causing immense pain at their lowest setting. At the highest setting, the pikes could be used to cut through durasteel. The Red Guard of Galactic Emperor Palpatine wielded Controller FPs. They were able to fire purple blasts from their tips.
