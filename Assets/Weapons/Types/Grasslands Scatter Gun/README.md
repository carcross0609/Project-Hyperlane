# Grasslands Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grasslands Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grasslands_Scatter_Gun)

The Grasslands Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This gun featured a camouflaged pattern, as well as a crosshair painted on the bottom of the grip.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Grasslands Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
- **Owners:** Charr
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
