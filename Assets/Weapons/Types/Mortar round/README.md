# Mortar round

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mortar round](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mortar_round)

A Mortar round was a type of explosive with a range of twenty meters that destroyed everything in its blast radius. Darth Scabrous' HK droid was equipped with mortar rounds.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mortar round
- **Type:** Explosives
- **Range:** 20 meters
- **Purpose:** Warfare
