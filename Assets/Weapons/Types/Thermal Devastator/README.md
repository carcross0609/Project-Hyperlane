# Thermal Devastator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Thermal Devastator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thermal_Devastator)

The Thermal Devastator was a grenade-like incendiary explosive which was in use during at least 3630 BBY. Feared among the galaxy for its potency, the Devastator's effects were twofold: first lighting the immediate area around it ablaze before emitting a concussive wave which launched enemies through the air. The Commander of the Eternal Alliance utilized Thermal Devastators while defending against a series of uprisings which occurred following the fall of the Eternal Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Thermal Devastator
- **Type:** Grenade
- **Shape:** Spherical
- **Size:** Hand-held
- **Capacity:** 1 detonation
- **Range:**
  - Thrown weapon range
  - 30-meter blast radius
- **Affiliation:** Various
