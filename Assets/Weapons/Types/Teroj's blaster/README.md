# Teroj's blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Teroj's blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teroj%27s_blaster)

Teroj's blaster was a blaster pistol owned by the First Order operative Teroj Kee. In 34 ABY, he wielded the weapon on the supertanker fuel depot Colossus while stealing a phase connector mining device from the platform's Office of Acquisitions. At the Colossus loading docks, he threatened the mechanic Kazuda Xiono with the blaster before returning to the Office of Acquisitions and firing repeatedly at Xiono's droid, BB-8. Aboard his freighter, he fired several shots at the gorg Bitey after the creature sabotaged the vessel.
