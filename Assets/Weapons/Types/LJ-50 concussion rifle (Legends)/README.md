# LJ-50 concussion rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LJ-50 concussion rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LJ-50_concussion_rifle/Legends)

The LJ-50 Concussion Rifle was a heavy field weapon manufactured by Arakyd Industries, and used in medium to long distance engagements. It utilized a concussive energy charge that imploded on impact and created a 5 meter radius area of effect. Then the gun sent out a burst of energy. This often killed its target. The weapon was sometimes equipped with an EMP bayonet, which was effective at stunning and disabling droids, or electrocuting an enemy.
