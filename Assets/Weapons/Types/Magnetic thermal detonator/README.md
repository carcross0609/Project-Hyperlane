# Magnetic thermal detonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Magnetic thermal detonator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Magnetic_thermal_detonator)

Magnetic thermal detonators were hemispherical versions of the Class-A thermal detonator manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.. The flat surface was magnetized, allowing the detonator to be attached to metal objects, or to a bandolier for carrying. In 21 BBY, Jedi Commanders Ahsoka Tano and Barriss Offee attempted to use magnetic thermal detonators to destroy the control room of a major droid foundrey during the Second Battle of Geonosis. When a group of Geonosians took the bombs, they improvised by using a super tank to fire on the generator.
