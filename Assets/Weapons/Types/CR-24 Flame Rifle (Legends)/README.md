# CR-24 Flame Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CR-24 Flame Rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR-24_Flame_Rifle/Legends)

The CR-24 Flame Rifle was an incendiary weapon produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.. Its Czerka counterpart was known as the CZ-24 Flamemaster flame rifle. The rifle had a fire range of 10 meters which meant the CR-24 was better utilized for close combat.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CR-24 Flame Rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** Model CR-24
- **Type:** Flame projector
- **Cost:** 700 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:** 25 bursts
- **Range:**
  - 5 meters (optimum)
  - 10 meters(maximum)
- **Affiliation:** Corporate Sector
