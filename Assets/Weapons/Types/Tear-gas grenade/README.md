# Tear-gas grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tear-gas grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tear-gas_grenade)

Tear-gas grenades were non-lethal grenades. They released an eye-irritating gas that incapacitated targets. They were used by the Kajain'sa'Nikto privateer Ga'p'tashi; the Nikto among his crew were immune to the effects of the gas, and the Klatooinians in his crew wore goggles.
