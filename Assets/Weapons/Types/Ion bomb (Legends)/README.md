# Ion bomb

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ion bomb/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ion_bomb/Legends)

The Ion bomb was reputed by Qui-Gon Jinn to be the most destructive explosive in the galaxy. Boxes of ion bombs stored around a planet exploding at the same time; the reaction could destroy a planet. It was a bomb that used ion energy to disrupt electronics and cause malfunctions.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ion bomb
- **Purpose:** To disrupt electronics
