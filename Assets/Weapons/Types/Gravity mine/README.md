# Gravity mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Gravity mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gravity_mine)

Gravity mines were a model of space mine used in space combat which used gravity based technology. They were designed to latch onto the hulls of starships and warp the local space around them in order to prevent them jumping to hyperspace before the mine could explode. Gravity mines were sold for 1,000 credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gravity mine
- **Type:** Space mine
- **Locations:** Castell system
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits
