# Ceremonial poleaxe

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ceremonial poleaxe](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ceremonial_poleaxe)

A ceremonial poleaxe was a vibro-ax without a power cell. Dewt Kluskine armed his honor guard with these weapons during his stint as the warden of the Imperial detention facility on planet Kessel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ceremonial poleaxe
- **Model:** Vibro-ax
- **Type:** Vibroweapon
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
