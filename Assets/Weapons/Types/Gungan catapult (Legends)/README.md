# Gungan catapult

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gungan catapult/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gungan_catapult/Legends)

Gungan catapults, also called gungan energy catapults or whizboomas, were mobile weapons that were used by the Gungans in the Battle of Naboo/Legends against invading Trade Federation droids. The catapults launched large boomas that deactivated the enemy droids. The catapult was made of hornweed.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gungan catapult
- **Manufacturer:** Otoh Gunga Defense League
- **Type:** Catapult
- **Culture:** Gungan
- **Locations:** Naboo
- **Capacity:** 1 Booma
- **Purpose:** Providing fire from a distance with boombas
- **Affiliation:** Gungan Grand Army
