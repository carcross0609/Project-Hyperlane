# Mo/Dk power harpoon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mo/Dk power harpoon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mo/Dk_power_harpoon/Legends)

The Ubrikkian Transports Mo/Dk power harpoon was a weapon located at the rear of the T-47 airspeeder used by the Rebel Alliance. During the Battle of Hoth, it was used to great effect to bring down AT-ATs.

- **Name:** Mo/Dk power harpoon
- **Manufacturer:** Ubrikkian Transports
