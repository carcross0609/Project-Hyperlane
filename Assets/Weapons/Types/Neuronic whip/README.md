# Neuronic whip

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Neuronic whip](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neuronic_whip)

Neuronic whips were a type of whip with a metallic cable that, by the time of the Galactic Civil War, had a reputation for being the preferred weapon of enslavers. Borstel Galactic Defense produced a type of neuronic whip called the neuronic lash that they tried to distance from this reputation by changing its appearance and overall function, replacing the metallic cable with a flexible energy strand that was incapable of causing physical injury.
