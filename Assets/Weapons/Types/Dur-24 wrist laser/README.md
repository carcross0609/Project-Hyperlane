# Dur-24 wrist laser

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Dur-24 wrist laser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dur-24_wrist_laser)

The Dur-24 wrist laser was a wrist-mounted laser weapon manufactured by BlasTech Industries that combined the full firepower of a standard blaster rifle with a range of up to 50 meters. Boba Fett incorporated one into his left wrist gauntlet. During a skirmish at the Great Pit of Carkoon, Fett attempted to shoot Luke Skywalker with his wrist laser after the Jedi destroyed his EE-3 carbine rifle, but was knocked into the sarlacc after firing a few blasts that missed the target. Some time later, after escaping the sarlacc and retrieving his armor, Fett made further use of the weapon against a platoon of stormtroopers belonging to Moff Gideon's Imperial remnant whilst aiding Din Djarin on Tython.
