# Three-way Ceiling Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Three-way Ceiling Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Three-way_Ceiling_Gun)

Three-way Ceiling Guns were dome shaped turrets that were mounted on the ceilings of the Ugnaught and Carbon-freezing chamber sub-levels of Cloud City. The two halves of the dome would separate before the Three-way Ceiling Gun would fire three separate lasers in opposite directions. The majority of the Three-way Ceiling Gun, excluding the dome, remained embedded in the ceiling.
