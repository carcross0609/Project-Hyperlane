# Emperor-4 beam tube

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Emperor-4 beam tube](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor-4_beam_tube)

The Emperor-4 beam tube was a beam tube produced by Timms UniStar Armaments during the early years of the Galactic Republic.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Emperor-4 beam tube
- **Manufacturer:** Timms UniStar Armaments
- **Type:** Beam tube
- **Size:**
  - Over 15 kilograms (weapon)
  - Over 30 kilograms (power cell backpack)
- **Capacity:** Less than 100 shots
- **Range:**
  - 20 meters (optimum)
  - 50 meters (maximum)
