# Siang lance

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Siang lance/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Siang_lance/Legends)

The siang lance was a weapon used by the Kilian Rangers. It was a modified sporting blaster rifle equipped with a bayonet. It was often enhanced by the Force.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Siang lance
- **Type:** Exotic sporting blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Capacity:** 100 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 90 meters
  - Maximum: up to 450 meters
- **Purpose:** Combined melee and ranged weapon
- **Affiliation:**
  - Kilian Rangers
  - Nobles of Kilia IV
