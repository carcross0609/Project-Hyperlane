# F-9 Heavy Ion Cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [F-9 Heavy Ion Cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/F-9_Heavy_Ion_Cannon)

The f-9 Heavy Ion Cannon was a starship ion cannon made by CoMar Combat Systems. It cost 3,000 credits, weighed between one and two metric tons, and had an atmospheric range of three-point-six kilometers. The weapon was no more powerful than CoMar's f-4 Medium Ion Cannon but did have a superior fire control computer.

## Specifications

- **Name:** f-9 Heavy Ion Cannon
- **Manufacturer:** CoMar Combat Systems
- **Type:** Ion cannon
- **Cost:** 3,000 credits
- **Weight:** 1—2 metric tons
- **Range:** 3.6 kilometers (atmospheric)
- **Purpose:** Starship-mounted weapon
