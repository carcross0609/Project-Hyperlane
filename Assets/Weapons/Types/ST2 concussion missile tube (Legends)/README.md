# ST2 concussion missile tube

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [ST2 concussion missile tube/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ST2_concussion_missile_tube/Legends)

The ST2 concussion missile tube was a concussion missile launcher manufactured by Arakyd Industries. The launcher had a magazine of four concussion missiles, typically Arakyd's ST2 concussion missile. The price of 1,500 credits made it popular with independent starship owners. The YT-1300 light freighter Millennium Falcon was equipped with a pair of ST2 concussion missile racks located between the forward mandibles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** ST2 concussion missile tube
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Type:** Concussion missile launcher
- **Cost:** 1,500 credits
- **Capacity:** 4 missiles
