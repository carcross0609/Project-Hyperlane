# Sil-50 Sonic Pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sil-50 Sonic Pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sil-50_Sonic_Pistol)

The Pacnorval Defense Systems, Limited Sil-50 Sonic Pistol was the size of a DL-44 heavy blaster pistol and had a maximum range of 20 meters (40 meters underwater) and effective range of 10 meters (30 meters underwater). Unlike the Sg-82 sonic rifle, however, the Sil-50 was designed to incapacitate the target without causing any harm.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sil-50 Sonic Pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Pacnorval Defense Systems, Limited
- **Type:** Sonic pistol
- **Cost:** 2,000—3,000 credits (100 credits for additional chips)
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 1 kilogram
- **Capacity:** 15—50 shots
- **Range:** 10 meters
