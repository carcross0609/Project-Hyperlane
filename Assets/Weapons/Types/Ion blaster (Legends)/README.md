# Ion blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ion blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ion_blaster/Legends)

Ion blasters, or ionization blasters, were any handheld weapons that fired ion energy instead of blaster bolts. They were commonly used in response to droids and other technology on the battlefield.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ion blaster
- **Cost:** 800 credits
- **Weight:** 3 kg
- **Purpose:** To immobilize droids
