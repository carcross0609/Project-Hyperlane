# Massiff Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Massiff Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Massiff_Slugthrower)

The Massiff Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This slugthrower was black, silver, and bronze in coloration, with a wooden butt, and a small illustration of a massiff painted on the stock.
