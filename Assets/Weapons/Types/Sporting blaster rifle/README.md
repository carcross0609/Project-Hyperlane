# Sporting blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sporting blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sporting_blaster_rifle)

The sporting blaster rifle was a less lethal version of the blaster rifle that was popular with the galactic elite as well as among fringers living in isolated colonies.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sporting blaster rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Culture:** Fringers
- **Cost:** 800 credits
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 50 shots or 100 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 90 m
  - Maximum: up to 450 m
