# Ax-20 blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ax-20 blaster cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ax-20_blaster_cannon)

The Ax-20 blaster cannon was a model of small blaster cannon manufactured by BlasTech Industries. The weapon was capable of firing repeated blasts without overheating and consisted of a long barrel with a widened neck around the muzzle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ax-20 blaster cannon
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** Ax-20
