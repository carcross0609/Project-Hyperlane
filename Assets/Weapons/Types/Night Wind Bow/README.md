# Night Wind Bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Night Wind Bow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Wind_Bow)

The Night Wind Bow was a composite bow utilized by the Zabrak Hunter Nox that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard bow. It resembled the aesthetic of the Order of the Night Wind criminal organization.
