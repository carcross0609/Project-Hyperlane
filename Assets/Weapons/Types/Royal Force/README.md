# Royal Force

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Royal Force](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Royal_Force)

The Royal Force was a pair of blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols.
