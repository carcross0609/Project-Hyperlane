# DLT-18 laser rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DLT-18 laser rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DLT-18_laser_rifle)

The DLT-18 laser rifle, also known as the DLT-18 heavy blaster rifle, was a model of heavy blaster rifle manufactured by BlasTech Industries. The human bounty hunter Dengar carried a DTL-18 during the Clone Wars. A rugged variation of the DLT-18 was used by the rebel leader Steela Gerrera as a sniper rifle. Steela’s sniper rifle was modified with a scope and a thinner barrel. The bounty hunter Onca carried a DLT-18. Several members of Hondo Ohnaka’s pirate gang and former governor Ryder Azadi used a similar rugged rifle.
