# Tourist Set

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tourist Set](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tourist_Set)

The Tourist Set was the name given to a set of two weapons, an Arc Caster and a Pulse Blaster, utilized by the team of Jawa brothers known collectively as the Hunter Utooni that they could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to their standard set. These sandy weathered weapons featured an orange, white, blue, gold, silver, and dark gray color scheme. The Arc Caster featured a yellow and white circular component on the top side, while the Pulse Blaster possessed a blue and silver circular component in the same general area. The weapons resembled the aesthetics of droids like RX-24 who were employed by the Star Tours Travel Agency.
