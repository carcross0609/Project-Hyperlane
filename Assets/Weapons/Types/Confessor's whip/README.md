# Confessor's whip

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Confessor's whip](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Confessor%27s_whip)

The confessor's whip was a serrated barb tipped whip of Geonosian make, used in the martial arts form known as Petranaki. The barb was capable of inflicting major lacerations on a target, and was often coated in poison.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Confessor's whip
- **Manufacturer:** Geonosians
- **Type:** Slashing
- **Cost:** 750 credits
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg
- **Range:** 6 meters
- **Affiliation:** Geonosians
