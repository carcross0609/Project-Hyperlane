# Baradium charge

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Baradium charge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baradium_charge)

Baradium charges were explosives that made use of the radioactive chemical baradium. The charges cost 750 credits per unit. Though their blast radius was rather small, the radiation produced by the charges could affect entire planets. During the Clone Wars, Bounty hunter Boba Fett made an attempt on Jedi Master Mace Windu's life utilizing a shaped baradium charge concealed within his father's helmet.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Baradium charge
- **Type:** Explosive
- **Cost:** 750 credits
