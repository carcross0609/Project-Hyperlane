# Electrified net gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Electrified net gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electrified_net_gun)

An electrified net gun, also known as an electronet launcher or energy net gun, was a type of net gun that could fire a paralyzing, electrically charged stun net in the form of five yellow energy balls, between which the webbing of the net formed and expanded as it approached its target. During the Second Battle of Felucia during the Clone Wars, the Trandoshan Lo-Taren utilized an electrified net gun to capture the Jedi padawan Ahsoka Tano, who was active in the battle on the behalf of the Galactic Republic. Tano was knocked unconscious by the weapon and was subsequently kidnapped by the Trandoshan.
