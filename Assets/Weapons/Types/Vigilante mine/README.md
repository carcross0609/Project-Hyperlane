# Vigilante mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vigilante mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vigilante_mine)

A Vigilante mine was a model of space mine developed and produced by Merr-Sonn around 41 ABY used during Second Galactic Civil War in Second Battle of Fondor. They were known for being quick and easy to lay and self-dispersing. Another feature of the Vigilante was its ability to be deactivated and picked up for later use.
