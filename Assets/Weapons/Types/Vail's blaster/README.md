# Vail's blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Vail's blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vail%27s_blaster)

Vail's blaster was a one-of-a-kind blaster pistol, that resembled a S-195 blaster pistol, and was owned by the human bounty hunter Vail Tormin during the reign of the Galactic Empire. The double-barreled blaster used a multi-trigger setup which relied on the dexterity of Tormin's finger prosthetics in order to fire. Tormin used the blaster while hunting the thief Kay Vess in order to collect the death mark placed on her by the Zerek Besh crime syndicate during 3 ABY.
