# Teta's Blade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Teta's Blade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Teta%27s_Blade)

Teta's Blade was a sword that was once used by Empress Teta during the Great Hyperspace War. Revan may have found it during his quest for the Star Forge in 3956 BBY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Teta's Blade
- **Type:** Short sword
- **Owners:** Empress Teta
