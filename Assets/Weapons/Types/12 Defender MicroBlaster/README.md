# 12 Defender MicroBlaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [12 Defender MicroBlaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/12_Defender_MicroBlaster)

The Gee-Tech No. 12 "Defender" MicroBlaster was a disposable hold-out blaster. It was primarily designed as a weapon for a surprise attack or distraction. It had almost no range or power and could not be recharged or reloaded.
