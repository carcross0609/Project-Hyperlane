# SG-4 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SG-4 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SG-4_blaster_rifle)

The SG-4 blaster rifle was developed by the Imperial Department of Military Research due to the fact that a regular blaster's range was reduced in water. The SG-4 was a combination weapon that fired normal blaster bolts out of water and miniature harpoon rounds underwater.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SG-4 blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Imperial Department of Military Research
- **Model:** SG-4
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 400 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 50 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
  - Autofire mode available
  - Underwater miniature harpoons
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 m
  - Maximum: up to 450 m
- **Purpose:** Underwater combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Seatroopers
