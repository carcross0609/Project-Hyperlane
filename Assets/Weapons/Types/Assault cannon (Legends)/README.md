# Assault cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Assault cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_cannon/Legends)

The assault cannon was a weapon with two firing modes used by the Galactic Empire's Phase II dark troopers. Each assault cannon fired plasma shells as their primary ammunition, and the weapon could also launch long-range explosive rockets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Assault cannon(A Dark Trooper armed with an assault cannon)
