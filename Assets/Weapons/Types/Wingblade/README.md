# Wingblade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wingblade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wingblade)

Wingblades were a curved bladed weapon associated with the Talortai species. The Talortai navigator Udi Dis carried a pair of wingblades; during his time with the Nihil, the wingblades were his only remaining possession from his home of Talor.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wingblade
- **Type:** Bladed weapon
- **Culture:** Talortai
- **Locations:** Talor
- **Shape:** Curved
