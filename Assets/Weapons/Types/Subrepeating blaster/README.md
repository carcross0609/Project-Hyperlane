# Subrepeating blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Subrepeating blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Subrepeating_blaster)

A subrepeating blaster was a pistol-sized repeating blaster with a built-in folding stock. They were popular with space pirates and bounty hunters who wanted a small, easy-to-wield weapon with a lot of firepower. The subrepeating blaster was restricted to operating in autofire-only mode, that is, rapid automatic fire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Subrepeating blaster
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Culture:**
  - Pirates
  - Bounty hunters
- **Cost:** 750 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Shape:** Pistol-shaped
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 2 kg
- **Capacity:** 50 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 120 m
