# Hutt Blades

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hutt Blades](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt_Blades)

The Hutt Blades were a set of unique Dual Vibroblades owned by the Kubaz Hunter Glikkin that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard vibroblades.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hutt Blades
- **Type:** Petar-like dual vibroblades (Glikkin's vibroblades)
- **Owners:** Glikkin
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
