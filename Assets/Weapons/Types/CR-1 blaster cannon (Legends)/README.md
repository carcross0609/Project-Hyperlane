# CR-1 blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [CR-1 blaster cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR-1_blaster_cannon/Legends)

The CR-1 blaster cannon (also called the CR-1 blast cannon) was a shotgun-like heavy blast cannon developed by Golan Arms.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CR-1 blaster cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Model:** CR-1
- **Type:** Modified blaster cannon
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 6 kg
- **Capacity:** 5 shots
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 90 m
- **Purpose:**
  - Anti-infantry
  - Suppressing riots
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
