# Sun Burst Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Sun Burst Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Burst_Slugthrower)

The Sun Burst Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It was a basic dark gray, silver, and white slugthrower with unique markings that were blue, red, and orange, as well as a golden sun like design printed on the stock. Fellow Hunter duo Utooni seemed to like this rifle.
