# Turbolaser II

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Turbolaser II](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Turbolaser_II)

The Turbolaser II was a type of heavy turbolaser emplacement manufactured by Golan Arms, used to protect a variety of military compounds and fortress from starfighter attacks. It drew power from a dedicated power generator and required a crew of three to operate, although if incapacitated the weapon could also be controlled from a central command center.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Turbolaser II
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Type:** Anti-aircraft battery
- **Cost:**
  - 50,000 credits (new)
  - 30,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 3
- **Capacity:** Unlimited (power generator)
- **Range:** 140 kilometers
