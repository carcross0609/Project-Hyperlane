# Beastmaster's vibro-glaive

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beastmaster's vibro-glaive](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beastmaster%27s_vibro-glaive)

Beastmaster's vibro-glaive was a type of vibro-glaive, that was most often found in the hands of the Weequay. They were popular among the dark wolf beastmasters of Sriluur, who used the flat edge of the polearm to keep their packs in line, exploiting the glaive's reach to maintain a relatively safe distance from the lethal tails of the raquor'daan. The Weequay also made use of the vibro-glaives in their wars with the Houk, wielding these vicious weapons to even the odds in close combat against their larger, thick-skinned adversaries.
