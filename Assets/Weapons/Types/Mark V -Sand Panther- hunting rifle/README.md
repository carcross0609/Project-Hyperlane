# Mark V "Sand Panther" hunting rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mark V "Sand Panther" hunting rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mark_V_%22Sand_Panther%22_hunting_rifle)

The Mark V "Sand Panther" hunting rifle was a type of slugthrower rifle manufactured by Coronet Arms, and was based on an ancient rifle once used to hunt Corellian sand panthers. This slugthrower was a design of beauty, as much artwork as functional weapon, it employed golden creshik wood imported from Tralus for its hand-carved furniture and stock. Aimed largely at collectors and enthusiasts, the Mark V rose in popularity for several decades as a retirement gift among Corellians.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Mark V "Sand Panther" hunting rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Coronet Arms
- **Type:** Slugthrower rifle

- **Range:** Long-ranged
