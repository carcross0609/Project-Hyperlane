# Rogue E-Web

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rogue E-Web](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rogue_E-Web)

The Rogue E-Web was an E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon owned by the human Hunter Sentinel that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard E-Web. It resembled the MWC-35c "Staccato Lightning" repeating cannon used by Rogue One member Baze Malbus just before his death at the Battle of Scarif.

## Specifications

- **Model:** E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon (Sentinel's E-Web)
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Owners:** Sentinel
