# Sith sniper rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sith sniper rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_sniper_rifle)

The Sith sniper rifle was a type of modified blaster rifle used by the Sith Empire during the Jedi Civil War against the Galactic Republic. One such blaster rifle was owned by the Human female Kebla Yurt in 3956 BBY. She had the weapon in stock at her Equipment Emporium in the Upper City of the Outer Rim planet Taris. Unlike much of her other weaponry, the sniper rifle was not confiscated by the Sith when they occupied and quarantined the ecumenopolis. In the same year, Sith sniper rifles were present at the Sith Academy on the Outer Rim planet Korriban and the Sith-occupied Temple of the Ancients, located in the Unknown Regions on the Rakatan homeworld Lehon.
