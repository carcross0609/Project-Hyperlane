# E-10.5 rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [E-10.5 rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-10.5_rifle)

A type of blaster rifle that was effectively an E-10.5 rifle was an interim design which incorporated elements of the earlier E-10 blaster rifle and the later E-11 medium blaster rifle. The rifle retained the longer barrel and retractable stock of the E-10 but included the optical rangefinder scope found on the E-11. While the E-11 had already become the standard issue blaster rifle of the Stormtrooper Corps, many of the Imperial Army troopers who served as part of the Aldhani Garrison and Lieutenant Krole's contingent on Mina-Rau were equipped with the earlier rifles. They were also later used by Imperial security troopers during the Ghorman Massacre in 2 BBY.
