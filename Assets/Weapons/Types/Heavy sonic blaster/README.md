# Heavy sonic blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy sonic blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_sonic_blaster)

The heavy sonic pistol or heavy sonic blaster was a sonic weapon that fired compressed bolts of sonic energy. It was a more powerful version of the sonic pistol.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy sonic blaster
- **Type:** Sonic weapon
- **Cost:** 1,250 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 50 shots
  - Cannot be deflected by a lightsaber
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 meters
  - Maximum: up to 120 meters
- **Purpose:**
  - To counter lightsaber-wielding Jedi and Sith
  - To disorient an opponent
