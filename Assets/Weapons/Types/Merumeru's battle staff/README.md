# Merumeru's battle staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Merumeru's battle staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Merumeru%27s_battle_staff)

Merumeru, a Wookiee Elder, wielded a battle staff in combat. The staff was brown and roughly half the height of Merumeru. He used the staff in 19 BBY during the Clone Wars, when the Confederacy of Independent Systems invaded the Wookiee homeworld Kashyyyk. Merumeru gathered a group of soldiers and, holding the staff aloft, he roared for his soldiers to charge into battle against the Separatists.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Merumeru's battle staff
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Culture:** Wookiees
- **Owners:** Merumeru
- **Locations:** Kachirho
- **Height:** Roughly half that of Merumeru
- **Affiliation:** Merumeru's volunteer soldiers
