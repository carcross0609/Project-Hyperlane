# Composite bow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Composite bow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Composite_bow)

Composite bow was a type of bow that could launch physical arrow projectiles. The Zabrak Hunter Nox used a Composite Bow called the Wild Bow during the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition within the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara.
