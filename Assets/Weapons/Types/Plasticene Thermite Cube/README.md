# Plasticene Thermite Cube

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Plasticene Thermite Cube](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plasticene_Thermite_Cube)

Plasticene Thermite Cubes, also referred to as plasticene thermite gel, was a controlled explosive produced by Gatrellis. The thermite gel was a malleable substance that could cut through a variety of substances. It was often used by pirates and privateers to cut through hulls or bulkheads.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Plasticene Thermite Cube
- **Manufacturer:** Gatrellis
- **Type:** Controlled pyrotechnic explosive
- **Cost:** 1,000 credits per kilogram
