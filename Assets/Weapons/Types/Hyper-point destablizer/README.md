# Hyper-point destablizer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Hyper-point destablizer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyper-point_destablizer)

A hyper-point destablizer was a type of weapon. During the Clone Wars, the Shadowblade, a Separatist cruiser and superweapon, was armed with a hyper-point destablizer. Around 20 BBY, the Shadowblade and its hyper-point destablizer were seemingly destroyed following an attack by the Galactic Republic on Separatist forces in the Kro'eval system, where the Shadowblade was under construction. However, the warship secretly survived the battle and later reappeared in the Cularin system in 19 BBY, where it was drawn into a temporal rift by Jedi Master Darrus Jeht and never seen again.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hyper-point destablizer
- **Purpose:** Armament of the Shadowblade superweapon
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
