# Krakana Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Krakana Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krakana_Blaster)

The Krakana Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This colorful blaster pistol featured a metallic silver and black frame, with blue and orange elements, a white grip, and a decal depicting a krakana, the mascot and logo for the Mon Cala Krakanas huttball team.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Krakana Blaster
- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
- **Type:**

- **Owners:** Zaina
