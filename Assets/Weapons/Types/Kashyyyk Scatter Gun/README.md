# Kashyyyk Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kashyyyk Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kashyyyk_Scatter_Gun)

The Kashyyyk Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. It was designed to resemble the overall aesthetic, both in color and construct, of Wookiee technology.

- **Name:** Kashyyyk Scatter Gun
- **Type:** Scatter gun
