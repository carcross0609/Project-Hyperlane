# LX-4 proton mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LX-4 proton mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LX-4_proton_mine)

LX-4 proton mines were a model of proton mine produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. They were retrofitted versions of the explosive warheads that Merr-Sonn used in its LX-4 proton torpedos, which the company manufactured and supplied simultaneously during the reign of the Galactic Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** LX-4 proton mine
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** LX-4
- **Type:** Proton mine
- **Material:** Explosive warhead
