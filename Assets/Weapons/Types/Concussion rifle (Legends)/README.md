# Concussion rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Concussion rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Concussion_rifle/Legends)

The concussion rifle was an uncommon rifle that fired a concussive energy charge, creating a powerful shock wave that was able to knock down a full-grown person.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Concussion rifle
- **Manufacturer:**
  - Arakyd Industries
  - BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Concussion rifle
- **Culture:**
  - Trandoshan mercenaries
  - Slavers
  - Bounty hunters
- **Cost:** 1,800 credits
- **Shape:** Rifle-shaped
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 2.1 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 25 shots
  - Cannot be deflected by a lightsaber
  - Collateral damage to adjacent targets
  - Blast diameter of 4 meters
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 45 meters
  - Maximum: up to 450 meters
