# Magna-gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Magna-gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Magna-gun)

The magna-gun was a powerful brand of ion cannon on the planet Brentaal IV. During his campaign for power in 21 BBY, Separatist warlord Shogar Tok took control of the magna-guns and established them at his fortress on the world. They were sabotaged by Jedi Master Quinlan Vos during the Battle of Brentaal IV of the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Magna-gun
- **Type:** Ion cannon
- **Locations:** Shogar Tok's fortress, Brentaal IV
- **Range:** From planetary surface to near orbit
- **Purpose:** Artillery
- **Affiliation:** Confederacy of Independent Systems
