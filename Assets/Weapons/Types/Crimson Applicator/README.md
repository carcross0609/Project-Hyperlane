# Crimson Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Crimson Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crimson_Applicator)

The Crimson Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This applicator featured a red, black, white, and silver color scheme.

- **Name:** Crimson Applicator
- **Type:** Long-range dart gun
