# Hoth Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Hoth Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoth_Blaster)

The Hoth Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This snub blaster pistol featured a black frame and large silver barrel.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Hoth Blaster
- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
- **Type:**

- **Owners:** Zaina
