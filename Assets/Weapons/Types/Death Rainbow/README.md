# Death Rainbow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Uncategorized  
**Source:** [Death Rainbow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Rainbow)

The Death Rainbow was an experimental superweapon created for the Galactic Empire under the rule of Emperor Palpatine. Between 4 ABY and 5 ABY, an Imperial stormtrooper wrote in his handbook, The Official Stormtrooper Training Manual, that prior to the construction of the Death Star battle station, Palpatine experimented with other superweapons such as the Death Rainbow along with the Death Cloud and the Death Moon.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Death Rainbow
- **Type:** Superweapon
- **Creators:** Galactic Empire (Under the direction of Palpatine)
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
