# Sg-82 sonic rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sg-82 sonic rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sg-82_sonic_rifle)

The Sg-82 sonic rifle was a sonic weapon produced by Pacnorval Defense Systems, Limited. The Sg-82 functioned both as a sonic blaster and as a stun club.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sg-82 sonic rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Pacnorval Defense Systems, Limited
- **Model:** Sg-82
- **Type:** Sonic rifle
- **Cost:** 2,000 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Capacity:** 20 shots
- **Range:**
  - 15 meters (optimum)
  - 35 meters (maximum)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Santhe Security
  - White Thranta Shipping Company
