# Jinkins J-1 rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jinkins J-1 rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jinkins_J-1_rifle)

The J-1 blaster rifle was an experimental blaster rifle created by Jinkins of the Lok Revenants. After the Battle of Yavin, the weapon was awarded to a spacer who joined the Lok Revenants and provided exceptional service to Nym and the Revenants.
