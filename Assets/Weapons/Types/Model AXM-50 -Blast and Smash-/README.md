# Model AXM-50 "Blast and Smash"

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Model AXM-50 "Blast and Smash"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_AXM-50_%22Blast_and_Smash%22)

The Model AXM-50 "Blast and Smash", or Prax "Blast and Smash" Energy Rifle, was an over-under, twin-barreled weapon designed by Prax Arms. The AXM-50 combined a Drearian Defense Conglomerate rapid-fire blaster rifle with a modified Locris Syndicates micro-grenade launcher underneath. A bipod could be attached to increase accuracy.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model AXM-50 "Blast and Smash"
- **Manufacturer:** Prax Arms
- **Type:** Combined blaster rifle and micro-grenade launcher
- **Cost:**
  - 4,500 credits
  - 250 credits (bipod)
  - 1,000 credits (Micro-grenade magazine)
- **Capacity:** 250 shots per power pack; 30 in the micro-grenade magazine
