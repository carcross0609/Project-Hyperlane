# RAX-1

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [RAX-1](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RAX-1)

The RAX-1 was a concussion missile launcher system designed by Alliance engineer Yunji Oryn. The original protoype was known as the Repulsor-Assisted Ground-to-Orbit Concussion Missile Launcher (RAGOC), and was designated as the RAX-1 when pushed into full production by Oryn Engineering.

## Specifications

- **Name:** RAX-1
- **Manufacturer:** Oryn Engineering
- **Type:** Concussion missile emplacement
- **Cost:**
  - 200,000 credits (launcher, new)
  - 75,000 credits (launcher, used)
  - 2,000 credits ("smart" rocket)
  - 5,000 credits ("hound" rocket)
- **Crew:** 6
- **Capacity:** 4 concussion missile
- **Range:** High orbit
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  - Pirates
