# Clan Vizsla MT-97

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clan Vizsla MT-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_Vizsla_MT-97)

The Clan Vizsla MT-97 was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. Though part of Clan Tal himself, who had a complicated history with Clan Vizsla, Aran brandished these weapons, bearing the rival clan's traditional colors and displaying their branch sigil on the grips of each pistol.
