# HOB heavy repeating blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [HOB heavy repeating blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HOB_heavy_repeating_blaster)

The HOB heavy repeating blaster was a type of heavy repeating blaster manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation. It was a high-output repeating blaster and was SoroSuub's attempt to loosen BlasTech Industries' and Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.'s strangleholds on the heavy repeater market. Its design integrated an adjustable tripod that allowed for comfortable standing, seating, lying, and trench operation. Lauded for its high rate of fire, effective range, and maintenance and repair ease, the HOB was popular with planetary defense forces and private security firms.

## Specifications

- **Name:** HOB heavy repeating blaster
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
