# Kyuzo war shield

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kyuzo war shield](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyuzo_war_shield)

Kyuzo war shields, also known as Kyuzo war helmets or simply Kyuzo hats, were wide-brimmed metal hats worn by the Kyuzos of Phatrong. Immune to blaster bolts, they were also sometimes used as shields and could be thrown as missiles. The bounty hunter Embo was known to use his hat in battle.
