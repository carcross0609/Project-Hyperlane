# Baragwin Disruptor-X weapon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Baragwin Disruptor-X weapon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Baragwin_Disruptor-X_weapon)

The Baragwin Disruptor-X weapon was a disruptor rifle of Baragwin design that had been extensively modified by the Rodian Suvam Tan. Similar to his Baragwin Ion-X weapon, the most significant alteration was the incorporation of a shaped-energy delivery system that delivered concussive force in addition to normal energy discharge.
