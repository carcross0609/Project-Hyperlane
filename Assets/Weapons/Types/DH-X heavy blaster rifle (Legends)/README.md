# DH-X heavy blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DH-X heavy blaster rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DH-X_heavy_blaster_rifle/Legends)

The DH-X heavy blaster rifle was a long-ranged model of long-range heavy blaster rifle produced by BlasTech Industries. The design included a scope, a stock and a power pack point below and behind the barrel. It was particularly popular with mercenaries who favored heavy weaponry, and saw large amounts of use during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. As the largest heavy blaster rifle that Blastech produced it was fairly cumbersome and slow firing, but was capable of dropping most foes in a single blast. The weapon cost a total of 1,980 credits and was thought to fill the niche between blaster rifles and repeating blasters.
