# Magna Grafted Shields

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Magna Grafted Shields](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Magna_Grafted_Shields)

The Magna Grafted Shields were a pair of shields owned by the KX-series security droid Hunter K-A0S that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard Grafted Twin Shields. When combined, these shields took on the appearance of a IG-100 MagnaGuard's chest, surrounded by an electric design reminiscent of that produced by electrostaffs.
