# Guardian Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Guardian Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian_Slugthrower)

The Guardian Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This slugthrower was gold, silver, gray, burgundy, and emerald in coloration, with a green tassel tied below the barrel.
