# E-11 blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [E-11 blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-11_blaster_pistol)

The E-11 blaster pistol was a type of weapon used in the days of the Old Republic. The Human Zeerid Korr sometimes wore an E-11 blaster pistol in a single-sling holster under his jacket, and carried the blaster in place of a pair of GH-44 blasters.
