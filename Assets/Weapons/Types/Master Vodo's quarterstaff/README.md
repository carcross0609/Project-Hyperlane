# Master Vodo's quarterstaff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Master Vodo's quarterstaff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Master_Vodo%27s_quarterstaff)

Jedi Master Vodo-Siosk Baas carried a wooden quarterstaff that offered support to his aged physical being. When the need for combat arose, Master Baas imbued his staff with the power of the Force, whereupon the cane served him in place of a lightsaber.
