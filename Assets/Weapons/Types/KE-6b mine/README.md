# KE-6b mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [KE-6b mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KE-6b_mine)

The KE-6b mine was a modified version of the KE-6 proximity mine manufactured by Golan Arms and utilized by Galactic Empire during the Galactic Civil War. Imperial shock troopers were known to drop proximity mines to effectively wipe out entire squads of Rebel soldiers and defend locations.

## Specifications

- **Name:** KE-6b mine
- **Manufacturer:** Golan Arms
- **Model:** KE-6b
- **Type:** Proximity mine
- **Purpose:**
  - Anti-personal
  - Anti-vehicle
- **Affiliation:** Galactic Empire
