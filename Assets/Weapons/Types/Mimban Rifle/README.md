# Mimban Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Mimban Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mimban_Rifle)

The Mimban Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This streamlined blaster rifle consisted of a black, silver, and tan frame, with red and yellow painted portions along the top and sides, as well as brown strips of clock wrapped around the grip. According to Imara Vex, she may have used this blaster during her time on the swamp planet Mimban.
