# Tatooine blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tatooine blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tatooine_blaster_pistol)

The Tatooine blaster pistol was a model that saw use during the last decades of the Galactic Republic. The pistol was similar in appearance to other BlasTech Industries' blaster models like the DL-44 heavy blaster pistol. The blaster featured a sighting scope above its thin barrel. Vilmarh Grahrk was known to carry one of these blasters and at least one was seen in Mos Espa on Tatooine around the time of the Invasion of Naboo.
