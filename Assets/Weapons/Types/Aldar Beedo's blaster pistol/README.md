# Aldar Beedo's blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Aldar Beedo's blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aldar_Beedo%27s_blaster_pistol)

A silver blaster pistol served as a weapon for the Glymphid hit-man Aldar Beedo. The Fluggrian crime lord Kam Nale also owned one. The blaster pistol had a round, streamlined receiver and a long narrow barrel, and heavily resembled a ELG-3A blaster pistol. The differences were that the ELG-3A pistols did not have a trigger-guard, and the also unlike the ELG-3A this blaster did not have a wooden grip, the receiver was not as smooth near the front of the blaster. The gun fired green blaster bolts.
