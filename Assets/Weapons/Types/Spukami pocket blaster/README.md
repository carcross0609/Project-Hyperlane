# Spukami pocket blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Spukami pocket blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spukami_pocket_blaster)

Manufactured on Talus and Tralus by Fed-Dub Protective Services, the Spukami pocket blaster was sold throughout the Corellian system as a home-defense weapon. Named after a common Corellian breed of vermin-hunting housecat, the Spukami managed to pack an impressive stopping power for a holdout blaster. The pocket blaster also included a stun setting, which made it popular throughout the Corellian sector, and while it was technically effective out to thirty meters, in practice, the beam cohesion degraded after just a few meters, and caused a reduction in damage and accuracy at range.
