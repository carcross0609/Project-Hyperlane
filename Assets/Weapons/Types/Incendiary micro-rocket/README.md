# Incendiary micro-rocket

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Incendiary micro-rocket](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Incendiary_micro-rocket)

Incendiary micro-rockets were a class of micro-rocket. The rockets typically sold for 175 credits apiece and were popular with bounty hunters. Incendiary micro-rockets released burst of flaming chemicals that could be used to force individuals out of buildings or kill swarms of insects.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Incendiary micro-rocket
- **Type:** Micro-rocket
- **Cost:** 175 credits
