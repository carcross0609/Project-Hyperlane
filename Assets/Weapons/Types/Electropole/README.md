# Electropole

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Electropole](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electropole)

The electropole was a close-quarter electric melee weapon, its primary form of attack placed on the end of a long shaft. The Mandalorian Guard, the Gungan Grand Army, Separatist Droid Army, and the Rebel Honor Guard wielded such weapons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Electropole
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Purpose:** Melee combat
- **Affiliation:**
