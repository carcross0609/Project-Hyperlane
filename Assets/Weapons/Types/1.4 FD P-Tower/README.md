# 1.4 FD P-Tower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [1.4 FD P-Tower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1.4_FD_P-Tower)

The 1.4 FD P-Tower, also known simply as a P-Tower was a light fixed anti-vehicle weapon emplacement manufactured by the Atgar SpaceDefense Corporation. It held enough power to disable an AT-ST, and its rugged dependability meant it could function in even the harshest environments.

## Specifications

- **Name:** 1.4 FD P-Tower
- **Manufacturer:** Atgar SpaceDefense Corporation
- **Model:** 1.4 FD
- **Type:** Light anti-vehicle laser cannon
- **Height:** 2.80 meters
- **Crew:** 4 operators
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
