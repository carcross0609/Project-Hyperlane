# Heavy repeating blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Heavy repeating blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_repeating_blaster/Legends)

The heavy repeating blaster was a large and powerful variant of a blaster rifle.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Type:** Repeating blaster
- **Cost:** 4,000 to 6,000 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 12 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 20 shots on a single power pack
  - can be attached to a power generator for longer use
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 75 m
  - Maximum: up to 750 m
- **Affiliation:**
  - Grand Army of the Republic
  -   ARC Troopers
  - Alliance Special Forces heavy weapon specialists
