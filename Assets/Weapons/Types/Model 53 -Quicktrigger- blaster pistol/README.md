# Model 53 "Quicktrigger" blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Model 53 "Quicktrigger" blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_53_%22Quicktrigger%22_blaster_pistol)

The Model 53 "Quicktrigger" blaster pistol was a type of blaster pistol manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc., and was popular across the galactic frontier backwaters due to its superior customizability and reliability. At some point, Merr-Sonn lost a shipment of Model 53s in an explosion over the planet Coruscant. However, within a month after the explosion, the Coruscant Security Force released a report on an increase in crime due to the prevalence of salvaged Model 53s. And as a result, Merr-Sonn could not have paid for better publicity regarding the durability of the Quicktrigger.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model 53 "Quicktrigger" blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 450 credits
- **Range:** Medium-ranged
