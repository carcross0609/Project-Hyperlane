# Elrik Vonreg's RK-3 blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Elrik Vonreg's RK-3 blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elrik_Vonreg%27s_RK-3_blaster)

A customized red-accented RK-3 blaster was utilized by First Order Major Elrik Vonreg. The pistol was manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. and Sonn-Blas Corporation. In 34 ABY, after the First Order hired pirate Kragan Gorr and his Warbird gang to abduct the pilot Torra Doza, Vonreg employed the weapon to betray the pirates aboard their sail barge, the Galleon. The pilot shot the pirates Drell and Skreek during the blaster fight and retrieved Doza from her captors.
