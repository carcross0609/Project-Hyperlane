# Midnight Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Midnight Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Midnight_Applicator)

The Midnight Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This worn applicator featured a black, gray, and cream color scheme, with a glowing pink core, pink splatters on the barrel, and cream cloth wrapped around the stock.
