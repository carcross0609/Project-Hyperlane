# DL-6H heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DL-6H heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DL-6H_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The DL-6H heavy blaster pistol was a model of heavy blaster pistol produced by BlasTech Industries during the New Order. Because of its relatively small size and high power output, the DL-6H was outlawed in many star systems. The Pirate Captain Venslas Beeli used a pair of these weapons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DL-6H heavy blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 800 credits
- **Capacity:** 25 shots
- **Range:** 120 meters
