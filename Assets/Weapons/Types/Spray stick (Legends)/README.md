# Spray stick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Spray stick/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spray_stick/Legends)

Spray sticks, also known as stokhli spray sticks or simply stokhli sticks, were nonlethal ranged weapons used to stun and capture living beings. They were developed by the Stokhli nomads of the planet Manress.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Spray stick
- **Manufacturer:** Stokhli
- **Model:** Stokhli spray stick
- **Type:** Personal stunning ranged weapon
- **Culture:**
  - Stokhli
  - Big-game hunters
- **Locations:** Bimmisaari
- **Cost:** 14,000 credits
- **Shape:** Rod-like
- **Size:** Medium
- **Material:** Metal
- **Length:**
  - 1 meter (large version)
  - Small enough to be concealed (short-range version)
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 5,000 meters of netting
  - Can also be used as stunning melee weapon
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 100 meters
  - Maximum: up to 200 meters
- **Purpose:**
  - To create a stunning net to immobilize and bring down a target without killing it
  - Crowd control and riot suppression
  - Hunting large game
- **Affiliation:**
  - Thrawn's confederation
  -   Noghri Death Commandos
