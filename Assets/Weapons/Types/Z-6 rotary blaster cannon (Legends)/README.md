# Z-6 rotary blaster cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Z-6 rotary blaster cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-6_rotary_blaster_cannon/Legends)

The Z-6 rotary blaster cannon was a powerful rotating blaster cannon chaingun developed by Merr-Sonn Munitions and used during the Clone Wars and the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Z-6 rotary blaster cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Model:** Z-6 rotary blaster cannon
- **Type:** Chaingun
- **Cost:** 5,500 credits (available for military use only)
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 16 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 20 shots
  - Autofire-only
  - Can be attached to a power generator for longer use
- **Range:**
  - Up to 75 m (optimum)
  - Up to 750 m (maximum)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  -   Grand Army of the Republic
  - Galactic Empire
  - Kota's Militia
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
