# DC-15A blaster carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DC-15A blaster carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DC-15A_blaster_carbine)

The DC-15A blaster carbine, also referred to as the DC-15S blaster, was a blaster carbine model used by the Grand Army of the Republic. It was part of the DC-15 family. While smaller than the larger DC-15A blaster rifle, both could be referred to as blaster rifles. It was one of the most common weapons issued to clone troopers during the Clone Wars and were used during many battles. Clone stormtroopers and other Imperial troops, such as TK stormtroopers, continued to make use of the blaster throughout the early years of the Galactic Empire. Eventually, both variants of the DC-15A blaster rifles were phased out of Imperial military use, and were succeeded by the E-11 medium blaster rifle and the DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle.
