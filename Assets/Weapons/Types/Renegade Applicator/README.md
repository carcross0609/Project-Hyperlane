# Renegade Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Renegade Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Renegade_Applicator)

The Renegade Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This plain applicator featured black metal base, with bronze and pink components around the barrel, a glowing pink core, and a stock painted to resemble wood.
