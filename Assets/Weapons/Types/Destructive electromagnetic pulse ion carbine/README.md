# Destructive electromagnetic pulse ion carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Destructive electromagnetic pulse ion carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Destructive_electromagnetic_pulse_ion_carbine)

The Destructive Electromagnetic Pulse Ion Carbine (abbreviated as DEMP Ion Carbine or just DEMP) was an electromagnetic pulse weapon in use by the Galactic Empire's Inferno Squad, and manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.. The DEMP Ion Carbine was the blaster carbine version of the DEMP gun.

- **Name:** Destructive electromagnetic pulse ion carbine
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** DEMP gun
- **Purpose:** Electromagnetic pulse weapon
