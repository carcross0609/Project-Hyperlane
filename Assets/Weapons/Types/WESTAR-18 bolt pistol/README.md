# WESTAR-18 bolt pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [WESTAR-18 bolt pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WESTAR-18_bolt_pistol)

The WESTAR-18 bolt pistol was a model of a blaster pistol and the predecessor to the WESTAR-34 blaster pistol that was considered ancient by the time of the Cold War. One such pistol was owned by Corso Riggs, who gave it the name "Sparkles," like he did with all his weapons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** WESTAR-18 bolt pistol
- **Model:** WESTAR-18
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
