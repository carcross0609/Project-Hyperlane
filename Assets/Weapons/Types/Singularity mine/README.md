# Singularity mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Singularity mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Singularity_mine)

Singularity mines were small, black, seedpod-like mines used by members of the Yuuzhan Vong species during the Yuuzhan Vong War. They were capable of creating a two-meter-wide singularity via a dovin basal that crushed any being to death who broke its shell and triggered it. In 27 ABY, Yuuzhan Vong–allied Senator Viqi Shesh and Yuuzhan Vong warriors used the mines to slow down a group of New Republic pursuers aboard the starferry Byrt over the planet Coruscant.
