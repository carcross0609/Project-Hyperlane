# Geonosian sonic blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Geonosian sonic blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geonosian_sonic_blaster/Legends)

The Geonosian sonic blaster, also known as Geonosian carbine, was a powerful, hand-held blaster incorporating sonic wave technology developed by the Gordarl Weaponsmiths. These weapons were used by Geonosians, and saw increased use during the Clone Wars.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Geonosian sonic blaster
- **Manufacturer:** Gordarl Weaponsmiths
- **Model:** Geonosian sonic blaster
- **Type:** Sonic blaster carbine
- **Culture:** Geonosian
- **Capacity:** 30 shots
- **Range:**
  - 15 meters (optimum)
  - 40 meters (maximum)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Geonosians warriors
  - Confederacy of Independent Systems
  - Separatist holdouts
