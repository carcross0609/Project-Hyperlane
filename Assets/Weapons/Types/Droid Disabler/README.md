# Droid Disabler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droid Disabler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_Disabler)

The Felebreck Electrical Defense Systems Droid Disabler was released in an era of increased malicious droid activity. It packed a great deal of power and was primarily designed to terminate, not incapacitate, automatons.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Droid Disabler
- **Manufacturer:** Felebreck Electrical Defense Systems
- **Type:** Ion pistol
- **Cost:** 250—700 credits (25 credits per power pack)
- **Size:** Small
- **Weight:** 2.2 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 5
- **Range:** 10 meters
