# Gentler rod

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gentler rod](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gentler_rod)

The gentler rod was a type of double-strength stun baton that was popular among slavers, who used them to discipline captives that they did not want to kill. The rods were also used by the Hutts, and some Hutts disguised their gentler rods as ornamental accessories, such as scepters. Gentler rods weighed 2.2kg and cost 1000 credits to purchase.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Gentler rod
- **Type:** Stun baton
- **Cost:** 1000 credits
