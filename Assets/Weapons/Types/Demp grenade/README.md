# Demp grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Demp grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Demp_grenade)

Demp grenades were explosives designed to disable all energy weapons and equipment, leaving them without power. They generally retailed for 800 credits. Droids with ion shielding could withstand the effects of a demp grenade.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Demp grenade
- **Type:** Grenade
- **Cost:** 800 credits
