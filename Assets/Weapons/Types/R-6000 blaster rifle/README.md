# R-6000 blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [R-6000 blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-6000_blaster_rifle)

The R-6000 blaster rifle was a blaster rifle produced by Czerka Corporation during the era of the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The rifle was known to have been demonstrated to Moff Kresk, leader of the Imperial Ordnance Acquisition Corps, by Czerka's weapon division's chief operating officer Eva Kaayz. After its presentation, the Imperial Moff ordered sixty-thousand units.
