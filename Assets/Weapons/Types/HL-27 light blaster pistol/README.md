# HL-27 light blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [HL-27 light blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HL-27_light_blaster_pistol)

The HL-27 light blaster pistol was a small, yet powerful blaster pistol manufactured by Coronet Arms. A superb example of the company's superior craftsmanship, the HL-27 was favored by gentry and those wanting to carry a first-rate blaster without announcing that they were armed. An excellent choice for those preferring discretion without sacrificing firepower, the HL-27 was an accurate blaster that was easily concealed; as a result it was not unheard of for CorSec agents to carry an HL-27 instead of the more common CDEF blaster pistol, as the two weapons shared nearly identical range and firepower. The weapon came equipped with a stun setting and was sold for 450 credits.
