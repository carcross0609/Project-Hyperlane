# Oneshot Slugthrower

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Oneshot Slugthrower](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oneshot_Slugthrower)

The Oneshot Slugthrower was a slugthrower rifle owned by the Miraluka Hunter Diago Velaar that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. This slugthrower as blocky in design, with a silver, red, white, black, and brown coloration. It also fearured a glowing red core, as well as the word "SHOT" pained in red aurebesh along the top.
