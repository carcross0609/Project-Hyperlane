# Power crystal

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Power crystal/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Power_crystal/Legends)

A power crystal was a rare crystal weapon found in the galaxy > by 41.5 ABY. Power crystals were used to bore a hole in deflector shields. They fire a red beam of energy that split into a spiral of red lines upon impact with the shields; the red lines then began to corkscrew, opening up a hole in the shield. Power crystals required a large amount of energy to operate. The salvagers Khedryn Faal and Marr Idi-Shael kept one on their freighter, the Junker.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Power crystal
- **Purpose:** Boring holes in deflector shields
