# Marshall's Special

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Marshall's Special](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Marshall%27s_Special)

The Marshall's Special was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This blaster pistol featured a weathered golden black metal frame, with wood-like plates attached to the side of the grip and a circular ring clip attached to the bottom.
