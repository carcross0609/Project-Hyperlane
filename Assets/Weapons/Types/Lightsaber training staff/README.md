# Lightsaber training staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lightsaber training staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_training_staff)

Lightsaber training staffs were staffs used in the place of real lightsabers by Jedi Padawans who had not yet constructed their own blades. The Human Jedi Initiate Petro practiced with a training staff while he was on board the Jedi training cruiser Crucible, deflecting shots from a Marksman-H combat remote and then showing his fellow trainees an original move he had created named the "Whirlwind of Destiny."

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lightsaber training staff
- **Purpose:** Training to use a Lightsaber
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
