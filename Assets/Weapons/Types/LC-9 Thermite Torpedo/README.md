# LC-9 Thermite Torpedo

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [LC-9 Thermite Torpedo](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LC-9_Thermite_Torpedo)

The LC-9 Thermite Torpedo was a type of starfighter torpedo with a thermite payload that was a mainstay of Czerka Corporation's explosive ordnance line during the Galactic War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. The LC-9 was primarily effective at extreme range and against armored starship hulls, and though it was less effective against shielding, the thermite detonation from its payload would often superheat nearby sections of the target's hull and thereby weaken them against further attack.
