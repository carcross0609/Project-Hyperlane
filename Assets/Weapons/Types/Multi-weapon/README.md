# Multi-weapon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Multi-weapon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Multi-weapon)

The multi-weapon was a melee weapon designed specifically for Infiltrators. It was an attempt to cut down on the weight of their equipment by combining three weapons into one tool. One end contains a weighted club, the other a sliding dagger secured by a twist lock, and a coiled garrote. Variations on the tool replace the garrote with a Slicewire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Multi-weapon
- **Manufacturer:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
- **Type:** Personal combat multi-weapon
- **Purpose:**
  - Club
  - Dagger
  - Garrote
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
