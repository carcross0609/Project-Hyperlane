# Lightning gun (Goroth Prime)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Lightning gun (Goroth Prime)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_gun_%28Goroth_Prime%29)

The lightning gun was a specialized energy weapon produced by Jer'N Weapons Systems. The only lethal energy weapon produced by the company, the lightning gun fired packets of charged sub-atomic particles. One packet was positively charged, while the other was negatively charged antiparticles. The two packets were kept apart by a "shepherd packet" which dissolved when they hit the target. When the two charged packets annihilate each other they generated a lethal blast.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Lightning gun
- **Manufacturer:** Jer'N Weapons Systems
- **Type:** Specialized energy weapon
- **Cost:** 875 credits; 35 credits per power pack
- **Capacity:** 6
- **Range:** 75 meters
