# Cinnagar carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Cinnagar carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cinnagar_carbine)

The Cinnagar carbine was one of several blaster rifle variants produced by factories in Cinnagar, the capital of the Empress Teta system, during extensive militarization efforts in the wake of the Great Hyperspace War of 5000 BBY. This militarization continued through the year 3956 BBY, when this particular variant was available.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Cinnagar carbine
- **Manufacturer:** Cinnagar
- **Created:** After 5000 BBY
- **Range:** 25 meters
