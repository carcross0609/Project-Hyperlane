# Corellian cutlass

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Corellian cutlass](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellian_cutlass)

The Corellian cutlass was a short, curved saber presented to officers of the Corellian Naval Forces once they attained the rank of Lieutenant. While primarily ornamental in nature, they were still quite useful in close-quarters combat and were highly prized for their excellent craftmanship.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Corellian cutlass
- **Model:** Cutlass
- **Type:** Sword
- **Culture:** Corellian Navy
- **Cost:** 300 credits
- **Shape:** Curved blade
- **Purpose:** Melee combat
