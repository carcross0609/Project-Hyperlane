# Private Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Private Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Private_Blaster)

The Private Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This small worn blaster pistol featured a brown, silver, and dark gray frame, with the brown portions displaying a striated texture, as well as a side-mounted scope connected to the blaster via black couplings.
