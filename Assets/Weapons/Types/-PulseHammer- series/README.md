# "PulseHammer" series

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** ["PulseHammer" series](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%22PulseHammer%22_series)

The "PulseHammer" series was a tripod-mounted repeating blaster manufactured by BlasTech Industries. It was a powerful weapon, but had a lengthy recycle time on the energy cell meaning that during the cool down cycle, the cell was too busy recharging itself and the weapon could not fire. BlasTech discontinued the series due to this flaw.

## Specifications

- **Name:** "PulseHammer" series
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Repeating blaster
