# Grav charge

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grav charge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grav_charge)

A grav charge was a type of explosive device. In 9 ABY, the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin placed a grav charge on a wall of the Imperial Remnant safe house, which caused an explosion that created a large opening. Djarin later used grav charges to destroy the Klatooinian wikiup and the AT-ST Raider. He restocked his munition later in the Mandalorian covert, and used a charge to destroy Gideon's Outland TIE fighter. It was also used by Mandalorians who bore the symbol of Death Watch on their pauldrons. They tried to kill a dinosaur turtle with the charge without success.
