# Tuskbeast pike

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Tuskbeast pike](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tuskbeast_pike)

Tuskbeast pikes were melee weapons used by members of the sentient Nikto species native to the planet Kintan in Hutt Space. When the play Evocar was released glorifying the Tionese despot Xim, some Nikto carved dialogue from the play onto their tuskbeast pikes. Tuskbeasts were a non-sentient species of animal native to Kintan.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tuskbeast pike
- **Type:** Melee
- **Culture:** Nikto
- **Markings:** Dialogue from Evocar
