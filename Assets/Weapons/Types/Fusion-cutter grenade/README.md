# Fusion-cutter grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fusion-cutter grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fusion-cutter_grenade)

Fusion-cutter grenades were a type of grenade used during the time of the New Republic. During the Black Fleet Crisis, fusion-cutter grenades were carried by a group of Wookiees led by Chewbacca when they boarded the Pride of Yevetha to rescue Han Solo, a New Republic general.
