# CS-12 Stun Master

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CS-12 Stun Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CS-12_Stun_Master)

The CS-12 Stun Master was a non-lethal weapon that was produced by the SoroSuub Corporation and popular with many Sullustans. The weapons, which were fifteen centimeters in length and cost 575 credits, were cylinders with thirteen contact studs that could deliver an electric shock.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CS-12 Stun Master
- **Manufacturer:** SoroSuub Corporation
- **Culture:** Sullustan
- **Cost:** 575 credits
- **Length:** 15 centimeters
