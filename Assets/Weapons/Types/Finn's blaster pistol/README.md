# Finn's blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Finn's blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Finn%27s_blaster_pistol)

Finn's blaster pistol was a Glie-44 blaster pistol used by Finn, a defected stormtrooper from the First Order who joined the Resistance in 34 ABY to aid them in their war against the First Order. Finn's lifetime of combat training made him adept with a wide variety of small arms, well beyond the standard-issue weaponry of the First Order. The pistol had a power cartridge as ammunition and a reactant gas port cap in its stock. He used it as a sidearm during his Mission to Canto Bight and a mission to Kijimi.
