# Autoturret

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Autoturret](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Autoturret)

Autoturrets were defensive emplacements which could fire at range to kill attackers. The mercenary Saponza advised his partner to construct an additional autoturret in his homestead on the planet Tatooine.
