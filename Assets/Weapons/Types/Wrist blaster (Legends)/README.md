# Wrist blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Wrist blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrist_blaster/Legends)

A wrist blaster was a blaster that was worn and fired from the wrist.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Wrist blaster
- **Type:** Blaster
- **Cost:** 800 credits (Illegal) (wrist-worn model)
- **Shape:** Like a bracelet (wrist-worn model)
- **Size:** Tiny
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg (wrist-worn model)
- **Capacity:**
  - Wrist-worn model:
  -   1 shot
  - Built-in droid model:
  -   50 shots
- **Range:**
  - Wrist-worn model:
  -   Optimum: up to 30 m
  -   Maximum: up to 90 m
  - Built-in droid model:
  -   Optimum: up to 45 meters
  -   Maximum: up to 450 meters
