# DL-18 Blaster Pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DL-18 Blaster Pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DL-18_Blaster_Pistol)

BlasTech Industries' DL-18 Blaster Pistol was an inexpensive blaster commonly used during the time of the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DL-18 Blaster Pistol
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 500 credits; 25 credits per power pack
- **Weight:** 1 kilogram
- **Capacity:** 100 shots
- **Range:**
  - 3—10 meters (short range)
  - 11—30 meters (medium range)
  - 31—120 meters
