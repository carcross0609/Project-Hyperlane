# DT-12 heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DT-12 heavy blaster pistol/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DT-12_heavy_blaster_pistol/Legends)

The DT-12 heavy blaster pistol was a heavy blaster pistol designed for species with long fingers such as Rodians.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DT-12 heavy blaster pistol
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DT-12
- **Type:** Heavy blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 900 credits (Available for military use only)
- **Shape:** Pistol-like
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 2 kg
- **Capacity:**
  - 50 shots
  - Close-range stun setting available
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 90 m
