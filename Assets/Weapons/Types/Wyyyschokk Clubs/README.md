# Wyyyschokk Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wyyyschokk Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wyyyschokk_Clubs)

The Wyyyschokk Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. They were decorated to resemble wyyyschokk spiders.
