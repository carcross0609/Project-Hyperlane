# Viper 2 Grenade Launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Viper 2 Grenade Launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Viper_2_Grenade_Launcher)

The Viper 2 Grenade Launcher was an upgraded version of the original Viper grenade launcher. A favorite among Rebel Alliance soldiers, the primary difference compared to the older model was the Viper 2's much larger magazine. It could fire a variety of grenades.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Viper 2 Grenade Launcher
- **Manufacturer:** Zone Control
- **Type:** Grenade launcher rifle attachment
- **Cost:** 500 credits (launcher); 200 credits (grenades)
- **Capacity:** 5 grenades
- **Range:** 500 meters
