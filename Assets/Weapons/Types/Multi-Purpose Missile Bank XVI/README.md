# Multi-Purpose Missile Bank XVI

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Multi-Purpose Missile Bank XVI](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Multi-Purpose_Missile_Bank_XVI)

The Multi-Purpose Missile Bank XVI was an anti-vehicle/starfighter ground emplacement weapon produced by Bryn & Gweith. Consisting of seven banks of six launchers each, a standard magazine of 36 missiles were held in reserve to be auto-loaded as necessary. These missile banks were linked to a variety of targeting sensors and could use an appropriate missile to attack targets on the ground, in the air or in space. A crew of six was necessary to operate the missile bank although a skeleton crew of three could manage.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Multi-Purpose Missile Bank XVI
- **Manufacturer:** Bryn & Gweith
- **Type:** Multipurpose missile launcher
- **Cost:**
  - 18,000 credits (new)
  - 9,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 6
- **Capacity:** 42 missiles
- **Range:** Low orbit
