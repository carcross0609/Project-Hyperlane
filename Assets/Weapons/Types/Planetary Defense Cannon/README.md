# Planetary Defense Cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Planetary Defense Cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planetary_Defense_Cannon)

The Planetary Defense Cannon was a heavy turbolaser built by Loronar Corporation. It bore an uncanny resemblance to the W-165 planetary turbolaser and there were accusations of corporate espionage. Regardless it used four times as much energy as the W-165 and was capable of completely obliterating an Imperial Star Destroyer. It required a crew of 46 to operate but could get by with a skeleton crew of 21.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Planetary Defense Cannon
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Corporation
- **Type:** Heavy turbolaser surface-to-space cannon
- **Cost:**
  - 13,000,000 credits (new)
  - 6,000,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 46
- **Capacity:** Unlimited (power generator)
- **Range:** High orbit
