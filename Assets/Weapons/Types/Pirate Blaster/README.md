# Pirate Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pirate Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pirate_Blaster)

The Pirate Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This small blaster pistol consisted of variously assembled components, including a bronze, silver, gray, black, and orange base, with the orange portions possessing a wood-like appearance, with yellow and gray decals, a thin side-mounted scope, a red and yellow cord wrapped around the grip, and a wide, almost cannon-like barrel..
