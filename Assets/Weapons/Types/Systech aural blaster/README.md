# Systech aural blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Systech aural blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Systech_aural_blaster)

The Systech aural blaster was a sonic blaster pistol designed by the Systech Corporation. Designed by Systech Corporation weapons manufacturing company, it was an awkward weapon, which was difficult to wield without special training. Shaped like a pistol with auxiliary sonic emitters attached to either side of the barrel, the aural blaster could deafen a target with a successful hit. Though inferior in damage potential to traditional blasters, the blaster's design made it a viable weapon, with its deafening and disorientating capabilities. This weapon dealt a type of damage called sonic damage which meant it could not be deflected by a lightsaber as a normal blaster bolt could.

- **Name:** Systech aural blaster

  - Could not be deflected by a lightsaber
- **Range:**

  - Maximum: up to 120 m
- **Purpose:** To deafen targets with sonic damage
