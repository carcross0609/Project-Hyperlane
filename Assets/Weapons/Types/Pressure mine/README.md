# Pressure mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Pressure mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pressure_mine)

The pressure mine was an explosive placed on the ground. It was mainly utilized as a trap. During or after 18 BBY, CT-9904 "Crosshair" stepped on a pressure mine, but Clone Commander Mayday helped him disarm the explosive.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Pressure mine
- **Type:** Mine
- **Locations:** Barton IV
- **Shape:** Flat triangle
- **Purpose:** Disguised trap
