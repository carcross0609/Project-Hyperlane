# DH-17 short carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DH-17 short carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DH-17_short_carbine)

The DH-17 short carbine, otherwise known as the DH17 Short Carbine, or DH-17 Snubnose, was a subnosed variant of the DH-17 blaster carbine that was used at the time of the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DH-17 short carbine
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DH-17
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
