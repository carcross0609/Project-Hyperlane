# Droid ion blast Mark I

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Droid ion blast Mark I](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid_ion_blast_Mark_I)

The droid ion blast Mark I, also known as the "Scrambler", was a weapon designed by Aratech to destroy the electronics of enemy droids by sending a pulse of highly-charged ions. This weapon was marketed toward beings who were faced with the threat of massive droid armies.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Droid ion blast Mark I"Scrambler"
- **Manufacturer:** Aratech
- **Model:** Mark I
- **Range:** Short
- **Purpose:** Destroy enemy droid electronics
