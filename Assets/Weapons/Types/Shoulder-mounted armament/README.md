# Shoulder-mounted armament

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shoulder-mounted armament](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shoulder-mounted_armament)

Shoulder-mounted armaments were a diverse style of weapon configurations utilizing shoulder and often back attachments that fired some form of over-the-shoulder weaponry intended to free the hands of the user. These armaments were generally autonomous, and sometimes had built-in blaster technology, while others allowed for the attachment and removal of external blaster pistols. The projectile types of these blasters could vary widely, with some firing lasers while others could even launch micro-missiles.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Shoulder-mounted armament
- **Type:** Hands-free weaponry
- **Owners:**
  - Derrown
  - Imara Vex
  - K-A0S
  - Migs Mayfeld
- **Purpose:** Auto-fire a weapon while keeping all hands free
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
