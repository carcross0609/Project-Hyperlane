# Bespin Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Bespin Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bespin_Blaster)

The Bespin Blaster was a modified heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This blaster pistol featured a golden body, barrel, and scope, a small silver plate and connector, and a blue grip with white markings and an almost cloudy white overlayer.

- **Model:** Modified DL-44 heavy blaster pistol
