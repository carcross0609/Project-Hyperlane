# Silencer (superweapon)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Silencer (superweapon)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Silencer_%28superweapon%29)

The Silencer was a superweapon consisting of a rapidly recharging megalaser that could be mounted on a capital ship. This laser was called a "fleet killer" because it had both the power and the speed of fire to destroy several capital ships in rapid succession. Because of this, it was considered a superweapon by the reconstituted Sith Empire.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Silencer
- **Manufacturer:** Sith Empire
- **Type:** Ship-mounted superweapon

- **Owners:** Darth Nox

- **Purpose:** Elimination of enemy fleets
