# FS-1 Farshot sporting blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FS-1 Farshot sporting blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FS-1_Farshot_sporting_blaster_rifle)

The FS-1 Farshot was a long-range sporting blaster rifle that was popular on the frontier. It was less powerful, but had longer range than most other sporting blaster rifles. It was particularly good for situation where the ability to hit the target at longer range was more important than doing great damage at closer ranges.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FS-1 Farshot sporting blaster rifle

- **Model:** FS-1 Farshot
- **Type:** Sporting blaster rifle
- **Cost:** 880 credits
- **Size:** Medium
- **Weight:** 3 kg
- **Capacity:** 50 shots
