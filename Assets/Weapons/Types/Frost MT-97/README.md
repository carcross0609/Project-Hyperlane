# Frost MT-97

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Frost MT-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frost_MT-97)

The Frost MT-97 was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. These white and light gray pistols featured a red Rebel Alliance starbird displayed on the sides of each.
