# Icy Scatter Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Icy Scatter Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Icy_Scatter_Gun)

The Icy Scatter Gun was a scatter gun owned by the Trandoshan Hunter Charr that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard slugthrower. Both fur and bone were bound to the exterior of this gun via weathered straps, with much of the gun colored to resemble a dusting of snow.

- **Type:** Scatter gun
