# D'skar

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [D'skar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/D%27skar)

D'skar were simple, yet durable daggers constructed by Ekkar Armaments on Coyn and easily the most common weapons to be seen in the possession of a Coynite. The blade was named after D'Skar, the innermost planet of the Coyn system.

## Specifications

- **Name:** D'skar
- **Manufacturer:** Ekkar Armaments
- **Model:** Dagger
- **Culture:** Coynite
- **Cost:** 150 credits
- **Size:** Various
- **Length:** 30 centimeters toa half-meter
