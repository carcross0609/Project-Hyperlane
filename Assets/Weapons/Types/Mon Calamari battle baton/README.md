# Mon Calamari battle baton

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mon Calamari battle baton](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Calamari_battle_baton)

The Mon Calamari battle baton was a simple energy weapon in the shape of a ribbed scepter. They were wielded as a status symbol by Mon Calamari officers. One such weapon was wielded by Captain Ackbar during the Second Battle of Mon Calamari.
