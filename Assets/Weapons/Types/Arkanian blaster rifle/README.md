# Arkanian blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Arkanian blaster rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arkanian_blaster_rifle)

The Arkanian blaster rifle was a blaster rifle used during the time of the Mandalorian Wars and its aftermath. The design, created by the Arkanians, could equal that of modern blasters. The one problem faced with these weapons was their lack of upgrade options as they could not accept newer firing chambers and power cells.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Arkanian blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Arkanians
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Range:** 28 meters
