# Bothan stun stick

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Bothan stun stick](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bothan_stun_stick)

A more useful weapon than the stun baton, the Bothan stun stick possessed high density energy cells making it unusable on the off-hand. They were considered good weapons for espionage missions and were very readily available on many worlds.

- **Name:** Bothan stun stick
- **Type:** Stun baton
