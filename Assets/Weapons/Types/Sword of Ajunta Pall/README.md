# Sword of Ajunta Pall

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Sword of Ajunta Pall](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Ajunta_Pall)

The sword of Ajunta Pall was used by the Dark Lord of the Sith Ajunta Pall during the Hundred-Year Darkness, and was laid to rest with him in his tomb on Korriban.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Sword of Ajunta Pall
- **Type:** Double-bladed sword
- **Culture:** Sith
- **Owners:**

  - Revan
- **Locations:** Tomb of Ajunta Pall
- **Material:** Steel
