# PY-6 Proton Torpedo Site

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [PY-6 Proton Torpedo Site](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PY-6_Proton_Torpedo_Site)

The PY-6 Proton Torpedo Site was an anti-capital ship/starfighter ground emplacement weapon, consisting of a massive barrage style array of proton torpedo tubes. It was designed to engage enemy starships in orbit and prevent them from moving into position to begin an orbital bombardment. It was large and hard to conceal, but was capable of inflicting massive amounts of damage on attacking fleets by firing hundreds of torpedoes into orbit. The weapon required a crew of four to operate.

## Specifications

- **Name:** PY-6 Proton Torpedo Site
- **Manufacturer:** Arakyd Industries
- **Type:** Anti-orbital proton torpedo site
- **Cost:**
  - 200,000 credits (new)
  - 75,000 credits (used)
- **Crew:** 3
- **Capacity:** 4 proton torpedoes
- **Range:** High orbit
