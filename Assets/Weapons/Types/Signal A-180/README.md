# Signal A-180

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Signal A-180](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Signal_A-180)

The Signal A-180 was a modified A-180 heavy blaster pistol utilized by the human Hunter Zaina that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard blaster. This weathered blaster pistol featured a metallic brown, silver, and dark gray frame, with a vented barrel, striated brown plates on the sides of the grip, and white and red painted elements.
