# Wesell Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Wesell Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wesell_Applicator)

The Wesell Applicator was a long-range dart gun owned by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard applicator. This applicator resembled the appearance of the infamous bounty hunter Zam Wesell, specifically the outfit she wore just prior to her death, featuring a gray and purple base, with a glowing pink core, pink cloth wrapped around the front of the stock, and an added scope.
