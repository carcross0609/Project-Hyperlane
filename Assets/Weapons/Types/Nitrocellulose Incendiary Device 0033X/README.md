# Nitrocellulose Incendiary Device 0033X

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Nitrocellulose Incendiary Device 0033X](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nitrocellulose_Incendiary_Device_0033X)

The Nitrocellulose Incendiary Device 0033X, more commonly known as the 0033X grenade, was a combat explosive produced by Greff-Timms Industrial. The 0033X was the standard-issue grenade of the Old Republic's rocket jumpers. It was the size of a Human fist and could be set to either explode instantaneously or on a ten-second delay. While some examples survived into the time of the Empire, whether they were still capable of working or not could depend greatly on their condition.
