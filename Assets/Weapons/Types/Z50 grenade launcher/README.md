# Z50 grenade launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Z50 grenade launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z50_grenade_launcher)

The Z50 grenade launcher was a type of grenade launcher produced by Malaxan Firepower Incorporated that costed 1,250 credits. Used by planetary defense forces and mercenaries, the Z50 was a rifle-sized launcher that could send grenades into the center of enemy formations to maximize casualties. It possessed a rotating, revolver-style magazine that could up to six specialized grenades, and was capable of firing slug-like grenades for a launcher of its size, which meant that a variety of payloads were available from manufacturers. The Z50 also came standard with a forward vertical grip and shock-absorbing stock to aid in firing stability, as well as a range-finder optic to help shooters gauge target distance and optimal trajectory.
