# High Republic MT-97

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [High Republic MT-97](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Republic_MT-97)

The High Republic MT-97 was a pair of MT-97 blaster pistols owned by the Mandalorian Hunter Aran Tal that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard blaster pistols. These pistols resembled the aesthetic of the Jedi Order during the High Republic Era, featuring their gold and white color scheme, as well as a part of the High Republic crest.
