# A43 Hushabye

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [A43 Hushabye](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A43_Hushabye)

The A43 Hushabye, or shortened to A43, was a model of blaster pistol. It was owned by Coruscant Security Force Lieutenant Tanivos Exantor Divo. He wore the blaster on the left side of his police utility belt with the handle facing forward. The criminal Lauli Wahlo also owned a A43 Hushabye pistol around that same time, although he added a silencer.
