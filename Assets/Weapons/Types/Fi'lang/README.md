# Fi'lang

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fi'lang](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fi%27lang)

Featuring a curved blade, the fi'lang was the traditional combat knife of the reptilian Trogodile species. In 22 BBY the Trogodile Swyy'm-Ee wielded a pair of fi'langs during a gladiatorial match in the Cauldron, a fighting arena on the planet Rattatak. However, Swyy'm-Ee was killed midway through the match by the Dark Jedi Asajj Ventress.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fi'lang
- **Type:** Combat knife
- **Culture:** Trogodile
- **Owners:** Swyy'm-Ee
- **Locations:** The Cauldron, Rattatak
- **Shape:** Curved blade with sharpened points
