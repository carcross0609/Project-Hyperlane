# Beskar Destroyer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Beskar Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beskar_Destroyer)

The Beskar Destroyer was an E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon owned by the human Hunter Sentinel that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard E-Web.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Beskar Destroyer
- **Model:** E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon (Sentinel's E-Web)
- **Type:** Heavy repeating blaster
- **Owners:** Sentinel
- **Material:** Beskar
- **Markings:** Beskar patterns
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Hunters of the Outer Rim
