# Rat hammer

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rat hammer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rat_hammer)

The rat hammer was a battered white pipe owned by Bansee, the Third Girl of the White Worms, a criminal gang based on the planet Corellia. Bansee earned her rank by proficiency in hunting rats, and her skill with the rat hammer caught the attention of Lady Proxima, the gang's leader. Bansee was carrying the pipe in Coronet City on the planet Corellia in the year 13 BBY around the time that the scrumrat Han fled from the gang.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Rat hammer
- **Type:** Pipe
- **Locations:** Coronet City, Corellia
- **Affiliation:** White Worms
