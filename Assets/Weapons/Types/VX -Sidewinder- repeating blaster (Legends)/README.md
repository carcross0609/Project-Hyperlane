# VX "Sidewinder" repeating blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [VX "Sidewinder" repeating blaster/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VX_%22Sidewinder%22_repeating_blaster/Legends)

The VX "Sidewinder" was a type of Repeating blaster manufactured by SoroSuub Corporation during the Galactic Civil War and cost 3,350 credits. Its high rate of fire was due to its five rotating barrels.
