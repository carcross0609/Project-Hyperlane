# Omega's energy crossbow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Omega's energy crossbow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Omega%27s_energy_crossbow)

Omega's energy crossbow was a modified energy crossbow used by the clone Omega after it was gifted by her clone companion Echo during or after 18 BBY, who had acquired it from one of his many contacts from his work with the Clone Underground. The energy crossbow fired green energy bolts and could easily fold away for travel, as it was more compact then Omega's previous weapon, a Zygerrian energy bow. The weapon was also equipped with a scope and a foregrip. Shortly after she was gifted the weapon, Omega wielded it while she and her squad, Clone Force 99, attempted to escape from Imperial forces on the planet Teth.
