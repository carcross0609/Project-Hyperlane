# Blaster fist

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Blaster fist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blaster_fist)

A blaster fist was a melee weapon which saw use during the time of the Galactic Civil War. The weapon would be gripped by a being's hand, and could be used in hand-to-hand combat. Blaster fists were typically constructed out of steel and copper, and consisted of a vibroblade unit.
