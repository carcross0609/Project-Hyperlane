# LC-70 laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [LC-70 laser cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LC-70_laser_cannon)

The LC-70 laser cannon was a model of laser cannon that could be equipped to starships. Upgrading an EML-850 light freighter previously equipped with the slower LC-60 laser cannons to LC-70s cost 1500 credits and required a X-FP-C28 ship computer, fifteen units of quadanium steel, and seven enhanced durasteel hulls.
