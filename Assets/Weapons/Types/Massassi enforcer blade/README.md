# Massassi enforcer blade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Massassi enforcer blade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Massassi_enforcer_blade)

A Massassi enforcer blade was a two-handed sword used by the Massassi for close combat. After the Battle of Yavin, the dark spirit of Exar Kun used a Massassi enforcer blade to fight a group of spacers who attacked the Cult of Exar Kun in the Temple of Exar Kun on Yavin 4.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Massassi enforcer blade
- **Type:** Sword
- **Culture:** Massassi
- **Purpose:** Melee combat
