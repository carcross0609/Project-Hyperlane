# Darth Vader's electrostaff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Darth Vader's electrostaff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vader%27s_electrostaff)

An electrostaff was wielded by the Sith Lord Darth Vader after he lost control of his Force powers as a result of a Force Wave being released into the galaxy. The weapon was capable of firing beams of energy as well as electricity.
