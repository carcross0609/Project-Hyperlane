# BD-1 Cutter vibro-ax

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BD-1 Cutter vibro-ax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD-1_Cutter_vibro-ax)

The BD-1 Cutter vibro-ax was a long vibro-ax manufactured by the SoroSuub Corporation. Pagetti Rook, a Weequay under the employment of the crime lord Jabba the Hutt, used a BD-1 Cutter vibro-ax to nudge Luke Skywalker into the Great Pit of Carkoon during the rescue of Han Solo. During the mission, Han used one to save Lando Calrissian from falling into the Sarlacc, accidently hitting Boba Fett's Z-6 jetpack, sending him flying and fell into the Sarlacc.
