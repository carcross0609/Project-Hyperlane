# Model 38 sharpshooter's rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Model 38 sharpshooter's rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_38_sharpshooter%27s_rifle)

The Model 38 sharpshooter's rifle was one of Czerka Arms' best-selling firearms of the slugthrower product line, and each were laser measured to accommodate the wielders' natural stance and included a custom-fitted stock, trigger, and optic for optimal shooting comfort. Most often sold to hunters and marksmen, the Model 38 had an incredible accuracy and felt as it they were an extension of the shooter's body.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Model 38 sharpshooter's rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Czerka Arms
- **Model:** Model 38
- **Type:** Slugthrower
- **Cost:** 3,000 credits
- **Range:** Long range
