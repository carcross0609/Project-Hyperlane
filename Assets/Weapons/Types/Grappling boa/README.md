# Grappling boa

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Grappling boa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grappling_boa)

The grappling boa was a long and flexible weapon made of triple rows of metallic scales. Also called a scaled boa, it was both a stylish decoration and a practical weapon. It was named after a variety of snake, and could be used like a whip or lariat in hand-to-hand combat. When not used in combat, the boa could be draped around the shoulders as a fashionable scarf. During the Clone Wars, the Theelin bounty hunter Latts Razzi wielded triple rows of a grappling boa as her preferred weapon.

## Specifications

- **Type:** Whip/lariat
- **Owners:** Latts Razzi
