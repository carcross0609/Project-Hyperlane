# DLT cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [DLT cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DLT_cannon)

The DLT cannon was a custom blaster that was based on the DLT chassis but modified for greater firepower. It was a large cannon and was the length of an adolescent human boy. The Ithorian gangster Herf wielded a DLT cannon during a confrontation with the teenage junk dealer Temmin Wexley. The cannon was destroyed during a fight with Wexley's B1-series battle droid bodyguard "Mister Bones," who sliced it into three pieces with his vibroblade arm.
