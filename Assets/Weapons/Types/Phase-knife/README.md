# Phase-knife

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Phase-knife](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phase-knife)

Phase-knives were knives developed on the planet Philaxia. They were popular before the planet was discovered by the Galactic Republic. They appeared as only a knife hilt but when activated it projected a weightless but solid blade. Its blade was sharper than a vibroblade, but its balance made it difficult to wield.
