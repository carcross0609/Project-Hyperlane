# Proton charge

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Proton charge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proton_charge)

A proton charge was an explosive device capable of significant destructive power. It could be fitted to the Spiral-class Assault Ship. It detonated upon the ship's impact on a target, disrupting the deflector shields long enough for the Spiral to penetrate and bury itself in the target's hull.
