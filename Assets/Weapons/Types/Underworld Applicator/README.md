# Underworld Applicator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Underworld Applicator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Applicator)

The Underworld Applicator, also known as Skora's applicator or Skora's dart gun, was a specialized long-range dart gun utilized by the Rodian Hunter Skora that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara. The weapon fired stim and poison darts filled with small doses of Skora's unique concoctions, quickly able to switch between the two ammo types, though this was a tricky mechanism that only Skora knew how to manipulate.
