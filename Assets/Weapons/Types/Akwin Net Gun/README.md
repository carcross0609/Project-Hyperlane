# Akwin Net Gun

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Akwin Net Gun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Akwin_Net_Gun)

The Akwin Net Gun was a rifle-like variant of net gun utilized by the Akwin, an aquatic species residing on the planet Lazerian IV. It fired metal mesh nets that restrained and stunned their victims with an electric shock. Each gun carried two nets.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Akwin Net Gun
- **Type:** Net gun
- **Shape:** Rifle-like
- **Material:** Metal (nets)
- **Capacity:** 2 nets
- **Purpose:** Restraining and stunning targets
- **Affiliation:** Akwin
