# Point-defense laser cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Point-defense laser cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Point-defense_laser_cannon/Legends)

A point-defense laser cannon, also called a point-defense cannon when not a laser cannon, was a kind of starship emplacement, typically laser cannons, blaster cannons or light ion cannons, that were used to defend large capital starships against smaller starfighters. Point-defense cannons were also used to defend against missiles. Adumari Meteor-class Aerial Forts had their laser cannons optimized for shooting down incoming missiles in this way.
