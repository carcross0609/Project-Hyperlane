# Ion grenade

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Ion grenade](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ion_grenade)

Ion grenades were hand-held explosive devices that sent out an electric shock and delivered massive damage against vehicles, shields, and droids. Its time until detonation was based on the proximity to its target. Models of the ion grenade included the "Lightning" 22 Ion Grenade.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ion grenade
- **Type:** Grenade
- **Cost:** 65 credits
- **Size:** Hand-held
- **Capacity:** Single use
- **Range:** Thrown weapon
- **Purpose:** Disabling vehicles, shields, and droids.
