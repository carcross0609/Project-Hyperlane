# CryoBan rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [CryoBan rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CryoBan_rifle)

The CryoBan rifle, manufactured by Loronar Corporation, was a model of rifle made in the tradition of Ulban Arms and several other manufacturing companies who have weaponized CryoBan technology. Capable of flash freezing targets, the CryoBan rifle made inorganic substances particularly brittle. Mercenaries engaging battle droids often brought at least one such rifle to make the droids vulnerable to allied attacks. The rifle was also popular among bounty hunters, firefighters, and scientists.

## Specifications

- **Name:** CryoBan rifle
- **Manufacturer:** Loronar Corporation
- **Model:** Rifle
- **Type:** CryoBan
- **Cost:** 450 credits
- **Range:** Medium-ranged
