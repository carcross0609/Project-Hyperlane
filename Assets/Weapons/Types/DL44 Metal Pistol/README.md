# DL44 Metal Pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DL44 Metal Pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DL44_Metal_Pistol)

The DL44 Metal Pistol, also stylized as the DL 44 Metal Pistol, was a type of blaster pistol used during the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic. It was a lighter version of the DL-44 XT, and was similar in design to the DL-44 heavy blaster pistol.

## Specifications

- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DL-44
- **Type:** Blaster pistol
