# DC-15A blaster rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DC-15A blaster rifle/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DC-15A_blaster_rifle/Legends)

The DC-15A blaster rifle was the weapon of choice for the Galactic Republic's clone troopers in the Grand Army of the Republic. A version with a wooden stock was the standard weapon of the Senate Commandos. It was a larger and more powerful long-range alternative to the DC-15S.

## Specifications

- **Name:** DC-15A blaster rifle
- **Manufacturer:** BlasTech Industries
- **Model:** DC-15A blaster rifle
- **Type:** Blaster rifle
- **Cost:** Not available for public sale
- **Length:** 1.3 meters
- **Weight:** 4.4 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 500 shots
- **Range:** 10 kilometers (on tripod)
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Republic
  - Galactic Empire
