# Acid Stream Launcher

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Acid Stream Launcher](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Acid_Stream_Launcher)

Acid Stream Launchers were a type of kinetic-based heavy weapon commonly used by commandos during the time of the Galactic Civil War which projected a high-velocity stream of acid. They were available in a couple different designs, including a rifle-like configuration, and a more tubular, over the shoulder firing design. Another variant was the Devastator Acid Launcher.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Acid Stream Launcher
- **Type:** Heavy weapon
- **Size:** Large
