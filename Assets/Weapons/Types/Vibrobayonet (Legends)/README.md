# Vibrobayonet

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Vibrobayonet/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vibrobayonet/Legends)

A vibrobayonet was an advanced version of the bayonet. It consisted of a vibroblade that attached to the barrel of a blaster rifle or other rifle, thus turning into a powerful melee weapon. A vibrobayonet detached from a rifle could be used as a vibrodagger.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Vibrobayonet
- **Type:**
  - Melee weapon
  - Vibro-weapon
- **Culture:** Soldiers and Infantry
- **Cost:** 350 credits
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Purpose:** Use rifle as spear-like melee weapon
