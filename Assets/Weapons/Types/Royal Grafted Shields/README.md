# Royal Grafted Shields

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Royal Grafted Shields](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Royal_Grafted_Shields)

The Royal Grafted Shields were a pair of shields owned by the KX-series security droid Hunter K-A0S that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard Grafted Twin Shields. When combined, these shields took on the appearance of the elevator doors in Emperor Palpatine's throne room in his tower on the second Death Star.
