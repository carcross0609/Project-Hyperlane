# Armored Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Armored Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armored_Rifle)

The Armored Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle featured a silver and black frame, with blue and red decals, and sandy-colored weathering, resembling the armor worn by the Gen'Dai bounty hunter Durge.
