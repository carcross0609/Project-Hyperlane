# Gladius vibro-shortsword

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gladius vibro-shortsword](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gladius_vibro-shortsword)

The Gladius vibro-shortsword was a model of vibrosword produced by SoroSuub Corporation. The sword was available in the period following the Battle of Yavin during the Galactic Civil War between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire and cost 750 credits.
