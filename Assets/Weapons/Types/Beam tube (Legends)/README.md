# Beam tube

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Beam tube/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beam_tube/Legends)

Beam tubes were early energy weapons, developed prior to the formation of the Galactic Empire. The precursor to the modern blaster, beam tubes were most often associated with the empire of Xim the Despot.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Beam tube
- **Type:** Energy weapon
- **Created:** Over 20,000 years prior to the Galactic Civil War
- **Weight:** 30 kilograms
- **Capacity:** 100 shots
- **Range:** 50 meters
