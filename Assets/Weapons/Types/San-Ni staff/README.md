# San-Ni staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [San-Ni staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/San-Ni_staff)

The San-Ni staff was a melee weapon favored by martial artists throughout the galaxy, including Jedi Weapon Masters who used the weapon as an alternative to the lightsaber. It consisted of three metal rods held together by power couplings.

## Specifications

- **Name:** San-Ni staff
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Cost:** 4,500 credits
- **Shape:** Flexible staff
- **Size:** Two-handed weapon
- **Weight:** 2.2 kg
- **Protection:** Power couplings impervious to lightsabers
- **Purpose:** Melee combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Martial artists
  - Jedi Weapon Masters
