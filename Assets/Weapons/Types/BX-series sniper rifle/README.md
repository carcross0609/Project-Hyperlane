# BX-series sniper rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [BX-series sniper rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BX-series_sniper_rifle)

The BX-series sniper rifle was a type of sniper rifle used by BX-series droid commandos. In 21 BBY, during the Battle of Saleucami, Clone Captain Rex was sniped while leading his men through the Saleucami wetlands. A Death Watch assassin sent by Pre Vizsla, the leader of the Death Watch, used a BX-series sniper rifle to assassinate Duchess Satine Kryze. Although the weapon was destroyed by Obi-Wan Kenobi, using his lightsaber to cut the weapon in half.
