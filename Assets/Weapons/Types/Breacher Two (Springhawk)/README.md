# Breacher Two (Springhawk)

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Breacher Two (Springhawk)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Breacher_Two_%28Springhawk%29)

Breacher Two was a breacher missile launcher used by the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet Chiss heavy cruiser Springhawk. It was damaged during a battle against the task force of the Kilji General Crofyp, in 18 BBY. After the task force's defeat, Chiss Lieutenant Commander Brisch reported the damage sustained during the battle to the Springhawk commanding officer, Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo, core name "Thrawn."
