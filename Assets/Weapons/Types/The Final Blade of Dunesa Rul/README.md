# The Final Blade of Dunesa Rul

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [The Final Blade of Dunesa Rul](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Final_Blade_of_Dunesa_Rul)

The Final Blade of Dunesa Rul was a durasteel dagger that was an important artefact in Mandalorian culture, despite the fact that it had been lost ever since the death of its creator, the master bladesmith Dunesa Rul. Dunesa Rul's weapons were highly prized, and both Clan Mettir and Clan Ironstone attacked each other in an attempt to possess Dunesa's latest work, however the altercation between the two clans killed Dunesa as well, leading to this dagger becoming his final work. Both Clan Mettir and Clan Ironstone were hunted down and destroyed in retaliation. The dagger itself was never found.
