# Clay Grafted Shields

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Clay Grafted Shields](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clay_Grafted_Shields)

The Clay Grafted Shields were a pair of shields owned by the KX-series security droid Hunter K-A0S that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to her standard Grafted Twin Shields. This shield resembled elements of the large stone guardian droids utilized by the Frangawl Cult on the moon Zardossa Stix.
