# TX-2020

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [TX-2020](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TX-2020)

The TX-2020 was a model of double laser cannon manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. It was an anti-vehicle laser emplacement used for protecting bases and installations. The Alliance base Dalgeer's Rim used a concealed TX-2020 as part of its defensive setup. The emplacement was protected by a pair of shield doors and was recessed into the rim of a volcano caldera.

## Specifications

- **Name:** TX-2020
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
- **Type:** Anti-vehicle laser emplacement
- **Cost:** 12,000 credits
- **Capacity:** Power generator
- **Range:** 600 meters
- **Purpose:** Anti-vehicle weapon
