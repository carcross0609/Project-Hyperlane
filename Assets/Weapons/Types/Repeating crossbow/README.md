# Repeating crossbow

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Repeating crossbow](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Repeating_crossbow)

The repeating crossbow was a crossbow made of modern materials capable of firing multiple shots.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Repeating crossbow
- **Manufacturer:** Drolan Plasteel
- **Type:** Repeating crossbow
- **Cost:** 400 credits
- **Shape:** A cross between a bowcaster and a rifle
- **Size:** One-handed weapon
- **Material:** Lightweight plasteel
- **Weight:** 1.2 kg
- **Capacity:** 10 arrows
- **Range:**
  - Optimum: up to 30 m
  - Maximum: up to 120 m
