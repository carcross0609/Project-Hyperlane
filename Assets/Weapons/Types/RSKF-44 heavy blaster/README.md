# RSKF-44 heavy blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [RSKF-44 heavy blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/RSKF-44_heavy_blaster)

The RSKF-44 heavy blaster was a model of heavy blaster pistol produced by BlasTech Industries that was of a two-barreled blaster design, firing two blaster bolts per shot. It also had a power ampule socket. It was used by Tobias Beckett until it was destroyed while fighting Enfys Nest. Later Beckett used a black RSKF-44, killing several Pyke sentinels on Kessel and killing Dryden Vos's Hylobon Enforcers on Savareen. In his final stand off with Han Solo, Beckett was about to kill Solo but Solo shot him first and Beckett dropped the blaster in defeat before dying.
