# Kanan's knife

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Kanan's knife](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kanan%27s_knife)

Kanan's knife was a knife owned by Kanan Jarrus and later Sabine Wren. Jarrus used the knife to cut his hair and shave his beard, shortly before the rescue of Hera Syndulla. The knife was later collected by Sabine Wren, and it became symbolic and important to her. She used the knife to cut her hair, before rejoining her master Ahsoka Tano.
