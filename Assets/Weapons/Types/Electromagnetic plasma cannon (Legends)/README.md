# Electromagnetic plasma cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Electromagnetic plasma cannon/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electromagnetic_plasma_cannon/Legends)

The electromagnetic plasma cannon was a cannon utilized by the Umbaran militia on its mobile heavy cannon, hover tank, and starfighter. They were noted for their power and destruction. A notable example of the sheer might of these cannons was when a few clone troopers used them to blast open the blast doors of the Separatist supply ship.
