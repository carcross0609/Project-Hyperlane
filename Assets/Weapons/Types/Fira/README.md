# Fira

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Fira](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fira)

The Fira was the traditional weapon of Selkath warriors belonging to Order of Shasa on the planet Manaan. The Fira was a curved sword able to inflict grievous wounds, every one forged by each member of the Order.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Fira
- **Type:** Melee weapon
- **Culture:** Selkath
- **Creators:** Order of Shasa members
- **Cost:** 100 credits
- **Shape:** Curved sword
- **Weight:** 2 kg
- **Protection:** Cortosis alloy to resist a lightsaber
- **Affiliation:** Order of Shasa
