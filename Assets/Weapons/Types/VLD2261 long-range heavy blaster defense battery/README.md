# VLD2261 long-range heavy blaster defense battery

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [VLD2261 long-range heavy blaster defense battery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/VLD2261_long-range_heavy_blaster_defense_battery)

A VLD2261 long-range heavy blaster defense battery was a type of blaster cannon. At least two such batteries defended the Grand Republic Medical Facility on Coruscant, where Darth Vader was reconstructed after his duel with Obi-Wan Kenobi on Mustafar. The facility's batteries were controlled by Red Guards.
