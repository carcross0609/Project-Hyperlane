# SM-01 High Explosive land mine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [SM-01 High Explosive land mine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SM-01_High_Explosive_land_mine)

The SM-01 High Explosive land mine was a model of anti-personnel mine available in the period following the Battle of Yavin during the Galactic Civil War between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire and cost 850 credits.

## Specifications

- **Name:** SM-01 High Explosive land mine
