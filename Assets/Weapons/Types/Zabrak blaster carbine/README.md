# Zabrak blaster carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Zabrak blaster carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zabrak_blaster_carbine)

The Zabrak blaster carbine was a blaster available in the year 3956 BBY. but one that suffered from the inability to be upgraded due to the complexity involved in its construction. Fortunately, its sheer power made the need of an upgrade unnecessary.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Zabrak blaster carbine
- **Manufacturer:** Zabraks
- **Type:** Blaster carbine
- **Range:** 25 meters
