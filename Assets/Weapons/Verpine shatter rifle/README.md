# Verpine shatter rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Verpine shatter rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verpine_shatter_rifle)

The Verpine shatter rifle was a type of shatter gun created by the insectoid Verpine. The rifle used magnetic fields to fire small slugs at high speeds unattainable by typical slugthrowers, and produced a distinctive, audible crack as their metal-alloy projectiles broke the speed of sound. And while their rounds were relatively small, the speed with which they impacted a target inflicted great kinetic damage.
