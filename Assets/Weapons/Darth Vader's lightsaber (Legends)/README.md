# Darth Vader's lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Darth Vader's lightsaber/Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Vader%27s_lightsaber/Legends)

Darth Vader's lightsaber was the weapon created and used by the Sith Lord Darth Vader from 19 BBY to 4 ABY.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Darth Vader's lightsaber
- **Model:** Dual-phase Sith lightsaber
- **Type:** Single-blade
- **Culture:** Sith
- **Creator:** Darth Vader
- **Created:** 19 BBY
- **Destroyed:** 4 ABY (39:3), Death Star II
- **Owners:**
  - Darth Vader
  - Starkiller (stolen)
- **Hilt Shape:** Cylindrical, straight
- **Hilt Material:** Alloy metal and carbon composite
- **Crystal:** Synthetic lightsaber crystal, later Adegan crystal
- **Color:** Red
- **Blade Length:** Adjustable
- **Capacity:** Diatium power cell
- **Precede:** Anakin Skywalker's second lightsaber
- **Purpose:** Lightsaber combat
- **Affiliation:**
  - Galactic Empire
  - Order of the Sith Lords
