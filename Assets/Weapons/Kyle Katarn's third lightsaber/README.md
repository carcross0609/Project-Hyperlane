# Kyle Katarn's third lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Kyle Katarn's third lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyle_Katarn%27s_third_lightsaber)

This blue-bladed lightsaber was created and used by Jedi Battlemaster Kyle Katarn after he joined the Jedi Praxeum on Yavin 4. He later left it behind when he abandoned the Jedi, only to take it up again during the Empire Reborn crisis. This lightsaber had a longer hilt and two handgrips.
