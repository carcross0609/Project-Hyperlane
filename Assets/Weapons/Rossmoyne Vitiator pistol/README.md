# Rossmoyne Vitiator pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Rossmoyne Vitiator pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rossmoyne_Vitiator_pistol)

The Rossmoyne Vitiator pistol was a model of bolt-thrower blaster pistol from an elegant age of craftsmanship. Rossmoyne pistols were created during an elegant age of the galaxy when art and craftsmanship was put into such designs. The original family responsible for their creation had artisans who hand-engraved scrollwork on each Vitiator that came off the production line. This work included whorls and curves on the grip.
