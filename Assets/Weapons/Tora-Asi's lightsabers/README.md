# Tora-Asi's lightsabers

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Tora-Asi's lightsabers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tora-Asi%27s_lightsabers)

Tora-Asi's lightsabers were two green-bladed lightsabers utilized by the Jedi Lily Tora-Asi during the High Republic Era. The two lightsabers were identical and had distinctive handguards.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Tora-Asi's lightsabers
- **Type:** Two single-bladed handguarded lightsabers
- **Culture:** Jedi
- **Owners:** Lily Tora-Asi
- **Crystal:** Kyber crystal
- **Color:** Green
- **Affiliation:** Jedi Order
