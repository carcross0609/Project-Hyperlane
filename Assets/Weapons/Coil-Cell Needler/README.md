# Coil-Cell Needler

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Coil-Cell Needler](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coil-Cell_Needler)

Coil-Cell Needlers were a variety of blaster pistol available during the Cold War between the reconstituted Sith Empire and the Galactic Republic. In the year 3643 BBY, the Arcona smuggler known as "Lefty" Grandarak owned a pistol of this make, although he was willing to give it away to an upcoming young smuggler. The pistols were bronze in color and could be sold for 105 credits. Parts of the pistol were modifiable, including its barrel and color crystal.

- **Type:** Blaster pistol
- **Cost:** 105 credits
