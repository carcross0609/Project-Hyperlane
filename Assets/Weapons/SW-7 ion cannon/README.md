# SW-7 ion cannon

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [SW-7 ion cannon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SW-7_ion_cannon)

The SW-7 ion cannon was an ion cannon manufactured by ArMek that was equipped to the MC80 Star Cruiser. During the Battle of Endor, two dozen of these cannons were equipped to every MC80 Star Cruiser.
