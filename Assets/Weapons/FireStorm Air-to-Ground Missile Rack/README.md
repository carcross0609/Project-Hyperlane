# FireStorm Air-to-Ground Missile Rack

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [FireStorm Air-to-Ground Missile Rack](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FireStorm_Air-to-Ground_Missile_Rack)

The FireStorm Air-to-Ground Missile Rack was a model of starship-mounted missile launcher. The racks were retractable and held two-hundred and fifty missiles that could be fire singly or in one salvo. The HT-2200 medium freighter Reekeene's Retribution mounted two FireStorm launchers. The launchers could only be fired in atmosphere.

## Specifications

- **Name:** FireStorm Air-to-Ground Missile Rack
- **Type:** Missile launcher
- **Range:** 800 meters
