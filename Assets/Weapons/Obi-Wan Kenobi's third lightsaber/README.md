# Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi%27s_third_lightsaber)

Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber was the third, and final, lightsaber to be constructed and wielded by Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi. Kenobi wielded the saber during the Clone Wars and the Great Jedi Purge as well as the first, two intermediate, and final duels against Darth Vader.
