# Nomad Firestorm

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Nomad Firestorm](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nomad_Firestorm)

The Nomad Firestorm was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle's frame consisted of dark gray and silver metal components in the front, as well as brown wooden elements in the back, feature a painted red streak that spanned both. The rifle was bound with tan and brown strips of cloth, as well as dark red cords.
