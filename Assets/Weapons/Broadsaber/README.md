# Broadsaber

**Kind:** Lightsaber  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Broadsaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Broadsaber)

The broadsaber was a type of specialized lightsaber model. Due to specialized focusing lenses and emitters, the broadsaber's hilt made a blade that was broader and flatter than a standard lightsaber's cylindrical blade, and as such, gave it a profile that was closer to that of a traditional metal sword. The broadsaber provided a larger overall surface for the blade, while losing the advantages of an omnidirectional cutting edge.
