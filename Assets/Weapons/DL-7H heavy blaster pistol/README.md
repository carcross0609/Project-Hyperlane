# DL-7H heavy blaster pistol

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [DL-7H heavy blaster pistol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DL-7H_heavy_blaster_pistol)

The DL-7H heavy blaster pistol was a medium-ranged model of heavy blaster pistol made by BlasTech Industries, and sold for 850 credits. The pistol was extremely powerful and drew heavily from its power pack, which gave it enough power to punch through laminated armor. The heavy draw drained the pak quickly however, and also meant the weapon lacked a stun setting. This earned it a deserved reputation as being deadly, and the weapons were outlawed or restricted even on some planets that normally only had very lax weaponry laws. DL-7Hs were favored by large numbers of pirates and smugglers during the Galactic Civil War between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic.
