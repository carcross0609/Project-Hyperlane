# Ultra-power blast

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Ultra-power blast](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ultra-power_blast)

The ultra-power blast was a modification that allowed laser cannons to release their entire energy stores in one shot, thus creating a boosted energy bolt. It was installed by the Rebel Alliance on the modified TIE/LN starfighters of Flying Bantha Squadron.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Ultra-power blast
- **Capacity:** 6 shots at standard strength, followed by 1 boosted energy bolt
- **Affiliation:**
  - Alliance to Restore the Republic
  -   Flying Bantha Squadron
