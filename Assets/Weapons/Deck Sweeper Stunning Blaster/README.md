# Deck Sweeper Stunning Blaster

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Deck Sweeper Stunning Blaster](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deck_Sweeper_Stunning_Blaster)

The Deck Sweeper Stunning Blaster was a stun blaster manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc. during the Galactic Civil War. Based on BlasTech Industries' Firespray DL-87 stun rifle, the Deck Sweeper only fired on the stun setting and had no lethal option. The Deck Sweeper was designed to stun a large number of targets at once; the wide barrel at the front of the weapon fired in a forty-five degree arc with a range up to ten meters.

- **Name:** Deck Sweeper Stunning Blaster
- **Manufacturer:** Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.
