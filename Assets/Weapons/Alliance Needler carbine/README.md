# Alliance Needler carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Alliance Needler carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance_Needler_carbine)

The Alliance Needler carbine was a small carbine firing small sharp darts effective against personal shields. Used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War, it had a maximum range of 50 meters. Uff Wogo owned at least one of these before he was killed by "Project Durge", who then gave the weapon to a spacer working for Boba Fett. The spacer handed this weapon over to Fett as proof that Uff was dead, then Fett returned it to him.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Alliance Needler carbine
- **Type:** Slugthrower carbine
- **Range:** 50 meters
- **Affiliation:** Alliance to Restore the Republic
