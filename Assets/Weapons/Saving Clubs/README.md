# Saving Clubs

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Saving Clubs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saving_Clubs)

The Saving Clubs were a pair of clubs owned and utilized by the Wookiee Hunter Grozz that he could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition on Vespaara as an alternative to his standard KX Clubs. This pair of clubs consisted of the arms of an unidentified model of droid, featuring circular logos on each shoulder, as well as the word "free" in aurebesh painted over one.

## Specifications

- **Name:** Saving Clubs
- **Model:** Grozz's Clubs
- **Type:** Club
- **Owners:** Grozz
- **Inscription:** "Free" in aurebesh
- **Purpose:** Combat
- **Affiliation:** Hunters of the Outer Rim
