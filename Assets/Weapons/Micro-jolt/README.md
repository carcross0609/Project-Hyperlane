# Micro-jolt

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Micro-jolt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Micro-jolt)

A micro-jolt was a small handheld weapon. When the Serp King S'Shah lost a bout against the Human Luke Skywalker during the Doom-Joust tourney of the planet Serphidi, the monarch attempted to kill Skywalker with a micro-jolt he had hidden on his person. However, Skywalker blasted the King with an energy lance, killing him.

- **Name:** Micro-jolt
- **Type:** Ranged weapon
- **Culture:** Serp
- **Locations:** Serphidi
