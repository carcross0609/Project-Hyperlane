# IDX-9 Ion Stunner

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [IDX-9 Ion Stunner](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IDX-9_Ion_Stunner)

The IDX-9 Ion Stunner was an ion stunner produced by Tenloss. The IDX-9 incorporated a collapsable focusing dish, a barrel-mounted dish required to focus an ionic flash, that enabled it to be worn in a normal blaster holster. Without its safety on, the IDX-9 instantly expanded when drawn, although difficulties with Tenloss' unique focusing dish caused the weapon to jam. Tenloss' IDX-9 could fire out to a medium range and cost 300 credits.

## Specifications
