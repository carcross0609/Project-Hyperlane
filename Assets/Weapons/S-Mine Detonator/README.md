# S-Mine Detonator

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [S-Mine Detonator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-Mine_Detonator)

S-Mine Detonators, or Photon Grenades, were detonators used by the Eighth Brother, an Imperial Inquisitor. He was equipped with the small detonators on multiple occasions, including early in the Imperial Era and on a search for the former Sith Lord Maul on the planet Malachor in 3 BBY. When the Eighth Brother encountered the Jedi Kanan Jarrus, his apprentice Ezra Bridger, and the former Jedi Ahsoka Tano on Malachor, the outnumbered Inquisitor threw three S-Mine Detonators at them, allowing himself to retreat.
