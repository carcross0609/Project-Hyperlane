# Staff

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Staff](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Staff)

A staff was a stick or rod that could be used to assist a being in walking or as melee weapon which could be used for offense or self-defense. A staff could also be a symbol of office or serve as a religious icon.
