# Gand discharger

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Gand discharger](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gand_discharger)

A Gand discharger was an extremely deadly weapon that was capable of paralyzing an enemy just as easily as it was slaying them. They were considered the pinnacle of Gand technology and, as such, were rare items which were treasured by the few Gand who possessed them. Developed around 4000 BBY, the discharger was considered a sturdy and long-lasting weapon.

- **Type:** Stun baton
