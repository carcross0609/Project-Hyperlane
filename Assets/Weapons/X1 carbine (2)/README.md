# X1 carbine

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [X1 carbine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X1_carbine)

The X1 carbine was a disruptor carbine weapon produced by the Wookiees. A variant of this weapon called the Malevolent bowcaster also existed. Non-Wookiee spacers were known to obtain modified versions of the weapon from the Avatar Orbital Platform. This weapon was used during the Battle of Kashyyyk to defend the tree-city of Kachirho.

- **Name:** X1 carbine
- **Type:** Disruptor carbine
- **Creators:** Wookiees
