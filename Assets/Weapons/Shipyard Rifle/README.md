# Shipyard Rifle

**Kind:** Model  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shipyard Rifle](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shipyard_Rifle)

The Shipyard Rifle was a modified blaster rifle utilized by the human Hunter Imara Vex that she could wield for the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition in the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara as an alternative to her standard rifle. This blaster rifle consisted of a black, dark gray, and silver frame, covered in neon blue and purple paint splashes, as well as a trio of red triangles with black borders printed on each side, and white strips of cloth bound around the grip, stock, and base of the barrel.
