# Mission Vao

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Mission Vao](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_Vao)

Mission Vao (pronounced /'mɪʃɪn 'veo/) was a female Rutian Twi'lek who lived as a street urchin on the ecumenopolis planet Taris during the Mandalorian Wars and the Jedi Civil War, with her older brother, Griff Vao. In 3963 BBY, Mission helped out the Jedi Zayne Carrick by disarming his attacker, Brejik. After Griff had left Taris with dancing girl Lena, Mission became the closest friend of the exiled Wookiee Zaalbar.
