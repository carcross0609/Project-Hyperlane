# Shaak Ti

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Canon  
**Source:** [Shaak Ti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shaak_Ti)

Shaak Ti ( pronounced /ʃɑk ti/) was a Force-sensitive Togruta female Jedi Master who served as one of the final members of the Jedi High Council before the Great Jedi Purge. Highly regarded amongst the Jedi Order for her wisdom, Ti excelled as a teacher and was a highly skilled combatant and swordsman, as well as a devoted believer in the ways of the Jedi. Ti ascended to the prestigious High Council following the death of Master Yaddle, and retained her seat until the Order's dissolution at the end of the Clone Wars. During the Clone Wars, a galaxy-wide conflict between the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, Ti was given the rank of Jedi General in the Grand Army of the Republic and took part in the pivotal battles of Geonosis, Kamino, and Coruscant, but spent much of the war stationed on Kamino, where she oversaw the production and training of the Republic's clone soldiers.
