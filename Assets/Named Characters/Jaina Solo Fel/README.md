# Jaina Solo Fel

**Kind:** Individual  
**Continuity:** Legends  
**Source:** [Jaina Solo Fel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina_Solo_Fel)

Jaina Solo Fel was a Force-sensitive Human female who was a Jedi Master of the New Jedi Order. She eventually earned herself a seat on the New Jedi High Council, and was named Sword of the Jedi by her uncle, Grand Master Luke Skywalker, for her courageous acts during the Yuuzhan Vong invasion. She was the twin sister of Jacen Solo, the older sister of Anakin Solo, and the wife of pilot Jagged Fel.
